## Common questions

**How often is content updated?**
Five times daily — at 04:00, 08:00, 12:00, 17:00, and 22:00 UTC.

**How do I enable daily briefing notifications?**
In the iOS or Android app, open Settings (the gear icon) and set Daily Briefing to "on."

**How do I listen to the audio briefing?**
Tap the play button in the app header. The briefing is published each morning.

**Can I read articles offline?**
Yes. Articles are cached on your device and available without an internet connection.

**How do I report a correction?**
Email [contact@zuhd.news](mailto:contact@zuhd.news) with the article title and the correction.
