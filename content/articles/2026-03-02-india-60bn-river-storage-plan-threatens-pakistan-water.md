$60 billion will give India control of Pakistan's rivers. The plan would raise India's storage on rivers flowing to Pakistan from 15 days of flow to 2 months — enough to cause drought or flood, Pakistani officials warn. Pakistan proposed 4 counter-dams along the Chenab, but the 1960 Indus Waters Treaty's dispute process takes years.
