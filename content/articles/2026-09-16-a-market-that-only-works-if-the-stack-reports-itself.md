Surat — One factory cut particulate emissions by 30% after joining a pollution market.

The scheme could spread to Rio de Janeiro, [Brazil](country:BR)'s first market of its kind in South America.

Continuous stack monitors report emissions automatically, so the cap is enforced by hardware, not inspectors.

Rio must still decide its cap, monitoring system and which pollutants the market covers.
