Citi, Deloitte, and PwC emptied Gulf offices. Iran named US- and Israel-linked economic centres as military targets, ending Dubai's role as the neutral hub where Iranian, Israeli, and American capital coexisted. No firm has given a timeline for return, and banks including HSBC have also shuttered Gulf branches.
