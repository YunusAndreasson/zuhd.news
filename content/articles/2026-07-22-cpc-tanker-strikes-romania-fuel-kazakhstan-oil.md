Bucharest — [Kazakhstan](country:KZ) halted oil piped through Novorossiysk.

The CPC pipeline carries 80% of Kazakh oil exports through one Black Sea terminal, supplying 63% of Romania's imports, an economist said.

Kazakhstan may seek damages; Romania sources the rest from Azerbaijan, Norway and Libya.
