Mecca — Three countries signed a mutual defense pact Friday.

[Pakistan](country:PK) gains a Gulf security patron as [Egypt](country:EG) risks losing regional leadership.

[Turkey](country:TR) and [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) joined Pakistan in pledging joint defense and, implicitly, a nuclear umbrella.

Turkey says Egypt remains a candidate to join, calling it a "natural partner."
