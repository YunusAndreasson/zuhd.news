US intelligence found Iran's government stable, not collapsing. Multiple assessments provide "consistent analysis" that Tehran retains public control — directly undercutting the stated US aim of forcing capitulation through bombardment. The Trump administration has not stated when or whether it will revise its Iran strategy in light of its own intelligence.
