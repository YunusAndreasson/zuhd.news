11 South Africans are returning from Russia's war in Ukraine. Recruited with promises of lucrative jobs, the men ended up on Russia's front lines until President Cyril Ramaphosa negotiated their release with Russian President Vladimir Putin. South African authorities are investigating 3 suspects linked to the recruitment pipeline, including the daughter of a former South African president.
