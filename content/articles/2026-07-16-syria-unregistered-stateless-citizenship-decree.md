Qamishli — A medical degree could not get her an ID card.

Helbest Mohammed is among 517,000 Kurds a rushed 1962 census left stateless; a 2011 decree naturalized some but excluded her "unregistered" category, Syrians for Truth and Justice found.

A January 2026 decree opened a citizenship path her family is still navigating.
