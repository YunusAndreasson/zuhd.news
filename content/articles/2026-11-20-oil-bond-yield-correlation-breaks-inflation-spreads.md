United States — 10-year Treasuries still yield above 5% while oil falls.

Governments and households keep paying high borrowing costs after crude's retreat.

The rolling correlation between oil and bonds fell from 65% in September to under 20%, Cboe analysis shows.

"Inflation typically is a virus," said Greg Peters, co-chief investment officer at PGIM Credit.

The Federal Reserve meets in December, and traders expect at least another quarter-point rate rise.
