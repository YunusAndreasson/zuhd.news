Myanmar — Landmines kill or wound 5 people there daily.

Casualties hit a five-year high of 6,929 in 2025, 84% of them civilians.

The Landmine Monitor found government and armed groups both use mines in [Myanmar](country:MM), contaminating almost two-thirds of townships.

On [Syria](country:SY), report co-author Yeshua Moser-Puangsuwan said: "People are now returning, and that is driving the casualty figure."

[Finland](country:FI), [Estonia](country:EE), [Latvia](country:LV), [Lithuania](country:LT) and [Poland](country:PL) quit the Mine Ban Treaty, and [Ukraine](country:UA) suspended its compliance.
