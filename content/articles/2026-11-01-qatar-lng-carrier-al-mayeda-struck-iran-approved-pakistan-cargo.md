Doha — Qatar's gas carrier Al Mayeda was hit, with no injuries.

People with direct knowledge say [Pakistan](country:PK) had negotiated Iranian safe passage for the ship first.

Qatar Gas Transport Company said unidentified objects struck the vessel Friday evening, and it remains operational while the impact is assessed.

The company's statement names no attacker.
