87% of news publishers now block the Internet Archive. Publishers say artificial intelligence companies use the archive as a backdoor to training data. The New York Times hard-blocks its crawlers, and other outlets including the largest US newspaper chain followed in 2025. The Internet Archive, a nonprofit that has preserved web pages since 1996, says the restrictions threaten the historical record of online journalism.
