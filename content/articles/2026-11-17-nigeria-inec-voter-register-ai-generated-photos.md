Abuja — [Nigeria](country:NG)'s electoral commission blames 54 officials for fake voter records.

Voting starts 16 January, and the commission's biometric system flagged 1.5 million of 11 million registrations.

Registration officers used their logins and the exemption for people missing fingerprints, the commission's technology director said.

Former education minister Oby Ezekwesili says "the one accused does not become a judge in their own affairs."

Ezekwesili wants the National Assembly to invoke the doctrine of necessity before the 16 January vote.
