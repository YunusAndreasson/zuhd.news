London — Atoms were filmed forming a crystal seed.

The century-old equation behind that seed predicts everything from cloud rain to drug shelf life.

Classical nucleation theory held because no technique could watch atoms assemble directly inside opaque bulk material until now.

Researchers say this adds a pathway, not a replacement, for one material observed at atomic resolution.
