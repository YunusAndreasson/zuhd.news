A billion years of Earth's rock record vanished. Columbia University geologists traced the gap to tectonic collisions during an ancient supercontinent's formation, disproving theories that glaciation or the Cambrian explosion caused it. The finding reopens debate over what triggered the abrupt emergence of complex life 530 million years ago.
