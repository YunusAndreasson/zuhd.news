Berlin — [Germany](country:DE) will borrow more than €210 billion in 2027.

Defence rises to €139.6 billion while development aid falls 4.7%.

Much of the borrowing sits in special funds outside the constitutional debt brake, which lets rearmament and infrastructure spending grow.

The government's 2028 plan still carries an unresolved shortfall of more than €20 billion.
