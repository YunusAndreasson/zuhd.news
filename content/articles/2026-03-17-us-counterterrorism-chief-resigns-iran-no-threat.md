"Iran posed no imminent threat to our nation," Joe Kent wrote, resigning as director of the National Counterterrorism Center. Kent served as a Green Beret and lost his wife to an ISIS bomber in Syria; he said the war was launched "due to pressure from Israel and its powerful American lobby." Imminence is a legal prerequisite for a US president to strike a sovereign nation without congressional approval — the war's top counterterrorism official just said that bar was never met.
