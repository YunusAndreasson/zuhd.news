Beijing — [China](country:CN)'s solar installations fell 79% year-on-year in April.

The government replaced fixed-price tariffs with market-priced contracts in February 2025; developers can no longer secure financing without guaranteed revenue.

The government's 200GW combined solar and wind target for 2026 is now "difficult" to achieve, analysts said.
