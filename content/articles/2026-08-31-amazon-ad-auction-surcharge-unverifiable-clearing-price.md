Seattle — Amazon overcharged advertisers $20 billion, regulators say.

Small sellers who must buy Amazon ads to be seen absorb the cost.

The Federal Trade Commission says Amazon used an invented rival bid to push prices toward each advertiser's own maximum.

Twenty-two states joined the suit, filed Monday in Amazon's home state of Washington.
