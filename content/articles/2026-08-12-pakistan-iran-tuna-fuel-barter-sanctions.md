Karachi — Fishermen traded Iranian fuel for tuna, no dollars involved.

Thousands of Balochistan households lose their only income as the barter collapses.

[Iran](country:IR) subsidizes diesel while Pakistani boats can't get dollars to pay for fuel.

[US](country:US) strikes on Iranian fishing ports have halted trips for a month, fisherman Shah Zamin Khan said.
