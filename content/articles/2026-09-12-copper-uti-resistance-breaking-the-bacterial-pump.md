College Station — Immune cells flood infections with copper to kill bacteria.

Antibiotic-resistant urinary infections are surging, and copper offers a target that isn't an antibiotic.

Texas A&M researchers found resistant E. coli survive by pumping the copper back out through dedicated efflux pumps.

The work is in cells and animals so far, with no drug yet built to block the pump.
