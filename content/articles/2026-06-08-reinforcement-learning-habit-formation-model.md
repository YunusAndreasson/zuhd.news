Tokyo — One reinforcement-learning model reproduced habit formation and conditioning.

The dominant theory requires 2 separate brain systems for habits, but the new model reproduces the same behaviors in 1, researchers found.

Whether the single-system model holds across the full range of animal conditioning data remains untested.
