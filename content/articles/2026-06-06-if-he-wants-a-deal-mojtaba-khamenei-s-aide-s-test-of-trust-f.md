Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) demands $24 billion in frozen assets before any deal advances.

Mohsen Rezaei, military adviser to Supreme Leader Khamenei, said Tehran wants $12 billion at signing and $12 billion later, framing both as Iran's own frozen funds.

Rezaei warned Iran would expand conflict to the Red Sea and Mediterranean if talks collapse.
