Kuala Lumpur — Gulf crude arriving in [Malaysia](country:MY) has dropped.

The Strait of Hormuz closure is reaching its first Asian buyer, forcing refiners to source oil from further afield at a higher shipping cost.

Malaysian refiners must now find substitute suppliers, testing contracts across Southeast Asia built on the strait staying open.
