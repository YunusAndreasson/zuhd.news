Washington — [US](country:US) law bars living people from currency; Congress has not amended it for Trump's proposed $250 portrait bill.

Treasury is preparing the note, but both the denomination and the living-person bar require Congressional amendments.

The enabling legislation has stalled in the House Financial Services Committee since February 2025 with no scheduled vote.
