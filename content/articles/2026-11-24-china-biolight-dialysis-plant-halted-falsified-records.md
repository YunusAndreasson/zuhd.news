Tianjin — Staff never tested citrate in dialysis powder.

Excess citrate can trigger low calcium, arrhythmia and seizures in patients.

[China](country:CN)'s drug regulator found fabricated records in a surprise inspection and halted Biolight's only north China powder plant.

Biolight says only A powder is affected, and retained samples and a third-party test met standards.

Biolight says plants in five other regions can cover north China's supply.
