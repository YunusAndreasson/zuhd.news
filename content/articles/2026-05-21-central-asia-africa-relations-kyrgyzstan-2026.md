Bishkek — Kyrgyzstan is leading Central Asia's outreach to Africa.

Both regions have historically been brokered through Moscow and Paris; both are now diversifying those ties.

Kyrgyzstan has led embassies, trade missions, and high-level visits across Africa in 2026.
