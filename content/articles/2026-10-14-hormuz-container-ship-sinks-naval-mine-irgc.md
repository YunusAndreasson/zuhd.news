Tehran — A ship carrying 2,500 containers hit a mine and sank, Iran says.

A mined lane raises the risk for every crew and insurer crossing the strait.

The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps Navy said the ship left [Iran](country:IR)'s authorized route, but named no vessel, flag, operator or crew.

CENTCOM rejects Iran's claim of control, saying [US](country:US) forces helped partners move 19 million barrels of crude Monday.

Traffic ran at 4.1 ships a day on 11 October, its 90-day normal, before the sinking.
