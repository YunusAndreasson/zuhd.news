Kolkata — West Bengal madrasas must now sing Vande Mataram.

Vande Mataram has Hindu devotional content; BJP opposition leader Suvendu Adhikari of [India](country:IN)'s West Bengal framed compliance as: "if you want to live here."

The All India Muslim Personal Law Board demands immediate withdrawal or a Muslim student exemption.
