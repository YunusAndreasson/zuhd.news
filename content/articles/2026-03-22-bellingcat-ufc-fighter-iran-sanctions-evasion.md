Dubai — Ex-UFC fighter Mounir Lazzez directed 2 US-sanctioned Iranian oil tankers. The US Treasury blacklisted both vessels in 2024 for delivering Revolutionary Guard oil to a Hezbollah-linked company in Lebanon, Bellingcat reported. Lazzez called Irish crime boss Daniel Kinahan his "brother" and deleted his social media after the report published.
