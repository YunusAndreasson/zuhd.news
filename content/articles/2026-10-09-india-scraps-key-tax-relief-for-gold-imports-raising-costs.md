Mumbai — Banks have paid 3% tax on gold imports since April.

The government told the Goods and Services Tax Council only on Thursday.

The exemption covered gold, silver and platinum imported by banks and state-nominated agencies, the main channels supplying [India](country:IN)'s bullion market.

Revenue Secretary Arvind Shrivastava said the change ensures parity of taxes on metals imported through different routes.
