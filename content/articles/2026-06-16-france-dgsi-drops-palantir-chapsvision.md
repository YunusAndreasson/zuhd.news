Paris — [France](country:FR)'s spy agency will swap Palantir for ChapsVision.

PM Lecornu announced the switch; [Germany](country:DE)'s domestic intelligence agency made the same choice last month, citing mistrust of Palantir's US ties.

Palantir says its contract with the agency remains "fully in force" until 2028; no switchover date was announced.
