United States — Intel says no US-made robot fits its fabs.

The Federal Communications Commission's foreign-robot ban was unclear enough that Intel requested a waiver.

The commission granted it on 22 October, six days after Intel filed, covering the robots plus hardware, software and firmware changes.

International Data Corporation estimates the rule could cut [US](country:US) humanoid-robot revenue by 58% by 2030.

Intel's one-year waiver expires in October 2027.
