Karachi — Pakistan internet faces week-long degradation starting Tuesday. PTCL is repairing one of 3 submarine cables it manages; Pakistan's 7–8 Tbps demand already consumes 60% of total 13 Tbps capacity. The repair window closes April 20; Pakistan has no announced backup capacity if further cables are disrupted.
