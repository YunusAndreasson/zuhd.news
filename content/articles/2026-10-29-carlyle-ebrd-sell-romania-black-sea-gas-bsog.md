Bucharest — A field supplying 10% of Romanian gas changes owners.

[Romania](country:RO) produces most of its gas domestically, so the sale moves a rare EU supply source.

Carlyle and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development will sell Black Sea Oil & Gas to Mazarine Energy, price undisclosed.

The sale awaits regulatory approval, and OMV Petrom and Romgaz expect Neptun Deep gas in 2027.
