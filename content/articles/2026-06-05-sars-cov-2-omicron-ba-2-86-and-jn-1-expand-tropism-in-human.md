London — JN.1 and BA.2.86 now infect the upper intestine.

Both enter proximal intestinal cells earlier Omicron lines couldn't reach, using ACE2 and TMPRSS2 receptors, with limited inflammation, the study found.

JN.1's low gut inflammation suggests faecal shedding that could pass undetected; no surveillance framework for this route exists.
