Brent rose 10% this week to $101 a barrel. A US waiver on Russian oil and a 400 million-barrel emergency reserve release failed to offset the Hormuz blockade driving prices. Iranian boats struck 2 fuel tankers in Iraqi waters Thursday, halting Iraq's oil port operations entirely.
