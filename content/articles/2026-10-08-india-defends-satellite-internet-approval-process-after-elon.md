New Delhi — Starlink holds a licence but still cannot sell service.

Rivals Reliance Jio and Airtel agreed last year to distribute Starlink, but the deal appears soured.

Starlink has waited since 2022, and security assessments of all 3 licensed operators are still underway, [India](country:IN)'s Communications Ministry says.

"Is Ambani the real boss of India?" Elon Musk asked on X.

The ministry called the claim "baseless and misconceived," and no operator has final clearance.
