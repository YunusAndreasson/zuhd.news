New York — Google engineer bet $3.8M on searches he monitored.

The [US](country:US) attorney's office alleges Michele Spagnuolo accessed Google's internal search-trend tool hours before placing each Polymarket bet, profiting $1.2M.

Spagnuolo faces commodities fraud, wire fraud, and money laundering charges; no trial date has been set.
