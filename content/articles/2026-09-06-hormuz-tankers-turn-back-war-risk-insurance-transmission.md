Tehran — Three tankers turned back with no shots fired.

A radio warning from [Iran](country:IR)'s navy, not a strike, shuts a global shipping route.

The warning becomes a war-risk premium within hours, since insurers price each voyage, not navies.

Brent crude, up 10.4% in the 30 days to Sept. 1, already prices that risk.
