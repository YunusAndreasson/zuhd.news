Lagos — Uche Nnaji never earned the degree he claimed.

A government panel confirmed the former minister lacked the university certificate he submitted for 2023 screening; lawyers now press President Tinubu not to shield him.

They want [Nigeria](country:NG)'s anti-graft agency to also claw back his ministerial salary if convicted.
