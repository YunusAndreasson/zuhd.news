United States — Nvidia doubled profit to $65.3 billion without Jensen Huang.

Its forecast excludes [China](country:CN) data-centre sales, where a spy inquiry and new US export rules loom.

Revenue reached $115.8 billion against $109 billion expected, and Nvidia guided $130 billion, plus or minus 3%, for the next quarter.

Shares fell more than 36% in the past month, and the quarterly 10-Q filing will come weeks late.

Polymarket traders give Huang a 12% chance of rejoining Nvidia by the end of 2026.
