11 million people have fled Sudan's civil war. African Union Commission Chair Mahmoud Ali Youssouf opened the 39th AU summit in Addis Ababa by demanding an immediate ceasefire in what he called the continent's most urgent crisis, now approaching its 3rd year. United Nations Secretary-General Antonio Guterres backed the call, but Sudan remains suspended from the AU for a 5th consecutive year with no peace process in sight.
