Abuja — A police witness says the naira was recoloured, not redesigned.

The 2022 note policy caused a severe cash shortage before the 2023 election.

Buhari approved samples with scannable codes, but the notes printed lacked the codes and got no fresh approval, the investigator testified.

Godwin Emefiele denies the allegations, and the witness said he never interviewed him personally.

Justice Maryann Anenih adjourned the trial until 2 December.
