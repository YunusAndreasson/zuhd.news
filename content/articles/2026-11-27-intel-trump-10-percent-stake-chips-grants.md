Washington — Intel's board found the 10% stake unattractive, then signed.

The [US](country:US) government had already awarded Intel $8.9 billion in CHIPS Act grants.

A Commerce Department lawyer told Intel's finance chief rejection carried consequences he could not predict, notes in a shareholder lawsuit show.

Intel's lawyers argue back-and-forth talks made the agreement more favourable to the company.

Intel has asked a court to dismiss the shareholder suit, and a second suit targets Nvidia.
