London — This El Niño is on track to be the strongest on record.

It floods some regions while drying others, not warming the planet evenly.

Weakening trade winds let warm Pacific water pool eastward, reversing atmospheric circulation and rerouting rainfall worldwide.

The event began in June, and forecasters expect it to persist into 2027.
