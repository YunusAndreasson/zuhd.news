An Israeli drone killed 2 people in Ein el-Hilweh, Lebanon's largest Palestinian refugee camp. Israel claimed it struck a Hamas command center, but Lebanon's state news agency reported the building housed a food-aid kitchen. The strike breached the November 2024 ceasefire between Israel and Hezbollah.
