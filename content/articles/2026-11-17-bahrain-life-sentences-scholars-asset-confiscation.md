Manama — [Bahrain](country:BH) jailed scholars for life, state-run Mehr News Agency reported.

Iran's Foreign Ministry says the courts also confiscated their assets.

The ministry calls the trials collective punishment and says prosecutors relied on baseless accusations of links to [Iran](country:IR).

The report names no defendants, charges or court, and carries no Bahraini response.

The ministry demands Bahrain stop suppressing scholars and respect their rights.
