New York — Asian investors locked out of $75bn SpaceX IPO.

The IPO was gated to non-Asian investors, pushing traders from [South Korea](country:KR) to [China](country:CN) into proxies: Rocket Lab and space exchange-traded funds.

SpaceX has set no secondary-market timeline, leaving Asian investors sidelined indefinitely.
