Beijing — Twenty-five rounds, zero metres of border settled.

The talks manage a 3,488-kilometre dispute rather than resolve it, keeping tension off the frontier.

[India](country:IN)'s Ajit Doval meets China's Wang Yi under a Special Representatives mechanism set up in 2003.

Neither side has set a date for actually delimiting the Line of Actual Control.
