San Francisco — Oracle PeopleSoft zero-day hit 100 organizations before disclosure.

ShinyHunters exploited the 9.8-severity flaw for 2 weeks undetected; 68% of the roughly 100 targeted organizations were in higher education, Google's Mandiant said.

Oracle has issued a stopgap mitigation but no full patch; victims are receiving extortion demands.
