Ivory Coast's 110-year-old talking drum arrived home from Paris. France required a parliamentary law to authorize the return — its national museums legally own every looted colonial object until overridden by legislation. France's National Assembly must still vote on a broader restitution framework before the remaining 147 objects can follow.
