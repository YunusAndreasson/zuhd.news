Police detained Olfa Hamdi at Tunis-Carthage airport on Sunday. Hamdi leads the Third Republic party and has demanded early elections to replace President Kais Saied, who has arrested multiple opposition figures since assuming expanded powers in 2021. Tunisia was the only democracy to emerge from the 2011 Arab Spring, and rights groups warn Saied has closed more political space than any leader since.
