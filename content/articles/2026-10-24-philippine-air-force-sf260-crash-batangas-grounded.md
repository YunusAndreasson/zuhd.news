Lipa — The [Philippines](country:PH) grounded all SF-260 trainers after four pilots died.

A May crash killed 2 pilots, and the air force has not said when flights resumed.

Both aircraft went down inside Fernando Air Base at 3:51 p.m. Friday, and the air force has not confirmed a collision.

Defense Secretary Gilberto Teodoro Jr. said the inquiry will examine pilot training, command, flight planning and maintenance.
