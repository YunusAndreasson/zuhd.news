Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) says its Hormuz terms reached Washington through mediators.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s interior minister Mohsin Naqvi arrives for talks while the strait stays shut.

Security chief Mohsen Rezaei named three conditions: an end to the war, frozen funds released, the naval blockade lifted.

Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf says the strait stays closed until the US honours its commitments.
