Cairo — 5 Matsda2sh journalists remain missing after a colleague surfaced.

[Egypt](country:EG)'s Supreme State Security Prosecution ordered Abu Emaira held 15 days on terrorism and false-news charges.

The Egyptian Initiative for Personal Rights says the National Security Agency held him incommunicado under Article 40 of the counterterrorism law.

The Interior Ministry claimed Thursday that Matsda2sh, an independent fact-checking platform, was a Muslim Brotherhood cell.

The rights group demands the public prosecutor drop all charges and release the other five.
