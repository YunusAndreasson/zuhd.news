The International Seabed Authority is negotiating whether to permit commercial deep-ocean mining. The polymetallic nodules on the seabed hold cobalt, nickel, and manganese needed for EV batteries, but extracting them would permanently destroy ecosystems formed over millions of years. France and Chile are pushing for a precautionary moratorium before any permits are issued; Norway, China, and The Metals Company are lobbying for commercial licences this year.
