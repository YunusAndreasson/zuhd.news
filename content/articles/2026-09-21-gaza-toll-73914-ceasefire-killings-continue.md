Gaza — Israeli attacks killed 4 more Palestinians in 24 hours.

The Gaza Health Ministry says they died under a ceasefire that took effect on 11 October 2025.

The ministry counts 1,394 killed since the ceasefire and 73,914 since October 2023, with 174,952 wounded.

[Israel](country:IL)'s attacks also wounded 17 people, and 3 of the 4 dead were bodies pulled from rubble.
