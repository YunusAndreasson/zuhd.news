Kuwait City — Kuwait pumps about 75% of prewar oil, its state oil company chief says.

Brent eased as Gulf exports recovered, but stays well above prewar levels.

Analysts credit US escorts and offshore ship-to-ship transfers, and Kpler says 40% of regional crude now bypasses Hormuz.

[Iran](country:IR) hit 21 vessels in September, up from 12 in August, ACLED data show.

On Monday the Revolutionary Guards ordered a tanker in the strait to turn back or risk attack, a shipping-monitor group said.
