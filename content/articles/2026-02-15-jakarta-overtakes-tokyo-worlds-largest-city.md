42 million people now live in greater Jakarta. A United Nations report confirmed the Indonesian capital overtook Tokyo as the world's most populous metropolitan area. Most residents do not use public transit, creating gridlock that costs $3.6 billion annually in lost productivity. Indonesia is building a replacement capital, Nusantara, on Borneo. For millions navigating 4-hour daily commutes, relief remains years away.
