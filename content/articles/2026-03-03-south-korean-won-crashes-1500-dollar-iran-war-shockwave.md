South Korea's won briefly broke 1,500 per dollar. Seoul stocks fell 7% as Iran-triggered risk aversion hammered the currency of a country that imports nearly all its oil. The Iran conflict has no announced end date, leaving South Korea's currency and equity markets exposed indefinitely.
