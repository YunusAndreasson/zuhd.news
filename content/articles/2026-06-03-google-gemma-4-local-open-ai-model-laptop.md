Mountain View — Google released Gemma 4 12B, a multimodal artificial intelligence model that runs on a 16 GB laptop.

The model bridges mobile and large Gemma 4 variants by dropping dedicated image and audio encoders, halving the next tier's memory footprint.

Weights are available immediately on Kaggle and Hugging Face.
