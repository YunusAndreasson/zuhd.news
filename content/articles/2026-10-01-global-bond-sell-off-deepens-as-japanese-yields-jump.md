Tokyo — [Japan](country:JP)'s 10-year yield hit 3.11%, near a 30-year high.

The sell-off spans 4 markets, with [US](country:US) 10-year yields at 5.31% and Australia's at 5.42%.

The Bank of Japan's Tankan survey showed all-industry conditions at their best since 1991, bolstering bets on more rate rises.

Currency traders doubt an October hike, and the yen fell 0.5% to ¥158.16 against the dollar.

The Federal Reserve decides on 28 October and the Bank of Japan on 30 October.
