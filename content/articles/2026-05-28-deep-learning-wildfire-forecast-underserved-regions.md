London — AI now forecasts wildfire risk 31 days ahead.

The study's model cuts forecast bias in 85% of high-exposure, high-poverty grid cells — regions that historically had no wildfire prediction capacity.

The researchers call for operational deployment; current global fire forecasts are optimised for countries with ground-based weather networks.
