A veteran who burned a flag faces no charges. Jan Carey, a 20-year US Army veteran, burned the flag hours after Trump signed an order imposing year-long sentences for the act. Trump's executive order remains in force despite the Supreme Court's 1989 ruling that flag burning is protected speech.
