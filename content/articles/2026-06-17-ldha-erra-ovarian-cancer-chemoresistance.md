London — A metabolic switch lets ovarian cancer survive chemotherapy.

Estrogen-related receptor alpha activates lactate dehydrogenase A, shifting glucose to lactate and blocking the cell-death proteins cisplatin triggers.

Blocking the receptor with drug XCT790 restored chemo sensitivity in mouse models; no clinical candidate exists yet.
