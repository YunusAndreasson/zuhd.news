Bengaluru — Gold fell 3.6% this week, its worst since June.

Capital.com's Monte Safieddine says a break below $4,000 would mean markets price sticky inflation, not rate relief.

Oil rose after Houthi attacks in [Saudi Arabia](country:SA), reviving inflation fears, lifting yields and raising the cost of holding non-yielding bullion.

Polymarket traders give 94% odds the Federal Reserve holds rates on 28 October, up 23 points in a week.
