Lagos — Airlines suspended flights as terminal access was restricted.

Ground handlers and engineers, among aviation's lowest-paid staff, are the workers this shutdown tests.

Unions accuse [Nigeria](country:NG)'s airports authority of blocking their right to unionise and withholding a 5% ticket levy.

Aviation Minister Festus Keyamo is negotiating a resolution with no date set.
