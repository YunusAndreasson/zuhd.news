London — A rare gene defect is now treatable in children.

GATA2 haploinsufficiency wrecks the immune system and drives leukemia; a busulfan-based conditioning regimen let transplants succeed even in advanced cases, researchers report.

Early transplantation in young patients may improve outcomes further, the study's authors say.
