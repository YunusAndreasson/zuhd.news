Guayaquil — The Tiguerones split into rival factions after their leader's 2024 arrest.

The label reaches ordinary Esmeraldas residents through "support network" powers, not a command structure.

Terrorism designations target groups with named leaders, so labeling a fragmented gang expands sanctions past any real chain of command.

[Ecuador](country:EC) designated the group two years before Washington's own listing this month.
