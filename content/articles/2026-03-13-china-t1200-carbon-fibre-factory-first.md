China opened the world's first T1200 carbon fibre factory. Japan's Toray developed T1200 in 2023 but hasn't disclosed production capacity, leaving China's 100-tonne facility as the world's only commercial source. With Toray's output still secret, China's factory will compete for aerospace and defense contracts Japan and the US once dominated.
