New Delhi — [India](country:IN) taxes fuel exports to protect home supply.

The Finance Ministry adjusts levies fortnightly on crude benchmarks; this fortnight's rates: ₹1.5/litre petrol, ₹13.5 diesel, ₹9.5 jet fuel.

The next fortnightly reset falls in mid-June, when global crude benchmarks are next reviewed.
