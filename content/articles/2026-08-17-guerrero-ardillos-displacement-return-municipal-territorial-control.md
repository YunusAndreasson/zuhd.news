Chilapa — 15 families went home after two months.

One arrest reversed a displacement of more than 1,000 Indigenous residents in [Mexico](country:MX).

Los Ardillos are small next to national cartels, but control several Guerrero municipalities through political ties, not manpower.

The interior ministry named no date for remaining displaced residents to return.
