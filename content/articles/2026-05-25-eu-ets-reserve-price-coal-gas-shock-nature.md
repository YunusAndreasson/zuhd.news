Brussels — EU gas price cap failed the 2022 test.

Gas price spikes push utilities to coal; a 13-country study found an automatic carbon floor blocks that switch without raising consumer bills.

Whether policymakers adopt a carbon floor before the next gas shock drives another coal surge is unresolved.
