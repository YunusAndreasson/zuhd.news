Armenia — 96% of shallow tropical reefs may collapse by 2075, conservation scientists warn.

Up to 1 billion people rely on reefs for fishing, tourism and storm protection.

Bleaching ejects the algae that feed coral, and reefs cannot recover from more than 3 events in 5 years.

Palau corals are gaining 0.1°C of heat tolerance a decade, researcher James Guest says.

Experts fear the current record El Niño will trigger a fifth mass bleaching.
