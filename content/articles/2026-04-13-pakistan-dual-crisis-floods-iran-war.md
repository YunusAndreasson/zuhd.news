Islamabad — Pakistan faces floods and war simultaneously this year. Monsoon forecasts show rainfall 22–26% above normal for July–August, while the Iran war drives fuel prices up and medicine supply chains close. Pakistan's 375-project flood plan failed to prevent the 2025 floods — the monsoon tests its finances again.
