Dublin — Both prime ministers backing European Central Bank candidates skip the summit.

Leaders want three top bank posts settled before next year's elections in [France](country:FR) and elsewhere.

Appointment needs 16 euro countries holding 65% of the eurozone population, and Paris and Berlin usually agree first.

Two people with knowledge said no deal is sealed and Dutchman Klaas Knot could still win.

Lagarde leaves in February, and leaders aim to seal the package as soon as this month.
