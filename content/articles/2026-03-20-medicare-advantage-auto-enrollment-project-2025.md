Seniors could be enrolled in private Medicare by default. Chris Klomp, Trump's Medicare administrator, proposed flipping the enrollment default — currently seniors who don't choose a plan receive traditional Medicare, but under the proposal would receive private coverage instead. Private insurers would gain millions of new enrollees if Congress approves the change.
