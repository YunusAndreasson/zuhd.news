En Nahud — At least 60 miners died in West Kordofan.

No rescue teams have reached the site, so trapped miners remain unrecovered.

Gold is [Sudan](country:SD)'s top export and hard-currency lifeline for both warring sides, mined by hand in unshored shafts.

The toll may still rise, as local Kordofan Observatory says miners remain missing underground.
