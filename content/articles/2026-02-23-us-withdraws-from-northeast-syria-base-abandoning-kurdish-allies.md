900 troops began leaving America's largest base in Syria. The Kurdish-led Syrian Democratic Forces, who lost 11,000 fighters defeating ISIS alongside the US, now face Turkey and a new government without their backer. Al-Hol camp, holding 24,000 ISIS-linked detainees, depends on forces losing their last external support.
