Ecuador deployed 75,000 soldiers and police to 4 provinces. About 70% of cocaine from Colombia and Peru moves through Ecuador; the murder rate rose 30% in 2025 despite 2 years of emergency decrees. The FBI opened its first Ecuador office last week as part of a 17-country US-led alliance, though previous security escalations have failed to stem the violence.
