London — UK arms imports from [Israel](country:IL) rose 100-fold during the Gaza war.

UN Comtrade data shows UK purchases of Israeli arms rose from £55,000 in 2023 to £5.9m in 2025, even as [Britain](country:GB) publicly restricted what it sold to Israel.

Parliament faces calls to account for the import side; the government publishes no itemised breakdown.
