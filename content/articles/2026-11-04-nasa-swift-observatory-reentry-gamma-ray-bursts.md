United States — A 3,200-pound NASA telescope falls to Earth Wednesday.

Scientists are now working out how to replace Swift's early-warning role.

Increased solar activity dragged Swift's low orbit down faster than expected, and a $30 million rescue craft failed in August.

"We've known this day would come since before Swift was launched," said John Nousek, a Penn State astronomer.

NASA's reentry map spans Latin America, Africa, southern Asia and northern Australia, outside the [US](country:US) and Europe.
