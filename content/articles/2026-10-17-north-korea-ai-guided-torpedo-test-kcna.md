It follows [North Korea](country:KP)'s submarine cruise-missile launch and AI-assisted hypersonic test this month.

Professor Lim Eul-chul says [South Korea](country:KR) and the [US](country:US) use decoys that AI could help torpedoes see through.

Analyst Yang Moo-jin says fast torpedoes are noisy and easy to detect, and North Korea does not yet appear to have fixed that.
