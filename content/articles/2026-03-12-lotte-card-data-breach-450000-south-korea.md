Lotte Card stored 450,000 South Korean government IDs unencrypted. Hackers who breached the company's online payment system also extracted the broader credit records of 2.97 million customers. South Korea fined Lotte 9.62 billion won ($6.5 million), with affected customers still awaiting any remedy.
