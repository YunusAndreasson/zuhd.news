Damascus — A planted bomb killed at least 9 lawyers and clerks.

The device was hidden under a table 100 meters from the Palace of Justice, where [Syria](country:SY)'s new government is trying former Assad-era officials.

No group has claimed the attack; up to 10,000 former regime loyalists remain at large, analysts say.
