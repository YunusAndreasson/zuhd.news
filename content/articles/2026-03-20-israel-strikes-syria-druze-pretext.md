Israel bombed Syrian army bases and weapons depots overnight. The Israeli military cited Druze civilian protection, but Israel already occupies Syria's Golan Heights under a 1981 annexation the UN considers illegal and has expanded its military presence in southern Syria since December 2024. No Druze leader in Sweida has asked Israel to intervene.
