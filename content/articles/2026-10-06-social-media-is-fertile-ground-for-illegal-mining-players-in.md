Madre de Dios — A Facebook post offered permitted mining land to would-be partners.

[Peru](country:PE)'s registry meant to formalise informal miners gives illegal operations legal cover, experts say.

A permit holder lets miners work his land for a percentage, and they extract gold with hydraulic pumps and mercury.

The post sat in Facebook groups for La Pampa, a gold boom town built by wildcat miners.
