London — A model estimates sex from routine computed-tomography scans.

Every hospital already archives these images, unlike the pelvis scans forensic identification usually needs.

Trained on 764 patients, accuracy fell from 93% internally to 81% when tested on scans from another institution.

The authors call for wider multicenter validation before any forensic use.
