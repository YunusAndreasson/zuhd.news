London — 32,000 venues will save £1,100 apiece next year.

Prime Minister Burnham's 20% business-rates cut for pubs and clubs, his third giveaway this week, is funded partly by reviewing relief for vape shops.

Downing Street has not detailed how the review's savings will materialize.
