Cape Town — Cape Town house prices rose 11.5%, Buffalo City's just 2.7%.

The Auditor-General gave Cape Town the only clean metro audit, and Buffalo City a qualified one.

Statistics South Africa's index shows prices up 8.0% across eight metros in the year to May 2026.

Tshwane and Nelson Mandela Bay broke the pattern, growing 5.3% and 4.9% despite qualified audits.

[South Africa](country:ZA) votes in local elections on 4 November.
