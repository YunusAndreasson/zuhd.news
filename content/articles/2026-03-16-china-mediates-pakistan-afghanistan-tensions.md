China offered to mediate as Pakistan struck Afghan soil. China's $62 billion Belt and Road investment in Pakistan gives it unique leverage and a direct financial stake in stopping the conflict. Pakistan's military said Operation Ghazab lil-Haq continues until all objectives are met — Kabul has not agreed to talks.
