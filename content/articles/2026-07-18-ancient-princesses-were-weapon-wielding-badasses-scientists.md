Dahshur — Egyptian princesses' bones show real weapon use.

Bone strain in the 4,000-year-old Dahshur mummies matches archers, not symbolic burial goods, researchers led by Zeineb Hashesh of Beni-Suef University found.

The find revives debate over how ancient [Egypt](country:EG) armed women of royal rank.
