Rawalpindi — Lawyer Imaan Mazari-Hazir and her husband each got 10 years.

[Pakistan](country:PK) rearrested them after its Supreme Court suspended an earlier 17-year sentence on 17 September.

An anti-terrorism court on 29 October convicted them over alleged removal of security barriers, at a trial held inside Adiala jail.

Amnesty and 22 other groups demand the convictions be quashed while a Supreme Court petition remains pending.
