Shusha — Aliyev skipped Erdogan's 30 October summit, then hosted him Sunday.

The rift opened in September, when [Azerbaijan](country:AZ) banned headscarves in schools.

Baku declared 31 [Turkish](country:TR) nationals persona non grata, and Erdogan fired adviser Oktay Saral, who demanded a reversal.

"The hijab dispute is a symptom of a deeper rift," wrote Gonul Tol, a Middle East Institute analyst.

Turkey is normalising ties with [Armenia](country:AM), but links progress to Armenian-Azerbaijani peace efforts.
