Dubai — Trade between the Emirates and Oman jumped 175%.

The war has closed the Strait of Hormuz to much shipping.

Trucks now carry [United Arab Emirates](country:AE) cargo that used to move by sea through the strait.

Road capacity limits how much of the lost sea trade the route can absorb.
