Jakarta — [Indonesia](country:ID)'s flagship tech firms have not turned profitable.

Once heralded as proof Southeast Asia could build its own tech giants, many have failed to translate market scale into shareholder value.

None of the country's major firms has disclosed a credible path to sustained profitability.
