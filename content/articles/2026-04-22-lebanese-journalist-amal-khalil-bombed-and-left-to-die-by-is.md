Tyre — [Israel](country:IL) bombed a south [Lebanon](country:LB) house sheltering journalists. Al-Akhbar's Amal Khalil had taken refuge there after a drone struck her car; Israeli shelling blocked Red Cross rescuers for hours. The Committee to Protect Journalists called the strikes and rescue obstruction a grave breach of international humanitarian law.
