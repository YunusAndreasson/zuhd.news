New Delhi — [India](country:IN) held Umar Khalid 6 years without verdict.

The government compared Khalid to Kasab — executed for the 2008 Mumbai attacks — as the Supreme Court weighed whether trial delay justifies bail.

Khalid got 3 days' interim bail; co-accused Sharjeel Imam and dozens more await the ruling.
