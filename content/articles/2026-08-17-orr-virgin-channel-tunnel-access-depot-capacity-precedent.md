London — Regulators handed Virgin depot slots Eurostar wanted.

It ends three decades of single-operator rail across the Channel Tunnel.

The [UK](country:GB)'s rail regulator ruled Eurostar's own expansion needed only a modest capacity increase.

Virgin plans six daily Paris trains by October 2030, pending tunnel access on the French side.
