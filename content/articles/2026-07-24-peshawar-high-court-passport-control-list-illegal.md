Peshawar — Minister Meena Khan was barred with no conviction.

The Passport Control List blocks travel on an intelligence report alone; the court ruled that violates the constitutional right to free movement.

The ruling sets a precedent as courts also weigh curbs on judges and [Pakistan](country:PK)'s 27th Amendment.
