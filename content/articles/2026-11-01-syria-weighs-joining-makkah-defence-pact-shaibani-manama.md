Manama — [Syria](country:SY) is evaluating entry to a three-nation defence pact.

Its foreign minister counts more than 1,000 Israeli attacks on Syria in under two years.

The Makkah pact binds [Saudi Arabia](country:SA), [Pakistan](country:PK) and [Turkey](country:TR) to treat an attack on one as an attack on all.

Turkish officials say any support for Riyadh would not include Turkish troops fighting the Houthis in Yemen.

Pakistan's military says the pact's wording bars no newcomers, and the three founding members' leaders decide.
