Matamoros — SpaceX seeks 456 Starbase launches a year, up from 25.

Mexican authorities say debris from a destroyed Starship washed ashore at Playa Bagdad this month.

[Mexico](country:MX)'s Claudia Sheinbaum wants [US](country:US) and Mexican airspace authorities to settle permission for Mexican airspace, launch frequency and relocation.

SpaceX disputes that the debris is its own and says tests show its materials pose no toxic risk.

The Federal Aviation Administration is still weighing the request, and Washington has not answered Mexico.
