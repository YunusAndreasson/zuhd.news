London — 33 of Britain's 58 butterfly species are declining. 44 million sightings across 50 years show warming helping generalists — red admiral up 330% — while woodland and wetland specialists collapse. Nitrogen pollution from vehicles is reshaping plant chemistry; the management fixes exist but reach too few sites.
