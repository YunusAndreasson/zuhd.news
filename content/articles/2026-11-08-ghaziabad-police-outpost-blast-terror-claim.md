Ghaziabad — One delivery worker was injured in Friday night's outpost blast.

A group calling itself Tehrik Ul Mujahideen claimed responsibility online, and police are verifying the claim.

Police in [India](country:IN) recovered an abandoned scooter, are checking camera footage and sent forensic samples to the Agra lab.

Police registered the case against unidentified persons and have not confirmed the claim.

Police sources say investigators are probing links to Shahjad Bhatti's network, and the state Anti-Terrorism Squad is involved.
