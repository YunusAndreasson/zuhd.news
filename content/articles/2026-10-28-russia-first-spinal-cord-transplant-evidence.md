Russia — Surgeons glued a donor spinal cord segment into a patient this month.

Evidence it will restore movement is thin, and details of the operation are scant.

They cut a section out of a completely injured cord, in what New Scientist calls a world first.

Nothing released so far shows whether the patient regained movement.
