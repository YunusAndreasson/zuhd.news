Frankfurt — [France](country:FR)'s borrowing premium over [Germany](country:DE) hit 1.75 points.

The European Central Bank says markets, not its bond purchases, should force France to cut spending.

Higher yields enlarge a 5.5% deficit, the euro zone's highest per the European Commission.

The central bank says bond markets still function smoothly and the average euro area spread remains low.

It meets on 17 December, with French presidential elections due in April.
