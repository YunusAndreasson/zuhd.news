Dhaka — The Digital Security Act produced 7,001 cases by 2023.

Critics say a new draft cyber law aimed at AI fakes revives that censorship.

The technology division's draft targets AI-generated images, after a study found 72 manipulated items before the February election.

Pro-Awami League pages targeted women leaders with fabricated images hinting at sexual impropriety.

A five-member cabinet committee under Minister Faqir Mahbub Anam is reviewing the draft.
