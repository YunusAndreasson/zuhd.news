A CEO used ChatGPT to dodge $250 million. Krafton, a South Korean game publisher, had promised Unknown Worlds developers that bonus when Subnautica 2 hit its sales targets — so the CEO asked ChatGPT how to void the contract instead of consulting lawyers. A court ordered Krafton to pay in full; it must now deliver the game under the developers it tried to cut out.
