India — [Canada](country:CA)'s Fairfax wins 60.72% of IDBI Bank for ₹52,878 crore.

The government calls it [India](country:IN)'s largest disinvestment and largest foreign investment in financial services.

The government sells 30.48% and state-owned Life Insurance Corporation 30.24%, keeping a combined 34%, enough to block special resolutions.

The ₹81 share price sits 10.4% below Wednesday's close, and IDBI shares fell about 7% Thursday.

The Reserve Bank of India's fit-and-proper review comes first, then a share purchase agreement before 2026 ends.
