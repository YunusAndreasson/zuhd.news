Kyiv — [Russia](country:RU) told foreign diplomats to leave the capital.

Its foreign ministry warned of "massive" strikes and said those who stay face mortal danger.

Russia's defence ministry confirmed striking Kyiv's Northern Bridge, saying it served military logistics.

Russian attacks killed 5 people in Kharkiv and a sailor in Odesa, Ukrainian officials said.

Russia issued a similar warning in May, and diplomatic efforts to end the war remain stalled.
