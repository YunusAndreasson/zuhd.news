Madrid — Unions say 90% of port and auto workers struck.

[Spain](country:ES)'s first general strike in 14 years targets rents, not wages.

The average rent of €1,176 equals 98.7% of a young person's monthly salary, the Youth Council says.

Police confirmed an arrest at Madrid's Mercamadrid market but not the pepper spray unions say sent 13 people to medics.

Spain votes on 29 November after parliament rejected Sánchez's eviction and rent decrees.
