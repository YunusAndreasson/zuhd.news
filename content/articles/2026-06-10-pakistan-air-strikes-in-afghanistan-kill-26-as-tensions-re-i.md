Khost — Pakistani airstrikes killed 11 children in Khost, the Taliban said.

[Pakistan](country:PK) struck Tehrik-e-Taliban sanctuaries after the group killed 6 paramilitary personnel; [Afghanistan](country:AF)'s Taliban counted 13 civilian dead.

The UN reported 372 Afghan civilian deaths in cross-border fire in Q1 2026; no ceasefire talks are scheduled.
