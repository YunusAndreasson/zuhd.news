Mexico City — [Mexico](country:MX)'s US-Mexico-Canada Agreement review becomes a [China](country:CN) policy test.

[Washington](country:US) demands proof Mexico is not a backdoor for Chinese goods; Mexico's factories depend on Asian inputs for the exports that cross duty-free.

The review escalates this year, turning the pact into a China-policing instrument on Mexico's factory floor.
