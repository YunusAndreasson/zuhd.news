London — A field could replace the fermentation tank.

Storing casein-family proteins inside seeds sidesteps the bioreactor bottleneck that has kept cow-free dairy expensive, a new study found.

Researchers say the discovery paves the way for engineered crops to supply cheaper animal-free dairy ingredients.
