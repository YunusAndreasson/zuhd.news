South Korea wants AI embedded in UN agencies. UN Secretary-General António Guterres endorsed the plan, which would merge South Korea's public and private sectors with UN bodies to build AI tools for global challenges. South Korean official Kim Min-seok travels to Geneva on Tuesday to pitch 3 UN agencies on the proposal.
