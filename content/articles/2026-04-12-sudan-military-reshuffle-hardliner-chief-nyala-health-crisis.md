Khartoum — Sudan named a war-only general its military chief. Yasser al-Atta—the military's leading advocate for defeating the RSF without negotiation—was appointed chief of staff as Burhan abolished civilian-military hybrid command posts. The military is now pushing to retake Kurmuk near the Ethiopian border, lost to the RSF in late March.
