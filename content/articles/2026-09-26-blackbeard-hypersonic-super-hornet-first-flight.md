United States — Navy footage shows Blackbeard hypersonic missiles under Super Hornet wings.

The [US](country:US) Navy wants a cheap, fast-to-build anti-ship hypersonic in limited service by 2028.

A booster carries an apparently unpowered glide vehicle to hypersonic speed, with range Castelion has hinted at just under 500 miles.

The Navy ordered a first lot of 444 rounds from Castelion for $43.65 million on 14 September.
