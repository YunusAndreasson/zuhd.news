New Delhi — Eighteen criteria now justify a school fee hike.

Private schools in [India](country:IN)'s capital must form parent-inclusive fee panels by July 15, backing any increase with audited records on infrastructure or staffing gains.

Panels get three-year fee proposals by July 31; noncompliant schools risk fines, pending Delhi High Court review.
