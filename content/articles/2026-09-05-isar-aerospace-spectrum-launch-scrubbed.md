Andøya — Isar Aerospace scrubbed its Spectrum launch again.

It is Europe's leading attempt at an orbital launcher independent of Arianespace.

Road and sea closures around the spaceport took effect hours before the scrub, the fifth this year.

The approved launch window stays open until 10 September.
