London — A dissolving bone screw needs no second surgery.

Researchers found faster-cooled magnesium alloy castings gave finer grains and higher strength, but slower cooling produced better corrosion resistance — a trade-off set at the foundry.

The unedited study is bench-only; authors say no in-vivo testing has yet confirmed the results.
