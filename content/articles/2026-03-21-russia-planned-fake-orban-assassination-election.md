Budapest — Russia planned to fake an assassination of Viktor Orbán. A report obtained by the Washington Post says the goal was to shift Hungary's April election to emotional terrain favoring stability — and Orbán. The Kremlin denied the report; if true, Orbán's government was the intended beneficiary of a Russian intelligence operation two weeks before Hungary votes.
