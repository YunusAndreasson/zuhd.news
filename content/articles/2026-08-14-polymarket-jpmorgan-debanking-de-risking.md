New York — JPMorgan kept courting Polymarket after cutting it off.

No regulator ordered the exit — a bank's own risk calculus ended the relationship.

Polymarket returned to the [US](country:US) in late 2025 after regulators eased rules that barred it since a 2022 CFTC settlement.

JPMorgan is still angling to underwrite Polymarket's eventual IPO despite ending its banking ties.
