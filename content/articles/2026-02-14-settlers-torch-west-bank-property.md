Israeli settlers burned a Palestinian animal pen in the Jordan Valley. The fire struck Ein al-Maita, a Bedouin community, after footage showed settlers near the property roughly an hour earlier. Witnesses said the settlers returned afterward to threaten residents. Israeli police made no arrests. The arson followed Israeli military demolitions and settler harassment that drove 2 families from the village earlier that week.
