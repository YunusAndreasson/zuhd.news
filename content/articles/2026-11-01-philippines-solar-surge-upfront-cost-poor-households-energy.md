Manila — A Las Piñas family paid 700,000 pesos for solar and saves 20,000 monthly.

The median Filipino household spends around 12% of monthly income on electricity, a June estimate says.

Meralco's rate is up 16% since January, and Ember analyst Alnie Demoral says a rooftop system pays back in about 3 years.

Updated official figures on rooftop solar have yet to be released, Demoral says.
