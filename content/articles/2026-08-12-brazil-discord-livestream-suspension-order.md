Brasilia — A 13-year-old died after being coerced into a live suicide stream.

The order strips a whole feature from every user, a step few states have taken against a live platform.

Regulators gave Discord three days to suspend "Go Live," saying it cannot scan real-time video for self-harm content.

Discord has 10 days to appeal, facing fines up to 50 million reais per violation.
