San José — [Brazil](country:BR) is on trial for denying a trans woman surgery.

It is the Inter-American Court's first examination of trans people's right to health.

Luiza Melinho completed years of required evaluations before a university hospital cancelled her 2001 surgery and told her to look elsewhere.

The court has set no date for a ruling, which would bind Brazil.
