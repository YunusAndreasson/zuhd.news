London — Apple pulled its strongest iCloud encryption from new UK users.

Identical iPhones in [Britain](country:GB) now differ in security by when owners enabled Advanced Data Protection.

The government ordered Apple, under the Investigatory Powers Act 2016, to provide access to data protected by its strongest iCloud encryption.

Users who enabled the feature before February 2025 keep it, and no new UK user can.
