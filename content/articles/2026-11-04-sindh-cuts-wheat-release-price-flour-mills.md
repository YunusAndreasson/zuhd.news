Karachi — Flour should fall Rs15 a kilogram, Sindh's senior minister says.

The cabinet ordered flour mills to pass the saving on to consumers.

Sindh cut the wheat release price Rs500 to Rs4,500 per 40kg, matching other provinces after market prices dipped.

World wheat still averaged $246.9 a tonne in September, up 23.6% over 3 months.

Karachi's flour counters will show whether mills pass on the cut.
