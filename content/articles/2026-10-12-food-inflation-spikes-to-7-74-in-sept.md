New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s food inflation hit 7.74% in September, up from 5.97%.

Headline inflation reached 5.54%, nearing the top of the central bank's 2%-6% tolerance band.

Onions rose 108% and rice 8.25% a year on, as deficient monsoon rains left paddy sowing 4% behind last year's, the agriculture ministry said.

Potato prices fell 11.5% and tomato 8.62% from a year earlier.

The central bank raised rates last week, and Oxford Economics expects two more 25-basis-point hikes, in December and February.
