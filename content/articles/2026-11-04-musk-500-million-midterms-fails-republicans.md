Austin — Musk's reported $446 million bought Republicans almost nothing.

Democrats flipped the Texas Senate seat for the first time since 1988.

Musk gave $158 million to the Senate Leadership Fund, a super PAC that raises unlimited sums but cannot coordinate with campaigns.

Democrat James Talarico said in his victory speech that "all the money in the world is no match for the people of Texas."

Robert Garcia, the next House Oversight chair, has scrutinised the federal contracts and subsidies flowing to Musk's companies.
