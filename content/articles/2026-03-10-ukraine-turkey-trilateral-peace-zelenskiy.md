Turkish President Erdoğan will host Ukraine-Russia-US peace talks. Ukrainian President Zelenskyy confirmed the trilateral format that Russia has sought since 2022, making the US a direct party to negotiations. Talks open as the US war in Iran dominates Washington's attention, widening Erdoğan's room to set the agenda.
