Every civilian killed in the UAE so far has been a migrant worker. Saleh Ahmed, 55, a Bangladeshi delivery worker, was killed on Day 1 of the war; the kafala sponsorship system bars all workers from leaving without employer approval, while their families' survival depends on the income. Equidem, a rights group embedded in 4 Gulf states, says no government has signaled evacuation plans for the region's 10 million migrant workers.
