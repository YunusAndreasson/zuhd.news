Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK) borrowed dearer dollars to buy costlier barrels. The 3-year Eurobond, upsized from $500m to $750m via a green-shoe option, carries 6.975% interest — the price of war-era oil imports. Pakistan mediates [US](country:US)-[Iran](country:IR) ceasefire talks this week; a deal would lower the oil import bill driving the bond.
