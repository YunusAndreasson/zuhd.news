Kathmandu — Depression deepens among Bhutanese with no citizenship anywhere.

[Bhutan](country:BT) expelled Lhotshampa in the 1990s; third-country resettlement in the [US](country:US) was their only legal anchor before deportation severed it.

No South Asian government has offered legal recognition one year on.
