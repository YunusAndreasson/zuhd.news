San Francisco — Jason Wei quit Meta's TBD Lab after about a year.

He becomes chief scientist at Mercor, valued at $20 billion after a $750 million round on 10 November.

Mercor began as an AI recruiting platform and now sells expert training data, model evaluation and post-training.

Wei will help build the research team Mercor formed on 8 October to study evaluation and post-training.
