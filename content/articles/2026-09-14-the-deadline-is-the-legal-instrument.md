Rabat — Zahra watches excavators from her bedroom window in L'Ocean.

Elderly and disabled residents get weeks of notice before their homes come down.

Hundreds of buildings along the coastline have been razed for redevelopment tied to [Morocco](country:MA)'s 2030 World Cup hosting.

Rights group AMDH says tens of thousands of families have been displaced since January.
