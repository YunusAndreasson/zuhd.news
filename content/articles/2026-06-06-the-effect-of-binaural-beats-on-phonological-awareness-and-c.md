London — Binaural beats improved reading scores in 45 dyslexic children.

The tones synchronize brain rhythms; at theta frequency they enhanced frontal-temporal connectivity linked to decoding sounds into words, a study found.

The sample of 45 children is small; the authors call for replication before any clinical use.
