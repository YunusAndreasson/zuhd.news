Geneva — Over 95 states wanted a treaty and got more talks.

Stop Killer Robots says weapons that kill without human sign-off are already deployed, including in Ukraine.

Consensus rules let [Russia](country:RU) and the [US](country:US), activists say, soften the mandate: states will elaborate an instrument, not negotiate one.

"Technology is moving faster than diplomacy," said Frederico Meyer, [Brazil](country:BR)'s ambassador.

Chair Julien Thoni says the process will decide which weapons are banned and which regulated by 2028.
