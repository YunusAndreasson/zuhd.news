Hong Kong — A metasurface detects corners without a processor.

It removes the compute and power budget that limits always-on cameras.

The metasurface applies an azimuthal Hilbert transform to light itself, finding edges before any sensor reads them.

The team has shown potential for motion tracking, not yet a deployed camera.
