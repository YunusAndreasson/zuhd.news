São Paulo — Brazilian companies fled into dollar stablecoins before Lula's win.

The dollar then jumped more than 8% against the real, its largest daily gain since 1999.

Corporate accounts made 78% of Mercado Bitcoin's Sunday trading, and Tether turnover rose nearly 800%, the exchange said.

Lula's team targets a budget surplus of 1.3% of GDP by 2030, but [Brazil](country:BR)'s public debt exceeds 82%.

Investors now watch Lula's choice of finance minister for his fiscal approach.
