China — AI hardware drove 40.3% of [China](country:CN)'s export growth in nine months.

Trade economist He Weiwen cites that to rebut the overcapacity charge from the [US](country:US) and EU.

His figures show a $123.5 billion deficit in chips and data-processing equipment, as imports outgrew exports.

His figures also put September's surplus at $133.5 billion, an annualised peak of $1.6 trillion.

The EU, he writes, plans tougher trade restrictions after a G20 statement blamed China's surplus.
