Puerto Ayora — Galápagos coral cores reveal a fiercer El Niño.

The swings reshape rainfall across a third of humanity's food systems.

Coral strontium ratios track temperature, and University of Michigan researchers found today's swings 36.5% stronger than before 1850.

Study co-author Cole expects the current El Niño, still developing, to break records.
