40,000 citizens began receiving $800 per year in the world's first national universal basic income. The Marshall Islands funds the program through its US military access compact, delivering payments via bank transfer or cryptocurrency to reach remote atolls without banks. The government designed the scheme to stem emigration from a nation that may lose most of its land to rising seas.
