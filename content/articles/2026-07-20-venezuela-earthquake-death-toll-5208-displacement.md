Caracas — 5,208 confirmed dead in [Venezuela](country:VE).

The National Assembly's daily count also lists 16,740 injured and 17,907 left homeless after June's twin 7.2 and 7.5 magnitude quakes.

Rescue teams have pulled 6,462 survivors as 1,388 aftershocks continue to hit Yaracuy state.
