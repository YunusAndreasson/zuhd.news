Trina Lau sent 50 job applications and received 6 callbacks. Lau was laid off after 6 months at her first Hong Kong job, part of a wave of entry-level cuts that analysts blame on AI adoption. Analysts warn of a "broken talent chain" if graduates cannot build experience before automation eliminates their starting roles entirely.
