San Francisco — OpenAI traced a model-copying campaign's core to Moonshot AI.

Extracted reasoning can train rival models without the original safeguards, OpenAI says.

Distillation trains a rival on a model's answers, and OpenAI counted 16,000 such requests from 4,000 users on 24-25 July.

OpenAI says it is unclear whether all operators worked for one company, and Moonshot has not yet responded.

OpenAI has not said which of its models the campaign targeted.
