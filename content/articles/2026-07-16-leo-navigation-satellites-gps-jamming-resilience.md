Burlingame — A closer orbit means a jammer-proof signal.

Xona's Pulsar satellites fly low enough to broadcast 100 times stronger than the Global Positioning System, shrinking a jammer's effective range by 95%, the company said.

Six production satellites launch in October, with the full 258-satellite constellation to follow.
