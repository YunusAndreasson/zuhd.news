Karachi — Sindh faces 22% water deficit for 10 days.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s Indus authority is equalising shortages between Sindh and Punjab by cutting the downstream province's allocation, Sindh says.

The authority plans to equalise shortages by June 10; Sindh's minister is demanding federal intervention.
