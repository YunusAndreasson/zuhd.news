Washington — The Supreme Court blocked Roundup cancer suits against Monsanto.

[US](country:US) law bars state suits when the Environmental Protection Agency omits a cancer warning, regardless of scientific consensus.

Talc and social-media cases face the same federal preemption argument, the court said.
