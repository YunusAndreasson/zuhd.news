Adelaide now has 4 times as many extreme heat days as it did in the 1950s. Australia's average summer temperature rose only 1°C since then, but that 1°C has quadrupled extreme events in inland cities; in Melbourne, days above 42°C since 2000 equal the total from the prior 90 years. Cape Town hit 43°C for a 3rd consecutive autumn day this week, confirming the same pattern across the Southern Hemisphere.
