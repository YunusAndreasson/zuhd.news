Geneva — Survodutide cut liver fat 59% versus 10% for placebo in a 48-week phase 3 trial of 218 adults.

The drug targets both glucagon-like peptide-1 and glucagon receptors; the glucagon arm acts on the liver directly, not just appetite.

Regulatory submission timelines were not disclosed; a filing date has not been set.
