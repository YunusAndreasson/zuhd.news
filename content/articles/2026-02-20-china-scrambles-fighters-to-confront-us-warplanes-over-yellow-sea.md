China scrambled fighters over the Yellow Sea on Wednesday. 10 US F-16s from Osan Air Base in South Korea approached China's air defence identification zone in a training operation that prompted the rare interception. Neither side's aircraft crossed into the other's airspace, but the encounter underscores rising miscalculation risks as both militaries operate in close proximity.
