London — Police arrested a British-Iranian man, 27, on terrorism suspicion.

The prime minister links [Iran](country:IR) to Fairford, but police cite only a line of inquiry.

Revolutionary Guard officials have called any base used to attack Iranian territory a legitimate target, and Fairford hosts US bombers.

Iran's foreign minister Abbas Araghchi denied involvement, telling London it is "barking up the wrong tree."

Andy Burnham has promised an update, but police have not said what the five bailed men planned.
