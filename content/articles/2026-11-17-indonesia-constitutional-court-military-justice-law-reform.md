Jakarta — [Indonesia](country:ID)'s 1997 Military Justice Law is void in 2 years unless amended.

Soldiers who commit crimes could face civilian courts, as a slain journalist's daughter petitioned.

Article 9 holds active-duty soldiers accountable only under military justice, even for ordinary crimes and human rights violations.

The Constitutional Court's 15 October ruling gives lawmakers until October 2028 to amend the law.
