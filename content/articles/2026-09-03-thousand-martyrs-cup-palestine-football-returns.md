Deir al-Balah — 240 clubs open Friday's Thousand Martyrs Cup.

The tournament asserts that Palestinian football, and its people, still exist.

It is named for more than 1,000 athletes from [Palestine](country:PS) killed since October 2023, the football association said.

Continued strikes by [Israel](country:IL) near Deir al-Balah threaten whether Gaza's players can even reach matches.
