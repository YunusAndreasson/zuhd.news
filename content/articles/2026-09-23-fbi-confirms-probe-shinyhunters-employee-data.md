Washington — The FBI is investigating claimed theft of agents' home addresses.

ShinyHunters says it holds data on almost all agents, their spouses and job applicants.

The group says it entered through an Oracle PeopleSoft server, an account neither the FBI nor the companies confirmed.

The bureau cited only FBIjobs.gov and has confirmed neither a breach nor its scale.

ShinyHunters demands the FBI withdraw its May advisory, and has not said what it will do if refused.
