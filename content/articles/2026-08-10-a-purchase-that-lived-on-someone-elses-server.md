San Jose — Cold Iron shut the servers, no refunds.

Buyers who paid up to $60 now own nothing playable at all.

Because the Switch version only streamed, the shutdown erased the whole game, not just its online features.

Cold Iron's only offer is a discount on its own sequel, out August 25.
