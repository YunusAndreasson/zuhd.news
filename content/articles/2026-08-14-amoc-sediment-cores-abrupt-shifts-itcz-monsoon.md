Recife — Seabed cores show Atlantic currents can flip abruptly.

The weakest currents are the likeliest to jump suddenly, not fade slowly.

Researchers from [Brazil](country:BR) and Germany studied ocean-floor records off the northeast coast to reconstruct past circulation swings.

Scientists still don't know how close today's current sits to that threshold.
