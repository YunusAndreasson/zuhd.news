Kuala Lumpur — Malaysia sells subsidized fuel it can no longer afford. Petronas, which funds 10-15% of Malaysia's government budget, cannot offset the subsidy gap as oil prices rise. Malaysia must either cut fuel subsidies — which cover basic prices for millions — or accept a widening deficit as long as prices stay high.
