Almaty — Kazakhstan's super-apps can freeze users out financially.

Kazakh law allows blocking apps with no recourse window; one suspension cuts payments, identity, and credit at once.

[Indonesia](country:ID), [Pakistan](country:PK), and [Nigeria](country:NG) face the same gap as their super-apps approach the scale of Kaspi, Kazakhstan's dominant platform.
