Washington — Trump Jr.'s firm invested $300 million in Polymarket, Kalshi's rival.

He held Kalshi equity while the [US](country:US) administration backed Kalshi against state regulators.

The Commodity Futures Trading Commission has sued 11 states over regulation and filed briefs backing Kalshi.

Kalshi chief executive Tarek Mansour said Trump Jr. never had regulatory involvement.

New Jersey is asking the Supreme Court to overturn Kalshi's Third Circuit win.
