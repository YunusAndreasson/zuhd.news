Cambridge — LHS 1140-b has a detectable atmosphere.

Astronomers detected helium in its atmosphere — the first proof a rocky, habitable-zone world can retain one, since hydrogen escapes but helium lingers.

Researchers now plan follow-up observations to search the atmosphere for signs of life.
