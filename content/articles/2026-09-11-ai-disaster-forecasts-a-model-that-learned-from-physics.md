Switzerland — New AI models forecast disasters in seconds.

That speed could let countries without supercomputers issue their own warnings.

The models train on decades of satellite and reanalysis climate data, so they inherit its blind spots on rare extremes.

The approach depends on continued NASA data access as [US](country:US) science agencies face budget cuts.
