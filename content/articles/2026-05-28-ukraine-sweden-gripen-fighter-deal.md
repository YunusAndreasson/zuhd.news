Uppsala — [Sweden](country:SE) donates 16 Gripens, sells [Ukraine](country:UA) 20 more.

Saab's Gripen is built for dispersed road-base ops — it can land on highways — matching Ukraine's survival tactic against Russian airfield strikes.

The 20 Gripen E jets, financed via a €2.5B EU loan, target delivery from 2030.
