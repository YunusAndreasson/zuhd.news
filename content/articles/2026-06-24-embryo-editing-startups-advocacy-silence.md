New York — 2 startups are commercializing heritable embryo gene editing.

Preventive and Origin Genomics combine gene editing with IVF to correct germline mutations — edits inherited by all future descendants of treated embryos.

Disease advocacy groups whose communities are targeted have stayed silent as both companies move toward clinical trials.
