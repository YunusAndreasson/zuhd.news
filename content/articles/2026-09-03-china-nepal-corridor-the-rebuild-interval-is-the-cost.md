Kathmandu — Nepal's China trade corridor has closed again.

Traders lose their only alternative to routing through India for years.

The route crosses an unstable Himalayan corridor linking [China](country:CN) and Nepal, prone to repeated flood and landslide closures.

Kathmandu must now decide whether to rebuild the corridor in the same valley.
