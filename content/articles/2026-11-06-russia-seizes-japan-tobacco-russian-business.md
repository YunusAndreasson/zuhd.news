Moscow — Putin seized the maker of 37% of Russia's tobacco market.

[Japan](country:JP) Tobacco drew a quarter of its operating profit from [Russia](country:RU) last year.

A decree put four manufacturing units under a local company's temporary administration, so Japan Tobacco owns them but no longer controls them.

Finance Minister Satsuki Katayama said Tokyo protested and demanded withdrawal, while Japan Tobacco says it is assessing the impact.
