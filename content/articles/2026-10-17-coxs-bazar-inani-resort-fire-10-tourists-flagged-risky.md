Inani — A resort flagged risky in June burned, killing 10 tourists.

[Bangladesh](country:BD)'s Fire Service says Tuptup Resort never had approval and ignored its June notice.

The wooden building had a kitchen below tourists' rooms, and the nearest fire station is 22 kilometres away.

The Fire Service will review how to stop risky establishments ignoring notices, its director general said.
