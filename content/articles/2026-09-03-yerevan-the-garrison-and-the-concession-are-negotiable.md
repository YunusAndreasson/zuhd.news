Yerevan — Armenia would not oppose its Russian base leaving.

It signals the garrison and railway concession Moscow has held since 1991 are now negotiable.

Prime Minister Nikol Pashinyan wants Russia's railway rights transferred to a third country, after [Russia](country:RU) warned EU membership would break [Armenia](country:AM)'s trade bloc ties.

Yerevan has promised a referendum on EU accession "in the near future."
