Islamabad — Inflation forecast hits double digits: 9-10%.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s finance ministry names Gulf tensions a downside risk, since nearly all its crude and LNG are priced off a war zone, straining reserves and the rupee.

The ministry ties relief to a durable US-Iran peace deal it does not control.
