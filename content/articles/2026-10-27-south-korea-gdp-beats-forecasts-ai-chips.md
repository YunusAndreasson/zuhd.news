Seoul — [South Korea](country:KR)'s economy grew 1% last quarter, 3x the forecast.

Memory chips carry the boom while private consumption fell 0.2%.

Customs data show chips made nearly half of September's $120.9bn exports, and SK Hynix and Samsung hold about 80% of high-bandwidth memory.

The finance minister expects 3.4-3.6% growth for 2026, the strongest since 2011 outside the pandemic.
