Paris — Petrol bombs thrown at 15 gendarmes prompted an attempted-murder probe.

The interior ministry says more than 770 people were arrested across [France](country:FR) at the weekend.

A firebomb burst at one gendarme's feet, and the gendarmerie said 13 members of his unit were injured.

The ministry counts 159 officers injured across the protests and Halloween celebrations.

Investigators have arrested no suspects, and presidential elections are six months away.
