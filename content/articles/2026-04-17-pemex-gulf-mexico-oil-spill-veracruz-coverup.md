Veracruz — Pemex hid a pipeline spill for 2 months. Oil from the Abkatun field leaked Feb 6 and spread 600 km across 7 protected reserves before Pemex's CEO demanded records in writing to confirm the breach. 3 officials were fired, but spill volume remains uncalculated; Greenpeace counts 1,146 Pemex spills since 2008.
