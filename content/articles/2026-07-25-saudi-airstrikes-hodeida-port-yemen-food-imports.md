Saudi airstrikes wounded 2 at [Yemen](country:YE)'s biggest port.

Hodeida handles most of Yemen's food and fuel imports, and Houthis responded by firing missiles at Saudi Aramco sites in Yanbu and Jizan.

Houthis threaten to close the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, which carries 12% of world trade, unless [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s blockade ends.
