Brussels — [India](country:IN)'s oil traders listed in EU's latest [Russia](country:RU) sanctions.

The EU froze its $44.10 oil price cap through January, citing Hormuz shocks, and named 50 third-country firms for circumventing restrictions.

The package requires unanimous EU approval; Indian listings complicate a trade agreement finalised in February.
