Blackouts broke what 65 years of US sanctions could not. Venezuela's political collapse cut Cuba's oil imports in January, triggering nationwide power cuts that drove Havana to offer exiles the right to own businesses for the first time since 1961. Cuba is in early talks with Washington, but the US embargo will determine whether any exile actually invests.
