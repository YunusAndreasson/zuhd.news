London — Jury cleared 3 Kurdish activists of Kurdistan Workers' Party membership charges.

The [UK](country:GB) banned the Kurdistan Workers' Party in 2001 under NATO pressure from [Turkey](country:TR), criminalizing diaspora cultural organizing alongside armed activity.

3 other defendants face retrial after the jury deadlocked; a final verdict could take years.
