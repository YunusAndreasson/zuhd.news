Cuba's power grid shut down entirely Monday. Trump blocked Venezuelan oil tankers 3 months ago, starving Cuba's thermoelectric plants of the heavy crude they were built to burn. Cuban President Díaz-Canel confirmed emergency talks with Washington are underway as Trump said a deal is imminent.
