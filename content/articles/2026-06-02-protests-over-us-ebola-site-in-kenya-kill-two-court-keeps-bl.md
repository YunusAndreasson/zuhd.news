Nanyuki — 2 Kenyans were shot dead protesting a US Ebola quarantine base, an organiser said.

The [US](country:US) planned the base to quarantine Americans exposed to Ebola in Kenya rather than repatriate them.

[Kenya](country:KE)'s High Court barred construction; it ordered facility agreements disclosed within 7 days, with a June 23 hearing.
