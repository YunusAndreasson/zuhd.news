Malaysia cut its annual logging limit by 275,000 hectares. The government pays states for leaving forests intact through Ecological Fiscal Transfers — a mechanism that makes unlogged land more profitable than cleared land. State forestry departments must build ecotourism programs to replace logging income, with targets set under the 13th Malaysia Plan running to 2030.
