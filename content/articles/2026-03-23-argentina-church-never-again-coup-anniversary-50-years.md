Buenos Aires — Argentine bishops condemned a coup their predecessors had blessed. The 1976 military junta killed 30,000 people over 7 years, with Church leaders informing on dissidents and blessing officers throughout the Dirty War. Argentine President Javier Milei disputes the 30,000 death toll and has not commented on the bishops' statement before Monday's 50th anniversary.
