Calabar — 9 in 10 Cross River residents want out.

SBM Intelligence found 89.9% of [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Cross River residents intend to leave, citing failed roads, healthcare, and electricity.

Cross River ranked last in 11 of 15 quality-of-life indicators; SBM warned the state risks a demographic haemorrhage within a decade.
