Washington — The Senate holds a cloture vote in 48 hours.

The Clarity Act decides who regulates crypto: the Securities and Exchange Commission or the Commodity Futures Trading Commission.

A decade of US crypto enforcement has run by lawsuit, not by statute.

Without 60 votes this month, the bill likely dies for 2026.
