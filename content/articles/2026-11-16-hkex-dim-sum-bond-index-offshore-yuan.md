Hong Kong — The city's stock exchange launched an index of 305 offshore yuan bonds.

The bonds were worth $42 billion as of 30 October.

Sub-indexes by duration and credit rating let providers build ETFs on dim sum bonds, the exchange said.

Only bonds of at least 1 billion yuan with a year or more to maturity qualify, and the average yield is 2.08%.

Dim sum issuance hit 590 billion yuan in 9 months, above any full year, the exchange said.
