San Francisco — 30 WordPress plugins bought, backdoored, and silently pushed. The attacker acquired the plugins through legitimate transfers, then used WordPress's auto-update to push malicious code to every installed site. Plugin marketplace transfers are unaudited; the plugin you installed last year may now have a new owner.
