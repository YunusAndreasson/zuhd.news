India got tankers through Hormuz; Sri Lanka got rationing. The Strait of Hormuz war cut oil supplies to Sri Lanka and Thailand, which lack the diplomatic leverage India used to negotiate passage for 2 tankers with Iranian permission. Both governments imposed emergency fuel measures Monday with no alternative supply routes and no timeline for Hormuz to reopen.
