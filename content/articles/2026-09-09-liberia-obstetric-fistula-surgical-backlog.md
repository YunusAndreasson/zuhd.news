Monrovia — Some women wait years for a single operation.

Obstructed labour without a caesarean tears tissue between bladder and vagina, causing lifelong incontinence.

Specialist repair surgeons remain scarce across [Liberia](country:LR), so a backlog stacks on top of the original injury.

The report names no timeline for expanding Liberia's supply of repair surgeons.
