New York — The archdiocese will pay $800 million to 1,300 survivors.

It is the second-largest [US](country:US) clergy abuse settlement, after Los Angeles's $880 million in 2024.

Each survivor is expected to get at least $250,000, more for graver abuse, and the deal ends years of litigation without trial.

Archbishop Ronald Hicks and the plaintiffs formally sign the settlement on 6 November.
