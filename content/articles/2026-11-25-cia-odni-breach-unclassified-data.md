Washington — Central Intelligence Agency breach also exposed other agencies' data, officials say.

Commercially bought data, unclassified alone, can be highly revealing when combined with other tranches.

The intrusion hit an open-source analysis system that both spy agencies use.

The Office of the Director of National Intelligence said classified systems and critical capabilities saw no disruptions.

Investigators have not identified the perpetrators, and officials have not said what data was taken.
