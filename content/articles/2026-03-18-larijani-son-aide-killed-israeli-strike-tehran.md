Israeli forces struck Tehran, killing former parliament speaker Ali Larijani. Larijani's son Morteza and an aide also died — the first Israeli strike to kill a figure of his seniority inside Iran. Iran's foreign minister said the government rests on institutions, not individuals, and confirmed Iran's response had already begun.
