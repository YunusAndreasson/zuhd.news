Boston — Robots solved walking but still cannot grasp reliably. 3 paradigm shifts — deep learning, proprioceptive motors, and large language models — transformed locomotion but left dexterous manipulation unsolved. Researchers from Boston Dynamics and Agility Robotics say progress requires understanding how human fingers work rather than scaling AI models further.
