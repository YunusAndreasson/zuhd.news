Novartis spent $2 billion on an unproven cancer drug. SNV-4818 targets a mutated PI3K enzyme in cancer cells while leaving its healthy counterpart intact, a selectivity that earlier drugs in the class failed to achieve. Novartis will pay up to $1 billion more in milestone payments if the drug clears later-stage trials.
