Patna — Welfare cuts are severing transgender women from antiretroviral therapy.

[India](country:IN)'s amended transgender rights law stripped Bihar welfare payments that also served as the paperwork trail linking women to HIV treatment.

Interrupted therapy risks viral rebound and drug resistance among a population with high HIV prevalence.
