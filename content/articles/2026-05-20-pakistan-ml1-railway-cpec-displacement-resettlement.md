Karachi — Railway expansion threatens residents with no resettlement plan.

ML-1 starts in July; rights groups say [Pakistan](country:PK) Railways is using right-of-way rules to target slums while commercial plots along the same track stand untouched.

No environmental impact assessment has been published and no family list released before construction begins.
