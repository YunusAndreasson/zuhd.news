Dimona — Iran struck Israel's undeclared nuclear site, wounding 39. The attack came hours after US-Israeli forces hit Iran's Natanz enrichment complex — the first strikes on each other's nuclear infrastructure in the war. A military source told Tasnim that Tehran has abandoned proportional retaliation and will strike back with broader, more damaging force.
