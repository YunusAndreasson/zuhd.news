London — Rice blast fungus destroys enough grain yearly to feed millions.

The finding opens a path to new fungicides against the disease.

An enzyme, Hos2, switches on a fungal catalase that neutralizes rice's reactive-oxygen defense.

A newly found molecule, orcinol gentiobioside, already blocks that switch in lab tests.
