70,000 Facebook users belong to groups selling protected animals. Members use codes like "WC" for wild-caught and alphanumeric price tags to evade Facebook's AI moderation, investigative outlet Bellingcat found. Fewer than 2,500 silvery gibbons survive — the species cannot recover at the pace trafficker groups re-form after takedowns.
