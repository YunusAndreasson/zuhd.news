São Paulo — Heat could kill 13,300 Brazilians this summer, University of Chicago researchers project.

Only 385 of [Brazil](country:BR)'s 5,500 municipalities reported no extreme weather event.

In the statistics institute's survey, floods hit 49.5% of cities from 2021 to 2024, up from 35%.

A columnist doubts mayors are preparing for the worst of this summer's Super El Niño.
