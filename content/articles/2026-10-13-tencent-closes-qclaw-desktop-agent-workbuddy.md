Shenzhen — Tencent's QClaw desktop agent shuts on 24 December.

Users move to WorkBuddy, an office agent tied to Tencent Cloud, documents and enterprise tools.

Chinese tech outlet TMTPost argues QClaw failed as demand faded and agents controlling a whole computer created security anxiety.

Alibaba and ByteDance have also merged separate agent teams into single office products.
