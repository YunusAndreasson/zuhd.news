China — Researchers corrected a qubit's errors without ever measuring it.

Conventional correction needs extra operations that add errors of their own.

A second ion cools the pair's shared motion, which absorbs the error's entropy from a calcium ion's four-level qubit.

The Chinese team corrects only certain phase errors, those caused by fluctuating magnetic fields.

Nature Physics published the result, which covers one logical qubit in one trapped calcium ion.
