Geneva — 92 antiprotons drove 4 km on the CERN campus. CERN's portable container uses liquid helium and magnetic fields to prevent the antiprotons from annihilating on contact with air. CERN shuts for Large Hadron Collider upgrades through 2028, delaying the next test of delivering antiprotons to labs outside the campus.
