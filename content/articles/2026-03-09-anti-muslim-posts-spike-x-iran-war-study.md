Anti-Muslim posts on X tripled when the Iran war started. The Center for the Study of Organized Hate found posts spiked from under 2,000 to over 6,000 daily, reaching 279,417 users through reposts — an 11-fold amplification; of 30 posts reported to X for violations, only 11 were removed. Researchers warn wartime hate speech on social platforms historically precedes hate crimes; X announced no new moderation measures.
