Berlin — Meta erased PRAZZLE Arts' Instagram page without giving a reason.

In [Germany](country:DE), bans fall often on queer and progressive accounts, which lose reach overnight.

An automated decision took down journalist Fran Ñurinda's linked Facebook account, and the appeal link showed documents under review though he had filed none.

Festivals now question whether PRAZZLE is stable, founder Ann Austin said, and some stopped replying.

The EU's Digital Services Act requires a statement of reasons, which neither account holder received.
