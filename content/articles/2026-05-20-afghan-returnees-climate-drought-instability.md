Kabul — 3 million Afghans returned home in 2025 alone.

[Afghanistan](country:AF) combines 4 decades of war with frost waves, floods, and summer heatwaves — returnees arrive into compound stress, the International Rescue Committee said.

[Pakistan](country:PK) and [Iran](country:IR) continue expelling Afghans into a country the UN says offers no viable return.
