GPS navigation has failed across the UAE. Gulf states are spoofing GPS to misdirect Iranian drones — broadcasting false signals that reach every receiver in range, disrupting shipping, aviation, and personal navigation. The UAE has absorbed over 1,500 drone attacks since the war began; spoofing will continue as long as Iranian strikes persist.
