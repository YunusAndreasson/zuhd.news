Rawalakot — A spare bedroom now serves as a trauma ward.

Since July 27, the clinic has treated 50-60 gunshot patients as protesters against flour and electricity prices avoid the main hospital, fearing arrest.

Most wounded are 15 to 35; doctors say advanced hospitals are needed for chest and head injuries.
