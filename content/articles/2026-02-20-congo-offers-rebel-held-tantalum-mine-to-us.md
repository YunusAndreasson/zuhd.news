Congo put the Rubaya tantalum mine on a shortlist for US investment. The North Kivu deposit holds 15% of world coltan output but is controlled by M23 rebels under US sanctions. Kinshasa hopes the offer draws Washington into recovering the territory as part of a broader critical minerals deal.
