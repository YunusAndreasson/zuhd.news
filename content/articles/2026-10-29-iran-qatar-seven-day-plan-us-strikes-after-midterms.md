Tehran — US officials warn Gulf states of strikes after 3 November.

[Iran](country:IR)'s security officials have drawn up plans to attack Gulf undersea cables if strikes resume.

A US carrier group and Marine unit are expected to join about 20 warships next month, and a Patriot system is moving from Texas.

A senior Iranian official called Qatar's latest ideas unacceptable and not an offer.

Tehran owes [Qatar](country:QA) a response on its seven-day plan by the end of the week.
