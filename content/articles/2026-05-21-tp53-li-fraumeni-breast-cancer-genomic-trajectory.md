London — Li-Fraumeni syndrome and sporadic breast cancers are genomically distinct.

Li-Fraumeni tumors show lower CD8+ T-cell infiltration and only HER2 amplification, unlike sporadic disease's broader oncogene profile.

Women with germline TP53 mutations remain on sporadic-disease protocols; the study's authors argue for separate trial cohorts.
