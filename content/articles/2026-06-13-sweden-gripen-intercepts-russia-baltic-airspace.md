Stockholm — [Sweden](country:SE) scrambled jets twice against Russian Baltic aircraft.

The Baltic carries [Russia](country:RU)'s sanctioned shadow fleet and undersea cables; Sweden joined NATO in 2024 and now patrols both.

Prime Minister Kristersson called it a recurring pattern; no Swedish airspace was violated.
