"Death to the dictator" echoed from Tehran rooftops. 40-day mourning memorials for protesters killed in earlier unrest became anti-government rallies across Iran, with crowds directing anger at Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei. Iranian authorities have not commented on the protests, which erupted amid soaring prices and political frustration.
