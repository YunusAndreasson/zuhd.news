Alexandria — Management shut the mill workers were striking inside.

An indefinite closure stops strike wages without a single dismissal a labour court could review.

Workers say bonuses are calculated on base pay, not insured wages, cutting a legally mandated allowance by two-thirds.

Buses to the plant stopped Saturday, and no reopening date has been set.
