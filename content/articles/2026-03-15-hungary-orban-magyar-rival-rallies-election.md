100,000 gathered for opposition leader Péter Magyar in Budapest. Prime Minister Viktor Orbán's Fidesz party has trailed Magyar's Tisza in polls since 2025 — the first time in Orbán's 16-year rule he faces a credible rival. Hungary votes April 12 in what polls show is the closest election of Orbán's era.
