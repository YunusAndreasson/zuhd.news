Companies are orbiting AI infrastructure beyond any nation's reach. Data localization laws — designed to keep citizen data within borders — become legally ambiguous once computing infrastructure orbits above them. Countries without launch capacity — South Africa, India, Brazil — cannot follow their data into orbit; no multilateral framework exists.
