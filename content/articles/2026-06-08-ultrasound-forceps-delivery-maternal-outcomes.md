Tokyo — Pre-delivery scans cut neonatal injury risk 53% in a [Japan](country:JP) trial.

A study of 216 matched cases found maternal injury also fell by 47% when ultrasound preceded forceps use.

Forceps remain common in low-resource settings without imaging, though whether the benefit holds there is untested.
