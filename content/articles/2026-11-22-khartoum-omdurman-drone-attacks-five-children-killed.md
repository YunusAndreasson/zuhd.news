Khartoum — Dawn drones killed 5 children and wounded at least 10 people.

Four of the dead belonged to a family that had already fled fighting in West Kordofan.

A drone believed to be the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces' hit an Omdurman house, while another killed a 10-year-old in Jabal Awliya.

Denise Brown, the UN coordinator, said drones also damaged a UN building with staff inside.
