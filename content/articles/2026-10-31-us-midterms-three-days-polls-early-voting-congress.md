Washington — 43 million Americans have voted early, 10 million more than in 2022.

A Democratic House could block Trump's agenda and open investigations of his administration.

Republicans hold the Senate 53-47, and Democrats need a net 4 seats for a majority.

Kyle Kondik of Sabato's Crystal Ball says the House is almost certain to flip and the Senate far less settled.

Polymarket traders price a Democratic House and Senate at 71%, up 4 points in a week, before Tuesday's vote.
