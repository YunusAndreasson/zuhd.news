Beijing — [China](country:CN) plans 30% clean energy by 2030, up from 22%.

China's plan pairs clean-energy expansion with critical-mineral supply-chain dominance that the G7 is trying to cap at 60% of rare-earth imports from any single supplier.

The G7's cap requires redirecting supply chains China spent two decades building — no timeline exists.
