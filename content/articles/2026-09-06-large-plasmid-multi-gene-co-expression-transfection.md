London — A single DNA circle outperformed splitting genes across three.

Multi-protein therapies like cell treatments and combination vaccines get cheaper to manufacture at scale.

A plasmid, a circular DNA cassette forced through a cell's membrane, entered readily even at sizes up to 16.4 kilobases.

The study remains an early manuscript awaiting final publication.
