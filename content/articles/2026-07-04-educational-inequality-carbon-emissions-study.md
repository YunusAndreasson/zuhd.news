Beijing — 282 Chinese cities show a carbon link.

Researchers found wider education gaps in [China](country:CN) correlate with higher per-capita emissions, partly by slowing green-technology innovation and industrial upgrading.

The manuscript is an unedited early release; peer review is still pending.
