Tokyo — [China](country:CN) ran a live-fire drill near Okinotorishima this week.

Islands grant a 200-nautical-mile zone under the UN Convention on the Law of the Sea; rocks don't — [Japan](country:JP) calls the reef an island, China calls it a rock.

The dispute tests that rock-versus-island line as China-Russia naval coordination in the Pacific deepens.
