Jakarta — The [US](country:US) asked Jakarta for blanket military overflight rights. [Indonesia](country:ID) has maintained a non-aligned posture for decades; blanket clearance would mark the first permanent access granted to any military power. Approval sets a precedent every rival — [China](country:CN), [Russia](country:RU), [Australia](country:AU) — will immediately press to claim.
