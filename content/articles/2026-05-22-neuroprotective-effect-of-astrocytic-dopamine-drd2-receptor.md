London — Brain support cells protect dopamine neurons against Parkinson's.

Removing astrocyte Drd2 in mice worsened dopamine neuron death and motor function; the study links astrocyte metabolism to disease progression.

The finding opens an astrocyte-targeting drug pathway distinct from existing neuron-focused Parkinson's treatments.
