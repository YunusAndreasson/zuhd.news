London — 77% of converted CO2 became usable hydrocarbons.

A potassium-promoted copper-iron catalyst worked at 250°C and 20 bar — industrial conditions, not idealized lab settings, the study reports.

Solar CO2-to-fuel has stalled for years because bench results collapse at industrial scale; this catalyst did not.
