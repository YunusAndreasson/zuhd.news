New Jersey — [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s $5 billion LIV Golf bet ends in liquidation.

Seven executives received $15.6 million before the 8 September bankruptcy filing, including CEO Scott O'Neil's $6.375 million.

A $300 million BC Partners rescue collapsed when top players, including Dustin Johnson and Bryson DeChambeau, declined to sign.

LIV says it will stay open to third-party offers during the wind-down.
