Qaqortoq — The most fertile water sits against solid ice.

That plume anchors local fisheries, Greenland's largest export industry.

Meltwater surges up from the glacier's base, dragging deep nutrients to the surface and triggering a bloom.

Once a glacier's edge retreats onto land, the pump stops, and the hotspot ends entirely.
