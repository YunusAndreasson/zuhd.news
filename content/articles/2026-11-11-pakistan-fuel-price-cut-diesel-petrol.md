Pakistan — Diesel falls to Rs364 a litre, above the pre-war Rs281.

Diesel runs heavy transport and power plants, so its price reaches the whole public.

Taxes still take Rs100 a litre of diesel and Rs114 of petrol, and refineries absorb Rs15 billion of levy for 30 days.

Diesel peaked at Rs520.35 a litre on 3 April, and petrol at Rs458.41.

The government and Jamaat-i-Islami are still negotiating relief on petroleum prices.
