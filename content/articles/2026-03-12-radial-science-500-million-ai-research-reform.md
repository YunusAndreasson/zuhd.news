Radial has $500 million to fix how science works. Founder Seemay Chou argues AI cannot deliver in science until replication, peer review, and data sharing are rebuilt. CEO Becky Pferdehirt says Radial will publish all results — including failures — as a test of whether open science can outperform the closed academic model.
