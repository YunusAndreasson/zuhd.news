London — Standard susceptibility tests report resistant bacteria as safe.

Patients can be prescribed a drug that then fails for reasons unrecorded.

Glucose availability, not genetic mutation, switches on resistant, fosfomycin-tolerant cells within a single bacterial population.

Doctors currently have no simple bedside test to catch this metabolic resistance.
