Ljubljana — vCATCH maps small-molecule drugs inside living tissue. Small molecules are too small for fluorescence imaging; vCATCH uses click chemistry to tag covalent drug binding with single-cell resolution across organisms. Researchers validated it on 2 clinical cancer drugs; the pipeline could reshape how off-target effects are characterized.
