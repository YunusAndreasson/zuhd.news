Sydney — A donated liver stayed alive outside the body for 7 weeks.

Donor livers normally last hours on ice, so longer storage could mean more transplants.

An automated pump system feeds the organ nutrients and blood at body temperature, and across 55 livers automation tripled average survival to about 4 weeks.

Apersys CEO Waldemar Hoffmann says peers do not know how healthy the liver was at the end.

Surgeon Carlo Pulitano plans a Sydney trial transplanting low-quality livers kept on perfusion for 4 days.
