Karachi — 42,000 HIV patients face treatment gaps as global aid collapses.

[Pakistan](country:PK) spends 0.9% of its GDP on public health; donor money covered surveillance and supply chains the state budget never funded.

Health officials said Balochistan's tuberculosis programme faces an 80% funding gap; each undetected patient infects 12 more on average.
