Isfahan — Rescuers pulled 7,250 people alive from rubble.

It is the first civilian toll accounting either side has offered in the war.

Red Crescent chief Pirhossein Kolivand gave the figure at an Isfahan commemoration event, unverified by outside monitors.

Kolivand said damage documentation has gone to international bodies for legal follow-up.
