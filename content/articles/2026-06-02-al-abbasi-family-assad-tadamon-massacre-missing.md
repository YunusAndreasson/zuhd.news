Damascus — [Syria](country:SY)'s commission certified 6 disappeared children dead.

The commission used massacre footage to certify deaths Syria's Assad government engineered — no remains have been recovered in 13 years.

The findings sparked nationwide demands for transitional justice as the commission continues searching for remains.
