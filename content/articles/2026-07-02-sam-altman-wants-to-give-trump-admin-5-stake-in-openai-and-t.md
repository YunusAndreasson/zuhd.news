Washington — OpenAI's chief wants the government as shareholder.

Sam Altman has proposed a 5% government stake, modeled on the Alaska Permanent Fund, as OpenAI faces scrutiny over data centers and jobs.

Any deal needs an act of Congress; Google, Anthropic and Meta have not said whether they'd follow.
