Washington told New York courts to ease off Argentina. The US needs Argentina's Vaca Muerta shale gas to offset Hormuz supply losses, making a $16 billion court judgment inconveniently costly to enforce. The Second Circuit is reviewing both the judgment and enforcement orders, with an evidentiary hearing set for April.
