Muzaffarabad — The state banned the group it had just conceded to.

[Pakistan](country:PK) People's Party chief Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari asked the government to form a truth and reconciliation commission over the proscribed Joint Awami Action Committee, which led cost-of-living protests in Azad Kashmir.

Neither the state nor the committee has agreed to halt the standoff first.
