Santa Clara — AI agents trained robots to insert GPUs overnight.

Nvidia's GEAR lab gave AI coding agents full control; an 8-agent team reached 99% success on manipulation tasks in 2 hours without human design input.

Jim Fan, Nvidia's GEAR lab lead, said Nvidia will open-source the framework so anyone can host a self-running robot lab.
