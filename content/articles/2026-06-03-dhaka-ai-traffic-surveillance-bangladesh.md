Dhaka — [Bangladesh](country:BD) deployed artificial intelligence traffic enforcement with no human review.

The system cross-references plates against a national database and texts owners prosecution notices — 1,000 cases in its first 10 days.

Unregistered battery-rickshaws have no plates to scan; their legal status under automated enforcement is unresolved.
