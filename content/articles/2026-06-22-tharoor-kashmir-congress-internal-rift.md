New Delhi — India's Congress rebuked MP Shashi Tharoor for defending Prime Minister Modi.

Tharoor met [India](country:IN)'s Kashmir Lieutenant Governor, then defended Modi's remarks to US officials about protecting Indian sailors killed in the Gulf of Oman.

Congress has not confirmed censure; Tharoor says his party is politicizing Indian deaths.
