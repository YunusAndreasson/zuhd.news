Bengaluru — Residents may pay ₹25,000 to park curbside.

The fee reveals a subsidy: free curb storage for private cars on public land.

Bengaluru can't widen its roads, so parked cars take lane space buses and footpaths need.

Karnataka is taking public objections before the draft rules take effect.
