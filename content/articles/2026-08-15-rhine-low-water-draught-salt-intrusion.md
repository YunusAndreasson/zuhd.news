Nijmegen — A beach appeared where the Rhine has dropped.

Falling river levels in the [Netherlands](country:NL) raise shipping costs and threaten drinking-water intakes downstream.

A metre of lost draught roughly halves each barge's cargo, and low flow lets seawater push farther up the delta.

Hydrologists have not said whether this dry summer is now the new normal.
