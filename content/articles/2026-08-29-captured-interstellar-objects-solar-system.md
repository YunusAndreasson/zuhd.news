Bordeaux — Thousands of interstellar objects may already orbit our sun.

If confirmed, one could be sampled directly, the only reachable piece of another star system.

A passing star's gravity, plus a tug from Jupiter, can strip enough energy from a fast-moving object to trap it here, researchers modeled.

The Vera Rubin Observatory's decade-long survey could search for one within reach.
