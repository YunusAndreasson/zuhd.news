Projectiles hit Turkey and Oman on Thursday. Mojtaba Khamenei, a senior Iranian leader, denied responsibility, blaming an Israeli "trick" designed to divide Tehran from its neighbors. Oman, the Gulf's primary diplomatic back-channel, now faces pressure to condemn Iran — a choice that would end its mediating role.
