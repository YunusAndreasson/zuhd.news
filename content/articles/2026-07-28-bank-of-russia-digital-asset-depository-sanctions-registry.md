Moscow — Russia's central bank published draft crypto depository rules.

A depository registers who owns what — a domestic one lets [Russia](country:RU) settle crypto ownership without a foreign custodian sanctions can reach.

The rules follow fresh EU sanctions on Russian-linked crypto firms and take effect under law by September.
