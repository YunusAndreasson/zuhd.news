China — The Pentagon delisted WuXi AppTec to comply with a court ruling.

The listing would have paved the way to a biotech company-of-concern designation.

WuXi argued the listing was arbitrary and capricious under the Administrative Procedure Act, and a [US](country:US) judge blocked it temporarily.

China hawks in Washington still see the contract drug developer as a rising national security threat.
