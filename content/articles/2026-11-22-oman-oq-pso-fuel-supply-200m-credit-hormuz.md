The US-Iran conflict has choked the Strait of Hormuz into its ninth month.

OQ Trading, Oman's state trader, has sold the company over 1m tonnes of gasoline this year, [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Petroleum Division said.

OQ Trading expects liquefied petroleum gas supplies to Pakistan to exceed 120,000 tonnes by the end of 2026.
