Volgograd — Flamingo cruise missiles struck Titan-Barrikady, [Russia](country:RU)'s missile-parts factory.

Titan-Barrikady supplies components for Russia's Oreshnik missiles; [Ukraine](country:UA) targets the weapons supply chain, not the front.

Russia logged 1 dead and 11 wounded; Zelensky declared all Russian defence facilities legitimate targets.
