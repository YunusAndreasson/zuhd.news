Nasdaq and NYSE's parent both announced blockchain ventures. Nasdaq partnered with crypto exchange Kraken to issue blockchain shares by mid-2027, while NYSE's parent invested in OKX, a platform with 120 million users. Blockchain settlement cuts trades from 1 day to near-instant, freeing billions in collateral trapped by current delays.
