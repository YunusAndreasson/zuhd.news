Chapare — UNODC counts 65.5% more coca than [Bolivia](country:BO)'s law allows.

Surplus leaf feeds cocaine labs, with potential output of 275 metric tons, UNODC estimates.

The Cochabamba Tropics has 7,700 authorised hectares, planted 17,470 in 2025, and sold under 10% of its leaf legally.

"The excess coca leaf produced goes directly to the laboratories," said Colonel Jorge Santistevan, Santa Cruz's security secretary.

The [US](country:US) listed Bolivia on 16 September among countries that failed on counternarcotics.
