New Delhi — [India](country:IN)-[UK](country:GB) tariffs fall July 15.

Bilateral trade reached £47.9 billion through 2025, per the UK India Business Council; the deal opens IT, financial, and professional services markets.

The council said the deal advances India's Viksit Bharat 2047 vision — its framework for developed-economy status.
