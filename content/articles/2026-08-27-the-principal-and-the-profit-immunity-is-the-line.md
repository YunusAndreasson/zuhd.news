Brussels — Four EU states want [Russia](country:RU)'s frozen billions unlocked.

Confiscating the principal, not just its profits, breaks sovereign immunity for reserves held abroad.

[Belgium](country:BE) hosts Euroclear, which holds 185 billion euros of the assets, so it alone bears legal and retaliation risk.

EU foreign ministers revisit the idea Sept 1-2 in Ireland, where Belgium remains opposed.
