Geneva — Pacific temperatures rose over 2C in six months.

South Asian crops and East African rains face record swings, the WMO warns.

El Nino redistributes ocean heat rather than adding it, so a hotter baseline turns typical swings into extremes.

The World Meteorological Organization puts the event's persistence through February 2027 at nearly 100%.
