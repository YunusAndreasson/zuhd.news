Ozoro — Police arrested 15 after sexual assault videos went viral. Videos of men stripping women at the Alue-Do fertility festival drove the hashtag #StopRapingWomen and arrests that police made only after public pressure. Nigeria's First Lady has demanded prosecution of all attackers, placing pressure on investigators to identify every suspect visible in the footage.
