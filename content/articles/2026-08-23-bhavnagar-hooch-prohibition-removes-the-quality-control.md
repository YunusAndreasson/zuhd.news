Bhavnagar — Police said the liquor was 38.59% methanol.

Prohibition in [India](country:IN)'s Gujarat state pushes buyers toward unregulated distillers with no quality control.

Bootleggers who skip a required distillation step leave methanol and ethanol indistinguishable to drinkers.

Police have arrested 13 of 21 named suspects, with 3 victims still hospitalized.
