Wellington — New Zealand swapped emissions reductions for pine plantations. Courts must decide whether planting fire-prone pine trees legally substitutes for cutting emissions under New Zealand's climate obligations. Climate lawyers in Australia and the UK are watching the ruling as a template for their own pending cases.
