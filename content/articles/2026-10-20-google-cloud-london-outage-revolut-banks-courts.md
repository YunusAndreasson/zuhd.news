London — Google's only UK cloud region failed at 06:08 GMT Tuesday.

Revolut, HSBC, Halifax and the courts' video-hearing platform were disrupted.

Google blamed a power event in one zone of europe-west2, the region serving the UK and Ireland.

The [UK](country:GB) designated Google Cloud a critical third-party supplier to finance in July.

Google says most workloads are recovering but gives no time for full restoration.
