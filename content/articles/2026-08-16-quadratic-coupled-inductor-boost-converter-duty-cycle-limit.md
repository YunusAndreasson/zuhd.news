London — A prototype turned 20 volts into 400.

Cheap, reliable boost converters decide whether distributed solar pays for itself in poorer grids.

A coupled inductor gets its ratio from transformer turns and cascaded stages, not extreme duty cycle, cutting switch losses.

Only a 200-watt lab prototype exists, with no test yet at grid-scale power.
