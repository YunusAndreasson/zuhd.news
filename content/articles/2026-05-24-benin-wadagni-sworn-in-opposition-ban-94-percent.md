Cotonou — Wadagni took [Benin](country:BJ)'s presidency on a 94% vote.

Main opposition parties were barred from competing; Wadagni, Talon's long-serving finance minister, ran as the sole approved candidate.

Benin's opposition holds no legal route back; the same threshold that cleared the field governs all future elections.
