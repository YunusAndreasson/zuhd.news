Lakshmipur — Maksudur Rahman paid a broker $9,800 for a janitor's job.

Within weeks he was on the front line of [Russia](country:RU)'s war, he said.

A broker takes the fee, then recruits sign Russian-language papers they cannot read and report to an army camp.

Russian officials expect a shortfall of 3.1 million workers by 2030.
