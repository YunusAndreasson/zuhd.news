Jakarta — [Indonesia](country:ID) and [Australia](country:AU) have no formal security treaty across their shared sea border.

Both sides have built shared maritime drone surveillance and undersea cable networks across the archipelagic frontier.

No treaty is under negotiation; the Lowy Institute says sensor infrastructure now substitutes for a formal pact.
