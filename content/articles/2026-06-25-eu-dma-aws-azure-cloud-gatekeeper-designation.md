Brussels — Amazon Web Services and Azure face EU interoperability rules for the first time.

Gatekeeper status, previously limited to search, social, and app stores, now forces interoperability in cloud infrastructure half of European businesses rely on.

Both can contest preliminary findings; a Commission decision follows in months.
