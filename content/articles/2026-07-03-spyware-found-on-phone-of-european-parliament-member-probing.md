Brussels — Pegasus infected the lawmaker probing Pegasus twice.

Stelios Kouloglou served on the European Parliament committee investigating commercial spyware abuse when researchers found the implants on his phone.

Investigators have not named who deployed the spyware or when the infections occurred.
