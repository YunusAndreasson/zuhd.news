Washington — Ports have no way to see them coming.

Reverberation and hull noise hide small submersibles from harbour sonar, the Coast Guard said, citing Ukraine's daylight sub strike in Novorossiysk.

Its Futures directorate has requested industry solutions; no funded programme or timeline exists yet.
