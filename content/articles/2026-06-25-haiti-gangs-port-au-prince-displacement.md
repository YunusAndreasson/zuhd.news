Port-au-Prince — Gangs strike displacement camps beyond the capital.

Armed groups controlling [Haiti](country:HT)'s national highways pursue the displaced, inflicting sexual assault, kidnapping, and arson as the state cedes territory.

More than 1.5 million remain internally displaced, the UN reports; successive international missions have not restored order.
