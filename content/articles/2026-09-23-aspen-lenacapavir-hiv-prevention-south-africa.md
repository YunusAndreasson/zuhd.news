Pretoria — Aspen dropped plans to make generic lenacapavir, HIV's best shield.

[South Africa](country:ZA) has the world's largest HIV epidemic, and only 60,000 people use the injection.

Aspen blames the health department's uncertain procurement, betting instead on an unproven $3 pill from Merck's early license.

"We need greater certainty before we make these investments," said Stavros Nicolaou, Aspen's senior trade executive.

Two Phase 3 trials of alimatravir report in 2027, before its safety is proven.
