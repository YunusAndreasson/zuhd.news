New Delhi — Mercator renders Greenland the size of Africa.

The map fix meant to correct that distortion instead triggered a border dispute.

The Assembly adopted the Equal Earth projection 164-1, but it draws Kashmir inside [China](country:CN) and [Pakistan](country:PK)'s borders.

[India](country:IN) demands its official map prevail, while Pakistan calls the claim baseless.
