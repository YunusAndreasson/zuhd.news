Astana — President Tokayev just named his own successor-in-waiting.

Adviser Erlan Karin now stands first in line to lead [Kazakhstan](country:KZ), an energy and minerals giant.

A new constitution created the vice presidency this year; Tokayev filled it with his ideologist rather than an elected figure.

Tokayev, 73, remains eligible to seek another seven-year term before his current one ends in 2029.
