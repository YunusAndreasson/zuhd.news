California — TP-Link's Wi-Fi 8 routers cannot be sold in the [US](country:US).

A March 2026 ban on new foreign-made routers has produced 26 waivers, none for TP-Link.

The Federal Communications Commission, Pentagon and Homeland Security review waivers in secret, while models approved before March stay on sale.

"They're all equally awful," said security expert Bruce Schneier of router makers.

Representatives Frank Pallone and Brett Guthrie have proposed limiting the commission's power to ban whole equipment categories.
