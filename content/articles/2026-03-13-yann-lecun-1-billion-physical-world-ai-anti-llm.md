Yann LeCun raised $1 billion to build AI that doesn't rely on language models. LeCun, Meta's chief AI scientist, argues that predicting the next word will never reach human-level intelligence — his startup AMI will model how objects interact in the physical world instead. AMI, valued at $3.5 billion, targets manufacturing and biomedical sectors with offices planned across 4 cities.
