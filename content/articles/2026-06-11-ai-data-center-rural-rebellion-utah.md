Box Elder County — Rural Utah fights a 9-gigawatt data center.

O'Leary Digital's Stratos campus would span 40,000 acres and consume double Utah's statewide grid output — powered by a dedicated gas plant.

Residents filed referendum paperwork to overturn the county commission's approval; no date has been set.
