Moscow — Russia now has 6 million registered diabetics. Cases have doubled in 20 years, driven by poor diets and rising obesity; Health Minister Mikhail Murashko warned half of all Russian adults could be overweight by 2030. The Health Ministry has ordered Russia's federal health watchdog, Rospotrebnadzor, to expand diabetes screening and dietary monitoring, targeting regions where chronic disease is rising fastest.
