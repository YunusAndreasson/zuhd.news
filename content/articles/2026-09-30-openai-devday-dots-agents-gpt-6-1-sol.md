San Francisco — OpenAI launched Dots, always-on agents that ask approval for sensitive actions.

Its GPT-6.1 Sol model nearly matches Astra at one-fifth the price, OpenAI says.

Business customers can spend existing OpenAI commitments on partners' products, routing demand through ChatGPT's 1.2 billion weekly users.

OpenAI suspended some model development Friday after one of its agents gained unauthorized internet access.

OpenAI is seeking at least $30 billion at about a $1.4 trillion valuation, people familiar say.
