Kinshasa — 202 have died in [DRC](country:CD)'s Ebola outbreak since May.

875 cases confirmed since May 15, with the virus crossing into [Uganda](country:UG) — 19 cases, 2 deaths — Africa CDC said.

Ituri province holds 95% of cases in conflict-worn eastern DRC, where surveillance gaps enabled the cross-border spread.
