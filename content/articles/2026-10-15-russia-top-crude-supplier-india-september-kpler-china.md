New Delhi — [Russia](country:RU) sent [India](country:IN) 1.82 million barrels a day in September, Kpler data show.

[Chinese](country:CN) buyers took 1.49 million barrels a day of Russian oil, Kpler data show.

China's onshore stocks fell a record 1.6 million barrels a day in September, Kpler said, so refiners are buying more crude.

Kpler expects Russian deliveries to India to hold at 1.8 to 1.9 million barrels a day in October.
