Chiang Rai — Hundreds walked six days along a poisoned river.

Arsenic and lead now exceed safety limits in several riverside communities.

The Kok River rises in [Myanmar](country:MM)'s Shan State, where Chinese-financed mining operates beyond [Thailand](country:TH)'s jurisdiction.

Walkers demanded Thailand investigate the contamination's source and strengthen cross-border cooperation.
