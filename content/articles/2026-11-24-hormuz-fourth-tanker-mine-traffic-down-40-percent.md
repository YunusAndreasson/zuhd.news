Tehran — Ship crossings of Hormuz fell more than 40% in three weeks.

A fourth tanker hit a mine Tuesday after sailing without [Iran](country:IR)'s approval, its Revolutionary Guards said.

Ship-tracking data counted 28 crossings in 24 hours, against 125 to 140 a day before the war.

Brent crude still fell to $88.35 a barrel, down from above $120 at the peak.

Polymarket traders give a [US](country:US)-Iran Hormuz agreement by 31 December a 14% chance.
