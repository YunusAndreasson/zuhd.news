Iran struck the world's largest LNG hub on Wednesday. QatarEnergy chief Saad al-Kaabi says he warned US energy officials almost daily before Israel struck Iran's gas infrastructure. He told US Energy Secretary Chris Wright that LNG deliveries cannot restart until hostilities end, with full restoration taking 5 years.
