London — Four UK nationals face a fresh war-crimes scoping exercise.

The Metropolitan Police had closed the case in April, calling convictions unlikely; it reversed course after a threatened judicial review over a 2025 referral alleging killings in Gaza.

The referral covered 10 Britons who served in the [Israeli](country:IL) military; scoping now decides if a full probe opens.
