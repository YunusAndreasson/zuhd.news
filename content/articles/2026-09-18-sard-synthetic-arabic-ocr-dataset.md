Cairo — 2.6 million Arabic pages entered the public domain.

Undigitized Arabic archives from Cairo to Timbuktu have stayed unsearchable for lack of data.

Arabic's cursive, connected script defeats recognisers built for spaced Latin letters, so researchers generated pages with known ground truth instead.

The authors say synthetic pages still lack the stains and skew of real scanned books.
