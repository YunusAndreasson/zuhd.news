Sofia — 11 sailors are missing from a cargo ship sunk off Krapets.

It is the second deadly sinking in a week off a NATO member's coast.

[Russia](country:RU) bombs [Ukraine](country:UA)'s Black Sea ports, so Ukrainian cargo now hugs the coasts of [Bulgaria](country:BG) and [Romania](country:RO).

Prime Minister Rumen Radev said Bulgaria lacks solid evidence against Russia or Ukraine, but the strikes benefit Moscow.

Sofia summoned Russia's ambassador, and Radev is due to speak with NATO's Mark Rutte.
