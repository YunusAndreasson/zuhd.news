United States — Movie and television employment fell nearly one-third in four years.

Apricitas Economics counts 200,000 lost creative-industry jobs in four years, without a recession.

All digital arts subsectors are losing jobs while in-person entertainment grows, the analysis's clearest evidence of AI's influence.

The analysis cannot isolate AI's share, because firms disclose little and streaming, consolidation and offshoring also cut jobs.

Losses show no sign of abating, with about 50,000 gone in the last year alone.
