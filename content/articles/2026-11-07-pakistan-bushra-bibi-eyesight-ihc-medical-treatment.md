Islamabad — Bushra Bibi cannot tolerate light, Imran Khan's lawyer says.

She asks the Islamabad High Court to let her own doctors treat her at her expense.

In an affidavit, Salman Safdar says she wore dark tinted glasses on 2 November and reported a mature cataract diagnosis.

Doctors advised a second eye surgery around 15 November, her lawyer says.
