Gwalior — A runaway truck killed at least 15 devotees.

It broke through a police checkpoint on a road closed to heavy vehicles during Navratri.

Police said the paddy-laden truck lost control on a steep slope, and investigators suspect brake failure.

Chief Minister Mohan Yadav ordered an investigation after removing the city police superintendent and suspending 8 officers.
