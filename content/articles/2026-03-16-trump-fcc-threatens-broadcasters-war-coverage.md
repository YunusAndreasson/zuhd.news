US regulators threatened to revoke TV licenses over war reporting. The Federal Communications Commission, which controls broadcast spectrum permits, warned networks their licenses could be pulled for coverage the Trump administration deems misleading — revoking a permit shuts a station as effectively as destroying its transmitter. No network has changed its coverage, but any revocation would trigger First Amendment challenges that take years to resolve.
