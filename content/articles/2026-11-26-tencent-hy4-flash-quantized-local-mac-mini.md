Shenzhen — Tencent shrank Hy4 flash from 410GiB to 126GiB.

A 64GB Mac mini can now run it locally, Tencent says.

Strata streams expert weights from solid-state drive storage, giving about 17 tokens a second on a 64GB M4 Pro Mac mini, Tencent reports.

Every speed figure is Tencent's own report.

A 128GB M5 Max Mac reaches 47 to 50 tokens a second with the Q3_K version, Tencent reports.
