Johnson City — A vendor pitched police on adding facial recognition to Flock's license-plate cameras.

Flock's own camera network, built to read plates, could become face identification without a new purchase vote.

VIDIZMO emailed Johnson City's deputy police chief offering to merge Flock plate data with Axon body-camera footage into one face-searchable platform.

VIDIZMO's CEO said he wants the integration built and expects facial recognition to spread regardless of Flock's stance.
