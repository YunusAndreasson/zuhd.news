Britain refused to let the US launch Iran strikes from 2 British bases. The government warned that using Diego Garcia and RAF Fairford could breach international law, prompting President Trump to threaten blocking the Chagos Islands handover. The refusal narrows US basing options as 2 carrier groups mass in the region.
