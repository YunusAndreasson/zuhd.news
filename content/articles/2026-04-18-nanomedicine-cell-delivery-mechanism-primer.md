Toronto — Lipid nanoparticles deliver drugs directly inside human cells. Particles dock to surface receptors the cell uses to pull cargo inward, bypassing barriers that block conventional pills. The platform now carries mRNA vaccines, CRISPR edits, and targeted chemotherapy — chronic disease treatment is the next frontier.
