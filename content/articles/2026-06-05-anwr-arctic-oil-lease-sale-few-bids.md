Anchorage — Major oil companies skipped the Arctic refuge auction.

9 bids covered 10% of offered acres, raising $3.7 million — half from a state-owned development corporation; no major international producer entered.

3 more mandatory sales through 2035 remain; stranded-asset risk, not regulation, is keeping majors out.
