Hong Kong — Hong Kong's ground-floor rents have fallen below upper-floor rates. Landlords have slashed ground-floor rents so deeply that businesses from cheap upper floors are moving down, reversing decades of commercial geography. Recovery depends on when Hormuz fighting ends and tourists return to the city.
