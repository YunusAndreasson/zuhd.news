Tallahassee — Florida's attorney general sued to stop ChatGPT's development entirely.

It is the first [US](country:US) state seeking a court order against a frontier AI model itself.

The filing asks the court to bar minors from ChatGPT and halt further development until a third party approves safeguards.

"Stop calling it safe. Stop pretending it's human. Stop selling it to kids," said Florida Attorney General James Uthmeier.

A Florida circuit court in Highlands County will decide whether to grant the injunction.
