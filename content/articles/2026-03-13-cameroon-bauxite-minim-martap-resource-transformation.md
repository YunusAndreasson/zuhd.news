Cameroon will smelt its own bauxite. Guinea provides 25% of global bauxite but captures little profit — the developer Canyon Resources is betting domestic aluminium processing avoids the same trap. Rail and port access are still unbuilt, infrastructure Cameroon has not committed to fund.
