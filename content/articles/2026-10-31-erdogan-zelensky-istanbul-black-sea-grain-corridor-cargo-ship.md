Istanbul — Zelensky says [Turkey](country:TR) will help restore [Ukraine](country:UA)'s grain corridor.

Wheat importers rely on that route, and wheat rose 23.6% in the 3 months to September.

Turkey controls the straits ships must transit, and says it is talking with the parties and will raise it with [Russia](country:RU).

Erdoğan called attacks on civilian ships unacceptable to Turkey, whoever carries them out.

Erdoğan has given no date for his planned talks with Russia.
