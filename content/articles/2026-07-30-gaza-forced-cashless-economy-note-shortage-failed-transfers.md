Gaza — "We've forgotten what paper money feels like," a father of seven said.

[Israel](country:IL) stopped accepting cash from Gaza merchants, forcing e-wallets on a strip with no reliable power or internet — and cash-out fees exceeding 50%.

No new banknotes have entered since the war began; a failed transfer means lunch, residents told Mada Masr.
