Riyadh — The capital's airport cancelled 82% of Monday's flights, Cirium data show.

Houthi strikes have killed 14 people since Saturday at [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s second-busiest airport.

Lufthansa halted Riyadh flights until 16 October, Air France-KLM until month's end, and 7 governments have issued travel advisories.

Jets still cross Saudi airspace and land at Jeddah, though Cirium counts 46.4% of all Saudi flights cancelled.

The kingdom's flagship investment conference, due in Riyadh this month, is under pressure.
