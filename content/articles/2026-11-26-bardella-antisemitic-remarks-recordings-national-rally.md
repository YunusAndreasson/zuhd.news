Paris — Unaired recordings allege teenage Jordan Bardella made antisemitic remarks.

The 31-year-old is the top ally of presidential candidate Marine Le Pen.

French media published extracts of the previously unaired conversations online on Thursday.

Bardella has denied any wrongdoing since September, when allegations of conspiracist Facebook comments from 2013 surfaced.
