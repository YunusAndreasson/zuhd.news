Jürgen Habermas died at 96, still publishing political philosophy. His 1961 book argued democracy requires spaces of open reasoning beyond state and corporate control, a concept that shaped democratic theory for 60 years. His death leaves Germany's intellectual left without its most prominent voice against nationalism as far-right parties gain ground across Europe.
