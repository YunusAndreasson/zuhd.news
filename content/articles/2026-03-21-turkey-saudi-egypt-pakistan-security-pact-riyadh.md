Riyadh — Pakistan's nuclear weapons joined a new regional security pact. Turkey, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, and Pakistan held their first joint defense talks in Riyadh, combining drone manufacturing, oil wealth, and nuclear warheads. Formalizing the pact requires the 4 nations to agree on whether Iran or Israel is the primary threat.
