Washington — Fed minutes: almost all officials expect more rate hikes.

Dollar borrowers worldwide pay more as [US](country:US) rates rise from the current 3.75%-4.00% range.

Prices are rising above a 3% annual rate, and Lisa Cook and Beth Hammack dissented in October for a quarter-point hike.

The Trump administration argues elevated inflation is temporary and the Fed should ignore it.

The Fed decides on 9 December, and Polymarket traders give a quarter-point hike 92%, up 6 points in a week.
