Kampala — Uganda suspended non-governmental organizations and froze their bank accounts. The suspended groups deliver healthcare, education, and legal aid across Uganda — and serve as the primary monitors of government conduct. Rights groups warn transparency will collapse before Uganda's 2026 elections if the suspensions continue.
