Tokyo — One flaw canceled 46,000 paid accounts.

A student near Tokyo allegedly exploited a broken access-control bug in an anime streaming platform that trusted requests it never checked, [Japan](country:JP) police said.

Police have not said whether the platform has patched the flaw that let it happen.
