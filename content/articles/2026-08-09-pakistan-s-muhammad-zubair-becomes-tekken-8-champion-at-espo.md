Paris — Muhammad Zubair swept the grand final 5-0.

The player becomes [Pakistan](country:PK)'s first Tekken 8 Esports World Cup champion.

Zubair beat fellow Pakistani star Arslan Siddique in the semifinal before sweeping South Korea's LowHigh in the final.

Zubair collects $250,000 while Siddique, June's Evo champion, settled for fourth place.
