Arabian Sea — [Oman](country:OM)'s humpback population just violated a migration assumption.

At roughly 80 individuals, this is the only humpback population believed to never migrate; a satellite-tagged member made an unexpected long-distance journey.

Conservation plans for Gulf shipping and fishing zones now rest on a range assumption that needs revision.
