ISIS declared a "new phase" of operations against Syria. Its spokesperson released the first audio message in 2 years, claiming attacks that killed 2 people in Raqqa and Deir al-Zor. The UN has documented 5 foiled assassination attempts against President Ahmed al-Sharaa since Syria joined the anti-ISIS coalition.
