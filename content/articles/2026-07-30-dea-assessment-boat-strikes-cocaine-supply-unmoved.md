Washington — DEA and Pentagon reviews found no drop in cocaine supply.

Lethal strikes on smuggling boats have not changed the quantity or purity reaching the [US](country:US); traffickers instead diversified routes and craft.

Each strike kills people at sea with no published charge, identification or court review.
