Cairo — [Egypt](country:EG) set aside 112 million square metres of coast to cut debt.

Finance Minister Ahmed Kouchouk gave no value or timetable for the Gulf of Suez land.

Options include private partners, usage rights or debt-for-investment swaps like Abu Dhabi's 2024 Ras El Hekma deal.

Kouchouk says debt fell from 96% of GDP, but he cited budget-sector debt, the narrowest measure.

Lawmaker Mohamed Fouad has asked the Speaker whether the 2025 Ras Shukeir allocation reduced debt.
