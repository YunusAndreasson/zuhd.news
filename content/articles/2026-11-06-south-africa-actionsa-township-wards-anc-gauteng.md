Johannesburg — ActionSA, which won no Gauteng wards in 2021, now leads 51.

Gains span Soweto, Tembisa and Atteridgeville, townships where the African National Congress long held strong support in [South Africa](country:ZA).

Provisional results show the congress's wards falling from 87 to 44 in Johannesburg, 78 to 26 in Ekurhuleni, 70 to 29 in Tshwane.

Chairperson Michael Beaumont says 54 wards, 3 more than the official data recorded at 1pm Friday.

Counting continues, and ActionSA is on track for second in Tshwane, third in Johannesburg and Ekurhuleni.
