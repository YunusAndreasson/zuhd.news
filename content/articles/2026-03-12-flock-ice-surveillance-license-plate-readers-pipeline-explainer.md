A parking lot camera can trigger an immigration arrest. City councils across the US contract with Flock Safety, a venture-backed startup, without a public vote, routing license plate reader data through local police directly to Immigration and Customs Enforcement. Some cities have dropped Flock contracts after investigative reporting exposed the pipeline, but dozens more are signing on each month.
