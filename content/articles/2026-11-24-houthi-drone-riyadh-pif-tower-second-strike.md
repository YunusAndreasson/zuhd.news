Riyadh — The city's tallest tower was hit twice in 5 days.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s Civil Defense said 11 people were injured, including 4 Egyptian and 3 Nepalese residents.

Houthi forces have struck Saudi airports, oil sites and offices since [Yemen](country:YE)'s war reignited in July, grounding most international flights to Riyadh.

Saudi Civil Defense blamed the Houthis, who have not claimed the strike.

Polymarket traders give a Saudi-Houthi ceasefire by 31 December a 9% chance, down 4 points in a week.
