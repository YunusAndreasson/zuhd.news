London — 57 women with subthreshold depression joined a trial.

Six to seven weekly sessions of aerobic exercise for two weeks eased symptoms and altered prefrontal cortex activity, tracked by near-infrared brain imaging.

The small single-blind trial needs replication before the protocol reaches clinics.
