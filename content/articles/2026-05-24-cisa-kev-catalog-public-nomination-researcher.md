Washington — [US](country:US) cyber agency now accepts public vulnerability nominations.

Entry in the agency's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog triggers a 21-day patch deadline for all federal agencies under Binding Operational Directive 22-01.

The channel opens as other CISA functions are being cut; durability of the new process is unresolved.
