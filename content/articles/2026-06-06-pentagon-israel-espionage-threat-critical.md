Washington — Pentagon rated [Israel](country:IL) a critical spy threat.

US personnel found surveillance software on their phones in Israel; Israeli officers planted listening devices at Defense Intelligence Agency headquarters in 2021, the Pentagon said.

The Pentagon may restrict Israeli officers' access at US Central Command while [Iran](country:IR) peace talks continue.
