Amman — Iran accused Jordan of enabling US airstrikes against it. Jordan hosts US military bases under a 1994 peace treaty with Israel but has not publicly denied permitting US strike operations. Iran's UN letter now forces Jordan to publicly confirm or deny whether its territory was used to strike Iran.
