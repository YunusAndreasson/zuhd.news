Moscow — [Russia](country:RU) will add Western-independent payment systems to the October Russia-Africa Summit agenda, Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov announced.

The summit, planned for October 28-29 in Moscow, will also cover energy, nuclear power, and AI.

No African government has announced commitments to the alternative financial infrastructure.
