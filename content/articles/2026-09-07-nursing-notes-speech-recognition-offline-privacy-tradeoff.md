London — Paper scored 59.76 versus 70 for dictation.

Documentation quality directly shapes safety in wards already stretched thin on staff.

Seventy nurses tested both cloud and local dictation software, and the two scored almost identically, both far above paper.

Hospitals unable to afford cloud licenses can keep dictation accuracy by running it locally.
