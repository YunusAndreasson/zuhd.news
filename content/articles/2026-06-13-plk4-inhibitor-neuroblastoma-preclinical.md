Philadelphia — RP-1664 suppressed neuroblastoma growth in preclinical models.

Polo-like kinase 4 governs tumour cell division; the inhibitor hits it via 2 mechanisms, reducing resistance risk, researchers found.

RP-1664 has not entered clinical trials; neuroblastoma preclinical success often fails to translate to patients.
