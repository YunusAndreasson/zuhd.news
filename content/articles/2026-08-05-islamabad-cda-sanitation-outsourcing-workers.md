Islamabad — 1,100 sanitation workers face outsourcing to a contractor.

The Capital Development Authority opened bids on a Rs17.9 billion cleaning contract; the workers' union says it was never consulted on the decision.

The authority says regular jobs stay protected, but has not opened talks with the union.
