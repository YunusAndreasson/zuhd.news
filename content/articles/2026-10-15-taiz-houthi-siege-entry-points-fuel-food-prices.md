Taiz — Ansarallah now holds every road into [Yemen](country:YE)'s third city.

Fuel and other essentials cost up to 3 times their normal price.

The entry points are active frontlines, so drivers wait for lulls of a few hours a day.

"I went into debt even though I have a salary," said Hael Mohammed, a Taiz pharmacist.

Fighting is under 5 kilometres from his home, and residents are stockpiling food.
