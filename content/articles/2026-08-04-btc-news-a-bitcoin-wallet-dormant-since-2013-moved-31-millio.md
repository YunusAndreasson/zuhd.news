San Francisco — A wallet untouched since 2013 moved 500 bitcoin.

The $31 million transfer coincided with a spike in decade-old coins moving amid the Coldcard hardware wallet hack — the ledger shows what moved, never who moved it or why.

Self-custody users in Nigeria, Turkey and Argentina bear firmware failures hardest, with no issuer or insurance.
