Tokyo — 13 Japanese brokerages plan crypto exchange-traded funds.

SBI, Rakuten, Nomura, and Daiwa are among those awaiting the Financial Services Agency's final rules as Japan reclassifies crypto from payment tool to security.

Parliament must pass the draft amendment before reclassification takes effect in fiscal 2027.
