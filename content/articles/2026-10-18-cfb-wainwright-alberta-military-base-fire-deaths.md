Wainwright — Fire crossed the fence of one of Canada's busiest army training bases.

[Canada](country:CA)'s defence minister, David McGuinty, confirmed fatalities and injuries but gave no toll.

High winds spread the blaze from a training area across the perimeter fence, the local municipality said.

The Royal Canadian Mounted Police are investigating, and McGuinty has not said what caused it.
