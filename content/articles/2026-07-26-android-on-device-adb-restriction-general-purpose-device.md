Mountain View — A Google engineer floated restricting on-device debugging.

On-device Android Debug Bridge (ADB) access lets phone-only users strip carrier bloatware, grant hidden permissions and recover blocked backups; the maintainer cited stopping "bad actors."

Nothing is official yet; a developer whose disability app depends on ADB wants loopback connections kept open.
