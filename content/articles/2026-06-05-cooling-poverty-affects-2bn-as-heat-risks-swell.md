2 billion people cannot afford cooling — no fans, cold storage, or shaded work — while contributing least to warming.

Informal workers and women face highest exposure where grids are weakest; heat stress cuts productivity and raises mortality.

Financing cooling infrastructure without additional emissions remains unsolved in countries that need it most.
