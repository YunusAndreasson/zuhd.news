St Petersburg — Putin announced Stephen Hubbard's release during Steve Witkoff's visit.

Hubbard, 74, had been held since April 2022 on mercenary charges for fighting for [Ukraine](country:UA).

[Russia](country:RU) has long jailed Westerners as bargaining chips, and Dmitri Medvedev alluded to refilling its exchange fund on 9 October.

Russian state media have not reported Putin committing to trilateral talks with Volodymyr Zelensky and Donald Trump.

Putin reportedly wants to meet Trump in [China](country:CN) next month, without Ukraine at the table.
