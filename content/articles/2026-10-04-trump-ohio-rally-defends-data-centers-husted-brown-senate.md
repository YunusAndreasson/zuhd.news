A Times/Siena poll found 63% of Ohio voters oppose building them, against 31% in favour.

Brown's campaign says Husted, as lieutenant governor, gave Big Tech tax breaks and left Ohioans with higher electric bills.

"You can't just turn them off," Trump said of the data centers. "They'll go to China."

Democrats blocked Husted's consumer-cost bill last week, and voters decide the race on 3 November.
