Moscow — European states have been detaining aging Russian tankers.

Putin warned Moscow would respond in kind, in any waters it chooses.

The states cite insurance and flag-registry violations, not a formal blockade, as grounds.

Putin called the restrictions "piracy and banditry" but set no retaliation date.
