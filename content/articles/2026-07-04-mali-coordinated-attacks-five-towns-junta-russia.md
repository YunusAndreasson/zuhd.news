Bamako — Fighters struck Aguelhok, Anefis, Gao, Sevare and Kenieroba at once.

Government troops and Russian fighters hold Anefis and Aguelhok, the last [Mali](country:ML) army positions in Kidal region after April's losses to Tuareg and al-Qaeda-linked forces.

The Azawad Liberation Front says it has taken positions in Anefis; fighting for the town continues.
