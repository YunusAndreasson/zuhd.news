Santa Clara — $4.6 billion in China revenue became zero.

Export controls erased Nvidia's Chinese business; the company excluded [China](country:CN) from its $91 billion second-quarter forecast entirely, CFO Kress said.

China discouraged imports and is building domestic alternatives; no US export licenses for Nvidia chips to China are currently active.
