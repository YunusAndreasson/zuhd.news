Paris — The European Space Agency awarded €544 million to three rocket startups.

It replaces the agency's old rule of splitting work by member-state contribution.

Geographic-return contracts prioritized spreading work across members, not cost, which is why Ariane 6 arrived late and expensive.

MaiaSpace's award was left out, with the agency giving no reason for its absence.
