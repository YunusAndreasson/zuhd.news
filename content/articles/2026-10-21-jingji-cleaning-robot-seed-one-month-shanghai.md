Shanghai — A month-old cleaning-robot startup raised tens of millions of yuan.

Haowen Tianshu Robotics and Shanghai Lanyun Industrial Development funded Jingji Intelligent before it validates a product.

It applies force control, machine vision and precision actuation from medical robotics to clean walls, countertops and sanitary fixtures.

Jingji's next step is product validation, with partnerships in Australia, Spain, Singapore and Vietnam already established.
