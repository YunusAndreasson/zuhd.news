Washington — 20,000 laser-guided rockets cleared for Saudi Arabia.

The State Department approved $1.96 billion in precision munitions for [Saudi Arabia](country:SA), built by BAE Systems, plus $484 million for [Kuwait](country:KW)'s C-17 fleet, as both states absorb Iranian strikes.

Congress can still object before the sales, framed as regional stability aid, proceed.
