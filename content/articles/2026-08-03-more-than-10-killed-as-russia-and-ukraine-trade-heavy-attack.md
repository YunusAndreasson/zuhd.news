Krasnodar — A [Ukrainian](country:UA) drone struck a Krasnodar beach Monday.

Regional officials in [Russia](country:RU) count six to 14 dead there while Ukraine reports three killed in a separate Russian strike on a Dnipropetrovsk gas station.

Neither government has independently verified the other's casualty count.
