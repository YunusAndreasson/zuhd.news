Washington — Trump wants snipers, drones and ammunition stored in a 250-foot arch.

The arch faces a Vietnam veterans' lawsuit and would stand beside Arlington National Cemetery.

Trump cites national security, as with his $400 million ballroom, which the Supreme Court let proceed last month.

"It looks like it's tacked on in order to strengthen the rationale for construction," said Mark Cancian, a retired Marine colonel.

Judge Tanya Chutkan has ordered 48 hours' notice before any work at the site.
