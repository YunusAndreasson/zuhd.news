Varna — All 12 crew survived a reported sea-drone strike off Bulgaria.

Betul 8 is the 17th vessel attacked off Bulgaria and Romania since mid-July, security firm Diaplous says.

The Somali-flagged ship, carrying 3,000 metric tons of wheat from Romania to Egypt, sank 74 km southeast of Cape Kaliakra.

The Transport Ministry has said only that the ship capsized, not that drones hit it.

Bulgaria says it has no proof who launched them, though officials in both countries point at [Russia](country:RU).
