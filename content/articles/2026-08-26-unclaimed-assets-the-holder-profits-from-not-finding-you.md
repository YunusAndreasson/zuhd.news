Pretoria — R88.6bn sits unclaimed in dormant accounts and pensions.

Whoever holds that money today earns its return while bearing no duty to find owners.

[South Africa](country:ZA)'s Treasury wants one central administrator to trace beneficiaries instead of each bank tracing its own.

Public comment is open, but Treasury must still pick a 45-year or 110-year claim cutoff.
