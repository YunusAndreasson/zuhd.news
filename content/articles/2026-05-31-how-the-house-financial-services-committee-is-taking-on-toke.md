Washington — Clarity Act targets who regulates tokenised securities.

Tokenising moves real-world assets — equities, treasuries — onto blockchains; [US](country:US) House chair French Hill expects bipartisan Senate passage this year.

Passage triggers a 12-month joint securities-and-commodities rulemaking defining access and clearing rules for tokenised markets.
