A Coruña — Fin whale calls dropped 50% near seismic surveys. Seismic air guns fire compressed pulses detectable hundreds of kilometers away; whales depend on sound to navigate, forage, and mate. As offshore energy exploration expands post-Hormuz, regulators must decide whether surveys near active migration corridors require seasonal bans.
