Brussels — ChatGPT topped 45 million average monthly EU users.

Crossing that threshold binds OpenAI to obligations it could until now avoid.

Designation as a "very large" platform forces risk audits on minors and elections, with fines up to 6% of turnover.

ChatGPT, Reddit and Roblox have four months to show compliance.
