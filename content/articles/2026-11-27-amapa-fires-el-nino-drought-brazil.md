Macapá — Amapá recorded 2,152 fire hotspots, nearly 4 times 2025's.

The fire reached the 57 farming families of Ponta do Socorro, and Maria Tavares's crops burned.

[Brazil](country:BR)'s space research institute counted the hotspots from 1 August to 16 November, the fastest growth in the Legal Amazon.

State meteorologist Jefferson Vilhena says El Niño drove October peaks past 38°C and humidity below 30%.

Tartarugalzinho, the municipality Tavares left to vote, logged 227 hotspots, nearly double 2025.
