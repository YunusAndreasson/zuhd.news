India agreed to stop buying Russian crude. US Secretary of State Marco Rubio announced the commitment at the Munich Security Conference, a reversal for the world's largest buyer of discounted Russian oil since Western sanctions began in 2022. Neither government has set a public timeline, leaving open how India replaces cheap Russian barrels that now account for over a third of its imports.
