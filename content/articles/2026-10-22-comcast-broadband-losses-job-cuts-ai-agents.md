Philadelphia — Comcast lost 144,000 [US](country:US) broadband customers last quarter.

Fixed wireless and fiber rivals are taking share from a company that once faced little competition.

Comcast's finance chief called $30 to $40 fiber offers irrational because building fiber costs $1,500 or more per home.

Net income still rose to $3.45 billion from $3.33 billion a year earlier.

Comcast will book a charge of over $1 billion in the fourth quarter for a restructuring reportedly including job cuts.
