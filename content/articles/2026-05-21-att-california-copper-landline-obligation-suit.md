San Francisco — 40,000 low-income Californians risk losing landline service.

AT&T sued California to end its carrier-of-last-resort obligation, which requires it to serve every potential customer.

The [US](country:US) Federal Communications Commission must now rule on whether federal authority preempts California's mandate.
