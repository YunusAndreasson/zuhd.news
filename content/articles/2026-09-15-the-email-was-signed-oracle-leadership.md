For H-1B staff in India and the US, the pre-dawn email starts a 60-day legal-residence clock.

Access was cut before the message arrived, so no named executive had to deliver the news directly.

Oracle spent $28.5 billion on AI datacenters last quarter while cutting the headcount that funds it.
