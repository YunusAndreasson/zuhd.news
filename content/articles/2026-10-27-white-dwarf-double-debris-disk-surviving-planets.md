Europe — Two surviving planetary bodies appear to sculpt a dead star's debris.

The system offers a glimpse of what may happen to our solar system.

Astronomers using the European Southern Observatory's Very Large Telescope found former planets' debris in two parts split by a clear gap.

Astronomers say one of the two bodies is possibly as big as Pluto.
