Bolzano — Ötzi's 5,300-year-old gut contains living bacteria.

Researchers found both ancient and modern strains; separating them from contamination introduced since 1991 is the core challenge.

If ancient strains are confirmed authentic, Ötzi's gut could map Chalcolithic human microbiome composition for the first time.
