Sheikh Zuwayed — Four Nakhlat parents remain in police custody.

The office that never rebuilt their school reported them to police.

[Egypt](country:EG)'s Operation Sinai destroyed Nakhlat's school and children still walk 3 km to the nearest one.

Families are pressing North Sinai's governor to set a rebuild date.
