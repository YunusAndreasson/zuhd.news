London — Officers raided three cash-for-crypto desks.

No peer-to-peer crypto firm is registered with [Britain](country:GB)'s Financial Conduct Authority, the regulator said.

Cash handed over in person in an unregistered shop buys coins sent to a wallet no exchange ever checks.

The regulator and Britain's tax authority issued cease-and-desist orders, with no arrests yet.
