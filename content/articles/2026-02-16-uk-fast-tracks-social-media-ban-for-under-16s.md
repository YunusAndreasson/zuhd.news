Britain wants under-16s off social media "within months." Prime Minister Keir Starmer announced the government will amend existing child-protection legislation rather than draft new laws, and separately force all artificial intelligence chatbot providers to comply with the Online Safety Act after non-consensual intimate images appeared on the X platform. A public consultation opens in March, following Australia — where platforms have already removed 4.7 million underage accounts since a similar ban took effect.
