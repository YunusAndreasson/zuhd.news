London — A fatty acid from the oral bacterium Fusobacterium periodonticum recruits immune cells to fuel colon tumors, a Nature study found.

The bacterium secretes decanoic acid, which draws neutrophils into colon tissue via a G-protein pathway, promoting tumor growth.

Blocking the G-protein pathway suppressed tumor growth in mouse models, the study found, identifying a potential drug target.
