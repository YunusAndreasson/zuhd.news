More than 300 environmental incidents have been recorded in the Persian Gulf since the war began February 28. Greenpeace estimates 21 billion litres of oil are trapped aboard tankers in the Gulf, threatening the world's second-largest dugong population and over 2,000 other marine species. The sea renews itself only every 2 to 5 years, meaning contamination from this war could persist as long as the 1991 conflict's did.
