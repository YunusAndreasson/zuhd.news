Sarkozy allegedly took Gaddafi's money for France's 2007 election. A French court convicted former President Nicolas Sarkozy of accepting Libyan government funds for that campaign; he later helped lead the NATO operation that toppled Gaddafi in 2011. Sarkozy appealed Monday, seeking to erase the sentence that made France one of few democracies to jail a former president.
