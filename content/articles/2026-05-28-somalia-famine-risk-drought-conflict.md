Mogadishu — 6 million in [Somalia](country:SO) face acute hunger, UN food monitors found.

5 consecutive below-average rainy seasons, al-Shabaab conflict, and Red Sea fuel-price shocks have compounded to break household coping capacity.

Somalia received $288M of the $1.42B needed for its intervention plan; another below-average season is forecast.
