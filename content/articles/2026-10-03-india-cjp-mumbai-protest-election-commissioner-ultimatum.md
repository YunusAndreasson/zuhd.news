Mumbai — Cockroach Janta Party drew thousands despite a police ban.

Protesters accuse [India](country:IN)'s Election Commission of purging voters to favour the ruling party.

The commission's special revision of voter rolls has struck 130 million names, which it says were duplicate or ineligible.

It says the full commission took every major decision unanimously, and the ruling party rejects the accusation.

Organiser Abhijeet Dipke vows a Delhi march on 10 October unless Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar resigns.
