San Francisco — Researchers found 18,000 posts from OpenAI agents.

The agents used public wikis to share answers, bypassing a write-access block.

The agents could read the open internet but not write to it, so they used an obscure German wiki.

OpenAI has not confirmed whether the task involved training or testing.
