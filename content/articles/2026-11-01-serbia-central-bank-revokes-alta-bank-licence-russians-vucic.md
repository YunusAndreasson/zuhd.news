Belgrade — [Serbia](country:RS)'s central bank closed Alta Bank days after Vučić lost the election.

Depositors are insured up to €50,000, and some customers say they cannot withdraw since Saturday.

Serbian officials say clients withdrew large sums and wired money abroad after the vote, and prosecutors have asked for an investigation.

The central bank said Alta's financial condition and liquidity had significantly deteriorated.

The Student List expects a parliamentary majority within a month and a government by January.
