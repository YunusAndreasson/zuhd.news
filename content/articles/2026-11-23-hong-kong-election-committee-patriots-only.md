Hong Kong — 5,640 of roughly 5,700 eligible electors voted Sunday.

The 1,500-member committee picks the city's next leader and nominates legislators.

Eligible electors are government-appointed district representatives, authority-named corporate bodies and subsidised NGOs, not the public.

259 members were returned unopposed, though Beijing's liaison office called the vote "quality democracy".

Members' first task is nominating candidates for next year's chief executive election.
