Washington — 45 million people could face acute hunger, a UN official warns.

A third of the world's fertiliser crossed Hormuz before the war, and prices are up 30%, the official said.

Mines hit three ships in two days, [Iran](country:IR)'s Revolutionary Guards said, as US and Iranian blockades both choke the strait.

A third US aircraft carrier is moving into the region, and Israel is lobbying for another campaign.

Trump told advisers he may skip new strikes if Iran and the Houthis stay quiet, three sources say.
