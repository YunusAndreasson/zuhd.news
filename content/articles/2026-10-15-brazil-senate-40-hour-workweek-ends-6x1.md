Brazil — 75 senators to 1 ended the six-day work week.

More than 37 million formal workers, the government estimates, gain a second weekly rest day.

The amendment cuts [Brazil](country:BR)'s legal week from 44 to 40 hours without a pay cut, phased in over 14 months.

The amendment leaves out freelancers and delivery drivers in the informal economy.

Lula is expected to sign it next week, before the 25 October runoff with Flávio Bolsonaro.
