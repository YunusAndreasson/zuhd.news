Newbury — A 6mph fender-bender totaled a test EV's charging unit.

Battery packs are glued together as single units to cut weight, so any damage forces full replacement, driving repair costs 30% above petrol cars, Thatcham Research found.

Manufacturers are now redesigning battery casings to make partial repairs possible.
