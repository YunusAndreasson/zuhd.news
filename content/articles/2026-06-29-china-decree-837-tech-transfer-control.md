Beijing — China's Decree 837 extends state control abroad.

[China](country:CN)'s new investment rules give Beijing legal authority over technology transfers from nominally private Chinese firms operating in recipient countries.

Countries hosting Chinese investment now face a Beijing veto on what technology moves beyond them.
