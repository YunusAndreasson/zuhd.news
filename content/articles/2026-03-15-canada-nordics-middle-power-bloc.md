6 NATO allies plan joint defence procurement outside Washington. Canadian PM Mark Carney is building what he calls a "middle power" trading order after Trump's unpredictability made Washington an unreliable partner. Carney is also approaching China, India, and Middle Eastern states to build a US-alternative trading network.
