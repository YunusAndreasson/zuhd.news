Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s president escalated a tax-on-junk-food petition.

Zardari forwarded a heart association's petition urging higher taxes on ultra-processed foods to the finance secretary and revenue board.

Revenue and finance officials have not confirmed whether or when they will act.
