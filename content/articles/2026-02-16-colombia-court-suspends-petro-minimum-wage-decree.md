A 23.7% minimum wage hike, struck down. Colombia's Council of State suspended President Gustavo Petro's December decree, ruling the government failed to justify the increase and giving the executive 8 days to produce a replacement with stronger economic reasoning. Petro called a tripartite meeting with unions and business leaders for February 16, but the court will still decide the case on its merits.
