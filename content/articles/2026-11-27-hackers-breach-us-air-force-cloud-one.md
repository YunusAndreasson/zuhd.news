Washington — Cloud One tracks munitions at over 200 Air Force locations.

The [US](country:US) military's main cloud platform also holds personnel, pay and air-defense data, and three sources say hackers broke in.

One source said intruders exploited a software flaw to reach the control plane, which manages the whole environment.

Investigators have not identified the hackers, and Cyber Command is assessing the damage, one source said.
