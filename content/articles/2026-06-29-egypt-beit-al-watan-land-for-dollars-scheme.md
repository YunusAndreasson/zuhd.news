Cairo — [Egypt](country:EG) doubled land prices for its diaspora scheme.

Beit al-Watan sells Egypt's diaspora land for dollars; speculators account for up to half the plots and resell them at a premium.

Higher state prices will flow through to buyers as larger resale premiums, a Cairo real-estate developer said.
