Gaza — Israeli strikes killed 5 overnight, 4 of them women.

A Christian mother and daughter were among the dead, Shifa Hospital said.

[Israel](country:IL) has killed at least 1,439 Palestinians since the ceasefire nearly a year ago, Gaza's Health Ministry says.

Israel's military did not immediately comment and said it was checking for information.

Shifa Hospital reports people still missing beneath the debris of a multistory building.
