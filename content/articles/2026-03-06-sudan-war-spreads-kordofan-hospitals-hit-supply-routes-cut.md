A drone killed 4 patients at Dilling General Hospital. The Rapid Support Forces, a paramilitary group fighting Sudan's army, have brought the war into Kordofan — a region relatively calm for most of the 2-year war. The UN humanitarian office OCHA warned the road between Kosti and El Obeid, Kordofan's main supply route, faces disruption.
