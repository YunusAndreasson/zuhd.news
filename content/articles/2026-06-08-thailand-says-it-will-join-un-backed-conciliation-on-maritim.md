Bangkok — [Thailand](country:TH) accepted UN conciliation on a [Cambodia](country:KH) sea dispute.

Southeast Asia customarily rejects outside arbitration; this dispute involves overlapping claims to offshore energy resources in the Gulf of Thailand.

Bangkok will halt all other bilateral talks with Cambodia while the UN process runs, Foreign Minister Sihasak said.
