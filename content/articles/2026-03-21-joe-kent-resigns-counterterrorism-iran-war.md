Washington — The US counterterrorism director quit over the Iran war. Joe Kent had previously said Iran posed no counterterrorism threat to the US, contradicting the administration's stated basis for the conflict. His resignation makes him the highest-ranking Trump official to break with the war, which Congress has not authorized — and which Kent says has no counterterrorism justification.
