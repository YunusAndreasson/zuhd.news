Bengaluru — Gold lost 8.9% this week, the most since March.

A dollar near a 19-month high makes bullion costlier for holders of other currencies.

Markets price a 97.3% chance of a December Fed hike, CME FedWatch shows, raising the opportunity cost of holding gold.

Capital.com analyst Kyle Rodda described "forced liquidation going on across the market in general."

The [US](country:US) Federal Reserve decides on 9 December, with the 10-year Treasury yield at 5.07% as of 18 November.
