Manila — [Canada](country:CA) pledged up to C$560 million ($392 million) to a Brazil-led forest project.

Prime Minister Mark Carney is courting Asia and Brazil to cut Canada's reliance on the [US](country:US).

He announced it after meeting [Brazil](country:BR)'s Lula on Sunday, a day after concluding trade talks with the Philippines.

Modi visits Canada in the second week of December, when the two plan to conclude a trade deal.
