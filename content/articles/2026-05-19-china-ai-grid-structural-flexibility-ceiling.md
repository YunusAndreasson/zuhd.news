Beijing — [China](country:CN) targets 5 AI dispatch models in its grid by 2027.

The International Energy Agency projects AI-optimised grids could cut outages 50%; China's data centres will consume 3-5% of generation by 2030.

Whether AI saves more energy than data centres consume is China's central grid trade-off.
