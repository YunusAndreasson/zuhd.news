Rovereto — Autism has 2 biologically distinct brain connectivity types.

Functional MRI of 940 autistic people found 2 opposing connectivity patterns — hypo and hyper — each tied to distinct gene mechanisms, researchers at Italy's technology institute found.

59% of participants fit neither pattern; researchers say many more subtypes remain to be identified.
