"We totally reject global governance of AI." White House technology adviser Michael Kratsios told the AI Impact Summit in New Delhi that safety-focused regulation inhibits competition and isolates developing countries. UN Secretary-General António Guterres responded by announcing a new expert panel modelled on the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change.
