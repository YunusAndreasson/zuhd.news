Jerusalem's Mufti called Eid prayers outside Al-Aqsa's sealed gates. Al-Aqsa has been closed since the war on Iran began — the first Ramadan without Friday prayers there since 1967. Israeli forces are deployed in the Old City ahead of Sunday's Eid, when Palestinians will gather at the mosque entrance.
