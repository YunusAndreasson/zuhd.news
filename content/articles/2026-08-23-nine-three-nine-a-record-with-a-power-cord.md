Beijing — Tiangong Ultra ran 100 metres in 9.39 seconds.

That beats Bolt's 9.58, but no start gun, fatigue or race season applied.

The real feat is balance recovery within milliseconds, the control skill that also drives warehouse and battlefield robots.

[China](country:CN)'s humanoid games run through Wednesday with 2,056 robots competing.
