4 astronauts will fly around the Moon on March 6. The Artemis II mission would be the first crewed flight beyond Earth orbit since Apollo 17 in 1972, after a hydrogen leak pushed the launch from its original 2024 date. A successful flyby clears the path for a crewed lunar landing on Artemis III.
