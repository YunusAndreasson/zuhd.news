Kinshasa — High-resolution maps reverse a decades-old assumption.

Global net-zero pathways count on Congo Basin forests to absorb carbon overshoot.

Earlier satellite estimates tracked canopy cover, missing degradation from logging and drought that thins biomass beneath an intact canopy.

The authors have not yet published the final peer-reviewed version of record.
