Tokyo — [Japan](country:JP) extradited a 28-year-old Russian Qilin ransomware suspect to Germany.

Researchers counted 127 Qilin attacks in July, making it the second most active ransomware gang.

[Germany](country:DE) issued the warrant over an attack on a German company, and Japan arrested the Russian at an Osaka hotel in May.

German law enforcement has not commented, and Japan has not released the suspect's name.
