Addiction deaths fell 50% among veterans taking weight-loss drugs. A 3-year British Medical Journal study of 600,000 US veterans found the drugs activate reward circuits in the brain, suggesting appetite and addiction share neural pathways. These drugs cost over $1,000/month in the US and remain inaccessible in poorer countries where addiction deaths are rising.
