Kolkata — Rivals got "Football Player," "Envelope" symbols.

Symbols let illiterate voters identify parties on paper ballots.

The Election Commission of [India](country:IN) froze the Trinamool Congress name during a leadership feud before West Bengal's October 6 bypolls.

Mamata Banerjee's Supreme Court challenge over the original name and symbol remains unresolved.
