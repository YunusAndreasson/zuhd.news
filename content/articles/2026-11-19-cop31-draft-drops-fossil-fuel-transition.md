Antalya — The new COP31 draft deletes all fossil fuel transition language.

Published under 48 hours before talks close, it omits the transition COP28 in Dubai agreed.

Carbon capture and removal goals appear in several paragraphs, and the 35% electrification goal does not require renewable power, 350.org says.

"We cannot build this future on crumbs thrown by rich polluting nations," said Rukiya Khamis, 350.org's East Africa lead.

Talks close in under 48 hours with no baseline set for tripling adaptation finance.
