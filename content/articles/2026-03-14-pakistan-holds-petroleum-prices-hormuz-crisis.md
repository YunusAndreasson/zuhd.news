Pakistan froze fuel prices despite a sharp rise in global oil costs from the Hormuz crisis. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and military leadership used emergency budget reserves to absorb the difference, one week after petrol rose 55 rupees per litre. The premier pledged no further increase at Sunday's next review, regardless of market movements.
