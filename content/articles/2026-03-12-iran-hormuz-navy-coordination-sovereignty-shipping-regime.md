Ships must now clear Iran's navy to cross Hormuz. Iran's foreign ministry declared the strait a checkpoint — no longer an international waterway — covering roughly 20% of global oil and gas. Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei said the closure will remain as leverage until the US and Israel halt their campaign.
