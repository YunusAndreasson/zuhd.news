1 drone approached Riyadh's diplomatic quarter on Thursday. Saudi Arabia intercepted 56 in total, spending orders of magnitude more per interception than each cheap attack drone cost to produce. The defence ministry has named no attacker, raising questions about whether stockpiles can sustain the pace.
