Mumbai — Rubio linked [India](country:IN)'s Russian oil waiver to its trade deal.

Protests over war-driven inflation leave Modi more exposed to friction with Washington, analyst Sadanand Dhume says.

Trump paused sanctions on Russian oil buyers including India last week, and Rubio said India cannot pivot from Russian energy overnight.

Atlantic Council analyst Michael Kugelman says Rubio has not addressed tariffs, Pakistan outreach or Washington's transactional approach.

Rubio visits Reliance's Jamnagar refinery Wednesday, and the trade deal still has no announcement date.
