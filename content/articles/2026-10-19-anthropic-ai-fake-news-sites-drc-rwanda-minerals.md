Kinshasa — 70 fake news sites published 318 articles on Congo's war.

Fact-checkers in [Congo](country:CD) say they missed the network until Anthropic's September report.

Coordinated accounts on X amplified the sites, mimicking real outlets and rewriting articles for different audiences.

Anthropic traced the network to LKM Company, a [French](country:FR) advertising agency, and found no evidence of government involvement.

Anthropic's findings leave open which clients LKM Company served.
