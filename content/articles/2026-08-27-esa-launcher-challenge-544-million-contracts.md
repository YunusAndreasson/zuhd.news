Paris — MaiaSpace's award is "expected to resume," undated.

Europe's incumbent rocket maker did not clear the bar its three challengers passed.

The European Space Agency split €543.6 million among Isar Aerospace, Rocket Factory Augsburg and PLD Space, ending single-champion procurement.

Each awardee must reach orbit by the end of 2027 to keep the operational funding flowing.
