Muscat — A projectile killed one sailor off Qeshm Island.

Hours later, Gulf states pulled Monday's Hormuz shipping talks between [Iran](country:IR) and Oman.

Regional countries, not Tehran, requested the postponement, an Iranian official said, of talks meant to manage the blockaded strait.

Oman gave no new date, and Saudi Arabia has submitted separate amendments to the Iran-Oman plan.
