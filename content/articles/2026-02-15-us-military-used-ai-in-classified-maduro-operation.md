Anthropic's AI model Claude was used in a classified US military operation. The Wall Street Journal reported that the Department of Defense accessed Claude through Palantir Technologies during last month's operation to capture Venezuelan President Nicol\u00e1s Maduro. The tool's specific role remains unclear. The system is capable of tasks ranging from analysing documents to guiding autonomous drones. No AI developer had previously been linked to a covert military operation, raising unresolved questions about accountability when civilian technology is deployed in classified actions without public oversight.
