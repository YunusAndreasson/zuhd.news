China's CO2 output has been flat or falling since March 2024. The Centre for Research on Energy and Clean Air found annual emissions dropped 0.3% as cheaper renewables displaced coal across nearly every sector. If the trend holds, the world's largest emitter will have peaked years ahead of its 2030 target.
