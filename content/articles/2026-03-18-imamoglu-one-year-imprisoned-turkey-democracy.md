İmamoğlu has spent 1 year in a Turkish prison. He won Istanbul's mayorship twice and polled ahead of Turkish President Erdoğan in hypothetical presidential surveys before Turkey charged him with corruption. His corruption trial at an Istanbul court continues with no verdict date set, keeping Turkey's leading opposition candidate jailed through the 2028 election.
