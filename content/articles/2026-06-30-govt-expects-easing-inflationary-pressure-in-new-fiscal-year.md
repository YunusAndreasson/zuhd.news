Islamabad — Pakistan's June inflation hit 12%, a fresh peak.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s finance ministry links the relief forecast for FY2027 to the US-Iran ceasefire and falling global oil prices.

Whether the US-Iran ceasefire holds determines whether the relief materialises; Pakistan imports most of its oil.
