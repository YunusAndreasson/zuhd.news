The Jalisco New Generation Cartel killed 25 soldiers overnight. The cartel launched 250 roadblocks across 20 Mexican states in retaliation for government forces killing its founder El Mencho. Mexico deployed 2,500 additional troops to Jalisco with no strategy for a cartel that operates without its leader.
