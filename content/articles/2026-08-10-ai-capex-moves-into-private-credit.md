New York — BlackRock won the financing for Meta's data center.

Artificial-intelligence infrastructure risk is shifting from tech balance sheets to private credit funds.

Private credit places that debt with pension and insurance investors, not the tech firms building the data centers.

BlackRock and rival asset managers are redesigning debt deals to avoid a market selloff.
