Bristol residents own a wind turbine returning £100,000 annually. The 4.2-megawatt turbine on the Lawrence Weston estate took a decade to build, surviving a Conservative government ban on onshore wind before construction in 2023. The development manager said the model is replicable across the UK's most economically deprived areas.
