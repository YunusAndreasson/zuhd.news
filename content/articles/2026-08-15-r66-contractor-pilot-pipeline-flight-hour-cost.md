Fort Rucker — A $10 billion contract replaces Army trainers.

The Army now depends on a vendor for aircraft, instructors and training data.

Military helicopters cost several times more per flight hour than a civilian turbine, and pilot output is the bottleneck.

The contract can run 26 years, replacing the Army's Lakota-based training fleet.
