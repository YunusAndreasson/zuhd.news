Abuja — [Nigeria](country:NG)'s quarterly budget reports are months overdue.

The Budget Office says the 2025 Appropriation Act's re-enactment extended the fiscal year to June 2026, legally delaying the quarterly publication timeline.

Outstanding reports will be released in phases over coming weeks; no specific dates were given.
