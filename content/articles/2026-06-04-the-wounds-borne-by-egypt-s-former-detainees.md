Cairo — Al-Nadeem documented 309 disappearances in [Egypt](country:EG) in 2025.

Released detainees face re-arrest in new cases the center calls "recycling"; 78 died in custody that year.

Three rights defenders who documented the pattern were arrested in June; no legal mechanism limits recall.
