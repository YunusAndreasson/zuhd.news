Amritsar — A sacrilege law bypassed Sikhism's highest authority.

The Akal Takht summoned all Sikh legislators in [India](country:IN)'s Punjab to answer for the law, enacted without consulting the community.

The Jathedar wants specific provisions removed, but the ruling Aam Aadmi Party has not committed to amendments.
