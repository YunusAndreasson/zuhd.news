Guwahati — Assam police detained 41 opposition volunteers, including their leader, before a meeting.

It signals pre-emptive policing against a new party challenging Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma.

Officers barricaded the meeting venue and held Ashutosh Ranka's group without stating charges, the Cockroach Janta Party said.

"He is scared that if we show the schools here, the country will know the reality," Ranka said.

The party is weighing legal action and demands immediate release, with police yet to respond.
