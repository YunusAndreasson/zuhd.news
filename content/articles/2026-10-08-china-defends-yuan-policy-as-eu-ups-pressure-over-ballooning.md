Beijing — The EU's 2025 deficit with China reached 360.6 billion euros.

European leaders want a stronger yuan to curb [China](country:CN)'s record exports.

[Germany](country:DE)'s Friedrich Merz calls the yuan 25% to 30% undervalued.

The central bank says the yuan has gained 23% on the dollar since 2005, to about 6.7.

EU trade chief Maroš Šefčovič is in Beijing to discuss narrowing the deficit.
