London — Britain gave the US wider strike rights from UK bases Friday. Human Rights Watch warned UK Prime Minister Keir Starmer that Geneva Common Article 1 requires states to ensure allies respect humanitarian law on their soil. HRW cited a US school strike killing 175 and demanded Starmer establish a legal oversight mechanism.
