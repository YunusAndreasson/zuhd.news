Tashkent — [Uzbekistan](country:UZ) will supply nuclear fuel outside Moscow's orbit.

It gives [India](country:IN)'s reactors fuel bypassing Non-Proliferation Treaty supply restrictions.

Uzbekistan ranks among the top global uranium producers, and this deal pulls supply away from Russian and Chinese buyers.

Both governments still need to finalize the uranium supply contract's terms.
