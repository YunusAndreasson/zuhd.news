Abu Dhabi — Nvidia is building the [United Arab Emirates](country:AE)'s path to AI power.

The Gulf state's AI capacity still depends entirely on chips and models built in the U.S.

Nvidia plans to release a free, open-weight AI model designed to rival [China](country:CN)'s best, running on hardware it sells across the Gulf.

How much control Washington keeps over the model's terms remains the open question for Gulf allies.
