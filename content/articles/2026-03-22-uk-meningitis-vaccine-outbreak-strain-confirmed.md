London — 2 people died in a southeast England meningitis outbreak. The UK Health Security Agency confirmed the meningococcal B vaccine Bexsero covers strain ST-41/44 after 18 cases — meningococcal disease can kill within hours. Vaccination clinics expanded, but authorities cannot say the outbreak is contained, with 11 additional cases under investigation.
