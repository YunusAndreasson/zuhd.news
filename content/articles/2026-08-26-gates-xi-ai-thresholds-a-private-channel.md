San Francisco — Gates reversed his stance: AI will destroy far more jobs than he predicted in January.

With no US-China government track on AI, a private citizen is filling the gap.

Gates seeks a November meeting with [China](country:CN)'s Xi Jinping to propose joint limits on releasing dangerous AI models.

No date is set, and the meeting binds neither government to act.
