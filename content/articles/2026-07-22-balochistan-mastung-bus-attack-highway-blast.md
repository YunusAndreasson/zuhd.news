Mastung — Gunmen firing from mountains killed three bus riders.

Hours earlier, a bridge blast on the same highway cut Quetta-Karachi traffic, [Pakistan](country:PK)'s link between Balochistan and the rest of the country.

An indefinite curfew now covers Khud Kocha as security forces hunt those behind both attacks.
