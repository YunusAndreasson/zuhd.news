Kuwait City — 70,000 Kuwaitis have lost nationality since March 2024.

A law now bars naturalized citizens and their descendants from voting, while revocations proceed by committee with no judicial appeal.

2,000 more were stripped within days of the Iran ceasefire, with no announced end to the campaign.
