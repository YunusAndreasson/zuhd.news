Hong Kong black-market petrol stations earn HK$10,000 daily. Pump prices rose 56% since the Iran war began February 28 — from HK$15.43 to HK$24.13 per litre — pushing buyers to illegal operators in industrial lots. Authorities have raided 3 stations and arrested 2 operators but say illegal networks continue to expand.
