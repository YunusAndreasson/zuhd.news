Washington — [US](country:US) gasoline prices jumped 9.1% in September, consumer price data show.

Trump promised in 2024 to cut energy prices by half within 12 months of taking office.

Brent crude stood at $109.90 on 16 October, up 4.9% in 7 days, with the [Iran](country:IR) war disrupting supply.

Midterms are 3 November, and 78% blame the White House for prices, an Ipsos poll found.
