Tokyo — Hayabusa2 found all 5 DNA bases in samples from asteroid Ryugu.

Unlike meteorites contaminated on impact, the Ryugu samples were sealed in space and returned intact — confirming for the first time that an asteroid carries DNA's building blocks free of Earth contamination.

NASA's OSIRIS-REx found the same set in Bennu, giving scientists 2 independent sources to test whether life's building blocks arrived on Earth from space.
