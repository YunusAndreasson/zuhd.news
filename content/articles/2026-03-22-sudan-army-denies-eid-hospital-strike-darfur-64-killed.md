El-Daein — A drone struck el-Daein Hospital on Eid, killing 64. WHO has documented 2,036 deaths in 213 attacks on healthcare since Sudan's civil war began in April 2023, which the UN calls the world's largest humanitarian crisis. El-Daein Teaching Hospital can no longer function; the Emergency Lawyers advocacy group has called for an independent investigation.
