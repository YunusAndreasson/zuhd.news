North Korea convened its 9th Workers' Party congress in Pyongyang. Kim Jong Un announced new military goals and presented 50 rocket launchers to delegates, calling for accelerated weapons development against outside threats. The congress, the first since 2021, is expected to set nuclear and economic policy for the next 5 years.
