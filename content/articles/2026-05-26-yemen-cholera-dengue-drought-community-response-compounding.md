Sana'a — Cholera, dengue, and respiratory illness converge in [Yemen](country:YE)'s capital.

WHO said Yemen had the world's highest cholera burden in 2024; war-damaged drainage creates the stagnant pools where dengue breeds.

Cholera peaks every harvest as unsafe irrigation water is reused; no international aid has arrived to address the infrastructure.
