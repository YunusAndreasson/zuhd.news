Palmdale — Vectis hides its air intake inside the fuselage, costing it thrust.

That trade only makes sense for a drone meant to be sent where pilots aren't.

An exposed inlet is one of a jet's biggest radar returns, so Skunk Works chose stealth over raw performance.

Lockheed says a first prototype should fly by the end of next year.
