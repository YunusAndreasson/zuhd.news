Islamabad — 400 lawyers demanded urgent bail for Imaan Mazari. She and husband Hadi Ali Chattha face 17 years under Pakistan's PECA cybercrime law for tweets the court classified as cyberterrorism. The Islamabad High Court dissolved the bench hearing their appeal; no new bench has been constituted.
