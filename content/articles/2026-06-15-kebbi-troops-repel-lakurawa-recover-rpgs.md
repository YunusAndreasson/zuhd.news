Kebbi — Nigerian troops seized rocket-propelled grenade launchers and improvised explosive devices at Dogon Daji.

Lakurawa is a newer armed group on [Nigeria](country:NG)'s northwest frontier with Niger, distinct from Zamfara bandit networks and still expanding.

The weapons suggest external arming; Nigeria has not identified the supply chain.
