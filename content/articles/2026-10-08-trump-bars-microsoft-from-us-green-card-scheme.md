Washington — Microsoft's shares fell 7.5% after the suspension.

Vice President JD Vance says it laid off 6,000 Americans while obtaining 6,300 H-1B visas.

The suspension blocks permanent labour certification, where employers show the [US](country:US) Labor Department no American can fill a job.

The administration also suspends Adobe, Infosys, Tata, Wipro, Cognizant, Capgemini and HCL until they prioritise American workers.
