Manila — Political dynasties monopolize offices in [the Philippines](country:PH).

Established dynasties including Marcos and Duterte cycle through offices using patronage networks that make independent candidates structurally uncompetitive.

An anti-dynasty constitutional clause has never been enforced; 2027 elections are the next test.
