Russia's Urals crude now fetches a $5 premium per barrel. Alfa Bank, a major Russian lender, forecasts March oil revenues at 900 billion rubles, up from pre-war sales that carried a $13-per-barrel discount. Russian President Putin offered to supply Europe with oil as US and EU sanctions enforcement against Russia eased during the Iran war.
