Iran let Indian ships through Hormuz while blocking others. Iranian officials said they do not want neutral nations to suffer — India had abstained on the UN Security Council vote backing US operations in the Gulf. The selective blockade tests whether Asian nations break from Washington's coalition to preserve Gulf energy access.
