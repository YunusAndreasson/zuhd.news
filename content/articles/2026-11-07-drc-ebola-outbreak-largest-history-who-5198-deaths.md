DR Congo — Ebola has killed 5,198 of 10,668 confirmed cases, WHO says.

It is the largest Ebola outbreak ever recorded in the [DR Congo](country:CD), whatever the virus species.

WHO says 335 of 512 recent deaths happened in communities, pointing to late detection and delayed care.

France and Uganda have controlled their cases since August, and Kenya and Nigeria reported none since 23 October.

As of 3 November, transmission continued in 51 of 67 health zones, with no approved vaccine for Bundibugyo virus.
