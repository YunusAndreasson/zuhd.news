Chicago — A cell built from nonliving parts just divided.

Biologists assembled molecules piece by piece into a membrane that grew, copied its DNA and split, completing a full cell cycle for the first time.

Origins-of-life researcher Jack Szostak called it "an impressive step" toward defining where chemistry ends and life begins.
