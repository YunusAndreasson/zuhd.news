Washington — [US](country:US) prices rose 0.6% in September, 3.7% over the year.

Gasoline jumped 9.1% in the month and accounted for more than half the increase.

Core inflation, which strips out food and energy, rose to 2.6% from 2.4%, so the pressure is spreading.

The Federal Reserve decides on rates 28 October, and market pricing points to a pause then a December hike.
