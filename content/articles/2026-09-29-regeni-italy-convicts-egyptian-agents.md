Rome — An Italian court sentenced three Egyptian agents to 10 years.

It marks the first European conviction of serving foreign intelligence officers for killing a citizen abroad.

Italy tried the men in absentia after [Egypt](country:EG) refused for years to serve legal papers or disclose their whereabouts.

A fourth defendant, the former head of state security, was acquitted of the same charge.

Rome cannot enforce the sentences without an extradition Egypt has never granted.
