Beirut — Cancer cases rose 162% in [Lebanon](country:LB) since 1990.

The 2019 currency collapse cut state funding for oncology drugs while diesel generators replacing the failed grid began burning constantly in dense streets, a Lancet study found.

Doctors say many patients now present too late for treatment to succeed.
