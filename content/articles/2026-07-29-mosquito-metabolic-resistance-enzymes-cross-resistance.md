Geneva — Mosquitoes are defeating insecticides faster than programmes can react.

Researchers report populations rapidly upregulating detoxifying enzymes that break down chemicals before they reach the nervous system, unlike the slower resistance public health has long managed.

Bed-net and spraying programmes across malaria-endemic regions rest on chemistry the finding says is being outrun.
