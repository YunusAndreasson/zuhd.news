New Delhi — Maruti WagonR goes E85, a first for India.

[India](country:IN) imports most of its crude; flex-fuel engines adjust injection for any ethanol blend, substituting domestically grown supply for imported oil.

E85 pumps are not yet available at scale across India, limiting how fast the import substitution can compound.
