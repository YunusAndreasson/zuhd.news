A fire hit [Poland](country:PL)'s WB Electronics drone plant.

The factory makes components Ukrainian and Polish militaries need for FlyEye and Warmate drones.

Prosecutors opened a terrorism probe, since one supplier's plant can be the single source for parts thousands of drones need.

No suspect has been named, though [Russia](country:RU) is widely suspected in past sabotage cases.
