Bucharest — An unelected cabinet cannot pass Romania's crucial laws.

Sorin Grindeanu's Social Democrats, the largest faction, want a cross-party taskforce to approve legislation a caretaker government legally cannot enact.

[Romania](country:RO) remains under EU deficit scrutiny while the fiscal bills the taskforce would tackle stay stalled.
