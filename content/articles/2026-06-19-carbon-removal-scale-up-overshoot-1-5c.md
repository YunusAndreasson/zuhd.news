Milan — Climate scientists have accepted 1.5C will be breached.

The State of Carbon Dioxide Removal report targets 8.8 billion tonnes per year by 2050 — nearly 4 times current capacity.

No funding mechanism exists to close a gap that requires trillions in new land, energy, and capital.
