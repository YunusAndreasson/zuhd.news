Northern Europe — Airbus says its U740 Valkyrie flew at Mach 0.8.

[Germany](country:DE) wants a combat-ready uncrewed aircraft from Airbus and Kratos by 2029.

Airbus adapts Kratos's American XQ-58A with a European-controlled mission system, rather than waiting for a new airframe to mature.

The next test phase adds that mission system, which Airbus says keeps humans in charge of critical decisions.
