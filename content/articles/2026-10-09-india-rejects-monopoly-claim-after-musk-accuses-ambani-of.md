New Delhi — Musk asked whether Ambani runs [India](country:IN), offering no evidence.

Starlink has waited 4 years to launch in a market of over 1 billion users.

Starlink holds a licence, but a commercial launch still needs security compliance and a spectrum assignment.

Telecom minister Jyotiraditya Scindia said India allows no monopoly in any sector.

Security assessments of all 3 licensed operators, Starlink included, are still underway, the communications ministry says.
