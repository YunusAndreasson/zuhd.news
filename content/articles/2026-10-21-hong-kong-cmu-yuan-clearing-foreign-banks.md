Hong Kong — The city's bond clearinghouse has around 200 members, and wants more.

Many foreign lenders have not yet engaged in Bond Connect, the mainland link.

The Central Moneymarkets Unit holds and settles bonds, and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority has run it since 1990.

Deputy Financial Secretary Michael Wong Wai-lun said Hong Kong will encourage more foreign banks to join.
