Virginia — A child younger than 4 died of measles.

Virginia has reported 220 cases this year, 179 of them in unvaccinated people.

Outbreaks in Buckingham County, with 127 cases, and neighbouring Charlotte County, with 34, drive the state's increase.

Virginia's health department would not say where the child lived or whether the child was vaccinated.

The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention has confirmed 6 measles deaths nationally, fewer than Pennsylvania's 8.
