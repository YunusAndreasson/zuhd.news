Kalat — A reporter was told to order his coffin in advance.

42 journalists have been killed in Balochistan since 2008, per the Balochistan Union of Journalists, with no convictions; [Pakistan](country:PK)'s largest province has almost no other reporters left to cover it.

A journalist protection bill promised in February by the chief minister's aide has not passed.
