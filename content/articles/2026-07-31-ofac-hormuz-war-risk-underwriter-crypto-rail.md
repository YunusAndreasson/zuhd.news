Washington — Treasury sanctioned two bitcoin-paid marine insurers.

Persian Gulf Marine Insurance and Hormuz Safe forced tankers to buy coverage, funneling bitcoin proceeds to [Iran](country:IR)'s Revolutionary Guard, Treasury said.

Foreign firms, including crypto payers, now face secondary sanctions for dealing with the designated insurers.
