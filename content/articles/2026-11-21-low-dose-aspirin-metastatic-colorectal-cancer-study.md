Sweden — 18,943 colorectal cancer patients already had distant metastases at diagnosis.

A register study asks whether low-dose aspirin users were less likely to arrive with spread disease.

It covers every Swedish patient diagnosed with stage I-IV colorectal cancer from 2008 to 2021, linked to prescription records.

The ASPREE randomized trial in older adults found more metastatic cancer among aspirin users.

Register data show association only, and randomized trials have so far been mixed.
