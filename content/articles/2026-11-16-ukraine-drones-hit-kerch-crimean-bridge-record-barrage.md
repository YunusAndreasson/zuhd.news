Kerch — Russian-installed Crimea chief says Ukraine hit the bridge.

The bridge is [Russia](country:RU)'s main road and rail link to occupied Crimea.

Road traffic is closed until about midday, four trains are delayed, and the government is arranging ferries between Kerch and Kavkaz.

[Ukraine](country:UA) has not claimed the strike, and Russian officials are still assessing damage to the road section.

A government commission under Deputy Prime Minister Marat Khusnullin has yet to say when repairs finish.
