A federal judge threw out mandatory detention for thousands of arrested immigrants. Judge Sunshine Sykes in Riverside, California, vacated a Board of Immigration Appeals decision and accused the Trump administration of "terrorizing immigrants and recklessly violating the law." She ordered detainees receive bond hearings and immediate phone access to attorneys.
