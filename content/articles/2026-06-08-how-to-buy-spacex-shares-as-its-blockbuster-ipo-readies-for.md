New York — SpaceX prices its $75 billion IPO Thursday.

The $75 billion raise would triple Saudi Aramco's 2019 record, valuing SpaceX at $1.77 trillion with Musk retaining primary control, per SpaceX's prospectus.

Retail investors receive 30% of shares—triple the usual allocation; Nasdaq trading opens Friday under ticker SPCX.
