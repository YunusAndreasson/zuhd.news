Mumbai — Tata topped a global EV efficiency ranking, ahead of Tesla and BYD.

Unable to afford big battery packs, [India](country:IN)'s makers optimise kilometres per kilowatt-hour instead of range — the design a battery-scarce market demands.

Tata and Mahindra trail on range and charging speed; that trade-off is the template countries like Nigeria and Bangladesh will need.
