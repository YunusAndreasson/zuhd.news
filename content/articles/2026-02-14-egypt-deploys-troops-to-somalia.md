Egypt is sending 1,000 troops to Somalia. The deployment falls under an African Union mission officially aimed at countering al-Shabaab. Egyptian President Abdel Fattah el-Sisi and Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud met in Cairo to coordinate the operation. The strategic stakes run deeper: Egypt and Somalia declared Red Sea security the "exclusive responsibility" of coastal states, challenging Ethiopia's January 2024 port deal with breakaway Somaliland. Egypt fears an Ethiopian Red Sea naval base would shift power around the Bab el-Mandeb strait, a chokepoint for global shipping.
