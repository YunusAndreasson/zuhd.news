London — Active flaps cut pitch-link loads in flight tests.

Lower fatigue loads could extend how long rotor parts last between replacements.

A blade meets faster air advancing and slower air retreating each spin, loading it unevenly every revolution.

The test used one unmanned aircraft, so fleet-wide gains remain unproven.
