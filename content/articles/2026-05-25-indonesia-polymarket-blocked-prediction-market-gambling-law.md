Jakarta — Indonesia blocked Polymarket as online gambling.

[Indonesia](country:ID) applied an existing gambling statute to crypto-settled contracts, setting a template other Muslim-majority governments can cite.

The ministry will restrict similar platforms; the block covers Indonesia's 280 million residents on one order.
