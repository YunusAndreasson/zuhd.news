Washington — Regulators granted World Liberty Trust a conditional national bank charter.

The president's family holds a stake in the firm the president's own regulator just chartered.

The charter lets it custody and issue its USD1 stablecoin directly, bypassing the bank it currently rents access from.

Full approval awaits unspecified "preopening requirements."
