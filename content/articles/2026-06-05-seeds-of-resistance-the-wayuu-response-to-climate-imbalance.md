La Guajira — Wayuu communities in [Colombia](country:CO) combine ancestral seeds and jagüey wells to survive deepening drought.

They document heritage varieties alongside formal research; climate scientists have begun studying the approach.

Researchers call on Colombia to fund and scale indigenous adaptation strategies before seasonal rains fail further.
