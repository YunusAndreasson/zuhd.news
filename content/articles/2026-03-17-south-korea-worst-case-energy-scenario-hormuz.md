South Korea may restrict driving as Hormuz stays shut. South Korean President Lee Jae Myung proposed the restrictions, citing South Korea's 70% dependence on Hormuz crude, which has been blocked for 3 weeks. Lee also declined to send warships to reopen the strait, citing constitutional constraints on overseas deployment — leaving rationing as South Korea's only lever.
