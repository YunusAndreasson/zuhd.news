title: "Glass Stores Data for Millennia"
date: "2026-02-18T00:00:00Z"
source: "Nature"

category: "tech"

A single glass square stores 2 million books. Microsoft's Project Silica encodes data by firing ultrafast laser pulses into borosilicate glass, creating deformations that survive 10,000 years at 290°C. The technology targets archival storage, where today's hard drives and tape degrade within decades.
