New York — Nuclear Nonproliferation Treaty talks failed a third time.

[US](country:US) and [Russia](country:RU) test-fired intercontinental ballistic missiles during the conference; New START expired in February, leaving no verification regime in place.

Conference chair Do Hung Viet warned another failure would cost the treaty its credibility.
