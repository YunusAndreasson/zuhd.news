Brasília — [Brazil](country:BR)'s attorney general's office runs software its spies rejected.

Abin, the intelligence agency, dropped its own purchase in 2023, citing risk to digital sovereignty.

The office pays R$3.7 million over two years for Palantir's Foundry and AI platform, hosted on Amazon's cloud.

The office says Palantir and Amazon took no part in building its prototypes.

The contract expires in December and the office says it will not renew.
