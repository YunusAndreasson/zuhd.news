Paris — 410,000 marched across [France](country:FR) on Saturday, the government says.

Marchers opposed Marine Le Pen's National Rally and Emmanuel Macron, 6 months before the presidential election.

Police fired tear gas and water cannon at Paris crowds the interior ministry put at 110,000, and arrested 135 people nationwide.

Police seized lead pellets, fireworks and a katana, Interior Minister Laurent Nunez said.

Marine Le Pen's party holds the second day of its Orléans convention Sunday.
