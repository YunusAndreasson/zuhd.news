Birmingham — One control centre grounded almost every train.

Passengers get only Delay Repay compensation, no statutory guarantee of alternatives.

A single Birmingham site now runs crew rostering and control for CrossCountry's whole network, so trains sit idle though undamaged.

This is the 2nd such control-centre outage in 2 weeks, after Manchester's Aug. 7 failure.
