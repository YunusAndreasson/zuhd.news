Kolhapur — Empty soybean pods didn't make Maharashtra's drought list.

Exclusion means no compensation, loan relief or power-bill waivers for [India](country:IN)'s affected farmers.

The state measures rainfall by taluka, and a wetter neighboring village can average out a dry pocket's deficit.

Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis says 5 to 6 excluded talukas will be added after further crop assessment.

Compensation isn't expected before October, after the crop assessment Fadnavis has promised for excluded talukas.
