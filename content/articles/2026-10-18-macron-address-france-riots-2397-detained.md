Paris — Police arrested nearly 2,400 people in one night.

Nearly 1,000 officers were injured, 7 seriously, the interior ministry said.

Rioters looted shops and burned Limoges' town hall after 550,000 people marched across [France](country:FR) over fuel prices and living costs.

"Those who have destroyed must pay," President Emmanuel Macron said in a national address.

Opposition politicians have called for Interior Minister Laurent Nunez to be sacked over police preparations.
