Walldorf — SAP replaced the keys that prove its software is genuine.

SAP software runs accounting, procurement and supply chains for businesses and public institutions worldwide.

Hackers reportedly used stolen employee tokens on GitHub to copy and modify source code.

SAP says it has found no evidence of unauthorized access to customer business data.

SAP has not said whether altered code reached customers or whether the intrusion is contained.
