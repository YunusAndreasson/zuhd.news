Karachi — Sindh chose [Pakistan](country:PK)'s state telecom, not the cloud.

"Government data is a national asset," Sindh's chief minister said; the National Telecom Corporation already runs Tier-III centres in Islamabad, Lahore and Quetta.

A committee will decide within three months whether a private partner builds it.
