Doha — Washington and Tehran agreed to halt mutual strikes.

A senior US official confirmed the ceasefire as both sides prepare Tuesday consultations in [Qatar](country:QA) over their Strait of Hormuz dispute.

Tuesday's talks are the first formal channel since [Iran](country:IR) and the [US](country:US) exchanged strikes earlier this month.
