Anthropic barred its AI from surveillance and autonomous weapons. The Pentagon is considering labelling the company a supply chain risk, which would end a 2-year contract for classified and enterprise AI tools. The standoff may set terms for how OpenAI, Google, and xAI negotiate their own military deals.
