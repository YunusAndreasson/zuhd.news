Paris — 39–41C heat across [France](country:FR) prompted a ban on outdoor alcohol during Sunday's Fête de la Musique festival.

Alcohol worsens heat dehydration, raising the risk of fatal heat stroke in outdoor crowds.

Red heat alerts remain in effect across the southwest into Paris.
