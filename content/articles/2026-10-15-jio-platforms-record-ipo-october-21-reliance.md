Mumbai — Jio Platforms seeks up to ₹30,213 crore, [India](country:IN)'s largest offering.

Every share is new, and proceeds prepay Reliance Jio Infocomm's borrowings, the prospectus says.

The band of ₹1,065-1,119 values Jio at ₹10.3 lakh crore, short of the ₹11 lakh crore once sought.

Bidding runs 21 to 23 October and shares are expected to begin trading 28 October.
