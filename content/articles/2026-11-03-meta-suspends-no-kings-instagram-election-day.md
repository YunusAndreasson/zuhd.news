United States — Meta locked No Kings out of its Instagram for 4 hours.

Meta declined to promise its automated systems will not suspend the account again on election day.

Meta's Andy Stone said the group's Claude AI tool read profiles and hashtags in bursts resembling bot traffic.

No evidence shows the Trump administration asked Meta to restrict the account.

A coalition of 14 attorneys general has asked Meta to explain, and No Kings demands a meeting with senior leadership.
