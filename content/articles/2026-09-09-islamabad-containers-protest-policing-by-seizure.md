Islamabad — 100 container trucks sit in a police yard.

Truck owners say they weren't compensated for stalled cargo and lost income.

Authorities in [Pakistan](country:PK) commonly requisition private containers to barricade roads before protests, avoiding arrests or new laws.

The Islamabad High Court is forming a bench to review the planned September 27 march.
