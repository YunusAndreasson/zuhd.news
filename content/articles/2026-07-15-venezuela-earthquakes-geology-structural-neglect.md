La Guaira — 4,336 dead, 20,000 still missing.

A rare seismic doublet struck just 39 seconds apart on shallow faults, but decades of unreinforced construction and institutional decline turned the quakes into a mass-casualty disaster.

Recovery teams are still searching for the 20,000 unaccounted for across seven states.
