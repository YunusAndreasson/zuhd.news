Markets expect 2 Australian rate hikes in 6 weeks. The International Energy Agency called the Iran war the largest oil supply disruption in history, a shock that now threatens Australia's 3-year effort to keep unemployment below 4.4 percent. If both hikes proceed, Australia's unemployment rate could return to 5 percent for the first time since COVID.
