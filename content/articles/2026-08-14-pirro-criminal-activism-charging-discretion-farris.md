Washington — Melissa Farris faces 10 years for spray-painting a memorial.

US Attorney Jeanine Pirro called it "criminal activism," a label that shapes how the case is prosecuted.

Farris's own videos show her homeless, sheltering from storms and saying federal marshals were following her.

She was arraigned Friday without an assigned lawyer.
