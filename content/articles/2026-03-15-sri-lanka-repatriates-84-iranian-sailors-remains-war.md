84 sealed boxes left Sri Lanka for Iran on Friday. A US submarine torpedoed the IRIS Dena off Sri Lanka on March 4, pulling 251 sailors from 2 Iranian warships into custody. India is sheltering 183 survivors from a third ship while Washington presses both governments to halt repatriation.
