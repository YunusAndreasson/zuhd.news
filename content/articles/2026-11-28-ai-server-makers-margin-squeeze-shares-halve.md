Round Rock — Dell's shares nearly halved despite a $132.3 billion AI order backlog.

Server assemblers take the most orders and the thinnest margins in the AI hardware chain.

Micron's gross margin is 87% and Nvidia's 74.9%, against Dell's 21.2%, so memory and graphics-chip makers capture the value.

Dell raised its full-year revenue forecast to $202 billion, and Hewlett Packard Enterprise raised its growth guidance too.

Dell expects its infrastructure group's operating margin to fall from 17% to 15% next quarter.
