New York — Advising London gamblers, De Moivre stumbled on the bell curve. The central limit theorem shows that sums of many independent random variables converge to a bell curve regardless of each variable's distribution. Drug trials and climate models rest on this theorem — and break down in systems where extreme events cluster.
