Mandelson and Prince Andrew were photographed in bathrobes with Epstein. The image — buried in US Justice Department documents released this year — is the first known photograph of both men with the convicted sex trafficker, taken on Martha's Vineyard between 1999 and 2000. Peter Mandelson, tipped for senior diplomatic roles in the current Labour government, had previously denied the closeness of their relationship.
