Paris — A curfew starts at 11pm after 2,400 arrests.

Weeks of student protest in [France](country:FR) now meet curfews and a demonstration ban.

Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu told prefects to set localised curfews, ruled out a Paris-wide one, and kept a state of emergency open.

Jean-Luc Mélenchon demanded evidence for Lecornu's claim that radical left groups caused the unrest.

Unions have called nationwide strikes for 5 November.
