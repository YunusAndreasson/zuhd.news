Pakistan and India hold no strategic oil reserve. Brent crude crossed $100 as the Strait of Hormuz closed, sending the full price shock directly into food and fuel costs with no cushion. Iran has threatened oil could reach $200 — a level economists say would push dollar-import-dependent economies into famine-level food inflation.
