Shandong — A prototype robot scores wheat ears like a breeder.

[China](country:CN)'s State Council wants AI in crop breeding under a five-year farm blueprint.

Researchers say an arm envelops one wheat ear, eight micro-cameras image it, and software outputs grain count and ear length.

The robot sprays paint on promising plants for a human to inspect.

The team has a preliminary agreement with Shandong Jizhi Biotech to commercialise it.
