Eating fewer calories lowers demand, so producers plant less, prices fall and trade shifts, their simulation of the global food system shows.

Cheaper food could raise consumption among people not taking the drugs, offsetting some of the benefit, the authors warn.

Adding healthier diets lifts the 2050 estimate to 46 million hectares and 7 million deaths avoided a year.
