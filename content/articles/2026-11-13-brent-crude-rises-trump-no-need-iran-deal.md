London — Brent jumped to $94.30 minutes after Trump's Iran post.

Trump said the [US](country:US) no longer needs a deal with [Iran](country:IR).

Iran's military said Thursday four tankers were hit by explosions in Hormuz, the route for crude from major Middle Eastern producers.

Brent still headed for a 2.9% weekly loss Friday, after a 16% plunge the week before.

Analyst Sugandha Sachdeva says any fresh supply disruption would quickly push prices back up.
