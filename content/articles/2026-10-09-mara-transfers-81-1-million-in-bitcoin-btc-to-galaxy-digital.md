Hallandale Beach — MARA's bitcoin holdings fell by 18,245 coins since February.

The miner is reducing debt and expanding into AI and high-performance computing infrastructure.

In March it sold 15,133 BTC for about $1.1 billion, mostly to repurchase convertible debt.

Lookonchain data show 996 BTC ($81.13 million) went to an address attributed to Galaxy Digital, which does not confirm a sale.
