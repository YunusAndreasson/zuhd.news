Sarajevo — Crude is under 28% of Bosnia's 3.78-mark diesel price.

Brent fell 18.7% in 30 days to $89.11 as of 17 November, yet diesel is falling slowly.

Distributors say refining margins doubled or tripled this year, and [Bosnia and Herzegovina](country:BA) subsidises no pump prices.

The International Energy Agency says margins softened in October, but the diesel crack spread stayed nearly 90% above last year.
