Rome — Thales Alenia Space plans private investors for its cargo spacecraft.

Rival The Exploration Company won up to €760 million from the European Space Agency in September.

If the agency accepts its bid, it would create a separate legal entity to finance and sell the service.

The agency confirmed in September it would continue tendering with Thales Alenia Space to keep competition.

Thales Alenia Space expects the agency's verdict by the end of November.
