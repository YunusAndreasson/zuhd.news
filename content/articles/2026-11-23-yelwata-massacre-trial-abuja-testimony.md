Abuja — A farmer lost his wife and 3 sons at Yelwata.

[Nigeria](country:NG) is trying 9 men on 57 terrorism counts over the massacre that reportedly killed 150+.

Police and soldiers were stationed at the market, yet officers told him they were outnumbered during the 4-hour attack.

"My youngest child, Kingsley, was nine months old," Ukeyima David testified Monday.

David could not identify the attackers, and the trial continues before Judge Joyce Abdulmalik.
