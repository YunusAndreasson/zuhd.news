Ottawa — Quebec teachers and police remain barred from wearing hijabs.

Some Muslim women have removed hijabs at work or left Quebec to keep their jobs.

[Canada](country:CA)'s Supreme Court ruled legislatures may pre-emptively invoke the notwithstanding clause against several Charter rights at once, as Quebec did.

The court struck down only the rule barring face coverings for members of Quebec's National Assembly.

Premier Paul St-Pierre Plamondon has promised to extend secularism laws to schoolchildren.
