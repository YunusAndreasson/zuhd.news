New York — G7 ministers demanded [Iran](country:IR) halt Houthi arms support.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA) and global shipping through Hormuz depend on compliance the statement cannot enforce.

The G7's foreign ministers issued the statement after an informal meeting on the UN General Assembly's sidelines in New York.

The G7 threatens further sanctions on Iran if the Houthi arms flow does not stop.
