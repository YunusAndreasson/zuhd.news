Baja California Sur — Polo's central pressure fell 88 millibars in 24 hours.

The hurricane center expects landfall on [Mexico](country:MX)'s Baja peninsula Monday, with life-threatening flooding and mudslides from Sunday.

A near-historic El Niño and warm water took it from tropical depression to Category 5 in about 40 hours.

The hurricane center rated Polo a Category 4 with 155 mph winds Saturday morning.

The National Weather Service warns Polo and Odalys moisture could bring flash flooding to the [US](country:US) Desert Southwest.
