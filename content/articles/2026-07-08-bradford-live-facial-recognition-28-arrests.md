Bradford — Live facial recognition led to 28 arrests.

The cameras scan every passing face against a police watchlist, a method [Britain](country:GB)'s High Court upheld in April despite civil-liberties objections.

West Yorkshire Police have not said how long non-matches' faces are retained.
