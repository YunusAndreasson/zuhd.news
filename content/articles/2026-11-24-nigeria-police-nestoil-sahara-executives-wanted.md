Lagos — [Nigeria](country:NG)'s police declared 9 executives wanted.

The list includes Nestoil's chairman and a Sahara Group executive director.

Police invited 10 people over a 7 May parcel of 2 rounds and a live explosive sent to First Bank's managing director.

Only 1 appeared on 12 November, and the police statement does not say what evidence links the others to the parcel.

Police say they will prosecute anyone found culpable under Section 8 of the Firearms Act.
