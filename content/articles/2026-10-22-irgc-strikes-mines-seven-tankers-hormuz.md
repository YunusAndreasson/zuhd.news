Tehran — [Iran](country:IR)'s Revolutionary Guard says 7 tankers were hit overnight.

Strait traffic averaged 3.7 ships a day to 18 October, 10% below normal.

It says 4 tankers burned during ship-to-ship oil transfers in the northern Gulf and 3 struck mines west of the strait.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 2 November a 10% chance, down 4 points in a week.
