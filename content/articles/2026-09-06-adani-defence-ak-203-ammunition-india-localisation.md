New Delhi — Adani Defence won a 5-year AK-203 ammunition deal.

It gives one private conglomerate a permanent foothold inside a sovereign defence function.

Rifles sell once, but ammunition recurs, which is why [India](country:IN) is localising a system built jointly with [Russia](country:RU).

Adani has not disclosed the contract's value, leaving its terms outside public scrutiny.
