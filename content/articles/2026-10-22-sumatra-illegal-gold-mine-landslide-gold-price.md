Padang — A hillside collapse killed four illegal gold miners on 6 October.

Walhi counts 52 miner deaths in West Sumatra in 15 years and says the true toll is higher.

Gold peaked above $5,600 an ounce in January, and a village head says that draws young men.

Police raided forests and seized excavators, but some mines resumed once inspectors left.

Authorities in [Indonesia](country:ID) are pursuing special licences to bring informal mines into the formal sector.
