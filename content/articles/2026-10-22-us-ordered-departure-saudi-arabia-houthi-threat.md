Riyadh — The [US](country:US) ordered non-emergency staff and families out of Saudi Arabia.

Houthi strikes on airports and oil sites now kill civilians, after a four-year truce collapsed.

State Department spokesperson Tommy Pigott said the order covers Saudi Arabia only and called reports of a Gulf-wide order inaccurate.

Four US officials told Reuters that missions in other Gulf states would also send families home.

The State Department has confirmed neither account, and the Riyadh embassy warns of further Houthi attacks.
