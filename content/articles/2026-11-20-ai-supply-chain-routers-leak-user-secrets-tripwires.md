United States — Attackers copied every test key one AI provider received.

Dragos's HD Moore seeded 2 million prompts with traceable secrets and found 3 providers breached.

Prompts decrypt at every router, reseller and host in the chain, and each can log the plaintext.

Moore calls on-premises hosting the most effective fix, and it is expensive.
