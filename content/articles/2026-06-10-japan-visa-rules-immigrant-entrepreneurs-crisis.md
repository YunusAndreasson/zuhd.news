Tokyo — [Japan](country:JP)'s business-manager visa now bars founders who cannot meet new capital and staffing floors.

The requirement was designed to curb fraud but is closing ethnic restaurants run by immigrant entrepreneurs unable to qualify.

No revision is planned despite a record national labor shortage.
