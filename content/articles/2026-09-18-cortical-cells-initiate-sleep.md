New York — One in a thousand cortical neurons can switch a mouse's brain to sleep.

The find challenges sleep-disorder research built on a decades-old assumption about where sleep starts.

Einstein medical school researchers found these Sst-Chodl cells fire when triggered, overturning the belief that deeper brain regions alone start sleep.

The team still doesn't know what naturally switches these cells on.
