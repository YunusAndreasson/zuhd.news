New Delhi — Apple and Samsung blocked India's 6th attempt to mandate Aadhaar on smartphones.

Aadhaar links fingerprints and iris scans for 1.34 billion residents; the firms cited security, compatibility, and production-cost objections.

Industry documents show all 6 government preload attempts in 2 years collapsed under the same pressure.
