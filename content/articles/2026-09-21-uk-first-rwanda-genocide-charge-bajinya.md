London — Vincent Brown faces Britain's first Rwanda genocide charges.

It tests whether Britain will prosecute atrocity crimes committed abroad, not just harbor suspects.

UK courts refused to extradite Brown to [Rwanda](country:RW) in 2017, and police then investigated him for seven years.

"This case demonstrates the UK is not a safe haven for war criminals," said Commander Helen Flanagan, counterterror police.

Brown appears at Westminster Magistrates' Court Tuesday as police probe other Rwandan suspects in Britain.
