San Bruno — YouTube terminated creators of ASMR, or autonomous sensory meridian response, videos this week.

The company cited its nudity policy in termination emails, then said the channels linked to outside content that broke it.

Slight Sounds, an ASMR creator since 2013, says she lost her income and nine years of archived videos.
