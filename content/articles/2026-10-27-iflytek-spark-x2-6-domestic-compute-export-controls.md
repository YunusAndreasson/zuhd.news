China — iFlytek says it trained Spark X2.6 without Nvidia.

Chairman Liu Qingfeng says domestic compute trails Nvidia by nearly 2 generations, in memory and bandwidth.

A 4-billion-parameter on-device model handles easy or sensitive tasks locally, while a cloud model takes the hard ones.

Liu says only Huawei and iFlytek are mainly training on domestic chips, and he wants more firms to join.

iFlytek's target is 60% on-device task completion, up from 41.9% now.
