Tokyo — Researchers tracked 120 elders across 46,720 sleep-nights.

Each 1-point regularity gain meant up to 70% higher odds of sharper cognition.

The metric measures the minimum work to reshape one day's activity pattern into another, not just total hours.

The finding comes from one cohort in [Japan](country:JP), and whether it generalizes elsewhere is untested.
