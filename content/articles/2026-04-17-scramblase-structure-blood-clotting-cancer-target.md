New York — Researchers caught a scramblase flipping membrane lipids. The TMEM16F protein scrambles lipid arrangement across cell membranes — a step central to blood clotting initiation and tumor-cell signaling. The structural map gives drug developers a binding target for clotting disorders and cancers where TMEM16F misfires.
