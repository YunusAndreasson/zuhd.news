New York — The Securities and Exchange Commission conditionally approved cash-settled bitcoin index options.

No bitcoin changes hands at expiry — only the dollar difference, removing custody barriers for regulated funds.

Commodity Futures Trading Commission approval is pending before the contract trades on standard brokerage accounts.
