Islamabad — Planners objected, but the loan passed anyway.

[Pakistan](country:PK) already borrowed $4.7 billion for the same unmet tax-reform goal.

The loan carries a 25-year term at 1.5–2% interest, targeting a 13.5% tax-to-GDP ratio by 2029.

The Planning Commission still wants an independent Pakistan Institute of Development Economics review.
