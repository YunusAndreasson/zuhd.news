Seoul — [North Korea](country:KP) laid 960,000 border mines since 2024, Seoul's military says.

Soldiers on both sides now patrol a line they disagree on.

Pyongyang began fortifying the border in April 2024 after redefining ties as those between two hostile states, South Korea's military told lawmakers.

Pyongyang denies responsibility for the 21 September blasts that wounded 3 South Korean soldiers.

[South Korea](country:KR) proposed military talks Friday, but Pyongyang ignored a similar offer last November.
