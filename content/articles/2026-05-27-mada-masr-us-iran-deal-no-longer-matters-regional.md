Cairo — Hormuz reopening drives the Trump-Iran deal.

A Pakistani mediator said the terms cover a 60-day ceasefire extension and uranium "custody" under the International Atomic Energy Agency — not dismantlement.

"Custody" — whether enriched uranium stays in [Iran](country:IR) — remains the sticking point, the mediator said.
