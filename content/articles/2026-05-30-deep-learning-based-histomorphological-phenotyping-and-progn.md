London — AI finds a survival gap invisible to pathologists.

The 2 tumour types look identical to pathologists; the model found slide features predicting a 100% versus 39.5% gap in 5-year survival across 670 patients.

The model is an unedited pre-publication study; clinical validation in treatment settings has not yet been done.
