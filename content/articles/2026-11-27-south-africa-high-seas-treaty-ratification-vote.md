Cape Town — [South Africa](country:ZA) has until 12 December to claim a vote.

Non-parties at the January conference can speak but cannot vote on the High Seas Treaty's founding rules.

Cabinet took nearly 400 days to approve the treaty, and Parliament's committee does not sit again until late January.

African states hold about 20% of parties, short of the quarter needed to block decisions, researchers at the Institute for Security Studies note.

The conference opens in New York on 11 January, and committee chair Nqabisa Gantsho calls timely ratification unlikely.
