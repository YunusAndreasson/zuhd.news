United States — An Ethereum Foundation developer counts 15,000 contracts at risk.

Immutable contracts cannot be patched, yet they may behave differently under new network rules.

Proposal EIP-8037 raises the gas to deploy a contract from 32,000 to 183,600, breaking apps that assumed predictable costs.

Marius van der Wijden puts the money at risk at only a few hundred thousand dollars.

Core developers revisit the proposal Thursday, with Hoodi testing on hold until the bugs are fixed.
