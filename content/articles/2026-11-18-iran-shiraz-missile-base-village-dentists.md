Bid Zard — 80 villagers queued for dentists beside a Revolutionary Guards missile base.

Residents of about 7,000 households were ordered out during the 40-day war in March.

The Guards' Aerospace Force, which runs [Iran](country:IR)'s missiles, flew over 300 volunteer medics to Shiraz in late October.

The clinics ran 3 days, and the missile facility still borders the community.
