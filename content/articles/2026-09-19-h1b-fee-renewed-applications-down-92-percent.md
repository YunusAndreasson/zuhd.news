Washington — H-1B applications fell 92% after the fee.

Indians, the visa's largest beneficiary group, absorb most of the loss.

The $100,000 fee falls on [US](country:US) employers, not workers, pricing out sponsors that cannot absorb it annually.

The Labor Secretary has 30 days to review employer layoff data under a new order.
