Beijing — A 42-light-year X-ray tail matches once-unexplained gamma rays.

It offers a candidate answer to a century-old question: where cosmic rays originate.

[China](country:CN)'s Einstein Probe satellite and a Tibetan Plateau observatory traced the tail to a pulsar 4,600 light-years away.

Researchers still need to confirm the pulsar wind mechanism against other candidate accelerators before ruling out alternatives.
