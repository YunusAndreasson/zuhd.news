Taoyuan — About 12 Micron workers staged [Taiwan](country:TW)'s first chip-industry strike.

Taiwan plants make over 60% of Micron's memory chips for AI servers.

Workers want profit sharing like Samsung's and SK Hynix's in [South Korea](country:KR), as Micron posts record AI-driven profits.

Micron reached a pay and bonus deal with the Taichung union on 22 October, but most Taoyuan members are dissatisfied.

The Taoyuan union, registered separately with 2,300 members, can still strike.
