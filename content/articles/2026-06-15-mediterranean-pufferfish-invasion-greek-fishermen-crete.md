Ierapetra — Red Sea pufferfish now swamp Cretan fishing nets.

Red Sea fish enter the Mediterranean via the Suez Canal — Lessepsian migration — and warming seas have expanded [Greece](country:GR)'s exposure.

Fishermen in Ierapetra cannot sell the catch; pufferfish carry tetrodotoxin lethal to humans.
