Zurich — Amazon fire ozone erases carbon equal to 24% of fire emissions.

Ozone damage in the 2023/24 drought nearly doubled the previous decade's average, lead author Flossie Brown said.

Fire pollutants react in sunlight to form ground-level ozone, which damages leaves, a 1997-2024 study of observations and vegetation modelling found.

Researchers warn this year's strengthening El Niño could repeat the record damage seen in 2024.
