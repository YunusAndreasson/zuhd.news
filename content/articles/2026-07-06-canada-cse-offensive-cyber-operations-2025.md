Ottawa — A spy agency named its 2025 hacking targets, for once.

[Canada](country:CA)'s Communications Security Establishment struck a ransomware-as-a-service gang, an extremist group and drug traffickers with offensive operations.

The agency did not disclose which minister or court approved the operations.
