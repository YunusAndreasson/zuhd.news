Antofagasta — Adani will finance Codelco's Kaburkaala copper exploration in northern [Chile](country:CL).

[India](country:IN) imports copper to cover shortfalls, and October's price was 35.1% above a year earlier.

Adani's unit Kutch Copper Limited signed the deal, and financing could give its Mundra smelter long-term supply, Codelco said.

The statement gives no figure for Adani's financing or Mundra's supply.
