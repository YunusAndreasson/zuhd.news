Asaba — A fake police officer carried 292 kilograms of skunk.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s drug agency says a forged ID got him through checkpoints for years.

Operations across 8 commands seized 1.3 million opioid pills and 6,000 kilograms of cannabis, valued at N3.4 billion.

The agency's statement does not say whether any police officer helped him.
