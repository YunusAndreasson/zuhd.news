Beaverlodge — A decade of rotations left different soils behind.

Regions pushed toward annual cropping may be trading drought resilience for yield.

Perennial roots feed soil microbes year-round, while annual tillage breaks the aggregates that let rain soak in.

This early-access manuscript's soil gains may not transfer to warmer climates.
