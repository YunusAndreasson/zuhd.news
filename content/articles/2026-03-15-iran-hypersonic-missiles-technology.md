Iran fired 10 hypersonic missiles in a single wave. Hypersonic missiles travel above Mach 5 and manoeuvre in flight, making them nearly impossible for Israel's Arrow and Iron Dome systems to intercept. Iran joins the US, Russia, China, and North Korea as the only countries to have used hypersonics in combat.
