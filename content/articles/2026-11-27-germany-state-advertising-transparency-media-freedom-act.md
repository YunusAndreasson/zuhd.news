Berlin — German ministries publish advertising spending in unsortable tables.

The EU's Media Freedom Act, in force since August 2025, requires public bodies to disclose what they pay media outlets.

Each ministry publishes on its own website, some as PDFs, and none appear on the open-data portal govdata.

Whether [Germany](country:DE) adopts a single register like Austria's is doubtful, given state media regulators and Alternative for Germany influence.
