Kerch — Satellite imagery shows at least 2 hits on the rail span.

It is [Ukraine](country:UA)'s third strike on Russia's vital Crimea supply link in 4 days.

The rail span carries heavier loads than the road span, so it is harder to destroy.

Damage looks limited to 1 of 2 tracks, and trains again cross on one line.

A source in Zelensky's office says the military is still checking how badly the tracks were damaged.
