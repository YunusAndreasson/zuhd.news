Phnom Penh — A WhatsApp tip led OpenAI to ban scam-linked accounts.

The accounts, tied to [Cambodia](country:KH)-based compounds, used ChatGPT to write investment-fraud lures aimed at Indian nationals; such compounds are typically staffed by trafficked workers.

OpenAI's ban addresses the tool used, not the compounds still running the fraud.
