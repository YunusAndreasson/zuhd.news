Saudi Arabia intercepted 56 drones on Wednesday alone. Pakistani Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif flew to Riyadh for a closed meeting with Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, pledging solidarity as the kingdom faces a sustained drone campaign. Pakistan depends on Saudi remittances to stay solvent and faces US tariff hearings on April 28 — it cannot afford to anger both Riyadh and Washington.
