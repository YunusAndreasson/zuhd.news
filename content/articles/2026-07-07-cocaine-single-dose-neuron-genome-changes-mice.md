Berlin — One dose altered genomes for two weeks.

Researchers mapped genome architecture in dopamine neurons, finding structural changes that persisted at least two weeks after one exposure.

Ana Pombo calls it a "silent injury" that may prime a stronger addictive response to a second exposure.
