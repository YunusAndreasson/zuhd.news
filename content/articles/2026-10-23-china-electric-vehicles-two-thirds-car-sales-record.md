China — New-energy sales fell 17% yet took a record 67% share.

Combustion-engine sales in [China](country:CN) fell 43% in September as petrol prices rose after the Hormuz crisis.

Battery electric sales fell 8.5% and plug-in hybrids 36%, against 43% for combustion cars, China Passenger Car Association data show.

Overall passenger-car retail sales still fell 28% year-on-year, so the record share came from a shrinking market.

Brent crude stood at $111.20 a barrel on 22 October, up 1.3% in a week.
