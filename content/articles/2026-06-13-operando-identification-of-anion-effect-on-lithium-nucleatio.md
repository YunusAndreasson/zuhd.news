London — Scientists filmed how lithium dendrites grow.

Electron microscopy showed how electrolyte anions determine whether lithium grows as dangerous spikes or stable layers; specific anions suppress dendrites by regulating ion flux.

The findings let engineers design electrolytes that prevent dendrites in lithium-metal batteries.
