Abuja — 86 Nigerian troops flew to Guinea-Bissau.

The company-sized unit joins the ECOWAS mission that succeeded a force deployed after [Guinea-Bissau](country:GW)'s 2012 coup, aimed at security-sector reform.

The mission's mandate and end date are undisclosed; Guinea-Bissau's post-coup transition timetable remains open.
