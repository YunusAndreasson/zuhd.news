Washington — 60% of terminated early-career NIH grants went to women. Women are overrepresented in NIH training grants, the category most targeted in cuts, and less likely than men to hold multiple grants that could offset the loss. NIH Director Jay Bhattacharya's replacement funding strategy, launched in December, must prove it reaches the women who lost grants — or the disparity widens further.
