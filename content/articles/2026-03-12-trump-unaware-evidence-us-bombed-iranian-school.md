Trump claimed ignorance of a Tehran school bombing. An unnamed White House official told AP that US forces were "very likely" responsible for the Resalat girls' school strike, which killed 148 children on Day 1 of the war. Senate Democrats have written to Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth demanding answers; Trump said military operations will continue with "more of the same."
