Washington — $125 million buys continuous monitoring of millions.

Procurement documents show Immigration and Customs Enforcement will pay Thomson Reuters for its CLEAR database — names, Social Security numbers, ethnicity, geolocation — framed as a hunt for "voter fraud."

The five-year, $25-million-a-year deal expands ICE surveillance without new legislation.
