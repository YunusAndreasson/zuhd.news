Karachi — Rape convictions in Sindh reached 22% in 2025.

Dedicated gender-based violence courts and investigation units drove the improvement, police said, but 78% of disposed cases still ended in acquittal.

357 cases carried into 2026 unresolved, and trial delays mean most will not conclude this year, the report said.
