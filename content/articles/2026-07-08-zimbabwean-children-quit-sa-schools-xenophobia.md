Johannesburg — Thousands of Zimbabwean children are quitting school.

[South Africa](country:ZA)'s Exemption Permit committee says enforcement makes no distinction between documented and undocumented migrants amid rising unrest.

Returning students face a 3-year wait to rejoin [Zimbabwe](country:ZW)'s curriculum, the committee says.
