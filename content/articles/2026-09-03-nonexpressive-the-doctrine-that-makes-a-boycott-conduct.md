Washington — The House passed the bill in a 237-169 vote.

Universities that boycott [Israel](country:IL) commercially risk losing federal funding entirely.

The law requires annual certification that colleges treat Israeli academic ties the same as any other country's.

Committee Democrats warned it targets conduct no American university has actually undertaken.
