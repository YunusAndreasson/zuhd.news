Spacetime may be fractal at quantum scales. Heidelberg physicist Astrid Eichhorn leads the asymptotic safety program, which proposes gravity stabilizes at the Planck scale rather than requiring string theory's extra dimensions. Eichhorn's models predict specific particle mass ratios that the next generation of colliders will either confirm or rule out.
