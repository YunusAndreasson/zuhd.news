Socialists defend 25 years of Paris control Sunday. Rachida Dati, a center-right former minister, trails the incumbent Socialist in polls but has momentum from her party's national rise. The race will test whether left alliances with France Unbowed, which critics accuse of antisemitism, help or hurt the left's position ahead of France's 2027 presidential election.
