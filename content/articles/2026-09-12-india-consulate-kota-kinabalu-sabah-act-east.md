It gives New Delhi a foothold on Borneo's contested South China Sea approach.

A consulate needs no treaty or base, the cheapest way to turn summit goodwill into standing presence.

Modi and Anwar Ibrahim also discussed semiconductor cooperation, with no date set for the post's opening.
