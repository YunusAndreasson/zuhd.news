Durban — The main terminal handled 30% less cargo after a mid-August software switch.

It handles more than 60% of [South Africa](country:ZA)'s containers, the transport minister's team estimates.

A 12-hour changeover to the Navis N4 system ran on for over a month, the freight forwarders' association said.

Volumes rose 35% in the week to 13 September, reaching about 85% of target.

A presidential delegation was reported headed to Durban in August, but the government has issued no statement.
