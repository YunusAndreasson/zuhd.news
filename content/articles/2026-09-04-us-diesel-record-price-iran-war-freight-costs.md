Washington — US diesel hit $5.85 a gallon, a record.

Diesel, not petrol, moves freight and farm equipment, so the rise enters the price of food and goods.

Reduced tanker traffic through the Strait of Hormuz since the [Iran](country:IR) war began has pushed Brent above $95 a barrel.

Analysts say more of the cost reaches grocery shelves as freight contracts reprice.
