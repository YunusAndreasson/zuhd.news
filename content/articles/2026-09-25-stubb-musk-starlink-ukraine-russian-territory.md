New York — Starlink does not work for Ukraine inside Russian territory.

[Finland](country:FI)'s president, Alexander Stubb, says lifting that limit "would save lives" and has pleaded with Elon Musk.

[Ukraine](country:UA) is severely short of air defence against Russian ballistic missiles.

Kyiv argues coverage inside [Russia](country:RU) would let it strike missile launchers at their source.

SpaceX owner Musk alone decides whether the coverage map changes.
