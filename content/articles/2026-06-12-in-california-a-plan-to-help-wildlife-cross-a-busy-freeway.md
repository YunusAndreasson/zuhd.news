Agoura Hills — A 210-foot bridge over US-101 nears completion.

The [US](country:US) structure aims to reconnect mountain lion populations genetically isolated by the freeway — road fragmentation drives inbreeding and eventual local extinction.

Organisers call it the world's largest wildlife crossing; whether cougars use it will take years to verify.
