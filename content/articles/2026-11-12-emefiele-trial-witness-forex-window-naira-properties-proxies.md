Ikeja — A witness alleges $17 million reached Emefiele through two intermediaries.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s former central bank governor faces 19 charges involving $4.5 billion and 2.8 billion naira.

The Economic and Financial Crimes Commission witness alleged Emefiele made foreign exchange hard to obtain, pushing applicants into special arrangements.

Defence lawyers objected that the property evidence was irrelevant, but the judge overruled them.

The trial resumes on 13 November.
