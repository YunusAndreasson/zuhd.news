Jakarta — [Indonesia](country:ID) leads global platforms on age-ban enforcement.

Tech companies deleted 4.7 million child accounts after Indonesia banned under-16 social media, the first deletion count published by any country.

How platforms identified minors — and what data they collected in the process — remains unaddressed.
