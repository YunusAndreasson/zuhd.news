Cairo — Rasha Azab was questioned on terrorism charges over one tweet.

[Egypt](country:EG)'s security prosecution summoned her without stating charges, then freed her on LE10,000 bail.

Her tweet asked how long it takes to go from calling the Interior Ministry thugs to demanding the regime's fall.

Twelve people filed police complaints against her the day after she posted, her lawyer Ragia Omran said.

Case 8025/2026 stays open against her, with no referral to trial reported.
