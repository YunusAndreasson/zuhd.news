Microsoft's Copilot summarized emails marked confidential for weeks. A code bug bypassed sensitivity labels on drafts and sent items, letting the AI assistant process messages it should never have accessed. The European Parliament has since blocked Copilot on staff devices over concerns about confidential data reaching the cloud.
