Moscow — A Putin-Xi-Trump summit agenda is being drafted, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

It would be the first meeting of the three leaders in that format, Kremlin aide Yury Ushakov said.

Ushakov said Putin and Trump will also continue Ukraine talks at their planned bilateral at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit in Shenzhen.

TASS wrote that the trilateral meeting may be held, and carried no confirmation from Washington or Beijing.

Polymarket traders price Trump, Putin and Xi appearing together by 2027 at 94%, up 8 points in a week.
