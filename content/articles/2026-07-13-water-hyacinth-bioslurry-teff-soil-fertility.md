Addis Ababa — A lake-choking weed raised teff yields 66.6%.

Researchers digested the plant into bioslurry; a moderate dose beat untreated plots and synthetic fertilizer on [Ethiopia](country:ET)'s staple grain.

The result is early-access and unreviewed; field-scale trials beyond the test plots remain undone.
