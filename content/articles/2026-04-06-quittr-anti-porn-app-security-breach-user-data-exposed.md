Miami — Anti-porn app Quittr ignored 3 security warnings. A Firebase misconfiguration exposed users' ages, stated porn habits, and written confessions — including data self-reported by minors — for 10 months. Quittr fixed the bug only after media coverage; it had ignored 3 prior researcher warnings for 10 months.
