Mumbai — A new tax now applies to fees on India's free payment network.

The world's largest zero-fee payment rail is starting to carry a cost.

The National Payments Corporation of India says the goods-and-services tax hits only transactions above ₹2,000, under 4% of the network's volume.

Banks and fintechs have pushed for payment fees for years, and countries copying the network are watching closely.
