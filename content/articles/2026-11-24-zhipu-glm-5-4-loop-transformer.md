Beijing — Zhipu's GLM-5.4 reruns its layers instead of growing.

Zhipu says the loop adds 7.3 to 11.3 points on olympiad-math and research tests for about 10% more training compute.

Layers with shared weights repeat over several rounds, each refining the last round's hidden state, and harder questions get more rounds.

Those figures come from Zhipu's own comparison on a 30B model, not an independent test.

Zhipu will release the model weights after what it calls safety hardening.
