Dubai — 137,000 Gulf tourism jobs are forecast lost.

Most hospitality staff are migrant workers from South Asia whose visas end with the job.

Airspace risk and rerouted flights push leisure travelers away from the whole region, not just Iran.

Households in Kerala and Punjab depend on the wages those jobs send home.
