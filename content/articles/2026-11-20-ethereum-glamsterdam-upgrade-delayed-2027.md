United States — Ethereum developers estimate 15,000 programs could break.

Developers moved the launch from December to late January so companies can patch their software.

The upgrade raises the gas cost of creating a contract from 32,000 to 183,600, so old code can fail mid-transaction.

Erigon still wants the rules changed, but one workaround would break different apps, developer Marius van der Wijden warned.

Developers decide 26 November whether to hold the Hoodi test-network rehearsal planned for the week of 14 December.
