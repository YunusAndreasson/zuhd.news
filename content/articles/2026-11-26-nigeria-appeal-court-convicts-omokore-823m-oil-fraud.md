Abuja — Olajide Omokore, acquitted in 2023, now faces 7 years in prison.

A lawyer in court said judges also ordered his two Atlantic Energy companies wound up.

The Economic and Financial Crimes Commission says the companies lifted 7.5 million barrels from a state oil company's Brass leases without meeting payment obligations.

Trial judge Nnamdi Dimgba had called it a commercial arrangement gone bad, noting arbitration awarded the government $1.69 billion.

Omokore is expected to appeal to the Supreme Court, and the full judgement is not yet available.
