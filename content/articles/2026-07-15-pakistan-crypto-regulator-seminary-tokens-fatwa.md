Karachi — A fatwa is testing [Pakistan](country:PK)'s crypto ambitions.

Scholars ruled crypto purchases impermissible in June; the regulator now wants asset-backed tokens like sukuk judged separately from speculative coins.

Pakistan is building a licensing framework for stablecoins as scholars decide what Shariah recognizes as wealth.
