Brussels — UniCredit's €45 billion bid for Commerzbank won unconditional EU approval.

Competition chief Teresa Ribera warned the Commission will act if [Germany](country:DE) tries to block it.

Berlin holds about 12% of Commerzbank and demands job guarantees, a Frankfurt headquarters and two supervisory board seats.

Andrea Orcel will call a Commerzbank shareholder vote on a new supervisory board once he gains control.
