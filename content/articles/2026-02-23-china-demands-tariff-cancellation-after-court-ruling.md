6 justices struck down Trump's emergency tariffs on Thursday. Trump imposed a 15% global surcharge within hours under Section 122, which allows temporary tariffs for up to 150 days. China's Commerce Ministry demanded all tariffs be canceled — but the new surcharge expires in August with or without congressional action.
