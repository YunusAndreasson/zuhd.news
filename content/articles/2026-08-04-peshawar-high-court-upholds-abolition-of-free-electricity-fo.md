Peshawar — Court rejected two petitions to restore free power.

Judges ruled the free-electricity perk for [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Wapda and Discos staff was an administrative concession, not a guaranteed right.

The court ordered monetisation applied fairly; Wapda and Discos staff now get cash instead of free units.
