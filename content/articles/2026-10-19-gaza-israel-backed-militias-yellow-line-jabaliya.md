Jabaliya — Masked gunmen beat Youssef Al-Sheikh before his 8 children.

The attackers were an [Israel](country:IL)-backed Palestinian militia, Al-Sheikh says, and two of his brothers-in-law were beaten too.

Seven armed men broke down the door around 2 a.m. on 24 September, bound his hands and demanded to know where weapons were hidden.

"I'm a peaceful citizen. I don't have anything," Al-Sheikh says he told the lead gunman.

Al-Sheikh says the lights blinded him, so he cannot identify any of the seven men.
