Islamabad — Fourteen newborns died where they could not be carried out.

The dead were in incubators inside [Pakistan](country:PK)'s flagship federal hospital.

Oxygen-enriched nursery air turned an electrical fault into a fast fire, faster than staff could evacuate infants.

A fact-finding panel must report within 24 hours, after past hospital fires drew similar inquiries.
