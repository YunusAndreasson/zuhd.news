Atyrau — [Kazakhstan](country:KZ)'s Tengiz oilfield displaced Karaton village 30 miles.

Hydrocarbons fund nearly half Kazakhstan's national budget; Tengiz workers describe respiratory illness and towns relocated to escape the extraction zone.

Western joint ventures operate both fields; no government investigation into the health complaints has been announced.
