Dublin — A decade of exile in Dubai just ended in an Irish government jet.

Daniel Kinahan ran a boxing company from a city with no Irish extradition treaty.

Promotional fees and broadcast rights moved his cartel's money as ordinary sport business, evading bank-level scrutiny.

He faces an organised-crime trial after the United Arab Emirates rejected his extradition appeal last month.
