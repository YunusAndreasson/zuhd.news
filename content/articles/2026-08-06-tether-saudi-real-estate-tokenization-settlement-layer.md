Riyadh — A stablecoin issuer just entered property settlement.

Tether's Hadron platform will tokenize institutional real estate in [Saudi Arabia](country:SA), with local partner First Data issuing tokens and BKN301 wiring in bank compliance systems.

The plan could later expand into energy and infrastructure finance, putting an offshore issuer inside the settlement path.
