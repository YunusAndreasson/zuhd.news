New Delhi — Concrete sprawl traps heat across [India](country:IN)'s cities.

Vanishing green cover amplifies urban temperatures; informal and outdoor workers who cannot retreat indoors absorb the highest heat exposure.

Delhi is a preview of what unplanned urbanization delivers to fast-growing Asian cities facing warmer summers.
