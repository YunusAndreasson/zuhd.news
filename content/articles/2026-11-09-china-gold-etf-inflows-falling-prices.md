Beijing — Chinese gold ETFs drew $784 million in October.

The People's Bank of China added 830,000 ounces in October, its 24th straight month of buying.

World Gold Council data ranks those inflows second globally, behind the [UK](country:GB), as falling bond yields lifted gold's appeal.

Jewellery demand fell 22% year on year in the third quarter, the council said.

Goldman Sachs cut its year-end gold forecast to $4,150, citing an expected US rate rise next month.
