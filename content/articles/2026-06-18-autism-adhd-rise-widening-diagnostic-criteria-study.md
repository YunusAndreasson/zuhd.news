Copenhagen — Rising autism and attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder diagnoses reflect broader criteria, a 140,000-person Danish study found.

Recent cases carry lower but above-average genetic risk scores, consistent with widened thresholds, researchers said.

Non-genetic drivers — environment, culture, reduced stigma — remain unquantified.
