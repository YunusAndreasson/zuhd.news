Punjab deployed its first anti-drone gun in Rawalpindi. Police recommended the systems specifically to protect Chinese nationals working on infrastructure projects, revealing how Pakistan's security priorities track Beijing's investments. Punjab plans to expand anti-drone coverage as Chinese nationals across the province remain targets of militant groups.
