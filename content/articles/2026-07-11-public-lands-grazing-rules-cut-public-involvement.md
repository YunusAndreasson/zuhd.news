Washington — 155 million acres of public land, fewer public objections.

The [US](country:US) Bureau of Land Management's first grazing rewrite since 1995 narrows who counts as "interested public" and drops comment steps ranchers' permits once required.

The rules return to the agency in mid-July after public comment; tribes fear lost bison-grazing permits.
