Manila — 22.6% of Filipino families went involuntarily hungry in September.

Hunger rose from 20.3% in June, above the 21.1% average of 2020's pandemic.

Inflation hit a 3-year high of 7.2%, with rice up 20.3%, the [Philippine](country:PH) Statistics Authority said.

Hunger fell in the Visayas to 16%, but reached 27% in Metro Manila.

Severe hunger, meaning often or always, rose from 4.5% to 6.4%.
