Tehran — Iran's Strait Authority updated its non-compliant vessel list Monday.

Without cover from a warned insurer, a tanker can't dock, get financed, or be chartered.

[Iran](country:IR) is enforcing the warning through insurers rather than patrol boats, reaching further into trade than any warship.

No insurer or protection-and-indemnity club has yet said publicly whether it will comply.
