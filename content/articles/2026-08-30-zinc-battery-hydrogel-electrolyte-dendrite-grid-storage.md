London — Zinc batteries fail when metal grows sharp dendrites.

Zinc is cheap, safe and needs no cobalt, unlike lithium packs.

A hydrogel electrolyte's polymer network restricts how zinc ions move, spreading deposits evenly instead of at one point.

Researchers tested the best formula only in lab coin cells, not full battery packs.
