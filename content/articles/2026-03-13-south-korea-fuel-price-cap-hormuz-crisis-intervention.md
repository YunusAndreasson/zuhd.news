South Korea imposed fuel price controls for the first time. The measure caps gasoline at 1,724 won per litre — low enough to absorb the Hormuz crisis's price spike — and readjusts every 2 weeks. Refiners are resisting the cap as inspectors report 20 hoarding violations already.
