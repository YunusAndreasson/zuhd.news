Moscow — Russia-Africa summits will recur, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

Putin said every leader at the third summit backed holding them regularly.

He told a news conference that the Sahara, Sahel and northern, central and southern Africa face threats needing collective solutions.

TASS gave no date or host for a fourth summit.
