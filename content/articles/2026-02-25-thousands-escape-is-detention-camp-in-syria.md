Al-Hol camp held 23,500 people; fewer than 1,500 remain. Kurdish guards withdrew in January to fight Turkey-backed groups; inspectors found 138 holes cut through the 17-kilometre perimeter wall. Syria confirmed the mass escape but cannot track thousands of ISIS-linked women and children now scattered across the region.
