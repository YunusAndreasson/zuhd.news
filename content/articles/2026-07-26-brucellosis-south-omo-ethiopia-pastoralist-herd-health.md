Jinka — 8% of surveyed cattle carry brucellosis.

In [Ethiopia](country:ET)'s South Omo zone, the bacteria spread through raw milk and birthing fluids, causing fevers routinely misdiagnosed as malaria.

No vaccination program exists yet; researchers are calling for pastoralist education first.
