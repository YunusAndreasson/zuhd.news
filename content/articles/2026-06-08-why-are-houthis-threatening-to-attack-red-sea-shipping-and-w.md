Sana'a — [Yemen](country:YE)'s Houthis threatened to close the Red Sea.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA) diverted more than 70% of daily crude exports to Yanbu after [Iran](country:IR) closed the Strait of Hormuz; Houthis now threaten that Red Sea corridor too.

A Houthi source called the shipping ban a first step, warning of broader targeting if [Israel](country:IL) keeps striking Iran.
