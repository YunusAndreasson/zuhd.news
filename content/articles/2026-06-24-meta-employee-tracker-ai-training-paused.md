Menlo Park — Meta tracked employee keystrokes to train AI.

Meta's Model Capability Initiative logged keystrokes, mouse clicks, and screens from corporate laptops; all staff could access the data.

A staff petition signed by 1,600 employees called for the tool's removal; Meta said it is pausing to investigate but set no deletion timeline.
