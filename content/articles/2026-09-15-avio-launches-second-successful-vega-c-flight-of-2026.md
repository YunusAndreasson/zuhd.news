Kourou — A Vega-C rocket deployed ESA's FLEX satellite.

FLEX is the first satellite built to measure whether plants are actually thriving.

It detects chlorophyll fluorescence, the faint glow plants emit while photosynthesizing, catching drought stress weeks before crops visibly fail.

Sentinel-3C also launched, eventually replacing a satellite past its seven-year design life.
