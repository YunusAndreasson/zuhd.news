AI translations injected false citations into 1,500 Wikipedia articles. The Open Knowledge Association, a Wikipedia translation nonprofit, paid contractors to copy articles into AI tools and published the output with minimal human review. Wikipedia said it will block repeat offenders, but the 1,500 corrupted articles remain live.
