Kuala Lumpur — Najib Razak may not use phones without prison approval.

[Malaysia](country:MY)'s convicted former prime minister serves his graft sentence at home after a royal pardon.

Home Minister Saifuddin Nasution Ismail told parliament any breach returns Najib immediately to Kajang Prison, and visitors need written permission.

Najib also faces a 15-year sentence in a second 1MDB case, which he is appealing.

Saifuddin rejected a second residence but is weighing Najib's other requests.
