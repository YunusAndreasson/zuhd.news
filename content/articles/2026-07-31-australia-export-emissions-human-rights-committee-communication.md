Canberra — A UN complaint targets exported, not domestic, emissions.

[Australia](country:AU)'s coal and gas, burned abroad, sit on no nation's climate ledger; the case argues the exporting state still bears responsibility for the harm.

The Human Rights Committee's findings aren't binding, but courts and financiers increasingly cite them.
