Islamabad — Khyber Pakhtunkhwa meets 57% of disclosure requirements, election watchdog Fafen found.

The watchdog audited 190 public bodies; the province's Information Commission cannot enforce deadlines or issue binding orders.

Fafen called on the provincial assembly to grant the commission binding powers and establish a transparency fund.
