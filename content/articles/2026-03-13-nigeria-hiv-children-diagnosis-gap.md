Nigeria's children with HIV are dying from preventable complications. Nigeria, home to the world's 2nd-highest HIV burden, built adult treatment without a parallel paediatric supply chain or diagnostic network. An international HIV research consortium, IMPAACT4HIV, has called on Nigeria's government to fund dedicated paediatric programmes before more children die from treatable disease.
