Puerto Jiménez — Cocaine money is funding illegal mines inside Corcovado. Rangers are outgunned: 46.5 tons of cocaine passed through Costa Rica in 2025, with drug profits laundered through the same jungle mines. Park rangers lack resources and operational support; drug-backed miners outpace what an 8-person patrol can reach.
