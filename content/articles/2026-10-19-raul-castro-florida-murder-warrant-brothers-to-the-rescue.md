Miami — Raúl Castro, 95, now faces a second murder case.

Florida's attorney general says the warrant could limit his travel to countries with [US](country:US) extradition agreements.

Florida claims jurisdiction because spies allegedly passed Brothers to the Rescue flight data to [Cuba](country:CU) from Miami and Key West.

Cuba says the planes entered its airspace, but the International Civil Aviation Organization found they were over international waters.

Trump says there will be no escalation, and Castro's appearance in a US courtroom is uncertain.
