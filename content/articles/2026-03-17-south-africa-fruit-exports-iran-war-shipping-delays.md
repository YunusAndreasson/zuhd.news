South Africa's fruit harvest has hit a closed shipping lane. $4 billion in annual fruit exports pass through shipping routes now blocked by fighting near the Strait of Hormuz and the Red Sea. Citrus and grapes are perishable with no strategic reserve — every shipping day lost is harvest that spoils unsold.
