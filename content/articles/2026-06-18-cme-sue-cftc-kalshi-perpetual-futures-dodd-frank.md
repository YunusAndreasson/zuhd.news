Chicago — The Chicago Mercantile Exchange will sue the [US](country:US) derivatives regulator over Kalshi's perpetual futures approval, CEO Duffy said.

Kalshi's product meets Dodd-Frank's swap definition — stricter requirements Kalshi didn't meet, Duffy argues.

The exchange won't list perpetual futures until courts rule.
