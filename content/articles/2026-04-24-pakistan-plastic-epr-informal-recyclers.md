Karachi — [Pakistan](country:PK) is drafting an Extended Producer Responsibility plastic law that would charge companies for disposal. The draft excludes 200,000–333,000 informal waste pickers who handle 40% of urban plastic. Experts say excluding them makes the scheme unworkable; no legislation date is set.
