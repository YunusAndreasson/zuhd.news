Port Moresby — [Papua New Guinea](country:PG), [Fiji](country:FJ), and [Vanuatu](country:VU) agreed to connect and expand their marine protected areas to 6 million km².

The nations said reserves must match ecosystem scale; fish stocks and ocean currents ignore national borders.

Fiji has pledged 15% of its waters this year; no external funding source has been announced.
