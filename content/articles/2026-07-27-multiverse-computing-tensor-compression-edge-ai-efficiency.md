San Sebastián — Multiverse targets $570 million to shrink AI models.

Its CompactifAI method, tensor networks borrowed from quantum physics, cuts model size 80-95% and runs on ordinary hardware, the company says.

Qatar Development Bank and other sovereign funds are among the backers; the round remains open, not closed.
