Beijing — China's grid curtails 9% of solar output. Grid operators commit $146bn annually to new energy connections while Beijing simultaneously expands petrochemicals, locking in fossil demand through 2030. Beijing's climate envoy cites the Iran war as proof of transition urgency; the petrochemical plan contradicts that argument.
