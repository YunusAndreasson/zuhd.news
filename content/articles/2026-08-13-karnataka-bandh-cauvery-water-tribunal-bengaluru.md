Bengaluru — A tribunal order over river water shut the city.

Daily-wage workers and transport operators lose a day, though core services stay open.

A regulator ordered Karnataka to release 12,000 cusecs to Tamil Nadu, reviving a quota fixed in wetter decades.

[India](country:IN)'s Supreme Court now holds Tamil Nadu's petition, with the Mekedatu reservoir the next flashpoint.
