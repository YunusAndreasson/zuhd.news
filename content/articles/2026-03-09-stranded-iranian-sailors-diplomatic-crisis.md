Sri Lanka must hold 219 Iranian sailors until the war ends. The 3 vessels were disabled or sunk by US-Israeli strikes; the 1907 Hague Convention on naval warfare requires neutral ports to intern surviving crews until hostilities cease. Washington publicly respects both countries' sovereignty while media report US pressure against repatriation of 183 more sailors held at India's Kochi port.
