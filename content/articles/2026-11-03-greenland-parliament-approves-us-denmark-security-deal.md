Greenland — Parliament passed the US security deal 16-0, with 7 abstentions.

The deal paves the way for more [US](country:US) bases on an island Trump threatened to seize.

It lets the US expand its operations at Pituffik Space Base, and it still applies if Greenland chooses independence.

Opinion polls show most of Greenland's 57,000 people reject US control.

The deal takes effect only after [Denmark](country:DK) ratifies it, and Danish lawmakers debate it later this month.
