Malta — A boat from [Libya](country:LY) capsized 45 miles off Malta; 10 died, 48 were rescued.

The EU has paid Libya €700 million since 2015 to block crossings; the International Organization for Migration logged 827 deaths on the route this year.

EU members backed "return hubs" outside the bloc this week; no country has volunteered to host them.
