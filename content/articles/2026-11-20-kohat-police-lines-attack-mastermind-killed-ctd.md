Peshawar — Police say Abdul Hai, the Kohat attack planner, is dead.

The September Police Lines bombing killed 21, including 15 police officers.

The Counter Terrorism Department says the banned Ittehadul Mujahideen Pakistan fired first at a Bazeed Khel Road checkpoint, and 3 militants died.

The department has not identified the third man killed, and some associates escaped.

Searches for the escaped men continue in [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province.
