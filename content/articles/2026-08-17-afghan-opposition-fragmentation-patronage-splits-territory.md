Kabul — Five years on, no faction leads the opposition.

Analysts say the divided opposition poses no threat to [Afghanistan](country:AF)'s Taliban rule.

Opponents have split into rival fronts and exile councils since the Taliban's return, the Lowy Institute said.

The Taliban's opponents have not named a single figure to unite behind.
