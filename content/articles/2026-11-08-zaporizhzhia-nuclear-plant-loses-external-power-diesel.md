Zaporizhzhia — Diesel generators now power Europe's largest nuclear plant.

It is the plant's 16th complete blackout of 2026, Energoatom said.

Its last working 330 kV line, Ferrosplavna-1, tripped at 02:36 after damage, and automatic reconnection failed.

Energoatom says [Russia](country:RU) is deliberately disabling the lines, while the plant's own report names no cause.

Energoatom warns fuel cooling is at risk if the generators fail or run out of fuel.
