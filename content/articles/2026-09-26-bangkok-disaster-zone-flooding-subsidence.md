Bangkok — Governor Chadchart Sittipunt says almost 300mm of rain fell since Thursday.

The disaster declaration covers all 50 districts, and about 900 people sit in more than 200 shelters.

Bangkok rests 1.5 metres above sea level on former marshland, and every canal is full, the governor said.

"I think this time is worse than the 2011 flood," said Somkid Pheuk-ngam, a 67-year-old canal-side vendor.

[Thailand](country:TH)'s meteorological department expects rain through Sunday, and draining needs 2 or 3 dry days.
