London — A 25-protein signature tracks cellular aging.

Researchers treated skin cells with blood serum across the adult lifespan and found mitochondrial energy output fell as the donor's biological age rose, the study reports.

The signature, called MitoAge, is an unedited early-access finding pending peer review.
