New Delhi — Child traffickers now face India's toughest sexual-offence law.

[India](country:IN)'s Supreme Court ruled the Protection of Children from Sexual Offenses Act applies when a trafficking victim is a child, making consent irrelevant.

The bench also issued rehabilitation directives; implementation falls to state governments.
