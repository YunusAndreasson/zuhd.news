Hundreds of thousands cannot pray at Al-Aqsa this Ramadan. Israeli authorities sealed the mosque for a 3rd consecutive day on Monday, the first such Ramadan closure since Israel occupied East Jerusalem in 1967. Over 1,000 Jerusalem residents have been banned from the compound, with no timeline for reopening.
