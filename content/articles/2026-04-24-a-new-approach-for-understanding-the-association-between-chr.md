London — 2 long non-coding ribonucleic acids mediate how childhood stress converts to schizophrenic psychosis, a 400-person study found.

NONHSAT089447 and NONHSAT021545 accounted for 54.8% and 41.5% of the stress-to-symptom pathway, measurable in peripheral blood.

Researchers say the markers could enable screening before psychosis onset.
