Islamabad — A margin rise ended [Pakistan](country:PK)'s fuel strike.

Dealers had threatened to shut every pump nationwide starting Saturday.

The cabinet's Economic Coordination Committee raised the margin 15.5%, to Rs9.98 a litre, far below dealers' Rs26 demand.

The new margin takes effect September 1; oil marketing companies' margin stays unchanged at Rs7.87.
