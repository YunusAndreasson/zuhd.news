Pyongyang — Xi Jinping landed in Pyongyang on Monday.

Xi's first visit in 7 years aims to rebuild influence [China](country:CN) lost as [North Korea](country:KP) deepened ties with Russia, analysts said.

Xi will offer food aid and tourism deals without pressing Kim Jong Un on denuclearization, officials said.
