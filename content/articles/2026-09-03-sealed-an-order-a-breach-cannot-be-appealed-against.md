Toronto — Hackers breached Thomson Reuters' court-records platform.

Sealed filings protect minors and witnesses, and exposure can't be undone.

The platform serves courts in a dozen US states, the US Virgin Islands and Canada, sharing one point of failure.

Thomson Reuters has not disclosed how many sealed cases were exposed.
