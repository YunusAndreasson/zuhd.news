Pecém — ByteDance is weighing a second, larger data center phase.

People familiar with the matter say the first 300 megawatts are under construction at the port in [Brazil](country:BR)'s Ceará state.

The port project can qualify for tax incentives by serving customers abroad.

ByteDance has held only preliminary talks on a separate facility outside the port for Brazilian customers.
