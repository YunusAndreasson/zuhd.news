Ghana is cutting electricity bills 4.81% on April 1. Ghana's public utilities regulator cited improved generation and currency stability as global energy costs rise above $100 a barrel from the Hormuz closure. The next quarterly tariff review is in July, when Hormuz-driven fuel costs may erase the reduction.
