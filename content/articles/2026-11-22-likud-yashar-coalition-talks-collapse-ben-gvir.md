As national security minister, Ben-Gvir commands police across [Israel](country:IL).

Gadi Eisenkot's bloc holds 48 seats, 13 short of 61, so any government needs Likud, ultra-Orthodox or Arab parties.

Eisenkot's mandate expires 6 December, extendable 14 days, and Yashar plans a minority confidence vote.
