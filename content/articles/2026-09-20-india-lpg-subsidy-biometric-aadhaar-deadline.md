New Delhi — 27.43 crore LPG users face October 1 deadline.

Consumers who skip biometric Aadhaar authentication lose [India](country:IN)'s subsidised refill price.

The ministry says 89.9% of consumers finished it, aiming at duplicate connections and commercial diversion.

Non-compliers pay the market price, against an implicit subsidy of ₹210 per cylinder in September.
