Moscow — Russia launched 5G that no iPhone can join.

Apple alone decides whether Russia's iPhone users ever get access, the ministry said.

With Ericsson and Nokia gone under sanctions, [Russia](country:RU) built the network on frequencies allocated free to operators at home.

Android access is coming "in the near future," the ministry said, with no date set for iPhones.
