New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s top court left Lalu Yadav free.

He was convicted over a ₹89 lakh Deoghar treasury fraud from the decades-old Bihar fodder scam and sentenced to a three-year term.

Jharkhand's High Court has six months to resolve appeals still pending since 2019.
