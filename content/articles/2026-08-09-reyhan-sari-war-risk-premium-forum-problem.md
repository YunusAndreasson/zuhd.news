Istanbul — A drone killed one crewman on a coal-hauling tanker.

Every strike on a neutral hull raises Black Sea war-risk premiums Turkish shipowners pay.

MV Reyhan Sari's owner is filing at the International Criminal Court, which prosecutes individuals, not companies, over the July 22 attack, TASS reported.

Turkey has proposed a Black Sea combat moratorium; no party has agreed to one.
