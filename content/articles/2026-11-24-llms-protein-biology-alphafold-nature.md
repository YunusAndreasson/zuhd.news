San Francisco — In one test, Claude beat specialist protein models.

Some scientists predict general-purpose models will eventually outperform specialist tools like AlphaFold.

OpenAI's model designed binders for 5 targets, and lab tests confirmed about 11% of designs bound their target.

Some researchers say the models may be regurgitating training data, and OpenAI's was far off on 3D structure.

OpenAI researcher Masatoshi Uehara predicts the models will beat every specialist model at every capability.
