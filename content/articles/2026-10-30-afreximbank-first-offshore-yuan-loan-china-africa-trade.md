Hong Kong — A $1 billion, 3-year loan may carry Afreximbank's first yuan tranche.

Africa's trade bank would borrow in yuan as trade with [China](country:CN) grows, people familiar say.

Hong Kong holds the largest offshore yuan pool, with 1.3 trillion yuan in deposits on 31 July.

Nothing is signed, and the yuan tranche itself remains undecided.

Yuan-denominated Stock Connect counters in Hong Kong are due to launch by July 2027.
