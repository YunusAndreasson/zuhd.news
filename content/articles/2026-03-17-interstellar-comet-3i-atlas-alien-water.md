Comet 3I/ATLAS carries water unlike anything in Earth's oceans. Astronomers measured deuterium levels 30 to 40 times higher than Earth's — the first time anyone has sampled water chemistry from an interstellar visitor. It is only the 3rd interstellar object ever detected, leaving scientists a narrow window before it exits the solar system.
