Dublin — Some Accenture staff spent $1,000 a month on Claude.

Since 1 October, Accenture funds only $29 a month, and workers who go over must find a project to pay.

Costs are consumption-based, and Premium plans include $250 of token usage a week, so heavy General-plan users bear the change.

Accenture's internal notice says licences are reviewed periodically and may be reallocated.
