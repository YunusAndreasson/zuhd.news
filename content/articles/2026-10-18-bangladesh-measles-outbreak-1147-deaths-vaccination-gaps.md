Dhaka — Measles has killed 1,147 people in [Bangladesh](country:BD) since March.

More than 202,000 have been infected, in a country that was a vaccination success story in 2024.

First-dose coverage fell to 90% and second-dose to 86% in 2025, below the 95% WHO says prevents outbreaks.

A separate June campaign targeted 4 million more children than the emergency measles drive, which the government said covered all.

Another campaign launched in late September targets 3.4 million children the government says were missed.
