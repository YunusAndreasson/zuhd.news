Karachi — Sindh cut official vehicle fuel allowances by 60% as the US-Israeli war on Iran disrupts global oil supply. The province is now weighing a "smart lockdown" to restrict mobility further; minister Nasir Hussain Shah warned of "terrifying" effects if the war continues. Sindh's chief minister chairs a daily austerity committee — the same tool used during the pandemic, now repurposed for wartime energy rationing.
