Manila — Philippines climate plans bypass Indigenous consent.

[The Philippines](country:PH) is finalizing a climate submission that excludes Indigenous communities, whose forests and watersheds the Paris Agreement targets depend on.

The Climate Change Commission says the updated plan is in final review; no submission date has been set.
