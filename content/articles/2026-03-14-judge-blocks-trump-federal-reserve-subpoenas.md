A judge quashed subpoenas targeting Federal Reserve Chair Powell. The judge found "essentially zero evidence" of any crime and wrote the subpoenas were designed to pressure Powell into cutting rates or resigning. US Attorney Jeanine Pirro called the ruling "outrageous" and vowed to appeal.
