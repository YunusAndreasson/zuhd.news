Beijing — China and Oman signed 14 memorandums in one visit.

[Oman](country:OM) gains weight as Gulf states seek alternative energy export routes during the Iran war.

The memorandums cover BeiDou, [China](country:CN)'s alternative to the US-run GPS, central bank digital currencies, space technology and supply chains.

Natixis economist Alicia Garcia-Herrero says the gains for China are incremental.

China's top economic planner says the two sides will set up a regular mechanism to advance BeiDou use.
