Madrid — Global sea surface temperature hit 20.96C on June 21.

Warmer oceans hold less oxygen and fuel stronger storms; June's heat killed over 2,000 people across [Spain](country:ES) and France.

The marine heatwave shows no sign of easing as the Northern Hemisphere summer begins.
