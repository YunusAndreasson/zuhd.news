Johannesburg — Rioters torched 24 cars in Durban and Soweto.

[South Africa](country:ZA)'s withdrawn directive implemented a Constitutional Court ruling barring rejection of asylum seekers on procedural grounds alone.

Protesters looted foreign-owned shops, and spokesperson William Baloyi blamed deliberately false claims that the ruling grants refugee status automatically.

The court ruling stands, and the Inter-Ministerial Committee on Migration says every asylum office is overwhelmed.
