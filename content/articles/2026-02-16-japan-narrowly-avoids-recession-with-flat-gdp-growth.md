Japan's economy grew just 0.1% last quarter. The annualized rate of 0.2% reversed a prior contraction but fell far short of the 1.6% economists expected, with private consumption providing the only lift against weak exports and flat public spending. The underwhelming rebound pressures the Bank of Japan to delay further interest rate increases as the world's 4th-largest economy stalls.
