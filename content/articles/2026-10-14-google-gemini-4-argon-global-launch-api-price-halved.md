Mountain View — Gemini 4 Argon now costs $2 per million input tokens.

Output costs $10 per million tokens, and cached input is 95% cheaper.

Google's demos show Argon calling tools, checking its own output and revising, including 1,000 render loops for a Pixel animation.

Google says Argon ranks first on the Vals Index of finance, coding, legal and tax tasks.

The half-price launch rate ends 30 November, when developers see what Argon really costs.
