New Delhi — Refusing to sing now risks 3 years' jail.

[India](country:IN)'s upper house passed the Bill by voice vote after an opposition walkout, placing the song beside the anthem under 1971's honour law.

Muslim and Sikh scholars have objected to the song's address to a mother-goddess since the 1930s.
