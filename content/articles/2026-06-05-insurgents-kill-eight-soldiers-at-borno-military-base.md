Maiduguri — 8 soldiers died in an overnight raid on a [Nigeria](country:NG) army base in Borno.

The Islamic State West Africa Province and Boko Haram both operate along the Mandara-Burtai axis, where the attack occurred.

The army issued no statement; attribution remains contested between the two groups.
