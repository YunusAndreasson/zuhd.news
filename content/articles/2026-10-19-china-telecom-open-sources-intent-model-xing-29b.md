China — A state-owned telecom released a model that fills in command parameters.

It published the weights, training corpus and inference toolkit together, not the model alone.

One model classifies a spoken request and extracts values such as room and brightness into structured data, with latency under 150 milliseconds, the lab says.

The lab's own benchmark puts its zero-shot Micro-F1 at 70.27, above GPT-4.1.

Zhidx's 11 tests through the lab's interface are the only outside check reported.
