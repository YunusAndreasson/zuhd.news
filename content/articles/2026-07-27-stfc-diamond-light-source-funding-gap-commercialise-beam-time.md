Harwell — A £42 million funding gap, council figures show, threatens Diamond Light Source.

Peer-reviewed beam time, open to any qualifying researcher, could shift toward paying industrial clients as the facilities council cuts staff and hours.

Sites have three years of bridge funding to find savings or commercial income before deeper cuts land.
