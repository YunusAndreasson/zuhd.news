London — 4 antenna ports cut mutual interference below -17.5dB.

The board covers 5G and Wi-Fi 6E in a compact form factor, solving the key interference challenge for multi-antenna consumer devices.

Researchers reported 80-96% radiation efficiency and full regulatory safety compliance, supporting commercial handset integration.
