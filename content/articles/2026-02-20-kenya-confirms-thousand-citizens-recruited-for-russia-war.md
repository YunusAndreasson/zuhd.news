Over 1,000 Kenyans were lured to fight for Russia in Ukraine. An intelligence report found rogue immigration staff, airport officials, and the Russian embassy in Nairobi coordinated a trafficking ring promising recruits $2,700 a month. 89 are on the front line, 28 are missing, and families are demanding their return.
