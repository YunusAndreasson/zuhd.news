Gothenburg — Pro-Russian hackers targeted a Swedish power plant. A Swedish defense official confirmed the group attempted to disrupt a thermal plant in western Sweden last year. Whether the attack succeeded and who carried it out remain unconfirmed; no group has been publicly named.
