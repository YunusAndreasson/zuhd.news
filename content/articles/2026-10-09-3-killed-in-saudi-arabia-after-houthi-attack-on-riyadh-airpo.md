Riyadh — Lufthansa and Air India stopped flying to Riyadh.

Cirium data show almost half of departures were cancelled by 1500 GMT Thursday.

Three briefed people say interception shrapnel burned a parked Saudia plane, while the Houthis claim a ballistic missile hit, without evidence.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA) said 3 people died in Wednesday's strikes on Riyadh and Abha airports.

The Houthis have warned workers to stay away from Saudi energy plants, which they say they will target.
