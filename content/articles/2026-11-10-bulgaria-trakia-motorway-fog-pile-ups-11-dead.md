Yambol — 11 dead across two fog pile-ups, dozens injured.

[Bulgaria](country:BG)'s roads are the EU's deadliest, at 71 deaths per million people in 2025, Commission figures show.

Visibility fell from 70-80 metres to about 10 within minutes, the interior minister said, and officials said drivers were going too fast.

Investigators start once rescue ends, and the government wants Wednesday as a day of national mourning.
