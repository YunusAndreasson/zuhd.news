Pakistan failed to unlock $1.2 billion from the IMF. The fund's third review of Pakistan's $7 billion bailout stalled after 14 days, with Hormuz-driven energy costs upending the program's assumptions. Talks continue in coming days, with the next $1 billion tranche contingent on a deal.
