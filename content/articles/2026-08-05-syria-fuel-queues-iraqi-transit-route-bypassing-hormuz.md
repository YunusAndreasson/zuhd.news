Aleppo — Gas stations sit empty as 5,000 tankers pass daily.

[Iraq](country:IQ) rerouted oil through Syria to the Mediterranean after the Strait of Hormuz closure, but the convoys move under armed escort without stopping at a single Syrian pump.

The Energy Ministry has not said when refinery gasoline will resume reaching stations.
