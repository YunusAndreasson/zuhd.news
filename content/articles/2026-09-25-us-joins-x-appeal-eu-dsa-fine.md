Brussels — [US](country:US) Justice Department joins X's appeal of €120 million fine.

It is the first Digital Services Act enforcement challenge to reach the General Court.

The commission fined X for a deceptive blue-tick system and breaches of transparency, advertising and data access rules.

The commission insists the act is non-discriminatory and applies equally to all large platforms operating in Europe.

Trump has threatened new tariffs on European goods unless the EU rolls back its penalties.
