Lagos — United Bank for Africa skipped its arraignment for the 2nd time.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s Economic and Financial Crimes Commission has charged the bank and 4 others with N4.29bn in forex racketeering; the bank's lawyer denied receiving court documents, while prosecutors said its compliance officer did.

The court adjourned to June 16.
