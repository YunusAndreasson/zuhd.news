Moscow — OPEC+ may roll current output quotas into January, two delegates say.

The alliance meets online Sunday on 2027 targets, citing uncertainty such as the [Iran](country:IR) war.

Novak, [Russia](country:RU)'s deputy prime minister, said the review of members' maximum capacity, which sets 2027 baselines, will finish by year-end.

Four people familiar with the matter said [Iraq](country:IQ) is pressing OPEC+ to raise its capacity estimate.

Hormuz traffic runs 25% below normal as of 22 November, so Sunday's decision lands on a strained market.
