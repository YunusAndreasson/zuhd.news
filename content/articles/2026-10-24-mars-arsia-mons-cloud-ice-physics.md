Paris — Arsia Mons' cloud grows 1,000 miles each morning, then vanishes.

Researchers say the model needs physics textbooks treat as theory, never seen in nature.

Vapour turns straight to ice without dust after a 30°C drop in 10 minutes, as wind hits the 65,000-foot volcano.

No measurement yet confirms the humidity the model needs, 100,000 times any value measured on Earth.
