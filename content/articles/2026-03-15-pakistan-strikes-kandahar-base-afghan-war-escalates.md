Pakistan bombed Kandahar after Taliban drones hit Pakistani cities. Islamabad says the base trained Pakistani Taliban insurgents behind the attack — though its own strikes on Kabul killed 4 civilians. Afghan officials promised a measured response but named no terms for de-escalation as fighting enters its 3rd week.
