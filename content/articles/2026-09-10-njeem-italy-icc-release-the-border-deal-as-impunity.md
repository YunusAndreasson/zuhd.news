Tripoli — An Italian state plane returned Gen. Osama Almasri Njeem to the airport where his prison sits.

An ICC member state let a war-crimes suspect walk free, weakening the court's only enforcement tool.

Arrested in Turin on an ICC warrant over torture and rape at Mitiga prison, he was freed after Italy's justice ministry stayed silent for days.

[Italy](country:IT)'s Tribunal of Ministers found 3 ministers deliberately aided his escape, but parliament blocked the case in October.
