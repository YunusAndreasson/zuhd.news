Beijing — [China](country:CN) controls most global EV battery-recycling capacity.

China mandates shredding old packs to reclaim cobalt and lithium; the [US](country:US) plans to redeploy them as grid storage.

The US grid-storage approach means this battery cohort's cobalt and lithium won't be recovered domestically.
