Mumbai — Scammers in [India](country:IN) operate through the same apps used to bring people online.

Scammers use Google, Facebook and WhatsApp's brand trust as cover, targeting users onboarded to the internet without fraud literacy.

Google, Facebook and WhatsApp have not published fraud-removal rates for India's first-time internet users.
