Barcelona — Human proteins break down slower than mouse proteins.

It offers the first molecular explanation for why human embryos develop slower.

Researchers compared degradation rates across 4,000 shared proteins and found a consistent, species-wide offset, not scattered outliers.

Whether the decay rate can be tuned would decide if it helps grow human tissue faster.
