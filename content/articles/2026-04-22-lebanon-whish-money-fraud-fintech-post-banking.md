Beirut — Each Whish Money takeover is a total wipeout for [Lebanese](country:LB) households. After the banking collapse, Whish holds savings and remittances with no substitute, digital rights group SMEX documents, making fraud rings' impersonation attacks catastrophic. The platform has no recourse mechanism and has not announced anti-fraud measures.
