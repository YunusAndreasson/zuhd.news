Jakarta — One president's imprint now runs the economy.

Delivery is lagging the ambition, the Lowy Institute finds, across [Indonesia](country:ID)'s 280 million people.

President Prabowo Subianto has centralized economic direction from rice policy to free school meals to cooperatives.

Thousands of local units without procurement capacity must still carry out the rollout.
