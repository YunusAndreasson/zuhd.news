Berlin — [Germany](country:DE) recorded more than 280,000 domestic violence victims in 2025.

Recorded cases rose almost 3% while violent crime in general fell.

Police figures show 142,800 women suffered partner violence, more than 16 an hour, family minister Karin Prien said.

Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt blamed a "supposed dominance of the male sex over the female."

Parliament will soon receive a draft law treating gender-based killings of women as murder.
