London — Termite-nest geometry now guides 3D-printed earthen construction. An alginate binder — found by analyzing 90% of global subsoil minerals — raises print speed 33% and structural stability 10°, no cement required. Cement generates roughly 8% of global CO2; this method replaces it with local soil and biomineral binders.
