Quetta — Three districts lost their local election Sunday.

Khuzdar, Kalat and Hub residents get no elected council deciding water and sanitation.

The Election Commission cited law-and-order conditions, while Zhob, Loralai and Sibi held polls the same day.

No date has been set for Hub, Khuzdar or Kalat to vote again.
