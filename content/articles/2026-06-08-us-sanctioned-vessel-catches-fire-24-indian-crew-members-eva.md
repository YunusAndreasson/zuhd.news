Muscat — 24 sailors rescued from a burning blockade-runner off Oman.

The US Treasury-sanctioned MT Marivex made 4 runs at [US](country:US) naval blockade lines before being disabled; [India](country:IN)'s shipping ministry confirmed all 24 nationals evacuated by Omani authorities.

US Central Command has not commented on how the vessel was disabled.
