Kolkata — Bengal's Eid al-Adha cut to 1 day by executive order.

West Bengal joins states where [India](country:IN)'s BJP trims Muslim-festival holidays by executive notification, not legislation.

BJP holds the legislature for 5 years; the state's Eid al-Fitr 2-day holiday has not yet been revised.
