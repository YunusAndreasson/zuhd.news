Kurla — A hillside collapsed onto Gaushiya Chawl before dawn.

Two are feared dead and 6 to 8 remain trapped under debris.

Chawls sit on Mumbai's slopes because flatter land was priced out decades ago, leaving residents on unstable ground.

National Disaster Response Force and fire crews continue digging as [India](country:IN)'s monsoon rains keep falling.
