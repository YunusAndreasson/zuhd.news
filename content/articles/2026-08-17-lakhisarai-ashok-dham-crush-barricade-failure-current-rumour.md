Lakhisarai — A rumor of live current triggered the crush.

Six devotees died and nineteen more were hurt when barricades gave way.

A fallen pole carried only a cable wire, but the district magistrate said the rumor spread anyway.

[India](country:IN)'s Bihar state government offered families 400,000 rupees each in compensation.
