Santa Marta — 80 scientists will judge fossil-fuel phaseout plans.

The Science Panel for the Global Energy Transition, launched at [Colombia](country:CO)'s phaseout summit, split leadership between the Global South and North.

Its guidance is due before November's COP31, where a global phaseout roadmap is expected.
