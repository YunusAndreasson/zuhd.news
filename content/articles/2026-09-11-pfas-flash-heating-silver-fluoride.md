Houston — Rice chemists turned forever-chemical waste into silver fluoride.

A costly-to-destroy waste now becomes an industry reagent.

Electrical pulses break the carbon-fluorine bond, the strongest in organic chemistry, freeing the fluoride.

The process remains a lab demonstration, not yet a working plant.
