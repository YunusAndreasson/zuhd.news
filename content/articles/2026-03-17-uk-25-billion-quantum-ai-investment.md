Chancellor Rachel Reeves pledged £2.5 billion for quantum computing and AI research to stop British talent "drifting abroad." Reeves linked the announcement to years of frontier tech firms and researchers moving to the US, promising the fund will create 100,000 jobs. The UK's commitment remains a fraction of US and Chinese state investments in the same technologies, leaving open whether it will reverse the brain drain.
