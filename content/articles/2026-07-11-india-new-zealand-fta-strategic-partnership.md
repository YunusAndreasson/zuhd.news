Auckland — [India](country:IN) and [New Zealand](country:NZ) concluded a free-trade deal.

New Zealand pledged NZD 20 billion in Indian investment as both sides target NZD 7 billion in trade by 2030.

The FTA's early entry into force depends on both governments' pending ratification steps.
