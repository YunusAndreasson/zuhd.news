Islamabad — The IMF cleared [Pakistan](country:PK)'s auto policy in 2 days.

A new levy on conventional cars will pay for electric-vehicle and export incentives.

The policy targets $6bn of exports over 5 years, with a 5% tax drawback for manufacturers that meet localisation goals.

Most of Pakistan's auto exports today are parts, not vehicles, the industries adviser said.

The federal cabinet takes up the policy next.
