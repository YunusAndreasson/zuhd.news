Berlin — Over 100 groups plan Europe-wide data-centre protests from Friday.

They demand an immediate moratorium on large data centres across Europe.

The European Commission's April 2025 plan aims to at least triple EU data-centre capacity within five to seven years.

"Data centers are worsening the cost of living crisis for Europeans everywhere," said Michelle Thorne, strategy director, Green Web Foundation.

Actions run in at least nine capitals, with a south Berlin site targeted Friday.
