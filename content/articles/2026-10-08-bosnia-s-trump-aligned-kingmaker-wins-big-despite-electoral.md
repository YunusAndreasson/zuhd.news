Sarajevo — Dodik is barred from office, yet his party won Republika Srpska, advocacy outlet Responsible Statecraft reported.

Republika Srpska spent over $4 million on Trump-linked lobbyists since 2025, and Washington lifted Dodik's sanctions.

Political authority in [Bosnia](country:BA) can sit outside formal institutions, said Leila Bičakčić of the Center for Investigative Reporting.

"It is a quid pro quo," said Dejan Sajinovic, a journalist from Republika Srpska.

The outlet says the terms of any understanding between Dodik and the Trump administration remain unclear.
