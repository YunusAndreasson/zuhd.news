Sudan — Prime Minister Kamel Idris weighs a cabinet reshuffle, sources close to him say.

Infighting and curbs on its remit have dogged [Sudan](country:SD)'s civilian government for a year.

A source in his office says the plan covers ministers and governors, mainly over economic and service failures.

The armed movements that signed the 2020 Juba agreement insist any reshuffle must not change power-sharing.

The planned national dialogue will take up the Juba movements' relationship with the government.
