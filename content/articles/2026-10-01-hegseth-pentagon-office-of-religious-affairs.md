Washington — Hegseth put the Armed Forces Chaplains Board under a new office.

Critics say the office erodes church-state separation in the [US](country:US) military.

A Pentagon memo gives the director authority to issue religious policy for the secretary and oversee chaplain training, funding and procurement.

The memo says the office will not compel religious participation or favour any faith.

The Pentagon has not named a director, and a funding plan is due within 60 days.
