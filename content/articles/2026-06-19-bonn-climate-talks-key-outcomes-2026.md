Bonn — The COP30 tripling target for adaptation finance has no baseline or contributor list after UN talks in [Germany](country:DE) collapsed.

Rich and poor nations deadlocked for two weeks over who funds adaptation; the finance type also remains undefined.

Unresolved texts move to COP31 in Antalya, Turkey, where countries must set the baseline.
