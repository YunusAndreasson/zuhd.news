Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s unfrozen glacial lakes jumped from 40 to 105.

More unfrozen lakes raise the risk of floods threatening villages downstream in Gilgit-Baltistan and Chitral.

Meltwater pools behind unstable moraine dams, and lakes that exceed their historic size raise the odds a dam fails.

"Glacial melt is a serious concern," said Karamat Ali, a glaciologist at Karakoram International University.

Suparco says satellite monitoring continues through the melt season to catch any lake's rapid expansion.
