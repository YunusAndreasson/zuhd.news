Planet Labs paused satellite imagery of the Iran war. The company is the primary source of near-real-time imagery used by open-source investigators, including Bellingcat, to verify airstrikes and civilian damage. Without the imagery, open-source investigators cannot independently verify the scope of strikes on Iranian cities.
