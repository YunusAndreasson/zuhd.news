Paris — ESA cannot launch Rosalind Franklin without SpaceX. NASA selected Falcon Heavy for the 2028 launch after Russia's Proton was cut; ESA has no comparable heavy-lift vehicle. NASA's contributions are among 53 science missions proposed for cuts in the White House's FY2027 budget request.
