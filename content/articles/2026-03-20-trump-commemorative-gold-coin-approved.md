Trump's appointees approved a gold coin bearing his image. Trump fired all 6 original commissioners in October, replacing them with loyalists after a bipartisan panel refused the same proposal last month. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent is expected to order the coin minted, with 2 more Trump-image coins in the pipeline — 1 destined for general circulation.
