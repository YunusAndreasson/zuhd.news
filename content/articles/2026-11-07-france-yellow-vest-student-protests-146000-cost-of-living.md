Paris — 146,000 marched across [France](country:FR) for a fourth weekend.

Unions count 250,000 marchers, against the interior ministry's 146,000.

High school students began the protests in September over classroom conditions, and unions added wage demands.

Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu meets unions Monday, and students and teachers strike Tuesday.
