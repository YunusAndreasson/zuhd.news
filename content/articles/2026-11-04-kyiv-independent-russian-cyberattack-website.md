Kyiv — Hackers emailed subscribers a fake Zelensky nuclear strike on Kyiv.

The outlet reaches nearly 3 million monthly readers, mostly outside [Ukraine](country:UA).

Attackers entered the website management system and a newsletter service, so they could publish false messages and email subscribers.

The outlet blames suspected Russian actors from message content but offered no technical evidence linking the breach to Moscow.

Specialists are tracing the breach, and nobody has confirmed a link to the air-raid app hack.
