San Salvador — The human rights ombudswoman skipped her own hearing.

Rights groups say [El Salvador](country:SV)'s detainees held since March 2022 lost their state defender.

Ombudswoman Raquel Caballero de Guevara missed the 26 October hearing in Panama, and no government representative attended either.

Its last public statement dates to February 2021, and the commission awaits a reply.
