DeepSeek's next model was trained on Nvidia's Blackwell chip, which US export controls bar from China. A senior Trump administration official confirmed the breach but declined to say how the chips reached DeepSeek's data centre in Inner Mongolia. The model is set for release next week, demonstrating the enforcement gap in Washington's chip containment strategy.
