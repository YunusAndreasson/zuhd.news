Shanghai — Qualcomm's public 6G prototype reaches peak speeds near 14 Gbps.

Qualcomm says it follows the latest 3GPP definition and was tested with China Mobile and ZTE.

The prototype uses 400 MHz bandwidth each way, 4 times 5G's downlink.

Release 21, the first 6G standard, is due in 2029, when Qualcomm wants commercial service to begin.
