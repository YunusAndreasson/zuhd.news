Washington — Two pilots, not one, will fly the B-21.

The Air Force dropped a single-pilot-plus-sensor-officer plan for the stealth bomber, matching the B-2's crew despite years of automation gains.

Weapon systems and combat systems officers now enter a pilot-transition program before fielding begins next year.
