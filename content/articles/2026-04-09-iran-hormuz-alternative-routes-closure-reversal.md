Tehran — Iran rerouted Hormuz shipping hours into the ceasefire.

The Revolutionary Guards cited sea mines and announced alternative transit routes, effectively maintaining the closure of the strait that carries one-fifth of global oil.

Brent held near $98; the reopening pledged in the truce has not materialized for commercial shipping.
