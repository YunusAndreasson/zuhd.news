Mumbai — One society's stamp duty fell from ₹101 crore to ₹10.68 lakh.

Maharashtra state capped duty on 99-year leases at 0.5%, down from 5%, unlocking decades-stalled registrations on [India](country:IN)'s costliest property market.

Registration now lets thousands of societies pursue redevelopment financing and legal title.
