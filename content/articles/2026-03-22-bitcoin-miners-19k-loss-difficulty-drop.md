New York — Bitcoin costs $88,000 to mine and sells for $69,200. Oil above $100 has raised electricity costs for miners, with 8-10% of bitcoin production drawing from energy grids exposed to the Strait of Hormuz closure. Mining difficulty fell 7.8% — its second-largest drop this year — as miners shut off rigs and pivoted to AI computing.
