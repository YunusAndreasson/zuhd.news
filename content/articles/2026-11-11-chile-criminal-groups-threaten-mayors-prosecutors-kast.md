Criminal groups target officials who obstruct them, testing [Chile](country:CL)'s President Kast.

Police uncovered a plot to kill San Bernardo's mayor while hunting an escaped prisoner, and 18 prosecutors now have protection.

Investigators are still determining why the 27 September shooting that killed Carabinero Cristopher Aguilera began.
