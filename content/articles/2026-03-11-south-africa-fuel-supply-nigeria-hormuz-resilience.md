South Africa sources 48% of its crude from Nigeria, not the Persian Gulf. Nigeria is an Atlantic coast producer outside the Hormuz blockade's reach; Saudi Arabia contributes just 18% of South Africa's imports, leaving the country largely insulated from the crisis. Astron Energy, one of only 2 operating South African refineries, remains offline for maintenance through April, tightening domestic processing capacity regardless.
