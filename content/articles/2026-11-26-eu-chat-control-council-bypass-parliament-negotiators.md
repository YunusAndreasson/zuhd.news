Brussels — [Ireland](country:IE)'s EU presidency weighs bypassing Parliament's chat-control negotiators.

Parliament demands targeted scanning with a judge's approval, which the Council and Commission dispute.

Parliament's rapporteur reached compromises, but shadow rapporteurs from other groups reject them, so the presidency considers involving group leaders.

The Irish presidency denies having contacted group leaders.

The presidency hopes to close the talks on 8 December.
