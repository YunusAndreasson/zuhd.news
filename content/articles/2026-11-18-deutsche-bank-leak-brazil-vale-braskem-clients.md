São Paulo — Leaked files show Deutsche Bank staff excusing 4 Brazilian clients.

Vale operated the Córrego do Feijão mine whose 2019 dam collapse in [Brazil](country:BR) killed 272.

The files are internal emails, reports and spreadsheets from a leak of more than 15 million Deutsche Bank documents.

Vale, Safra, Braskem and Andrade Gutierrez all banked there, and staff downplayed accusations or bypassed internal controls.

Further reports will cover meatpackers BRF and JBS and juice exporter Cutrale.
