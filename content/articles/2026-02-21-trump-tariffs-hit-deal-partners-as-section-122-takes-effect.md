10% tariffs take effect Monday on all imports. US President Donald Trump signed the order hours after the Supreme Court struck down his emergency tariffs 6-3, this time citing a 1974 trade law. The 150-day measure exempts only North American trade pact goods, leaving countries that negotiated lower rates exposed.
