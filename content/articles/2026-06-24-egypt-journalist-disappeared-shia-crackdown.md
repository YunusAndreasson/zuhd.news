Cairo — Al-Dostor journalist Haider Qandil vanished Monday evening from his Giza office.

[Egypt](country:EG)'s security swept at least 8 Shias ahead of Ashura, his brother said — including a New Zealand citizen seized at home.

The Journalists Syndicate is seeking Qandil's location; documentation of the detained is still ongoing.
