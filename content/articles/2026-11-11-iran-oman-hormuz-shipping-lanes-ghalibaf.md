Doha — [Iran](country:IR) says [Oman](country:OM) agreed Hormuz shipping lanes, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

Parliament speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf says Hormuz's prewar status is gone, one of Tehran's seven conditions.

The deal covers a shipping corridor and a traffic separation scheme, which assigns inbound and outbound ships to separate lanes.

TASS carried no statement from Oman confirming it.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement a 33% chance by 31 December.
