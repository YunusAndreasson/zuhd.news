Dublin — A six-year inquiry ends in a €403 million fine.

[Ireland](country:IE)'s Data Protection Commission will fine Google over how it processed location data.

Under the EU data law's one-stop shop, Ireland leads enforcement against most [US](country:US) tech firms, and critics have long faulted its pace.

No corrective orders beyond the fine have been reported.
