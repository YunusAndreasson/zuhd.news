Garching — The record quasar's black hole weighs 360 million suns.

It shines from 13.1 billion years ago, at under 5% of the universe's age.

A German-led team found it in Euclid telescope data, and Webb measured the mass of its slow-feeding black hole.

Lead author Julien Wolf says the record could fall within months or stand for years.
