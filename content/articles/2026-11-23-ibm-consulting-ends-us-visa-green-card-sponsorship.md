United States — IBM gives immigrant workers 3 weeks to move abroad or leave.

Moving to [India](country:IN) could mean an 80% pay cut, one employee wrote on Blind.

IBM Consulting is ending visa and green-card sponsorship for most roles, and listed 1,125 India openings on 19 November.

IBM says its use of high-skilled visa programs has been declining for years.

Workers who decline get three months' severance and a final day of 31 December, Blind's chief executive said.
