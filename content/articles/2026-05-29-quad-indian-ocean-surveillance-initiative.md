New Delhi — Quad launches Indian Ocean maritime-surveillance network.

[The US](country:US), [India](country:IN), [Japan](country:JP) and [Australia](country:AU) pooled maritime-domain data covering routes that carry most Gulf energy to Asia.

The 4 member states have not confirmed whether smaller Indian Ocean nations will receive access to shared data feeds.
