Islamabad — The bill does not touch retail acid sales.

Convictions have stayed rare under existing harsh sentences, an enforcement gap the bill does not address.

A Senate committee, chaired by Senator Faisal Saleem Rehman, passed the bill after an acid attack on a Quetta doctor.

Its fate now depends on a full Senate vote, with unregulated acid still sold freely.
