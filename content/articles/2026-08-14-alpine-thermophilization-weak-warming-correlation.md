Vienna — European summit plants shifted toward warmth-loving species.

That shift barely tracks each summit's own warming, undercutting warming-only forecasts elsewhere.

Researchers resurveyed century-old plots, but snow cover, soil and migration speed shape outcomes more than local heat.

The team, publishing in Nature Ecology & Evolution, has not tested the method beyond Europe.
