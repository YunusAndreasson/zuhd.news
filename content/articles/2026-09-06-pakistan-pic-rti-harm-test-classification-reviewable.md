Islamabad — Pakistan's Information Commission voided an unexplained secrecy order.

Blanket secrecy, not refusal, is how right-to-information laws are usually defeated in [Pakistan](country:PK).

The ruling requires the speaker to write reasons whenever assembly records are classified, making secrecy reviewable.

The commission gave the secretariat 10 days to release the disputed employee records.
