A decision model routes requests, approves tool calls and attaches a confidence score for AI agents.

The 73.37 versus 57.96 gap over [US](country:US) rival TypeSafe's Jev comes from StartLux's own tests.

Outside developers, who have downloaded the open weights nearly 120,000 times, can now check the claim.
