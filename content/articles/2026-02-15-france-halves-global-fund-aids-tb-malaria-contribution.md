France slashed its pledge to the Global Fund by 58%. The contribution to the fund that fights AIDS, tuberculosis, and malaria dropped from €1.6 billion to €660 million for the 2026-2028 cycle, part of an €800 million reduction in French development aid. The Fund dispensed antiretroviral treatments to 25.6 million people in 2024, and health groups warned the cuts would shrink testing in rural communities where infections spread fastest.
