Hangzhou — Researchers unlocked inactive sodium sites in iron-based cathodes.

Iron-based cathodes are cheap and stable but waste capacity on inert sodium sites; cation substitution activates them, a [China](country:CN) team showed.

Sodium-ion batteries avoid lithium's supply chain; the finding addresses their core capacity limit.
