Israeli airstrikes killed 50 in Lebanon Tuesday, including 7 children. Israel still occupies 5 Lebanese border positions it was due to vacate after November's ceasefire, signaling intent to eliminate Hezbollah's military capacity permanently. Hezbollah official Mohamoud Komati declared "open war"; France deployed its aircraft carrier to the Mediterranean.
