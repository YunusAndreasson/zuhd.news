Washington — 900 ocean sensors are being switched off.

The National Science Foundation is removing 4 of 5 [US](country:US) ocean arrays; scientists warn broken data records cannot be back-filled.

The arrays tracked CO2 and ocean heat uptake; their removal, researchers say, breaks baselines needed for climate forecasting.
