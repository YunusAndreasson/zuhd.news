$175 billion signed for 760 AI projects. Andhra Pradesh is building a data centre cluster in Visakhapatnam anchored by Google's $15 billion hub — its largest outside the US — with land offered at 1 cent per acre and 6 gigawatts of capacity backed by 6 approved nuclear plants. The announcement comes 2 days before Prime Minister Narendra Modi hosts the AI Impact Summit, where India will position itself as the world's 3rd AI power despite questions about whether infrastructure equals technology sovereignty.
