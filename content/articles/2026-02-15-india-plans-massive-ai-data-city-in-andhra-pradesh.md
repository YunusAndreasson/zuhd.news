Andhra Pradesh is building a 6-gigawatt data centre hub in Visakhapatnam. The state secured $175 billion in investment agreements across 760 projects, led by a $15 billion Google commitment for its largest artificial intelligence infrastructure site outside the United States. India approved 6 nuclear power plants nearby to supply the energy, positioning the country as a competitor to the United States and China in the global race to host AI computing.
