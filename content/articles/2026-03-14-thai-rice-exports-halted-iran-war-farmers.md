80,000 tonnes of Thai rice are stranded in Bangkok. Iraq, Thailand's largest rice buyer, requires shipments through the Strait of Hormuz, where Iranian projectiles struck a Thai cargo ship this week. Shipments could be suspended for months, threatening the livelihoods of farmers who make up a quarter of Thailand's workforce.
