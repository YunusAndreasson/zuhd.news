Abu Dhabi — The UAE cut off all Iran trade and finance Wednesday.

Dubai has been Iran's main channel to the international banking system under sanctions.

The suspension follows two Iranian ballistic missiles fired toward the [UAE](country:AE), one landing inside its waters.

The foreign ministry set no date for resuming trade or financial ties.
