Iran struck Kuwait with missiles and drones. Kuwait is the 4th Gulf state targeted; it hosts Camp Arifjan and Al-Jaber Air Base — 2 US military installations that made it a target. Gulf monarchies cannot expel US forces without risking regime collapse, and cannot keep them without absorbing more strikes.
