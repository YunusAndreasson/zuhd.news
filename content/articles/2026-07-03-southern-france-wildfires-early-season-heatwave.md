Bordeaux — Nearly 3,000 evacuated as fire season arrives a month early.

Prime Minister Sebastien Lecornu linked the outbreak to fuel dried by [France](country:FR)'s record-breaking June heat wave, compressing what was once an August risk.

Firefighters battle the blazes as forecasters watch whether the early start signals a longer season.
