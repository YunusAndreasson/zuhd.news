Cambridge — Arm released the AGI CPU for AI inference only. Unlike previous Arm designs built for general-purpose computing, the AGI CPU prioritizes the matrix operations and memory bandwidth patterns that dominate large model inference — the layer where Nvidia currently holds near-monopoly revenue. Arm licenses its architecture rather than selling chips, leaving adoption to hyperscalers who must choose AGI CPU-based inference clusters over established Nvidia GPU infrastructure.
