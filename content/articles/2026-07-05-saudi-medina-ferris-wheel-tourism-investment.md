Medina — [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) wants private investors for a $135 million ferris wheel.

The project would draw leisure spending from pilgrimage traffic near one of Islam's holiest cities, part of the kingdom's push to diversify past oil.

The government has not named a bidding deadline or shortlisted investors.
