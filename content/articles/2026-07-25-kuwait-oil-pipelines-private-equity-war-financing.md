Kuwait City — Kuwait sold 49% of its pipelines for $16bn.

Blackstone, KKR and Brookfield take a long-term lease over 320km of pipeline after nearly 1,400 Iranian missile and drone strikes forced [Kuwait](country:KW) to raise war-repair capital.

The lease sets decades of tariffs and dividend priority; Kuwait Petroleum still owes its 4m-barrel 2035 target.
