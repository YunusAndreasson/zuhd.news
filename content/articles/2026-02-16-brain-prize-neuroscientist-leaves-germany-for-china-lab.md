A top neuroscientist left Germany for China. Arthur Konnerth won the 2015 Brain Prize for pioneering two-photon brain imaging and held tenure at the Technical University of Munich for decades. He resigned to join Shenzhen Bay Laboratory under director Yan Ning, marking one of the highest-profile scientific departures from Europe to China as Beijing builds research institutions in fields overlapping with strategic technologies like artificial intelligence.
