Pakistan charged India at the UN with weaponising water. India suspended the 1960 Indus Waters Treaty despite a 2025 arbitration ruling confirming it remains binding; the treaty irrigates 80% of Pakistan's farmland. World Water Day falls on March 22 with India yet to respond to the arbitration ruling.
