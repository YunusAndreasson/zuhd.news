Geneva — Forecasters warn this El Niño could rank among the strongest on record.

The Pacific warming pattern reorganizes rainfall worldwide, months ahead of impact, yet funding for smallholder farmers typically arrives only after floods or droughts hit.

Scientists are pressing governments to fund preparedness now, before the anticipated disruption lands.
