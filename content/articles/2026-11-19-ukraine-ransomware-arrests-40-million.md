Kyiv — [Ukraine](country:UA) arrests 4 suspects in a $40.5 million ransomware scheme.

Named victims include 4 [US](country:US) firms in oil, energy, tyres and health care.

Members allegedly phoned US employees posing as technical support to get logins, then stole data and demanded cryptocurrency.

Newpark Resources paid $1.5 million after attackers demanded $10 million, Ukrainian court records show.

The suspects face up to 15 years in prison, and police are still identifying other members.
