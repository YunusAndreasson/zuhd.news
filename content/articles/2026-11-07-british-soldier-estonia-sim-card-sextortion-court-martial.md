Tapa — A corporal ignored a phone-security order and was sextorted.

The phone, not the base, was the weak point 90 miles from Russia's border.

Daniel Barton-Kennedy ignored orders in [Estonia](country:EE), and an attacker took nude photos and demanded £100, then £250.

A court-martial reduced his rank and ordered 10 months in detention, and his appeal failed in August.
