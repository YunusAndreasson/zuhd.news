Sanaa — Houthis claim hitting a Saudi power plant, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

Houthi spokesman Yahya Saria called it retaliation for coalition strikes on Saada and Mokha power plants.

The group claims serious damage to a power plant in Al-Shuqaiq and the port of Jizan in [Saudi Arabia](country:SA).

Coalition spokesman Turki al-Maliki says forces destroyed seven Houthi military sites and six launcher workshops.

Saria warned Jeddah's port and all Saudi Red Sea ports are targets if the coalition attacks Hodeidah.
