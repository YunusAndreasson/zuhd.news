Islamabad — Chief Minister Sohail Afridi says the 4 October long march proceeds.

A sitting provincial chief minister will lead Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf's march against Imran Khan's imprisonment, which the party calls illegal.

Government and opposition negotiators meet Saturday at 2pm in the Speaker's chambers, after a first round that only aired positions.

The march leaves Lakki Marwat at 1pm on 4 October, and Afridi vows no repeat of May 9.
