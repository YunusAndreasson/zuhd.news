Johannesburg — Four Nigerians dead in [South Africa](country:ZA)'s wave.

Metro police tortured one to death in Pretoria; soldiers killed two others earlier, the consulate said.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s probe into the earlier deaths remains open after two months, with no arrests made.
