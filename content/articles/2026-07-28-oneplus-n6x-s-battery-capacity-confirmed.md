New Delhi — The N6x's battery drops 1,000mAh from its predecessor.

OnePlus confirms a 7,000mAh cell for the July 31 [India](country:IN) launch, even as the [China](country:CN)-based brand ends new phone sales across North America and Europe.

The N6x goes on sale on Amazon August 4; pricing has not been disclosed.
