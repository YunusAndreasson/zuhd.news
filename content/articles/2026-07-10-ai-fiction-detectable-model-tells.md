College Park — GPT overuses dream sequences; Gemini over-describes characters.

University of Maryland and Google DeepMind researchers built a detector, StoryScope, that found AI fiction clusters tightly while human stories vary in moral ambiguity and plot complexity.

The team studied over 50,000 AI-generated stories; broader "idea-level" detection tools remain unbuilt.
