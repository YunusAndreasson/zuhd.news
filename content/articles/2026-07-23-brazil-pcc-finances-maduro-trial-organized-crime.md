São Paulo — Nearly 20 people were arrested this week.

"Operação Janus" froze about $1.8 million in assets per suspect tied to the First Capital Command's laundering network, which now spans oil and influencer marketing.

[Brazil](country:BR)'s prosecutors have not said whether the seizures dent the group's broader financial infrastructure.
