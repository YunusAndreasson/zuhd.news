Vientiane — Myanmar's junta chief gets his first ASEAN welcome.

ASEAN's five-point consensus froze top-level invitations to [Myanmar](country:MM)'s generals; one host's hospitality now cracks that collective non-recognition.

The visit lands as the junta plans elections to launder its rule, testing whether ASEAN unity survives the gesture.
