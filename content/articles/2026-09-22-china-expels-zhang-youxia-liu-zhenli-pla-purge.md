Beijing — Xi's own military commission now has just two members.

Loyalty to Xi, not command experience, now decides who leads [China](country:CN)'s military.

Beijing expelled Zhang Youxia, the commission's vice-chairman, and Liu Zhenli, its joint staff chief, for forming factions and taking bribes.

"Zhang was presented as proof a new generation would stay loyal to the revolution," said Joseph Torigian, a China scholar.

Their cases go to military prosecutors, days before Xi meets Trump in Washington.
