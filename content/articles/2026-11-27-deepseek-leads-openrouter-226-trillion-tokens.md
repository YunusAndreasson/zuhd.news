Beijing — DeepSeek served 226 trillion tokens on OpenRouter in 30 days.

[China](country:CN)'s models hold the top 3 places, with Zhipu's GLM 5.3 Flash third at 56.7 trillion.

DeepSeek's price list charges 0.02 yuan per million cache-hit input tokens against 1 yuan for misses, so repeated code rereads cost 50 times less.

The 745% jump compares a full 30 days with a version that launched 17 October.

Retention depends on whether trials move from sampling orders to checking every one, the report's author argues.
