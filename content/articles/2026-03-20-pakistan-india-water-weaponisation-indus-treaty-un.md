Pakistan raised India's water suspension at the UN Friday. India suspended the 1960 Indus Waters Treaty — which survived 3 wars — citing Pakistan's alleged support for terrorism, affecting rivers that sustain 25% of Pakistan's economy. India demands Pakistan "credibly end terrorism support" before restoring the treaty — a condition Islamabad rejects.
