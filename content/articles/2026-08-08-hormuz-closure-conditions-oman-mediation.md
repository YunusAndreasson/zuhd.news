Tehran — Iran keeps the Strait of Hormuz closed.

Brent crude fell 15.6% last week as tankers and insurers avoid the route entirely.

[Iran](country:IR)'s security council secretary Mohammad Bagher Zolghadr listed troop withdrawal, sanctions relief, compensation and four other conditions for reopening.

Washington has not publicly responded to any of the six demands.
