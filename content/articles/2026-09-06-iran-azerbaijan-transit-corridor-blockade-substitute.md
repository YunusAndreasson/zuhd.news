Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) and [Azerbaijan](country:AZ) discussed a stalled rail corridor.

The talks follow war damage to Iran's transport infrastructure, the roads minister said.

The joint commission is pushing to finish a railroad that Azerbaijan has already extended to the Agband border area.

Azerbaijan's deputy premier said completing Iran's remaining stretch would boost regional transport capacity.
