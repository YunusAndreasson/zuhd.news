China approved the world's first commercial implantable brain interface. The device, made by Chinese startup Neuracle, sits on the brain's outer surface without penetrating tissue, reading neural signals that restore hand movement in spinal cord patients. Neuracle is preparing a Shanghai stock market listing as rivals including Neuralink await comparable US approval.
