United States — Seven free apps now copy Adobe's Photoshop, Illustrator and Premiere.

Free under MIT and Apache licences, they are funded by selling tokens for Artcraft's image model.

Developer Brandon Thomas says he used Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 to build "clean-room replacements" in Rust.

Hacker News commenters have listed many shortcomings, and Thomas calls the release a super early alpha.

The apps could face legal trouble if their look too closely mimics Adobe's trade dress.
