Lima — [Peru](country:PE)'s electoral agency declared Keiko Fujimori president with 50.14% on her 4th attempt.

The daughter of former strongman Alberto Fujimori takes control of South America's top copper exporter after a recount of disputed ballots.

Her Popular Force party controls Congress, which must seat her cabinet before the August 28 inauguration.
