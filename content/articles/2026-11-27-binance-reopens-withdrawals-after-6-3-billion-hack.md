United States — Binance says it restored 98% of $6.34 billion hacked.

Binance fully lifted the withdrawal suspension on 26 November, four weeks after the 29 October hack.

Binance says attackers used fake job interviews and malware to reach wallet systems, and TRM Labs blames [North Korea](country:KP)'s TraderTraitor group.

Binance says a few networks remain paused for maintenance, unrelated to the hack, without naming them.

Scammers often follow such events, and Binance says it will never ask for a password, two-factor code or seed phrase.
