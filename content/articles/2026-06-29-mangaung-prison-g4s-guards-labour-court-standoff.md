Bloemfontein — Popcru threatened contempt charges after [South Africa](country:ZA) defied a court order on G4S guard transfers.

Section 197 of the Labour Relations Act mandates automatic staff transfer when a privatized prison reverts to state control.

The 25-year concession expires Wednesday; the department is instead recruiting new staff.
