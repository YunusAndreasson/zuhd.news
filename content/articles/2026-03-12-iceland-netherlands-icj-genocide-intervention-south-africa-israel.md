Iceland filed against Israel at the ICJ. The country of 380,000 joined the Netherlands to bring the total intervening states to 18 in South Africa's genocide case; Article 63 of the ICJ statute lets states formally argue how the Genocide Convention applies. Israel's counter-memorial was due Thursday after multiple extensions; the court had not confirmed receipt by publication time.
