Mocoa — A copper company's letter linked mine opponents to armed groups.

Global Witness says it saw no evidence for the claim and calls it part of a campaign to silence critics.

Libero Cobre, a subsidiary of Canada's Copper Giant Resources, sent the 2025 letter to the mayor's office naming an Indigenous leader and elected representatives.

Libero Cobre says it based the letter on security reports and did not intend to link peaceful advocacy to armed groups.

Global Witness asks [Colombia](country:CO)'s government to ensure defenders can speak out safely.
