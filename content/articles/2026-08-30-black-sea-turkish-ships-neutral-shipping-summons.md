Ankara — Turkey summoned Kyiv's ambassador over strikes on its ships.

Turkey's four-year mediator role between [Russia](country:RU) and [Ukraine](country:UA) now costs its own merchant fleet.

Drones hit two Turkish-operated vessels near Russia's Tuapse port this week, injuring five crew, Turkish officials said.

Ankara wants both sides to accept the Black Sea attack moratorium it proposed in August.
