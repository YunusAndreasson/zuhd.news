Mountain View — Assistant disappears from Android on 4 September.

Assistant matched commands locally with a fixed vocabulary; Gemini is a cloud model that guesses instead of failing to understand.

Users get no opt-out, and older devices that don't meet Gemini's requirements may lose the assistant outright.
