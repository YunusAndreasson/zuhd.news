London — Most microbes cannot break nitrogen's triple bond.

Nitrogenase enzymes, found in a narrow set of soil microbes, convert atmospheric nitrogen gas into ammonia that plants can use.

Researchers are still working out why some of these enzymes convert nitrogen far more efficiently than others.
