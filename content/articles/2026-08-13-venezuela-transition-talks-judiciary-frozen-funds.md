Judicial reform and Bank of England reserves settled first, an election date did not.

The Supreme Tribunal validated Maduro's disputed 2024 win, so its overhaul precedes any vote count.

Nobel laureate María Corina Machado stayed outside the talks, which she has not endorsed.
