Washington — A judge made prosecutors rewrite their Ilhan Omar search plan.

A sitting congresswoman gets a chance to flag protected legislative material before agents search her home.

Magistrate Judge Matthew Sharbaugh applied the Speech or Debate Clause, which shields legislative records from criminal cases, before approving the 12 October search.

Omar, who is uncharged and denies the allegations, awaits Chief Judge James Boasberg's ruling on releasing more records.
