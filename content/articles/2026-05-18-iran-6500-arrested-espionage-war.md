Tehran — [Iran](country:IR)'s police chief announced 6,500 espionage arrests.

General Ahmad-Reza Radan said identification operations are continuing and none of those detained in January's unrest have been released.

Radan said police operations have "not stopped" and gave no end date or suspect threshold.
