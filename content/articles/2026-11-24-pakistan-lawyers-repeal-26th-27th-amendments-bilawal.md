Islamabad — 100 lawyers demand repeal of amendments Bilawal's party passed.

The signatories say the coalition holds its numbers through seats taken from the opposition.

The 26th Amendment removed judges' majority on the commission choosing constitutional benches, and the 27th made those benches a separate court.

Bilawal later said he is not disowning the 27th Amendment, only noting it lacked consensus.

The letter demands the pre-26th order back and reserved seats returned to what it calls their lawful claimants.
