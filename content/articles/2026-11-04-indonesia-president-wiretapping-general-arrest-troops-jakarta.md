Jakarta — 20,980 troops are deploying while police detain 3 generals.

The palace says tapping President Prabowo Subianto's communications could amount to treason.

Police allege Listyo Sigit Prabowo and 2 generals ordered wiretaps without procedure in 2025-26, then ordered the files deleted in October.

[Indonesia](country:ID)'s military calls the deployment routine, and officials have not linked it to the investigation.

State Secretary Prasetyo Hadi says the government awaits police findings on who ordered the taps and why.
