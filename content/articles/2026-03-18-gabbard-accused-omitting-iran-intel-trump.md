America's top spy omitted Iran war intelligence, senators charge. Director of National Intelligence Tulsi Gabbard withheld assessments showing Iran posed no imminent threat — Trump's stated justification for military action. Bipartisan senators have demanded the unredacted assessments her office prepared before the war began.
