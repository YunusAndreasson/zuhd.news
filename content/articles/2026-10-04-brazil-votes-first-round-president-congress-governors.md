Brasília — [Brazil](country:BR) elects a president, governors and lawmakers on Sunday.

[Iran](country:IR)'s state-affiliated Mehr agency says all lower-house seats and a third of the Senate are contested.

Presidents, governors and senators are chosen by majority system, federal and regional lawmakers proportionally.

A runoff on 25 October follows if no executive candidate wins an absolute majority.
