Tripoli — Two rival families are splitting Libya's presidency and premiership.

US envoy Massad Boulos is brokering the arrangement as ExxonMobil, Chevron and Halliburton raise investment in [Libya](country:LY), which holds Africa's largest proven oil reserves.

Analyst Claudia Gazzini said oil access has driven US Libya policy under both parties.
