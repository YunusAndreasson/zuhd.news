Houston — Gasoline stockpiles are the lowest since 2012.

[US](country:US) diesel inventories fell to the lowest seasonal levels in years, as unprecedented overseas demand strains Gulf Coast to Eastern Seaboard reserves.

The drawdown leaves the world's top producer with a thinner buffer against a Hormuz-driven supply shock.
