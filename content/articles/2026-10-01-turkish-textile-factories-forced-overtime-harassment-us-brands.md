Malatya — A Baykan Denim veteran earns minimum wage: $533 a month.

The Confederation of Turkish Trade Unions says a single worker needs $916 a month for basics.

Workers say overtime is mandatory and managers threaten to move production to [Egypt](country:EG), where labour is cheaper.

Baykan Denim, which makes jeans for Calvin Klein and Tommy Hilfiger, was sent questions about the allegations.
