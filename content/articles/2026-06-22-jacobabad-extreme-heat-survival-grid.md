Jacobabad — 51°C, 3-day blackout, 1 solar-powered fan.

At 51°C, heat can kill regardless of hydration; 14-16 hours of load-shedding in [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Sindh strips the only protection households can afford.

Heat remains outside Pakistan's disaster governance framework; no state cooling infrastructure has reached the affected settlements.
