300,000 households live with contaminated water and air. Foreign companies extract copper and cobalt for batteries and electric vehicles while acid drainage poisons rivers and farmland across the Copperbelt. Affected farmers filed an $80 billion lawsuit even as Zambia plans to quadruple output to 3 million tonnes by 2031.
