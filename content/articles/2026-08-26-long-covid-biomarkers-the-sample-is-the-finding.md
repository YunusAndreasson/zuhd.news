Tórshavn — A study of 218 Faroese patients found no convincing long COVID marker.

400 million people worldwide carry the diagnosis with no objective test to confirm it.

Unlike earlier clinic-based studies, this nationwide sample avoided selecting only patients already sick enough to seek care.

One weak signal, the clotting marker INR, showed no clinical significance despite statistical association.
