Karachi — Pakistani women die of heart disease more than men do.

The gap points to heart attacks caught too late in women, not fewer occurring.

A JACC: Asia study found Pakistan alone, among 5 countries, had a higher female death rate than male, 4.4% versus 3.1%.

Cardiologists say [Pakistan](country:PK)'s primary care network remains too thin to catch these risk factors early.
