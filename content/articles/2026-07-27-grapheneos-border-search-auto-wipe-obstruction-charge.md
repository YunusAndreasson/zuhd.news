Atlanta — A phone's own security feature became a charge.

Prosecutors treat Sam Tunick's GrapheneOS wipe, triggered as he entered his passcode at a border search, as evidence destruction under federal law.

A judge is not expected to rule on suppressing the evidence until late October.
