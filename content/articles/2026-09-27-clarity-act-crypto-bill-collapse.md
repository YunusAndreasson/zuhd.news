Washington — The Digital Asset Market Clarity Act died in the [US](country:US) Senate.

The $3 trillion crypto market stays split between two federal regulators with undefined authority.

An ethics provision meant to limit Trump's crypto holdings split lawmakers, and the bill failed on a Senate floor vote amid bipartisan opposition.

No federal market-structure law exists as the crypto industry heads into a contested midterm election season.
