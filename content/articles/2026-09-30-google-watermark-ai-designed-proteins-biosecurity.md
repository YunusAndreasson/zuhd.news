Mountain View — Google DeepMind's watermarked AI proteins still bound their targets.

Screening software at DNA makers cannot assess AI-designed proteins that resemble nothing known.

A secret key nudges each amino acid choice in ProteinMPNN, and only functional picks stay, leaving a statistical signature.

Very short proteins carry too few watermark amino acids, and fusing a natural protein can dilute the signal.

Its security rests on how keys from trusted universities and biotech firms get distributed and protected.
