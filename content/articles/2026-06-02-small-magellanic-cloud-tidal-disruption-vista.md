Cerro Paranal — A decade-long VISTA survey caught the Small Magellanic Cloud mid-disruption.

The dwarf galaxy is being pulled apart by its larger Magellanic neighbor, tracked across millions of stellar motions.

The findings, in Astronomy & Astrophysics, require revising models that treated it as a stable structure.
