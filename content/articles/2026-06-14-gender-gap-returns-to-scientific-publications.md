Washington — Medical science: identical papers pay women 10.7% less.

A Nature study of [US](country:US) professors finds women earn more for solo papers but less from large co-author networks than men — the mechanism that turns equal output into unequal pay.

The gap averages 15.4%, from 5.5% in agricultural sciences to 20.9% in social sciences.
