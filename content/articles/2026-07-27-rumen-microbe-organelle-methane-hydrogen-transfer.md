Global — Scientists found an organelle inside a gut microbe.

The structure shuttles hydrogen to methane-making microbes as cattle digest cellulose, burning off about 6% of their feed energy as gas.

Livestock methane makes up a third of agricultural emissions, and remedies like feed additives reach few smallholder herds.
