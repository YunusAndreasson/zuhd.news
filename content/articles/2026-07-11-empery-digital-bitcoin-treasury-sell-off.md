New York — Empery Digital sold 1,400 bitcoin for $87 million.

The firm, one of 2025's "treasury company" copycats of MicroStrategy, says it won't buy more bitcoin, funding a data-center stake instead.

Empery's 1,514 remaining bitcoin may also be sold to fund new AI data-center deals.
