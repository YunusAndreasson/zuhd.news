Apple Mac Mini stock has sold out across China, with official wait times stretching to one month. OpenClaw, a Chinese AI tool requiring a dedicated local machine for privacy, identified the 4,499 yuan model as its preferred hardware after users rejected cloud alternatives as unsafe. Vendors in Beijing's Zhongguancun market are marking up units by 500 yuan.
