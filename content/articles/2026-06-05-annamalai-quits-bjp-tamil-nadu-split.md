Chennai — [India](country:IN)'s Bharatiya Janata Party lost its most prominent Tamil figure to a split.

K Annamalai cited 18 months of internal disputes and announced a new party hours after resigning; the party has never governed Dravidian-dominated Tamil Nadu.

His new party faces entrenched Dravidian incumbents in the next state election.
