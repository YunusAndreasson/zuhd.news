Kathmandu — About 100 people may be trapped underground.

Families in Nepal, India, China and beyond are still counting missing relatives.

Some of the missing are inside hydropower tunnels cut into the gorge, needing boring gear and shoring, not flood relief.

[Nepal](country:NP) has now accepted foreign rescue teams from [India](country:IN), China, Australia and South Korea.
