Nuuk — Permafrost froze 4,500 years of Greenland garbage.

Permafrost preserved Paleo-Inuit, Norse, and Danish-era bones, shells, and waste intact — an archive destroyed by decay everywhere else.

As permafrost thaws, [Greenland](country:GL)'s midden sites are disappearing.
