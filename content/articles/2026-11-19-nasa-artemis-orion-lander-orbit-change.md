United States — Starship would push Orion at up to 1 g.

NASA's lander shakeup shifts the engineering burden onto the capsule Lockheed Martin built.

Orion must fly backward, so its European-built Service Module needs modified propellant tanks and a structure certified for the reversed load.

"We're working three shifts," said Kirk Shireman, Lockheed Martin's Orion program manager.

NASA and Lockheed expect to finish their orbital-debris assessment by January, targeting an Artemis III launch in 2027.
