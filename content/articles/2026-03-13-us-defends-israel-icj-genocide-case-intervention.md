Civilian deaths don't prove genocide, Washington told the ICJ. The US invoked Article 63 to formally enter South Africa's December 2023 genocide case — the first US intervention in these ICJ proceedings. South Africa must now convince the court that Israel's intent was specifically to destroy Palestinians, not merely defeat Hamas.
