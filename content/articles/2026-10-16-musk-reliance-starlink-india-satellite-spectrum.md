New Delhi — Reliance says its own satellite service awaits clearance too.

Elon Musk accused Mukesh Ambani of blocking Starlink to protect a monopoly, which Reliance denies.

[India](country:IN)'s government says Starlink, Jio Satellite and OneWeb India hold preliminary licences and sit at broadly the same stage.

Musk says Starlink spent 5 years complying with every requirement and is licensed in over 165 countries.

All three are undergoing security assessment before they may launch.
