Washington — [Hungary](country:HU) signed the first known US pact against antifascist groups.

It lets an EU member share information on left-wing groups with Washington.

Foreign Minister Anita Orbán and Secretary of State Marco Rubio signed it Thursday, targeting a German network called Antifa Ost.

Trump declared antifa a domestic terrorist organisation by decree after Charlie Kirk's killing in September 2025.

[Germany](country:DE)'s government is preparing a second US summit on left-wing extremism.
