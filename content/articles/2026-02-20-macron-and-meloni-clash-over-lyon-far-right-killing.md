Left-wing activists beat a far-right protester to death in Lyon. Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni called it "a wound for all of Europe"; French President Emmanuel Macron told her to "stay in their own lane." The dispute deepened a rift between the 2 leaders already divided over Ukraine and European defence.
