Bahawalpur — A harsh question over his whereabouts turned fatal.

Muhammad Zafar allegedly struck his father, Khan Muhammad, with an axe he was carrying home from the fields after an argument, [Pakistan](country:PK) police said.

Police have arrested Zafar and opened an investigation into Wednesday's killing.
