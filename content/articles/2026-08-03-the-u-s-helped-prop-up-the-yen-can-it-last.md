Tokyo — A joint US-Japan move pushed the dollar to a one-month low.

The [US](country:US) rarely intervenes in currency markets; solo interventions by [Japan](country:JP) burned billions for weeks of effect, since the carry trade selling yen is arbitrage no spending can outweigh.

Last week's Fed meeting, not the intervention, narrows the rate gap driving the trade.
