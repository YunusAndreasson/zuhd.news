Gut microbes may determine who survives a peanut reaction. Specific bacteria break down peanut proteins faster than the immune system can mount a response, meaning gut composition — not avoidance — determines who reacts. Researchers are developing a probiotic to protect the 1 in 50 Western children with peanut allergy.
