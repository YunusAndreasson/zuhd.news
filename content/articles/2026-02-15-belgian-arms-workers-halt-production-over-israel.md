Workers at FN Herstal stopped the machines and left. Staff at Belgium's largest weapons manufacturer staged a spontaneous strike Friday after learning an Israeli military officer was visiting the factory as part of a defense attache delegation. Members of the General Federation of Belgian Labour, the country's socialist trade union, walked off the production floor in protest. The Hind Rajab Foundation, a Palestinian rights organization, identified the visitor as the Israeli military attache in Brussels and filed a complaint with the International Criminal Court. The walkout marks a rare case of arms-industry workers intervening directly in a supply chain debate that has mostly played out among governments and courts.
