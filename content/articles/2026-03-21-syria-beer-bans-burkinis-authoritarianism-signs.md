Damascus's alcohol trade is now confined to Christian neighborhoods. Syria's ruling Islamist authority imposed the ban this week, alarming minorities watching whether the new order will uphold pluralism. The ban will cost hundreds of jobs and deter tourists that Syria's shattered economy needs.
