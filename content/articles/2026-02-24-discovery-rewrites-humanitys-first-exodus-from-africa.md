Human ancestors left Africa 400,000 years earlier than believed. Dating of Israel's Ubeidiya site to 2 million years ago found Homo erectus and Homo habilis tools in the same layers — they migrated together. Researchers must now reexamine other sites along the Levant corridor, the only land bridge out of Africa.
