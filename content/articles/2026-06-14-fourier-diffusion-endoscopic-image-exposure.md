London — FSADiff fixed endoscope lighting without fabricating anatomy.

Most enhancement models work spatially, fabricating detail; FSADiff operates in frequency space, separating light from anatomical content before correction.

The model outperformed state-of-the-art across 3 hospital datasets; independent clinical validation has not begun.
