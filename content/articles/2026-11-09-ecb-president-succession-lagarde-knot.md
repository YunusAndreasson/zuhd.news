Brussels — Christine Lagarde leaves the central bank on 7 February.

She goes nine months early, with the deposit rate at 2.5% in October, and leaders must fill two more board seats.

Ministers recommend, European Parliament holds a hearing, leaders decide, and [France](country:FR), [Germany](country:DE), [Spain](country:ES) and the [Netherlands](country:NL) are likely to negotiate the three seats as a package, officials said.

Klaas Knot and Pablo Hernández de Cos are the candidates, and leaders aim to decide by year's end.
