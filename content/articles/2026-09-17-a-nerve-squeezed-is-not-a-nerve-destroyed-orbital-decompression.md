London — Eyes with zero light perception recovered vision.

Doctors had treated total light loss as permanent nerve damage.

Swollen eye muscles compress the optic nerve without destroying it, and 16 of 18 surgery patients regained some vision.

Most improvement came within a week, but orbital surgeons remain scarce outside wealthy countries.
