Gilgit — 958,480 voters cast ballots for Gilgit-Baltistan's assembly.

[Pakistan](country:PK) governs the territory without constitutional integration, limiting the assembly's authority over a region [India](country:IN) and China both contest.

Results will show whether PML-N or PPP leads a region whose status Islamabad has not resolved.
