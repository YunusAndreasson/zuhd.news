Jizan — Houthi missiles set Aramco's Red Sea refinery ablaze.

It hit the outlet [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) built as its Gulf backup.

The kingdom pipes crude overland to the Red Sea precisely so a blocked Strait of Hormuz would not end exports.

The coalition has vowed retaliation, with Brent already at $96.02 as of Sept 1.
