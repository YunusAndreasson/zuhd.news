Abuja — Gas discovered in 1973 finally has $800 million behind it.

[Nigeria](country:NG) holds one of Africa's largest gas reserves, yet significant volumes remain undeveloped.

Ima would feed Nigeria LNG Limited, with the presidency projecting 300 million standard cubic feet a day at peak.

Gas minister Ekperikpe Ekpo puts start-up at 2028, with 350 million standard cubic feet a day.
