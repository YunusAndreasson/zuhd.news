Abu Dhabi — [France](country:FR) gets 200,000 more tonnes of diesel monthly, its presidency said.

A weekend of violent protests over soaring living costs preceded the deal.

Brent crude stood at $111.10 on 19 October, up 5.2% in 7 days, as Emmanuel Macron tours Gulf suppliers.

The presidency did not say when cargoes arrive or what share of French demand they cover.
