A Petronas veteran is tokenizing crude oil. Each LITRO token represents 1 litre of physical crude, replacing paper contracts that lock capital for up to 90 days in the $6 trillion market. Testnet runs March through May before a January 2027 launch, as Hormuz disruption has already broken traditional oil settlement infrastructure.
