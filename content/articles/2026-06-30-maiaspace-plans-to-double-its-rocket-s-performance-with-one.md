Paris — One added engine would double Europe's Maia rocket payload.

ArianeGroup subsidiary MaiaSpace says a 4th Prometheus engine lifts capacity to 8 tonnes to low Earth orbit without raising average launch cost.

The upgrade depends on market demand after Maia's 3-engine debut, planned for early 2027.
