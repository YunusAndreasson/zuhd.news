26 Chinese military aircraft entered Taiwan's defense zone on Saturday. The previous 2 weeks had seen a sharp drop in flights, coinciding with US carriers shifting to Gulf operations. Washington has not said whether it will restore Pacific deterrence as long as its carriers remain in the Gulf.
