Taipei — Nvidia entered the PC market with RTX Spark.

The RTX Spark system-on-chip packs a Blackwell GPU and 1 petaflop of AI compute into Windows laptops from 5 PC makers, shipping fall 2026.

Pricing is unset; the chip ends Qualcomm's exclusive hold on ARM-based Windows devices.
