London — Algorithm cuts microgrid daily costs up to 10%.

Hybrid battery-hydrogen microgrids create scheduling problems standard solvers can't solve; the algorithm cut mean daily costs 4–10% across test runs, researchers said.

The study used simulation; deployment on a live grid would test whether gains hold under real-time uncertainty.
