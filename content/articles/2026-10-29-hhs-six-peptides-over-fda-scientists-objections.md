United States — The US health department lets pharmacies compound 6 experimental peptides, including BPC-157.

Career scientists at the US drug regulator say there is scant evidence the substances are safe or effective.

Licensed pharmacies and physicians can compound the peptides, including TB-500, without fear of federal enforcement for now.

The allowance is temporary, and the scientists' objections stand.
