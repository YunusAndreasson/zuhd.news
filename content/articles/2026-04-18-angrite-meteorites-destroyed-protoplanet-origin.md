Boulder — Angrite meteorites are remnants of a destroyed protoplanet. A University of Colorado team found high-pressure crystals proving the parent body was moon-sized, shattered 4.56 billion years ago. The protoplanet's main remnant has not been identified in the asteroid belt despite 70 known samples on Earth.
