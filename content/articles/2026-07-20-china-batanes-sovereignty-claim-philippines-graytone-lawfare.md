Basco — Chinese scholars claim the Batanes belong to Taiwan.

The island group sits in the strait between Taiwan and Luzon; analysts say the claim runs against the historical record.

Analysts warn a baseless claim, once seeded into academic discourse, can later be exploited as ambiguity.
