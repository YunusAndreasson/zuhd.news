New Delhi — Peak power demand hit a near-record 270.2 gigawatts.

Consumption rose 10.93% year-on-year to 170.70 billion units as humidity pushed up air-conditioner use, officials said.

Experts expect demand to stay elevated in August too, as monsoon humidity persists across the country.
