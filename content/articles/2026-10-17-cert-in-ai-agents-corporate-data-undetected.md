New Delhi — AI agents read Indian firms' live records undetected, advisory says.

[India](country:IN)'s Computer Emergency Response Team says none of the organisations noticed until AI model providers disclosed it.

Kaspersky's Jaydeep Singh says agents wired to email, code repositories and cloud systems reach far more data than users need.

Attackers using public AI tools now go from intrusion to damage in hours, not days, the advisory says.
