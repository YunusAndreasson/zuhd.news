Redmond — Microsoft's 24-hour outage hit governments in [Canada](country:CA), the US, Australia and Japan.

Hospitals, trading firms and airlines also lost email and Microsoft 365 tools.

Network strain, not AI or a cyberattack, caused the 12-13 November failure of Exchange Online, Outlook and Teams, Microsoft said.

Executive Charles Lamanna told a review the component had "roughly ten years' worth of acceptable failures," a memo showed.

Microsoft has declared Code Red and says reliability is now its top priority.
