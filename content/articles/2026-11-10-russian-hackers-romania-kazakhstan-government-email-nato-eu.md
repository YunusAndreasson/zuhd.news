Romania — Russian hackers intercepted government mail with NATO and the EU.

Researchers found the toolkit on a [Kazakhstan](country:KZ) government server in November 2025, then in Romania.

ESET says Turla, linked to [Russia](country:RU)'s FSB, used the victims' own Exchange servers as command hubs, leaving no external servers to block.

ESET could not determine how the attackers got in or how much they stole.
