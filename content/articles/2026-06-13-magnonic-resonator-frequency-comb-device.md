London — 350 comb lines in a chip-scale magnon resonator.

Frequency combs underpin atomic clocks and spectroscopy; generating one in spin-wave hardware opens chip-scale microwave processors without optical components.

Applications in neuromorphic computing and precision sensing are years away; the result is a foundational device demonstration.
