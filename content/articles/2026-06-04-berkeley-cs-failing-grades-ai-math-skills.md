Berkeley — Spring CS 10 fail rates tripled; AI overreliance blamed.

35.3% of students failed — triple prior years. Instructors said AI offloading left skills absent at the exams where language models are barred.

30 students were caught cheating in one class; the department has set no corrective exam policy.
