Meta's glasses send nude footage to Nairobi workers. A Swedish investigation found workers at Nairobi contractor Sama annotate footage from 7 million Ray-Ban glasses to train Meta's AI. Meta is developing facial recognition for the same glasses, prompting regulatory inquiries from Italy and Ireland.
