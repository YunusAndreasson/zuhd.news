Paris — France's Future Combat Air System with Germany has collapsed.

It leaves [France](country:FR) racing to keep its air force credible into the 2030s alone.

Dassault and Airbus could not agree who would lead the design and own the intellectual property.

The upgraded Rafale F5 enters service around 2033, arming export buyers like [Egypt](country:EG) and Qatar.
