Abuja — One federal force has policed [Nigeria](country:NG) since 1966.

Two former defence chiefs joined the push after both legislative chambers passed amendments adding state police to Nigeria's single federal force.

Ratification needs two-thirds of the 36 state assemblies before the president can sign it.
