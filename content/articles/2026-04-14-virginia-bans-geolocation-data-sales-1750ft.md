Richmond — Virginia banned selling location data within 1,750 feet. The threshold is large enough to cover homes, workplaces, and mosques — the first distance-based location privacy rule in the US. Other states are modeling legislation on it; no federal equivalent exists, leaving the distance threshold as the US ceiling.
