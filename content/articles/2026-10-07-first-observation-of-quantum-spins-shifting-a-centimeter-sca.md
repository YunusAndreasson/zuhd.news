Okinawa — Electron spin from a green laser moved a levitating diamond.

The Okinawa Institute team calls it the first quantum effect directly moving an object under gravity.

The laser shifts spin states in the centimeter-wide diamond's nitrogen-vacancy centers, pushing it toward a magnet while an interferometer tracks it.

The authors say it could lead to sensors for dark matter and gravitational waves.
