The UAE publicly demanded Iran stop striking Gulf states. State Minister Lana Nusseibeh told the BBC more than 1,800 projectiles have struck UAE territory — including near Dubai International Airport. Nusseibeh neither confirmed nor denied retaliatory strikes, invoking "the right to collective self-defence under international law."
