Paris — The [US](country:US) military already owns more than 500 OneWeb terminals.

It is the Space Force's second Warrior Plans order, after one to SpaceX.

The contract covers OneWeb low-orbit connectivity, alternative positioning, navigation and timing, and up to 1.8 petabytes of shared data.

Only $6.1 million is committed so far, procurement records show, against a ceiling of $64.46 million.

Eutelsat's new €600 million bonds carry 7.75% interest, replacing 1.5% debt due in 2028.
