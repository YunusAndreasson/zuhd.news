Kathmandu — Nepal declared trails open with 4,500 still missing.

Tourism guides and lodges depend on ten weeks in autumn for their annual income.

Tourism supplies most of [Nepal](country:NP)'s foreign-exchange earnings outside remittances, forcing recovery and reopening at once.

Rescuers are still racing to reach about 100 trapped hydropower workers.
