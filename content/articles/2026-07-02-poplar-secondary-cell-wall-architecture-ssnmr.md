London — 13 poplar genotypes, one conserved molecular scaffold.

Solid-state NMR and molecular modeling show wood's cellulose-and-matrix architecture stays structurally fixed across genetically diverse trees, researchers report.

The mapped structure could guide engineering of stronger biomaterials and more efficient plant-fiber processing.
