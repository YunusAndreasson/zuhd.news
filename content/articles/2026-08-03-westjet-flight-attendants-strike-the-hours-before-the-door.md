Calgary — 4,400 flight attendants walked out mid-holiday weekend.

The union wants pay from check-in to clock-out, not just from when the aircraft moves; WestJet has cancelled 615 flights, stranding 250,000 travelers.

[Canada](country:CA)'s jobs minister has not invoked labour code powers used to force last year's rail and port disputes to arbitration.
