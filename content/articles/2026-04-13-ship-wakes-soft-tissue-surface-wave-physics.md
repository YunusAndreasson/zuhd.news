Cambridge — A boat wake appears in living tissue too. Harvard researchers found that pressure moving through ultrasoft elastic material creates the same V-shaped wake as a ship's hull. The finding unifies fluid and solid wave physics, opening applications in ultrasound imaging and tissue diagnostics.
