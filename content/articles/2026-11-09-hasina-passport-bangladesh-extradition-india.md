Dhaka — Sheikh Hasina applied for a Bangladeshi passport and identity card.

[Bangladesh](country:BD) wants [India](country:IN) to extradite her under the treaty, not issue new papers.

A tribunal sentenced her to death in absentia in November 2025 over a crackdown that killed up to 1,400, UN estimates say.

Hasina told Reuters she intends to surrender before a court on arrival.

Her aides say she returns in December, with the date announced once formalities finish.
