Bolivia — Washington sanctioned [Bolivia](country:BO)'s attorney general 2 days before his arrest.

Drop Site News reports Roger Mariaca had been investigating Fernando Cerimedo, a top aide to President Rodrigo Paz.

Charges centre on his ties to trafficker Sebastián Marset, and the [US](country:US) alleged he took bribes to facilitate narcotrafficking.

Presidential spokesman José Luis Gálvez calls the timing a coincidence, saying the probe dates to Marset's March arrest.

Nadia Beller, who accused Cerimedo, told Drop Site she expects arrest as soon as Friday.
