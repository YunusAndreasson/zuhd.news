Ziarat — Two motorcyclists were shot dead riding to Quetta.

No group has claimed the killings, leaving [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Balochistan province short of an answer.

The same day, a bomb hit a Frontier Corps vehicle in Turbat, injuring five during Independence Day security duty.

Police have yet to determine what caused the Turbat blast or who was behind it.
