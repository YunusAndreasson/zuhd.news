Bahrain arrested 5 Pakistanis for filming Iranian drone strikes. The kafala system ties migrant workers' legal status to their employer — they cannot leave or document what they witness without risking arrest. Pakistan has announced no legal assistance for the 5 workers; no release date has been set.
