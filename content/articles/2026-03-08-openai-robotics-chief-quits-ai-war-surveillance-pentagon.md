OpenAI's robotics chief quit over a Pentagon contract. Caitlin Kalinowski said the deal enables surveillance without judicial oversight and lethal autonomy without human authorization, calling it "rushed without guardrails." The contract remains in force with no timeline for the safeguards Kalinowski demanded.
