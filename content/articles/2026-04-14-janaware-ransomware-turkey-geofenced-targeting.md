Ankara — JanaWare ransomware runs only on Turkish machines. Active since 2020 and undetected until now, it checks system locale and external IP before executing, skipping every non-Turkish machine. Researchers have not identified the operators; whether the targeting serves political, competitive, or extortion motives remains open.
