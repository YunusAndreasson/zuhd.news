Johannesburg — The [UAE](country:AE)'s Adnoc pays $1bn for 580 stations.

It's Adnoc's fourth foreign market, following stakes in [Egypt](country:EG) and Saudi Arabia, as Gulf firms buy assets Western majors are shedding.

The deal, expected to close next year, ends Shell's century-long presence in [South Africa](country:ZA)'s fuel market.
