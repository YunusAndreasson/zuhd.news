Abuja — Funds Tinubu approved have sat frozen for over a week.

Permanent secretary Binyerem Ukaire withheld payments to 5 [Nigerian](country:NG) media agencies after their heads declined to appear before her, sources say.

Minister Mohammed Idris has intervened without success; the file remains unsigned.
