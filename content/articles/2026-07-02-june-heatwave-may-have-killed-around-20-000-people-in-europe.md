Paris — Up to 25,000 died.

Christopher Callahan of Indiana University estimates the 22-28 June heatwave killed 20,390 across [Europe](country:FR), inferred statistically since heat rarely appears on death certificates.

WHO's confirmed count is just 1,300 so far; Raquel Nunes says the true toll won't be known for months, and most deaths remain preventable.
