Guatemala City — 7 police officers are arrested, yet no killer is named.

Gunmen killed ex-prosecutor Juan Francisco Solórzano Foppa and a judo instructor on 17 October, wounding 5 others.

Seven officers were arrested 23 October for allegedly planting a gun on the first two suspects, the Choc Toz brothers.

His uncle Julio Solórzano Foppa calls the killing political and says it aims to spread fear.

A court postponed the officers' first hearing to Tuesday.
