Massachusetts — 16 qubits produced particles that are neither boson nor fermion.

The result challenges a decades-old assumption that every quantum particle is a boson or a fermion.

Pairs of superconducting qubits, linked like ladder rungs, behaved as paraparticles in swap, collision and pile-up tests.

"They've seen emergent paraparticles," said Kaden Hazzard, a Rice University physicist who co-wrote the 2025 theory.

Max Hays's team next plans paraparticles that interact with each other, in more complicated geometries.
