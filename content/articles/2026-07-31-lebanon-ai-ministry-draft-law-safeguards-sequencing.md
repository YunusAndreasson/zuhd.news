Beirut — Parliament sent the draft AI ministry law back for review.

[Lebanon](country:LB) has no data-protection law or independent regulator, yet the bill would hand one ministry broad, largely unchecked power over AI, SMEX said.

Joint committees now set the timeline; digital-rights groups want privacy safeguards written in before any vote.
