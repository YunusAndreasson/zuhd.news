Manila — Chinese fishermen poured cyanide into Spratly reefs. Manila's National Security Council says poisoning began last year at Second Thomas Shoal, killing coral and depriving Navy personnel of their food source. Beijing has not formally responded; the shoal already saw axe-wielding Chinese coastguard board Philippine Navy boats in 2024.
