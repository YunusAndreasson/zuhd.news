Istanbul's Saraçhane square has become a weekly arrest site. Turkish police detained 35 at a Wednesday rally backing Mayor Ekrem İmamoğlu, who has been jailed for a year. 4 of those detained face charges not yet announced, a test of Turkey's constitutional right to assembly.
