Nottingham — Blocking Earth's magnetism extended sick fruit flies' lives by 20%.

Healthy flies died sooner but moved better, the reverse of the Parkinson's-model flies.

Pink1 mutants carry a defect linked to early-onset Parkinson's that disrupts mitochondria, the cells' energy generators.

"I was really confused at first," said Jacob Reed, the Nottingham PhD student who co-led the study.

The study, published in Aging, tested only fruit flies, so the risk to future space travellers is untested.
