Navi Mumbai — Navi Mumbai airport opens internationally July 15.

The Adani-built greenfield airport debuts with 2 weekly [UAE](country:AE) flights, giving [India](country:IN)'s Mumbai metropolitan region a second gateway to the Gulf migrant corridor.

Frequency scales to 3 weekly from July 29; Bengaluru and Delhi domestic routes are already active from the airport.
