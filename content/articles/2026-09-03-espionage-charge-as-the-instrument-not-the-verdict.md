Astana — Dr Ikboljon Qoraboyev now faces espionage charges.

Colleagues call it a warning to every foreign scholar [Kazakhstan](country:KZ) recruited into its universities.

An espionage charge moves proceedings behind classified evidence, unlike lesser charges that stay open to a public defence.

Kazakhstan's security service has not disclosed what evidence it holds against him.
