Brussels — €890 million, split across two rulings.

The European Commission found Google ranked its own shopping and travel results above rivals' on search, and blocked app developers from steering Play users to cheaper offers elsewhere.

Google has 60 days to comply or face daily penalties; it says it may take the case to court.
