Asadabad — 7 dead, more than 90 wounded in a school explosion.

Students were among the dead and wounded, Kunar's police spokesperson said.

[Afghanistan](country:AF)'s Kunar information director said the blast appeared to come from a neighbouring depot storing explosives for mining companies.

No group has claimed responsibility, and investigators are still examining the cause.

Up to 15 of the wounded remain in hospital, the director said.
