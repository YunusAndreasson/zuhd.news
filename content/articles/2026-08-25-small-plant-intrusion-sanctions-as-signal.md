Washington — The [US](country:US) sanctioned Iranian nationals after a small UK power plant intrusion surfaced.

Minor generators and water utilities, run by skeleton crews, sit exposed to a conflict they have no part in.

A named-individual sanction freezes assets in jurisdictions the target never enters, signaling attribution, not blocking capability.

Whether the UK confirms the intrusion officially remains open.
