Paris — France admits military personnel are working inside Ukraine.

[France](country:FR) had trained Ukrainian troops only at home and in Poland, to avoid direct conflict with [Russia](country:RU).

Foreign Minister Jean-Noël Barrot says staff do training and equipment maintenance far from the front, which he says is not co-belligerence.

Candidate Florian Philippot says the government broke article 35 by not notifying parliament, Russian state news agency TASS reported.
