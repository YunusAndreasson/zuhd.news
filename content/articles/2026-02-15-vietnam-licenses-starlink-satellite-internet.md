600,000 satellite terminals can now operate in Vietnam. The country's Radio Frequency Management authority granted Starlink a licence for 4 gateway stations, opening satellite internet to rural and mountainous areas where roughly 20 million of Vietnam's 100 million people lack coverage. Starlink will compete with state-backed carriers in a country whose government tightly controls terrestrial internet infrastructure.
