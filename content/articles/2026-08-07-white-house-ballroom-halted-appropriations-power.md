Washington — A court just halted Trump's privately funded ballroom.

A federal appeals court ruled 2-1 that reshaping the building requires congressional approval, halting above-ground work on Trump's $400 million ballroom.

The ruling is stayed two weeks while the administration appeals to the Supreme Court.
