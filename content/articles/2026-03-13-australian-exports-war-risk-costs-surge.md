Red Sea closure is costing Australian exporters $4,500 per container. Shipping lines imposed war-risk surcharges after the Red Sea closed again to commercial traffic, forcing vessels around the Cape of Good Hope and stranding wool bound for Italian fashion houses at anchor. Economists warned the costs will pass to consumers, pushing inflation higher and making interest rate rises more likely.
