London — The £108 gas standing charge disappears for 23 million homes.

The Treasury says 60% of households would save an average £45 a year.

A standing charge is a fixed daily fee paid even by a household using no gas, and its costs would move onto the per-unit price.

Chancellor John Healey will ask Ofgem to remove it in 2027 or soon after.
