Minenhle Makhanya must repay R147 million.

The judge said he "clearly did not act alone," yet stands alone as defendant.

A civil tribunal in [South Africa](country:ZA) can order repayment on a balance of probabilities, not proof beyond reasonable doubt.

Officials who signed the requisitions face no findings, and Makhanya can still appeal.
