Cape Town — [South Africa](country:ZA)'s fuel levy falls until January 2027.

Opposition parties call Finance Minister Enoch Godongwana's cut electioneering before the 4 November vote.

The cut lowers petrol and diesel prices as Brent crude, $108.60 on 20 October, gains 2.4% in a week.

African National Congress Secretary-General Fikile Mbalula says the cut puts money back in working families' pockets.

[South Africa](country:ZA)'s President Ramaphosa says more allocations will follow in the February 2027 budget.
