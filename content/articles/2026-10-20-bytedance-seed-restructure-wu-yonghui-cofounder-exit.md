China — ByteDance split its model lab into 5 divisions.

ByteDance gave safety its own division, reportedly a first among frontier labs in [China](country:CN).

Divisions cover pretraining data, reinforcement learning, enterprise models, consumer chat models and alignment, all reporting to Wu Yonghui, formerly of Google DeepMind.

Huang Wenhao, who ran model evaluation, has left and may start a company, according to unnamed sources.
