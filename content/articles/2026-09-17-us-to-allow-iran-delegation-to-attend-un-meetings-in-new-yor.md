New York — [Iran](country:IR)'s president received a US visa this week.

The same week, Washington barred the [Palestinian Authority](country:PS)'s president from attending.

A 1947 treaty obliges the [US](country:US), as UN host, to admit member states' delegates except on narrow security grounds.

The Iranian delegation faces travel curbs and a luxury-goods ban through the Sept. 28 session.
