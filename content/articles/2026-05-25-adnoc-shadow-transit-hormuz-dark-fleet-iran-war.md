Abu Dhabi — ADNOC's tankers are crossing Hormuz dark.

[UAE](country:AE)'s state oil company switched tanker transponders off and transferred cargoes ship-to-ship near Fujairah, adopting tactics pioneered by sanctioned [Russia](country:RU) and [Iran](country:IR).

Other Gulf producers are following suit as [US](country:US)-[Iran](country:IR) talks on strait access remain unresolved.
