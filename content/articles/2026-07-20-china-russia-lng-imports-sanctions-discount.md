Beijing — [China](country:CN) bought 28% more Russian LNG this year.

Import value rose just 8.2% to $1.81 billion as volume jumped 27.8%, customs data show — a widening discount on sanctioned gas.

[Russia](country:RU)'s pipeline gas sales to China also topped $4.65 billion in the first half.
