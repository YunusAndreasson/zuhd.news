New York — Polymarket priced the Netanyahu death rumor at 5 cents. The prediction market's judgment proved accurate when US-Israeli strikes killed Iranian Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei — the contract on his death immediately spiked to 100%. A group of US Democratic senators has since introduced legislation to ban prediction market contracts tied to deaths of public figures.
