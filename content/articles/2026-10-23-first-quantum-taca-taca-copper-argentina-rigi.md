Argentina — A $5.25 billion copper mine wants state-backed tax breaks.

[Argentina](country:AR) is courting foreign capital under President Javier Milei to become a bigger copper supplier.

Taca Taca is one of the world's largest undeveloped copper projects, and copper traded at $14,470 a tonne in September, up 6.8% in 3 months.

First Quantum applies to the large-investment incentive regime before year-end, Foreign Minister Pablo Quirno said.
