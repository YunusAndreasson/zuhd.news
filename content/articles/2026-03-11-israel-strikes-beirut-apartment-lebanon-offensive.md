An Israeli airstrike hit an apartment block in central Beirut's Aicha Bakkar neighbourhood Wednesday. 570 people have been killed and 700,000 — one in 10 Lebanese — displaced since Israel's offensive began on March 2. Israel has ordered residents to evacuate southern Beirut, southern Lebanon, and eastern Lebanon ahead of further operations.
