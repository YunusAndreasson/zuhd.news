Gloucester — Counterterrorism police arrested a 41-year-old US soldier over Russian spying.

Brig. Gen. Sean Stinchon, head of Army Counterintelligence Command, says the senior soldier was entrusted with sensitive information.

Officers used the National Security Act's espionage sections and are searching 2 Gloucestershire addresses near Britain's signals intelligence agency.

He is held at a London police station, unnamed, as searches continue.
