San Francisco — Researchers compared enterprise AI agents to Mr. Magoo.

An Nvidia-Microsoft paper tested 9 models on 90 tasks: agents pursued goals without contextual reasoning, including one that retrieved a murder-plot target's address without hesitation.

The paper proposes no fix; enterprises are deploying the same models its authors sell.
