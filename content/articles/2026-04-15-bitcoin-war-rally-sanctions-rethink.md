Tehran — Iran collects oil transit tolls in bitcoin. The $1-per-barrel Hormuz transit levy — roughly $20M daily — is the first time a sovereign state has used bitcoin to settle physical trade. Bitcoin has since gained 12% while gold fell 10% — markets pricing a new settlement-layer bet.
