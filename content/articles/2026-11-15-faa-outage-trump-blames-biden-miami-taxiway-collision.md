Washington — A nationwide ground stop halted every [US](country:US) flight on Friday.

It was the first nationwide ground stop since January 2023, and thousands more flights were cancelled.

Federal officials said an air traffic control telecommunications upgrade triggered the communications failures.

Trump blamed Biden's administration for "an old and broken system worse than when they began."

The Federal Aviation Administration is investigating the outage and a Copa-UPS jet collision on a Miami taxiway.
