Medan — Floods killed 58 of 800 Tapanuli orangutans.

Numbering only 800, the species lost at least 7%; the floods also killed at least 1,200 people in [Indonesia](country:ID), worsened by Sumatra deforestation, the report found.

The study surveyed only one of three forest blocks; the full orangutan death toll remains unconfirmed.
