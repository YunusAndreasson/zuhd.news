Daejeon — 14 workers died in a car parts plant fire. Sodium at the plant created explosion risks that hampered rescue, adding to a toll of over 10,000 worksite deaths South Korea has recorded since 2000 despite being a top-10 economy. Prosecutors have opened an investigation, with a precedent set last year when a battery factory CEO received 15 years for worksite negligence.
