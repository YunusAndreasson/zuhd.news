Washington — Trump called AI-safety warnings a "sick conspiracy."

He rejected calls from OpenAI's Sam Altman, Elon Musk and Anthropic's Dario Amodei to slow development.

Trump says existing law already lets the US prosecute AI harms, framing restraint as ceding ground to [China](country:CN).

Bernie Sanders and Steve Bannon share a Washington stage Tuesday to demand restrictions.
