Leipzig — Prehistoric graves held chosen, not just blood, family. Ancient DNA analysis of thousands-year-old burials shows step-parents, adoptees, and social relatives buried with the same honor as biological kin. Researchers are now mapping how often chosen and biological family overlapped across cultures and regions.
