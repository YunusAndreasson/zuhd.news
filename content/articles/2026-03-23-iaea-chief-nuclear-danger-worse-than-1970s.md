Vienna — Nuclear risk is now worse than the 1970s oil shocks, the UN atomic energy chief warned. Rafael Grossi, director general of the International Atomic Energy Agency, said unlike 1970s embargoes, this war has struck energy facilities on both sides, forcing his emergency mandate to expand into nuclear safety. The agency has no power to compel Hormuz reopening before Trump's 23:44 GMT Monday deadline.
