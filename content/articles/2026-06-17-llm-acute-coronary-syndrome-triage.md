Beijing — A large language model triages cardiac emergencies 39% faster per case.

TriageMaster-70B classifies emergencies from narrative and vital signs before blood tests arrive, validated on 16,428 cases.

The study is retrospective from 4 Chinese hospital sites; authors say it may support rapid triage, not replace it.
