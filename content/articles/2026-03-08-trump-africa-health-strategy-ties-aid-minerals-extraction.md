Health aid to Congo comes with a minerals condition. Washington's strategy ties health funding to mineral rights and data access, replacing the US AIDS relief program PEPFAR with bilateral deals. Zambia's $1 billion deal remains stalled while talks continue across at least 3 other African countries with no deadline set.
