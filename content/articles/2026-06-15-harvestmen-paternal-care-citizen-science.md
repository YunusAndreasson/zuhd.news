London — iNaturalist photos traced paternal care in harvestmen arachnids.

Citizen-science observation data proved powerful enough to reconstruct the evolutionary history of a behavior that field collection alone couldn't cover.

The study raises questions about what evolutionary biology can now extract from open wildlife photo databases.
