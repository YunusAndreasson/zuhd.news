Banja Luka — Savo Minić won 55 of Republika Srpska's 64 municipalities.

Milorad Dodik, barred from office, says his party and allies hold two-thirds of the assembly.

Preliminary results show Croat nationalist Darijana Filipović replacing civic-oriented Željko Komšić on [Bosnia](country:BA)'s three-member presidency.

Coalition talks come next, and historian Dragan Markovina expects the three largest nationalist parties to govern together.
