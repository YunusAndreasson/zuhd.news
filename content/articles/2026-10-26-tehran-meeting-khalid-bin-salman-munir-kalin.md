Tehran — Saudi, Turkish and Pakistani chiefs will visit, Iran's state-run Mehr News Agency reported.

Defence minister Khalid bin Salman, army chief Asim Munir and intelligence chief Ibrahim Kalin would meet.

Deputy security council secretary Ali Bagheri Kani said General Asim Munir announced the trip to council secretary Mohsen Rezaei.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA), [Turkey](country:TR) and [Pakistan](country:PK) had not confirmed the visit in the report.

Bagheri Kani gave no date for the joint meeting.
