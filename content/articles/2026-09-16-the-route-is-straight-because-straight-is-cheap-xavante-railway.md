Geneva — "Let the A'uwẽ-Xavante Keep Dreaming" was projected onto Lake Léman.

The 25,300-strong people say a railway through their territory in [Brazil](country:BR) threatens their existence.

The line runs straight through Ró to cut costs moving soy from the cerrado to northern ports.

The campaign launched at the UN's Indigenous forum, demanding consultation before routes and financing are fixed.
