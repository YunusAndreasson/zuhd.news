Havana — A single substation fault blacked out Cuba.

[Cuba](country:CU)'s national grid runs on aging, fuel-starved plants; Friday's failure at Havana's Victoria de Giron substation tripped two power units nationwide.

Cuba's energy company has not said when full power will return.
