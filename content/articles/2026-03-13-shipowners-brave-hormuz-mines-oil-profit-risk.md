Greek tankers earn $500,000 a day running Iran's blockade. Iran has struck 16 ships in the strait and vows to push oil to $200 a barrel; the US military has refused to escort tankers through. Owners of ships already hit by Iranian drones have not confirmed whether they will attempt another transit.
