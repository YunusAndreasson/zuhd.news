Aliağa — 16 Habaş steel workers are dead and 13 injured, President Erdoğan said.

Roughly 200 workers were on site for furnace replacement work, against 300-plus on regular shifts.

A ceiling crane collapsed into an electric arc furnace and damaged a natural gas line, the governor said.

Earlier counts of 3, 6 and 10 dead rose, and the mayor says the exact toll awaits cleared debris.

[Turkey](country:TR)'s justice minister assigned 4 prosecutors, and Erdoğan promised to establish who was negligent.
