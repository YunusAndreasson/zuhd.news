Houston — An immigration officer admitted on camera hitting a fleeing man.

Mario Alberto Morales faces an aggravated assault charge, rare for federal immigration officers in the [US](country:US).

Harris County District Attorney Sean Teare says surveillance footage contradicts the agency's claim that Miguel Parra-Hernandez, 48, ran in front of the vehicle.

Teare's office still investigates the July 7 fatal shooting of Lorenzo Salgado Araujo, blamed on officer Gregory Feathers.
