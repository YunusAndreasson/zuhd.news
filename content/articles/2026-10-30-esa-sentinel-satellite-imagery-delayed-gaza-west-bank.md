Gaza — Satellite images of Gaza now arrive 24 hours late.

Researchers confirming Israeli army demolitions in Hebron waited a day for images of the city's western half.

The EU says member states unanimously ordered the delay, begun in late March, over threats linked to Iran's attacks.

The EU says the delay is unrelated to events in Gaza.

The EU declined to say whether other areas are delayed.
