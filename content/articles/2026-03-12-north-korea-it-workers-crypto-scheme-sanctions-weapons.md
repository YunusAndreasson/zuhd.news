Pyongyang infiltrated Western tech companies with fake developers. The US Treasury sanctioned 8 individuals for routing crypto earnings from false-identity contracting to North Korea's ballistic missile and nuclear programs. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent pledged to "continue to follow the money" in future sanctions targeting Pyongyang.
