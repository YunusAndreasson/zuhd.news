Israeli forces killed 4 Palestinians in their car. Among the dead was Othman, 6, blind and disabled, shot on his mother's lap as the family drove home in the West Bank from Eid preparations. Israel's army has not responded to the eyewitness account contradicting its claim the car had accelerated toward soldiers.
