Quetta — Two labourers died riding to a tube-well job.

Killing work crews raises the security cost of every project until contractors stop bidding.

The IED hit a contractor's pickup on Wali Tangi road, wounding three, and [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Frontier Corps sealed the site.

Police have not named a suspect or the labourers' home province.
