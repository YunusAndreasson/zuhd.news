Zaporizhzhia — A drone struck [Ukraine](country:UA)'s Zaporizhzhia nuclear plant turbine hall Saturday.

Rosatom's CEO called it the first deliberate strike on core machinery; the company said primary equipment survived but a wall was breached.

The International Atomic Energy Agency warns combat near the site risks an uncontrollable accident.
