Brasília — 45 senators voted for the government's fiscal adjustment bill.

Transfers to states and municipalities drop from R$4 billion to R$1 billion.

Planning Minister Bruno Moretti says the bill, which lets [Brazil](country:BR)'s government block spending, could cut 2027 outlays by R$60 billion.

Deputy-elect Ana Elisa of the governing Workers' Party says the bill sacrifices the population for financial markets.

Senators vote on 4 amendments on 17 November, then Hugo Motta fast-tracks the bill in the Chamber.
