Pakistani commodity traders suspended trade as shipping costs surged. 80% of Pakistan's crude transits Hormuz — Iran's blockade has doubled freight rates and added war-risk premiums making imports unviable. Industry groups forecast a 10-20% textile export drop in March; some producers hold only 15 days of inventory.
