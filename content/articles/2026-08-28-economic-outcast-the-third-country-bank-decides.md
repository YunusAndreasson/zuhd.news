Tehran — The [US](country:US) unveiled "Operation Economic Outcast" Monday.

Third-country banks, not US officials, now decide whether Iranian trade survives.

Washington's reach comes from secondary sanctions, so banks abroad often exit a country rather than assess each transaction.

[Iran](country:IR)'s foreign ministry, which calls the sanctions "state terrorism," wants states to reject them outright.
