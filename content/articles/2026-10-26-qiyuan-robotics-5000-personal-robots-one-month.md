China — Qiyuan Robotics now builds one personal robot every 2.5 minutes.

Its 5,000th robot left the line 25 October, the company said, a month after launch.

Qiyuan built a supplier-fed materials warehouse and semi-automated lines, lifting output from about 10 robots a day to over 200.

Qiyuan plans an over-the-air update at the end of October adding children's English practice and remote video calls.
