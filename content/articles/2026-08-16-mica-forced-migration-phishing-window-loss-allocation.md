Paris — 10 million crypto users got a move deadline.

Scammers impersonated the deadline itself, costing careless movers money banks won't refund.

Only 323 of 1,700-plus platforms held valid EU licenses when the mandatory migration began, leaving a gap fraudsters filled.

France's markets regulator is now fielding fake-recovery-fee scams from victims.
