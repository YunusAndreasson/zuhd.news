Sheikhupura — Police allegedly raped a woman inside their station.

Over 70 staff were suspended after the assistant sub-inspector brought her, 20 and mentally impaired, to an empty upper-floor room.

A criminal complaint under Section 376 implicates the officer; her father's report led [Pakistan](country:PK) police to the room.
