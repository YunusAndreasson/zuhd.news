Hong Kong — 3 major banks test 24-hour cross-border payments.

Banks want stablecoin speed without losing deposits to private issuers.

Tokenized deposits record bank-account money on a blockchain, so it stays a bank liability, unlike stablecoins issued by private companies.

Swift's ledger is to connect with Project Ensemble, Hong Kong's settlement platform, though the pilot remains exploratory.
