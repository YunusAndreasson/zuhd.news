United States — A 26,000-word post reopened AI interpretability.

Goodfire researcher Nick Cammarata says language models may be easier to monitor and control than assumed.

Cammarata says Google's 2014 Inception v1 has a car-and-cat neuron that tracks a V shape, not two unrelated things.

Jacob Hilton of the Alignment Research Center says researchers understand less than 0.01% of top models' inner workings.

His team has not yet published technical results on modern language models but plans to soon.
