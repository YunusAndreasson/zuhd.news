9 Palestinians killed in one day. Israeli forces struck a tent camp housing displaced families in northern Gaza, killing 4, while a separate airstrike killed 5 in Khan Younis. The Israeli military, whose actions in Gaza the International Court of Justice has called plausibly genocidal, said it targeted Hamas fighters who emerged from a tunnel, while Palestinian health officials reported more than 600 killed by Israeli fire since the ceasefire began.
