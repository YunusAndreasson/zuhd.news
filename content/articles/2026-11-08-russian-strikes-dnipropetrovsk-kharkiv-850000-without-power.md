Dnipro — About 850,000 people lost power after Russian strikes.

Winter is arriving, and the energy provider DTEK says repeated attacks are slowing repairs.

More than 600,000 are in [Ukraine](country:UA)'s Dnipropetrovsk region after one facility was hit, and 250,000 more in Kharkiv region.

[Russia](country:RU)'s defence ministry said it will keep striking energy sites it says aid Ukraine's army.

President Zelenskyy says Russia is escalating to pressure Kyiv before US-brokered talks in Abu Dhabi on Tuesday and Wednesday.
