London — Risk curves sharply upward past one threshold.

Researchers tracked 15,464 nondiabetic adults and found body roundness — a measure of waist size relative to height — nonlinearly predicted steatotic liver disease.

The manuscript is unedited and early-access; researchers call the finding observational, not proof of cause.
