The Sun's 2008–2009 quiet phase produced anomalous vibrations. A helium ionization layer ran hotter than usual, causing trapped sound waves to travel faster and shift the Sun's oscillation pattern. The PLATO space mission, launching in 2027, will use the same method to study oscillations in other stars.
