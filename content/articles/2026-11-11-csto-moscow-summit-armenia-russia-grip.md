Moscow — [Armenia](country:AM) loses voting rights in Russia-led bloc over unpaid dues.

Armenia froze its participation in February 2024, blaming the bloc for no meaningful support.

Neither [Russia](country:RU) nor the other members deployed troops to Armenia in 2020, though the bloc's charter envisages aid.

Members will "keep mum or watch how a bad student is being whipped in class," said expert Galiya Ibragimova.

[Tajikistan](country:TJ) takes the chairmanship from Russia next year, TASS reported.
