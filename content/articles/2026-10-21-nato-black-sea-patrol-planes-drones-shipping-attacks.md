Brussels — At least 23 sailors are missing or dead.

Drone strikes on merchant ships off [Romania](country:RO) and [Bulgaria](country:BG) are the latest spillover from [Russia](country:RU)'s war on Ukraine.

NATO is sending patrol aircraft, radars, drones and forensic teams to trace the explosives, but has not blamed any country.

Bulgaria's Prime Minister Rumen Radev says a US P-8 Poseidon already patrols and Sofia expects more help.
