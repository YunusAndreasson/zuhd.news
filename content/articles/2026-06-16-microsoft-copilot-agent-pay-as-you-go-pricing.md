Seattle — Microsoft abandoned its flat-fee model for AI software.

Copilot Cowork bills per computing unit consumed, breaking the per-seat licence Microsoft has used for 20 years, the company said.

Enterprises face variable costs; Microsoft's GitHub subsidiary moved to usage billing in June and triggered developer backlash.
