Paris — Patents cite open-access papers 1.38 times more than expected.

Counting Sci-Hub copies, freely available papers appear 3.41 times more often in patents than in the literature.

Researchers matched almost 500,000 patents to the papers they cite, normalising for field differences in open-access publishing.

Cornell's Matt Marx cautions that inventors may cite papers they can read, or that important papers are likelier to be open.

Only large firms and universities can staff contract talks with thousands of publishers, [France](country:FR)'s Marin Dacos said.
