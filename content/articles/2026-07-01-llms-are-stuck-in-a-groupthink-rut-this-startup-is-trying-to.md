Sydney — Ask any chatbot for a random number: it's 7.

Researchers found 25 language models converge on near-identical answers because most train on similar data for similar tasks, a paper that won a top AI award found.

Startup Springboards built a rival model that injects randomness at chosen points, betting variety beats the average.
