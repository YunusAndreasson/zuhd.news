Iran fired 2 ballistic missiles at Diego Garcia. At over 3,000 km from Iran, the base hosts B-2 bombers and is the farthest US asset Iran has targeted in the war. A US warship fired an SM-3 interceptor but the Pentagon has not confirmed if it connected.
