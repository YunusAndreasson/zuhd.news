Mexico City — AT&T sells 24.7 million Mexican subscribers for $2 billion.

Grupo Televisa gains [Mexico](country:MX)'s second-largest mobile operator and can bundle it with its broadband.

AT&T built the network in 2015 from Iusacell and Nextel Mexico, and the buyers will use cash and debt.

Mexican regulators must approve the deal, which is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027.
