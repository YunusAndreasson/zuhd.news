Ekombitie — Farmers must now geolocate every cocoa plot.

The compliance cost lands on smallholders, while distant buyers still set the price.

The EU's deforestation regulation requires growers to document land use before next year's import deadline.

Farmers want compensation for the added costs, and buyers have not responded.
