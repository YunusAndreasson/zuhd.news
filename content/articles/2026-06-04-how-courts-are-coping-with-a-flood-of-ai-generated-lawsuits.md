Denver — Self-represented [US](country:US) litigants are filing AI-drafted briefs.

AI-drafted content in pro se filings rose from 1% to 18% in 3 years, a study of 4.5 million federal cases found.

Nippon Life Insurance sued OpenAI claiming ChatGPT practiced law without a license; that case is pending.
