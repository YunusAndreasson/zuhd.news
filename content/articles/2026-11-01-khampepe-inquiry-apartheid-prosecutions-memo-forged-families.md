Johannesburg — A handwritten index dates a disputed memo to 2003.

It backs Anton Ackermann's claim that a 2006 memo painting him as rogue was forged.

Lawyers for victims' families inspected the files on 24 June after evidence leaders had not shared Ackermann's index.

The commission has not ruled, and the hearing is adjourned to a date yet to be announced.
