Islamabad — Police beat activist Mavra Bari at a Women's Day march. Pakistani law lets authorities invoke national security to detain protesters at independent gatherings while state-aligned events proceed freely. Rights groups are demanding the release of all detainees, including Imaan Mazari, a lawyer jailed for social media posts.
