St. Petersburg — Ukraine struck Primorsk — Russia's main Baltic oil terminal. Ukraine targets oil infrastructure to cut the Kremlin's war revenue — the 2 suspended Baltic ports handle 1.7 million barrels daily. The strike adds a second supply shock to oil markets already spiking from Iran's Hormuz closure.
