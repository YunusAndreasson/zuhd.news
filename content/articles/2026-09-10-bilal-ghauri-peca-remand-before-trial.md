Islamabad — A magistrate extended journalist Bilal Ghauri's police custody by 2 more days.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s 2025 cybercrime law criminalizes "false" content without requiring proof of harm.

The cybercrime agency already holds his phone, laptop and accounts, yet keeps seeking more time.

Ghauri returns to court September 12, with prosecutors yet to specify what content was false.
