Lahore — [China](country:CN) will train 300,000 Pakistani youths yearly.

Planning minister Ahsan Iqbal said the training aims to grow [Pakistan](country:PK)'s freelance economy, already the world's fourth-largest, and its IT exports.

Punjab is racing to fix international payment barriers so freelancers can access their earnings.
