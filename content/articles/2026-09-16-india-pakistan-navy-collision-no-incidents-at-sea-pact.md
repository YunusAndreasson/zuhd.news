New Delhi — A Pakistani warship collided with an Indian navy vessel.

The two nuclear-armed neighbours now have no crisis hotline to draw on.

[India](country:IN) says the collision breached a 1991 pact requiring warships stay 3 nautical miles apart in international waters.

New Delhi summoned [Pakistan](country:PK)'s envoy over the incident, and Islamabad has not yet responded publicly.
