San Francisco — Amazon faces fraud lawsuits for labeling revocable digital licenses "Buy."

US courts ruled first-sale doctrine doesn't apply to digital files; a platform's closure or policy change can erase an entire library with no recourse.

Courts have not yet required Amazon or any platform to restore access or refund purchases.
