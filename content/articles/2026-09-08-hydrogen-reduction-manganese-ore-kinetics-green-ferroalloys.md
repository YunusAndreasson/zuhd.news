London — Zambian ore converted up to 97% under hydrogen.

Steelmaking still relies on carbon-emitting coke to produce nearly all manganese today.

The reaction rate is limited by diffusion through each particle's product layer, not simply thermodynamics.

The study is lab-scale, so industrial furnace design would still need to follow.
