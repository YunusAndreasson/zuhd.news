$2.2 million buys a sickle cell cure — per patient. Each dose requires custom-built cells in facilities costing millions to construct; none exist in sub-Saharan Africa, where 80% of cases occur. Uganda launched free newborn screening this month, diagnosing children for a disease whose cure they cannot afford.
