Power cuts have stopped Benghazi's water wells.

Households across eastern Libya lose drinking water within days of blackouts.

The Great Man-Made River's wells depend entirely on grid power, since [Libya](country:LY) has almost no surface water.

Rebuilding failed substations depends on a revenue-sharing dispute between Tripoli and eastern authorities.
