Santa Clara — Nvidia futures fell nearly 12% on reports the board may oust Jensen Huang.

Huang co-founded Nvidia more than three decades ago and has led it since.

Perpetual futures trade around the clock, so Hyperliquid priced the news at $160.99 before the Nasdaq reopened.

Nvidia says it has no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised.

Directors can remove Huang by majority vote, and none has announced a decision.
