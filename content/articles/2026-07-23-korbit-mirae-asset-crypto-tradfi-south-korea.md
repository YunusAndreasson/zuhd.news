Seoul — A $1 trillion asset manager now owns 97% of Korbit.

Mirae Asset Consulting became the top shareholder of [South Korea](country:KR)'s first crypto exchange, founded in 2013, folding it into a regulated finance conglomerate.

User trading, deposits and withdrawals continue unchanged under the new ownership.
