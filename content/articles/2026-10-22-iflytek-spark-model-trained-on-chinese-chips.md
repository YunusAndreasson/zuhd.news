China — iFlytek cut one training run from 4.5 months to 1.2.

It says Spark X2.6 ran entirely on [China](country:CN)-made chips and prices input below DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash.

iFlytek says its 10,000-card Feixing-2 cluster lifted cache hit rates from 63% to 95.7% and inference efficiency 4.5 times.

On SWE Verified, iFlytek's own results put it behind DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash.

Chairman Liu Qingfeng says 60% of AI tasks will run locally.
