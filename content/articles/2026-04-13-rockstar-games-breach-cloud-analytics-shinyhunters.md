New York — Rockstar was breached through a third-party analytics tool. ShinyHunters accessed Rockstar's internal systems through a cloud analytics vendor's credentials — not through Rockstar's own security perimeter. The group is demanding a ransom and threatening to release stolen data; Rockstar has not responded.
