Washington — [US](country:US) employers added 165,000 jobs in October, against 65,000 forecast.

Unemployment fell to 4.1%, keeping a December Federal Reserve rate hike in play.

Traders had cut hike odds to about 70% from roughly 95% weeks ago as stocks fell.

The Bureau of Labor Statistics releases US consumer price data on 10 November.
