London — Ten-micron gel posts bend under laser light.

No wires or motors are needed to move objects this small.

Light-sensitive azobenzene molecules flip shape under illumination and bend along whichever direction the polarized beam is rotated.

The authors call it a lab demonstration for microrobotics, with no real application yet validated.
