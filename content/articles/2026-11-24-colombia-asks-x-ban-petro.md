Bogotá — One video of Cali protesters prompted two bids to ban Petro.

[Colombia](country:CO)'s information ministry accuses ex-President Gustavo Petro of inciting violence against President Abelardo de la Espriella's government.

Petro posted the video Saturday, and Florida Congresswoman Maria Elvira Salazar followed the ministry with a letter to Elon Musk.

Lawmaker Maria del Mar Pizarro of Petro's Historic Pact calls the ministry's request anti-democratic.

Musk, who reinstated Donald Trump's banned account in 2022, decides whether X acts.
