Pakistan and Afghanistan traded airstrikes again this week. China's foreign minister Wang Yi urged direct talks between Islamabad and Kabul, with Beijing positioned as the only power with economic ties to both — CPEC financing in Pakistan and mineral interests in Afghanistan. Wang Yi set no deadline, leaving open whether either government will negotiate before the next escalation.
