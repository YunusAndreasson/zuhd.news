Rmeish — Israeli soldiers turned back an aid convoy at a swing gate.

Only 3 Maronite Christian villages still stand inside [Israel](country:IL)'s occupied buffer zone, cut off from [Lebanon](country:LB).

Aid staff must request Israeli permission through a ceasefire monitoring body to cross the so-called yellow line.

Israel and Lebanon hold their next round of talks with no set date.
