New York — AI treats the genome as text; it isn't.

Sequence-trained models can't see how DNA folds in three dimensions — the physical configuration that determines which genes activate and when.

No training set yet captures 3D folding; researchers say current AI genome models carry a structural blind spot that sequence data cannot resolve.
