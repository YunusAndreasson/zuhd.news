Tehran — A 4th Hormuz tanker burns, Iranian state news agency Mehr reported.

It carried over 2 million barrels, with Brent crude at $117.6 as of 30 October.

[Iran](country:IR)'s Revolutionary Guards Navy says the ship ignored radio warnings, took the unauthorised southern route and struck a naval mine.

Nothing in the report names the ship or its flag, and no other source confirms the fire.

The test is whether tankers keep using the southern route despite the Guards' warnings.
