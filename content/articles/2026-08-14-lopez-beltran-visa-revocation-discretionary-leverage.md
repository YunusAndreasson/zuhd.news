Mexico City — Washington cancelled Andrés Manuel López Beltrán's US visa.

Sheinbaum, president of [Mexico](country:MX), called it foreign interference against a Morena official preparing to run for congress.

A visa revocation needs no charge, no evidence and no public explanation, making it a cheap, unappealable sanction.

The State Department has not commented on the cancellation.
