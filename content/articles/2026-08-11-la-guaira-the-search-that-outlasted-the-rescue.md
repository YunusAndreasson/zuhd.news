La Guaira — Miriam Villalta has waited by rubble five weeks.

Rescue has ended, but [Venezuela](country:VE)'s accounting of the dead has not, relatives say.

Search-and-rescue windows run days, so five weeks on, families dig by hand for a body to bury.

No agency has confirmed how many remain missing five weeks after the quakes.
