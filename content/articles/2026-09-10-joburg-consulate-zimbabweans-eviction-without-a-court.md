Johannesburg — Simpiwe Ndlovu, 7 months pregnant, was ordered from her flat by anti-immigrant vigilantes.

Over 300 Zimbabweans now camp at their consulate, beyond the reach of any court or landlord protection.

Employers and landlords ended jobs and tenancies without paperwork, as anti-migrant protests spread across [South Africa](country:ZA).

Zimbabwe's government has halted repatriation buses over budget constraints, with no restart date set.
