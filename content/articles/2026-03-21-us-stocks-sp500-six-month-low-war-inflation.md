New York — Energy stocks posted their longest weekly rally since 1987. Every other S&P 500 sector fell as the Middle East oil shock renewed inflation fears and pushed rate expectations toward a Fed hike. Rate futures now price a hike before year-end; the market's path depends on whether the oil shock eases or deepens.
