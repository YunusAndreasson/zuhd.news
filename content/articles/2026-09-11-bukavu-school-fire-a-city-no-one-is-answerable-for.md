Bukavu — At least 26 students died in the blaze.

No recognized government enforces fire safety in [DR Congo](country:CD)'s rebel-held second city.

Densely packed wooden homes and narrow school exits turned the fire into a fatal stampede.

The M23 administration is investigating the cause, but Kinshasa has no access to the site.
