Islamabad — Pakistan's diesel tripled as the Strait of Hormuz closed. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif told 240 million Pakistanis to work from home and avoid travel, as Saudi Arabia reroutes crude to bypass the closed strait. The government created a Rs27 billion austerity fund, and Pakistan now waits on whether the Strait reopens, with imports rerouted through Fujairah adding weeks to delivery.
