Johannesburg — Apex court rulings now take 214 days on average.

Chief Justice Mandisa Maya says [South Africa](country:ZA)'s judiciary controls its own rulings but not its budget, staffing or buildings, leaving courts dependent on the executive it must check.

Draft legislation for a fully independent judiciary reaches Cabinet early next year.
