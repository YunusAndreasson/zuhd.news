Logistics software firm WiseTech Global is eliminating 2,000 jobs. Chief executive Zubin Appoo declared "the era of manually writing code is over," cutting 29% of its workforce across 40 countries while posting record profits. Shares rose 11% on the announcement, pricing in lower labour costs before a single worker has been replaced.
