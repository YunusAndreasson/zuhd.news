Los Angeles — B2U Storage Solutions will repurpose Waymo's retired batteries into California and Texas grid storage.

The project will add hundreds of megawatt-hours of capacity; worn cells retain enough charge for stationary use.

B2U has already received initial battery quantities; Waymo's expanding fleet will accelerate supply.
