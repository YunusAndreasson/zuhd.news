London — A quadruped robot that mimics spinal reflexes matches animal locomotion on rough terrain.

A central-pattern generator drives rhythmic foot trajectories; separate terrain sensing handles balance, as in animal nervous systems.

It outperformed a reinforcement learning baseline on unfamiliar terrain, the researchers report.
