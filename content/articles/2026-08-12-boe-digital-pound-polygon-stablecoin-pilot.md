London — The Bank of England is testing life alongside stablecoins.

Cross-border remittance corridors, the costliest banking layer, are the experiment's first target.

Phase 2 runs on Polygon, a public blockchain, testing whether central-bank and private tokens can settle together.

The Treasury still has not decided whether to issue a digital pound at all.
