Melbourne — Australia will now sell uranium to India.

[Australia](country:AU) holds the lithium and rare earths [India](country:IN) needs to cut reliance on Chinese processing; the new pact adds a critical-minerals supply corridor.

Both governments still must finalize a broader economic cooperation agreement.
