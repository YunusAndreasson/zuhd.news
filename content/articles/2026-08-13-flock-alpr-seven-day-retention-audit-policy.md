Atlanta — Plate data now expires after seven days, mostly.

A shorter database window limits how far police can trace anyone's movements.

Every police customer must adopt "Audit Assistance," built after officers ran plates for personal reasons.

Which cases stay exempt from the seven-day limit remains unspecified in the new policy.
