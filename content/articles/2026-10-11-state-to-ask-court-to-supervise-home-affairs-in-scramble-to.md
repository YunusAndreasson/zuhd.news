Pretoria — The state will ask a court to supervise Home Affairs.

Home Affairs has 5 reception offices, and deportations continue for people who have not applied for asylum.

On 7 July the Constitutional Court struck down Refugees Act limits on asylum for irregular entrants and anyone reporting after five days.

The new online registration system is due 10 November, and a High Court panel sits 29 October.
