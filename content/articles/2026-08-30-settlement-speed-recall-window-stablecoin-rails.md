New York — Hawala moved money with no attached message.

When transfers settle instantly, the sender alone bears any fraud loss.

SWIFT added a message trail and a pause that let banks catch fraud before instant blockchain settlement removed both.

Wider bank adoption of instant settlement, following HSBC's test, remains undecided.
