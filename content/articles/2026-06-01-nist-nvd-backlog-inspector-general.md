Washington — The US cybersecurity standards agency's flaw database backlog hit 27,000 unprocessed vulnerabilities.

The National Vulnerability Database feeds patch-priority data to [US](country:US) security tools; unprocessed flaws are invisible to automated scanners.

An inspector general found the backlog doubled in under 2 years; no remediation timeline has been announced.
