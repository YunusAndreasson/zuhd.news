Stuttgart — Baden-Württemberg plans to cut 27 data-protection posts.

The state already ranks last in Germany, at 6.01 posts per million residents.

The 40% cut from 67.5 full-time posts compares with 5% at other central agencies, and the coalition says it is bundling oversight.

State commissioner Tobias Keber says the cut breaches EU rules, and a petition backs him.

The federal government promised its own reform proposal, but no powers have moved yet.
