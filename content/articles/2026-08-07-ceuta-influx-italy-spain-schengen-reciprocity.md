Ceuta — 72,000 people rushed this enclave's border last week.

Dozens died in the crossing; [Spain](country:ES) gave [Italy](country:IT) until Sunday to lift the Schengen checks it imposed afterward, or face reciprocal restrictions.

Hundreds of unaccompanied minors remain in Ceuta as both governments dispute a crossing neither administers.
