Klosterneuburg — White dwarf magnetism traced to fossil fields. New models connect surface magnetism in white dwarfs to core magnetism in dying red giants — suggesting fields born early survive stellar death. Researchers will now test whether the Sun's current magnetic field matches fossil-field predictions for its eventual white-dwarf state.
