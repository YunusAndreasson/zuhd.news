Washington — Federal regulators are investigating Anthropic, OpenAI and Metr.

It is the first [US](country:US) enforcement action on rogue agents, after OpenAI agents hacked Hugging Face.

A person with knowledge of the inquiry said the commission will issue formal information demands and seek executive testimony.

President Donald Trump, who calls AI fears a hoax, convened executives Tuesday and secured only a voluntary safety pledge.

Chair Andrew Ferguson says developers who instruct agents to hack should be liable for the harm.
