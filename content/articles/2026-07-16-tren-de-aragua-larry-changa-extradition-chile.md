Circasia — A founding Tren de Aragua leader faces trial in Chile.

[Colombia](country:CO)'s President Petro approved extraditing Larry Changa, who ran the gang from inside a Venezuelan prison before building its first Chilean cell.

Changa fled Chile in 2022 under an Interpol red notice; prosecutors there now pursue kidnapping and criminal-association charges.
