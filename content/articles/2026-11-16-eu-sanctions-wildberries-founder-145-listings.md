Brussels — [Russia](country:RU)'s richest woman is now under EU sanctions.

The EU listed 145 more people and entities, including 50 Russian banks and shadow-fleet enablers.

The EU says Wildberries supplies Russia's military with dual-use goods, so Tatyana Kim faces an asset freeze and travel ban.

Wildberries says the sanctions change nothing because it holds no EU assets and does no business there.

Ukraine has attacked Wildberries facilities since mid-July, and the Kremlin denies its warehouses serve the military.
