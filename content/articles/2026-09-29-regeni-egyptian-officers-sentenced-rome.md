Rome — 3 Egyptian officers got 10 years for kidnapping Giulio Regeni.

Amnesty International Italy says the verdict scratches the wall of impunity [Egypt](country:EG) built.

Tried in absentia, Major Magdy Sherif escaped the murder conspiracy charge prosecutors backed with a life-sentence request.

"Egypt has not been convicted, three people have been convicted," said Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani of [Italy](country:IT).

Italian law allows several levels of appeal before the ruling becomes final.
