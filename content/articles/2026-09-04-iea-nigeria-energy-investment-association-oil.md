Abuja — International Energy Agency chief Fatih Birol says [Nigeria](country:NG)'s investment could double in five years.

Associate status gives Africa's top oil producer a seat writing policy, not just its subject.

Nigeria joined in July as the agency's first oil-cartel member, seeking "trusted" suppliers.

The doubling is Birol's projection, not a commitment, and Nigeria's last surge stalled on refining.
