Hungary will veto €90 billion in EU Ukraine aid. Foreign Minister Péter Szijjártó accused Kyiv of blocking Russian oil through the Druzhba pipeline, which supplies Hungarian refineries. The veto freezes the EU's largest financial package for Ukraine as member states fracture over Russia policy.
