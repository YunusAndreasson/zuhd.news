FBI AI now matches nation-state hacking capability. An FBI official confirmed the bureau uses AI to automate vulnerability discovery and social engineering at machine speed, replacing skilled operators who once wrote custom exploits for each target. The official declined to name specific cases, leaving the program's scope and oversight undisclosed.
