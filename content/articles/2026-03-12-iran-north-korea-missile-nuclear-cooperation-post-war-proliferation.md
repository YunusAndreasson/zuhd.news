North Korea-Iran missile ties predate the Islamic Republic itself. US-Israeli strikes have destroyed Iran's domestic missile factories, prompting analysts at Seoul's Korea Institute for National Security to forecast purchases from Pyongyang, where past cooperation has included nuclear technicians alongside rocket engineers. North Korea endorsed Iran's new supreme leader on Wednesday, signalling the alliance is deepening before any ceasefire.
