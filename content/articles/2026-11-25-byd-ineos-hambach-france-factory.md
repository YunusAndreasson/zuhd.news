Hambach — BYD could own its first passenger-car plant in Western Europe.

Trade barriers are pushing Chinese carmakers to produce locally.

INEOS bought the plant in [France](country:FR) from Mercedes-Benz in 2021 and builds its Grenadier off-roader and Quartermaster pickup there.

The companies announced the exclusive talks Tuesday, and no deal has been signed.
