La Paz — [Bolivia](country:BO) declared emergency to clear 50-day road blockades.

President Paz cancelled fuel subsidies months into office; the resulting blockades left hospitals without oxygen and killed 17, Bolivia's ombudsman said.

Congress has 72 hours to approve the decree; indigenous groups blocking key roads say they will not negotiate.
