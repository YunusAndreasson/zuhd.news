London — Farm sensors now share updates, not raw data.

Transmitting drains battery fastest, so smallholders' sensors die before precision farming pays off.

The framework trains models on-node and sends only learned updates, cutting the energy cost of each transmission.

It has run only in simulation, with no field deployment yet reported.
