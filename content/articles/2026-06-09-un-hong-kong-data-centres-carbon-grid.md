Hong Kong — Hong Kong data centres are 43% above carbon average, a UN study found.

A data centre inherits its grid's emissions; Hong Kong's 67% fossil-fuel mix makes on-site efficiency irrelevant.

By 2030, data centres could consume 945 terawatt-hours — nearly triple [Pakistan](country:PK), [Bangladesh](country:BD) and [Nigeria](country:NG) combined, the UN said.
