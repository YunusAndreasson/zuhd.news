Cox's Bazar — Rohingya refugees cannot enroll in Bangladeshi schools.

[Bangladesh](country:BD) restricts refugees from its national curriculum and formal exams, citing their unresolved status nine years after fleeing [Myanmar](country:MM).

UN refugee agency funding for camp schooling has fallen as donor states cut contributions.
