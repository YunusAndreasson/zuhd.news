China — BYD's new car folds its steering wheel away in 10 seconds.

[China](country:CN)'s December 2025 standard deleted the mandatory mechanical link between wheel and steering.

Steer-by-wire replaces the steering column with signals, letting the wheel fold and free 25 centimetres of leg room, BYD says.

Online critics say folding is a side effect, and the real gains are variable steering ratios and crash safety.

Sales of the 329,900-yuan trim, 50,000 yuan above base, will show whether buyers want it.
