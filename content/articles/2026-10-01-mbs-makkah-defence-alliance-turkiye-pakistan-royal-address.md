Riyadh — [Saudi Arabia](country:SA), [Turkey](country:TR) and [Pakistan](country:PK) signed a mutual defence pact on 7 August.

Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman called it a shield against regional conflict, as the US-Iran war continues.

In the royal address to the Shura Council, he said a multinational defensive maritime coalition aims to protect shipping lanes.

He vowed to respond firmly to any attack and condemned [Israel](country:IL)'s attacks on Palestinians in Gaza.
