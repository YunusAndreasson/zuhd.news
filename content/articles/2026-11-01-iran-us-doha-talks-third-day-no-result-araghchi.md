Doha — Three days of Qatari-mediated talks have produced no deal.

[Iran](country:IR) and the [US](country:US) both say they are ready to resume fighting after 3 November.

Iran's security chief says Hormuz traffic stays restricted until Washington lifts its naval blockade and waives oil sanctions.

Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says any result depends on US answers to Iran's questions about the two-day plan.
