Hong Kong — A 2020 trade-status order expired unrenewed.

The 2020 order had stripped [Hong Kong](country:HK)'s preferential trade and export treatment after Beijing's national security law; letting it lapse restores that access without new legislation.

[China](country:CN) calls it a Madrid trade-talks commitment fulfilled; Xi Jinping is expected to visit the US later this year.
