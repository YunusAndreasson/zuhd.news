Abuja — [Nigerian](country:NG) lending equals 12.9% of GDP, against 20.1% regionally.

Armstrong Takang of Ministry of Finance Incorporated says under 10% of properties have proper titles.

Homes without verifiable ownership records cannot serve as loan collateral, so owners cannot borrow against them.

House Speaker Abbas Tajudeen said Nigeria "lacks enough trust in property."

The House of Representatives is weighing a bill to create a National Real Estate Regulatory Authority to license agents and developers.
