Pakistan's grid demand fell 11% over 3 years. The Strait of Hormuz closure made fuel unaffordable, pushing Pakistani households onto solar panels that generate electricity at under half the grid cost. Whether the boom survives peace depends on utility profit-sharing that would turn consumers into energy sellers.
