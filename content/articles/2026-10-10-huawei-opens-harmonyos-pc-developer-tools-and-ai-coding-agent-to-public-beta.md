Shenzhen — Huawei's HarmonyOS PCs can now develop HarmonyOS apps natively.

Huawei says devices on HarmonyOS 6 and 7 have passed 90 million.

Huawei says its DevEco CLI packages the toolchain and over 70 skills as commands that outside AI coding agents can call.

All three tools entered public beta on 8 October.
