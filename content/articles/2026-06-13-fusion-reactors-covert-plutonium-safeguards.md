London — Fusion reactors carry a covert bomb-material risk.

Physicists warn fusion reactors could be misused to produce nuclear weapons material and must be safeguarded before any commercial deployment.

No safeguard protocol yet covers commercial fusion; the window to require design-stage controls narrows as private reactors advance.
