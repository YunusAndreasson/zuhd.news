Dubai crude hit $153 on near-zero volume. Platts, a commodity price agency, dropped 3 crude grades that transit Hormuz from its benchmark calculation, leaving the $153 price set by a handful of buyers. Platts announced Monday it is seeking immediate feedback on whether the Dubai benchmark methodology still functions.
