12 villages seized in 2 weeks. Russia's Chief of General Staff Valery Gerasimov announced his forces captured the settlements in eastern Ukraine, moving closer to Sloviansk in Donetsk, though Agence France-Presse could not verify the claim. The advance came days before US-mediated peace talks open in Geneva, where Moscow will demand Ukraine withdraw from 4 regions Russia claims — terms Kyiv rejects.
