Atlanta — 12,883 fell ill, two died, from tainted lettuce.

It is the largest cyclosporiasis outbreak the US has recorded.

The parasite spread through contaminated water touching Taylor Farms' lettuce fields in [Mexico](country:MX), not person to person, officials say.

The Food and Drug Administration's produce-traceability rule, delayed since the outbreak began, still won't take effect until 2028.
