Jakarta — Millions of Indonesian drivers are ride-hailing "partners."

That label keeps minimum wage and injury law from ever applying to them.

Analysts propose a private safety net instead of labour law, since platform apps set fares while drivers own the capital.

Indonesia's decision on driver protections will likely be copied across Southeast Asia's ride-hailing markets.
