London — The "cut-to-fuse" method rebuilds molecular frames, not decorations.

It lets chemists fix failed drug candidates without restarting synthesis from scratch.

"Skeletal" means altering a molecule's carbon backbone itself, rather than just the functional groups attached to it.

Researchers have not said which molecular frameworks the method can edit.
