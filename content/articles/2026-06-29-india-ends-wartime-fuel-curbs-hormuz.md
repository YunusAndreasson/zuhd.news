New Delhi — [India](country:IN) ends war-time fuel rationing from July 1.

The petroleum ministry capped diesel at 200 litres per vehicle on June 12 after Hormuz disruptions drove bulk buyers to retail pumps, the ministry said.

[Iran](country:IR) denied it is in technical talks with US envoys, leaving Hormuz access unresolved and India's supply outlook uncertain.
