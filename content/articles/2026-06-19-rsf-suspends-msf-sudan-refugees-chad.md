N'Djamena — [Chad](country:TD)'s government, aligned with Sudan's Rapid Support Forces, expelled Médecins Sans Frontières and cut care for Sudanese refugees.

Authorities cited the group's own findings — 18 staff dismissed after 59 abuse allegations — as grounds.

A fact-finding committee has 30 days; the group has not said whether it will contest the order.
