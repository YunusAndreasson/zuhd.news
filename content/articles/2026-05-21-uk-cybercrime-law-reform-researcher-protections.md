London — [UK](country:GB) cyber reform bars researchers from verifying vulnerabilities.

The update to the 1990 Computer Misuse Act requires stopping at first suspicion — before confirming a flaw is real or exploitable, experts said.

Researchers warn UK infrastructure will be disclosed from abroad, where equivalent work is legal.
