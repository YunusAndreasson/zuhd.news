Belgrade — [Serbia](country:RS)'s Vucic says he lost, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

Vucic said a movement born of student protests, the Students' List, beat his Progressive Party.

He cited stratified sample frames at Progressive Party headquarters, TASS reported.

The Students' List said earlier that it rejects anti-Russian sanctions.

Vucic predicts the new government will sanction [Russia](country:RU) by March or April.
