Pretoria — No one counted the 16,621 jobs blamed on sugar tax.

The sugar industry cites it in its campaign to scrap [South Africa](country:ZA)'s Health Promotion Levy.

It is a model's estimate for 2019 against a no-levy scenario, the Treasury says, not an audited count.

A 2025 study of national labour surveys found no significant link between the levy and sugar-sector employment.

Canegrowers urge Finance Minister Enoch Godongwana to scrap the levy, and the Treasury has commissioned no jobs study.
