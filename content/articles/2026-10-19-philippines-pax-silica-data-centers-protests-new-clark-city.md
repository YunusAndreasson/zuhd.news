Manila — 17,000 police met tens of thousands of protesters on 21 September.

Responsible Statecraft, the Quincy Institute's magazine, reported that [US](country:US)-funded data-centre projects are among the protesters' targets.

The marches follow a flood-control scandal in the [Philippines](country:PH) over $9 billion in projects under investigation as shoddy or never built.

Vice President Sara Duterte's impeachment trial continues, after day 34 revealed 30 undeclared accounts.
