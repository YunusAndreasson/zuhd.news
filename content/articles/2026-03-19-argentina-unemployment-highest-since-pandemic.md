Argentina shed 200,000 formal jobs since late 2023. President Javier Milei cut public sector payrolls and slashed spending to close the fiscal deficit, contracting labor-intensive industries that depend on domestic demand. The next quarterly report, due in June, will show whether Milei's February labor reform translates into new formal hiring.
