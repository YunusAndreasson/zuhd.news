Beijing — Xi vowed Taiwan's reunification without mentioning force.

[China](country:CN) has never renounced force, and 29 military aircraft patrolled around Taiwan the same day.

Xi spoke on Sun Yat-sen's 160th birthday, restating "one country, two systems," which no major Taiwanese party supports.

"It isn't much different from the past," said Liang Wen-chieh, spokesperson for Taiwan's Mainland Affairs Council.

Xi meets Trump at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit in Shenzhen next week, and Taiwan holds local elections two weeks later.
