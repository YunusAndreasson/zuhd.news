Indonesia holds 28 days of fuel with Hormuz closed. As a net oil importer, Indonesia faces direct supply risk from the strait's 3-week closure. Indonesia's legal minimum reserve is 21 days — the 7-day cushion will narrow as Eid travel peaks this week.
