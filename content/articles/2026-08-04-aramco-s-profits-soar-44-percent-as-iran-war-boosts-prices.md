Dhahran — Aramco's quarterly profit rose 44%.

Net income hit $32.7 billion as [Iran](country:IR)'s closure of the Strait of Hormuz pushed crude to $108 a barrel — the disruption taxes importers but prices into the barrel as revenue for producers with intact export routes.

Aramco pays most of the profit to the [Saudi](country:SA) state budget as the kingdom's dividend.
