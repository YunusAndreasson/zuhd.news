Slovenia, first EU state to recognize Palestine, votes Sunday. Incumbent PM Robert Golob recognized Palestinian statehood, banned Israeli ministers from entry, and backed ICC arrest warrants against Israel's prime minister. Challenger Janez Jansa has pledged to reverse Slovenia's Palestine recognition and end its ICC cooperation if he wins.
