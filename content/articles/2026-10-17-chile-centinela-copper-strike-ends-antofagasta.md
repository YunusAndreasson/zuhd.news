Santiago — Two unions struck Antofagasta's Centinela mine for over a week.

Copper's September price was $14,470 a tonne, up 6.8% in three months.

Union leaders put the preliminary contract to members in [Chile](country:CL) on Friday night, ahead of a ratifying vote.

Members vote on whether to ratify the deal on Saturday.
