London — Heart pump implants create 3 distinct clot-prone zones.

Researchers mapped the aortic root, outflow graft, and cannula junction as primary thrombosis sites; pump workload adjustments can shift risk between them.

The method guides patient-specific surgical planning; clinical validation has not begun.
