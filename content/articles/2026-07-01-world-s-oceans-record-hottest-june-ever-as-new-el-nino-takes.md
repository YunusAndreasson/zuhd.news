Brussels — Marine heatwaves hit 82% of the world's oceans.

A new El Niño is stacking atop rising background heat, pushing June sea surface temperatures to 20.98°C, past 2023 and 2024 records, Copernicus said.

Scientists expect more records to fall in the coming months, worsening droughts and cyclone risk.
