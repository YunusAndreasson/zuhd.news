Bahir Dar — A model now forecasts where hyacinth mats form.

Removal crews can now be sent ahead of a bloom, not after it forms.

The study, on [Ethiopia](country:ET)'s Blue Nile source lake, ranked nitrogen and phosphorus runoff above temperature as the top driver.

This accepted, not-final paper has not been tested against a live removal season.
