Bama — Troops freed 11 hostages, arrested 21 suspects.

Coordinated raids across Borno, Zamfara and Kogi states swept up Islamic State West Africa Province logistics suppliers, an informant tied to bandit leader Kachallah Babangida, and drug couriers in the same operation.

Search-and-rescue continues in Ondo state after two more abductions.
