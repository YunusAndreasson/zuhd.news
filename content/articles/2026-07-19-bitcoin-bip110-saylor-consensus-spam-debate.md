Tysons — Michael Saylor published "110 reasons" BIP-110 is a bad idea.

The proposal would use a consensus rule to restrict non-monetary data on Bitcoin. Saylor argues fee markets and relay policy should decide instead.

The debate over what counts as "spam" pits Bitcoin's no-leader design against calls to narrow its permissionless use.
