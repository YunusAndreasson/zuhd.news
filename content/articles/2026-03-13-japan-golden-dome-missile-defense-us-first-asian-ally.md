Japan is set to become the first Asian nation in the US Golden Dome missile shield. Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi is expected to confirm participation at a March 19 meeting with US President Donald Trump. The system uses space-based interceptors to target hypersonic missiles — a capability China and North Korea have been developing.
