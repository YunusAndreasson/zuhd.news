Milan — ICEYE offers €41 a share for Avio, a 21.3% premium.

ICEYE wants launch capacity for its satellite constellation, and Avio wants long-term launch demand.

An Italian subsidiary offers €2.05 billion in cash, funded by existing cash and debt, and plans to delist Avio.

Avio's board is assessing the offer, which needs clearances from [Italy](country:IT), [France](country:FR), Britain and the US.
