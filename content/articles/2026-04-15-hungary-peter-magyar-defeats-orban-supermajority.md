Budapest — Peter Magyar won a two-thirds supermajority. His Tisza party took 138 of 199 parliamentary seats, ending Viktor Orbán's 15-year grip and Hungary's status as Trump's closest EU ally. Magyar inherits $17 billion in frozen EU aid and a supermajority strong enough to rewrite Orbán's constitutional architecture.
