Bonn — COP31 presidency proposed a 35%-by-2035 global electrification target.

Backed by International Energy Agency and International Renewable Energy Agency analysis, the target requires doubling electricity's current 20% share of final energy demand.

No financing mechanism was announced; COP31 meets in Antalya, [Turkey](country:TR) this year.
