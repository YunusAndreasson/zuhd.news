Tehran — A general, not a diplomat, is traveling.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s military runs its Iran policy directly, bypassing the foreign ministry.

Munir will meet senior officials in [Iran](country:IR) Monday, Tehran's foreign ministry said.

The visit comes as Pakistan mediates between Iran and the US following weeks of conflict.
