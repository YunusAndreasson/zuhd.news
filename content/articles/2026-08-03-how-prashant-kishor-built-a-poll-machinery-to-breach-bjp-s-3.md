Patna — Jan Suraaj ended 35 years of BJP rule in Bankipur.

Founder Prashant Kishor built booth-level enumeration from scratch, starting campaigning two months before rivals and leading by nearly 20,000 votes, [India](country:IN)'s Election Commission showed.

The bypoll had no government at stake; it tests whether the model scales to 2027's general election.
