The US attacked Iran mid-negotiation for the second time. Turkey's Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan said Iran "feels betrayed" and that the repeated pattern will make future negotiations structurally harder. Fidan said Iran remains open to backchannel talks, but Turkey has named no formal channel or timeline.
