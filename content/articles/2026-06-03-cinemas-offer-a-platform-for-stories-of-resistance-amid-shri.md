Lagos — Nigeria hosts the 5th Africa International Human Rights Film Festival, December 8-10, 2026.

The festival convenes filmmakers and journalists to document what legal crackdowns and censorship suppress across the continent.

It runs in Lagos under the theme "Stories of Resistance," with submissions open to filmmakers across the continent.
