Brasília — Flávio Bolsonaro left [Brazil](country:BR) through no regular border post, a police official said.

The [US](country:US) will issue visas to him and his family, an administration official said.

The Supreme Court ordered his passports surrendered in a money-laundering probe he calls persecution, and police cancelled them Tuesday.

"Brazil is their second nation, the US is their first," said President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva.

Justice Flávio Dino has ordered police to trace foreign funding of his campaign.
