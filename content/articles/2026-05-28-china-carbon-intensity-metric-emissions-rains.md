Beijing — [China](country:CN) counts carbon intensity, not absolute emissions.

The metric lets absolute emissions rise if GDP grows proportionally faster — allowing progress claims while volumes increase.

The EU debates China's overcapacity in June; whether China's carbon targets count as climate progress is unresolved.
