London — Average global government bond yields near 4%, the highest since 2007.

Mortgages, business loans and government interest bills all reprice upward.

Every $1 move in oil has tracked roughly 2 basis points on the 10-year Treasury yield since 2012, TS Lombard estimates.

[Japan](country:JP)'s 10-year yield hit a 3-decade high Thursday as the Bank of Japan unwinds easy money.
