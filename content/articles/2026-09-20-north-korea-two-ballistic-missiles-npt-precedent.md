Wonsan — 2 ballistic missiles flew 3 hours apart.

[North Korea](country:KP) rejected a UN nuclear watchdog resolution days earlier, calling its nuclear status irreversible.

South Korea's military said the first missile flew 450 kilometres and the second more than 600.

[South Korea](country:KR) and the US are analysing the second missile's exact specifications.
