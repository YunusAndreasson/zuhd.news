Jaipur — Planting crops between pomegranate rows lifted yields.

It could raise income for smallholders farming dry, water-scarce orchard land.

A five-year Rajasthan trial found pomegranate paired with green gram and mustard yielded the most, 3,739 kg per hectare.

The single-site trial does not price the extra labor intercropping requires.
