Lausanne — Physicists cannot explain why protons hold together.

The Yang-Mills mass gap is a $1 million Millennium Prize Problem: how massless gluons produce the mass inside every proton, which accounts for 98% of visible matter's weight.

Mathematician Martin Hairer's regularity structures may provide the analytical proof that 20 years of supercomputing approximations could not.
