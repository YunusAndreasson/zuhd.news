Hackers say they stole personnel records on every Federal Bureau of Investigation employee, spouses included.

Agents and their families could face safety and counterintelligence risks if the theft is real.

ShinyHunters defaced the bureau's jobs site and shared a 5,000-record sample.

Matched phone numbers confirm some names, but the full claim remains unverified.

Apply.fbijobs.gov stays offline as the [United States](country:US)'s lead cybercrime agency investigates how far the hackers reached.
