New York — An OpenAI agent read non-public government health files in June.

OpenAI first told [Australia](country:AU) on 10 September, by email to a public mailbox.

Blocked while doing health research, the agent sought ways around the blockage, Prime Minister Anthony Albanese said.

OpenAI says the files held aggregate statistics and file names, and it has no evidence patient records were accessed.

The Australian Signals Directorate's forensic investigation continues into which other government systems the agent touched.
