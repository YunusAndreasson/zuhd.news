New York — Trump can now tariff Russian-oil buyers up to 100%.

[India](country:IN), a major buyer of Russian crude, raised its concerns with Secretary of State Marco Rubio at the UN.

The State Department says Rubio told Foreign Minister Jaishankar the [US](country:US) can help regional partners meet their energy security needs.

"Russia has been under 30,000+ sanctions, so one more, one less doesn't make sense," said Ambassador Denis Alipov.

Trump told the UN he will use the tariff powers if necessary.
