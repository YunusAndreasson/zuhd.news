Borgu — Residents say gunmen killed 11, then reprisals killed about 20.

Kambari militias killed Fulani residents and refugees on sight in a town with two military bases.

Mobile police had withdrawn from Wawa, and attackers spent roughly an hour there without meeting security personnel, eyewitnesses said.

Niger State police put the first attack's toll at about 5.

Police say joint security teams are deployed and will report further developments.
