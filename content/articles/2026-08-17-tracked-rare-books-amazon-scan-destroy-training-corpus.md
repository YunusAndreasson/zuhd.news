Seattle — A tracked rare book led to Amazon.

The find exposed a previously unreported Amazon book-buying operation for artificial intelligence training.

Amazon shreds each original afterward, since copyright law permits replacement copies but not duplicates.

Amazon has not said how many books move through the operation or from whom.
