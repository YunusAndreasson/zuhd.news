Bern — Swiss voters decide Sunday whether to write neutrality into the constitution.

A Yes would bar defence cooperation with military alliances unless [Switzerland](country:CH) is attacked.

The initiative would also bar sanctions the UN has not approved, curbing Bern's adoption of EU measures against [Russia](country:RU).

A GFS Bern poll for broadcaster SRG found 63% opposed and 34% in favour.

Passage needs a majority of voters and a majority of cantons.
