Czech Republic — Bitcoin is down 44% since the Czech central bank announced its portfolio.

The bank has never said what it paid, how much it holds or its value.

An outside custodian holds the coins, and the bank books them as intangible assets at cost, so price swings do not show.

The $1 million portfolio is 0.0006% of the bank's assets, the bank says.

The bank declined to say whether it will write the holding down at year-end.
