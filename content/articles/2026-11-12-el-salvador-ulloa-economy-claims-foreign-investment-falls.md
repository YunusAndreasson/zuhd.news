San Salvador — Foreign investment fell 37.2% while construction grew 24.4%.

Vice President Félix Ulloa had cited foreign investment as proof of an economic boom in [El Salvador](country:SV).

Fact-checker Voz Pública tested 12 of his economic claims and found 6 false or misleading, each missing context.

Growth is on course for the 4.5% he cited, and tourism passed 4 million, though not all visitors are tourists.

His office has not answered a comment request sent 23 October.
