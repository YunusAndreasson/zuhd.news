Hangzhou — Alibaba's Qwen is coming to Chinese iPhones.

It puts a Chinese-built model inside Apple's assistant for users in [China](country:CN).

Qwen will handle text and image understanding and content generation in Siri, writing tools and visual intelligence, TMTPost reported.

Beta access opens 4 November, and the full release arrives with iOS 27.3 in December.
