The only survivor, conductor Nasiru Garba, had worked the route four days.

Attacks recur on unpoliced roads between [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Plateau settlements, not through any single conflict line.

Army chief Waidi Shaibu, visiting the state a day later, ordered troops to sustain pressure.
