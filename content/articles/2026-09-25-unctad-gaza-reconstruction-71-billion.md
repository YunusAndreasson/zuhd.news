Geneva — UN: 92% of Gaza's economic establishments damaged or destroyed.

Over 90% of working-age Gazans are unemployed, and output per person fell 83% since 2022.

[Israel](country:IL) has also withheld $3.67bn of Palestinian revenue since 2019, equal to 83% of 2025 net revenue, the report says.

Banks may soon be unable to finance fuel, water and medicine imports, the report warns.
