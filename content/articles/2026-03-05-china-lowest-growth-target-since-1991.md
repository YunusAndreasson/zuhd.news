China's 2026 growth target is its lowest since 1991. Beijing set a 4.5-5% range as weak domestic consumption and a cratered property market exhaust decades of stimulus, even as military spending rose 7%. With Hormuz now sealed, China's import-dependent economy faces energy disruptions the target did not anticipate.
