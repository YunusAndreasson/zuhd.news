Paris — TotalEnergies is buying Shell's entire European onshore renewables arm.

The deal adds 500 megawatts and a 3.5-gigawatt pipeline across [Italy](country:IT), the UK and Spain, as Shell narrows focus back to oil and gas.

TotalEnergies separately sold KKR a 50% stake in 1.2 gigawatts of German, Spanish, French and Polish assets.
