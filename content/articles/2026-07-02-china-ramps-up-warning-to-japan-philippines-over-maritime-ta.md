Beijing — A legal opinion just claimed Taiwan's entire seabed.

[China](country:CN) argues its Taiwan sovereignty claim extends to the island's exclusive economic zone, making Japan-Philippine maritime talks there an intrusion on Chinese territory.

The legal argument sets a precedent Beijing can invoke against any future security pact near Taiwan.
