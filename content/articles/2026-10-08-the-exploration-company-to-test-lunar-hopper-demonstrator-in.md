Abu Dhabi — ONEIROS, one tonne, will fly in the desert in 2027.

The privately funded test aims to validate technology for cheaper Moon access.

A throttleable methane engine, autonomous navigation and hazard avoidance will demonstrate controlled landings.

The [United Arab Emirates](country:AE) space agency has signed only a memorandum of understanding, and no binding agreement has been announced.

Hilal, a precision-landing demonstrator, is the company's next step after ONEIROS.
