London — A billion people have waited 80 years.

Scrub typhus kills up to 30% of untreated cases across Asia's tsutsugamushi triangle.

The bacterium's surface protein varies by region, so researchers targeted its unchanging fragments instead.

The vaccine sped bacterial clearance in macaques, and no human trial exists yet.
