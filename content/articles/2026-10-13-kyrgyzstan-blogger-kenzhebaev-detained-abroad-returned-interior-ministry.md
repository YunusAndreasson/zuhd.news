Bishkek — [Kyrgyzstan](country:KG) detained blogger Syrgak Kenzhebaev abroad and took him home.

For 2 months he alleged President Sadyr Japarov's brother Sabyr settled political questions and influenced customs.

The interior ministry suspects him of calling for mass unrest and of defrauding a woman of $150,000.

Kenzhebaev said officers led by criminal-police chief Ulan Aitbayev tried to detain him in [Cambodia](country:KH) on 31 August.

No account names the country that held him or the legal procedure used.
