Sudan's army killed 33 civilians in market drone strikes. The strikes wounded 59 more in West Kordofan, an area controlled by the Rapid Support Forces, as 25 million Sudanese face famine. UN rights chief Volker Türk called the strikes illegal and demanded an independent investigation.
