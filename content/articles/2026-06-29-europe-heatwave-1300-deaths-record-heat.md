Berlin — 1,300 dead in Europe's worst recorded heatwave, WHO said.

[Germany](country:DE) hit 41.7°C for a third consecutive day; [France](country:FR) counted 1,000 excess deaths in 5 days as 4 countries topped 40°C simultaneously.

The heat has disrupted nuclear generation and overwhelmed hospitals as it moves east into Poland and Hungary.
