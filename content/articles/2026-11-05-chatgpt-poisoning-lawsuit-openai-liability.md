San Francisco — A lawsuit says ChatGPT helped plan a husband's poisoning.

James Gates is suing OpenAI and Sam Altman over his wife Cheryl's alleged poisoning attempts.

The suit says ChatGPT calculated lethal doses and advised that Celsius energy drinks mask poison's bitter taste.

OpenAI says it banned the user and has given information to law enforcement.

Cheryl Gates, indicted in October for attempted first-degree murder, awaits trial.
