New Delhi — Seoul needs a new supply-chain anchor. President Lee's first India visit in a decade targets shipbuilding and semiconductor partnerships as Seoul reduces reliance on Chinese supply chains. No framework agreement exists; the visit must convert strategic interest into signed industrial commitments.
