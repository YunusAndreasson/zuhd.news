Beijing — One AI ad license now lasts forever.

Models and everyday people appear in campaigns they only agreed to once.

Likeness law assumed a per-use economy, but generative models make one signature cover unlimited reuse.

This gap is surfacing in [China](country:CN) first, where synthetic advertising is already mainstream.
