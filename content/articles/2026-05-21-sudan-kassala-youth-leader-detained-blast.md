Kassala — Youth leader jailed for disputing explosion account.

In [Sudan](country:SD), security officials arrested Humrai Omar within 24 hours of his Facebook post calling the Kassala blast account "false and misleading."

Activists demand his release or a fair trial as competing accounts — landmine or drone strike — remain unresolved.
