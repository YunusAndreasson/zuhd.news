Abu Dhabi — Brent crude fell more than 3% to $110 a barrel.

Hormuz traffic averaged 3.3 ships a day to 25 October, 18% below normal.

Trump signalled more time for diplomacy with [Iran](country:IR) before renewed strikes, and the [US](country:US) votes in midterms Tuesday.

UK Maritime Trade Operations reported a ship hit by a projectile in the strait Sunday, after Friday's attack on a gas tanker.

UBS expects Brent near $110 at year-end, citing record freight costs and constrained tanker capacity.
