Seoul — [South Korea](country:KR) recalled its Kyiv envoy, a first since 2008.

Seoul says Kyiv broke a secrecy deal over 2 captured North Korean soldiers.

Volodymyr Zelenskyy announced the transfer at the UN General Assembly, and [Ukraine](country:UA) denies any confidentiality agreement existed.

Foreign Minister Cho Hyun said Kyiv's message included words of apology but fell short of Seoul's expectations.

President Lee Jae-myung has warned of further measures unless Ukraine apologises publicly.
