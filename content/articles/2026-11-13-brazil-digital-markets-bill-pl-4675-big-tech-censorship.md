Brasília — [Brazil](country:BR)'s big-tech lobby brands a competition bill censorship.

Civil-society groups are preparing a letter backing PL 4.675/2025 after the Presidency convened them.

The bill gives the competition authority Cade a dedicated superintendency to police digital platforms, modelled on the EU's Digital Markets Act.

The Novo party says the bill expands state control, a claim Data Privacy Brasil's Rafael Zanatta rejects.

The letter is due out in coming days, after the Presidency's meeting with signatories.
