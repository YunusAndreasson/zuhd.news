Washington — A Senate-passed bill lets Trump tariff Russian oil buyers 100%.

The tariff falls on Indian, Chinese and Turkish exporters, not on [Russia](country:RU) itself.

One Democratic amendment would name [India](country:IN) directly; another would strip the president's tariff power from the bill entirely.

The House has four working days to act before its pre-midterm recess.
