World Gymnastics cancelled April's Doha World Cup. Qatar built its post-oil economy around hosting global events, a strategy generating billions in tourism and construction contracts. Qatar's sports-hosting economy, built to replace oil revenues, depends on 2026 events it can no longer guarantee will proceed.
