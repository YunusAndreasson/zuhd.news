Kampala — [Uganda](country:UG)'s army chief shut press via X post.

Muhoozi Kainerugaba — Museveni's son and presumed heir — ordered the outlets offline, saying Uganda's media must follow "revolutionary principles."

Daily Monitor said it had not been told when it could reopen.
