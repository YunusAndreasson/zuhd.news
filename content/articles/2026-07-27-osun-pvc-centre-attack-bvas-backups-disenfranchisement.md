Osogbo — Hoodlums stormed Osun's voter-card centre Sunday.

INEC counters with 664 backup BVAS units among 4,427 deployed — but a stolen voter card can't be reissued before Aug. 15.

Police have made no arrests, and [Nigeria](country:NG)'s electoral commission says the Aug. 15 vote proceeds regardless.
