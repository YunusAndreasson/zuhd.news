Hanoi — Two men vanished in Phnom Penh, then surfaced in Hanoi's custody.

It confirms a tactic [Vietnam](country:VN) and its neighbors use to silence exiles abroad.

Vietnam charged the men under a terrorism statute Hanoi uses broadly against overseas dissident groups like Viet Tan.

Vietnam's ministry says the men confessed to illegal entry, a claim Viet Tan calls fabricated to hide an abduction.

No trial date has been set for the three men held under Vietnam's Penal Code.
