Trump offered missile strikes to 17 Latin American governments to target drug cartels. The Shield of the Americas anti-cartel coalition, signed at Doral, excludes Brazil, Mexico, and Colombia — the region's 3 largest economies. Trump warned Cuba it faces imminent action; Kristi Noem becomes special envoy March 31.
