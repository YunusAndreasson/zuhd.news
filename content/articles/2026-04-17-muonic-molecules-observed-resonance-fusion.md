Zurich — Scientists first observed muonic molecules in resonance states. Resonance states are the intermediate that makes muon-catalyzed fusion work in theory; they've been the breakeven bottleneck for 50 years. Science Advances published the result; whether the bottleneck can be solved enough for practical μCF energy remains unresolved.
