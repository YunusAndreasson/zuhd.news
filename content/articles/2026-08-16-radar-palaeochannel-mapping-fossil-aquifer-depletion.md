Cairo — Satellite radar found buried river channels under [Egypt](country:EG)'s Eastern Desert.

Better maps mean fewer dry boreholes, which can cost tens of thousands of dollars to drill.

Long-wavelength radar penetrates dry sand to reveal ancient channels invisible to ordinary satellite images.

The method ranks probability, not volume, and mapped water does not refill on human timescales.
