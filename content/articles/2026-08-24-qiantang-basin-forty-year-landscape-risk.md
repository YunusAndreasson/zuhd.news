Hangzhou — Forty years show risk rising, then falling.

Tens of millions downstream depend on a flood pattern set by construction, not rainfall.

Researchers found farmland loss fragmented the watershed until the 2000s, when connectivity recovered.

The study, not yet finalized, offers a template for less-monitored deltas in South Asia.
