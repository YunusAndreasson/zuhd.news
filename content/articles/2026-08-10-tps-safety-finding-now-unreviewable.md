Washington — A [US](country:US) court cleared [Myanmar](country:MM) nationals for deportation.

Every Temporary Protected Status holder now faces a country-safety finding courts can barely review.

June's Supreme Court ruling narrowed judicial review of TPS, leaving the executive's safety call nearly unchallengeable.

No timeline for removals has been set, and Myanmar's junta remains at war with resistance forces.
