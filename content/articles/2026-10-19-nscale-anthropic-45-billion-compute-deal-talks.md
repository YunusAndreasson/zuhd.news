London — Nscale is in talks for a second $45 billion Anthropic contract.

Microsoft pulled out of Nscale's flagship data center after power connection delays pushed supply into the 2030s.

A second West Virginia building at Monarch would deliver 460 megawatts on Nvidia's Vera Rubin chips, people familiar said.

Investors have urged Anthropic to lower its stock-market listing valuation target after AI stock setbacks, people familiar said.

Anthropic holds first refusal on Monarch's third building, and the plan could still change.
