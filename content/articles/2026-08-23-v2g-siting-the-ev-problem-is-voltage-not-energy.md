London — A test network cut power losses 51.62%.

The real constraint on electric vehicles is grid voltage, not power supply.

Researchers modeled optimal charger and capacitor placement on a standard 33-bus test grid, not a real one.

Countries importing cheap EVs faster than upgrading grids face this tradeoff first.
