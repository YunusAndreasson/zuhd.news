Pengshui — Residential buildings collapsed downhill Friday.

An unknown number were buried after the slope gave way in this mountainous [China](country:CN) county, state media reported, as monsoon rain saturates hillside terrain.

Rescuers have not yet reported a confirmed casualty count from the site.
