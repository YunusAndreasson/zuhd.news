A Maronite Christian village in Lebanon's Bekaa Valley is sheltering Shiite families displaced by Israeli strikes. Deir al-Ahmar's residents opened their homes without political agreement or NGO coordination, bridging a sectarian divide Lebanon's ruling class has exploited for 50 years. More than 1 million people are displaced across Lebanon, with Baalbek under continued Israeli strikes and no ceasefire announced.
