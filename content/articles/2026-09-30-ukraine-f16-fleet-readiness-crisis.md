Kyiv — Over half [Ukraine](country:UA)'s F-16s await repair, a Western source said.

At the current tempo, the source says, Ukraine could run out of usable jets by year-end.

Intercepting [Russia](country:RU)'s jet-powered drones and cruise missiles burns missile stocks and adds hours to worn airframes, so pilots ration shots.

Ukraine's air force declined to give fleet figures, and open-source analysts count 40-50 jets delivered.

[Belgium](country:BE), [Denmark](country:DK), the [Netherlands](country:NL) and [Norway](country:NO) have promised about 110 more F-16s.
