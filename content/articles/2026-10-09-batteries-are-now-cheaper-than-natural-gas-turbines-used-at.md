Edinburgh — Wood Mackenzie finds batteries cheaper in all 43 markets.

Open-cycle turbines are the peaking plants many data centre developers have been buying.

Data centre developers have driven up turbine prices, and open-cycle units now take 2 to 4 years to procure.

In the Middle East and Africa, four-hour batteries will be 33% cheaper by 2035, Wood Mackenzie projects.
