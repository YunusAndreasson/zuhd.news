Beijing — [China](country:CN)'s April 23 order tightens fossil-fuel controls nationwide. The directive from the 2 highest governing bodies swaps "reasonable" for "strict" — a wording shift that sets multi-year targets across the entire bureaucracy. Provinces now face evaluations tied to total emissions and coal consumption.
