Tegucigalpa — A carton of 30 eggs now costs 118 lempiras.

[Honduras](country:HN)'s inflation reached 6.28% in September as war-driven fuel costs and drought lifted prices.

Egg producers agreed a price rise with the economy ministry on 22 October, citing feed at 65% of costs.

Vendors say shoppers now buy only essentials, and avocados have become a luxury.
