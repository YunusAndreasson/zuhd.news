Israeli helicopters entered Lebanon's Bekaa Valley from Syria. Israel said it was targeting Hezbollah's supply lines; a similar Israeli operation Friday killed 41 people. Lebanese authorities said Israeli strikes killed 394 people in Lebanon this week — 83 of them children — as Israeli forces push east.
