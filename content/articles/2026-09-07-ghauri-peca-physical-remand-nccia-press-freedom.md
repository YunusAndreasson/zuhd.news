Islamabad — A magistrate gave police three days to hold Bilal Ghauri themselves.

Physical remand puts a journalist inside the investigating agency's own cells, not a court jail.

The Cyber Crime Investigation Agency filed the case under [Pakistan](country:PK)'s electronic crimes law over a vlog on a security appointment.

Ghauri's lawyer says no line in the vlog was false; the agency has not named one.
