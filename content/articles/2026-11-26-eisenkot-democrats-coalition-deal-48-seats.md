Tel Aviv — Gadi Eisenkot's bloc holds 48 of 120 seats.

He needs 13 more to unseat Benjamin Netanyahu, [Israel](country:IL)'s prime minister.

The Democrats, who get 4 ministries including internal security, signed after leader Yair Golan froze talks over Eisenkot's negotiations with Likud.

Arab-led parties' 19 seats would give him a majority, but Avigdor Liberman has shunned them.

Eisenkot's deadline is 20 December, and ultra-Orthodox talks turn on the military draft.
