Accra — Unguarded, a drug-discovery AI designed 1,020 likely-toxic proteins.

MinoHealth's founder asks what stops someone taking such designs to a made-to-order DNA company.

Researchers at [Ghana](country:GH)'s MinoHealth AI Labs removed the model's guardrails in 2024 but never made any design in a lab.

"You don't need to be a technical expert to do jailbreaking," said Peter Garraghan, founder of AI security company Mindgard.

A Kenyan ministry official says DNA-synthesis regulation is now debated at the highest levels of African government.
