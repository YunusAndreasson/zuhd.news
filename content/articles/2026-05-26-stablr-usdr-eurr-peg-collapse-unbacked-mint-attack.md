[Malta](country:MT)-based StablR's 1-of-3 multisig let any single owner mint; the attacker added themselves as admin and offloaded tokens for $2.8 million.

StablR's euro token now trades at $0.548 against a euro worth $1.16; no restoration date for redemptions has been set.
