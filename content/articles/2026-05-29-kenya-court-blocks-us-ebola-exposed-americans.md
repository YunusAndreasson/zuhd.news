Nairobi — [Kenya](country:KE) courts halted a [US](country:US) Ebola offloading plan.

Washington refused to repatriate, planning a Kenyan facility instead of its own specialized biocontainment hospitals.

WHO reports 1,041 Ebola cases in Congo; a June 2 court hearing will decide the facility's future.
