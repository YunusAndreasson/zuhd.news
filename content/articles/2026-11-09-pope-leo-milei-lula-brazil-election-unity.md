Buenos Aires — Javier Milei has refused to recognise Lula's election win.

[Argentina](country:AR) and [Brazil](country:BR), its largest trading partner, face one of their tensest moments.

Brazil recalled its ambassador and summoned Argentina's envoy after Milei backed Flávio Bolsonaro, who fled to the [US](country:US) through Argentina.

"Argentina is called to preserve this willingness to engage in dialogue," Pope Leo XIV said beside Milei.

Leo is not visiting Brazil, and leaves Argentina for Peru on Wednesday.
