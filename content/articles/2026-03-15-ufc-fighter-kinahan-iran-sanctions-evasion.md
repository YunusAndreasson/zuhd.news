Former UFC fighter Mounir Lazzez directed 2 Iranian oil tankers. Both ships were sanctioned in 2024 for carrying Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps-linked oil; Lazzez had also publicly called organized crime boss Daniel Kinahan his 'friend and advisor.' Investigators are now asking whether Lazzez knew the ships' purpose when he signed the directorship documents.
