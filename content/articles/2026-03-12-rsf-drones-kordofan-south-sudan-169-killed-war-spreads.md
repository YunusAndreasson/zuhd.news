RSF-armed militias killed 169 people in South Sudan. The Rapid Support Forces inherited the Janjaweed tactic of arming proxy militias to extend territorial control without deploying regular forces across borders. The RSF's drone campaign has also expanded into Kordofan and Blue Nile, approaching the Ethiopian and Chadian borders.
