Reno — The Navy unveiled its extreme-range AIM-424 "Malice" missile.

At that range the real targets are tankers and radar planes, not enemy fighters.

Guiding that shot depends on a satellite and aircraft datalink chain a jammer can break.

The Navy has not disclosed Malice's range, cost, or when it enters service.
