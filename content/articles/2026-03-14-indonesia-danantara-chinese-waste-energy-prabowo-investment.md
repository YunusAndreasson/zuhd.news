Indonesia's Danantara chose its 3rd Chinese waste plant operator. The sovereign wealth fund has awarded all 3 of its waste-to-energy contracts to Chinese firms, extending Beijing's infrastructure presence under President Prabowo Subianto. Zhejiang Weiming, the selected Chinese firm, must form a local consortium for technology transfer before Bogor operations begin.
