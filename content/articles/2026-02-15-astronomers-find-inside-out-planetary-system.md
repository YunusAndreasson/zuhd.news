4 planets orbit in reverse order. Astronomers using the European Space Agency's CHEOPS satellite discovered the red dwarf star LHS 1903 hosts rocky, gaseous, gaseous, then rocky planets — the opposite of the arrangement in our solar system. Standard formation models predict rocky worlds close to the star and gas giants farther out. Lead author Thomas Wilson of the University of Warwick proposed the outermost rocky planet formed last, after the system's gas had been swept away. The findings, published in Science, suggest planetary formation is less predictable than assumed.
