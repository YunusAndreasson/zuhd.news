Pakistan — 2 of 3 signatories have ratified the Makkah defence pact.

Pakistan and [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) finished ratifying as Houthi attacks on the kingdom continue.

Under Article 2, an attack on one member is an attack on all, and the three decide aid together.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s Foreign Office calls the pact defensive and says it is not directed against any country.

[Turkey](country:TR)'s parliament, which received the pact this week, has yet to ratify it.
