Lahore — Police dropped rape charges against 5 of 8 suspects.

One of the 5 is reportedly linked to a senior minister, and the complainants are 2 foreign women.

The report still charges all 5 with illegal detention and extortion, and was filed before forensic results arrived.

Defence lawyer Salman Shahid says police made his low-paid clients scapegoats and favoured the key suspects.

Judge Rafaqat Ali Gondal hears the case on 11 November.
