North Richland Hills — City asked $2.3 million for Flock records.

The Texas Privacy Coalition wants records on errors, misuse and effectiveness of the licence-plate cameras.

Officials said searching about a terabyte of communications would take 14 years of labour at $15 an hour.

Some cities gave 400,000 pages free, while officials quoted Houston station KPRC up to $121,000.

The Department of Public Safety is asking the attorney general to let it withhold all the Flock data the Texas Tribune requested.
