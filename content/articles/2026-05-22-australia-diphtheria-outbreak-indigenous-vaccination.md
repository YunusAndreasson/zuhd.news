Darwin — [Australia](country:AU) recorded 230 diphtheria cases and 1 death.

Most cases are in remote Indigenous communities; the Northern Territory's chief health officer says adolescent booster coverage has fallen to 67%.

The government pledged A$7.2 million and extra medical staff for affected communities, with case numbers still accelerating.
