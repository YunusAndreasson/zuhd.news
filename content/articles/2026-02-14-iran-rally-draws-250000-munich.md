250,000 rallied against Iran's government in Munich. Protesters travelled from across Europe to demand accountability for a crackdown on nationwide protests last month that rights groups say killed thousands. Reza Pahlavi, the exiled son of Iran's last shah, told the crowd he would lead a transition to secular democracy. US Senator Lindsey Graham addressed the rally, saying "help is on the way." Parallel demonstrations took place in Toronto and Los Angeles.
