title: "Immune Cells Hunt Hidden Tumours"

category: "science"

Redesigned immune cells erased tumours that current immunotherapy missed. Solid tumours survive by hiding target proteins at near-undetectable levels, but the redesigned receptor detects signals 50 times fainter. Columbia University researchers plan a Phase I trial targeting ovarian cancer, with potential applications in glioblastoma and pancreatic cancer.
