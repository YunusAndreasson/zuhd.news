Tokyo — Commodity Futures Trading Commission data shows 115,000 yen short contracts at a 9-year peak.

Investors borrow cheap yen to fund higher-yield global assets — a carry trade sensitive to Japan's rate path.

If Tuesday's Bank of [Japan](country:JP) raises rates, those shorts unwind and cascade through global risk markets.
