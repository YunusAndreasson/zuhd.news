Islamabad — Retired Lieutenant General Nauman Mahmood will run the Makkah Defence Alliance.

The alliance of [Pakistan](country:PK), [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) and [Turkey](country:TR) gets its first secretary-general, based in Riyadh.

Mahmood was appointed on 23 September and is expected to assume his responsibilities in Riyadh soon, the Foreign Office said.

Pakistan will keep mediating between [Iran](country:IR) and the [US](country:US), its Foreign Office said, alongside Qatar.
