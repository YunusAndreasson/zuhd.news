Bara — Police fire killed 13-year-old Habeel Khan, witnesses say.

[Pakistan](country:PK) suspended the province's elected government, led by Imran Khan's party, on 11 October.

Emergency rule removed the Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf government, and arrests of party workers and communications cuts followed across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Irfanullah, a party worker whose 19-year-old brother was shot in the foot, says the family will not abandon the party.
