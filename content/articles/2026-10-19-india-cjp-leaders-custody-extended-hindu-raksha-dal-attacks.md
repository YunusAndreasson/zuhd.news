New Delhi — A magistrate kept 2 flag-waving protest leaders jailed.

[India](country:IN)'s youth movement wants the election chief out over voter rolls, which the commission denies.

Video from 10 October shows men beating protesters with a policeman present, and police detained four members of the Hindu Raksha Dal.

The magistrate said they breached their bonds and their promise not to repeat the offence did not inspire confidence.

Ashutosh Ranka and Saurav Das return before the magistrate on 21 October.
