Los Angeles — Terry Tao now co-authors proofs with machine-checked steps.

Proof formalization splits any theorem into machine-verifiable chunks, letting large teams and AI co-author without trusting each other's reasoning.

Whether AI can originate new proof steps — not just verify them — is now mathematics' open question.
