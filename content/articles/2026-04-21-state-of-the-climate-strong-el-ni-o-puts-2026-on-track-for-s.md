London — Q1 2026 was the fourth-warmest quarter on record across Carbon Brief's 5-dataset average. A developing El Niño gives 2026 a 19% chance of topping 2024; a 'super' El Niño by autumn would push 2027 to a new heat record. Arctic sea ice tied 2025 for its lowest winter peak.
