98 nations met February's UN biodiversity deadline. The 2022 Kunming-Montreal framework requires $200 billion a year to protect 30% of Earth by 2030; pledges cover a fraction of that. Countries that missed the deadline won't appear in the global review; COP17 in Armenia in October 2026 will assess progress.
