London — The same catheter setting burns unevenly.

Undertreated patients relapse into arrhythmia while overtreated ones risk perforation or injury.

Current returns through the torso to a skin electrode, so body fat sets impedance and therefore delivered energy.

Researchers, using virtual patients rather than real trials, call for testing weight-based settings.
