Redwood City — 2 drugs turn up the brain's built-in cleaning system. The glymphatic system flushes brain waste during deep sleep; ACX-02 boosted that clearance in 19 adults, delaying estimated Alzheimer's onset by 7 years. Trials in early-stage Alzheimer's patients are next; researchers say the mechanism may also slow Parkinson's disease.
