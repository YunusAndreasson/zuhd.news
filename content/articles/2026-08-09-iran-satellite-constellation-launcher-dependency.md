Tehran — Three satellites are meant to launch on one mission.

An Iranian rocket, not a purchased Russian one, would show real space capability.

[Iran](country:IR)'s ICT minister Sattar Hashemi says the Martyr Soleimani constellation plans 24 satellites total.

Hashemi did not name a launch vehicle, and Iran's orbital record has lagged its announced schedules.
