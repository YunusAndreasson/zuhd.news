Spain — 31.1% survived 18 months with the virus, versus 8.5%.

Median overall survival rose from 8.6 to 10.6 months in metastatic pancreatic cancer.

A virus engineered to make hyaluronidase, an enzyme that breaks down hyaluronan, is infused with chemotherapy.

The overall-survival gain missed significance in the intent-to-treat group, and 22.6% of virus patients had serious events.

The authors say the results warrant a blinded phase 3 trial.
