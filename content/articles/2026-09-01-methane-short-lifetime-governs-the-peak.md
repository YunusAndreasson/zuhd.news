London — Methane fades from the atmosphere in decades, not centuries.

Cutting it is the only lever that shows up in temperature within a couple of decades.

The largest tractable sources are fossil-fuel venting and flaring, measurable from orbit.

Even under existing net-zero pledges, unchecked methane still pushes peak warming past 1.85C, a new study found.
