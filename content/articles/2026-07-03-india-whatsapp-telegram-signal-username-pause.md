New Delhi — India ordered Meta to pause WhatsApp usernames.

Usernames let users message without sharing a phone number, which the ministry says makes it harder to trace scammers in [India](country:IN)'s fraud-heavy market.

Meta has 3 days to respond; identical notices now target Telegram and Signal.
