Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s traders demanded turnover tax cut to 0.5%.

Turnover tax charges gross sales regardless of profit, hitting loss-making small retailers unable to pass costs on.

The demand arrives ahead of the annual budget, when the Federal Board of Revenue sets new revenue targets.
