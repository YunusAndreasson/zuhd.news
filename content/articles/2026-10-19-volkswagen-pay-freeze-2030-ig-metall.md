Germany — 660,000 Volkswagen workers face zero raises until 2030.

The union IG Metall fears real wage losses of at least 10%.

The directive, in force since early October, ends budgeting that covered inflation and bargained raises, the union says.

Works councils call it absurd, noting personnel costs are a fraction of Volkswagen's cost of sales.

Talks open in late November in [Germany](country:DE), where IG Metall demands 5%.
