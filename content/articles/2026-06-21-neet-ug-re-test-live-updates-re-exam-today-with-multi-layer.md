New Delhi — 2.2 million students re-sat India's medical entrance exam.

The National Testing Agency cancelled [India](country:IN)'s May 3 test after paper-leak allegations triggered protests and calls for the Education Minister's resignation.

Results timing has not been announced; Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan faces renewed resignation demands.
