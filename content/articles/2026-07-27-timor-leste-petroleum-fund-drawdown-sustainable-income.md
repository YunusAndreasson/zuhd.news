Dili — [Timor-Leste](country:TL)'s oil wealth is running out.

A Lowy Institute analysis warns the fund's depletion could weaken the patronage networks that have kept the founding generation in power.

Timor-Leste's youngest citizens, with no memory of the independence war, will inherit whatever is left.
