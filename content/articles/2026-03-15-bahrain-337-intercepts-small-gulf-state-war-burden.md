Bahrain intercepted 337 missiles and drones in 23 days. The country hosts the US Fifth Fleet, making its 1.5 million people targets in a conflict their government did not initiate. The Bahrain Defence Force has given no figure for remaining interceptor stocks or for when the strikes might pause.
