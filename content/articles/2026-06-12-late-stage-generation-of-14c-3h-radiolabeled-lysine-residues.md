Aarhus — Chemists label finished peptides without rebuilding from scratch.

Hydroformylation inserts carbon-14 and tritium into lysine residues on finished peptides — skipping costly rebuilds from labeled precursors.

Radiolabeling tracks drug distribution; the authors say the method could cut time and cost in peptide therapeutics development.
