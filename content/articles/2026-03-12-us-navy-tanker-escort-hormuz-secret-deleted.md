Brent crude fell $40 on a deleted tweet. Energy Secretary Chris Wright briefly posted that the Navy had escorted a tanker through Hormuz before the White House denied it. Iran's Revolutionary Guard mocked the episode and renewed its threat that no oil would leave the strait.
