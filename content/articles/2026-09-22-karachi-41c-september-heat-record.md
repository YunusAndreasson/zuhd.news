Karachi — Mercury hit 41°C, the hottest September day in a decade.

High humidity turns this heat lethal for outdoor workers and slum residents facing power cuts.

Humid heat blocks sweat from cooling the body, and a low-pressure system trapped over [Pakistan](country:PK) blocked the cooling sea breeze.

"El Nino conditions are driving regional atmospheric warming," said Ameer Hyder Laghari, the city's chief meteorologist.

The city's 2015 heatwave killed more than 1,200 people, a toll officials hope isn't repeated.
