Washington — Congress passed the biggest housing-supply law in decades.

The 21st Century Road to Housing Act cleared Congress by wide margins to ease a shortage measured in millions of [US](country:US) units.

Whether the law lifts supply enough to lower prices, or mainly benefits developers, remains untested.
