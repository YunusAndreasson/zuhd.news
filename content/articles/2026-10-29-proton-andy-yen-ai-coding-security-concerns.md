Switzerland — Slack messages show Proton's CEO rejecting pre-release security testing.

Users chose Proton's encrypted email for privacy, and its code increasingly comes from AI.

Proton tracks how much AI code it generates, and Andy Yen says this is the last year it hires engineers.

"Of course it's possible to be fast and secure," CEO Andy Yen wrote in the security channel.

Proton said it will re-interview staff about their AI use.
