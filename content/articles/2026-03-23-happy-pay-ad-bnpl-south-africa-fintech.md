Johannesburg — 11 million South African adults have impaired credit records. Happy Pay, a buy-now-pay-later startup, raised $5 million from French VC Partech to expand merchant partnerships, replacing interest fees with advertising revenue. Its 600,000 users represent less than 6% of that pool — the gap the company must close to prove the ad-funded model works at scale.
