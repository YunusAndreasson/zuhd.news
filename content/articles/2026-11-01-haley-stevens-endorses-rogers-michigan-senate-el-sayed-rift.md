Detroit — Stevens stood beside Republican Mike Rogers at a church Sunday.

The Democratic congresswoman endorsed him over her party's nominee, Abdul El-Sayed, two days before Tuesday's vote.

She had demanded El-Sayed condemn an outside group's ad linking [US](country:US) military aid to Israel to grocery prices, which she called antisemitic.

El-Sayed said the ad "was not made by me" and that he disagrees with its contents.

Michigan voters choose between El-Sayed and Rogers on Tuesday.
