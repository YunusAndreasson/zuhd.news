Houston — The Moon race is about oxygen, not footprints.

[The US](country:US) and [China](country:CN) both target bases requiring oxygen extracted on-site; launching it from Earth's gravity well dominates mission mass and cost.

NASA's Artemis and China's lunar base plans both require on-site oxygen production before any crewed Mars mission, but neither has demonstrated it on the lunar surface.
