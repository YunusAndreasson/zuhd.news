Chicago — Aiming below 120 mm Hg cut post-stroke deaths and events.

A third of stroke patients miss blood pressure guidelines, said Bruce Ovbiagele.

OPTIMAL-STROKE compared a systolic target of 120 mm Hg with the standard 140, and requires kidney monitoring.

OPTIMAL-DIABETES found no reduction in major events or deaths from a sub-120 target in type 2 diabetes.

The spironolactone trial REDUCE is unpublished and measured blood pressure, not outcomes.
