Dutse — 4 ruling-party incumbents ousted in [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Jigawa State primaries.

The 4 were blocked at stakeholder meetings before balloting, replaced by fresh candidates including a former aide to President Buhari.

Defeated incumbents reaffirmed loyalty to the party; 2027 general elections will test whether its northern base holds.
