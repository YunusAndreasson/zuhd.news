Washington — Xi Jinping's first state visit to the [US](country:US) since 2015 begins 23 September.

It is his second meeting with Trump this year, after their May summit in Beijing.

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Vice-Premier He Lifeng agreed Sunday to open an AI dialogue and activate a bilateral trade body.

Washington accuses [Chinese](country:CN) firms of extracting US AI models, and Beijing calls the claim groundless and threatens retaliation.

Trump greets Xi at Joint Base Andrews on Wednesday, ahead of Thursday's White House talks.
