Apia — A 1,700-year pause in Pacific voyaging abruptly ended.

Sediment isotopes show a severe drought hit Samoa and Tonga just as populations grew, researchers found, around 900 AD.

The study, in the Journal of Pacific Archaeology, doesn't resolve whether drought or canoe technology mattered more.
