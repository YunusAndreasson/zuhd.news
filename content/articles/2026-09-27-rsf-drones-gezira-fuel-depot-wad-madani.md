Wad Madani — Sudan's Rapid Support Forces drones hit a fuel depot.

The depot supplies petrol and diesel for pumps, bakeries and hospitals across [Sudan](country:SD)'s recaptured Gezira.

Drones struck the Umm Alila depot near Wad Madani at dawn, and crews contained the fire to its fuel tanks.

Gezira's civil defence has not said how long fuel supplies to Wad Madani will be disrupted.
