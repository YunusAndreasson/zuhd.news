Fairford — B-1 bombers left [Britain](country:GB) over a reported drone threat.

President Trump said threats prompted the exit from a base where B-1s park in the open.

Reports cite fear of a Spiderweb-style attack, as when [Ukraine](country:UA)'s explosive-laden quadcopters destroyed at least 10 Russian bombers on open flightlines.

Trump would not say whether [Iran](country:IR) got armed drones into Britain, and many details remain unconfirmed.
