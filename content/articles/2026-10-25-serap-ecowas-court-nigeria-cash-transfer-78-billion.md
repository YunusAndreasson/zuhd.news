Abuja — Auditor-General questions ₦33.751 billion sent to 3.2 million households.

The transfers were meant for vulnerable households in 35 states of [Nigeria](country:NG).

A rights group sued at the West African regional court over ₦78.8 billion questioned in 2023 and 2024.

The Auditor-General found ₦36.744 billion paid without prepayment audits and ₦4.616 billion in unsupported spending.

The Socio-Economic Rights and Accountability Project asks the court to order a full account and an independent investigation.
