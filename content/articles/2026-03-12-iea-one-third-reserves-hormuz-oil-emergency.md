400 million barrels will be released from International Energy Agency member reserves. The release doubles the 182 million barrels unlocked after Russia's Ukraine invasion; Japan alone adds 80 million barrels, covering roughly 45 days of its oil supply. Hormuz remains effectively closed to Western tankers, and Saudi Arabia's bypass pipeline can carry only 7 million of the 18 million barrels that previously transited daily.
