UK coal use returned to 1600 levels last year. The last British coal plant closed in September 2024; total greenhouse gas emissions fell 2.4% to 364 million tonnes, now 54% below 1990 levels. UK gas prices already doubled in March after Hormuz closed, and analysts warn the fossil fuel scramble could reverse years of decarbonization progress.
