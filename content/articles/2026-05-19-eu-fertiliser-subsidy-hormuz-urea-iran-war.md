Brussels — The [EU](country:DE) pledged "exceptional support" for farmers after urea prices surged on Gulf shipping disruptions.

Nearly half of traded urea transits the Strait of Hormuz; Gulf plants have cut or halted production as gas supply faltered.

The EU will increase its agriculture crisis reserve but named no figures or timeline.
