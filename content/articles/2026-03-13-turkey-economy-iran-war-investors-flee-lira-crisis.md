Investors pulled $30bn from Turkey in 2 weeks. Turkey borders Iran and imports all its energy — oil above $100 simultaneously hits the lira, widens the trade deficit, and rattles investors. If oil stays near $100, economists warn Turkey's inflation rises by 5 points above its current 33%.
