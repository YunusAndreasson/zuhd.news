Bogotá — [Colombia](country:CO) has 746 pending copper mining licenses, mapping data show.

The IEA projects a 30% supply shortfall by 2035, pushing miners onto Indigenous and protected land.

[Argentina](country:AR) already has 76 active licenses on file, with hundreds more under review.
