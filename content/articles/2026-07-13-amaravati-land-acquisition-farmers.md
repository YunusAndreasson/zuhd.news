Vijayawada — Police bulldozed farmland for Andhra's new capital.

The opposition says police and earthmovers pressured farmers who refused to pool land into Amaravati's capital-city project.

The dispute remains before the High Court as both parties trade blame ahead of [India](country:IN)'s 2029 state elections.
