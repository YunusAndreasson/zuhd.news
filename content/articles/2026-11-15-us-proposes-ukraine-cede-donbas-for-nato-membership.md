Kyiv — Washington asked Ukraine to trade Donbas for NATO entry, Zelensky says.

The offer asks Kyiv and Moscow to drop a red line each.

[Russia](country:RU) already holds about 88% of Donbas, so Kyiv would give up the rest in exchange for a membership path.

Zelensky says any such deal would go to a national referendum, and the Kremlin has not yet responded.

France, Germany, Italy, Britain and Poland will send envoys to the next round of talks.
