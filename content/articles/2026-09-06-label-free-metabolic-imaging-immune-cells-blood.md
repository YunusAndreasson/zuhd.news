London — Light from cells' own chemistry reveals their activity.

It reads immune-cell function from an ordinary blood draw already banked worldwide.

Energy-carrying molecules glow faintly on their own, and that glow shifts between a resting and a working cell.

The method still needs testing against a clinical reference before any diagnostic use.
