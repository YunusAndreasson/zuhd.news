Washington — Judge blocked [US](country:US) executive editing of park history.

Judge Kelley ordered at least 45 altered signs — on slavery, Native history, and climate — restored by July 4, citing viewpoint-based censorship.

The Interior Department said it would examine appeal options; further changes to park exhibits are blocked.
