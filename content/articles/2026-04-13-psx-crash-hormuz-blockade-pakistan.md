Karachi — Pakistan's KSE-100 shed 5,000 points at open. Trump's Sunday order to blockade Iranian ports triggered the selloff; Hormuz has been effectively closed since US-Israeli strikes began on February 28. IRGC says military vessels approaching Hormuz violate the ceasefire; Pakistan imports most fuel through the strait.
