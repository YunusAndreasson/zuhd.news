San Mateo, California — PlayStation discs end for new games in January 2028.

Digital-only titles exist as licenses, not property: players cannot resell, lend, or preserve them, and publishers can revoke or delist at will.

Titles already released or announced before 2028 stay on disc; after, new releases go digital-only, ending physical retail.
