Ishigaki — Winds of 162 kph are bearing down on Sakishima.

Airlines grounded dozens of flights to this remote chain near Taiwan, whose isolation makes it [Japan](country:JP)'s first line of exposure to Pacific storms.

Neighboring [Taiwan](country:TW) shut markets and offices as the storm tracks toward both.
