Prague braces for its largest protest since 2019. A proposed law would force foreign-funded NGOs to register and pay fines up to €612,000, a structure critics say mirrors Russia's foreign agent legislation. Parliament votes this week, with rights groups warning passage would put Czechia on the authoritarian path of Hungary and Slovakia.
