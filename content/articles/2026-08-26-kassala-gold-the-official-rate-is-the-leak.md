Kassala — Street dealers now outbid the licensed market.

Licensed dealers shut Kassala's oldest gold market rather than keep losing sales.

[Sudan](country:SD)'s central bank buys gold below the street rate, so sellers take it to the unlicensed trade instead.

Traders want government protection from itinerant sellers; authorities have not yet responded.
