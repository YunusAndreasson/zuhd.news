Cardiff — Only England and Scotland will screen newborns.

Babies born just across the border get a different medical future.

Spinal muscular atrophy destroys motor neurons from birth, and treatment only saves neurons still alive at diagnosis.

The Welsh government says it awaits a [UK](country:GB)-wide recommendation before deciding on screening.
