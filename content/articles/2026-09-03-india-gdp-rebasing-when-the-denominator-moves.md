New Delhi — [India](country:IN) shrank last year's economic base by 7%.

A smaller base year makes growth look stronger without a rupee of new output.

The statistics ministry revised the base year from 2011-12 to 2022-23, changing prior estimates too.

Q1 figures won't be finalized until February 2029, after further rounds of revision.
