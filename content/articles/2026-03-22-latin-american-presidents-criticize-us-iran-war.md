Bogotá — 33 hemispheric presidents demanded an immediate ceasefire in the US-Iran war. The Community of Latin American and Caribbean States — CELAC — became the first major regional bloc to formally oppose the conflict; Colombian President Gustavo Petro said the strikes risk a global financial crisis and world war. Petro called for international mediation; no ceasefire has been agreed.
