Cape Coast — 52% of sub-Saharan women now have bank access.

A new study of 25,511 women-headed households found financial access measurably strengthens resilience to droughts, floods and other climate shocks.

The gender gap in account access has widened to 12 percentage points since 2011, researchers say.
