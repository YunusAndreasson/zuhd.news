Chandigarh — 3 senior Indian Administrative Service officers' homes were raided overnight.

[India](country:IN)'s Central Bureau of Investigation alleges the officers routed Rs 661 crore ($79m) from 10 state departments through a consultancy.

Two private banks remain under investigation; investigators are tracing whether officers received personal benefits.
