Cambridge — Historians say climate scientists reversed cause and effect in Roman Britain.

Tree-ring drought data was matched to the 367 Barbarian Conspiracy, but the sole Latin source, Ammianus, shows famine as revolt consequence, not trigger.

The dispute reveals a pattern: climate teams apply inference to ancient sources without historians.
