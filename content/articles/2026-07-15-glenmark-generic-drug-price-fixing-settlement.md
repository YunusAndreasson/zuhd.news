Mumbai — Glenmark will pay $29.6 million to settle.

Dozens of [US](country:US) states accuse generic drugmakers — including [India](country:IN)-based Glenmark — of coordinating prices in a market meant to stay cheap.

Glenmark admits no wrongdoing, continuing a decade-long, multistate probe into the generics industry.
