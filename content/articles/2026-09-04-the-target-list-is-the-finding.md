Belgrade — Spyware infected 14 Serbians since December, researchers found.

Victims include a sitting lawmaker, an opposition politician and student protesters.

Forensic researchers can prove device infection but not procurement, the exact gap export-licensing regimes were meant to close.

Which vendor sold it, and who in [Serbia](country:RS) signed, remains unnamed.
