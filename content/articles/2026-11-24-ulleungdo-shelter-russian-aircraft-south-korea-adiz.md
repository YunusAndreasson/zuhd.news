Ulleungdo — An air raid alert sent 8,700 islanders toward shelters Tuesday.

It was the fourth Russian military entry into South Korea's air defence zone this year, and the second in a week.

An air defence identification zone is not sovereign airspace, and entering it without notice breaks no international law.

Russia's defence ministry says its flights follow international airspace rules, TASS reported, and [Russia](country:RU) does not recognise the zone.

[South Korea](country:KR) summoned Russia's defence attaché Tuesday and says it will keep responding actively to aircraft in the zone.
