New Haven — A [US](country:US) single-payer system could save $1 trillion yearly.

It ties the savings to 114,000 avoided deaths a year, the study finds.

The model attributes most gains to cutting administrative overhead across multiple insurers, not care itself.

The Yale-led preprint has not yet been peer-reviewed.
