Gaza — Israel killed 929 since the October 11 ceasefire.

The Gaza health ministry counts nearly 73,000 dead since October 2023; relief teams cannot reach bodies still under rubble and sealed roads.

7 more bodies reached hospitals in the past 48 hours; the ministry said others remain unreachable.
