Washington — Trump set June 14 to sign the [Iran](country:IR) nuclear deal.

Iran confirmed a deal was "very likely" in coming days but denied signing Sunday, as conservative MPs and protesters in Tehran condemned uranium dilution terms.

Iran's enriched uranium stays underground pending a separate [US](country:US) removal process Trump said follows "when all is calm."
