Most laptops are sealed; 2 new Lenovo ThinkPads are not. Lenovo's T14 Gen 7 and T16 Gen 5 earned iFixit's first 10/10 for any major laptop, pushed by the EU's 2025 right-to-repair law. Lenovo said 10/10 is "not the destination"; no rival manufacturer has matched the score since the law took effect.
