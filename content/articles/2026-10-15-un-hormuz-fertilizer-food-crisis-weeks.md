New York — Fertilizer needs a deal within weeks, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

Asia and Africa miss planting season otherwise, said UN task force head Jorge Moreira da Silva.

Before the war the strait handled about a third of traded urea and nearly half of seaborne sulphur.

[Iran](country:IR)'s state-run Mehr News Agency quoted Rear Admiral Habibollah Sayyari saying the strait is closed and transit needs coordination.

Moreira da Silva wants the [US](country:US) and Iran to separate fertilizer from the comprehensive talks.
