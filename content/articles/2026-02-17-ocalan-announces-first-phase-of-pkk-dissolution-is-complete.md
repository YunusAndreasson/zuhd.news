The first phase is complete. Abdullah Ocalan, imprisoned leader of the Kurdistan Workers' Party (PKK), told a visiting lawmaker the armed campaign that killed tens of thousands over 40 years has ended. He called the next phase "democratic integration," requiring formal recognition of Kurdish rights.
