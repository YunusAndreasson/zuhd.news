Nairobi — War extends child abuse years past the ceasefire. 35,000 young people across 9 African countries were surveyed; results show family and peer violence rise for years after every ceasefire. For Sudan, Lebanon, and DRC, every ceasefire is now the start of a new child-violence curve.
