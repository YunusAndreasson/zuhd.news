Johannesburg — South Africa's commercial crime nearly doubled since 2014. Murder, robbery, and sexual offences all fell last year, while police recorded 143,600 commercial crime cases in 2025 — up from 76,744 in 2014. Police data doesn't break down what kind of fraud is growing, leaving victims unable to trace where the money goes.
