Chicago — Fungicide seed treatments barely lift Midwest soybean yields. A Nature randomized trial found only 22 kg/ha yield gain, below observational estimates and rarely enough to turn a profit. Researchers called for individual farm-gate evaluation and flagged potential harm to soil microbes from widespread use.
