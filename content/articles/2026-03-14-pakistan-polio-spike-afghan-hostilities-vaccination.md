War blocked polio vaccinators from 120,000 children in Pakistan. Pakistan's cases had fallen 6-fold in early 2026, but the conflict has cut off southern Khyber Pakhtunkhwa just as the high-transmission season approaches in April. A polio expert warns the virus could rebound through that unvaccinated pocket if fighting continues into spring.
