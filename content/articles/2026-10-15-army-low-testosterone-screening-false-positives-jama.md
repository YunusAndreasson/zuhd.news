Louisiana — A medical journal study estimates 26% of active-duty soldiers would test low for testosterone.

Clinical hypogonadism affects 1% of men at most, so most flags would be false alarms.

The Pentagon's 300 ng/dL cutoff flags 26%, and a lower 264 cutoff used by [US](country:US) health officials still flags 15%.

The mandatory screen covers only men 30 and older, and symptom questions come first, so fewer would be tested.

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth's order stands despite objections from the Endocrine Society and the American Urological Association.
