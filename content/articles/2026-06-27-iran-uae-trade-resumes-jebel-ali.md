Dubai — War-delayed Iran cargo began clearing at Jebel Ali port, [UAE](country:AE)'s main hub.

[Iran](country:IR)'s Trade Promotion Organization confirmed bilateral trade resumed Friday, weeks after the 12-day conflict halted Gulf shipments.

Bilateral trade is recovering but still below pre-war levels, even as strikes continue in the surrounding strait.
