Palu — A 6.7 magnitude quake struck central Sulawesi, damaging buildings.

Shallow depth — 10 kilometres — amplifies ground shaking; Palu's strike-slip fault killed more than 4,000 in a 2018 quake and tsunami.

[Indonesia](country:ID)'s disaster agency said no casualties were confirmed and no tsunami warning was issued; aftershock monitoring continues.
