Johannesburg — Basic living is now the top reason members withdraw.

Emergency retirement savings are becoming grocery money for households near the edge.

[South Africa](country:ZA)'s two-pot system split contributions so workers no longer resign just to access savings, Old Mutual's Acton said.

Two-pot data show preservation rates up 33%, but each withdrawal is taxed and compounds away decades of growth.
