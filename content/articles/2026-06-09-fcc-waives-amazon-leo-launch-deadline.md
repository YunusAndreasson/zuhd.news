Washington — Amazon has launched 333 of 3,232 Kuiper satellites.

The Federal Communications Commission waived its July milestone, citing Starlink as the sole active low-orbit broadband provider; the deadline had blocked spectrum warehousing.

Both Amazon heavy-lift rockets are grounded with no launch date set.
