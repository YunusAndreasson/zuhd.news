London — Mango waste pulled manganese from unsmeltable ore.

Mango-growing countries gain a domestic manganese source instead of imports.

Researchers treated kernel biomass with sodium hydroxide, binding metal ions at room temperature for 98.9% removal.

The process held up over three reuse cycles but has only been tested at laboratory scale.
