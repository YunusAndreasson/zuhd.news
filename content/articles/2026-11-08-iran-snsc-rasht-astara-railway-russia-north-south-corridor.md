Tehran — [Iran](country:IR)'s security council backed a Russian-run railway, state agency Mehr reported.

The 162 km Rasht-Astara line is the missing link in the corridor to the Persian Gulf, Mehr says.

Deputy roads minister Houshang Bazvand said the final 37 km of land passed to the [Russian](country:RU) company on Wednesday.

Iranian engineers will join the surveys, which Bazvand expects to speed the work.
