Iran won't go nuclear even after Israel struck Bushehr. The anti-nuclear fatwa is political theology — breaking it would fracture the Islamic Republic's religious legitimacy more than any military defeat. New Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei has yet to issue his own ruling on nuclear weapons.
