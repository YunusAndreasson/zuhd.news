Peshawar — Rs6.4 billion was deducted without provincial consent.

Left unchallenged, the move sets a precedent for unilateral cuts to any province's share.

Deductions taken at source happen before a province can dispute or vote on them.

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's government has petitioned [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Federal Constitutional Court to enforce its share.
