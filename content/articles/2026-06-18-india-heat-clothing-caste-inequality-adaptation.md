Delhi — For Dalit families, cotton clothing — the basic heat adaptation — costs double what they can afford.

[India](country:IN)'s Dalit sanitation workers cannot prioritise clothing over food, leaving them in heat-trapping synthetics.

Nearly 70% of India's sewer workers are from oppressed castes — the same heat, unequally distributed.
