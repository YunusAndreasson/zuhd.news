Hong Kong — Air drops 12.8 million tonnes of organic nitrogen into oceans.

That is 27% of deposited nitrogen, and about half in the central Atlantic and Indonesian seas.

Researchers measured nitrogen in 418 marine aerosol samples from 21 research cruises, then fed the results into a global atmospheric chemistry model.

"Far from a minor player in marine ecosystems," said Yu Jianzhen, the study's co-leader.

The findings are published in the journal Science.
