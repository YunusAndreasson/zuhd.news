New Delhi — An Army band opened the Red Fort ceremony with Vande Mataram.

It changes, for the first time, the order millions across [India](country:IN) watch every August 15.

Modi hoisted the flag afterward, and the band closed with the National Anthem.

Whether the sequence becomes permanent for next year's ceremony has not been said.
