London — A urine sample matched a cervical swab 94.7% of the time.

Cervical cancer kills 350,000 women yearly, most in poorer countries where a clinician exam is the barrier; self-collection removes it, a pilot study found.

The 104-woman trial is an unedited manuscript, not a screening-programme test; treatment access after diagnosis remains unfunded.
