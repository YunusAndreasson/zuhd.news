"Rearmament is not warmongering." Germany's defence chief General Carsten Breuer and his British counterpart Air Chief Marshal Sir Richard Knighton published a joint letter in The Guardian and Welt warning that Russia's military posture has "shifted decisively westward" — the first time both nations' top generals have made a shared public case for European rearmament. Britain is building 6 new munitions factories while Germany has stationed a combat brigade on NATO's eastern flank, with the alliance now targeting 5% of gross domestic product on defence by 2035.
