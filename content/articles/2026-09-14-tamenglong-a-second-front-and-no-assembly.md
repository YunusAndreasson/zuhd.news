Tamenglong — Seineh Singsit and his wife were shot dead before their six-year-old son.

Four Kuki villagers died in 24 hours, opening a second front beyond the Kuki-Meitei conflict.

Kuki groups blame Naga armed groups NSCN(IM) and ZUF(K), and [India](country:IN)'s Manipur has no elected assembly to answer for it.

Surgeons operate today to remove the bullet from the surviving child.
