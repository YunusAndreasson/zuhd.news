The US military is planning weeks-long operations against Iran. Pentagon officials told Reuters the preparations go beyond last June's one-off stealth bomber strikes on nuclear facilities. The military envisions sustained attacks on Iranian state and security infrastructure with an additional aircraft carrier group deploying to the region. President Donald Trump floated regime change at Fort Bragg on Friday, saying it "seems like that would be the best thing that could happen." US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner begin nuclear negotiations with Iranian representatives in Geneva on Tuesday, with Oman mediating, while Iran's Revolutionary Guard warned it would retaliate against any US base in the region.
