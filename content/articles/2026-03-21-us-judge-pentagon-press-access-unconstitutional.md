A federal judge voided the Pentagon's press credentialing policy. Judge Paul Friedman ruled it discriminates based on viewpoint, weeding out critical reporters and replacing them with outlets "willing to serve" the government. The Pentagon has 1 week to file a compliance report reinstating credentials for 7 New York Times journalists.
