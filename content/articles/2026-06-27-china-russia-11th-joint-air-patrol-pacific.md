Sea of Japan — 10 aircraft entered [South Korea](country:KR)'s air defense identification zone Friday.

[China](country:CN) and [Russia](country:RU) conducted their 11th Pacific joint patrol; none of the aircraft crossed into sovereign airspace, Seoul's Joint Chiefs confirmed.

Both governments offered no explanation for the patrol's frequency or timing.
