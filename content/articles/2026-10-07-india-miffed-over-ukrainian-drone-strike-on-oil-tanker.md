New Delhi — 23 Indian crew escaped a burning tanker off Sochi.

[India](country:IN)'s foreign ministry demanded an end to attacks on commercial shipping and seafarers, without naming [Ukraine](country:UA).

Russia's transport ministry said naval drones hit the Liberian-flagged Aframax Rio on Tuesday, and Indian sources said it carried crude for India.

Indian sources noted the strike came despite New Delhi's truce proposal, which Ukraine had called the most comprehensive.

Ukraine has not claimed the strike.
