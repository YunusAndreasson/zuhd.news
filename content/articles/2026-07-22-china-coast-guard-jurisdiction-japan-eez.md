Tokyo — China's coast guard says it is "exercising jurisdiction" inside Japan's exclusive economic zone.

The phrase converts a routine patrol into a sovereignty claim, the same incremental language [China](country:CN) has used to harden disputed claims elsewhere.

Tokyo must now decide whether to formally contest the framing or let it stand.
