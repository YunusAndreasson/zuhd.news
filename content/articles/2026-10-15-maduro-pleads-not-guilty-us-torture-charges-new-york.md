New York — Maduro twice refused to plead, so the judge entered it.

The [US](country:US) indictment now charges torture, including of 3 Americans, on top of drug trafficking.

Prosecutors allege he recommended methods and received updates on victims, but do not accuse him of torturing anyone personally.

The indictment rests on "false accusations and false testimony," Maduro told the judge through an interpreter.

Trial begins 14 June 2027, and the couple return for a pretrial hearing on 17 December.
