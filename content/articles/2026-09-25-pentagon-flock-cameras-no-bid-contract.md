Arlington — 9 Flock cameras now log cars entering the Pentagon.

Flock's critics say its cameras can be accessed by unauthorised users, exposing who enters the Pentagon.

The Defense Department ordered them under a 2021 M.C. Dean security contract, without competitive bidding.

"The Pentagon might as well send the data from those cameras directly to China, Russia and Iran," said Senator Ron Wyden.

Flock insists its cameras have never been hacked, and critics dispute that.
