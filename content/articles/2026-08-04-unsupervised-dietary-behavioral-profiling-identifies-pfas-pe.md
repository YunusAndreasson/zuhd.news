Seoul — Eating habits, not food choice, predict forever-chemical exposure.

Researchers grouped 4,239 Korean adults by cooking and packaging habits; the packaging-exposed group had higher forever-chemical levels, a national survey found.

The forever chemicals migrate from grease-resistant wrapping, and no Asian regulator yet screens for the pattern.
