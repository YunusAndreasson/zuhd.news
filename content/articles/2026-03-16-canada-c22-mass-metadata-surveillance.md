Canada's Bill C-22 requires surveillance backdoors in telecom networks. The bill replaces some warrantless subscriber data access with court orders while also mandating telecoms build permanent interception infrastructure into their networks. The bill faces review over whether its surveillance mandates comply with the Supreme Court's recent privacy rulings.
