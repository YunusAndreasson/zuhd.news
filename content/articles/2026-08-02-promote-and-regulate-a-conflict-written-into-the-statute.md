Beirut — Parliament just slowed the ministry bill down.

The draft law lets one ministry both promote artificial intelligence and write its own rules, with no independent privacy authority, SMEX said.

Joint committees will now review the bill; [Lebanon](country:LB) still lacks basic data-protection law.
