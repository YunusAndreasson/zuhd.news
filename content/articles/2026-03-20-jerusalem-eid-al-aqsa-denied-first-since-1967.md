Israeli police tear-gassed Al-Aqsa worshippers. Israel closed the mosque on Eid for the first time since 1967, citing a ban on gatherings over 50 amid Iranian missile strikes. The site remains closed with no reopening date as the US-Israel war on Iran enters its 21st day.
