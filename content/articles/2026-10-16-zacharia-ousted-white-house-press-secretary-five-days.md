Washington — Katie Zacharia admitted failing the bar, then lost her job.

The White House loses its spokesperson weeks before the November midterm elections.

Trump called her an attorney, but she told Fox's Sean Hannity she failed the 2011 California bar exam.

Trump told reporters he urged her to reconsider and did not say he fired her.

He said he would speak with her again after returning to Washington on Sunday.
