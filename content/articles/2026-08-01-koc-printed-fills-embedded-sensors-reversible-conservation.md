Istanbul — The missing piece of a broken pot now reports on it.

Koç University researchers scan and 3D-print fills for amphora fragments, embedding sensors that track temperature, humidity and strain — reversible, unlike plaster.

The method needs only a scanner and a printer, reaching collections in Syria, Yemen and Sudan that lack climate control.
