New York — Analysts eye $300,000-$500,000 bitcoin by 2029.

Bitcoin's 4-year cycle tracks its mining-reward halving, next due April 2028, but growing ETF and institutional ownership is damping volatility, analysts say.

The next cycle's peak is expected around 2029, testing whether deeper liquidity caps future rallies.
