Berkeley — Off-equilibrium quantum data fixes machine learning materials models' core blind spot.

Models trained on stable crystals fail under strain or defects; Open Materials 2024 adds quantum off-equilibrium simulations to close that gap, researchers said.

The open dataset lets labs train more generalisable models; scale-up and stability remain open challenges.
