Johannesburg — [South Africa](country:ZA)'s National Prosecuting Authority retracted its 2019 interference admission.

A 2019 affidavit said it "does not deny" political pressure halted apartheid-era prosecutions; counsel now denies this.

Former president Mbeki contests a court summons before the Khampepe Commission's December deadline.
