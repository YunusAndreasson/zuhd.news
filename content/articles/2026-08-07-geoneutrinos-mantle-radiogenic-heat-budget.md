Sudbury — Particles that ignore rock are now mapping it.

Detectors 2 kilometers underground count antineutrinos from decaying uranium, thorium and potassium, measuring how much of Earth's heat is radiogenic.

That number sets the budget for mantle convection, which drives plate tectonics and the magnetic field shielding Earth.
