Seoul — Pyongyang threatens tactical strike weapons and loudspeakers on the border.

Loudspeakers would risk reversing the de-escalation South Korean President Lee Jae Myung touts as an early achievement.

[North Korea](country:KP) has mined and fenced the line since April 2024, and Seoul says its troops crossed it about 50 times.

[South Korea](country:KR)'s defence ministry vowed to "punish it resolutely" if Pyongyang answers its warning shots with provocation.
