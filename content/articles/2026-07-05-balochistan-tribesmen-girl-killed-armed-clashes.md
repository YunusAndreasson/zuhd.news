Quetta — A young girl died with four tribesmen in Qila Abdullah.

Police called it revenge for an earlier killing of six from the attackers' side; a second Dera Bugti clash over a marriage proposal killed four more.

Officials named no suspects; the injured tribe blocked the Quetta-Chaman highway demanding arrests.
