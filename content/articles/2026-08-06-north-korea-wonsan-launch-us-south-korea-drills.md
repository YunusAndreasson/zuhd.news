Wonsan — North Korea resumed missile testing after 6 weeks.

Short-range missiles fired from the eastern coast typically signal [South Korea](country:KR), not distant [US](country:US) bases, days before their annual joint drills.

South Korea's military has not disclosed the drills' dates, but Pyongyang has answered past exercises with more tests.
