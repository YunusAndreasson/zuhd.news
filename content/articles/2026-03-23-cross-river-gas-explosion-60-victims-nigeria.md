Calabar — A gas plant exploded Saturday, burning 60 people. The Edibe Edibe facility sat inside a residential area, one of many storage sites Nigeria's gas sector places near homes without mandatory safety buffers. Cross River State pledged hospital coverage, but Nigeria's gas industry has no enforcement mechanism to shut down similar unregulated plants in residential areas.
