Engels — 17 shelters sized for nuclear bombers are rising here.

[Ukraine](country:UA)'s drone campaign against parked aircraft forced the reversal; [Russia](country:RU) had kept its Tu-95 and Tu-160 fleet in the open at Engels for decades.

Defense Minister Belousov was shown shelter blueprints last week; no completion date has been given.
