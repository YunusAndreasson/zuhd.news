Paris — NASA has delivered Rosalind Franklin's braking engines.

[Russia](country:RU)'s 2022 withdrawal cancelled the landing platform; [the US](country:US) committed to supply replacement components, with radioisotope heaters still being finalized.

Heaters warm rover electronics in Martian cold; Europe has no flight-ready supply of its own.
