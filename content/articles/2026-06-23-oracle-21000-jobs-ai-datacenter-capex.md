Austin — Oracle warned of further cuts after 21,000 jobs fell.

Oracle's annual report attributes the reduction to artificial intelligence; [US](country:US) headcount fell 9,000, international by 12,000.

The company stated that artificial-intelligence deployment "may continue to result in reductions"; no target headcount was disclosed.
