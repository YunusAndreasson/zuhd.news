New Delhi — Iran asked BRICS chair India to halt US strikes. The US can veto any UN Security Council resolution, so Iran brought its ceasefire request to BRICS, the bloc India currently chairs. New Delhi must now decide whether to use its BRICS chairmanship to challenge US strikes or defer to Washington.
