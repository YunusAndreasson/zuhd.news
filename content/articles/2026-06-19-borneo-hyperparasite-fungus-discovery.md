Kuching — Borneo hides a parasite of a parasite.

A new species described by scientists in [Malaysia](country:MY) preys on Ophiocordyceps — the "zombie fungi" that hijack and kill insects — a hidden population regulator in the rainforest.

Most of Malaysia's fungal biodiversity remains uncatalogued as the habitat shrinks.
