New Zealand's court ruled pine planting cannot substitute for emissions cuts. The High Court found the government's plan to credit pine forests as equivalent to industrial emissions cuts violated climate law — a distinction no court had previously drawn. The centre-right government must now propose revised emissions targets or face a second court order.
