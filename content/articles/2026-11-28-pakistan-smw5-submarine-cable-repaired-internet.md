Pakistan — A cable fault 11 km offshore lasted 10 days before repair.

[Pakistan](country:PK) relies on 7 submarine cables for international internet traffic.

Temporary rerouting through alternative international links limited the disruption, the telecom regulator said.

A fault in the same SEA-ME-WE 5 cable caused widespread connectivity problems in July.

The regulator's statement names no cause for either fault.
