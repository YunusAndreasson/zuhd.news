The Hormuz blockade drove Samsung to green ammonia. Samsung C&T, the conglomerate's trading arm, signed a $3 billion deal with India's Reliance to lock in supply through 2028. Deliveries begin in October 2028, ranking it among Asia's largest green energy supply contracts.
