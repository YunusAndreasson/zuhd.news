Beirut — SMEX says Arab governments are copying European and Australian child social media bans.

Those laws rely on stronger courts and data-protection bodies than the region has.

SMEX argues bans leave algorithmic amplification, children's data collection and weak platform accountability untouched.

It urges shared responsibility among governments, platforms, schools and parents instead.
