Moscow — [Russia](country:RU)'s Security Council added Rostec's cybersecurity chief to its staff Friday.

Andrei Kozlov ran Rostec's cyber division with alleged military intelligence ties; the move places offensive cyber and defense-industrial roles under a single secretariat.

No government has publicly assessed the appointment.
