Beijing — [China](country:CN) ordered a recall of 3 million Teslas.

Flush handles can trap occupants once a crash cuts power to unlock them.

Regulators require mechanical handles on all new electric cars from 2027, a rule that already forced a 400,000-car Xiaomi recall.

Tesla will add warning labels and an over-the-air update that lowers windows after a crash.
