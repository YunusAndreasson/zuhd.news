Seattle — Twitch opted every streamer in by default.

Creators' faces and voices become training data unless each one finds the toggle and turns it off.

The new setting, buried in security settings, feeds streams, clips and chat into models built by owner Amazon.

Opting out stops future use, but content already absorbed cannot be withdrawn from a trained model.
