Paris — Europe approved 2 Scout missions at €35m each.

The European Space Agency counters flagship cost-and-delay: approval to launch in 3 years, against billions and decade-long development times.

Contracts require Member State approval at the next Industrial Policy Committee meeting, expected by year's end.
