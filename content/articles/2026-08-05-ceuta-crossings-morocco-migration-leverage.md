Ceuta — Nearly 60,000 swam across in a week, officials said.

Viral videos misread a court ruling as barring deportation from Ceuta, sparking a rush that left over 80 dead.

[Morocco](country:MA) has leveraged past crossings for concessions from [Spain](country:ES) but has issued no statement now.
