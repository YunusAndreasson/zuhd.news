Taybeh — Settlers beat French mayor Bernard Baudoux, 78, with iron bars.

Baudoux says the attack shows the Israeli government tolerates settler violence.

Witnesses say masked men in off-road vehicles surrounded a diplomatic car and smashed a window while Palestinians tried to pick olives.

[Israel](country:IL)'s military says the visit was not coordinated and no soldiers were present.

Israeli police have opened an investigation, but Baudoux expects no one to be punished.
