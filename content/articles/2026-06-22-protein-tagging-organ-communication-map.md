London — Scientists traced organ signals to individual source cells.

Organs exchange thousands of protein messages — fat to liver, immune to tissue — but the sending cell was unidentifiable; new tags trace each to its origin.

The method opens investigation into how inter-organ signaling drives metabolic and inflammatory disease.
