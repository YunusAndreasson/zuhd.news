New York — All 7 UN chief candidates drew a veto-holder's thumbs-down.

A candidate needs 9 of 15 council votes and no veto from the 5 powers.

Friday's poll used red ballots to mark the 5 powers' votes, and frontrunner Rebeca Grynspan drew 2 red discouragements.

Macky Sall withdrew Saturday, and a red-ballot poll next week tests the remaining six.
