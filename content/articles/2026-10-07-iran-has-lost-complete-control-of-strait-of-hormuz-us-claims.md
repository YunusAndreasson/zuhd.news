Athens — Rubio says Hormuz oil flows are near prewar levels.

Gulf exporters and [Iraq](country:IQ) depend on the strait for oil revenue.

Kpler puts Gulf crude exports at 18.3 million barrels a day on 30 September against 18 million prewar, all routes included.

Revolutionary Guards adviser Mohammad Reza Naqdi says [Iran](country:IR) keeps the strait closed and only smuggling boats use it.

Trump rejected Tehran's latest offer, and [US](country:US) Vice President JD Vance demands concrete cuts to Iran's enrichment.
