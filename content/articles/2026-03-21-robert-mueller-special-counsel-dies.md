Washington — Special Counsel Robert Mueller died Saturday without charging Donald Trump. Mueller documented Russia's interference in the 2016 election but held that Justice Department guidelines barred indicting a sitting president. The rule he followed — that a sitting president cannot be indicted — has never been tested by any court.
