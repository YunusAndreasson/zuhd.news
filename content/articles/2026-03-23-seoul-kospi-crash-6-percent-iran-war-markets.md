Seoul — Korea's benchmark index lost 6% in a single session. Trump's 48-hour ultimatum to Iran over the Strait of Hormuz triggered a $4.9 billion foreign sell-off from a country that imports 85% of its energy. Samsung lost 6.57%, with the won at 1,517 to the dollar — within striking range of its 2009 crisis low of 1,549.
