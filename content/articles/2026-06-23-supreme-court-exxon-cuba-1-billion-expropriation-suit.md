Washington — Supreme Court stripped [Cuba](country:CU) of sovereign immunity.

A 6-3 ruling held Helms-Burton strips Cuba's state firms of that shield, exposing 65-year-old nationalizations to [US](country:US) courts for the first time.

Nearly 6,000 claimants holding $1.9bn in frozen Cuban assets, the Justice Department found, can now file suit.
