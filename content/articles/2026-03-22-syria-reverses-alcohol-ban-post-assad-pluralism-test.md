Damascus — Syria reversed its alcohol ban after Christian communities objected. Hay'at Tahrir al-Sham — a former al-Qaeda affiliate now governing Syria — had banned alcohol citywide but exempted Christian neighborhoods; Christian leaders said the carve-out singled out their areas and invited extremist attacks. Ahmed al-Sharaa's government has not answered a joint letter from Syrian Christian organizations demanding a formal rights commitment.
