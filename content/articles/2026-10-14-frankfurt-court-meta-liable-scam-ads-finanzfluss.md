Frankfurt — A court ruled Meta responsible for ads impersonating Finanzfluss.

Internal Meta estimates put fraudulent advertising at about 10% of its 2024 revenue.

Finanzfluss reported over 1,000 ads from 300 accounts, and some stayed up for 2 months.

Finanzfluss now demands disclosure of how far the ads spread and how much Meta earned, then damages.
