Yerevan — Armenian remittances from [Russia](country:RU) could fall to $1.8 billion.

Gas could also cost 69% more, rising to $300 per 1,000 cubic metres, the report says.

Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan wrote it, citing Armenia's EU bid as grounds to suspend its Eurasian Economic Union membership.

Foreign Minister Ararat Mirzoyan says Armenia knows of no mechanism to expel a member.

Mirzoyan says Yerevan is still studying the report and will respond.
