title: "Marburg Virus Enters Cells Faster"

source: "Nature"

category: "science"
---

Researchers solved Marburg's cell-entry mechanism for the first time. Cryo-EM scans of the glycoprotein in 3 configurations show how Marburg docks to human cells in a distinct, tighter orientation than Ebola. These structures give drug designers their first precision molecular target against a virus that has no approved antiviral or vaccine.
