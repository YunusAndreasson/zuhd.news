The Hormuz blockade is raising electricity bills worldwide. Electricity markets use marginal pricing — the costliest source (gas) sets the rate for all power, even in grids that run mostly on renewables. Japan rebuilt gas dependency after Fukushima and sends 70% of its crude through Hormuz — making it the most exposed economy if the blockade holds.
