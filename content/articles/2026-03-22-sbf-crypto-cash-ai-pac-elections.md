New York — Think Big PAC is attacking New York Assemblymember Alex Bores over an AI safety bill. The crypto-backed group is using Bores's 2022 donations from convicted FTX founder Sam Bankman-Fried as an attack angle in Manhattan's NY-12 Democratic primary. Bores faces 2 other candidates; the seat has never left Democratic hands, making the primary decisive.
