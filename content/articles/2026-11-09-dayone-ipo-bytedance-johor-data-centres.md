Singapore — One customer paid DayOne nearly 70% of its revenue.

DayOne plans a Nasdaq listing raising up to $5bn at roughly $20bn.

Sources say the customer is ByteDance, which committed 838 megawatts across [Malaysia](country:MY), [Thailand](country:TH) and [Finland](country:FI).

Its share of committed 2027 contracts falls below 50%, those sources say.

DayOne lost $77.2 million in the first half of 2026.
