New Delhi — Honda's two-wheelers sold 579,000 units, up 8%.

Passenger-vehicle wholesales jumped too: Maruti up 21%, Tata Motors up 56%.

Wholesale figures count shipments into dealer inventory, not confirmed purchases by buyers.

Automakers count on the festive season starting next month to hold this pace.
