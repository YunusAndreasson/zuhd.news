Lagos — [Nigeria](country:NG) has 1 confirmed Ebola case and 44 monitored contacts.

Lagos, a gateway city with constant travel to and from Abuja, is on high alert.

Health commissioner Akin Abayomi says Lagos is upgrading its isolation centre, tightening airport screening and aiming for lab results within 24 hours.

Health minister Muhammad Pate says 15 states have yet to update their Ebola response plans.
