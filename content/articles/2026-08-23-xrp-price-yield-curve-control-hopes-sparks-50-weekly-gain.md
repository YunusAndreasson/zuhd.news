New York — XRP jumped 51% to $1.50 this week.

Traders are pricing a policy no official has actually announced.

The [US](country:US) Treasury will buy back $4 billion or more in long-dated bonds through Nov. 4, double its prior cap.

No yield curve control policy exists yet, and crypto gains could reverse fast.
