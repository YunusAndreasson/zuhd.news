South Korea's parliament passed a $350 billion US investment commitment. Prime Minister Kim Min-seok flew to Washington the same day to present it to Vice President Vance, as Trump's tariff threats target Korea's chip and car exports. Korea must still replace Gulf oil lost to Hormuz disruption — buying tariff protection while absorbing a second economic shock.
