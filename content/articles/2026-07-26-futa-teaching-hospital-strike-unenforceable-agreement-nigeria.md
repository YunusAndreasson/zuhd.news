Akure — Health workers walked out at midnight Sunday.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s two labour federations struck once a seven-day ultimatum over unpaid payroll enrollment lapsed unanswered — their only leverage is withholding care.

Unions picket the hospital Monday; the health ministry's reconciliation meeting has already been postponed once.
