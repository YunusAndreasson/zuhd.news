Frankfurt — Rioters injured more than 100 of 1,300 deployed police.

[Germany](country:DE)'s ministers promise severe penalties after a youth protest over education and living costs.

Hesse's interior minister counted 17 stores damaged, 5 looted, and gave no figure for injured protesters.

Most of the 54 people arrested were German nationals, though Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt blamed immigrant-community troublemakers without evidence.

Police are now identifying perpetrators, and Dobrindt says he expects severe penalties.
