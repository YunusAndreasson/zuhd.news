A Bangladeshi madrasa head beat a 10-year-old student critical. Bangladesh has no mandatory safeguarding standards for the residential schools where millions of poor families' children board away from home. A court jailed the head and 2 accomplices Tuesday — no residential madrasa inspection has been announced.
