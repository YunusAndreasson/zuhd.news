Manila — The [Philippines](country:PH) posted PISA's largest reading-score gain.

Score gains inside a single testing cycle are rare for lower-income countries.

PISA tests only enrolled 15-year-olds, so a rising national score can also reflect poorer students staying in school longer.

The Philippines still ranks near PISA's bottom overall despite the gain.
