Deraa — One Jordanian strike killed 5 relatives, Syria's defence ministry says.

[Syria](country:SY) closed Nassib, Jordan's only commercial border crossing, and opened an investigation.

[Jordan](country:JO)'s military said it hit groups linked to the 23 October Karak church shooting, which killed 9.

Jordan says Damascus coordinated, but its military named no group and no location.

Syria's investigation has no announced end date, and Nassib remains closed.
