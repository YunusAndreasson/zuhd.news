Baltimore — A new method reconstructs particle paths in turbulence.

Every model of mixing, combustion and pollution dispersion relies on this measurement layer.

Cameras see overlapping blurs, so the method asks which trajectories most likely produced the recorded images.

Researchers tested it only on simulated and lab data, not real atmospheric turbulence.
