Khartoum — Sudan ordered all armed groups absorbed into its national military. The directive came from Yasser al-Atta, Sudan's military council chief, risking the political leverage armed movement leaders gained under the 2020 Juba Peace Agreement. The Rapid Support Forces, a paramilitary rival, still hold western villages after attacking Kosti and Dalang, leaving the order untested.
