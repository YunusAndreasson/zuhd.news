Johannesburg — 129,000 Zimbabweans have left [South Africa](country:ZA) since June.

Migrant leaders say police are not stopping a vigilante campaign but moving with it.

Zimbabwe's state newspaper says a movement set a 30 June deadline for migrants to leave voluntarily.

Home Affairs has not responded to reports that it froze migrants' bank accounts.
