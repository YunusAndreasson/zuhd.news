New York — Bitcoin ETFs bled $1.72 billion last week, the largest single-week redemption in over a year, SoSoValue data show.

Prices sit near $62,000 as in February, but buyers who appeared then have not returned — outflows have accelerated 4 straight weeks.

No institutional floor has formed below $60,000.
