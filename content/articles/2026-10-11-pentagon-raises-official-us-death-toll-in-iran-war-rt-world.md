Washington — Pentagon now counts 21 [US](country:US) war dead, Russian state broadcaster RT reported.

The figure rose from 18 after a newspaper reported the database understated deaths, RT said.

Friday's update added a National Guard major who died in Kuwait on 6 March and a Navy suicide in September.

"There is no excuse for a mistake of this magnitude," Democratic Representatives Adam Smith and Chrissy Houlahan said.

Trump says he must decide whether to resume major attacks on [Iran](country:IR), with elections in November.
