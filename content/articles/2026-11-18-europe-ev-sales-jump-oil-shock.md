Brussels — Electric cars made up about a third of European sales in October.

Registrations rose more than 60% from a year earlier across 18 key markets.

Subsidies, high oil prices and cheaper models drew buyers, data from E-Mobility Europe, New Automotive and Fier Automotive show.

Brussels is discussing softening the EU's 2035 car emissions targets.
