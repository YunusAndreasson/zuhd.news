The African Union's 39th summit condemned the "extermination" of Palestinians and demanded Israel lift its blockade on humanitarian goods. AU leaders also called for an end to conflicts across Sudan, the Sahel, and eastern Congo. The summit adds to growing Global South pressure for UN Security Council reform, though any change requires approval from current permanent members.
