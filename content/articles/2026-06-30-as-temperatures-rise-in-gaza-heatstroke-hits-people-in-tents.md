Gaza — Gaza children are collapsing in 32°C summer heat.

[Israel](country:IL) has blocked shelter materials; nearly 1 million displaced Gazans live in tents that aid agencies call heat traps.

Hospitals are limited to basic emergency aid as heatstroke cases rise, doctors said.
