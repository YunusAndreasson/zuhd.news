Moscow — Deputy PM Novak admitted fuel lines are real.

[Ukraine](country:UA)'s drone strikes have knocked refineries offline, so [Russia](country:RU) banned gasoline and diesel exports through July 31 to stabilize supply.

Novak has not said whether the ban extends if strikes continue past July 31.
