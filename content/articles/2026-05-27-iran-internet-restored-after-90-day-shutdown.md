Tehran — Day 88: [Iran](country:IR) partially restored international internet.

The shutdown — imposed when [the US](country:US) and [Israel](country:IL) struck on February 28 — forced banking and communications onto domestic platforms.

Mobile internet remains cut; the judiciary suspended the presidential body that ordered restoration, leaving access uncertain.
