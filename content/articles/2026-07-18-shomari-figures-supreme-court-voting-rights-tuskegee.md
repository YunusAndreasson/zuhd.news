Tuskegee — Shomari Figures became this city's first Black member of Congress in modern history.

The [US](country:US) Supreme Court gutted a Voting Rights Act protection in April, letting Republican-led Southern states redraw majority-Black districts like his.

If Figures loses his seat under a new map, Tuskegee loses the federal funding pipeline he built.
