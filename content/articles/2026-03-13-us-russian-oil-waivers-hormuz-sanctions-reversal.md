Washington waived Russia oil sanctions to cool Hormuz-driven markets. Oil prices surged after the US struck Iran — Washington is now reversing the Russia sanctions it imposed after Russia invaded Ukraine. An earlier 30-day waiver for India has been replaced with a global authorization that carries no stated expiry date.
