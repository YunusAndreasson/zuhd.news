San Francisco — Mozilla wants AI built as layered public infrastructure, like the early internet.

Its CTO argues that model access, not just ideas, decides who actually controls AI.

Mozilla spent decades pushing open standards for the internet; it now applies that argument to artificial intelligence.

Whether open-weight models spread depends on who controls the computing power to run them.
