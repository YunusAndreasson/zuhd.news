Iran is preparing for its supreme leader's death. Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei ordered all senior military and political officials to designate replacements after last year's conflict with Israel depleted command ranks. Tehran's succession planning treats a military strike as plausible, even as nuclear talks with the US resume in March.
