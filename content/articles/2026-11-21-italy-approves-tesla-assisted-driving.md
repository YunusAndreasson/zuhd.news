Rome — Italy approved Tesla's driver assistance before any EU vote.

[Germany](country:DE) and [France](country:FR) have yet to decide, and France is testing the system.

Dutch regulators gave provisional approval in April, and Denmark, Belgium and Czechia followed, each acting nationally.

The EU's vote on the system is slated for later this year.
