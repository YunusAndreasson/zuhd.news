New Delhi — 800,000 bank workers struck India Friday, a union claims.

Public-bank branches shut, stranding rural and elderly account holders who lack banking apps.

Unions want a five-day week promised in a 2024 wage deal and oppose performance-linked pay, still unapproved by [India](country:IN)'s government.

A three-day strike follows September 28-30, with an indefinite strike threatened from October 26.
