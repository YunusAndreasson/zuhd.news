Moscow — [Russia](country:RU)'s IT revenue hit $39bn in Q1, up 8%.

Sanctions expelled Western vendors after 2022; domestic software grew 21% year-on-year, said the state Digital Economy organization — figures from ministry-accredited companies.

Russian IT figures are self-reported through state channels; independent audit is unavailable under sanctions.
