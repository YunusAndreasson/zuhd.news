Mumbai — Tata Trusts petitioned a court against Chandrasekaran's reappointment as chair.

The trusts' 66% stake controls Jaguar Land Rover, Tetley and [India](country:IN)'s largest information technology firm.

Major decisions need trust nominee directors' votes, but the board has only two, split between Noel Tata and Venu Srinivasan.

Srinivasan and trustees Vijay Singh and Jimmy Tata back Chandrasekaran, leaving Noel Tata isolated.

The Bombay High Court hears the petition Friday, before a Monday board meeting on a stock market listing.
