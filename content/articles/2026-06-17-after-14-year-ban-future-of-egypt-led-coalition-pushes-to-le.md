Cairo — [Egypt](country:EG)'s state Future of Egypt Authority and Bayer are coordinating to legalise GMO seed imports, ending a 14-year ban.

The deal would grant privileges modelled on Monsanto's global framework, pending parliamentary approval expected within months.

Previous attempts failed when public opposition mobilised; no organised opposition has emerged yet.
