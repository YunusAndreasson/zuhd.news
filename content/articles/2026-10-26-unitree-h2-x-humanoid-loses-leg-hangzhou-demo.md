Hangzhou — Unitree's day-old humanoid lost a leg landing a side flip.

Unitree markets the H2-X for security patrols and emergency rescue work.

Landing a flip can send several thousand newtons through the leg joints, and a lag of milliseconds in balance control can overload them.

Unitree has yet to respond publicly, and no cause of the failure has been confirmed.
