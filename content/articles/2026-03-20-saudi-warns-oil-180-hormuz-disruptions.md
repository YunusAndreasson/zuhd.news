Oil hit $119 a barrel as the Strait of Hormuz remains disrupted. Saudi officials warned prices could reach $180 if disruptions persist through April — above the 2008 record of $147 — a level that would force rationing in fuel-importing nations from Pakistan to Bangladesh. Analysts say Iran may target Saudi Arabia's Yanbu port, the kingdom's only oil export route bypassing Hormuz.
