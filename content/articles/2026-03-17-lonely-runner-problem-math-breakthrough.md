An Oxford undergraduate advanced a 60-year math problem. The conjecture predicts each runner at a unique speed will eventually stand alone — a question connecting number theory, geometry, and graph theory. The proof covers 10 runners now, up from 7 since 2007, with 11 still unsolved.
