Tokyo — Mt. Gox moved 10,422 bitcoin to a new wallet.

Trustee Nobuaki Kobayashi still holds 34,504 bitcoin ($2.43bn at current prices) for 19,500 creditors who bought before the 2014 collapse.

All repayments must complete by October 31; a Tokyo court extended the deadline twice after processing delays.
