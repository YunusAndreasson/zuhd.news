A developing heart tears its scaffold to build muscle. Researchers found that cracks in the heart's protein scaffold seed its trabeculae — muscular strands essential for pumping — not the genes biologists assumed. A review in the journal Development in February 2026 found fracturing shapes organs across species — a mechanism researchers say could transform tissue engineering.
