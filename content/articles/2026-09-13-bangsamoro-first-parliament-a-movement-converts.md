Cotabato City — 2.4 million Bangsamoro voters cast ballots Monday.

It tests whether a former armed movement can govern the [Philippines](country:PH)' south by ballot.

The Bangsamoro parliament splits seats among party lists, districts and reserved indigenous seats to prevent clan dominance.

A gun ban covering the region runs through September 29 to secure the vote.
