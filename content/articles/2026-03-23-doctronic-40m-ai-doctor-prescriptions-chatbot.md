United States — $39 buys an AI prescription renewal in Utah. Doctronic, which launched in January 2025 after Utah became the first US state to authorize AI prescription renewals, raised $40M this week for a total of $65M in funding. The company is now preparing for its first FDA meeting to license the AI doctor to health systems — a decision that would set the legal standard for AI prescriptions across all 50 states.
