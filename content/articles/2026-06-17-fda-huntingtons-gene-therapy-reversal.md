Washington — UniQure's Huntington's disease gene therapy cleared after the Food and Drug Administration regulator who blocked it was ousted.

Vinay Prasad, the FDA official, had demanded sham brain surgeries as placebo controls — a 10-hour procedure the company called unethical.

UniQure expects to file for accelerated approval in Q3 2026.
