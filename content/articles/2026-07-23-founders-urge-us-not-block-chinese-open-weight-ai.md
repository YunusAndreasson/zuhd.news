Washington — US builders now depend on Chinese AI weights.

Startup founders are lobbying the administration against restricting open-weight models from [China](country:CN), arguing a block would cut off domestic developers, not Beijing's labs.

Once released, open weights can't be un-shared — the debate turns on whether Washington restricts access anyway.
