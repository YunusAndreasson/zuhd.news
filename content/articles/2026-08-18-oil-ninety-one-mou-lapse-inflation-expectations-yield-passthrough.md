London — Trump signaled he won't renew the [Iran](country:IR) deal.

That pushed oil above $91, raising costs for people who own no assets.

Reviving inflation fears, the signal pushed bond yields higher across global markets Tuesday.

Higher yields now tighten dollar debt costs for import-dependent economies like [Pakistan](country:PK) and [Egypt](country:EG).
