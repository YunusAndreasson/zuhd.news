Tehran — Iran's intelligence ministry runs Handala, the US Justice Department confirmed Friday. After the FBI seized its websites, Handala attacked Stryker, a US medical device company, in its first destructive strike on American targets after years of targeting Israel. Stryker has disclosed nothing about what data was accessed — US Cyber Command must now decide whether to respond to the first Iranian destructive attack on American soil.
