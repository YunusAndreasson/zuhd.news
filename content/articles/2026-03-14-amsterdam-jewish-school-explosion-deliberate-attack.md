A bomb exploded at a Jewish school in Amsterdam overnight. Mayor Femke Halsema called it a deliberate attack, the latest targeting a European Jewish institution since US-Israeli strikes on Iran began. Police identified a suspect placing the device on CCTV but made no arrests.
