The US could slide into 1970s-style stagflation. Sam Ori, an energy economist at the University of Chicago, says oil above 4-5% of GDP has always triggered recession; the indefinite Hormuz closure is approaching that threshold faster than any prior shock. US President Donald Trump told reporters the US profits from high oil prices even as the strait stays closed.
