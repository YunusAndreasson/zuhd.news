Beijing — [China](country:CN)'s solar capacity hit 1,286 gigawatts by July.

Yet coal still generates more electricity than solar delivers today.

Capacity measures noon-clear-sky output, not what plants actually deliver, so shares diverge for years.

A multi-trillion-yuan grid program must add transmission and storage to convert capacity into power.
