Robertson — 300 people now shelter in a community hall.

Landlords evicted them after an anti-immigrant campaign went door-to-door threatening residents who housed foreigners across [South Africa](country:ZA)'s Western Cape.

Officials have not said when buses will return for those still waiting to be sent to [Malawi](country:MW).
