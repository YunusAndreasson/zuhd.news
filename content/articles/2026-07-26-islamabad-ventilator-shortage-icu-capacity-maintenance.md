Islamabad — A journalist died waiting for a ventilator.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s Pims hospital runs 90 ventilators for a population that grew tenfold since it opened; almost all stay occupied around the clock.

A 40-ventilator block is under construction, but Pims' director cites a staff shortage to run it.
