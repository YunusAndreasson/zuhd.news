Paris prosecutors assembled a special magistrate team to investigate French nationals in the Epstein files. The team will work with France's national financial crimes unit and police to examine newly released US Department of Justice documents on convicted sex offender Jeffrey Epstein. Prosecutors are reopening the case of Jean-Luc Brunel, a French modelling agency executive with ties to Epstein who died in prison in 2022. The Foreign Ministry asked prosecutors to examine 3 other French nationals named in the documents, including a senior diplomat. France is the first European government to form a dedicated team for cross-border accountability in a case where victims span multiple continents.
