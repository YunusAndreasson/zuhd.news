Strategy needs $22bn to own 5% of all bitcoin. The company funds purchases through convertible notes and equity issuance — using traditional corporate finance to accumulate an asset designed to resist institutional concentration. Strategy must buy 6,158 bitcoin per week through year-end, a pace executive chairman Michael Saylor claims is achievable.
