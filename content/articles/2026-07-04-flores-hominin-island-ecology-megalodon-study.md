Washington — Homo floresiensis scavenged Komodo dragon kills rather than hunting.

Bone marks at Flores island's Liang Bua cave show the small-statured hominins ate raw meat; charring there came from modern humans who arrived later.

The finding challenges assumptions the hobbits could make fire or hunt big game.
