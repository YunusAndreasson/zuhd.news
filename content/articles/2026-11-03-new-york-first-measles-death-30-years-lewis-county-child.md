Lowville — Measles killed a child in Lewis County, the state's first death in 30 years.

New York counted 178 cases by 24 October, 170 of them outside New York City.

The [US](country:US) stopped domestic spread in 2000 by holding coverage above 95%, and it has since slipped below.

The CDC has confirmed 3 measles deaths nationwide this year, while Pennsylvania alone reports 5.

The US loses its measles elimination status in November if one strain keeps spreading past 12 months.
