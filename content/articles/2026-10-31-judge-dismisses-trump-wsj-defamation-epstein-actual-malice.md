Miami — Trump cannot refile his Wall Street Journal suit.

A public figure's denial does not stop a newspaper from publishing, the judge ruled.

Judge Darrin Gayles found Trump never pleaded actual malice, meaning the Journal knew its birthday-letter report was false.

A spokesman for Trump's legal team said the president will keep pressing the case.

Trump's separate defamation suits against the BBC and The New York Times remain pending.
