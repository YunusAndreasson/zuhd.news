Aden — Houthis say [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s airspace "will become a theater of military operations."

[Yemen](country:YE)'s deputy defence minister says the army will retake every province, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

Saudi air cover backs a push at Bab el-Mandeb, the strait the Houthis seized last month, but fighting continues there.

Five government military sources say the Houthis have encircled Taiz, a city of over half a million.

Polymarket traders give 17% odds that Bab el-Mandeb is effectively closed by 31 December.
