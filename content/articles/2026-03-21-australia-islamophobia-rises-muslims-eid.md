Islamophobia in Australia has risen 636% since October 2023. The Islamophobia Register records 18 cases per week — up from 2.5 — a figure that surged 201% after December's Bondi massacre and anti-Muslim political rhetoric. Muslim leaders have called on the Australian government for permanent security funding; without it, mosques pay for their own protection as Eid al-Fitr approaches.
