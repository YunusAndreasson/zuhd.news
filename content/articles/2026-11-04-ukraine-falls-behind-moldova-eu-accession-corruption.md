Brussels — [Moldova](country:MD) can finish EU talks a year before [Ukraine](country:UA).

Kyiv and Chisinau applied together in 2022, but the European Commission now splits their timetables.

The Commission blames Kyiv's sputtering anti-corruption effort and expects it to close 7 chapters in 2027, against Moldova's 25.

Zelenskyy warned last month that decoupling the two would be "morally wrong."

EU enlargement commissioner Marta Kos says Montenegro could sign its accession treaty in June 2027.
