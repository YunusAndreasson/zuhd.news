Seoul — One North Korean missile flew over 900 km, Seoul says.

[North Korea](country:KP) has tested missiles more than 10 times this year.

[South Korea](country:KR)'s military called the launches from near Pyongyang a show of force after its first hypersonic test on Thursday.

"They definitely need to raise the value of their status before meeting Trump," said Song Seong-jong, a Daejeon University professor.

Seoul demanded an immediate stop, saying the launches violate UN Security Council resolutions.
