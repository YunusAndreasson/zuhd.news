Buenos Aires — A court in [Argentina](country:AR) reopened a 1956 massacre.

Judge Alicia Vence found the 1955 dictatorship ordered the killings; crime-against-humanity status carries no statute of limitations.

The court ordered reparations — honor restored, a memorial site, school curricula — though all named officials are dead.
