Pasadena — NASA has no Mars lander or rover planned.

Its stopgap, three helicopters, can't drill, sample or cache what they find.

Landing is the expensive risk; helicopters skip it, spinning rotors past Mach 1 in Mars' thin air.

SkyFall could launch as soon as late 2028, riding a nuclear-propulsion test mission.
