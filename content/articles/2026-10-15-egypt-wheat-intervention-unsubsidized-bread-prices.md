Cairo — [Egypt](country:EG) offers mills wheat from LE16,500 a tonne.

Millers and bakers want higher unsubsidised bread prices, but the government held its price caps.

Open-market wheat costs LE16,800-17,200, and exchange registration and trading fees narrow the gap, industry sources say.

Industry sources say the same release in 2023 only fuelled profiteering on a parallel secondary market.

Sales are capped at 500 tonnes per mill, but the ministry has not said the total.
