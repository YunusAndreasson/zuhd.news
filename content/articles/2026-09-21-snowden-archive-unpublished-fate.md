Washington — No one has published a Snowden document since 29 May 2019.

A few newsrooms decided what the public saw from the roughly 50,000-document archive.

The Guardian stopped in February 2014, Der Spiegel in January 2015, and The Intercept closed its archive in March 2019.

Laura Poitras gave copies to 3 holders, and it is unclear whether any can read them.
