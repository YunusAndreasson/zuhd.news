Osogbo — Minister Oyetola told Adeleke to prepare for defeat.

Adeleke says the remark was an open threat to rig Saturday's vote.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s election body and police report to the federal government, so an opposition state contests elections its rivals referee.

Adeleke's spokesperson made the claim, and neither Oyetola nor the election body has responded before Saturday's vote.
