San Francisco — XCancel, the last major account-free X mirror, went dark.

Every news citation, court filing or paper linking through it now points to a dead page.

Nitter tools ran on X's guest API; each tightening cut instances until only scarce authenticated ones survived.

No replacement mirror has been announced for readers who cannot open an X account.
