An Iran-backed militia offered Washington 5 days of quiet. Kataib Hezbollah demanded Israel halt strikes on Beirut's southern suburbs — the first de-escalation signal from any party since the war began. The group has not said what happens if Israel rejects the conditions when the 5 days expire.
