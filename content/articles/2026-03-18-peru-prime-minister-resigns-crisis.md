Peru has had 8 presidents in 10 years. The prime minister resigned before her congressional confirmation vote, the latest episode in a standoff between Congress and the executive that has handed power to mining interests. Presidential elections are set for April 12 with more than 2 dozen candidates and a likely June runoff.
