New Delhi — [India](country:IN) raised commercial gas cylinder prices Rs 42 June 1.

State oil companies cited Hormuz supply risk; a 19 kg cylinder now costs Rs 3,114 in Delhi, state oil marketing company figures show.

Domestic household cylinders remain unchanged; the next pricing review is tied to Hormuz flow data.
