Brussels — Requirements, not coding speed, are software's real bottleneck.

Queueing theory shows speeding a non-bottleneck leaves cycle time unchanged; in software, the constraint is requirements and review, not code.

Most enterprise AI deployments target code generation, leaving requirements-cycle time unmeasured.
