Junta airstrikes killed 17 at a Rakhine village market. Myanmar's military routinely targets civilian gathering places held by the Arakan Army, an ethnic armed group, in a state the junta has cut off from humanitarian aid. 3 of the dead were children; 14 wounded in Ponnagyun have no medical access.
