London — Pre-cellular DNA systems hit an evolutionary ceiling without maintained sequence diversity, a study found.

Diversity prevents convergence on a single optimum, enabling open-ended complexity, researchers said.

Findings hold across prebiotic scenarios, from surface-attached polymers to dual selection environments.
