Only Spain refuses US base access for Iran strikes. US President Donald Trump threatened to cut all trade with Spain, the last NATO ally to refuse opening its bases to US bombers targeting Iran after the UK reversed under pressure. US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent was tasked with implementing the embargo against a country whose trade is governed by Brussels, not Washington.
