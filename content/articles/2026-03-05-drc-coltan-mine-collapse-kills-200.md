200 died at Congo's Rubaya coltan mine. Rubaya supplies 15% of global coltan — tantalum for smartphones — and 70 of the dead were children working unregulated shafts. M23, which controls Rubaya, denied the toll and blocked investigators — the mine's second collapse killing 200 this year.
