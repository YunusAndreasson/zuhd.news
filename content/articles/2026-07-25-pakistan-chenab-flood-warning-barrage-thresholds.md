Chiniot — The Chenab is at medium flood level again.

The Chenab rises in Indian-administered Kashmir, so [Pakistan](country:PK)'s flood warnings depend on upstream data shared under the Indus Waters Treaty.

NDMA expects Chenab levels to keep rising, with urban flooding forecast for Lahore and Faisalabad within 24 hours.
