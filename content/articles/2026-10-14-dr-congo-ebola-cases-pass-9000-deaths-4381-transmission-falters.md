Kinshasa — Ebola has killed 4,381 of 9,066 confirmed cases, the Health Ministry says.

Bundibugyo has no approved vaccine or treatment, and [Kenya](country:KE) found its first imported case last week.

The virus has reached 7 provinces of [DR Congo](country:CD), where community mistrust and M23 fighting block responders.

A politician who defended the response on radio was beaten to death in September, his party says.

A vaccine trial against the Bundibugyo strain starts in the coming weeks, the World Health Organization's Chikwe Ihekweazu said.
