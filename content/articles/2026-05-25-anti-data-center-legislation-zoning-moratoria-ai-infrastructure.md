Washington — Microsoft cancelled a 244-acre [US](country:US) data centre site.

Zoning moratoria have halted projects in Michigan, South Carolina, and Wisconsin; Georgia, Oklahoma, and Virginia are considering statewide pauses.

Oklahoma's proposed bill would pause all data centre permits through 2029 pending water and utility-rate research.
