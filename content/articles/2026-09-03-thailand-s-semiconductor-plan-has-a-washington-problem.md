Bangkok — A handful of firms control chip fabrication equipment.

That gives Washington veto power over [Thailand](country:TH)'s semiconductor ambitions.

Any national chip plan becomes, in practice, an application for a licence from [US](country:US) export-control regulators.

[Malaysia](country:MY) and [Vietnam](country:VN) are running the same playbook, facing the same Washington gate.
