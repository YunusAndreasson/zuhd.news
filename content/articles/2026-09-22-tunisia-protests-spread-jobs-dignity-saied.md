Kasserine — Unemployed graduates marched with their children in Kasserine this week.

Protests from Sidi Bouzid have reached northern [Tunisia](country:TN), testing Saied's closed political system.

Demonstrators in Sidi Bouzid, Kasserine and Gabes demand jobs and enforcement of a 2025 law guaranteeing graduates public posts.

Republican Party spokesman Wissem Sghayer called it "at its core, a political movement."

The Tunisian General Labour Union is weighing a general strike as Saied promises regional development.
