London — A ceramic coating trick boosts capacitor storage.

Multilayer ceramic capacitors power every phone, EV and grid inverter; the coating suppresses the electrical breakdown that caps their energy density.

The peer-reviewed findings, published in Nature Communications, still await final editing before formal release.
