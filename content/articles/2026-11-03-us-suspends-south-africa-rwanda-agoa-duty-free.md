Pretoria — [South Africa](country:ZA) exported $1.7 billion duty-free to the [US](country:US) in 2025.

[Rwanda](country:RW) joins it: both lose duty-free access on 1 January 2027.

Trade Representative Jamieson Greer cited human rights, corruption and threats to US interests but gave no examples of the barriers.

Trade Minister Parks Tau says the programme covers only 1% of South Africa's global exports.

Pretoria has 60 days to address Washington's concerns before the suspension takes effect.
