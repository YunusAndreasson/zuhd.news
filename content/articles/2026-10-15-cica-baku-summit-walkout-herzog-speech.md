Baku — 11 delegations walked out on Herzog, state broadcaster TRT World reported.

[Azerbaijan](country:AZ), the summit host, gave [Israel](country:IL)'s president the floor.

Hezbollah's Al-Manar TV counted 13 of 28 delegations, among them [Iran](country:IR), [Saudi Arabia](country:SA), [Pakistan](country:PK) and [Egypt](country:EG).

The one-day summit is due to adopt a Baku declaration on Thursday.
