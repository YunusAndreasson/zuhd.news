Atlanta — 260 analysts, 708 record checks, one settled election.

The Federal Bureau of Investigation is surging staff into Fulton County's 2020 vote, already audited and cleared by courts and officials of both parties, per an internal memo.

Analysts must finish every records check by July 17, the memo instructs.
