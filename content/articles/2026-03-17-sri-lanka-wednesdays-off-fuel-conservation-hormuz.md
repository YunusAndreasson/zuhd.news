Sri Lanka canceled Wednesdays to conserve oil. Sri Lanka imports nearly all its oil through the Strait of Hormuz, the route that carried 90% of Gulf crude to Asia last year. The ration caps private cars at 15 litres per fill, a level drivers say is too low to reach work.
