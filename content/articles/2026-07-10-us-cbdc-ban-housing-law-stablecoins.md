Washington — A 4-year ban on a Fed digital dollar takes effect at midnight.

The clause rides inside a housing bill [Trump](country:US) refuses to sign but cannot block; the Constitution makes it law after 10 days regardless.

Private stablecoin issuers like Circle and Tether inherit the digital-dollar space the Fed is now barred from entering.
