Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s wheat flour costs 44.2% more than a year ago.

The Pakistan Bureau of Statistics put weekly inflation at 10.41% for the year to 19 November.

Petrol fell from Rs396.06 to Rs322 a litre in 21 days, yet flour rose 4.42% in a week.

Planning Minister Ahsan Iqbal ordered provinces to issue revised fares in line with falling fuel prices.
