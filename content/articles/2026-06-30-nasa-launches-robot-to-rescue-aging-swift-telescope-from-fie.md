Cape Canaveral — Swift faces fiery reentry; a rescue robot launched.

The National Aeronautics and Space Administration sent a robotic servicer; it must autonomously rendezvous with the 20-year-old gamma-ray telescope and dock.

A successful mission would prove satellites can be serviced in orbit rather than built new.
