IAEA chief Grossi confirmed Yongbyon's reactor, enrichment, and reprocessing all accelerating simultaneously — with estimated output now at a few dozen warheads. Washington, absorbed in its Iran war, has no open diplomatic channel to Pyongyang and no stated response.
