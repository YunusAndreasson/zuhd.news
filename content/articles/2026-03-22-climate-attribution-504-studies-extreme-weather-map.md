London — 600 studies found climate change worsened nearly 3 in 4 extreme weather events. Attribution science calculates how much human activity shifts the odds of specific disasters; Carbon Brief's updated map now covers 800 events worldwide. Courts in the Netherlands and Germany have used attribution findings to force emissions cuts, and the methodology is now standard evidence in climate litigation worldwide.
