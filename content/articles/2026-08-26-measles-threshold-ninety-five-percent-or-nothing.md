Lancaster — Pennsylvania's measles outbreak has killed two people.

Hundreds more are infected, with further deaths likely as cases are confirmed.

Measles needs roughly 95% vaccine coverage to stop spreading; below that threshold outbreaks ignite fast.

Governor Josh Shapiro blames Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr.'s rhetoric for the outbreak.
