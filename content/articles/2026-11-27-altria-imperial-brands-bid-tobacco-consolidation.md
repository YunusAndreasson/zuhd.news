London — Imperial Brands jumped 13.3% when Altria said it may bid.

A cash deal would put the Marlboro maker back into international cigarettes after two decades.

Marlboro shipments fell about 6% last quarter, and Imperial trades at 8 times forward earnings against Altria's 13.

Imperial said neither Altria nor any third party has approached it.

Altria must announce a firm offer or walk away by 5pm London time on 24 December.
