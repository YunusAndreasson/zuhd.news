Philadelphia — Mathematicians debate what AI proofs mean.

Machine-generated proofs that are verifiable but unreadable challenge what counts as mathematical knowledge.

AI systems have spotted cross-field connections and solved some decades-old problems, mathematicians say.

Mathematicians have not settled what human proof is for if machines can already do it.
