In March 2023, $3.3 billion of Circle's reserves froze in Silicon Valley Bank's collapse, breaking the stablecoin's dollar peg to 87 cents.

A limited trust charter carries no Federal Reserve backstop, and federal stablecoin legislation remains pending.
