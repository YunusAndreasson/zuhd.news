Beirut — [Lebanon](country:LB)'s president offered [Israel](country:IL) a non-aggression pact Monday.

Aoun accused [Iran](country:IR) of using Lebanon as a bargaining chip in Tehran's talks with Washington; Hezbollah opposes the US-mediated deal.

Aoun won't meet Netanyahu before a deal; Israel has struck Lebanon 3,500 times since the April ceasefire, Lebanese officials say.
