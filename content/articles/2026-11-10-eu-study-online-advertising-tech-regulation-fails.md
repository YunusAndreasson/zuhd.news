European Union — Advertisers spent €63 billion online in the EU last year.

A Commission-ordered study finds only intermediaries gain, while consumers, advertisers and publishers lose.

Google and Meta dominate, the 570-page study says, by running closed ecosystems and open-web ad infrastructure.

The Commission unveils its Digital Fairness Act on 11 November, but a leaked draft adopts none of the study's proposals.
