Washington — Muslims overtake Christians as the largest religion by 2035, Pew projects.

Pew counts 2.57 billion Muslims against 2.52 billion Christians that year.

Muslim women average 3.1 children against 2.5 for Christian women, and Islam loses far fewer members to switching faiths.

[India](country:IN) stays 76% Hindu in 2070, demographers note, rejecting claims that Muslims will outnumber Hindus.

Pew's projection assumes current trends hold and excludes wars, epidemics and economic shocks.
