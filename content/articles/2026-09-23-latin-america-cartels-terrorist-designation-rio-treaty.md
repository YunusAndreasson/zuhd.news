New York — 14 Latin American nations declared cartels foreign terrorist organizations.

The designation opens a legal path to U.S. military strikes on cartel territory.

The declaration invoked the 1947 Rio Treaty, under which [Colombia](country:CO) and four other nations already host U.S. joint operations.

[Mexico](country:MX), Brazil, Venezuela and Cuba were not invited to join the 19-nation coalition.

Colombia is set to borrow three U.S. Reaper drones and receive $1 billion in new security aid.
