Copenhagen — Hundreds of little red dots, unexplained.

Webb's deep-field images keep turning up compact reddish objects; astrophysicist Charlotte Mason says no single theory yet explains their origin.

Mason, of the Cosmic Dawn Center, says more Webb observations are needed to settle which explanation holds.
