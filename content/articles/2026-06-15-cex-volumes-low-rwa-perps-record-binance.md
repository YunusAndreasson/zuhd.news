New York — Crypto spot hit a 32-month low in May.

Real-world-asset perpetual futures — leveraged contracts on tokenized bonds and commodities — reached a $211B record even as overall exchange volumes fell to a $4.41T low.

Binance holds 55.7% of that real-world-asset perpetual futures market; no regulator has announced action on the concentration.
