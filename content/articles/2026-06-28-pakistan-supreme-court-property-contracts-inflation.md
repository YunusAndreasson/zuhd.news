Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Supreme Court ruled late buyers forfeit property rights in inflation-era disputes.

A missed payment deadline voids the buyer's right to force a sale, the Court held, upholding forfeiture of earnest money in a Sialkot case.

Justice Aurangzeb ordered lower courts to weigh economic realities, binding future property contracts.
