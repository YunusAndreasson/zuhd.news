Pacific nations facing the worst damage are the ones Prime Minister Anthony Albanese says he speaks for.

Energy minister Chris Bowen leads negotiations for a country that remains a major coal and gas exporter.

"This is crunch time, and Australia can't keep dodging this issue," said Adam Bandt, chief executive of the Australian Conservation Foundation.

Talks close on 20 November, with a proposed road map away from fossil fuels still unagreed.
