Bitcoin now trades as a duration asset: [US](country:US) 30-year Treasury yields above 5% raise the cost of holding non-yielding positions, HashKey Group said.

The firm said Iran de-escalation could ease yields and restore inflows; without it, Bitcoin holds near $75,000.
