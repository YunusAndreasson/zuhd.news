Frankfurt — [Germany](country:DE) needs 160,000 more renewable energy workers.

Private brokers, not governments, now control the loans and visas of arriving technicians.

A 2023 [India](country:IN)-Germany deal aimed to place 20 technicians, but zero had been verified by July 2026.

The same technicians are now being recruited simultaneously for Gulf solar projects.
