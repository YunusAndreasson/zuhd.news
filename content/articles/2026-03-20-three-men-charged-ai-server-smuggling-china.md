3 men smuggled $2.5B in Nvidia servers to China. Prosecutors say the men worked for Supermicro, a US server maker, and funneled orders through a Southeast Asian front company using forged documents to bypass chip export controls. Supermicro Senior VP Yih-Shyan Liaw faces trial while a Taiwan-based co-defendant named in the indictment remains a fugitive.
