Berlin — Hackers published city login credentials for a second time.

Residents in government databases they cannot opt out of face a second exposure of the same systems.

A repeat leak means the first breach's access path or credentials were never fully closed off.

[Germany](country:DE)'s cyber agency separately warned about the Rhysida ransomware group as authorities investigate the new leak.
