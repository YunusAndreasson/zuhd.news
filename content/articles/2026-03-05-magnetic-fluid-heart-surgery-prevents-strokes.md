title: "Magnetic Gel Plugs Stroke Source"

category: "science"

A magnetic fluid hardens inside the heart to block strokes. 90% of strokes from atrial fibrillation originate in the left atrial appendage; current procedures require invasive surgery or devices that can dislodge. Researchers showed no harm in animal trials but say years of testing remain before human use.
