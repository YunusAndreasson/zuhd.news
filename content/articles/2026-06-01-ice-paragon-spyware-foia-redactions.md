Washington — ICE's phone-hacking contract came back blacked out.

[The US](country:US) immigration agency paid $2 million for spyware that reads Signal and WhatsApp remotely, then redacted 77 pages of the contract it released.

596 further pages remain; Paragon advertises that buyers can use Graphite "without being caught doing so."
