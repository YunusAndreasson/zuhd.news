Benghazi — A Russian corvette exploded at dawn Sunday.

It is the second Russian ship hit in two days, after a Baltic strike on Saturday.

The ship had stopped for fuel under an agreement with [Libya](country:LY)'s eastern government, which suspects a drone attack.

Nobody has claimed the strike, and Moscow has not commented.

The eastern government and [Russia](country:RU) have opened a joint investigation into who attacked.
