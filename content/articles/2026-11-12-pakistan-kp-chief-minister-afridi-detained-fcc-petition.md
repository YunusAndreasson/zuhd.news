Islamabad — Chief Minister Sohail Afridi unseen for 32 days.

A federal minister confirmed his custody a day after the government imposed emergency rule on 11 October.

Lawmaker Shandana Gulzar asked the Federal Constitutional Court to make officials disclose where he is held, by whom, and why.

On 5 November the court restrained the Peshawar High Court from hearing cases on activists detained under the public order ordinance.

The Peshawar High Court already dropped about 105 related petitions from its 10 November cause lists.
