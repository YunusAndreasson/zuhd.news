Frankfurt — UniCredit will put its own chief executive atop Commerzbank.

Andrea Orcel would succeed Jens Weidmann as chair after a two-year fight with Berlin.

UniCredit holds close to 50% and asked Commerzbank to call a shareholder meeting that could happen as early as January.

Berlin waived two board nominations last week for UniCredit's pledge to keep Commerzbank headquartered and listed in Frankfurt.

Orcel, who wants thousands of German jobs cut, could take voting control by 7 December.
