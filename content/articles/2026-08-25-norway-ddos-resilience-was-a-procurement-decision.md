Oslo — A cyberattack knocked Norway's public services offline.

Citizens lost access to government systems with no paper alternative.

The distributed denial-of-service attack exhausts capacity rather than breaching systems, leaving defense dependent on bandwidth from a handful of vendors.

The digitisation agency has not said who was behind the attack.
