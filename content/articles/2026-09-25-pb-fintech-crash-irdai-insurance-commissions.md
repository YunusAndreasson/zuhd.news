Gurugram — ₹30,000 crore of market value vanished in one session.

PB Fintech fell at least 34% Thursday, its steepest one-day drop since its 2021 listing.

[India](country:IN)'s insurance regulator proposed restoring commission caps on life, health and motor policies, removed in 2023.

The proposal is a consultation paper, and Jefferies notes it could change after stakeholder feedback.

Brokerage Motilal Oswal says the stock stays under pressure until the regulator publishes final rules.
