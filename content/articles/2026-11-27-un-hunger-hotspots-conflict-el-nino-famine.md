Rome — UN agencies expect hunger to worsen in 16 places.

Aid to the five worst-hit countries fell from $5.1bn to $2bn since 2022, the agencies said.

Conflict drives hunger in 12 of the 16, and El Niño, forecast to intensify, drives 3 of the 4 new ones.

Gaza was downgraded from the highest concern after small gains since the October 2025 ceasefire, but conditions remain fragile.

Famine remains a risk in 13 areas of North Darfur and South Kordofan in [Sudan](country:SD) through January 2027.
