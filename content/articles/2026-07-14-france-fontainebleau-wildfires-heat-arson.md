Two fires burned 1,920 hectares near Paris.

Record heat has turned the rarely-flammable Fontainebleau forest to tinder; 59 people nationwide are held on suspicion of arson.

About 600 firefighters remain deployed as [France](country:FR) enters a third heatwave in three months.
