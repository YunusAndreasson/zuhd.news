Paris — Sterile soil's chemistry bolsters metabolism-first origins theory.

Gamma-sterilised soil ran self-sustaining carbon cycles for 6 years, supporting the idea that chemical cycles preceded genes and cells as life's first step.

The research team continues the experiment; whether abiotic reactions can generate self-replicating chemistry remains unresolved.
