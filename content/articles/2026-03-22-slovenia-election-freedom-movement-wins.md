Ljubljana — Slovenia's Freedom Movement led Sunday's vote but shed 11 seats. The party needs coalition partners after former Prime Minister Janez Jansa's conservative bloc finished just 3 seats behind. Jansa said he would wait for final results before announcing his coalition strategy, leaving Slovenia's next government unresolved.
