Kolkata — Attackers hit Abhishek Banerjee's home for 5 hours, he says.

The Trinamool lawmaker calls it the seventh attempt on his life in six months.

He says Bharatiya Janata Party supporters attacked at 2 am while police stood by or were beaten when they intervened.

"How many bulldozers do you have? Come, I am standing right in front of you," former Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee said.

Mamata says she may approach the Supreme Court over what she calls lawlessness in West Bengal.
