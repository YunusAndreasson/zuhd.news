19 civilians died in a Najafabad double-tap Monday. The first US-Israeli missile hit people queuing at a traffic licensing office; the second struck the rescuers who responded — targeting first responders is prohibited under the laws of war. Iran's Red Crescent filed a formal International Criminal Court complaint citing 6,600 civilian structures damaged since the war began.
