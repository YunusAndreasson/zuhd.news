Washington — FAA lifted criminal penalties for drones near ICE. The original restriction grounded journalists filming unmarked ICE vehicles in Minneapolis; a First Amendment lawsuit filed last week forced the change. The new rule adds DOJ to the protected list and authorizes federal forces to destroy filming drones.
