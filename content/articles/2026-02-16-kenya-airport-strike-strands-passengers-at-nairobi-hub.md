Jomo Kenyatta International Airport halted flights. Aviation workers walked out of East Africa's busiest air hub after 7 days' notice, demanding a collective bargaining agreement that civil aviation authorities refused to finalize. Kenya's Civil Aviation Authority sought a court order to end the strike, but regional airlines reported cascading delays with no resolution in sight.
