Brasília — Senate vote could cut [Brazil](country:BR)'s 44-hour week to 40 hours.

It would also guarantee 2 paid rest days, one preferably Sunday, ending the 6×1 schedule.

The amendment needs 49 of 81 senators in each of 2 rounds, after 5 discussion sessions before the first.

Rogério Marinho, who runs Flávio Bolsonaro's campaign, called the debate opportunistic and backs a flexible workday.

Senate president Davi Alcolumbre will put a fast-track calendar to a vote if the government files one.
