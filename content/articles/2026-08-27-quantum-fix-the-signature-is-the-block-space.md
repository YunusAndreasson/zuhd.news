Victoria — Blockstream proposed SHRINCS to quantum-proof Bitcoin.

Ordinary post-quantum signatures are too large, threatening to price out small transactions.

A quantum computer running Shor's algorithm could theoretically derive a private key from any public key already exposed on-chain.

The draft lacks a completed security proof and needs a network-wide soft fork.
