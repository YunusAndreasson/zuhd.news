Cairo — A statistical method, not isolation, found the active compound.

It lets labs without isolation budgets identify drug candidates from local plants.

Researchers correlated LC-MS chemical profiles of Tecoma stans, a common street shrub, against cell-culture activity to flag chrysoeriol among 107 metabolites.

The compound killed ovarian cancer cells in a dish, but human testing has not begun.
