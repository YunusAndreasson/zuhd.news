Canberra — [Australia](country:AU) chose Japan's Mogami frigate over Germany's MEKO.

The deal marks [Japan](country:JP)'s first major warship export since 1945 and depends on Mitsubishi successfully building hulls in Australia.

Whether Mitsubishi builds on schedule — unlike Australia's submarine programme — will determine the deal's political survival.
