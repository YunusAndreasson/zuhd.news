Slavyansk-on-Kuban — Krasnodar refinery fire: drone fragments, no plant casualties.

The regional crisis center also reported 1 wounded and 4 houses damaged by drone fragments in nearby Trudobelikovsky, [Russia](country:RU)'s Krasnodar Region, authorities said.

First responders were working the refinery fire; the origin of the drones was not confirmed.
