Suva — Fraud networks expelled from Southeast Asian crackdowns are scouting Pacific Island broadband for new bases.

New subsea cables and Starlink have expanded connectivity across island states that previously lacked reliable internet.

The Lowy Institute warned Pacific states lack the laws and enforcement capacity to police transnational scam syndicates.
