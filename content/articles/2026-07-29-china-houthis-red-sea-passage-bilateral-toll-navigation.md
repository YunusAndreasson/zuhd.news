Sanaa — Four Chinese tankers already sailed through Bab el-Mandeb.

[China](country:CN) negotiated vessel-by-vessel clearance directly with [Yemen](country:YE)'s Houthis after they blockaded Saudi ports on July 20, six sources say.

Ships without such a bilateral deal keep rerouting; the alternative around Africa adds 34 days.
