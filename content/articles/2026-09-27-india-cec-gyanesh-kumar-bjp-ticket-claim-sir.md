New Delhi — Kerala's former chief secretary says India's election chief offered him a ruling-party ticket.

Gyanesh Kumar heads the commission running [India](country:IN)'s voter-roll revision, and opposition parties demand his resignation.

G.G. Thomson says Kumar, then a civil servant, urged him on a flight to contest Pathanamthitta in 2019.

The commission says all three commissioners approved every revision order and the Supreme Court upheld the Form 6 changes.

The opposition INDIA bloc meets 30 September with Kumar's removal as its main agenda.
