Brussels — Companies would keep free carbon permits for longer.

The European Commission proposed shrinking the Emissions Trading System's allowance supply more slowly after 2031, quietly permitting more emissions without changing any headline target.

Member states now negotiate the plan, which sets whether the bloc's 2040 target stays reachable.
