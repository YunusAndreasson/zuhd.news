Rome — Meloni filed 4-second voice samples with the EU's trademark office.

[Italy](country:IT)'s prime minister is the first major politician known to seek a voice trademark.

Guido Scorza, a technology lawyer, says platforms act faster on trademark complaints than on personal-data complaints about a voice.

"AI will go around it," said Gianni Riotta, director of the Italian Digital Media Observatory.

The EU Intellectual Property Office is reviewing the filing before next year's election.
