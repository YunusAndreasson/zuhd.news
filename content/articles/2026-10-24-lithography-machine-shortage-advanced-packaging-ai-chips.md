Eindhoven — Customers are asking ASML for lithography tools in 2029 and 2030.

ASML has finished price talks with major customers, and high-end tools will cost more.

ASML will raise capacity 30% each for extreme-ultraviolet and deep-ultraviolet tools, yet 2027 and 2028 demand still exceeds it.

ASML takes formal orders only through 2028 and will allocate 2029 and 2030 slots after tallying all customers.

[Japan](country:JP)'s Canon plans next year's output at up to nearly double this year's, and Nikon's DSP-110 ships in April 2027.
