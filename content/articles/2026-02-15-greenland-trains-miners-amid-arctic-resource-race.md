A dozen students operate heavy equipment in Sisimiut. The Greenland School of Minerals and Petroleum, founded in 2008, trains workers aged 18 to 35 in geology, rock mechanics, and mining operations. The autonomous Danish territory holds 24 of the 34 critical raw materials the European Union considers essential for green and digital industries, including rare earth elements. Deputy Minister Jørgen T. Hammeken-Holm acknowledged "there is a lack of educated people" for the processing facilities that large-scale extraction would require. Only 2 mines currently operate on the island, and the government views resource development as a path toward self-determination while the United States, China, and European nations compete for Arctic access.
