London — Three signaling routes link gut microbes to brain circuits.

A review of imaging, immune and transcriptomic studies ties microbial metabolites, immune signaling and vagal nerve pathways to autism-linked brain changes.

Researchers say small trials show promise, but causal proof requires larger, standardized studies still unscheduled.
