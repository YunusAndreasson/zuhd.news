Mumbai — Sensex opened 0.79% lower Thursday as Brent held near $102.5.

[India](country:IN) imports most of its crude, so oil above $100 a barrel raises its import bill.

Brent gained 4% Wednesday after Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian told the UN Tehran will never surrender to US pressure.

A senior Iranian official said diplomacy must continue, though Tehran and Washington remain divided over ending the war.

The National Stock Exchange lists on the BSE Thursday, with analysts expecting a 2-3% debut gain.
