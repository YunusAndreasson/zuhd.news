Rome — 645 million people faced hunger in 2025, the UN said.

That is 14 million fewer than 2024, but Africa's 309 million hungry keep rising with population even as Asia and Latin America recover.

Gaza has the world's worst catastrophic hunger, at 640,700 people, UN agencies report.
