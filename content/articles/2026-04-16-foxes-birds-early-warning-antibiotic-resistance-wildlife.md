Rome — Wild foxes carry antibiotic-resistant bacteria at 5 times hospital rates. An Italian study found K. pneumoniae resistant to cephalosporins in foxes and birds — acquired from wastewater — spreading into ecosystems beyond clinical reach. Researchers called for wastewater reform and limits on agricultural antibiotic use.
