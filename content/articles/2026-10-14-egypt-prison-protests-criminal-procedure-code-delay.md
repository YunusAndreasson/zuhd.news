Egypt — Prisoners have protested in several jails for 3 weeks.

[Egypt](country:EG) postponed the new Criminal Procedure Code until October 2027, dashing hopes of release from remand.

Lawyers say security forces answered with "taghreeb," punitive transfers to prisons farther from detainees' families.

The Interior Ministry denies any protests or abuses in prisons, in a 2 October statement.

The state's National Council for Human Rights plans prison visits, but lawyers want an independent investigation.
