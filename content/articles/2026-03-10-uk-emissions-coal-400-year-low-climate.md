UK coal use fell below any level since 1600. Wind and solar cut UK emissions 2.4% in 2025 to their lowest since 1872 — but the Iran war has since doubled gas prices, threatening to reverse the gain. The UK achieved only 9 of the 15 million tonnes of annual cuts needed for net zero, a deficit that rising gas prices are now widening.
