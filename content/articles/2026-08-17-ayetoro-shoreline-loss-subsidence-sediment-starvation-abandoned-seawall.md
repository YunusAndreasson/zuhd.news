Ayetoro — This coast has swallowed homes for decades, some just months ago.

Residents lose their fishery and livelihood along with their houses when they relocate inland.

Oil and gas extraction under [Nigeria](country:NG)'s delta causes land subsidence, while dredging starves beaches of the sediment that once rebuilt them.

Past shoreline-protection contracts for the town were funded, then abandoned.
