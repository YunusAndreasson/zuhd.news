Magadi — President Ruto ordered Tata Chemicals to leave.

The order threatens roughly 500 jobs at Africa's largest soda ash producer in [Kenya](country:KE).

Tata's 99-year colonial-era lease predates Kenyan independence, leaving revocation as the government's main leverage over royalties and investment.

Ruto says two new firms will replace Tata's soda ash and glass operations.
