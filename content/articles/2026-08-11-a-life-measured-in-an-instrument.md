Tehran — She didn't pack her double bass.

Years of savings and a professional life vanished with the instrument she left behind.

A strike hit [Iran](country:IR)'s Niloufar Square on March 2, destroying her home the night rescuers said at least 20 people died.

Sanctions leave musicians like her with no way to replace what the war took.
