Englewood Cliffs — Samsung cut 739 US consumer-electronics roles, mostly here.

The unit is moving its headquarters to Texas, near Samsung's new chip fab, drawn by lower taxes and the state's chip-plant cluster.

Most affected workers received relocation offers; others were let go with no word on further cuts.
