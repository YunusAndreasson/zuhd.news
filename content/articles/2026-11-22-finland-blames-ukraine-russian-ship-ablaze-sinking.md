Finland — A burning Russian military cargo ship is at risk of sinking.

[Finland](country:FI)'s prime minister, Petteri Orpo, blamed Ukraine, though Kyiv has not claimed the strike.

The Sparta II, owned by a Russian defence ministry logistics arm and US-sanctioned, drifted into Finnish waters after the hit.

"The Sparta II is under Ukrainian sanctions for a reason," said Vladyslav Vlasiuk, President Zelenskyy's sanctions adviser.

Finnish border guards, with Estonian and Russian vessels, are still fighting the fire to stop it sinking.
