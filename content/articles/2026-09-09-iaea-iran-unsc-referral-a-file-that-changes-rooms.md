New sanctions remain unlikely because [Russia](country:RU) and [China](country:CN) hold Security Council vetoes.

The International Atomic Energy Agency's 35-nation board voted 23-3, citing missing access to sites [Israel](country:IL) and the [US](country:US) bombed twice.

Iran says it will restore access only as part of a wider settlement ending the war.
