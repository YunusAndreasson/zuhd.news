Google closed its acquisition of cloud security company Wiz. Wiz was founded by veterans of Israel's Unit 8200 intelligence corps and secures cloud workloads for the majority of Fortune 500 companies; Google paid $32 billion, its largest-ever acquisition. Wiz now operates across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud simultaneously — securing the clients of all 3 clouds, including Google's own competitors.
