Madrid — [Spain](country:ES)'s first former premier faces criminal suspect status.

A court alleges Zapatero pressed ministers to approve Plus Ultra's €53M bailout and took €10M in commissions via a linked firm.

He faces a suspect hearing at the Audiencia Nacional on June 2; Zapatero denies wrongdoing.
