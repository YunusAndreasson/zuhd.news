Tel Aviv — [Israel](country:IL)'s Tax Authority received Flydubai's crew list before departure.

[Oman](country:OM) had barred co-pilot Hamam al-Hammami from flying over alleged extremist views, sources said.

A 2023 law requires airlines to file crew lists 48 hours ahead, but responsibility then splits between the transport ministry and Mossad.

Transport Minister Miri Regev says her ministry never received the list, but the Tax Authority says it did.

Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu calls the gap a loophole and says Israel will tighten checks.
