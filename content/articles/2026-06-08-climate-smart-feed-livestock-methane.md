Nairobi — Reformulated livestock feed cuts methane without shrinking herds.

Feed additives alter gut fermentation — cattle's largest climate output — while raising yields, requiring no income loss for smallholder farmers.

Field trials are underway in sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia; no commercial approval pathway has been announced.
