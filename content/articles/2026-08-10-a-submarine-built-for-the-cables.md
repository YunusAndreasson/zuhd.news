Washington — One new Virginia-class submarine will target the seafloor.

Nearly all intercontinental internet traffic runs through cables it can tap or cut.

A cable severed in 4,000 meters of water leaves no witness, so attribution takes weeks of ship-tracking forensics.

The [US](country:US) Navy has not named which hull gets the seabed role.
