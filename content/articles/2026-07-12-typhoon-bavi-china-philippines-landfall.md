Taizhou — 1.7 million evacuated as Bavi flooded Zhejiang villages.

At its widest the storm spanned 620 miles, the size of France; it had killed 17 in the Philippines before reaching [China](country:CN).

More than 1,200 flights were canceled Sunday as the weakened storm continues dumping rain across six provinces.
