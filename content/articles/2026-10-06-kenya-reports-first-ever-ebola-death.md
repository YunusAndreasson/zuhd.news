Nairobi — An Ebola patient landed with 23 passengers and 4 crew.

The man fell ill in [Congo](country:CD), crossed [Uganda](country:UG) overland and flew to Nairobi on 3 October.

The Bundibugyo strain, which has no vaccine, killed 4,082 of 8,463 Congolese cases by 2 October, the World Health Organization said.

[Kenya](country:KE)'s Health Minister Aden Duale says none of the 28 listed contacts has tested positive.

Duale says authorities are tracing the 27 others on the flight, with quarantine prepared for those at risk.
