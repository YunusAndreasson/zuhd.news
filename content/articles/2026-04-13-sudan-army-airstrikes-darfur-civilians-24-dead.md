Al-Daein — SAF drones killed 24 across Darfur Monday. Strikes hit a march in Al-Daein, a market in Al-Sarif, and El Geneina's customs district, killing women, children, and health workers. The Darfur Victims Organisation called on the UN Security Council to act; drones kept circling El Geneina through dawn.
