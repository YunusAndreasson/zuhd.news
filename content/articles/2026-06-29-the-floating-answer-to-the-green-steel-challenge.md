Alang — 16,000 ships need recycling this decade.

Their scrap steel could feed low-carbon electric furnaces, but Rushi Kanakiya's yard has sat idle 4 years despite capacity, snarled by trade rules and shipping politics.

Over 80% of scrapping happens in South Asia, where labor and environmental oversight remain contested.
