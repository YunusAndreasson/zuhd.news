Sydney — Ghost Shark launched an aerial drone from a 12-tube launcher.

Anduril says [Australia](country:AU) confirmed payloads of sea mines, logistics caches and drones for surveillance and strike.

The sub surfaces, hull doors slide open, and the launcher rises, with Ghost Shark staying surfaced during the launch.

The video does not identify the variant, but Anduril rates the ALTIUS-600 at about 275 miles.

The footage leaves open what the second launcher, still lowered in the other bay, carries.
