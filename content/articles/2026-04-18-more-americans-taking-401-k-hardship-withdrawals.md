Washington — Hardship 401(k) withdrawals tripled since the pandemic. Vanguard said 6% of participants withdrew in 2025, up from 3.6% in 2023, as housing and food costs squeezed household budgets. Intuit Credit Karma data shows more than half of Americans withdrew or planned to in 2025 and 2026.
