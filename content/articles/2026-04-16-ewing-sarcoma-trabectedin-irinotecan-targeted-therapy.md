Bethesda — 33% response in relapsed Ewing sarcoma. Trabectedin, derived from sea squirts, combined with low-dose irinotecan to disrupt EWS::FLI1, the fusion protein driving Ewing sarcoma. Phase 2 showed 48% progression-free survival at 6 months; patients with no circulating tumor DNA on day 8 survived significantly longer.
