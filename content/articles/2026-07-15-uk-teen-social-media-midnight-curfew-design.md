London — 16 and 17-year-olds face a midnight app curfew.

The default setting blocks [UK](country:GB) teens' access from midnight to 6am and disables autoplay unless they opt out.

Parliament receives the rules this year; enforcement begins spring 2027, testing lessons from [Australia](country:AU)'s stumbling age checks.
