New Delhi — Cheap portable ultrasounds are powering [India](country:IN)'s sex-selection underground.

Online sales ship the devices to private buyers, routing around laws that assume clinics are the enforcement chokepoint.

India's 1994 sex-selection law covers only licensed clinics; no regulation reaches private portable device sales.
