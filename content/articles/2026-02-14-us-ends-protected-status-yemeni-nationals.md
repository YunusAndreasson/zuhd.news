The US ended Temporary Protected Status for ~1,400 Yemeni nationals, giving them 60 days to leave. The State Department's own advisory warns against all travel to Yemen. The move continues TPS terminations under Trump affecting Venezuela, Haiti, Somalia, and Ukraine.
