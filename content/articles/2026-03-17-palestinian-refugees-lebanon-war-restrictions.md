Israel bombed the Beddawi refugee camp in northern Lebanon. Lebanon bars Palestinians from owning property, holding most jobs, or resettling abroad — the bombed camp and its destroyed homes cannot be replaced. 1 million Lebanese are also displaced; Palestinians have no safe camp left and no legal route out.
