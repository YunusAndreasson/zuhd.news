Istanbul — Seven foreign ministers meet Friday on the Iran war.

[Pakistan](country:PK), [Egypt](country:EG), [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) and [Turkey](country:TR) have mediated between Washington and Tehran since March.

[France](country:FR), [Britain](country:GB) and [Germany](country:DE), the E-3 formed over 20 years ago on Iran's nuclear programme, join them for the first time.
