Washington — Bank of America reported $5 billion in suspicious transactions late.

Penalties exceed $1.21 billion, including the third-largest in the [US](country:US) Treasury's history.

The Treasury found the bank outsourced much of its detection to third parties, missing Chinese laundering networks and border illicit finance.

The bank says it is still finishing the fixes regulators require, after a further $11.6 million sanctions settlement.
