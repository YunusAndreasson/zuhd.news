New Delhi — [India](country:IN) wants BRICS states running its exam platform.

The move would extend India's digital exports, following Aadhaar and the Unified Payments Interface.

The National Testing Agency plans AI and biometric fraud checks before offering the system abroad.

Leaks in India's medical entrance exam forced cancellations and remain unresolved as the agency pitches the product.
