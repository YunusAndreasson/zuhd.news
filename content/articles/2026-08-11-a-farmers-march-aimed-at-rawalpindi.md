Lahore — Punjab farmers threatened a march on Rawalpindi.

They want Punjab's chief minister removed over a withdrawn wheat price floor.

[Pakistan](country:PK) dropped its wheat support price under an IMF condition, exposing growers to market prices at old input costs.

Pakistan Kissan Ittehad's Million March on Rawalpindi is set for September 25.
