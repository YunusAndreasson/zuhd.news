Beijing — All 31 of [China](country:CN)'s provinces pledged to peak carbon before 2030.

The 15th Five-Year Plans show divergence: Zhejiang targets 90 gigawatts of solar while coal-heavy inland provinces balance fossil and renewable capacity.

Provincial targets tie to officials' performance reviews; delivery, not summit pledges, decides China's emissions path.
