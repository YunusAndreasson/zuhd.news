Nairobi — Female baboons with stronger maternal bonds survive longer, a study across 4 populations shows.

Bond strength — not dietary flexibility alone — explains how baboons thrive from Saharan desert to tropical forest and into Arabia.

The findings suggest kin cooperation functions as survival infrastructure across primates.
