Panama City — A 7.7 earthquake toppled a tower in Penonomé.

The US Geological Survey says 4.6 million people felt moderate to severe shaking.

It struck 12.6km deep near Pitaloza Arriba in Herrera province, and tsunami warnings covered [Colombia](country:CO), Costa Rica, Ecuador, Peru and Mexico.

[Panama](country:PA)'s geological institute rated it 7.2, and the Panama Canal was not affected.

Casualty counts are not yet in, and Panama's education ministry has closed schools nationwide.
