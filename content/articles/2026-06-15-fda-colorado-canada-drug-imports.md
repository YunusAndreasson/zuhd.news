Washington — Colorado became the 2nd [US](country:US) state cleared to import drugs from [Canada](country:CA).

[Canada](country:CA)'s drug industry blocked imports despite a 2020 federal law; Florida gained approval in 2024 but imported nothing.

Colorado must negotiate with suppliers who rejected Florida's program over domestic supply concerns.
