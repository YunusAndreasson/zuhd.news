London — Earth's energy imbalance reached its highest 10-year average on record, the annual Indicators of Global Climate Change report found.

The "energy imbalance" measures incoming solar heat minus what Earth radiates back; 2024 emissions hit 56.8 billion tonnes of CO₂ equivalent.

The report warns surface temperatures could accelerate further unless emissions fall.
