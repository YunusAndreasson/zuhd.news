Washington — Average [US](country:US) drug shortages now last 5.3 years.

The count fell 23% last year, US Pharmacopeia found, but nearly two-thirds of shortages have lasted more than 3 years.

The driver, US Pharmacopeia said, is cheap generic injectables with margins too thin to support backup supply.
