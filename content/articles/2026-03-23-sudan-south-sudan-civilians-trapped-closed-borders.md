Khartoum — 12 million people cannot escape 2 simultaneous wars. The Rapid Support Forces control Darfur's exit routes while South Sudan's 2018 peace deal has collapsed in Jonglei State, displacing 280,000 more. UN agencies warn Darfur's famine will intensify unless the RSF and South Sudan's warring factions open humanitarian corridors.
