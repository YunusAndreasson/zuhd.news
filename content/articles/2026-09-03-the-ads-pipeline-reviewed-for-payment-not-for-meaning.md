Sarajevo — Meta ran paid ads using Ratko Mladić's image.

One AI-generated ad reached thousands before press inquiries got it removed.

Moderation systems verify ad payment automatically but rarely catch contextual glorification, an expert said.

Meta has not said whether it will screen ads for banned figures before they run.
