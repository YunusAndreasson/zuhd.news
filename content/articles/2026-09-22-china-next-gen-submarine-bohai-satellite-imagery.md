Huludao — Satellite images reveal [China](country:CN)'s next-generation attack submarine.

The design's stealth features signal a leap in China's undersea warfare capability.

Commercial satellite images from September 18 show a pumpjet propulsor and X-shaped stern planes, both quieter than older cruciform designs.

Analysts at Janes call it a Type 095, though that designation remains unofficial.

Beijing has released no official details, leaving foreign navies to track the design through satellite passes alone.
