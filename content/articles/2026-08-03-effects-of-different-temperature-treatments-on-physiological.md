London — Lettuce fails at a threshold, not a slope.

Researchers found high-temperature stress pushed two lettuce varieties toward bolting faster than cold stress, raising catalase and malondialdehyde markers of plant stress.

The unedited manuscript studied only two cultivars; authors say it cannot yet predict field-scale crop loss.
