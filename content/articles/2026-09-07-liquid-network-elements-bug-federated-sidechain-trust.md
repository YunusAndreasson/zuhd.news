New York — Roughly 4,000 bitcoin drained from Liquid Network's reserve.

No key was stolen, so the custody safeguards exchanges rely on never engaged.

Blockstream blames a software bug in Elements, not compromised keys, with funds moved through the approved SideSwap platform.

Attackers call themselves white-hats, a claim worth nothing until the funds return.
