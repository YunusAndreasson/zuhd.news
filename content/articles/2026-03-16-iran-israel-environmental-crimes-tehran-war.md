Oil rained on Tehran when Israel hit fuel tanks. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi accused Israel of ecocide, warning that burning fuel depots contaminated soil and groundwater with generational effects. Araghchi declared Israel must be punished for war crimes, signaling Iran will pursue an international legal case.
