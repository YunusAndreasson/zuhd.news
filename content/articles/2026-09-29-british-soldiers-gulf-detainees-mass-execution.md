Sur — British soldiers interrogated Omani dissidents before their 1973 executions.

[Oman](country:OM) controls half the Strait of Hormuz, the route Britain's crackdown protected, contested again today.

Declassified UK, an investigative outlet on UK security policy, found British officers questioned over 77 of roughly 90 detainees before a one-day trial condemned 11 to death.

Britain's ambassador sought clemency for the condemned men, but Declassified UK found no evidence he succeeded.
