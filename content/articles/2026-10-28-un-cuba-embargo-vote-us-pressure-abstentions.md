New York — A record 17 states opposed ending the [Cuba](country:CU) embargo.

130 states still condemned it, down from 165 last year, and 25 abstained.

Cuba's foreign minister Bruno Rodríguez Parrilla alleged, without presenting evidence, that Washington threatened tariffs, visa denials and aid cuts.

[US](country:US) ambassador Mike Waltz told delegates a yes vote hands Havana a propaganda victory.

Waltz says Secretary of State Marco Rubio has ordered a Cuban diplomat expelled within 24 hours.
