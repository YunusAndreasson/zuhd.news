Iranians filmed Basij checkpoints and sent footage to Israel. Israel has drone-struck Basij posts across Tehran since Wednesday, killing multiple Basij members in a campaign to dismantle Iran's street policing capacity. Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps confirmed the strikes and announced counter-adaptation plans as Tehran's internet blackout enters its 16th day.
