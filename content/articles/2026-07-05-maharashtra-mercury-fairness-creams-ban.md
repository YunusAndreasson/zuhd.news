Nagpur — 18 women developed kidney problems from one skin cream.

Maharashtra's drug regulator found mercury 752 times the legal limit in Goree Beauty Cream, made in [Pakistan](country:PK); the metal suppresses melanin while accumulating toxic damage in kidneys.

The banned creams still sell online through Meesho and Instagram, regulators said.
