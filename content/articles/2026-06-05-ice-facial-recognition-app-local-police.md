Washington — US Immigration and Customs Enforcement plans a facial recognition app for local police.

Officers would scan a face to query 250 million [US](country:US) government images and check immigration status — no court order required.

The American Civil Liberties Union said the app has a known false-match rate and stores biometric data for 15 years.
