Islamabad — The Asian Development Bank said [Pakistan](country:PK) insures 0.7% of its economy.

It approved $700 million to shift from rules-based to risk-based coverage for farmers, women, and flood-hit households.

The programme targets parametric products that pay after floods without claim disputes.
