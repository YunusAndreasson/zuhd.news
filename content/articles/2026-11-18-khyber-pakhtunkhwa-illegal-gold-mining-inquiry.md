Peshawar — A retired judge will investigate who protected illegal gold mining.

10 police officers died on 11 November returning from an anti-mining operation in Kohat.

The 8-member commission can summon witnesses on oath and will trace beneficiaries, proceeds and links to terrorist financing.

The notification cites repeated complaints, government directions and criminal cases that failed to stop the mining.

The commission reports to the governor within 45 days.
