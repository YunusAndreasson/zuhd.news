Seoul — Washington scrapped next month's Twin Dragons exercise.

Allied planners now question whether [US](country:US) amphibious forces will show up when needed.

Marine rotations and shipping are tied up in the Iran war, eroding landing-force readiness.

[South Korea](country:KR) has not been told if the cancellation is temporary or permanent.
