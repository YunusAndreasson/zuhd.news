Abuja — Ex-minister Uche Nnaji faces six corruption counts.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s anti-graft commission charges he forged degree and service certificates used to win 2023 Senate confirmation, plus N29.5 million in salary.

Nnaji, now a governorship candidate, is expected to be arraigned within days.
