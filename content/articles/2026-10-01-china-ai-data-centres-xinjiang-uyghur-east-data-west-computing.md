Urumqi — Shanghai Cooperation Organisation officials met behind closed doors on 14 September.

Global Voices reports [China](country:CN) is recasting the Uyghur region from energy supplier to AI computing source.

Beijing calls the region a national energy and resource strategic security base, and Karamay hosts a cloud computing industrial park.

The forum's public language left out the region's history of mass surveillance, Global Voices notes.

Global Voices says Chinese officials want to extend computing capacity, data systems and standards from Urumqi into Central Asia.
