Washington — 60-plus [US](country:US) strikes killed 202 boat crews without trial or charges.

Operation Southern Spear has struck vessels in South American waters since November; no charges were filed against any crew.

US Southern Command confirmed a May 30 strike; Congress has held no public hearings on the program.
