Baltimore — The James Webb Space Telescope mapped morning clouds and clear evenings 690 light-years away.

A Johns Hopkins team split the transit signal into morning and evening spectra, producing the first spatially resolved atmospheric map of an exoplanet.

Single-sphere analysis had inflated the planet's oxygen enrichment 100-fold, researchers reported in Science.
