Myanmar's first parliament since the 2020 coup convened Monday. The military voided an election where Suu Kyi's party won 83% of seats, then ran a replacement vote with opposition parties banned. Junta chief Min Aung Hlaing is expected to become civilian president when parliament votes in April.
