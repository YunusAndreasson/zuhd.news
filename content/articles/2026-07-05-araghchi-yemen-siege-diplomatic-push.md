Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) vowed to help end [Yemen](country:YE)'s blockade Saturday.

Yemeni deputy premier Jalal al-Rowaishan raised the blockade at Khamenei's funeral, where Araghchi pledged to use every diplomatic channel to lift it.

No timeline was set for lifting the siege or implementing the peace roadmap.
