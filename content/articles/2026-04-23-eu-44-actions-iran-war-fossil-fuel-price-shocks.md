Brussels — [Iran](country:IR)'s war cost the EU €24bn in extra fossil-fuel imports, the Commission said. Brussels published a 44-action plan covering renewables, gas storage, and electrification to cut price-shock exposure. Taxation measures require unanimous consent from all 27 member states.
