Ahmedabad — Copper now supplies nearly 40% of Adani Enterprises' revenue.

[India](country:IN)'s Adani Enterprises returned to profit after losses in January-March and April-June.

Its Gujarat smelter, the world's largest single-site copper plant, lifted copper sales to Rs 14,772 crore in July-September.

Net profit of Rs 1,492 crore is still 55% below last year, which included a Rs 3,583 crore stake-sale gain.

Operating margin slipped to 14.8% from 15.56% a year earlier as expenses rose 71%.
