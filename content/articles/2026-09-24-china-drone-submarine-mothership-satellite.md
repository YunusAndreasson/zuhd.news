Shanghai — A 204-metre ship built around a 50-metre cradle just got navy-gray paint.

It appears to support [China](country:CN)'s growing fleets of large uncrewed submarines.

A 10 September satellite image shows a well deck, a semi-submersible cradle and 12 struts that may lower payloads.

The ship could also serve other roles.

The gray paint points to future People's Liberation Army Navy service.
