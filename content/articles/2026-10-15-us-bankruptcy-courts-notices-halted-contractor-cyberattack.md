Washington — A cyberattack on BAE Systems halted all notices for [US](country:US) bankruptcy courts.

Every federal bankruptcy court relies on the one system, the Administrative Office of the US Courts said.

The outage also took down 3 websites and the National Creditor Registration Service.

BAE says the incident is isolated to the system, and the courts office has not said whether data was stolen.

BAE is investigating, and no restoration date has been given.
