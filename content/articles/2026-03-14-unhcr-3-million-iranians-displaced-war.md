A million Iranian households fled Tehran for the rural north. UNHCR's preliminary assessment puts the total at 3.2 million people, a scale not seen since the Syrian civil war's opening months. Afghan refugee families inside Iran face the sharpest exposure as UNHCR calls for borders to remain open.
