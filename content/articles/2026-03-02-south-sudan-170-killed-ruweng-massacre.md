Armed youths from Unity state killed 169 in Ruweng. The attackers, from a predominantly Nuer region, stormed the oil-rich Dinka area after South Sudan opposition leader Riek Machar's arrest shattered the 2018 peace agreement. Doctors Without Borders has suspended all medical services in Jonglei state, where 26 of its staff remain missing.
