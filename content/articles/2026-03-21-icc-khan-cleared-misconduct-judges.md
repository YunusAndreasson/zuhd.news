The Hague — 3 ICC judges cleared Karim Khan of misconduct. The allegations surfaced as Khan pursued war crimes charges against Israeli officials over Gaza; critics used them to challenge the court's legitimacy. Khan returns to prosecuting Gaza war crimes, but most ICC member states will never see the confidential report that cleared him.
