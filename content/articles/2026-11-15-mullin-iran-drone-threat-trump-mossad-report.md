Washington — Mossad reportedly warned the [US](country:US) of explosive drones aimed at Trump.

Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin says Trump and his family are [Iran](country:IR)'s top target.

Mullin says his department gets daily briefings on Iran's aspirational threats and knew of them before Israel shared intelligence.

Mullin said much of the intelligence amounts to chatter, though officials treat every threat at face value.

Trump has told advisers he may hold off on Iran attacks barring new provocations, sources said.
