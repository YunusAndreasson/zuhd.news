A US-funded vaccine trial faces global condemnation. The World Health Organization called the study in Guinea-Bissau unethical. The trial, backed by the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, planned to give half of 14,500 newborns a hepatitis B vaccine at birth while withholding it from the other half until 2 months old. World Health Organization Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus said withholding the vaccine exposes newborns to chronic infection, cirrhosis and liver cancer. Guinea-Bissau suspended the trial last month.
