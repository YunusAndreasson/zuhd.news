Malaysia shielded lower earners from an 80-sen diesel hike. The government held its BUDI95 targeted subsidy at RM1.99 while raising diesel to RM3.92, passing the oil price shock to wealthier consumers. Malaysia's weekly price review is due March 18, with crude markets at their worst volatility since 2022.
