London — Britain's cyber strategy, due by end-2025, has slipped again to 9 December.

The plan is meant to defend the wider economy against state-backed and criminal hackers.

Officials had aimed for mid-November, after July's near-publication was postponed amid Keir Starmer's resignation, several sources said.

"Delay and avoidance is simply not an option," said Baroness Northover, a Liberal Democrat member of the Lords resilience committee.

The Cyber Security and Resilience Bill is not expected to be enforced until 2028.
