London — Human schizophrenia RNA impaired mouse spatial memory.

Researchers injected patients' hippocampal RNA into mice, causing spatial memory loss and synaptic changes mirroring schizophrenia patients' brains.

The identified sRNAs — including microRNAs and tRNA-derived fragments — are now candidate targets for cognitive symptom treatment.
