Srinagar — A new model ranks Kashmir's landslide risk zones.

The ranking can decide where roads, towns and pilgrim routes are permitted to expand.

The ranking blends expert weighting with a landslide inventory that is thinnest exactly where roads are newest.

Researchers caution the ranking shows susceptibility for [India](country:IN)'s Kashmir, not where a landslide will occur.
