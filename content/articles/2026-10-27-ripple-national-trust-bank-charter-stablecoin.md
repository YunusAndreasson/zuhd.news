United States — Ripple's $2.5 billion stablecoin gained a federal regulator.

The [US](country:US) comptroller of the currency approved the licence, pulling crypto deeper into regulated finance.

Ripple National Trust Bank will manage RLUSD's reserves and offer custody, while a New York-regulated unit still issues the token.

Ripple will keep working with the comptroller's office and New York regulators as GENIUS Act rules arrive.
