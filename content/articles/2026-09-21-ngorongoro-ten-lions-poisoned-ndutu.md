Ndutu — 10 lions were found dead in one Ngorongoro incident.

Ngorongoro is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of [Tanzania](country:TZ)'s most prominent wildlife destinations.

The conservation authority says preliminary findings suggest poison, a recurring East African threat where lions prey on livestock.

Authorities have not established whether a livestock conflict was involved.

Investigators will test whether poison caused the deaths and identify those responsible.
