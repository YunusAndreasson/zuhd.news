Madyan — A nine-year-old orphan was assaulted, then shot dead.

Women marched publicly in a valley where the Taliban once banned that.

Police arrested suspect Sultan Mohammad as protesters marched to lawmaker Mian Sharafat Ali's home demanding swift justice.

No trial date has been set for a case Swat's history shows can end in settlement.
