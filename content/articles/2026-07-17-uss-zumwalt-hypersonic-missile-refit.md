Washington — USS Zumwalt returns to service by September, 10 months late.

The [US](country:US) Navy is swapping the stealth destroyer's two costly 155mm guns for four hypersonic missile launchers.

Sister ship Lyndon B. Johnson gets the same refit, due for delivery April 2027.
