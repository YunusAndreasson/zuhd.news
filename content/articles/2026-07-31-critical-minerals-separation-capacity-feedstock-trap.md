Canberra — [Australia](country:AU)'s new mines are not supply-chain ready.

Rare earths are chemically near-identical, so separating them takes hundreds of solvent-extraction stages — capital and permitting patience [China](country:CN) spent decades building.

[Indonesia](country:ID) and the [Democratic Republic of Congo](country:CD) face the same choice: export ore or build refineries.
