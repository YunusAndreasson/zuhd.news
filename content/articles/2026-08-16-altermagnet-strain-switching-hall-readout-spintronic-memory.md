Prague — Squeezing a crystal flipped its electrical signal.

A magnet-free memory bit could pack denser without fields corrupting its neighbors.

Manganese telluride has internal magnetic order but no external field, so strain, not a magnet, wrote and read its state.

Room-temperature performance and switching speed remain unreported outside this lab result.
