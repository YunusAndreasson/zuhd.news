London — A phased-array chip hits 85% accuracy.

It could let autonomous sensors tell ice from wet asphalt in real time.

It reads polarization in reflected light to classify wood, stone and steel, not just shapes.

The demonstration remains a lab chip, with no timeline given for a shipping sensor.
