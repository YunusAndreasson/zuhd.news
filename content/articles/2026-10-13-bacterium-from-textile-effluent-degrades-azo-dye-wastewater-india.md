Guwahati — A bacterium from textile effluent degraded 98.5% of a synthetic dye.

Azo dyes resist treatment and endanger aquatic life and people.

Researchers at the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati found Brevundimonas AJZ05 secretes azoreductase, which broke down Direct Blue-6 despite copper, zinc and iron.

Mung bean tests showed far less toxicity, but the report describes no plant trial.
