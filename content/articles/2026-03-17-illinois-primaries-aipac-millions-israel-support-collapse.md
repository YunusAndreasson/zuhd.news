The American Israel Public Affairs Committee spent $13.7 million targeting 6 Illinois progressives. AIPAC channeled the money through shadow PACs whose names don't mention Israel, as US public support for Israel fell to a historic low of 32%. Tuesday's results will test whether covert funding can defeat candidates openly opposed to the Iran war.
