Washington — A 31-year-old lawyer now directs America's nuclear regulator. Seth Cohen, a Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE) lawyer with no nuclear background, dismissed radiation risks near test sites and told Nuclear Regulatory Commission staff the agency would follow his orders. Cohen has forced out career nuclear experts and is accelerating reactor approvals on a timeline that bypasses years of independent safety review.
