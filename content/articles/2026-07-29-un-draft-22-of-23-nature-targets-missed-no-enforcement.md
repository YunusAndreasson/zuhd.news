Montreal — 22 of 23 nature targets are off track.

A draft UN report finds the Kunming-Montreal framework carries no enforcement and no financing schedule that materialised, leaving states to self-assess.

Tropical megadiverse nations, promised finance for conservation, are left funding protection themselves before October's COP17 in Armenia.
