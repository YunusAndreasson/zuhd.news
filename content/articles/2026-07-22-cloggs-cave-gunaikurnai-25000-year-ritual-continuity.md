Buchan — GunaiKurnai practiced cave rituals for 25,000 years.

Burnt-grass residues in the cave's layers match 19th-century ethnographic records of healing and cursing rituals.

GunaiKurnai custodians co-directed the dig, validating oral tradition in [Australia](country:AU) as reliable long-term memory.
