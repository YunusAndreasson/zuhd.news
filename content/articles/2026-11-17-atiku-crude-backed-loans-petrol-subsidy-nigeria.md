Abuja — Atiku would review crude pledged against loans to subsidise petrol.

His aide says much of [Nigeria](country:NG)'s crude is already pledged against loans.

Refineries including Dangote would get crude below market prices, and the campaign has not said who bears the cost.

President Bola Tinubu said on 10 November that the petrol subsidy will not return.

Phrank Shaibu named no agreements, and did not mention the $4.5 billion Project Gazelle refinancing.
