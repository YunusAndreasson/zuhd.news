New York — FIFA shut down a disabled charity's ticket raffle.

New York and New Jersey subpoenaed FIFA, alleging deceptive variable pricing raised 90 of 104 fixture prices by 34% on average.

The tournament opens June 11 across the [US](country:US), [Canada](country:CA) and [Mexico](country:MX); FIFA must produce pricing records under subpoena.
