Vladivostok — Rosatom wants 100 million tonnes shipped by 2032.

That threshold is what Rosatom says would make the Arctic route pay for itself.

[Russia](country:RU)'s Rosatom owns both the nuclear icebreakers and the route's administration, certifying the transits it profits from.

Rosatom has moved this target twice already, with no independent tonnage figures released yet.
