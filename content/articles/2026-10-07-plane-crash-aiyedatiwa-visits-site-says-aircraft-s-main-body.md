Igbokoda — No survivor found among 26 aboard Nigerian Air Force ATR-42.

Governor Lucky Aiyedatiwa said the pilot steered the stricken aircraft away from the community, landing 200 metres off.

Divers recovered only body parts and debris, and the governor said the wreck may lie up to 30 metres deep in mud.

Navy and air force officers began sonar mapping Wednesday to locate the wreck, and swamp buggies are excavating the site.
