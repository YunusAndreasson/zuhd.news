Nashville — Christa Pike is on a ventilator after 2 lethal injections.

Her lawyer says doctors are still clearing the drug and the prognosis is unknown.

Tennessee says its protocol allows no procedures beyond those carried out, after her lawyers warned of small veins and IV access.

"I think that the state owes me a big apology," said May Martinez, mother of victim Colleen Slemmer.

Governor Bill Lee halted 2026 executions, but the attorney general has not said whether Tennessee will try again.
