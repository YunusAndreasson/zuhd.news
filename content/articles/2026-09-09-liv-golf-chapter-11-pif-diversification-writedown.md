Newark — [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s fund wrote off a golf bet.

The fund put over $5 billion into a league that never found an audience.

The fund owns LIV outright and is now lending it $49.6 million to restructure under Chapter 11 protection.

Creditors Jon Rahm, Bryson DeChambeau and Dustin Johnson await a 2027 exit backed by BC Partners.
