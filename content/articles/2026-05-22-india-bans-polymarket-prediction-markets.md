New Delhi — [India](country:IN) blocked Polymarket with a single ministry directive.

India's technology ministry classified prediction markets as banned money gaming under the Online Gaming Act 2025 and ordered providers to block them.

Kalshi faces a similar blocking order within days, local media reported, citing a ministry source.
