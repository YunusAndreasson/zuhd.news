Seychelles — Monero fell 11% Thursday, its biggest drop since 4 June.

KuCoin delists Monero on 30 November, shrinking its market to fewer offshore venues.

A Hyperliquid contributor says over 50% of users sourced monero from KuCoin, and instant-swap sites wrap its order books.

Eigenwallet's atomic swap lets 2 people trade bitcoin for monero directly, with no exchange in between.

FCMP++, which hides each transaction among 150 million outputs instead of 16, is in security audits.
