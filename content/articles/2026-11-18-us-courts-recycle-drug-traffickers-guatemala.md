Cobán — [Guatemala](country:GT) arrested a trafficker the [US](country:US) freed after six years.

Horst Walther Overdick faced a 15-year minimum but walked free in April 2019.

About 97% of federal drug defendants pleaded guilty in those years, and a quarter of them won reduced sentences for cooperating.

The reasons for Overdick's release are unclear because most of his court files are sealed.

Guatemalan prosecutors allege Overdick and 3 gang members, arrested 5 and 7 November, were trafficking drugs in Cobán.
