Pakistan killed 707 before declaring an Eid truce. After Afghan-based fighters attacked Pakistani civilians, Pakistan launched Operation Ghazab — striking 81 locations across Afghanistan in 3 weeks. Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and Turkey secured the pause through March 23 — both sides warn fighting resumes if either strikes first.
