Constanța — Navy divers blew up two drifting Russian-made drones.

The debris sat beside Neptun Deep, the field meant to make [Romania](country:RO) the EU's top gas producer.

Ukraine confirmed the Gerbera-type drones were not theirs, Minister Radu Miruță says, and divers cleared them with explosives.

Neptun Deep is due to start production in 2027.
