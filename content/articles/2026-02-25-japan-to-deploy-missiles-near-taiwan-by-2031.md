Yonaguni, 110km from Taiwan, will get missiles by 2031. Defence Minister Koizumi set the timeline for the fishing island of 1,700, deepening a military build-up driven by Japan's pledge to defend Taiwan. Beijing restricted exports to 20 Japanese firms the day before and has not yet responded to the missile timeline.
