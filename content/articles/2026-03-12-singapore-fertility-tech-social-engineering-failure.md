Singapore's fertility rate fell to a record low of 0.87 in 2025. After 40 years of incentives — baby bonuses, subsidized housing, and state-backed dating apps — the government now calls falling births "an existential challenge." At 0.87, 100 Singaporeans will produce only 44 children; Japan, South Korea, and China face the same trajectory despite identical programs.
