San Francisco — Patreon now blocks AI crawlers network-wide.

The Cloudflare partnership blocks training bots at the infrastructure level on every post, rather than leaving creators to fight scrapers alone.

Patreon's CEO says creators deserve credit, compensation and consent before any AI training use.
