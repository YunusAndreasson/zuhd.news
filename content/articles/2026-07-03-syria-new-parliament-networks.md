Damascus — Syria's new parliament seats 210 members, no majority.

[Syria](country:SY)'s elected members rose through wartime networks — Sharia councils, field hospitals, armed factions — not parties, analysts say; al-Sharaa appointed 70 more.

Whether these networks harden into parties or close ranks against rivals remains unsettled.
