Islamabad — Solar now powers 960,000 of the nation's tubewells.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s new agriculture census shows farmers shifting from state-run canals to private groundwater, more than diesel and grid electricity combined.

No basin-level plan yet governs the falling water tables the shift is producing.
