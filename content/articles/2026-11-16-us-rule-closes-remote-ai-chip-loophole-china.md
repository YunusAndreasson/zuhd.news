Washington — Firms headquartered in 5 countries will need AI chip licences.

[China](country:CN) rents banned Nvidia chips from Southeast Asian data centres, sidestepping Commerce Department rules.

Overseas data centres supplying large volumes of computing to firms headquartered in China, Russia or Iran must vet those customers.

The licences cover companies based in Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil, where smuggling risk runs higher.

President Trump meets Xi Jinping at an economic summit in China within days.
