San Francisco — Anthropic's third-quarter revenue hit $16.8 billion, an investor says.

Investors expect it to list before OpenAI, at a target valuation above $2 trillion.

Quarterly growth slowed from 147% to 45%, and its adjusted profit comes without itemised adjustments.

Anthropic lost over $8 billion on an operating basis last year, excluding mainly financing-related charges.

The listing may wait until after November's [US](country:US) midterms, and its $518 billion ten-year compute pledge stands.
