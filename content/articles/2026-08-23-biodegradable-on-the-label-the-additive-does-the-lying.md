London — Balloons are getting a green makeover.

Marine wildlife swallows and chokes on the debris long after the party ends.

[UK](country:GB) researchers are reworking the additives that keep latex balloons from truly biodegrading.

The team has not said when a reformulated balloon reaches shelves.
