Geneva — A Swiss firm profiled 11,300 [UAE](country:AE) residents on Facebook.

Liking pages of outlets critical of the UAE counted as grounds for inclusion.

Leaked documents show Alp Services logged names, residences and employers for €55,000 under the code name Arnica 2.

The UAE detained hundreds this year over Iran war posts, and its foreign ministry did not respond.
