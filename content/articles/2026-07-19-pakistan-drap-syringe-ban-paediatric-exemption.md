Karachi — Neonatal wards risk losing a lifesaving tool.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s drug regulator banned 10cc syringes to curb HIV spread from reuse, but neonatal wards have no substitute for feeding infants.

The Pediatric Association wants an exemption before the ban takes effect January 1st, pending a clinical review panel.
