Running a primary election is subversion in Hong Kong. The Court of Appeal dismissed all 12 challenges from the 47-defendant trial, ruling that organizing an unofficial 2020 primary violated Beijing's national security law. Former lawmakers and activists must now serve sentences of up to 10 years with no legal recourse remaining.
