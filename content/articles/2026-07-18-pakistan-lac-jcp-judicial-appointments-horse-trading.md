Islamabad — Lawyers just declared judge selection a "horse-trading" farce.

The Lawyers Action Committee says two constitutional amendments let judges reach the bench through political loyalty, not competence.

It named Ali Ahmed Kurd to lead a movement, with a first rally planned for Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
