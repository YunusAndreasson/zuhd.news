New Delhi — Over 20 protesters were detained in central Delhi.

Any change to [India](country:IN)'s caste quotas requires amending the constitution, not a street protest.

A standing order bars gatherings of five or more people in the area, making the assembly illegal regardless of conduct.

Delhi Police have denied permission for further gatherings at Jantar Mantar too.
