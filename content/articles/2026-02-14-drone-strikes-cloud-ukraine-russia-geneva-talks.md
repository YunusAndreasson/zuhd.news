Drone strikes killed civilians on both sides this week. Russian strikes in Kharkiv killed 3 children, including 2-year-old twins, and their father, while a Russian drone hit a residential building in Odesa. Ukrainian strikes on Russia's Belgorod and Bryansk regions killed 3 civilians. The attacks came days before US-brokered peace talks in Geneva, where Russia demands Ukraine withdraw from all of Donetsk and the US has set a June deadline. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy warned negotiations keep demanding concessions from Ukraine while putting no pressure on Russia.
