Rome — 11.6% of pupils are non-citizens.

Meloni's plan caps their share per classroom and bans burqas and niqabs in schools.

She announced it at her party's youth festival, as the ultranationalist National Future party's surge pressures her coalition before next year's election.

Meloni named no cap, and parliament has 60 days to approve after [Italy](country:IT)'s Cabinet acts.
