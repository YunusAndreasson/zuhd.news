Washington — Trump's approval fell to 32%, a career low.

Republicans defending narrow congressional majorities face voters angry over living costs in November.

Republican approval dropped from 82% to 73% in a week as diesel and gasoline prices rose since the [Iran](country:IR) war began.

Trump said Wednesday the war was "hopefully toward the end," though Iran has not confirmed any talks.

Voters decide whether Republicans keep their majorities in the November 3 midterms.
