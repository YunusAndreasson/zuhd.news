Berlin — Merz's party won 5.5% in Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania.

It is the party's worst state result in [Germany](country:DE) since 1949, exit polls show.

The far-right Alternative for Germany won 37%, though a firewall of non-cooperation keeps it from governing.

"The reforms have to come," Chancellor Friedrich Merz said, vowing to stay on.

Alternative for Germany leader Leif-Erik Holm says the party will now aim to displace Merz's party.
