Washington — [US](country:US) Treasury seized $1bn in [Iran](country:IR)-linked crypto wallets.

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent tied the seizure to Operation Economic Fury and said Iran now faces 200% inflation, unpaid military personnel, and food vouchers.

Nuclear talks proceed alongside the pressure campaign, with no timeline announced.
