Cape Town — Oil reached shore in 75% of simulated blowouts.

Small-scale fishers on [South Africa](country:ZA)'s West Coast generally cannot move to clean waters.

WILDTRUST scientists simulated a 20-day blowout 60km off Cape Point 200 times, and oil reached shore in 98% of April-September starts.

Those figures are not the odds of a blowout, and TotalEnergies says 99.985% of wells drill without one.

A court judgment on TotalEnergies' drilling authorisation is expected soon.
