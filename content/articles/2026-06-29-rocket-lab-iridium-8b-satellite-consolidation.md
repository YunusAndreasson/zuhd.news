Long Beach — Rocket Lab paid $8bn for Iridium's licensed spectrum.

Iridium's L-band frequencies, licensed for 2.55 million users including [US](country:US) government and polar coverage, cannot be replicated by new entrants.

Shareholder and regulatory review runs until mid-2027.
