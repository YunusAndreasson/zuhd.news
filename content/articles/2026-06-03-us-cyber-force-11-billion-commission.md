Washington — [US](country:US) Cyber Force would cost $11bn, a commission said.

The force needs roughly 11,000 people — 5,000 National Guard and 6,000 civilians — and 12-18 months to stand up, the commission said.

A congressional decision on establishing the branch, independent from Cyber Command, remains pending.
