La Paz — It took ten years to name this cat.

Leopardus tilcayo is the first new cat species described in a century.

Genomic comparisons across 38 cats showed the lineage in [Bolivia](country:BO) split from relatives roughly 1.4 million years ago.

Conservation status now depends on IUCN reviewing each of five tiger cat lineages separately.
