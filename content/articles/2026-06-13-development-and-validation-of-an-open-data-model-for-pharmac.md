London — Genetic drug tailoring stalls on data incompatibility.

A team of 24 experts from 10 countries validated an open model linking genetic tests to prescribing systems using openEHR and Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources standards.

No health system has implemented it; the model is published on openEHR's platform for open adoption.
