100 million people use ByteDance's chatbot daily. The Chinese company's Doubao AI processes over 50 trillion tokens a day, rivalling OpenAI and Google in scale. ByteDance was Nvidia's largest Chinese client in 2024 and plans to spend billions more on chips this year. The AI push matters as TikTok shrinks: a forced US sale left ByteDance with under 20% of the American operation, and Europe cited the app for content violations. Whether ByteDance can replicate TikTok's cultural reach in AI remains untested.
