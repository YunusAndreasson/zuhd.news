Islamabad — The World Bank approved $70 million for Punjab broadband.

The loan cuts right-of-way permitting from 90 to 21 days, aiming to connect 2.1 million more people in [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Punjab by 2031.

The programme also targets tripling women's use of digital government services to 30% by June 2031.
