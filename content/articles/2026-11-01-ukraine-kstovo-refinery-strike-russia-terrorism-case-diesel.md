Kstovo — Over 100 drones targeted [Russia](country:RU)'s Kstovo district overnight.

[Ukraine](country:UA) claims the Kstovo oil refinery, one of Russia's largest, was the target.

Governor Gleb Nikitin reported significant damage to an unnamed plant and no casualties, and Russia opened a terrorism case.

Russian authorities have not confirmed the damaged plant is the refinery, which makes fuel and lubricants.

Brent stood at $113.40 on 30 October while US President Donald Trump pressed Kyiv to stop strikes blamed for diesel prices.
