London — Standard tests miss resistant cells hiding inside bloodstream infections.

Rare resistant subpopulations — as sparse as 1 in a million — survive drug exposure and multiply into lethal treatment failure.

The method returns results in 12-30 hours; no clinical adoption timeline has been set.
