Conservatives demanded a permanent ban on Trafalgar Square's Eid prayer. Shadow justice secretary Nick Timothy branded the 6-year gathering "an act of domination" at the Conservative election launch. Conservative leader Kemi Badenoch has not endorsed or rejected the ban, leaving the gathering's future uncertain.
