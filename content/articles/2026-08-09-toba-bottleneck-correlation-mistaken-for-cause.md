Mainz — Toba cooled the planet half a degree, not a catastrophe.

The finding undercuts a genetic theory blaming Toba for a near-extinction human bottleneck.

Annual mud layers from Kenya's Lake Chala, read like tree rings, show cooling lasted 18 months, geoscientist Jinheum Park's team found.

The bottleneck's cause stays unresolved; researchers now want ash records from more sites.
