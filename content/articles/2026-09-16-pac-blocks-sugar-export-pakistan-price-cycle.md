Islamabad — Lawmakers blocked a 200,000-tonne sugar export.

The reversal could spare consumers from paying twice through export, then costlier import.

[Pakistan](country:PK) has repeatedly let mills export surplus sugar, watched domestic prices rise, then imported sugar back at a premium.

The Public Accounts Committee will review the ministry's case before deciding whether to release the export.
