Israeli strikes killed 12 medical workers in southern Lebanon. Medical workers accounted for more than half of Saturday's 23 Lebanese deaths, leaving over 1 million displaced people without primary care. Lebanon's health ministry warned the toll could rise, with no ceasefire announced for the south.
