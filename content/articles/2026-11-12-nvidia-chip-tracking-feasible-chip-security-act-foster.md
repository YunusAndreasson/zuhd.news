United States — Nvidia privately told a lawmaker it can track its chips.

Its trade group, the Semiconductor Industry Association, opposed the tracking mandate in March as untested.

The Chip Security Act would verify locations through inspections, attestations or ping signals, to stop smuggling to [China](country:CN).

Nvidia's representatives answered "yeah, of course we can do this," said Representative Bill Foster, an Illinois Democrat.

Foster plans a bill next year requiring licences to operate advanced chips, verified before connection.
