Pasadena — A command to Voyager 1 now takes 24 hours to arrive.

No other human-made object has travelled so far, about 26 billion kilometres.

Its plutonium generators lose about 4 watts a year, so engineers have switched off its cameras and all but 2 of 10 instruments.

A Jet Propulsion Laboratory spokesperson says power could last 3 to 4 more years, through next year's 50th launch anniversary.
