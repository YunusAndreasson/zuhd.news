Abuja — Councillors who deliver wards to Tinubu get N10 million.

Nyesom Wike, the Federal Capital Territory minister, announced the offer Monday at a town hall in Kwali.

Wike says it is a reward, and defines vote-buying as paying someone to vote for a specific candidate.

[Nigeria](country:NG) votes in 2027, and the payment hinges on councillors delivering their wards to President Bola Tinubu.
