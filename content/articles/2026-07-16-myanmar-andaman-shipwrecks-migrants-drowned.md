Naypyidaw — 500 feared dead off Myanmar's coast, the UN says.

The toll adds to 300 already missing or drowned in the Andaman Sea and Bay of Bengal this year, as Rohingya flee persecution on unseaworthy smuggler boats with no legal route out.

No state has opened a rescue or accountability inquiry into the latest wrecks.
