Washington — The Senate killed an Iran war-powers curb 49-47.

The House had passed it 214-208 hours earlier, continuing a months-long pattern of symbolic votes that have never stopped a single night of strikes.

[Iran](country:IR) keeps striking US bases as nightly US airstrikes continue, and Congress has not said whether it will try again.
