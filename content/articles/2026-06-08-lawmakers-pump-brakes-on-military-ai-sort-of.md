Washington — War AI bills include a defense-secretary waiver clause.

Both bills require human oversight for lethal AI decisions but let the defense secretary waive restrictions for "extraordinary circumstances."

Arms control experts warned the waivers could render both bills toothless; neither has cleared committee.
