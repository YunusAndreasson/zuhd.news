11 million Cubans lack reliable electricity. A Mexican aid convoy carrying fuel and food departed Yucatan on Saturday — the first external relief mission since the US embargo blocked Venezuelan oil shipments last year. Cuba must authorize the flotilla to dock, or the convoy returns to Mexico with no other supply behind it.
