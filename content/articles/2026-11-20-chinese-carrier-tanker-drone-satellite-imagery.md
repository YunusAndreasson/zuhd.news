Pucheng — Satellite imagery shows an MQ-25-shaped drone in [China](country:CN).

The MQ-25 refuels [US](country:US) Navy carrier jets, extending their range.

Vantor imagery puts the wingspan at 66 feet and length at 49, against the MQ-25's 75 and 51.

Its function is unknown, and its tapered nose and dorsal intake differ from the MQ-25's.

Its role stays unconfirmed, and the test site also holds a CH-7 stealth drone and a Jiutian mothership.
