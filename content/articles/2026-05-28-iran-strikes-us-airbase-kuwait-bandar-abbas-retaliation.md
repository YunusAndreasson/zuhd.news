[Iran](country:IR)'s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps said it targeted the airbase that launched the Bandar Abbas strike; [Kuwait](country:KW) said its defenses intercepted missiles and drones.

The Guard Corps warned any further US strike will draw "a more decisive response."
