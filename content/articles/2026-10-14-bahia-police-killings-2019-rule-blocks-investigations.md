Salvador — Police killed 1,570 people in Bahia last year.

The Brazilian Public Security Yearbook ranks Bahia first in [Brazil](country:BR), nearly double São Paulo's 835.

A 2019 rule let detectives probe police killings only on prosecutors' or security chiefs' orders, and deaths rose 47% by 2020.

Criminal factions have also expanded in Bahia and are often cited to justify aggressive operations in communities.

Officers from the 37th Military Police Company shot Curuzu leader Ivonei de Assis da Cruz, 42, on 1 August 2025.
