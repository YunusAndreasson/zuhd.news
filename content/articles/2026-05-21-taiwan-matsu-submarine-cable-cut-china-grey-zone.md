Matsu — [Taiwan](country:TW)'s outer-island cable cut twice, weeks apart.

The Matsu line is Taiwan's outer-island redundancy link; 2 cuts in weeks — attributed to China's military-linked vessels — leave the territory dependent on mobile signal during repairs.

Taiwan's Coast Guard is now logging vessel evidence to build a formal grey-zone attribution case.
