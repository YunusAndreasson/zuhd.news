New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s food regulator failed a Nestlé dairy whitener sample.

The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India barred the product from sale immediately.

One sample, taken from a Delhi hotel three months ago, failed a laboratory test, and the regulator named no parameter.

Nestlé India says the laboratory counted milk's natural phosphorus against the limit for added phosphorus.

Nestlé says it is engaging the authorities through due process while the appeal period runs.
