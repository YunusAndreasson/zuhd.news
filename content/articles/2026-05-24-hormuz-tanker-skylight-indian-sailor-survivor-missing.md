Bandar Abbas — Sunil Puniya, 26, survived; his crewmate is missing.

The Skylight, a [US](country:US)-sanctioned tanker, was the first commercial vessel struck in the war; Puniya was on his first posting at sea.

Puniya's friend is still missing 12 weeks after the March 1 strike; no recovery has been reported.
