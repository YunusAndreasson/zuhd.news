Tallahassee — Florida wildlife police ran ICE searches. The agency holds no Flock contract but accesses its national camera network through data-sharing agreements — lookups Flock publicly denies providing. A 2025 investigation let agencies opt out; records show most still share data with Florida's wildlife police.
