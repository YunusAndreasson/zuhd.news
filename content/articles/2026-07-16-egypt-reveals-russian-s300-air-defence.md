Cairo — A decade-old secret purchase went public.

[Egypt](country:EG) confirmed the Russian S-300VM system, part of a 2014 arms deal reportedly worth over $1 billion, sits in its air-defense network.

Israeli officials say they remain unsure why Cairo needed a system that can strike aircraft 200 kilometers out.
