Washington — Americans importing medicines keep access 90 more days.

Patient groups and dozens of lawmakers warned the rule could drastically cut medicine flows from [Canada](country:CA).

The [US](country:US) rule tightens the de minimis exemption, which eased customs for shipments under $800, to curb opioids entering untracked.

The delay pushes the rule 90 days past its 22 October start.
