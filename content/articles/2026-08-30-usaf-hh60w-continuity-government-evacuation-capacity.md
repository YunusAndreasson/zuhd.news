Washington — 26 combat-rescue helicopters now guard continuity of government.

The reassignment shows how seriously officials rate losing the capital's leadership.

HH-60Ws built to rescue downed pilots will instead ferry officials under the [US](country:US) Air Force's new 5th Helicopter Squadron.

Each aircraft carries few passengers, leaving open how the full evacuation list gets moved.
