Akure — Ondo State is resettling nearly 3,000 returnees from [South Africa](country:ZA).

[Nigeria](country:NG) absorbs the cost of South Africa's failure to protect migrants on its soil.

Anti-immigrant attacks in South Africa forced the evacuations by mid-July, officials said.

Pretoria has announced no compensation or protection measures for Nigerians who remain.
