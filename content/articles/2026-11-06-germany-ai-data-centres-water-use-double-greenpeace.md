Lübbenau — German data centres may need 27 billion litres by 2030.

The Öko-Institut, for Greenpeace, estimates 13 billion litres in 2025, so demand more than doubles.

Data centres of 100 megawatts or more use 200 to 930 million litres, depending on cooling technology.

[Germany](country:DE)'s federal government plans to double capacity by 2030 and cannot give reliable water figures.
