Physicist Astrid Eichhorn says space-time is fractal, not smooth. Her framework, asymptotic safety, predicts physics stabilizes at the Planck scale into a fractal geometry — unlike string theory, which has produced no testable prediction in 50 years. Future particle colliders could test Eichhorn's model, the first empirical check any quantum gravity theory has ever faced.
