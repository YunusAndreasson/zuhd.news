Mexico City — A parasite eradicated in 2003 is back.

[Mexico](country:MX)'s health ministry counts over 500 human cases since Panama's border barrier failed in 2022; eradication needs constant releases to hold.

Researchers report the infestation rate tripled in 2026, with cases spreading north toward Texas.
