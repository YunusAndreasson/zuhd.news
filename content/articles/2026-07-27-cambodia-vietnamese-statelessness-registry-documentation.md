Phnom Penh — "We don't have anything here," one resident said.

A 1994 immigration law reclassified generations of ethnic Vietnamese as foreign residents; rights groups estimate up to 700,000 lack land titles or bank accounts.

The same registry mechanism strips Myanmar's Rohingya and Kuwait's Bidoon, with no single decree to challenge in court.
