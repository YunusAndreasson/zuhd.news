Washington — El Niño begins as Atlantic current monitoring threatened.

The [US](country:US) weather agency declared El Niño on Thursday; the event may reach "super" strength, risking record global heat in 2027.

Funding for the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation monitoring — which warns of Northern Hemisphere climate disruption — is at risk.
