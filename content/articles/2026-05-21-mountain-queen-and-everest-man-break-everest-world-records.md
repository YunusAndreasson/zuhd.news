Kathmandu — 274 climbers summited Everest in a single day.

Both of [Nepal](country:NP)'s record-holders — Kami Rita Sherpa (32nd summit) and Lhakpa Sherpa (11th, female record) — also summited that day.

Nepal issued 494 climbing permits this season at $15,000 each; [China](country:CN) closed the northern Tibetan route entirely.
