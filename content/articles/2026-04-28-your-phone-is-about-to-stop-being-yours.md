Mountain View — F-Droid called Google's new app-registration rule existential.

From September 2026, all Android apps — sideloaded or open-source — silently fail unless developers register, pay a fee and submit government ID.

A 9-step power-user override requires a mandatory 24-hour wait that Google can alter remotely.
