Clouds cause over half of all climate model disagreement. Current simulations predict anywhere from 2°C to 6°C of warming over 50 years because no supercomputer can model cloud physics directly. Researchers are developing 2 rival approaches — physics-based and AI-driven — to resolve the uncertainty.
