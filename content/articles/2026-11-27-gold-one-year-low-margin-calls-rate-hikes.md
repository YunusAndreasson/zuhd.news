United States — Gold has lost over a third since January's $5,447 peak.

Spot gold fell to its lowest since September 2025 on Wednesday, then rose 1.6% to $3,623.

Traders sold gold to meet margin calls after the Nasdaq 100 fell 10% last week, and Fed hikes loom.

[China](country:CN)'s central bank has added to its gold reserves for 24 straight months.

Polymarket traders give the Fed's 9 December meeting a 95% chance of a quarter-point hike, up 3 points in a week.
