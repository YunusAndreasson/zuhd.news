Uyo — Governor Eno scrapped his own science park.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s Akwa Ibom state allocated ₦60 million for the park in 2023-24, then dropped it from budgets without explanation.

Eno offered the public land free to a private investor; no account of the ₦60 million spent was given.
