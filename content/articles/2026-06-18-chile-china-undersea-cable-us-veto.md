Santiago — Washington blocked [Chile](country:CL)'s planned cable to Hong Kong.

Subsea cables carry the physical internet; a [US](country:US) State Department email to Chile's telecom minister forced the route away from [China](country:CN)-linked infrastructure.

Chile has not announced a rerouted alternative, leaving South America without a direct Asia link.
