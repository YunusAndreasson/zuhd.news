Beirut — A joke about electricity can trigger a cybercrime summons.

Vague terms like "public order" let prosecutors punish speech under laws written against hacking.

Oman, Jordan and Iraq have passed near-identical statutes letting investigators seize devices without a warrant.

Some draft laws propose life imprisonment for posts said to harm "the state's highest interests."
