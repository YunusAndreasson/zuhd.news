Geneva — Copernicus said August hit 1.65°C above preindustrial levels.

El Niño's spike will fade, but the warming trend underneath will not.

El Niño works by shutting down cold-water upwelling off South America, which reshuffles global rainfall patterns.

Copernicus expects further records as the El Niño peaks through December.
