Khartoum — Deepfakes now steer civilian behavior in active conflicts. In [Sudan](country:SD), [Gaza](country:PS), and [Ukraine](country:UA), fabricated clips have moved civilians toward or away from danger, researcher Rachel Adams argues. No deepfake-detection tools are localised for Arabic, Hausa, or Amharic — the highest-exposure languages, Adams said.
