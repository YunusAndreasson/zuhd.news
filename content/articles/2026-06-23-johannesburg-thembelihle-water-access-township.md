Johannesburg — Simphiwe Zwane has fetched water by bucket in Thembelihle for 30 years.

The 20,000-person settlement sits beside a city tank depot; trucks pass without stopping, Johannesburg Water confirmed, citing illegal connections.

No new pipes since apartheid; [South Africa](country:ZA)'s constitution guarantees the right to sufficient water.
