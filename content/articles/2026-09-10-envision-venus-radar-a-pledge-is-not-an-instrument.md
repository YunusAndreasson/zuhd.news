Paris — NASA won't deliver the promised Venus radar.

Europe must now build its own instrument, delaying the search for active volcanism.

International missions built on instrument pledges leave partners holding any funding gap.

UK and Italian teams are now designing a replacement radar for the delayed 2032 launch.
