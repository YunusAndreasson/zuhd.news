Tbilisi — A Tbilisi protester was fined 5,000 lari.

The fine came weeks later, after cameras identified her from footage.

[Georgia](country:GE)'s facial-recognition system was bought from a Moscow-based company reported to have ties to Russia's Federal Security Service.

Many Georgians have stopped protesting rather than risk a fine identical to hers.
