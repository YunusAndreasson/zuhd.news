Melbourne — Chalcopyrite gives 70% of the world's copper. Monash University researchers identified the mechanism behind its resistance — and how to exploit that chemistry for faster, cleaner extraction. Their Nature Geoscience paper could ease the copper bottleneck limiting EV batteries, grid storage, and renewables.
