Boulder — East and west hemispheres reflect identical sunlight.

NASA's 25-year satellite record found matching clouds, ocean, and reflectivity across east and west — a triple symmetry no climate model reproduces.

Both hemispheres are already darkening; if the symmetry breaks, climate projections carry new unquantified error.
