China — A robot's coir-raincoat cucumber cut now succeeds 90% of the time.

[China](country:CN)'s Robam is turning chefs' tacit knife skill into training data.

Robam trained a 22-joint hand with chef teleoperation and real-robot data, because simulation cannot model food deforming under a blade.

The company said the first version succeeded 30% of the time, and a full cut still takes 4 to 5 minutes.

Robam is adding dishes, but ingredient prep and plating still need people.
