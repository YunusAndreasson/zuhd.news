Iran began naval drills in the Strait of Hormuz. The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps exercises in the waterway that carries 20% of the world's traded oil came one day after the United States deployed a carrier group to the Persian Gulf, with Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi arriving in Geneva for a second round of indirect nuclear talks on Tuesday. United States Secretary of State Marco Rubio warned that a deal would be "difficult," leaving the Geneva round's outcome uncertain as both sides project military strength.
