Vilnius — The EU withdrew sponsorship over a panel on [US](country:US) interference.

Disinfo2026, run by the independent non-profit EU Disinfo Lab, is Europe's annual counter-disinformation gathering.

Internal State Department documents quoted by reporters show officials thanking Lithuania, which withdrew support with Canada, after US officials contacted several governments.

"We were not comfortable with the framing of some of the discussions," said EU spokesperson Christian Wigand.

Reporters name Undersecretary of State Sarah Rogers as behind the US action.
