Islamabad — India suspended a treaty with no exit clause.

The 1960 treaty gives [Pakistan](country:PK) the western rivers, irrigating roughly 80% of its farmland, and has no exit clause.

The Hague's Court of Arbitration ruled India's suspension illegal; [India](country:IN) has rejected the ruling.
