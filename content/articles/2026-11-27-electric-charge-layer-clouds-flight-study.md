Reading — Layer clouds with more electric charge held droplets 10% larger.

Droplet size sets how much sunlight a cloud reflects back to space.

A research jet flew into and out of one cloud top 16 times while balloons measured charge.

The finding rests on a single flight over southern England on 12 January 2024.

The team says charge readings add droplet information that conventional cloud measurements miss.
