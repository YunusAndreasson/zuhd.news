El Triunfo — 70,000 of 73,000 farming households reported crop losses, Oxfam found.

Drought threatens food security in 103 municipalities of [Honduras](country:HN) and 13 departments of [Guatemala](country:GT).

El Niño brought a dry spell of up to 100 days to the Dry Corridor, home to over 10 million people, the UN food agency says.

78.6% of the households that planted lost 75% to 100% of their harvest, reducing their capacity to plant next season.
