Nigeria — Atiku Abubakar says no court ever found him guilty.

The presidential candidate faces five foreign probes into money tied to him, unresolved for decades.

A 2010 US Senate committee traced over $40 million through offshore firms and accounts linked to Atiku and his former wife, Jennifer Douglas.

His spokesman calls an international arbitration tribunal's finding on a Mambilla dam payment scrutiny, not evidence he corruptly won the contract.

Nigeria's anti-corruption commission has not charged Atiku over any of the five cases as his campaign continues.
