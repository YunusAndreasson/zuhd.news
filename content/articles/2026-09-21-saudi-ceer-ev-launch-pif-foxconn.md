King Abdullah Economic City — Ceer's first 2 electric cars make 1,111 horsepower.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA) has never had a domestic automaker, and its Public Investment Fund owns Ceer outright.

Ceer builds with Foxconn near Jeddah and expects 35,000 direct and indirect jobs by 2034.

The 2 flagships are aspirational, Ceer admits, and mainstream models must compete with Chinese brands already sold in Saudi Arabia.

Ceer plans 7 models by 2030, sold only in the Gulf region unless demand justifies expansion.
