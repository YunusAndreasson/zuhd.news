Moscow — Cash registers across [Russia](country:RU) froze for a week.

A cyberattack on Astral, an e-invoicing and document-management provider, cut corporate email, digital certificates, and regulated-goods sales.

Astral has not named the attacker; businesses and government agencies dependent on the platform were disrupted for 7 days.
