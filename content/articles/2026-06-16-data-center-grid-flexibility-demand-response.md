Washington — Curtailable data centers get grid access years sooner.

New power takes 8 years to reach the [US](country:US) PJM Interconnection grid; curtailable centers connect 3-5 years faster, the Princeton study found.

Grid operators stalled $150bn in US projects last year; more than 12 states are considering bans on new data centers, the study found.
