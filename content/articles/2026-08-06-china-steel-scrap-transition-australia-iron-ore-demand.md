Canberra — [China](country:CN)'s steel stock is now old enough to recycle.

Electric-arc furnaces running on domestic scrap need no imported iron ore, unlike the blast furnaces Australia's mining exports assumed would keep buying.

Pilbara ore producers and the federal budget that leans on their royalties now face a transition that may route around them.
