Vienna — [North Korea](country:KP) finished a new Yongbyon enrichment wing in October.

Pyongyang adds capacity as the [US](country:US) courts Kim Jong Un for a summit.

The International Atomic Energy Agency has had no access since 2009 and tracks Yongbyon by satellite imagery.

Secretary of State Marco Rubio said no Trump-Kim meeting is scheduled, though Trump is prepared to open dialogue.

Kim says he will return to talks only if Washington drops its hostility and recognises his nuclear status.
