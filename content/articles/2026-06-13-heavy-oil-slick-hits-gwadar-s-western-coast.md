Gwadar — Crude oil coated 20km of Gwadar's coast.

[Pakistan](country:PK) officials said westerly winds likely carried oil from a Strait of Hormuz tanker incident to the Makran coast, threatening fishing communities.

Cleanup is underway; the source vessel is unconfirmed and authorities have opened an investigation.
