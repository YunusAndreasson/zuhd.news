Baghdad — Hormuz traffic sits 41% below normal, 4 October data show.

[Iraq](country:IQ) has asked [Syria](country:SY) to truck crude to the Mediterranean, bypassing [Iran](country:IR)-restricted Hormuz.

Kpler data put pre-war Hormuz flows near 20 million barrels a day, falling to about 6 million in March.

Pipelines through [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) and the [United Arab Emirates](country:AE) and ship-to-ship transfers have lifted flows since, without restoring normal.

Ghalibaf, Iran's parliament speaker, said 29 September that if Iran cannot sell oil, neighbours cannot sell theirs.
