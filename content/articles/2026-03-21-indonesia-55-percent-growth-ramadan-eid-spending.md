Indonesia paid $3.1 billion in Eid allowances. State employees received the payments; the government also cut transport fares 30% and subsidized meals for 35 million families to support a 5.5% Q1 growth target. Ending a 50% electricity discount this year will push household costs higher in Q2, testing whether the growth target holds.
