United States — Researchers listed 6,800 shared Meta Muse links with one query.

Links they opened held a loan application, a CEO-marked revenue review and a bank fraud alert, security firm Claroc found.

Muse puts the conversation title in the share address, and passive domain-name records log that address once a recipient opens the link.

Claroc says Meta never replied to its report, and it concluded the behavior is by design.

Meta did not immediately answer emailed questions about the links.
