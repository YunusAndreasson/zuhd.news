Montreal — A Canadian company will build antennas for 66 EU satellites.

[Canada](country:CA) and the EU announced exploratory talks on Canadian participation in IRIS2.

MDA Space valued its initial Thales Alenia Space agreement above C$100 million (€64 million), though no definitive contract exists yet.

Launches of IRIS2's first 66-satellite layer are expected to begin in 2029.
