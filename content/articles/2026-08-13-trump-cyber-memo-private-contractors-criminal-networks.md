Effects can spread past a criminal target's own servers into everyday infrastructure nearby.

The memo lets vetted companies run "cyber effects operations" under nominal federal "direction, control, and authority," each bonded at $1 million.

The memo itself is not public, and no legal line yet fixes contractor liability abroad.
