Seoul — High Bandwidth Memory now makes up 63% of AI chip costs.

Its share rose from 52% to 63% in 2 years, placing [South Korea](country:KR)'s memory makers at the AI supply chokepoint, Epoch AI found.

Epoch AI concluded that memory export controls carry more leverage over AI capacity than controls targeting chip fabrication.
