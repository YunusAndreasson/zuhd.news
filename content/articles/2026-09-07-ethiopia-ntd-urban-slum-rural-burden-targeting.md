Harar — Rural children carry double the slum burden.

That gap tests whether school-based drug programmes reach rural children at all.

29.8% of rural children carried a neglected tropical disease versus 15% in slums, a study of 1,048 children found.

The study surveyed one site in eastern [Ethiopia](country:ET) and awaits peer-reviewed publication.
