Kwara — A bomb hit the Wawa-Kaiama highway Monday. Woro, the nearest village on that road, lost more than 70 people to a Boko Haram attack years earlier and is the only route linking surrounding communities to state services. The village head, displaced since that earlier attack, confirmed the explosion — if the road closes, surrounding communities lose their only link to state services.
