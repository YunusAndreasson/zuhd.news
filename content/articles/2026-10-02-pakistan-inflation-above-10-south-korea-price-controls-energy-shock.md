Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s inflation stayed at 10.26% in September, bureau data show.

The Middle East conflict kept energy costs high and disrupted Gulf supplies.

[South Korea](country:KR) held inflation to 2.9%, its finance ministry says, by capping domestic petroleum prices.

Even in Seoul, petroleum products rose 11.8% and diesel 20.0% from a year earlier.

Pakistan's central bank faces a complicated outlook while Gulf supplies stay disrupted.
