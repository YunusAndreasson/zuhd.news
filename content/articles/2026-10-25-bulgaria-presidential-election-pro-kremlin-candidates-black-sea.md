Sofia — [Bulgaria](country:BG)'s frontrunner opposes military aid to [Ukraine](country:UA).

Four ships were struck off its coast this month, leaving 21 people missing.

The presidency is ceremonial, but the president commands the armed forces, can veto laws and appoints ambassadors.

Prime Minister Rumen Radev says the attacks benefit [Russia](country:RU), but his government cites no evidence linking Moscow.

Polls give Iliana Iotova 41% and Andrey Gyurov 28%, with a runoff likely on 1 November.
