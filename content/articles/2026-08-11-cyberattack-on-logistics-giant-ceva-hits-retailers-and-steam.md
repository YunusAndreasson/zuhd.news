Amsterdam — A hack froze eight CEVA warehouses across Europe.

Steam hardware buyers and Dutch retailers turn out to be CEVA customers too.

Retailers outsourced fulfilment to a few firms, so ransomware in one warehouse system can freeze goods nobody can reroute.

CEVA has not confirmed whether the attack was ransomware or how long recovery will take.
