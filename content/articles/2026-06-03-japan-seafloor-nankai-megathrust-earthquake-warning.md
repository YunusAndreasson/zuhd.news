Tokyo — New seafloor data may sharpen megathrust warnings for southern [Japan](country:JP).

The Philippine Sea Plate locks against the Japanese mainland at the Nankai Trough; measuring imperceptible seafloor creep reveals how stress accumulates before rupture.

A catastrophic megathrust at the site is expected within decades, researchers say.
