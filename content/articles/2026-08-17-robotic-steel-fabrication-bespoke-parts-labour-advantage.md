Cincinnati — Three ex-SpaceX engineers now automate steel welding.

If bespoke steel parts get robot-cheap, cheap labour stops being a manufacturing advantage anywhere.

Software translates each custom structural drawing into machine instructions, the step that has kept fabrication manual for decades.

The startup, 1872, targets 2027 for a mostly autonomous prototype factory.
