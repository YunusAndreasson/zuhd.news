3I/ATLAS water contains 30 times more deuterium than Earth's oceans. Only the third confirmed interstellar visitor to our solar system, 3I/ATLAS formed under conditions that produce water chemistry unlike anything found in it. Astronomers have weeks to take measurements before 3I/ATLAS exits the inner solar system.
