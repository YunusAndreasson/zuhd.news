Seoul — People Power Party held Seoul's mayoralty for an unprecedented 5th term despite a national opposition surge.

Oh Se-hoon, the incumbent, won with conservative support intact; the mayoralty has historically been a launchpad for presidential bids.

His win cements the party's grip on [South Korea](country:KR)'s capital ahead of the next national election.
