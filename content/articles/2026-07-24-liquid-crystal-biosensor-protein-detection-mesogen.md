London — A protein binding to a molecule flips a crystal's color.

Researchers built biotin-tagged "fork-like" molecules that reorganize a liquid-crystal interface on contact with a target protein, giving a visible readout with no electronics.

Spacer length between the molecules tunes sensitivity, the study reports — a dial for cheap, power-free diagnostics.
