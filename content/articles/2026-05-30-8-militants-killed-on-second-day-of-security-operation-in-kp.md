Darra Adamkhel — 13 militants killed in 2 days; operation ongoing.

2 killed Saturday were Afghan nationals in the Pakistani Taliban-linked Tariq Gidar group; [Pakistan](country:PK) has pressed Kabul on cross-border sanctuaries since 2021.

Security forces said the operation will continue until no militants remain in the cordoned area.
