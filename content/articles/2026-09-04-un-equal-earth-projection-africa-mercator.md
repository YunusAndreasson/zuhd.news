Lomé — On Mercator, Africa looks the size of Greenland.

Africa is actually 14 times larger than it appears, distorting perception in classrooms worldwide.

Mercator preserves compass bearing for 16th-century sailors, while Equal Earth preserves each country's true relative area.

[Togo](country:TG), sponsor of the nonbinding resolution, plans new textbooks by year's end.
