Yida — Fighting in Heiban has displaced thousands since 12 March.

The Sudanese Council of Churches and the Islamic Council of the Nuba Mountains issued one joint statement.

They met 21-23 September at a refugee camp in [South Sudan](country:SS) and demanded an end to hostilities and hate speech.

The statement names no armed group and sets no deadline.
