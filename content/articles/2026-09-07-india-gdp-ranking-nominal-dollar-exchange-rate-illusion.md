New Delhi — 7.8% growth, yet [India](country:IN) fell to 6th-largest economy.

Rankings that move on currency, not output, mislead households reading them as economic news.

Dollar GDP multiplies real growth by inflation by the exchange rate, so a weaker rupee alone can shrink the ranking.

The finance ministry cites IMF data putting nominal GDP near $3.92 trillion this fiscal year.
