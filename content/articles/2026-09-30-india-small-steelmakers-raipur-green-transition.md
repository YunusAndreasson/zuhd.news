Raipur — Small mills make 40% of [India](country:IN)'s steel, heavily on coal.

Urla and Siltara's clusters support tens of thousands of workers on tight margins.

Coal-based sponge iron emits 2.7-3.1 tonnes of CO2 per tonne of steel, the Council on Energy, Environment and Water estimates.

Saurabh Agrawal, president of Raipur steelmaker Real Ispat & Power, calls the way forward "a bit confusing."

Government and overseas markets will require smaller producers to invest in cleaner technology, efficiency gains or new fuels.
