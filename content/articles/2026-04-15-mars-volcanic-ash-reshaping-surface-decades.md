Paris — Dark ash is visibly reshaping Mars in decades. Geological change on Mars typically takes millions of years; Mars Express shows volcanic ash migrating across the surface within a human lifetime. The discovery shifts baseline assumptions for landing site selection on future crewed and robotic missions.
