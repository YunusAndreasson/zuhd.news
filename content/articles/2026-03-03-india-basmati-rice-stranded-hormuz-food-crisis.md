400,000 tons of Indian basmati rice are stranded. Insurers cancelled coverage for vessels transiting Hormuz; Gulf states import 85% of their calories — and India supplies most of their basmati. Basmati prices fell 6% despite India's record harvest; traders may invoke force majeure as storage costs mount.
