Zaporizhzhia — Zaporizhzhia's nuclear power supply came under attack.

[Ukraine](country:UA)'s occupied nuclear plant depends on one power line; each blackout forces diesel-generator cooling of 6 reactors, the International Atomic Energy Agency warned.

International Atomic Energy Agency Director General Rafael Grossi called for an end to attacks on the site.
