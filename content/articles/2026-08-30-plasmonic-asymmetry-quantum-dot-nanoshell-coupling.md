London — Off-centre shells change a nanoparticle's light response, not just its strength.

The effect could sharpen sensors, cancer therapy and solar-cell light harvesting.

Breaking shell symmetry lets electron-oscillation modes couple that centred designs forbid.

The result is computational, modelling a hybrid system, not a built device.
