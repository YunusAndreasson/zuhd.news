Iran cited international law to justify striking US tech offices in the Gulf. Iran's state Tasnim news agency framed the offices as dual-use military infrastructure — the legal basis under international law for striking civilian facilities. Striking those offices would cut cloud, payment, and content services for millions who rely on US-hosted infrastructure across the Gulf.
