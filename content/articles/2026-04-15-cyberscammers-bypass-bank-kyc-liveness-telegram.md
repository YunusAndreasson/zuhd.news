Singapore — A static photo defeated bank biometric verification. MIT Technology Review identified 22 Telegram channels selling kits that replace live camera feeds with static images to open money-mule accounts. Telegram removed the flagged channels; dozens of replacements persist, and no targeted bank has disclosed countermeasures.
