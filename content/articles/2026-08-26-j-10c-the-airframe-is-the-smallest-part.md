Dhaka — A government panel is drafting terms for up to 24 J-10CE jets.

A fighter buy locks Dhaka into [China](country:CN)'s missiles, radar and spares for decades.

[Bangladesh](country:BD) now flies aging F-7s and MiG-29s, while Pakistan remains the only confirmed J-10CE buyer.

Negotiations, not a signed contract, are next; no price or delivery date is set.
