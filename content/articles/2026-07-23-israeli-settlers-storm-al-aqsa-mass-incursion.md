Jerusalem — Over 4,200 settlers entered Al-Aqsa in a single day.

[Israel](country:IL)'s police escorted the crowds into Islam's third-holiest site on Tisha B'Av, the Jewish day marking the Temples' destruction.

Palestinian worshippers were barred from the site that day, and police have not said whether the restrictions will continue.
