Brooklyn — Sellers are closing Etsy shops of 13 and 15 years.

Etsy's proposed terms let it and partners train AI on seller content, subject to opt-outs.

CEO Kruti Patel Goyal said Etsy does not and will not train AI to copy sellers' items.

Illustrator Aaron Meshon said, "I would say I'm losing 20 percent of my yearly income."

Meshon shuts his store on 1 December despite the revision.
