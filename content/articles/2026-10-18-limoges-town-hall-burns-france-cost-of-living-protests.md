Limoges — Fire took two-thirds of the 1883 town hall's exterior.

Interior ministry figures show 550,000 people marched across [France](country:FR) over living costs, with 400 arrests.

Witnesses said masked protesters torched a police car, broke windows and set fire inside the town hall.

Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez said most demonstrations stayed peaceful and blamed far-left groups on their fringes.

Mayor Guillaume Guerin has pledged a formal complaint, and officials are still assessing the damage.
