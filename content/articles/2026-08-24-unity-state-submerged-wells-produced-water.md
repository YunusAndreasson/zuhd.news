Rotriak — Over 50 wells leach oil into the marsh.

Families displaced by 2021's floods now drink and wash in the same contaminated water.

The floods never receded, and clinicians report rising birth defects near the abandoned, unmaintained wellheads.

[South Sudan](country:SS)'s government owes cleanup and shared revenue that residents say has never arrived.
