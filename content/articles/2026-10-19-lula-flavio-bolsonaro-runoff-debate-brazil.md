São Paulo — Two polls this week put Flávio Bolsonaro ahead 51% to 49%.

Both candidates are chasing the 33 million voters, one in five, who skipped the first round.

Bolsonaro promised 500,000 new prison places and a narco-terrorist label for gangs, while Lula defended above-inflation minimum wage rises.

"You have to work two or three jobs just to pay the bills," said Sara Machado, 36, at the São Paulo rally.

A second debate comes 23 October, two days before the run-off.
