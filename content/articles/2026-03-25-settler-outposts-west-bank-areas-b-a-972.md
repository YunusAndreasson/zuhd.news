West Bank — Israeli settlers are establishing outposts inside Palestinian-administered territory.

The push into Areas B and A — zones under Palestinian Authority civil or full jurisdiction — erases the last operational distinction in the Oslo Accords, making annexation a fact without any formal declaration.

The expansion is accelerating as US-Israel strikes on Iran absorb international attention, and the question is no longer whether Israeli sovereignty extends into Areas B and A but when it will be formalized.
