Stockholm — Spotify will stop promoting AI-tagged artists.

It is one of the first major platforms to demote AI music by policy.

Spotify will tag artists it identifies as AI-generated and exclude them from algorithmic promotion, the company said.

It's unclear how the streamer will verify a track's origin or handle disputed labels.
