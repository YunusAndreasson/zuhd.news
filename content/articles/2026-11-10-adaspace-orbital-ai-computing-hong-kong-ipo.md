Chengdu — Adaspace ran an AI traffic-analysis model aboard a satellite already in orbit.

Processing in orbit lets a satellite send back road-network analysis instead of every pixel.

Adaspace has launched 20 computing satellites, 12 in May 2025 and 8 this October, and engineers on the ground can upload new software to them.

The company's shares begin trading in Hong Kong on 13 November.
