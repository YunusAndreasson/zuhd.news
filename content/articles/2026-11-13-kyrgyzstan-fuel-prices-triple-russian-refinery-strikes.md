Bishkek — [Kyrgyzstan](country:KG)'s fuel prices nearly tripled in 6 years, a data analysis found.

Kyrgyzstan moves most freight by truck, so fuel costs feed every other price.

Attacks on refineries set off a November fuel crisis in [Russia](country:RU).

The analysis expects Kyrgyz fuel prices to keep climbing, and prices of goods hauled by road with them.
