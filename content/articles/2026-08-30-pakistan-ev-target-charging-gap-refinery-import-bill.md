Karachi — Oil marketing companies keep building pumps, not chargers.

It threatens [Pakistan](country:PK)'s plan to cut $4.5 billion in fuel imports.

Industry data show two-wheeler assembly, the cheapest vehicles to electrify, up 43% to 2.4 million units this year.

Refiners plan to sign $6 billion modernization deals next month, deepening reliance on imported crude.
