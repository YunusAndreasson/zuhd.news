Komyshuvakha — 24 people died at a school where residents collected weekly aid.

A police officer and 2 rescuers are among the dead, Governor Mykhailo Semikin said.

[Russia](country:RU) dropped nine more bombs on the shelter after the first strike, President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said.

Rescuers are still searching the rubble for 27 missing people, Semikin said.
