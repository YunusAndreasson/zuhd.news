Australia — Coles disclosed a cyber attack without saying what happened.

A small number of customers have trouble with Liquorland click-and-collect.

Coles told the stock exchange it has found no evidence customer, team or supplier data was compromised.

Rival Woolworths is investigating reports of digital gift cards drained by unauthorised transactions over the weekend.

Coles has set no date for further updates while it works with the Australian Cyber Security Centre.
