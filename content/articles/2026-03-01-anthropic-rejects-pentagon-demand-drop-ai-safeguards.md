The Pentagon declared Anthropic a national security risk Friday. US Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth ordered Anthropic to drop its bans on AI surveillance and autonomous weapons, a demand CEO Dario Amodei refused as a matter of conscience. The Pentagon is now negotiating the same terms with Google and OpenAI.
