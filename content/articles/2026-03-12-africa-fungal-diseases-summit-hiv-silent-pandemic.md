Fungal diseases cause nearly half of AIDS deaths in Africa. HIV strips the immune system, leaving Africa's 26 million infected people vulnerable to fungal infections like cryptococcal meningitis that are rarely diagnosed before death. The continent's first dedicated fungal summit closed with pledges to fill diagnostic gaps confirmed across 48 member states in 2022.
