Birmal — Gunmen killed Maulana Muhammad Ismail after Friday prayers.

He is the third JUI-F figure killed in the merged districts since January; [Pakistan](country:PK)'s 2018 tribal-areas merger promised courts and policing that never followed.

Police have opened an investigation but named no suspects; convictions in past clerical killings here are rare.
