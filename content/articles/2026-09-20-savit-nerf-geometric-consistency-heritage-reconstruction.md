London — Photographs often outlast the buildings they show.

Distorted 3D reconstructions cannot reliably guide restoration of ancient architecture.

SAViT-NeRF adds structure-aware attention and depth and normal constraints, and beat 4 rival methods on 4 public heritage datasets.

The paper is an accepted manuscript awaiting its final version.
