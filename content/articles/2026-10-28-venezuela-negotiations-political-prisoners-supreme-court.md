Caracas — Talks agreed a committee to nominate new Supreme Tribunal justices.

Both sides also reported movement on freeing political prisoners and unblocking some media outlets.

Washington oversees the talks: [US](country:US) forces captured Nicolás Maduro on 3 January, and Trump's administration backs interim leader Delcy Rodríguez.

Political scientist Jesús Castellanos calls it regime change, not democratic transition, and says Rodríguez's government remains authoritarian.

Opposition leader María Corina Machado returned from 10 months' exile on Sunday.
