Brasília — Washington is taxing a rail stablecoins already ride.

The [US](country:US) will impose a 25% tariff on [Brazil](country:BR) July 22, targeting Pix, the free instant-payment system used by 90% of adults.

Dollar stablecoins already circulate widely in Brazil's digital economy, a dependency the tariff does not touch.
