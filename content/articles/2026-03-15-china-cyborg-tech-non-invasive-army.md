China built a 4-legged human-robot hybrid for combat. Robotic legs and a torso attach via elastic harness, cutting metabolic cost by 35% and foot pressure by 52% under a 20 kg load. Southern University of Science and Technology says the centaur's autonomous navigation is ready for mountain and urban combat, where China's People's Liberation Army already fields basic exoskeletons.
