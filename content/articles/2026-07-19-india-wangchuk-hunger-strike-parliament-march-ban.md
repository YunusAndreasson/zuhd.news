New Delhi — Police banned Monday's Cockroach movement march on Parliament.

Activist Sonam Wangchuk, hospitalized by force after 3 weeks fasting over exam-paper leaks, will end his strike only if leaders pledge action in Parliament.

A Delhi court declined to free him Sunday; another hearing on his detention is set for Friday.
