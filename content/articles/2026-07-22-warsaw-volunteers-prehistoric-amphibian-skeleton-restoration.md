Warsaw — 2,000 volunteers are chiseling rock off ancient bone.

Fossil preparation, not discovery, is paleontology's real bottleneck; the public effort at Copernicus Science Centre frees a massive Triassic amphibian predator's skeleton.

The specimen will help researchers reconstruct the ecosystem that existed before dinosaurs came to dominate it.
