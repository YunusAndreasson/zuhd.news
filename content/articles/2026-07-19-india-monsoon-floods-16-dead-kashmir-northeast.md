Rajouri — Landslides and floods have killed 16 across northern and eastern [India](country:IN).

Poonch and Rajouri districts took 11 deaths as a Surankote landslide buried one house. Nagaland and Uttarakhand also reported deaths separately.

The Meteorological Department forecasts widespread rain through July 23, with several people still missing.
