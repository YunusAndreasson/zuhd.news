Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s government says it will limit the extra fuel charge to Rs0.83 a unit.

Households would avoid the Rs2.88 a unit the power sector sought for December billing.

The Power Division proposed a quarterly forward-looking adjustment to limit winter fuel-cost swings, when thermal fuels can exceed 80% of generation.

Regulator official Amina Ahmed welcomed the concept but said a technical review would follow government guidance.

The government files its policy directive with the regulator by Friday morning.
