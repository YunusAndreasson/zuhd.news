Tehran — [Iran](country:IR)'s Revolutionary Guards say 2 tankers hit mines in Hormuz.

Iranian state media reported it, and the report includes no confirmation from ship owners.

Iran has restricted passage through the strait and attacked ships it says violate those rules, amid its confrontation with the [US](country:US).

Brent stood at $113.4 on 30 October while Hormuz traffic ran 19% below normal on 25 October.
