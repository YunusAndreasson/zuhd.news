Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) blacklisted 26 tugs, some registered in the [UAE](country:AE), [India](country:IN) and Singapore.

Tugs tow and manoeuvre larger ships, so the list targets the support tanker traffic relies on.

Iran's Persian Gulf Strait Authority says the tugs served ships using the alternative southern route, and it will list any firm cooperating with offenders.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 31 December a 13% chance.
