New Delhi — 50 brands will run the first ChatGPT ads.

[India](country:IN) is ChatGPT's largest user base and its lowest revenue per user.

Ads run in the Free and Go tiers after a terms update let conversations inform ad targeting.

OpenAI's ad manager launches next month, requiring a ₹725 daily minimum campaign budget.
