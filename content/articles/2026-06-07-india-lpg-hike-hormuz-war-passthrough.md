New Delhi — 90% of [India](country:IN)'s gas imports transited Hormuz.

The Petroleum Ministry hiked household liquefied petroleum gas 29 rupees to ₹942; the Saudi benchmark rose 46% as Hormuz closed.

Refiners are scrambling for alternative sources; the ministry said daily consumption has fallen 20,000 tonnes from pre-war levels.
