Ankara — Erdogan wants the UN Security Council veto abolished outright.

The five permanent members can each block resolutions the world's majority supports.

Erdogan proposed a Security Council without permanent seats, with members elected by the General Assembly for fixed terms they must renew.

[Turkey](country:TR)'s proposal remains a roadmap, not a resolution, with no vote scheduled at this week's General Assembly session.
