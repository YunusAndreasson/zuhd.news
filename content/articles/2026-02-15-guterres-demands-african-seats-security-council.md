1.4 billion Africans lack a permanent Security Council seat. United Nations Secretary-General António Guterres told the 38th African Union Summit in Addis Ababa that the body authorizing military interventions on the continent has no permanent African member. He outlined 3 priorities: ending hostilities in Sudan, fair distribution of critical mineral wealth, and tripling climate adaptation finance. Guterres called the $4 trillion annual financing gap for developing countries "unconscionable" as Angola handed the AU chairmanship to Burundi at a summit dominated by Sahel security threats and strained ties with Washington.
