Tokyo — New Japanese shipyard capacity is already booked.

That leaves little room for [US](country:US) Navy ships needing forward repair.

Naval repair capacity, not hull count, decides how fast a damaged fleet returns to sea.

[Japan](country:JP) and the US have not said how the capacity crunch will be resolved.
