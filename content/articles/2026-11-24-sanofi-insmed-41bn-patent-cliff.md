Paris — Sanofi offers $41bn, 46% above Insmed's pre-leak price.

Chief executive Belén Garijo has dropped 20 older medicines since her predecessor was ousted for failing to deliver new treatments.

Insmed brings 2 marketed drugs: Brinsupri, with $1.09bn revenue through September, and Arikayce, with $433.8m in 2025.

Sanofi's Dupixent loses patent protection in the early 2030s, the deadline Insmed's sales must help beat.
