London — Age verifier Yoti auto-reports GrapheneOS users to authorities.

Yoti, a [UK](country:GB) age-verification company, treats the OS fingerprint as evasion under proliferating age-verification laws.

GrapheneOS has not responded; Yoti has not explained the legal authority for automatic police referrals.
