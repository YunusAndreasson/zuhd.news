Washington — Perpetual crypto futures reach [US](country:US) retail investors legally.

Previously offshore-only, perpetuals — crypto derivatives with 50:1 leverage — now fall under Commodity Futures Trading Commission oversight.

The commission reviews each new contract individually, slowing but not blocking product expansion.
