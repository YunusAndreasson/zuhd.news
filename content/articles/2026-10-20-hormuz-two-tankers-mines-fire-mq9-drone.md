Tehran — Guards say mines set 2 tankers ablaze, Iranian state news agency Mehr reported.

The Guards warn oil companies will pay a bigger price if US pressure continues.

The Guards say their mines in the southern route exploded under the vessels, and air defences shot down a US MQ-9 drone.

The statement names no tanker, flag or owner.

Polymarket traders give an Iran-Oman Hormuz agreement by 31 October an 8% chance, down 6 points in a week.
