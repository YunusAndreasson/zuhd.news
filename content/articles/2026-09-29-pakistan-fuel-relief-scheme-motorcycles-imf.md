Islamabad — 7.6 million riders have registered for Pakistan's fuel subsidy since Sept. 17.

The scheme shields low-income riders as Brent crude, at $114.90 as of Sept. 22, sat 24% above its level a month earlier.

Owners of two- and three-wheelers get 100 rupees off per litre on 20 litres a month, funded as an IMF mission reviews Pakistan's loan programme this week.

The IMF's fourth review, underway in Islamabad, typically discourages the energy subsidies Deputy PM Ishaq Dar is expanding.
