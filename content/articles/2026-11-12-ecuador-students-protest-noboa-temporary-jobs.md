Quito — Ecuador's statistics institute counts 40% of the jobless aged 15-24.

Thousands of students marched in Quito on 10 October against President Daniel Noboa's youth-employment plan.

Marchers cited medicine shortages in hospitals, education budget cuts, rising insecurity and forced disappearances by state agents.

Students keep staging marches and sit-ins across the city, demanding medicines, security and an education budget.
