Brazil — Misleading gambling-win posts on X drew nearly 500,000 views.

[Brazil](country:BR)'s Supreme Court held a public hearing Tuesday on challenges to President Lula's betting ban.

The accounts follow and amplify each other, and one promoted Greenasbet, a California-registered site absent from July's authorised list.

Justices have not ruled on the ban, which Lula decreed by provisional measure on 25 September.
