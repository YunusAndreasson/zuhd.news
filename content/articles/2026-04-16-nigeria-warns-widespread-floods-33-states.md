Abuja — 14,118 Nigerian communities face high-probability flooding this year.

Nigeria's hydrological agency predicts peak risk in July–September with severe urban flood warnings across 33 of 36 states.

Nigeria's 2022 floods killed over 600 and displaced 1.4 million; the response was relief, not flood-prevention infrastructure.
