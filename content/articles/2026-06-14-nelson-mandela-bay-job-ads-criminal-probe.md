Cape Town — [South Africa](country:ZA)'s parliament demands a criminal probe into Nelson Mandela Bay's job ads.

The Cogta committee wants an investigation into irregular posts at the metro, which has no permanent city manager — MPs demand to know who authorised them.

No investigators have been named; the metro still has no permanent city manager.
