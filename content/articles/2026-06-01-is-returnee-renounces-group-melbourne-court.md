Melbourne — El Houli told court she renounces Islamic State.

[Australia](country:AU) charged Rayann El Houli, 34, with joining the group between 2013–14; prosecution argues she fled Syria only after the caliphate fell, not from disavowal.

A risk-assessment expert will evaluate El Houli before a bail hearing; she remains in custody.
