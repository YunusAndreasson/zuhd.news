London — 400 UK firms cheated 60,000 workers of £7.3m. His Majesty's Revenue and Customs named them publicly; criminal prosecution for wage theft is reserved for more serious cases. Firms on the list face prosecution for any future violations as the minimum wage rises to £12.71 in April.
