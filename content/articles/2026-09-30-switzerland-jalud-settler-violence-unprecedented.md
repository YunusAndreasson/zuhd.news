Its foreign ministry said the "unprecedented level of settler violence cannot be tolerated" and demanded that [Israel](country:IL) prosecute those responsible.

Turkish state broadcaster TRT World reported soldiers forced the Al-Tubasi family out again, minutes after they returned under a court order.

TRT World's report names no arrests, and the family remains displaced.
