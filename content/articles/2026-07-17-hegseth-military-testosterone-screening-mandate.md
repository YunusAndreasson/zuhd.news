Washington — Every soldier over 30 must now get tested.

Testosterone naturally declines with age and has no single diagnostic threshold; the Endocrine Society says screening symptom-free men risks false positives, unneeded treatment and cardiovascular harm.

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth ordered the policy; treatment stays technically optional under the mandate.
