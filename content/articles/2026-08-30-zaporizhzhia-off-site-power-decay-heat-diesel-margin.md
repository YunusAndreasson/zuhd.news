A shut-down reactor still needs power for years to cool its fuel.

Decay heat keeps generating after shutdown, so pumps must run constantly on fuel trucked across [Ukraine](country:UA)'s front line.

The plant holds only a 10-day diesel reserve, the International Atomic Energy Agency said.
