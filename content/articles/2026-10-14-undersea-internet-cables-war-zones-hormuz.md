Iran — Between 150 and 200 undersea cables are damaged every year.

Elisabeth Braw says most damage is accidental, but governments now prosecute crews for suspected sabotage.

[Iran](country:IR) has sought fees from operators of cables through Hormuz, and the war stalled 3 cable projects there.

"We just know that these incidents were extremely suspicious," said Braw, an Atlantic Council senior fellow.

[Finland](country:FI) has attempted to prosecute 3 senior officers of the Eagle S over the 2024 Baltic cuts.
