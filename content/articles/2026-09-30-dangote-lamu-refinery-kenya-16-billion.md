Lamu — Dangote offers governments 30% of a $16 billion refinery.

Fuel price surges from the Iran war have already sparked deadly protests in [Kenya](country:KE).

The plant, due in 2030, targets regional demand of 20 million to 30 million tonnes a year, Kenya's economic adviser says.

Kenya's High Court ordered parts of the site preserved pending a residents' case, which could delay work.

Doubts remain over crude supply, since Kenya and Uganda are only starting to produce oil.
