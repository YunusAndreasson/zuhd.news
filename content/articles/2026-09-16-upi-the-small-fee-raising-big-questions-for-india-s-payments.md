New Delhi — UPI's zero-fee decade ends Oct. 15 in [India](country:IN).

Small merchants now pay a 0.4% fee that retailers warn will push transactions back to cash.

The fee applies above ₹2,000 per transaction, capped at ₹300, while person-to-person transfers and small payments stay free.

UPI processed a record 24.51 billion transactions in August, all due for the new fee starting Oct. 15.
