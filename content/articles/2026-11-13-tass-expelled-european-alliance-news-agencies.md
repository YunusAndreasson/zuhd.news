Moscow — Members voted in secret to expel TASS, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

The vote ends a suspension that began in February 2022.

Expulsion failed at earlier assemblies, so the alliance suspended TASS without formally removing it, the agency said.

In 2022 the alliance said restrictions by [Russia](country:RU)'s regulator Roskomnadzor left TASS unable to provide unbiased news.

TASS cited Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov's claim that Western elites restrict media to deprive voters of information.
