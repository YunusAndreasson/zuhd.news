Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) blames a blockade, not strikes.

A blockade is itself an act of war once declared and enforced, and it stops neutral ships carrying fuel, food and medicine.

Iran's foreign ministry petitioned the UN Security Council; Washington has not confirmed any blockade exists.
