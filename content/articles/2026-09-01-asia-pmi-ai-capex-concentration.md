Beijing — [China](country:CN)'s factory gauge rose to 51.5 in August.

The Purchasing Managers' Index blends a booming AI segment against flat manufacturing.

[Japan](country:JP) hit 54.9 and [South Korea](country:KR)'s exports rose 68.7%, both driven by chips and AI hardware demand.

A pullback in hyperscaler AI spending would hit these economies as hard as it lifted them.
