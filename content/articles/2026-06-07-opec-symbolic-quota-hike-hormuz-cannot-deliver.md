Vienna — OPEC+ raised July quotas it cannot deliver.

The Strait of Hormuz has been closed since February. Group output fell from 42.77 to 33.19 million barrels daily, OPEC figures show.

When Hormuz reopens, Rystad analyst Jorge Leon warns, the market could shift from shortage fear to surplus fear.
