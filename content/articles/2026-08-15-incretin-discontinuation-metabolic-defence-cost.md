London — Two-thirds of lost weight returns after stopping.

The drugs work only as long as patients keep taking them.

Fat loss cuts leptin and raises ghrelin, so appetite rebounds once the drug's outside signal stops overriding it.

The review names no maintenance protocol, and no generic pathway yet exists.
