London — Antibodies attacking the brain cause psychosis. Anti-NMDAR antibodies bind to brain receptors producing hallucinations and delusions identical to schizophrenia, reclassifying some mental illness as treatable autoimmune disease. Immunotherapy targeting the immune system could now reach patients that antipsychotics have failed for decades.
