Shenzhen — Tencent Music's third-quarter revenue grew 5.6% to 8.94bn yuan.

Without the Ximalaya acquisition, core growth falls to low single digits or negative, one analysis estimates.

Ximalaya supplied 407 million yuan of second-quarter growth of 491 million, and operating margin fell from 32.0% to 30.4%.

Revenue from concerts, merchandise and digital albums reportedly grew about 50%.

Tencent Music stopped publishing quarterly user data, so outsiders cannot tell whether growth is subscribers or prices.
