Boston — Regulators moved against all 12 RegalCare homes on 13 October.

An investigation found RegalCare's chief executive cut nursing and steered funds to his businesses.

RegalCare bought homes after the pandemic and cut costs, and regulators moved right after the reporting appeared.

RegalCare keeps control of its homes for now, under enhanced monitoring by the Department of Public Health.
