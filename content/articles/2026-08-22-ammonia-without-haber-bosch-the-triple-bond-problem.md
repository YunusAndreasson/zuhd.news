London — Ammonia synthesis burns 2% of world energy.

That single reaction underpins fertilizer feeding roughly half of humanity's food supply.

Breaking nitrogen's triple bond needs 400-500°C and 150-200 atmospheres, tying plants to cheap natural gas.

Computational screening has flagged candidate catalysts, but no lab has confirmed ambient-condition ammonia yields.
