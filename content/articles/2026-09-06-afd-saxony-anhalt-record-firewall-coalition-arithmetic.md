Magdeburg — Alternative for Germany took 44.5% and still can't govern.

Every rival party has ruled out joining it, leaving [Germany](country:DE)'s Saxony-Anhalt without a government.

A "firewall" barring cooperation with a party classed as extremist forces the Christian Democratic Union into a fractured multi-party coalition.

Whether Sahra Wagenknecht's party clears the 5% threshold will decide if any coalition holds a majority.
