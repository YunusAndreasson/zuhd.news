New Delhi — Pollution's health toll tracks India's caste lines.

Indian Institute of Technology Delhi researchers found [India](country:IN)'s poorest face more PM2.5 disease because weaker baseline health amplifies identical exposure.

The researchers argue targeted clean-air interventions yield the greatest gains — India has no PM2.5 equity standard.
