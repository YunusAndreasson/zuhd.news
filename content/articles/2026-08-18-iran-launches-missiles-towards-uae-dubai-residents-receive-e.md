Dubai — Two ballistic missiles triggered the city's first alert in months.

The Gulf's re-export and logistics hub now carries the same risk as the strait beside it.

The [UAE](country:AE)'s Defence Ministry said it detected the missiles from Iran and told residents to shelter.

The alert coincided with a US-Iran memorandum of understanding expiring the same day.
