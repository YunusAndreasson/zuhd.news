São Filipe — A bus plunged into a Fogo ravine.

Most of the 25 dead were schoolchildren the driver brought yearly on this trip.

The bus, carrying 32 passengers down from the Pico do Fogo volcano in [Cabo Verde](country:CV), veered off the road.

Emergency crews kept searching the ravine overnight for those still trapped in the wreckage.
