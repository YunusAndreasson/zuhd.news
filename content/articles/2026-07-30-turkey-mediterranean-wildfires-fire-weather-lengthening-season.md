Antalya — Hundreds of firefighters battle Mediterranean blazes.

Drought-dried fuel, sustained heat and a specific wind pattern turn ground fires into crown fires in minutes; that conjunction's season is lengthening across the eastern Mediterranean.

[Turkey](country:TR)'s forestry directorate has not yet published this season's burned-area figure against the ten-year mean.
