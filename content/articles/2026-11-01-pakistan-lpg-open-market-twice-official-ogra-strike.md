Islamabad — Cooking gas sells up to Rs630 a kilo, double the official rate.

The official price of an 11.8 kg cylinder in [Pakistan](country:PK) is Rs3,470, up Rs173 for November.

The gas regulator raised the official rate by Rs14.73 to Rs294 a kilo, but the [Iran](country:IR) border closure has cut supply.

Distributors association chairman Irfan Khokhar says at least 70% of Pakistan's liquefied petroleum gas comes from Iran.

Gas marketers threaten a strike from 16 November if the new policy sidelines the private sector.
