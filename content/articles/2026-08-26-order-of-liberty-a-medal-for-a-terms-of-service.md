[Ukraine](country:UA)'s battlefield internet runs on terminals one man can switch off or restrict.

Starlink's owner sets what terminals may target, by policy rather than treaty, so no parliament can compel or appeal a refusal.

Kyiv wants Starlink enabled over Russian territory for strikes; Musk has called that escalation.
