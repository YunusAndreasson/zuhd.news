Port of Spain — Jason Jones won again, 8 years after his first ruling.

London's Privy Council struck down [Trinidad and Tobago](country:TT)'s ban on consensual anal sex between adults.

The case tested the savings clause that keeps pre-independence laws in force after independence.

Other Caribbean territories also use it as final court, and Jones counts 37 Commonwealth states with similar laws.
