The NSA has a secret program Senator Ron Wyden can't discuss. Section 702 — the law authorising warrantless surveillance of foreign targets that routinely captures American communications — was renewed in 2024 with powers allowing the government to conscript anyone with infrastructure access, including cable installers and IT workers, to secretly spy on its behalf. Wyden's last such warning, in 2011, preceded Edward Snowden's revelations of bulk phone metadata collection three years later.
