China — 7 ministries drafted a 54-article gig-worker rulebook.

It is [China](country:CN)'s first departmental rule covering workers managed by platforms without formal employment.

Article 29 requires a human to review account bans, order cutoffs and large compensation demands, not an algorithm alone.

Public comment closes 8 November, and platforms would owe workers 7 days' notice of rule changes.
