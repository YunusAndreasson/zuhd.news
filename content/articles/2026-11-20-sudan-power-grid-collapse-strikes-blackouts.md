Khartoum — The UN counts 29 drone strikes on Sudan's power sites.

Outages cut dialysis, surgery and water far from [Sudan](country:SD)'s front lines, the UN says.

Between 20 October and 4 November they hit electricity and fuel sites in 11 states, attributed to the Rapid Support Forces.

Shaker Ismail, an engineer at the Sudanese Electricity Company, says repairing major stations could take months or longer.
