Reading — The Niño3.4 zone hit an all-time 29.70C in September.

The EU's Copernicus service says that beats the 1982-83, 1997-98 and 2015-16 peaks.

El Niño warms the central and eastern equatorial Pacific, shifting winds, pressure and rainfall worldwide, and recurs every 2 to 7 years.

Copernicus says a new index adjusting for ocean warming does not yet show this El Niño as the strongest.

[Philippines](country:PH) forecasters expect a peak at end-2026 and up to 31 dry days a month in southern Mindanao.
