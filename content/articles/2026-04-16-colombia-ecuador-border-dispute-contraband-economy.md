Bogotá — Ecuador's 100% tariff drove trade into contraband routes. Armed groups controlling illegal crossings absorbed the redirected volume; Colombia deployed troops but lacks joint security coordination with Ecuador. The tariff takes effect May 1; without cross-border cooperation, criminal control of the corridor consolidates.
