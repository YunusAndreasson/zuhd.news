Paris — [France](country:FR)'s maize harvest hits a four-decade low.

The shortfall reaches import-dependent Egypt and the Sahel within months, through shared wholesale prices.

Heat, drought and wildfire caused roughly €2bn in cereal losses after June's heatwave, Carbon Brief found.

Most EU countries expect further cereal declines this year, Carbon Brief's data shows.
