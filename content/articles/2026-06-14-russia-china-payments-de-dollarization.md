Moscow — [Russia](country:RU) says its [China](country:CN) payment impasse is resolved.

Presidential envoy Boris Titov said Russian state bank clearing platforms now handle bilateral settlement without dollar intermediaries.

Titov said the two sides are exploring deeper financial integration, without defining scope or timeline.
