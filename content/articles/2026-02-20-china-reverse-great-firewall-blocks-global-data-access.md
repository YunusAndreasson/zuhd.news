Over half of China's government websites are inaccessible abroad. A Leiden University study tested 13,000 sites and found 1 in 10 deliberately geo-blocked foreign users via server-side filtering. Beijing frames the restrictions as cybersecurity, but researchers warn they fragment global access to Chinese economic data.
