New Delhi — [India](country:IN) blocked Telegram for 100 million users ahead of a medical re-examination.

Rackets exploited Telegram's edit feature to backdate fake exam papers into existing posts, the National Testing Agency said.

The access block lifts June 22; a separate order requires Telegram to disable message editing until June 30.
