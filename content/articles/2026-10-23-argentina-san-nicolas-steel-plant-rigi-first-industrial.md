San Nicolás de los Arroyos — A $300 million steelworks will make 360,000 tonnes from scrap.

It is the first factory, not a mine, in [Argentina](country:AR)'s big-investment incentive regime.

Electric furnaces melting scrap cut emissions about 70% against coal blast furnaces, but only if the grid powering them is clean.

The national grid, dominated by gas-fired plants, has been saturated for over a decade, energy engineer Adrién Sergenty says.

Sidersa starts production in late 2027 and has only said it is considering direct renewable power.
