Gqeberha — Municipal bulldozers demolished 14 Airport Valley homes on 28 September.

Families refuse to move into relocation units with no roofs, water, power or sewerage.

A 2025 ruling bars evictions until the relocation site has water and sanitation, and on 30 September the court barred more demolitions.

"They are quick to destroy our homes when we have nowhere to go," said Nomboniso Ntunduse, whose shack was demolished.

Community leader Mara Payi says the judge will make the order permanent if officials again skip court.
