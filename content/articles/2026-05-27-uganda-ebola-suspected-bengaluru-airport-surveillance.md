Bengaluru — [India](country:IN) isolated a Uganda traveler on Ebola suspicion.

She flew from [Uganda](country:UG) on May 23; airport surveillance flagged fatigue during screening of passengers arriving from Ebola-affected regions.

Samples are at the National Institute of Virology in Pune; WHO declared both African outbreaks a public health emergency.
