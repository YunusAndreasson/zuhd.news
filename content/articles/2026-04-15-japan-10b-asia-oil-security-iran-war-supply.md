Tokyo — Japan launched a $10 billion Asian oil fund. JBIC will finance 1.2 billion barrels — equivalent to 1 year of ASEAN crude imports — as Hormuz stays blocked. Tokyo will also release 36 million barrels from national reserves in May; fund terms and country access remain unannounced.
