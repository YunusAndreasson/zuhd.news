Aswan — 76 South Sudanese are stuck on a ferry a week.

[Sudan](country:SD) refused to let them disembark under [Egypt](country:EG)'s "voluntary return" scheme, then Egypt refused to take them back.

Officials from all 3 governments are negotiating a handover that already failed once at Wadi Halfa.
