Washington — The [US](country:US) will pay $1.2 billion to halt wind projects.

German developer RWE will redirect the sum into gas and LNG projects, including a Louisiana export terminal.

The deal is the third such federal buyout this year, after agreements with TotalEnergies and Duke Energy.
