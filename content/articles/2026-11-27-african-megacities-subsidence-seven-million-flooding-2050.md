Lagos — Lagos and Alexandria hold 5 million of 7 million people exposed by 2050.

The study's moderate warming scenario puts a million buildings and $180bn of assets at risk in an extreme sea level event.

Pumping groundwater or drilling for fossil fuels sinks land, and researchers measured it using more than 4,000 satellite images.

The analysis of 20 coastal cities appears in Science Advances.
