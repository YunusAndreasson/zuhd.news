US-Israeli strikes hit 15 Tehran police stations. Planet Labs satellite imagery, analyzed by open-source outlet Bellingcat, confirmed all sites as civilian law enforcement buildings — none appeared on any US or Israeli public target list. No military authority has responded, and no independent inquiry into the strikes' legality has been announced.
