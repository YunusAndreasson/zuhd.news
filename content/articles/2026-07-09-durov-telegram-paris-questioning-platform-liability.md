Paris — French investigators questioned Pavel Durov for six hours, his fourth session.

The Telegram founder faces charges of complicity in a platform's use for organized crime, punishable by up to 10 years, nearly two years after his 2024 arrest.

His lawyers say no evidence has surfaced yet and plan to challenge the case in [France](country:FR) and European courts.
