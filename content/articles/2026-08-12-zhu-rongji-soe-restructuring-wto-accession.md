Beijing — Tens of millions lost jobs under his reforms.

That layoff wave still shapes [China](country:CN)'s social contract today.

As premier he shut and privatized state firms first, then negotiated China's 2002 World Trade Organization entry on the terms that followed.

His 2011 published speeches still circulate as an unflattering mirror for current leaders.
