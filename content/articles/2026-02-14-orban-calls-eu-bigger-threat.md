Viktor Orbán called the EU a greater threat than Russia. Hungary's prime minister accused Brussels of backing his rival Peter Magyar ahead of April elections. Magyar's Tisza party leads Orbán's Fidesz in most polls. Orbán said "fear-mongering about Putin is primitive and unserious" and called the EU a "source of imminent danger."
