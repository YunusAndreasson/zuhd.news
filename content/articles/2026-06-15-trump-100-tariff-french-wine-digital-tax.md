Paris — French digital tax drew Trump's wine tariff threat.

[France](country:FR) charges a 3% levy on US tech firms' French revenue; Trump threatened to raise wine tariffs to 100% from the current 15% unless Paris repeals the law.

Macron, meeting Trump at the Évian G7 summit Monday, said foreign governments do not decide European tax law.
