China — An AI-made series reached 1,000 episodes across 10 seasons since August.

Hongguo has 430 million users, QuestMobile says, overtaking ByteDance's chatbot Doubao.

Over 90% of 430,000 short dramas released in [China](country:CN) in eight months used AI, the State Council Information Office says.

Douyin vice president Li Liang warned creators against rushing into AI short dramas on 10 November.
