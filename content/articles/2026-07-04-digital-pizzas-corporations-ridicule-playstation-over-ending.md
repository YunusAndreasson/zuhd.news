Tokyo — January 2028 marks Sony's last disc release.

Buyers will hold marketplace licenses instead of discs, ending resale, lending, and offline play once the platform revokes access.

Domino's, KFC and other brands mocked the shift; Sony has not addressed preservation concerns.
