Menlo Park — WhatsApp parents can now block group invites to teens.

Parents still cannot read messages or calls, which stay end-to-end encrypted.

One parent-set PIN governs photo visibility, group invites, Channels, Status and Meta AI, and teens need it to switch off.

Analyst Matt Navarra calls the optional design an "obvious weakness" that is "fairly convenient" for Meta.

[Britain](country:GB)'s under-16 social media ban, expected March 2027, excludes WhatsApp as a messaging service.
