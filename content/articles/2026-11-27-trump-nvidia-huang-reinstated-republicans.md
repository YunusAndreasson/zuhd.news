Washington — Trump personally lobbied Nvidia's board to reinstate Jensen Huang.

Republican senators fear [China](country:CN) gained access to Nvidia's chips through Huang's affair.

Nvidia lost more than $1tn in market value after Huang resigned and recovered when the board brought him back.

Trump called Huang exonerated, but Attorney General Todd Blanche sidestepped questions about an investigation.

Huang faces a House Foreign Affairs interview and a Senate Banking hearing next week.
