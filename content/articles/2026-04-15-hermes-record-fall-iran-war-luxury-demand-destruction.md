Paris — Hermès posted its biggest intraday drop on record. Revenue grew only 5.6% against a 7.44% forecast as Middle Eastern shoppers vanished from European stores; LVMH and Kering also missed. Hermès reopened Middle East stores, but Asia Pacific also missed forecasts; no timeline exists for full demand recovery.
