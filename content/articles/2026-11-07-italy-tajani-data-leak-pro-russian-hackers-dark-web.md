Rome — Hackers offer to pay for Tajani family details, he says.

Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani says his data has circulated on the dark web for days.

Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni blames pro-Russian hackers, and Tajani says [Italy](country:IT)'s support for Ukraine made him a target.

Tajani did not say what was exposed or link the leak to Europe's hoax bomb threats.

He said the foreign ministry is coordinating its response with allies as the threat continues.
