Vladivostok — Novak claims 94% of oil goes 'friendly'.

That reframes sanctions as redirecting [Russia](country:RU)'s oil, not cutting the volume sold.

Buyers in [India](country:IN) and [China](country:CN) take the barrels at a discount to Brent, near $96 on September 1.

Independent tanker-tracking will test whether Novak's 94% figure holds up.
