Yahodyn — A drone hit a Warsaw-bound train two kilometers from Poland.

It brings Russian strikes to NATO's doorstep, testing the alliance's response.

[Ukraine](country:UA)'s railway operator said a monitoring crew warned the train in advance, sparing all 208 passengers aboard.

Poland's foreign minister called it an escalation, with no Article 4 talks convened yet.
