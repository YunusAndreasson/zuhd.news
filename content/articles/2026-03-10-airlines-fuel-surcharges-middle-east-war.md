Jet fuel doubled after US-Israeli strikes on Iran began. Air New Zealand reported fuel costs rising from $85 to $200 per barrel, with major carriers adding surcharges as high as 35%. Most US carriers are unhedged, leaving passengers exposed to further cost increases if the war continues.
