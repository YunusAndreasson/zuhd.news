Cairo — Egypt disappeared filmmaker Omar Marei for 7 days.

[Egypt](country:EG)'s State Security Prosecution interrogated him incommunicado and without counsel, then charged him with publishing false news.

A prosecution-appointed lawyer notified his defense only after the session ended.
