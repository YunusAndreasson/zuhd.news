Srinagar — Fist fights broke out as Omar Abdullah sought statehood.

Abdullah says files from state bodies never reach his elected government while statehood remains unrestored.

Abdullah says [India](country:IN)'s government promised delimitation, then elections, then statehood, and only the first two happened.

"We will not allow discussion on the issue of autonomy in the House," said BJP legislator R S Pathania.

The Speaker scheduled debate on the resolution for Monday.
