United States — One user says Claude Code stripped 25 Xiaomi packages from a TV.

Owners are reviving bricked and ad-laden devices, and cloning software, with a prompt.

AI decompiles proprietary code, replicates its features and installs open-source replacements, work once left to hobbyist repair communities.

"It's becoming so easy to recreate the functionality that you want," said Mike Nolan, who studies the politics of open-source software.

Nolan doubts companies will adopt the clones, given the work of maintaining them.
