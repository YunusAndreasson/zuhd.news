China — Composted medicine residue now stabilizes antibacterial oil.

Researchers extracted humic acid from tangerine meat, soy-sauce residue and Chinese-medicine waste to bind cinnamon-oil droplets 8 microns wide, killing gram-positive and gram-negative bacteria.

The recipe offers the country's food industry a green emulsifier from waste otherwise burned or dumped.
