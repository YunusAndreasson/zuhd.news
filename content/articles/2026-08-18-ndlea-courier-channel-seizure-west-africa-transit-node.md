Lagos — Nigeria intercepted 184.5kg of cocaine via courier.

The record haul confirms [Nigeria](country:NG) has become a transit corridor, not just a market.

As European sea routes tightened, traffickers rerouted shipments through West African couriers bound for the UK.

Agents are tracing the network's masterminds beyond the two couriers already arrested.
