Redwood City — A daily pill doubled survival for pancreatic cancer. Revolution Medicines' daraxonrasib extended median survival from 6.7 to 13.2 months in metastatic patients — nearly twice the result of standard chemotherapy. Fewer than 12% of pancreatic cancer patients survive 5 years; daraxonrasib now heads to a Phase 3 trial.
