Budapest — A landlocked pipeline habit is testing sanctions unity.

Hungarian lawmaker Marton Hajdu said he lobbied US Congress to exempt [Hungary](country:HU) from a 100% duty on Russian-energy buyers.

Hungary imported over 8.5 million tons of Russian oil in 2025; it targets diversification only by 2035.
