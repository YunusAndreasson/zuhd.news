Manta — A gunman killed Alexandra Bravo and her sister.

Bravo was probing [US](country:US) strikes on three Ecuadorian boats and survivors' torture accounts; she is Ecuador's 26th slain justice official since 2020.

The UN demanded Washington disclose the whereabouts of eight still-missing crew by April 27; it has not responded.
