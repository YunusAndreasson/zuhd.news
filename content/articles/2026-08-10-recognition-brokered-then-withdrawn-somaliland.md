Bissau — [Guinea-Bissau](country:GW) was days from recognizing Somaliland.

It would have been the first African recognition of Somaliland's 35-year independence bid.

Somaliland's foreign minister flew to Bissau for talks before [Somalia](country:SO)'s government intervened and Guinea-Bissau denied any recognition plan.

Somaliland's president has not received a recognition offer from any African government.
