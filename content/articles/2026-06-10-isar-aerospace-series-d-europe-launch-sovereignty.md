Munich — Isar Aerospace raised €270 million to scale rocket production.

The Munich factory will scale to 40 Spectrum vehicles per year, filling Europe's commercial launch gap, Isar said.

Spectrum's second launch window opens June 15; the first flight failed within a minute of liftoff.
