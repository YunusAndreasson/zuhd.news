Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s opposition leaders say Imran Khan's life is in danger.

A Supreme Court order moving him to hospital has gone unimplemented for three months, they wrote.

Mahmood Khan Achakzai and Allama Raja Nasir Abbas asked three chief justices to treat their letter as a petition and issue a writ of mandamus.

Information Minister Attaullah Tarar blamed supporters of Khan's party for the August diversion to a state hospital.

The letter demands his transfer to Shifa International Hospital within 24 hours of a court order.
