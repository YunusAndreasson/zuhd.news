San Francisco — A scientist was cut the day before she gave birth.

Twenty-six ex-Meta employees allege internal AI tools scored them on protected medical and family leave, disproportionately selecting disabled and pregnant staff among 8,000 cut.

Plaintiffs want a court to block their firings pending an independent audit of the scoring system's inputs.
