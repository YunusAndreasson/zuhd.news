Moscow — [Russia](country:RU) collected $3.77bn in migrant fees.

Those fees are paid mostly by laborers from [Tajikistan](country:TJ), [Uzbekistan](country:UZ) and [Kyrgyzstan](country:KG).

Missing a monthly work-patent payment lapses legal status, letting Moscow freeze bank accounts under a 2025 registry.

Deputy Interior Minister Andrey Kikot said the legal labor segment, and revenue, will keep growing.
