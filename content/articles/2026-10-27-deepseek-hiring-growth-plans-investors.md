Beijing — DeepSeek's Harness team advertised about 150 openings in September.

People familiar with the matter say DeepSeek is outlining new growth plans to investors.

In June it announced plans to at least double every department, advertising 33 positions including data-centre roles in Ulanqab.

The report gives no size or timing for any investment.
