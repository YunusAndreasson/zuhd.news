Sudan recorded 1,238 dengue cases in a single week. Sudan's civil war has displaced millions into makeshift camps where standing water breeds dengue, with no functioning health system to treat the sick. The same report logged 165 measles and 54 hepatitis E cases — 3 simultaneous outbreaks with no peace process in sight.
