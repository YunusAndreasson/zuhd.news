Tehran — [Iran](country:IR)'s navy says 2 supertankers hit mines Sunday.

Supertankers usually carry 2 million barrels or more, twice a standard tanker's load.

The navy says both ships switched off tracking and entered a restricted area north of Ghanam Island.

British maritime authorities received a tanker-incident report Sunday but could not say whether it involved these ships.

Polymarket traders give a [US](country:US)-Iran Hormuz agreement by 31 December 12%, down 5 points in a week.
