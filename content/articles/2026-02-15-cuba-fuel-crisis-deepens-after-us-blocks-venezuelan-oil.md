Cuba postponed its Habanos cigar festival indefinitely. The island has suffered weeks of rolling blackouts after the United States cut off Venezuelan oil deliveries following President Maduro's ouster in January, while a Trump executive order threatens tariffs on any country that sells fuel to Havana. Airlines have cancelled flights because Cuban airports cannot refuel planes, and the cigar auction — which raised $18 million last year for healthcare — has no new date.
