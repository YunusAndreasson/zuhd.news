120 parties are competing for Nepal's parliament. Campaigning opened Sunday for the March 5 election, the first since anti-corruption protests killed 76 people and toppled the government in September 2025, bringing interim Prime Minister Sushila Karki to power. Over 915,000 first-time voters have registered — two-thirds of them young people — testing whether the protest movement can break through Nepal's entrenched party system at the ballot box.
