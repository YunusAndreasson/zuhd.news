Beijing — [China](country:CN)'s exports rose 25% to $401 billion.

The surge beat forecasts despite [US](country:US) tariffs meant to suppress it.

Extreme weather disrupted some ports and trade tensions persisted, yet growth still accelerated from July, customs data showed.

Some of the strength reflects pre-Christmas orders pulled forward into August, tempering the outlook.
