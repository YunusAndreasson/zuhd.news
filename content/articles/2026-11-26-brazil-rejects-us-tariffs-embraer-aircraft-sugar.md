Brasília — Aircraft, Brazil's fourth-largest US export, would face a 10% duty.

[US](country:US) airlines have no domestic alternative for regional routes to smaller cities.

The proposal uses Section 301, an unfair-trade statute, after the Supreme Court struck down emergency-law tariffs in February.

Brazil's sugar-cane industry body says [Brazil](country:BR)'s sugar exports to the US fell from over 1 million tons in 2024 to 146,000.

The US Trade Representative holds a hearing on 14 January before the duties can take effect.
