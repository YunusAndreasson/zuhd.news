Bhopal — Madhya Pradesh court ruled Bhojshala a Hindu site.

[India](country:IN)'s Madhya Pradesh High Court used an archaeological survey to reach its ruling — the same mechanism that resolved the Ayodhya dispute.

The precedent may be cited against Gyanvapi and other contested sites in future litigation.
