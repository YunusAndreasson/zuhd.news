Menlo Park — Meta blocked hiding its glasses' recording light.

Bystanders, not wearers, bear whichever privacy risk the fix leaves unresolved.

The camera now stops recording if a user covers the LED after capture starts, closing a bypass users had found.

EU regulators are still deciding whether the light itself satisfies consent rules.
