Budapest — Europe's far-right parties held their first coordinated continental summit. France's Marine Le Pen, Dutch leader Geert Wilders, and Austria's Herbert Kickl gathered in Budapest days after French voters rejected far-right candidates in municipal elections. Hungary's upcoming elections will test whether Viktor Orbán's Patriots for Europe bloc can translate that coordination into governing power at home.
