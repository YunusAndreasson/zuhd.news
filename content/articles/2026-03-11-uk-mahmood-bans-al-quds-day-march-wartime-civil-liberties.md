Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood banned London's annual pro-Palestine march. Mahmood approved a Metropolitan Police request, citing "threats to public disorder," ending a march that had taken place in London for 40 consecutive years. The Islamic Human Rights Commission announced legal action against the ban, though authorities permitted a static protest on March 15.
