Gelephu — A special zone handed part of its bitcoin reserve to a manager.

[Bhutan](country:BT)'s Gelephu Mindfulness City tapped Toronto's 3iQ to run part of a treasury pledged at up to 10,000 bitcoin for market-neutral yield, undisclosed in size.

Officials say the mandate will create jobs for Bhutanese youth, with no programs yet detailed.
