Awka — Gunmen killed 4 people, including 2 police officers, in an ambush on [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Anambra chief-of-staff convoy.

The attack struck Ben Nwankwo's convoy in Orumba South.

No group claimed responsibility and police have not named suspects.
