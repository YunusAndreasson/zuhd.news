Belgrade — Students nominated chemist Radomir Saicic, 65, for president.

Aleksandar Vucic held that office until he left to run for parliament and lost.

The Student List won 140 of 250 seats, and the president nominates prime ministers and commands the armed forces.

Vucic's party may boycott the 27 December vote and decides on 13 December, after nominations close.
