Praia — More Cape Verdeans live abroad than on the islands.

Cape Verde's 300,000 voters are choosing among 6 candidates, including Joana Rosa, its first woman.

Neves's party won 37 of 72 seats in May, and the president holds veto power and a mediating role.

Rosa, the opposition candidate, wants a government that lets young people "build their futures here at home."

Results are due in 2 to 3 days, with a run-off on 29 November if no one passes 50%.
