Brazil — A WhatsApp channel grew from 50,000 to 11 million members.

It helped Lula overturn a 2 million-vote first-round deficit and win [Brazil](country:BR)'s runoff by over 4 million.

Lawmaker André Janones assigned members missions, from amplifying Lula's posts online to talking with potential voters in person.

Jair Bolsonaro, whom his son promised to pardon, remains jailed on a 27-year coup sentence.
