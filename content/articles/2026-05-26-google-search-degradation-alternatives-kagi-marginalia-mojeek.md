San Francisco — Google went AI-only, ending link-based search.

At I/O 2026, Google made AI the default, abandoning the link-based index it has maintained since 1998; a [US](country:US) court ruled in 2024 that index was an illegal monopoly.

Paid alternatives like Kagi ($5/month) gain users; whether open-web publishers survive without Google traffic remains open.
