London — Healthwatch England would fold into National Health Service.

Independent complaint channels are how UK patient-safety failures have historically surfaced.

The Modernisation Bill moves Healthwatch's duties to local boards and shifts safety probes to the Care Quality Commission.

The bill reaches its third reading in the [UK](country:GB) parliament on September 7.
