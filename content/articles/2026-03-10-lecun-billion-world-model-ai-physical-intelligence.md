Yann LeCun raised $1 billion to replace large language models with AI that understands physical reality. LeCun — Turing Award winner and Meta's chief AI scientist — argues language models cannot achieve general intelligence because they model only text, not gravity, causality, or physical consequence. His "world model" company must now demonstrate that physically-grounded AI outperforms language models on tasks requiring causal reasoning.
