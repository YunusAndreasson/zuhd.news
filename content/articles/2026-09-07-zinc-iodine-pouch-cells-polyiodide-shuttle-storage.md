London — A 1.6-amp-hour pouch cell survived 1,200 charge cycles.

Zinc and iodine in water need no lithium, cobalt or flammable solvent.

A hydrogel layer traps dissolved iodine that would normally drift across the cell and drain its charge.

Researchers tested wearables and high-power devices, not yet grid-scale storage products.
