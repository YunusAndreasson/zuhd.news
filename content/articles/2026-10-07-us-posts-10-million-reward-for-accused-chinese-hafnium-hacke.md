Washington — [US](country:US) officials say Hafnium victimised more than 12,700 US entities.

The State Department now offers up to $10 million for information on accused hacker Zhang Yu.

Prosecutors allege Zhang and Xu Zewei hacked for [China](country:CN)'s Ministry of State Security from February 2020 to June 2021.

Zhang remains at large, while Xu, arrested in Milan in 2025, was extradited to the US in April.
