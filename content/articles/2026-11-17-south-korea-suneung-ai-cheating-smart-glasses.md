He photographed questions with a miniature camera, sent them to AI and read answers on a phone, he told police.

Four proctors told organisers they saw nothing suspicious, though phones were only switched off, not collected.

The education ministry will station one smart-glasses inspector at each of 1,312 exam sites Thursday.
