Tirana — [Albania](country:AL) protests over Kushner's Sazan resort reached day 5.

The [US](country:US) president's son-in-law won protected-land approval only after Albania amended its own law in 2024 to allow five-star development on coastal conservation areas.

Prime Minister Rama cited €4 billion in projected investment; protesters demand the project be scrapped.
