Hong Kong — Iran's Hormuz blockade is squeezing Hong Kong manufacturers. US buyers switched to short-term contracts after oil prices spiked, leaving small firms unable to raise prices to cover rising fuel costs. Business leaders called on the government to open Central Asian and Southeast Asian markets before more manufacturers close.
