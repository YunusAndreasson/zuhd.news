New York — Virgin mice learn to answer pup cries.

Repeated exposure to pups, not giving birth, reorganizes the auditory cortex via oxytocin, the lab found.

Whether that change passes to unexposed offspring, researcher Bianca Jones Marlin says, remains far less certain.
