US health regulators blocked annual flu vaccine effectiveness trials. The Food and Drug Administration's advisory committee maintained the WHO-recommended strain selection for 2026-27 and rejected outgoing FDA biologics chief Vinay Prasad's proposal to require annual testing before licensing new strains. Prasad's departure leaves the vaccine division without a director heading into the 2026-27 production cycle.
