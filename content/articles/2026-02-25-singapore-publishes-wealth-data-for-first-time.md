Singapore published wealth distribution data for the first time. The Finance Ministry found that mandatory savings and public housing hold the top 1%'s share to 14% of assets, compared with 35% in the United States. The ministry acknowledged the data excludes overseas holdings and unlisted assets, with the next survey due in 2028.
