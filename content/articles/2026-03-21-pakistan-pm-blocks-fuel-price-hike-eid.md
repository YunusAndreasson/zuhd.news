Pakistan's PM blocked a Rs50 petrol hike before Eid. Oil tripled from $72 to $158 per barrel in 3 weeks of war, forcing Pakistan to absorb Rs24 billion by cutting development budgets. Shehbaz Sharif warned a further hike is imminent if the conflict intensifies; at $158 per barrel, Pakistan's development budget cannot absorb another round of cuts.
