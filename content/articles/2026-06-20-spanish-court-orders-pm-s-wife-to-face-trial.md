Madrid — A judge ordered [Spain](country:ES)'s PM's wife to trial.

Judge Peinado, using Spain's investigating-magistrate system, charged Begoña Gómez with embezzlement and influence peddling; she must appear in court fortnightly.

No trial date is set; Sánchez, facing elections by next year, calls the case a right-wing smear.
