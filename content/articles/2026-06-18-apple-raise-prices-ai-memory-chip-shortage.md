Cupertino — Apple will raise prices as AI demand for memory chips has become "unsustainable," CEO Tim Cook said.

AI server demand has captured memory chip supply from consumer devices; memory prices more than doubled since October 2025.

Cook gave no date or products; he hands the CEO role to John Ternus in September.
