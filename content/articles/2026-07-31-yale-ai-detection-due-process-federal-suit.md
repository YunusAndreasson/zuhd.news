New Haven — A $208,500 exam grade became 13 lawsuit counts.

Yale suspended Thierry Rignol after detector GPTZero flagged his exam as AI-written, a tool known to misfire on non-native English writers.

Rignol has since graduated but is suing to reverse his F and expunge the record.
