Karachi — [UAE](country:AE) investors bought $395.7 million of Pakistan's bonds since July.

Foreign bond purchases hit $509.2 million, the highest for July-October, State Bank data show.

Pakistan's central bank raised its policy rate 50 basis points to 12% on 26 October, and the six-month T-bill pays 12.54%.

Some analysts say Pakistanis themselves buy through overseas accounts, and costly state borrowing leaves private firms little credit.

The bonds count as near-risk-free only while the exchange rate, stable for over a year, holds.
