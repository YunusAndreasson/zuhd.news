Ntan Mbat — Oil sheen empties fishing waters every rainy season.

Fishers, fish dryers and fish sellers all lose income when the catch fails.

Residents blame three wells drilled and capped in the 1950s but cannot pinpoint the source, and the water joins the Calabar-Itu River.

Residents will petition the petroleum regulator, the oil spill agency and the National Assembly to investigate.
