Los Angeles — Prerecorded faces of friends let hackers steal $400 million.

Targets are crypto staff and open-source developers, and the [US](country:US) says proceeds fund [North Korea](country:KP)'s weapons.

Hackers join calls from hijacked accounts, play a prior victim's recorded clip, then offer a fake-error link that installs malware.

North Korea denies involvement in the Binance hack, which the US Federal Bureau of Investigation blames on a separate military group.

A judge will decide who owns the $1.2 billion the Justice Department moved to seize.
