Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s budget devotes 43% to debt service.

The budget cuts tax rates for high earners while hitting the salaried class hardest; the opposition called it 'elite self-preservation.'

The government's 3.7% growth target rests on revenue projections Pakistan has consistently missed.
