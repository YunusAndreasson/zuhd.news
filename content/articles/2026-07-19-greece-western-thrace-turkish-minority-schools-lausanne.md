Xanthi — 8 Turkish-minority schools in Western Thrace just closed.

[Greece](country:GR) cited low enrollment; the 1923 Lausanne treaty guarantees the minority's mother-tongue schooling, now at 76 schools.

[Türkiye](country:TR)'s foreign ministry also flagged new Xanthi enrollment barriers, calling both moves systematic erosion.
