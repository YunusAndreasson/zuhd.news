Quetta — Eighteen miners have died so far.

A methane explosion trapped 36 miners in [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Balochistan at a 4,000-foot depth. Fourteen remain unaccounted for, a senior mining official said.

The chief mine inspector called survival chances low as rescuers keep searching for them.
