Deep-sea ecosystems take centuries to recover from mining. The International Seabed Authority is finalizing mining rules, but the US is pushing to extract EV battery minerals before they take effect. ISA chief Leticia Carvalho says rules could be final by year's end, but Indigenous advocates say environmental protections remain unresolved.
