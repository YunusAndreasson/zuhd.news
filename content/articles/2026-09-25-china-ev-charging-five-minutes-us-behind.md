Hangzhou — Geely's battery charges from 10 to 70% in 4.5 minutes.

[US](country:US) chargers top out at 600 kilowatts, against the 2.2 megawatts Geely's pack accepts.

Geely says artificial intelligence thermal control holds pack temperature near 55°C and micro pulses of current extend battery life 20%.

[China](country:CN)'s Contemporary Amperex Technology still leads, announcing a lithium iron phosphate battery in April that charges from 10 to 80% in 3 minutes 44 seconds.

ChargePoint's roadmap pairs two 600-kilowatt chargers to deliver 1.2 megawatts through one port.
