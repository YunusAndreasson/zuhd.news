Uyo — Five households have fled weeks of flooding in one estate.

Resident Shedrach Mattias says the environment ministry told him to wait for the dry season.

Drains along the abandoned road end halfway, leaving runoff from four directions to pool in the estate's low ground, residents say.

The state environmental agency says the area was a brook and residents built on a natural waterway.

The state has produced no approvals, drainage plans or road contract documents, and gave no date for intervention.
