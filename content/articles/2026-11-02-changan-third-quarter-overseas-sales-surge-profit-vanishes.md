Chongqing — Changan's overseas sales rose 64% as its nine-month profit fell 98%.

A third-quarter loss of 758 million yuan nearly erased the first half's profit, the filing shows.

In [China](country:CN), domestic petrol sales shrink while electric rivals force costly launches, and nine-month sales fell 18.07% to 1.69 million.

Chairman Zhao Fei, appointed 16 October, inherits a nine-month operating cash outflow of 11.38 billion yuan.
