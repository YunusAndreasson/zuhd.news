Managua — [Nicaragua](country:NI) deported 222 prisoners, then stripped their citizenship.

Law 1145 lets the National Assembly declare critics "traitors to the homeland" and revoke nationality by decree.

The 1961 Statelessness Convention forbids exactly this, but carries no enforcement mechanism against Nicaragua.
