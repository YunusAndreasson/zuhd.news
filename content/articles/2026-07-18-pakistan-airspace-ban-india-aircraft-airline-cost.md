Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK) bars Indian jets until August 23.

Closing a flight corridor forces airlines into longer, fuel-heavier detours, quietly taxing carriers and passengers on both sides.

Islamabad has extended the ban since the 2025 Pahalgam attack; whether it lifts by August 23 remains unclear.
