Lucknow — 14 died in an animation studio fire in Lucknow's Aliganj district, officials said.

Dense smoke sealed the stairwells; survivors lowered themselves on makeshift ropes from upper floors.

Uttar Pradesh's chief minister promised accountability but no fire-safety audit has been ordered.
