Pakistan hit Kabul, destroying the Taliban's 313 Corps headquarters. Pakistan accuses the Taliban of harboring Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan, the group behind most attacks on Pakistani soil, since the Taliban recaptured Kabul in 2021. Pakistan says the operation continues, with Kabul yet to announce a formal response.
