Oxford undergraduate Paul Trakulthongchai cracked a 60-year math problem. The Lonely Runner Conjecture, proved for 7 runners by 2007 and untouched since, connects to number theory problems with applications in cryptography. Trakulthongchai extended the proof to 10 runners, and mathematicians say 11 — the next target — is exponentially harder.
