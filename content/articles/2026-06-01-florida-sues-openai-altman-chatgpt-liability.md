Tallahassee — Florida sued OpenAI's CEO for chatbot violence.

The 83-page complaint invokes consumer-protection law, tying ChatGPT to a 2025 mass shooting and teen suicides, and names Sam Altman personally.

No trial date is set; the ruling would establish whether state consumer law reaches a chatbot's conversational outputs.
