Beijing — [Pakistan](country:PK) priced its first yuan bond at 2.5%.

The 3-year bond attracted 5-times oversubscription, opening Pakistan's first onshore [China](country:CN) capital-market channel, the finance ministry said.

The full panda programme targets $1 billion equivalent; further tranches depend on sustained Chinese investor demand.
