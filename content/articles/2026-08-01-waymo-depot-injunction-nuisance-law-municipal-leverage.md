Santa Monica — A noise nuisance claim did what safety debates couldn't.

A Los Angeles Superior Court judge barred overnight charging at two Waymo lots, since driving is regulated by states but parking and zoning stay with cities.

The case continues in October; cities weighing robotaxi deals now have a local lever to test.
