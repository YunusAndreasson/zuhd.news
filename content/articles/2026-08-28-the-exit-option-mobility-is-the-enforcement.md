Doha — Workers who can quit report far less discrimination.

The effect comes from employer incentive, not stronger anti-discrimination law.

Where one employer dominates a local market, workers cannot credibly threaten to leave, so discrimination costs the employer nothing.

Gulf kafala rules tie a worker's legal residence to one employer, removing that exit option entirely.
