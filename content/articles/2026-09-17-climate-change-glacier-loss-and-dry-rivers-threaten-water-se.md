Geneva — 2025 was rivers' driest year in decades.

The same trend raises risk of both drought and flood.

Retreating glaciers act as a buffer, not a source, temporarily raising flow before supplies fail later.

The WMO blames a warmer climate and rising water use for a less predictable water cycle.
