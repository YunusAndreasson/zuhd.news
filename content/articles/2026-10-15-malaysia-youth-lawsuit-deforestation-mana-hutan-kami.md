Kuala Lumpur — 6 young people sued their government over deforestation.

They want [Malaysia](country:MY) held to its pledge to keep 50% of land forested.

The claim invokes legitimate expectation, the public trust doctrine, and constitutional rights to life and equality.

The high court will decide whether to issue a binding directive halting deforestation.
