[Iran](country:IR) destroyed up to 30 Reaper drones, the Pentagon says.

The report offers the first official accounting of a war Washington has stayed largely quiet on.

The Congressional Budget Office counted 24 Reapers destroyed at $30 million apiece, a loss between $720 million and $900 million.

Independent tracking put the toll higher: at least 45 Reapers, a quarter of the prewar fleet, by August.

Washington still must decide whether to rebuild its battered Gulf bases in Bahrain and Qatar.
