Caracas — Back-to-back 7.2 and 7.5 magnitude quakes killed 1,430 in Venezuela and left 68,000 missing.

The UN Development Programme estimates $6.7 billion in damage to a sanctions-strained economy; the 72-hour rescue window has passed.

The EU pledged €5 million and activated civil-protection teams in [Venezuela](country:VE).
