USDC has surpassed Tether in trading volume for the first time since 2019. Circle's US-regulated stablecoin now accounts for 64% of 2026 crypto trading volume, against Tether's offshore model. Mizuho raised Circle's price target to $120 on the shift, betting the gap widens.
