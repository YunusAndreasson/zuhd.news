Berlin — Russian state agency TASS reports 5,800 marched against conscription.

Organisers including the Left Party and the Sahra Wagenknecht Alliance want sanctions lifted and arms shipments to [Ukraine](country:UA) halted.

TASS attributes the Berlin count to police, and says Stuttgart organisers claimed about 15,000 for rallies against an arms buildup.

TASS does not say when, or whether, [Germany](country:DE)'s government will reintroduce conscription.
