Nairobi — AI moderation covers 42 of Africa's 2,000 languages. A 2025 study found only 4 African languages are handled consistently; training-data economics make expanding coverage a negative-margin service for platforms. No African regulator mandates native-language AI coverage; unchecked content falls to human moderators who cannot read it.
