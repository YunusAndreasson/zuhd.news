Sofia — [Bulgaria](country:BG)'s deficit already breaks the eurozone ceiling.

The European Commission forecasts the gap at 4.1% of GDP this year; Prime Minister Radev says the previous government cut state prices to hit the inflation entry threshold.

The formal report publishes June 3; failure to correct below 3% risks [EU](country:EU) funding freezes and biannual fines.
