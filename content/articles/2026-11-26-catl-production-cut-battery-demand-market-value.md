Ningde — CATL's market value is down 54% since May, to 964 billion yuan.

Analyst Lucas Zhang reads the production cut as demand weakness, not supply discipline.

Citigroup says CATL trimmed November output by 3 gigawatt-hours to 93, as [China](country:CN)'s energy storage installations lag.

Barclays stays overweight on CATL, citing resilient Chinese energy storage demand through 2030.

Citigroup expects the cuts to continue next month and into the first quarter.
