Samarkand — Rubio brought almost 300 US businesses.

Washington and Beijing are competing for Central Asia's manganese, titanium and cobalt.

The [US](country:US) and five Central Asian governments pledged mineral processing and Trans-Caspian trade links, but named no funding, projects or deadlines.

Premier Li Qiang of [China](country:CN) left [Uzbekistan](country:UZ) on Sunday after signing trade, customs and healthcare agreements.

Rubio says some companies have agreements, unnamed so far, that will be announced later.
