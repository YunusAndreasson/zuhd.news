Seoul — Miners are voting on whether Friday happened.

Traders risk having completed payments erased if the network chooses to rewrite its own history.

A critical flaw let bad blocks into Ravencoin's ledger, giving two pools enough hashpower to rebuild it.

Upbit and Bitvavo have frozen deposits until the network settles which history is real.
