The Hague — 80,000 shared cars run across the Netherlands.

[The Netherlands](country:NL) works through subsidies, parking limits, and dense neighborhoods; cooperatives charge €15/month per household.

Amsterdam ended new-project subsidies; replication requires density and public trust most cities lack.
