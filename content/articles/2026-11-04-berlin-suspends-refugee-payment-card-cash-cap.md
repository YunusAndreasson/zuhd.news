Berlin — Berlin will suspend asylum seekers' 50-euro monthly cash cap.

Not every shop accepts cards, and the cap left asylum seekers little cash.

Since August, asylum seekers in [Germany](country:DE)'s last state to adopt it get a debit card instead of cash.

The Berlin Refugee Council calls the suspension a partial success on a longer road.

Berlin's coalition negotiators decide whether the card itself is scrapped.
