Islamabad — Pakistan's fuel supply is secured only through April. Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb said the Hormuz crisis may last months, forcing Pakistan away from blanket subsidies that cost Rs69 billion in March. The government must announce its targeting criteria before April's supply window closes.
