Bannu — A quadcopter, not a fighter jet, killed two people.

The weapon costs a few hundred dollars, cheap enough that no one can be held accountable.

Police blame militants for the strike in [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Wazir subdivision, while residents elsewhere have blamed security forces.

A search operation continues, but no independent investigation has been announced.
