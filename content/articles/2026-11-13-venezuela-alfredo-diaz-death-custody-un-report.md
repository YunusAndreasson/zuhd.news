Venezuela — Former governor Alfredo Díaz died after medical neglect in state custody.

At least 18 officials the UN implicates in abuses still hold power in [Venezuela](country:VE).

The UN fact-finding mission's report rests on 213 confidential interviews with 121 victims, relatives and witnesses, plus forensic data and court documents.

Interim president Delcy Rodríguez has released hundreds of prisoners since the [US](country:US) captured Nicolás Maduro on 3 January.

The Human Rights Council extended the mission for two years in October, keeping the case file open.
