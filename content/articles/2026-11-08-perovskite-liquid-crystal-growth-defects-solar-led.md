Suwon — One perovskite film produced both solar cells and LEDs.

Seong Sik Shin's team at Sungkyunkwan University reports dramatically fewer defects in the film.

Liquid-assisted crystal growth built the film, with the Korea Research Institute of Chemical Technology, UC San Diego and Princeton.

The announcement gives no efficiency figure or durability result.
