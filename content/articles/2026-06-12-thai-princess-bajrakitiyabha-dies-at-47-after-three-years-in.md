Bangkok — [Thailand](country:TH)'s presumptive successor died Thursday at 47.

King Vajiralongkorn, 73, has not named an heir; succession rules favor 21-year-old Prince Dipangkorn, who rarely appears in public.

The privy council can name a princess, but lese-majeste law blocks any public succession debate.
