Washington — SpaceX may launch 1 million satellites, 50 times today's total.

The [US](country:US) Federal Communications Commission skipped environmental review, ruling launch emissions outside its purview and reentry pollution unsupported by the record.

SpaceX must deploy one satellite within 7 years, 100,000 by November 2035, and all 1 million by November 2040.

Amazon's Leo unit and others say SpaceX is unlikely to deploy them all.

SpaceX still needs a waiver to retire satellites above 600 km anywhere but the atmosphere.
