Cairo — [Egypt](country:EG)'s new state-firm tax has undefined exemptions.

Linked to the IMF loan, the bill taxes state firms 5% of profits but grants the prime minister undefined exemption power.

The bill awaits a final vote; the military's 97-company empire is expected to seek a prime minister exemption, sources say.
