UK opposition refused to meet Nigeria's visiting president. Conservative leader Kemi Badenoch, raised in Nigeria and publicly critical of its corruption, declined the traditional opposition meeting with Tinubu. He meets Prime Minister Keir Starmer on Thursday, two days after a bombing killed 23 people in Nigeria's northeast.
