Pyongyang — A stealth drone mockup stood among real drones Thursday.

Beside it sat reconnaissance drones, armed UAVs, loitering munitions and small attack drones.

Korea Institute for National Unification fellow Hong Min estimates the tailless flying wing spans 56 to 66 feet, resembling the X-47B.

Nobody has shown evidence [North Korea](country:KP) can fly it, and it released no program details.

Kim Jong Un demanded superiority in drone technology and announced unspecified measures to accelerate military artificial intelligence.
