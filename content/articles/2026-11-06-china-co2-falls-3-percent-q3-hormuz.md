China — Emissions fell 3.1% in the third quarter, the sharpest drop since 2022 lockdowns.

[China](country:CN)'s emissions have been flat or falling for more than two years, since March 2024.

Solar, wind and nuclear added 110 terawatt hours, twice demand growth, while transport oil use fell 9% amid the Hormuz crisis.

Coal plants are still being built at near-record rates, which cuts running hours and profits, analyst Lauri Myllyvirta wrote.

The outlook turns on energy demand, clean-power growth and the government's response to a GDP slowdown.
