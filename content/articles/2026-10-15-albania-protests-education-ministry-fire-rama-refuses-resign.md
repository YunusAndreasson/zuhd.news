Tirana — 89 people were hurt Wednesday, 61 of them police.

Students and a four-month anti-corruption movement now both demand Prime Minister Edi Rama's resignation.

The movement began over a resort project linked to Jared Kushner and spread to tuition costs and youth unemployment.

Rama says voters gave him a mandate last year to complete European Union membership talks.

[Albania](country:AL) hopes to join the EU by 2028, and the EU delegation urged a proportionate response.
