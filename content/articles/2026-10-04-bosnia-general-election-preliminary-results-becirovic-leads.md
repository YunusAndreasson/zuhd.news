Sarajevo — Denis Becirovic leads the Bosniak seat with 38.12%, Russian state agency TASS reported.

Darjana Filipovic leads the Croat seat on 46.56%, Zeljka Cvijanovic the Serb seat on 55.34%.

The 1995 Dayton agreement splits [Bosnia](country:BA) into two entities and shares presidency seats among Bosniaks, Croats and Serbs.

10% of votes remain uncounted, and Milorad Dodik says his alliance will take two-thirds of Republika Srpska's assembly.
