United States — AI training clusters now exceed 100 MW.

Data centers are shifting to direct current to cut the energy lost at each AC-DC conversion stage, reversing Edison's 1880s defeat by Tesla's AC standard.

As AI infrastructure expands, facilities that delay the shift face energy costs that compound with scale.
