Hokkaido — Silver atoms briefly adopted the arrangement radioactive polonium uses.

The new structure shows unusually high chemical reactivity.

Arrangement matters: the same carbon atoms form diamond or graphite, with vastly different properties.

The Nano Letters paper says the finding could open avenues to materials with desirable properties.
