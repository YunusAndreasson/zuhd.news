Canberra — Australia's fuel agency predicted a 75% shortage last May. The report, obtained under freedom-of-information laws, warned of severe damage to agriculture and logistics — yet the government took no action for 10 months. Regional towns are already running dry, and if the crisis deepens, Australia's emergency reserves cover only one week.
