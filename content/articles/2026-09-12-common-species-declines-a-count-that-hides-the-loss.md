London — Common species, not rare ones, are vanishing fastest.

Biodiversity counts can stay flat while the abundance ecosystems depend on collapses.

The study analyzed 4,748 sites across 129 studies, finding common species declined most in marine and terrestrial systems.

Freshwater systems showed no clear pattern, and long-term monitoring still barely covers the Global South.
