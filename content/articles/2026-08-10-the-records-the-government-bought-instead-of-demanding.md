Washington — Immigration and Customs Enforcement, ICE, will pay LexisNexis $6.7 million for data.

The purchase supplies [US](country:US) deportation targeting with records ICE cannot lawfully compel.

A warrant binds ICE's own collection, not its shopping, so Palantir links purchased records into one searchable profile.

It is not yet clear whether case files or active manhunts receive the data.
