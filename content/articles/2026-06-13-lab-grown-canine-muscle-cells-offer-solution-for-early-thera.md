London — Lab-grown dog cells can now pre-screen therapies.

The cell-based model identifies which treatments work before any animal is dosed, narrowing the candidate pool upstream and reducing dogs needed in early veterinary trials.

The study does not specify which disease targets the model covers, leaving its translational scope undefined.
