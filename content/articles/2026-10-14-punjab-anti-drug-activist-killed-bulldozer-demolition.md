Mansa — Authorities razed a murder suspect's house within a day.

Anti-drug activist Parvinder Singh Jhota was shot dead on Facebook Live, and pharmacist Ashwani Kumar is accused.

Officials said the demolition targeted unauthorised construction, while a mob including some Nihangs smashed and burned Kumar's shop under heavy police presence.

Singh's family has refused to cremate his body, and protesters demand the administration's dismissal.
