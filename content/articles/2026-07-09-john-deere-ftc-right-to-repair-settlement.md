Washington — Farmers got real right-to-repair teeth.

An earlier Illinois class-action deal paid farmers just 79 cents per acre and left Deere's repair-parts monopoly intact, one advocate said.

The Federal Trade Commission settlement sets the repair-access template other manufacturers, from phones to medical gear, may face.
