Iran named US corporate facilities as retaliation targets. Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi warned that any US-linked company in the region becomes a target if Iran's energy is struck — one day after US-Israeli strikes hit Kharg Island, Iran's main oil terminal. Thousands of US corporate workers in the Gulf now face a named threat as the bombardment pushes into civilian economic infrastructure.
