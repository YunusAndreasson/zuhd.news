United States — Researchers propose Zcash-style private payments for Bitcoin without a fork.

Bitcoin's public ledger exposes every payment, and shielded notes would hide sender, recipient and amount.

Spending a note publishes a used marker and a proof that the sender owned the funds and created none.

Critics cite visible fees, higher transaction costs and reliance on a trusted cryptographic setup.

The research is preliminary, with no launch date and no mechanism yet for depositing and withdrawing bitcoin.
