London — Fertility can fail without soil looking dead.

Metals, pesticides, plastics and forever chemicals don't sterilise soil — they select tolerant microbes that no longer fix nitrogen or cycle nutrients.

Heavy metal exposure also selects for antibiotic resistance genes, linking farm pollution directly to clinical medicine.
