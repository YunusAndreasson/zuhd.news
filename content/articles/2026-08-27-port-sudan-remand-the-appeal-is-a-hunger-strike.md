Port Sudan — 83 remand prisoners have never faced trial.

Their hunger strike is the only appeal left against years of pretrial detention.

War displaced [Sudan](country:SD)'s courts, but case files and judges did not all move, leaving cases in limbo.

Officials pledged 3 more judges and rulings within 2 months, or the strike resumes Sunday.
