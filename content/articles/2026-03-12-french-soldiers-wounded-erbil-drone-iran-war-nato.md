Drones hit French troops near Erbil, wounding 6. Iranian-backed Iraqi militias have repeatedly targeted Western bases since the US-Israeli campaign on Iran began, with Britain reporting a similar attack this week. French President Emmanuel Macron has not invoked NATO's Article 5 and must now decide whether to reinforce or withdraw the 600 French troops in Iraq.
