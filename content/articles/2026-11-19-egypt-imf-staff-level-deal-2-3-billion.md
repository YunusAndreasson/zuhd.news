Cairo — IMF staff agreed to release $2.3bn to [Egypt](country:EG).

The agreement follows the final reviews of 2 financing programmes, and the IMF says reforms held Egypt steady through the Middle East war.

If the board approves, Egypt receives $1.5bn under its 48-month loan and $800m through the Resilience and Sustainability Facility.

Nothing is paid until the IMF's executive board approves.
