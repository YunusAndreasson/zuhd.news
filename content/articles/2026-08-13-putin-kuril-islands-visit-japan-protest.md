Iturup — Putin sampled fish roe on the disputed island.

It is his first visit to the chain, asserting possession over land [Japan](country:JP) still claims.

Soviet forces seized the islands in 1945, the reason [Russia](country:RU) and Japan never signed a peace treaty.

Foreign Minister Toshimitsu Motegi lodged a formal protest, with no legal recourse to reverse the visit.
