Moscow — Mayor says 2,730 Ukrainian drones flew toward the city.

[Russia](country:RU)'s own count averages 390 drones a day over the week to 17 October.

Mayor Sergey Sobyanin wrote that air defences destroyed most at distant approaches and 534 near the city, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

TASS's report does not say how many drones reached the city or what they hit.
