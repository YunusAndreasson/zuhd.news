Brussels — 13 Gaza students hold Belgian scholarships and no way out.

Belgium's own top universities call the delay a bargaining chip in migration politics, not a security question.

A scholarship needs an exit permit too, and one coalition party tied evacuation to new deportation centers.

Rectors demand Brussels assess the students on merit alone; the coalition has not set a date.
