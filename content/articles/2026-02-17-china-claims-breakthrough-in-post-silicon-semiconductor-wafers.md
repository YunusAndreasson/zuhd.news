A 6-inch single-crystal molybdenum disulfide wafer could succeed silicon. Researchers at Southeast University in Nanjing published a technique in Science that grows 2D semiconductor films 100 times faster than existing methods. The material enables thinner, more efficient transistors as conventional chips approach physical size limits.
