2 tankers burned in Iraq, killing at least 1 sailor. Attackers struck near al-Faw, Iraq's primary crude export terminal handling 4 million barrels daily, signalling that Iraq's decision to host US forces now carries a military cost. No group claimed responsibility, leaving Iraq's oil exports under unresolved threat.
