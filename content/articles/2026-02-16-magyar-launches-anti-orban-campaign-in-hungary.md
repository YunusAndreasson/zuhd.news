Hungary's opposition launched its strongest challenge in 16 years. Peter Magyar, a former ruling-party insider who broke with Prime Minister Viktor Orban in 2024, opened his Tisza party's campaign at a Budapest rally Sunday pledging to fight corruption and unlock 22.5 billion euros in frozen EU funds. Independent polls give Tisza a double-digit lead ahead of the April 12 parliamentary election.
