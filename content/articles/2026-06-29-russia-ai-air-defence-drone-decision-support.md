Moscow — [Russia](country:RU) will integrate artificial intelligence into air defence by November.

Defence Minister Andrey Belousov said civilian firms have built the system since April to give commanders decision support for detecting and countering [Ukraine](country:UA)'s drone swarms.

The system is 1 of 7 defence projects the ministry must complete by November.
