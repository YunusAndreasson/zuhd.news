Washington — Tokenized stocks get a five-year exemption.

The Securities and Exchange Commission's path favours Coinbase, Robinhood and Circle, analysts say.

[US](country:US) stock tokens must preserve dividends and voting rights, and venues face caps on trading volume.

Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong says voting rights are coming soon.
