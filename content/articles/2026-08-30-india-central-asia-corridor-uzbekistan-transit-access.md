Tashkent — [India](country:IN) has no land route to Central Asia.

Every India-to-Tashkent trade corridor currently transits Iranian ports strained by war.

[Pakistan](country:PK) blocks the direct path, so India's push here competes with [China](country:CN)'s rail links and [Russia](country:RU)'s security ties.

Modi's four-day tour of [Uzbekistan](country:UZ) and Kyrgyzstan yielded a friendship medal but no transit deal.
