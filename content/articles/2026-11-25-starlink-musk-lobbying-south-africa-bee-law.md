South Africa — SpaceX hired ex-Democratic Alliance leader Tony Leon's lobby firm.

[South Africa](country:ZA)'s empowerment law requires 30% ownership by disadvantaged South Africans, and keeps Starlink out.

Resolve arranged a SpaceX meeting with then-party leader John Steenhuisen to complain that Communications Minister Solly Malatsi moved too slowly.

Resolve confirms it organised the meeting but says it did not attend and cannot influence government decisions.

Malatsi, of the Democratic Alliance, was seeking changes to the law that keeps Starlink out.
