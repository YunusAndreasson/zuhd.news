Kermadec microbes get 13% of genes from others.

The finding bears on origin-of-life questions and lands as Pacific deep-sea mining pressure grows.

Ten kilometres down, these genes pass directly between unrelated species, not parent to offspring, aiding pressure and cold survival.

Further study of trench ecosystems could sharpen extremophile biology and environmental reviews for prospective seabed mining.
