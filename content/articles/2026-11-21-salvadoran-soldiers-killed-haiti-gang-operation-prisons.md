Port-au-Prince — 2 to 3 Salvadoran soldiers died handling explosives.

They are the first reported deaths since more than 2,000 troops deployed last month.

[El Salvador](country:SV) sent them to find 4 missing airmen, then Bukele began posting strikes on gang leaders' homes.

Experts say the deployment is a political gamble for Bukele, who seeks a third term, and risks civilians.

Haiti's justice minister said the first of 3 Salvadoran-built prisons launches soon, with no cost disclosed.
