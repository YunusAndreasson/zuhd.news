Karachi — 3 fishermen got life for 1.3 tonnes of charas bound for [Somalia](country:SO).

That volume is wholesale cargo on the Makran-to-Horn-of-Africa hashish route, not a courier run.

Maritime agents intercepted their boat 45 nautical miles offshore; the cargo's financiers went unnamed in court.

A fourth suspect, still at large, has his case file held dormant until arrest.
