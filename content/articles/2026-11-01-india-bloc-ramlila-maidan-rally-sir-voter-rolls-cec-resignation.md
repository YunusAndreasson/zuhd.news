New Delhi — Thousands rallied demanding the election chief resign.

Opposition parties allege the voter-list revision wrongly deleted names from [India](country:IN)'s electoral rolls.

Leaders including Rahul Gandhi, Mallikarjun Kharge and Mamata Banerjee marched from Connaught Place, a day after a mock impeachment.

The Bharatiya Janata Party rejects the allegations, and Home Minister Amit Shah said people "can only buy hatred" from Gandhi.

Polymarket traders give 19% odds that Gyanesh Kumar is out as commissioner by 31 December.
