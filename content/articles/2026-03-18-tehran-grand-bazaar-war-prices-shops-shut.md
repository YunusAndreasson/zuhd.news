Tehran's grand bazaar stands half-empty 3 weeks into war. Prices have surged past Iran's already-36% annual inflation rate, leaving one dental worker who earns $130 a month unable to afford new clothes for her children. Merchants who depend on the Nowruz and Eid shopping season — their highest-revenue weeks — now face an empty bazaar and no fallback income.
