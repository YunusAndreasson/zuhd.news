Washington — 49-50 vote saved Trump's $1.8bn ally fund.

Embedded in a $70bn immigration bill, the "anti-weaponization" fund can pay January 6-linked allies at [US](country:US) executive discretion, without congressional approval.

Republican Sen. Thom Tillis's counter-amendment to redirect the money to fraud enforcement failed 15-84.
