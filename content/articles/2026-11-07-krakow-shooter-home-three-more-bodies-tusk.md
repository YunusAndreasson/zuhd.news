Kraków — 3 more bodies found in gunman's home, Russian state agency TASS reported.

[Poland](country:PL)'s Prime Minister Donald Tusk confirmed two women and a man died there.

Polsat television reported it first, and Tusk confirmed it to the Onet news portal.

Tusk said many signs suggest the dead were members of the gunman's immediate family.
