China — BYD will own 18 car-carrier ships by 2029, up from 8.

Chartering a carrier costs $95,000 a day, up from $50,000 in February, Clarksons Research says.

Owning ships could cut BYD's per-car shipping cost by up to 40%, one industry estimate says.

BYD targets 2.5 million overseas sales next year, up from 1.9 to 2 million.
