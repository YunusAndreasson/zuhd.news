Abuja — [Nigeria](country:NG)'s drug regulator alerted consumers to a contaminated ibuprofen recall.

Strides Pharma recalled 90,000 [US](country:US) bottles after consumers found black particles in the suspension; the product was manufactured in [India](country:IN).

Regulators ordered quarantine of lots 7261973A and 7261974A; parents holding either should stop use immediately.
