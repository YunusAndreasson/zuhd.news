Mountain View — Google's generative-imagery feature in Google Earth lasted 24 hours before removal.

Courts and investigators treat satellite imagery as hard-to-fake evidence of strikes and mass graves.

The tool surfaced during the information war around February's [Israel](country:IL)-[US](country:US) strikes on [Iran](country:IR).

Most imagery circulating in the war carries no signature proving it wasn't generated.
