São Paulo — A defendant in a timber-smuggling case becomes the state's justice secretary.

Ricardo Salles cut federal environmental enforcement as [Brazil](country:BR)'s environment minister from 2019 to 2021.

Salles has been a defendant since 2023, accused of ties to an organisation that smuggled Amazon timber to the [US](country:US) and EU.

He takes office in January 2027, with the Supreme Court case against him still open.
