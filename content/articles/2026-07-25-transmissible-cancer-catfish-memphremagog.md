Newport — The tumor cells themselves are spreading between fish.

Genetic testing on Lake Memphremagog bullhead found tumors more related to each other than to their own hosts — only the fourth transmissible cancer ever documented, a University of Vermont team reported.

Anglers described the black lesions for over a decade before the July 22 study named the cause.
