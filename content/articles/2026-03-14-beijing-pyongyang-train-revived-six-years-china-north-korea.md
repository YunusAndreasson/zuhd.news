North Korea's only foreign rail link reopened Saturday. China is reinforcing ties with Pyongyang as US military attention shifts to the Iran conflict — reducing strategic pressure on East Asia. US President Donald Trump has signaled openness to Kim Jong-un talks, though no schedule exists as North Korea continues ballistic missile tests.
