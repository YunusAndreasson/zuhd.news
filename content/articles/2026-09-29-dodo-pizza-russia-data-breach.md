Moscow — Dodo Pizza, with 1,500 stores in 28 countries, confirmed a data breach.

Delivery databases double as address books of civilians, making them wartime targets inside [Russia](country:RU).

Dodo Pizza confirmed names, addresses, phone numbers and order histories were exposed, though it said no payment data was stored or taken.

DataSuckers claims 68 million records and 15 years of history, which Dodo Pizza has not confirmed.

DataSuckers says it will publish some of the data and is offering to sell the database for $100,000.
