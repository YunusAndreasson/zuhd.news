Seoul — A platinum-free catalyst ran 1,000 hours splitting water. Green hydrogen requires platinum-group metals costing ~$30,000/oz, concentrated in a handful of countries; this catalyst matches industrial current density without them. 1,000 hours is the lab-to-deployment threshold; whether it scales beyond bench quantities is unresolved.
