San Francisco — Communities are pooling data to bargain collectively.

Data collectives let creators jointly govern how their data is gathered and licensed, pushing back against Big Tech's extraction as AI's demand for training data grows.

The model echoes labor-union bargaining logic, aiming to shift leverage back toward the people who produce the data.
