Donetsk — Russian-installed head tells Ukrainian-held cities' residents to enter Russia.

The Geneva Conventions bar an occupying power from transferring an occupied population.

Denis Pushilin told Russian state news agency TASS residents must exit [Ukraine](country:UA) via third countries, enter [Russia](country:RU), then reach Donetsk.

No Ukrainian or independent account of the program is available.

Pushilin said housing preparations began early, and each participant will get a direct channel to a specialist.
