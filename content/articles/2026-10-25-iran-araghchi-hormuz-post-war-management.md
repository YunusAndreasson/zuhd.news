Tehran — Hormuz traffic averaged 11% below normal to 18 October.

[Iran](country:IR)'s foreign minister says the strait will not be managed as it was before the war.

Iran restricted transit after strikes began on 28 February, and a June interim deal with the [US](country:US) has since stalled.

Washington demands unrestricted navigation, while parliament speaker Mohammad Baqer Qalibaf threatens a response to any attack.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 2 November an 8% chance.
