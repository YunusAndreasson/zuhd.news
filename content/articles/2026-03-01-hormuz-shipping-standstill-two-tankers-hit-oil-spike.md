German container line Hapag-Lloyd halted all Hormuz transits Saturday. The Strait of Hormuz carries 20 million barrels of oil a day — 20% of global supply. UK bank Barclays warned Brent crude could reach $100 per barrel, a price that would cascade into food and electricity costs across Africa and South Asia.
