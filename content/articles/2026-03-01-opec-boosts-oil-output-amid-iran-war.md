Iran's Revolutionary Guards shut the Strait of Hormuz. The closure cut off a quarter of world seaborne oil, prompting the OPEC+ oil alliance to approve a 206,000 barrel-per-day increase — 1% of the strait's normal traffic. Analysts warned the move is unlikely to prevent a price spike when markets open Monday.
