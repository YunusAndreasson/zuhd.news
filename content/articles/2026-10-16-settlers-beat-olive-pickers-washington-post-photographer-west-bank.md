Abu Falah — Masked settlers beat American photojournalist Heidi Levine, 64.

Yesh Din has logged at least 170 settler attacks since 27 September, around the start of the olive harvest.

Settlers throw stones, torch trees and steal crops, and soldiers arrive afterward and fire on Palestinians, witnesses said.

Haitham Taysir, a US citizen, was beaten Thursday in his family's Burqa olive groves, the Vulnerable People Project said.

As of Friday, US ambassador Mike Huckabee had said nothing about either beating.
