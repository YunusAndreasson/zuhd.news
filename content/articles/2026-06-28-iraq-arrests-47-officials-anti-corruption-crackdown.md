Baghdad — Counter Terrorism Service raided the Green Zone overnight.

[Iraq](country:IQ)'s PM Ali al-Zaidi ordered 47 arrests including Ali Maarij, a deputy oil minister the [US](country:US) sanctioned in May for routing Iraqi oil to Iran-backed militias.

Several suspects fled before forces arrived; authorities sealed the zone and said the campaign was ongoing.
