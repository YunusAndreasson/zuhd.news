Golfito — Traffickers dissolved 560 kilograms of cocaine into liquid.

Dissolving cocaine into liquid strips its smell, letting it pass scanners and dogs; [Cuba](country:CU) found 10 kilograms hidden in shampoo bottles.

[Costa Rica](country:CR) is now checking whether clandestine labs process the drug on domestic soil.
