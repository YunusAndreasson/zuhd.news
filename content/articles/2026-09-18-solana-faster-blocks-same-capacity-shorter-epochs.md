San Francisco — Solana blocks now land 17% faster.

Total transaction capacity stayed flat, since the per-block compute budget did not move.

Block interval and capacity are separate knobs, so a faster clock buys latency, not scale.

Epochs now close in about 30 hours, leaving validators using offline signing less time to act.
