Kurmuk — [Sudan](country:SD)'s army recaptured the city from RSF forces.

The city sat on the RSF's main supply corridor from [Ethiopia](country:ET), and nearly 100,000 people fled its capture in March.

Displaced residents can only return once military engineers clear explosive remnants and repair utilities.
