India sent 2.5 tons of emergency medicine to Kabul. Pakistan struck a Kabul hospital on March 16, killing at least 400 people — the attack New Delhi cited in sending aid despite refusing to recognize the Taliban government. The Eid ceasefire between Pakistan and Afghanistan expires at midnight Monday.
