Rasuwagadhi — Flood deaths jumped to at least 160.

Hundreds more are missing across the closed [Nepal](country:NP)-[China](country:CN) border region.

Confirmed deaths track how far rescuers have reached on the valley's one road, not how much water fell.

Experts say an avalanche, not rainfall, triggered the flood before warning systems could fire.
