Culiacan — An alleged betrayal split the Sinaloa Cartel in two.

Factions loyal to "El Chapo" and "El Mayo" have fought since July 2024, after Guzmán López allegedly delivered El Mayo into [US](country:US) custody.

The rival Jalisco New Generation cartel is seizing territory left uncontested by the war.
