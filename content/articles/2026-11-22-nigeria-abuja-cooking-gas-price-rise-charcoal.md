Abuja — A 12.5kg cooking gas cylinder now costs up to N25,000.

Households are rationing gas and buying charcoal stoves to cut fuel bills.

Prices rose from N1,360–N1,700 per kg in October to N1,600–N2,000, with [Nigeria](country:NG)'s regulator citing low import volumes and weak distribution.

"Refilling my cylinder at N24,000 is no longer possible," said Obi Nwosu, a Gudu resident.

Regulators were ordered in June to clamp down on gas hoarding, and prices have risen since.
