San Francisco — Bitcoin's core developer proposed freezing 5.6 million BTC. Lopp says the coins are worth $420bn and quantum-vulnerable; their recovery by an attacker would trigger market panic before a sell-off. BIP-361 requires network-wide consensus; critics say freezing coins violates Bitcoin's unconditional ownership guarantee.
