Kfarshouba — [Israeli](country:IL) forces expelled farmers, then bulldozed their olive grove.

Olives cover 62,000 hectares, nearly a quarter of [Lebanon](country:LB)'s farmland, and harvest peaks now.

Defence minister Israel Katz says civilians cannot return south of the Litani, leaving hundreds of thousands of people displaced.

"The olive season is gold for people," said Chebli, a Kfarshouba farmer.

The June ceasefire required Israeli withdrawal, but Israel still holds 55 to 62 villages, about 6% of Lebanon.
