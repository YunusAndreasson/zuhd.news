Israeli settlers shot and killed 19-year-old Nasrallah Abu Siyam on Wednesday. Armed settlers attacked the village of Mukhmas northeast of Jerusalem, stealing livestock and injuring 4 others who tried to intervene. Abu Siyam is the first Palestinian killed by settler gunfire in 2026, according to the Palestinian Authority.
