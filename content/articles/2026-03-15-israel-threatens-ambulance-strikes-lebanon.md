The Israeli military threatened to bomb Lebanese ambulances. Israel has killed 26 medics in Lebanon since March 2; the Geneva Conventions allow ambulance strikes only with proof of military use, which Israel has not provided. Lebanon's Health Ministry has not said whether it will halt ambulance operations in combat areas.
