Sri Lanka twice refused US warplanes from Djibouti. Washington sought to base 2 planes armed with 8 anti-ship missiles at Mattala Airport as it wages war against Iran. Sri Lankan President Anura Kumara Dissanayake told Parliament on Friday that Sri Lanka would do everything possible to remain neutral amid ongoing US military pressure.
