Abuja — Dispenser water ran short across Abuja and Kano.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s cities replaced piped public water with private bottled networks that are now buckling as distributors fail to restock.

Supermarkets in Kano sold 13 bottles in 3 days, with customers booking ahead for the next delivery.
