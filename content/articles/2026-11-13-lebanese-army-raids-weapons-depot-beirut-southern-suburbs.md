Beirut — [Lebanon](country:LB)'s army seized a large weapons depot, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

TASS said the depot presumably belonged to Hezbollah, after 5 members were arrested in Bsharri.

Army intelligence raided Musharafieh, the army said, and detained 1 person while seizing weapons and ammunition from a secret depot.

President Joseph Aoun vowed on 19 October that authorities "will not tolerate security breaches."

The army's statement, as TASS quoted it, does not name the depot's owner.
