Sderot — Cabinet ministers of [Israel](country:IL) marched to Gaza's fence.

Finance Minister Bezalel Smotrich and National Security Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir joined the Nachala-organized march demanding rebuilt settlements evacuated from Gaza in 2005.

Prime Minister Netanyahu's Likud sponsored the rally despite his public denial that Gaza will be resettled.
