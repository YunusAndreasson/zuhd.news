Britain — A flexible solar cell reached 27.58% certified efficiency.

Flexible cells have a high power-to-weight ratio and bend, unlike glass-covered rigid panels.

A rigid molecule called Py6 orders itself on rough copper-indium-gallium-selenide cells, templating dense, void-free perovskite layers above.

The 27.58% cell measures 0.0749 square centimetres, and the paper's 1.01-square-centimetre device certified 25.19%.

Durability beyond 400 hours of 1-sun operation, where devices kept 93.8% of efficiency, remains the open test.
