Washington — Chegg and Penske lost their antitrust suits against Google.

The ruling is a setback for publishers losing traffic to Google's AI Overviews.

Judge Amit Mehta ruled Google had no formal agreement with either company, and an expectation of search traffic is not one.

Mehta wrote that the court is not unsympathetic to publishers, but antitrust law cannot replace legislation.

The European Commission is weighing the same questions, while the [UK](country:GB) has ordered an AI opt-out.
