Washington is negotiating a stake in Venezuelan oil.

It hands a foreign government equity in the ground beneath [Venezuela](country:VE)'s post-Maduro state.

The reserves are extra-heavy Orinoco crude that only a narrow band of Gulf Coast refineries can process.

The deal's structure — equity, contract or debt swap — determines if it outlasts Caracas's next government.
