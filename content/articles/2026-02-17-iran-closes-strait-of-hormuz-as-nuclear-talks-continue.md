Iran shut the Strait of Hormuz for live-fire drills. The closure blocked the inbound lane of a waterway that carries 20% of global oil supply — the first since US carrier groups began deploying to the region — while negotiators held indirect nuclear talks in Geneva. The timing leaves open whether Tehran is signaling leverage or testing Washington's response ahead of a deal.
