Gaza City — 67% of Gazans face acute hunger by December.

Child malnutrition eased after the 2025 ceasefire, but the Integrated Food Security Phase Classification says gains are reversing as aid volumes decline.

[Israel](country:IL)'s military-run aid body calls the finding biased, citing 1.8 million tons of food admitted since the truce.
