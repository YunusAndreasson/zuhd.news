Beijing — China's Commerce Ministry blocked exports to 10 US rare-earth miners.

MP Materials and USA Rare Earth — Washington's picks to displace Chinese processing — are among those barred from dual-use materials.

China's Finance Ministry separately banned 46 US defense contractors from government procurement.
