Riyadh — Saudi Arabia expelled Iran's military attaché and 4 diplomats. The expulsion ruptures the 2023 Beijing-brokered rapprochement that had stabilized Gulf relations — China's main diplomatic achievement in the region. A Turkey-Saudi-Egypt-Pakistan security pact signed days ago now becomes the vehicle for any Saudi military escalation against Iran.
