title: "In-Womb Stem Cell Therapy Works"
date: "2026-02-26T00:00:00Z"

Spina bifida brain damage reversed in all 6 cases. UC Davis surgeons augmented standard fetal surgery with placental stem cells, preventing nerve damage that surgery after birth cannot reverse. Results, published in The Lancet with FDA backing, will inform a larger Phase 2 trial.
