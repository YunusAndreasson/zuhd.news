Beijing — Huiyuan units lose the name and owe 97 million yuan.

Huiyuan Group stopped making Huiyuan-branded juice and switched to Zanjia Guoyuan on 19 October.

Beijing Huiyuan holds the trademark after its 2022 restructuring, and the court doubled 48.5 million yuan in losses as punitive damages.

Huiyuan Group says investor Shanghai Wensheng withheld 850 million yuan and breached the restructuring agreement.

Wensheng's 60% stake in Beijing Huiyuan stays frozen while the breach lawsuit awaits a hearing.
