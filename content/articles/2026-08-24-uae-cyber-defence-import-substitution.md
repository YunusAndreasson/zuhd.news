Abu Dhabi — Hackers followed February's missiles across the Gulf.

Banks, airlines and energy operators face attacks aimed at critical infrastructure.

AI cuts attackers' reconnaissance costs near zero, so the [UAE](country:AE) is building detection instead of importing US and Israeli tools.

Whether that domestic capability can keep pace with attack volume is still untested.
