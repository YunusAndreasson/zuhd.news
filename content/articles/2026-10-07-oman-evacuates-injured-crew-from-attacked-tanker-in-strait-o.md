Khasab — A projectile hit tanker On Peace, injuring 12 crew.

11 of the injured are Indian, and [India](country:IN) counted at least 10 seafarers killed by July.

[Iran](country:IR) effectively closed the strait after the US-Israel war began and attacks ships that avoid its designated route.

No group has claimed the attack, and the UK Maritime Trade Operations calls the weapon unknown.

[Oman](country:OM)'s air force evacuated 10 crew, and Brent futures rose to $101.51 Tuesday, against $72.48 before the war.
