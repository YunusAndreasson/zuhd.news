San Francisco — New York Times blocked the Internet Archive. The Archive stores 1 trillion web pages, including the only record of how articles looked before Times editors revised them; publishers cite AI training data as their reason for blocking access. The Guardian is following suit, and copyright lawsuits won't restore what disappears before any verdict.
