The valuation is a quarter of Shein's 2022 peak near $100bn.

New York and London rejected the listing over labor concerns, as the [US](country:US) and EU closed its duty-free import loophole.

Investors will watch whether Shein's margins survive tighter regulation without its old duty exemption.
