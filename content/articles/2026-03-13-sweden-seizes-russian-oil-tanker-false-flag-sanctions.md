Sweden seized a false-flagged EU-sanctioned oil tanker Friday. Russia maintains over 120 sanctioned tankers under opaque ownership and misregistered flags to keep oil flowing despite EU sanctions. Prosecutors must decide whether to charge the crew as Europe tests how far its sanctions can reach.
