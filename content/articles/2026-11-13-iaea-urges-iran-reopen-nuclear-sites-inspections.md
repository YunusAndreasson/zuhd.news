The agency cannot verify Iran's stockpile of near-weapons-grade uranium, its report says.

Iran passed a law suspending cooperation after the June 2025 [US](country:US) and Israeli strikes, and the UN agency withdrew its inspectors.

Iran told the UN agency this month that talks cannot resume while the US president threatens further armed aggression.
