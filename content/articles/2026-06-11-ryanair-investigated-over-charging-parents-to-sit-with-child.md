Ryanair charges £8 to seat parents with children.

[Britain](country:GB)'s watchdog opened an investigation, saying the fee may make parents pay for the airline's own child safety obligations.

Ryanair denied wrongdoing; the Competition and Markets Authority has not concluded whether the airline broke consumer law.
