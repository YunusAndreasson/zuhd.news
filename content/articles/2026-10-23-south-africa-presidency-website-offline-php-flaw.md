[South Africa](country:ZA)'s Presidency took its site down Friday as a precaution against what it called a global cybersecurity event.

The US Federal Bureau of Investigation said 2 critical PHP flaws could let attackers run code remotely on servers.

The [UK](country:GB)'s National Cyber Security Centre says a fix has been released.

The Presidency has not said when its site returns.
