Irkutsk — Dozens are quarantined after a plague-institute technician, 28, died.

Pneumonic plague spreads person to person through the air and kills within days untreated.

[Russia](country:RU)'s health watchdog Rospotrebnadzor says she died of pneumonia of unknown origin, and tests found no pathogens from her work.

"I don't think it's cause for alarm," said [US](country:US) Secretary of State Marco Rubio.

If tests confirm Yersinia pestis, Russia is supposed to notify the WHO.
