Washington — Reports say Trump will name the spy chief AI tsar.

The official who oversees [US](country:US) spy agencies would also oversee AI industry development.

Reports do not say whether Jay Clayton, a former Securities and Exchange Commission chairman, would keep the intelligence post.

A White House official called the reporting baseless speculation and said Trump will announce personnel moves himself.

Trump could announce the pick as early as Friday.
