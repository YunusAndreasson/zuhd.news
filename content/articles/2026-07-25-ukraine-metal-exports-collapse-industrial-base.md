Kyiv — Metal exports fell threefold in five years.

[Ukraine](country:UA)'s National Bank data show exports down from $15.7 billion in 2021 to $4.7 billion by 2025, per Russian state calculations.

The industry faces further losses as the EU cuts its steel import quota.
