Paris — France banned the annual Muslim gathering outright.

Police cited terrorism risk to cancel the event — absent since 2019 — while France advanced an anti-separatism law targeting Muslim associations.

Organizers sought an emergency injunction; a ruling was expected before the event's Friday opening.
