Kulevi — The refinery received its first Libyan oil Friday.

It costs [Russia](country:RU) a customer inside Moscow-friendly [Georgia](country:GE).

EU sanctions take effect in January 2027 if Kulevi keeps processing Russian crude, its owner previously said.

The sanctions deadline gives Kulevi refinery five months to make the switch permanent.
