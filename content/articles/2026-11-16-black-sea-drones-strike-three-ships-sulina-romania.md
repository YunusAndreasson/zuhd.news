Bucharest — Drones hit 3 ships inside Romanian waters.

[Romania](country:RO), a NATO member, scrambled fighter jets twice overnight.

Two ships were struck 4 and 5 nautical miles off the Sulina canal, Ukraine's Danube export route.

Romania did not name the drones' origin, and [Russia](country:RU) denies targeting Ukraine's neighbours.

Defence Minister Stefan Radu Oprea asks NATO allies for counter-drone equipment in Brussels on Tuesday and Wednesday.
