Brasília — [Brazil](country:BR) now delays $10,000-plus crypto transfers 24 hours.

The hold applies to withdrawals sent to self-custody wallets or foreign exchanges, targeting fraud recovery.

Resolution BCB No. 584 lets the central bank reverse the hold if a compliance review finds no wrongdoing.

The rule takes effect in January 2027.
