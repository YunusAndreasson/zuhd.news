Brisbane — AI designed proteins that switch on when triggered. QUT's international team made proteins that bind a target and flip conformation to produce a binary signal — previously only discoverable, not designable. Published in Nature Biotechnology, the work opens cheap biosensors for medicine and monitoring; no commercial product exists yet.
