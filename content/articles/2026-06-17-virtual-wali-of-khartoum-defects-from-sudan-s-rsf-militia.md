Khartoum — Fares El Nour, a presidential councillor in [Sudan](country:SD)'s Rapid Support Forces militia, has defected.

The RSF built a parallel government to claim legitimacy; El Nour was its Khartoum governor and a senior adviser to RSF commander Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo.

The RSF has not responded and separately arrested its South Darfur finance minister on corruption charges.
