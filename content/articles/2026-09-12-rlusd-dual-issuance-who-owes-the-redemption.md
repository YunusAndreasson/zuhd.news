San Francisco — Ripple plans two legal issuers for one stablecoin.

A European holder's redemption claim runs against a different entity than a [US](country:US) holder's.

The EU's Markets in Crypto-Assets law requires e-money-token issuers to hold reserves redeemable at par, a rule US frameworks don't impose.

Ripple's stablecoin chief pegs the corporate-treasury opportunity at $13 trillion a year, an unverified estimate.
