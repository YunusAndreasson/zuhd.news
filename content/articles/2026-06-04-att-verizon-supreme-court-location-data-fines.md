Washington — [US](country:US) Supreme Court upheld $104m carrier location-data fines.

Carriers sold user location to bounty hunters and others, then argued the Federal Communications Commission's fine process denied their jury-trial right.

Chief Justice Roberts ruled that carriers who refuse to pay can still demand a jury trial in collection proceedings.
