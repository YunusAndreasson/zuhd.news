São Paulo — US terror label traps [Brazil](country:BR)'s banks, not gangsters.

The designation makes banks unknowingly holding Primeiro Comando da Capital funds liable for [US](country:US) terror charges; the gang launders $2 billion annually, prosecutors said.

A federal prosecutor warned no bank in Brazil can be certain its books are clean of those funds.
