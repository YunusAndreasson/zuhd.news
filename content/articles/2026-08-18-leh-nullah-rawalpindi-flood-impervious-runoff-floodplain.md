Rawalpindi — Leh Nullah hit 20 feet as sirens sounded along its banks.

Traders lost millions and nine people were pulled from floodwater in the garrison city.

Decades of construction narrowed the channel and sealed its floodplain, so the same rainfall now runs off faster and higher.

The Met Office forecasts more rain through Tuesday evening.
