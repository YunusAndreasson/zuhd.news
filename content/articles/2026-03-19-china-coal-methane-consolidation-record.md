Beijing — Shanxi emits half of China's total coal mine methane. Consolidation shifted production to high-methane northern mines while abandoned southern mines continue leaking, pushing total emissions to a record 24 million tonnes. 4.8 million tonnes leaking from abandoned mines fall below China's 8% capture threshold, with no plan to bring them under regulation.
