Brasília — Hackers falsified [Brazil](country:BR)'s disaster alert system Saturday.

The platform warns of floods and landslides; false alerts erode the public trust that makes such warnings actionable when real disasters strike.

Authorities confirmed a suspected cyberattack; the breach mechanism has not been disclosed.
