The National Science Foundation terminated nearly 2,000 research grants and cancelled the lease on the only US icebreaker dedicated to Antarctic research. NOAA's National Snow and Ice Data Center lost access to military satellite data on sea ice, forcing a switch to Japanese satellites. US polar scientists are seeking positions abroad as decades of continuous monitoring datasets face permanent gaps.
