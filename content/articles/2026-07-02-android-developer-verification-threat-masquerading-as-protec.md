Mountain View — Roughly 4 billion Android devices face a new install gate.

Google's Developer Verification requires every app maker to register a verified identity before software runs, even outside the Play Store, F-Droid said.

F-Droid warns the rule structurally threatens anonymous, hobbyist and open-source app distribution on Android.
