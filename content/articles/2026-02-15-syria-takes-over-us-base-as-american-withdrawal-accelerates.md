US forces in Syria dropped from 1,500 to 900. The Syrian army took control of the al-Shaddadi base in Hasakah province after American troops withdrew under a ceasefire between Damascus and the Kurdish-led Syrian Democratic Forces, the latest in a series of handovers as Washington consolidates its pullback. Kurdish communities that relied on US presence as a security guarantee now face a Syrian government reasserting control over territory it lost a decade ago.
