Santa Marta — 24 scientists demanded 50 attending nations reject gas as a bridge fuel. The panel, outside UN climate channels, gave [Colombia](country:CO)'s summit 12 action steps including halting new fossil expansion. The summit runs April 24–29; implementing the steps requires the global consensus that failed at November's UN climate summit.
