Kyiv — Strikes killed at least 38 people across [Ukraine](country:UA) and [Russia](country:RU).

A Kh-101 missile killed 22 in Pryluky, 5 of them children, Chernihiv's governor said.

Ukrainian drones also set fire to a Yandex data centre in Ryazan region, the first major confirmed strike on a Russian data hub.

Russian officials say a Ukrainian drone attack on Novorossiysk killed 4, including a child, and wounded 29.

Yandex is restoring cloud services and has not said when the centre reopens.
