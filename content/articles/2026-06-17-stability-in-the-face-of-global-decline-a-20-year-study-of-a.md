Azores — Insects stayed stable here as global populations crashed.

Long-term same-site monitoring — the only method separating real trends from year-to-year noise — found no decline in the archipelago's arthropod communities.

Whether island isolation delays or permanently prevents the crash continental ecosystems face remains unresolved.
