New York — Hackers took customer data from 3 payment giants.

One breached vendor exposed customers of JPMorgan Chase, Visa and Mastercard at once.

The attackers accessed marketing data that included customer names, email addresses and phone numbers, the companies said.

Visa says its systems were untouched and no card numbers were affected.

None of the three has named the vendor or said how many customers were hit.
