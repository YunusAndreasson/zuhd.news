Rapper-turned-mayor Balen Shah contests Nepal's PM job today. Security forces killed 77 protesters, forcing Nepal's PM to resign and triggering a constitutional mechanism that installed former Chief Justice Sushila Karki as caretaker. Results are expected Friday, with India and China watching as any new coalition will tilt Nepal's foreign alignment between them.
