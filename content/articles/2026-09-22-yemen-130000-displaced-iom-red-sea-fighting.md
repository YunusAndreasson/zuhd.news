Mocha — 129,438 Yemenis have fled seven governorates, the UN says.

Families bear the cost of a strait fight most will never see.

Houthi missile and drone strikes toward Mocha and the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, chasing control of the chokepoint, drove people from coastal areas.

The International Organization for Migration says displacement keeps growing without adequate aid capacity for the newly arrived.
