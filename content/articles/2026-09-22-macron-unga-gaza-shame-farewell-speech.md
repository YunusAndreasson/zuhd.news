New York — not a single Gaza aid corridor has reopened.

Gazans still lack reliable food and medical supplies months after a declared ceasefire.

In his final UN address, [France](country:FR)'s Emmanuel Macron rebuked Trump's claim of ending the war, calling the gap a shame.

"Some boast of a supposed peace, but that peace has not reopened humanitarian access for a single second," Macron said.

France has sanctioned West Bank settlers but has not said whether recognition brings further action.
