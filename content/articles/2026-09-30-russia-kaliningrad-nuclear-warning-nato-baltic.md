Brussels — [Russia](country:RU) threatens nuclear response if NATO isolates Kaliningrad.

A private document also warns of Russian strikes on alliance decision-making centres from the conflict's outset.

Kaliningrad, a heavily militarised exclave, is sandwiched between [Poland](country:PL) and [Lithuania](country:LT), with no land link to Russia.

NATO spokesperson Allison Hart says the alliance is defensive and no exercise poses a risk to Russia.

[France](country:FR) has offered to discuss deploying its nuclear weapons in other European NATO states.
