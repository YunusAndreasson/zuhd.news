Ganjam — Odisha has 135 cyclone shelters and 1,600 temporary centres ready.

Arnab is due to cross the coast of Andhra Pradesh, in [India](country:IN), near Yanam around Wednesday noon.

The India Meteorological Department forecasts winds of 115-125 kmph and extremely heavy rain in Gajapati and Ganjam on 28-29 October.

Beaches in Ganjam stay closed until the forenoon of 30 October.
