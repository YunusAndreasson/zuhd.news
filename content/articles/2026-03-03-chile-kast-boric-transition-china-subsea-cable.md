A Chinese fiber cable derailed Chile's presidential handover. President-elect Kast accused Boric of hiding a China Mobile cable concession to run fiber from Chile to Hong Kong; Washington revoked 3 officials' visas over the project. Transition meetings remain suspended; Boric refused to retract the briefing claims Kast demanded.
