A right-wing activist died after a beating. The man, identified only as Quentin, was a member of Collectif Némésis. He was beaten on the sidelines of a conference at Sciences Po Lyon hosted by European Parliament member Rima Hassan. Lyon prosecutors opened an investigation for aggravated manslaughter but made no arrests. French President Emmanuel Macron called for calm.
