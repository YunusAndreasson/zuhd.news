Abuja — Lawmakers suspended all visits to South Africa.

It signals [Nigeria](country:NG) is now at open diplomatic odds with [South Africa](country:ZA).

The boycott follows attacks that have killed at least 98 Nigerians there since 2022, a minister said.

State assemblies may join, hitting Pan-African Parliament sessions hosted in Midrand.
