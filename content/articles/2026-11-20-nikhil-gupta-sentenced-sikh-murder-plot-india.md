United States — Nikhil Gupta drew over 19 years for murder-for-hire.

[US](country:US) prosecutors say an Indian intelligence officer, still at large, directed him.

Gupta was recruited in May 2023 to hire a hitman to kill Sikh separatist Gurpatwant Singh Pannun, a dual US-Canadian citizen.

[India](country:IN) denies involvement, and Canada's 2023 allegation over Hardeep Singh Nijjar's killing remains unresolved.
