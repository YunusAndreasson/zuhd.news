San Francisco — After training, more than 80% of recipients could read.

Until now, no treatment restored vision for more than 5 million people with macular degeneration.

Camera glasses beam infrared light onto a 378-pixel implant that replaces dead photoreceptors and powers itself from that light.

Europe approved Prima in July, and the system awaits [US](country:US) Food and Drug Administration clearance.
