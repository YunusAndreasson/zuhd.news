Seoul — Samsung forecasts 107.4 trillion won profit, a tech world first.

Higher memory prices raise costs for phones and consumer electronics, including Samsung's own.

AI data centres are absorbing high-bandwidth memory and ordinary DRAM and NAND alike, and supply is lagging demand.

TrendForce expects DRAM contract prices to rise 10% to 15% in the fourth quarter, slowing from roughly 60% in the second.

Samsung's detailed results, with earnings split by division, arrive on 29 October.
