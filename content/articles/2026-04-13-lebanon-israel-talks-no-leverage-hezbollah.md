Beirut — Lebanon is negotiating with Israel without leverage. Hezbollah's arsenal, built over decades with Iranian support, is degraded; Iran is now at war and cannot resupply, leaving Lebanon's government exposed. UN Resolution 1701 demanded Hezbollah disarmament in 2006; Israel now has the military position to enforce what diplomacy never did.
