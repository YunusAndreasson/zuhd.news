Rome — 312 schools evacuated over one emailed bomb threat.

Identical threats reached Ireland, Northern Ireland and Bulgaria, and leaders fear a coordinated hybrid campaign.

The email, signed with a Russian name already sent to European broadcasters, claimed a remote-detonated bomb sat in each [Italy](country:IT) school.

Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni said investigations found no evidence of concrete danger, and no authority has attributed the emails.

Meloni's cabinet holds an emergency meeting Wednesday afternoon.
