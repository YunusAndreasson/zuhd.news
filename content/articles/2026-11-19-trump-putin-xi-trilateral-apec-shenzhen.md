Shenzhen — Trump, Putin and Xi met, [Iran](country:IR)'s state-affiliated Mehr News Agency reported.

Xi said none of the three can change the others, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

Trump and Putin met earlier for 35 to 40 minutes and issued no joint statements, Mehr reported.

Mehr said reports expect the agenda to cover nuclear disarmament, the Ukraine war, the war on Iran and trade.
