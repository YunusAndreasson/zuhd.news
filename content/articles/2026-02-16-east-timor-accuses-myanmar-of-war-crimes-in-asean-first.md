Myanmar expelled East Timor's senior diplomat this week. The expulsion came after East Timor's prosecutors accepted a universal jurisdiction complaint on 2 February naming Myanmar military leader Min Aung Hlaing and documenting massacres, a hospital airstrike that killed 8, and sexual violence — the first time one Association of Southeast Asian Nations member has pursued war crimes charges against another. East Timor joined the bloc only 4 months ago, and the case now tests whether the group's tradition of non-interference can survive internal accountability demands.
