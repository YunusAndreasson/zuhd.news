El Geneina — A sack of millet has doubled to SDG600,000, a local journalist says.

West Darfur relies heavily on food imported through [Chad](country:TD), and currency swings add to the pressure on households.

The cost of 1,000 Chadian francs rose from SDG35,000 to SDG46,000 in a month, journalist Mohiuddin Zakaria said.

North Darfur's El Hamra area, by contrast, has a promising season, administrator Mohammed Bakr Suleiman said.

Darfur Bar Association head Saleh Mahmoud wants a 90-day truce so aid can reach camps.
