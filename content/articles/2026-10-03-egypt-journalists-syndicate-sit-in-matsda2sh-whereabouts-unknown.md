Cairo — Journalists refused to leave [Egypt](country:EG)'s syndicate after Saturday's sit-in ended.

Five days after their arrest, no authority has said where 6 Matsda2sh journalists are held.

The Supreme State Security Prosecution denied a report of 15-day detention and says it has not yet questioned them.

The Interior Ministry alleges Muslim Brotherhood affiliation, and Matsda2sh calls the accusations baseless.

The syndicate's board holds an emergency meeting Monday on how to respond.
