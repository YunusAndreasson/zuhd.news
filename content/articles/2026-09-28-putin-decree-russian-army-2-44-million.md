Moscow — Russian state news agency TASS said Putin set [Russia](country:RU)'s army ceiling at 2.44 million.

It puts Russia's wartime troop levels on a permanent legal footing as NATO debates Baltic defenses.

The decree raises the cap by 15,500 from July, marking the third increase since March, per TASS.

Troop numbers grew by nearly 50,000 between March and September, TASS said, with no ceiling in sight.
