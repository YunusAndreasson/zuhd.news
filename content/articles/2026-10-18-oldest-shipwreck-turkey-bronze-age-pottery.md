Kekova — Pottery cargo tentatively dates a wreck to 2700–2400 BC.

It would be the world's oldest shipwreck, 300 years older than the wreck at Dokos in [Greece](country:GR).

No timbers survive, so archaeologists dated it from large two-handled storage jars embedded in the rocky slope.

Earlier or later dates are possible, the team says.

Hakan Öniz of Akdeniz University in [Turkey](country:TR), who found the wreck in 2024, has yet to publish the results.
