[Ukraine](country:UA)'s Thursday strike on Sasovo shut a hub housing two of Yandex's three AI supercomputers.

Zelenskyy says Kyiv answers in mirror-like fashion for Russian strikes on its data centres, which caused temporary outages for roughly 100,000 households.

Yandex says the attacks struck its core operations and it is working to keep services running.

Zelenskyy has not said which targets come next, saying he cannot share all the details.
