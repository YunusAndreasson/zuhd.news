Hezbollah fired on Israel, breaking a 16-month ceasefire. The group re-entered the war after US and Israeli strikes killed Iran's Supreme Leader Khamenei, calculating Israel cannot fight on 3 fronts at once. Israel struck Beirut and ordered 50 south Lebanese villages to evacuate — the second mass displacement since 2024.
