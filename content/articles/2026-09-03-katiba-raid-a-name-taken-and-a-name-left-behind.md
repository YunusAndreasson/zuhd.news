Gaza City — Special forces abducted officer Muain al-Arabid.

His wife was killed and children hurt, though the raid's stated target was him alone.

[Israel](country:IL)'s military infiltrated the Katiba area in civilian vehicles, then used roughly ten airstrikes to cover its withdrawal, witnesses said.

Israel calls al-Arabid a Hamas figure; the Interior Ministry disputes that label.
