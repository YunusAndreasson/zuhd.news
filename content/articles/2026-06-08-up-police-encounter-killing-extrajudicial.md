Ayodhya — Uttar Pradesh police shot a wanted man dead.

Bhanu Pratap Singh, wanted across 3 districts for murder and extortion with a Rs165,000 bounty, had 40 pending cases.

State police have not opened an independent inquiry into the shooting.
