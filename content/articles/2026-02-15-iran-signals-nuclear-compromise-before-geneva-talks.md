Iran offered to dilute its uranium stockpile. Deputy Foreign Minister Majid Takht-Ravanchi told the BBC that Tehran would curb 60%-enriched reserves if Washington offers sanctions relief, but rejected demands to halt enrichment or discuss ballistic missiles. US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner meet Iranian negotiators in Geneva on Tuesday, with Oman mediating.
