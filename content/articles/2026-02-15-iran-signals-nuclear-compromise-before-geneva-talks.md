Tehran offered to dilute its enriched uranium. Deputy Foreign Minister Majid Takht-Ravanchi told the BBC that Iran would reduce its 60%-enriched stockpile if Washington discussed lifting sanctions, but ruled out halting enrichment entirely. A second round of indirect talks begins Tuesday in Geneva, with Omani mediators relaying between Iranian and US delegations.
