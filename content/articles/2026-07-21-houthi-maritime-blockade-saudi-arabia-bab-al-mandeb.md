Sanaa — Six ships have already turned back.

[Yemen](country:YE)'s Houthis banned shipping to [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) through Bab al-Mandeb, the Red Sea route that carries oil once Hormuz is blocked.

Yemen's government asks allies to treat the Houthis as part of a wider Iran-linked threat network.
