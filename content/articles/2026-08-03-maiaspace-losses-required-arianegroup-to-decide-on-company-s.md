Paris — MaiaSpace lost €37.5 million, wiping out its equity.

A law in [France](country:FR) forced parent ArianeGroup to vote on dissolving the reusable-rocket startup after equity turned negative.

ArianeGroup chose to continue MaiaSpace, which targets its first orbital flight in late 2027.
