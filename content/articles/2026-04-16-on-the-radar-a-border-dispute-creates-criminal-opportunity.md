Quito — Ecuador is imposing 100% tariffs on Colombian goods. A January security dispute opened informal border crossings to criminal groups; contraband is rising as Colombia deploys troops without coordinating with Ecuador. The tariffs take effect May 1; neither government has agreed to joint border enforcement.
