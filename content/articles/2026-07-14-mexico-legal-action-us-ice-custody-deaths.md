Mexico City — 17 Mexicans have died in ICE custody.

[Mexico](country:MX) sent cease-and-desist letters to Adelanto, California, where 4 citizens died, and asked the UN to review deaths recorded since 2025.

Mexico's embassy plans to file a formal complaint with the [US](country:US) Justice Department.
