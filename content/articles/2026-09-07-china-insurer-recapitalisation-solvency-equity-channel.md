Beijing — Regulators gave five insurers and three banks $53.6 billion.

More capital frees state insurers to buy stocks, propping up China's markets.

[China](country:CN)'s insurers must hold more capital against stocks than bonds, so more capital lets them buy more equities.

Insurance stocks fell 2.5% Monday on investor fears of dilution from the plan.
