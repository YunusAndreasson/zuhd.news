Washington — The National Academies refused to delete their climate chapter. Republican attorneys general forced the Federal Judicial Center to delete the chapter in February before targeting the National Academies. Fossil fuel lawsuits now depend on whether judges use the National Academies' intact chapter or the judiciary's deleted version.
