Manila — The Philippine Senate elected Sherwin Gatchalian as its new president, ousting a Duterte ally.

Gatchalian controls trial procedure for the chamber set to try Vice President Sara Duterte on impeachment charges.

The trial in the [Philippines](country:PH) opens within weeks, per the chamber's schedule.
