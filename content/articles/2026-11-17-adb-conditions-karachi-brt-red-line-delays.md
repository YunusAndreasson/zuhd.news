Karachi — Asian Development Bank gives the Red Line 10 months.

The bus line's cost has risen to $725 million since construction began in March 2022.

Officials say the bank extended the loan on condition TransKarachi meets milestones, including a new contract for the 12.8-kilometre Lot 2.

An official says most milestones are met, but TransKarachi's own completion date is June 2028.

The Numaish underground bus facility and airport fence relocation must finish by April 2027.
