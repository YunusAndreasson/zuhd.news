Beijing — Now the export control is on the person.

Foreign firms' [China](country:CN)-based staff carry personal exit risk in any technology transfer, a new due-diligence problem for joint ventures.

Citizens who break tech import or export rules abroad face exit bans of six months to three years after returning.

Analysts warn the "security" categories are vague and enforced case by case.
