Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) executed 2 men over police deaths, state-affiliated Mehr News Agency reported.

No lawyer, family or rights group has been heard, so only the judiciary's account exists.

Mizan, the judiciary's agency, says the Supreme Court upheld sentences for killing 4 police officers in Isfahan, including burning one alive.

The report names the men as Alireza Sepahi and Alireza Raisi and gives no trial date.
