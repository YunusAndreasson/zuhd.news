Mauritius — The curfew now runs 10 p.m. to 4:30 a.m.

It follows the worst unrest on the Indian Ocean island since 1999.

The government shortened the hours and extended the curfew for 3 more nights in a statement Friday.

The government decides after those 3 nights whether to lift it.
