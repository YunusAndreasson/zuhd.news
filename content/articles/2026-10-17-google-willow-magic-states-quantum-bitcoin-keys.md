United States — Google says Willow cut magic-state errors about 40-fold.

Some of bitcoin's oldest coins have had their keys exposed on-chain since they were mined.

Magic states fuel certain quantum operations and are consumed on use, so hardware making them cannot do other work.

Willow kept only 8% of attempts, and the result is not a 40-fold cheaper attack.

One modelled attack needs roughly 500,000 reliable magic states a second, and the researchers say larger-scale runs are still ahead.
