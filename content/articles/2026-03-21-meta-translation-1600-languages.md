Menlo Park — Meta released a machine translation system covering 1,600 languages. The new system extends the previous 200-language frontier to cover Indigenous, minority, and endangered languages whose speakers — 40% of humanity — had no reliable digital translation. Meta owns both the model and the training data for languages whose speakers have no alternative.
