London — Tesco is moving 40,000 server workloads off VMware.

Broadcom acquired VMware in 2023 and refused to honor Tesco's perpetual licenses, demanding 175% more — breach of contract, per a [UK](country:GB) High Court filing.

Full migration won't complete until end-2027; the High Court trial is scheduled for November 2027.
