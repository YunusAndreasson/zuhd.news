Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s National Assembly has 10 days to disclose committee perks.

Journalist Saddia Mazhar's information request went unanswered from 21 June until the commission intervened.

The Pakistan Information Commission ruled that publishing data online does not satisfy a specific request under the 2017 access law.

The commission reviews compliance on 19 November.
