Nicosia — RAF Akrotiri sits on 98 square miles Cyprus ceded to Britain in 1960.

Cypriot police arrested dual British-Azerbaijani national Rashad Sultanov, 44, there July 17, accused of surveilling the base for [Iran](country:IR)'s Revolutionary Guard.

London's first overseas National Security Act case now tests whose courts and extradition rules apply inside a sovereign base.
