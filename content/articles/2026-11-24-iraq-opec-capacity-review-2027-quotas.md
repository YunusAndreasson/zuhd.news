Baghdad — [Iraq](country:IQ) rejects an OPEC+ estimate of 4.9 million barrels daily, sources say.

Capacity sets each member's 2027 quota, so the estimate decides oil revenue.

The first-ever OPEC capacity review measures what each member can sustainably pump, and Iraq says its investment earned more.

OPEC+ meets on 29 November to decide its 2027 output policy.
