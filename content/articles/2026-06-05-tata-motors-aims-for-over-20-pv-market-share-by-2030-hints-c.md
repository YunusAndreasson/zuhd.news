Mumbai — Tata holds 40% of India's electric-vehicle market.

[India](country:IN)'s passenger-vehicle market is forecast at 6 million units by 2030; Tata holds 13.48% overall and targets 20%, chairman Chandrasekaran said.

The company has committed ₹35,000 crore in capital investment and will recalibrate the figure as it progresses.
