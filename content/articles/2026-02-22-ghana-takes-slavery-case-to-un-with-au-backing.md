40 African Union members back Ghana's push to declare the transatlantic slave trade the "gravest crime against humanity." President John Mahama will table the resolution before the UN General Assembly in March — the first formal bid to name a crime that colonial powers ensured was never codified in international law. The vote will test whether former slave-trading nations accept legal recognition before reparations talks begin.
