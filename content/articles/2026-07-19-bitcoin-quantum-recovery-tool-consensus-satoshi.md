New York — 34% of bitcoin sits in quantum-vulnerable addresses.

Project Eleven built a zero-knowledge recovery tool for BIP-361, which would freeze exposed coins after five years but exclude Satoshi Nakamoto's 1.1 million dormant BTC.

The tool still needs contentious consensus changes bitcoin's leaderless network has never agreed to make.
