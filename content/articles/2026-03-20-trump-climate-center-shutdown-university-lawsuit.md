Federal agencies are dismantling America's top climate research center. A university consortium sued to halt the Trump administration's breakup of the National Center for Atmospheric Research — NCAR powers hurricane, flood, and climate models for 130 US universities. The suit targets NOAA and 3 other federal agencies, but NCAR's computing facilities and headquarters are already being transferred.
