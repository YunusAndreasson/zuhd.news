Gravity may heal itself at the smallest scales. Physicist Astrid Eichhorn's asymptotic safety theory holds that space-time becomes a self-similar fractal below the Planck scale — instead of the laws of physics breaking down. Eichhorn is now computing how matter and gravity couple at Planck scales, the next test of whether the theory holds.
