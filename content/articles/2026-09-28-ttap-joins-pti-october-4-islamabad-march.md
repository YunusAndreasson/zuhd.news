Islamabad — Opposition alliance TTAP will join PTI's Oct. 4 march.

The march demands the release of Imran Khan, [Pakistan](country:PK)'s most popular jailed politician.

TTAP initially resisted a week's postponement of the march but agreed to join once PTI reset the date to October 4.

"As the PTI is our partner, we will support them for the Oct. 4 march," said Yousafzai, TTAP's spokesperson.

Whether authorities let the Oct. 4 march reach Islamabad, after a week's delay, remains unclear.
