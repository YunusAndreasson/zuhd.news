Islamabad — El Niño heat could kill 451,000 extra people by February.

[Nigeria](country:NG) projects 31,400 of those deaths and Indonesia 19,300, the University of Chicago's Climate Impact Lab says.

Eight of 11 El Niño events from 1951 to 2012 brought below-normal monsoon rain to [Pakistan](country:PK), its meteorological department found.

Three El Niño years, 1977, 1994 and 1997, brought above-normal rain, so the pattern guarantees no drought.

The World Meteorological Organisation expects a peak near year-end, with effects lasting well into 2027.
