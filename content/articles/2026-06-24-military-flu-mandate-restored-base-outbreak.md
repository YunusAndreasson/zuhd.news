San Antonio — 222 recruits sickened; flu mandate restored.

Only 40% of Lackland Air Force trainees were vaccinated when the outbreak began, 2 months after Defense Secretary Hegseth ended the requirement.

The [US](country:US) Army is expanding the restored flu mandate to troops deploying overseas.
