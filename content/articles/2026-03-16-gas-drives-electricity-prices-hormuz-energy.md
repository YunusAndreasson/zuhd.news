Gas sets every electricity price, even in mostly-renewable grids. In merit-order markets, the last plant dispatched to meet demand — almost always gas — sets the price for every kilowatt sold. When Hormuz closed, oil hit $100 and the spike reached household electricity bills across Europe and Asia.
