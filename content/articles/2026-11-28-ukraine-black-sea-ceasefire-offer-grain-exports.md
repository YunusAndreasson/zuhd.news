Kyiv — [Ukraine](country:UA)'s grain exports had fallen 76% year on year by August.

Both Ukraine and [Russia](country:RU) are major grain exporters, so port strikes reach global food supplies.

Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha said Moscow wants oil transport included, but Kyiv says it belongs in an energy ceasefire.

Kyiv awaits Moscow's reply, while Washington said on 31 October it was reviewing Turkish and Indian proposals to keep grain and energy ships moving.
