Oxford — Ships with trackers off filled 32% of seven gannets' audio.

Ships can switch off tracking to evade pirates or to conceal illegal fishing.

Researchers fitted gannets, shearwaters and a petrel with location loggers and audio recorders, then matched encounters against Automatic Identification System data in Science Advances.

The study did not set out to learn why any vessel switched its tracker off.

Lead author Jonathan Rutter says the recordings could improve risk assessment, not enforcement.
