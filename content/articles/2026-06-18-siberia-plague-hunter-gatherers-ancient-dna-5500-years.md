Irkutsk — 5,500-year-old plague DNA found in Siberian hunter-gatherers.

Oxford researchers found plague DNA in teeth from 4 burial sites — overturning the theory that epidemic disease requires farming and dense settlement.

The strain killed children disproportionately, triggering an immune overreaction, researchers said.
