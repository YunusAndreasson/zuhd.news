Cairo — [Egypt](country:EG)'s parliament preserved Al-Azhar's control over divorce law.

Critics say Al-Azhar's state-granted family-law monopoly makes the deadlock a political choice, not a religious one.

The bill leaves unchanged women's need to sue in court to collect 2 years' post-divorce support.
