Basel — The Bank for International Settlements warned the AI boom rests on circular financing.

The bank said AI firms' interlocking financing deals concentrate systemic risk; a bust could trigger inflation atop high sovereign debt.

The bank offered no policy prescription; high sovereign debt limits central banks' room to respond to an AI shock.
