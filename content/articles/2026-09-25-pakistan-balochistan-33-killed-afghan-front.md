Nushki — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s military says it killed 33 militants in 4 days.

The army's media wing supplied every figure, and no independent count accompanied the statement.

Islamabad brands Baloch groups as [Indian](country:IN) proxies and the banned Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan as khawarij, framing both as foreign-backed.

The military says clearance under Operation Shaban continues until it neutralises the threat.
