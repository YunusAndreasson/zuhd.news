Beijing — [China](country:CN) freed 550 billion yuan ($82 billion) of unused local borrowing room.

Cash-strapped lower-tier governments get liquidity quickly as policymakers intensify efforts to bolster the economy.

Debt quotas are borrowing room already authorised, so the Ministry of Finance needs no new legislative approval to release them.

Local governments now choose how much to borrow to plug budget gaps and fund infrastructure.
