Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK) gave its anti-corruption bureau an AI investigation system.

The National Accountability Bureau partnered with a state AI center to automate complaint screening, financial analysis, and case referrals, officials said.

The bureau has not disclosed how AI decisions will be reviewed or contested.
