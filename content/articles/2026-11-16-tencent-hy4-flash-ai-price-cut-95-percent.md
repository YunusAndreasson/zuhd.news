China — Tencent prices Hy4 flash at $0.07 per million input tokens.

It targets office work: research, slide creation and spreadsheet editing.

The model has 224 billion parameters but activates only 12 billion per forward pass, with a one-million-token context window.

The 95% cost cut, one-twentieth the full Hy4's cost per task, comes from Tencent's own WorkIndex benchmark.

Tencent plans to release the model's weights and list it on OpenRouter, with WorkBuddy free until 30 November.
