Mokha — The Houthis have seized the historic coffee port, 75 kilometers from Bab al-Mandab.

Holding the coast lets the group threaten tankers with shore missiles and radar, not just strikes at sea.

The rebels first held Mokha in 2014 before a Saudi-led coalition retook it in 2017.

Fighting has reportedly killed hundreds, and the Houthis are now advancing on Dhubab, nearer the strait itself.
