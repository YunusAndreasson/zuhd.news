Jakarta — ASEAN lacks a collective response to US Section 301 tariffs.

Section 301 lets the [US](country:US) impose tariffs unilaterally; members negotiating alone lose the leverage a unified bloc could hold.

No ASEAN summit has produced a collective tariff response; bilateral deals with Washington remain each member's default.
