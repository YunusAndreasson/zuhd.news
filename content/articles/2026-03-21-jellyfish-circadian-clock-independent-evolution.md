A jellyfish keeps time without clock genes. Hydrozoans lost the clock genes nearly all animals share but evolved an independent 20-hour timer, suggesting biological timekeeping has more than one origin. Scientists are now studying how the jellyfish's timer synchronizes to sunlight without the genes considered essential for animal circadian rhythms.
