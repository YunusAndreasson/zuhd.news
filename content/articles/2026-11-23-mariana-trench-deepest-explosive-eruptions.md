Mariana Trench — A robot found the world's deepest explosive volcanic eruptions.

Challenger Deep lies almost 11,000 metres down, and scientists have asked why it is that deep.

The Pacific plate began sliding under the Philippine Sea plate 52 million years ago, forming the trench.

A remotely operated vehicle collected the samples around the Deep.
