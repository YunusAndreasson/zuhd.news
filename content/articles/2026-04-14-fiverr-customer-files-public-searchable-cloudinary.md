Tel Aviv — Hundreds of freelancers' tax returns are Google-searchable via Fiverr's file storage. Fiverr used unsigned Cloudinary URLs for all client-worker transfers, making Form 1040s indexable — a GLBA violation it compounded by buying Google Ads for tax-filing keywords. Fiverr gave no response after 40 days of responsible disclosure.
