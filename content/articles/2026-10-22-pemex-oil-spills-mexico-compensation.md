Mexico — Pemex has reported nearly 100 spills a year for four years.

A February Gulf spill closed fishing for 30 days and crashed the local market.

Tallies over different periods disagree: 798 to financial regulators, 5,517 in sustainability reports, 274 counted by the environment agency.

Nothing suggests Pemex broke reporting rules, and the company has not commented.

Fishers like Andrés Palma still face contamination fears and a crashed market months after the ban lifted.
