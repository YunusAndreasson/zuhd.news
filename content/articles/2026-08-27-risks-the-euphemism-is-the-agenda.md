Beijing — Wang Yi told Washington to "remove disruptions."

The remark signals discord ahead of a possible Xi Jinping-Washington summit.

Foreign Minister Wang urged implementing "the important consensus" from the last [China](country:CN)-US leaders' summit, a diplomatic signal Washington has not delivered.

The [US](country:US) ambassador called talks "productive," without naming what still divides them.
