Cambridge — MIT injected working liver tissue without surgery.

Hepatocytes in hydrogel microspheres attached to mouse blood vessels and survived 2 months — a bridge for patients awaiting a donor organ.

Researchers are testing whether microspheres can deliver immunosuppressants locally to limit rejection.
