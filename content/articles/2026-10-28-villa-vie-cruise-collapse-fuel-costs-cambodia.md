Sihanoukville — 203 residents got 7 hours to leave their ship.

Some sold everything to buy cabins starting near $85,000.

Villa Vie said monthly fuel costs per cabin rose from $1,800 to between $6,000 and $8,000 in parts of Asia after the [Iran](country:IR) war.

Passenger Randy Cassingham wrote that the ship peaked at 350 residents against 650 capacity and launched four months late.

Villa Vie says it is seeking temporary housing, and the ship leaves 31 October.
