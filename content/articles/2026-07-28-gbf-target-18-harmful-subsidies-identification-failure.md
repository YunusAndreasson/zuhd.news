Montreal — Just 21 countries named their own harmful subsidies.

The 2025 deadline only asked states to list subsidies damaging biodiversity, not cut them, and naming one creates a domestic political cost with no offsetting benefit, Carbon Brief's analysis found.

The 32 reporting countries logged $270bn yearly against a $500bn 2030 phase-out target.
