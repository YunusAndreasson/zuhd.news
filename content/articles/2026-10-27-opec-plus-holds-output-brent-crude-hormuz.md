Moscow — OPEC+ is set to hold targets that Gulf members miss.

[Iran](country:IR) war disruption has cut Gulf exports for months, with Brent at $110.80 on 26 October.

Three sources said the 7 core members meet online Sunday, with no final decision made.

Putin told the Gas Exporting Countries Forum OPEC+ "provided for predictability of the market situation and stable supply volumes."

The group has not finished the capacity review that sets 2027 baselines.
