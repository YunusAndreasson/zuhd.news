Montreal — No facility on Earth is built to open the container.

McGill and Strategic Threat and Analysis researchers propose a lunar biocontainment lab, since Mars sample return needs both total containment and zero contamination in one building.

Whether such a facility gets built rests with the few states that can fly a sample-return mission.
