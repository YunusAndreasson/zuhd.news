Jerusalem — Judges told prosecutors a Netanyahu bribery conviction is unlikely.

[Israel](country:IL)'s caretaker prime minister demanded the whole trial end immediately as he fights to stay in power.

Prosecutors allege Netanyahu granted Bezeq regulatory favours worth about 1.8 billion shekels in exchange for positive coverage on a news website.

The court said it was not a ruling, and the attorney general will review it.

Gadi Eisenkot has until 6 December to form a government, or Netanyahu gets the next try.
