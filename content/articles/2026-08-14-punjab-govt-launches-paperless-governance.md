Lahore — Punjab linked 6,000 government systems to one network.

Digital services aim to cut corruption by removing face-to-face contact with officials.

E-procurement alone processed 1.29 million tenders worth 6 trillion rupees, officials said.

Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz ordered continuous applicant feedback as the rollout continues.
