Nawalparasi — [Nepal](country:NP) uses biodykes to protect riverbanks from erratic rainfall.

Less than 1% of global climate finance reaches indigenous peoples, who bear the most severe flood, drought, and landslide risk from the crisis.

Whether donor finance will reach indigenous stewards — currently excluded — is the unresolved question.
