Argentina's Senate gutted decades of Perón-era labor protections. The 42-28 vote shifts wage negotiations from national agreements to individual companies and cuts severance pay. President Javier Milei heads to New York to court Wall Street investors while unemployment has overtaken inflation as Argentines' chief concern.
