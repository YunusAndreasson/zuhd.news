Seremban — A Malay-nationalist wave took Negeri Sembilan from Pakatan Harapan.

Rural seats carry disproportionate weight in [Malaysia](country:MY)'s electoral map, so a governing coalition can hold cities and still lose states seat by seat.

The defeat is the latest ahead of the next general election, testing Anwar Ibrahim's multiracial coalition.
