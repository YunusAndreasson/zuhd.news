Ulm — Nickel electrodes work as nickel dioxide, not oxyhydroxide as assumed.

Nickel is a cost-effective, robust catalyst for alkaline water electrolysis, a key route to climate-neutral hydrogen.

A research team at Ulm University determined the structure under reaction conditions and published it in Nature Catalysis.

The announcement describes no catalyst built on the finding yet.
