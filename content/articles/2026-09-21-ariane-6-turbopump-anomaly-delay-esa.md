Paris — Ariane 6 may fly 1 or 2 more times in 2026, not 3 or 4.

ArianeGroup and Arianespace said they must adjust this year's mission schedule after additional production-line checks.

Reports say engineers found a turbopump anomaly in August ground tests, and turbopumps feed propellant into the engine's combustion chamber.

The companies say the industrial ramp-up to 9-10 launches a year by 2027 remains on track.

Officials are deciding whether to repair the pump in French Guiana or replace it.
