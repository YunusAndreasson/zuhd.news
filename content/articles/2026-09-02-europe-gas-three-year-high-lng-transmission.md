Amsterdam — European gas topped €75 per megawatt-hour Wednesday.

Households across the continent face higher heating bills as the price hits its highest since early 2023.

Europe buys almost no Iranian gas, so LNG competition after renewed [US](country:US) strikes on [Iran](country:IR) drove the jump, not lost supply.

Traders are watching whether the Strait of Hormuz standoff escalates further.
