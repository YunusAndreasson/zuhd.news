London — A microRNA suppressing one gene impairs womb lining.

Polycystic ovary syndrome affects roughly one in ten women of reproductive age; researchers link miR-301a-3p's suppression of TSPYL2 to failed embryo implantation, tested in mice and patient cells.

The manuscript is an unedited early release; the mechanism has not been tested as a treatment.
