Tehran — Fars province holds 161 billion cubic meters recoverable.

[Iran](country:IR) has gas reserves but lacks the sanctioned technology to extract and export it.

US sanctions since 2018 have barred the compression and LNG equipment needed to develop new fields, Oil Minister Paknejad said.

Strikes this year on gas facilities have pushed new production years away.
