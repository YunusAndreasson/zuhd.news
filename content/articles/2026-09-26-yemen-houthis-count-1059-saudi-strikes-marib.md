Yemen — Iranian state outlet Mehr reports 1,059 Saudi strikes, per Houthis.

Spokesman Yahya Saree says 27 strikes in 24 hours hit bridges and roads across 6 provinces.

Saree said F-15 and Typhoon jets flew from Khamis Mushait and Taif airbases to hit Taiz, Marib, Saada, Amran, Ibb and Hajjah.

The report carries no response from [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) and no independent verification of the count.
