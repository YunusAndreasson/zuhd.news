Moscow — [Russia](country:RU) offers the [US](country:US) as much diesel as it wants, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

Deputy Prime Minister Alexander Novak made the offer to a Vesti reporter asking how much Moscow would supply.

Vladimir Putin said Moscow told Donald Trump in a 9 October call that it stands ready to supply petroleum products.

TASS gives no volume, price or start date for the diesel deal.
