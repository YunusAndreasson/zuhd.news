Tigray — Fighting resumed in Sharari, western Tigray.

Amnesty International warned civilians face renewed abuse; the 2022 Pretoria agreement stopped the shooting but left Eritrean troop presence and disputed territory unresolved.

[Ethiopia](country:ET)'s federal government has not responded to the Tigray forces' allegations of attack.
