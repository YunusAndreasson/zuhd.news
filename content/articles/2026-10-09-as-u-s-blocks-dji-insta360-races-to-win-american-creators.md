New York — The [US](country:US) brings 30% to 40% of Insta360 revenue, its co-founder says.

The Federal Communications Commission has blocked DJI from selling new drones and cameras in the US.

DJI took 73% of global handheld camera shipments to Insta360's 20%, and the Defense Department calls it a Chinese military company.

Insta360's drone brand Antigravity won approval for its A1 just before the ban on new foreign drones took effect.

Consultant Chris Pereira says Antigravity could shield the camera business if restrictions on Chinese drones widen.
