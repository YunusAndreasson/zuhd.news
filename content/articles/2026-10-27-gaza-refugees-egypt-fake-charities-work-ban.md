Cairo — A fake charity emptied one refugee's mobile wallet of $1,200.

Palestinians in [Egypt](country:EG) cannot get residency or legal work, and aid has thinned since the ceasefire.

Anonymous forms collect passport photos, callers then ask for a wallet code, and victims without residency cannot file police reports.

Mohammed Odeh Mahmoud, who paid $140 an adult for permits, has been unable to renew them.
