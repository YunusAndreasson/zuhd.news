Drug money allegations have reached Colombia's sitting president. US prosecutors in Brooklyn and Manhattan are examining whether Gustavo Petro solicited campaign donations from drug traffickers, with Colombia's May 31 presidential election 2 months away. Petro denied the claims, but the probe remains open as Colombia heads into the vote.
