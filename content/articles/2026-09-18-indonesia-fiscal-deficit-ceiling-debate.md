Jakarta — Lawmakers debate breaking [Indonesia](country:ID)'s 3% deficit ceiling.

Some legislators argue the government needs room beyond it to fund the agenda.

The cap dates to the aftermath of the 1997-98 Asian financial crisis, when unchecked debt cost Jakarta its economic sovereignty.

The rupiah has held near 17,740 per dollar as of Friday, with the committee's decision pending.
