Berkeley — Earth's most abundant enzyme, rubisco, forms dimers far more widely than assumed.

A diversity-driven survey of its superfamily found protein pairing prevalent across evolutionary variants — not limited to the few forms previously studied.

The structural map is groundwork for engineering more efficient carbon fixation into crops.
