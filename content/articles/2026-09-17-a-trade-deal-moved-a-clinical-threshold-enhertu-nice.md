London — NICE will now fund Enhertu for 1,000 women.

The reversal followed a [US](country:US)-[UK](country:GB) trade deal, not a new clinical trial.

NICE funds drugs only below a cost-per-QALY threshold that has sat flat for two decades.

Whether the raised threshold will unlock other refused drugs remains unclear.
