Two-thirds of Cuba lost power on Wednesday. A pipe burst at the Antonio Guiteras thermoelectric plant east of Havana, whose ageing infrastructure has been starved of spare parts by US sanctions. Energy officials estimated 72 hours to restore power, with the plant's repeated failures this year raising fears of permanent grid collapse.
