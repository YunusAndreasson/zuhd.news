Quantico — The 31st Marine Expeditionary Unit deployed without its own sea drones.

Col. Chris Niedziocha says Marines should never go where a robot hasn't.

He wants uncrewed surface vessels to scout contested beaches with sonar before landings, a mission the Navy largely holds.

Niedziocha calls the Corps "fast followers" on robotics, not leaders.
