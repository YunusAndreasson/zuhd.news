6.98 million bitcoin — $440 billion — sit in wallets whose cryptography quantum computers will eventually crack. Early transactions, including Satoshi Nakamoto's estimated 1 million coins, exposed their public keys permanently on-chain, and researchers now estimate the decryption timeline at 2 to 3 years. The community is debating whether to freeze those coins preemptively — the most consequential governance decision in Bitcoin's history.
