Singapore — 260 square meters of plants cooled a street 5°C. Conventional cooling parks require hectares — this study found dense native plantings produce the same effect at city-block scale. The findings offer other tropical cities a low-cost urban cooling blueprint ahead of El Niño, forecast to return by late 2026.
