WHO's Dubai hub is frozen, blocking 50 emergency requests. Jebel Ali port, the region's largest container terminal, caught fire from intercepted missile debris, cutting Dubai's aid hub off from sea and air transit. The UN Refugee Agency warned Africa-bound cargoes rerouting around the Cape of Good Hope face an extra 3-week delay.
