Santa Clara — Nvidia authorized a record $150 billion share buyback.

It surpasses Apple's 2024 record even as Nvidia trades near its cheapest valuation in a decade.

Nvidia trades at 16.5 times forward earnings, half its 15-year average, even as it forecasts 70% revenue growth.

Export controls went undiscussed at the Trump-Xi summit, yet Huang's influence may still reopen [China](country:CN)'s market to Nvidia chips.

Nvidia expects to use the $235 billion authorization through fiscal 2028, betting artificial intelligence demand outlasts scrutiny.
