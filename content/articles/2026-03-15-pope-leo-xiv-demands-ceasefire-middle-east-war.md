A US missile killed 165 people at an Iranian school. Pope Leo XIV, the first American pope, called on "those responsible" to stop the war — a break from the Catholic Church's tradition of avoiding explicit warmaker attribution. The US, which launched the strike, says an investigation is ongoing and has not responded to the pope's demand.
