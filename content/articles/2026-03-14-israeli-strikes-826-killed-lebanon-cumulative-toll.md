826 dead in Lebanon — 106 of them children, 31 paramedics. Israeli forces killed them over roughly 3 weeks, approaching the 1,200 killed in all of Israel's 34-day 2006 Lebanon war. Rescue teams are still recovering bodies from a healthcare centre struck overnight in southern Lebanon.
