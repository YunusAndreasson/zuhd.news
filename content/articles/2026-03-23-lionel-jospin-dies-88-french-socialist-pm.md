Paris — Jospin, France's last Socialist prime minister, died at 88. His 5-year government gave France the 35-hour work week and civil unions — reform achievements the left has not matched since. The Socialists have not governed France since Jospin lost the 2002 presidential first round to Jean-Marie Le Pen.
