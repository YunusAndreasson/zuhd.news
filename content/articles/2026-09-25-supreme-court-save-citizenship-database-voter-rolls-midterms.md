Washington — Texas officials found SAVE wrongly flagged 578 citizens.

The [US](country:US) Supreme Court on Friday let states screen voter rolls against that database.

An unsigned emergency order lifted a lower court's block on SAVE, over dissents from all 3 liberal justices.

The court confirmed federal law bars systematic purges within 90 days of an election, blunting the effect before November.

The court will decide next term, in an Arizona case, whether that ban covers noncitizens.
