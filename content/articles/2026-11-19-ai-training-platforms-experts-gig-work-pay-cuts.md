Venezuela — Rogelio's family earned $20 in a day, then under $5 a week.

The same platforms now recruit physicians, lawyers and PhDs to train models.

Outlier, run by Scale AI, advertises up to $180 an hour for [US](country:US) physicians but $7.50 for Tagalog work in the Philippines.

Scale AI attributed its March 2024 exit from Kenya, Nigeria and Pakistan to security, then acknowledged Kenyan workers got no notice.

Higher credentials can raise the advertised rate, but the platform still sets pay and conditions.
