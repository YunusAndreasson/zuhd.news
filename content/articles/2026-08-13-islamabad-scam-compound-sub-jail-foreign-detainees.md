Islamabad — Officials wired a housing-society block with barbed wire.

More than 500 foreigners became overnight detainees, with no purpose-built jail to hold them.

Cyber-scam compounds tied to Myanmar and Cambodia have spread into [Pakistan](country:PK), which has no legal category for these workers.

Which nationalities are held, and whether any consulate has been given access, remains unstated.
