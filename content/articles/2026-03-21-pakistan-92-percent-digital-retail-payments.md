23% of Pakistani adults have no bank account. Pakistan's retail payments hit 92% digital last quarter, driven by 645 million transactions on the government-run Raast instant payment system and 126 million mobile banking users. The unbanked 23% face exclusion from basic commerce as cash becomes the exception, not the norm.
