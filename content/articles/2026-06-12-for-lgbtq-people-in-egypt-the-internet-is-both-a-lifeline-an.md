Cairo — [Egypt](country:EG) weaponises cybercrime law against private conduct.

Under the 2018 Anti-Cybercrimes Law, security forces use apps to lure targets, turning platform data into prosecution evidence without a physical act.

The Transat report documents systematic targeting; no cybercrime law amendment is pending.
