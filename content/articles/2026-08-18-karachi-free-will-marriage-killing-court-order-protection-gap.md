Gulshan-i-Hadeed — Fatima Zehra, 18, was shot dead.

She was killed hours after a court affirmed her free-will marriage.

Police dropped the couple at a jurisdiction boundary instead of escorting them home, as the court had ordered.

One suspect is under arrest, as [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Sindh sees few convictions for such killings.
