Opelika — A modern engine's power is a settings file.

Tuner APR now rewrites over 500 calibration parameters per car to unlock factory-limited horsepower, up from 10-15 on 1990s models.

Automakers keep tightening firmware security, turning each new model into a fresh reverse-engineering fight over code control.
