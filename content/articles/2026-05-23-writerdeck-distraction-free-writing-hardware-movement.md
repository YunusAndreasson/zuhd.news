San Francisco — Writers are removing internet capability from their hardware.

The writerdeck — a stripped laptop or e-ink device with no browser — emerged after focus apps proved easy to bypass.

Unlike apps, hardware with no Wi-Fi is a barrier that notifications and operating system updates cannot override.
