Cuba postponed its flagship cigar fair indefinitely. Habanos S.A. pulled the Habanos Festival scheduled for February 23 after Venezuela halted oil deliveries and the United States threatened tariffs against any nation selling fuel to Cuba, triggering extended blackouts and flight cancellations. The government warned it cannot supply jet fuel — 3 Canadian airlines have suspended Cuba routes.
