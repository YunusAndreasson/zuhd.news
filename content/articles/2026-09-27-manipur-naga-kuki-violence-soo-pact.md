Senapati — Unknown armed men killed 2 Naga villagers at a chief's home Saturday.

It came 2 days after 4 Tangkhul Nagas were killed in Kamjong district.

The United Naga Council wants [India](country:IN)'s ceasefire pact with Kuki armed groups suspended, saying they are involved in the violence.

The council suspended its economic blockade from 6pm Saturday after tripartite talks in Assam.

It also demands Deputy Chief Minister Nemcha Kipgen's removal and her husband Thangboi Kipgen's arrest.
