London — B2C2 has held takeover talks for 18 months.

The market maker, 90% owned by [Japan](country:JP)'s SBI Holdings, is seeking a valuation above $1 billion as crypto firms consolidate.

A deal at that price may be difficult in today's market, one person familiar said.
