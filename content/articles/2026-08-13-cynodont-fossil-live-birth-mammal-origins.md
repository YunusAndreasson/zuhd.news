Cape Town — A 236-million-year-old cynodont may have birthed live young.

Live birth was treated as a mammal invention, tens of millions of years before mammals existed.

Cynodonts sit in the lineage that gave rise to mammals, so the trait predates mammals themselves.

The Frontiers in Mammal Science authors call the finding provisional, resting on a single specimen.
