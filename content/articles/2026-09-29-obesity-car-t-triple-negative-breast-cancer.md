Houston — Obesity blunted CAR-T cell attacks on breast tumors.

Most cell-therapy trials don't screen patients by body weight, the group most likely to receive it.

In obese mice, engineered T cells lost glycolytic capacity and nearly failed to form the tissue-resident memory cells needed for lasting control.

The findings come from mouse models, and whether the same effect occurs in human patients remains untested.
