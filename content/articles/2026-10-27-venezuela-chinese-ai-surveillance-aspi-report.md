Caracas — 21.8 million people held the Carnet de la Patria ID by 2023.

The Australian Strategic Policy Institute says it is one of six layers of [Venezuela](country:VE)'s largely Chinese-built surveillance.

Firms including Huawei, ZTE, Hikvision and Dahua built or upgraded the six layers.

The report, funded by the Human Rights Foundation, calls the system fragmented and unevenly deployed.

Delcy Rodríguez negotiated the July 2025 AI agreement with iFlytek, sanctioned by the [US](country:US) since 2019.
