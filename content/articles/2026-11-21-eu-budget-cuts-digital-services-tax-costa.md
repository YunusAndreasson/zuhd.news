Brussels — The EU proposes taxing 3% of big tech's European revenue.

The levy would raise about €5bn a year, the Commission estimated, and needs all 27 states.

Council president António Costa cut the 2028-2034 budget from €1.76tn to €1.58tn, still a quarter above current spending in real terms.

[Germany](country:DE)'s Chancellor Friedrich Merz said the total remains too high, and a Parliament budget lawmaker called it unacceptable.

EU leaders meet at a special summit on 26-27 November, aiming for a deal by year-end.
