Bengaluru — Congress fulfilled an unacknowledged Karnataka rotation deal.

Shivakumar replaced Siddaramaiah under the pact; his campaigns delivered 9 of Karnataka's Lok Sabha seats to Congress in 2024.

Congress must hold Karnataka through 2028 while managing simultaneous reshuffles in Punjab and Tamil Nadu.
