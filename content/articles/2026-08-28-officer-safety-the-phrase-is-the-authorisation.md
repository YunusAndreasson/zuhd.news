Washington — Immigration and Customs Enforcement plans to spend $1 million.

The purchase moves surveillance robots from demonstration into a standing budget line.

The justification is officer safety, the same week the agency separately contracted $16.7 million for 6,000 pairs of shock gloves.

Homeland Security has not said how many robots the contract buys or where they will deploy.
