Port Harcourt — Rivers State's capital budget jumped 22% to N1.4 trillion.

Governor Siminalayi Fubara's N1.85 trillion 2026 plan leans on revenue from [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Federation Account Allocation Committee, months after a power struggle over the state's governance.

The state assembly has already reviewed the framework; implementation details follow through 2028.
