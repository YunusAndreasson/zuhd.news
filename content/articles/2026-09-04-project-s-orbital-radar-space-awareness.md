Munich — Project-S launches active radar into orbit next month.

Active radar can spot objects too small or dark for today's ground-based systems.

Passive telescopes only see what reflects sunlight, while onboard radar illuminates its own targets directly.

The demonstration flies on a SpaceX rideshare, the company owning the most tracked objects.
