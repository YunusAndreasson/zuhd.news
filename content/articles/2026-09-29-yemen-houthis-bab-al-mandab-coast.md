Mokha — Government forces fled this port town at dawn on Sept. 10.

Within a week the Houthis held the Bab al-Mandab coast, the strait that carries Red Sea shipping.

[Yemen](country:YE)'s government and Saudi backers had promised Sanaa's "liberation" on social media before the Houthis struck first on Sept. 4.

The Yemeni fact-checking group Sidq found the campaign's viral video was spliced from unrelated footage of Syria's president.

The UN refugee agency counted more than 130,000 people displaced in September alone.
