No family has visited Imran Khan in 80 days. The Pakistani government has blocked all access to Adiala jail — lawyers waited hours Thursday and left without seeing the former prime minister. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Chief Minister Sohail Afridi has written to Pakistan's prime minister requesting Eid visits, with the outcome uncertain as Eid approaches.
