Germany — Schaeffler cuts 3,500 jobs, three quarters of them German.

With the late-2024 round, it will have shed a quarter of its workforce in [Germany](country:DE).

Schaeffler is closing four sites for overcapacity and cut its 2028 revenue forecast from €28bn to €25bn in July.

Executives and staff are being asked to waive part of their pay, with terms still under discussion.
