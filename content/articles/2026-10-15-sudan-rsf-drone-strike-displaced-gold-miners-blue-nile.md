Ad-Damazin — A drone shell killed 32 displaced people digging for gold.

26 of the dead were women, with 4 children and 2 elderly people.

Blue Nile's acting health minister, Mustafa Jabrallah, blamed the Rapid Support Forces, which are pushing into army-held territory there.

"After the shell fell, dozens went down, and whoever could flee fled," said Ahmed Ragab Abu Shina, a survivor.

The Sudan Doctors Network calls the strike a war crime, and the UN wants those responsible held accountable.
