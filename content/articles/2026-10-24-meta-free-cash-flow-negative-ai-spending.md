United States — Meta ended the quarter $2.12 billion cash-negative.

Meta's long-term debt rose from $58.7 billion to $80.9 billion in nine months.

Meta spent $86 billion on capital expenditure in nine months, and guides to $135 billion to $145 billion for 2026.

Revenue still rose 26% to $64.6 billion, yet the shares fell about 7% on 22 October.

New Mexico's $25 billion penalty, which Meta is appealing, is not yet fully booked.
