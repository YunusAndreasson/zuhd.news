Lisbon — Rents in [Portugal](country:PT) are up 68% since 2020, the government says.

Tens of thousands marched in more than 30 towns, the movement's largest turnout yet.

Parliament approved looser rental contracts and faster evictions while home prices rose almost 20% in a year, the national statistics institute said.

The government promises 145,000 new homes and short-term rental limits but concedes the effects will not be immediate.

Casa Para Viver wants Prime Minister Luís Montenegro to meet its representatives directly.
