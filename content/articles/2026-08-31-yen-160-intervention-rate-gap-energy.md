Tokyo — The yen fell past 160 per dollar.

Households in [Japan](country:JP) pay costlier energy just as their currency weakens too.

The Bank of Japan's rate gap with the Fed drives the slide, and reserve sales only buy weeks.

The Ministry of Finance hasn't intervened yet, though 160 was the trigger point last time.
