Beijing — Xi Jinping de facto recognized [North Korea](country:KP) as nuclear-armed in summit.

[China](country:CN) chairs the UN body tasked with North Korea sanctions enforcement; Xi's framing treats the nuclear arsenal as established, not negotiable.

The UN Security Council still formally rejects North Korea's nuclear status; China holds veto power over any resolution.
