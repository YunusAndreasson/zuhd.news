Islamabad — Pakistan pays fuel subsidies by digital wallet. Transport and freight operators receive Rs70,000–100,000 monthly — launched after a 43–55% fuel price hike triggered by the US-Israeli war on Iran. Fuel rationing continues; the program scales Pakistan's digital payment network to all transport operators.
