Guangzhou — XPENG opened robotaxi sign-ups in [China](country:CN) by invitation code.

XPENG won permission in August to test without a safety operator in Guangzhou.

The car runs XPENG's vision-language-action model on four in-house Turing chips, without lidar or high-definition maps.

Chairman He Xiaopeng says revenue will come from vehicle sales, services and commission sharing, with partners running local fleets.
