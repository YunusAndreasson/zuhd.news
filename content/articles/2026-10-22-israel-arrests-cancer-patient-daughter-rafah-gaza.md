Rafah — Israel arrested a cancer patient and daughter returning to Gaza.

The crossing reopened Tuesday after [Israel](country:IL)'s 3-week closure, and returnees now risk arrest.

Returnees pass Israeli security checks at the Karam Abu Salem checkpoint, where a number have been arrested.

Amal Ghazy al-Rify and her daughter Yasmin held prior approval, her relative Haitham al-Rify said.

The family has not been told where they are held and has asked the Red Cross.
