Lahore — Police jailed Aleema Khan, in her 70s, for 30 days.

She is the sister of jailed ex-premier Imran Khan, arrested a week before his party's march.

Punjab's public order law allows preventive detention, and Lahore's administration cited a "grave threat."

His party marches on Islamabad September 27, and [Pakistan](country:PK)'s interior minister vowed to stop it.
