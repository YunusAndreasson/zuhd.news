Jõhvi — [Estonia](country:EE)'s 1st Brigade moves within 50 km of [Russia](country:RU)'s border.

The base lies just west of Narva, a border city seen as a flashpoint.

Commander Tarmo Kundla says the posture is active defence: fight from the first battle and strike targets deeper in Russia.

Putin says Moscow has no plans to attack Estonia or any other NATO member.

An Estonian defence ministry official says Russia is already planning to double its forces across the border.
