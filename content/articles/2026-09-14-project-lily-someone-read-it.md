San Francisco — Hundreds of contractors read real ChatGPT chats.

Users typing medical symptoms or confessions may not know a person reads them.

Internally called Project Lily, the program has contractors read, summarize and rate each response in stages.

OpenAI says it strips personal details first but admits some still slip through.
