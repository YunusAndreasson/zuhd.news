Philadelphia — A viscous liquid cracked like a solid, not a fluid.

Drexel University's Thamires Lima stretched polypropylene and crude oil between metal plates using extensional rheology, testing the force needed to make them flow.

The finding challenges the textbook line separating how liquids flow from how solids break.
