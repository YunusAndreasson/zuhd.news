New Delhi — Police detained 3 former chief ministers on Saturday.

They were heading to a rally against [India](country:IN)'s Chief Election Commissioner, Gyanesh Kumar.

Police stopped Mamata Banerjee at a bus stand, held Arvind Kejriwal at home, and took Pinarayi Vijayan to Chhatrasal Stadium.

BJP spokesperson Sambit Patra said Rahul Gandhi tried to drag the President into a "political street fight."

Reports do not say whether those held were released, and the opposition plans a larger protest Monday.
