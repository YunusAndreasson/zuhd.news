[Russia](country:RU) will import fuel and lower standards.

Novak said lower-grade fuel admits refineries, not crude, cannot meet demand.

Octane and sulphur grades depend on refinery units that drone strikes and sanctions have damaged.

Novak did not say when imports or the new fuel grades take effect.
