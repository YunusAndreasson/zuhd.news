Airstrikes killed 150 people at a school in Iran. Neither the US nor Israel has claimed the attack, though analysts say artificial intelligence targeting systems guided it — the school sat near 2 Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps facilities. Center for Strategic and International Studies analyst Benjamin Jensen warned militaries will not reconsider AI-guided targeting for a generation as civilian casualties mount.
