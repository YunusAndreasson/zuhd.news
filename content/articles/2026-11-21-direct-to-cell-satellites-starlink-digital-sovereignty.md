Germany — Starlink alone flies over 10,000 satellites.

Estimates put direct-to-phone satellite revenue near $100 billion by 2034.

Ordinary phones, not dishes, will connect to constellations, but signals are weaker, penetrate walls poorly and carry far less capacity than masts.

Most constellations are still being built, so who structures the market remains open.
