Ukraine fired 283 drones at Russia hours before US talks. The barrage, one of Kyiv's largest since the war began, hit Moscow and 3 other regions. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said his envoys would seek clarity on restarting three-way talks with Russia, stalled since the Middle East war began.
