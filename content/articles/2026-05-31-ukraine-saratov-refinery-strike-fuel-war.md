Saratov — [Ukraine](country:UA) struck Rosneft's military-fuel refinery.

Ukraine's General Staff confirmed a large-scale fire in control units; the Saratov plant produces fuel for [Russia](country:RU)'s frontline forces.

Rosneft has not confirmed damage or estimated disruption to fuel deliveries.
