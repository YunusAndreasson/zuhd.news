Jayapura — An American pilot's killing was not random.

West Papua's independence rebels increasingly see the Catholic Church as a rival for the loyalty of the indigenous population under [Indonesia](country:ID)'s decades-long rule.

The church's growing influence among Papuans sets it up as a continuing target in the conflict.
