Jos — A father waited three days for a psychiatric bed.

Stabilized patients are discharged early to free space for emergencies.

[Nigeria](country:NG) has fewer than 300 psychiatrists for over 200 million people, concentrated in a few teaching hospitals.

Jos University Teaching Hospital is training more psychiatric nurses to ease the backlog.
