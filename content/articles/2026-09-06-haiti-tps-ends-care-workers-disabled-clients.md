Long Island — A disabled man's caregiver of years lost his job.

Disabled Americans, not the policy's targets, absorb the cost of pulled work permits.

Temporary Protected Status is a discretionary label the government can revoke at will, stripped from 350,000 people from [Haiti](country:HT).

Care agencies already short-staffed now compete for workers domestic hiring alone cannot supply.
