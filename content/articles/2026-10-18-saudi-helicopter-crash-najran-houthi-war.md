Najran — 2 Saudi National Guard officers died in a helicopter crash.

They are the first Saudi military deaths since fighting with the Houthis resumed in July.

The Houthis have hit Riyadh's airport at least 20 times since July, including one attack last week that killed 13.

The coalition blames a technical malfunction, but Houthi spokesman Yahya Saree claimed Saturday his forces downed an Apache.

[Egypt](country:EG)'s parliament meets in emergency session Sunday after Abdel Fattah el-Sisi offered to defend [Saudi Arabia](country:SA).
