Jaramana — A bomb planted on a minibus killed two, wounded 13.

The Damascus suburb is home to [Syria](country:SY)'s Druze and Christian minorities, whose safety tests the post-Assad government's claim to protect them.

No group has claimed responsibility; forensic teams are still identifying remains, health officials said.
