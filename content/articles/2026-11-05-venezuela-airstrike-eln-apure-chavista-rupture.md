Apure — Drones killed at least 19 at a Colombian guerrilla camp.

It is [Venezuela](country:VE)'s first major attack on the National Liberation Army, which has sheltered there for years.

The strike reportedly had [US](country:US) assistance, and Secretary of State Marco Rubio said in September Washington had discussed such operations with Caracas.

Venezuela has issued no statement and Washington is silent, so whether more strikes follow is unknown.
