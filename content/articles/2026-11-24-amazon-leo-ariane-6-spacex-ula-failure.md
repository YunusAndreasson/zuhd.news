Kourou — Ariane 6 lifted 36 Amazon Leo satellites into orbit.

A Vulcan failure on 15 November destroyed 40 Amazon Leo satellites and grounded the rocket.

The launch took Amazon Leo to 432 satellites, and Amazon is moving up Falcon 9 launches it bought from rival SpaceX.

SpaceX has reportedly stopped taking commercial Falcon 9 bookings after 2028.

Amazon's next Falcon 9 launch moves up to December.
