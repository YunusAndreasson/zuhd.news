Abuja — The Attorney-General showed up in person to block one witness.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s chief law officer, Lateef Fagbemi, asked the court to quash a subpoena summoning the security service chief to testify for Sowore, who faces cyberstalking charges over posts about President Tinubu.

Judge Mohammed Umar has not yet ruled on the subpoena request.
