India — Two [US](country:US) charities tied to its Hindu nationalist movement took millions anonymously.

Almost 100 US politicians pledged to refuse money from groups linked to the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh.

Donor-advised funds pass gifts on under the fund's name, so Form 990 filings show the fund, not the donor.

The group did not respond to requests for comment, and it maintains it has no formal membership.

A bipartisan US commission on religious freedom has urged sanctions on the group over violations.
