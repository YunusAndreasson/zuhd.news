Moscow — A drone matched its camera feed to stored maps and flew a route with no satellite signal.

Once drones stop needing GPS, jamming no longer works as a defense against them.

[Russia](country:RU)'s Geoscan says the system, tested over 3 days, can hold position across hundreds of kilometers without GNSS.

The company is now building a nighttime version using thermal imaging instead of a daylight camera.
