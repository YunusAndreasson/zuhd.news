London — Nanoengineered materials absorb hydrogen at room temperature.

Storage, not production, was hydrogen's gating problem; cryogenic tanks and high-pressure vessels are too heavy and complex for most applications.

Deployment remains years away; the advance resolves storage, not the economic case for hydrogen over batteries.
