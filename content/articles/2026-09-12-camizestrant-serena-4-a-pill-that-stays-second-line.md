London — Camizestrant failed as a first-line breast cancer therapy.

The pill won't replace fulvestrant's monthly injection for most newly diagnosed patients.

It degrades the estrogen receptor by pill rather than shot, but SERENA-4 found it no better than standard care upfront.

AstraZeneca's drug stays approved only for patients with a resistance mutation caught by blood test.
