Dhaka — [Bangladesh](country:BD)'s PM Tarique Rahman asked [Malaysia](country:MY) to lift its labour immigration restrictions.

[Malaysia](country:MY) tightened intake citing migrant abuse and debt bondage; Bangladesh depends on Southeast Asian remittances.

Whether Malaysia agrees determines if workers regain formal channels or Bangladesh forgoes a key remittance source.
