Kimberley — [South Africa](country:ZA) found just 83 wild cheetahs.

The old "300" figure was a guess; researchers camera-trapped 100,000 square kilometers for 3 years to get a real count.

Conservationists want cheetahs reclassified as endangered, but no illegal killing has ever led to prosecution.
