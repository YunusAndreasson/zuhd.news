Zaragoza — Leapmotor's B10 now shares a line with the Corsa.

Building in [Spain](country:ES) lowers reliance on exports from [China](country:CN), which face EU countervailing tariffs.

A battery plant 30 km away assembles modules and packs from imported cells, and Leapmotor puts local content at 40%.

Tariff treatment still depends on origin rules, so local assembly does not guarantee relief.

Leapmotor will start building the B05 in Zaragoza in the first half of 2027.
