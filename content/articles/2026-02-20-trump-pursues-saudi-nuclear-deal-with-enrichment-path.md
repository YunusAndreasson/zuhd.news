The US is negotiating a nuclear deal that would let Saudi Arabia enrich uranium. Unlike agreements with the United Arab Emirates, the draft omits enhanced inspections by the International Atomic Energy Agency. The deal reaches Congress while Washington simultaneously demands Iran halt its own enrichment program.
