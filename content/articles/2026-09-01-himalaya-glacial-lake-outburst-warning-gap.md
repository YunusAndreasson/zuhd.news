Kathmandu — Crews have rescued nearly 12,000 people so far.

More than 1,000 people are now confirmed dead across [Nepal](country:NP) and [China](country:CN).

Satellite imagery shows bedrock beneath a glacier failed, a collapse strong enough to register as a magnitude 5.2 quake.

Nepal's disaster agency still lists 3,916 people missing, including 583 foreigners.
