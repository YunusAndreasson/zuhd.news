Thai troops occupy Cambodian land despite a December ceasefire. Cambodian Prime Minister Hun Manet told Reuters that Thai soldiers placed shipping containers and barbed wire in areas Thailand itself has long recognised as Cambodian territory. Bangkok cited its February election as reason to delay boundary demarcation.
