Cambridge — Gene-edited pig kidneys prolonged survival in primate recipients.

3 pig carbohydrate antigens trigger human immune rejection; the study knocked them out and added human regulatory transgenes.

Xenotransplant trials require sustained primate survival over months; this study is a step but human trials remain unannounced.
