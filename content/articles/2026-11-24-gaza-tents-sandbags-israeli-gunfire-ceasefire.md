Khan Younis — Gaza families sandbag their tents against Israeli gunfire.

The Bir 19 camp lies near the yellow line where [Israel](country:IL)'s troops are stationed, under a declared ceasefire.

Families fill flour sacks with sand and stack them three high on east-facing tent walls.

"Every day, around the clock, we are caught off guard by stray, deadly bullets," said Nofal Al-Akhras of Bir 19.

Polymarket traders give 4% odds that the ceasefire is cancelled by 31 December.
