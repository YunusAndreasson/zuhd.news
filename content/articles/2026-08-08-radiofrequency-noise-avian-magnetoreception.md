London — Weak radiofrequency noise scrambles birds' magnetic sense, researchers find.

Migratory birds depend on this sense to navigate thousands of kilometers accurately.

Pulsed radiofrequency fields far weaker than anything affecting human tissue disrupt cryptochrome proteins in birds' eyes, the study found.

No policy yet restricts infrastructure sited along migration flyways.
