Geneva — New measurement resolves the 16-year proton radius puzzle. Ultra-precise hydrogen spectroscopy agrees with muonic hydrogen results from 2010, confirming the proton is smaller than long assumed. The technique opens precision tests of quantum electrodynamics that could reveal physics beyond the Standard Model.
