Yerevan — 113 of 196 countries have filed new nature plans.

An analysis found only 55 had filed a year ago, and COP17's first global review draws on these plans.

Only 15% met the October 2024 deadline, so the review of the 2030 targets rests largely on late filings.

Countries meet in Yerevan next week to review progress on the framework's 23 targets for 2030.
