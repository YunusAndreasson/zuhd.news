New Delhi — [India](country:IN) has zero offshore wind turbines. Tariff ceilings set below bid-viable rates have killed every auction since 2018, with developers citing insufficient viability-gap funding. India and the [UK](country:GB) launched a joint offshore wind taskforce in February to develop supply chains and financing.
