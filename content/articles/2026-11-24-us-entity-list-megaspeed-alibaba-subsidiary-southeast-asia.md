Singapore — Alibaba's Apex unit joined 6 other firms on the US export blacklist.

The Commerce Department cited risks that advanced chips could be diverted to unauthorized end users.

The [US](country:US) listed 7 Southeast Asian companies, including Megaspeed International and Alibaba's Singapore subsidiary Apex.

Alibaba calls the listing a mistake and says Apex never diverted chips subject to export controls.

Alibaba has pledged to pursue all legal avenues to protect its rights and reputation.
