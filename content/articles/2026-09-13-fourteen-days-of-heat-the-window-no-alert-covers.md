Johannesburg — Heat strain builds over 14 days, not one.

That's a risk current single-day heat warnings in [South Africa](country:ZA) don't catch.

Each 1°C rise in heat raised symptom odds by 15% to 39%, the study found.

Researchers now want personalized warnings based on symptoms, not fixed temperatures.
