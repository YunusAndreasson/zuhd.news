Qatar's LNG shutdown has stranded global helium supplies. Algeria produces 50 million cubic meters of helium per year, an element that cannot be synthesized yet is essential for MRI scanners and chip fabrication, making it the nearest available source. QatarEnergy's chief said output will not resume "until the conflict is finished," tying global helium supply to Iran's military timeline.
