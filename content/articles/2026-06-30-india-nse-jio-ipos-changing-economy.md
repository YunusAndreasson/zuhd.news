Mumbai — [India](country:IN)'s National Stock Exchange and Jio Platforms plan to raise $7.3bn combined.

The exchange is valued at an estimated $57bn; Jio at up to $160bn, Emkay Global said.

Both are expected to list before year-end, ranking among India's largest-ever share sales.
