Iran's foreign minister left Tehran for Geneva Sunday. Abbas Araghchi will lead a delegation into a second round of Oman-mediated indirect talks with the United States on Tuesday after the first round in Muscat on February 6 failed to narrow the gap between Washington's demand for zero enrichment and Tehran's offer to dilute its 60% uranium stockpile to 20%. The USS Gerald R. Ford carrier group is deploying to the region as both sides negotiate 8 months after Israeli strikes destroyed Iranian nuclear facilities.
