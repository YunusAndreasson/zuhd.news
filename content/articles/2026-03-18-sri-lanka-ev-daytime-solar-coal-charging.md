Sri Lanka's nighttime grid runs almost entirely on coal. The government told electric vehicle owners to charge during daytime solar hours — or risk a higher carbon footprint than a petrol car. The guidance follows fuel-saving measures Sri Lanka introduced after the Hormuz closure cut off its oil imports.
