Pryluky — A Russian cruise missile killed 14 in one apartment block.

Across [Ukraine](country:UA), authorities count 24 dead and 134 injured, including at least 4 children dead in Pryluky.

Ukraine's Air Force says [Russia](country:RU) launched 48 cruise missiles and 130 drones, and its defences stopped 39 and 117.

Russia's defence ministry says it struck targets in Kyiv and Odesa-region port facilities, plus vessels serving Ukraine's military.

Rescuers were still working through the Pryluky building at last report, with 50 injured, 7 of them children.
