Paris — Unions say 300,000 struck as police detained 440 people in school protests.

Unions say [France](country:FR)'s 2027 budget freezes state salaries for a fourth straight year.

The freeze saves 2 billion euros, and unions say earlier freezes already cost workers a double-digit share of purchasing power.

The Education Ministry counts 11% of school staff striking, and under 10% of other civil servants.

Union leader Sophie Binet gave the government 48 hours to reverse the cuts before more strikes.
