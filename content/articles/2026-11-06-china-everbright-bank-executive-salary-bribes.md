China — Bribes to a bank executive arrived as 4.29 million yuan in salaries.

Nominal jobs disguised payment to Zhang Huayu, China Everbright Bank's former deputy president, and his wife.

[China](country:CN)'s top judicial authorities said the appointments rewarded his help securing loans and expanding business.

A court sentenced Zhang to 12½ years in August 2023 for taking bribes.
