Abuja — Officers flogged editor Sunday Ogwu with a cable.

The coalition wants a probe run outside the command it says ordered the beating.

Officers seized his phone at a Lugbe demolition site after he identified himself as a journalist.

Ogwu is set to give his statement to that same command, with lawyers, on Monday.
