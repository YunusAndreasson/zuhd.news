Santa Clara — $500 billion will flow through six lenders.

The exposure lands on pension funds and insurers, not Nvidia's balance sheet.

Nvidia books the revenue immediately while Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR hold the debt.

Nvidia has not said whether it would backstop losses if a borrower defaults.
