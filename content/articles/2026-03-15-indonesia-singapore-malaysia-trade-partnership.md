Hormuz disruptions are pushing Southeast Asia's largest economies together. Indonesia-Malaysia bilateral trade reached $24 billion in 2025 as Indonesia, Malaysia, and Singapore formalized cooperation at an ASEAN summit. Indonesia committed to CPTPP membership talks, which would link it to 11 Pacific economies.
