London — Parasitic plants steal genes from their hosts.

Stolen genes can transfer host defenses, making resistance breeding fail.

The parasite's haustorium fuses with host vascular tissue, creating a conduit for genes as well as sugars to cross.

Researchers have not yet shown whether the stolen genes function in the parasite.
