Massachusetts — Hackers issued 15,000 gift cards worth over $5 million.

A [US](country:US) Homeland Security warrant, unsealed last week, describes a Chinese organised crime ring shipping goods to [China](country:CN).

Hackers dumped memory from an internal support system, hijacked logged-in agents' sessions and emailed themselves the cards.

The warrant never names the retailer, and 404 Media identified Staples from details in it.

Staples did not respond to requests for comment.
