South Africa's illegal mining gangs outgun its police. President Cyril Ramaphosa deployed 2,200 soldiers across 5 provinces to suppress them, with unemployment above 33% — among the world's highest — sustaining a shadow economy of informal shaft operations that fund armed gangs. Parliament was notified under constitutional provisions for military deployment, leaving unresolved whether armed force alone can shrink a criminal economy that 33% unemployment keeps rebuilding.
