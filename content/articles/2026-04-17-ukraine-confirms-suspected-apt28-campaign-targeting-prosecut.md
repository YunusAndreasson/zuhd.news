Kyiv — APT28 hit Ukraine's prosecutors via Roundcube webmail. The flaw executes malicious code when victims open an email; the group targeted the anti-corruption agencies Western donors require Ukraine to sustain. The platform is widely deployed; Ukraine confirmed the campaign is ongoing and administrators should patch immediately.
