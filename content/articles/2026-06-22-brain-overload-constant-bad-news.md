London — 40% of people globally avoid news sometimes — the highest share ever recorded, the Reuters Institute found.

The brain's negativity bias evolved for local threats; a study of 105,000 headlines found each negative word raised click-through rates.

Researchers warn the same bias driving clicks degrades sustained attention in readers.
