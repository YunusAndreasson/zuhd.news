Abuja — [Nigeria](country:NG) is isolating 2 suspected Ebola patients in the northeast.

Neither case is confirmed, and the agency's announcement on X gave no date for results.

Officials are identifying and listing the patients' contacts while laboratories test samples, the Nigeria Centre for Disease Control and Prevention said.

Laboratory results decide whether Nigeria has an Ebola outbreak.
