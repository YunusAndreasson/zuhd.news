Paris — Cold calls now need consent first.

An outsourced call-centre industry across North Africa loses a business model built on unsolicited contact.

Most do-not-call rules put the burden on the person called, but [France](country:FR) shifted it onto the caller.

Whether the opt-in default actually cuts nuisance calls is now the live test regulators watch.
