Russia suspended its Isfahan consulate Sunday. US-Israeli strikes had killed 15 in Isfahan's industrial district the day before, prompting Moscow to evacuate its own staff despite nominally backing Tehran. The Kremlin gave no reopening date while oil at $106 per barrel, driven by the same war, keeps Russian revenues elevated.
