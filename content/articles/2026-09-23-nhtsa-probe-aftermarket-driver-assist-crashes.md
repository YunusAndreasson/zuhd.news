Washington — [US](country:US) regulators are probing 3 deaths involving comma.ai's driver assist.

Comma says openpilot runs on more than 325 models, and more than 30,000 drivers use it.

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration doubts the system detects stopped or slow cars, and 1 crash hit a first responder's vehicle.

At least two crashes ran forks of the main build, including the fatal one, which used FrogPilot.

The Office of Defects Investigation is now tracing which software version each of the 5 cars ran.
