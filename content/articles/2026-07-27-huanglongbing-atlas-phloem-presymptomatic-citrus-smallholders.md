Gainesville — Citrus greening spreads for years unseen.

A psyllid-borne bacterium colonizes the tree's phloem and chokes sugar transport years before any leaf yellows, University of Florida researchers found.

No cure exists, and resistant rootstocks bred from the atlas' findings could take years to reach smallholders.
