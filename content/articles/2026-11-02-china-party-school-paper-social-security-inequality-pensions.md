Beijing — Pension fund spending is 14 times higher for urban employees than for residents.

A Party School journal paper argues [China](country:CN)'s pensions and medical insurance widen income gaps.

Monthly per capita spending in 2025 was 3,900 yuan from the urban employee fund and 276 yuan from the residents' fund.

Co-author Zheng Gongcheng sits on the expert committee for the 15th Five-Year Plan, but the paper is not policy.

The authors propose cutting civil servants' pensions to about half of pre-retirement pay and raising farmers' pensions.
