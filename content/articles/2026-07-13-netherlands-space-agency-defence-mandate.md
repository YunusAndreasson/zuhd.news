The Hague — Defense now sits on the space agency's board.

The renamed agency's steering committee, which approves funding and programmes, added the Defence Ministry while dropping the national research council.

[Netherlands](country:NL) commits €344.3 million to ESA programs through 2028 under the new mandate.
