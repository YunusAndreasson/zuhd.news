Bahawalpur — A 17-year-old faces 14 days' remand for alleged poisoning.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s cyber-crime agency says he asked AI how to poison his father undetected.

An automated system alerted the agency's Punjab office, which arrested him in Bahawalpur on 23 September.

His father has no objection to the case, an investigating officer told the court.

Judicial Magistrate Naeem Wattoo ordered investigators to file a charge sheet within the stipulated time.
