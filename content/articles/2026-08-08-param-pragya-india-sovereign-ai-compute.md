Sonipat — Modi inaugurated [India](country:IN)'s Param Pragya AI supercomputer.

The facility, opened during IIT Delhi's 57th convocation, will support the institute's AI, data science and computing research, the government said.

Modi did not disclose the system's processor count, chip supplier, or computing capacity.
