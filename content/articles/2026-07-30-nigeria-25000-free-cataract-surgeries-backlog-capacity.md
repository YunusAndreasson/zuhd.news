Abuja — 25,000 free cataract surgeries pledged by 2027.

Cataract surgery is short and cheap, so [Nigeria](country:NG)'s real constraint is ophthalmologists and functioning theatres outside Lagos and Abuja, not the operation itself.

Funding is already in the 2025 and 2026 budgets; implementation starts late October.
