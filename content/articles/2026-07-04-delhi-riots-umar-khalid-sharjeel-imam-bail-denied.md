Delhi — Umar Khalid and Sharjeel Imam stay jailed, again, without trial.

A sessions judge rejected fresh bail for both, held over five years on conspiracy charges tied to the 2020 riots that killed 53 people, under [India](country:IN)'s anti-terror UAPA law.

Prosecutors call the pair the conspiracy's "intellectual architects"; no trial date has been set.
