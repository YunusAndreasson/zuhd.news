Hudson's Hope — A 230-by-460-foot forest island vanished, then reappeared.

It shows how satellite imagery, not official statements, resolves an unverified viral claim.

Flooding to create the [Canada](country:CA) reservoir loosened forested peat rafts that drift with wind and water drawdown.

BC Hydro says the floating forest may break apart or reattach to shore.
