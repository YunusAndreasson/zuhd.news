Hebron — [Israel](country:IL)'s army displaced nearly 5,000 Palestinians in 11 days.

The army sealed the city's entrances and disrupted schooling for 80,000 students.

Soldiers knocked on doors, took identity cards and ordered families out with only essential belongings.

"We returned on the same day the army withdrew," said Baher Sultan, whose home soldiers ransacked.

Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has said the army is prepared for an extensive operation across the West Bank.
