New York — Binance users withdrew a net $3.64 billion once withdrawals reopened.

Binance says it covered 95% of the stolen $6.3 billion with company funds.

It processed $4.649 billion in withdrawals against $1.01 billion in deposits, Binance said, and volumes then stabilized.

Binance said about $3 billion is frozen, mostly stablecoins, while stolen bitcoin and ether cannot be frozen.

Binance has promised a full proof-of-reserves report to show it holds what customers are owed.
