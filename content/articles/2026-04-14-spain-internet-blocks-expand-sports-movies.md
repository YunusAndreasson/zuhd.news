Madrid — Spain now blocks the internet during broadcast windows. Telefonica won a court order expanding IP-level blocks from football to tennis, golf, and movies — the same technique used by authoritarian states. Every rights holder now has a Spanish court template to demand time-based ISP blocking elsewhere in Europe.
