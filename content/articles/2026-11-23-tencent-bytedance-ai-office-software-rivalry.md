China — Tencent claims 10 million daily AI-office users, ByteDance 8 million.

ByteDance's Doubao Work reports 2 billion yuan in recurring revenue about three months after launch.

Feishu, ByteDance's workplace app, already generated over 3 billion yuan in recurring revenue in 2025.

Tencent's $500 million figure annualises one week's revenue, while ByteDance's is recurring revenue.

Whether companies renew, and at what margin once compute costs bite, is still untested.
