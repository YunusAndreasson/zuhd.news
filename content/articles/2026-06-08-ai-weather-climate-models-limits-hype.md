Reading — AI forecasts run 1,000 times cheaper than physics.

Machine-learning models learn patterns, not physics; they underestimate extremes outside their training data and require guardrails like capping negative rainfall.

That blind spot grows as climate change pushes conditions beyond the historical record these models rely on.
