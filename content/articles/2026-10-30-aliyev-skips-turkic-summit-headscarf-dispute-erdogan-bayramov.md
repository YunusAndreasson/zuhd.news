Ankara — [Azerbaijan](country:AZ)'s president skipped a summit hosted by [Turkey](country:TR).

The 2 countries are feuding over headscarf limits in Azerbaijani schools.

Baku declared 31 Turkish citizens persona non grata over social media posts criticising the rules, and Oktay Saral, a chief presidential adviser, resigned.

Azerbaijani media said Aliyev was attending the U-15 World Cup closing ceremony in Baku.

Kazakhstan's Kassym-Jomart Tokayev pressed the Trans-Caspian route, which crosses Azerbaijan, as a priority.
