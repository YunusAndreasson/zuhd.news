Singapore — Wintermute Asia launched over-the-counter WTI crude oil CFDs on Tuesday, joining Hyperliquid's existing perpetual futures in a race to give traders access when traditional markets close on weekends. The Iran war exposed the gap: oil moved double digits over weekends while CME and ICE sat dark, trapping institutional positions until Monday open. Wintermute's model — trading directly against the firm using crypto or fiat as margin — lets counterparties act on price moves that traditional finance can't reach.
