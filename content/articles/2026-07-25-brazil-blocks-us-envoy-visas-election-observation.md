Two senior [US](country:US) diplomats were refused entry.

They planned to inspect [Brazil](country:BR)'s electoral system ahead of the October 4 vote; Brasília's foreign ministry confirmed the denial.

Brazil's electronic voting system has a decades-long audit record, and no bilateral pact obliged it to admit anyone.
