Trump declared the Iran war "winding down" on Friday. The claim contradicts Pentagon ground invasion planning leaked the same day; South Korea, China, and Japan had all refused his Hormuz escort requests. Hormuz has been closed for 29 days, and no allied navy has agreed to reopen it — leaving Asia's energy supply at risk.
