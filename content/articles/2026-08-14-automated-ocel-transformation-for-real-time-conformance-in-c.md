London — New software reviews just 1.2% of factory events.

Manufacturers could catch compliance violations without inspecting every logged event.

An uncertainty-based algorithm flags only ambiguous cases for review, researchers said, raising accuracy from 75.6% to 92.4%.

The authors have not tested the system outside timber and metal-tube manufacturing.
