Cairo — 64 million Egyptians depend on one payment switch.

[Egypt](country:EG)'s new direct-debit system makes mills and bakeries pay upfront for wheat, replacing consignment supply the state settled later — a cash-timing risk bakeries now carry.

Supply Minister Sherif Farouk says the 20-piaster loaf price won't change; bakery federation sources are less certain.
