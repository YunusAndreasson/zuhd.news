Port-au-Prince — Untrained vendors sell medicine from buckets on sidewalks.

Viv Ansanm gangs have looted and burned health facilities, leaving patients to self-medicate.

Licensed drug stores are few, and the street stock is often expired, spoiled or fake.

No [Haitian](country:HT) government has effectively fought the trade, and most patients cannot afford private clinics.
