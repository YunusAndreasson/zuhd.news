Niamey — [Niger](country:NE) accuses France of backing a coup.

[France](country:FR) calls it "pure fiction," leaving Niger's claim untested by any independent probe.

Soldiers who seized Niamey's airport August 28 were pulled from front-line positions, the pattern behind Sahel coup-proofing since 2020.

Niger says it has compiled evidence and may bring the case before international courts.
