New York — A plasticiser in intravenous tubing disrupts premature hearts during oxygen therapy.

Di-2-ethylhexyl phthalate leaches from hospital lines; oxygen therapy amplifies cardiac harm, a Nature study found.

The chemical is unregulated in [US](country:US) medical devices; 29% of preterm infants exceed its toxicity threshold in neonatal care.
