Paris — Ifop puts Le Pen at 32-36%, Mélenchon at 17-19.5%.

The centre that has governed [France](country:FR) since 2017 cannot break past 11%.

Édouard Philippe rejects a joint primary and pledges to unite right and centre behind himself, while Retailleau and Attal sit at 4%.

Former prime minister Dominique de Villepin says Philippe is "chasing after the National Rally" on immigration.

The first round is 18 April, and Philippe has until then to unite right and centre.
