Bangkok — WHO counts 138,000 snakebite deaths a year, concentrated in rural Asia and Africa.

Each 1°C rise increases bite likelihood, per Emory University research; floods push snakes into villages during a monsoon season now made more erratic by warming.

Antivenom production is weakest in the countries gaining the most new exposure.
