Lisbon — Lufthansa and Air France-KLM filed binding TAP bids.

Both groups seek a 44.5% stake in [Portugal](country:PT)'s carrier, whose value is a Brazil and Lusophone Africa network neither buyer can build.

Parpública has 30 days to report to the government, which may still demand a better final offer.
