Eglin — A charter boat hauled a delta-wing drone from the Gulf.

It resembles a US low-cost attack drone, a reverse-engineered copy of [Iran](country:IR)'s Shahed-136.

Eglin's spokesperson confirmed a test article went down but would not say it was this drone.

The Pentagon could not immediately provide further details.
