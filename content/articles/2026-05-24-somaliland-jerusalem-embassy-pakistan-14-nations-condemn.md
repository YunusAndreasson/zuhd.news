Hargeisa — 14 governments condemned Somaliland's planned embassy in Jerusalem.

[Israel](country:IL) recognised Somaliland in December and remains its sole state backer; Somaliland has no other diplomatic recognition.

[Pakistan](country:PK), [Somalia](country:SO), [Egypt](country:EG) and 11 others declared the move null and void.
