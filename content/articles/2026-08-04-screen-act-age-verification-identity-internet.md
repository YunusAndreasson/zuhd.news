Washington — The SCREEN Act would age-verify visitors to most of the internet.

Verification has no anonymous form at scale — it resolves to an ID upload, a face scan, or device attestation, linking identity to browsing history.

The Senate Commerce Committee takes up the bill and four others at Wednesday's markup.
