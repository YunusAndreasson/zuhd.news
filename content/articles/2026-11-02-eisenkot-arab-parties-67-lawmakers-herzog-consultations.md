Jerusalem — Gadi Eisenkot could win 67 of 120 Knesset recommendations.

Arab parties hold 19 seats, but the Joint List says it will not join his government.

Benjamin Netanyahu's bloc won 53 seats and the opposition 48, so neither reaches 61, and no coalition deal exists.

Avigdor Lieberman refuses any government that includes Arab parties or relies on their votes.

President Isaac Herzog holds consultations Wednesday and Thursday, then names who gets 28 days to form a government.
