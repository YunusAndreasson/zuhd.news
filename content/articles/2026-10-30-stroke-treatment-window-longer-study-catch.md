China — Clot removal helped some patients 24 to 72 hours after stroke.

Until now, evidence supported the procedure to 24 hours, leaving later arrivals with fewer options.

A 336-patient trial in the New England Journal of Medicine enrolled only patients whose scans showed salvageable tissue.

Brain bleeds hit 28.3% of treated patients against 5.5% of controls, and 6% died against 1.8%.

With 13 deaths in total, the researchers cannot rule out chance as the cause of the gap.
