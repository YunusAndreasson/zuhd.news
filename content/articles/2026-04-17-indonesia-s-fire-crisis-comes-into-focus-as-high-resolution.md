Jakarta — Satellites mapped 5.62 million burnt hectares in Indonesia. Sentinel-2 now produces monthly burn maps at 20-metre resolution, replacing ministry estimates with verifiable orbital data. As the dry season arrives, climate-finance payouts tied to forest preservation face their first satellite-verified audit.
