Sweden cut its 2026 GDP growth forecast to 2.8% over the Middle East war. Finance Minister Elisabeth Svantesson cited war-driven energy price spikes, making Sweden one of the first European governments to formally revise a growth projection because of the conflict. Sweden's central bank will factor the downgrade into its next rate decision.
