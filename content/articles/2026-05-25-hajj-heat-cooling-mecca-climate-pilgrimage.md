Mecca — Saudi authorities deployed ice trucks and misting fans for 2026 Hajj.

More than 1,300 pilgrims died of heatstroke in 2024; this year's temperatures are reaching 45°C.

The Arafat standing, the central rite, occurs later this week as heat peaks.
