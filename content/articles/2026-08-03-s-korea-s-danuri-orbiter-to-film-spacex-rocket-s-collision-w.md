Seoul — A SpaceX rocket stage hits the Moon Wednesday.

The stage, adrift since a January 2025 launch, will strike near Einstein crater at roughly 8,700 km/h, carving a fresh crater.

[South Korea](country:KR)'s Danuri orbiter will film the impact as scientists debate who governs lunar debris.
