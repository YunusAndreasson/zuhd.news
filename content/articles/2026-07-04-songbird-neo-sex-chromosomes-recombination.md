London — Sex chromosomes are supposed to only decay. Three songbird lines rebuilt them instead.

Researchers found Cisticola, Sylvietta and Alauda independently fused new chromosomes onto the Z chromosome, then evolved fresh recombining regions to replace the ones they lost.

The finding reframes sex-chromosome evolution as an active, repeatable process rather than one-way decay.
