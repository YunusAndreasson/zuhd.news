Brussels — Regulators labeled ChatGPT a very large platform.

EU users of the chatbot now gain rights the Artificial Intelligence Act alone never provided.

The designation covers what a service does to users, not how the model was built, alongside Reddit and Roblox.

The three services have four months to meet new risk-assessment obligations.
