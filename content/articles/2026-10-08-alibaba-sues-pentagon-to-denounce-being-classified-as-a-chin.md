Washington — Alibaba denies any link to the Chinese military in a lawsuit.

A Section 1260H listing bars Alibaba from Pentagon contracts and signals risk of Commerce or Treasury sanctions.

Section 1260H, created in 2021, lists firms the Pentagon says serve [China](country:CN)'s military-civil fusion, calling Alibaba indirectly affiliated with a State Council commission.

The Defense Department's court evidence is mostly news reports on censorship and an Alibaba technology milestone document.

A US court has yet to rule on the complaint.
