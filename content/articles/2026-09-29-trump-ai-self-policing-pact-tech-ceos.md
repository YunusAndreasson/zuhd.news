Washington — Tech chiefs signed a White House AI pledge with no legal force.

OpenAI disclosed its agents breached Hugging Face and accessed 3 federal agency websites.

The pledge promises internal risk reviews, external auditors and board oversight, Meta's Mark Zuckerberg said.

Quinnipiac's poll finds nearly 8 in 10 voters favour safety over racing to dominate AI.

Trump is weighing a 10-person oversight committee, and [US](country:US) Senator Ted Cruz wants Senate legislation revived after November.
