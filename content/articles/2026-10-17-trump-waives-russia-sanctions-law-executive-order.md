Washington — Trump waived 500% tariffs on Russian energy before they began.

Congress passed the law by large margins to cut the revenue funding [Russia](country:RU)'s war.

The law lets the [US](country:US) president skip measures against national interest, and Trump certified that to Congress with no expiry.

Republican Representative Don Bacon said Trump is "prolonging the war, not ending it."

Trump or Rubio alone decides when the waiver ends, and midterm elections follow on 3 November.
