Bhutan — Electricity sales fund 36% of government revenue.

[India](country:IN) is the only buyer, and most of Bhutan's hydropower debt has historically traced to Indian-financed projects.

The 26 August floods in Nepal and China damaged at least 15 hydropower plants, and Thorthormi lake threatens two Bhutanese dams.

Glaciologists are testing whether Thorthormi can be drained, while Bhutan upgrades early warning toward forecasting.
