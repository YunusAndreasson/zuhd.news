Mansehra — Officials barred tourists before any cloudburst hit.

A cloudburst can dump 100mm an hour on one narrow [Pakistan](country:PK) valley, turning its only road into a flash-flood channel.

Kaghan's traders want the ban lifted; an August weekend is much of their year's income.
