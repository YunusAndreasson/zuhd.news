Paris — Conservative Rachida Dati conceded before final results arrived. Socialist Emmanuel Grégoire succeeded outgoing mayor Anne Hidalgo as leftists also won Marseille, despite National Rally claiming its biggest-ever municipal breakthrough in smaller cities. Edouard Philippe, re-elected mayor of Le Havre, said the extremes "can be beaten" ahead of France's 2027 presidential race.
