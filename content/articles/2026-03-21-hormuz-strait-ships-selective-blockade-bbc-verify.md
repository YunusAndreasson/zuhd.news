Strait of Hormuz — Hormuz carries 5 ships a day, down from 138. BBC Verify found a third of recent crossings were Iranian-flagged or sanctions-linked ships, while Iran directed others through its territorial waters. Iran retains additional coastal batteries that can still target ships the US has already cleared to pass.
