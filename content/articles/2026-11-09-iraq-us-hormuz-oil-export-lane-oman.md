Baghdad — [Iraq](country:IQ) says the Iran war has cost it $60 billion.

Iraqi exports rose to 3.5 million barrels a day in October, from 2.65 million.

Foreign Minister Fuad Hussein says Washington agreed to let Iraqi oil use a Hormuz shipping lane near Oman.

The [US](country:US) Treasury also asked Iraq to close all but 2 border crossings with Iran, Hussein said.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 31 December a 36% chance, down 3 points in a week.
