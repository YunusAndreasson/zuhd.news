Kurdistan halted flows on Iraq's only Hormuz bypass pipeline. Iraq's oil ministry threatened legal action to restore crude exports through the Kirkuk-Ceyhan route in Turkey. The ministry set no deadline, leaving the dispute unresolved as both sides need every barrel the war allows.
