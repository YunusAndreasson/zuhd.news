Oxford — Animals may be hundreds of millions of years older than fossils show.

They may have originated before some of Earth's most extreme ice ages, the Oxford-led study finds.

[Mongolia](country:MN)'s Kheseen microfossils are as well preserved as [China](country:CN)'s older Weng'an deposit, yet hold no clear animals.

The Tonian-era calibration pushes molecular clock estimates of animal origins earlier than the Ediacaran one.
