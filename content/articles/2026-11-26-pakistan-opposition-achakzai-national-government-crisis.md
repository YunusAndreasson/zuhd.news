Islamabad — A national government would free Imran Khan, its backers say.

It would restore fundamental rights and hold elections under an independent election commissioner, the alliance says.

Achakzai, the National Assembly opposition leader, called for it this week, with a multi-party conference first to agree the framework.

Imran Khan's party, the main opposition, distanced itself, and most members opposed the idea, a participant said.

The alliance says the government must only facilitate elections and never become permanent.
