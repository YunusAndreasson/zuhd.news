London — Researchers paired surface and underwater drones that route to keep acoustic contact.

The method targets cable inspection and mine hunting near chokepoints like the Strait of Hormuz.

Underwater vehicles lack GPS and radio, so they communicate only by sound at slow, modem-era speeds.

The unedited manuscript does not say whether the system was tested in water or only simulated.
