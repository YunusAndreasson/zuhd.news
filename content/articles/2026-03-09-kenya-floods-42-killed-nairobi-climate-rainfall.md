Floods killed 42 in Kenya, double last week's toll. Nairobi's informal settlements fill flood-prone valleys because no other land is affordable — climate change has doubled the likelihood of such rains. Kenyan President William Ruto pledged relief food and hospital care, but flood warnings remain in effect.
