Geneva — 2025 locked in record heat for thousands of years. The World Meteorological Organization confirmed 2025 as the second or third hottest year on record, with ocean warming doubling in rate since 2005. WMO chief Celeste Saulo said the heat is permanently locked in, leaving only the question of how much more accumulates before emissions are cut.
