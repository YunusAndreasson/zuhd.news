Korla — Pakistani troops are training in Xinjiang on drones and sniping.

It follows a September joint patrol at 4,700 metres near Khunjerab.

[China](country:CN)'s defence ministry said the drills cover high-altitude clearance operations, drone operation and long-range sniping, with joint command exercises.

Neither [Pakistan](country:PK)'s army nor China's has said how long Karakoram 2026 will run.
