Israeli sonic booms panicked Beirut worshippers on Eid morning. Israel's campaign in Lebanon has killed over 1,000 people and displaced more than a million since the US-Israel war on Iran began in February. Hezbollah and Israel continue fighting with no ceasefire agreed, leaving the displaced with no return date.
