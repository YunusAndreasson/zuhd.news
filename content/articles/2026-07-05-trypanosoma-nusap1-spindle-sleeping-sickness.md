Kinshasa — A single protein builds a deadly parasite's spindle.

Trypanosoma brucei, which causes African sleeping sickness, divides using cell-division machinery distinct from human cells, making NuSAP1 a drug target.

No drug based on the target exists yet, researchers said, calling for further study.
