General Santos — A 7.8 quake killed 45 off southern Mindanao.

The [Philippines](country:PH) Office of Civil Defense counted 630 injured, 17 missing and 145,000 residents affected; 2,500 homes were damaged.

A state of emergency was declared in General Santos as 460 homes were completely destroyed and search operations continue.
