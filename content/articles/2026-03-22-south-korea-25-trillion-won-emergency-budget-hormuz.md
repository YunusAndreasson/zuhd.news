Seoul — South Korea agreed on a $16.6 billion emergency budget. South Korea imports nearly all its oil; the Hormuz crisis has driven the won past 1,500 to the dollar. The National Assembly votes on the bill April 10; if it fails, the government has no plan to offset the oil cost surge.
