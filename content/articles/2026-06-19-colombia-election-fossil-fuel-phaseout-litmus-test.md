Bogotá — Colombia's oil moratorium is on the ballot Sunday.

[Colombia](country:CO) was the first major producer to halt new oil exploration; frontrunner de la Espriella campaigns on reversing it.

De la Espriella, the frontrunner, has pledged to lift the moratorium if he wins Sunday.
