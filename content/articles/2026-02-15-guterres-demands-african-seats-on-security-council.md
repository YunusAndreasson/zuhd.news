No African nation holds a permanent Security Council seat. UN Secretary-General Ant\u00f3nio Guterres called that absence "indefensible" at the 39th African Union Summit in Addis Ababa, telling leaders "this is 2026, not 1946." He demanded immediate ceasefires in Sudan, South Sudan, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo, where conflicts have displaced millions. Developing countries lose more to debt servicing and illicit financial flows than they receive in aid, a gap Guterres said must close with $1.3 trillion in annual financing by 2035. He insisted that whenever decisions about Africa and the world are made, Africa must be at the table.
