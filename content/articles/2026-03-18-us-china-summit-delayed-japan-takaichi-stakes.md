Japan faces Trump alone after the US-China summit collapsed. Tokyo refused to deploy support ships to Hormuz, citing Japan's constitutional ban on overseas military operations, drawing Trump's ire ahead of the summit. Analysts warn Trump may demand investment commitments or tariff concessions in exchange for not pressing Japan on Hormuz.
