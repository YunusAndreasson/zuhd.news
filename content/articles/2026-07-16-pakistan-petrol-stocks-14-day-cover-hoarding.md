Islamabad — Pakistan's petrol supply now covers just 14 days.

Consumption jumped 18 to 20 percent after a price cut, then renewed tension with [Iran](country:IR) doubled import premiums, spurring fears of hoarding.

The government's coordination council must decide whether to reimpose fuel conservation measures used earlier this year.
