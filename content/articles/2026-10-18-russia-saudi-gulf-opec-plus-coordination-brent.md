Doha — [Russia](country:RU) and [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) reaffirmed OPEC+ ties, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

Brent crude rose 4.9% in the 7 days to 16 October.

Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov met his Saudi counterpart Faisal bin Farhan on the sidelines of the Russia-Gulf Cooperation Council ministerial session, Russia's foreign ministry said.

[Bahrain](country:BH)'s foreign minister urged faster delivery of the joint action plan on energy, transport and food security.
