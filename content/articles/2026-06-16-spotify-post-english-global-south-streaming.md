Stockholm — Spotify's majority audience now listens in non-English.

AI-driven localization and tiered pricing in Africa, Asia and Latin America have shifted Spotify's listener base away from its English-speaking core.

Spotify has not disclosed per-stream payment rates for its tiered African, Asian, and Latin American markets.
