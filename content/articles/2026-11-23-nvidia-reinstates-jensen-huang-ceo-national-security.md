Santa Clara — Nvidia stock rose nearly 31% as Jensen Huang returned as CEO.

Unnamed [US](country:US) officials left the board satisfied he poses no security risk, 11 days after he resigned.

The board voted unanimously, formed an export-control committee and will finish its inquiry into whether foreign intelligence targeted him.

Board chair Harvey Jones said on an earnings call last week it did not intend to reinstate him.

Huang is set to testify before Congress.
