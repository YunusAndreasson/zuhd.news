Orenburg — Ukrainian drones struck Gazprom's helium plant at Orenburg, near the [Kazakhstan](country:KZ) border.

The complex processes gas and helium, a scarce element used in chip manufacturing and medicine.

[Ukraine](country:UA) claimed the strike; Kazakhstan has not confirmed disruptions to its oil and gas output.
