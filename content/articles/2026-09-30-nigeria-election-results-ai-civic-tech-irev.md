Abuja — Mundx says its AI tallied 3,754 of Osun's 3,763 polling units.

Each total links to its source sheet, so readers can check the numbers themselves, Mundx says.

The electoral commission's portal posts handwritten result sheets as photographs, so Mundx Analytics uses AI to read, validate and total them.

Mundx flagged 1,864 of 3,754 Osun sheets as blurry, mutilated or inconsistent.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s general election in January and February 2027 is the next test.
