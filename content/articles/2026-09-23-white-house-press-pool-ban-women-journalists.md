Washington — Trump banned CNN, Politico and MS NOW from the White House.

All 3 reporters blocked at the door on 19 September were women, Human Rights Watch said.

The [US](country:US) government told a court access is a privilege, and the outlets' reporting threatens national security.

Trump's first-term bans on a CNN reporter and a Playboy correspondent ended after both sued.

A Washington judge heard arguments Wednesday on whether to restore their access immediately.
