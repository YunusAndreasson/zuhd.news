Islamabad — The Islamabad High Court reserved its verdict Monday.

The ruling will license or forbid PTI's planned Sept 27 march.

A citizen sued to block the march, forcing [Pakistan](country:PK)'s courts to weigh assembly rights against public order.

PTI wants Imran Khan freed, and the court has not set a ruling date.
