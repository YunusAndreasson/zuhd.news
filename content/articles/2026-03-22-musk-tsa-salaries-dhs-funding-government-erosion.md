Washington — Elon Musk offered to fund federal airport security. The Transportation Security Administration's 65,000 screeners have worked without pay since February 14, when Congress deadlocked over Immigration and Customs Enforcement raids that killed 2 people in Minneapolis. Democrats refuse to fund the Department of Homeland Security until ICE ends warrantless raids, leaving airport lines growing longer each day.
