London — A model reads tumor genetics from MRI scans alone.

Researchers trained it on 1,303 brain metastases from lung cancer patients, reaching 83% accuracy identifying EGFR mutations that guide targeted therapy.

Biopsying a brain tumor is dangerous; validated imaging could let more patients skip surgery entirely.
