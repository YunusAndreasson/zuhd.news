Tokyo — Piyush Goyal brought 200 firms from [India](country:IN).

The trip aims to convert a decade-old ¥10 trillion investment pledge into actual factories.

[Japan](country:JP) supplies chip materials and equipment India's fabs cannot source elsewhere at scale.

Goyal continues to Nagoya and Osaka before the four-day visit ends Thursday.
