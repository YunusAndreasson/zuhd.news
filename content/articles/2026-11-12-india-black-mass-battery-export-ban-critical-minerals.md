New Delhi — A kilogram of one industrial black mass held 25g of lithium and 18g of cobalt.

[India](country:IN) banned black mass exports on 10 November to feed domestic recyclers short of feedstock.

Shredded cells yield a powder of graphite and metal oxides, which recyclers dissolve in acid to recover cobalt, nickel and lithium.

The International Energy Agency estimates announced Indian capacity would cover only 10% of battery feedstock in 2040.

End-of-life batteries become the largest feedstock source from 2035, the agency projects.
