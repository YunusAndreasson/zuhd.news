Washington — The [US](country:US) Federal Communications Commission proposed mandatory subscriber-ID rules.

Telecoms would collect every customer's government ID and address, ending anonymous prepaid phones the agency says enable scammers.

The American Civil Liberties Union said the mandate mirrors states that require ID registration to track mobile users.
