12 million Sudanese have fled their homes. The UN Security Council condemned the Rapid Support Forces siege of Kadugli in Kordofan, citing systematic killings, sexual violence, and famine after 22 months of blockade. No relief corridor reaches Kadugli, where residents have been without food or medical supplies for months.
