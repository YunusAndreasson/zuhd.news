Los Angeles — T. rex teeth read 36.3°C, like an elephant.

Size alone can keep giants warm, so the result does not settle metabolism.

Heavy-isotope bonding in enamel records temperature, and crocodilians from the same rivers read 30.9°C.

The California team next plans to test other dinosaurs with the same method.
