Houston — The International Space Station's Russian transfer tunnel is leaking again.

Engineers tracked these cracks since 2019; the US space agency rates the leak a '5' on likelihood and consequence — its highest category.

The agency and Congress are debating extending the station to 2032; no replacement is ready.
