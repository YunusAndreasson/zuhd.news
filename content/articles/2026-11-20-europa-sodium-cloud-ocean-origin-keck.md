Maunakea — Europa's sodium cloud has barely changed in 20 years.

Astronomers trace the cloud to salt from the ocean believed to lie beneath Europa's ice.

W. M. Keck Observatory tracked the diffuse cloud for more than two decades across Jupiter's dynamic environment.

NASA's Europa Clipper arrives at Jupiter in 2030 and can test the ocean origin.
