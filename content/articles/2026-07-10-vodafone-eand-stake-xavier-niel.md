London — e& sold its entire 16% Vodafone stake for $5.95 billion.

The [UAE](country:AE) group is exiting to refocus on core business; French billionaire [Xavier Niel](country:FR)'s Vega vehicle becomes Vodafone's top shareholder at a 15% premium.

Niel previously cut Tele2's workforce 15% after a similar stake; his board-representation plans are still unstated.
