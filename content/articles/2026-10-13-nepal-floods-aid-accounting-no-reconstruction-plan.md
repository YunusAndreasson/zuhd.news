Nuwakot — [Nepal](country:NP)'s finance ministry logs $24.89 million in flood relief received.

The Prime Minister's Office says $50.47 million in cash, materials and teams was pledged.

A Bhotekoshi flash flood hit Rasuwa and Nuwakot, washing away roads and bridges, then a landslide flood hit Gorkha on 11 October.

The government has moved to reconstruction and acknowledges the risk of glacial outburst floods.
