Karachi — A 20kg flour bag now costs Rs3,000.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s poorest households absorb the rise first, since flour is their largest food expense.

Announcing a million-tonne import without issuing the tender lets traders price in the shortfall before a ship is chartered.

Punjab, Sindh, Balochistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa have pledged imports, but no tender date is set.
