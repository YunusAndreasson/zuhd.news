Al Shuqaiq — Missiles and drones set two Saudi oil tankers ablaze offshore.

The Houthis say they are enforcing a Red Sea ban on Saudi vessels, threatening the Yanbu pipeline route [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) uses to bypass a shuttered Strait of Hormuz.

The US Navy warned Iran told the Houthis to stand ready to close the route entirely.
