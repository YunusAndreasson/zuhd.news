Israel launched ground forces into Lebanon while bombing Tehran. Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu rejected US President Donald Trump's request to spare Iranian oil infrastructure, even as Israeli forces ran simultaneous air campaigns against 2 capitals. Yemen's Houthi forces have warned they will join the war, forcing Israel to plan for a third simultaneous front.
