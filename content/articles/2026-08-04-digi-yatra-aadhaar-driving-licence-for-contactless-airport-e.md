New Delhi — Digi Yatra now accepts a driving licence too.

The face-recognition airport system, live at 39 airports, stores the credential on-device, says the Digi Yatra Foundation, a private body.

[India](country:IN) plans international trials next; 26 more airports join, narrowing any non-biometric queue.
