Antalya — 92,934 people registered for COP31, a record.

Provisional UN lists top Dubai's COP28, which drew more than 80,000.

UNFCCC lists show more than 14,000 delegates are host country guests, against about 1,000 at COP30.

The [US](country:US) sent no delegation, as at COP30. Israel and Myanmar are also absent.

The UNFCCC publishes final figures, counted from badges collected at the venue, after the summit ends.
