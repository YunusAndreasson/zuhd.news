Guwahati — Six people gained citizenship meant for 500,000.

[India](country:IN)'s Citizenship Amendment Act requires applicants to declare themselves foreign migrants, forfeiting any existing claim by birth or descent.

Tens of thousands of cases still sit before Foreigners Tribunals, where the burden of proof falls on the accused.
