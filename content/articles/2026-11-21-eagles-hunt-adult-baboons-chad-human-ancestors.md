Chad — Eagles likely hunt baboons weighing up to 57 pounds.

A study suggests giant eagles preyed on human ancestors far more recently than believed.

Baboon remains on Ennedi Plateau cliffs show talon punctures in the skull and scrapes on the shoulder bones.

Researchers cannot definitively rule out scavenging, but the severity of the punctures suggests a killing grip.

The authors use it to rebut critics who say a raptor could not have killed the Taung child.
