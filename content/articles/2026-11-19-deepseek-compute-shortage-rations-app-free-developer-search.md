Hangzhou — DeepSeek rationed its app, then made search free for developers.

Developers now pay only for tokens when the model runs server-side search or page fetches.

On 16 November the app cut long chats, shortened input limits and capped daily use, citing insufficient compute.

Direct calls to the standalone Search and Fetch APIs still cost 5 yuan per 1,000 successful requests.

Users on DeepSeek's social media pages have asked for a paid tier to keep long conversations.
