Doha — Three Iranian pilots have vanished for six months.

Geneva Convention protections for prisoners of war apply only if captors admit holding them.

Iran says the pilots ejected after [Qatar](country:QA)'s air defences downed their jets during a March strike on its territory.

The Red Cross has not said whether it will investigate the men's status.
