Spain — Pink noise lifted recall 0.88 word pairs in 37 Alzheimer's patients.

The 19% gain is small, and its clinical significance remains uncertain.

Pulses of 50 milliseconds fired at slow-wave peaks, a window only 200 milliseconds wide, helping the brain consolidate memories.

The study is not peer-reviewed, and several authors have commercial ties to Bitbrain or Elevvo, three on a pending patent.

Participants now use home devices to test whether the benefit lasts.
