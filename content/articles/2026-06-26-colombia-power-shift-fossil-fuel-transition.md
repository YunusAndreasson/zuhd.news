Bogotá — [Colombia](country:CO)'s next president pledged "fracking to the max."

Petro had made Colombia the first major oil producer to ban new fossil-fuel expansion and sign a non-proliferation treaty at COP28.

The Santa Marta transition process, co-led with [the Netherlands](country:NL), risks losing Colombia ahead of COP31 in November.
