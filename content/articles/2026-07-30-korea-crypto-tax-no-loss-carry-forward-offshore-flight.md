Seoul — Crypto gains above $1,740 face tax from 2027.

[South Korea](country:KR)'s rule taxes a good year's gains at up to 22% but permits no loss carry-forward, so a bad year after offers no deduction.

Opposition lawmaker Kim Sang-hoon warned the rule pushes traders to offshore exchanges the tax authority cannot see.
