New Delhi — India may end a decade of free digital payments.

Banks and apps have absorbed transaction costs since the payment system's 2016 launch.

Proposals under discussion include a 0.3-0.5% fee on big-business transactions above a set threshold.

[India](country:IN)'s government has not decided the fee's rate or which transactions it covers.
