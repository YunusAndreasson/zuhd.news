Gravelines — Jellyfish shut three nuclear reactors in [France](country:FR).

Gravelines is western Europe's largest nuclear plant, and it lost 3.2 gigawatts Tuesday.

Every coastal reactor draws seawater through screens for cooling, and warm seas make jellyfish blooms a recurring hazard.

Électricité de France has not said when the three shut reactors will return to the grid.
