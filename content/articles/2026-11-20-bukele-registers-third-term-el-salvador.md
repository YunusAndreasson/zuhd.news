San Salvador — Thousands chanted "six more years" as Bukele registered Thursday.

Polls give him 55% to 70% support, while opposition parties poll in single digits.

The Supreme Court lifted [El Salvador](country:SV)'s re-election ban in 2021, and in 2025 Congress approved indefinite re-election and six-year terms.

Human rights groups say the state of emergency has jailed more than 1% of the population with little due process.

The election is on 28 February 2027, against four opposition candidates.
