San Francisco — Judge Susan Illston blocked disaster-agency cuts Friday.

The ruling tests whether courts can stop an agency being hollowed out by understaffing.

A 2005 [US](country:US) law passed after Hurricane Katrina bars Homeland Security from significantly weakening the disaster agency.

Illston will rule on reversing the cuts next month.
