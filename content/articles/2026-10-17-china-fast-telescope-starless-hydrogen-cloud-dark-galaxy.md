China — A starless cloud weighs 130 million suns.

Standard dark matter theory predicts such starless halos, yet only 1 has been confirmed.

[China](country:CN)'s giant radio telescope measured 500,000 solar masses of hydrogen, and deep optical images found no stars, leaving dark matter as the bulk.

The team cannot rule out an ultra-faint galaxy hidden below the optical survey's brightness limit.

The paper, posted to arXiv on 24 September, has not been peer reviewed.
