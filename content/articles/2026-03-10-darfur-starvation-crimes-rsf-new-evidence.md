Sudanese paramilitaries burned crops before massacring El Fasher civilians. A new report links Sudan's Rapid Support Forces to the deliberate crop destruction — using starvation as a weapon is a war crime under the Rome Statute. If El Fasher falls, the RSF would control all of Darfur and encircle millions of civilians.
