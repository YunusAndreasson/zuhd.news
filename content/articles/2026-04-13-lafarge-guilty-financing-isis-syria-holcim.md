Paris — Paris court convicted Lafarge of financing ISIS. Judges ruled Lafarge paid €5.6 million to Islamic State and al-Nusra between 2013 and 2014 to maintain operations in northern Syria. Former CEO Bruno Lafont received 6 years in prison; Lafarge faces confiscation of assets worth tens of millions.
