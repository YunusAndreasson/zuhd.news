Exeter — Researchers simulated a cell, not a planet, to find life elsewhere.

Methanogens need no sunlight or oxygen and emit methane detectable across interstellar distances, making them Earth's oldest and most findable metabolism, astrophysicist Arwen Nicholson said.

The model narrows which exoplanets deserve scarce, expensive telescope time next.
