Islamabad — Wheat flour costs 53.53% more than a year ago.

Flour rose 5.72% in a week while petrol fell Rs83 a litre in 25 days.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s statistics bureau put weekly inflation at 9.49% year on year, its first single-digit reading in weeks, as fuel prices fell.

Cooking gas is up 67.49% on the year and chicken 30.10%, the bureau's data show.

Provincial governments have yet to cut passenger and freight fares to match the fall in fuel prices.
