China — ByteDance is advertising engineer pay of up to 975,000 yuan.

That is more than 22 times the 43,377 yuan average disposable income in 2025.

Forward-deployed engineers embed with customers to fit AI models into operations, and postings rose 1,523% in seven months, Maimai data show.

"Most positions do not pay that much," said George Wang, a senior human resources professional at a Beijing AI company.

Anthropic has pledged $100 million to train 10,000 such engineers by the end of 2027.
