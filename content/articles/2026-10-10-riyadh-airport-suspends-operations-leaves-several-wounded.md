Riyadh — Dozens wounded, 5 in intensive care, after a missile hit the airport.

Every flight to the capital was diverted or cancelled, shortly before it hosts major energy and investment conferences.

A diplomatic source attributed the missile to the Houthis, and [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s civil aviation authority suspended operations pending a damage assessment.

The Houthis have not claimed Saturday's strike, though they claimed Thursday's, which killed 3 people.

Donald Trump said he is weighing whether the [US](country:US) joins Saudi strikes on the Houthis.
