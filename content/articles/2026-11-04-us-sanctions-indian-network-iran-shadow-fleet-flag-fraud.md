Washington — Treasury sanctioned a Chandigarh couple for faking Iranian tankers' flags.

The Treasury says the network let the shadow fleet of [Iran](country:IR) evade restrictions on its operation.

Suniel Kumar Sharma and Jaspreet Preeti Shahid purported to represent nations from Guyana to Eswatini, supplying false flags and fake certifications.

The [US](country:US) will sanction anyone aiding Iran by land, sea or air, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said.
