Kano — [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Kano State has banned durbar processions and horse-riding.

The ban aims to head off clashes between rival claimants to one traditional throne.

The security council acted after deposed emir Aminu Ado-Bayero drew thousands in an unscheduled procession through the city.

Sanusi already holds the state's official backing, while Ado-Bayero's camp challenges that recognition at the Supreme Court.

The Supreme Court hearing on Ado-Bayero's challenge is scheduled for April 2027.
