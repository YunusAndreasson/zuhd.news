Leipzig — 40,000 bitcoin could end a money-laundering trial.

Prosecutors estimate the defendant holds 56,000 bitcoin, worth about $3.57 billion.

He would also pay 38 million euros to compensate potential victims, and the court considers the copyright charges too old.

Judge Karsten Nickel has questioned whether all the coins came from crime.

Both sides review the terms before the trial resumes on 3 December, aiming to finish before Christmas.
