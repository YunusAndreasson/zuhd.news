London — A heart-monitor AI can learn one hospital.

That failure hits health systems too poor to safely retrain it locally.

Full fine-tuning overwrites a model's general knowledge with one hospital's own quirks and equipment noise.

The study is an accepted manuscript, not yet the final published version.
