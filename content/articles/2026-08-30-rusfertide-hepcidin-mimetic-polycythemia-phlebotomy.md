Washington — Rusfertide mimics a hormone that gates iron.

It replaces repeated therapeutic bleeding with a weekly injection instead.

Unlike cytotoxic therapy, the new drug starves bone marrow of iron instead of directly killing blood cells.

[Japan](country:JP)'s Takeda, which will sell the drug under the brand Mimrylo, has not disclosed a price.
