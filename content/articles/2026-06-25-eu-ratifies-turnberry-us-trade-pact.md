Brussels — EU vote suspends $4 billion in retaliatory duties.

The Turnberry pact ends the Boeing-Airbus dispute while the [US](country:US) retains a 15% tariff on most European goods.

Terms expire end of 2029; the European Commission will assess the deal's impact before any extension.
