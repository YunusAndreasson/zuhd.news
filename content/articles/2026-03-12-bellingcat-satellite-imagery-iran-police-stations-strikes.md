15 Iranian police stations were destroyed in 3 days. Bellingcat compared PlanetScope satellite images taken 2 days apart, cross-referencing destruction patterns with OpenStreetMap to confirm each building as a police facility. Police stations are civilian infrastructure under international humanitarian law; no party has claimed the strikes.
