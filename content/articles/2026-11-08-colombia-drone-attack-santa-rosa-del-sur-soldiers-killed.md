Santa Rosa del Sur — Explosive drones killed 5 soldiers and wounded 33.

No armed group has claimed the attack, and authorities have named no suspect.

Footage reported as verified shows drones hitting barracks and administrative buildings, collapsing roofs, with 8 of the wounded in serious condition.

[Colombia](country:CO)'s President Abelardo de la Espriella ordered operations intensified to capture those responsible.
