Khartoum — Sudanese cattle carry FMD serotypes A and O.

Vaccinating against the wrong serotype gives [Sudan](country:SD)'s herds no real protection.

Livestock is Sudan's largest non-oil export, and importing states like Saudi Arabia close borders at any outbreak.

The sampling window closed in 2022, months before war collapsed Sudan's veterinary surveillance system.
