Savasleyka — Ukraine says it damaged 5 aircraft at a base east of Moscow.

Three were MiG-31s used to fire hypersonic missiles, the Ukrainian General Staff said.

Ukraine's drone campaign has cut Russian refinery processing 45% since late October, industry sources say.

[Russia](country:RU) did not immediately comment on the claim.

Kyiv says it will stop striking Russian energy targets only when Moscow does the same.
