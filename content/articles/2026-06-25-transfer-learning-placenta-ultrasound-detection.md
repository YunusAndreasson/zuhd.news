Vancouver — 46 placenta ultrasounds trained a preeclampsia AI.

Transfer learning — adapting a general-image model to the dataset — reached 0.86 diagnostic discrimination across samples from [Canada](country:CA)'s British Columbia Women's Hospital.

The study used placentas collected post-delivery, not real-time scans; no clinical deployment has been announced.
