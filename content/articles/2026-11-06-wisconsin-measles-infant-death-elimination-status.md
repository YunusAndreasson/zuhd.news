Wisconsin — 3 measles deaths in the [US](country:US) were reported this week.

The infant was the state's first measles death since 1990.

The outbreak has sickened over 430 people in 13 counties, and officials say many cases go unreported.

The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention counts 4 deaths this year against 9 reported by states, after changing how it tallies them.

A Pan American Health Organization committee meets 10 to 13 November to decide whether the US keeps measles elimination status.
