A US Tomahawk cruise missile killed 170 people at a school in Minab. Amnesty International's satellite analysis confirmed the school was directly struck — not an adjacent military compound — by a weapon only US forces deploy in this conflict. The Trump administration announced an investigation but set no timeline for accountability.
