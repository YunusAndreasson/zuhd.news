Urumqi — [China](country:CN) made Uyghur religious life a state secret.

A March 1 regulation extends national secrecy law to religious practices and cultural expression, embedding information control into Xinjiang's existing surveillance system.

Journalism, cross-border family contact, and legal defence now qualify as secrecy violations.
