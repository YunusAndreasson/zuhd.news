London — 2050 aviation emissions will run 50% above the [UK](country:GB) government's own prior forecast.

Heathrow's third runway approval, expected by 2029, rested on those now-abandoned numbers.

Officials cut expected sustainable-fuel use from 50% to 30%, since crop- and waste-oil feedstock is limited.

The gap must now be closed by costly carbon removal or deeper cuts elsewhere.
