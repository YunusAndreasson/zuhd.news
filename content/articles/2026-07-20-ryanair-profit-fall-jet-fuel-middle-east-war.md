Dublin — Ryanair profit fell over a third to €538 million.

The carrier hedged 80% of fuel costs, but unhedged jet fuel more than doubled since strikes shut the Strait of Hormuz to oil traffic.

Ryanair declined full-year guidance, calling fares "heavily dependent" on late bookings through September.
