8 dead, 2 burned in a fireworks store explosion. A villager improperly discharged fireworks near a shop in Donghai county, Jiangsu province, at 6:30 a.m. Sunday — the first day of China's 9-day Lunar New Year holiday — triggering a blast and fire that took nearly 10 hours to extinguish. Authorities detained those responsible and ordered nationwide safety inspections at all fireworks production, transport, and sales operations.
