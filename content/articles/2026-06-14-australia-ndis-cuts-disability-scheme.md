Canberra — 200,000 [Australians](country:AU) face disability scheme removal by 2031.

Health Minister Mark Butler is pushing cuts through parliament this month; the opposition may force a 6-month delay.

The disability scheme committee reports Tuesday; state ministers say they cannot replace support for those removed.
