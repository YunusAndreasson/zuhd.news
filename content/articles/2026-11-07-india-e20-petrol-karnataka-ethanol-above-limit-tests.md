New Delhi — Rahul Gandhi says 11 of 14 Karnataka petrol samples breached the ethanol cap.

Gandhi says higher blends cut mileage, raise repair costs and go unlabelled at pumps.

He cited 31.2% ethanol in an Indian Oil sample and 30.5% in a Hindustan Petroleum sample, drawn from seven districts.

Gandhi asked Prime Minister Narendra Modi what action will follow, and challenged the government to a debate on [India](country:IN)'s ethanol policy.
