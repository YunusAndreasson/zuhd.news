Many WorkBuddy users hit usage limits on the agent's first day.

Tencent cites Artificial Analysis benchmarks that put Hy4 at about $0.78 per task, second among Chinese models.

Tencent raised free usage allowances and says it will keep adjusting resources to meet demand.
