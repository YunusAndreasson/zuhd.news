Pakistan — Flour now sells at Rs150-170 a kilo, up over 200% since 2021.

Roti has gone from Rs8 to Rs25, and Rawalpindi flour tops Rs200.

Punjab handed wheat buying to private aggregators, 9 of 11 never started, and 160,000 of 3 million tonnes were bought.

The government has not firmed up a wheat import plan, and Punjab releases 3,500 of the usual 25,000 daily tonnes.
