London — Male dragonflies pull 6-G turns defending territory.

They chase each other's tail into tight spirals guided by one simple rule, unlike the flatter dives they use hunting prey, a Royal Society Interface study found.

Researchers say the rule could help engineers build drones that navigate on simple vision alone.
