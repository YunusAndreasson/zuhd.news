Paris — France ordered internet providers to block Polymarket.

The regulator says a 2024 transaction ban failed — 205,057 French users visited in June, often bypassing it with a VPN.

[France](country:FR) joins 30-plus countries blocking the platform; violators face fines up to 100,000 euros.
