Beijing — [China](country:CN)'s metric revision hides 700 million annual tonnes of emissions.

An independent analysis found China retroactively redefined carbon intensity, creating a gap equal to Germany's entire annual emissions.

Under the new metric, China could meet its 2030 Paris pledges even if actual emissions rise further.
