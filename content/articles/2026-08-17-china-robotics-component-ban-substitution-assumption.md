San Francisco — Startups can't buy the parts they need.

Washington's ban on [China](country:CN)'s components assumed substitutes exist, but for many none do.

Component manufacturing needs deep capital and tacit know-how, so a ban without a domestic line just removes production.

Startups say their runway is months, not years, before the parts run out.
