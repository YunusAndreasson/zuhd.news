Quito — Insects fleeing heat uphill may hit a second, deadlier limit.

Pollination and decomposition in mountain farm belts that feed dense populations are at risk.

Insects draw oxygen by diffusion through tracheal tubes, and falling air pressure at altitude starves flight muscles regardless of temperature.

A Functional Ecology review calls the ceiling likely, not yet an established forecast.
