Iran labeled its women's footballers "wartime traitors" for a silent anthem in Australia. The team is playing in Australia while Iran is at war; athletes who have previously defied the government faced arrest and career bans. Human rights groups are urging Canberra to offer protection before the squad departs, with no government response announced.
