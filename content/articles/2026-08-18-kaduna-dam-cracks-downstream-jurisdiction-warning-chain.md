Kaduna — Cracks at the Kangimi Dam threaten downstream floods.

A failure could flood communities across [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Kaduna, Niger states and the capital territory.

Federal agencies own the dam, but the state government warning of danger has no power to inspect it.

Niger's emergency agency has urged federal authorities to conduct an urgent technical assessment.
