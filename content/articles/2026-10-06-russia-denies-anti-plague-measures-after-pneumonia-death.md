Irkutsk — Authorities say pneumonia killed a 28-year-old anti-plague institute technician.

[Kazakhstan](country:KZ), [Kyrgyzstan](country:KG), [Tajikistan](country:TJ) and [Uzbekistan](country:UZ) announced emergency measures to prevent plague spreading.

Irkutsk's governor says no microorganisms linked to her lab work were found in her samples.

[Russia](country:RU)'s health watchdog calls reports of anti-plague measures untrue, though one local outlet reported up to 200 people quarantined.

The WHO rates the risk moderate to low for Irkutsk and is still verifying Russia's test results.
