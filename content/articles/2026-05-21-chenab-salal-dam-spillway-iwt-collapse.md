Sialkot — Salal Dam opened; [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Chenab rose 3 metres.

Sialkot authorities activated emergency controls; [India](country:IN) suspended the Indus Waters Treaty in 2025, ending mandatory advance notification for dam releases.

Silt flushing runs through May 30; authorities have banned movement along river banks and ordered 24-hour monitoring.
