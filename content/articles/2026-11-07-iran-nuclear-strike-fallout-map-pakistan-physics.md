Natanz — Enrichment plants hold almost no fission products.

A viral map claiming fallout would blanket [Pakistan](country:PK) and [Afghanistan](country:AF) rests on no calculations.

Uranium carries far fewer fission products than irradiated reactor fuel, so contamination stays local.

The UN nuclear watchdog said radiation outside struck facilities stayed normal after the June 2025 attacks.

Bushehr's reactor, on the Gulf coast, is the one site where a severe accident could cross borders.
