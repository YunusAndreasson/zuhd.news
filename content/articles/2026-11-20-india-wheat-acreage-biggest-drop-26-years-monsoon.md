Jalna — Wheat sowing in [India](country:IN) trails last year by 15.3%.

A smaller crop in the world's second-largest wheat producer threatens to lift food prices.

El Niño pushed monsoon rain up to 41% below average in key wheat states, so farmers are switching to chickpeas.

"We don't have enough water even to sow the crop," said Vilas Bhutekar, a Jalna farmer.

Ten industry officials expect planting to fall about 6%, the steepest drop since 2000/01.
