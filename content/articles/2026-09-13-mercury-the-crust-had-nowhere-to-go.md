Washington — Mercury's radius shrank up to 6 miles.

The revised contraction rewrites when Mercury's core stopped driving its magnetic field.

Its oversized iron core cooled and shrank, buckling the crust into visible thrust-fault scarps.

The BepiColombo probe, now orbiting Mercury, is expected to settle the question.
