Lagos — Treasury designated Mukhtar Muhammad and 3 Lagos firms for financing Islamic State West Africa Province.

Informal currency changers are how the group moves funds across [Nigeria](country:NG)'s north and the Lake Chad basin, Treasury alleged.

All [US](country:US) persons are now barred from transacting with the firms; foreign banks face secondary sanctions risk.
