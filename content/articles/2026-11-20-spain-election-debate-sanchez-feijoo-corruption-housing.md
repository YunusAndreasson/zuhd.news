Madrid — Ateneo del Dato poll: 45% say Feijóo won, 32% Sánchez.

[Spain](country:ES)'s Popular Party leads but likely needs far-right Vox to govern after 29 November.

A 40dB poll projects 192 seats for the right-wing bloc against 155 for Sánchez's backers, with 176 needed.

"My government has not been perfect, I have made mistakes," Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez said at the outset.

Vox leader Santiago Abascal joins Sánchez and Feijóo in a four-way debate next Thursday.
