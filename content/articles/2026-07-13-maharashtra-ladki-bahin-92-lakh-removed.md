Mumbai — 9.2 million women lost cash transfers.

A Comptroller and Auditor General audit found nearly 40% of enrollees in [India](country:IN)'s Maharashtra state failed identity verification, with others exceeding income or age limits.

Officials say no money will be recovered except from male or government-employee beneficiaries wrongly enrolled.
