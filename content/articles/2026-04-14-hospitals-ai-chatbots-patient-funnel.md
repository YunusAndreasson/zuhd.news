Hartford — Hospitals are deploying chatbots to intercept patient searches. US health systems are building branded AI chatbots to route patients into their billing systems; no evidence yet shows they improve outcomes. Hartford HealthCare's PatientGPT launches this year; no outcome data exists to evaluate whether it helps patients.
