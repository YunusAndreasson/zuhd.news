Washington — China called the Hormuz blockade "dangerous and irresponsible" as its cargo ships face US Navy turnaway. China absorbed 90% of Iran's oil exports before the Feb. 28 attack; the blockade cuts that supply as Trump's Beijing summit is 4 weeks away. Whether Beijing lets the summit proceed signals its actual Hormuz red line.
