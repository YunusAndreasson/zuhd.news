Geneva — 2.5 million researchers gain free publishing services on one platform.

Funders from 11 countries back it, including [Germany](country:DE)'s research foundation and [Switzerland](country:CH)'s national science foundation.

Authors post a preprint, reviewers sign public reports, and the platform then flags it "peer reviewed" on the same site.

Stanford metascientist Mario Malički warned that running it at scale "becomes a major undertaking of who runs the checks."

The expanded platform launches in early November, and indexing services have not said how they will treat it.
