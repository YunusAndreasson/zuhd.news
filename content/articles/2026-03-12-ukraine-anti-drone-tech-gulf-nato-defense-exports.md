Ukraine became a defense exporter while fighting for survival. Gulf states facing Iranian drone swarms are buying Ukrainian jamming and intercept systems developed under 3 years of live Russian drone fire. Gulf demand will keep growing as long as Iranian drone campaigns continue, with sales funding Ukraine's own defense production.
