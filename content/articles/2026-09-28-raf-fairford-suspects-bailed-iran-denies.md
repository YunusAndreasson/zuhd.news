Gloucestershire — Police bailed all five men arrested near RAF Fairford.

A major incident and home evacuations followed claims of Iranian proxies, before evidence held anyone.

RAF Fairford hosts the US B-52s that struck Iranian missile sites, the reason investigators weighed a state-linked plot.

[Iran](country:IR)'s embassy in London called the claims "fabricated speculations" and threatened legal action.

The five stay barred from contacting each other as counter-terror police pursue multiple lines of inquiry.
