Gilgit — 7.1 million people live downstream of unstable glacial lakes.

Meltwater pools behind ice and moraine dams; a fresh westerly wave bringing rain and above-normal heat risks a sudden release, the [Pakistan](country:PK) Meteorological Department said.

The agency ordered round-the-clock monitoring through the coming week's storms.
