London — A stained slide can now predict tumor weaknesses.

Gene-dependency data today needs CRISPR screens few hospitals can run.

The model infers gene activity from a biopsy image, then predicts which genes a tumor needs to survive.

The unedited, early-access study has not been tested outside its mostly European-ancestry training data.
