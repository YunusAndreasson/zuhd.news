Jakarta — Wood pellets bound for [Japan](country:JP) are clearing fresh forest.

Indigenous communities lose land where new wood-pellet concessions are cheapest to grant.

Global rules count biomass burning as zero-emission at the smokestack, booking the deforestation instead against [Indonesia](country:ID)'s own ledger.

The carbon debt from each cleared hectare outlasts every climate deadline it's meant to meet.
