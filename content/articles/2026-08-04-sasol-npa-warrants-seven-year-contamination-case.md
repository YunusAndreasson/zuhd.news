Secunda — Prosecutors seized evidence seven years after a whistleblower's complaint.

A broken valve allegedly sent vanadium and other waste into the Klipspruit River in 2019; delay let river samples wash away before regulators arrived.

[South Africa](country:ZA)'s prosecuting authority aims to finish the investigation by the end of September.
