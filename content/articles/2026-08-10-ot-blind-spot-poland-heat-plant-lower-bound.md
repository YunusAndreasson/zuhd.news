Warsaw — A second Polish heat plant was compromised undetected.

The breach happened alongside January's attack on 30+ energy sites.

Industrial control systems rarely log intrusions, since they are built for safety, not for tracking attackers.

How many more incidents from January remain undiscovered is still unknown.
