3 US aircrew are missing after a tanker aircraft crashed in western Iraq. The US military said the KC-135 refueling plane — in service for over 60 years — went down with no hostile fire reported. Rescue operations were underway Thursday, and the crash raises questions about the strain of round-the-clock combat sorties on aging US aircraft.
