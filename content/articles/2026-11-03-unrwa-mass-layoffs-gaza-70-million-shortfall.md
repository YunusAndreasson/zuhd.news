Gaza City — The UN refugee agency ended 43 microfinance staff contracts in September.

Hundreds of staff are already out, and thousands more across the agency face dismissal.

Acting Commissioner-General Christian Saunders told the staff union he is acting on a $70 million deficit, and microfinance was halted only in Gaza.

Employees and union officials reject that explanation, saying the layoffs strike at the refugee question itself.

The Trump-chaired Board of Peace, which rejects UNRWA's role in Gaza, is preparing to take charge.
