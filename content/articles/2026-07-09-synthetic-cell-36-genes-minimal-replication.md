Minneapolis — Built from non-living parts, "SpudCell" just copied its own DNA.

Kate Adamala's team assembled a minimal cell from only 36 genes that can replicate, though it's not yet shown it can evolve.

Researchers open-sourced the design so labs elsewhere can build on the breakthrough next.
