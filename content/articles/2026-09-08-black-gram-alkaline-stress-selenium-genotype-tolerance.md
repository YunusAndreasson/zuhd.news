London — Selenium priming raised one landrace's shoot growth 45%.

Black gram, a South Asian staple pulse, is losing yield to alkaline soils.

High soil pH locks iron and zinc out of reach, so alkaline-stressed plants starve despite nutrient-rich soil.

The response varied by landrace, meaning tolerance already exists within the breeding pool.
