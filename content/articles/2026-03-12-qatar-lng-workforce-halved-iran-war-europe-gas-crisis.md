Qatar declared force majeure on LNG shipments. Qatar halved the workforce at its North Field expansion, which was meant to lift LNG output from 77 to 126 million tonnes per year by 2027 — Europe's main alternative to Russian gas. Europe's gas supply now depends on a project running at half capacity, with Qatar setting no restart date.
