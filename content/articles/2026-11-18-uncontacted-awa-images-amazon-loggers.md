Tawari — About 20 uncontacted Awá emerged from the forest Monday and asked for food.

Survival International says loggers, hunters and ranchers are closing in from all sides of their territory.

Contact can kill: about 40% of Colombia's isolated Nukak died of disease after outsiders reached them in the 1980s.

[Brazil](country:BR)'s Indigenous affairs agency Funai says it is reviewing the footage and is not seeking contact.
