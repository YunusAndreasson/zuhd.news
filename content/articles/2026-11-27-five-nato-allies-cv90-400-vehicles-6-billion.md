Stockholm — Five countries ordered more than 400 CV90s for about $6 billion.

European states have bought land systems through separate national programs, duplicating development and support costs.

[Sweden](country:SE), Finland, Norway, Lithuania and the Netherlands share one CV9035 configuration, but each keeps its own communications and battle management systems.

Past attempts at common European armor, including a Franco-German tank, ended in separate national designs.
