Al-Qaeda-linked fighters have pushed to the 500-kilometre Mali-Senegal border. The Group for the Support of Islam and Muslims expanded "exponentially" into southwestern Mali after military juntas expelled French forces, creating ungoverned territory along the porous frontier. President Bassirou Diomaye Faye toured the underdeveloped border region this month, pledging a stronger state presence.
