Milan — Edison chose Qatar and US LNG over Algeria's pipelines. The Italian subsidiary of French energy company Électricité de France holds a 6.4 billion m³/year QatarEnergy contract but cut Algerian pipeline imports in September 2025, betting American supplies would compensate. Italy is now in emergency talks with Algeria, the US, and Azerbaijan to fill the gap as European gas hits €70/MWh.
