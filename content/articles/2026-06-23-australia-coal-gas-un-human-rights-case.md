Canberra — Australians filed the first UN climate-as-human-rights complaint.

The claim targets [Australia](country:AU)'s coal and gas export approvals — the first action at an international body since the 2025 ICJ ruling that states can be sued over climate change.

A UN finding is not binding but Australia would face formal demands to respond.
