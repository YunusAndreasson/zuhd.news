Kuala Lumpur — Malaysia spends RM2 billion monthly to hold fuel prices. The government caps fuel at fixed prices, absorbing every cent of the 50-percent post-war crude rise through public finances. Brent crude at $112 already exceeds the $100 threshold Deputy Prime Minister Fadillah Yusof named as Malaysia's subsidy review trigger.
