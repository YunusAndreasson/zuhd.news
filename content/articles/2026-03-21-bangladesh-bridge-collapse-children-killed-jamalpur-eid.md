Jamalpur — 4 children drowned when a floating bridge collapsed. Hundreds packed the structure for Eid celebrations despite its known instability, with no authority restricting or replacing it. Families of the 5 children still missing in the river are demanding answers from authorities who allowed the condemned bridge to remain open.
