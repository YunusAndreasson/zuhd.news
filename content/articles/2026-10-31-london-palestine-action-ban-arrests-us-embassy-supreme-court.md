London — Police arrested 139 people for holding Palestine Action placards.

Defend Our Juries, which organised the protest, counts more than 4,000 such arrests since July 2025.

Terrorism law makes support for a proscribed group punishable by up to 14 years, so a placard saying so is an offence.

Labour MP John McDonnell said opposing the ban does not automatically mean supporting the group.

The [UK](country:GB) Supreme Court hears the group's appeal over two days from Wednesday.
