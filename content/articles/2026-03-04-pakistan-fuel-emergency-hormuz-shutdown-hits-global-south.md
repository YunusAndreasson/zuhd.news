Pakistan declared a fuel emergency as Hormuz closed. Freight costs jumped from $900,000 to $4 million per ship and insurance from $30,000 to $400,000 — Pakistan imports all its fuel through the now-disrupted strait. The cabinet ordered weekly price revisions and asked Saudi Arabia to route oil through the Red Sea.
