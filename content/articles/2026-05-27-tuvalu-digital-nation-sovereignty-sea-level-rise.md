Funafuti — [Tuvalu](country:TV) builds digital statehood before its land submerges.

A digital twin of 124 islands, a cultural archive and e-government system would preserve Tuvalu's sovereignty even without physical territory.

International law has never recognized a digital replica as a substitute for physical territory.
