Peshawar — 4 in 10 children under five in [Pakistan](country:PK) are stunted.

Poor nutrition risks lifelong losses in learning, income and productivity, the research finds.

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa cut stunting most from 2011 to 2018 by pairing cash transfers with fortification and health-worker visits.

The province's next test is reaching the districts its own average conceals.
