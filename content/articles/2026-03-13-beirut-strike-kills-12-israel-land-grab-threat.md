Israel bombed a Beirut refugee camp, killing 12. Israeli Defense Minister Israel Katz threatened to annex Lebanese territory and issued evacuation orders for a tenth of Lebanon, where 687 people have been killed since the ceasefire ended. Israel ordered its military to prepare a ground expansion after Hezbollah fired 200 rockets at Israeli bases near Tel Aviv.
