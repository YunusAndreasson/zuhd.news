Moscow — Putin met Iran's security chief 3 hours, state agency Mehr reported.

Ambassador Kazem Jalali said [Iran](country:IR) will speed up the Rasht-Astara railway on the North-South corridor.

Jalali said Mohsen Rezaei told Putin talks alone cannot end the war, citing a letter from US Vice President JD Vance.

Jalali said Iran requires the US to accept its 7 strategic conditions before any diplomatic initiative.
