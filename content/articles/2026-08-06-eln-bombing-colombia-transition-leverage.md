Cúcuta — A truck bomb wounded 14 police and civilians.

The ELN struck days before president-elect Abelardo de la Espriella takes office in [Colombia](country:CO), after he gave armed groups a surrender ultimatum.

His one-month deadline for the groups to disarm expired the same day as the blast.
