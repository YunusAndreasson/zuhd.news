Chandigarh — Punjab farmers were barricaded from a Delhi protest.

[India](country:IN) says paddy, wheat, pulses and milk are excluded from talks with the [US](country:US) over a trade deal.

No deal is finalized yet; farmers plan a Kisan Mahapanchayat protest regardless.
