Washington — Video-game lobby wants Anbernic on a piracy list.

The Entertainment Software Association represents Nintendo, Microsoft, PlayStation, Amazon, Epic, EA and Ubisoft.

It says Anbernic devices routinely ship with memory cards preloaded with unauthorised copies of its members' games.

Anbernic does not appear to preload cards when selling from its own website.

A [US](country:US) Trade Representative listing carries no direct legal penalty but could presage a Section 301 investigation.
