Washington — Deportation orders issued in absentia nearly tripled since January 2025, Inkstick, a foreign-policy outlet, reported.

A Princeton report ties the [US](country:US) rise to courthouse arrests that make appearing a risk.

Master calendar hearings can issue a dozen removal orders in half an hour, and some people never learn their date moved.

Congress wrote the law that gives the attorney general this power, says Princeton anthropologist Amelia Frank-Vitale.
