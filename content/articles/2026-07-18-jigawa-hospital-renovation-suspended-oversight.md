Gumel — A surprise visit exposed 1.3bn naira wasted.

Governor Umar Namadi suspended the Gumel General Hospital renovation after finding low-quality materials and non-compliant work by contractor Yasmeen Automobile [Nigeria](country:NG) Limited.

Namadi says he will meet the contractor; no recovery or rework timeline has been set.
