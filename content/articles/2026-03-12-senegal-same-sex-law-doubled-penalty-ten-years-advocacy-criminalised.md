135 to 0: Senegal's parliament criminalised same-sex advocacy. The bill raises prison terms for same-sex relations to 10 years and adds up to 7 years for promoting or financing them — making lawyers, journalists, and NGOs who document the crackdown into criminals. President Bassirou Diomaye Faye, elected on a reformist platform, has not signalled opposition to the bill and must now sign or reject it.
