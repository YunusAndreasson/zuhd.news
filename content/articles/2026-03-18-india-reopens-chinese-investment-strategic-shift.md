India reversed its 5-year ban on Chinese investment. New Delhi imposed the restriction after border deaths in 2020 and now allows Chinese stakes up to 10% automatically, with strategic sectors fast-tracked within 60 days and majority-Indian ownership required. Chinese investment in Indian solar and electronics — the 2 sectors still needing approval — awaits its first test cases.
