Biu — Troops freed 92 kidnapped civilians in Borno.

Islamic State West Africa Province fighters abducted 52 men, 33 women, and 7 children from a road in Biu.

The rescued were escorted to Damaturu; [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Borno roads carry no civilian security guarantee.
