Bishkek — At least 2 extraditions of [Kyrgyzstan](country:KG)'s critics used chartered private jets.

Authorities now routinely detain critics abroad, fly them home and prosecute them, a joint journalists' investigation found.

The flights cost roughly 10 million som, the investigators estimate.
