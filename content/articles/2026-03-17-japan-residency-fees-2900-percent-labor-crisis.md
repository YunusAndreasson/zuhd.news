Japan hiked permanent residency fees 2,900%. Japan's Liberal Democratic Party-led ruling coalition passed the increase despite record service-sector vacancies, with conservative lawmakers insisting AI and automation will replace workers. Existing residents must pay the higher renewal fees immediately under the new law.
