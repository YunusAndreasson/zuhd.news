Jenin — A journalist freed after 22 months found his camp empty.

[Israel](country:IL)'s military displaced over 30,000 people from three camps, the largest West Bank displacement since 1967.

Administrative detention kept Mujahed Al-Saadi from reporting the operation on Jenin, Tulkarem and Nur Shams camps, so he met the destruction at once.

The camp stays occupied and its residents displaced, Al-Saadi says, as he tries to resume reporting.
