Kuala Lumpur — 5,000 Rohingya face repatriation to Myanmar.

[Malaysia](country:MY) is not a party to the Refugee Convention, so Anwar Ibrahim's safety pledge is policy, not enforceable law.

The government has set no return date, and Myanmar's junta controls whether it holds.
