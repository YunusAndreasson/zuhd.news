New York — Google will train AI on Spirit Airlines' estimated 100 million emails.

It is the first data sale of this scale in the AI era.

Google agreed to exclude health, drug-testing, disciplinary and identification records and to de-identify the rest, with an independent assessor reviewing.

"Someone always finds an opening to connect all the data points," said Arjun Bhatnagar, CEO of privacy startup Cloaked.

Google, which intends the data for AI model training, did not respond to a request for comment.
