Berlin — Police just deployed AI cameras at a subway stop.

A printed shirt renders wearers invisible to those detectors.

The pattern doesn't hide the wearer — it's tuned via gradient ascent to sit on the detector's wrong side.

Detectors get retrained and printed patches degrade with angle and light, artist Simon Weckert said.
