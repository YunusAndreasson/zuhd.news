Beijing — XREAL lost RMB456 million in 2025 on RMB516 million revenue.

XREAL leads display-based augmented-reality glasses with 27% of 2025 global revenue, iResearch data show.

Gross margin rose to 35.2% from 22.1%, yet operating expenses still produced a RMB245 million operating loss.

"Everybody's losing money," said XREAL chief executive Chi Xu in May.

XREAL has filed to list in Hong Kong, with AURA's first holiday season ahead.
