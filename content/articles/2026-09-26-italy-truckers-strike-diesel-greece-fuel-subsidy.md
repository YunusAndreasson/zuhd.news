Rome — [Italy](country:IT)'s truckers threaten a strike over €2.40 diesel.

One day's stoppage would halt the country, Assotir general secretary Claudio Donati warned.

An excise rise adds 6.1 cents a litre, taking diesel past €2.40 in 9 of 20 regions, the National Consumers Union says.

The consumer group Adusbef says a strike would harm citizens without lowering pump prices.

[Greece](country:GR) announces its October diesel subsidy on 30 September, after cutting it from 20 cents to 10.
