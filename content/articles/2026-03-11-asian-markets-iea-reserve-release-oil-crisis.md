The International Energy Agency proposed a record strategic oil reserve release Wednesday. The proposed drawdown would exceed the 182 million barrels released after Russia's 2022 invasion of Ukraine — the IEA's largest response since it was created to manage the 1973 Arab oil embargo. Tokyo and Seoul both rose over 1%; no IEA decision has been confirmed and the Strait of Hormuz remains closed.
