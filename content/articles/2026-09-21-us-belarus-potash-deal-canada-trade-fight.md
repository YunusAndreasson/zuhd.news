Washington — Trump is negotiating a potash purchase from [Belarus](country:BY).

[Canada](country:CA) supplies over 39% of world potash exports, and more than half go to the US.

Washington lifted sanctions on Belarusian potash exports in December 2025, and over 100 political prisoners were released afterward.

Reports say Washington asked Lithuania, Poland and Ukraine to let Belarusian fertiliser cross their territory.
