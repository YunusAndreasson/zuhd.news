London — The same obesity gene score produces different body weight by income.

A review of 98 studies found lower education and area deprivation strengthen genetic obesity risk; DNA methylation marks tracking disadvantage are a plausible but unproven link.

Researchers say reverse causation and confounding by diet and stress remain unresolved.
