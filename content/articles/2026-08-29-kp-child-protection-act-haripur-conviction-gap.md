Haripur — A 5-year-old girl was raped and murdered here.

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa named its child protection law after her, following [Pakistan](country:PK)'s 2018 law named for Zainab.

Her body was found August 24 in a well, 14 days after 4 suspects were named in the case.

The chief minister has promised "exemplary punishment" once suspects reach trial.
