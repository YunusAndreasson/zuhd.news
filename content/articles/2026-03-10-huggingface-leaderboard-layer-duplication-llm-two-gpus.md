A researcher beat HuggingFace's leaderboard by copying model layers. Duplicating 7 middle layers of a 72-billion-parameter model with no new training raised benchmark scores 2.6% and math performance 8.2%. By early 2026, the top 4 leaderboard positions were all descendants of his 78-billion-parameter approach.
