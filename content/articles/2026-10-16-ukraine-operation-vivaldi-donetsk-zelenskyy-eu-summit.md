Brussels — Zelenskyy asked EU leaders for nearly $80 billion more in 2027.

The [US](country:US) eased Russian oil sanctions this week and Starshield capped Ukraine's interceptor navigation.

Starshield limited navigation to 160 km/h, so interceptors lost sight of Russian Geran-4 drones flying above 500 km/h.

Elon Musk and the White House both deny responsibility for the slowdown.

European Council President Antonio Costa asked the Commission to propose 2027 funding, with no figure or mechanism yet.
