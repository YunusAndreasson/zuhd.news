Geneva — Every record-hot year has come since 2015.

The World Meteorological Organization gives 75% odds the 2026-2030 mean breaks 1.5°C, with El Niño arriving late 2026.

Arctic winters are forecast 2.8°C above normal, triple the global anomaly, the agency said.
