Jerusalem — [Qatar](country:QA) called [Colombia](country:CO)'s embassy opening illegal, Iran's state-run Mehr News Agency reported.

The ministry said it rejects any step altering Jerusalem's legal and historical status.

Mehr's report carries no statement from Colombia or Israel, and the opening itself rests on its account.

Qatar's ministry urged the international community to act against what it called attempts to impose a new reality.
