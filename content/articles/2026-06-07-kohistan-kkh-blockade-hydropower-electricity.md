Mansehra — Kohistan generates power for [Pakistan](country:PK); residents have none.

Ulema in Lower Kohistan have blocked Pakistan's Karakoram Highway and cut power transmission, demanding local electricity from the Dubair-Khawar plant.

The daily 6-hour blockade continues; grid upgradation is nearly complete but no supply has been promised.
