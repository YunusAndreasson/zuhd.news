Kebbi — Lakurawa killed 20 in Fasken Rafi Sunday.

Lakurawa, a newer armed group distinct from Zamfara's bandits, exploits the [Nigeria](country:NG)-Niger border and raids villages beyond state security reach.

Nigeria's army has set no suppression timeline; Kebbi's emir had already called for legal self-arming before the raid.
