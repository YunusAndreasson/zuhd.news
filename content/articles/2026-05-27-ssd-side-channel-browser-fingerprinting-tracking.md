San Francisco — Solid-state drive timing exposes visitors across all browsers.

JavaScript measures drive timing to infer open tabs regardless of browser; virtual private networks and ad-blockers cannot stop it.

Researchers propose browsers cap the file-size allocation the attack exploits; a security conference paper presents in July.
