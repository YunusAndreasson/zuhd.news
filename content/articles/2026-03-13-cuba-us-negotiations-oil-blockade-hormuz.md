Cuba has received no fuel deliveries in 3 months. Venezuela once supplied 35,000 barrels a day — half Cuba's oil needs — until the US captured President Nicolás Maduro in January, ending the arrangement. Cuba's President Miguel Díaz-Canel said talks with Washington are in initial stages, with 51 prisoners to be released as goodwill.
