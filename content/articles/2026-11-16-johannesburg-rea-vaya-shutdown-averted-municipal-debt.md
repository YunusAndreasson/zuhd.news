Johannesburg — The city scrapped a Rea Vaya shutdown hours after announcing it.

The network carries about 15,000 to 20,000 commuters daily.

The city pays private operators because fares cover only part of costs, and late payments leave them unable to buy fuel.

The city did not disclose the amount paid, the operator or whether all arrears were settled.

Debts of R25.2 billion against R3.9 billion cash in April hang over a coalition still being negotiated.
