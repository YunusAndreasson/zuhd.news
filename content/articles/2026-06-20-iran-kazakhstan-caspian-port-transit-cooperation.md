[Iran](country:IR) is sanctioned; [Kazakhstan](country:KZ) is landlocked — the Caspian is their shared exit from Western-controlled shipping lanes.

No volumes or timelines were agreed in Aktau; both sides plan further talks after Sunday's Switzerland negotiations.
