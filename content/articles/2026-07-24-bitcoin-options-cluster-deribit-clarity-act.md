Amsterdam — $5 billion has clustered at two bitcoin strikes.

Traders bought $70,000 and $72,000 calls at nearly 10-to-1 over puts, Laevitas data show, betting on the [US](country:US) CLARITY Act.

Traders have recently scaled back those bets as the act's passage remains undecided.
