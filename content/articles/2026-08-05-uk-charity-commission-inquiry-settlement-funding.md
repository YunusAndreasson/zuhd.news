London — Regulators opened inquiries into 8 charities.

The Charity Commission is probing whether Gift Aid — a 25% taxpayer top-up — funded flows to settlements the [UK](country:GB) calls illegal.

Statutory inquiries can freeze accounts and disqualify trustees; the Commission has not named the charities.
