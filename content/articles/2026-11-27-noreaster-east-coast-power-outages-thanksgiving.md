New York — A storm blacked out 900,000 [US](country:US) customers on Thanksgiving.

The Macy's parade was postponed for the first time in its 100-year history.

Winds up to 112 km/h toppled trees and power lines while king tides flooded low-lying coast.

Outages had fallen to 400,000 by Friday morning, poweroutage.us said.

The parade now runs Saturday.
