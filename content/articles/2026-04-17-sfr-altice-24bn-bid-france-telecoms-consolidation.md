Paris — France's 3 carriers bid €20 billion for SFR. If approved, the deal ends France's 4-carrier price war; Altice granted exclusivity until May 15 to finalize terms. Each buyer faces a separate antitrust review, which Morgan Stanley expects to last 12-24 months.
