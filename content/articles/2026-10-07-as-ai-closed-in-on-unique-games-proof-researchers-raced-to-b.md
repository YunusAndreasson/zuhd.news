Cambridge — Researchers rushed a 95-page paper out ahead of an OpenAI proof.

A proof of the unique games conjecture would show current methods for many unrelated problems cannot be improved.

Dor Minzer of the Massachusetts Institute of Technology and 2 students posted a related milestone proof on 14 September.

"This is another truly great one," said Ryan O'Donnell, a Carnegie Mellon University computer scientist.

OpenAI announced on 6 October a proof of the conjecture and 376 other results.
