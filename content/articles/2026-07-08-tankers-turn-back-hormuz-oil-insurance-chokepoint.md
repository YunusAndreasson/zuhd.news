Muscat — 4 tankers turned back from Hormuz on Wednesday.

The strait carries a fifth of global oil; insurers flagged transit severe after strikes hit a Qatari and a Saudi tanker nearby.

[Iran](country:IR) says it will charge passage fees and strike ships that deviate from its route.
