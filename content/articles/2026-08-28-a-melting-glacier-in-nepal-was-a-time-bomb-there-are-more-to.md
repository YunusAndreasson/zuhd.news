Kathmandu — Imja glacier's lake now spans 1.7 miles.

Valley communities live below dams made only of loose rock.

Retreating glaciers leave meltwater trapped behind loose moraine rubble, and dark silt in the lakes speeds the ice's collapse.

Hundreds of glacial lakes across [Nepal](country:NP)'s Himalaya could fail without warning systems.
