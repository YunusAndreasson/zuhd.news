Doha — A Qatari military helicopter crashed in the Persian Gulf, killing all 6 aboard. Qatar hosts Al Udeid Air Base, the US military's largest regional installation, in a Gulf under Iranian blockade for 4 weeks. Whether the crash resulted from hostile fire or mechanical failure has not been confirmed — an answer that could determine whether it triggers wider escalation.
