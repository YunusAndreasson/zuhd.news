$137m will connect 5.2 million West Africans to broadband. The World Bank is funding the program in 3 nations that rank among the world's least-connected, where power grids remain too unreliable to sustain consistent internet access. AI is already eliminating entry-level digital roles elsewhere, narrowing the window for programs like this to succeed.
