United States — NSA Net went offline early Tuesday, six sources say.

The agency has staggered staff arrival times while it investigates the disruption.

NSA Net carries internal communications, and a US official says backup networks still deliver urgent intelligence to Trump in Asia.

Those sources saw no sign of a cyberattack on NSA Net.

No one has said when NSA Net returns.
