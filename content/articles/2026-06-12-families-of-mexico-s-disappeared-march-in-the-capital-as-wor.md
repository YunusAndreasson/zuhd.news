Mexico City — 135,000 missing Mexicans forced onto the World Cup stage.

Families called madres buscadoras marched to the World Cup opener in [Mexico](country:MX) City, demanding state accountability for the mass disappearance crisis.

Protesters graffitied "desaparecidos" on monuments; the government says locating the missing is a national priority.
