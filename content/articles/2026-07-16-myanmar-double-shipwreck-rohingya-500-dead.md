Rakhine — 530 Rohingya are feared dead in two sinkings.

Boats carrying 250 and 280 people left [Myanmar](country:MY)'s Rakhine coast in June, some passengers fleeing Bangladesh's Cox's Bazar camps, the UN refugee agency and migration agency said.

The toll would push 2026 Andaman Sea deaths past 800; officials have not confirmed casualty figures.
