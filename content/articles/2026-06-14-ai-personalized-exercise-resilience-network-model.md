London — AI-personalized exercise beat standard training in 60-athlete trial.

Personalized athletes outperformed standardized training 21.4% to 12.0% on athletic performance and 18.6% to 8.3% on resilience, the study found.

The 12-week trial used 60 competitive athletes; the model has not been tested outside that training population.
