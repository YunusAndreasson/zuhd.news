Dhaka — Most fertilizer factories in Bangladesh have already shut down. Bangladesh is seeking $2 billion from multilateral lenders as the Hormuz blockade cuts the LNG supplies its power grid runs on. Lenders must be secured and fuel imports restored before Bangladesh's shuttered fertilizer factories can restart production.
