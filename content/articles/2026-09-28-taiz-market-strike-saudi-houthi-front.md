Taiz — A strike on Taiz's Mawiya market killed 7, wounded 40.

Civilians in [Yemen](country:YE)'s third-largest city are caught in a war reignited after a four-year ceasefire collapsed.

Houthi forces seized the Red Sea port of Mokha this month and now press Taiz's western approaches, drawing renewed [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) air strikes.

Yemen's government says its forces targeted a Houthi military camp at the same junction, not shops.

The Houthi spokesman has vowed "severe consequences" for what he calls a Saudi attack on civilians.
