Nairobi — [Sudan](country:SD)'s anti-war bloc warns talks now would legitimize its warring authorities.

The bloc says a process anchored to one warring party risks entrenching the country's division permanently.

It wants a ceasefire before talks, arguing dialogue-first lets the army chief spend the war as leverage.

UN envoy Pekka Haavisto's mandate carries no power to enforce either sequence.
