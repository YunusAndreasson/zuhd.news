Santa Fe — A jury found Meta deceived consumers 44 million times.

Each violation carries up to $5,000 in penalties, exposing Meta to billions in damages.

Jurors found Facebook lied about controlling user data sharing, not profiting from misinformation, and enforcing its community standards evenly.

A judge will set final damages in the coming weeks, and Meta didn't respond to a request for comment.
