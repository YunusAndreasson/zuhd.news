Amsterdam — October futures hit $1,004 per 1,000 cubic meters.

It's Europe's highest gas price since the first winter without Russian pipeline supply.

Europe now buys seaborne LNG on the same global market as Asian and South Asian buyers, competing cargo by cargo.

Spot prices already topped $1,000 last week, ICE data show, with winter still ahead.
