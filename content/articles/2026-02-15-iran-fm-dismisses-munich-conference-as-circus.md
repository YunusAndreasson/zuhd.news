Iranian officials were barred from attending. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi, posting on X, called the Munich Security Conference a "circus" and declared that Europe has become "completely absent from the scene" in nuclear negotiations. He singled out Germany for having "fully handed over its regional policies to Israel" and dismissed the European Union and the E3 — Britain, France, and Germany — as paralyzed bystanders. The remarks contrast with Iran's quiet diplomacy through Oman, where backchannel nuclear talks continue ahead of a Geneva round next week. Over 60 heads of state attended the conference, but Tehran's public contempt for the forum suggests it sees little purpose in engaging European powers directly.
