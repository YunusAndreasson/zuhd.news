India — Accused ₹6,000-crore betting-app mastermind Sourabh Chandrakar returned from Oman on Sunday.

The Enforcement Directorate estimates ₹94,000 crore in related proceeds, with ₹4,000-5,000 crore reaching the promoters.

The directorate says promoters ran the app from Dubai and outsourced risk to 3,200 franchise-style panels, each depositing ₹15 lakh.

The directorate alleges ₹508 crore in bribes to ex-chief minister Bhupesh Baghel, who denies all charges.

Agencies now press the [UAE](country:AE) to extradite co-founder Ravi Uppal from Dubai.
