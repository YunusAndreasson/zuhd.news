Bannu — An armed police-allied committee blocked highway routes.

The "peace committee" is a locally recruited body raised to assist police in [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province.

Talks collapsed Tuesday; protesters demand the district police chief's transfer and reversal of other officers' transfers.
