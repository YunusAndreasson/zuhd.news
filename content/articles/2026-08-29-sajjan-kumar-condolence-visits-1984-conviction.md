Delhi — Three Bharatiya Janata Party MPs mourned a mass-killing convict.

The party reprimanded them just days after a Congress leader faced the same backlash.

Sajjan Kumar was convicted for the 1984 anti-Sikh riots that killed thousands, decades before his death.

Party chief Nitin Nabin summoned the three MPs and called their visit improper.
