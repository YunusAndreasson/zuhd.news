Muzaffarabad — Azad Kashmir residents cannot vote in [Pakistan](country:PK)'s federal elections.

Islamabad administers the territory by constitutional arrangement but excludes it from parliamentary representation.

Opposition groups demand representation; Islamabad has not proposed constitutional changes.
