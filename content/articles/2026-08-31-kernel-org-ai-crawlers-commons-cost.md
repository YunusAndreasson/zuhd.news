Toronto — 14 CPU cores render commit pages nonstop.

The infrastructure behind nearly every server's operating system pays this bill.

AI crawlers ignore robots.txt, rotating IPs to reharvest 1.48 million commits across 922 duplicate forks.

Maintainers can block scraper IPs, but have no way to bill anyone for the load.
