Manila — Ships sailed days after water-cannon clashes at two shoals.

The [Philippines](country:PH), the US and Japan ran the drill after Manila accused [China](country:CN) of ramming its vessels — force kept below their mutual defense treaty's threshold.

Beijing and Manila continue disputing what happened; Filipino fishermen who work those waters bear the pushback.
