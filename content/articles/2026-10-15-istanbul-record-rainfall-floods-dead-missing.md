Istanbul — Authorities pulled 3,000 people out of floodwater, the governor said.

3 people are dead and 5 missing after the heaviest rainfall on record in [Turkey](country:TR)'s largest city.

Beykoz recorded nearly 194mm, and Acting Mayor Nuri Aslan said parts of the city got nearly 100mm in 12 hours.

Governor Davut Gul told residents to stay home, and the motorcycle ban stays until further notice.
