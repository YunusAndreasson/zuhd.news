Washington — Estimates put Trump's $5,000 checks above $1 trillion.

Every adult [US](country:US) citizen would get one, but only if Republicans keep both chambers.

Congress controls spending, so the plan needs legislation, and Trump has not said how to pay for it.

"This is just a payoff, and he's trying to buy votes," said Senator Mark Kelly, an Arizona Democrat.

Voters decide in November's midterms whether Republicans keep the House and Senate.
