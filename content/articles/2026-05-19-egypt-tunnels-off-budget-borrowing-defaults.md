Cairo — Egypt routed 60% of off-budget loans to the tunnels authority in FY2027.

The authority left the Finance Ministry covering LE38.7 billion in defaults; [Egypt](country:EG)'s new allocation sits outside the IMF-audited budget.

Half the new lending is in foreign currency — a sovereign FX obligation the IMF's audit does not capture.
