Tehran — Every Iranian airline goes dark abroad from Wednesday.

Ordinary travelers, pilgrims and medical patients pay for a policy aimed at Tehran's government.

[US](country:US) Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said any firm that fuels, services or sells tickets for Iranian carriers risks losing dollar-system access.

[China](country:CN) called the sanctions "illegal" and, with Russia, is expected to keep servicing Iranian flights.

[Iraq](country:IQ) will likely seek an exemption, analyst Saj Ahmad said, given deep tourism ties with Tehran.
