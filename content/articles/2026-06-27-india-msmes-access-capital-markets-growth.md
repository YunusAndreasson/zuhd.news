New Delhi — 328 million [Indian](country:IN) small-business workers lack capital.

The sector generates 31% of GDP and 48% of exports, but working-capital gaps prevent firms from fulfilling orders when demand arrives.

Analysts say digital commerce platforms and supply-chain financing, not just credit, will determine which small firms scale.
