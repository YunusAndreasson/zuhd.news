Brussels — AI-assisted hackers kept European Commission staff systems offline over 10 days.

The tool, ARTEX, is public, and the US Federal Bureau of Investigation counts 1,600 exposed consoles.

Federal Bureau of Investigation cases show attackers reaching privileged access in under 3 hours, because agents plan and exploit with little human input.

Spokesperson Thomas Regnier said individual systems were targeted and those concerned have been informed.

The Commission has not said whether attackers read staff payslips or medical claims.
