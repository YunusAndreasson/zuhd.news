Mumbai — [India](country:IN)'s retail inflation hit 6.09% in October, a two-year high.

It breaches the central bank's 6% tolerance limit, and economists now expect a rate hike.

Statistics ministry data show food inflation jumped to 8.94% from 7.74% in September.

The Reserve Bank of India's policy meeting on 4 December decides whether to hike.
