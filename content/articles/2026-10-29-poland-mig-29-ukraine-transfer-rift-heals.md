Warsaw — [Poland](country:PL) will hand [Ukraine](country:UA) two MiG-29s, officials say.

Ukrainian F-16 pilots short of missiles reportedly ration shots and increasingly rely on their 20mm cannon.

Warsaw is also reported to be sending 20mm cannon rounds, artillery shells and Piorun anti-aircraft missiles.

One of the two jets has reached the end of its service life and may serve for parts.

Deputy Defence Minister Paweł Zalewski says talks on Poland's remaining MiG-29s are ongoing, and Kyiv has not responded.
