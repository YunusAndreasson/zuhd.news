A midnight strike killed 26 at a displacement shelter. Sudanese army drones hit a veterinary building in West Kordofan housing displaced families — 9 women and 12 children died. The Rapid Support Forces launched drone strikes on North Kordofan the same day, marking an escalation as both sides now bomb from above.
