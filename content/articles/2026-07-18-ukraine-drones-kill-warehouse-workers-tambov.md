Kotovsk — Seven Wildberries warehouse workers died overnight.

[Ukrainian](country:UA) drones hit the e-commerce depot and a second Wildberries site in Elektrostal, injuring dozens, the regional governor said.

A separate strike sparked a fire at a Moscow-region oil depot, evacuating a maternity hospital nearby.
