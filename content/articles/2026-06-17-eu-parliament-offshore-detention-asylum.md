Brussels — Europe voted to detain asylum seekers offshore.

The European Parliament passed a Return Regulation letting member states build detention centers outside the bloc and accelerate deportations.

The 27 governments must still ratify; the Commission has invited Taliban officials to Brussels for Afghan deportation talks.
