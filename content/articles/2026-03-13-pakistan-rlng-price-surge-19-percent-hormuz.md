Pakistan's gas prices jumped 22% in a single month. Iran's Hormuz blockade has cut liquefied natural gas imports, compounding a domestic network that already loses 12.55% of supply to leaks. Pakistan imposed speed limits to conserve fuel, with IMF bailout talks providing no near-term relief.
