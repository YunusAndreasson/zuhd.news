Israeli strikes killed 10 and wounded 50 in Lebanon. Israel killed a senior official of Hezbollah — the Lebanese militant group designated a terrorist organization by the US and EU — in strikes on command centers in the Bekaa Valley. The strikes test a US-brokered ceasefire already strained by recurring violations.
