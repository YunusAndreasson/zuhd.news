The report calls a two-front war with [China](country:CN) and [Pakistan](country:PK) a growing concern for [India](country:IN).

Beijing backed Pakistan during the May 2025 conflict without entering the fighting, the US-China Economic and Security Review Commission found.

Retired Brigadier Hemant Mahajan alleges Chinese satellite and repair support to Pakistan, but says Indian-made systems outperformed Chinese weapons.

Congress leader Shama Mohamed wants Washington to keep Pakistan at a distance after Trump hosted Asim Munir.
