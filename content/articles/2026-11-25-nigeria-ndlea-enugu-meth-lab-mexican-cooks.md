Enugu — [Nigeria](country:NG) seized 1,512 kg of methamphetamine and arrested 5 Mexicans.

Mexican specialists were smuggled in to produce the drug in Nigeria, not just move it.

After Nigeria turned them back on 28 August, they returned on forged passports via Cotonou and crossed the land border.

Agency chairman Buba Marwa put the haul at ₦303.9 billion.

The 63-year-old suspect, a businessman with an Anambra chieftaincy title, was arrested in Enugu hotel Room 106.
