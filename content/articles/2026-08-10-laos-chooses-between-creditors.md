Vientiane — [Laos](country:LA) is turning to [Vietnam](country:VN), not [China](country:CN).

A landlocked economy squeezed by Chinese debt is choosing a cheaper patron.

Landlocked nations depend on a neighbor's ports for exports, so infrastructure loans buy transit routes as much as repayment.

Whether Vietnam can match China's loan volumes long-term is unclear.
