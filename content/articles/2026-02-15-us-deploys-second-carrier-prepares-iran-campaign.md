2 carrier strike groups now face Iran. The Pentagon ordered the USS Gerald R. Ford from the Caribbean to the Arabian Sea to join the USS Abraham Lincoln already stationed there. President Donald Trump framed the deployment as leverage: "In case we don't make a deal, we'll need it." The dual buildup comes days before a 2nd round of indirect nuclear talks in Geneva. Whether Iran reads the deployment as negotiating pressure or preparation for strikes will shape the talks.
