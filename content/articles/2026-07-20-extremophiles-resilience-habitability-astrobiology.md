Yellowstone — Some microbes thrive in boiling, acidic environments.

Heat-stable proteins and DNA-repair enzymes let extremophiles survive conditions that destroy ordinary cells.

Their heat-stable enzymes are why PCR testing works, and why astrobiologists study them for signs of life elsewhere.
