Geoscience Australia drilled 3,023 metres into Queensland's Adavale Basin. The campaign reached a 500-metre-thick salt deposit, eastern Australia's only formation suitable for storing compressed air or hydrogen at grid scale. Results due in 2026 will determine whether the 400-million-year-old caverns can back renewable energy.
