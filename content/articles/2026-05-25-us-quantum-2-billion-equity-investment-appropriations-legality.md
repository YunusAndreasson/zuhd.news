Washington — $2 billion in [US](country:US) quantum equity may be illegal.

The money comes from a semiconductor research fund Congress approved; Rep. Lofgren says using it as equity in quantum startups violates spending law.

A lawsuit is the likely challenge, but the money would be spent before any court rules.
