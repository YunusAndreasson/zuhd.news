China — Apple's iPhone AI in [China](country:CN) is Alibaba's Qwen.

Hugging Face counted 2 billion Qwen downloads in 2026's first seven months, ahead of Google and Meta.

Beta testing began 4 November, with Qwen in selected Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence features.

Qwen's post-training chief Bowen Yu and researcher Binyuan Hui have also left this year.

Alibaba has not said why technical leader Junyang Lin quit in March.
