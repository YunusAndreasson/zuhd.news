United States — Binance Pool found 1 of 925 bitcoin blocks, mempool.space shows.

Its computing power fell 65% since December, to about 9.2 exahashes per second in October.

Miners can redirect machines to another pool without shutting down, potentially adding to Foundry USA, AntPool and F2Pool's share.

Block discovery is random, so one block in a week cannot fix the pool's remaining hashrate.

Binance disconnects miners at 07:00 UTC on 10 November, ending a pool it launched in April 2020.
