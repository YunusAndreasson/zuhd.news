Brisbane — A six-year-old died of a 19th-century disease.

Doctors and child-safety services had flagged his diet for years before he died.

Scurvy is now so rare in [Australia](country:AU) that his deficiency was found only in post-mortem scans.

The multivitamin that lacked vitamin C remains on sale in Australia.
