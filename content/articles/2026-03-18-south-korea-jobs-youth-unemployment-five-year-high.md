South Korea's youth unemployment hit 7.7%, a 5-year high. The economy added 234,000 jobs overall, but all growth came from health care and transport while manufacturing shed positions for the 20th consecutive month. Those aged 15-29 lost 146,000 jobs in February, with no government stimulus targeting youth employment announced.
