Phnom Penh — A new law can make citizens stateless.

[Cambodia](country:KH)'s amended constitution lets officials revoke citizenship for treason or "undermining national security," terms rights groups say target dissidents and activists.

Fifty civil society groups warn the vague wording risks leaving critics without passports, rights, or a country.
