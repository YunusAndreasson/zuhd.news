---
title: "Rising CO2 Starves Boreal Forests"
date: "2026-02-18T00:00:00Z"
source: "Nature"
sourceUrl: "https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-10039-5"
category: "science"
---

Nitrogen in Swedish forests declined from 1961 to 2018. A Nature study of tree-ring isotopes found rising atmospheric CO2 drives growth that outstrips soil nitrogen, locking nutrients in plant litter. Climate models that count on these forests as a growing carbon sink may overestimate their capacity.
