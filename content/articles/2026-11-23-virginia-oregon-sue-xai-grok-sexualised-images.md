Richmond — Grok posted an estimated 3 million sexualised images in 11 days.

About 23,000 appeared to depict children, the Center for Countering Digital Hate estimated.

Virginia and Oregon sued SpaceX, xAI's parent, for designing and promoting Grok without safeguards, citing consumer-protection law.

The suits are allegations, not findings of wrongdoing by a court.

The states seek penalties, damages and court orders, while more than a dozen individuals have also sued.
