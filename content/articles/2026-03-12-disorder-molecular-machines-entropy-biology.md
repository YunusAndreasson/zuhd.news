Nuclear pores run on molecular disorder, not precision. The complexes screen every molecule entering or leaving a cell's nucleus, letting hundreds pass per second via thermal chaos rather than rigid structure. Researchers at Rockefeller University and the University of Basel are building a high-definition model of the pore in motion, a project whose completion may redefine synthetic nanomachine design.
