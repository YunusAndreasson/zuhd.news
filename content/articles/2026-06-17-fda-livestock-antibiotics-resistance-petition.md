Washington — Advocates petitioned the Food and Drug Administration to end livestock antibiotic approvals.

34 million pounds of [US](country:US) livestock antibiotics breed resistant bacteria blamed for 35,000 deaths a year, the petition says.

Earthjustice argues the FDA is legally obligated to withdraw the approvals; the agency has not responded to the petition.
