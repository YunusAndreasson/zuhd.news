Seoul — South Korea's opposition demanded self-reliance with US forces overcommitted. Opposition leader Lee Jae-myung warned that 85% of South Korea's energy passes through the Strait of Hormuz, which Trump's 48-hour ultimatum to Iran has put at risk. The ultimatum expires Monday — if Iran refuses to comply, a strait closure cuts South Korea off from its primary energy supply.
