Washington — The Pentagon inspector general declined a nine-senator Starlink probe.

Questions about who throttled [Ukraine](country:UA)'s drone links now go to the Air Force and Space Force.

Ukraine said drones lost connectivity above 161 km/h for about 10 days, until the limit was lifted.

Musk says the US military and ultimately the president set the limits, and Trump denies ordering them.

The inspector general says it may reconsider if new information emerges.
