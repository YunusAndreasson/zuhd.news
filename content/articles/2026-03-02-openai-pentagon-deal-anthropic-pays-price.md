AI firm Anthropic lost every US government contract. The Pentagon labeled Anthropic a national security risk after the company declined to let its models power weapons systems, then contracted OpenAI instead. OpenAI has not disclosed the safety "red lines" it claims govern its classified Pentagon contract.
