Bangladesh found no gas sellers for 2 straight days. The Strait of Hormuz closure cut Gulf gas exports and doubled spot prices, pricing Bangladesh's 170 million people out of the open market. Bangladesh secured 2 cargoes at double the normal price but has ordered fuel rationing, with the Hormuz closure unresolved.
