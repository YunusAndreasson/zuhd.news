Sydney — Firmus sought a valuation 600 times its revenue.

The scrapped float would have been [Australia](country:AU)'s largest since Telstra in 1997.

Firmus rents out chips it owns, and just 46 of the 912 megawatts it has contracted are running.

"It just doesn't have a compelling valuation," said John Pearce, chief investment officer of pension fund UniSuper.

Nvidia, Blackstone and Coatue receive extra Firmus shares if it has not listed by 30 November.
