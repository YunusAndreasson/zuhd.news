London — PET scans found tau protein in most late-onset psychosis patients.

Researchers scanned 37 patients; tau appeared even without amyloid, the marker usually defining Alzheimer's, suggesting some cases are neurological, not purely psychiatric.

Antipsychotics carry raised mortality risk in undiagnosed neurodegeneration, so misclassification becomes a prescribing hazard.
