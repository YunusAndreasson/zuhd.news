China — A [US](country:US) commission counted over 100,000 Alibaba Qwen derivative models by March.

A Chinese commentator argues the AI race now turns on cost and deployment, not benchmarks.

Open-weight models can be downloaded, fine-tuned and run privately, while closed models arrive through cloud APIs the vendor controls.

Nvidia, Microsoft, Meta and IBM publicly backed open-weight models in July, the commentary notes.

The US government and Anthropic accuse Chinese firms of industrial-scale distillation, which Beijing denies.
