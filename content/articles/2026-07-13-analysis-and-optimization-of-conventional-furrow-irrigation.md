Islamabad — Conventional furrows waste up to half their water.

[Pakistan](country:PK) draws 97% of freshwater for farming; the WinSRFR model raised efficiency to 84% by tuning flow rate and furrow length alone.

Researchers say adoption now depends on farmers replacing habit with hydraulic design.
