Johannesburg — Scientists forecast a "Godzilla" El Niño by July.

El Niño warms Pacific sea surfaces, cutting southern Africa's summer rains; [Zimbabwe](country:ZW) lost 60% of its maize harvest in 2023-24, [Zambia](country:ZM) half, official figures show.

The World Meteorological Organization said a 2026 El Niño makes 2027 the next likely record-breaking year.
