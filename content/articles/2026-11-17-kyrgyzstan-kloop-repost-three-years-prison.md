Kyrgyzstan — A court gave a social media user three years for one repost.

An extremism label on a newsroom now makes its audience criminally liable, not only its journalists.

Kloop, which a Kyrgyz court declared extremist, reports that reader Baktybek Moldaliev is the first convicted for sharing its video.

The conviction is the first test of prosecuting ordinary users for sharing Kloop material.
