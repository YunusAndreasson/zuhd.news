New Delhi — App payouts halved as crude hit $120 per barrel, unions said.

[India](country:IN)'s 68 transport unions froze Delhi-NCR roads through May 23; fares are locked to a 2011 scheme that predates the energy shock.

Unions warn the 3-day stop becomes indefinite without fare revision by Saturday.
