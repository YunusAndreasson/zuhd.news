Tegucigalpa — [Honduras](country:HN)'s police investigators received 219 reports of missing women and girls this year.

The national university's violence observatory counted 211 violent deaths of women from January to September.

In 2025 the security ministry received 447 such reports, one woman or girl missing every 20 hours, the Centre for Women's Rights found.

Women's groups marched on Congress on 25 November, demanding funded search mechanisms, femicide investigations and an end to impunity.
