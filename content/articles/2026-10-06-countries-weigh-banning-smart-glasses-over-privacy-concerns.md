Oslo — [Norway](country:NO) will propose a temporary ban on AI glasses in parks and beaches.

Smart glasses can photograph or record bystanders without their knowledge.

Oslo already bars them from schools, and New York's courts have required visitors to surrender them since 20 July.

The European Commission has not proposed legislation targeting smart glasses specifically.

[Australia](country:AU) has asked its Public Service Commission whether to ban them from government workplaces.
