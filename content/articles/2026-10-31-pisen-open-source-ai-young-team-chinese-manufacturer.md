China — Pisen's AI tool turns engineers' 3D models into patent drawings.

Patents hold unpublished research, so local deployment spares companies from uploading data.

It drives open-source design software to produce exploded views and part labels, which professionals still check.

Team lead Cong Zelong says it is unproven whether others will keep using the tools.

Pisen plans to open-source a candidate-screening tool and launch an AI Lab website collecting its tools.
