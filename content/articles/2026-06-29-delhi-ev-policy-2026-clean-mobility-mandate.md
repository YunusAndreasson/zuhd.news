New Delhi — Delhi bans petrol auto-rickshaws from January 2027.

[India](country:IN)'s capital committed Rs 7,000–15,000 crore to subsidies, scrapping incentives and charging infrastructure, targeting 95% electric registrations by 2027.

Electric two-wheelers face the same mandate from April 2028.
