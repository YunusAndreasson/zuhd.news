Gwadar — A fish barely bigger than a palm split a coastline.

Balochistan's fishermen, once united against trawlers, now divide over selling forage fish to feed factories.

Bigger species follow schools of the banned sardine, so removing it collapses the fishery from below.

Younis Anwar wants the ban enforced across the whole coast, not just Gwadar tehsil.
