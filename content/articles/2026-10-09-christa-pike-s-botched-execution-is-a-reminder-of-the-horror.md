Nashville — Christa Pike survived two doses of pentobarbital.

She is believed to be the only person to survive a lethal injection attempt.

Her lawyers say she reached hospital in critical condition with badly injured arms, and now walks with assistance.

Pike was 18 when she and her 17-year-old boyfriend killed 19-year-old Colleen Slemmer in 1995.

Governor Bill Lee paused executions for 2026, and Pike's lawyers ask him to commute her sentence.
