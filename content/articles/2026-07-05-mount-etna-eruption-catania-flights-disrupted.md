Catania — An ash column rose 1.5 kilometers above Etna's crater.

Italy's geophysics institute raised the aviation alert to red, the threshold at which drifting ash can damage jet engines and blind sensors.

Catania airport's airspace stays restricted until 7pm local time as the cloud drifts south over [Italy](country:IT).
