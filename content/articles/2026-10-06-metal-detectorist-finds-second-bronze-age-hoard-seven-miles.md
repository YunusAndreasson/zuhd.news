Peebles — Second Bronze Age hoard contains rare mould materials.

Moulds show where and how bronze was made, not just what people owned.

Mariusz Stepien found the Blyth Hoard seven miles from the Peebles Hoard, which includes a complete horse harness.

The Treasure Trove Unit assesses the find, and the Crown may claim it for a museum.
