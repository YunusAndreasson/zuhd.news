Seven major AI firms now run research labs in Zurich, a city of 400,000.

Apple, Google, Meta, Microsoft, and OpenAI are among the firms anchored by ETH Zurich's talent pipeline.

[Switzerland](country:CH) ranks first globally in AI researchers per capita; its neutrality is under pressure as AI becomes a geopolitical asset.
