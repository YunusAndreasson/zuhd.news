London — A wild yeast strain escapes its own metabolic brake.

Ornithine production is normally throttled by tight regulation within the pathway, so brewers previously needed genetic modification to boost the amino acid in beer.

Researchers say the strain lets brewers add the nutrient without triggering GM labeling rules.
