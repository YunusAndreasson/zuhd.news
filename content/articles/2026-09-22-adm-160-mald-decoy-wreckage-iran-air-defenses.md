It shows US and Israeli forces still probing [Iran](country:IR)'s air defenses despite quieter fighting.

The ADM-160 mimics a strike aircraft to trigger Iranian radars, mapping air-defense positions without risking a manned jet.

Iranian and Western analysts are still examining whether the wreckage ties to a separate drone downed the same day.
