[Vietnam](country:VN) is treating halal certification as trade infrastructure, competing with [Malaysia](country:MY) and Indonesia for Islamic export lanes and Muslim tourism.

The law requires establishing certification bodies; acceptance in Muslim-majority export markets remains to be negotiated.
