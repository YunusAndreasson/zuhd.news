Antalya — Recovering from overshoot needs 330 billion tonnes of CO2 removed.

The UN Environment Programme expects 1.8 °C of warming this century even in its best case.

Removal today is about 2 billion tonnes a year, mostly from reforestation, so technological removal must scale up too.

"COP31 will be judged a failure if we don't address the overshoot," said Ilana Seid, Palau's ambassador to the UN.

[Ethiopia](country:ET), which hosts next year's summit, co-chairs a group of 8 countries pushing net-negative targets with [Denmark](country:DK).
