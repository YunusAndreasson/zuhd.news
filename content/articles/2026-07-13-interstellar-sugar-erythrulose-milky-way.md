Yebes — Erythrulose was found near the galaxy's core.

Spain's Yebes and IRAM radio telescopes matched 12 spectral signals to erythrulose's lab-measured signature.

The findings, published in Nature Astronomy, suggest the same chemistry could seed life elsewhere.
