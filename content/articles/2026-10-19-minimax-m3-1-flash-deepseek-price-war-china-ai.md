China — MiniMax's M3.1 Flash charges ¥4 per million output tokens.

DeepSeek's V4.1 Flash, released 17 October, charges ¥8 at peak hours.

MiniMax's own benchmarks put it level with DeepSeek's on agent tasks, and it generates 200+ tokens a second.

On the DeepSWE coding test, DeepSeek's model still leads, 77.0 to 73.7.

MiniMax's Pro model is the next release developers are waiting for.
