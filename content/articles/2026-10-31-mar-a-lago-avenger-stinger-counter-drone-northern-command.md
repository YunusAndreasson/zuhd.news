Palm Beach — Avenger air defences sit on Mar-a-Lago's shoreline.

The Secret Service asked for them, citing a heightened threat environment.

Humvee-mounted short-range systems stand at Mar-a-Lago and at the airport 3 miles west, with counter-drone kits from Northern Command.

Officials declined to give a specific reason for the deployment.

Northern Command says it cannot give more details and refers questions to the Secret Service.
