New York — Replacement jails will hold 4,100 people against Rikers' 6,600 today.

The plan misses the city council's 2027 closure deadline by 5 years.

Mayor Zohran Mamdani says he will cut detention through alternatives to incarceration, online bail payments and a task force on court delays.

A New York Post columnist argues about 1,000 people charged with violent crimes would have to go free.

The first Brooklyn jail opens in 2029, when the population must fall to 5,500.
