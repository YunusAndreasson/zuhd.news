Jocotán — Rain stopped on 10 June, and the maize never ripened.

Without a harvest, families lose their ancestors' native seed, and health workers report anxiety and hopelessness.

El Niño's drought, worsened by climate change, hit the Dry Corridor of [Guatemala](country:GT) and [Honduras](country:HN), emptying Lenca and Ch'orti' cobs.

These families have no saved seed to plant next year.
