Oil topped $100, stocks fell — Bitcoin gained 4.2%. The US bombing of Kharg Island — Iran's main crude export facility — triggered only a 3.5% Bitcoin dip before it recovered to $71,000. The Federal Reserve meets March 17-18 to decide whether oil above $100 forces a rate shift.
