London — 73% of 11-to-17-year-olds encountered harmful content online, Ofcom research found.

Recommendation feeds are the primary exposure route; TikTok and YouTube rejected feed changes while Snap, Meta, and Roblox agreed to new protections.

Ofcom can fine up to 10% of global turnover; a [UK](country:GB) consultation on banning under-16s closes next week.
