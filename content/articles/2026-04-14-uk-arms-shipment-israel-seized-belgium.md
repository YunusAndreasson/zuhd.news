Liège — Belgium seized 2 UK arms shipments for Israel. Customs found fire control systems and aircraft parts in 2 consignments scheduled for Tel Aviv; no transit licence was sought, which Belgian law requires and would have refused. The Walloon government confirmed the law was violated; how many shipments passed undetected remains unanswered.
