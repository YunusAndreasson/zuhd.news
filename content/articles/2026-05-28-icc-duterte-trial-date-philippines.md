The Hague — ICC set Duterte's crimes-against-humanity trial for 30 November.

[The Philippines](country:PH) quit the Rome Statute in 2019; the ICC ruled pre-exit conduct remains triable — Duterte faces 3 counts, 76 killings minimum.

He is the first former Asian head of state to face ICC trial; Southeast Asian governments are watching.
