Lourdes — Pope Leo called clerical sex abuse the Church's own scourge.

He also pressed [France](country:FR)'s lawmakers on assisted dying days after they legalized it.

A 2021 inquiry found clergy had abused 330,000 minors in France over 7 decades, prompting his renewed call for vigilance.

"Medicine must never become the servant of programmed death!" said Pope Leo XIV.

Leo meets 7 French abuse survivors later Sunday, in a meeting some victims call too small to matter.
