Pakistan raised fuel prices 21% in 1 week. The country imports most of its oil through Gulf routes; US-Israeli strikes on Iran have disrupted shipping and pushed crude above $100 per barrel. The government was advised to raise prices further; it has not ruled out a 2nd hike.
