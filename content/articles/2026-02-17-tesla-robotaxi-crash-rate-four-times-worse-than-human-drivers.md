Tesla's Austin robotaxis crash at 4 times the human rate. 14 collisions in 8 months include one that Tesla quietly reclassified to add a hospitalization 5 months after it occurred — while redacting all crash narratives as confidential. The company is now offering unsupervised rides with no safety monitor, and no federal investigation has been opened.
