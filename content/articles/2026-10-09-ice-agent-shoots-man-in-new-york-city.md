New York — An agent fired 7 rounds, hitting the driver's neck, police say.

The Immigration and Customs Enforcement agent fired with a 5-year-old boy inside the car, unharmed.

The [US](country:US) Department of Homeland Security, which employs the agent, says it is investigating, with the Federal Bureau of Investigation at the scene.

The department says the man, a Dominican national, has a grand larceny conviction and alleged gang ties.

Governor Kathy Hochul wants body-camera footage released, and Mayor Zohran Mamdani says agents moved the wounded man out of hospital.
