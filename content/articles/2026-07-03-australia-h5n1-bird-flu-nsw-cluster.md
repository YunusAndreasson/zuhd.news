Sydney — A 6th suspected H5N1 case emerged in 3 weeks.

[Australia](country:AU) stayed the only H5N1-free continent until June 14; migratory wild birds have since breached that isolation in New South Wales.

Officials are targeting at-risk wildlife as they weigh containment before the strain reaches livestock.
