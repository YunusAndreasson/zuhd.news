100 roads in western Fiji remain cut. Cyclone Urmil's 2nd day of flooding has shut schools and overwhelmed drainage built for lighter 20th-century rainfall. The Fiji Red Cross warned of typhoid and leptospirosis spreading through floodwater as rain eases Tuesday.
