Minna — 37 detainees died in Corps custody.

The interior ministry published the names of 20 suspended officers, not of the 37 dead.

Survivors describe a packed, poorly ventilated cell, while the Corps blamed a suspected disease outbreak.

A 10-member committee will identify the dead, though the ministry announced no charges.
