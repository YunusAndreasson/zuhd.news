Bengaluru — Markets price 80% odds of a December Fed hike.

Gold is falling even as war keeps the Strait of Hormuz tense.

Gold, which pays no interest, touched its lowest since 5 August on Wednesday as Treasury yields and the dollar rose.

Fed minutes show policymakers divided on whether energy shocks or demand-driven inflation justify a hike.

The Fed decides on 28 October, and [US](country:US) inflation data lands 14 October.
