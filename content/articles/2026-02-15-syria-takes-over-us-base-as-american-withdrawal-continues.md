Syria's army took al-Shaddadi base. American troops had operated from the northeastern facility since 2016, when Kurdish-led forces seized it from ISIS. The handover follows Thursday's transfer of al-Tanf near the Iraq-Jordan border, reducing US troop levels in Syria from 1,500 to roughly 900 with no announced end state.
