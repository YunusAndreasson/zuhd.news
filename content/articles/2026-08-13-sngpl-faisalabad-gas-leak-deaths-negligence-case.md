Faisalabad — Three relatives died in a gas-leak house fire.

Police named the utility's regional GM personally, a rare step in [Pakistan](country:PK)'s infrastructure cases.

The family says it warned the contractor and gas officials of a leak before the pipeline exploded during a funeral gathering.

No arrests have been made, and whether the case reaches prosecution is unresolved.
