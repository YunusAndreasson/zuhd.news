2,000 US Marines left Japan for the Persian Gulf. The Okinawa-based 31st Marine Expeditionary Unit is the only US rapid reaction force in the Pacific, leaving Japan and Taiwan less defended. The assault ship Tripoli arrives in the Gulf in 1-2 weeks as Iran continues attacking the Strait of Hormuz.
