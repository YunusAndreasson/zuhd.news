London — A new pressure-heat process makes gains permanent.

That could turn a lab curiosity into a manufacturable waste-heat material.

High pressure and heat, then quenching, locks a distorted electronic structure in place at room conditions.

Whether this quenching treatment can scale beyond small laboratory samples remains untested.
