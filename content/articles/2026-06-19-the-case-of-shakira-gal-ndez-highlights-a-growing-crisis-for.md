New York — Venezuelan trans asylum seeker Shakira Galíndez was detained by [US](country:US) agents at her immigration hearing.

Held in a men's facility since September 2025, she faces deportation to [Venezuela](country:VE), where she fled persecution as a trans woman.

Immigration and Customs Enforcement standards require individual housing decisions for trans detainees; her deportation date has not been set.
