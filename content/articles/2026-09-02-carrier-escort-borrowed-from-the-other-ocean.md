Singapore — USS Abraham Lincoln crossed the Malacca Strait after 285 days deployed.

A cruiser normally screening the USS George Washington group escorted Lincoln instead, exposing a fleet-wide shortage.

Two other US carrier groups remain in the Middle East enforcing a blockade as [Iran](country:IR) and the US trade fire.

The Navy has not explained the cruiser reassignment, confirmed only by tracking data.
