Milwaukee — Salah Sarsour, a [US](country:US) resident for 30 years, can be deported.

A secretary of state's memo alone can make a non-citizen removable on foreign policy grounds.

Judge Jayme Salinardi ruled he could not look behind Marco Rubio's memo, Sarsour's lawyer Patrick Taurel said.

Sarsour says deportation to the occupied West Bank "means the end of my life."

His lawyers have 60 days to argue he faces torture if returned, then plan to appeal.
