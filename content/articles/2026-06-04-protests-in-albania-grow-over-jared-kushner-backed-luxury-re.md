Tirana — Bulldozers entered a protected Adriatic marine park.

An Albanian conservation group said Jared Kushner's $1.6bn resort covers [Albania](country:AL) wetlands and monk-seal habitat with no published permits.

Albania's anti-corruption prosecutor opened an inquiry into 2024 laws that cleared the site, but Prime Minister Edi Rama says it will not stop.
