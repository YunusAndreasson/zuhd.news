Washington — 4 weeks into the Iran bombing, Congress hasn't voted. House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries called the strikes a "reckless war," invoking the War Powers Act, which requires congressional authorization within 60 days. Democrats are demanding a floor vote before the deadline — but the Republican majority controls the schedule.
