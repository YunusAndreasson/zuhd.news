Malmö — A medieval herring fishery took 400 years to recover.

It's a stark data point for scientists gauging how fast overfished stocks might bounce back.

The fishery in the strait between [Denmark](country:DK) and [Sweden](country:SE) fed Europe's trade until it collapsed suddenly in 1570.

Fisheries scientists watching today's depleted stocks now have a four-century worst case to weigh.
