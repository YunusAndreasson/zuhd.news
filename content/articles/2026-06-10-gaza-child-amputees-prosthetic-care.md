Gaza — [Israel](country:IL)'s siege severs prosthetic follow-up care for child amputees.

A growing child outgrows a prosthetic limb yearly; a single fitting starts a decade-long supply chain that the blockade has now cut.

Prosthetics and physiotherapy remain inaccessible; without sustained care, a survivable injury becomes permanent disability.
