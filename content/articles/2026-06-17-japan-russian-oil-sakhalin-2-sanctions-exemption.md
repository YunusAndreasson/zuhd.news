Tokyo — Japan bought 364,000 barrels of Russian oil in May.

[Japan](country:JP) used the Sakhalin-2 carve-out — exempt from Group of Seven price caps — after Middle East supply disruptions blocked its usual sources.

Finance Ministry data put the May price at $103 per barrel; Tokyo has not listed [Russia](country:RU) among planned alternative suppliers.
