Kramatorsk — A guided bomb killed at least 30 bus passengers Thursday.

Governor Vadym Filashkin suspended public transport in Kramatorsk and Sloviansk, the core of Ukraine's fortress belt.

[Russia](country:RU) dropped a record 8,700 guided bombs in August, Ukraine's military says.

"The Russians are deliberately targeting civilians," said Vadym Filashkin, head of the Donetsk regional military administration.

Moscow demands Ukraine surrender the rest of Donetsk, and Kyiv has rejected the demand.
