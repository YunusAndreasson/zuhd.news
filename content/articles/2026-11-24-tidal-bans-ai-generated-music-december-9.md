United States — Tidal will delete 27 million AI-generated tracks on 9 December.

Spotify, YouTube Music, Deezer and Apple Music still allow AI music with a label.

Tidal reports 43% of new uploads over 90 days were fully AI-generated, peaking at 60% on 6 November.

Tidal says 96% of the AI-labeled tracks had zero plays in 90 days, so deletion costs little.

Tidal has not said how it will detect AI tracks, and open models run locally need no watermark.
