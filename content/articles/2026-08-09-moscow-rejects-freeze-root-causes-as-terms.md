Moscow — Deputy Foreign Minister Galuzin rules out a freeze.

The rejection stalls [US](country:US)-backed efforts to negotiate a ceasefire with [Ukraine](country:UA) this year.

Galuzin accused [Ukraine](country:UA) of drone strikes on the Caspian Pipeline Consortium, which serves Kazakhstan's economy.

Galuzin said Moscow wants Washington and Europe to rein in Kyiv's strikes.
