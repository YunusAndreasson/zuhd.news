Kuopio — Loss of smell can predate Parkinson's by years.

The olfactory bulb is one of few brain routes bypassing the blood-brain barrier, and researchers tested six gases against it.

The unedited findings await peer review, with no comparable exposure data yet for South Asia or the Gulf.
