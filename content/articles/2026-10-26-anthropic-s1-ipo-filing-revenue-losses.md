United States — Anthropic lost $80.7 billion on $16.3 billion of revenue.

Investors get their first look at the books of a frontier AI lab.

Most of the six-month loss is a rise in the fair value of convertible bonds, while compute stays the largest operating expense.

The Nasdaq listing under ANT is targeted for November, but the filing sets no price range.
