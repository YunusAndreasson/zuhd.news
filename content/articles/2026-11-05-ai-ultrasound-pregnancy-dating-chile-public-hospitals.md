Santiago — An AI model dated pregnancies within 6.9 days on average.

Conventional due dates missed by 11.6 days, failing women who arrive without a first-trimester scan.

Most of the gain, found in 4 hospitals in [Chile](country:CL), reflected shorter local gestation and fell below 1 day against recentred comparators.

The model's preterm-birth risk score reached an area under the curve of 0.86 but was miscalibrated.

No woman was managed with the model, so the authors call for a prospective study.
