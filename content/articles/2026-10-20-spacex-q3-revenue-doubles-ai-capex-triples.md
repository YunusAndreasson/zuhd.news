United States — SpaceX spent $16.4 billion on AI infrastructure last quarter.

AI revenue of $7.6 billion passed connectivity's $5.25 billion for the first time, SpaceX reported.

Most of it is rented data-center capacity for Anthropic, Google and Reflection AI, closer to a cloud provider than a lab.

Up to 1.3 billion shares become freely tradable on 22 October, the largest release of the 180-day lockup.
