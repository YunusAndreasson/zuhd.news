Hours after the Supreme Court voided his tariffs, President Trump signed a 10% global tariff under a different law. Section 122 of the 1974 Trade Act allows a president to impose tariffs up to 15% for 150 days without congressional approval. Congress must vote to extend them, setting up another constitutional confrontation over trade powers.
