Abu Dhabi — Construction has begun on a high-speed line to Dubai.

A half-hour trip merges two rival emirates into one shared labour market.

The passenger line runs on Etihad Rail's freight spine, built first to move granite, steel and sulphur between Gulf ports.

The project's cost, contractors and completion date have not yet been disclosed.
