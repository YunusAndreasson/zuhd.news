Bangkok — A Thai royal decree cleared all parolees with under 1 year remaining.

Thaksin Shinawatra, a billionaire ex-PM, qualified with 3 months left on his sentence; his daughter Paetongtarn now leads the government.

The pardon removes the last legal bar on his political involvement.
