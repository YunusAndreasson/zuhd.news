Lisbon — Adults, not children, drove [Portugal](country:PT)'s 2020 wave.

A Bayesian model reconstructed two counterfactuals: schools staying open in spring 2020, and closing again that autumn.

The authors call it a provisional, unedited manuscript, and say closures alone rarely stop a wave.
