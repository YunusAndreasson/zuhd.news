Hyderabad — Vikram-1 targets a July 12 launch window.

Skyroot Aerospace's rocket is the first privately built orbital vehicle from [India](country:IN), whose space program had been a state monopoly for decades.

The window runs through August 4; the flight is the company's test of turning subsidy into working orbital capability.
