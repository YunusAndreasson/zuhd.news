London — Climate displacement is accelerating language extinction.

A review of 37 studies found migration and livelihood collapse scatter small tropical speech communities, erasing Indigenous ecological knowledge embedded in their languages.

Researchers argue biodiversity and climate policy still overlook language loss as a measurable harm.
