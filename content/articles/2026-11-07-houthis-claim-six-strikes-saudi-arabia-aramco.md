Saudi Arabia — Houthis claim 6 strikes inside the kingdom, Iranian state agency Mehr reported.

Brent crude stood at $98.32 on 6 November, down 16.4% in a week.

Houthi spokesman Yahya Saree said missiles and drones hit Aramco sites in Riyadh, Khurais and Dawadmi, plus King Khalid airport.

Mehr carried no independent confirmation and no response from [Saudi Arabia](country:SA).

Polymarket traders give a Saudi-Houthi ceasefire by 31 December a 23% chance.
