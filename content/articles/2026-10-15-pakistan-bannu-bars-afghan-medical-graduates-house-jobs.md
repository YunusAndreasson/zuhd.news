Bannu — A hospital terminated every Afghan graduate's house job training.

Afghan postgraduate trainees at 2 other hospitals were also dismissed and told to leave immediately.

The 9 October notification cites a letter from the [Pakistan](country:PK) Medical and Dental Council and one from the Prime Minister's Office.

The Nowshera notification says the trainees' visas have expired.

Neither notification says where the displaced doctors can resume training.
