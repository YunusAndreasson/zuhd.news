A UN fact-finding mission determined the Rapid Support Forces' assault on El-Fasher bears "hallmarks of genocide." The investigation found ethnically targeted killings, mass sexual violence and deliberate starvation of Zaghawa and Fur communities during an 18-month siege. The US Treasury sanctioned 3 RSF commanders in response.
