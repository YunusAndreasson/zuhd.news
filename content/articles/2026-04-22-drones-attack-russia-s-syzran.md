Syzran — [Ukraine](country:UA)'s drones killed a woman and child. The strike hit an apartment on Astrakhanskaya Street — the same road as Syzran's Rosneft refinery; 12 others were injured, the Samara governor confirmed. [Russia](country:RU)'s Defense Ministry said 155 drones were destroyed overnight across 11 regions; the refinery's damage is unconfirmed.
