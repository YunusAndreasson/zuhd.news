Former President Yoon Suk Yeol received life in prison for insurrection. A Seoul court found he deployed soldiers to storm parliament and arrest opposition leaders during his December 2024 martial law declaration, which lawmakers voted to overturn within hours. Prosecutors had sought the death penalty, but South Korea has not carried out an execution since 1997.
