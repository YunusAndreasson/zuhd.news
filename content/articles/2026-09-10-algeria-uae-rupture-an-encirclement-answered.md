Algiers — [Algeria](country:DZ) gave the [United Arab Emirates](country:AE)'s envoy 48 hours to leave.

Algeria joins Sudan and Somalia in breaking ties with Abu Dhabi.

Abu Dhabi backs Morocco's claim to Western Sahara and Libya's Haftar, both opposed by Algiers.

Abu Dhabi has not publicly responded, leaving Algerian-Emirati investment and flight ties in question.
