Seattle — Shayan Oveis Gharan won the 2026 IMU Abacus Medal.

He combined geometry, probability and computer science to break a decades-old approximation barrier on the Traveling Salesperson Problem.

Other theorists now use his methods to chase remaining unsolved approximation limits.
