Paris — Runoff voters blocked National Rally in Toulon and Nîmes. The party led both cities' first rounds but fell in the runoffs after centre-left and centre-right voters united against it. Centre-right former prime minister Édouard Philippe also won Le Havre, positioning him as the main rival to Marine Le Pen in the 2027 presidential election.
