Islamabad — Food imports hit $7.85bn; exports fell 32%.

[Pakistan](country:PK) imported 309,000 tonnes of sugar, official data show — against 3,000 tonnes a year earlier — as domestic harvests faltered.

The trade imbalance feeds into Pakistan's next IMF review, where import-dependent food costs become a fiscal variable.
