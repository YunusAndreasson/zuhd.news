Tehran — 4.1 million Iranians fell below the poverty line. 23,000 factories struck, 125,000 civilian buildings damaged — 10 times the Iran-Iraq War's toll — as Trump claimed the campaign supported ordinary Iranians. UNDP puts 10-12 million jobs at risk; reconstruction is estimated at $300 billion.
