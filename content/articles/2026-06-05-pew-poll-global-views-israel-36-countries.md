Washington — [Turkey](country:TR) reached 99% unfavorable toward [Israel](country:IL).

Pew's 36-country survey of 44,000 found a 67% global median unfavorable — near-universal in Muslim-majority nations, rising sharply among young Europeans.

[South Korea](country:KR)'s unfavorable rating jumped 10 points this year — the survey's sharpest single-year rise.
