Evo 2 wrote complete genomes across all life. The Arc Institute trained it on 9.3 trillion DNA letters from 128,000 species — enough to learn the grammar of life itself. 16 of 285 AI-designed viruses were functional; a full cell requires 100% accuracy, which no design has yet achieved.
