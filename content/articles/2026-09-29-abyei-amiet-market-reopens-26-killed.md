Abyei — Abyei's shared market reopened after violence killed 26 people.

It is one of few institutions still binding the two communities together.

Peace committees for the Ngok Dinka and Misseriya will share security and must withdraw combat vehicles from the market by October 10.

"The detailed points on the market and the methods of operation have not yet been resolved," said Ngok Dinka leader Sultan Belbek Ding.

The two committees meet again Thursday, and the Misseriya administration arrives from Nyala Saturday to address the violence.
