Washington — SpaceX wants to launch nearly 10 times its current Starlink fleet.

The Gen3 filing to the [US](country:US) Federal Communications Commission covers roughly 100,000 satellites, promising a 100x bandwidth jump over today's ~11,000.

Regulators must weigh the request against collision-risk and orbital-spectrum limits before deployment can begin.
