St. Petersburg — Germany's consulate here must close by 18 September.

Residents needing visas or document legalization will now have to travel to Moscow instead.

[Russia](country:RU) ordered the closure after [Germany](country:DE) shut a Russian consulate over a drone found near Leipzig airport.

Goethe-Institut branches across Russia must also empty out by 13 September.
