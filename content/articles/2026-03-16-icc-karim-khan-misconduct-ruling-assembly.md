Governments Khan investigated will decide his fate. The ICC's 21-member executive bureau met today on misconduct charges against chief prosecutor Karim Khan — removal requires 63 of 125 member states to vote against him, including countries whose leaders he has indicted. The bureau may rule today or set a process for a later vote.
