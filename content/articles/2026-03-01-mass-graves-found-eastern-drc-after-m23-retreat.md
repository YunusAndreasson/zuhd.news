171 bodies filled 2 mass graves left in Uvira. Rwanda-backed M23 rebels, occupying Uvira since December, killed the victims on suspicion of ties to the Congolese military. No Security Council member has sanctioned Rwanda despite UN documentation of its role in a conflict that has displaced 7 million people.
