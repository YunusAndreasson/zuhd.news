Saudi Arabia intercepted drones over Riyadh this week. Iran's ambassador to Saudi Arabia denied any Iranian role in the attacks, as Tehran calculates that Gulf neutrality is essential to avoiding a wider war. A confirmed Iranian strike on Saudi oil infrastructure could trigger Gulf states to join the coalition against Tehran.
