Qom — Shia jurist Vahid Khorasani died, [Iran](country:IR)'s state-run Mehr News Agency reported.

His office says he trained a large number of scholars over decades at the Qom seminary.

As a marja', he was a jurist whom Shia followers consult for rulings on Islamic law.

His office said it will announce funeral, burial and mourning arrangements later.
