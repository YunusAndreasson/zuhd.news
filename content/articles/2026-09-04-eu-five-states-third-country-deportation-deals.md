Copenhagen — Five EU states will pay a still-unnamed country to take migrants that are not its own.

The deal moves deportation outside the reach of European courts, which oversee EU soil but not a third state.

[Germany](country:DE), Austria, Greece, Denmark and the Netherlands aim to sign deals this year despite Council of Europe warnings.

Denmark expects to send people to the hub by the end of 2027.
