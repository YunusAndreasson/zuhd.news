Redmond — Microsoft's new Outlook takes 10 seconds per notification.

The app is a WebView2 browser wrapper loading Outlook.com; Microsoft postponed forced enterprise adoption to March 2027 after performance complaints.

Microsoft has announced no fix for the notification lag, leaving enterprises dependent on the slower app until the forced migration date.
