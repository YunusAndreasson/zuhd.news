Proton Mail helped the FBI unmask an anonymous protester. The service cannot share encrypted email content, but payment records transferred via Swiss authorities to the FBI identified the user. This is Proton Mail's 2nd data disclosure to law enforcement; the first unmasked a French climate activist in 2021.
