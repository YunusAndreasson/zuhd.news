London — Floodwater reached a neonatal unit in England.

Floods have forced 67 National Health Service ward closures since 2021; incidents nearly doubled to 400 in 2024-25.

The Environment Agency projects 33% of UK medical sites at flood risk by 2050, up from 25% now.
