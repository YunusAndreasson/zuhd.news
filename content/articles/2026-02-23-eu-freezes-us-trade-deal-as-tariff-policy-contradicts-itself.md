The European Parliament froze a trade deal with Washington. The Supreme Court struck down Trump's tariffs Friday; he imposed 15% levies under different authority Saturday, leaving any agreement's legal basis in doubt. A committee vote set for Tuesday is postponed until Washington clarifies which tariffs are in force.
