Iranian drones struck Qatar's Ras Laffan gas complex. Qatar, which supplies 20% of global liquefied natural gas, gave no timeline for resuming exports and rejected Israeli accusations that it coordinated the shutdown with Iran. European gas prices have surged with no alternative supplier to fill the gap, and analysts see no credible substitute if the suspension extends through spring.
