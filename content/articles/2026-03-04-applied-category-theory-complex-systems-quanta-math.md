Category theory now safeguards AI systems controlling nuclear plants. The UK's Safeguarded AI project applies it to nuclear plant AI, where confusing units — doses with people, dollars with quantities — can be lethal. John Baez proposed applying it to climate modeling in 2011; climate science has not yet adopted it.
