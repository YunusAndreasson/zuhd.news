Oslo — Ukraine got no US warning of 16 incoming ballistic missiles, and Kyiv officials suspect Washington withheld it.

The [US](country:US) is pressing [Ukraine](country:UA) for concessions, and Trump wants someone other than Zelensky to negotiate.

Zelensky told US senators the Pentagon is delaying paid-for missiles awaiting Hegseth's signature, and European Patriot transfers await US approval.

US officials deny slowing intelligence, and Trump says he did not order limits on Starshield.

Zelensky says the US side also plans to meet Putin, with Kushner joining the Oslo talks online.
