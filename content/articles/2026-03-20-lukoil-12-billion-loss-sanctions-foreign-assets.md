US sanctions stripped Lukoil of 3 European refineries. October 2025 measures forced Russia's second-largest oil producer to write off $20 billion in foreign assets, including oilfield stakes across 7 countries. A planned sale to Saudi Arabia's Midad Energy awaits US Treasury approval after the divestiture licence expired last month.
