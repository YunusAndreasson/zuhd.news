Washington — Migrants can again be deported to countries where they have no ties.

The Supreme Court suspended a judge's rule that migrants first get a chance to object.

An unsigned, unexplained emergency order restarts a programme that has sent an estimated 25,000 people to more than two dozen countries.

"Today's order does not decide that the government's policy is lawful," said Trina Realmuto, a lawyer for the migrants.

The justices hear arguments in December on whether the policy is lawful.
