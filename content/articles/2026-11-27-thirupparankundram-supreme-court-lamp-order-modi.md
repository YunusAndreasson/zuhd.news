Madurai — [India](country:IN)'s top court issued contempt notices to Tamil Nadu's chief secretary and police chief.

It suspended the temple's trustees and put hill security under the Central Reserve Police Force.

The bench said the record shows wilful disobedience of its 20 November order to light the Deepathoon lamp.

Tamil Nadu's Bharatiya Janata Party president demanded Chief Minister C Joseph Vijay resign, after Prime Minister Narendra Modi hailed the order.

The court ordered the lamp lit on 28 November.
