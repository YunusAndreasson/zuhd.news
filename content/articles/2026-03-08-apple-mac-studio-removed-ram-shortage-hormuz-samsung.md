Apple's 512GB Mac Studio silently vanished from its store. Memory makers have diverted production to high-bandwidth chips for Nvidia AI accelerators, leaving consumer DRAM scarce and pushing Apple's remaining 256GB upgrade to $2,000. Delivery of 256GB models has slipped to May; market researcher TrendForce sees no supply relief before 2027.
