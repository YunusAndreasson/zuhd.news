New Delhi — [India](country:IN) is pairing state contracts with private space startups.

The government offers launch access and anchor procurement to private earth-observation and satellite firms — mirroring how the [US](country:US) and [China](country:CN) built commercial space sectors.

India's sovereign launch capability still lags rivals; that gap is the strategy's unresolved constraint.
