Traders are betting Iran war outcomes on unregulated prediction markets. Commodity Futures Trading Commission Chair Michael Selig plans to classify them as "event contracts" subject to oversight — a rulemaking that would also cover decentralised finance platforms. The agency has set no timeline, coordinating with the SEC through a joint initiative called "Project Crypto."
