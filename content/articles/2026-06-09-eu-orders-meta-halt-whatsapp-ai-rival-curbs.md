Brussels — Meta must open WhatsApp to rival AI firms.

Meta banned third-party AI from the WhatsApp Business API in December; the EU ruled the restriction a likely abuse of dominance.

Meta has 5 working days to comply; non-compliance risks fines up to 10% of annual revenue; it says it will appeal.
