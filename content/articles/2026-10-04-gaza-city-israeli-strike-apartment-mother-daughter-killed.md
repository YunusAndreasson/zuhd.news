Gaza City — An Israeli strike killed Maisa Abu Dawoud and her daughter Mary.

At least 5 died when two missiles hit a Rimal apartment building, Gaza health and civil defence officials said, and 4 of the dead were women.

Gaza health officials count 1,446 Palestinians killed since last October's ceasefire, against 4 Israeli soldiers killed, Israel says.

[Israel](country:IL)'s military said it killed a Hamas official overnight but did not say if it was this strike.

Defence Minister Israel Katz ordered every crossing into Gaza closed for the 7 October anniversary.
