London — Mars regolith kills roots; fungi may fix that.

Martian soil's perchlorate toxicity blocks root growth; mycorrhizal fungi evolved to free phosphorus in low-organic substrates — the same chemistry barrier.

Lab trials are testing whether fungi achieve perchlorate detoxification and phosphorus liberation simultaneously.
