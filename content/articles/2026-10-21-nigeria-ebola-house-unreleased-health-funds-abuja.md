Abuja — Money appropriated for health agencies sits unreleased, the House of Representatives says.

A woman who arrived from [Uganda](country:UG) tested positive for Ebola, and 44 contacts are being traced.

Sponsor Amobi Ogah cited inadequate public awareness, shortages of infection-control supplies and gaps in health worker preparedness.

[Nigeria](country:NG) contained its 2014 outbreak at 19 confirmed cases and 8 deaths, WHO records show.

The House ordered its Committee on Infectious Diseases to report on preparedness.
