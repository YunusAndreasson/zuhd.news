A Bolsonaro win would hand [Brazil](country:BR)'s farm lobby more influence over a top food supplier.

Bolsonaro says he will use the conservative Congress elected on 4 October to change the Constitution.

More than 160 million Brazilians choose between them in Sunday's runoff on 25 October.
