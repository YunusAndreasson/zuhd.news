Denmark — Two Danish satellites will hunt for minerals around the Moon.

The €8 million contract is for Blue Origin, a name GomSpace left out of its announcement.

The craft will orbit the Moon mapping water ice, helium-3, rare earths and precious metals to find extraction sites.

A critical design review comes next, with delivery expected in late 2027.
