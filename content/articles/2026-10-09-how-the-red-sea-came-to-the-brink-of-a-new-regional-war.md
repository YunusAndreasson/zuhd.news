Addis Ababa — [Ethiopia](country:ET) accuses [Eritrea](country:ER) of sending thousands of troops.

The Rapid Support Forces attacked [Sudan](country:SD)'s Blue Nile from Ethiopia, displacing an estimated 2,800 people.

Ethiopian drones struck Eritrean troops Wednesday and Thursday, a conflict monitor recorded, after the Tigray People's Liberation Front's September offensive.

[Egypt](country:EG), Sudan, Somalia and Eritrea met in El Alamein and agreed to coordinate militarily if the Tigray war expands.
