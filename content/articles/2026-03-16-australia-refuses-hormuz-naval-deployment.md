Australia will not send warships to Hormuz. Transport Minister Catherine King said Australia would contribute aircraft to the UAE instead — a departure from its pattern of joining every US naval coalition since WWII. Australia's navy is also stretched by AUKUS nuclear submarine commitments that take priority over regional deployments.
