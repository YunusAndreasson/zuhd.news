Pune — [India](country:IN)'s top security official told youth freedom has limits.

National Security Advisor Ajit Doval spoke days into student protests over a leaked NEET exam, framing the unrest as a lapse in national duty rather than exam fraud.

More than 200 police were reportedly hurt in the protests; no leak investigation findings have been published.
