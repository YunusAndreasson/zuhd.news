Mukalla — Some Yemeni teacher-training departments now enrol no students at all.

Colleges of education across [Yemen](country:YE), from Aden to Hadramawt, report the same, New Lines Magazine found.

Departments have closed as enrolment fell from hundreds of students a decade ago, and al-Mukalla's dormitories stand abandoned.

Dean Saeed Salmen Balafair now sits in the classroom where he studied among more than 100 students in 1996.
