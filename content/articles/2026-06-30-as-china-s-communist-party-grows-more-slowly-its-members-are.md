Beijing — The Chinese Communist Party growth slowed again in 2025.

The Organization Department reported 101 million members at end-2025; 30% are over 60, the highest senior share in at least 5 years.

Annual membership growth has fallen every year since 2021; [China](country:CN)'s national population is also declining, compressing the cadre pipeline.
