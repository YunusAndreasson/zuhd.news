A botnet outnumbered the Invisible Internet Project's nodes 39-to-1. Kimwolf, built from millions of hacked devices, overwhelmed I2P after the botnet's operators lost their command servers and rerouted traffic through the network. I2P developers patched the network within 6 days, enabling post-quantum encryption and stronger flood defenses by default.
