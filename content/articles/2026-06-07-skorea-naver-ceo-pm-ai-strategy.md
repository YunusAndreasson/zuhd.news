Seoul — Former Naver CEO named [South Korea](country:KR)'s PM.

President Lee Jae Myung chose Han Seong-sook, South Korea's Small and Medium Enterprise and Startup Minister, to lead the government's artificial intelligence push.

Han requires National Assembly approval; Lee said the appointment signals AI as his government's top policy priority.
