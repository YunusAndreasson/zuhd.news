Pakistan's child TB drug supply has nearly run out. Producers halted manufacturing after raw material costs rose but government price caps remained frozen, making production unprofitable. The Pakistan Medical Association warns untreated children face 24-month drug-resistant regimens carrying a risk of permanent hearing loss.
