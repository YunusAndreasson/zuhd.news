New Delhi — US Trade Representative Greer says an India deal is not imminent.

[India](country:IN) still awaits a [US](country:US) tariff framework that makes its exporters competitive against China and Bangladesh.

The US Supreme Court invalidated the legal basis for the tariff preference offered in February, leaving the interim deal unsigned.

Trade Minister Piyush Goyal called the deal finished on 24 September, pending a competitive tariff edge.

Greer says Modi and Trump may speak again soon to assess progress.
