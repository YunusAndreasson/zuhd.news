Chengdu — Family conflict and trauma were the top depression predictors in a [China](country:CN) student study, outperforming standard screening.

Recurrent neural networks trained on 1,500 students over 4 years reached 0.952 predictive accuracy.

Generalizability and the risk of predictive mental-health surveillance on students remain unaddressed.
