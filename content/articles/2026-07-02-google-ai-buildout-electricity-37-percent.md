Mountain View — Electricity use jumped 37% in a single year.

Google's data centers drew over 42 million megawatt-hours in 2025, its sustainability report shows, matching some countries' consumption.

Google says emissions still fell 2% via renewable purchases, even as its AI buildout outpaces grid decarbonization.
