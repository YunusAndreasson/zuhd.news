Cotonou — Benin voted Sunday with one real candidate. Romuald Wadagni ran against a token rival after the ruling coalition blocked all main opposition candidates from the ballot. Turnout — not the outcome — is the legitimacy question; the Constitutional Court validates results by April 16.
