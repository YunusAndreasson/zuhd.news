Berlin — Elif Eralp pledges to expropriate more than 200,000 housing units.

A housing shortage and rising rents gave her Left Party its best Berlin result in September.

Vonovia, [Germany](country:DE)'s largest landlord, calls the plan wrong and legally highly questionable.

Vonovia chief executive Luka Mucic said expropriation would add no housing and undermine confidence in property ownership.

Nothing can begin until Eralp's coalition takes the mayor's office.
