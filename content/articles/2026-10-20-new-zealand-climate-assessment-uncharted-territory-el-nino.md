New Zealand — Temperatures have risen 1.36°C since 1909.

The Our Atmosphere and Climate 2026 report expects glaciers to lose 50% to 85% of their remaining mass this century.

Seas around [New Zealand](country:NZ) have warmed 34% faster than the global average, and the Tasman Sea is a marine heat wave hot spot.

A record El Niño this summer is expected to bring heavier rain to the South Island's west and drought to eastern regions.
