Singapore — [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) will store crude in Singapore's Jurong caverns from 2027.

Asia imported two-thirds of its crude from the Middle East before the war with [Iran](country:IR).

The caverns sit 150 metres underground and hold up to 1.47 million cubic metres, outside the Strait of Hormuz.

Saudi Aramco signs a memorandum Monday and aims to conclude negotiations in a few months, Singapore's energy minister said.
