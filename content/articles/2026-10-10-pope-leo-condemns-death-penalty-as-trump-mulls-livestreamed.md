Vatican City — Pope Leo XIV declared capital punishment inadmissible on Saturday.

His deputy, Cardinal Pietro Parolin, called the [US](country:US) plan to livestream an execution unacceptable.

Pope Francis rewrote the catechism in 2018 so the church no longer allows capital punishment in any case.

Republican Representative Austin Scott, who backs the execution, said "livestreaming executions is what Jihadists do."

Trump has not decided whether to stream Nidal Hasan's 3 December execution for killing 13 at Fort Hood.
