Ilorin — Bandits kept Kwara's Muslims from Eid grounds Sunday.

The League of Imams in Isin, [Nigeria](country:NG), banned outdoor prayers after 6 people were abducted in the area days before Eid.

The Kwara government has issued no security response; 4 district school closures ordered in 2025 remain in force.
