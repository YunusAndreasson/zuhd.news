Bandar Abbas — A tunnel and three bridges fell overnight.

US strikes on Hormozgan, which flanks the Strait of Hormuz, severed roads to the rest of [Iran](country:IR), the governor's office said.

Some routes have partially reopened; Hormozgan's full network remains restricted until further notice.
