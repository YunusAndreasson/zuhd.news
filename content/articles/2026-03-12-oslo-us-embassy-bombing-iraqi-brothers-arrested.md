3 brothers were arrested for the US embassy bombing in Oslo. Norway's security service had warned last month that Iran could rely on criminal networks as proxies; the brothers — Norwegian citizens of Iraqi origin in their 20s — were not previously known to police. Police say one brother placed the bomb and are still investigating whether a foreign government ordered the attack.
