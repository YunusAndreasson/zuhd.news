Sanaa — Jets flew from Khamis Mushait, Ansarallah military spokesman Yahya Saree says.

The strikes follow Houthi advances toward Bab al-Mandab, a tenth of global oil traffic.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA) has blockaded [Yemen](country:YE) for over a decade, since Ansarallah took the capital.

Saree vowed retaliation — Riyadh has not confirmed the sortie count publicly.
