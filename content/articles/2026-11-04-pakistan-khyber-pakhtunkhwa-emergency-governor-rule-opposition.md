Islamabad — Governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has united [Pakistan](country:PK)'s opposition.

The Centre declared an emergency on 11 October, displacing the provincial government of Imran Khan's Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf.

Fazlur Rehman accuses the Centre of misusing the Constitution and reneging on commitments during his mediation.

PTI Secretary General Salman Akram Raja said "some may walk with us for one mile, some for 10 miles."

The leaders are expected to discuss a multi-party conference, with no date announced.
