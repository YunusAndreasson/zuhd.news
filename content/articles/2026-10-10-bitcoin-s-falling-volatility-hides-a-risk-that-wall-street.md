New York — Bitcoin has had 10 extreme price days this year.

The 2018 bear market, which cost bitcoin 73%, had only 8 such days, the analysis found.

An extreme day is a move of at least 3 times the previous 30 days' realized daily volatility.

Deeper liquidity and institutional participation have helped the market absorb such shocks.

Macroeconomic shocks hitting crowded derivatives trades can still amplify sudden swings, the analysis says.
