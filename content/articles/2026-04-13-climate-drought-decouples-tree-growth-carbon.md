Beijing — In droughts, trees photosynthesize but stop absorbing carbon. China's northern plantations show canopy productivity and stem growth decouple under drought, invalidating carbon sequestration models that assume they track together. Wood production recovers from drought faster than canopy; carbon credit models treat both as equivalent.
