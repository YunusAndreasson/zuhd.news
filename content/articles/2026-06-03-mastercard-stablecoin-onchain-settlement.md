New York — Mastercard will settle card transactions in stablecoins 24/7.

Card transactions authorize instantly but settle in batch on banking schedules — stablecoin rails remove that lag and the correspondent banks between them.

The rollout starts in the [US](country:US) and Latin America with 5 banks, initially using USDC and Ripple's RLUSD.
