San Francisco — Ethereum now has dozens of incompatible layer-2 chains. Co-founder Vitalik Buterin told layer-2 builders "You are not scaling Ethereum" — the chains lower fees but fragment the network that made Ethereum valuable. The Ethereum Foundation must now choose whether to impose coordination on its layer-2 ecosystem or watch faster-moving competitors absorb users priced out of the fragmented network.
