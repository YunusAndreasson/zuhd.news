Khyber Pakhtunkhwa — Governor says emergency rule would follow if Islamabad orders it.

Emergency or governor's rule would put the province under [Pakistan](country:PK)'s federal government.

Governor Faisal Karim Kundi said the prime minister and president decide, citing Balochistan, where the Pakistan Peoples Party ended its government.

Kundi said whether it is imposed comes first and declined to discuss whether it could last.
