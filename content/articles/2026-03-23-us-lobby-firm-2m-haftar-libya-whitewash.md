Washington — Ballard Partners signed Khalifa Haftar, Libya's eastern commander, for $2M. The firm, staffed by ex-Trump officials, took the contract despite a 2022 US court ruling that found Haftar liable for war crimes and Human Rights Watch documentation of torture under his command. Trump advisor Massad Boulos is brokering a deal that would give Haftar permanent control of Libya's security forces, bypassing elections.
