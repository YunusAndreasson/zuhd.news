Washington — Essential benefits drop from Affordable Care Act plans starting 2027.

The [US](country:US) health regulator finalized the rule Friday; healthy enrollees can now choose bare-bones plans, raising costs for the sicker who remain.

No court challenge has been filed; the rule takes effect for 2027 enrollments.
