Sassou-Nguesso's nearest rival got 1.48% of Congo-Brazzaville's votes. Denis Sassou-Nguesso, Congo-Brazzaville's incumbent president, claimed 95% of the vote; he has ruled the oil-rich but impoverished nation for most of the past 4 decades. The Constitutional Court must certify the result, which would make this his fifth consecutive term.
