Islamabad — Pakistan diverted Rs900bn from provinces to the Centre.

Three shocks — trade disruption, floods and the [Iran](country:IR) war — forced the recentralization; the government warns provincial development spending will remain frozen past this fiscal year.

Budget announced Friday targets single-digit lending rates for housing and agriculture; IMF talks continue.
