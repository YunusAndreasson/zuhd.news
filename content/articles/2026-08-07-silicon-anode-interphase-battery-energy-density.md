London — Silicon anodes swell 300% and usually crack apart.

A sulfur coating stabilizes the film as pouch cells built from 90%+ silicon expand, researchers report, holding 80% capacity after 1,700 cycles.

The manuscript is unedited; pack-level energy density and cost at automotive rates remain unverified.
