Monte Verde — Humans settled southern Chile 8,000 years ago, not 14,500. Radiocarbon analysis found the dated sediment washed in from an older riverbank, contaminating the 14,500-year estimate with older organic matter. Researcher Surovell's findings await peer review while archaeologists who staked careers on Monte Verde's 14,500-year date must reassess.
