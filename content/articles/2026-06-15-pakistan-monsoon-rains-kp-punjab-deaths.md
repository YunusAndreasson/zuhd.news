Peshawar — Monsoon storms killed 10 across [Pakistan](country:PK)'s north.

Roof collapses and electrocutions drove most fatalities in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Punjab — the Met Office forecast more storms this week.

The Siren and Kunhar rivers are receding but authorities warned tourists that levels can rise from upstream rain.
