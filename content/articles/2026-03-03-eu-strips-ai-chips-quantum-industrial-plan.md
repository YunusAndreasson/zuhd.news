category: "tech"
---

EU stripped 7 strategic technologies from its industrial plan. Germany and pro-market states vetoed protectionist measures needed to compete with China's state-backed chip and AI industries. The plan now covers steel and solar panels; Europe will remain dependent on China for AI and chips.
