Osaka — Narcolepsy destroys 20,000 orexin-making neurons for good.

Every prior drug just managed the loss with stimulants; Takeda's Orzeyful replaces the missing wake signal at its receptor.

Rivals are testing the same class for narcolepsy type 2 and Alzheimer's-linked fatigue next.
