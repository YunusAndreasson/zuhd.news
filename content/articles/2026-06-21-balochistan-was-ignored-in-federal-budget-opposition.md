Quetta — Balochistan received Rs200 billion for development — less than the cost of 1 expressway, opposition lawmakers said.

The province supplies [Pakistan](country:PK)'s gas and minerals; the federal government offered 5,000 youth jobs for the whole region.

No formal reply to opposition demands for equal allocation has been announced.
