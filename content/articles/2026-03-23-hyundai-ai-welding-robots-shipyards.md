Seoul — HD Hyundai is deploying humanoid welding robots at shipyards. Korean shipyards are at capacity on LNG carrier orders driven by Hormuz rerouting, making welding the main production bottleneck. Persona AI, the South Korean robotics startup building the machines, must prove its bipedal design can weld safely in live shipyard conditions.
