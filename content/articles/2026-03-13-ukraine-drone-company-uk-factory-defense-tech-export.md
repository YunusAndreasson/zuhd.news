Ukrainian drone firm Ukrspecsystems opened its first UK factory. Ukraine lifted its arms export embargo last year, converting battlefield drone expertise into an exportable product. The UK plant operates under a 2025 bilateral defense agreement; similar factories are planned across Europe.
