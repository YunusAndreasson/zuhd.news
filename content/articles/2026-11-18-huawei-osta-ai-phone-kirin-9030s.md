Shenzhen — Huawei's OSTA O1 Pro packs a 7,000mAh battery.

Huawei adds an AI-focused brand to its Mate, Pura and nova lines.

Leaked specs show a 6.39-inch 16:9.5 flat display and the Kirin 9030S chip, at a starting price near 4,000 yuan.

Huawei unveils OSTA on 20 November, and the Kirin 9030S has no published performance results yet.
