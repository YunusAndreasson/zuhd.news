Washington — The US pulled nonessential staff from Saudi Arabia on Wednesday.

Embassies in Jordan, Lebanon, Qatar and the UAE also cut staff over Houthi and Iranian threats.

Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton told Rubio that Iranian plots against US personnel have reached advanced stages, in a one-page memo.

The State Department cited Houthi threats, not Iran, and US officials deny any tie to new strikes on Iran.

US ambassador to Turkey Tom Barrack says strikes on Iran could begin as soon as this weekend.
