London — More green space upwind cuts urban heat and smog together.

Upwind siting and medium-density blocks let monsoon winds flush both pollutants and heat, researchers found in humid subtropical cities.

The framework awaits real-city testing; the humid subtropical zone spans South Asia, East Asia and West Africa.
