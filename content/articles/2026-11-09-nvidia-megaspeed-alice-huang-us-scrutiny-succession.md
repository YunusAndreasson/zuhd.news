Washington — Alice Huang's employer reportedly bought $4.8bn of Nvidia equipment.

House Republicans asked CEO Jensen Huang to testify on 18 November about his ties to her.

Washington has restricted advanced chip exports to [China](country:CN) for years, and Megaspeed has been accused of transferring technology there.

China's foreign ministry calls it "nothing but smears," and Nvidia's board says it has no reason to believe secrets leaked.

The Securities and Exchange Commission declined to say whether it will investigate, and Nvidia shares fell 5.29% Monday.
