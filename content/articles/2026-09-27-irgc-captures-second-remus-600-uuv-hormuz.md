Tehran — Mehr News says [Iran](country:IR) seized a 2nd US seabed drone.

The Remus 600 is built to map minefields and the seabed, and [Iran](country:IR) now holds its data.

Mehr News says Iran's navy intercepted the [US](country:US)-made drone using electronic warfare and is recovering its data.

The US military has not confirmed the loss of any underwater drone in the strait.

Iran has blockaded the strait since February, and a US counter-blockade of its ports remains in force.
