London — A new crystal blocks a wide vibration band.

That gap is where ship hulls, satellite buses and precision instruments need passive protection with no power draw.

A tapered "acoustic black hole" slows waves toward zero speed so they dissipate, covering 12 to 805 Hz alongside the crystal's own band.

Researchers say the design predicts target frequencies with error as low as 2.0%.
