Damage estimates remain unsettled under the UN's Latin America and Caribbean economic commission methodology, and [Venezuela](country:VE)'s sanctions limit access to international reconstruction financing.

Recovery will hinge on remittances, diaspora aid and any bilateral or IMF support offered.
