Pakistan's courts cannot force Imran Khan's hospital transfer. The Islamabad High Court ordered a medical board to examine the former prime minister in prison, but Pakistani law gives only the government — the same one detaining him — the power to move a prisoner to hospital. The board reports its findings to Islamabad's chief commissioner, who decides whether Khan is transferred.
