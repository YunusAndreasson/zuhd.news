San Francisco — The challenger is bidding to buy the pioneer.

Stripe and private equity firm Advent offered $60.50 a share, valuing PayPal at $53 billion, backed by $50 billion in committed bank financing.

PayPal has not engaged with the offer as new chief executive Enrique Lores pursues a turnaround.
