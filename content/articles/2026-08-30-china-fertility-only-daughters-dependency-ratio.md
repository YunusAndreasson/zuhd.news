Beijing — Women now outnumber men at Chinese universities.

A shrinking, high-earning female workforce must now fund [China](country:CN)'s retirement bulge.

Their parents raised them as only children, prizing careers over early marriage.

Beijing has offered no policy reconciling careers with earlier, more frequent childbirth.
