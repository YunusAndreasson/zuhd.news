Iran's 3rd missile in 5 days triggered Incirlik's sirens. Incirlik is NATO's largest forward-deployed nuclear stockpile, storing roughly 50 US warheads and hosting troops from Spain and Poland. Iran denied firing the missile as NATO deployed Patriot batteries 100km north in Malatya.
