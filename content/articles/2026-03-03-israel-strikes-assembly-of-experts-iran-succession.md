Israel struck Qom while Iran chose Khamenei's successor. Iran's supreme leader commands the Revolutionary Guards and controls nuclear policy; the role requires a vote by the Assembly's 88 clerics. Security chief Ali Larijani leads an interim council that must complete the succession under ongoing strikes.
