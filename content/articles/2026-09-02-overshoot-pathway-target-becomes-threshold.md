Nairobi — Warming will cross 1.5°C before 2030, UNEP says.

Adaptation plans in [Bangladesh](country:BD), [Pakistan](country:PK) and the Sahel targeted a threshold that's gone.

The report's overshoot pathway peaks near 1.8°C then aims to decline, but ice sheets and coral don't reverse.

UNEP says cutting methane, which clears in a decade, is the lever left within the window.
