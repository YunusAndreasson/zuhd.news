Milan — Rival banks filed 265 reports on 24 suspects, Revolut none.

Revolut, with over 5 million Italian customers, now answers to two court-appointed administrators for a year.

Its Italian branch operates on a licence from [Lithuania](country:LT), and the court says weak checks let the 24 hide €41.9 million.

Revolut is not under criminal investigation, and the measure is preventive, the ruling said.

Revolut owes a remediation plan in 60 days, and the court reconvenes on 25 February 2027.
