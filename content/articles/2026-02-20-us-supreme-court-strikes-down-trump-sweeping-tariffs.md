$175 billion in US tariffs fell Thursday. The Supreme Court ruled 6-3 that President Trump exceeded his authority by using emergency powers to set trade policy reserved for Congress. Steel and aluminum tariffs survive under different statutes, but the ruling voids the core of his trade agenda.
