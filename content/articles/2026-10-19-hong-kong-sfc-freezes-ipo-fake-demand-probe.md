Hong Kong — Regulator froze HK$4.38 billion over suspected faked public-offering demand.

Subscriptions backed by returned money mislead every later buyer of the stock.

Securities and Futures Commission notices describe circular funds: placees fill the book, issuer proceeds leave, and money returns to subscribers.

The commission has announced no finding of joint control between the two frozen clusters.

Shares in ChaPanda, Apollomics and Tianju Dihe stay suspended while the investigation runs.
