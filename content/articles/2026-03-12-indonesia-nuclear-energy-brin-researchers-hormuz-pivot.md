Indonesia's state research agency needs 200 new nuclear researchers. The Hormuz blockade made nuclear power an emergency priority for Indonesia, which imports most of its fossil fuels from the Middle East. The agency has not confirmed it can attract enough nuclear graduates before the first plant opens in 2032.
