Saudi Arabia — Brent hit $115.36 after a Houthi refinery strike claim.

UBS expects Brent near $110 through year-end on falling inventories and record freight costs.

Houthis said they hit [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s biggest refinery, which processes 550,000 barrels a day, and Saudi authorities have not confirmed it.

Tehran is due to answer Qatar's draft deal to end the war by the end of the week.
