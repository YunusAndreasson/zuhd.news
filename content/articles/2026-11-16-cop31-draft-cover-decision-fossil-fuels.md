Türkiye — COP31's draft calls for an immediate move off fossil fuels.

350.org says the draft omits any process to advance the fossil fuel roadmaps.

[Türkiye](country:TR)'s target of 35% electricity in final energy by 2035 appears without a requirement that the power be renewable.

Some countries have questioned the legality of a cover decision at all.

Ministers respond over the next 4 days, and the presidencies will issue a revised draft.
