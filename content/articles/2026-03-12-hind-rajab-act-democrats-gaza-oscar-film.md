A 6-year-old's killing is now a congressional bill. US Congressional Democrats introduced the Justice for Hind Rajab Act, requiring the State Department to determine within 45 days if Israeli soldiers in Gaza violated the Leahy Law — a federal bar on arming military units that commit rights abuses. The bill faces a Republican-controlled Congress, but a documentary about Rajab's death is building Oscar momentum that could amplify pressure.
