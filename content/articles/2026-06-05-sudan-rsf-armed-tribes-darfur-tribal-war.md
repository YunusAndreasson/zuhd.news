Nyala — Rapid Support Forces weapons turned Darfur's tribes against each other.

Weapon distribution turned land rivalries into armed contests; a separate Rapid Support Forces assault near Bara killed at least 58 over 3 days, residents said.

The Rapid Support Forces also established a parallel security council, formalizing a rival [Sudan](country:SD) government.
