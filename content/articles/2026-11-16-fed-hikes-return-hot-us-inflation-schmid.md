Kansas City — [US](country:US) consumer prices rose 0.4% in October, beating forecasts.

Deutsche Bank expects three straight 25-basis-point Fed hikes, reaching 4.625% by the end of 2027's first quarter.

Core prices, which exclude food and energy, rose 0.3% against the 0.2% forecast, and the 10-year Treasury yield climbed.

Deutsche economists say tighter financial conditions from falling equities could give the Fed pause if the moves extend.

The Federal Reserve decides on 9 December, and Polymarket traders give a hike 88% odds, up 11 points in a week.
