Asunción — Ueno Bank claimed 60% of [Paraguay](country:PY)'s adults as customers.

President Santiago Peña resigned Thursday, hours after regulators seized the bank.

Public funds supplied about a quarter of deposits, while the bank lent to its owners' companies beyond legal limits.

Deposit insurance covers up to $38,000 per person, enough for about 97% of depositors, preliminary estimates show.

Prosecutors charged 27 Ueno officials, and insured deposits must be paid within 15 days of the takeover.
