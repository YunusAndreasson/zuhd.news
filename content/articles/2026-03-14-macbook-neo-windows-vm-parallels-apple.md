$599 MacBook Neo runs Windows via Parallels. The A18 Pro chip's ARM architecture enables Parallels compatibility, with 8GB of fixed RAM capping Windows to light tasks. The Neo is Apple's first sub-$600 laptop and targets the price range where most Windows PCs are sold.
