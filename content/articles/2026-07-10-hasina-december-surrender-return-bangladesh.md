Delhi — Sheikh Hasina says she may be killed.

The 78-year-old, sentenced to death in absentia, plans to fly home in December with [Bangladesh](country:BD)'s banned Awami League leadership to surrender.

Dhaka has not said whether it will arrest her at the border on her chosen date.
