Washington — The [US](country:US), [UK](country:GB) and [Australia](country:AU) are jointly developing unmanned undersea vehicles.

The Australia-UK-US pact's Pillar Two turned to underwater drones after nuclear submarines fell behind schedule, Defense Secretary Hegseth said.

No deployment timeline was announced.
