Mountain View — Android will carry sideload permissions to new devices. Google added this friction barrier in Android 8; by migrating the setting on restore, it reverses years of tightening sideload controls. EU regulators are still pursuing mandated open installation paths; whether Google's move preempts a ruling is unresolved.
