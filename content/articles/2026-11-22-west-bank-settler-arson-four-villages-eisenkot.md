Ramallah — Settlers torched 8 to 12 cars in 4 West Bank villages.

Gadi Eisenkot, tasked with forming [Israel](country:IL)'s next government, blamed ministers Ben-Gvir and Smotrich.

Settler attacks rose from 647 in September to 1,369 in October, a Palestinian Authority commission said.

Shai Parnes of the Israeli rights group B'Tselem said settlers are often "escorted by Israeli squads and they do nothing to stop the attackers."

Eisenkot likely needs Arab parties' votes in the 120-seat parliament, or Israel faces another election next year.
