New Delhi — India's monsoon ended 12.6% below the long-term average.

It is the weakest in more than a decade, raising food-price risks in the world's most populous nation.

A powerful El Niño suppressed the June-to-September rains, threatening crops and draining reservoirs.

Crop output and reservoir levels will show how far the shortfall travels into [India](country:IN)'s food prices.
