Dar es Salaam — Tundu Lissu has already spent 19 months in jail.

Dropping the treason charge removes the death penalty but not President Samia Suluhu Hassan's main challenger.

The judge ruled his statements were meant to disrupt elections and instigate violence, and sentenced him to 4 years.

"Excuses were created to imprison me," Lissu, the Chadema party chairman, told the court.

Lissu can appeal to [Tanzania](country:TZ)'s highest court.
