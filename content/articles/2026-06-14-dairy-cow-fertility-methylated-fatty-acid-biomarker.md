London — Cow blood lipids predict 30-day embryo failure.

Blood fatty acid ratios at 30 days post-insemination predicted embryo loss by 45 days before visible symptoms appeared, the study found.

A scalable blood marker could replace guesswork breeding; the study used 36 cows and calls for wider validation.
