HIMARS struck Kharg Island from UAE bases, Iran says. Al Dhafra Air Base near Abu Dhabi hosts 5,000 US troops under a decades-old basing deal not designed for direct US-Iran war. Iran's military threatened to target US launch sites inside UAE cities, putting 9.3 million expatriates in the crossfire.
