New Delhi — 36 million farmer helpline queries tracked pest migration across [India](country:IN) without new sensors.

Researchers mined 14 years of the Kisan Call Centre to map seasonal outbreaks by district without new hardware.

AgriTrack also forecasts pest information demand, a low-cost surveillance model its authors say other nations can replicate.
