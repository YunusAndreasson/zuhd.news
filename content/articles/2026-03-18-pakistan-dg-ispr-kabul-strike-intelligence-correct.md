Pakistan's Kabul strike killed 143 people at a rehabilitation centre. The military denied it, calling the target an ammunition depot — one of 81 Afghan locations struck under Operation Ghazab lil-Haq, the UN confirmed. Afghan Taliban forces have struck 53 Pakistani locations in retaliation, leaving both nations in active military exchange.
