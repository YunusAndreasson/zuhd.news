Nigeria — JPMorgan, in Lagos since 1961, clears first licensing step.

The bank, now a representative office, plans wholesale banking, capital markets and advisory services in [Nigeria](country:NG).

The central bank's approval is only phase one, and JPMorgan must still incorporate and win operational approvals.

JPMorgan hopes to hold the licence by year-end or early 2027, people familiar with the deal say.
