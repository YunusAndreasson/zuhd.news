Nobel laureate Muhammad Yunus stepped down as interim leader. His replacement, Bangladesh National Party chairman Tarique Rahman, takes office after winning 209 of 297 parliamentary seats. Yunus urged the country to safeguard democracy and free speech in his farewell broadcast.
