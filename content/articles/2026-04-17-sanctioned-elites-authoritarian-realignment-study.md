Washington — Targeted sanctions accelerate authoritarian realignment, a study finds. Sanctioned officials forge deeper ties with Russia, China, and Gulf autocracies rather than becoming isolated, new political science research shows. The finding challenges the logic behind Iran-war sanctions and Washington's consecutive Russian oil wind-downs.
