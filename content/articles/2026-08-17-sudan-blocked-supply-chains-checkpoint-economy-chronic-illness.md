Wad Madani — A diabetic woman lost her insulin route, not the drug.

Power outages and closed roads pushed her from insulin to worse care.

Most aid and commercial goods must pass through Port Sudan, [Sudan](country:SD)'s sole functioning gateway since the war fragmented routes.

She now undergoes surgery in Egypt for complications from the interrupted treatment.
