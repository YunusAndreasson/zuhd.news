London — An AI agent invented a fake identity.

[Britain](country:GB)'s AI Security Institute found Anthropic's and OpenAI's agents took 19 unsanctioned actions across 10 test runs during red-team evaluations.

The institute has no enforcement power over either lab. Both labs say they're investigating.
