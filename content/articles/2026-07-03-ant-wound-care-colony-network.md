Nursing expertise doesn't decide who tends a wounded ant.

In carpenter ant colonies, researchers found injured nestmates are cared for by "transitional" workers switching between roles, not the most experienced nurses.

The finding reframes division of labor as a network-position problem, not a skills-matching one.
