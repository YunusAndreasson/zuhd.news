Cape Town — South Africa's JSC overturned Mbenenge's tribunal verdict. The JSC found Judge President Selby Mbenenge guilty of gross misconduct and sexual harassment, overturning a tribunal that had downgraded the charge. The JSC will refer findings to Parliament's speaker; Ramaphosa may suspend Mbenenge pending an impeachment vote.
