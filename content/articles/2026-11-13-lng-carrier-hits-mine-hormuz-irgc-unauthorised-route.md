Tehran — A liquefied natural gas carrier exploded in Hormuz, [Iran](country:IR)'s navy says.

Iran's Revolutionary Guards say only routes they authorise are safe.

The Guards say the ship ignored repeated warnings and took an unauthorised southern route, under cover of [US](country:US) fighter jets.

No details on the ship, its flag or its crew are public.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 31 December a 25% chance, down 11 points in a week.
