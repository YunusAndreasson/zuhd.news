Tokyo — No Cas12m protein had ever cut DNA until now.

Smaller editors are easier to package and deliver into cells.

Cryo-electron microscopy showed active and inactive forms look near-identical, so researchers swapped amino acids one by one to find the difference.

Morinaga's team at the University of Tokyo next aims to raise the cutting efficiency and precision of the active proteins.
