New Delhi — YOLOv5 now catches illegal crop fires automatically. Stubble burning is illegal but unenforceable: fires span millions of hectares at night across northern India, ground inspection cannot scale. Deployment needs satellite or drone integration; state pollution board adoption determines whether the gap closes.
