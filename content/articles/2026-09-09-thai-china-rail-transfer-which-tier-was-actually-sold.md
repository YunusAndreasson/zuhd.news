Bangkok — One technician's four months taught only basic maintenance.

Fewer than half his classmates found jobs in rail transport.

Thailand's 2014 deal with [China](country:CN) promised technology transfer, but courses taught only operating and maintenance skills, not design.

The Bangkok-Kunming line, still under construction, is not due to open until 2030.
