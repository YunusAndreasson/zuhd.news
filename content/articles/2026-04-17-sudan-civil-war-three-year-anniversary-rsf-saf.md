Khartoum — 2025 was Sudan's deadliest war year yet. UAE backs the RSF while Egypt and Turkey back the SAF — all three proxy patrons rejected the April 15 Berlin ceasefire conference. 31 million people — two-thirds of Sudan — need humanitarian aid, and no ceasefire framework commands all factions' agreement.
