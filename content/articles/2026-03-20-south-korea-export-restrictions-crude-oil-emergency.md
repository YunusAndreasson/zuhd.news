South Korea declared a crude oil supply emergency Friday. The Strait of Hormuz, sealed since the Iran war began, carries 70% of South Korea's crude imports, leaving it with 208 days of reserves at reduced consumption. Vice Industry Minister Moon Shin-hak warned the government may order refiners to divert exports to domestic supply if the strait stays closed.
