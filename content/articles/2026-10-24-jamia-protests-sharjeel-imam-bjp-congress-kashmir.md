New Delhi — Sharjeel Imam has been in custody since August 2020.

Jamia Millia Islamia students have protested since 12 October for a union election, last held 2005.

Bharatiya Janata Party MP Ravi Shankar Prasad linked Congress MP Imran Masood's campus visit to Kashmir graffiti and demanded Congress state its position.

The proctor's office will review disciplinary action against students who protested from 12 to 21 October.
