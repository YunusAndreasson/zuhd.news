Kiruna — Ten months of silence broke with a fuel-loaded countdown rehearsal.

ArianeGroup ran Themis, Europe's reusable booster demonstrator, through full tanking and abort checks at Esrange without ignition — the step before any hop flight.

No date is set for the next test; the delay measures how far Europe trails the launch cadence one American firm now sets.
