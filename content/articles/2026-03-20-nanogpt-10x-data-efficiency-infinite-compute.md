An 18B-parameter ensemble matched standard LLMs on 100M tokens. The technique, chain distillation, trains each model to learn from its predecessor, extracting new signal from the same data in every pass. Q Labs says the approach transfers to larger scales, which would let data-scarce languages and small labs train competitive models.
