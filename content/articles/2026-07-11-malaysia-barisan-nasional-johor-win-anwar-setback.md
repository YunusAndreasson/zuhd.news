Johor Bahru — Barisan Nasional won the Johor state assembly.

The party [Malaysia](country:MY) ousted in 2018 over the 1MDB scandal is rebuilding regionally, a setback for PM Anwar's reform coalition.

Johor's result serves as an early barometer ahead of national polls testing Anwar's coalition.
