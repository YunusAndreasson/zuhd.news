New Delhi — Compressed natural gas jumped ₹3.89 to ₹86.98 a kg.

Auto-rickshaw and taxi drivers absorb the increase since fares are fixed.

Disrupted Hormuz shipping doubled global spot liquefied natural gas prices, which set [India](country:IN)'s city-gas input costs directly.

A Bharatiya Janata Party spokesperson forecasts prices will fall once US-Iran tensions ease, without saying when.
