Stay 1 km from banks, Iran's military warned Gulf residents. After airstrikes struck Bank Sepah in Tehran overnight, Iran's Khatam al-Anbiya command declared US and Israeli banks across the Gulf are now military targets. A successful strike on Gulf financial infrastructure would transform the US-Iran-Israel conflict into a regional war.
