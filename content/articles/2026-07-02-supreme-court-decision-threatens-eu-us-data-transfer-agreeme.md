Vienna — Schrems moves to sue, a third time.

Max Schrems' [Austria](country:AT)-based noyb group will challenge the legal basis letting [US](country:US) firms handle European data, following Schrems I and II.

A win would void the Data Privacy Framework, disrupting cloud and SaaS transfers industry-wide.
