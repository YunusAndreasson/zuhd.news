Melbourne — Electric cars hit 23.4% of June sales, more than double last year.

[Australia](country:AU) sold more electric vehicles in six months than all of 2025, as buyers repriced a 15-year purchase against fuel costs they no longer expect to fall.

The fuel excise cut ends Sunday; diesel is already up 50 cents a litre this month.
