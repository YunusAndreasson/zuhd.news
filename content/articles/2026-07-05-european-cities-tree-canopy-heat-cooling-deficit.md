London — 4 in 5 homes lack cooling tree canopy.

An open-data mapping of 25 European cities found most workplaces sit too far from trees to meaningfully cool as heat waves intensify, an urban greening expert found.

The gap turns canopy into a street-level deficit cities can now measure and target directly.
