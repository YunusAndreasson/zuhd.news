London — Stiffer implants make bone waste away.

Researchers trained a neural network on 19 alloy features to predict titanium stiffness, letting engineers match implants to bone without lab-testing every mix.

The model explained 82% of stiffness variation, the study reports, ahead of trials in real implants.
