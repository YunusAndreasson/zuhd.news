Kenscoff — Gangs killed 164 people in 2 attacks this year.

Kenscoff's terraced farms supply Port-au-Prince markets, and many residents have fled to Pétion-Ville.

Gangs struck Kenscoff in [Haiti](country:HT) on 7–8 July and 23–24 August after two years of failed attempts to establish mountain bases.

The National Human Rights Defense Network says government apathy despite warnings contributed to the toll.

A unit of Salvadoran soldiers was ambushed on 19 October, and the situation remains tense.
