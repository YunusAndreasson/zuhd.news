Richmond — A 1971 candidate discount nearly reached parties too.

Broadcast ad space is fixed, so any discount to new buyers crowds out other advertisers.

The Fourth Circuit ruled 2-1 that only candidates, not parties or committees, qualify for the lowest ad rate.

The FCC can seek a full-court rehearing or ask the Supreme Court to review it.
