Yangon — 100,114 killed since Myanmar's 2021 coup, monitor says.

[Myanmar](country:MM)'s junta now fights over 1,200 armed groups, ACLED says — the world's most fragmented war, and Asia's deadliest.

Min Aung Hlaing's call for peace talks was rejected by rebels as an image-laundering ploy, with no ceasefire set.
