Amsterdam — [Netherlands](country:NL) police dismantled a 17-million-device proxy botnet.

Reportedly linked to [Russia](country:RU)-based ASOCKS, the botnet rented hijacked consumer devices as anonymous proxies to mask criminal traffic.

How 17 million devices were enrolled remains unclear; operators behind the network have not been publicly identified.
