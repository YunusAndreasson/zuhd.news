Kraków — A gunman killed 6 people in the Main Square.

15 million tourists a year cross that square, in a [Poland](country:PL) where mass shootings are rare.

He drove in and fired at random from his car, wearing military-style fatigues and carrying a military-type long gun, police said.

Police gave conflicting accounts on accomplices: one spokesperson said he acted alone, another said officers assumed he did not.

Police have given no motive, and Prime Minister Donald Tusk was due to address the country Saturday.
