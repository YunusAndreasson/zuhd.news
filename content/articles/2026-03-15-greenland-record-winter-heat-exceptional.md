Northern Greenland ran 14°C above normal this January. It was Greenland's warmest January in recorded history, on an ice sheet holding enough water to raise global seas by 7 meters. The warmth kept Nuuk's ski slopes closed all winter and is accelerating Greenland's contribution to sea-level rise.
