Chiang Rai — Hundreds marched six days along the Kok River.

Farming and fishing communities drink water contaminated by mines they cannot inspect or petition.

Arsenic and lead from mines in Myanmar's Shan State exceed safety levels, but no Thai regulator can reach the source.

Villagers are pressing [Thailand](country:TH)'s government to open cross-border monitoring with Myanmar.
