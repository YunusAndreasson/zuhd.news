Niamey — Niger's junta cancelled all French military agreements.

[Niger](country:NE) hosted French forces under pre-independence agreements; the 2023 junta terminated all 4 at once, a break no elected government had made.

Chad and Senegal retain French military agreements; [France](country:FR) has not announced a revised Sahel posture.
