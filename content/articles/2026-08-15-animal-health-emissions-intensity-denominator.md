London — A cow's miscarriage wastes an entire pregnancy's emissions.

Emissions are counted per litre of milk, hiding a sick cow's waste.

Veterinary care lifts milk output without changing methane output, cutting emissions per litre most in poorer herds.

The study does not say whether herd growth could erase these gains.
