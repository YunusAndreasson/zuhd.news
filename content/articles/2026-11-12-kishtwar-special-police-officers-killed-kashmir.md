Kishtwar — 2 Special Police Officers were found shot dead, their weapons missing.

Police say it is the third attack in Jammu and Kashmir since July.

The 2 Special Operations Group members vanished Wednesday in Dachhan forest, a zone with no mobile coverage, after gunshots were heard.

Sources in Delhi say investigators are examining a Lashkar-e-Taiba module and questioning 3 suspected overground workers.

Searches continue in Kishtwar and Kulgam, where gunmen wounded 3 paramilitary troops Wednesday.
