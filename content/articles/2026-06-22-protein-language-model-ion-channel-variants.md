London — MissION classifies ion channel variants with 91.8% discriminative accuracy.

Trained on 1,996 functional variants, the classifier uses transformer architecture to predict channelopathy risk without wet-lab electrophysiology.

Clinical uptake requires validation in diagnostic settings; a wrong prediction can shape a diagnosis with no lab alternative.
