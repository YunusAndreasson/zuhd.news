Cairo — Warraq Island residents asked to keep living on Warraq.

Nine years into a state redevelopment plan, they demand land instead of removal.

Egypt's government has pushed to clear the Nile island for foreign investment since 2017, tightening security and construction restrictions.

Housing officials have not responded to Warraq residents' formal demands for a rehousing timeline.
