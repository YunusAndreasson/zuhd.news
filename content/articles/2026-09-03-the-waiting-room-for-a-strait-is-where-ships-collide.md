The captain and nine Turkish crew remain unaccounted for in 900-metre water.

The Marmara is the busy approach to [Turkey](country:TR)'s Bosphorus, one of the world's tightest shipping straits.

Search teams have found an empty lifeboat; no survivors have been located.
