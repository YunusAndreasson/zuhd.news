Antalya — A 2,000-year-old Roman binder still outlasts modern cement here.

It gives conservators a low-carbon binder that heritage-site rules already permit.

Researchers rebuilt [Turkey](country:TR)'s harbor-city mortar using crushed recycled brick, whose fired clay reacts with lime.

Compressive strength ranged 2.55-12.36 MPa across mixes, guiding which ratio scales next.
