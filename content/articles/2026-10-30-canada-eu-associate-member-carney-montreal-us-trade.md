Montreal — [Canada](country:CA)'s associate EU membership is absent from the joint declaration.

The term has no legal meaning, and [Germany](country:DE) and other member states oppose it.

The text instead pledges closer association, a feasibility study on Canadian liquefied natural gas for Europe, and work on undersea cables until next year's summit.

President Donald Trump said he might halt trade with Europe in many areas if he sees the move as hostile.

Canada's Parliament must vote on any upgrade, and the sides meet again next year to define it.
