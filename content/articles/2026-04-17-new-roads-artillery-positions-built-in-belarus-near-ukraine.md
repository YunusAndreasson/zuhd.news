Minsk — Artillery emplacements are rising along Belarus's Ukrainian border. Belarus was Russia's 2022 invasion launch pad; Oreshnik missiles arrived in December and satellite imagery shows accelerating border construction. Zelensky shared the Oreshnik's location with Western allies; no government has stated what triggers a response.
