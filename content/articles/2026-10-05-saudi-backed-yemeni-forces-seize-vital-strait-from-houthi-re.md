Dhubab — Yemeni troops say they cut Houthi roads to Bab el-Mandeb.

The Houthis declared a blockade on [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s Red Sea ports in July.

Brigades under Saudi-led air cover seized positions, the army said, cutting Houthi supply lines and roads to the strait.

Houthi-aligned media deny the advance, and no independent party has verified either account.

[Pakistan](country:PK) and [Turkey](country:TR) join Saudi ministers in Riyadh to weigh political talks with the Houthis instead.
