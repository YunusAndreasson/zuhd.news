Simferopol — Moped engines fool Crimea's drone detectors.

Russian-installed governor Aksyonov banned motorbikes 8pm–6am after advisors said moped engines mask acoustic signatures air-defense crews use to detect incoming drones.

[Ukraine](country:UA) has intensified drone strikes on Crimea as the territory faces fuel rationing capped at 20 litres per car.
