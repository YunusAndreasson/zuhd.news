Dozens of nations face new US trade investigations. Section 301 of the 1974 Trade Act grants the president unilateral tariff power, bypassing Congress and courts that struck down previous duties. A US-China treasury meeting is set for March 15 in France, after Beijing called the probes "political manipulation."
