Dzaleka — The World Food Programme cut refugee rations from $100 to $7 monthly.

[Malawi](country:MW)'s Dzaleka holds 52,000 people at 4 times its limit; 5 cyclones since 2019 have kept the agency in permanent crisis mode as donor funding contracts.

The agency warned Dzaleka families may lose all support by end of June without emergency funding.
