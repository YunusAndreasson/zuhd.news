Washington — An unpublished Pentagon estimate puts the Iran war above $47 billion.

The Pentagon has not shared the figure publicly a week before the 3 November midterms.

More than $29 billion would replace spent munitions and over $6 billion would replace aircraft and drones lost.

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth's July estimate was $37.5 billion, and neither figure clearly counts repairs to [US](country:US) bases hit by Iranian missiles.

Anonymous officials say the Pentagon is planning to resume heavy fighting if ordered.
