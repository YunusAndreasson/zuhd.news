Structure Therapeutics' obesity pill hit 16% weight loss in trials. Injectable GLP-1 drugs cost over $1,000 monthly, and Eli Lilly's competing oral pill achieved only 11% over 72 weeks. Structure Therapeutics plans to advance aleniglipron to Phase 3, potentially reaching markets that lack cold storage for injectables.
