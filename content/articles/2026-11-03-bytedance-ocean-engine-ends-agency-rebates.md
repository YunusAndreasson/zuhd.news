Beijing — Thousands of Douyin ad agencies lose rebates in 2027.

Agencies used the rebate as sales commission, credit interest and a pricing tool.

From 1 January 2027, Ocean Engine replaces rebates with lower list prices and ranking-based incentives the platform can change yearly.

After Google ended its rebates in [China](country:CN), industry reports say many agencies closed or changed trade.
