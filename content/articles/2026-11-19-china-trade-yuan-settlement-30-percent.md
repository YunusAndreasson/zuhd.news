Shanghai — China settled nearly 30% of goods trade in yuan, its central bank says.

Yuan's share of China-[Russia](country:RU) trade hit 62% in June, up from 17%, Chinese Academy of Social Sciences data show.

Exporters paid in dollars lose when the yuan rises, and it has gained 4.6% this year against a 3.58% knitwear margin.

Hu Jie, a professor at the Shanghai Advanced Institute of Finance, says the yuan is not fully convertible, which caps its share.

Beijing's 2026 treasury bond supply is 84 billion yuan, up 24%, giving foreign holders more to buy.
