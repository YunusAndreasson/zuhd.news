Rome — Classes may hold at most 30% non-Italian speakers.

[Italy](country:IT)'s decree also bans burqas and niqabs in schools and fines parents who skip Italian lessons.

Pupils above the cap move to other schools, though the decree does not say where.

Education Minister Giuseppe Valditara says the cap hits about 1,000 classrooms, not the 34,000 with over 30% foreign pupils.

The decree lapses unless Parliament ratifies it within 60 days.
