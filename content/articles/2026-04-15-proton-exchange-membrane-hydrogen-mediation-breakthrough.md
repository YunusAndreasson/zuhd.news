London — Hydrogen gas now carries protons through membranes cleanly. Ion-exchange membranes have always traded selectivity for conductivity; hydrogen-mediated transfer solves this by passing gas instead of ions, eliminating crossover. Researchers tested it in a flow battery and acid-base stack; fuel cells are next.
