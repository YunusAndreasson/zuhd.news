Lhasa — Warming intensifies with altitude above 5km.

Researchers combined station data with 12 ice cores to map this pattern on the poorly-monitored plateau that feeds the Indus and Ganges rivers.

Hundreds of millions across [Pakistan](country:PK), India and Central Asia depend on ice that the least-observed elevations are losing fastest.
