Tehran — Messages, not negotiations, Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says.

The distinction matters because nothing exchanged through intermediaries binds either side.

[Iran](country:IR) is talking to Oman only about a new maritime route around the Strait of Hormuz, not reopening it.

Araghchi says talks resume only once the [US](country:US) makes up for violating the Islamabad memorandum.
