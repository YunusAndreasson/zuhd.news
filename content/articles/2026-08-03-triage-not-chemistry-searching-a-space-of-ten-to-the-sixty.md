London — researchers screened 44 billion molecules for one target.

Machine learning triaged compounds to dock against Mac1, a protein that strips host immune tags; 12 of 17 analogues bound it.

The unedited manuscript calls this a chemical lead, not a drug; cell-level antiviral activity remains untested.
