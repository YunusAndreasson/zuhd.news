Liberia's top election official resigned under corruption allegations. Davidetta Browne-Lansanah, who oversaw the 2023 elections, stepped down a year before her term ended amid corruption allegations and internal disputes — in a country where electoral credibility underpins post-conflict stability. President Joseph Boakai has not named a replacement.
