Rasuwa — No rain fell before the wall of water hit.

At least eight dead and 384 missing, most of them foreign tourists and pilgrims.

Warming has stripped the permanent ice binding [Nepal](country:NP)'s slopes, so a rock-and-ice collapse into the Bhote Koshi produced a surge no rainfall gauge could have flagged.

[China](country:CN)'s Xi Jinping ordered searches on the Tibetan side, where the flood struck first.
