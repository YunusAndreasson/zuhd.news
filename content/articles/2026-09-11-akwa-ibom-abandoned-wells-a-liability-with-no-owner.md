Ikono — A wellhead capped in 1958 still leaks oil.

The spill feeds illegal refining and has fouled farmland for over 50 years, a resident said.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s regulators have never confirmed who must plug the well Shell drilled decades ago.

Community leaders plan to petition regulators for an assessment and cleanup, with no date set.
