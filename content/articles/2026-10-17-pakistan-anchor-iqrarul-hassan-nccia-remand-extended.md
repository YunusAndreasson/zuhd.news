Karachi — Iqrarul Hassan stays in cybercrime custody 2 more days.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s cyber agency can hold a broadcaster in physical custody over his social media posts.

The agency booked him under four sections of the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, including cyberterrorism and false information.

His lawyer argues the video breaks no cybercrime law and no complainant filed the case.

He returns to court on 19 October while forensic teams examine 1,145.89 gigabytes from his devices.
