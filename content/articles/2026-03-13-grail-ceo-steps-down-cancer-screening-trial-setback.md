Grail's multi-cancer blood test failed its clinical trial. The test — piloted by the UK's National Health Service — screens for more than 50 cancer types from a single blood draw; Grail's CEO resigned following the results. Billions in cancer blood testing investment and the NHS rollout are now in doubt.
