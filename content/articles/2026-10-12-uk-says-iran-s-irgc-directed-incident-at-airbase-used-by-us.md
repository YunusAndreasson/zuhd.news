London — All 7 men arrested over the RAF Fairford plot have been released.

Prime Minister Andy Burnham's assessment, not a court finding, ties [Iran](country:IR)'s Revolutionary Guards to the base.

Detectives found petrol but no viable explosive in vans seized with 5 of the men.

Iran's ambassador, Seyed Ali Mousavi, said Tehran had no involvement and asked [Britain](country:GB) to present evidence.

Britain sanctioned 2 Iranian men Monday and promised further sanctions on Iran and the Guards.
