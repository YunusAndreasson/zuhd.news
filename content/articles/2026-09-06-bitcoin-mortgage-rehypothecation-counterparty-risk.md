New York — Better can re-lend your pledged bitcoin.

Failure turns a bitcoin pledge into an unsecured claim, as at Celsius and FTX.

Rehypothecation moves pledged bitcoin off the borrower's custody into the lender's own claims, not a vault.

Price drops trigger no margin call, and the bitcoin sells only on missed payments.
