Bogotá — Cactus fungi boost drought survival in cacao plants. Researchers inoculated seedlings with fungi from desert cacti—evolved over millions of years to survive extreme water stress—significantly improving survival rates. Cacao regions in Colombia and West Africa face worsening drought; field trials have not been announced.
