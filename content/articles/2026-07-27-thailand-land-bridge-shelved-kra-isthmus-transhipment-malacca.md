Bangkok — A spreadsheet killed a four-century strategic dream.

A feasibility study found [Thailand](country:TH)'s Kra Isthmus rail corridor can't beat two to three days of steaming through the Strait of Malacca.

The project is shelved "for now"; the strait's power rests on there being no viable bypass.
