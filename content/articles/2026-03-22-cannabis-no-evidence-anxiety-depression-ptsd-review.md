Sydney — Cannabis is no mental health treatment, a Lancet review found. The study found no evidence the drug treats anxiety, depression, or PTSD, despite 27% of North Americans using it medically — half citing mental health conditions. Researchers called for stricter oversight as governments expand legalization on the same unproven claims.
