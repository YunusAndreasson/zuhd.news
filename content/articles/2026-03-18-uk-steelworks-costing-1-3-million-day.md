Britain spends £1.3m daily to keep Scunthorpe steel running. The government has paid £377m since April 2025, classified as a loan — but the National Audit Office says British Steel has no path to repay it. If spending continues at current rates, the total could exceed £1.5 billion by 2028 with no buyer yet identified.
