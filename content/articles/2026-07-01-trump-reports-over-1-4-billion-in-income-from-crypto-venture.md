Washington — Trump reported $1.4 billion in crypto income.

An ethics filing showed nearly $800 million came from World Liberty Financial, the family's venture — a market his own administration regulates.

Don Fox, a former acting ethics chief, said presidents face no conflict-of-interest bar, and Trump "makes the case" for reform.
