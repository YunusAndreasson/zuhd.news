Seoul — Lee wants tools that block cyberattacks before they land.

President Lee Jae Myung says a series of leaks at financial and public institutions appears to involve AI.

He asked ministries to inspect national core infrastructure security and develop AI tools that block attacks pre-emptively.

His remarks set no budget or deadline for the tools.

Lee asked authorities to identify how the leaks happened and to contain the damage.
