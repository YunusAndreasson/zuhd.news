Hong Kong — Hong Kong performed its first incision-free Parkinson's treatment. Tuen Mun Hospital aimed MRI-guided ultrasound beams through the intact skull to destroy the neurons driving uncontrollable tremors — no incision required. The hospital plans to treat 40 patients within 2 years to determine whether the procedure is ready for wider clinical use.
