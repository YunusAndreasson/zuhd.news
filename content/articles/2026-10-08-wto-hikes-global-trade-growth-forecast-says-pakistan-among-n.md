Geneva — The World Trade Organisation doubled its 2026 goods-trade forecast.

AI-enabling goods made up 47% of merchandise trade growth in value, the organisation said.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s sea freight exports rose 73% in the first half as its operators absorbed traffic diverted from Hormuz.

Mediterranean Shipping Company added a $500 per container congestion surcharge on Northern Europe to South Asia cargo in July.

The organisation ties its 4.1% forecast for 2027 to a timely end of the Middle East conflict.
