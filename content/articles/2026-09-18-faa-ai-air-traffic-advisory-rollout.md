Washington — The Federal Aviation Administration deploys an $875 million AI advisor Monday.

Controllers who nominally stay in charge face the automation-bias risk of trusting a machine's default.

The system only proposes routes rather than issuing commands, a setup that research says erodes vigilance over time.

The Washington rollout is step one toward covering 29 million square miles of US airspace.
