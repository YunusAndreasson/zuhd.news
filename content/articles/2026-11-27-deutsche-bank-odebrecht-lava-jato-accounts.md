São Paulo — Deutsche Bank kept serving Odebrecht, a client since 2011, after weighing the risks.

The finding rests on 15 million confidential files from the Inside Deutsche Bank project.

Internal emails show executives scrutinised Odebrecht and the Panamanian banks it served as correspondent, then kept the relationships.

It did so even after the US Justice Department found laundering schemes financing corruption.

The excerpt carries no Deutsche Bank response and no regulator action.
