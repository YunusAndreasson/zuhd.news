Baghdad — Iran offers to reopen Hormuz within 6 days.

Before the war the strait carried about a fifth of global oil and liquefied natural gas trade.

Foreign Minister Abbas Araqchi's conditions include lifting the [US](country:US) blockade of Iranian ports, oil sanctions and hostilities on all fronts.

Iran's Revolutionary Guard Navy said Monday that another tanker struck a mine in the southern strait and caught fire.

Polymarket traders give an Iran-Oman Hormuz agreement by 31 October 6%, down 8 points in a week.
