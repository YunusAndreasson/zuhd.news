Washington — Out-of-network providers are winning $34,000 for a spine surgery that pays $1,400 in-network.

The No Surprises Act arbitration has no cap on awards; providers win over 80% of disputes, and private-equity staffing firms file the bulk of claims.

Award costs flow to insurer premiums; Congress has not moved to cap arbitration outcomes.
