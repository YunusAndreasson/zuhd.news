Brampton — Two officers allegedly asked why he was tracking police cars.

Brampton spent C$2 million on plate-recognition cameras, yet police balked when one watched them.

Anthony Sistilli built a plate-reading camera a month ago, aimed it at police cars, and posted how-to videos on TikTok.

Sistilli says an officer told him such data "could put them personally at risk."

Peel Regional Police had not responded to a request for comment by publication.
