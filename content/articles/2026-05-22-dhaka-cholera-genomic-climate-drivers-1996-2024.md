Dhaka — The 2022 surge was climate-predictable, genome study shows.

28-year Dhaka genome data links V. cholerae lineage flux and monsoon shifts to outbreak timing, the study found.

The finding gives [Bangladesh](country:BD) a climate-calibrated framework for anticipating surge years before each monsoon.
