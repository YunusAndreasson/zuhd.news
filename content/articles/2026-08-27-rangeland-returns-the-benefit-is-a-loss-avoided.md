Ulaanbaatar — Every $1 in rangeland restoration returns up to $6, researchers say.

But those returns are mostly losses avoided by herders, not cash investors can claim.

Researchers at [Mongolia](country:MN)'s UN desertification summit say the gains come as forage and soil that survive drought, not new revenue.

Who finances restoration, and who ends up holding the land, remains unsettled.
