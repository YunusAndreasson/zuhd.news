Brussels — 21 euro-area governments have until Friday to nominate Lagarde's successor.

The winner inherits a 2.5% deposit rate as of October and renewed inflation.

Leaders appoint the president, and a candidate needs 16 countries representing 65% of the euro area's population.

Pablo Hernández de Cos and Klaas Knot are the frontrunners, each backed by their government.

Lagarde leaves on 7 February, and EU leaders are expected to decide before year's end.
