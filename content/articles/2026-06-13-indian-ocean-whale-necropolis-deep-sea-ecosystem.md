London — 476 fossil whales carpet the Diamantina Zone.

The site's V-shaped topography funnels carcasses 6,700 metres deep; researchers found communities of 2,840 organisms per square metre, most potentially new to science.

The survey extended the known depth range of whale-fall ecosystems by 2,500 metres; deeper zones remain unmapped.
