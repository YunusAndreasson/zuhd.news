Peshawar — Hayatabad Medical Complex has run out of medicine.

Patients across the tribal districts must now buy drugs privately or go without.

The finance director blocked vendor payments, the hospital director wrote, since [Pakistan](country:PK)'s public hospitals run on supplier credit.

An anti-corruption inquiry into the hospital's appointments and finances continues, prosecutors said.
