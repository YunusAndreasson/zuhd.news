Port Moresby — [Papua New Guinea](country:PG)'s Highlands runs a hiremen market.

Patronage networks fund the militias — local patrons and elected officials contract protection; militias deliver votes, closing the cycle.

The gunmen are symptomatic; no announced policy in Papua New Guinea targets the patronage networks that commission and fund them.
