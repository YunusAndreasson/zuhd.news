Washington — A three-star general failed 3 polygraphs, the Pentagon says.

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth is polygraphing senior officials in leak hunts as the [Iran](country:IR) war enters its ninth month.

Lt. Gen. Brett Sylvia directed strategy on the Joint Staff, and officials offered no evidence beyond the polygraph results.

Pentagon spokesman Sean Parnell said the case went to the Justice Department for possible legal action.
