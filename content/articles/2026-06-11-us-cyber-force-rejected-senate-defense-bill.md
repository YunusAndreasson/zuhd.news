Washington — A 14-13 vote killed the Cyber Force amendment.

Senator Kirsten Gillibrand's proposal to create a 6th [US](country:US) military branch for cyber operations lost by 1 vote in the closed Senate Armed Services Committee.

The $1.2 trillion defense bill proceeds to the full Senate; Gillibrand may refile on the floor.
