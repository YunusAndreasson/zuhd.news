Singapore — Gold fell to a 7-week low near $4,125 an ounce.

Gold-saving households across [India](country:IN) and [Pakistan](country:PK) see their savings shrink as the metal slides.

Saxo Bank's Ole Hansen said [US](country:US) 10-year real yields hit an 18-year high near 2.85% as war-driven oil feeds inflation fears.

"Investors who had turned to gold will see a hit," said Sherif Othman, CEO of Poise Investment Advisors.

CME data show a 70% chance of an October Fed hike, pending this week's jobs and inflation figures.
