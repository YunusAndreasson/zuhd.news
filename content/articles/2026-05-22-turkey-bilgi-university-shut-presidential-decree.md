Istanbul — Erdogan closed Bilgi University by decree mid-academic-year.

The 20,000-student school was seized last year in a fraud probe; the closure invokes an "insufficient education" statute, effective immediately.

Students mid-exams transfer to Mimar Sinan University; founders face separate criminal fraud charges.
