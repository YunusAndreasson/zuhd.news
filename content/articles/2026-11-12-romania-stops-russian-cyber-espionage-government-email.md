Bucharest — Russian state-linked hackers targeted Romania's NATO and EU emails.

[Romania](country:RO)'s intelligence service says it stopped the attack and found no sensitive data stolen.

ESET says the previously unknown Cereal toolkit let the group monitor and collect messages on compromised Microsoft Exchange servers.

The agency did not name the targeted institution or say how the hackers first got in.

Romanian authorities are investigating other potentially related cyber operations linked to Russia, the agency says.
