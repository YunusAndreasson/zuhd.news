Doha — [Iran](country:IR) would reopen Hormuz within 2 days of a deal, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi demands recognition of a route discussed with [Oman](country:OM), and closure of all others.

Ship traffic through the strait averaged 3.3 a day as of 25 October, 19% below the normal 4.

Araghchi says reopening on Iran's terms would restart talks with Washington on outstanding issues.
