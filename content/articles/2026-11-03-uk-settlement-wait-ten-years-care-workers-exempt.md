London — 517,000 migrants now face a longer wait to settle.

Most workers already in the [UK](country:GB) must wait 10 years, not 5, for permanent residency.

Earnings set the clock: above £50,000 the wait stays at 5 years, above £125,000 it falls to 3.

Care workers who arrived before May 2025 and earn at least £25,000 keep the 5-year path.

Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood has not costed the exemption and will publish figures when she puts the rules before Parliament.
