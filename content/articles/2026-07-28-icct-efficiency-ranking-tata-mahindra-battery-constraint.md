Mumbai — Tata and Mahindra top a global battery-efficiency ranking.

The International Council on Clean Transportation ranked both ahead of Tesla and BYD on kilometres per kilowatt-hour, though behind on range and charging speed.

Smaller batteries mean less lithium and lower prices, which is what decides whether EVs reach Lagos or Dhaka at all.
