Maranello — Ferrari unveiled the Luce, a €550,000 electric 5-seater.

The Luce extends Ferrari's lineup without chasing volume as the EU's 2035 combustion ban approaches.

Ferrari said 20% of its lineup will be fully electric by 2030; the Luce is [Italy](country:IT)'s first combustion-free Ferrari.
