Washington — 425,093 children face [US](country:US) deportation without a lawyer.

Immigration courts provide no right to appointed counsel; children argue their cases against government lawyers, Vera Institute data show.

64% of children's completed immigration cases last year had no legal representation, Justice Department data show.
