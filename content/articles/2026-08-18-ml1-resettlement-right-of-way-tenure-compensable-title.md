Karachi — Up to 15,752 people risk eviction along the ML-1 rail corridor.

Homes, schools and mosques disappear so trains can run faster between three cities.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s 1894 land law compensates only proven ownership, and undocumented residents hold none.

Railways has not named resettlement sites or a compensation timeline before January's start.
