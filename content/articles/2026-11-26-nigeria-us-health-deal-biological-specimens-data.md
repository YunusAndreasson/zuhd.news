Abuja — [Nigeria](country:NG) must start shipping pathogen samples to the [US](country:US) within 5 days of a request.

Washington may pass them to American firms, and keeps using them even if Nigeria terminates the deal.

The US plans $2.08 billion through 2030 while Nigeria adds about $3 billion, and American aid falls each year.

Health minister Muhammad Pate says Nigeria's contribution stays in the country and is measured by actual spending.

The separate health-data agreement is unpublished, so no one can say which Nigerian records the US receives.
