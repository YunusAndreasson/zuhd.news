Protesters hurled petrol bombs at Albania's prime minister's office. Thousands marched in Tirana demanding Prime Minister Edi Rama resign over the indictment of his deputy in a corruption scandal. Rama's government aims to join the European Union by 2030 amid multiple corruption probes.
