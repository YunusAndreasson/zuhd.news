Shanghai — Intel says one Xeon chip supports over 1,000 AI agents.

Intel courts [China](country:CN)'s AI buildout at the import expo, running Doubao and Baidu agents on its PCs.

The Clearwater Forest chip, Intel's first data-centre processor on its 18A process, packs 288 efficiency cores and 576MB of cache.

Intel vice president Sarah Kemp speaks at the Hongqiao International Economic Forum on 6 November.
