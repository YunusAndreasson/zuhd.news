Lusaka — Hakainde Hichilema won a second term with 60% of the vote.

Cost-of-living anger nearly dented a win, exposing the limits of debt-repair politics.

[Zambia](country:ZM) spent his first term inside a G20 debt restructuring, whose relief lands on growth long before it reaches fuel prices.

Opposition leader Brian Mundubile is weighing a legal challenge to the results.
