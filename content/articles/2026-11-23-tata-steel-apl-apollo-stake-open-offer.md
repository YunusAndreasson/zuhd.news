Mumbai — APL Apollo shares fell more than 15% Monday.

Tata Steel's planned open offer for another 26% could give it control.

It pays up to ₹15,510 crore ($1.7 billion) to promoter S Gupta Holding for 26.4% of the tube maker.

Chola Securities says APL Apollo's 7%-8% margins trail Tata Steel's 23%-24% in India and the deal adds debt.

Tata Steel's long-term borrowings already stood at ₹1.02 lakh crore in September.
