Aden — The government says it launched an offensive on Houthis, Russian state agency TASS reported.

TASS says Houthis seized Mokha and the Bab al-Mandeb coast on 10-11 September.

Presidential Leadership Council chairman Rashad al-Alimi ordered the army forward and promised amnesty to Houthi fighters who disarm.

[Iran](country:IR)'s state-affiliated Mehr relayed a Houthi claim of dozens of Saudi-backed fighters killed or wounded in al-Jawf.

Neither account is independently confirmed, and al-Alimi told his government to draft a restoration plan for retaken areas.
