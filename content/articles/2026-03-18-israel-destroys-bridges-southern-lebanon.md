Israeli strikes killed 12 in Beirut's city centre Wednesday. Israel also destroyed 2 Litani River crossings in southern Lebanon — the same opening move it made before ground forces entered Gaza in 2024. The Beirut strikes were the first Israeli attacks beyond Lebanon's southern suburbs, following the pattern that preceded the Gaza ground invasion.
