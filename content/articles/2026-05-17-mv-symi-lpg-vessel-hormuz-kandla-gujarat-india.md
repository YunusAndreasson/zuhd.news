Kandla — 20,000 tonnes of Qatari liquefied petroleum gas reached Gujarat.

The strait carries one-fifth of global energy and has been disrupted since [US](country:US) and [Israel](country:IL) strikes on [Iran](country:IR) in February.

13 [India](country:IN)-flagged vessels have crossed since March; one was attacked off [Oman](country:OM) on May 13.
