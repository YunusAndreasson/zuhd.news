The European Union has finalized its comprehensive AI regulation framework, setting global precedent for governing artificial intelligence systems.

## Why it matters

This is the first binding international framework that classifies AI systems by risk level. It will affect every technology company operating in Europe and likely influence regulation worldwide.

## The details

- The framework bans real-time biometric surveillance in public spaces
- High-risk AI systems must undergo conformity assessments before deployment
- Companies face fines of up to 7% of global revenue for violations
- The regulation takes effect in phases over the next 24 months

## What's next

Member states have six months to establish national AI oversight bodies. The United States and United Kingdom are expected to reference the EU framework in their own pending legislation.
