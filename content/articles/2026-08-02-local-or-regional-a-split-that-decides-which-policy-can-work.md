Tehran — 90% of Tehran's ozone comes from elsewhere.

A mayor's traffic or diesel restrictions only target the local share; researchers found most of [Iran](country:IR)'s ozone burden blows in regionally.

Researchers say the local share peaked at 25% in June and July but has been shrinking since 2019.
