Abuja — Ovie Omo-Agege vowed to kill anyone who rigs 2027's vote.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s aviation minister Festus Keyamo alleges politicians are arming thugs ahead of that election.

Keyamo says the politicians sent their own children abroad and that law enforcement may lawfully neutralise armed attackers.

Keyamo named no politicians in his post on X.

Omo-Agege is seeking the Delta Central District seat under the Nigeria Democratic Congress.
