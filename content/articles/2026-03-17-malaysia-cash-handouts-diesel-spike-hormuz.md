Malaysia disbursed RM100 to households as diesel hit RM3.92. The Hormuz shutdown drove diesel up 80 sen on March 12, the steepest single-day increase in years, even as Malaysia remains an oil producer. The government kept subsidized BUDI95 at RM1.99 per litre but has not said how long the cash transfers will continue.
