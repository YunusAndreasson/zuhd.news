Nabatieh — Israeli strikes destroyed Ghandour Hospital, killing seven.

17 hospitals have now been damaged across Lebanon since March, the health ministry says.

Nabatieh's district hospitals form the region's only referral tier, so losing one forces distant travel.

[Lebanon](country:LB)'s president has asked Washington to force an immediate halt to the strikes.
