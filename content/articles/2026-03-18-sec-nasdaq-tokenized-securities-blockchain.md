Nasdaq stocks will now settle on blockchain. The US Securities and Exchange Commission approved an opt-in system letting firms clear trades instantly, around the clock, through the Depository Trust Company — bypassing the standard days-long book-entry process. NYSE parent Intercontinental Exchange is building competing tokenized products, and Nasdaq has already signed crypto exchange Kraken as a global distributor.
