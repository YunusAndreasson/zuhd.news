Tokyo — Washington sold $500 million of euros to buy yen.

It was the [US](country:US)'s first yen-buying intervention since 1998.

The New York Fed bought 79.4 billion yen on 31 July from the Exchange Stabilization Fund, alongside Tokyo's record 15.4 trillion yen.

The yen hit 160.03 per dollar Friday, and [Japan](country:JP)'s finance minister promises to respond.
