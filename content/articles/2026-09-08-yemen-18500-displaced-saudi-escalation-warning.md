They're arriving in [Yemen](country:YE) districts whose own residents already depend on aid.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s coalition said 73 people were wounded in Houthi strikes on four cities, an escalation after years of truce.

Saudi Arabia's foreign minister warned it "will not hesitate" to strike back.
