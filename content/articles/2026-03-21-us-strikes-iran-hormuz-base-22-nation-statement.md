Strait of Hormuz — 22 nations backed Hormuz reopening; none sent a warship. US bombs destroyed Iran's anti-ship missile bunker at the strait's entrance, but Iran retains additional coastal batteries that can still target tankers. The US Navy escorts tankers alone, and any Iranian strike on a cleared vessel will force Washington to decide how far to escalate.
