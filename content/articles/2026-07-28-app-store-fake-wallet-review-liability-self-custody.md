San Jose — A second user lost $840,000 after the first reported the app.

A federal lawsuit says Apple left a fake bitcoin wallet impersonating Sparrow Wallet live for over a week after the first theft report — the review gap that undercuts its no-sideloading justification.

The suit tests whether that publisher defense, which won Apple past dismissals, survives repeat wallet-fraud claims.
