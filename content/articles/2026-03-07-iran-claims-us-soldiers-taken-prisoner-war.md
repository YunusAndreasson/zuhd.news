Iran says it holds US soldiers prisoner. Larijani, head of Iran's National Security Council, alleged the Pentagon lists captives as killed in action; Iran has leveraged prisoner seizures for negotiations since its 1979 embassy seizure. The Pentagon has not responded; if confirmed, the US faces a hostage crisis atop a shooting war.
