Bengaluru — An AI venture fund fields 2-3 monthly inquiries from US-based Indian researchers seeking to return home, its India-based founder said.

Visa friction and rising domestic salaries have cut [US](country:US) big-tech's pull, founders say; [India](country:IN)'s product ecosystem now offers comparable routes.

Silicon Valley's draw for India's engineers is weakening.
