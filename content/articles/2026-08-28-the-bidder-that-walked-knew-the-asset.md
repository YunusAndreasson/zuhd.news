Islamabad — K-Electric, [Pakistan](country:PK)'s only private utility, withdrew.

The exit signals what Fesco's buyers actually think the utility is worth.

A regulator fixes the tariff and losses from theft, so buyers inherit capped revenue and uncapped costs.

Ten prequalified bidders now enter due diligence, with Islamabad's Iesco sale opening September 7.
