Brasília — Flávio Bolsonaro's undeclared support pages carry 2.5 million followers.

Lula's digital strategist André Janones has an undeclared page of 1.6 million followers too.

At least 7 pages, each over 100,000 followers, link to his campaign, and some repost Flávio's official content minutes apart.

Electoral law experts say support accounts are legal, but limits apply and platform opacity hampers policing.

[Brazil](country:BR)'s Superior Electoral Court holds no declaration of these pages, and the runoff is days away.
