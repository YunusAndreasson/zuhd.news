Pakistan's security forces killed 9 near Karachi's US Consulate. Two days after Pakistan condemned US-Israeli strikes at the UN Security Council, its security forces fired on citizens mourning the same deaths. The Sindh provincial Chief Minister ordered an investigation as protests spread to Islamabad by evening.
