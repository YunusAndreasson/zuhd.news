Qyzylsay — Women here rise at 4 a.m. for water.

That unpaid labor stays invisible to budgets, so pipelines never get funded.

[Kazakhstan](country:KZ) loses 40-60% of rural water to leaks, and supply depends on rivers rising in [China](country:CN) and Kyrgyzstan.

Ten women now measure their hours to present as evidence to local officials.
