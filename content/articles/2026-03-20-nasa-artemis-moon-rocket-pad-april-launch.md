Humans haven't flown to the Moon since December 1972. NASA's Space Launch System returned to its Florida launchpad Thursday after engineers fixed a helium system failure that aborted a March launch attempt. Mission managers meet in late March to confirm the April 1 launch date for the 4-person crew already in quarantine.
