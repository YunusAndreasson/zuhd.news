Beijing — [China](country:CN) accuses Chile and Belgium of a backroom deal.

The row threatens the first meeting of the treaty governing the ocean beyond national control.

[Chile](country:CL) and [Belgium](country:BE) merged their bids this month, offering the secretariat in Valparaíso and the data clearing-house in Brussels.

Delegates choose the secretariat's home in New York in January, by vote if consensus fails.
