San Salvador — Nearly 2% of Salvadorans are now imprisoned.

The world's highest incarceration rate followed a crackdown that gutted MS13 and Barrio 18's structures.

A 2022 state of emergency in [El Salvador](country:SV) suspended constitutional guarantees, enabling mass arrests and a 40,000-capacity megaprison.

Amnesty International has documented arbitrary arrests, abuse and deaths in custody inside the megaprison system.

Collective trials continue after hearings against 485 alleged MS13 members concluded in July, with more pending.
