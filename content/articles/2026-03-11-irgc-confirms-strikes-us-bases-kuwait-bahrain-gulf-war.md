Iran struck US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain Wednesday. Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps targeted Bahrain's Mina Salman port — the nerve centre of the US Fifth Fleet — and 3 Kuwait bases with missiles and drones. Kuwait and Bahrain have said nothing publicly, absorbing strikes on US bases on their soil without acknowledging them.
