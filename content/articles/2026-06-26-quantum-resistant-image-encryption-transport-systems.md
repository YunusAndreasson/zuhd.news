London — Traffic-camera encryption will fail when quantum computers arrive.

Current encryption breaks when quantum computers run Shor's algorithm; this design pairs chaos-based permutation with lattice cryptography to resist it.

Road sensor networks are already being deployed; the paper argues post-quantum encryption must be baked in now.
