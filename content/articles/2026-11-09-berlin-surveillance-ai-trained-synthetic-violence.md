Berlin — 30 cameras now scan everyone crossing Kottbusser Tor's northern half.

Real footage of violence is scarce, so the scanner trains on AI-generated clips.

Mannheim police have staged 2,000 fights and say another 8,000 are needed before the software works in practice.
