London — A microwave sensor reads blood without any reagent.

Reagent-free testing could remove the cold chain that limits diagnostics in poor regions.

The device is a resonator that rings at 2.1 gigahertz, and a sample shifts that frequency by its dielectric properties.

Researchers tested it only on engineered phantoms, not real tissue, leaving clinical specificity unresolved.
