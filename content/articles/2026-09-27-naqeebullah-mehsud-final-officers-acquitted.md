Karachi — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s anti-terrorism court has acquitted the final 7 police officers.

No one now stands convicted for a killing the state's own inquiry called staged.

Malir police said Naqeebullah Mehsud, 27, died as a militant in a 2018 shootout that a later inquiry found staged.

"Naqeebullah's killing was merely the spark," said Ismail Mehsud, a veteran activist who led the justice campaign.

No further appeal is possible, and the staged-encounter finding remains unanswered in any court.
