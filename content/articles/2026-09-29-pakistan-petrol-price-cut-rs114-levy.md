Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK) still taxes Rs114 on every litre of petrol.

That's roughly 30% of the pump price, a levy the government keeps fixed.

The government cut petrol Rs1.49 and diesel Rs2.73 per litre, but the petroleum levy stays fixed regardless of global oil moves.

Brent crude fell 2.7% over the past week but is still up 23.9% over 30 days, as of 22 September.

New prices take effect Wednesday, and the regulator now resets fuel rates daily as Middle East tensions continue.
