Augsburg — Rocket Factory Augsburg plans a 15-tonne reusable rocket.

Germany's startup must reach orbit by end-2027 to unlock €190 million from the European Space Agency; the larger vehicle's timeline is "beyond mid-term."

Europe has no reusable rocket in service; launch sovereignty depends on milestones the company has not yet demonstrated.
