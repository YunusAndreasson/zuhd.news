Heart disease prevention now starts at 30, not 40. Eleven cardiology organizations adopted the PREVENT calculator after it showed arterial damage accumulates long before standard screening ages, justifying a 30-year risk window instead of 10 years. Millions in their 30s may now qualify for statins under the new 160 mg/dL LDL threshold.
