Washington — Trump's panel may recommend firing Fed governor Lisa Cook without her.

The law lets a president fire a Fed governor only for cause.

A panel of Kevin Hassett, Andrea Lucas and Keith Sonderling will weigh 2021 mortgage-fraud allegations that Cook denies.

Her lawyer Abbe David Lowell wrote the panel "appears to be a facade designed to obscure a predetermined decision to fire Governor Cook."

Officials have threatened to hold next month's hearing without her and to draw adverse inferences from her absence.
