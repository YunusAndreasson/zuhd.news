Moscow — Sberbank will offer a crypto wallet by December.

[Russia](country:RU)'s new digital-currency law, effective September 1, licenses banks for crypto custody and cross-border settlement — the channel sanctions restrict most.

Sberbank plans to launch first inside its Sberbank Online and SberInvestments apps.
