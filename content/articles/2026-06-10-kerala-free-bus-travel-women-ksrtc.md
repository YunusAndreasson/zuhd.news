Thiruvananthapuram — Kerala grants all women free ordinary bus rides from June 15.

The scheme costs ₹800 crore a year, atop ₹1,500 crore subsidizing the state transport corporation, Chief Minister Satheesan said.

Phase one covers ordinary buses only; express services in [India](country:IN)'s Malabar region have no subsidy plan.
