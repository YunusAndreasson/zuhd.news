Banks blocked stablecoins from paying interest to users. They had argued that interest on dollar-pegged tokens would directly compete with savings accounts, threatening the deposit base that funds lending. A Senate Banking Committee hearing is possible by late April, with provisions on decentralized finance still unresolved.
