Quetta — [Pakistan](country:PK) cannot hold elections in part of Balochistan.

Balochistan National Party chief Mengal resigned the seat in September 2024 citing "consistent marginalisation"; militants now control the terrain, officials told the Election Commission.

The Election Commission is constitutionally bound to proceed but has set no polling date.
