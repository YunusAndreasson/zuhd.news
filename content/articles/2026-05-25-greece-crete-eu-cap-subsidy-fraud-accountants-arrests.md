Heraklion — 20 arrested in [Greece](country:GR) over EU farm subsidy fraud.

2 accountants filed false land declarations since 2019, claiming €3 million in EU farm subsidies, police said.

Greek authorities say investigations are ongoing; the total amount disbursed under false declarations has not been disclosed.
