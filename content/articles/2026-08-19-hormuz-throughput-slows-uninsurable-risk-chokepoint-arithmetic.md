Tehran — Six ships crossed Hormuz Tuesday, down from nine.

The strait isn't closed — shipowners simply can't get insured to sail.

Brent crude was $93.26 as of Aug. 11, down 3.8% that week, as insurers refuse to price the risk.

Shipowners are waiting for clear signaling that the strait has reopened.
