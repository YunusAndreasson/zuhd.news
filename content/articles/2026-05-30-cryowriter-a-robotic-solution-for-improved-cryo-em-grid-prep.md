London — Cryo-EM's reproducibility gap closes with a new robot.

Blotting grids by hand has poor reproducibility; the cryoWriter writes controlled nanoliter volumes via microfluidics, sidestepping the manual step.

The robot also reduced orientation bias in a test case, improving reconstruction quality over conventional blotting.
