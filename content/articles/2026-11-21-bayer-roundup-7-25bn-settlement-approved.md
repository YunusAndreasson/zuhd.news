St Louis — Sick Roundup users will receive between $10,000 and $165,000 each.

The class also binds people who fall ill later, capping Bayer's exposure for up to 21 years.

Bayer pays $7.25bn into a fund, and each award depends on use, diagnosis date and severity.

Some attorneys objected the fund is too small, but Judge Timothy Boyer said he would not delay payouts to sick victims.

Bayer, which denies Roundup causes non-Hodgkin lymphoma, had set itself a year-end target to contain the litigation.
