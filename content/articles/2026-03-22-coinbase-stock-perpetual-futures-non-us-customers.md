San Francisco — Coinbase now sells leveraged Apple futures to non-American traders. The contracts offer up to 20x leverage on stocks and ETFs and settle in USDC; US customers are barred under domestic regulations. Hyperliquid launched competing equity futures days earlier, leaving US regulators watching a race their own investors cannot enter.
