Copenhagen — A Russian frigate fired flares near a Danish helicopter off Gedser.

[Denmark](country:DK) summoned Russia's ambassador after the incident, its foreign minister calling the act unacceptable.

Moscow says the helicopter made dangerous manoeuvres near its ship during a photo mission.

Russia says it will investigate, but no naval channel exists to resolve the encounter directly.
