The Netherlands ousted Nexperia's Chinese CEO last September. China's Foreign Minister Wang Yi told his Dutch counterpart this week he sees "positive signals," while the seizure continues to disrupt global EV supply chains and keep Beijing's countermeasures in place. Both sides must settle who controls Nexperia before Beijing will lift those countermeasures.
