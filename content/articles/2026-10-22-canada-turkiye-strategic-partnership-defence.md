Ankara — Canada now signs defence deals with [Turkey](country:TR), which it once embargoed.

[Canada](country:CA) sends nearly 70% of its exports to the US and wants other markets.

Carney and Erdogan launched a three-year action plan Thursday, with defence, energy and air transport agreements signed.

Rights groups criticise Turkey's opposition crackdown since 2024, which Ankara says is not politically motivated.

Free trade talks open in Ankara next month, and Turkey hosts COP31 in Antalya from 9 November.
