Laayoune — UN mission expired 31 October, advocacy outlet Inkstick reported.

It monitored the ceasefire between Morocco and the Polisario Front and was mandated to hold a Sahrawi referendum.

A US draft dropping the referendum drew a Russian veto, and Russia's one-year rollover then failed to win support.

[Morocco](country:MA) blames [Algeria](country:DZ), which hosts about 200,000 Sahrawi refugees, for orchestrating the conflict.

Morocco's 2007 autonomy plan would leave Rabat in charge of defence, foreign affairs and religion.
