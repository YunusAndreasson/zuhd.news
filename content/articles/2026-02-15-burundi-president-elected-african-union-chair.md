Évariste Ndayishimiye takes the chair. The African Union Assembly elected Burundi's president as 2026 chairperson at its 39th summit in Addis Ababa, with Ghana, Tanzania, and Angola filling the bureau. AU Commission Chairperson Mahmoud Youssouf urged members to reduce foreign funding dependence through domestic revenue and reform, while leaders discussed unresolved conflicts in Sudan, eastern DRC, Somalia, and the Sahel.
