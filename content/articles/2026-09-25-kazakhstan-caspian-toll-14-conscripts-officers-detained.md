Mangystau — 14 of 17 servicemen swept into the Caspian died.

[Kazakhstan](country:KZ) detained 5 officers, including the first deputy navy commander, on suspicion of negligence.

12 of the dead were conscripts, swept out while disembarking from the Kaisar into water one metre deep.

The Defence Ministry blamed sudden weather, but prosecutors say officers failed to protect personnel as conditions worsened.

A criminal case for negligence and navigation violations is open, and a government commission is still investigating.
