Cartagena — Armed men hold about 25 people in a supermarket.

It follows an overnight car bomb in Villavicencio that injured 22 people.

Robbers who failed to seize an armoured vehicle fled into the Exito store after camera operators alerted police.

President Abelardo de la Espriella said 30 were seized and 5 older women freed.

Police and special units surround the store, and officials have not identified the hostage-takers.
