Brussels — 25 years of EU-Mercosur trade negotiations end May 1.

Paraguay's ratification triggered the deal covering 780 million people, finalized as US tariffs push other economies toward regional blocs.

Argentina and Brazil gain tariff-free European market access on May 1, opening new export routes for beef, soybeans, and ethanol.
