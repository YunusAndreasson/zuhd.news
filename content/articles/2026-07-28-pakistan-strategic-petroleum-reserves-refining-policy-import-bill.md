Islamabad — Prime Minister Sharif ordered bigger fuel reserves.

The Cabinet Committee on Energy also amended [Pakistan](country:PK)'s 2023 refining policy, aimed at drawing investment to upgrade refineries and cut import dependence.

Fuel is the largest single line in Pakistan's import bill; reserve-building now competes with debt service.
