Moscow — Russian state news agency TASS reported Dmitriev offered to reopen Nord Stream jointly.

Dmitriev praised the Alternative for Germany, whose co-chairman demanded both pipelines be repaired and reopened.

[Russia](country:RU)'s special envoy Kirill Dmitriev replied on X to Tino Chrupalla, who wants an international probe of the sabotage.

TASS carried no response from the German government.
