Washington — The nuclear deal awaits Saudi recognition of Israel.

The pact ties that recognition to reactors and uranium enrichment rights.

The 30-year pact lets [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) build reactors but sets no ban on enrichment or reprocessing, unlike the UAE's accord.

The [US](country:US) Congress has 90 days to block it, needing two-thirds votes in both chambers.
