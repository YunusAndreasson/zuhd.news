New York — Lab-grown donor cells achieved liver immune tolerance. A Nature Communications trial infused regulatory dendritic cells from living donors — replacing the lifelong immunosuppressants that cause diabetes, kidney damage, and cancer. No follow-on trial has been announced, leaving it unproven at scale.
