Riyadh — Turkey called 4-nation defense talks in Riyadh. Turkish Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan organized the meeting with Saudi Arabia, Egypt, and Pakistan, arguing that the 4 governments must build independent security structures rather than defer to outside powers. The 4 governments must now decide whether to formalize mutual obligations or remain an informal consultation forum.
