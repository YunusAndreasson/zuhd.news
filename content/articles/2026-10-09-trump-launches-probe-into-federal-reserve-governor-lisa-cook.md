Washington — Trump's panel will hear Lisa Cook for up to 4 hours.

No president has removed a sitting Fed governor in the central bank's 113-year history.

After the Supreme Court ruled a president needs cause and a clear process, the committee must report whether cause exists.

Cook, whose term runs to 2038, denies the allegations from the Federal Housing Finance Agency's director.

The White House hearing is set for 5 November, after which the committee must report.
