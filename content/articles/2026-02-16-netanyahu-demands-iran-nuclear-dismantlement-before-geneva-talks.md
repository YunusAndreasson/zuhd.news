Dismantle everything, Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu demanded. He set 4 conditions before the second round of US-Iran talks in Geneva on Tuesday — including destruction of all enrichment equipment and ballistic missiles — far beyond Tehran's offer to dilute its 60% uranium stockpile for sanctions relief. US President Donald Trump told Netanyahu to let talks continue, exposing a rift between the allies over whether diplomacy can succeed.
