A drone hit Adikong market, killing 11 including children. UN rights chief Volker Türk confirmed 200 civilians killed by drone in Sudan since March 4 alone — markets, schools, and hospitals among the targets. Türk demanded both sides halt drone strikes on civilians — neither the Sudanese Armed Forces nor the Rapid Support Forces has responded.
