Seoul — [South Korea](country:KR)'s ambassador left Kyiv, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

Park Ki-chang's departure follows a recall that Seoul's foreign ministry announced on 8 October.

TASS's report, citing Yonhap, names neither the issues in dispute nor any reply from Kyiv.

Seoul said it is ready to continue consultations with [Ukraine](country:UA) to resolve the issues constructively.
