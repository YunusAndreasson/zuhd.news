Brasília — [US](country:US) designates Primeiro Comando da Capital and Comando Vermelho as terrorist groups.

Crime analysts say both groups are profit-driven, not ideological; the designation freezes US assets in their accounts.

Both become the first [Brazil](country:BR)-based groups on the US terrorist list, effective June 5.
