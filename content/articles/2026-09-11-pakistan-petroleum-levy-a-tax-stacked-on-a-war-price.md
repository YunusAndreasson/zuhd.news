Gujranwala — Pakistan taxes petrol at 114 rupees a liter.

Jamaat-i-Islami will march on Islamabad Sept. 20 unless the levy is dropped.

The levy is [Pakistan](country:PK)'s main non-shareable revenue and an IMF loan benchmark.

The government has yet to say whether it will lift the levy before the march.
