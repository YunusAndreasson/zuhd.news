Gwadar — Police found five bullet-riddled bodies dumped roadside.

Two of the five were identified; Balochistan has recorded years of unexplained killings tied to security operations around the Chinese-funded port.

Police have not named suspects, and the three unidentified bodies remain unclaimed.
