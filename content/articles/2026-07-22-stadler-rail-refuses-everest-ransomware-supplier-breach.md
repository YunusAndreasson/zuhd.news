Bussnang — Stadler Rail will not pay $12.3 million.

The ransomware gang Everest stole technical data not from Stadler directly but from a supplier's file-sharing platform, exposing the train maker through its own supply chain.

Stadler's no-pay stance now tests whether refusal deters further extortion attempts against it.
