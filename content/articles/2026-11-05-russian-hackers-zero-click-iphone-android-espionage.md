Ukraine — Hackers compromised an analytics script serving thousands of sites.

Sekoia ties the campaign to APT29, an espionage group linked to [Russia](country:RU)'s foreign intelligence service.

Attackers identified visitors' devices and software versions, then exploited known flaws, sometimes without a click, to steal passwords, messages and location history.

Researchers found no evidence that Volodymyr Zelensky, who reportedly stayed at the Raffles in October, was targeted.
