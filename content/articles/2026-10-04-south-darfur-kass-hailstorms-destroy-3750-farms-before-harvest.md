Kass — Hail destroyed 3,750 farms across 30 kilometres of [Sudan](country:SD)'s Daous area.

Hafiz El Saleh, Kass locality's executive director, says farmers lost their crops and sources of income.

Hail struck at 15:00 while residents were away, which El Saleh says prevented any deaths or injuries.

A committee of local institutions will assess losses, and El Saleh asks international organisations for urgent aid.
