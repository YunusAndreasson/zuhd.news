Washington — Trump's stalled Fed pick now advises Treasury on China.

Treasury said she will advise Secretary Scott Bessent on currency policy, focused on [China](country:CN).

Trump tapped her in his first term, but bipartisan concern over the Fed's independence stalled the nomination.

Treasury's statement names no start date, title or remit beyond currency policy.
