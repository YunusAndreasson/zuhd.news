India's censor board banned The Voice of Hind Rajab. The documentary depicts Hind Rajab, a 5-year-old shot 335 times by Israeli forces, and was blocked because it would "break up the India-Israel relationship." The distributor can appeal in court, testing whether India's judiciary will override diplomatic considerations.
