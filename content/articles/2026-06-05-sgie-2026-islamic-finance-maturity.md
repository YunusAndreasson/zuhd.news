Dubai — The Islamic economy's growth era is ending.

The 2026 Islamic economy report says sukuk, takaful and halal food are now large enough to set standards rather than chase them.

The report identifies regulatory harmonization as the bottleneck holding back integration across Organisation of Islamic Cooperation member states.
