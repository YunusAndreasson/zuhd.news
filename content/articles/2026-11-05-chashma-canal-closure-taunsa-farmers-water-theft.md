Dera Ghazi Khan — Chashma canal water ran 96% short in August, an irrigation officer says.

240,000 Punjab acres depend on it, and the groundwater beneath them is brackish.

A breach in the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa reach on 14 September cut Punjab's supply to zero.

Punjab officials blame farmers upstream, who allegedly steal 1,000 cusecs, per Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's irrigation authorities.

The Water and Power Development Authority awarded a Rs385 million repair on 30 September, due in 45 days.
