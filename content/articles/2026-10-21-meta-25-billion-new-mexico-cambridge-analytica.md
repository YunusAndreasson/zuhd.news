Santa Fe — 43 million deception violations cost Meta $25 billion.

It is 5 times the $5 billion the Federal Trade Commission fined Facebook in 2019.

State law allows $5,000 a violation, about $215 billion here, and Judge Francis Mathew cut it to $25 billion.

Meta says it does not sell user data and New Mexico never proved a consumer was misled.

Meta will appeal, which could cut the sum, and all proceeds go to New Mexico's schools.
