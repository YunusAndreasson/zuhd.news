Washington — Textron replaced the cockpit with a cargo bay.

The uncrewed variant inherits an existing type certificate, skipping years of airworthiness testing.

SkyCourier UX keeps the same 6,000-pound payload and 900-mile range as the crewed Cessna 408 it modifies.

Textron is pitching it to the Pentagon for contested-logistics missions against [China](country:CN)'s own uncrewed cargo push.
