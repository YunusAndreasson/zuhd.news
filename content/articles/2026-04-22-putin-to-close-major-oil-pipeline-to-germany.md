Schwedt — [Russia](country:RU) will halt 43,000 barrels per day of Kazakh oil via the Druzhba pipeline from 1 May, citing technical capacity. The route fed [Germany](country:DE)'s PCK Schwedt refinery, which supplies 90% of Berlin's petrol and heating fuel, Germany's economic ministry said. Poland's pipeline operator offered seaborne alternatives via Gdańsk; Berlin said overall fuel security is not ultimately at risk.
