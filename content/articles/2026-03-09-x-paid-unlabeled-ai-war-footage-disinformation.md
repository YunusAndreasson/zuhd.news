X paid creators to post AI-generated war footage. Its ad-revenue program pays by engagement; one fabricated clip of missiles hitting Jerusalem's Dome of the Rock earned thousands of shares from a verified account. Accounts violating the new rule lose creator payouts for 90 days; enforcement relies on community notes, not platform review.
