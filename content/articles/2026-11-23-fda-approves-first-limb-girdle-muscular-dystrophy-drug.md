United States — BridgeBio estimates 2,000 to 3,000 [US](country:US) patients qualify.

It is the first drug for any form of limb girdle muscular dystrophy.

Welgemda, known in development as ribitol, is approved only for the 2i/R9 subtype, not other forms.

BridgeBio has not disclosed a price, and estimates another 4,000 to 5,000 patients live in Europe.
