Overseas customers supply 50% of revenue for ByteDance's video model, media reported on 3 November.

By token use and revenue, Tencent is among Seedance's top 3 Chinese customers and Bilibili the top 10.

Kuaishou's Kling disclosed second-quarter revenue above 850 million yuan, up more than 200% from a year earlier.

Volcano Engine subsidises film projects up to 1 million yuan, and renewal rates will show if buyers stay.
