An Iranian refugee who stopped a gunman faces deportation. Known as AB, he survived imprisonment and torture in Iran for protesting, fled as a refugee, then ran toward 2 active shooters at Bondi Junction in December — where police shot at him and bystanders beat him, mistaking him for an attacker. Iran has been under US-Israeli bombardment since February 28, and Australia's Home Affairs has not commented on whether he will be returned there.
