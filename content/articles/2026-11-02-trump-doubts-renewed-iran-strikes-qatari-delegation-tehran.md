Nothing is signed, and the Strait of Hormuz stays closed while a Qatari delegation flies to Tehran.

Iran says its seven-step plan, now condensed to two days, reopens the strait if the [US](country:US) lifts its port blockade and sanctions.

"One way or the other, we will do something, but there is no rush," Trump said separately.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 31 December a 40% chance, up 6 points in a week.
