Chisinau — Russian state news agency TASS quotes Igor Dodon: 46 lawmakers seek Sandu's impeachment.

[Moldova](country:MD) has never opened impeachment proceedings against a president, said Dodon, the pro-Russian Socialist leader.

A third of parliament, 34 of 101 lawmakers, can propose removal, and 46 signed, Dodon told TASS.

TASS itself notes the ruling party holds most seats, calling impeachment practically unimplementable.

A referendum within 30 days follows only if 67 lawmakers vote to remove her.
