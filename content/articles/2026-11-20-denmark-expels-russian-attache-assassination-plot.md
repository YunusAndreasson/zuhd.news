Copenhagen — Danish intelligence says [Russia](country:RU) hired a gang to kill Kyivstoner.

It calls this the first known Russian assassination attempt on Danish soil.

Investigators are probing ties to the banned gang Loyal to Familia, and 2 men face attempted murder charges.

Both charged men deny the allegations.

The attaché has 7 days to leave, and [Denmark](country:DK) is weighing suspending Schengen visas for Russians.
