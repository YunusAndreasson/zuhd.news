Riyadh — [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) shot down drones launched from [Iraq](country:IQ).

Defence officials blamed Iran-backed militias — deniable by Tehran, unattributable to Baghdad, and far cheaper to launch than the interceptors that destroyed them.

Iraq's government has not confirmed the launch site, and no group has claimed the attack.
