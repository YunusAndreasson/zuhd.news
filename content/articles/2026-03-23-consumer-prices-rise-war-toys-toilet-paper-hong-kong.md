Hong Kong — The Middle East supplies 85% of the world's polythene. The petrochemical, used in everything from toys to toilet paper, is becoming more expensive as the Iran-Israel war drives up oil prices. Hong Kong exporters have shifted to short-term contracts as overseas buyers refuse to commit to long-term volumes.
