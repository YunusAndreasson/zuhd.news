China — Xpeng's record 24.28 billion yuan quarter still lost money.

Xpeng's results show the 870 million yuan net loss more than doubled from 380 million a year earlier.

Vehicle margin rose 1 point to 13.1%, but group margin fell to 18.9% from 20.7% without Volkswagen milestone revenue.

Xpeng guides 169,000 to 174,000 deliveries this quarter, expects margin to dip and gave no 2027 profit target.
