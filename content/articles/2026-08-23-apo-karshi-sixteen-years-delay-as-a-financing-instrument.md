Abuja — The road was awarded in 2010.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s naira fell 1.4% over the past month, making delay costlier every year.

Unpaid contractors halt work, forcing costlier re-awarded contracts that make delay a financing tool.

Minister Wike has promised completion by December, a date this project has missed before.
