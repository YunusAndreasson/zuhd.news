Los Angeles — Bulletin boards from Venice Beach to Berlin now carry the same look.

Bright text, dark backgrounds and generic AI icons have flooded fundraiser and event flyers, erasing the visual cue that once signaled a real person made the effort.

Designers and small businesses report people now reflexively skip anything that looks AI-made.
