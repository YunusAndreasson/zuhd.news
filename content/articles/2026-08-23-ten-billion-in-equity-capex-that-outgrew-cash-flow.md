Hangzhou — Alibaba is selling new shares, not spending cash reserves, for AI.

Existing shareholders get diluted because the buildout has outgrown what operations alone can fund.

[China](country:CN)'s Alibaba plans HK$80 billion in Hong Kong shares as export controls push some of that money into costlier domestic chips.

The company has not said how the new infrastructure will be monetized.
