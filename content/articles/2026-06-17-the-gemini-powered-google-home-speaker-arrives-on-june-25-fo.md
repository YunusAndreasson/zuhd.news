Mountain View — Google Home Speaker ships June 25 for $100.

Google's first home speaker since 2020 pairs Gemini AI with a smaller driver, costing $100 with 6 months of AI subscription included.

Advanced Gemini Live features require a $10 monthly subscription after the 6-month trial.
