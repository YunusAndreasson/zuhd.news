China ended minority-language instruction in all schools. The law removes the legal ambiguity that let Tibet, Xinjiang, and Inner Mongolia maintain native-language teaching for decades. No exemption exists under the new law, leaving ethnic minority communities no legal path to preserve their languages in schools.
