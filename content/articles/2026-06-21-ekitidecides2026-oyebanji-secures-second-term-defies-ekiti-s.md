Ado-Ekiti — Ekiti Governor Biodun Oyebanji won a second term — the state's first re-elected incumbent.

He polled 319,224 votes — nearly 8 times the runner-up — in [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Ekiti state.

Opposition parties have not said whether they will contest the result before his inauguration.
