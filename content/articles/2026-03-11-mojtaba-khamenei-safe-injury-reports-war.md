The New York Times cited 3 Iranian officials saying supreme leader Mojtaba Khamenei suffered leg injuries. Iran's president's son said contacts confirmed Khamenei is "safe and sound"; Khamenei, 56, has not spoken publicly since his father was killed in US-Israeli strikes on February 28. Israel has named regime pressure as a war aim; Khamenei's silence leaves unclear whether Tehran can coordinate a unified response.
