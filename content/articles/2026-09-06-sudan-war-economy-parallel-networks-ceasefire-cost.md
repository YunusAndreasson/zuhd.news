Khartoum — Sudan exported 14 of 70 gold tonnes, bank data show.

The missing gold is arming both sides of [Sudan](country:SD)'s war, a leftist party says.

The Communist Party says army- and RSF-linked trading networks fused into one parallel war economy.

The party wants wartime debts and deals investigated once the fighting stops.
