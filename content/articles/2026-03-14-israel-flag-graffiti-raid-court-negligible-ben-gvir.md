A minister jailed a man for Palestinian flag graffiti. National Security Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir personally oversaw a live-broadcast arrest of Mohammed Sakallah, 27, and charged him as a foreign agent for the graffiti. A court called the foreign agent suspicion "weak if not negligible" and released Sakallah to house arrest; prosecutors have not withdrawn the charge.
