A smartphone maker's robot outran Usain Bolt.

It shows phone-industry supply chains can now build competitive humanoid robots.

Honor's Lightning clocked 9.32 seconds in a trial, and X-Humanoid's robot ran 9.39 at the Games, beating Bolt's 9.58-second record.

[China](country:CN)'s humanoid industry now faces a [US](country:US) ban on importing its finished robots.
