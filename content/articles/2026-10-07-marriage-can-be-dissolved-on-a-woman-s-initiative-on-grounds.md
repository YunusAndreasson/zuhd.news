Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Supreme Court lets wives divorce without proving cruelty.

Family courts had turned such claims into khula, a wife's paid release, forcing 2 petitioners to return dower.

Shiqaq, a serious breach where reconciliation has failed, falls under section 2(ix) of the 1939 Act and needs no dower surrender.

Mere incompatibility, anger or a short separation does not qualify, the five-judge bench said.

Dower claims from Zainab Bibi and Imran Naz Begum return to be determined under the 1939 Act.
