Kolkata — West Bengal ordered Rohingya and Bangladeshi holding centres statewide.

Chief Minister Adhikari's "detect, delete, deport" policy has a gap: [Bangladesh](country:BD) accepts few deportees, making [India](country:IN)'s centres indefinite warehouses.

About 550 km of the 2,216-km West Bengal–Bangladesh border stays unfenced; detentions will precede deportations.
