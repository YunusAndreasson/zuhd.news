Kathmandu — A second Indian aircraft carried 37.5 tonnes of aid.

Nepal's flood toll has passed 170 dead, with hundreds still missing.

[Nepal](country:NP) borders only [India](country:IN) and China by road, and both are now delivering flood relief.

India's disaster teams stay on standby, awaiting a formal request from Kathmandu.
