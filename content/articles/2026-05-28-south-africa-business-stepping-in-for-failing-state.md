Gqeberha — Business Leadership SA CEO Busisiwe Mavuso warned that firms replacing the state risk entrenching its failure.

Private capital absorbing government obligations creates dependency, removing the state's incentive to improve, she said.

Volkswagen, with 50,000 indirect regional jobs, could relocate operations if local conditions don't improve, she said.
