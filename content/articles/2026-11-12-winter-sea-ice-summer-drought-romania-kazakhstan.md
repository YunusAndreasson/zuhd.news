Romania — Shrinking winter sea ice is linked to summer drought from Romania to Kazakhstan.

This summer brought one of Europe's most severe droughts since 1540, worsening the humanitarian crisis in [Ukraine](country:UA).

The study ties the dry summers to two rapidly warming seas whose winter ice is shrinking.

Simulations show such droughts will grow more frequent and severe with man-made warming.

Researchers say the link could let forecasters warn of drought months ahead.
