Bishkek — A court reclassified Kamchybek Tashiev's charges on appeal.

It leaves [Kyrgyzstan](country:KG)'s former security chief's fate still legally unresolved.

His lawyers sought acquittal while prosecutors sought harsher sentences after his July conviction.

Neither side got what it asked for, and no further appeal date is set.
