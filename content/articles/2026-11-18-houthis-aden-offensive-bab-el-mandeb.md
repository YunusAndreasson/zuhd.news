Aden — Houthi forces now hold the Bab el-Mandeb coast.

[Yemen](country:YE)'s government forces withdrew from the coast over the weekend to defend Aden.

The strait carries roughly 12% of global shipping, and holding its coast lets the Houthis hit vessels more easily.

Government forces still hold several strongholds, and [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) replaced the coalition's military commander on Monday.

Polymarket traders give a Saudi-Houthi ceasefire by 31 December a 10% chance, down 17 points in a week.
