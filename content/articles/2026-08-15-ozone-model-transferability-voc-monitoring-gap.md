Beijing — Ozone jumped 69% even as pollution controls tightened.

A model without gas data would have missed why cutting pollution raised ozone.

Researchers found adding volatile organic compound readings let the model reproduce Shanghai's 2020 lockdown ozone rebound in [China](country:CN) accurately.

The same model, retrained on rural Changping data, also captured 2021 summer pollution peaks there.
