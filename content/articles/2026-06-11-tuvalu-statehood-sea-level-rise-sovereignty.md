Funafuti — Tuvalu passed laws to survive its own submersion.

[Tuvalu](country:TV) is pioneering a legal doctrine that statehood, maritime zones, and citizenship survive when its land is submerged.

Whether international law recognises a landless state is a live question for the UN and small island nations.
