23 died as floods shut Nairobi's international airport. Nairobi's drainage has not kept pace with decades of population growth, leaving informal settlements built on floodplains unprotected as Indian Ocean warming drives more intense rainfall. Kenya has announced no plan to relocate the informal settlement residents who remain on floodplains.
