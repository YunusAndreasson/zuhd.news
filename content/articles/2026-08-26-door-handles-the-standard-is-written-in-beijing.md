Beijing — 60% of [China](country:CN)'s best-selling EVs use flush handles.

Handles with no mechanical backup can fail exactly when a crash cuts power.

Electric handles depend on a car's 12-volt system, which a bad crash often knocks out first.

Xiaomi's SU7 remains under scrutiny after a Tongling crash trapped three passengers who died.
