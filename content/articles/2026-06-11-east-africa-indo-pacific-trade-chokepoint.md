Nairobi — East Africa's ports are now contested geopolitical terrain.

[China](country:CN) and the [US](country:US) are competing for these routes, pressing coastal states for infrastructure deals and base access.

[Tanzania](country:TZ), [Mozambique](country:MZ), and [Kenya](country:KE) sit at the center of that competition, with port and gas deals increasingly contested.
