Anthropic refused to weaponize its AI. US President Donald Trump designated the company a supply-chain security threat after it blocked the Pentagon from using its model for autonomous lethal warfare. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth gave the Pentagon 6 months to exit Anthropic's contracts, with no replacement model named.
