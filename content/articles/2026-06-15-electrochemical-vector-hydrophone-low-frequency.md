London — Engineers built a hydrophone sensitive at 10 Hz.

A horn-shaped cavity amplifies particle velocity at its throat, extending reliable underwater detection to below 10 Hz.

Better low-frequency sensing has direct consequences for maritime surveillance, seismic monitoring, and submarine detection.
