Bremen — The European Space Agency ordered a €102m satellite catcher.

OHB will build ClearSpace-1 to capture and remove the agency's Proba-1 once the satellite retires.

The original 2020 target, a Vega payload adapter, was struck by debris in August 2023, forcing a redesign around Proba-1.

Neither OHB nor the agency has confirmed RFA ONE as the launcher, though OHB owns 56.8% of its developer.

The mission is expected to launch in 2030.
