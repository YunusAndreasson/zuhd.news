Hangzhou — A walking robot now costs $13,500, not $100,000.

Western robot makers price similar machines above $100,000 for narrow industrial use.

Founder Wang Xingxing cut costs by building actuators and motors in-house instead of buying precision parts.

[China](country:CN) expects domestic makers to produce over 100,000 humanoid robots in 2026, ministry figures show.
