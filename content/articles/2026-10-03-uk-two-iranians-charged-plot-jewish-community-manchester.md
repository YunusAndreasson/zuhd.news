Manchester — 2 men are charged over a Yom Kippur terror plot.

Police say the pair contacted an overseas third party who may be in [Iran](country:IR).

Rahman Salehi, 34, and Salam Ahmadyan, 36, allegedly sought bomb components and scouted targets before their arrest in Manchester on 20 September.

Iran denies a role in a separate alleged plot at Royal Air Force Fairford, which detectives say is unconnected.

The pair appear at Westminster Magistrates' Court on Saturday.
