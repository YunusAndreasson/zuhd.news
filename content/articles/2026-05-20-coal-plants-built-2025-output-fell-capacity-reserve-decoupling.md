London — Global Energy Monitor said 97GW of coal opened in 2025; total output fell.

95% was built in [China](country:CN) and [India](country:IN), where renewables fill demand so fast that new coal runs below breakeven.

70% of coal units due to retire in 2025 did not close, extending the fleet's lifespan.
