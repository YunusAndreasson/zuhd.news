Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Tehreek-e-Insaf rejected Prime Minister Sharif's economy charter.

Senior party official Latif Khosa cited the 2006 Charter of Democracy — never honoured, he said — as proof elite pacts yield no structural reform.

Imran Khan remains imprisoned; Tehreek-e-Insaf demands guarantees no prior pact has ever delivered.
