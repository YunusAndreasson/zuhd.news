Pyongyang — Kim Jong Un installed loyalists and demoted his own sister. North Korea's state media now praises Kim without crediting his grandfather or father — a break from founding mythology that historically constrained his ambitions. Kim also won a 3rd consecutive presidential term, completing a consolidation that analysts say has historically preceded major North Korean provocations.
