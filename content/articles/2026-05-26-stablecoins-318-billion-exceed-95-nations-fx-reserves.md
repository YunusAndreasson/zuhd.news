New York — Stablecoins now exceed the FX reserves of 95 nations.

[US](country:US) dollar-pegged coins, mostly Treasury-backed, let savers bypass capital controls; the Bank of International Settlements says this accelerates currency depreciation in emerging markets.

Only 14 countries hold larger reserves; no international framework reaches the issuers.
