New York — Bitcoin wallets leak identity through fee-bump defaults.

Replace-by-fee sequence numbers vary by wallet, letting observers fingerprint users; developers propose a shared MAX-2 default to make transactions indistinguishable.

No deployment timeline has been set; the fix requires coordination across all wallet implementations.
