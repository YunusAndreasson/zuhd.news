Finland approached Seoul, seeking quantum and 6G expertise. South Korea and Finland are building a shared quantum-6G infrastructure that neither Washington nor Beijing controls, as US-China tech rivalry splits global research networks. Both sides will meet at the Quantum Information Network Standard Association assembly in Finland in May.
