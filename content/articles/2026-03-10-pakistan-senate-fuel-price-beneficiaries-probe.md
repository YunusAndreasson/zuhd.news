Pakistan raised fuel prices 20% as austerity bites. Senator Saifullah Abro's Senate committee is investigating whether oil marketing companies — not the government treasury — captured the gains from war-driven price spikes. The committee has ordered petroleum companies to submit financial records but set no timeline for findings.
