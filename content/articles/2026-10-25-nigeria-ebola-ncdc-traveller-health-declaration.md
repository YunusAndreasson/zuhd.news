Abuja — [Nigeria](country:NG) now requires health declarations from all international travellers.

A confirmed Bundibugyo Ebola case in the capital ended screening limited to Congo and Uganda arrivals.

The disease agency does not run airports, so it sent advisories to the airport and aviation authorities to enforce with airlines.

Health officials are monitoring 53 contacts of the patient for 21 days.
