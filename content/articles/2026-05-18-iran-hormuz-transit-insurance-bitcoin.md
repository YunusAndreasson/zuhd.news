Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) now sells Hormuz transit insurance, payable in Bitcoin.

The new authority charges for passage insurance through the strait, which carries 20% of global oil.

The [US](country:US) warned companies that paying Iran for passage risks sanctions; no shipper has agreed.
