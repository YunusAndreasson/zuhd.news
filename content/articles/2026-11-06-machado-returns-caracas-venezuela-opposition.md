Caracas — María Corina Machado ended 16 months in hiding.

Her party, Vente Venezuela, holds no seat in the talks deciding [Venezuela](country:VE)'s transition.

Interim president Delcy Rodríguez has negotiated with Assembly president Dinorah Figuera since August, after a [US](country:US) operation captured Nicolás Maduro on 3 January.

Secretary of State Marco Rubio credits those talks for her return and for hundreds of released political prisoners.

The open question is whether Rodríguez's government will admit her party to those talks.
