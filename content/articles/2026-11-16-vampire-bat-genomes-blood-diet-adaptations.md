Frankfurt — Vampire bats drink about half their body weight in blood.

Blood holds little sugar or fat and toxic iron, yet all 3 species live on it.

Their stomachs became stretchy, pH-neutral storage sacs, and a water-shedding gene runs more than 900-fold higher so bats can fly home.

The study is a bioRxiv preprint posted 7 November and not yet peer reviewed.

Fat-digesting genes dialled down in adults might let pups digest milk, physiologist Ken Welch Jr. says.
