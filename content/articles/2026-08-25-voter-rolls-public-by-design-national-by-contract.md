Washington — ICE wants one national voter database, not fifty.

Naturalized citizens and mixed-status households may stop registering once data is seen flowing to enforcement.

A contractor, not a law, merges fifty public voter files into a record matched against immigration databases.

Procurement records cite fraud detection as the purpose, though experts say voter fraud is rare.
