Washington — Prosecutors sued $61 million, not a person.

Civil forfeiture lets the [US](country:US) claim the money without charging any Iranian counterparty in court.

Stablecoins settle on public ledgers, so the Justice Department traced funds it says were bound for [Iran](country:IR)'s military.

Binance says it did not permit sanctioned transactions and is cooperating with investigators.
