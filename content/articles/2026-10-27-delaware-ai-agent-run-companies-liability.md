Dover — Delaware's draft bill shields owners from their AI agent's actions.

About two-thirds of Fortune 500 companies are incorporated in the state.

The single member funds the company and is liable only for fraud, willful violations or failing to capitalise it.

Representative James Comer, who chairs House Oversight, says it would protect investors from "reckless harmful actions".

Comer has asked Governor Matt Meyer for documents, and the bill is expected before the legislature next year.
