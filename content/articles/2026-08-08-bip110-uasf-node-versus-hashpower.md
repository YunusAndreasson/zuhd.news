New York — Miner support for BIP-110 sits near 2.5%.

A rule change with that little backing could split Bitcoin's ledger in two.

Supporters want node operators, not miners, to reject blocks that don't signal support — a user-activated soft fork.

If miners don't comply, node operators could cut them off entirely, forking the chain.
