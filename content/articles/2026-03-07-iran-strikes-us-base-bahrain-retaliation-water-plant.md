Iranian forces struck the US Fifth Fleet base in Bahrain. The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps said the strike was the first direct missile hit on US forces in the war, launched in retaliation for a US attack on an Iranian desalination plant. Bahrain's government has not publicly responded to the strike on its soil.
