Warsaw — Poland switched off ePUAP, its portal for state paperwork.

Citizens of [Poland](country:PL) cannot file documents or message agencies, and Trusted Profile digital identity may fail.

Officials say they shut it as a precaution after detecting and stopping an attack, not because it was damaged.

Authorities have not said who attacked, how, or whether hackers reached any data.

The ministry gave no restoration date and says services resume once security specialists finish.
