Cardano's ADA fell to $0.16 Thursday, down 75% over the past year to a 4-year low.

Founder Charles Hoskinson announced a break after warning of ecosystem failures; analytics platform TapTools shut down; the community voted against funding the 2026 Singapore summit.

Treasury funds remain undeployed as social engagement peaked alongside the price fall.
