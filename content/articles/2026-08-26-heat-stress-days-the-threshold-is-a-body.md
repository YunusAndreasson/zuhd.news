Dhaka — 580 million children now face 20 extra heat-stress days yearly.

Their bodies sweat less and control less of their own exposure to heat.

A heat-stress day means wet-bulb temperature above 28°C, the threshold where the body's cooling fails.

South Asia and West Africa carry the heaviest exposure, the study's authors found.
