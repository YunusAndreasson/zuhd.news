New Delhi — Adani will buy 2,000 tons of copper anode monthly.

[India](country:IN) depends on imported copper feedstock, and Adani wants to double its Kutch smelter's 500,000-ton capacity.

Recycler Attero will recover the copper from e-waste and batteries, cutting the smelter's imports by about 5%, two sources say.

Even so, India imported 859,509 tons of copper in 2025-2026, BigMint data show.

Adani also signed a deal with Chile's Codelco this month to develop the Kaburkaala copper exploration project.
