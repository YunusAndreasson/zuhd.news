Yerevan — Pashinyan stakes his job on erasing the Karabakh clause.

[Azerbaijan](country:AZ)'s foreign minister says no obstacle to a peace treaty remains once the claim goes.

[Armenia](country:AM)'s ruling party holds 64 of 105 seats, but 70 lawmakers must approve a referendum, and opposition parties refuse.

Analyst Tigran Grigoryan says the draft expands executive powers instead of adding checks on the prime minister.

A referendum passes only with yes votes from 25% of registered voters.
