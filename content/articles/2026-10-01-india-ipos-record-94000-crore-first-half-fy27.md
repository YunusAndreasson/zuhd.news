Mumbai — [India](country:IN)'s initial public offerings raised a record Rs 94,205 crore in 6 months.

Foreign funds sold Rs 1.64 lakh crore of Indian shares in the secondary market, PRIME Database says.

Retail investors bid for Rs 2.47 lakh crore of shares and received Rs 25,944 crore, PRIME Database data show.

The first quarter raised just Rs 3,794 crore, while September alone topped Rs 40,000 crore.

PRIME Database counts 145 approved companies waiting to raise Rs 2.78 lakh crore in the second half.
