Lau Fau Shan — Warming seas are killing Deep Bay oysters.

Scientists and sixth-generation farmers are breeding a heat- and salt-tolerant "super oyster" to save a 400-year-old aquaculture tradition in [Hong Kong](country:HK).

Rising temperatures and salinity in Deep Bay are cutting yields; the breeding program has no confirmed deployment date.
