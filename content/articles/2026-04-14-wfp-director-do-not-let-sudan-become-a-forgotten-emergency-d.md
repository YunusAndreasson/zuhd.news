Khartoum — Sudan's civil war entered its 3rd year with famine active and no ceasefire. WFP reaches 3.5 million monthly — mostly in Darfur and Kordofan — but 25 million face acute hunger as global attention shifts to Iran. WFP Director Smith warned Geneva mediators that parties are blocking aid access and demanded emergency funding.
