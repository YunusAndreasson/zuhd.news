New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s opposition will force an inflation debate.

Congress MP Imran Masood said the Monsoon Session, opening July 20, must confront inflation and the medical-entrance exam scandal.

Masood expects the ruling party to resist debate when Parliament convenes July 20 through August 13.
