Wisconsin — A third measles death brings the US toll to 14.

The [US](country:US) lost measles-elimination status last week, and nearly 6,000 cases this year are the most since 1991.

Only 92.4% of US kindergartners got the measles vaccine in 2025-2026, short of the 95% that blocks outbreaks.

Wisconsin has 920 cases, 88 of them since 20 November, and its outbreak is still growing.
