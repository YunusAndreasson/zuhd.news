Abu Dhabi — Netanyahu secretly flew to Abu Dhabi seeking a denial.

The trip shows Netanyahu using a Gulf ally to manage his own accountability over 7 October.

He asked [UAE](country:AE) leader Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed to deny a Haaretz report that he'd warned him before Hamas's 2023 attack on [Israel](country:IL).

The UAE's Foreign Ministry declined to confirm the meeting but says it shares "all relevant intelligence" with Israel.

Whether Sheikh Mohamed grants the denial Netanyahu sought remains unknown.
