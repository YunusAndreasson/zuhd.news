São Paulo — Investors fear Bolsonaro could unwind Lula's climate-finance programmes.

[Brazil](country:BR)'s Eco Invest programme has mobilised 190 billion reais ($37.83 billion) since 2024.

Clean-energy capital is mobile, the business council CEBDS warns, so regulatory uncertainty alone can send investors elsewhere.

His campaign pledges to end illegal deforestation by 2029, a year ahead of Lula, but won't say if green finance stays.

A Supreme Court justice issued an arrest warrant for his brother Eduardo before the 25 October runoff.
