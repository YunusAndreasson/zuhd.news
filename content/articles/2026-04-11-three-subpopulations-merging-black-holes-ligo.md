Pasadena — Black hole mergers are 3 different phenomena. LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA data identifies 3 subpopulations with distinct masses, spins, and merger rates, each pointing to a different formation mechanism. The March 18 arXiv preprint awaits peer review; the 3-class taxonomy could reshape stellar evolution models.
