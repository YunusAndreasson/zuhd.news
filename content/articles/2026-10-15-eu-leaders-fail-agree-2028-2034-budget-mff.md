Brussels — EU leaders rejected the 1.76-trillion-euro budget, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

A proposal to cut 140 billion euros also lacked enough support, a European source told TASS.

Parliament President Roberta Metsola had reported unprecedented divisions between net contributors and beneficiaries, TASS said.

Leaders meet again at an extraordinary summit on 26 November.
