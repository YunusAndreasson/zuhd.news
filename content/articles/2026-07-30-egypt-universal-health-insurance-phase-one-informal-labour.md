Cairo — Unpaid premiums now block patients' care.

Health officials warned before the 2018 rollout that a payroll-based levy would fail in an economy built on informal work.

[Egypt](country:EG) is expanding the scheme to five more governorates, starting with Minya, despite unresolved medicine shortages.
