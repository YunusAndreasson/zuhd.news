Nairobi — Local capacity beat external emergency funding in Africa's latest Ebola response, Catherine Kyobutungi argues.

Donor emergency grants are slower and less durable than embedded local scientific expertise.

Whether this converts into sustained investment in African research institutions remains unresolved.
