United States — Empire Market's 4 million transactions earned its co-creator 40 years.

Raheim Hamilton, 30, also forfeits more than $100 million in bitcoin and several Virginia properties.

Prosecutors say drugs made up about $375 million of the sales, and cryptocurrency mixers let users launder the proceeds.

Empire Market vanished in summer 2020, and its operators stole an estimated $30 million from users.

Co-owner Thomas Pavey, who pleaded guilty last year, faces sentencing in the [US](country:US) later this month.
