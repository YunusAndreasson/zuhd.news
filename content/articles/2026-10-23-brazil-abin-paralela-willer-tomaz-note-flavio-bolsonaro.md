Brasília — Police found a $180,000 note naming an indicted intelligence chief.

The note belongs to lawyer Willer Tomaz, a friend of presidential candidate Flávio Bolsonaro.

[Brazil](country:BR)'s Federal Police say Paulo Fortunato's Abin unit used Israeli spyware to track politicians and journalists without court orders.

Fortunato's defence denies police claims that he managed the purchase of the spyware.

Flávio Bolsonaro's runoff for the presidency is days away.
