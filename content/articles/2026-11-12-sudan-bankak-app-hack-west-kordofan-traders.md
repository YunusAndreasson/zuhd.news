West Kordofan — 50 Sudanese bank customers report millions stolen via app.

Losses run 18 million to 45 million Sudanese pounds per person, the Dar Hamar Emergency Rooms say.

Victims say the bank blames fraudulent links, but one customer says he opened none and received no messages.

The Emergency Rooms are preparing collective lawsuits against the bank's management and want its electronic banking licences suspended.
