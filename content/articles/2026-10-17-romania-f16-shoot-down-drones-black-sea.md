Sulina — Romanian F-16s shot down 2 jet-powered Geran drones.

Seven crew are missing from 2 chemical tankers attacked off [Romania](country:RO)'s coast on Thursday.

Geran-family drones fly up to 600 km/h, faster and higher than Russia's older models, making them harder to stop.

The defence ministry did not say which country launched the drones.

NATO's North Atlantic Council discusses the incident on Tuesday, Foreign Minister Oana Toiu said.
