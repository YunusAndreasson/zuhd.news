London — Steam turned barley straw into a drug-trapping sponge.

Drug residues that treatment plants miss keep entering rivers and drinking supplies.

Steam activation nearly quadrupled its surface area, from 119 to 423 square meters per gram.

Pelletizing lets the biochar work in flow-through filters that loose powder would clog.
