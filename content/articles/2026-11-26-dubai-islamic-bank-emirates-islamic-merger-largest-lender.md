Dubai — Dubai Islamic Bank will buy Emirates Islamic entirely with new shares.

The combined group would be the world's second-largest listed Islamic banking group by assets.

Emirates NBD, Dubai's largest bank, receives the new shares as payment. It delisted Emirates Islamic in June 2025.

Reports so far give no exchange ratio or closing date.
