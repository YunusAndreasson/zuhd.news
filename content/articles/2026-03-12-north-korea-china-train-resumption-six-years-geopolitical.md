North Korea's sealed border with China reopened Thursday. Closed since 2020 when Pyongyang shut its borders over COVID, the route is the main channel for Chinese food and fuel into North Korea. The US is simultaneously waging war on Iran and seeking to re-engage Pyongyang, overtures North Korea has not acknowledged.
