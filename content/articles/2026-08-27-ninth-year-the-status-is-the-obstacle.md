Cox's Bazar — Camp residents mark nine years since fleeing Myanmar's 2017 crackdown.

Neither justice nor return has arrived, so displacement is becoming permanent rather than temporary.

Refugees here are barred from citizenship in [Myanmar](country:MM), the state that expelled them and still won't take them back.

No resettlement date or citizenship path has been set for any camp resident.
