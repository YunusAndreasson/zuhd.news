4 astronauts docked at the International Space Station. The crew arrived Saturday aboard a SpaceX Crew Dragon capsule for an 8-to-9 month mission. They replace the crew evacuated on January 14 in the National Aeronautics and Space Administration's first medical evacuation in 65 years of human spaceflight. NASA has not identified which crew member had the emergency or what the condition was.
