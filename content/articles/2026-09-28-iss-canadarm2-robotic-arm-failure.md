Houston — The ISS's 58-foot Canadarm2 stopped moving after a routine maneuver.

Without it, NASA loses its only way to berth cargo ships that can't dock alone.

Cygnus and Japan's HTV-X vehicles need the 2-ton arm to grab and berth them, while Dragon and Starliner dock on their own.

NASA says it's still unclear whether the failure is a software glitch or a hardware fault.

SpaceX plans to retire Dragon, the last vehicle the arm can berth, within 4 years.
