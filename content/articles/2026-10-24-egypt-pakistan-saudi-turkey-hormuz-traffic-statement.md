Istanbul — Four foreign ministers demanded Hormuz reopen, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

Hormuz traffic averaged 3.7 ships a day as of 18 October, 10% below normal.

[Egypt](country:EG), [Pakistan](country:PK), [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) and [Turkey](country:TR) oppose any restrictions on civilian shipping and want diplomacy under the Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 2 November an 8% chance.
