United States — Bitcoin fell from about $74,000 to briefly below $62,000 last week.

OKX founder Star Xu accuses Binance's Changpeng Zhao of misleading customers about the selloff.

Xu blames Binance's lending and yield products for the leverage behind October 2025's $19 billion liquidation in a day.

"Correlation does not prove causation," wrote Changpeng Zhao, Binance's founder, who says Binance did not sell.

Binance co-founder Yi He reports a $97.3 million loss since 17 October and calls the decline temporary.
