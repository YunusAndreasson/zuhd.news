Greifswald — Solar peatlands generate power, bird habitat, and carbon.

Drained peat emits carbon; [Germany](country:DE)'s University of Greifswald found that rewetting it and adding solar panels turns degraded land into a power source, carbon sink, and bird habitat.

The study is a first-of-its-kind result; scaled deployment requires broader site assessment.
