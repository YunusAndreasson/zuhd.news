Gadchiroli — Modi says 500 million left extreme poverty in 12 years.

He also declared the Maoist insurgency, active in 125 districts in 2014, eliminated.

He credited a World Bank report and welfare schemes, including 40 million houses and 18,000 electrified villages.

Maharashtra's Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis said Gadchiroli, long hit by insurgency, is heading to become a major steel hub.
