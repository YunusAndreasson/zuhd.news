New York — [Ukraine](country:UA)'s foreign minister met his Iranian counterpart on Wednesday.

[Russia](country:RU) has used Iranian Shahed drones against Ukrainian cities for 4 years.

A July Ukrainian drone strike on an Iranian ship killed 1 sailor, [Iran](country:IR)'s foreign ministry said.

Vladimir Putin said on 1 September that Russia would keep supplying Iran with goods.

Neither minister has said what steps they agreed to prevent escalation.
