London — [Germany](country:DE), not [Britain](country:GB), will fly the rockets.

That leaves Britain's satellite access hostage to another country's scheduling decisions.

A satellite you cannot launch yourself is a capability an ally can withhold by simply rescheduling, without any embargo.

The government has not said what happens to unallocated launcher funding once earmarked for Orbex.
