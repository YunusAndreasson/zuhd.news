Tashkent — [Uzbekistan](country:UZ) is building a formal employment pathway to the [US](country:US).

The government is negotiating bilateral training agreements that convert irregular border crossings into legal job placements — a formal track for one of Central Asia's largest labor exporters.

No timeline or placement target has been made public.
