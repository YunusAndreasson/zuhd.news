Nigeria — Dangote Cement ends London listing plan after 6 months.

It wanted overseas investors and capital, and picked London over Dubai in July for speed.

The company secretary's filing cites market conditions and strategic priorities, and says shares stay listed on the Nigerian Exchange.

The company says the decision does not affect operations, and its nine-month profit rose 31%.

The company has not said when it might revisit London.
