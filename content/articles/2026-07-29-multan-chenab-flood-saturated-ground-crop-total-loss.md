Multan — 22 villages sit under 3-4 feet of water.

Cotton and fodder are submerged across [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Jalalpur Pirwala tehsil, worst hit, after a second monsoon spell fell on already-saturated ground.

No evacuation order has been issued; the district administration says the Chenab's 110,000-cusec flow is now receding.
