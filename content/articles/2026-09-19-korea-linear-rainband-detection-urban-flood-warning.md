Seoul — A storm can regenerate over one neighborhood for hours.

That repetition, not rainfall totals, is what causes lethal urban flooding.

[South Korea](country:KR)'s civil engineering institute is adding early detection for these rainbands to Seoul's flood forecasting platform.

The Han River Flood Control Office already runs the platform gaining the upgrade.
