Alexandria — [Egypt](country:EG) rejected Abu Dhabi's $608 million bid for its port stake.

AD Ports holds 51.3% of Alexandria Container and Cargo Handling via [UAE](country:AE)-backed subsidiaries; this is the second rejection in six months.

Egypt said the bid undervalues the company; no revised offer has been announced.
