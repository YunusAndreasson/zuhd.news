Tehran — [Iran](country:IR)'s Revolutionary Guards say 3 tankers hit mines in Hormuz.

The Guards say the tankers took routes Tehran had not authorized.

They say the ships switched off tracking systems in the strait's southern part, and the vessels are still burning.

No casualties have been reported, and no independent source has confirmed the explosions.

Polymarket traders give a [US](country:US)-Iran Hormuz agreement by 31 December a 34% chance.
