Armed attackers killed at least 50 in Zamfara, Nigeria. The raiders struck Tungan Dutse village Thursday night, burning homes, shooting those who fled, and abducting women and children. Zamfara has now seen 2 mass killings in a week, despite a recent US troop deployment to Nigeria.
