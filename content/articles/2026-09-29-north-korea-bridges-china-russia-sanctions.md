Sinuiju — A 3,000-metre bridge to China could open by Oct. 6, over a decade after completion.

Road links turn North Korea's trickle of rail trade with Moscow and Beijing into truck traffic, harder to reverse.

[Russia](country:RU)'s new Tumen River bridge opened Sept. 7, following North Korean troops and munitions sent for the Ukraine war since 2023.

Pyongyang is pacing trade growth with each patron to avoid excessive dependence on either, a former South Korean intelligence official said, as the Sinuiju crossing awaits its formal opening.
