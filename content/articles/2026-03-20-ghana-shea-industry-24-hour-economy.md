Ghana ships most shea unprocessed, losing billions. Women in Ghana's north harvest nuts that supply a $3.8B global ingredient market, nearly all processed abroad. Ghana's 24-Hour Economy initiative aims to shift processing home, with Tuesday's conference naming no investment figure or timeline.
