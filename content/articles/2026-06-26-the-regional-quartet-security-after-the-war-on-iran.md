Cairo — 4 Muslim states opened a post-war security forum.

Established after the US-Israel war on [Iran](country:IR), the forum coordinates Gulf, Lebanon, and Red Sea security without Washington.

The [United Arab Emirates](country:AE), relying on Israeli military cooperation, has stayed outside the forum, sources said.
