Pakistan is absorbing $100 oil rather than raise prices. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif overruled IMF-aligned cabinet members to freeze petroleum prices, backed by army chief Asim Munir. Pakistan's next price review is March 15, when the IMF will demand normalization as a condition for any resumed aid agreement.
