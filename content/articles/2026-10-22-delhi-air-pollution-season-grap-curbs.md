Delhi — Three monitoring stations logged "very poor" air above 300.

The capital's index stayed "poor" for a second day, with no relief forecast.

Dussehra smoke trapped by still, damp night air lifted the index 66 points in a day, to 245.

A government forecast puts stubble burning's share of pollution at 15.9% on Friday, up from 3.9%.
