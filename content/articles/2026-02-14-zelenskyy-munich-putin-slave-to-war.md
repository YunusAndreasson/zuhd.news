Ukraine's president criticized peace proposals at the Munich Security Conference. Volodymyr Zelenskyy said the United States keeps asking Ukraine to concede while putting no pressure on Russia. He described Russian President Vladimir Putin as "a slave of war" and compared the push to divide Ukraine to the 1938 Munich Agreement that sacrificed Czechoslovakia to Nazi Germany. North Atlantic Treaty Organization Secretary General Mark Rutte estimated 65,000 Russian soldiers killed or seriously wounded in the past 2 months.
