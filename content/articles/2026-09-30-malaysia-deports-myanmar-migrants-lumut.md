Lumut — 1,476 detainees sailed for Myanmar on navy ships.

Neither government has said how many are Rohingya, a people facing persecution in [Myanmar](country:MM).

The UN refugee agency says it had no role in the operation, and [Malaysia](country:MY) never signed the 1951 Refugee Convention.

Malaysia's interior ministry calls the returns voluntary and says Myanmar's government supplied the list.

Anwar Ibrahim plans to invite Min Aung Hlaing to Malaysia to speed the rest of the 5,000.
