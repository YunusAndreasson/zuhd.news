Manama — A tanker burned in [Iraq](country:IQ)'s waters.

Gulf states get almost all drinking water from desalination plants on that coast.

Oil from burning tankers fouls intake pipes, and Gulf spills quadrupled by March, University of South Florida found.

[Iran](country:IR)'s Revolutionary Guard warned tanker crews in [Bahrain](country:BH) and [Kuwait](country:KW) to flee port waters.
