New Delhi — India's one-third quota for women lawmakers stays unimplemented.

Kiren Rijiju ties implementation to a post-2026 census delimitation that would shift Lok Sabha seats from south toward Uttar Pradesh and Bihar.

Delimitation has been frozen since 1976; Rijiju wants it revived in time for 2029 elections.
