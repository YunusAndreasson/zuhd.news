Sriharikota — Vikram-1 is [India](country:IN)'s first orbital rocket built entirely outside ISRO.

Skyroot Aerospace's carbon-composite, 3D-printed-engine vehicle targets a 450 km orbit, ending the state's monopoly on launching Indian satellites into space.

Four technology-demonstration payloads ride along; the flight's data will shape Skyroot's push toward a regular launch cadence.
