Nigeria signed to host the 2027 Intra-African Trade Fair. The African Continental Free Trade Area spans 54 nations, with intra-African trade still below 15% of the continent's total — against 60% within the EU. The 5th edition in Lagos will test whether Africa's largest economy can translate trade policy into actual cross-border commerce.
