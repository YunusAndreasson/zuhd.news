Madrid — 1,000 TikTok abuse videos drew 48 million views, investigators found.

[Spain](country:ES)'s youth ministry is weighing a suspension and has asked prosecutors to act.

Fact-checkers Maldita.es monitored TikTok for 35 days and say its algorithm recommended 19 livestreams by minors in 3 days.

Minister Sira Rego demanded an urgent TikTok meeting, while the EU threatens fines over its feed.
