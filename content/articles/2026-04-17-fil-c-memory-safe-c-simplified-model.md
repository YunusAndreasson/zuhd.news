San Francisco — Fil-C makes C memory-safe at compile time. The dialect adds capability-like pointer bounds, catching buffer overflows and use-after-free without a full language rewrite or port to Rust. NSA and CISA mandate memory-safe languages for new federal code; Fil-C gives C codebases a port-free path.
