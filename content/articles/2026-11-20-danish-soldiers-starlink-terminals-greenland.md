Nuuk — Danish soldiers use banned Starlink terminals, politician Pele Broberg alleges.

Greenlanders cannot legally buy the service, and Greenland lacks the resources to enforce the ban.

The Telecommunications Act bans Starlink outside state operator Tusass, and use that interferes with no radio traffic gets low enforcement priority.

The government says it has no information confirming that Danish soldiers use private terminals.

Broberg forced a parliamentary debate on the claim on 20 October, and the government still defends the Tusass monopoly.
