Supermicro's co-founder allegedly sent $2.5B in chips to China. Prosecutors say co-founder Wally Liaw routed Nvidia B200 graphics processors through a Southeast Asian front company and used hair dryers to remove serial stickers. Supermicro shares fell 25% as co-defendant Steven Chang remains at large.
