Moscow — Roskomnadzor added Bluesky to Russia's banned websites registry. Built on AT Protocol to resist censorship, Bluesky became Russia's latest casualty — network-layer bans render decentralized protocols irrelevant to state-level blocks. Russia is now blocking new platforms before they build critical mass inside the country.
