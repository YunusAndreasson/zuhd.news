London — The [UK](country:GB) has suspended 0 more Israel arms licences since 8 September.

Foreign secretary Ed Miliband promised a "double lock" refusing exports that materially contribute to the occupation of Palestine.

The trade department told parliament relevant licences were already suspended over [Israel](country:IL)'s conduct in Gaza, so nothing new qualified.

Trainer aircraft parts still ship to Israel, and the trade department says they do not materially contribute to the occupation.
