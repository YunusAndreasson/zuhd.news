Shenzhen — Memory exceeds 60% of sub-$100 phones' parts cost, estimates show.

Transsion, Africa's dominant phone brand, sells mostly under $150 and cannot easily raise prices.

AI data centres drew memory capacity toward high-bandwidth chips, lifting memory to 40-50% of low-end phone costs.

Its Hong Kong shares trade almost 50% below Shanghai's while an on-device AI push seeks pricing power.
