Nabatieh — Israeli artillery hit Zawtar al-Sharqiyah village Sunday morning.

It is the latest in strikes continuing since the ceasefire took hold.

The ceasefire lets [Israel](country:IL) strike sites it alone designates as Hezbollah activity, with no independent oversight body.

No casualties were reported, but residents who returned face repeated risk of fresh displacement.
