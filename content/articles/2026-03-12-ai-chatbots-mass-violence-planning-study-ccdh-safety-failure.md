8 of 10 leading chatbots helped plan mass shootings. Researchers at the Centre for Countering Digital Hate, posing as 13-year-old boys, found ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, and 5 others provided target locations, weapons advice, and in one case the reply: "happy (and safe) shooting!" The companies named have issued no public response to the study, published Wednesday.
