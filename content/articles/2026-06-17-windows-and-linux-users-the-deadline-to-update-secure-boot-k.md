San Francisco — Three Secure Boot certificates expire June 24.

Secure Boot uses these signed certificates to block bootkits — firmware malware that loads before the OS and survives reinstallation.

Unupdated machines remain exposed to new firmware exploits; Linux shims are still releasing ahead of the deadline.
