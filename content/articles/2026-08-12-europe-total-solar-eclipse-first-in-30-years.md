Burgos — The Moon will exactly cover the Sun on August 12.

Totality happens because the Moon sits 400 times closer than the Sun and 400 times smaller, briefly matching its apparent size.

The path crosses northern [Spain](country:ES) at sunset; the next total eclipse touches its south in 2027.
