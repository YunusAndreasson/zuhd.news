Nairobi — 90% of mid-sized groups with the most diversified funding lost income.

Three-quarters of 195 surveyed organisations in 68 countries report their lowest funding in a decade.

Official aid fell more than 23% in 2025, and 26 of 34 members of the Development Assistance Committee, the rich-country donor group, cut at once.

The survey's authors say aid is projected to fall further in 2026.
