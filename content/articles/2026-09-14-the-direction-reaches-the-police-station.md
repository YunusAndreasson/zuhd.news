Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Supreme Court ordered gender-sensitive police training.

The directive binds every provincial police station handling domestic-violence complaints, not just courts.

Justice Kakar called domestic killings a "crime of difficult proof," since evidence dies behind closed doors.

No timeline was set for when provincial police must begin the training.
