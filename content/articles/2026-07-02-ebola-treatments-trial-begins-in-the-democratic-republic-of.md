Kinshasa — The first patient has enrolled in a live-outbreak drug trial.

No approved treatment exists for the Bundibugyo strain now killing people in the [Democratic Republic of Congo](country:CD); 1,400 cases and 438 deaths are confirmed, WHO said.

Oxford, Belgian and Congolese scientists are jointly running the trial as the outbreak continues.
