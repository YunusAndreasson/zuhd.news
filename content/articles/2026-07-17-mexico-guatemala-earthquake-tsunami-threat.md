Huixtla — A 7.3-magnitude quake struck 30 miles offshore.

The shallow rupture on the Cocos-North America subduction boundary is the geometry that generates destructive tsunamis, prompting warnings across [Mexico](country:MX), Guatemala and El Salvador.

Coastal residents were told to avoid beaches while forecasters track waves up to 1 meter above tide level.
