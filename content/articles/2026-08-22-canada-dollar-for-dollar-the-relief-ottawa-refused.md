Ottawa — [Canada](country:CA) rejected [US](country:US) relief on heavy trucks.

The refusal leaves 50% tariffs intact on $20 billion in Canadian exports.

Ottawa retaliates dollar-for-dollar, not proportionally, targeting steel, dairy and electronics — sectors picked for political pain in Washington.

Counter-tariffs start September 8; Greer says Washington will take further, unspecified retaliatory steps.
