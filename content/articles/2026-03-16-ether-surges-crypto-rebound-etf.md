Ether gained 10% Monday, tripling bitcoin's advance. US ether ETFs pulled in $160m last week, their strongest inflow since January, as corporate buyers rotated from bitcoin. ETH remains down 50% from its August high, and analysts are watching whether the bitcoin-to-ether rotation holds.
