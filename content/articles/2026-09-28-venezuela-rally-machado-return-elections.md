Her return could decide whether [Venezuela](country:VE) holds its first free vote in years.

Courts loyal to Maduro barred Machado from last year's vote, which her stand-in is believed to have won.

The government denies blocking her return, though it freed dozens of prisoners last week after [US](country:US)-backed talks.
