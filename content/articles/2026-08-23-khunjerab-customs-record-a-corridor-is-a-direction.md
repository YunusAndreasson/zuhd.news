Gilgit — Rs15 billion in duty crossed one mountain pass.

Imports from [China](country:CN) outweigh what [Pakistan](country:PK) sells back more than five to one.

Customs officials say the crossing, built for two-way trade, now mainly moves Chinese goods inward.

Traders want Islamabad to press Beijing on customs restrictions before the pass shuts for winter.
