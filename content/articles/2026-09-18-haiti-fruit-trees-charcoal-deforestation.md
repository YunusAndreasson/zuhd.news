Roseaux — Jean Noël turned his fruit trees into charcoal.

Families are burning their only lasting asset for one-time cash.

Some 1.4 million Haitians are displaced, the UN says, and the state offers no fuel aid or crop insurance.

Each felled tree removes cover that would blunt [Haiti](country:HT)'s next storm.
