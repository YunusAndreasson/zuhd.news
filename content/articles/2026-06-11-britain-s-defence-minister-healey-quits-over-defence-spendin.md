London — Defence Secretary John Healey resigned after Treasury capped UK military spending at 2.68% of GDP.

[Britain](country:GB) now leads NATO's Hormuz and Arctic missions simultaneously, with a budget Healey called insufficient for those commitments.

Prime Minister Starmer has not named a replacement, leaving the Defence Investment Plan unpublished since autumn 2025.
