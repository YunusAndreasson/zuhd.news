La Paz — Crypto transaction volume jumped 630% since 2024, central bank data show.

[Bolivia](country:BO)'s central bank lifted its crypto ban that year; officials now weigh letting the Tether stablecoin USDT circulate legally.

Any rollout needs anti-money-laundering rules, since Bolivia remains on the financial crime watchdog's grey list.
