America's asylum backlog would take 173 years to clear. 180,000 Afghans who aided US forces are stuck in the pipeline after the Trump administration's January 2025 refugee ban froze all resettlement. By March 31, when the last Qatar transit camp closes, the State Department is offering $4,500 per person to walk away.
