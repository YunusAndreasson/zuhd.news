Sabana de Torres — Ex-Choneros boss Fito's wife and daughter were arrested in [Colombia](country:CO).

Ecuadorian investigators allege they sought alliances with Colombian gangs to move arms, drugs and money.

Both were wanted in [Ecuador](country:EC)'s Blanqueo Fito laundering case, which convicted 6 people from his circle on 28 May.

Fito's brother, alias Javi, expelled from Bogotá in June, awaits trial in the same case.
