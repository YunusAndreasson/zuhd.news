Qamishli — Nowruz fires returned to Syrian Kurdish homes. Syria's new government vowed to incorporate Kurdish culture into the post-Assad state. The pledge faces scrutiny as the same government has imposed alcohol bans and dress codes in other areas while autonomy negotiations in northeastern Syria continue.
