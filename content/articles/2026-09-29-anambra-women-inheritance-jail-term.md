Awka — Anambra warns that denying women inheritance risks 4 years' jail.

Section 22 of the state's 2017 violence law makes gender-based disinheritance an offence.

Attorney-General Tobechukwu Nweke ordered police to send case files to his office, where a gender-violence unit prosecutes.

Supreme Court rulings already declare such exclusion unconstitutional, yet some people in [Nigeria](country:NG) still practise it, Nweke said.
