Peshawar — 11 unelected advisers now help run Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s federal government named them after imposing emergency and governor's rule.

Former officials, including 2 ex-tax chiefs, advise Governor Faisal Karim Kundi on home affairs, finance, law and energy.

One adviser, Samar Haroon Bilour, is a Pakistan Muslim League-N lawmaker, and another is a retired lieutenant general.

Officials have not said how long the council will serve.
