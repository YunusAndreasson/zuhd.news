San Jose — Hackers turned Cisco routers into attack launchpads.

A router breach survives even after every device it serves gets cleaned.

Researchers say the [China](country:CN)-linked group, named Fire Ant, compromised the "trust layer" network hardware depends on.

The group is using this foothold to launch further attacks, researchers say.
