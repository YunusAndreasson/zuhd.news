Pakistan's stocks surged 2.4%, defying $102-a-barrel oil. The Pakistan Stock Exchange had fallen 4 straight days as the Hormuz war spooked investors into panic selling. The State Bank is expected to raise interest rates at its next policy meeting as Hormuz-driven inflation accelerates.
