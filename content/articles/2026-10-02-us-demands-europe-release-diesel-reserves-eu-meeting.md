Brussels — US diesel has hit $6.39 a gallon, up 70% since the Iran war began.

Washington told Europe to release strategic diesel reserves immediately, and EU member states meet Friday.

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent says the [US](country:US) released 172 million barrels under a March International Energy Agency pact and wants allies to match.

The French presidency says Washington made no such demand when Emmanuel Macron met Donald Trump last week.

Macron plans a Group of Seven leaders' video call, expected mid-October, on coordinating reserve releases.
