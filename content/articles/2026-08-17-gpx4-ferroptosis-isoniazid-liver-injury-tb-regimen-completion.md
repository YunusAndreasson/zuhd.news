London — Liver damage forces patients off tuberculosis treatment worldwide.

An interrupted TB course is how drug resistance spreads across a disease killing over a million yearly.

Researchers found Schisandra lignans block degradation of GPX4, an enzyme that halts iron-driven cell death.

The finding is preliminary and untested in humans on isoniazid, though Schisandra already sells commercially.
