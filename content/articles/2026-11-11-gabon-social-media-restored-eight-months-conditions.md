Libreville — Gabon denied 1.84 million people social media for 248 days.

[Gabon](country:GA)'s regulator lifted the block on 24 October but kept a stricter legal framework.

The new rules impose joint liability on those who create and share illegal content, and an October law regulates AI-generated content.

Whether President Oligui Nguema restores digital freedoms or builds a state-controlled online model is unresolved.
