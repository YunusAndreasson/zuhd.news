Haldwani — Police filed a case over the word "untouchability."

Calling out a caste practice, not the practice, is now the alleged offense in [India](country:IN)'s Uttarakhand state.

Rahul Gandhi called an August 11 "purification" ritual untouchability, prompting a Dalit attendee to file a formal police complaint against him.

Congress plans nationwide protests Monday evening against what it calls the case's frivolous basis.
