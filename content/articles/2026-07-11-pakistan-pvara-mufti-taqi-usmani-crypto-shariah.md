Karachi — Mufti Taqi Usmani ruled crypto is not wealth.

His June 10 fatwa called crypto fictitious account entries; [Pakistan](country:PK)'s regulator, courting 40 million crypto users, sought a narrower, technology-specific review.

Whether Pakistan's planned stablecoin and tokenised assets can win scholarly approval remains open.
