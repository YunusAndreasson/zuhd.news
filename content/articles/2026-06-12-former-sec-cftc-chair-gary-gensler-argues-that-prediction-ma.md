Washington — Former US securities regulator Gary Gensler argues prediction markets must follow state gambling rules.

Kalshi argues its sports contracts are federally regulated derivatives, exempt from state gambling rules; a federal judge rejected that claim in March.

The Sixth Circuit's verdict sets whether prediction platforms can operate nationally without state gambling licences.
