Abu Dhabi — ADNOC Gas netted $1.34 billion through a disrupted Hormuz.

Its $4 billion 2026 profit guidance assumes fourth-quarter exports match the third.

Fujairah, outside the strait, now carries crude exports, but the Ruwais liquefied natural gas plant sits inside the Gulf.

Chief executive Fatema Mohamed Al Nuaimi said the company has made no final decision on new routes.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 31 December a 34% chance, down 5 points in a week.
