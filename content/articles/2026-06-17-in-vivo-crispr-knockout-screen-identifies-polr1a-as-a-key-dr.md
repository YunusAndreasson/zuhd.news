Hershey — One gene drives melanoma's spread; CRISPR found it.

The gene, Polr1a, is an RNA polymerase subunit; blocking it with compound CX-5461 cut tumor spread in mice and outperformed a human-designed benchmark, the study found.

CX-5461 showed no toxicity in mice at the doses tested; no human trials have been announced.
