Cambridge Bay — Seawater pumps added 26cm to Arctic sea ice.

Thickened ice is brighter — reflecting heat — but saltier; brine pores may speed summer melt, contradicting the method's premise.

[Canada](country:CA)'s Real Ice found melt delayed 7-10 days, but [Norway](country:NO)'s Arctic Reflections found no delay; both continue trials.
