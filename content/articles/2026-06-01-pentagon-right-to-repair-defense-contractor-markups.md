Washington — Pentagon pays $47,000 for a knob worth $15.

As [US](country:US) weapons contractors consolidated from dozens to 5, the Pentagon lost repair rights; maintenance can reach 70% of program lifetime cost, analysts say.

Congress has not passed a Pentagon right-to-repair law; contractors decide whether and when to service deployed gear.
