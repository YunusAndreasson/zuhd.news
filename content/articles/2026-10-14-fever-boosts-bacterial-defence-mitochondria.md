United Kingdom — The peptide LL-37 kills bacteria better at fever temperature.

Fever raises antibacterial killing yet limits mitochondrial damage when no pathogen is present, the study finds.

Peptides from other animals have temperature sensitivities tuned to their body temperatures, and cold-blooded animals avoid mitochondrial damage another way.

Whether suppressing fever with antipyretics blunts the effect in patients, the abstract does not say.
