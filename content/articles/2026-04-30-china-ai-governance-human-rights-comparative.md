Beijing — [China](country:CN)'s AI rules outnumber any democracy's, analysts say, mandating algorithm filings, deepfake labels, and generative-content registration.

The [US](country:US) and EU lack equivalent enforcement density, researchers argue.

Developing-country regulators now choose between Beijing's compliance model and lighter frameworks, researchers say.
