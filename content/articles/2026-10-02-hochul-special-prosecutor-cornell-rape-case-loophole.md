Ithaca — Hochul pulls alleged Cornell gang-rape case from local prosecutor.

Attorney General Letitia James now controls the investigation, and Hochul vows to close a legal loophole.

New York law counts intoxication as incapacity only when someone gave the drugs or alcohol without consent.

District Attorney Matthew Van Houten says Cornell did not give prosecutors the full picture in 2024.

Hochul will work with the legislature on a June bill covering voluntary intoxication.
