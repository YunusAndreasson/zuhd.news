Bengaluru — A microbe may cement Mars landing pads.

Astronaut-designate Shubhanshu Shukla found bacteria still bind soil into cement-like material even amid Martian perchlorate salts once thought lethal to them.

[India](country:IN)'s crewed Gaganyaan mission launches in 2028, well before any Mars construction test.
