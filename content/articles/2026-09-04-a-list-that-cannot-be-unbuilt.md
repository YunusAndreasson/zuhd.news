Washington — Documents show a $5 million voter database plan.

Voter rolls are run by states so no single federal file has existed.

U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement would hold the citizenship-verification layer, merging voter and immigration records into one list.

The agency has not said how it would verify citizenship claims.
