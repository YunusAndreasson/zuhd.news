Washington — 103 of 201 voting Democrats backed cutting Israel aid.

The Massie amendment to strip $3.3 billion failed 104-314, but a majority of voting Democrats supported it, splitting leadership: Whip Katherine Clark for, Hakeem Jeffries and Pete Aguilar against.

The underlying bill, which still funds [Israel](country:IL)'s military, now awaits a full House vote.
