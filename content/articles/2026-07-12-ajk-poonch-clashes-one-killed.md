Muzaffarabad — One dead as police retook a blockaded post.

Officers moved to reopen the Arja–Rawalakot road after banned group Joint Awami Action Committee activists blocked it and allegedly beat a trader on July 1.

Divisional commissioner Sardar Waheed Khan gave no word on further arrests or reopening.
