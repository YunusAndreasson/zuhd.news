London — 1,414 eye images, now open to any researcher.

Public data lets outside labs audit which patients an eyelid-screening model gets wrong.

Eyelid shape varies by ancestry, so a model trained on one population can misread another's disorders entirely.

The published dataset does not state its patients' demographic makeup.
