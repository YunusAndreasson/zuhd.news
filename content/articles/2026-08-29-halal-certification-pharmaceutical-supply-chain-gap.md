Kuala Lumpur — Drug makers decide ingredients years before any fatwa.

About two billion Muslim patients rely on rulings that arrive after launch.

[Malaysia](country:MY), [Indonesia](country:ID) and Gulf regulators certify separately, so the same drug molecule can get different rulings.

Most patients meanwhile rely on the ḍarūrah exception permitting the otherwise-forbidden to save a life.
