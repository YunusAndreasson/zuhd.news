Nairobi — Cryptococcal meningitis kills African HIV patients who go undiagnosed — yet antifungal treatment exists.

Diagnostic tests are scarce in under-resourced health systems where the infection is most prevalent, doctors say.

Health funding rarely reaches cryptococcal diagnostics; doctors say expanded testing could prevent most deaths.
