Puerto Jiménez — Drug runners launder cash through illegal gold mines. The trafficking networks that moved 46.5 tons through Costa Rica in 2025 — 70% more than 2024 — use Corcovado National Park for illegal gold mining and money laundering. 8 rangers with no air or armed backup face these crews inside one of the world's most biodiverse reserves.
