Rotriak — Five years on, the 2021 floods never receded.

Contaminants from over 50 submerged oil wells now leach into the water families drink and fish.

Flooding removed the lined pits that once contained produced water, releasing heavy metals into the marshes below Rotriak.

Clinicians report rising birth defects, but no study has funded the exposure link.
