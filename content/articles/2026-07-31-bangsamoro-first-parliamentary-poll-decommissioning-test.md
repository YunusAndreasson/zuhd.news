Cotabato City — Moro fighters face their first ballot test.

The [Philippines](country:PH)' Bangsamoro region holds its first elected parliament on September 14, testing a 2014 deal that ended a four-decade rebellion.

The peace process continues regardless of the count; decommissioning and revenue transfers remain unresolved.
