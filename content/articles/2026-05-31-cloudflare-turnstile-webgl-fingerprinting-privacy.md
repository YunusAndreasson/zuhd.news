San Francisco — Turnstile blocks privacy-mode browsers via fingerprinting.

Cloudflare marketed Turnstile as a privacy-preserving CAPTCHA alternative, but its WebGL requirement exposes device configurations to tracking.

WebKitGTK browsers are already excluded; Firefox users with fingerprint resistance enabled face the same lockout.
