Washington — NASA satellites found widespread ocean nutrient stress.

Surface warming stratifies seawater, blocking the upwelling that feeds phytoplankton — the ocean's main carbon sink and food-web base.

Researchers say nutrient stress is an early satellite-visible warning of ecosystem decline beneath a still-blue surface.
