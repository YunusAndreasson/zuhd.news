A mosque bombing in Islamabad killed 31 worshippers. Pakistan's military struck 7 Tehrik-i-Taliban Pakistan and Islamic State camps along the Afghan border in retaliation. Kabul has not commented, and cross-border tensions risk collapsing a fragile Qatar-mediated ceasefire.
