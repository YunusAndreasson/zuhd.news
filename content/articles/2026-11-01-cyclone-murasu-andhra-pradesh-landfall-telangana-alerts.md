Hyderabad — Six Telangana districts face very heavy rain on Monday.

The India Meteorological Department expects Murasu to cross Andhra Pradesh's coast Tuesday morning.

An orange alert means heavy to very heavy rain, issued for Khammam, Nalgonda, Suryapet, Mahabubnagar, Nagarkurnool and Wanaparthy.

Yellow alerts for heavy rain cover Hyderabad, Rangareddy, Medchal Malkajgiri, Jangaon and Yadadri Bhuvanagiri on Monday.
