Mountain View — Alphabet's capex jumped 114% to $51.4 billion in one quarter.

Free cash flow fell to minus $9.1 billion, and shares dropped 10% after hours.

Alphabet's chief business officer blamed AI Mode, a chatbot interface for search, for slower search ad growth and a $1.4 billion advertising miss.

Google Cloud revenue more than doubled to $30.71 billion, with a $561 billion backlog.

Alphabet has not priced 2027 capex, and Check Capital's Jeremy Saltsburg said a $300 billion figure could scare the market.
