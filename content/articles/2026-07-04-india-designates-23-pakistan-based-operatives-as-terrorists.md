New Delhi — 80 individuals now named terrorists by name, not group.

[India](country:IN)'s Home Ministry added 23 handlers to its terror list under a 2019 law letting agencies freeze assets and prosecute people directly, without banning an organization.

Agencies gain authority to seize property and block financing; no Pakistani outlet has yet named the accused.
