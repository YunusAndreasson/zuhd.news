Los Angeles — NZXT charged renters more than a PC costs. 19,322 customers enrolled in Flex, paying $79–$279/month for systems with specs they didn't receive, falsely marketed as rent-to-own. A judge must still approve the settlement; NZXT must maintain changed practices until December 2027.
