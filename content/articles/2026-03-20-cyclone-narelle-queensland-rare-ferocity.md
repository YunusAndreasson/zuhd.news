Category 4 Cyclone Narelle struck Queensland's northern tip. Australia's Bureau of Meteorology forecast the strongest winds in decades for remote coastal towns and Indigenous communities with few evacuation routes. Queensland Premier David Crisafulli warned conditions would worsen before easing and that flash flooding threatened low-lying areas.
