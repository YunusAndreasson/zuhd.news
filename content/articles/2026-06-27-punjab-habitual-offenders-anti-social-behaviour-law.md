Lahore — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Punjab bill classifies "annoyance" alongside organized crime.

The bill lets intelligence committees freeze accounts and mandate monitors on arrest history — not convictions — across a province of 120 million.

The Punjab Assembly votes Sunday; appeals run through 3 executive layers before reaching a tribunal.
