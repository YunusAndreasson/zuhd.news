Cotonou — A reef flagged dead in 1960s is alive.

Sonar and National Geographic's Deep Sea Camera System found the deep-water reef off [Benin](country:BJ) healthy after six decades of assumption, not survey.

Most of Africa's deep-sea coral remains unsurveyed, leaving other "dead" reefs unchecked.
