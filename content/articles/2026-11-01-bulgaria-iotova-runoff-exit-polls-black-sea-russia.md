Sofia — Iotova leads [Bulgaria](country:BG)'s run-off with 51%, exit polls show.

She opposes military aid to [Ukraine](country:UA), as 21 people remain missing from Black Sea drone attacks.

Three exit polls give her 50.7% to 52.5% against Gyurov's 43.4% to 44%, in a vote with 27% to 29% turnout.

Gyurov faulted her for not blaming [Russia](country:RU), while the government says no evidence directly links Moscow.

Official results are due Monday, and she would be the country's first elected female president.
