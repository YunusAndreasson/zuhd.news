Dilling — Rapid Support Forces drones killed 4 civilians in residential neighbourhoods, a doctors' network says.

A child and a woman died, and others were injured, the Sudan Doctors Network said.

Drones struck El Tomat, El Malikiyah and El Ma'asir in [Sudan](country:SD) on 2 October, the network said.

The network asks the UN and rights groups to condemn the attacks and hold those responsible accountable.
