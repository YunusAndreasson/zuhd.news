London — Gut bacteria mediate fasting's protection against myelin loss.

In a mouse study, chronic stress degraded myelin; intermittent fasting reshaped gut bacteria and reversed both the myelin damage and depression-like behaviors.

The study used mice under chronic restraint stress; human clinical data does not yet exist.
