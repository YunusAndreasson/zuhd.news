Tehran — IRGC destroyed two tankers off Hormuz Sunday.

The Revolutionary Guard says [US](country:US) pressure forced the vessels onto a route it has declared unsafe for any oil or gas shipment.

[Iran](country:IR) has kept the Strait of Hormuz closed to all shipping since early July.
