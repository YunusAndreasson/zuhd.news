Karachi — Spot gold hit $4,392 an ounce Wednesday.

For families in inflation-hit economies, gold is savings, not a trading position.

Fed rate-cut bets pushed gold up, and [Pakistan](country:PK)'s rupee depreciation adds further to the local price.

The Federal Reserve's September decision hinges on Wednesday's US inflation data.
