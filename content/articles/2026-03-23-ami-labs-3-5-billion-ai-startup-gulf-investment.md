Paris — Yann LeCun raised $1 billion before launching a product. AMI Labs, backed by Abu Dhabi's Shorooq Partners, builds 'world model' AI that reasons about physical space rather than predicts text — a direct alternative to the large language model approach LeCun has long criticized at Meta. Initial targets are manufacturing and aerospace — sectors where LeCun's physical-reasoning approach must prove itself against the systems he has spent years criticizing.
