San Francisco — Airbnb hosts now outsource guest replies to AI. Multiple companies sell chatbots to hosts; one guest bypassed theirs with a simple prompt injection, receiving a detailed French toast recipe instead of a booking answer. Airbnb permits the practice; guests have no way to know they are talking to a machine.
