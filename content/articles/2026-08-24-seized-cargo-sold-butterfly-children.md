Tehran — 771 epidermolysis bullosa patients will split tanker proceeds.

Sanctions block payment for imported wound dressings the patients are otherwise permitted to buy.

A court ordered a US oil cargo, seized in 2023, sold after ruling sanctions denied patients' treatment, judiciary officials said.

Iran's judiciary says proceeds are now set for distribution among the 771 plaintiffs.
