4 astronauts docked at the International Space Station on Saturday. The crew — 2 NASA astronauts, 1 European Space Agency astronaut, and 1 Russian cosmonaut — arrived aboard a SpaceX Dragon capsule after launching from Kennedy Space Center on 13 February. They replace Crew-11, whose mission was cut short last month in NASA's first medical evacuation in 65 years of human spaceflight. The emergency left only NASA astronaut Chris Williams and 2 Russian cosmonauts aboard the station for a month. The identity of the affected astronaut and the nature of the medical incident remain undisclosed, leaving open questions about long-duration spaceflight risk.
