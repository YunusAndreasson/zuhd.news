A South African bought his way into Thailand's government. Benjamin Mauerberger is charged with a 1 billion baht fraud and allegedly paid Thai generals, financial regulators, and the prime minister's inner circle in crypto and luxury cars. Singapore seized S$160 million from his network on March 9, as reports place him aboard a yacht in the Seychelles.
