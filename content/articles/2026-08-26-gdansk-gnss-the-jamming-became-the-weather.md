Gdansk — Navigation signals were jammed on 63% of days this month.

The same signal underpins mobile networks, power grids and ship and aircraft approaches nearby.

A GPS signal arrives weaker than background noise, so a modest transmitter can overwhelm it for miles.

[Poland](country:PL) has not named a source, but it previously blamed Russian territory for similar interference.
