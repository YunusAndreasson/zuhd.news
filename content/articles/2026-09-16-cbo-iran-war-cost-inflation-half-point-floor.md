Washington — The Pentagon never answered the budget office's questions.

That silence means the $38 billion war-cost estimate is a floor, not a ceiling.

The Congressional Budget Office also found 2027 inflation will run a half-point higher, mostly from fuel costs.

Base-damage costs excluded from the estimate could push the true total past $200 billion.
