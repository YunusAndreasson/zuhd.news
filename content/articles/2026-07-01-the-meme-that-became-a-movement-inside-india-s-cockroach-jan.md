New Delhi — 25,000 Indians registered as "cockroaches" in 48 hours.

Chief Justice Surya Kant's remark comparing jobless youth to "cockroaches" during a May court hearing was reclaimed online as a leaderless party identity.

Organizer Abhijeet Dipke's movement now presses grievances over unemployment and exam-paper leaks nationwide.
