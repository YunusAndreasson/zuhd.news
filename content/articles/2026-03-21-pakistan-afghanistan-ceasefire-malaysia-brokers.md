Pakistan suspended military operations in Afghanistan for Eid. Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and Turkey negotiated the 5-day pause in cross-border strikes that have killed hundreds. The ceasefire expires midnight Monday; if Pakistan does not renew it, strikes resume during the Eid holiday.
