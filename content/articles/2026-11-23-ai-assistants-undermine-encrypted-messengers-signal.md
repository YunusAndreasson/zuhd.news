Berlin — One member's AI assistant exposes an entire encrypted group chat.

End-to-end encryption does nothing once the phone's own assistant reads the decrypted screen.

Signal restricts screenshots on iOS 27 and Android whenever it detects an AI assistant reading the screen.

The protection is on by default, but users can switch it off in settings.

The protections ship in Signal's next version, covering assistants from Apple and Google.
