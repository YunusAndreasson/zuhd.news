Europe's tariff relief lasted hours. The Supreme Court voided Trump's emergency tariffs 6-3, but the White House invoked a 1974 trade law to impose 10% duties on all imports. The tariffs take effect Monday, exempting only North American goods and leaving EU exporters exposed.
