Moscow — The [US](country:US) warned of a mass-casualty attack at Tsaritsyno on Saturday.

Washington gave Moscow a similar warning weeks before the 2024 Crocus City Hall attack killed over 100.

The embassy told Americans to avoid the site, and [Russia](country:RU)'s Federal Security Service said the US tipped it off.

Neither government has named a suspect or said whether anyone is in custody.
