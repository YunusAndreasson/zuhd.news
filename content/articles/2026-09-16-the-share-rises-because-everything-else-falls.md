London — Flying could reach 80% of [UK](country:GB) emissions.

Net zero depends on removing carbon aviation cannot yet avoid emitting.

Other sectors are decarbonizing while jet fuel has no scaled substitute, so flying's carbon share keeps rising.

Ministers face a Heathrow expansion decision by 2029 that would add 2.4 million tonnes of CO2.
