Trump demanded Japan send warships to Hormuz this week. Japan's war-renouncing constitution bars combat deployments abroad despite the country routing 90% of its oil through Hormuz. Japanese Prime Minister Takaichi arrives at the White House on Thursday with no constitutional path to satisfying Trump's demand.
