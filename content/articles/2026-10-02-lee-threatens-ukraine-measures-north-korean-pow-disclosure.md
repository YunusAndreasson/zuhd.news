Seoul — Lee Jae Myung threatened unspecified measures against [Ukraine](country:UA).

Seoul says Zelensky's UN disclosure of two North Korean prisoners' transfer broke a confidentiality deal.

Analyst Cho Han-bum says measures would likely hit South Korean aid to Ukraine.

Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha called it a "diplomatic misunderstanding", and Ukrainian officials say no secrecy agreement existed.

Lee has not specified the measures, and Seoul relies on Kyiv for information on [North Korea](country:KP)'s military.
