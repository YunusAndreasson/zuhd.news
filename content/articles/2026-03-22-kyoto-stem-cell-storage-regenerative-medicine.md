Kyoto — Japan will bank personal stem cells for $63,000 each. Induced pluripotent stem cells, grown from a patient's own blood, can differentiate into almost any tissue — eliminating immune rejection and sidestepping the embryonic cell controversy. iPS Portal, the Kyoto company offering the service, targets 20 customers in its first year and promises to cut prices until the therapy is affordable for everyone.
