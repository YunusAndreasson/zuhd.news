Riyadh — [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s only Hormuz bypass has shut.

Without it, Riyadh's crude has no way out that avoids a contested strait.

Brent traded near $131 a barrel as of September 15, up 28% in a week.

The 1,200km line moves 7 million barrels daily, and Riyadh aims to restore half within days.
