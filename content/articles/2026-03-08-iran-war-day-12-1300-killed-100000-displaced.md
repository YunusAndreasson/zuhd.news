US and Israeli strikes destroyed Gandhi Hospital in Tehran. The strikes on dual-use sites in population centres killed 1,300 and displaced 100,000 people across Iran in 12 days, the United Nations reported. The UN refugee agency UNHCR warned that aid groups are overwhelmed and cannot meet growing demand.
