New York — [Pakistan](country:PK) cited UN Security Council plebiscite resolutions [India](country:IN) has never implemented.

Jammu and Kashmir has sat on the Security Council's agenda since 1947; the promised UN-supervised plebiscite has never been held.

India rejects the resolutions as obsolete; no enforcement mechanism exists without unanimous council action.
