Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni heads to Algiers on March 25 — Algeria covers 33% of Italy's gas via pipelines unaffected by the Hormuz closure. She will also press Algerian President Tebboune to expand Italian investment and launch a bilateral chamber of commerce.
