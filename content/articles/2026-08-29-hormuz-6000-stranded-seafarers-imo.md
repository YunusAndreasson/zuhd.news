Strait of Hormuz — 6,000 sailors remain stuck aboard anchored ships.

A crew cannot leave a vessel that cannot dock, so contracts expire while sailors stay captive.

The International Maritime Organization counts 19 seafarers killed and 70 attacks since the war began February 28.

Shipowners keep vessels waiting rather than write off cargo, as the IMO sets no resumption date.
