New York — OKX and Intercontinental Exchange filed for nonstop tokenized stock trading.

It starts with more than 60 [US](country:US)-listed companies, which get 30 days to object.

A tokenized share is a blockchain version of a stock, trading after hours with the same dividends and voting rights.

Launch awaits that window and further regulatory steps under the Securities and Exchange Commission's five-year Innovation Exemption.
