Jakarta — [Indonesia](country:ID) debates expanding the army's internal security role.

Post-Suharto reforms removed the military from domestic policing; analysts warn reverting that would erode civilian oversight.

No legislation is tabled; any change sets a precedent for military authority in Southeast Asia.
