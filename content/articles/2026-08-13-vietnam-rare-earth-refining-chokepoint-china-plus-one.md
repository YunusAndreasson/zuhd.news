Hanoi — Vietnam holds vast rare-earth reserves yet still depends on Chinese rare-earth inputs.

Firms fleeing [China](country:CN) are landing factories in Vietnam without shifting who controls the supply chain.

Vietnam has become the top beneficiary of companies de-risking from Chinese manufacturing.

Whether Hanoi builds its own refining capacity will decide if it escapes that dependence.
