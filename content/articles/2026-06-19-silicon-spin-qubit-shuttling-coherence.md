Delft — Shuttling breaks silicon qubits more than making them.

Valley-splitting energy varied 130-fold across the device; low-splitting regions destroyed qubit coherence during transport, the study found.

Routing around low-splitting zones preserved coherence; the study flags valley-splitting variation as a design constraint for silicon qubits.
