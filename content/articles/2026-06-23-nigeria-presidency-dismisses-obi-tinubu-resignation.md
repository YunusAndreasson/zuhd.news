Abuja — Nigeria's presidency dismissed Peter Obi's resignation demand.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s fixed presidential term bars resignation-by-demand; the Presidency cited All Progressives Congress by-election wins and unbroken quarterly GDP growth since 2023 as rebuttal.

Obi's challenge now waits for the 2027 presidential election, the Presidency said.
