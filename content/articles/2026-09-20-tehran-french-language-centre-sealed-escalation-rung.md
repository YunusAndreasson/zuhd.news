Tehran — A judicial order sealed the embassy-linked French Language Centre.

[France](country:FR) will summon Iran's ambassador and take "appropriate measures."

[Iran](country:IR)'s prosecutor says the centre refused a licence and networked elites, while Paris calls the closure "unjustifiable."

France expelled 2 Iranian diplomats last month after Tehran briefly arrested 2 French envoys.
