San Francisco — Coinbase, Cloudflare and Mastercard are building agent wallets.

Card networks assume a human can dispute a charge, but agents cannot.

Machine payments need instant, final settlement, so agents use stablecoins instead of reversible cards.

No US or EU rule yet assigns liability when an AI agent's purchase goes wrong.
