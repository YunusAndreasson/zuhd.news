Vienna — Hackers accessed names and contact details of Austrian diplomats.

[Austria](country:AT)'s Foreign Ministry says the list covers staff at its Vienna headquarters and missions abroad.

The breach hit a website run by an external service provider on 12 and 13 November, the ministry said.

The ministry has not attributed the attack, and no public evidence links [Russia](country:RU).

Austrian authorities have not established whether the same-day Microsoft outage was connected.
