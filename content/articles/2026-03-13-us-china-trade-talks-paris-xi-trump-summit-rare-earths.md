US and Chinese officials meet in Paris this weekend. Chinese President Xi Jinping now holds more leverage — Beijing controls 90% of global rare earth processing used in US defense manufacturing. Both governments want a deal before Xi meets US President Donald Trump later this month: rare earth exports for tariff relief.
