Iran struck a desalination plant in Bahrain. 400 desalination plants along Gulf coasts supply 40% of the world's desalinated water, serving populations with no alternative freshwater source. Bahrain says water supply is unaffected for now; hundreds more plants in Saudi Arabia and the UAE remain within Iranian drone range.
