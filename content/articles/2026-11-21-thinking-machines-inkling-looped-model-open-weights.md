United States — Thinking Machines' Inkling 2 Mini runs on a laptop in 20GB.

Its makers warn looping lets models think more, output less and may become harder to monitor.

The middle 12 of its 24 layers run 3 times per token, so it finds answers with fewer written tokens.

It trails Qwen3.8-27B on multi-turn chat and multi-constraint instructions.

CEO Mira Murati also released a variable-loop research model so outsiders can test monitorability.
