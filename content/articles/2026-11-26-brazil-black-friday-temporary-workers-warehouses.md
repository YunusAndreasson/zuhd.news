São Paulo — Amazon is hiring 12,000 temporary workers in [Brazil](country:BR).

Industry group Abiacom projects orders that week will rise 31.2% to 21.66 million.

Firms absorb the peak with temporary hires: DHL Supply Chain plans about 2,600 and Americanas 3,700 posts.

Abiacom projects R$16.6 billion in sales that week, up 24.4% on 2025.
