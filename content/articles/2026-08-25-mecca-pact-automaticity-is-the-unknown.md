Mecca — [Pakistan](country:PK), [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) and [Turkey](country:TR) signed a joint defence pact, and almost nothing in its text is public.

Whether it binds automatic response, not just consultation, decides if it is a guarantee or a communiqué.

A pact's value lies in automaticity — only the unpublished clauses distinguish a guarantee from a statement.

None of the three parliaments were consulted before signing, and the text remains withheld.
