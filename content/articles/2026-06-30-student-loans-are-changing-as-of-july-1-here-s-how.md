Washington — Trump's law caps most graduate borrowing at $100,000.

7 million [US](country:US) borrowers enrolled in an income-driven plan the government ended must switch within 90 days or face auto-reassignment to a costlier option.

Parent loans are also capped at $65,000 per student; a 1% interest discount for autopay begins July 1.
