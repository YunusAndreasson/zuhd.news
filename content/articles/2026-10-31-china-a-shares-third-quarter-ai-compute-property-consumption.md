Beijing — [China](country:CN)'s tech profit rose 140%, property lost 53.2 billion yuan.

TMTPost's tally of 5,572 firms shows third-quarter profit down 11%, though nine-month profit rose 7.76%.

AI demand for memory chips lifted Changxin Technology's nine-month profit 2,600% to 132 billion yuan, TMTPost's analysis found.

Consumer staples profit fell 32.36%, and 11 pig farmers lost 22.2 billion yuan combined.

Changxin told investors it expects revenue to keep growing next year.
