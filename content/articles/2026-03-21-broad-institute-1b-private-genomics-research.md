A family's $1 billion rivals US genomics funding. The Broad Institute, a Harvard-MIT psychiatric genomics center, laid off 75 scientists in 2025 after Congress cut federal research funding — a gap the Stanley Foundation's $280 million first payment begins to fill. The Broad now depends on the Stanley family for research Congress no longer funds.
