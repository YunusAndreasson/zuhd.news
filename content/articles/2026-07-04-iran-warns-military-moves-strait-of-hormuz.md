Tehran — "Not a military display arena for extra-regional powers."

Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi warned [France](country:FR) and [Britain](country:GB) against the strait, the passage for roughly a fifth of the world's oil.

Gharibabadi called [Iran](country:IR) the waterway's "guarantor," naming no specific incident behind the warning.
