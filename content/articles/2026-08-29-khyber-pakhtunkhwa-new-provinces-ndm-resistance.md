Peshawar — Mohsin Dawar vowed to resist splitting the province.

The plan would redraw electoral majorities in [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province.

Creating a province requires a two-thirds vote in parliament and the outgoing province's own assembly.

Dawar demanded the federal government act on unheeded peace jirga recommendations instead.
