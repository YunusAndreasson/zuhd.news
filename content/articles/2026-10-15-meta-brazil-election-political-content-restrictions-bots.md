Brazil — Researchers logged over 1,000 Meta restrictions on political content.

Yasmin Curzi of Rio's Getulio Vargas Foundation says the suspensions hit progressive groups and independent press hardest.

Lost reach is the commonest case at 356, ahead of 163 repost limits, 125 account limits and 119 post bans.

Meta says it removed posts by ministers, journalists and activists to obey [Brazil](country:BR)'s electoral court orders in the first round.

Lula's campaign has complained to the electoral court, days before the runoff against Flávio Bolsonaro.
