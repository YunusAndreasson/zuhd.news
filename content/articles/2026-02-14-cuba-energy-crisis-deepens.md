Cuba lost its main fuel supply. The US seized oil shipments from Venezuela that Cuba depended on, and power cuts spread across the island. Several airlines suspended flights for lack of aviation fuel. Multiple governments warned against non-essential travel. The annual cigar festival, which draws over 1,300 attendees from 70 countries, has been postponed with no new date set.
