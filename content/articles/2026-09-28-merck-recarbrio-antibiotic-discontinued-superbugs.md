Rahway — Merck quietly discontinued Recarbrio, a last-resort antibiotic.

It treated resistant hospital pneumonia as the WHO ranks resistance a top global threat.

Drugs held in reserve for resistant infections sell too little to justify production, so large drugmakers exit the field.

The same industry piles patents on high-volume drugs to block generics for years longer than in 1990.

Merck gave no explanation, and no other company has stepped in to keep Recarbrio available in the US.
