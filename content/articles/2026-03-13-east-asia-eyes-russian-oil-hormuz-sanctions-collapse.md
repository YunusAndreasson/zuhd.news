Russia earned $1.5 billion as East Asian buyers replaced Hormuz crude. The US waived Russia's oil sanctions to ease prices from its own Iran war, handing Moscow revenue that funds its Ukraine campaign. The Association of Southeast Asian Nations called Friday for an immediate end to the Iran war; the US sanctions waiver lapses April 11.
