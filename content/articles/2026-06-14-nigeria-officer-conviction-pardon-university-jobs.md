Abuja — [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Supreme Court upheld Bello Magaji's sodomy conviction; he never served.

The army changed his dismissal to voluntary resignation; a presidential pardon then erased his 5-year sentence for assaulting 4 young men.

Magaji now serves as Dean of Law at American University of Nigeria, which says it is investigating.
