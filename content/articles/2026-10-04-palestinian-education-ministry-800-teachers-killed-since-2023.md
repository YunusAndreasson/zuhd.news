Ramallah — [Israel](country:IL) has killed over 800 teachers, a Palestinian ministry says, Turkish state outlet TRT World reported.

The ministry also counts over 3,300 teachers injured and more than 170 detained since October 2023.

The statement covers Gaza and the West Bank, citing checkpoints and Israel's withholding of tax revenues that fund salaries.

TRT carried no Israeli response, and the ministry asks the UN for international protection of educators.
