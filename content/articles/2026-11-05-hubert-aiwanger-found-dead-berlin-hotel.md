Berlin — Hubert Aiwanger, 55, was found dead in his hotel room.

He led the Free Voters, the junior partner in Bavaria's government, and served as Markus Soeder's deputy.

Berlin police and prosecutors opened an inquest to establish the cause of death and rule out third-party culpability.

Police sources say the cause appears health-related, with no sign of foul play.

Police and prosecutors have given no further details or timeline.
