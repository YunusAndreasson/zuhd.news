Budapest — Police raids reportedly detained Viktor Orbán's brother Áron.

Péter Magyar won [Hungary](country:HU)'s April election promising to investigate corruption under Orbán.

Investigators from the National Asset Recovery and Protection Office probe a visa network that allegedly sold work visas to foreigners.

Viktor Orbán calls it political: "I am the target, my younger brother is just a tool."

Police promised details of the offences on Monday afternoon.
