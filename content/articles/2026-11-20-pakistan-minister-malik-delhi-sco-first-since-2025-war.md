New Delhi — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s first minister in India since the 2025 war landed Friday.

The Shanghai Cooperation Organisation is one of few forums still bringing the two governments together.

As host of the civilisational dialogue forum, [India](country:IN) invited every member, and Pakistan chose to send its climate change minister.

Congress spokesperson Supriya Shrinate said the invitation insults the families of the 26 civilians killed at Pahalgam.

Pakistan hosts the leaders' summit in August 2027, and India has not said who will attend.
