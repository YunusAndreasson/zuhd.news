Circle overtook BlackRock in tokenized US Treasuries. Circle's USYC token — US government debt issued on a blockchain — reached $2.2 billion as the market hit a record $11 billion, up 27% this year, driven largely by Binance adopting USYC as off-exchange collateral on its BNB Chain. Unlike conventional bonds, blockchain Treasuries settle instantly, trade around the clock, and serve as collateral simultaneously — a structure institutional investors are moving toward to earn yield without locking up capital.
