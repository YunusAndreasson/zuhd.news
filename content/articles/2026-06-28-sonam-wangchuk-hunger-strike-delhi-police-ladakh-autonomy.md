New Delhi — Police cut water at Wangchuk's hunger strike.

Wangchuk is demanding Ladakh autonomy and the education minister's resignation over a national exam leak in [India](country:IN) linked to 21 student deaths.

Delhi Police have not responded; Wangchuk said the strike continues until the government acts on either demand.
