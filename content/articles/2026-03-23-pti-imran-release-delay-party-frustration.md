Islamabad — Prison authorities denied Imran Khan family visits over Eid. Senior leaders of Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf split publicly over how to secure his release after 80 days in detention, with the party facing accusations of deliberate state fragmentation. Whether the fracture leads to open protest or further splintering will determine PTI's ability to contest the next election.
