Newark — Bubbles at 100nm don't float; they scour.

At 100nm, surface tension keeps nanobubbles submerged long enough to strip oil, algae, and other pollutants from water.

Researcher Wen Zhang is testing the technology at scale for open-water algal blooms and contaminated waterways.
