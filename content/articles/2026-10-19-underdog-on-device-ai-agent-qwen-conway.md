United States — An 8-month-old startup built an AI agent that runs offline.

Meta's Muse runs each agent in the cloud, but Underdog keeps data on the device.

Its Husky engine runs a 4B model 4.5 times faster than Apple's MLX machine-learning framework, Conway Research says.

The product is invite-only, and its speed figures come from the company's own tests.

Linux, iOS and Android versions are planned, and the company says it will never run ads.
