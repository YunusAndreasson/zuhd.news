Zamfara — Bandits killed 3, displaced 484 in Zamfara.

271 displaced were children; banditry has pushed northwest [Nigeria](country:NG)'s internally displaced count to 794,018 since December, the International Organization for Migration said.

Food and shelter are urgent; Nigerian emergency agencies had not responded.
