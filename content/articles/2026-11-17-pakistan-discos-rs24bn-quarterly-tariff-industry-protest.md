Islamabad — Power distributors seek Rs24 billion more from consumers.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s industrial consumers say the quarterly adjustment adds about Rs1.30 a unit for three months.

11 distribution companies claim Rs39 billion in capacity, maintenance and loss costs, with four offsetting Rs13 billion.

Industrial intervener Rehan Javed says discounted incentive-package units shift costs onto all consumers.

Pakistan's power regulator has yet to rule on charging from December 2026 billing, K-Electric consumers included.
