Moscow — The proposed summit would seat 3 leaders and no Ukrainian.

[Ukraine](country:UA) and Europe would be absent from a deal favouring Moscow, the Wall Street Journal reported.

Putin floated it on a 9 October call with Trump, for the 18 November Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit.

A senior [US](country:US) official said Trump has not agreed, and Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said no agenda exists yet.

[China](country:CN) has signalled it will not object if Trump attends the Shenzhen summit.
