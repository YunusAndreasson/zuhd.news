Washington — The Central Intelligence Agency's first bin Laden warning: February 1998.

The record undercuts claims that intelligence reached the [US](country:US) too late to act.

The agency released 71 presidential daily briefs, the government's top-priority intelligence product, spanning Clinton to Bush before the 2001 attacks.

Which agency was tasked to act on each warning remains undisclosed in the release.
