Islamabad — EU figures show [Pakistan](country:PK) received €732 million in 2024 tariff exemptions.

That access depends on ratifying and enforcing 27 international rights conventions.

The scheme cuts tariffs on Pakistan's textile exports in exchange for labor, rights and governance commitments the EU alone verifies.

The EU flagged enforced disappearances, media freedom and forced labor for continued attention.
