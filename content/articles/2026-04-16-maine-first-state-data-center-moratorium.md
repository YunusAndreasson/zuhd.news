Augusta — Maine's first-ever data center moratorium passed Tuesday. Democrats approved an 18-month pause on data centers over 20 megawatts — first in the US — over AI's electricity and water demands. Governor Mills has 10 days to sign or veto; Trump has threatened to withhold funding from states that restrict AI.
