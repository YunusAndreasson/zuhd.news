Washington — Global growth drops to 2.5%, a pandemic-era low.

Two-thirds of economies saw downgraded forecasts; [Iran](country:IR)'s Hormuz closure transmits fuel and fertiliser shocks hardest into low-income food budgets, World Bank said.

The World Bank's worst case is 1.3% growth if Hormuz supply disruptions deepen through the year.
