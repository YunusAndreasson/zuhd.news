Austin — Texas cave fossils broke Ice Age climate models. Giant tortoises cannot survive cold, dry conditions; the Edwards Plateau climate models predict exactly that for the Ice Age. Either the regional climate models are wrong or Hesperotestudo had unknown cold adaptations — both require further excavation.
