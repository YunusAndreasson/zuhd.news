Beijing — Eight regions will test 6G in factories, not labs.

The pilots feed a standards process that produces the first international 6G specifications around 2029.

Under [China](country:CN)'s ministry-provincial programme, each region builds on its own strength, from Beijing's standards bodies to Shanghai's chip supply chains.

Globally, 6G research remains focused on development, standardization and prototype validation.

International telecommunications organizations' timeline puts early commercial deployment around 2030.
