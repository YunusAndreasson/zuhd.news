Brussels — War-driven fuel prices outrun EU car tariffs.

[China](country:CN)'s electric vehicles are gaining European buyers despite duties meant to block them.

Tariffs raise the sticker price, but buyers weigh total cost of ownership against fuel prices the war pushed up.

Chinese manufacturers keep absorbing the duty while Brussels' main defense against it erodes.
