Seoul — Seven decades of no-man's-land became Asia's richest wildlife sanctuary.

[South Korea](country:KR) is redrawing its DMZ buffer zone for development, threatening habitat that treaties elsewhere failed to protect for migratory species like the red-crowned crane.

The ecological cost of any peace-dividend land use extends along flyways far beyond the peninsula.
