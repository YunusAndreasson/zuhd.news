Ukraine risks losing US support to the Iran war, President Zelenskyy warned. Washington's 2-week-old conflict has consumed US military resources, diplomatic attention, and air-defense missiles that Kyiv also needs. Ukraine sent 3 drone-warfare teams to Middle Eastern countries this week to preserve Washington's focus on Kyiv.
