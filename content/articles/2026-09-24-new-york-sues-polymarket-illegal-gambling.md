New York — Polymarket lets 18-to-20-year-olds bet where state law sets 21.

A state win would let states regulate prediction markets the federal government says it alone oversees.

The suit targets QCX LLC, the CFTC-licensed firm Polymarket bought before launching its US app in December 2025.

Polymarket calls the suit a recycled copy of the one New York filed against Kalshi in July.

Two appeals courts disagree, and New Jersey has asked the Supreme Court to settle it.
