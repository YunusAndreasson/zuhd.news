Paris — [France](country:FR) confirmed Europe's first Ebola case: a doctor back from Congo.

The Bundibugyo strain has no approved vaccine or treatment; contact tracing is underway in France.

Officials warn the peak is still ahead; [DR Congo](country:CD) has over 1,000 cases and more than 260 confirmed deaths.
