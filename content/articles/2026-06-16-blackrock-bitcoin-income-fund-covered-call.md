New York — BlackRock launched a covered-call bitcoin fund Tuesday.

The fund holds spot bitcoin, then sells call options on 25-35% of holdings monthly — generating income but capping upside for buyers.

The fund targets income-focused investors who have previously avoided bitcoin as a non-yielding asset.
