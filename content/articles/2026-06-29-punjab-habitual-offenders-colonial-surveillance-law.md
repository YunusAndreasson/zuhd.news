Lahore — Punjab bill lets police freeze accounts without conviction.

The law revives a British Raj habitual-offender designation, extending it to anyone executive committees — not courts — brand "anti-social."

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s Punjab Assembly standing committee cleared the bill pending a floor vote and expected legal challenges.
