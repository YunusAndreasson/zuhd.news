Islamabad — Pakistan's Oil and Gas Development Company found 2,000 barrels a day at Bobi Deep-1 in Sindh.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s fuel imports strain the current account; Bobi Deep-1 confirms reservoir potential but no production date is set, the company said.

The find follows the company's April discovery, its largest single-well yield.
