Quetta — Silver turned to lead inside a police convoy. Officers transporting 688 kg of seized bullion in 36 sealed boxes substituted 400 kg with lead before reaching Pakistan Mint Lahore. Both are in custody; Safe City CCTV captured the vehicle swap on camera.
