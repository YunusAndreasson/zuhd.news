Minsk — Two sanctioned states are nearing a visa deal.

A visa waiver can open undocumented routes for North Korean labor into [Russia](country:RU).

North Korean workers have appeared on Russian construction sites since 2024, outside official entry statistics.

[Belarus](country:BY)'s foreign minister says only technical details remain before the two sides sign.
