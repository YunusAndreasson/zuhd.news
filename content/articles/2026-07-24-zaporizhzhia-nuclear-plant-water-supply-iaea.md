Enerhodar — Monitors have had limited tap water since July 18.

Strikes on electrical infrastructure cut water to the plant and town, the International Atomic Energy Agency said; reactors need cooling water.

Director General Rafael Grossi named neither side but said plant staff "must be protected at all times."
