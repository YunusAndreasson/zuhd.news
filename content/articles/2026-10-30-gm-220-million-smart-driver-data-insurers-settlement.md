Washington — General Motors will pay $220 million for selling drivers' data.

35 [US](country:US) states and the District of Columbia sued after some drivers' premiums rose.

The automaker shared Smart Driver data with brokers, who sold it to insurers, and many drivers say they never agreed.

A third party, not the automaker, will verify its compliance with the settlement's privacy terms.
