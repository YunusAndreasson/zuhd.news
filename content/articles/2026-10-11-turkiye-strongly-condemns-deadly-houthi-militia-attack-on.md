Riyadh — Houthi missile killed 13, wounded over 300 at Riyadh airport.

Four states demanded an unconditional halt as Houthis declared Saudi airspace a target.

A projectile hit Terminal 4 on Sunday and started a fire, the Saudi-led coalition said, after Saturday's strike suspended operations.

The Houthis claim 3 further Saudi targets, including Dammam's airport, and Saudi Arabia has confirmed none of them.

EU foreign ministers meet Monday, after four states backed [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s right to counter the attacks.
