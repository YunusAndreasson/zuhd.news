Washington — Charges over 2023 testimony land 11 days before the midterms.

The Justice Department usually avoids overt acts that could influence an election.

A criminal complaint, not an indictment, accuses Nikki Floris and Bradley Benavides of lying to Congress about the Hunter Biden laptop probe.

Floris's lawyers say the case rests on hearsay and the say-so of politicians, not facts.

Magistrate Judge Zia Faruqui set a preliminary hearing for Tuesday, rejecting prosecutors' request for a delay.
