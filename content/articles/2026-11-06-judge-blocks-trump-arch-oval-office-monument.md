Washington — A 14-day order froze Trump's arch hours after approval.

The National Park Service says the 250-foot arch could harm as many as 42 historic sites.

A [US](country:US) judge, Tanya Chutkan, granted a restraining order sought by D.C. and Virginia after the planning commission voted 8-4.

Chutkan is still weighing veterans' motion to stop all work until the lawsuit resolves.
