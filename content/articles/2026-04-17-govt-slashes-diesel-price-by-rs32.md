Islamabad — Pakistan cut diesel by Rs32 a litre. The 8% reduction follows a crude price slump after Hormuz reopened, reversing energy-import pressure that had weighed on Pakistan's economy since March. Petrol prices and electricity tariffs face the same mechanism; Islamabad has not yet announced follow-through on those.
