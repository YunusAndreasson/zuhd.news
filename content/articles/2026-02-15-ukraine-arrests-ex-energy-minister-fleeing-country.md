Border agents pulled former Energy Minister Herman Halushchenko off a train at the Ukrainian border. The National Anti-Corruption Bureau detained him as part of Operation Midas, a probe into a $100 million embezzlement scheme in the energy sector that has already forced the resignation of presidential office head Andriy Yermak. The arrest tests whether Kyiv can deliver the anti-corruption reforms that the European Union has made a condition of membership.
