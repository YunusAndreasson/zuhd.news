Brent crude stood at $109.70 a barrel as of 16 October, up 4.7% in a week.

The [Iran](country:IR) Guards' navy says the tanker crossed a prohibited area of the Sea of Oman with its tracking switched off.

No independent source has confirmed the ship's name, flag, damage or any spill.
