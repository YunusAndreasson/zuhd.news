Perth — [China](country:CN) buys three-fifths of [Australia](country:AU)'s iron ore exports.

Canberra's mining economy depends on a market China is trying to shrink.

Steel makes up 15% of China's emissions, so Beijing is shifting to cleaner furnaces that need higher-grade ore than most Australian exports.

Some Australian miners have made 60% margins on raw ore exports, a rate a top analyst says China won't tolerate.

Australia and China signed a steel decarbonisation pact in December, but funding for green iron plants remains unresolved.
