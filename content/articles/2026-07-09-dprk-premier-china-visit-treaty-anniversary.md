Beijing — Pak Thae-song is North Korea's first solo premier visit since 2011.

[China](country:CN) and North Korea mark 65 years of a treaty binding each to defend the other if attacked, their only such pact still active.

Xi Jinping urged swift follow-through on last month's Pyongyang agreements as Pak departs Beijing on July 12.
