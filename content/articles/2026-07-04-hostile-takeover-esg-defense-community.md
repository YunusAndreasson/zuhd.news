New York — Poison pills fend off hostile bids.

Boards adopt stakeholder-friendly, environmental, social and governance (ESG) policies partly as a takeover shield — researchers find the tactic channels real benefits to communities.

Researchers next test whether the effect fades once takeover threats do.
