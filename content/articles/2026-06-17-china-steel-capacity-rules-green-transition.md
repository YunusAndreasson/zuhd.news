Beijing — China's industry ministry set a 1.5-to-1 retirement ratio for new steel capacity.

[China](country:CN) produces over half the world's steel; the ratio tilts approvals toward electric arc furnaces over coal-fired blast furnaces.

Blast furnaces produce 90% of Chinese steel; no compliance deadline for existing capacity has been set.
