Paris — Unions count 450,000 marchers, the interior ministry 256,000.

Students blockading hundreds of high schools since late September drew unions and teachers into the streets.

Students in [France](country:FR) cite missing teachers, overloaded timetables, dilapidated classrooms and buildings unfit for rising temperatures.

Government figures show 215 students and 715 police injured, and a police grenade took a 15-year-old's hand in Lens.

Macron was due to meet the interior and education ministers late Tuesday, a government source said.
