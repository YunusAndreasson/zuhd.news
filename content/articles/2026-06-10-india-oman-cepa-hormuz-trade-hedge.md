New Delhi — [India](country:IN)-[Oman](country:OM) trade pact took effect June 1.

Most of Oman's coast falls outside the Strait of Hormuz, giving India a Gulf trade gateway immune to the war chokepoint.

Whether India reroutes significant trade depends on how long the Hormuz conflict keeps the strait contested.
