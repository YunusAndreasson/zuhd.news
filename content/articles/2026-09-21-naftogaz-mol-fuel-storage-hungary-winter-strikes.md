Ivano-Frankivsk — [Ukraine](country:UA)'s Naftogaz signed a deal to build fuel storage in Hungary.

Ukraine expects a new wave of Russian drone and missile attacks on its energy infrastructure this winter.

The memorandum with Hungary's MOL covers oil products storage near the border, outside areas facing attacks.

Prime Minister Peter Magyar has ruled out military aid to Ukraine and a faster EU accession bid despite the thaw.

Naftogaz's acting head, Serhii Fedorenko, has announced no sites, cost or construction date.
