Moscow — Putin's aide says Washington cannot force Kyiv to comply.

Kremlin aide Yuri Ushakov says Europe, which backs [Ukraine](country:UA), must join the talks.

Ushakov said hopes after the 2025 Anchorage summit that the [US](country:US) could dictate terms to Kyiv had not worked out.

Ushakov expects the first technical meeting in Abu Dhabi, due in the first half of November, to be only exploratory.

Traders on Polymarket put 88% on Trump, Putin and Xi meeting by 2027, as Ushakov says Shenzhen preparations have begun.
