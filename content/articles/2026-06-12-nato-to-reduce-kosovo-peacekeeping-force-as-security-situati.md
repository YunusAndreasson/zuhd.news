Pristina — NATO will thin its Kosovo peacekeeping force through 2026 rotations.

The Kosovo Force has deployed since 1999; each rotation will leave slightly fewer troops, with NATO reserving the right to rebuild if conditions deteriorate.

The drawdown coincides with [US](country:US) pressure to cut NATO assets committed to Europe.
