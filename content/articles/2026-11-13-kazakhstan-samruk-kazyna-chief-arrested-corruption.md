Astana — Nurlan Zhakupov, arrested Thursday, ran assets worth almost 40% of [Kazakhstan](country:KZ)'s GDP.

President Tokayev says the arrests will not disrupt the fund, which owns Air Astana and KazMunayGaz.

Railway chief Yelzhas Otynshiyev was also arrested, after the state railway cleared a Hong Kong listing hearing for a planned offering.

Tokayev also said officials reviewed some contracts signed by mistake, without saying which industries or counterparties.
