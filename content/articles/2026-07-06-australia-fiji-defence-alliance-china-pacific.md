Suva — Fiji is now [Australia](country:AU)'s third treaty ally in 75 years.

The pact commits each to "act to meet the common danger" if either is attacked, and stays open to other Pacific states.

Albanese visits Solomon Islands next, then hosts Pacific leaders in Brisbane this week.
