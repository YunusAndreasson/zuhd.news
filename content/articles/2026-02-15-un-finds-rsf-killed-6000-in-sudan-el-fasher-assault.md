At least 6,000 people died in 3 days. The UN Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights documented the Rapid Support Forces' October 2025 assault on El Fasher in North Darfur, finding mass killings, systematic sexual violence, summary executions of boys and men under 50, and the targeting of non-Arab communities. RSF fighters opened heavy weapons fire on 1,000 people sheltering at El Fasher University's Al-Rashid dormitory, killing around 500. The office concluded the atrocities amount to war crimes and possible crimes against humanity, following a pattern seen in earlier RSF offensives including the April 2025 attack on Zamzam camp. High Commissioner Volker Turk called for credible investigations as the African Union Summit in Addis Ababa takes up Sudan's nearly 3-year war, which has killed more than 150,000 people.
