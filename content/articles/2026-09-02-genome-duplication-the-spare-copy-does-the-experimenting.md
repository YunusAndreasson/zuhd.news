Tekapo — A tiny New Zealand snail recently doubled its entire genome.

Every gene now has a spare copy free to experiment with new functions.

The duplication also doubles chromosome-pairing errors each division, killing off most lineages that attempt it.

Researchers are tracking which duplicated lineages, like this Lake Alexandrina snail, survive the gamble.
