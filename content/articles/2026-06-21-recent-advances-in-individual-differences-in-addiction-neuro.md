Washington — Opioid neuroscience shifts focus to individual susceptibility.

The [US](country:US) epidemic exposed a gap: two people given the same dose diverge into dependence or not, for reasons group studies miss.

Researchers found heroin preference predicted addiction severity; the approach has not yet been tested in clinical treatment programs.
