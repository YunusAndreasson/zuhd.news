Islamabad — 18.5% of workers blamed their own technology mistakes.

A breach caused by one worker's error is a design failure, not a gap.

Kaspersky, a security vendor, surveyed [Pakistan](country:PK) and other Middle East, Turkiye and Africa employees on self-reported behavior.

Pakistan's data protection bill remains pending, leaving no breach-notification duty on employers.
