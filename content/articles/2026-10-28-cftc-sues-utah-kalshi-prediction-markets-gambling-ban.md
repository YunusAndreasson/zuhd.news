Salt Lake City — The CFTC has sued 10 states to protect prediction markets.

A Utah shutdown of Kalshi could hurt Polymarket, DraftKings and FanDuel too.

The agency argues the Commodity Exchange Act gives it exclusive jurisdiction over event contracts, pre-empting state gambling law.

A federal judge in Utah ruled in August that the Act does not pre-empt state gambling laws.

39 other states and the District of Columbia back New Jersey's petition asking the [US](country:US) Supreme Court to resolve the circuit split.
