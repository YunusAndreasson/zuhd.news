Missaukee County — Four people were found dead at two homes Friday.

A fifth victim remains hospitalized in critical condition after the shootings.

Troopers traced suspect Chad Hickman's vehicle near Whitlock Lake, where he was found dead alongside another person.

Michigan State Police have not said whether the second death near the lake was self-inflicted.
