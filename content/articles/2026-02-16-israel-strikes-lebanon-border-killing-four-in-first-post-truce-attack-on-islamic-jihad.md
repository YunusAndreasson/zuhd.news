An Israeli airstrike killed 4 people in eastern Lebanon. The military claimed it targeted Palestinian Islamic Jihad members near the Syrian border at Majdal Anjar early Monday — the first strike against the group in Lebanon since the November 2024 ceasefire with Hezbollah — but provided no evidence. Lebanon has documented over 2,000 Israeli sovereignty violations in just the last 3 months of 2025, and the expansion of targets beyond Hezbollah threatens the ceasefire that ended the 2024 war.
