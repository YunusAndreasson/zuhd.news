Israeli strikes kill or wound 30 Lebanon children daily. Israel relaunched its Lebanon campaign March 2 after Hezbollah retaliated for the Iranian supreme leader's assassination, with 1 million now displaced. UNICEF called for de-escalation as Israel placed 14% of Lebanon's territory under evacuation orders.
