Karachi — Wheat fell Rs10 a kilo but chapati still costs Rs20.

Rauf Ibrahim, a wholesaler, says workers earning under Rs50,000 spend almost everything on food.

A tandoor owner says cooking gas at Rs500-550 a kilo cancels the saving from cheaper flour.

Millers say mills cut No 2.5 flour from Rs161 to Rs156, but retail stays at Rs170-180.

A second ship, carrying 58,132 tonnes, docks Tuesday, but the new crop arrives only between March and May.
