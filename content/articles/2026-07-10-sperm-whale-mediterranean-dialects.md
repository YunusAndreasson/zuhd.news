Athens — Eastern whales click faster than western ones.

Researchers analyzed 5,291 click sequences and found whales near Greece's Hellenic Trench developed their own tempo of the shared "3+1" call.

Scientists don't yet know why eastern whales still occasionally revert to the older pattern.
