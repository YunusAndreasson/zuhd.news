Columbus — Republican governor abandons death penalty he helped create.

Governor DeWine co-authored the 1981 law but says its machinery has collapsed: 2 death sentences statewide in the last 6 years.

30 executions are scheduled under the next governor from January 2027 with no repeal bill advancing.
