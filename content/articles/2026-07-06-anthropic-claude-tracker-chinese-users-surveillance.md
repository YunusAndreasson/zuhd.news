San Francisco — A researcher found Claude Code hiding a tracker.

Anthropic used prompt steganography to flag users' timezone and proxy since March, aimed at Chinese labs it accuses of distilling Claude's models.

Alibaba banned employee use of Claude Code, and Anthropic hasn't said when it removed the code.
