London — Nearly 4 in 5 seasonal visas now go to Central Asians.

[Russia](country:RU)'s squeeze on migrant workers rerouted Uzbek and Tajik labor toward [Britain](country:GB)'s fields, restocking one economy's farms as another cracks down.

Britain's food supply now leans on a labor corridor most of its citizens never see.
