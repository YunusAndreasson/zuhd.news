Cirebon — Salt output jumped 20-fold to 600 tons in July.

The same drought driving [Indonesia](country:ID)'s salt boom is drying out rice paddies elsewhere, farmers say.

The weather agency has warned the dry spell will persist into early 2027, threatening rice planting.
