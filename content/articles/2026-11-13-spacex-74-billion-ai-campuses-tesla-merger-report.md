Starbase — SpaceX plans $74 billion in AI computing campuses.

AI generated more than half of SpaceX's third-quarter revenue.

The $24 billion Starbase campus will run its own gas-fired power plant, and the $50 billion Arizona campus needs county approval.

2,400 residents signed a petition against the Arizona campus, which SpaceX says will use dry cooling and no industrial groundwater.

Starbase's commission weighs a tax abatement on 9 December, and Cochise County holds its hearing that month.
