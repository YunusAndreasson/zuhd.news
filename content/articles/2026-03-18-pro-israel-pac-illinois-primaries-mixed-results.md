Pro-Israel PACs lost 2 of 4 Illinois House primaries. The American Israel Public Affairs Committee spent $5.4m against Evanston Mayor Daniel Biss, who won. Lt. Gov. Juliana Stratton, backed by AIPAC donors, won the Senate primary and faces a Republican in November.
