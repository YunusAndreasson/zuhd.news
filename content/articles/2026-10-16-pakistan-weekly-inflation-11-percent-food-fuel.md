Islamabad — Petrol costs 49.6% more than a year ago.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s weekly inflation reached 11.38% year on year, official data show.

Fuel costs feed into food, with pulse gram up 4.97% and wheat flour 3.36% in a week.

The index slipped 0.04% on the week, with 23 of 51 items dearer and 7 cheaper.

Inflation has stayed in double digits for several weeks, and the government has not cut the petroleum levy.
