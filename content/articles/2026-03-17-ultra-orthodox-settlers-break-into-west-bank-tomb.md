Bratslav worshippers breached Joseph's Tomb in Nablus without army coordination. Israel's military permits coordinated pilgrimages but classifies unauthorized entries as minor infractions, enabling repeat incursions. Israeli forces removed the group hours later without explanation as Palestinians gathered in protest.
