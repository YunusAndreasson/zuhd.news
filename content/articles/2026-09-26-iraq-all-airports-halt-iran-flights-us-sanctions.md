Baghdad — All 4 Iraqi airports halted Iran flights.

Pilgrims, students and patients now face a land journey of at least 12 hours.

Ground-service firms reportedly won't risk secondary [US](country:US) sanctions and exclusion from dollar clearing, so Iraq enforced Washington's ban on Iranian airlines.

"People's lives are at risk," said Haidar Mansour, 50, who needs a kidney transplant in [Iran](country:IR).

Baghdad has issued no statement, and Iranian carriers now rely on routes via Turkey, Russia, China and Armenia.
