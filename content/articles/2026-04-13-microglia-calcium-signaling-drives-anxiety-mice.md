Salt Lake City — Immune cells drove anxiety in mice, not neurons. Optogenetically stimulating hippocampal microglia induced anxiety only when calcium ions entered — blocking calcium eliminated the behavior entirely. Whether the same calcium pathway drives human anxiety disorders — and whether blocking it would outperform SSRIs — remains untested.
