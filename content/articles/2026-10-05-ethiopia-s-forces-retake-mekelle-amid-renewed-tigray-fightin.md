Mekelle — Tigray's capital changed hands Saturday after the TPLF withdrew.

Residents fear a return of the 2020-22 war, which killed hundreds of thousands, estimates say.

[Ethiopia](country:ET)'s federal troops and the allied Tigray Peace Force entered, taking key sites including the airport, a resident said.

The Tigray People's Liberation Front calls the withdrawal temporary and says its forces will keep fighting from other positions.

Former interim president Abraham Belay says a new interim administration is being set up, aiming for regional elections within months.
