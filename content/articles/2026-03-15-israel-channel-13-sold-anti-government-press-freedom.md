Billionaire Len Blavatnik sold Channel 13 to Netanyahu's critics. He rejected a bid from Patrick Drahi of pro-government i24 News, which would have consolidated Netanyahu's allies' media control. Regulatory approval is still pending; Netanyahu's government may yet block the deal.
