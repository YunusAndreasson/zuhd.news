London — A 50-megapixel Earth model now resolves individual storm systems.

Previous models had about 10,000 pixels to represent the entire planet; storms appeared as blurs, misrepresenting rainfall intensity and duration.

4 systematic blind spots in storm and rainfall representation remain unresolved, the study found.
