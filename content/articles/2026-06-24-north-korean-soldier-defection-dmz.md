Seoul — A [North Korean](country:KP) soldier crossed the demilitarized zone on Tuesday night.

Defections across the land border are rare — the zone is forested, mined, and monitored on both sides; most North Koreans reach [South Korea](country:KR) via China.

South Korea's Joint Chiefs say authorities are investigating how the soldier crossed; no motive has been confirmed.
