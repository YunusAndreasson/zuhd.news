Santa Rosa — Machado re-enters [Venezuela](country:VE) after 10 months, route undisclosed.

Talks between the interim government and a rival opposition faction exclude her movement.

The [US](country:US) seized Nicolás Maduro in January and backed his deputy Delcy Rodríguez, who signed an oil deal with Washington.

President Donald Trump said he knew nothing of her return and that "we're doing really well in Venezuela."

Adviser David Smolansky says she will press for an election date.
