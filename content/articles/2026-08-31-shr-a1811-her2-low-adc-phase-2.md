Shanghai — A trial found an 81.5% response rate.

The trial recruited patients previously told they didn't qualify for targeted therapy.

The [China](country:CN)-developed antibody-drug conjugate targets a "HER2-low" category invisible to older tests.

Researchers say larger randomized trials are still needed before doctors can prescribe it routinely.
