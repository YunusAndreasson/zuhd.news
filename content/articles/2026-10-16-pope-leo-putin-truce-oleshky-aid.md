Oleshky — Residents cannot safely leave, and sometimes cannot bury their dead.

Pope Leo XIV, recovering from lung surgery, asked Putin by phone for a truce.

The Kremlin said Putin blamed Kyiv for conditions in Oleshky and welcomed Vatican help, but its readout mentions no truce.

[Russia](country:RU) and the Vatican agreed to coordinate on supplies, but neither has announced a date or route.
