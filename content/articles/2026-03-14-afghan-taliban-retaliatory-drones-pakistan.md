Taliban drones injured 4 in Pakistan, including 2 children. The Taliban launched 3 drones to retaliate for Pakistan's airstrikes on their 313 Corps headquarters in Kabul, and Pakistan's military intercepted all 3 before they reached their targets. Pakistan's Operation Ghazab lil-Haq will continue despite Taliban warnings of further strikes.
