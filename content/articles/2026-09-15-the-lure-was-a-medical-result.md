London — UK cyber officials say [Iran](country:IR) targets its own exiled critics.

The campaign helps repress dissidents, activists and journalists living abroad.

Operators lured targets with fake MRI results, showing they had targets' health data beforehand.

Britain's National Cyber Security Centre has not said how many people were targeted.
