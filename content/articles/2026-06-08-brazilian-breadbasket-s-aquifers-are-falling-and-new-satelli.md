Brasília — NASA satellites found [Brazil](country:BR)'s farm aquifers falling.

A NASA-Brazil satellite study revealed aquifer declines beneath one of the world's largest food exporters, making previously invisible drawdowns measurable.

No extraction limits exist for the affected aquifers; Brazil is the world's top soy exporter.
