London — Scientists call a viral heatwave claim "simply wrong."

A UK tabloid claimed falling pollution causes heatwaves; researchers say greenhouse-gas warming, not aerosol loss, drives Europe's extreme summers.

Reform UK's Richard Tice cited the claim on social media to attack net-zero policy.
