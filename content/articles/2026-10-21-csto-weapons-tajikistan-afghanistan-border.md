Moscow — Four allies will arm Tajikistan's Afghan border, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

Its source, Secretary General Taalatbek Masadykov, says the program does not threaten [Afghanistan](country:AF).

Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, [Russia](country:RU) and Belarus will send equipment and armaments, the Collective Security Treaty Organization's secretary general said.

Masadykov expects the first deliveries to [Tajikistan](country:TJ) before the end of this year.
