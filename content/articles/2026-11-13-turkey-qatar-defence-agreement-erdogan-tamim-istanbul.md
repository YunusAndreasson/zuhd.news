Istanbul — [Turkey](country:TR) has signed 2 mutual defence pacts since August.

President Erdogan said Turkey and [Qatar](country:QA) will work to prevent renewed war in the Gulf.

Turkey's parliament ratified August's Makkah pact with [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) and [Pakistan](country:PK) last week, making an attack on one an attack on all.

Erdogan's office gave no terms, so what each side owes the other is unknown.
