Boston — Sepsis hospitalizations in Massachusetts tripled since 2010. Researchers suspect hospitals are using AI coding tools to diagnose sepsis more broadly, capturing higher Medicare reimbursements rather than tracking a genuine disease surge. Hospitals across every US state use the same AI billing tools; if the pattern holds, US sepsis statistics — and the federal funding tied to them — cannot be trusted.
