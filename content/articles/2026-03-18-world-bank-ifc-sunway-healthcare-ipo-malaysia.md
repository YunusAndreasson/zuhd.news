Sunway Healthcare raised RM60m from the World Bank. The World Bank's International Finance Corporation purchased a cornerstone stake in the company's IPO, with proceeds earmarked for expanding hospital bed capacity across Malaysia. IFC targets 1.5 billion people with quality health services by 2030, with private hospital chains now central to that strategy.
