$200 million in State Department subsidies will fund cheap smartphones across the Indo-Pacific. The Pax Silica initiative requires handsets to run American operating systems, locking partner nations into US tech ecosystems as Chinese brands hold 52% of overseas markets. India, Japan, Australia, and 7 other countries have signed on.
