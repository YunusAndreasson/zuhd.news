Poland became the first NATO ally to quit the Ottawa Convention. Deputy Defence Minister Paweł Zalewski confirmed the withdrawal Friday, calling mines essential to fortifying Poland's borders with Russia and Belarus. Ukraine has also begun leaving the 1997 treaty, signalling that Russia's invasion is unravelling post-Cold War arms control norms.
