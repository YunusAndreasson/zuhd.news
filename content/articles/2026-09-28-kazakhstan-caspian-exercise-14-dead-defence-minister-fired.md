Astana — Tokayev fired his defence minister after 14 sailors drowned.

The firing signals accountability atop [Kazakhstan](country:KZ)'s historically opaque military.

Seventeen servicemen were swept into the Caspian Sea during a 24 September naval exercise as weather suddenly deteriorated.

The ministry initially reported 19 swept overboard, later revising the toll to 17, with 14 dead.

The presidential commission investigating the deaths has yet to report a cause.
