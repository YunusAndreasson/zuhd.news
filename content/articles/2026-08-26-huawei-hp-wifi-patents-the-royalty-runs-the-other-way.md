Beijing — Huawei now licenses Wi-Fi 7 patents to HP.

The US blacklisted [Huawei](country:CN) from chips, yet it still collects licensing fees from American laptops.

Huawei charges 50 cents per device under its Wi-Fi 6 patents, used in 1.6 billion gadgets by year-end 2025.

The deal follows Huawei's 2025 patent lawsuit against HP at Europe's Unified Patent Court.
