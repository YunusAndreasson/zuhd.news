Mumbai — Vedanta splits into 4 listed units, debuting Sunday.

[India](country:IN)'s Vedanta separates the entities so each can raise capital and be valued independently.

Existing shareholders receive shares in Vedanta Aluminium, Oil & Gas, Power, and Iron & Steel at Sunday's open.
