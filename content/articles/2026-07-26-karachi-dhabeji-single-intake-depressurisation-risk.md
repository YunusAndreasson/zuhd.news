Karachi — 75 million gallons a day just stopped moving.

A power cable fault shut 3 pumps at Dhabeji, the single station supplying nearly all of [Pakistan](country:PK)'s largest city.

K-Electric hasn't said when supply resumes; a depressurised network risks drawing in contaminated groundwater.
