South Africa's Eskom has surplus electricity after years of blackouts. Rooftop solar has flooded the midday grid faster than storage can absorb, creating excess power that no traditional consumer can use but Bitcoin mining can. Eskom plans to sell the surplus to Bitcoin miners at reduced prices, part of a R112-billion cost-cutting target.
