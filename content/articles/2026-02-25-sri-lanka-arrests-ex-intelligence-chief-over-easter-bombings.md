Police arrested former intelligence chief Suresh Sallay before dawn. He faces conspiracy charges over the 2019 Easter bombings that killed 279 — his agency had received Indian intelligence warnings and ignored them. Survivors expect the investigation to reach former President Gotabaya Rajapaksa, who won that year's election days after the attacks.
