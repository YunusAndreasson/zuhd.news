Brazil — A survey found 60 Telegram channels posted 418 doubts about voting machines.

58 of them back Flávio Bolsonaro, who faces Lula in [Brazil](country:BR)'s runoff on Sunday.

Electoral judges debunked a claim that machines froze the count on 6 October, and one channel reposted it 12 October.

Anthropologist Letícia Cesarino says Telegram's September compliance plan has no force of law and the court can barely compel it.

Eduardo Bolsonaro suggested Friday that machines could be rigged and his brother may have to leave if he loses.
