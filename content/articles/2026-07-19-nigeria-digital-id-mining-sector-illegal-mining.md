Abuja — Miners will soon need digital IDs.

The mining ministry is linking licence holders to [Nigeria](country:NG)'s national identity commission after Mining Marshals arrested over 300 suspected illegal operators.

Minister Dele Alake says the system must curb mining-funded insecurity, though no rollout date is set.
