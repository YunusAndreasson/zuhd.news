Milan — Intesa Sanpaolo can now bid €32 billion for Monte dei Paschi.

[Italy](country:IT)'s biggest bank would absorb a lender the state rescued in 2017.

The cash-and-share offer needed the European Central Bank's green light to take control, which was widely expected.

Markets regulator Consob should approve the offer early next month, and antitrust decides in the second half of November.
