Karachi — 78 children tested positive at one hospital.

A petition puts the true count near 200, all infected while receiving treatment.

The Sindh High Court found health-department reports too thin to show how syringes or equipment were reused.

An independent committee, led by the chief secretary, must report on 37 officials' responsibility.
