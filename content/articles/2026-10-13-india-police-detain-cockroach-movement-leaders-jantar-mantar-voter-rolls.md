New Delhi — Police detained 3 "cockroach" leaders demanding the election chief's resignation.

Delhi Police stripped Jantar Mantar of protest status on Monday and named 3 distant venues instead.

A police complaint charges trespass, violating prohibitory orders and breaching a peace bond, police sources said.

The Election Commission denies favouring any party and says wrongly deleted voters can re-enrol.

Founder Abhijeet Dipke says he joins the Delhi protest Wednesday and the party will challenge the detentions in court.
