Brazos County — A property tax break could decide a $22.6bn TSMC fab.

The chipmaker's unit ties its choice of site to local tax relief.

A TSMC unit told Texas officials the abatement would be a deciding factor, in a filing Monday with the state comptroller.

Arizona, where the unit is based, remains a rival site, the filing says.
