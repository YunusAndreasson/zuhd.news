Beijing — [China](country:CN)'s carbon emissions fell 1% last quarter.

It signals a lasting shift in oil demand, not a temporary recession effect.

Electric vehicles cut transport oil use by 16%, even as coal-fired power output rebounded.

New five-year energy plans set no quantitative targets to lock in the trend.
