London — Regulators opened probes into Virgin Atlantic and Trainline.

Hidden fees make it impossible to compare prices before booking in the [UK](country:GB).

Charges appear only late in checkout, which the Competition and Markets Authority says hides which company is actually cheaper.

A finding of wrongdoing could bring fines and mandatory customer refunds.
