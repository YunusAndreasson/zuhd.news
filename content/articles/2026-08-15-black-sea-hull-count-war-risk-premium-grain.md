Odesa — TASS says Russia struck 130 ships since July.

Most of the vessels TASS counted were bulk carriers moving grain, not weapons.

Strikes work by raising war-risk insurance premiums, and that cost lands on grain-importing countries like [Egypt](country:EG) and [Bangladesh](country:BD).

No independent source has verified TASS's tally of Russian defence ministry briefings.
