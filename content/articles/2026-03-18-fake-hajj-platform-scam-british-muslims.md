Hajj fraud cost British pilgrims £195,752 in 2025. The Council of British Hajjis estimates total losses reach millions of pounds annually; only 3% of victims report to police. Fraudsters are building fake versions of Nusuk, Saudi Arabia's official Hajj portal, with Hajj starting May 25.
