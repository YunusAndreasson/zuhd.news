Seoul — South Korea outranked the US in global AI adoption. Three South Korean apps entered the global top 50 most-used AI products, with South Korea placing 4th and the US 20th, per a report by venture firm Andreessen Horowitz. With Singapore leading globally, the rankings show countries using AI most are not the same as those building it.
