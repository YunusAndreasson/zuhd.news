Quito — [Ecuador](country:EC) swapped $1.6B of debt to protect Galapagos.

Sovereign debt is restructured at a discount; savings go to conservation — but critics say intermediaries capture the fees and control.

A $1.3 trillion UN climate finance pledge drives governments to seek instruments that deploy capital without new spending.
