The Hague — A 9–6 vote found Myanmar's military committed genocide against the Rohingya.

The 2017 "clearance operations" killed at least 10,000 people and drove 700,000 into [Bangladesh](country:BD).

[The Gambia](country:GM), backed by the Organization of Islamic Cooperation, filed the case in November 2019.

"Today, the truth has been recognized," said Monajat Alam, 58, a Rohingya refugee in a Bangladesh camp.

Myanmar's junta called the ruling unfair, and the National Unity Government says justice requires ending junta rule.
