The US declared Iran no threat while bombing it. White House press secretary Karoline Leavitt made the statement as oil held above $100 per barrel, defying US President Donald Trump's campaign promise of lower gas prices. The International Energy Agency released 400 million barrels of emergency reserves without lowering prices — Trump now argues the US benefits as a net oil exporter.
