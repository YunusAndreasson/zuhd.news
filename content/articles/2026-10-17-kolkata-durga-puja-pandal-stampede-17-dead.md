Kolkata — 17 people died in a Durga Puja crush.

Another 35 to 40 were injured, and Modi pledged 2 lakh rupees to each bereaved family.

Police say rain drove a dense crowd at the pandal's exit gate, which eyewitnesses call narrow and poorly lit.

"Where was the police when the actual incident took place?" asked West Bengal Congress president Subhankar Sarkar.

[India](country:IN)'s Home Minister Amit Shah visits the injured on Sunday as police investigate the crush.
