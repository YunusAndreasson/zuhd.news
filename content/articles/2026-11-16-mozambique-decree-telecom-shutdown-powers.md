Maputo — A decree lets authorities suspend networks for 48 hours.

No court order is needed, and the grounds are national security or public order.

Civil society groups in [Mozambique](country:MZ), including the Center for Democracy and Human Rights, asked the Ombudsman to test it against the constitution.

Communications Minister Américo Muchanga denied in March that it restricts rights, saying it protects society in critical moments.

The Ombudsman has not yet said whether Decree 48/2025 is compatible with the constitution.
