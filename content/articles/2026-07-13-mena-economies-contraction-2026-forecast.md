Dubai — MENA output is forecast to contract 0.5% this year.

Conflict and Hormuz-linked energy volatility weigh heaviest on oil-importing, non-Gulf states rather than the surplus-rich Gulf producers.

A sharp rebound is projected for 2027, contingent on the region's conflicts cooling.
