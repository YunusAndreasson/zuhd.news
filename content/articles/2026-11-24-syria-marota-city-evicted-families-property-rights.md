Damascus — 7,500 families were cleared for Assad's towers.

[Syria](country:SY)'s new authorities are building on the same land, not returning it.

Decree 66 paid owners in shares, and residents who fled abroad missed inspections and forfeited alternative housing.

Syria Indicator found 2,000 families received no housing, compensation or rent allowance by May 2024.

The governorate's investment head plans towers of 13 to 70 floors, one for tech companies.
