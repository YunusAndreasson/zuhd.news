Colombo — Trincomalee's oil tank farm has sat idle since 1987. India signed a development deal in April 2025, seeking an Indian Ocean energy foothold to counter China's nearby Hambantota port lease. Sri Lanka has submitted a concept note for India and the United Arab Emirates to review, but no investors have been selected and no timeline is set.
