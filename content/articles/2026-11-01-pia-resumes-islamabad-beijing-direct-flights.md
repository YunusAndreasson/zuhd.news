Islamabad — One weekly Friday flight now links Pakistan and Beijing.

Pakistan International Airlines resumed the route on 30 October and now partners with three Chinese carriers.

Air China, Shenzhen Airlines and Hainan Airlines supply the onward connections, with Air China serving domestic destinations through Beijing.

PIA says it aims to offer competitive fares on the weekly service.
