Zhengzhou — A 100,000-card domestic cluster won the Gordon Bell Prize.

[China](country:CN)'s all-domestic hardware, built under [US](country:US) export controls, took high-performance computing's top applications prize.

Sugon's Shuguang 8000 ran an AI wavefunction calculation of nitrogenase's iron-molybdenum cofactor, 113 electrons across 76 orbitals, mainland outlet Zhidx reported.

A 1.5km-resolution global weather model on the same cluster is a finalist for the climate-modelling prize.
