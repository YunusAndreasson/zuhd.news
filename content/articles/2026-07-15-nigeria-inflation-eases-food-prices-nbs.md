Abuja — Food inflation jumped to 3.75% monthly in June.

Base effects from 2025's currency collapse flatter the headline rate even as garri and yam prices keep rising, the statistics office said.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s Niger State faces 42% inflation even as the national rate shows relief.
