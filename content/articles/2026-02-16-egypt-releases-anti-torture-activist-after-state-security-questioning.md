Egypt detained an anti-torture researcher for 3 hours Sunday. State Security Prosecution questioned Dr. Aida Seif al-Dawla, co-founder of Al-Nadeem Center for Rehabilitation of Victims of Violence, and charged her with "broadcasting false news" after the organization published a 188-case report documenting torture in state facilities. She was released on 100,000 Egyptian pound bail — rights groups condemned the summons as part of a systematic campaign to silence human rights documentation.
