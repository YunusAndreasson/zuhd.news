US border agents tracked people's movements through phone ad auctions. Every app ad triggers an instant auction that broadcasts each device's location to dozens of buyers; a DHS document confirms CBP sourced this data without warrants. The DHS Inspector General found 3 agencies illegally used the data; 70 lawmakers are pushing legislation to ban it.
