Pompeii — An enslaved woman's prayer to Venus survived Pompeii's ash. The Ancient Graffiti Project, led by historian Rebecca Benefiel, recovered this history — an 1840s German archaeologist had dismissed all such inscriptions as trivial, halting the field for a century. The project has catalogued Pompeii and Herculaneum; the voices of ordinary people in the empire's other buried cities remain unread.
