US-Israeli strikes shattered Tehran's only UNESCO World Heritage Site. Precision-guided munitions struck multiple sites listed on UNESCO's public registry, which grants wartime protection under the 1954 Hague Convention. An Iranian official called it a declaration of war on civilization; UNESCO has not condemned the strikes.
