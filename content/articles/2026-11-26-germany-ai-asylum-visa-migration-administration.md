Berlin — [Germany](country:DE)'s AI bill covers asylum, visas and residence permits.

Experts warn the software would be fed especially sensitive data with too few limits on authorities.

The draft lets agencies train AI on personal data, monitor procedures automatically and match applicants against public internet data.

The Bundestag is debating the bill, which would amend the Asylum Act and the Residence Act.
