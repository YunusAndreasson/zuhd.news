Amman — [Iran](country:IR) used Chinese satellite imagery to strike [Jordan](country:JO).

The July strike killed three [US](country:US) troops at Muwaffaq Salti Air Base.

US officials told the Wall Street Journal that unnamed entities in [China](country:CN) supplied the high-resolution images, not the Chinese government.

The report surfaces before Trump and Xi Jinping meet on September 24.
