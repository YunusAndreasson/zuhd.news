Kathmandu — 7 nations now run entirely on renewable electricity. Nepal, Bhutan, and Iceland anchor the group, powered mainly by hydropower — a geography-dependent advantage that most fossil-fuel importers cannot replicate. As Hormuz drives oil to $115, their independence is a template — hydro requires geography most energy-poor nations lack.
