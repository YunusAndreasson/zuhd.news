Doha — Al Ruwais port reopened to Iranian cargo after 5 months shut.

[Qatar](country:QA)'s biggest gateway for Iranian produce, seafood and cement resumed accepting ships from Dayyer port, an Iranian trade official said.

The route's revival is an early market signal on whether Gulf states are pricing the Iran war as over.
