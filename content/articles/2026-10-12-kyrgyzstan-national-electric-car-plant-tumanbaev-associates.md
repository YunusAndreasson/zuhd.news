Bishkek — Chinese investors had already built [Kyrgyzstan](country:KG)'s BARS electric-car plant.

The economy ministry announced it in August as a launch under a national brand.

Reporters found the builders included the presidential affairs office and Module House, a company linked to office head Kanybek Tumanbaev.

The report carries no reply from Tumanbaev or the ministry.
