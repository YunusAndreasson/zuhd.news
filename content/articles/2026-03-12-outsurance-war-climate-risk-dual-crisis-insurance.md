Storm claims in Australia nearly doubled OUTsurance's natural disaster costs. Natural perils claims hit 12.4% of net earned premium — nearly double the prior period — while CEO Marthinus Visser called war-driven stagflation "navigable." The company's full-year results will show whether geopolitical stagflation and worsening climate storms compound in ways six-month numbers conceal.
