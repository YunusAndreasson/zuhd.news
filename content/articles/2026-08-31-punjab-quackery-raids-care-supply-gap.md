Regulators can shut a quack's clinic, not create the doctor to replace it.

The Commission ran 6,380 raids, sealing 1,153 active illegal centers alongside repeat and abandoned sites.

The Commission is now monitoring 4,068 more clinics to ensure only licensed staff treat patients.
