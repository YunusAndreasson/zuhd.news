Cornwall — A sea mirage off Cornwall reshaped a distant ship into a hovering bear.

A Fata Morgana forms when warm air sits over cold sea, bending light to stack and distort distant objects into unrecognisable shapes.

The same optics produce "floating ship" mirages worldwide; [Britain](country:GB)'s spring warm-overs make them especially common off its coasts.
