Rio de Janeiro — A siphonophore swam 552 meters deep offshore.

It's one of 31 species found off [Brazil](country:BR)'s coast in the ocean's midwater, the largest habitable ecosystem on Earth.

Researchers say the siphonophore may belong to a genus never yet described.
