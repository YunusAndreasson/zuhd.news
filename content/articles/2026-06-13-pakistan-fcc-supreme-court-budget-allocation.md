Islamabad — New court funded at double the per-judge rate.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s Federal Constitutional Court, with 7 judges, received Rs6bn; the 18-judge Supreme Court gets Rs7.44bn — Rs857m versus Rs413m per seat.

The court has no permanent building and will expand to 13 judges; its temporary seat remains unresolved.
