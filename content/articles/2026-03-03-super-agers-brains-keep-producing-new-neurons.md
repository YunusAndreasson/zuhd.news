---
title: "Super-Agers Still Grow New Neurons"
date: "2026-02-25T00:00:00Z"
source: "Nature"
sourceUrl: "https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-00599-5"
category: "science"
---

Super-agers grow new neurons twice as fast as peers. Their hippocampi keep genetic programs for neuron growth switched on — programs that Alzheimer's disease shuts off. Researchers now plan to test whether exercise and social engagement can replicate this in people at risk of dementia.
