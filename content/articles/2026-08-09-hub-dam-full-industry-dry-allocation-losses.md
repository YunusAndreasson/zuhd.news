Karachi — Hub Dam sits full while factories shut down.

Thousands of jobs and Rs7 billion in annual tax revenue are at risk.

Pakistan's water authority still splits supply 36.7% Hub, 62.3% Karachi under a 1980s formula, though Hub's population grew fiftyfold since.

The Chamber's appeal to [Pakistan](country:PK)'s prime minister for intervention awaits a response.
