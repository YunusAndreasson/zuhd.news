Raiganj — Police killed the suspect in a girl's murder before trial.

Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari says police need no permission from superiors in rape and murder cases.

Police say the suspect fired first at a raid near the Bihar border, hitting a vehicle tyre, and they returned fire.

Security-camera footage shows the 12-year-old riding behind him on a cycle hours before she vanished.

No court will now test the case against him.
