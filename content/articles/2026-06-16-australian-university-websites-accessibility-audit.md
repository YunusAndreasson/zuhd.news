Canberra — All 42 [Australian](country:AU) universities failed accessibility audits across 2022-2024.

Auditors found missing alt text, broken headings, and screen-reader incompatibilities across every campus.

The study argues accessibility regresses without enforcement; automated checks miss the critical barriers screen-reader users face.
