Tallinn — Estonia says Russian special services ordered an August arson attack on a drone maker.

A NATO state is formally naming Moscow for sabotage on its own soil for the first time.

[Russia](country:RU) runs sabotage through hired proxies across Europe to stay below the threshold of war, Estonia's minister said.

The Kremlin's Dmitry Peskov called the accusation "baseless" and said Russia would not take it seriously.

Estonia extradited three Latvian suspects and summoned Russia's ambassador after the August attack on Milrem Robotics.
