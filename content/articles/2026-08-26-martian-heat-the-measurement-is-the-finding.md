Providence — Mars's southern mantle is far hotter than its north.

The imbalance may explain Mars's split lowland-highland geography.

Scientists inferred the heat from 16 years of orbital gravity wobbles, without drilling the surface.

Researchers still don't know if an ancient impact or ongoing mantle convection caused it.
