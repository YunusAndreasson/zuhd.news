Bitcoin rose 8.5% this week as tech stocks fell. Since the Middle East conflict began, Bitcoin gained 13% while gold fell 6% and US equities also lost ground. US spot Bitcoin ETFs posted $1.3 billion in inflows in March — their first positive month since October 2025 — leaving open whether the decorrelation from tech stocks is a war-time anomaly or a permanent shift.
