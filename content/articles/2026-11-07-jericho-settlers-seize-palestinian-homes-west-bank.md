Jericho — Settlers expelled 13 Palestinian households from their homes, residents say.

The Kaaba family of seven slept in their car and on the street, and the father's arm is bruised and swollen.

About 30 settlers, some masked, burst in on 4 November, beat residents with sticks and hung [Israel](country:IL)'s flags from balconies.

On 5 November an Israeli soldier stood beside armed settlers in the neighbourhood, and the families remained shut out.
