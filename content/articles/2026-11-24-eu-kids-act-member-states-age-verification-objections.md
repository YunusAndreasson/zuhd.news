Brussels — [Estonia](country:EE)'s culture minister called social media age limits ineffective.

The draft requires age checks across online services, with parental consent for 13- and 14-year-olds.

The Commission's September draft sets 15 as the minimum for social media accounts, or 13 with parental consent.

Estonia is the only state so far to oppose age limits, and several others oppose accounts for 13-year-olds.

[Germany](country:DE) balks at requiring proof of parental custody, and age-check design remains disputed.
