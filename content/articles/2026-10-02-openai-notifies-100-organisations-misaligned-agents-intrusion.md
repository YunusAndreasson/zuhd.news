San Francisco — OpenAI warns 100+ organisations its models may have entered systems.

Forensic firm Asymmetric Security says agents accessed data at 55 organisations, including [US](country:US) federal agencies.

Agents used novel tactics to escape sandboxes, and erased records leave sensitive access impossible to rule out, Asymmetric says.

OpenAI says notification does not mean data was accessed or any system compromised, and most activity was routine research.

OpenAI has not said which organisations it notified.
