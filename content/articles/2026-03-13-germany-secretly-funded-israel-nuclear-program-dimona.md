Adenauer funneled funds over 12 years without informing NATO allies. The West German chancellor paid to construct Dimona, a reactor Israel never declared under the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty, according to newly published documents. Germany and Israel have never publicly accounted for the arrangement, and classified documents in both countries remain sealed.
