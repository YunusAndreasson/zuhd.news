Canberra — Brent crude hit $113 as the Strait of Hormuz stays closed. International Energy Agency chief Fatih Birol called the disruption the equivalent of the 1970s oil shock and Russia's 2022 gas cutoff combined. US President Donald Trump threatened to bomb Iranian power plants if Hormuz remains closed past Monday's deadline.
