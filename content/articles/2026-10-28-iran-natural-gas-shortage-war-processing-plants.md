Tehran — Strikes cut [Iran](country:IR)'s gas capacity by a fifth.

Households face a winter heating shortage in the world's third-largest gas-producing country.

Strikes on South Pars processing plants in March cut capacity, and Iran's energy authorities say less than half of it is restored, leaving a daily shortfall of 400 million cubic metres.

The head of the state Plan and Budget Organisation warned pressure drops could cut supply to entire cities.

Russian gas deliveries, still under negotiation, may not arrive in time for this winter.
