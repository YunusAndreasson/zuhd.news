Boston — National Football League donors showed 93% CTE.

A British Medical Journal study found the population estimate is far lower, near one in four.

Families donate brains when they suspect disease, enriching donor samples and inflating estimates of true prevalence.

No living test for CTE exists, and researchers are pursuing blood and imaging markers.
