Their pensions absorb risk at 153.4 yen per dollar, a choice never made.

Central banks selling reserves fight a market many times larger, but a sustained structural buyer shifts the underlying flow itself.

The Bank of Japan has resisted intervention while the pension fund's reallocation remains unconfirmed.
