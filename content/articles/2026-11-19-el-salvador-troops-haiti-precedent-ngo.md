Port-au-Prince — 2,500 Salvadoran troops operate in [Haiti](country:HT) outside UN command.

Analysts warn the deployment sets a precedent for future regional security operations.

President Nayib Bukele signed a bilateral deal for joint operations with Haitian police, bypassing the UN-backed Gang Suppression Force.

Officially it rescues 4 missing technicians, and Salvadoran Security Minister Gustavo Villatoro says troops leave once they are found.

The International Crisis Group says troops at Stade Sylvio Cator appear to be preparing a Village de Dieu raid.
