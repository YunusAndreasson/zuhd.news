Paris — [France](country:FR)'s space agency wants 1,000 telescopes.

Cheap, mass-produced optics let smaller states field their own imaging constellations.

The French space agency's request demands designs manufacturable in runs over 1,000 units, trading resolution for revisit rate.

It expects demonstrations by the end of 2026, with qualification targeted for 2027 or 2028.
