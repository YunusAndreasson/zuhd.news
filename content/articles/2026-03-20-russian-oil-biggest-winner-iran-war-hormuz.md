Russia emerged as the Iran war's biggest oil winner. Trump's sanctions waiver let Russia fill the Hormuz supply gap — 20% of global oil — earning €672m in extra revenue. Energy analyst George Voloshin warned $200 oil is "no longer far-fetched" if the war escalates.
