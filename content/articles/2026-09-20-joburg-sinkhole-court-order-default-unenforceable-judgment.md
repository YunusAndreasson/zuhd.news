Johannesburg — Sherley Markowitz, 81, has been displaced 6 years.

She won 4 High Court orders, yet the city keeps defaulting on them.

A collapsed municipal stormwater culvert swallowed her home, and the city pays rent, storage and guards but has not repaired it.

The Johannesburg Roads Agency says building would take 18 months and gives no start date.
