Washington — Xi wants 1 word changed in US Taiwan policy.

The [US](country:US) has never opposed independence, and doing so would curb arms sales, analysts say.

[China](country:CN)'s foreign ministry said Xi asked Trump to oppose independence, casting it as existing US policy to maintain.

Trump has called a $14 billion Taiwan arms package, held up for months, a "very good negotiating chip."

The White House has not released its account of the meeting.
