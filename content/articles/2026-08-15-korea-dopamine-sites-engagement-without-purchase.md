Seoul — Shoppers cart $44,860 watches they never buy.

The trend on [South Korea](country:KR)'s "dopamine sites" separates the ritual of shopping from any purchase.

One cart held a Patek Philippe watch, a Hermès bag, a Tiffany ring and a Cartier bracelet.

Whether the platforms treat these carts as data or as failed sales is unclear.
