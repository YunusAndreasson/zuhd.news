Oslo — Norse Atlantic suspended every flight on Thursday.

The airline will file for bankruptcy unless investors deliver short-term financing.

Fuel prices have risen sharply with the Iran war, and the carrier met holders of its $52 million bond on 25 November.

Shares rose 17.1% to 0.038 Norwegian kroner on takeover hopes, though the company says no viable solution is assured.

Norse is in talks with investors on a takeover that includes financing.
