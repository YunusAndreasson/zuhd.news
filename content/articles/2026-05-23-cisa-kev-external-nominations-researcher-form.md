Washington — The [US](country:US) cyber agency opened its exploited-vulnerability catalog to outside nominations.

The Known Exploited Vulnerabilities list drives patch deadlines for all federal civilian agencies; outside exploitation evidence can now trigger mandatory remediation orders.

The agency set no review timeline or criteria for accepting nominations.
