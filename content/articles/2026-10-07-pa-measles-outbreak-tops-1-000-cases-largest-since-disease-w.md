Pennsylvania — 1,078 measles cases and 5 deaths this year.

All 5 dead were unvaccinated, including 2 infants too young for the vaccine.

Cases began climbing in April, and state officials logged 145 in the past 7 days and 207 hospitalisations.

Health officials said 40 of the 74 cases added Wednesday were more than two weeks old.

The Pan American Health Organization reviews in November whether the [US](country:US) keeps measles elimination status.
