New York — Iran says Hormuz opens 7 days after the [US](country:US) agrees.

Brent crude stood at $114.90 on 22 September, up 23.9% in 30 days.

Under the plan, [Iran](country:IR) restores navigation if Washington lifts its blockade and eases oil-export limits, Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said.

Supreme National Security Council secretary Mohsen Rezaei said Hormuz stays closed until Iran's seven conditions are met.

The White House declined to comment, and Iran's seven-day clock starts only after Washington accepts.
