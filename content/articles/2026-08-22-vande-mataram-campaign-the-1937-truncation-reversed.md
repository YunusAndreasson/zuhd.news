New Delhi — The ruling party demands all six verses sung.

The demand pressures [India](country:IN)'s opposition to reverse a compromise reached with Muslims in 1937.

The Congress Working Committee re-adopted its 1937 resolution on August 19, restricting Congress functions to two stanzas.

BJP's campaign begins September 17, Modi's birthday, running for one month.
