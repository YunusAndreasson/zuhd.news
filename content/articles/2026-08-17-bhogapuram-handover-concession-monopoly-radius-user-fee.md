Visakhapatnam — The last scheduled flight departed Sunday night.

A navy-run airfield in [India](country:IN) returns to military use after decades of civil flights.

The airport code VTZ transfers to Bhogapuram at 12:01 a.m., alongside all scheduled traffic.

Details on non-scheduled flights at the old airfield have not yet been specified, officials said.
