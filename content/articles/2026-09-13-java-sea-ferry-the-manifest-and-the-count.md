Banjarmasin — 140 of 243 aboard remain missing, rescuers said.

[Indonesia](country:ID)'s ferries carry inter-island passengers who have no other way to travel.

The Virgo Transport 8 lost contact after three-meter waves hit it overnight in the Java Sea.

Search teams have not said when they will end the hunt for the missing.
