Beijing — A 35-billion-parameter AI now runs in 3GB on phones.

ModelBest says it exceeds 30 tokens a second, the minimum its co-founder calls smooth.

It predicts which expert modules the next layer needs and streams their weights from flash storage while computing.

Co-chief scientist Yao Yuan says power draw and phone-system permissions still block AI-native phones.

Yao predicts GPT-6-level intelligence will run on phones within two years, a view he says lacks consensus.
