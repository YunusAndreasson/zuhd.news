Kargil — [India](country:IN)'s Ladakh region grows 62% of its apricots.

Erratic rain and vanishing winter snow are killing blossoms in a high desert crop with no water-cheap substitute.

The meteorological department warns short, intense July downpours are becoming this Himalayan region's new normal.
