Islamabad — Court pushed sentence pleas to September 23.

Imaan Zainab Mazari-Hazir and Hadi Ali Chattha stay jailed for social media posts.

A sessions court gave the pair 17 years under [Pakistan](country:PK)'s cybercrime law after an August 2025 complaint.

Their sentence-suspension hearing resumes September 23, delayed after the prosecutor's absence.
