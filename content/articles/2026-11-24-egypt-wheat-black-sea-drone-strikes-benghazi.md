Cairo — Egypt took 9.44 million tons of Black Sea wheat since January.

A wheat ship bound for [Egypt](country:EG) sank off [Bulgaria](country:BG) this month, and drone attacks on Black Sea shipping have surged since October.

Egypt imports 62% of its wheat, and Vanguard, a maritime risk firm, says 2 sea drones hit the ship.

Russia and Ukraine both deny involvement, and no forensic evidence names who struck the ships.

Foreign Minister Badr Abdelatty pressed both countries this month to safeguard Black Sea navigation for grain.
