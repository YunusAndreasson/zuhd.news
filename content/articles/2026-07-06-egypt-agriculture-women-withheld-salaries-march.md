Giza — One worker has earned LE90 a month since 1996.

Hundreds of women on "temporary" Agriculture Ministry contracts marched after 5 years of unpaid wages despite a 2021 court order granting them permanent status.

A joint finance-agriculture committee is set to meet in September on the unpaid rulings.
