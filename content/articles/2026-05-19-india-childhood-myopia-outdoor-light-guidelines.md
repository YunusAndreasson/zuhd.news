New Delhi — 2 hours outdoors daily: [India](country:IN)'s new myopia prescription.

14% of urban Indian schoolchildren are already myopic; the spike tracks 4–6 daily screen hours, the All India Ophthalmological Society said.

Half the global population may be myopic by 2050 without preventive intervention, according to recent estimates.
