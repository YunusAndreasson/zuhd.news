Jerusalem — 67 of 120 lawmakers recommended Eisenkot over Netanyahu's 46.

Arab-led parties' record 19 seats supplied the majority, yet Eisenkot rules out governing with them.

Netanyahu's bloc won 53 seats and Eisenkot's Jewish-led parties 48, so neither reaches 61 without the Arab lists.

Netanyahu accuses Arab parties of stealing the vote, but the Central Election Committee said the election's integrity held.

President Isaac Herzog names his nominee Sunday, who then has 28 days to form a government.
