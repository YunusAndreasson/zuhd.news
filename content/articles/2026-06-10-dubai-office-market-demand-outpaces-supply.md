Dubai — Firms relocating to [the UAE](country:AE) have outpaced office supply.

Tax and connectivity advantages are pulling companies to Dubai faster than new buildings open, pushing rents to record highs.

No new supply is announced for the rest of the year, keeping upward pressure on rents.
