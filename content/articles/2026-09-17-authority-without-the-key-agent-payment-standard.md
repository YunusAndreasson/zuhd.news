San Francisco — AI agents can now pay without holding keys.

A rogue prompt can't override limits enforced by the wallet itself.

It works like a corporate card with a merchant lock, but the constraint lives in the wallet, not a prompt.

The standard has no mechanism yet for revoking or reversing a bad agent payment.
