Pakistan cut public-sector salaries by up to 30%. The Strait of Hormuz closure, triggered by the US-Israel war, has severed Pakistan's oil imports and forced fuel prices to crisis levels. Cabinet ministers will also forgo their next 2 months' salaries, with no end date for the austerity measures named.
