London — 85 reviewed studies show medical AI handles diagnosis but defers to doctors on high-stakes treatment decisions.

Task divisions follow inherent risk, the review found; evaluation is shifting from diagnostic accuracy to clinical efficiency.

No health regulator has issued binding rules on how hospitals divide tasks between AI and clinicians.
