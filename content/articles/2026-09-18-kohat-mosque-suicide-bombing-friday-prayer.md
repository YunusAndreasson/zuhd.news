Kohat — 15 killed, 56 hurt, at Friday prayer.

Five of the dead were police officers, whose offices sat beside the mosque.

A bomber rammed an explosives-laden vehicle into the building, and two gunmen tried to storm the adjoining police compound.

No group has claimed the attack in a district where the Pakistani Taliban and ISIS-Khorasan operate.
