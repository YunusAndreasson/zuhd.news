New Delhi — 8.3% wholesale inflation precedes a central bank decision.

[India](country:IN)'s finance ministry identified Hormuz and a below-average monsoon as the top risks; wholesale prices hit 8.3% in April against 3.48% retail.

The Reserve Bank of India sets rates within days; the ministry said policy responses require evidence of persistent second-round effects.
