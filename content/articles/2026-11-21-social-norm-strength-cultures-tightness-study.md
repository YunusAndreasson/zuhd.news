Japan — Norms often vary more within cultural regions than between them.

Existing theories may have overstated how much cultures differ in norm strength, the authors say.

Two studies of 3,344 people in [Japan](country:JP), [South Korea](country:KR), the [US](country:US) and the [UK](country:GB) varied norms by referent, type and context.

The paper is an early release that Nature will replace with a final version.
