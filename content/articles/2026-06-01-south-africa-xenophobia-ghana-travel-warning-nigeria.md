Johannesburg — Vigilante groups attacked foreign nationals, seizing businesses.

[Ghana](country:GH) evacuated 300 citizens last week — first African government to repatriate over xenophobia — and suspended travel to [South Africa](country:ZA).

[Nigeria](country:NG) is organising its own repatriation and has warned citizens against reprisal attacks inside Nigeria.
