London — A new study found nanoplastic toxicity correlates with surface chemistry, not particle size.

Current food, water, and air safety limits measure particle count; the authors say this metric has no biological basis and may leave harmful exposures unregulated.

The paper calls for chemistry-based exposure thresholds to replace size rules.
