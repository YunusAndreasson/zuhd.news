Athens — Two Greek tycoons allegedly funded the [US](country:US) ambassador's luxury trips.

Allegations say Kimberly Guilfoyle promoted a Greek construction company and let its lobbyist into private diplomatic meetings in [Greece](country:GR).

Trump donor Eric Deters says she asked him to wire $100,000 for her credit-card debt before her 2025 confirmation hearing.

Guilfoyle denies wrongdoing, and her lawyers say the money was owed for past speaking engagements.

The State Department's inspector general is investigating whether she took improper gifts, US media report.
