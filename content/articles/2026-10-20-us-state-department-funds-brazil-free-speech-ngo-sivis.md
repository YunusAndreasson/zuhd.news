São Paulo — The [US](country:US) approved $30,000 for a Curitiba free-speech think tank.

Instituto Sivis says speech is declining in [Brazil](country:BR), and Washington accuses the Supreme Court of censorship.

The São Paulo consulate approved it on 23 September for a First Amendment course for 150 students.

USAspending.org removed the grant's details days later, and no one has confirmed the money was paid.

The State Department says programmes in its $1 million Brazil fund remain under active review.
