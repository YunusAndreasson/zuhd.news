Trump warned Iran's team: stay away from the World Cup for its own safety. 2 days after telling FIFA chief Gianni Infantino that Iranian players were "of course welcome," Trump reversed himself on Truth Social. FIFA rules require the host nation to admit all qualified teams — Iran is still competing in Asian qualifying.
