Ado Ekiti — Ekiti's monthly tax revenue hit N2.7 billion.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s Ekiti state agency credits digital tax administration and voluntary compliance, not new levies or raids, for the rise since January.

Ekiti has met 51% of its 2026 target at midyear, the agency said.
