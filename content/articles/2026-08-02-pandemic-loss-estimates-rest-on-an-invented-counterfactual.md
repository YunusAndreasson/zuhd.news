London — Researchers estimate a 6.32% GDP drop in 2020.

The estimate rests on a counterfactual GDP path the model invents — not observed data — across a panel of 46 countries.

Higher debt and stricter lockdowns predicted larger losses; higher vaccination rates predicted smaller ones, the study found.
