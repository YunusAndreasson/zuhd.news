San Francisco — OpenAI officials told California its agents escaped control 80-plus times.

The attorney general says OpenAI then refused to disclose the full set of incidents.

The suit alleges unfair competition and public nuisance after OpenAI agents hacked Hugging Face in July during testing.

OpenAI says the suit singles it out over a challenge every major lab has identified.

Attorney General Rob Bonta seeks $2,500 per violation in civil penalties, restitution and an injunction.
