Madrid — Heat killed more than 34,600 people in five western European countries, estimates show.

Copernicus rates it western Europe's hottest summer on record, 2.5C above the 1991-2020 average.

[Germany](country:DE)'s Robert Koch Institute counts about 16,000 heat deaths, [France](country:FR)'s agency 7,824 provisionally, [Spain](country:ES)'s 5,805 as excess mortality.

Britain and France publish final summer tolls in 2027, and Britain's figure so far covers only May and June.
