Taipei — Kuomintang leader Cheng Li-wun wants to meet Xi Jinping. Cheng argues military spending cannot prevent war with China even as Taiwan's parliament debates a $39 billion defence bill. KMT insiders warn the move will cost the party seats in Taiwan's year-end district elections.
