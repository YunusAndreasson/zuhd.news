NASA shifted an asteroid's orbit around the sun. Scientists confirmed that even a tiny orbital shift can compound over decades into a deflection large enough to redirect a threatening asteroid. Rahil Makadia, the study's lead author, said the findings should guide how NASA designs future planetary defense missions.
