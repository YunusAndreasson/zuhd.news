Lula skipped Kast's inauguration over Flávio Bolsonaro's presence. The reversal came after Lula learned Flávio Bolsonaro, his likely 2026 opponent polling in a statistical tie, would attend as a guest. Argentine President Milei and Venezuelan opposition leader María Corina Machado attended, cementing a Latin American right axis.
