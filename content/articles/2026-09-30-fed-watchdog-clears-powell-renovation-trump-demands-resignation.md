Washington — Trump demanded Powell resign after a watchdog cleared him.

Powell, chair until May, can remain a Fed governor until January 2028.

The Federal Reserve Act lets a president remove Fed officials only for cause, long read as gross negligence or dereliction of duty.

The inspector general did find poor management: no cost estimate until January 2026 and no guaranteed maximum price on a roughly $2.4bn project.

Trump has told Attorney General Todd Blanche to study the report and says Powell should be sued otherwise.
