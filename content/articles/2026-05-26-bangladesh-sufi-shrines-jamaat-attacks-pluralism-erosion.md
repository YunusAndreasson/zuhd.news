Dhaka — Jamaat-e-Islami affiliates demolished Sufi shrines across Bangladesh.

Jamaat-e-Islami and affiliates expanded after Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina's ouster removed state protection from [Bangladesh](country:BD)'s centuries-old Sufi mazars.

The caretaker administration has not restored security for the shrines; attacks continue.
