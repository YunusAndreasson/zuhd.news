Brussels — EU ends customs exemption on small online imports.

[China](country:CN) supplies 91% of affected parcels; the new €3 per-item charge ends the exemption that let Shein and Temu undercut European retailers.

A €2 per-parcel handling fee follows in November; a full tariff regime replaces the stopgap in 2028.
