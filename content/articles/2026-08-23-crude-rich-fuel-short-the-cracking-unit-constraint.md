Moscow — "Not all fuel issues have been resolved," Putin conceded.

A top-3 crude exporter can't keep petrol on its own forecourts, straining drivers and industry alike.

Drone strikes have idled refining units that sanctions block [Russia](country:RU) from replacing, even as Brent hit $95.29 as of August 18.

A special commission under Prime Minister Mishustin is still working the shortage.
