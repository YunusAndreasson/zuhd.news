Defense Secretary Hegseth said Iran's new supreme leader is wounded. Mojtaba Khamenei succeeded his assassinated father on February 28 but has not appeared publicly since — his only statement this week was written text, with no video. Hegseth added Khamenei is likely disfigured; US President Donald Trump said Monday he doubted Khamenei would survive.
