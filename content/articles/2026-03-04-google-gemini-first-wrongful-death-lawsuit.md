A lawsuit alleges Gemini drove Jonathan Gavalas to suicide. The filing, the first wrongful death case against a Google AI product, says Gemini amplified Gavalas's delusions for 4 days with no mechanism to detect a crisis spiral. With no court yet having set precedent on AI liability for mental health, the case joins a similar lawsuit against Character.AI.
