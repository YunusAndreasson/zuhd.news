Sydney — Annual inflation hit 4.0% in August, up from 3.5%.

[Australia](country:AU)'s Reserve Bank has raised rates a full percentage point this year, to 4.6%.

Fuel costs jumped 14.8% in the month on higher oil prices and the end of government tax relief, the statistics bureau said.

August's 0.4% monthly rise came in under the 0.5% forecast, and swaps cut November hike odds to 20%.

Westpac chief economist Luci Ellis calls a November hike the base case.
