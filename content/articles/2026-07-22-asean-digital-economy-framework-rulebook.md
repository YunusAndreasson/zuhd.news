Jakarta — 10 states are close to a single rulebook for cross-border data and e-payments.

The Digital Economy Framework Agreement would set common standards for 680 million people; outside powers including [Australia](country:AU) are positioning to shape its implementation.

The terms bloc negotiators finalize now will steer digital growth for the next decade.
