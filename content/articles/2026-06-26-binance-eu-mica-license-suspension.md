Brussels — Customers in 4 EU countries received exit instructions as Binance quit the bloc.

The EU's Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation required all exchanges to be licensed by July 1; Spain's regulator refused any extension.

Binance plans to reapply for a license via [France](country:FR).
