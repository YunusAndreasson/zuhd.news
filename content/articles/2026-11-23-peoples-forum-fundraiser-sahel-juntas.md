New York — A tax-exempt Manhattan group sold tickets supporting Sahel military governments.

Human Rights Watch found [Burkina Faso](country:BF)'s forces drove Fulani villagers out, conduct it called ethnic cleansing.

The People's Forum told buyers proceeds would reach state structures in [Mali](country:ML) and [Niger](country:NE) too, all barred from US aid.

The group did not respond to questions, and no tax violation is alleged.

One attendee says a speaker named Mali's army as recipient, a claim no one has confirmed.
