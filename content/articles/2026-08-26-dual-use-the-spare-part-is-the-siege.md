Gaza City — A cancer patient's oxygen tube dangled unattached.

Dialysis and newborn oxygen machines are failing hospital-wide, not just running short.

Gaza's oxygen plants, once 34 strong, run beyond capacity because [Israel](country:IL) bars spare parts entry.

The Health Ministry has appealed to open crossings for spare parts and new plants.
