No US senator has visited Hong Kong since 2019. Hong Kong's mass protests and a subsequent national security law prompted US sanctions, freezing Senate visits for 7 years. Senator Steve Daines plans to arrive on March 30 to meet US business leaders as Washington negotiates a postponed Trump-Xi summit.
