London — No single clone dominates metastatic prostate cancer.

Tumours consistently harbour genetically distinct sub-populations; clearing the dominant clone leaves resistant minorities to repopulate, the study found.

Findings reframe the disease as an ecology problem, pointing treatment toward multi-target rather than single-mutation approaches.
