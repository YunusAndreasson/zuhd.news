Iran struck 5 Gulf refineries and gas plants Wednesday. The strikes came hours after Israel bombed South Pars, the world's largest gas reserve, and assassinated Iran's intelligence minister. Iran struck Riyadh in a second wave late Wednesday, threatening further attacks on Gulf energy infrastructure.
