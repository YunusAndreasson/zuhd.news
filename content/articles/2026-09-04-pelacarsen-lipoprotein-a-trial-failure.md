Basel — Pelacarsen failed to prevent heart attacks or strokes.

One in five people worldwide carry the risk factor it targeted, untreated.

The drug slashed Lp(a) levels sharply in earlier trials, but lower levels did not translate into fewer cardiac events.

No therapy has yet proven that lowering Lp(a) prevents heart attacks.
