Tokyo — Japan opens intelligence-sharing talks with [Philippines](country:PH).

A General Security of Military Information Agreement converts ad-hoc sharing into treaty-protected classified channels; [Japan](country:JP) holds 5 such pacts.

President Marcos visits Tokyo this week; no timeline has been set for a binding agreement.
