Port-au-Prince — Salvadoran helicopters fired missiles at another gang stronghold, President Nayib Bukele said.

The UN recorded 4,595 killings in [Haiti](country:HT) in the year's first 10 months.

Bukele has sent 2,000-plus troops from [El Salvador](country:SV) since 4 soldiers vanished during a UN-backed operation in late October.

The UN human rights office urged all forces Tuesday to use lethal force only as a last resort.

No source reports how many civilians the helicopter strikes killed.
