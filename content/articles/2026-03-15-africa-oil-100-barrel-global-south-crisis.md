Crude hit $100 per barrel, squeezing African fuel prices. Nigeria, Africa's largest oil producer, imports refined petrol, leaving 220 million people exposed to global price spikes without a buffer. The Nigerian National Petroleum Corporation and Dangote Refinery reversed price cuts after crude rebounded; analysts expect costs to climb further as the Strait of Hormuz stays closed.
