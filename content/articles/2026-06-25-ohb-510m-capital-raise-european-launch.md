Bremen — OHB raised €482M to fund European launch capacity.

The 26% discount share issue bolsters satellite manufacturing and Rocket Factory Augsburg, [Germany](country:DE)'s bid for independent European access to orbit.

The remaining 97,000 shares go to existing minority shareholders — the tranche closes no later than July 8.
