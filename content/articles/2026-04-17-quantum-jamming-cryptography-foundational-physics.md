New York — Post-quantum cryptography assumes the rules of physics hold. Quantum jamming shows that attacks assumed impossible by quantum theory become feasible if the underlying physics is even slightly more general. Post-quantum standards assume quantum theory is complete; quantum jamming forces cryptographers to price that assumption.
