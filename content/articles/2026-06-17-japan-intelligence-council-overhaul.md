Tokyo — Japan is creating a National Intelligence Council.

[Japan](country:JP) is also creating a National Intelligence Bureau, consolidating security agencies left deliberately fragmented since 1945 to prevent remilitarization.

Officials cited threats from [China](country:CN) and [North Korea](country:KP) to justify the reforms, which break with Japan's postwar constraints on centralized intelligence.
