Islamabad — Flight International counts 32 Indian Rafales, not 36.

The four missing jets match the four Rafales [Pakistan](country:PK)'s air force says it shot down.

The British magazine's annual directory lists each air force's active inventory, and India's fell after the May 2025 conflict.

[India](country:IN) acknowledged some losses in the four-day conflict but denied Pakistan's claim of six jets downed.

India has ordered 114 more Rafales while the fate of its first 36 remains contested.
