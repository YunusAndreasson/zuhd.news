London — The model hit 20% efficiency, on paper.

Humid markets like [Pakistan](country:PK) want a stable, lead-free cell, not just an efficient one.

The material swaps lead and moisture risk for barium, zirconium and sulfur, but yields far lower efficiency than today's cells.

No lab has yet built or tested the optimized device the model describes.
