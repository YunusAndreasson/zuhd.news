Washington — A grain-of-rice flower hid in rock for 130 years.

Researchers place the 100-million-year-old fossil among the monocots, the plant group that includes palms.

X-ray scanning imaged the void the flower left in clay-rich sandstone, then inverted the image into a positive.

The team infers palm ancestry from its shape, since the flower survives only as a cavity.
