Israeli forces killed 12 medics at a health centre in southern Lebanon. Attacks on medical personnel and facilities are prohibited under international humanitarian law; the Israeli military issued no comment on the strike. Lebanon's health ministry confirmed 12 deaths but did not name the facility, and accountability remains pending a UN investigation.
