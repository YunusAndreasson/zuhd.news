Lahore — The High Court left Imran Khan's 2 sisters in detention.

Punjab holds Uzma Khan and Noreen Niazi 30 days without charge, days before the party's 27 September march.

A 1960 public-order ordinance permits preventive detention, though a trial court already acquitted Uzma in a case registered against her.

Justice Abher Gul Khan will seek the respondents' replies before deciding whether to suspend the detention.
