Taipei — Microsoft's top Surface now has NVIDIA inside.

Surface Laptop Ultra combines Windows on Arm with an NVIDIA graphics processor and up to 128GB unified memory, launching this fall.

Microsoft says the device carries an ultra-premium price; global memory supply constraints add to the cost.
