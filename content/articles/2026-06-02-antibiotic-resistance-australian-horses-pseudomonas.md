Sunshine Coast — Horses in [Australia](country:AU) are developing Pseudomonas aeruginosa resistance.

Resistant strains in wildlife and livestock create animal reservoirs that feed back into human infections — the One Health link hospitals can't surveil.

The study covered wild birds, livestock and domestic pets; no policy response has been announced.
