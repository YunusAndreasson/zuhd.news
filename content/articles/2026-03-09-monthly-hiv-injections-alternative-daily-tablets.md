Monthly cabotegravir injections prevent and treat HIV. Sub-Saharan Africa carries 25.6 million of 39 million global HIV cases — poverty and clinic distance make consistent pill-taking the main barrier. ViiV Healthcare manufactures cabotegravir but has not finalized generic licensing for high-burden countries.
