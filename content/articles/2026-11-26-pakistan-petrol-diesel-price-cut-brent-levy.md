Islamabad — Petrol falls to Rs313 a litre, diesel to Rs346.

Diesel moves freight, power plants and generators, so its price reaches everyone.

Taxes still take Rs114 a litre of petrol and Rs100 of diesel, while refineries bear Rs15 billion of levy for 30 days.

Diesel was Rs281 before the US-Iran war began on 28 February and peaked at Rs520.35 on 3 April.

The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority now revises prices daily from international market trends.
