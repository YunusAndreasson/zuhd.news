Zurich — [Switzerland](country:CH) offers homes 25 Gbit internet.

Regulators forced Swiss carriers to share one physical fibre network, avoiding the duplicated wiring that inflates [US](country:US) and [Germany](country:DE) prices.

The US and Germany, both reliant on single-provider fibre, have no open-access mandate planned.
