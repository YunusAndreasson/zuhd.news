Brasília — Flávio Bolsonaro leads Lula 46% to 44%.

The poll's 2-point margin of error makes it a technical tie, 10 days before the runoff.

He won round one with 47% to Lula's 45.2%, as his father Jair serves 27 years for coup-plotting.

The Senate passed a 40-hour week 75 to 1, with Bolsonaro voting yes after opposing it.

[Brazil](country:BR) votes 25 October, and the two candidates have yet to debate face to face.
