Abuja — A Labour Party official sued the University of Nigeria, Nsukka, over Peter Obi's academic records.

At stake is whether [Nigeria](country:NG)'s disclosure law binds public universities at all.

The 2011 transparency law requires a response within 7 days, but courts disagree on whether it applies to universities.

The case has not yet been assigned to a judge.
