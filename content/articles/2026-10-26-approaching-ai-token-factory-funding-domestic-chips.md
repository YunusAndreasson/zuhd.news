Beijing — Approaching.AI has raised 2.4 billion yuan this year.

State-linked funds led its 1.3 billion yuan Series B, part of [China](country:CN)'s token-factory boom.

The company says it splits prefill and decode across domestic and foreign chips to run trillion-parameter models.

Its claimed output of over 1 trillion tokens a day is the company's own figure.

Rival Infinigence is preparing to list, and SiliconFlow has already filed to list in Hong Kong.
