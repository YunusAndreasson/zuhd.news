San Francisco — OpenAI is reportedly seeking at least $30 billion at a $1.4 trillion valuation.

Rival Anthropic is expected to list in November at above $2 trillion, its prospectus says.

Annualised revenue reportedly exceeded $40 billion this summer and has grown 70% since July.

CEO Sam Altman says OpenAI will not list this year, citing safety concerns over increasingly capable systems.
