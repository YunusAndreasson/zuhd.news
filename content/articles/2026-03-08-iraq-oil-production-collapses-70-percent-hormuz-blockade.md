43 million Iraqis face suspended salaries. The Hormuz blockade has cut Iraq's oil output 70%, from 4 million barrels a day before the war to 1.3 million, blocking all Gulf export terminals. Iraq has no alternative export route, leaving the government with no timeline for restoring hospital and salary payments.
