Naypyidaw — [Myanmar](country:MM) freed ousted President Win Myint.

Newly inaugurated President Min Aung Hlaing also reduced Aung San Suu Kyi's 27-year sentence; both were detained since the 2021 military coup.

Whether Suu Kyi will be freed and whether ethnic-minority political prisoners are included remain open questions.
