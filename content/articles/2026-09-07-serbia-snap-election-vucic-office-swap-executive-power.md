Belgrade — President Vucic wants to become prime minister.

The move resets his term and shifts him to the office holding real power.

[Serbia](country:RS)'s largely ceremonial presidency has left Vucic ruling for years through party control instead.

Prime Minister Djuro Macut's call to dissolve parliament sets up the vote that enables the swap.
