Bayelsa State — Residents say 300 were aboard, but police count 4 dead.

The dead are 2 adults and 2 children, and police say 24 people were rescued.

The overcrowded boat capsized in southern [Nigeria](country:NG) after a mechanical failure, police said, travelling from Ayama to Ukubie for a burial.

Police said the driver fled and the vessel's manager is in custody.

Police are coordinating with local divers as the search for more victims continues.
