London — Green tea's dominant compound reduced rat ischemic stroke brain damage.

The compound steers brain immune cells toward repair mode via the Nrf2 pathway, cutting the secondary inflammation that kills neurons after blood flow is restored, the study found.

The mechanism was tested in rat models; human stroke trials have not begun.
