Accra — 60 to 70% of Ghanaians use plant medicine, a 2023 study found.

That makes it the majority health system, not a fallback, as drug imports are priced in dollars.

Ghana's Center for Plant Medicine Research, founded in 1976, tries validating practitioners' knowledge scientifically.

Ghana's Food and Drugs Authority still hasn't standardised dosing for most preparations sold.
