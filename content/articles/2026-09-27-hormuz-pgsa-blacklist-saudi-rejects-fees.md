Tehran — Iran's state-affiliated Mehr agency says Hormuz authorities will blacklist charterers.

Every vessel a blacklisted company sends through the strait would face passage restrictions.

Mehr says the Persian Gulf Strait authority received reports that some charterers force ships onto invalid routes.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s foreign minister told the UN on Saturday that Hormuz must return to pre-28 February conditions, without fees.

Mehr's report names no charterer and gives no date for the non-compliance list.
