The pledge follows [Iran](country:IR)'s closure of the Strait of Hormuz this year, spotlighting how nations bordering a chokepoint can control global shipping.

Neither [Indonesia](country:ID) nor [Singapore](country:SG) set a timeline for revisiting the toll idea it just rejected.
