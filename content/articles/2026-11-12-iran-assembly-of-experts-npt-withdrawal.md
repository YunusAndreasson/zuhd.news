Tehran — [Iran](country:IR)'s Assembly of Experts urges leaving nuclear treaty, Mehr reported.

The clerical body that chooses Iran's Supreme Leader backs the move.

Mehr, Iran's state-affiliated news agency, said the statement also promises an immediate response without geographic limits to any new attack.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran final nuclear deal by 2026 an 11% chance, up 4 points in a week.
