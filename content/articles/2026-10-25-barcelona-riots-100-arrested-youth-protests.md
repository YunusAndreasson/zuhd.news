Barcelona — Police arrested between 100 and 128 people, many of them minors.

A call modelled on [France](country:FR)'s Lycéens Indignés drew about 500 to 600 young people.

Authorities counted 31 bins burned and 23 businesses looted or vandalised, after the call spread on TikTok, Telegram and WhatsApp.

The housing-rights encampment in the same square distanced itself from the violence.

Mayor Jaume Collboni said the city council will join legal proceedings, and parents of minors could face consequences.
