Kuching — Smoke from another country closed Sarawak's schools.

Malaysian children now breathe pollution from fires no [Malaysian](country:MY) agency can regulate.

Weeks of peat and plantation fires across Sumatra and Kalimantan, worsened by a severe dry season, carried haze over the border.

ASEAN's regional haze pact carries no enforcement mechanism against the fires' source.
