Damascus — 2 Iraq-Syria border crossings are becoming free-trade zones.

The Hormuz crisis is pushing [Iraq](country:IQ) toward an overland route through [Syria](country:SY).

Energy is the biggest driver, said Benjamin Feve of The Syria Dispatch, and customs authorities agreed practical measures in September.

Some analysts doubt it will last, citing regional instability and longstanding distrust between the two governments.

Polymarket traders give 91% odds there will be no Hormuz agreement by 2 November.
