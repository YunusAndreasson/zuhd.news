Beirut — 2 French soldiers died from a single April 18 ambush during a UNIFIL road-clearing patrol in southern [Lebanon](country:LB). UNIFIL called the attack deliberate; Hezbollah denied involvement and [France](country:FR) launched an investigation. Macron demanded accountability — 3 French soldiers have now died in the region since February, raising domestic pressure on Paris to review its 700-troop deployment.
