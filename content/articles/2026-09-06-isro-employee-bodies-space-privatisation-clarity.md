Bengaluru — Nine unions at the Indian Space Research Organisation demand clarity.

It is the agency's own workforce, not policy critics, questioning what's left of it.

India's space regulator says the agency will stop building rockets, having already shifted satellite launch vehicle production to Hindustan Aeronautics.

The unions want written assurance no irreversible transfer proceeds without consulting them first.
