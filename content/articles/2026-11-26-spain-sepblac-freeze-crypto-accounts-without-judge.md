Madrid — Sepblac can freeze crypto accounts for 5 business days.

No judge or foreign request is needed first.

It can also hold a pending transaction for 10 business days, and a prosecutor needs a judge to extend either.

[Spain](country:ES)'s parliament must validate the decree, which takes effect on 25 January 2027.
