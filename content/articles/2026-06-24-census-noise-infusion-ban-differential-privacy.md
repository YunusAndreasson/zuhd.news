Washington — A census privacy ban will shrink public data.

The June 4 order forbids 'noise infusion' — the anonymization method that lets the [US](country:US) Census Bureau publish granular data on small communities.

Five statistical associations condemned the change; data on small counties and industries now faces suppression or coarsening.
