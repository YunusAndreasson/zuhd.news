FIFA fined but refused to suspend Israel's football association. The Disciplinary Committee cited the West Bank's "undecided status" — a rationale not applied when FIFA suspended Russia within days of invading Ukraine. FIFA's own statutes bar clubs from playing on another association's territory without consent, yet Israeli clubs operate in West Bank settlements.
