Months of delay followed [China](country:CN)'s repeated requests that the [US](country:US) stop arming Taiwan.

The $8bn deal, approved in Trump's first term, covers Lockheed Martin jets with upgraded radar to counter China's J-20 stealth fighters.

Trump is holding out on a new $14bn Taiwan package, though he approved $11bn in December 2025.

Defence Minister Wellington Koo says more jets will arrive before year's end.
