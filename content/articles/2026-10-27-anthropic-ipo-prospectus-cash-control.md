United States — 7 co-founders control 50.1% of Anthropic's votes with one share.

Public investors buying in cannot outvote the founders, even if the founders sell nearly everything else.

The share sits in a Founder LLC, and a trust of 5 trustees elects 4 of 7 board directors, the filing says.

Anthropic holds $131 billion in cash, but footnotes list at least $542 billion in lease and chip payments due.

Reports say Anthropic targets a mid-November listing.
