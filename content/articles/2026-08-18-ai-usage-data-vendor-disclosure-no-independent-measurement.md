Stanford — No independent source corroborates AI companies' usage reports.

Lawmakers and investors cite those same numbers to write AI rules and fund data centers.

An independent Observatory found nearly half of real conversations differ from what firms disclose.

No regulator has yet mandated audited, independent usage reporting for AI companies.
