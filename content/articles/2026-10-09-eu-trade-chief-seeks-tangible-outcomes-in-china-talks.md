Beijing — Brussels says China's hybrid car exports could halve.

Trade commissioner Maros Sefcovic calls the EU's 1 billion euro daily deficit with [China](country:CN) unsustainable.

China agreed to follow price-undertaking procedures, lower tariffs on some European goods and keep approving rare-earth export licences.

A 2013 solar-panel limit let Chinese makers raise prices and build factories that now make nearly all panels.

Neither side has published terms, and the EU has not said what it gave in return.
