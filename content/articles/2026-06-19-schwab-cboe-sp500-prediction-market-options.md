New York — Schwab will bring prediction-market mechanics to regulated brokerage.

Cboe binary options pay a fixed sum when S&P 500 closes above a price target and expire worthless otherwise.

Schwab plans to launch within months, excluding markets tied to politics, sports, and other non-financial events.
