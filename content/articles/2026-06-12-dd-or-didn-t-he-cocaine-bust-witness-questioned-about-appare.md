Johannesburg — A commission witness linked cocaine to ex-deputy president David Mabuza.

The Madlanga Commission probes cartel links to [South Africa](country:ZA)'s criminal justice system; the chair called the witness untruthful.

Witness Mashaba was still testifying Friday; the commission reports to President Ramaphosa by August 31.
