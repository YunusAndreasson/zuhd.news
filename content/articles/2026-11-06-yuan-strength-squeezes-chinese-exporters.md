Ningbo — The yuan has gained 9% on the euro.

[China](country:CN)'s textile and appliance exporters say the rise has erased their margins and threatens layoffs.

Standard Bank estimates each 1% real appreciation cuts exporters' margins by about 30 basis points.

The IMF said in July that the yuan was undervalued by 17.3% to 25.3%.

The central bank raised the foreign-currency reserve ratio Tuesday, still 3 points below its 2021 peak.
