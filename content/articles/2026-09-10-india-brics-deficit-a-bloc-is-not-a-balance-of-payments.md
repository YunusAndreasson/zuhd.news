New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s BRICS deficit hit $226 billion, the Global Trade Research Initiative says.

[China](country:CN), [Russia](country:RU) and the [UAE](country:AE) now supply 41.5% of India's imports.

A shared political bloc doesn't guarantee balanced trade, as India imports inputs but sells finished goods to the West.

New Delhi hosts the BRICS summit this weekend, where local-currency settlement talks resume.
