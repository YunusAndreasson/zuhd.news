United States — A Reddit user says 3 Ray-Ban Metas saved conversations unprompted.

Bystanders within range of the microphone never agreed to be recorded.

Audio and transcripts appeared in the Meta AI app's history even with the hands-free response setting off.

Meta says the issue is resolved but has not said what caused it or how many devices were hit.

Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton is already investigating Meta's glasses after contractors reported viewing private footage.
