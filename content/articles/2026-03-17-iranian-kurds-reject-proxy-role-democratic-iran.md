The Kurdistan Free Life Party, an armed Iranian Kurdish movement aligned with the PKK, refused to join the US-Israeli war on Iran, saying "we do not act according to any other power." PJAK co-chair Peyman Viyan denied US media reports of weapons transfers to Kurdish militias and said the group had received no foreign support. The group seeks a democratic Iran — a goal that foreign-backed regime change, as Kurds witnessed in Iraq after 2003, has never delivered.
