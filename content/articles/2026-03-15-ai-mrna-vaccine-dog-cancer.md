Rosie's tumor halved after an AI-designed mRNA vaccine. University of New South Wales researchers built the AI to analyze the dog's tumor genomics and identify precise mutations — a step cancer drug companies spend billions attempting for populations. The team is developing a second vaccine and says the method could become standard for individual human cancers.
