London — A Nature study found spinal-cord rehab's evidence base is 78% male.

Female enrollment held at 22.4% across 24 years of spinal-cord injury trials, encoding male physiology as the default for rehabilitation guidelines.

Researchers found no trend toward parity and called for targeted enrollment strategies to bring women into future trials.
