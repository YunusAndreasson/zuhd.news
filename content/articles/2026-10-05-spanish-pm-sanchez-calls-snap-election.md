Madrid — Sánchez calls early election with [Spain](country:ES)'s Socialists trailing in polls.

A defeat would end one of Europe's last left-leaning governments.

Right-wing MPs struck down his housing measures Friday, a rare chance to rally the left, an analyst said.

"This is a window of opportunity," said Lorenzo Bernaldo de Quiros, an economist and consultancy president.

Polls favour a People's Party coalition with Vox, the far right's first government role since Franco.
