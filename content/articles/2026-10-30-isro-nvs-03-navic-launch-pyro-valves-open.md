Sriharikota — India's space agency launched NVS-03 with three pyro valves open.

The launch restores [India](country:IN)'s NavIC to its four-satellite minimum operating configuration.

A connector fault likely stopped NVS-02's oxidiser valve firing in 2025, a hurdle the open valves bypass, Narayanan said.

The agency's chief, V Narayanan, said NavIC reaches civilian phones only once the full seven-satellite constellation is complete.

India's space agency plans NVS-04 in five months and NVS-05 in 10.
