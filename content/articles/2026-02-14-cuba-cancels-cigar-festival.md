Cuba postponed its annual Habano cigar festival indefinitely. Organizers blamed the "economic, commercial and financial blockade" by the US, which has seized oil shipments from Venezuela that once supplied Cuba with roughly 35,000 barrels a day. Since Washington detained former Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro on 3 January, power cuts lasting up to 18 hours a day have crippled hospitals, dialysis wards and water pumping stations across the island. Several airlines have suspended Cuban routes because of aviation fuel shortages, and the United Kingdom has warned against non-essential travel there. United Nations human rights experts have called Washington's restrictions on Cuba's oil imports an "extreme form of unilateral economic coercion."
