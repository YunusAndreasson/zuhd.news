One-eighth of Earth's estimated 8 million species face extinction. The IPBES report, approved by 150 governments, found that GDP growth fails to account for the ecosystem services economies depend on. With the US withdrawn from IPBES and the EU relaxing environmental standards to boost competitiveness, the report's 150 signatories lack the 2 economies whose policies matter most.
