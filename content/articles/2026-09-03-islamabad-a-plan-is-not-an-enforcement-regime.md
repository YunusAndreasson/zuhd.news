Islamabad — 150mm of rain flooded three sectors Tuesday.

Residents living along blocked drains in [Pakistan](country:PK)'s capital face the same flooding every season.

One housing society narrowed a drainage channel from 40 feet to 18, with the capital authority's approval.

The capital authority has pledged to demolish encroachments and build new check dams.
