United States — A 4.3% growth revision drew a presidential demand for investigation.

Trump says the 3.4% advance estimate was a deliberate error, and offered no evidence.

The [US](country:US) Bureau of Economic Analysis revises GDP as complete data on inventories, trade and services arrive, by 0.52 points on average.

The bureau publishes revisions on a prescheduled calendar set without regard to elections.

The bureau publishes its next GDP estimate on 2 December.
