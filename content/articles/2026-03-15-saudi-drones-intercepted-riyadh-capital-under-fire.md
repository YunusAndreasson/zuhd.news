Saudi Arabia downed 7 drones over Riyadh. The kingdom deploys Patriot and THAAD missile batteries in the capital, which Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman has spent years positioning as a post-oil global financial center. The Defence Ministry named no attacker, but sustained strikes would directly threaten Vision 2030, the kingdom's economic diversification plan.
