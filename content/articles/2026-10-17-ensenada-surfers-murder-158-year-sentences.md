Ensenada — 158 years each for killing 3 surfers.

Australian brothers Jake and Callum Robinson and American Carter Rhoad were shot in 2024 while camping.

Prosecutors in [Mexico](country:MX) say the men wanted the truck's tyres, then burned it and dumped the bodies in a well.

Investigators found a fourth, older body in the well, and no one has been charged over it.
