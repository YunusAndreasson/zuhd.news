San Francisco — Gray whales enter SF Bay hungry; 18% die. Warming Arctic waters have depleted their food, pushing whales into one of North America's busiest shipping lanes. Most deaths were from vessel strikes; the Bay's shipping infrastructure is now a migration trap.
