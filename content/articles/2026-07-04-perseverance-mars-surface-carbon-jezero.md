Pasadena — Perseverance found organic carbon sitting on a rock's bare surface.

The shallowest such detection yet on Mars resembles terrestrial kerogen, but researchers say fluid-rock chemistry, not life, could equally explain it.

Confirming the carbon's origin requires flying the Bright Angel samples back to Earth.
