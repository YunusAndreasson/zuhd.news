Munich — [Germany](country:DE) is weighing its own launch site.

The gap isn't lifting mass, which Europe has — it's launching on demand within days, which no one there can yet.

Defense Minister Boris Pistorius named no site — [Norway](country:NO), [Sweden](country:SE) and a North Sea platform are the options, each politically fraught.
