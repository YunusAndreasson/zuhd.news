Ethiopian church forests host microbes absent from adjacent cropland.

A study found 81% of microbial variation follows local filtering — intact forests select specialists absent from cleared land.

Authors cautioned that nitrogen-cycling predictions were modeled, not measured; field validation before restoration use is needed.
