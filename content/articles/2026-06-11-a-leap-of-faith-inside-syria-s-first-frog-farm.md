Hama — A Turkish returnee launched [Syria](country:SY)'s first frog farm.

Hassan Duleybi learned the trade while displaced in [Turkey](country:TR), returned to find the region's wild frog population intact, and built 4 half-acre ponds.

Duleybi has no named buyer or export license; Syria's post-war trade rules are still being written.
