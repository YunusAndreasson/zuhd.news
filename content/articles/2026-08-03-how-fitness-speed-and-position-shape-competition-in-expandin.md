London — Being early can beat being fitter.

A statistical-mechanics model calibrated to bacterial colonies found expansion speed and starting position carry weight comparable to fitness at a growing frontier, researchers reported in the Journal of Statistical Mechanics.

Descendants at the leading edge inherit new space geometrically, a founder effect the authors say applies only within the model's own assumptions.
