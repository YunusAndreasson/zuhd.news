Deir al-Balah — One old solar panel powers Basma Shaheen's tent.

It charges from 9am into the afternoon, and Shaheen fears winter will leave her family without even lights.

The foreign-policy outlet Inkstick reports the World Bank found over 90% of Gaza's electricity assets destroyed or disabled.

Her family pays 50 cents to charge a shared phone twice a day.

One feeder line, 4 megawatts to a desalination plant, still works, and equipment restrictions continue.
