Beijing condemned US strikes but offered Iran only words. China's foreign minister Wang Yi called for an immediate ceasefire without sending arms to Tehran or calling for a UN Security Council meeting on Iran. China continues to buy Iranian oil under existing deals and has imposed no cost on Israel.
