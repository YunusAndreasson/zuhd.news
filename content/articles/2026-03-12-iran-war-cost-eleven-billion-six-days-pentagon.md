The US dropped $5.6bn in bombs in the war's first 2 days. The 6-day bombing campaign in Iran has cost $11.3bn total, with no public cost estimate or stated end goal released. Congress, which never authorized the war, now faces a White House request for $50bn more with no exit plan.
