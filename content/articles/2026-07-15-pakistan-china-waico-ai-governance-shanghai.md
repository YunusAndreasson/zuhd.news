Shanghai — [Pakistan](country:PK) signs [China](country:CN)'s rival AI governance body.

The World AI Cooperation Organisation, launched by Beijing last year, positions itself as an alternative to Western-led standards for the Global South.

Deputy PM Ishaq Dar signs as a founding member Friday, alongside China's premier.
