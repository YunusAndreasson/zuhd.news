Bishkek — At least 2,191 people born in Kyrgyzstan died in Russia's war.

228 of them were Kyrgyz citizens, according to the records.

The figures come from a classified database of death records compiled by Russia's statistics agency Rosstat from civil registry data.

The records end in August 2026, so the toll since then is unknown.
