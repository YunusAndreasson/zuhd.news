Phnom Penh — Cambodia invoked UN maritime law to compel a seabed dispute process with [Thailand](country:TH).

The 26,000 sq km contested zone holds an estimated $300 billion in energy; [Cambodia](country:KH) can force the process without Thailand's consent, the energy minister said.

Thailand has 21 days to appoint conciliators; Bangkok has previously rejected Cambodia's offers of arbitration.
