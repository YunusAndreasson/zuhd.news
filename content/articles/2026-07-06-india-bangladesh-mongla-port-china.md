Dhaka — A decade of inaction cost India a Bay of Bengal port.

[Bangladesh](country:BD) allotted [India](country:IN) land for a special economic zone at Mongla port in 2015; India never broke ground.

Dhaka has since handed the site to China, ceding ground India never contested.
