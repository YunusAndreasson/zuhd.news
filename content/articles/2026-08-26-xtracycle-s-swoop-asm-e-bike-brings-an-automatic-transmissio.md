Petaluma — A cargo bike now shifts itself.

Electronic shifting removes a common source of chain damage and stalled starts.

Sensors track pedal torque and speed, then the motor nudges the chain to finish gear changes without pedaling.

Xtracycle prices the setup at $4,500, a premium buyers must weigh against manual gearing.
