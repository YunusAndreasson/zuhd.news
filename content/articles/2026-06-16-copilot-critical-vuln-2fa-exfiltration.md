Washington — Copilot flaw let attackers steal live two-factor authentication codes.

A malicious link triggered Copilot to search the victim's mail and relay the codes via Bing before the guardrail activated, Varonis researchers showed.

The prompt-injection class is unfixable; new bypasses emerge as guardrails are patched, Varonis warned.
