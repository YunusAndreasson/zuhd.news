Washington — Broadcom, Verizon and 7 other issuers refused tokenization.

Opt-outs shrink the list of 63 companies OKXICE planned to launch with.

Under the Securities and Exchange Commission's 5-year exemption, tokens backed one-for-one by shares trade around the clock in Uniswap liquidity pools.

Nasdaq separately opted out, saying the venue does not display best prices in real time.

TD Securities analysts name temporary exemptive relief, which a future commission could reverse, as the largest hurdle.
