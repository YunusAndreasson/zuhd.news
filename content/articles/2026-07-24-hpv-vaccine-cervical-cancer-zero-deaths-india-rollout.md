London — Zero cervical cancer deaths among vaccinated young women.

A Lancet study tied 2008-18 vaccination records to zero deaths in the first cohort, an effect invisible for 15 years.

[India](country:IN) began its national human papillomavirus vaccine rollout this year; its cervical screening rate is just 1.9%.
