Vysotsk — Ukrainian drones struck Lukoil's Vysotsk terminal overnight. The corridor includes Ust-Luga and Primorsk, which carry roughly 40% of Russia's seaborne crude; Kyiv has struck it repeatedly this year. Overnight drones also hit refineries in Novokuybyshevsk and Syzran, reaching 1,000 km north of Kyiv.
