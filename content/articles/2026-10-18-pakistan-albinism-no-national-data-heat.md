Kot Radha Kishan — Pakistan keeps no national count of people with albinism.

UN expert Malooka-Anne Miti-Drummond found in June that policies, programmes and data systems overlook them.

[Pakistan](country:PK) keeps no national estimate, yet one genetic study found 94 previously unreported affected families.

"My abilities, my degrees, and my skills were ignored," said Adeel Nasir, 24, who has albinism.

Activists are pressing the government to recognise people with albinism and collect data on them.
