UK mortgage rates hit their highest since August 2025. Rising oil prices are driving inflation expectations higher, pushing gilt yields — and mortgage rates — up and forcing lenders to withdraw deals. Millions of homeowners remortgaging this spring will pay a wartime premium on their shelter with no say in the conflict.
