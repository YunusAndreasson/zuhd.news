South Korean chip exports hit a record $7.6 billion in the first 10 days of March. Chip supply chains bypass the Strait of Hormuz entirely, insulating South Korea from the blockade crippling oil and food trade. Chip exports to China jumped 91.2% as AI infrastructure investment drives demand beyond pre-war forecasts.
