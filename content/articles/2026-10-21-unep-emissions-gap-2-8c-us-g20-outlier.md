Copenhagen — Current policies point to 2.8C of warming this century.

The UN Environment Programme says the [US](country:US) withdrawal from the Paris Agreement alone could add 0.1C.

US emissions rose in 2025 while the rest of the G20 slowed, as data centres and a cold winter drove fossil fuel use.

Solar deployment has grown 7 times faster than expected a decade ago, said UN Climate Change chief Simon Stiell.

The UN's best case needs 45% emissions cuts by 2035, with the climate summit in [Turkey](country:TR) next month.
