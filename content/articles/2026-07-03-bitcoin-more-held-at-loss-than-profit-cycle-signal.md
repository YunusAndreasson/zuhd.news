New York — 10.83 million bitcoin now sit underwater.

Glassnode data shows loss-holding supply has overtaken profitable supply for the first time this cycle, a crossover that has historically marked price bottoms.

Whether it marks a bottom depends on ETF inflows returning and easing macro pressure, on-chain analysts say.
