Novorossiysk — Drones struck two tankers loading at the Caspian terminal.

The pipeline carries most of landlocked [Kazakhstan](country:KZ)'s crude exports, though Kazakhstan is not a combatant in the war.

The consortium, which called it the 5th such strike, has set no date to resume loading.
