Montgomery — TikTok will pay Alabama at least $100 million.

It is TikTok's first state settlement, reached days before a trial set for Monday.

Alabama pressed deceptive trade practices claims, and the deal caps teens at 2 hours daily, with a midnight-to-6am lockout.

More than a dozen states, including California and New York, still have suits against TikTok.

Alabama's payout could grow, to a maximum of $300 million, if 40 other attorneys general sign similar deals.
