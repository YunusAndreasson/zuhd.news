Hamburg — A new copepod family found 2,500m deep.

Copepods are the ocean's most abundant animals; a new family — not merely a new species — fills a gap in a poorly understood group's evolutionary tree.

The Irminger Basin has been sampled in only a handful of expeditions, the researchers note.
