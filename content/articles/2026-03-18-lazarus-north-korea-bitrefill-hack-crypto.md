North Korea's Lazarus Group stole 18,500 crypto purchase records. The group, operating within Pyongyang's intelligence bureau, has taken $6.8 billion in crypto since 2022 to fund North Korea's weapons program. Bitrefill, the crypto gift card service targeted in the breach, said it will absorb losses but has not disclosed how much was drained.
