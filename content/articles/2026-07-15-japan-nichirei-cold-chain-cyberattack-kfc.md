Tokyo — One intrusion left fried-chicken shelves bare.

A cyberattack on Nichirei Logistics, [Japan](country:JP)'s largest cold-chain operator, cut ingredient deliveries to KFC outlets and other major restaurant chains.

Nichirei has not disclosed how the attackers got in or when deliveries resume.
