Washington — Foreign-agent filings show over $1 billion in [Israel](country:IL)-funded messaging since October 2023.

Quincy Institute researcher Nick Cleveland-Stout says some contractors publish material aimed at shaping chatbot answers — a real but unproven lever, since no case ties a specific answer to a campaign.

The registrations are public; which chatbot answers reflect paid corpora remains unverified.
