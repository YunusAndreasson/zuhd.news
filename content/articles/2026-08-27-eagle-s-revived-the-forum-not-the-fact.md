Helsinki — [Finland](country:FI)'s appeals court revived the Eagle S case.

The officers accused of severing Baltic cables have already left the country.

Cables in international waters sit in no state's territory, so prosecuting damage depends on narrow treaty rules.

A Helsinki trial can now proceed, but no state can compel the men's return.
