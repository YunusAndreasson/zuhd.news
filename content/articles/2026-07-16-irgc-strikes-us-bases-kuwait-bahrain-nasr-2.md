The Revolutionary Guard says "Operation Nasr 2" struck radar and troops at Kuwait's Ali al-Salem base and jet-fuel infrastructure in [Bahrain](country:BH), retaliation it says for strikes that killed 30+ Iranian civilians.

The claims are unconfirmed; [Kuwait](country:KW) and Bahrain, hosting US forces, now sit inside the target set.
