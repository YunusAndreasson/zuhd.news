Physicists made electrons behave like a fluid. Researchers at Columbia University coaxed electrons into forming shock waves, a phenomenon normally seen only in flowing liquids. The result confirms a 1960s prediction that electrons can be forced to move collectively rather than independently. Standard electronic theory treats each electron as acting on its own, but the experiments show they can unite into a coherent flow at extremely high speeds. The findings could open new ways of thinking about quantum systems and electronic materials.
