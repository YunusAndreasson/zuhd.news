Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK) sought 22 winter gas cargoes but may get 12.

Sharif ordered a winter plan that gives households local gas and sends imported gas to industry.

Fighting between the [US](country:US) and [Iran](country:IR) has disrupted the Strait of Hormuz and Bab al-Mandab, major oil and gas routes.

Sources say imports may reach only 7 to 8 cargoes, at about $100m each.

The prime minister has yet to approve the import plan, and an IMF mission is negotiating $1.2 billion.
