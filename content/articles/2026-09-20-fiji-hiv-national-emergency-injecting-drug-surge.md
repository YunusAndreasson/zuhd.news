Suva — 1 in 60 Fijian adults now live with HIV.

Only 39% of patients know their status and 22% receive care.

Injected methamphetamine and shared needles drive it, with 2,000 new cases in 2025, 16 times 2019.

Health Minister Atonio Lalabalavu is expanding free testing, clean needles and treatment.
