Kagara — A mob tried to storm a hospital to kill admitted herders.

Soldiers blocked the attack with an armored carrier as Kamuku farmer-Fulani herder violence, rooted in a land dispute, spread from Tegina; police put the toll at 18, residents say near 50.

A reconciliation committee now works while [Nigeria](country:NG)'s curfew keeps Kagara's residents indoors.
