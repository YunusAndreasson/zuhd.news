223 Somali lawmakers adopted an official constitution Wednesday. Somalia had no permanent constitution since 1991, operating for 34 years on a provisional document amid civil war and al-Shabaab, an Islamist militant group the US designates a terrorist organization. The constitution now applies to a country where al-Shabaab controls large southern territories and Somaliland has declared independence.
