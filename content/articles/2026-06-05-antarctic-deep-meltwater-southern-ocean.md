Antarctic Peninsula — Researchers found meltwater deep in Antarctic coastal waters, not only at the surface.

Sea-level models assume meltwater stays shallow; the study detected it at depth, altering salinity in ways those models ignore.

If confirmed globally, sea-level rise and Southern Ocean projections are understated, researchers said.
