London — Coinbase can now sell [UK](country:GB) stocks too.

The license lets retail UK users trade equities and derivatives alongside crypto in one account, extending crypto exchanges into traditional finance.

Coinbase plans to add tokenized US stocks for eligible customers outside the country next.
