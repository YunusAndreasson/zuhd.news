Washington — Ocean eddies are intensifying, amplifying coastal climate extremes. Spinning currents that break from major flows redistribute heat and nutrients — a mechanism climate models have underweighted in coastal predictions. Coastal models must be recalibrated; the mechanism driving eddy intensification is warming, which is accelerating.
