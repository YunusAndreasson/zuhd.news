Riyadh — Aramco is shipping only Red Sea crude to Asia. The Hormuz conflict has kept the Gulf route blocked for 2 months, cutting Saudi Arabia's total exports from 7.1M to 4.4M barrels per day — a 39% collapse from the world's largest oil exporter. Yanbu loadings hit record volumes but cannot replace the Gulf route, leaving Asian refineries capping fuel output.
