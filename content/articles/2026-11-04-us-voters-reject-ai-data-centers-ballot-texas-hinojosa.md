Houston — Voters rejected data centers in 38 of 40-plus contests.

Texas ousted Greg Abbott for Gina Hinojosa, who promises to halt data center approvals.

Six cities banned data centers outright and 12 capped the megawatts they can draw.

Goldman Sachs expects any Texas, Georgia and Michigan pauses to be brief, since data centers are not voters' top priority.

The state grid operator tracks more than 400 gigawatts of projects awaiting connections that Hinojosa has vowed to freeze.
