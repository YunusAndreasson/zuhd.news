Erlangen — B cell depletion reversed fibrosis in systemic sclerosis.

The CASTLE trial's CD19-targeted T cells restored skin papillae and shifted fibroblast populations toward healthy function.

Researchers describe it as proof-of-concept for tissue regeneration in other fibrotic diseases.
