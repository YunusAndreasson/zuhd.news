Tegucigalpa — [Honduras](country:HN) bought $3.1 billion from [China](country:CN) in 2025 and sold $52.9 million, central bank figures show.

Traders in Comayagüela report falling sales and closures as Chinese-owned shops spread.

A municipal police judge says 42 to 45 of 78 inspected Chinese-owned shops have irregular operating permits.

One stallholder says sales fell to 40% of former levels but did not say whether other factors contributed.

Traders in other municipalities have also protested and asked the authorities to act.
