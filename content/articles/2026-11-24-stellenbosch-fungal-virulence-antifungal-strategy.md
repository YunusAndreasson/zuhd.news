Stellenbosch — 19 fungi top the WHO's priority threat list.

Rising temperatures and antifungal misuse are making fungi tolerate mammalian body heat.

Blocking polyamine synthesis stops Candida forming hyphae, the filaments it uses to stick to tissue and evade immunity.

The review in Clinical Microbiology Reviews summarises existing studies, and single-molecule inhibitors have often failed clinically.

Researchers still need compounds that disrupt fungal polyamines without harming healthy human cells.
