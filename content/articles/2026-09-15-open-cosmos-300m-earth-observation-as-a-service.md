London — Open Cosmos raised €300 million for satellite manufacturing.

The funding lets governments buy sovereign satellite capability instead of building their own programs.

Open Cosmos already builds one satellite a day across four European factories.

The raise adds to a private-capital surge competing with public European Space Agency budgets.
