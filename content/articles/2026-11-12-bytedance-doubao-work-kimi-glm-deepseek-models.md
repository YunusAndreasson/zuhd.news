Beijing — Doubao Work now runs rival Kimi, GLM and DeepSeek models.

Users choose by task and cost, with each model's quota multiplier shown.

The picker shows how fast each model burns a user's allowance, so lower multipliers stretch quotas further.

Enterprise customers already had third-party models, and simple small tasks stay free.

Limited-time discounts on Doubao 2.1 Lite and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash cut quota use, with no end date stated.
