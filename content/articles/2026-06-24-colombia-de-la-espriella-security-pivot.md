Bogotá — [Colombia](country:CO) elected Trump-aligned hard-liner de la Espriella.

He takes office in August, inheriting 60 years of unbroken conflict where Marxist rebels, right-wing paramilitaries and drug networks coexist.

Petro negotiated ceasefires with the National Liberation Army and others; de la Espriella has pledged to cancel all peace talks.
