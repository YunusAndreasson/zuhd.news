Washington — Trump gave aide Natalie Harp $45,000 in cash.

Federal law bars staff pay beyond salary — a personal gift isn't.

The gift exemption, meant for holiday tokens, lets a president fund aides personally with no oversight.

No agency has said whether it will investigate the payments.
