Quantico — Hegseth announced a 4-star command for drones and autonomous systems.

It carries service-like authorities to scale autonomous and robotic systems across the joint force.

Autonomous Warfare Command is loosely modelled on Special Operations Command and Space Command, and pairs with Projects Agincourt and Meridian.

It remains unclear how the command and its two projects will turn into fielded systems.
