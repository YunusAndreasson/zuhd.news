Pakistan killed 3 children in Khost overnight. Despite Pakistani claims of targeting Tehreek-e-Taliban Pakistan militants, 18 civilians have been killed across 3 consecutive weeks of cross-border fire. China's week of mediation failed to produce a ceasefire, leaving 20,000 displaced families dependent on World Food Programme aid.
