Quneitra — The largest olive press received 200 tons, against 2,000 in 2024.

Israeli patrols block picking, press owner Ibrahim Moussa al-Moussa says, leaving much of the crop on the trees.

[Israel](country:IL) has closed or seized 5,650 acres of farmland, the provincial agriculture director said, and allows farmers two hours.

Israeli forces detained Hassan Saad al-Din's 16-year-old son in 2024: "He was still a boy," the father said.

Two of 4 farmers detained on 31 October were taken to a military post, [Syria](country:SY)'s state news agency SANA reported.
