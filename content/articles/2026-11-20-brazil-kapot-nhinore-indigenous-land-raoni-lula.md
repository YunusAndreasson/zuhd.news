Peixoto de Azevedo — [Brazil](country:BR) declared 362,000 hectares permanent Indigenous land Thursday.

Demarcated Indigenous territories are among the best barriers against Amazon deforestation, experts say.

The Justice Ministry's declaration is one step before demarcation by presidential decree, and over 200 properties sit on the land.

A 2023 Greenpeace report found over 47,000 hectares used for pasture or soy, and rural producers oppose demarcation.

Lula wanted the issue resolved while Chief Raoni, 94 and in palliative care, is alive.
