Hyderabad — A US startup booked 2 Indian launches for orbital mirrors.

Reflect Orbital plans a constellation of about 50,000 mirrors to beam sunlight onto Earth after dark.

SpaceX has stopped accepting Transporter reservations after 2029, so operators now buy launches from firms like [India](country:IN)'s Skyroot.

Reflect did not disclose prices or dates, and Vikram-1U is not due to fly until Q1 2027.
