Trump removed Vietnam from the US export control list. The list had grouped Vietnam with China, Russia, and North Korea, blocking its access to advanced chip-manufacturing equipment. Viettel, Vietnam's state telecom, aims for trial chip production by late 2027 as Vietnam works toward 50,000 chip engineers by 2030.
