London — April debt interest hit £10.3bn, a record monthly high.

Index-linked gilts reprice with inflation; the [Iran](country:IR) war has pushed [UK](country:GB) yields to multi-decade highs, budget forecasters said.

Pantheon Macroeconomics estimates 2026/27 debt interest at £15bn above budget assumptions if yields hold, the firm said.
