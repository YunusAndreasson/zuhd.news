The UN Security Council passed a resolution condemning only Iran's retaliatory Gulf strikes. China and Russia abstained; the text demands Iran halt attacks on Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, and Jordan but says nothing about the US-Israel strikes that triggered the war. Iran, still firing missiles at Gulf states as the resolution passed, has not agreed to a ceasefire.
