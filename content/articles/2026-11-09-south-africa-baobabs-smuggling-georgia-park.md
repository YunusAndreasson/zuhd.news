Durban — 5 mature baobabs lay on their sides in a car park for 3 months.

The alleged destination was Bidzina Ivanishvili's park in [Georgia](country:GE), which took 8 baobabs from Kenya.

Someone dug them up in Limpopo without permits and trucked them 800km, journalist Tony Carnie found after an anonymous tip-off.

Criminal charges have been laid, but who answers for the missing permits is unresolved.
