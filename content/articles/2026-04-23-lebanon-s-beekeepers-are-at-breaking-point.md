Bint Jbeil — Israeli strikes cut ByBee's 120 hives to 8. [Lebanon](country:LB)'s bee population fell 60% over 20 years before the war, a ByBee founder said; Israeli airstrikes on southern apiaries have accelerated that loss. Bees pollinate most of Lebanon's wild plants and orchards; no government support program has been announced.
