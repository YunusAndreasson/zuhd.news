Moscow — Hackers reportedly stole hundreds of millions of Dodo Pizza orders.

A Moscow court placed Denis Fridman, 19, under house arrest until 8 December.

Police reportedly traced him to Barnaul after he exposed his internet address while accessing the stolen data.

ATB, [Ukraine](country:UA)'s largest grocer, denies losing customer data, though DataSuckers claimed 7.9 million records.

A fourth alleged member remains at large, and the suspects include citizens of Kazakhstan and Belarus.
