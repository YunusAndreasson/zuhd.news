Washington — The [US](country:US) Senate approved $70 billion for deportation.

$38 billion goes to Immigration and Customs Enforcement and $26 billion to border agents — a 3-year enforcement budget passed 52-47 through budget reconciliation.

The House takes up the bill next; it supplements $140 billion in enforcement already allocated last year.
