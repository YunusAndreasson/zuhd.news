Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) installed a 6,200-ton homemade Persian Gulf rig.

[US](country:US) sanctions on foreign technology since 2018 forced domestic engineering; the platform adds 35,000 barrels per day at Reshadat, Shana said.

The platform will inject 80,000 barrels of water daily to maintain Reshadat's pressure, the ministry said.
