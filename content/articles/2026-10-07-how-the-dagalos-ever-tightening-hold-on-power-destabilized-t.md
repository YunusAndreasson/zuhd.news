Daein — Abdel Rahim Dagalo threatened Rizeigat elders with prison in August.

Tribal leaders say they can no longer persuade their communities to send sons to the Rapid Support Forces.

He demanded fresh mobilisation, and the omdas cited wounded fighters, dead sons and no voice in the political order, an attendee said.

He accused them of colluding with the military and threatened Nyala's Dagris prison, but several elders cut him off.

Dagalo left Daein angry, and no source says the Rizeigat agreed to mobilise again.
