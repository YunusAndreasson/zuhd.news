Beijing — 889 of 5,712 tracked adults developed chronic respiratory disease.

Roughly a third of humanity still cooks over coal, wood or crop waste indoors.

Tracking the same people for years let researchers link fuel use to a 23% higher hazard, something a one-time survey cannot show.

The finding comes from an accepted manuscript, with the version of record still pending.
