Beijing — A dumpling shop became [China](country:CN)'s AI model unit.

Officials showcase small businesses to prove AI adoption has reached daily commerce.

Diners now book tables through AI agents, and the shop's owner invokes Deng Xiaoping to justify the technology.

Beijing has not said how many firms nationally match the showcase's reported adoption.
