Beijing — Huixi Technology raised over 2.5 billion yuan this year.

The Beijing Artificial Intelligence Industry Investment Fund led the round, with the Beijing Robotics fund joining.

Huixi designs the R1 system-on-chip and a computing platform for robot control, already used in AgiBot's Genie G2 robots.

Huixi says proceeds fund a new generation of high-performance embodied-AI chips and faster commercial deliveries.
