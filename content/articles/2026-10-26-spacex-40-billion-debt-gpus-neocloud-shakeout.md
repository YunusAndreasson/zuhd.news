United States — SpaceX has borrowed $40 billion for GPUs, mostly callable debt.

Musk says lenders have shut out AI cloud companies lacking revenue beyond data centres.

SpaceX could borrow, Musk said, because Starlink and launch revenue do not depend on AI data centres.

Musk predicts the 200-plus neoclouds will consolidate to a few survivors.
