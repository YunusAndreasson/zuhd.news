Vienna — The International Atomic Energy Agency says Syria's reactor was built for weapons.

[Syria](country:SY)'s new government invited inspectors in, unlike [Iran](country:IR)'s program today.

Reactor type, moderator and fuel geometry reveal whether a design favors electricity or weapons-grade plutonium, agency chief Rafael Grossi said.

Inspectors will keep monitoring the site as excavation continues, Grossi said Monday.
