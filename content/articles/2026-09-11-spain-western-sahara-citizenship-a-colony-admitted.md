Madrid — Spain's parliament backed Sahrawi citizenship, 168 to 31.

More than 100,000 Sahrawis born under Spanish rule stand to gain citizenship.

[Spain](country:ES) ruled the territory until 1975 without completing decolonization, and the UN still names Madrid its administering power.

The bill needs Senate approval, even as Spain backs [Morocco](country:MA)'s rival autonomy plan.
