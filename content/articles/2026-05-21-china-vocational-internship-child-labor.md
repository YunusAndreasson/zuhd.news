Beijing — [China](country:CN)'s mandatory internship rules send vocational teens into industrial worksites.

Roughly 4 million secondary vocational students must complete placements to earn diplomas; some face hazardous conditions with no legal exemption.

No official death toll exists for students killed on mandatory placements.
