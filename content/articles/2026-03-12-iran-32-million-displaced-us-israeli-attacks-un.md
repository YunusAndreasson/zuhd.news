3.2 million Iranians are displaced, surpassing Kuwait's population. After 19 days of US-Israeli strikes, the UN's first count shows those fleeing bombed cities arriving in rural provinces with no aid infrastructure during Ramadan. Afghan refugees inside Iran face a second forced displacement, with no country willing to receive people who had already fled Kabul.
