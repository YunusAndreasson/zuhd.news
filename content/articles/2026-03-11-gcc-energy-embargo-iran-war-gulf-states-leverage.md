Gulf states produce 20% of world oil and host the world's largest LNG complex — and none has cut exports since the war began. Each GCC monarchy depends on US military protection to survive; an oil embargo would destroy the security guarantee keeping it in power. Middle East Eye calls the embargo "the single most powerful non-military measure available" — with no monarch yet willing to act.
