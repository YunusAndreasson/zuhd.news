Kuwait City — 42,000 people lost citizenship, with no appeal.

A 2025 law amendment let [Kuwait](country:KW)'s Supreme Committee for Verifying Nationality revoke status without judicial review, mostly targeting women naturalized through marriage.

The Committee has set no deadline to restore judicial review as more denaturalization cases await decision.
