Pakistan's petrol price doubled this week. Qatar's LNG cutoff on March 2 left Pakistan with 11 days of crude reserves, pushing the government to offer subsidized rates to motorcycles and rickshaws while leaving cars at market prices. Gas will not be available after April 14, when Qatar's last contracted supply runs out.
