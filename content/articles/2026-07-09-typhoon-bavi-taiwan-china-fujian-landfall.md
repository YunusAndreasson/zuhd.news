Taipei — Bavi is roughly the width of France.

At 1,000km wide, it would be the largest storm to hit [Taiwan](country:TW) since 1987, forecasters said, drawing size from record-warm Pacific waters before skirting north and landing in Fujian.

The storm is due to make landfall in [China](country:CN)'s Fujian province Saturday evening.
