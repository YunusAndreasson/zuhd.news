South Korean chip exports jumped 160% in a single month. AI data center demand for high-bandwidth memory drove the gain, pushing ICT to 49.8% of all South Korean exports. South Korea's 2 major chipmakers are racing to expand high-bandwidth memory capacity as US data center spending accelerates.
