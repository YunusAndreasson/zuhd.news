New Delhi — [US](country:US) cleared $482 million to maintain [India](country:IN)'s Apache fleet.

Boeing, Lockheed, and BAE are the principal contractors; the package also covers India's M777 howitzers.

The Defence Security Cooperation Agency notified Congress June 17; final contract terms are pending.
