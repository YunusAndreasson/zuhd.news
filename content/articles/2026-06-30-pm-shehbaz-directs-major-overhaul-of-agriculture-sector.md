Islamabad — Shehbaz Sharif ordered a new national farm policy.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s premier is restructuring the food security ministry and pushing better seeds and financing to lift yields for 240 million people.

Provinces must still sign off on the policy, the real test for a country exposed to climate stress and import dependence.
