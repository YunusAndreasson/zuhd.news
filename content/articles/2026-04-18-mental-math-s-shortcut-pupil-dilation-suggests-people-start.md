Bordeaux — Pupils dilate before arithmetic is complete, researchers find. Université de Bordeaux and UCLouvain tracked pupil size and found the brain commits to a partial solution before all operands appear. The finding challenges reactive models of cognition, with implications for mental-load modeling in human-computer interfaces.
