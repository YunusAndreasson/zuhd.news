Brent crude topped $100 a barrel Thursday. The International Energy Agency's record 400 million barrel release cannot substitute for actual flow while the Strait of Hormuz stays closed to US-linked shipping. Iran's Revolutionary Guard warned of $200 oil and threatened any vessel linked to the US, Israel, or their allies.
