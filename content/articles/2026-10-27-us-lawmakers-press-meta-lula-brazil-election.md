Brasília — 9 in 10 identifiable Meta restrictions hit pro-Lula content.

13 [US](country:US) Democrats including Bernie Sanders wrote Zuckerberg on 22 October demanding explanations.

Ctrl Z counted over 1,000 restrictions and bans in October's first half, including lost reach and removed posts.

Meta restored the Lula campaign's ad account after 24 hours without explanation, the letter says.

The letter also asks whether Meta discusses [Brazil](country:BR)'s judiciary with Donald Trump's administration.
