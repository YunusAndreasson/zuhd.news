Riga — [Latvia](country:LV)'s legal assessment of tokenizing real estate, due 1 October, is unfinished.

The minister driving it lost his party's seats in this month's election.

Tokenization turns property into blockchain tokens, and Economics Minister Viktors Valainis says money-laundering controls come first.

Valainis expects rules in the first half of 2027, if the next government keeps the work.
