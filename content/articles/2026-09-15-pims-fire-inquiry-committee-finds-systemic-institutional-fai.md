Islamabad — Investigators blamed "systemic institutional failure" for the blaze.

That finding names no person, so no one yet faces consequences for 14 deaths.

The August 26 blaze hit a nursery ward at Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences, where infants cannot evacuate quickly.

Criminal action was ordered against 8 officials, with no charges confirmed yet.
