Kuala Lumpur — $12.3 million buys Najib house arrest until 2028.

Investigators say $4.5 billion was drained from 1Malaysia Development Berhad, the fund Najib co-founded.

[Malaysia](country:MY)'s king granted a conditional pardon converting prison to house arrest, without reopening the conviction.

Najib is appealing a second conviction carrying a $2.8 billion fine and 15 more years.
