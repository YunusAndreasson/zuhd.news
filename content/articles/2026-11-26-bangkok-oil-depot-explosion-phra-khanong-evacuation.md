Bangkok — Seven people were injured, 4 critically, in a refinery blast.

The 120,000-barrel-a-day plant sits in a residential district, and the government has suspended production.

[Thailand](country:TH)'s energy minister Akanat Promphan said the fire began in a distillation tower 100m from oil storage, limiting the damage.

Bangchak says it put the fire out within 30 minutes and air quality stayed in normal ranges.

Bangchak is investigating the cause, and production stays shut until the government is confident operations can resume safely.
