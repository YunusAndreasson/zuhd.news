Hong Kong — Hong Kong Easter rail tickets are nearly sold out. Airspace closures from the Iran conflict and rising fuel surcharges have made long-haul flights unaffordable, pushing travelers to rail. Rail capacity will determine whether Hong Kong's travel industry recovers or contracts further as Hormuz fighting continues.
