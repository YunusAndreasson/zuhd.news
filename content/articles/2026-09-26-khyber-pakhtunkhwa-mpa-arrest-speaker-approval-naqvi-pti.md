Peshawar — Khyber Pakhtunkhwa police need the speaker's approval to arrest lawmakers.

Imran Khan's party says dozens of its leaders and workers are detained ahead of a 4 October march.

Section 11 of the 2026 privileges act makes magistrates and executive authorities seek the speaker's permission, with reasons, before detaining a member.

"What an extraordinary version of 'equality before law'," Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi wrote on X.

The provincial cabinet approved a new police act, which the assembly has yet to pass.
