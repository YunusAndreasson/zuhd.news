Brasilia — Brazil deforested 985,000 hectares in 2025.

[Brazil](country:BR) fell below 1 million hectares for the first time since 2019 — a 20.6% drop — but annual reduction is now flattening.

A flattening rate means the Amazon remains a net loss even as headline numbers improve.
