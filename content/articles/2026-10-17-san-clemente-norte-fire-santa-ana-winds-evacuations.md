San Juan Capistrano — 16,000 residents were ordered to flee the Norte Fire.

At least 6 coastal homes burned in unseasonably hot weather, with a heat advisory in force.

Dry Santa Ana winds from the interior drove the fire from San Clemente into San Juan Capistrano.

Fire weather lasts through Saturday night, and authorities have not said how the Norte Fire started.
