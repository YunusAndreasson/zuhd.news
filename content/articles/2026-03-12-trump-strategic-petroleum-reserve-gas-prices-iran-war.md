Trump will tap the US Strategic Petroleum Reserve. The IEA had already activated reserves across 31 member states; Trump frames the same crisis as a domestic fuel price problem, not a wartime emergency. The SPR's stockpiles are finite; every barrel released now is unavailable if the Iran conflict deepens.
