Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK) arrested its oil regulator's member over pricing fraud.

The Federal Investigation Agency found Rs14bn in questionable pricing claims, and the government plans to install civil servants atop the independent regulator by presidential ordinance.

Budget sessions open this week, after which parliament could challenge the move.
