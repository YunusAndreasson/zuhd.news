Tegucigalpa — Tomás Zambrano raised an anti-abortion flag beside the national shield.

He has set the legislative agenda by the values of the ultraconservative groups he allied with.

He tours all 18 of [Honduras](country:HN)'s departments weekly, mixing official acts, rallies and evangelical services.

Ex-president Juan Orlando Hernández, Zambrano's mentor, returned after a Trump pardon and is uneasy at his haste.

Zambrano is positioned as the National Party's presidential candidate when the current term ends in 2030.
