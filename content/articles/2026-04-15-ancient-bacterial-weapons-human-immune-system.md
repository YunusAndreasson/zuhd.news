New York — Immune sensors are repurposed 3-billion-year-old bacterial defenses. Researchers found dozens of ancient microbial defense systems — including CRISPR families — operating unchanged inside mammalian innate immunity. Each new system identified is a potential drug target; the full catalog of repurposed defenses remains incomplete.
