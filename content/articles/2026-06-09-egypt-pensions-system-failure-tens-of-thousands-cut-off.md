Cairo — [Egypt](country:EG)'s new pension platform cut tens of thousands off.

The March migration to a fully digital system retired all paper records without a fallback mechanism, leaving claim backlogs growing for months.

Madbuly has extended the fix deadline to August; the platform serves 11 million beneficiaries.
