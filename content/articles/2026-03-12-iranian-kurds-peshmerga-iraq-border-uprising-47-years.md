US strikes opened Iran's border to Kurdish fighters. Diaspora Kurds are joining Peshmerga forces at the crossing — the first military opening Iran's 10 million Kurds have had since their parties were banned in 1979. Iran has fired over 200 rockets into Iraqi Kurdistan since the war began to seal the border.
