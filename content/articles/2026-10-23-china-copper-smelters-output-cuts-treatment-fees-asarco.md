China — Mysteel sees fourth-quarter refined copper output falling.

Smelters are curbing output with copper near a record: $14,470 a tonne in September.

In Arizona, Grupo México aims to restart Asarco's Hayden smelter, which could process 1.4 million tonnes of concentrate a year.

Grupo México plans $4 billion to $6 billion for Asarco over 3 years, mining head Leonardo Contreras said.
