Rio de Janeiro — 122 people died in [Brazil](country:BR)'s deadliest police raid.

Residents recovered bodies from the forest escape route and lined them up in a Vila Cruzeiro square.

Civil and Military police stormed Penha and Alemão on 28 October 2025 under then-governor Cláudio Castro, targeting the Comando Vermelho.

Five of the dead were police officers.

Tauã Brito, whose son Wellington died, first set foot in the square again on 12 October.
