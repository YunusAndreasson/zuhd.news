Jiangyou — Armed police detained 2 Early Rain Covenant leaders.

[China](country:CN)'s Communist Party bars unregistered churches, pressuring Christians to join state-sanctioned congregations with government-approved pastors.

Yan Hong and Wu Wuqing remain in custody; the church's founding pastor Wang Yi is serving a 9-year sentence.
