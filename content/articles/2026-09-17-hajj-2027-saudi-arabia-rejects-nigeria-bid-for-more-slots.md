Abuja — [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) capped [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Hajj quota again.

Nigeria's allocation stays at 50,000 pilgrims, unchanged despite years of rising demand.

Riyadh sets a global pilgrim ceiling by site capacity, then divides it by country before Nigeria splits its share again.

NAHCON's deadline for pilgrim registration data falls Sept. 26, with no allocation extensions.
