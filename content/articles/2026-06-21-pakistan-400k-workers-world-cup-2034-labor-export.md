Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK) plans 400,000 workers for [Saudi](country:SA) World Cup.

Official figures show 530,256 workers — 70% of overseas registrations — left for Saudi Arabia in 2025; remittances are the main foreign-exchange earner.

Islamabad has not set worker-protection terms for 2034; Gulf mega-projects have drawn repeated rights criticism.
