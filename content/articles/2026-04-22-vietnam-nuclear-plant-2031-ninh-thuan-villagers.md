Ninh Thuan — 2,000 villagers face relocation for Vietnam's first reactor.

[Vietnam](country:VN) shelved this same project in 2016 after 7 years of paralysis; it now targets 2031 with no move-out date set.

Rosatom holds the original partnership; no domestic nuclear regulator is in place, experts say.
