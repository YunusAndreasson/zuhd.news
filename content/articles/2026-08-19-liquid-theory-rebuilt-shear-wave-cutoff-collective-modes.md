New York — Liquid theory has stood unchanged since the 1800s.

Liquids make up most of the world and the body, yet lacked a theory.

Solids and gases got clean atomic theories a century ago, but liquids are dense like solids and disordered like gases.

The reframing still lacks direct experimental confirmation at extreme pressure and temperatures.
