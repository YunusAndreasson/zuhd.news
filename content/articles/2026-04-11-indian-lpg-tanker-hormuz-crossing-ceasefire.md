Khasab — An Indian tanker crossed the Strait of Hormuz. Jag Vikram, carrying 20,412 tonnes of LPG, is the first vessel to transit since the ceasefire — mines still unaccounted for. 15 more Indian ships are waiting; each transit tests whether the truce holds.
