Cairo — Egypt's Civil Democratic Movement collapsed after 9 years.

Founded in 2017, the movement fractured after a faction compared a party leader's demolished mansion to mass forced evictions of the poor.

Some leaders argue the movement retains political value; no alternative coalition has formed.
