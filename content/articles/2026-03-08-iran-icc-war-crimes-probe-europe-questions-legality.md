3 European governments called US-Israeli strikes on Iran potentially illegal. The 3 governments — Switzerland, Germany, and Spain — cite Article 51 of the UN Charter, which authorizes self-defense but not strikes on civilian infrastructure like oil depots and water systems. Tehran's burning oil depots release toxic smoke; the Iranian Red Crescent warns of poisonous rainfall over 9 million residents.
