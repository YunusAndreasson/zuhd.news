20,000 sailors are stranded in the Persian Gulf. Their 3,000 vessels have no resupply route while Hormuz is closed, with fuel and fresh water running low. The International Chamber of Shipping is raising the crew welfare crisis at an emergency UN maritime meeting in London on Wednesday.
