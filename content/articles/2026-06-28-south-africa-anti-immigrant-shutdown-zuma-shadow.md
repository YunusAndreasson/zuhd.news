Johannesburg — Xenophobic groups declared a 30 June [South Africa](country:ZA) shutdown.

The movement's directors link to former president Jacob Zuma's family networks; Zuma's uMkhonto we Sizwe party endorsed the shutdown.

Security forces say they are better prepared than 2021; whether mobilization translates to violence is unresolved.
