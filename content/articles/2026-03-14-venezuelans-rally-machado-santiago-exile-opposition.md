16,000 Venezuelans flooded central Santiago, Chile. Venezuelan opposition leader María Corina Machado led the march, drawing on 8 million Venezuelans who have fled since 2014 and whose opposition President Nicolás Maduro cannot suppress from Caracas. Machado promised to return to Venezuela but named no date, with Maduro retaining full state control.
