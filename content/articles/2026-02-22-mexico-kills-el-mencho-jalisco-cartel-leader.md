Mexico's army killed Nemesio Oseguera Cervantes, the Jalisco New Generation Cartel boss who carried a $15 million US bounty. The former police officer built a fentanyl and cocaine empire so dominant that his cartel now operates across all of Mexico. His fighters retaliated with roadblocks and gunfire in 8 states, trapping tourists in Puerto Vallarta and forcing Guadalajara — a World Cup host city — into lockdown.
