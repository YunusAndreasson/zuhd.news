Marburg now has a structural weak point. Researchers used cryo-EM to map the NPC1 receptor Marburg uses to enter cells, revealing where a nanobody can block viral entry. The blueprint provides drug developers their first precise target for a pathogen that kills up to 90% of those infected, has no approved treatment, and has never had a therapy reach clinical trials.
