Santiago — Elias 2-24 b is under a million years old.

Every other confirmed planet is finished, so scientists have never watched one form.

The planet was found by the gap it carves in its star's dust disk, a team in [Chile](country:CL) said.

Scientists say current planet-formation models are still missing key processes this detection reveals.
