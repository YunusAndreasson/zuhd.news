New Delhi — India's grid hit 270 GW last month.

Air conditioning already covers 25% of [India](country:IN)'s peak demand; a 2-star-to-5-star switch for 15m new buyers saves ₹69bn ($724m) a year, analysis finds.

Power shortages are projected as early as 2028 without sustained efficiency improvements, researchers warn.
