Gaza — 5 Israeli-backed militias now run compounds beside Israeli bases.

[Israel](country:IL) keeps control of Gaza's ground through armed Palestinian groups nearly a year into the ceasefire.

Forensic Architecture's satellite analysis shows compounds upgraded with berms, barracks and fences, resembling Israeli military infrastructure.

"These militias are Israel-created," said Muhammad Shehada, a Gaza analyst and visiting fellow at the European Council on Foreign Relations.

Israel holds nearly 40 bases east of the yellow line, and the compounds are still expanding.
