Brazil — Daniel Vorcaro offered pastor André Valadão R$100 million.

Vorcaro, arrested in November 2025 and March 2026, owned Banco Master when he made the April 2024 offer.

Messages intercepted by [Brazil](country:BR)'s Federal Police show the two traded advice on running church assets and avoiding taxes.

Valadão has said his relationship with Vorcaro was limited to the church and involved no business.

The Lagoinha network Valadão leads expects to move R$1 billion by 2030.
