Abuja — Consumer prices hit 146.3 points last month.

Headline inflation in [Nigeria](country:NG) barely moved, but food and core costs diverged sharply.

Monthly food inflation dropped to 1.02% from 5.56%, and monthly core inflation turned negative, the statistics agency said.

The central bank's next rate decision will weigh this print against still-high core costs.
