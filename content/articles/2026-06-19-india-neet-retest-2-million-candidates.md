New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s medical exam re-runs Sunday for 2.2 million.

The National Testing Agency cancelled the May 3 exam after a paper leak; Sunday's re-run deploys 250,000 personnel across 551 cities.

Telegram was blocked June 16 to cut off cheating rings; any irregularity triggers criminal charges.
