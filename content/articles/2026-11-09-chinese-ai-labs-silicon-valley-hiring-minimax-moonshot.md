San Francisco — MiniMax has listed 16 openings in the city.

The [US](country:US) gained 2,145 AI researchers in 2025 as [China](country:CN) lost 1,729, Carnegie China found.

MiniMax spent $84.3 million on pay in 2025, about $203,000 per employee, its filings show, including share-based pay.

Yale researcher Cheryl Wu said Chinese firms "simply don't have enough of it" to offer competitive wages.

MiniMax is also hiring a lead recruiter for Silicon Valley and Seattle.
