New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s court cleared Bihar's 65-lakh voter-roll citizenship purge.

Bihar's Special Intensive Revision was ruled constitutional despite challenges that it targets Muslim and migrant voters.

The ruling applies to all future voter-roll revisions nationwide.
