India and Canada signed a $2.6 billion uranium deal. Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney flew to Delhi for the signing, ending a 3-year diplomatic freeze after Canada accused India's government of ordering the assassination of a Sikh activist on Canadian soil. Deliveries start in 2027, making the deal India's most urgent energy hedge as Gulf oil routes stay closed.
