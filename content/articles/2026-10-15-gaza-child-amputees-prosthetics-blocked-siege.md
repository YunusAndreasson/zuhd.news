Gaza — Eleven-year-old Abdul-Rahman al-Nashash's bone has outgrown his prosthetic leg.

His doctors told him to stop wearing it, and he wears it anyway.

An amputated child's bone keeps growing and the limb does not, while [Israel](country:IL) restricts the parts and materials needed to make and repair them.

He awaits a third surgery, and his mother expects him to need one every year.
