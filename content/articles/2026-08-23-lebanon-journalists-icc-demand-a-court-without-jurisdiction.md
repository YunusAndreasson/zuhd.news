Beirut — Israeli strikes have killed 28 journalists since 2023.

No domestic court has convicted anyone, so families are turning to the ICC instead.

Neither [Lebanon](country:LB) nor [Israel](country:IL) has joined the Rome Statute, so the court they are petitioning has no automatic jurisdiction here.

Families say they are still compiling testimony into a legal file for submission.
