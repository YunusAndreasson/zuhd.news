Washington — A Caucasus peace deal turns one year old.

A trade corridor promised a year ago at the summit still hasn't broken ground.

[Armenia](country:AM) and [Azerbaijan](country:AZ) fought two wars over Karabakh before last year's White House summit ended hostilities.

Armenian Prime Minister Nikol Pashinyan's office says construction on the corridor will begin soon.
