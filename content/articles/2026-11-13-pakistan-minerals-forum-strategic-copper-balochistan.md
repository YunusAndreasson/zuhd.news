Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK) promised foreign miners predictable policy, support and security.

Petroleum Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik says exploration now targets minerals of strategic importance to global investors.

He cited geochemical and geophysical surveys, exploratory drilling and upgraded geoscience laboratories as the tools.

The minister's remarks named no buyer, stake or contract.
