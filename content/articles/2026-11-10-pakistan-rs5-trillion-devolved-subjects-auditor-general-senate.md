Islamabad — Over Rs5 trillion went to provincial subjects, a senator says.

Federal spending is Rs19 trillion against Rs20 trillion in revenue, Senator Zamir Hussain Ghumro said.

Citing the auditor general's data, Ghumro says [Pakistan](country:PK)'s federal ministries duplicate subjects the 18th Amendment gave to the provinces.

The prime minister has not answered summaries to abolish 24 such ministries, and the committee wants a fresh note.
