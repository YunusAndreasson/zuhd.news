Mumbai — Jio Platforms listed 25% above issue price at $137.1 billion.

Mukesh Ambani now controls [India](country:IN)'s two most valued companies.

Reliance Industries keeps over 64% of Jio, and 3% dilution wiped out nearly 300 billion rupees of debt, an analyst said.

Analysts say most of the value is already priced in, at 34 times earnings.

Angel One's Vaqarjaved Khan says returns now hinge on higher revenue per user and AI services.
