A 5.8 earthquake hit Cuba during a total blackout. Cuba's power grid had collapsed under the US oil blockade — hospitals, emergency communications, and earthquake-response systems had no electricity. US President Donald Trump vowed this week to "take" Cuba; the blockade leaves the island with no disaster-response capacity.
