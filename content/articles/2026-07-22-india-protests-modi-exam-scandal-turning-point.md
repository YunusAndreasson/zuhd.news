Delhi — Tens of thousands chanted "Modi must resign" in the capital's guarded core.

Students demand Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan quit over exam irregularities linked to at least 21 student suicides; activist Sonam Wangchuk fasted 17 days.

[India](country:IN)'s government has not said whether Pradhan will step down.
