San Francisco — Hyperscalers will spend over $1 trillion next year.

A Wharton economist says that bet requires a 2.7x productivity jump to break even.

Current AI revenue runs $150–200 billion a year, far short of what trillion-dollar capex needs to earn a 15% return.

Jessica Wachter says a shortfall risks missed interest payments and possible bankruptcy.
