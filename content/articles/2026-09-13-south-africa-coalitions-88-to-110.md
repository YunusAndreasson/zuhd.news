Johannesburg — [South Africa](country:ZA)'s hung councils could hit 110.

Analyst Susan Booysen's projection points to worsening service delivery in fragmented metros.

South Africa's local elections use no seat threshold, letting a single council seat swing a mayoralty.

A bill setting a 1% threshold won't pass before the Nov. 4 vote.
