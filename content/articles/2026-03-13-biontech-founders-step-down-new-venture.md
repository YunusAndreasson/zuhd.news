BioNTech fell 17% after its founders announced they're leaving. CEO Uğur Şahin and chief medical officer Özlem Türeci, the Turkish-German couple who built the company's mRNA platform, will depart by end of 2026 to start a third venture targeting mRNA treatments for cancer, HIV, and malaria. BioNTech will supply patent rights to the new firm in exchange for a minority stake, betting its founders can move faster outside the structure they built.
