Damascus — Attacks on trans women intensified after Assad's fall.

[Syria](country:SY)'s 1949 Penal Code criminalizes cross-dressing with up to 9 months in prison; the new government has not changed it.

Trans women told researchers they avoid hospitals and public spaces fearing arrest or forced disclosure.
