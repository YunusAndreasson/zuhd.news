Gaza — Displaced families share schools with no private space.

The UN relief agency reported 218,600 people sheltering in 92 schools in the war's first week; overcrowding has since become permanent.

WHO and the UN children's fund warn that polio vaccination and healthcare delivery are failing in overcrowded shelters.
