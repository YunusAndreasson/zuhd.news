Washington — Congress asked Boeing to restart C-17 production.

The [US](country:US) Air Force must report by March 2027 whether tooling, suppliers and a dispersed workforce can be reconstituted after 11 years.

Allied operators from [Australia](country:AU) to the [UK](country:GB) have approached Boeing; shared procurement could justify the restart cost.
