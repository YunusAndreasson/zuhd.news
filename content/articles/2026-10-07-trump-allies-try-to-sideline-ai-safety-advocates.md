Washington — Trump allies want consulting firms, not nonprofits, to audit AI.

The accord Trump signed with AI chief executives last week leaves open who audits the companies.

David Sacks said professional auditors should replace groups like METR, and Anthropic then partnered with Accenture to build outside evaluators.

"It almost felt like a new version of McCarthyism," said Rayan Krishnan, chief executive of the AI evaluation start-up Vals AI.

Investor Chamath Palihapitiya said EY will soon announce an AI audit offering built with his start-up 8090.
