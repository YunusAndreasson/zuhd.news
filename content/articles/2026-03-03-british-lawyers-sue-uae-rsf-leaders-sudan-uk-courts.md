British courts will hear suits against UAE over Sudan. Sudan's Bar Association initiated the case; 35 Sudanese lawyers died defending civilians in a war the Security Council has failed to address. The British legal delegation has yet to arrive; suits will name UAE officials and Rapid Support Forces commanders individually.
