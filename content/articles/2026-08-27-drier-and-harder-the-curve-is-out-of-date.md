Hawaiʻi is getting drier and stormier at once.

Flash floods, not annual rainfall totals, are what actually damage infrastructure and kill people.

A warmer atmosphere holds roughly 7% more moisture per degree, so rain falls harder even as rainy days grow fewer.

Storm drains and dam spillways built to old rainfall records may already be undersized.
