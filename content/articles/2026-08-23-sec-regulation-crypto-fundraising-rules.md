Washington — The US securities regulator proposed a crypto rulebook.

Token sales move from case-by-case lawsuits to a standard filing category.

The proposal sets disclosure tiers by how much a startup raises and how it operates, replacing Howey-test litigation.

The agency still needs months to finalize rules and let firms comply.
