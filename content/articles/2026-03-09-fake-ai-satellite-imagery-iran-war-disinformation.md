Tehran Times fabricated satellite images of a US military base. The fakes spread to millions in multiple languages before analysts identified them — satellite imagery had long been treated as unimpeachable evidence of military operations. Researchers tracking AI disinformation have demanded mandatory platform labels — no platform has adopted them.
