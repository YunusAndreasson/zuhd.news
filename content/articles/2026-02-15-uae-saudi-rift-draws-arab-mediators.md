No high-level contact since December. Saudi Arabia accused the United Arab Emirates of threatening its national security by supporting Yemeni separatists who carried out a brief land grab. Bahrain is now leading mediation between the two governments, with Egypt relaying messages after Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan met Egyptian officials in Cairo on January 5. A source close to the Saudi government insisted there was "no need for mediation" because direct channels remain open. If the two wealthiest Gulf states cannot resolve the dispute, it could destabilize regional cooperation on oil policy, the Yemen conflict, and normalization with Israel.
