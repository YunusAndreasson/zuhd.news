Baghdad — [Iraq](country:IQ) ordered a federal takeover of oilfields in 4 provinces.

The order also puts every border crossing in the Kurdish region under federal control.

The region may approve crude exports only through the federal oil ministry, after an inquiry into smuggling at North Oil Company.

Kurdish prime minister Masrour Barzani called the decisions "in no way acceptable."

Khormala, the main Kurdish-run field, appears covered, and one of three sources expects Kurdish authorities to resist.
