US jets bombed targets near Iran's Chabahar port. India invested $1.6bn in Chabahar as its only overland route to Central Asia, bypassing Pakistan's territory. India has not yet commented on the strikes, the sharpest test of New Delhi's declared neutrality in the Iran conflict.
