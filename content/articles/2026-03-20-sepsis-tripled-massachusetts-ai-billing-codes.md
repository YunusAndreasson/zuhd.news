Massachusetts sepsis hospitalizations tripled since 2010. Hospitals use AI coding tools that automatically flag borderline cases as sepsis, yielding higher insurance reimbursements and inflating disease statistics. Public health officials now must determine whether sepsis is genuinely surging or hospitals are gaming reimbursement codes.
