Madrid — Carles Puigdemont's case closed after 9 years of exile.

His party, Junts, helped re-elect Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez in 2023 in exchange for the amnesty.

October's Constitutional Court ruling extended the amnesty to embezzlement, so the Supreme Court shelved the case against Puigdemont and 2 former ministers.

Opposition leader Alberto Núñez Feijóo vows to repeal the amnesty law, which would not undo amnesties already granted.

Puigdemont speaks in Brussels on Wednesday and may return in December, after the 29 November election.
