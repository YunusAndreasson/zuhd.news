Belgrade — Zelenskyy landed in the EU's most Russia-friendly capital.

[Serbia](country:RS) has refused sanctions on Moscow throughout the war, a position President Aleksandar Vucic says predates the visit and will not change.

Vucic and Zelenskyy meet Saturday on trade, energy and security, with EU accession still stalled.
