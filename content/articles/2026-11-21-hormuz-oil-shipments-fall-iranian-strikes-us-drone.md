Hormuz — 263 ships transited in the week to 18 November, down 15%.

Insuring a tanker crossing costs $64mn, up from $54,000, the acting US Navy secretary said.

[Iran](country:IR) hits 1 to 1.5 ships a day, Rystad Energy says, and each damaged tanker must discharge cargo before repair.

Shipments exceeded pre-war levels on 6 days in October, before the strikes rose.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by December 31 a 10% chance, down 12 points in a week.
