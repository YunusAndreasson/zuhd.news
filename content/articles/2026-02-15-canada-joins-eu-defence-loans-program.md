Canada joined an EU defence loan program. Defence Minister David McGuinty confirmed Canada entered the Security Action for Europe initiative, which provides low-interest loans for joint military equipment procurement. The EU launched SAFE in March 2025 as part of a broader rearmament effort driven by Russia's war in Ukraine, with Belgium and 8 other European states receiving the first wave of funding. Prime Minister Mark Carney signed the underlying strategic partnership with the EU in June 2025. The agreement opens European defence contracts to Canadian manufacturers at a time when US commitment to European security has grown uncertain under President Trump.
