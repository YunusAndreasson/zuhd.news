[Nepal](country:NP)'s Gen-Z-led government inherited a Gulf economic shock.

Elected to stabilize the economy, the new government faces disrupted income from its primary Gulf remittance corridor, cut by the Hormuz standoff.

The government has outlined no alternative income plan while the [Iran](country:IR)-[US](country:US) standoff persists.
