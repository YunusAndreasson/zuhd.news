China — 35.4 trillion yuan ($5.3 trillion) now sits in bank wealth products.

Investors are moving money toward safer holdings, according to the registry's quarterly report.

The balance rose 10.1% in a year, and bank subsidiaries hold 93.1% of assets across 54,300 products.

Those products returned 141.7 billion yuan to investors in the third quarter.
