New York — A $327 billion pension fund turned down a private equity fund.

Its chief investment officer, Monte Tarbox, called the decision easy.

AI risk now runs through stocks, credit and infrastructure at once, so diversifying away from it is getting harder.

Other large allocators face the same test as AI exposure spreads across asset classes.
