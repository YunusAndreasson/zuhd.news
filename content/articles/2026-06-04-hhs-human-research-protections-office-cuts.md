Washington — 10 staff guard [US](country:US) human research protections.

The office oversees 13,000 institutions; its 10 remaining staff, against a recommended floor of 42, cannot conduct routine compliance visits.

A required site visit to New York State Psychiatric Institute, due since December, has not taken place.
