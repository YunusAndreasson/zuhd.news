San Francisco — OpenAI paused training over safety concerns last month.

Its safety tests run mostly in English, leaving other languages effectively unchecked.

It was the first time a major AI lab voluntarily froze its own model, the outlet reports.

It's unclear what triggered the pause, or when training will resume.
