Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s cabinet ended sugar export subsidies and price controls.

The country had been in breach of an IMF benchmark since June.

Cane prices, planting zones and new mills are now free of state control, and sugar moves freely between provinces.

Sindh blocked approval until 21 November, when it won amendments.

The IMF executive board meeting, which needed this approval, is set for 11 December.
