Tehran — [Washington](country:US) set [Iran](country:IR)'s uranium ceiling at 400 kg.

The US demands Iran keep only 1 nuclear facility, refuses compensation for the February strikes, and caps frozen-asset releases at 25%, Iranian state media reported.

Iran demanded sanctions relief and compensation for bombing; the US dismissed both.
