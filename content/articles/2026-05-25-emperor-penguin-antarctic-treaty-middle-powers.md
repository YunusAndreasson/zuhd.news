Canberra — Sea-ice loss is collapsing emperor penguin breeding habitat as treaty mechanisms go unused.

[Australia](country:AU) and other middle powers hold legal tools within the Antarctic Treaty to mandate protection, but none has invoked them.

Which state moves first — and through which legal pathway — remains unresolved.
