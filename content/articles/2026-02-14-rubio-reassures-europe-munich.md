Washington pledged unity with Europe at Munich. United States Secretary of State Marco Rubio told the Munich Security Conference the US and Europe "belong together" and Washington prefers to act jointly but is "prepared, if necessary, to do this alone." His tone contrasted with Vice President JD Vance's address at the same forum last year. French President Emmanuel Macron responded that Europe is "building its own architecture of security." German Chancellor Friedrich Merz appealed to Washington to "repair and revive transatlantic trust."
