New York — Foreign flows into [US](country:US) equities are at a record.

An AI sell-off could drag the dollar down instead of lifting it as a refuge.

Institutions hold the money in mega-cap stocks, hyperscaler bonds and grid assets, and sell when they reassess US growth or valuations.

Last week, as AI valuations fell, the yen and Swiss franc beat the dollar as havens.

The Federal Reserve announces its rate decision on Wednesday.
