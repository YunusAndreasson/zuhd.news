Mossel Bay — 5 Mozambicans killed in anti-immigrant attacks, Maputo confirmed.

Anti-migrant protests erupted Friday, trapping 800 Mozambican workers in [South Africa](country:ZA)'s coastal towns; the country has faced recurring xenophobic violence since 2008.

Over 500 remain sheltered in Western Cape as [Mozambique](country:MZ) begins repatriation.
