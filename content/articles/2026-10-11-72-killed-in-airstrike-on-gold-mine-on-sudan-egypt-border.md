Sudan — An airstrike killed 72 gold miners Saturday, lawyers say.

The Emergency Lawyers accuse [Egypt](country:EG)'s air force of killing civilians at work.

The Sudanese rights group called the toll preliminary and said efforts to document and verify the casualties continue.

No government has confirmed the strike, and the report carries no response from Cairo.
