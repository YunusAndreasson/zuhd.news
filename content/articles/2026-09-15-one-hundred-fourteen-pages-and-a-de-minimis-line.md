Washington — A 114-page bill would write crypto into US tax law.

Its de minimis threshold decides whether ordinary people can spend crypto without a taxable event on every purchase.

Every crypto purchase is currently a taxable property sale, which is why the digital-asset tax code has never let tokens function as cash.

The Ways and Means Committee votes on the bill Wednesday.
