The Senate voted 89-10 to ban a digital dollar. A CBDC would let the Federal Reserve issue money directly, bypassing commercial banks — a system China's digital yuan already operates. Trump warned he won't sign any bill until Congress passes voter-ID legislation, leaving the CBDC ban's fate uncertain.
