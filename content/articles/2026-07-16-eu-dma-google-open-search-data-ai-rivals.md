Brussels — The Commission found 60% of EU Android users rate rival AI assistants worse than Gemini.

The European Commission ruled Google's search index and Android system hooks essential infrastructure that rivals must be allowed to use.

Google must share search data with rivals by January 2027 and open Android features by July 2027, despite its privacy objections.
