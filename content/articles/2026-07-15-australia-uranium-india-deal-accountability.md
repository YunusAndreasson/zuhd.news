Canberra — The uranium moves; the responsibility doesn't.

[Australia](country:AU)'s Albanese and [India](country:IN)'s Modi struck a deal to export uranium, but left environmental and safeguards accountability for the material unresolved.

Who tracks the mining, transport and waste of that uranium remains an open question.
