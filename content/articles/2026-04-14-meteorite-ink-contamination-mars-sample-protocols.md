Bilbao — Ink traces from lab tools contaminated Martian meteorites. Basque researchers identified contaminants introduced during sample preparation — meaning prior analyses may have measured lab materials, not Mars. Mars Sample Return launches this decade; the project must now audit its own preparation labs for contamination.
