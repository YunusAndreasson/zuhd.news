Culiacán — Lookouts and narcocorridos have largely vanished from the city.

Targeted killings replaced open shootouts, InSight Crime's reporters found.

One faction's handover of Ismael Zambada to [US](country:US) authorities set the Chapitos against the Mayiza, and fighting has moved south toward Mazatlán.

A mass shooting at a city-centre restaurant, apparently targeting a narcocorrido singer, struck during their visit.

Fausto Isidro Meza, alias Chapo Isidro, may emerge as the top boss, the reporters say.
