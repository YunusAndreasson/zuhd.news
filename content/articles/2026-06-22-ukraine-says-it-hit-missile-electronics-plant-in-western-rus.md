Voronezh — Ukrainian drones struck a missile-electronics plant and a satellite center in [Russia](country:RU).

[Ukraine](country:UA) said the strikes targeted Russia's missile supply chain; the Voronezh plant makes guidance electronics for cruise missiles.

Russia said it downed 301 drones; damage to the Dubna satellite center is being assessed.
