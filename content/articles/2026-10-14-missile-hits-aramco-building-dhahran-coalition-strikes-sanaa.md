Dhahran — A missile set fire to an Aramco administrative building.

Dhahran anchors the Eastern Province, the centre of [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s oil industry.

The Saudi-led coalition struck Sanaa's Al Sabaha fuel station and Hodeidah's loading piers, saying the Houthis use civilian fuel sites for war.

The Houthis say they dispersed fuel reserves before the strike, a claim no one has verified.

Brent crude stood at $106.40 on 12 October, and Aramco has not said whether output will change.
