Iranian shrapnel set a Bahrain warehouse ablaze Friday. A separate strike hit Kuwait's Ahmadi refinery the same day, with authorities silent on the cause and casualties. Kuwait has not announced when the refinery will restart, leaving damage to Gulf supply chains unclear.
