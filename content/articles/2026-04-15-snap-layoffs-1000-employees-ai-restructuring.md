Santa Monica — Snap fired 1,000 employees and cited AI. CEO Evan Spiegel's memo cited AI's ability to reduce repetitive work as enabling the cuts, targeting $500 million in annualized savings by H2 2026. Snap's stock rose 10% premarket; activist Irenic Capital still demands spinning off the $3.5 billion AR glasses unit.
