France — Condorcet's 240-year-old voting paradox is a Klein bottle.

It gives mathematicians a geometric way to study why fair voting rules clash.

Three voter blocs ranking Alice, Bob and Carol in rotated orders can make Alice beat Bob, Bob beat Carol, Carol beat Alice.

Kenneth Arrow proved that every method of combining rankings must sacrifice at least one reasonable democratic requirement.
