Naypyidaw — Myanmar's fractured opposition has formed a single steering council.

Years of parallel wars against the junta let it fight and negotiate with rivals one at a time.

The Steering Council for the Emergence of a Federal Democratic Union aims to bring resistance factions under one roof.

Whether the council holds will decide if battlefield gains ever become a government.
