New York — BTCS can't rule out liquidation of a $54 million loan.

Outside traders can buy the Nasdaq-listed firm's ether at a discount, with no margin call.

BTCS borrows stablecoins on Aave against its ether, and by one estimate, ether below roughly $1,450 makes the whole position liquidatable.

BTCS cut debt from $64 million in 10 days, but its statement does not say how it paid.

Ether has fallen 15.4% in 7 days, and BTCS shares have lost 28.1% this week.
