Karachi — Roshan Hussain, 17, lies unburied five days on.

His family's refusal to bury him is their only leverage against police inaction.

Two of the six gunmen who shot him wore police uniforms, in a neighborhood where a uniform alone opens doors.

Punjab police are now hunting the suspects, who fled to Lodhran.
