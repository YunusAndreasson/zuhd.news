London — New tool traces soybean origins to 200km precision. Kew Gardens and Sheffield researchers used isotopic signatures in the beans themselves, turning each shipment into a verifiable provenance record. Soy is deforestation's third-largest driver; regulators can now audit sourcing claims that were previously impossible to verify.
