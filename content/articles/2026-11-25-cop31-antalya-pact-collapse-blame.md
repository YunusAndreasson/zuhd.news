Antalya — At least 32 countries rejected the Antalya pact.

Talks overran by two days and ended without the Turkey-Australia presidency's cover decision.

The EU, the [UK](country:GB) and some climate-vulnerable countries objected that the text never mentioned fossil fuels.

Melbourne researchers Jacqueline Peel and Kathryn Bowen say cover texts are add-ons, and COP31 still agreed many other decisions.

Commentators split on blame: oil-rich nations for some, Europe's reluctance on climate finance for others.
