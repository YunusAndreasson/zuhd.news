North Korea's first passenger train to Beijing arrived Friday. North Korean leader Kim Jong Un's September summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping restored ties that had cooled after North Korea began supplying weapons to Russia. The rail link runs 4 times weekly, giving Beijing a new channel to weaken US sanctions enforcement.
