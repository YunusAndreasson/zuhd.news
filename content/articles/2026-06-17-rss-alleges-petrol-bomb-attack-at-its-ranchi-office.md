Ranchi — 2 petrol bombs missed Ranchi's Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh office early Tuesday.

Security cameras caught the attackers at 12:37 am; police cite a local dispute, while [India](country:IN)'s Bharatiya Janata Party alleges a conspiracy.

No arrests made; the party is pressing for a probe in opposition-ruled Jharkhand.
