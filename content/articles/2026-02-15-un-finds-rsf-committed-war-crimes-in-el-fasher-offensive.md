6,000 people died in 3 days. The Rapid Support Forces killed at least 4,400 civilians inside El Fasher and 1,600 along escape routes during their October offensive to capture the last government stronghold in Darfur, according to a United Nations human rights office report that documented mass executions, sexual violence, and an attack on a university dormitory that killed 500. UN High Commissioner Volker Türk called the findings war crimes and possible crimes against humanity, but no accountability mechanism exists.
