Moscow — Putin reported Russia's GDP contracted 1.8%. He disclosed the figure himself at an economic meeting, saying manufacturing, industry, and construction all declined for 2 consecutive months. No recovery plan was announced; Putin called current macroeconomic indicators "below expectations and forecasts."
