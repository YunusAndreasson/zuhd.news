The Internal Revenue Service lost 40% of its IT workforce and 80% of its tech executives in 2025. The Department of Government Efficiency drove the cuts at an agency that processes hundreds of millions of tax returns annually. The Treasury Inspector General warned the 2026 filing season is now at risk.
