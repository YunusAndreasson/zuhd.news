Budapest — Tamas Sulyok signed his own dismissal Saturday.

Parliament Speaker Agnes Forsthoffer succeeds him at midnight Monday; lawmakers must elect a new [Hungary](country:HU) president within 30 days.

Peter Magyar convenes his Tisza party Monday to pick its nominee for the post.
