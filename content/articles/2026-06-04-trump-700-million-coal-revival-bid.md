Washington — Trump directed $700m of defense funds into coal.

[US](country:US) coal held 17% of generation in 2025; the funds flow through the Defense Production Act, written for wartime industrial emergencies.

A 12-million-ton California coal export terminal targeting Asian markets is among projects seeking funding.
