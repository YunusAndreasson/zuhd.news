China is opening airspace below 1 kilometer to commercial drones. Hong Kong received over 100 proposals as part of Beijing's "low-altitude economy" strategy to commercialize that airspace for delivery, inspection, and emergency services. Pilot projects launch in the first half of 2026, Hong Kong's first test of whether commercial drones can move beyond regulatory sandboxes.
