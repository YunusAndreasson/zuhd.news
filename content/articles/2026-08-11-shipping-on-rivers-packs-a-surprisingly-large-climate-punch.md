Beijing — Rivers cover under 1% of shipping's water.

Researchers found they produced nearly a quarter of shipping's 2022 CO2.

International shipping rules bind ocean voyages only, so small, old barge engines on rivers go essentially unregulated.

Researchers say regulators could cut most of the toll by targeting just 20 major waterways.
