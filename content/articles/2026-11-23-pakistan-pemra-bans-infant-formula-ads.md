Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s media regulator ordered formula ads off all regulated channels.

The health ministry demanded enforcement of a 2002 law that already barred formula promotion.

The Pakistan Electronic Media Regulatory Authority's order stays in force until advertisers obtain permission from the concerned health regulator.

The authority referred the matter to its Council of Complaints for recommendations.
