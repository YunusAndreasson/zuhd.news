Abuja — Benue filed 104,409 university entrance applications in [Nigeria](country:NG).

A university application count works as a census of whether schooling happened at all.

Kebbi, Sokoto and Zamfara, with 3 times Benue's population, filed fewer than half as many applications combined.

Nigeria's admissions board has not responded to requests to clarify the disparity in state-of-origin figures.
