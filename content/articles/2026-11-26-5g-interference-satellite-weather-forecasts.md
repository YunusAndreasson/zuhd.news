New Jersey — 5G leaks could alter storm forecasts by 2040.

Forecast models use those satellite readings as their starting conditions.

Satellites read water vapour at 23.8 GHz, beside the 24.25 to 27.5 GHz 5G band, and dense urban transmitters' leakage adds up.

The finding rests on a 2040 scenario and a 12-hour simulation of Hurricane Ida's remnants.

How much it matters depends on transmitter numbers and leakage, which the team varied across [US](country:US) counties.
