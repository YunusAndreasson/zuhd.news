Rubio labelled Afghanistan a state sponsor of wrongful detention. The designation — applied only to North Korea before — unlocks visa restrictions and sanctions against Taliban officials holding 2 named Americans without charges. The Taliban offered dialogue but denied holding Mahmoud Habibi, 38, leaving his family with no legal recourse.
