Abuja — Most of [Nigeria](country:NG)'s gold, lithium and uranium is mined illegally.

Billions of euros in minerals never enter the national GDP.

Two years ago the government launched the Mining Marshals, a paramilitary force, to police informal mines.

Gold sold at $3,782 an ounce on 17 November, and most output still sits outside the official economy.
