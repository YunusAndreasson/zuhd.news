Pretoria — Five men waited five years between indictment and arrest.

The gap illustrates how extradition, not identification, delays transnational cybercrime prosecutions.

[South Africa](country:ZA) held the defendants, members of a Nigerian confraternity accused of romance scams that stole from over 100 victims.

Prosecutors have not said when the men will face a US court.
