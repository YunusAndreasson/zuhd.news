Prague — 9 nations quit the Czech ammunition coalition.

Czech President Petr Pavel said the initiative delivers 50% of [Ukraine](country:UA)'s large-calibre ammunition but shed half its members after Babis took office.

The initiative has no ready replacement; Prague retains the intermediary role while contributing no funds.
