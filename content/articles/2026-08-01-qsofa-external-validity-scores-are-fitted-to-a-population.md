Abha — A sepsis score was built on foreign patients.

Researchers led by Abdullah Algarni tested qSOFA — respiratory rate, mental state, blood pressure — against outcomes in a [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) emergency department.

The manuscript is early-access and unedited; the Saudi Ministry of Health has not commented on the findings.
