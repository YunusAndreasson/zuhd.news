Ayodhya — [India](country:IN)'s Ram temple trustees resigned over donation theft.

Authorities arrested eight people and recovered 8 million rupees after counters were accused of stealing from offerings that reached 5.82 billion rupees.

Opposition parties are demanding the trust's full dissolution ahead of next year's Uttar Pradesh election.
