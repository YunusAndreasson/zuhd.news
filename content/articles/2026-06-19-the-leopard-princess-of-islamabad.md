Islamabad — Wildlife guards have encountered leopards within 10 feet in Islamabad's hills.

A 2019 camera trap confirmed what [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Margallah Hills shepherds had said for decades; 200,000 people live inside the national park.

The park holds 20 villages and livestock losses continue.
