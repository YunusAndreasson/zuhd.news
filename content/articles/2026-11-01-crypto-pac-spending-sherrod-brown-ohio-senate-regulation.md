Columbus — Crypto PACs are spending $63 million to defeat Sherrod Brown.

Brown could shape crypto bills in a Democratic Senate, alongside Elizabeth Warren at Banking.

Fairshake alone is devoting almost $56 million, and the Winklevoss twins' committee added $7.2 million, federal filings show.

"This is a staggering strategic blunder," said Dennis Porter, Satoshi Action Fund CEO.

Ohio votes Tuesday, and Kalshi gives Brown a 68% chance against Senator Jon Husted.
