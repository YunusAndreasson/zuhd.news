Warsaw — Poland stripped Zelensky of its highest state honour.

[Kyiv](country:UA) named a military unit after fighters behind the 1943 Volhynia massacre, which [Poland](country:PL) classifies as genocide of 100,000 Poles.

Prime Minister Tusk must countersign the revocation; he has called for both sides to ease tensions.
