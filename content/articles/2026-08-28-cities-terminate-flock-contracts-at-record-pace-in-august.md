Oakland — Lansing to Pflugerville dropped Flock cameras this month.

90 communities cut ties in August alone — a fourfold jump from July.

Councils bought the license-plate readers by contract, not law, so a renewal vote alone can end them.

Flock says new deals still outpace cancellations 10 to 1 this year.
