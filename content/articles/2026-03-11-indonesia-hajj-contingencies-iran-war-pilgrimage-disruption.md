A Middle East war now threatens the annual Hajj. Indonesia is rerouting its 220,000 pilgrims — the world's largest delegation — as the conflict approaches pilgrimage season; the first departure wave leaves April 22. Hajj has been suspended only twice since 1979; Indonesia has not yet delayed departures.
