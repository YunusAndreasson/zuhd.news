London — 516,490 EVs outsold 504,010 petrol cars in [Britain](country:GB) across the 12 months to May 2026.

It is the first annual period where battery vehicles led, driven by the zero-emissions vehicle mandate.

UK carmakers are pushing to weaken the mandate that drove the shift.
