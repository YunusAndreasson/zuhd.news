Mainz — Sliding raindrops build up to 9,000 volts.

Corrosion models for aircraft and bridge coatings omit this electrical mechanism entirely.

The charge punches through a coating's insulation before the drop even touches the metal, researchers found.

Thicker coatings resisted the damage, but aircraft and optical coatings must stay thin.
