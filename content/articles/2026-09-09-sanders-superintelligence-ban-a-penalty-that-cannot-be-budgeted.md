Washington — A 20-year prison term awaits superintelligence violators.

Violators would also face corporate dissolution, not fines companies can budget for.

Senator Bernie Sanders backs a bill banning superintelligence, though the term has no fixed legal definition.

Representative Greg Casar co-sponsors the bill, which has no stated path to a vote.
