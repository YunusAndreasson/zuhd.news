Damascus — 33 Somali women and children are held in a basement cell.

None of the women has been charged, and officials told them deportation to [Somalia](country:SO) is likely.

Syrian officials took them from Roj camp on 8 November to a cell with no heating, sunlight or clean water.

A Somali embassy representative said they were treated well, while admitting not knowing where they are held.

Syrian officials told the women deportation papers could take a couple of weeks.
