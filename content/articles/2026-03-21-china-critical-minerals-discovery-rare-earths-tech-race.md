Beijing — China confirmed 9.7 million rare earth tonnes in Sichuan. The site also holds "super-large" fluorite deposits used in semiconductor manufacturing and baryte essential for oil and gas drilling. China already controls 60% of global rare earth processing; the find extends that lead as the US and EU race to secure independent mineral supplies.
