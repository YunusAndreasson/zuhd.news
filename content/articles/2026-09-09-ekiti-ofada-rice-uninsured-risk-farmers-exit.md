Orun-Igbemo — Abu Ibrahim's rice rotted under the ground twice.

Neighbours who skipped planting show a system quitting after two failed seasons.

No crop insurance exists here, so a third loss is a bet most smallholders in [Nigeria](country:NG) cannot rationally make.

Ekiti's government says it will scale its farmer insurance scheme to 10,000 people in 2026.
