Israel struck Beirut's waterfront tent camp, killing 6. 700,000 people displaced by Israel's offensive had taken shelter in tents at Ramlet al-Baida beach, with nowhere farther to flee. Lebanon ordered its army to respond as the UN Security Council prepares to vote on a French-led ceasefire resolution.
