Hamburg — Hochbahn will scan passengers' behaviour at a 3rd station.

Almost no reliable figures exist on whether the software works.

Software analyses camera footage continuously and alerts the control room to 5 incident types, including a person on the tracks.

Two of the 5 test targets only checked for crowded or empty platforms.

Scanning begins at Landungsbrücken early next year.
