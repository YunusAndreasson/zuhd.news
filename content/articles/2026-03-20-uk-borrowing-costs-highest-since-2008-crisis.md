UK February borrowing hit £14.3bn — twice what economists forecast. The Iran war's energy shock stoked inflation and pushed yields to 5%, their highest since 2008. Capital Economics said a 2022-scale energy support package is now unaffordable, with household bills forecast to rise £332 in July.
