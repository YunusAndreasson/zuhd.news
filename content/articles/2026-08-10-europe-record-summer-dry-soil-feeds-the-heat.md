Paris — France's Seine river is running dangerously low.

Low water is straining nuclear cooling, freight and irrigation across the region at once.

Dry soil stops absorbing heat as evaporation, so the air heats faster instead, deepening the drought-heat loop.

Copernicus expects a new global temperature record within the year as El Niño strengthens.
