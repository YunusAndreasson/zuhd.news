Whelford — Bomb squads evacuated 85 homes near an Iran-strike base.

[Britain](country:GB)'s decision to host US Iran strikes has now endangered a Gloucestershire village.

Counter-terrorism police arrested five men under the Explosives Act and Terrorism Act after three vans were reported heading toward the base.

[Iran](country:IR)'s Revolutionary Guard Corps threatened in July to attack RAF Fairford and Britain's monarchy.

The five men remain in custody as a 400-metre cordon keeps residents out of Whelford.
