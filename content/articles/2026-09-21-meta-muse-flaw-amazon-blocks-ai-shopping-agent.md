Menlo Park — Muse hit No. 1 in app stores, then Amazon blocked it.

Amazon says agents buying for customers must respect stores' decisions on whether to participate.

Security researcher Patrick Wardle found an undocumented setting that lets local malware redirect Muse's dictation traffic to an attacker's server.

Walmart is partnering with Google and OpenAI to list its products in their agentic shopping interfaces.

McKinsey estimates [US](country:US) retail revenue from AI-mediated purchases could reach $1 trillion by 2030.
