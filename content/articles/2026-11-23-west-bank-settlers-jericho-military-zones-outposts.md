Jericho — [Israel](country:IL)'s army says an Israeli civilian likely shot Fadda Fheidat, 72, dead.

Settlers had moved into 8 Palestinian homes on 4 November, the first such takeover in decades.

Israeli group Yesh Din says military closure orders bind only Palestinian owners, handing land to settlers across the West Bank.

The army says a Palestinian driver rammed a settler's vehicle, which Fheidat's family says was an accident.

No Israeli has been arrested while the army investigates.
