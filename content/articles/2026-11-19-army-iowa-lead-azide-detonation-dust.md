Middletown — The [US](country:US) Army will detonate 5 tons of lead azide Friday.

Lead-bearing dust could reach communities up to 100 miles away.

Crystals formed in storage made the drums too unstable to move, so the Army and the Environmental Protection Agency chose open detonation.

The Army advises hosing dust off, but Peter Thorne of the University of Iowa says that moves lead into soil and water.

Environmental groups asked a court to stay the Environmental Protection Agency's permit by Friday morning.
