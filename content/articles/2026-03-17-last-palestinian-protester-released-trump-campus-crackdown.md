Leqaa Kordia, 33, left Texas detention Monday. The West Bank Palestinian had lived in New Jersey since 2016 before US President Donald Trump's administration detained her over pro-Palestinian campus protest activity. She was released on a $100,000 bond, but her immigration case continues with no hearing date set.
