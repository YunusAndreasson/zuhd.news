Singapore — Grab paid $600M for Foodpanda's Taiwan business Monday. The deal marks Grab's first expansion beyond Southeast Asia into a market generating €1.5B in annual deliveries that is already profitable. Delivery Hero, Foodpanda's parent company, will repay debt with the proceeds once Taiwan's regulatory approval clears, expected by late 2026.
