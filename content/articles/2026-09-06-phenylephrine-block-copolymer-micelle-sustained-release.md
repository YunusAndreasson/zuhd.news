London — Encapsulation extended phenylephrine's release to 72 hours.

Reformulating an existing drug is cheaper than inventing a new one for wider access.

The polymer chains fold into spheres with an oily core that traps the drug and releases it slowly.

The formulation has only been tested in a dish, not in animals or patients yet.
