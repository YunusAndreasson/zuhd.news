Hebron — 2 soldiers and 2 gunmen died in a 20-minute gunfight.

[Israel](country:IL) ordered the army to prepare a full closure of the West Bank within hours.

The army named Staff Sgt. Ron Roytman, 23, and Sgt. Yuval Svilem, 20, and said both gunmen were 27 and from Hebron.

The army has not named the gunmen, and no Palestinian account of the shooting is among the sources.

Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said a large-scale West Bank operation will begin soon.
