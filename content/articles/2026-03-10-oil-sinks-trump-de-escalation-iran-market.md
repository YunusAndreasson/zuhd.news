Oil fell 7% on Trump's "very complete" prediction. Brent crude fell from $119 to $92 in a single session as Iran's Revolutionary Guards simultaneously warned they would block all regional oil exports if strikes continued. The G7 declined to tap emergency reserves as Iran's Supreme Leader directly contradicted Trump's prediction.
