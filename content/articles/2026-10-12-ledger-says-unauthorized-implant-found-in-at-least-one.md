Manila — CryptoBilis sold every confirmed device in the 9 October drain, Ledger says.

Ledger told buyers not to initialise unused devices and to move funds to a new seed.

Ledger confirmed on 10 October that a device sold by CryptoBilis contained an unauthorised hardware implant.

Ledger says the volume looks limited and its internal systems and direct sales channels are unaffected.

CryptoBilis, which operates in [Indonesia](country:ID), [Malaysia](country:MY) and the [Philippines](country:PH), has suspended all hardware wallet sales until the investigation concludes.
