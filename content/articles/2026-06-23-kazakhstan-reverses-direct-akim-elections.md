Astana — [Kazakhstan](country:KZ) reversed direct elections of district governors.

The change returns appointment power to the center — local akims control patronage and resource distribution in one of Central Asia's largest energy states.

Astana has set no timeline to restore the reform, once cited as evidence of post-Nazarbayev progress.
