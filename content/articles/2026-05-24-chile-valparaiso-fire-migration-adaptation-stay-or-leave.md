Quilpué — 138 died in [Chile](country:CL)'s 2024 fires, government figures show.

21,000 were displaced in the Quilpué hills; the fires are now annual, splitting communities into those who leave and those who rebuild.

Which households retreat and which fortify depends on who can afford to leave.
