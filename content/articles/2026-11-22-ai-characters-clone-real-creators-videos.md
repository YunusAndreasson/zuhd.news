United Kingdom — AI accounts replaced guitarist Sophie Lloyd in her own videos.

Some copies draw millions more views than the originals, and some accounts sell adult content.

Cheap AI tools swap a synthetic character into a viral video, keeping its music, movements, facial expressions and background.

"It takes years to master an instrument and seconds to take and rip it off as something else," Lloyd said.

Instagram's head Adam Mosseri says it is working on catching copies that swap visuals under copied scripts.
