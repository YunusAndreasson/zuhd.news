Saint Petersburg — Putin says [Russia](country:RU) is not preparing for hostilities with Europe.

[Germany](country:DE) accused Moscow this month of planning an August drone attack on Leipzig airport.

European officials warn of sabotage, cyberattacks and covert operations as the war in [Ukraine](country:UA) enters its fifth year.

Poland warns Russia could fire drones and missiles at countries backing Ukraine.

Putin says negotiation proposals remain possible but Moscow will first weigh its response to Ukrainian attacks.
