Qinhuangdao — Imma-B conducts over a million times better than typical boron.

Every previously known boron form is superhard, and most conduct poorly.

Researchers at Yanshan University in [China](country:CN) compressed boron with sodium, then heated it at 400 °C under vacuum to remove the sodium.

Materials chemist Artem Oganov says the method opens a synthesis route to a whole family of boron materials.
