Gaza — Rain flooded 70,000 tents, Russian state agency TASS reported.

Gaza authorities report severe shortages of winterised tents, waterproof flooring, clothing and heaters.

Flooding and soil erosion collapsed temporary shelters and caused casualties, while civil defence teams lack rescue equipment to evacuate families.

A Gaza authorities spokesperson said dozens of displaced people in flooded areas still need emergency assistance.
