Beijing — Xiaomi's phone shipments fell 29% to 30.7 million.

Net profit fell 68% to 3.88 billion yuan as phone margins shrank to 9.2%.

Memory costs keep rising, president Lu Weibing said, and Xiaomi will not simply raise prices to pass them on.

Average selling price rose 39.8% to a record 1,486 yuan as Xiaomi pushed premium models.

AI took over 30% of nine-month R&D, and Xiaomi has yet to turn that into scaled revenue.
