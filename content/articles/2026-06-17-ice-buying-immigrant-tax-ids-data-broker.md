Washington — Court-blocked tax data reached Immigration and Customs Enforcement via a broker.

A [US](country:US) court blocked the Internal Revenue Service from sharing data with the agency; its $9.97 million broker contract sidesteps that order.

The contract's exact data scope is not public; no legal challenge to the broker route has been filed.
