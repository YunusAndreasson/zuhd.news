Beijing — AI made 2,000 engineers 20-40% faster, the company no faster.

Deloitte China's September survey found 48.5% of firms report AI returns below expectations.

Requirements, review, testing and release queue for approval, so faster individual work stalls at process gates, a Deloitte China partner says.

44.4% of surveyed firms cannot calculate AI projects' return, the same survey found.

Consultant Wang Cheng tells firms to put 70% of AI budgets into organisation and process.
