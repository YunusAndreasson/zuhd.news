Cairo — Two army hospitals join a new medical tourism company.

Both treat civilians in [Egypt](country:EG) for a fee or under Universal Health Insurance contracts.

Retired Major General Khaled Oweida chairs the company, which starts with the International Medical Center and Air Force Specialized Hospital.

The company plans to add more hospitals gradually.
