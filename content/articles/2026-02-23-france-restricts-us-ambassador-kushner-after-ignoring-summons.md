France barred US Ambassador Jared Kushner from meeting ministers. He ignored a French Foreign Ministry summons over US claims that a far-right activist's death in Lyon was left-wing political violence, which Paris called exploitation. The restriction leaves him with only 'certain diplomatic duties' and no timeline for restored access.
