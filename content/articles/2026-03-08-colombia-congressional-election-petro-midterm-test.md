Sunday's vote could end Colombia's peace accords. President Gustavo Petro cannot advance land redistribution or peace negotiations without a congressional majority — programs his left-wing coalition has championed since his 2022 election. Results are expected Sunday night; a right-wing Senate majority would give opponents the power to block both.
