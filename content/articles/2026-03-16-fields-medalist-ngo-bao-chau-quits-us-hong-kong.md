Ngô Bảo Châu quit Chicago over US academic decline. The Vietnamese mathematician — Vietnam's first Fields Medalist, in 2010 — spent 15 years at the University of Chicago. He joins the University of Hong Kong in June, where he plans to establish a mathematics research center.
