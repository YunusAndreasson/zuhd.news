New York City — Jonathan Saunders takes over Kate Spade's design.

Tapestry named the Scottish designer, previously at Calvin Klein and Diane von Furstenberg, as creative director, aiming to repeat the turnaround it engineered at Coach.

Saunders now has to reverse years of underperformance at the label under Tapestry's ownership.
