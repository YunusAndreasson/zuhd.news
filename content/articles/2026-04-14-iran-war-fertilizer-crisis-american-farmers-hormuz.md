Washington — Urea jumped $210/ton since Hormuz closed. Nearly half of global urea exports route through the strait; domestic US prices track international markets regardless of where fertilizer is made. A quarter of US farmers haven't ordered yet; even if shipping resumes, fields see fertilizer in 7 weeks.
