New York — Mamdani condemned Hamas, then called [Israel](country:IL)'s Gaza war genocide.

Several Democrats called it victim-blaming, while pro-Palestinian protesters outside City Hall said it fell short.

His statement, not a policy change, counts more than 1,100 Israelis killed on 7 October and 74,000 Palestinians in Gaza.

Representative Dan Goldman said "there's no excuse for victim blaming."

Some protesters who voted for Mamdani say they will not back his re-election.
