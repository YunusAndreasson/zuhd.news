Hong Kong — A chemistry team turned discarded pomegranate peel into a nanoscale carbon water filter.

The technique targets 4-nitrophenol, a common industrial toxin, using feedstock most available where contamination runs highest.

The method remains at lab scale; no industrial deployment partner has been named.
