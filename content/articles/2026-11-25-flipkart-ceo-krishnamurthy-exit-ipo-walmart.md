Bengaluru — Flipkart chief executive Kalyan Krishnamurthy leaves 4 December, 9 days after the announcement.

Walmart reportedly wants operating break-even before listing [India](country:IN)'s biggest online retailer.

Flipkart lost Rs 8,468 crore last fiscal year on operating revenue of Rs 1.46 lakh crore.

Neither company's announcement says why Krishnamurthy is leaving or whether he chose to.

Flipkart names a successor in the coming weeks, with Balaji Thiagarajan in charge until then.
