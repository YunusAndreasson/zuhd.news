Tehran — Iran submitted ceasefire terms the US dismissed.

Both sides sent competing frameworks via Pakistan; Tehran called the US insistence on its own terms "disingenuous" and suspects the process masks military escalation.

The US abandoned Oman-mediated talks Feb 28; Iran is still weighing whether to answer Washington's latest offer.
