Minna — The agency's own register counted 192 children harmed.

The count is a floor, most rural families never reach the agency.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s Child Rights Act binds only states that adopt it, and agencies that record cases rarely prosecute them.

Director-General Ummul Mohammed says the agency will keep pursuing prosecutions already before the courts.
