Taylor — A family's donated park became a data center.

A farming family deeded 87 acres to [US](country:US) city Taylor for $10 in 1999; the city sold the land to developer Blueprint for $10 million in 2025.

The 135,000-square-foot facility will sit 500 feet from the donor family's home.
