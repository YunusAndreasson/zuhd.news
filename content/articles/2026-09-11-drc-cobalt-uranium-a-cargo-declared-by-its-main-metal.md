Lubumbashi — [Congo](country:CD)'s cobalt carries undeclared uranium, study finds.

Mining communities absorb radiation risk while refiners, mainly in [China](country:CN), receive the metal.

Export paperwork tracks the mineral being sold, not the uranium riding inside the same cargo.

No agency has said whether it will screen cobalt cargoes for uranium content.
