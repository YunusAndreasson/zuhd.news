Belgrade — The Student List holds 140 of 250 parliamentary seats.

President Aleksandar Vucic's Progressive Party falls to 104 seats in [Serbia](country:RS)'s new parliament.

A grassroots student movement turned 2.3 million votes into a majority, and lawmakers now elect a speaker.

Parliament has 90 days to elect a government or face snap elections.
