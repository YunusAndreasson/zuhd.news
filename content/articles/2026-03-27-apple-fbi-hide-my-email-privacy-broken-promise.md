Cupertino — Apple unmasked a user to the FBI. Court records show the iCloud+ feature, which promises anonymous email aliases, handed over a user's real identity when the FBI requested it during a criminal investigation. The feature protects against data brokers, not subpoenas; users treating it as law enforcement anonymity were mistaken.
