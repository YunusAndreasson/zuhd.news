Preah Vihear — UNESCO documented damage from Thai strikes last week.

[Thailand](country:TH) says [Cambodia](country:KH) stationed troops first at the protected site.

The 1954 Hague Convention strips protection once a site is used militarily, making the accusation a legal defense.

No court enforces the convention, leaving both governments' claims unverified.
