Guasave — Gunfire emptied a ballpark as soldiers reportedly seized Chapo Isidro.

Burning trucks blocked highways into a city of roughly 300,000 as residents sheltered indoors.

The International Crisis Group lists his Meza-Flores group among Sinaloa's four main factions, one that expanded while rivals fought each other.

Mexican authorities have not officially confirmed the arrest.

Mexican media report the fugitive, wanted in the [US](country:US) since 2012, goes to prosecutors in Mexico City.
