Gaza — 18,500 critical patients need medical evacuation, Health Cluster figures show. The WHO counted 100 daily pre-war evacuations; [Israel](country:IL)'s queue cleared just 85 in April's first 2 weeks. The Rafah crossing partly reopened in February; Israel has set no evacuation timeline for 4,000 critical children.
