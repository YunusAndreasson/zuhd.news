Beijing — A survey followed 2,019 students for seven months.

It is rare evidence ordering cause before effect in this literature.

Psychological maltreatment leaves no physical evidence, so it is the child harm safeguarding systems in [China](country:CN) and elsewhere rarely register.

The design is still observational, so it cannot prove maltreatment causes later aggression.
