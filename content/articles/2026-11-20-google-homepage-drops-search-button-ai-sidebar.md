United States — Google removed its Search button and added 3 AI buttons.

A sidebar now lists logged-in users' recent AI Mode threads on the homepage and results page.

Autocomplete suggestions covered the button while users typed, so most pressed Enter instead.

Google search VP Robby Stein says the sidebar made a "big difference" in how people progress on searches.

Google has not said whether it will bring the button back.
