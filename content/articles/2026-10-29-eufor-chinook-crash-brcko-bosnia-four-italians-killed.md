Brčko — An Italian Chinook crashed in [Bosnia](country:BA), killing 4 and injuring 11.

[Italy](country:IT)'s defence ministry said the dead were two soldiers and two Red Cross volunteers.

The European Union force has kept the peace there since 2004 with 1,000 to 1,500 personnel.

The force is investigating, and 3 of the 11 injured are moving to a Tuzla hospital.
