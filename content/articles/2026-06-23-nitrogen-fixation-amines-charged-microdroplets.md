London — Droplets fix nitrogen without industrial heat or pressure.

Haber-Bosch demands 400°C and 200 atmospheres to fix nitrogen; microdroplet electrospray achieves the same at room temperature without catalysts, the paper reports.

The study used laboratory-scale electrospray; industrial-volume scaling is not yet demonstrated.
