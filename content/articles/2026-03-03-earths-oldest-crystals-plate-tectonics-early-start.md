Ancient zircons push plate tectonics 400 million years earlier. The 4.4-billion-year-old Jack Hills crystals show chemical signatures that only form under tectonic activity — subduction that recycles ocean chemistry and stabilizes climate. Geologists will now test other Hadean zircon samples to confirm whether subduction preceded life.
