Bangkok — [Thailand](country:TH)'s auto sector was already weakening.

Thailand subsidized EV assembly but built no local components — becoming a host to foreign supply chains rather than a manufacturer within them.

Without local component makers, Thailand's "Detroit of Southeast Asia" status depends entirely on foreign automakers' decisions.
