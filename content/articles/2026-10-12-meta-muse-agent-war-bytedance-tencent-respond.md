United States — Meta's Muse agent topped the US App Store within two weeks.

ByteDance, Tencent and Alibaba have each moved on personal agents since Muse launched on 8 September.

Muse takes a goal and works on it around the clock in a cloud virtual machine, sending emails and booking travel.

Amazon blocked Muse's purchasing function soon after launch, citing unauthorised persistent access by AI agents.

ByteDance is reportedly folding its agent project Spell into Doubao and aims to launch it soon.
