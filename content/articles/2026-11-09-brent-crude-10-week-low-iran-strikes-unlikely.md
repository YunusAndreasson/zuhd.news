Iran — Brent crude fell as much as 5.6% to $92.80, a 10-week low.

President Trump said Sunday renewed strikes on [Iran](country:IR) are highly unlikely, shifting traders' expectations.

Dated Brent, the physical price for immediate delivery, peaked at $136.39 on 5 October and traded at $92.25 Monday.

A tanker in the Strait of Hormuz was hit Saturday by an unknown projectile, UK Maritime Trade Operations said.

Polymarket traders give a [US](country:US)-Iran Hormuz agreement by 31 December a 40% chance, up 7 points in a week.
