Mac Minis carry 500-yuan mark-ups across China. Chinese developers are hoarding Apple consumer hardware to run OpenClaw, an open-source AI agent that requires Apple Silicon to operate locally — a workaround that US export controls, designed to block China from advanced AI chips, never anticipated. The US has not moved to restrict consumer hardware exports, leaving a gap in its AI containment strategy.
