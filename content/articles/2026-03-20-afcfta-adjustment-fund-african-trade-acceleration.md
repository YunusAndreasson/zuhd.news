Africa's trade transition fund debuted before Nigerian institutional investors. The fund cushions African governments from tariff revenue losses as the African Continental Free Trade Area single market takes effect — a shield against the short-term pain of trade liberalisation. Nigeria's Trade Minister chaired the Lagos event, with continent-wide investor outreach still underway and no implementation date confirmed.
