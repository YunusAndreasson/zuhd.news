Bern — [Switzerland](country:CH) rejected a population cap proposal 55% to 45% Sunday.

The Swiss People's Party bid would have triggered termination of the EU free-movement accord if population exceeded 10 million before 2050.

The result sustains Switzerland's EU labour deal, though 45% support shows anti-immigration pressure remains.
