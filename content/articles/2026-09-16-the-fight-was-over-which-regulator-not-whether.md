Washington — Bitcoin fell 5% as the crypto bill collapsed.

The bill's failure leaves financial regulators, not [US](country:US) Congress, writing crypto's rules.

Trump earned $1.4 billion from crypto ventures last year, prompting the ethics dispute that sank the bill.

Sponsor Cynthia Lummis said the bill "is over" for this Senate session.
