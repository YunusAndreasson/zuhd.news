The "extermination" of Palestinians must end, the chairman of the African Union Commission said. Mahmoud Ali Youssouf made the statement at the opening of the 39th African Union Summit in Addis Ababa on Saturday, as continental leaders gathered to address conflicts and governance across Africa. The African Union has previously called for a ceasefire in Gaza, but the use of the word "extermination" by the commission's chairman represents a sharpened tone from the 55-member continental body.
