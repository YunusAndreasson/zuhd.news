Georgia charged Alexia Moore with murder for taking misoprostol. Georgia's 2022 abortion ban gives fetuses personhood under criminal law, enabling murder charges against women for their own pregnancies. Camden County prosecutors will decide in coming weeks whether to pursue a murder trial — a conviction carries a potential life sentence.
