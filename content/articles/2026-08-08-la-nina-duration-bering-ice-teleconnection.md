Nome — Multi-year La Niñas cut ice loss 11%.

Persistent events reroute Pacific winds northerly over the Bering shelf, cooling water, while single-year events push warm southerly air in.

Multi-year La Niñas are becoming more frequent, researchers say — a trend that could buffer future ice loss.
