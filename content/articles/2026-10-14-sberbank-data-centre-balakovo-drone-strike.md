Balakovo — A drone hit Sberbank's data centre.

Systems at [Russia](country:RU)'s largest bank kept running, Turkish state broadcaster TRT World reported.

The strike follows at least 3 drone attacks on Yandex data centres reported since the start of October.

"We confirm the attack on the data centre," said Alexander Vedyakhin, Sberbank's deputy chairman.
