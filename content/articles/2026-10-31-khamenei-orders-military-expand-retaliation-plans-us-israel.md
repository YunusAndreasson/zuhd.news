Tehran — Khamenei ordered commanders to answer any attack without geographic limits.

His statement also orders an updated target list covering all American and Israeli assets.

Strikes by the [US](country:US) and [Israel](country:IL) in February killed his father, and a June deal on Hormuz navigation has stalled.

The statements cite no specific new threat, and Khamenei has not appeared in public since taking office.

A delegation from [Qatar](country:QA) reached Tehran Friday, and Iranian media say [Iran](country:IR) has not answered US Vice President JD Vance's letter.
