Dubai — Emirati prosecutors say a flydubai co-pilot was radicalised in Melbourne.

Australian agencies are investigating the 29-year-old's two years as a student there.

Attorney General Hamad Saif al-Shamsi says Hammam al-Hammami read extremist literature in 2017 and told prosecutors the 9/11 attacks inspired him.

Al-Shamsi says flydubai's screening showed no sign of extremist intent when it hired him in January.

Australian and [New Zealand](country:NZ) investigators are examining his training abroad while he sits in Emirati custody.
