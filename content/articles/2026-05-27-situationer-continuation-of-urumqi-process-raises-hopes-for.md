Beijing — [China](country:CN) plans a second [Pakistan](country:PK)-[Afghanistan](country:AF) mediation meeting after April's Urumqi talks.

China hosted Islamabad-Kabul talks in Urumqi in April; the Taliban accepted TTP discussion but refused to call them terrorists.

Pakistan wants a written TTP commitment; Afghan authorities reportedly moved border militants from frontier regions ahead of talks.
