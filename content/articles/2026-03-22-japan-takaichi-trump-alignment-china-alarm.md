Tokyo — Article 9 rewrite would let Japan deploy troops abroad. Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi is leading the reinterpretation as Japan deepens its military alignment with US President Donald Trump, a shift Beijing says is aimed at encircling China. China's foreign ministry warned the move would permanently embed Japan in a US-led coalition and called on Tokyo to reverse it.
