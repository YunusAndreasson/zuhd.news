Washington — Regulators cleared mirrors designed to light up the night sky.

The FCC approved Reflect Orbital's first Eärendil launch, a constellation meant to beam reflected sunlight to solar farms after dark, over astronomers' objections.

The night sky has no legal owner to defend it as more reflective satellites reach orbit.
