Brasília — [Brazil](country:BR) votes with 1 point between Lula and Bolsonaro.

It is the last big test of Latin America's rightward swing, in a country holding 33% of the region's economy.

Bolsonaro led round one 47.03% to 45.16%, but Datafolha's 23 October poll shows 47% to 46%, inside its 2-point margin of error.

Flávio Bolsonaro has pledged to move Brazil's embassy in [Israel](country:IL) from Tel Aviv to Jerusalem, while Lula condemns the war in Gaza.
