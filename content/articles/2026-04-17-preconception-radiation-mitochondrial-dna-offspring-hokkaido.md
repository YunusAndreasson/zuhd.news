Sapporo — Radiation harms children not yet conceived at exposure. Hokkaido researchers found organ-specific mitochondrial DNA changes in offspring of irradiated parents — a heritable effect invisible to current exposure-safety frameworks. Occupational and medical-imaging radiation limits, set without heritable effects in mind, are now under implicit challenge.
