Sirik — Villages in southern [Iran](country:IR) lost drinking water overnight.

US missiles destroyed 2 water tanks in Sirik county, the Hormozgan water company said, cutting supply to Bemani district.

Emergency teams are arranging alternative supply; no restoration timeline has been given.
