Israel — 67 lawmakers recommended Eisenkot, 46 recommended Netanyahu.

Gadi Eisenkot's letter warns the caretaker premier against ordering troops without an immediate threat.

President Isaac Herzog tasked Eisenkot on Sunday, but Netanyahu stays caretaker until a coalition wins the Knesset's confidence.

Netanyahu's office said his decisions are made solely for security reasons, in full coordination with the security establishment.

Eisenkot has 28 days to form a coalition, and his camp holds 48 of 120 seats.
