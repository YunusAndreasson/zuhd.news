Nepal's Rastriya Swatantra Party leads 101 of 165 constituencies. Founded 3 years ago from youth protests against corruption, the party dismantled patronage networks that 2 dominant parties had held for 3 decades. Counting ends this week with Kathmandu mayor Balen Shah, 30, on track to become prime minister.
