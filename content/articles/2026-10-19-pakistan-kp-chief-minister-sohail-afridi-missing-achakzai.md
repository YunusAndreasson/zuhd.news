Islamabad — Sohail Afridi has not been seen since 11 October.

He is Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's chief minister, sidelined since governor's rule and an emergency took effect.

A federal minister said a day later that Afridi and some cabinet members are in preventive custody.

Opposition Leader Mehmood Khan Achakzai told the National Assembly: "At least we and their families should know where they are."

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s National Assembly sits again Tuesday after an opposition motion seeking production orders for 6 MNAs.
