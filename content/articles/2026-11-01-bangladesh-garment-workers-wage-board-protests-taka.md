Dhaka — Garment workers demand 30,000 taka a month, up from 12,500.

About 30 factories on Dhaka's outskirts have declared holidays since protests began Saturday.

[Bangladesh](country:BD) formed a six-member board of government, worker and owner representatives on Sunday to set a new minimum wage.

Police fired tear gas and sound grenades at thousands of workers still protesting around the capital.

Labour Minister Ariful Haque Chowdhury warned prolonged closures could delay export shipments and buyer payments.
