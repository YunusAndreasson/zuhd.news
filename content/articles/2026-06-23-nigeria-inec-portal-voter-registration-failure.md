Abuja — [Nigeria](country:NG)'s voter portal has shown no appointment slots across most of the capital for weeks.

The Independent National Electoral Commission runs the only gateway to biometric registration; its failure narrows the electorate before July 10.

The commission spokesperson did not respond; it told residents to register in person.
