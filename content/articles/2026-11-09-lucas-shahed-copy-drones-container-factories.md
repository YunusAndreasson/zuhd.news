California — A $9 million award targets one-way attack drones built near front lines.

It shortens supply chains that could be severely degraded in a major conflict.

Two robotic arms cut metal LUCAS airframes to payload and range specifications set in software from nTop.

The 18-month contract only tests the system, and a decision on producing at scale comes later.

Machina will first set up 2 factories in the continental [US](country:US), with forward sites like Kuwait the goal.
