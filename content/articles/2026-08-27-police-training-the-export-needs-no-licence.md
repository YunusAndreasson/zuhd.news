London — 13 training sessions ran since 2023, records show.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA) and [Egypt](country:EG) both sit on the Foreign Office's own human rights priority list.

Unlike arms exports, police training needs no licence and produces no published assessment of who is trained.

A Foreign Office review of assistance rules, opened in 2024, is still ongoing.
