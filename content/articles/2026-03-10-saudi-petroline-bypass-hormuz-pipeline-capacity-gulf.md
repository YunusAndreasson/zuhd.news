Saudi Aramco activated its Red Sea bypass pipeline. Iraq, Kuwait, and Bahrain have stopped exports entirely — no bypass exists — and Qatar declared force majeure on its 20% of global LNG. Aramco CEO Amin Nasser said the pipeline will reach its 4.5-million-barrel daily limit within days.
