Boca Chica — A launch-tower hydraulic pin held Starship V3 40 seconds from liftoff.

The pin failed to retract from an umbilical arm; V3 underpins the US space agency's Artemis lunar contract and next-generation Starlink deployment.

Next attempt opens Friday at 22:30 UTC if engineers clear the fault tonight, Musk said.
