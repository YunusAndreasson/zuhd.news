Toronto — A breach exposed 1.5 million Crime Stoppers tips.

About half contained personal information, and reporters reached more than a dozen tipsters promised anonymity.

Vendor Navigate360 held the tips through its subsidiary P3 Global Intel, and a hacktivist group claimed the March breach.

Navigate360 told the chapter in April its data had not been taken, then in June that it had.

Navigate360 found 14,973 names in 176,859 Toronto tips and has not supplied their text, the chapter said.
