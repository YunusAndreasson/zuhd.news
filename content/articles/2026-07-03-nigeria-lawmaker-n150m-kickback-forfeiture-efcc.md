Abuja — A civil order succeeded where a criminal case failed.

A federal court forfeited N150 million to [Nigeria](country:NG) after the anti-graft agency traced it to kickbacks lawmaker Nicholas Mutu took while chairing an oversight committee.

A judge acquitted Mutu of related money-laundering charges in April; the agency is appealing.
