China's Moonshot AI quadrupled its valuation in 3 months. The frenzy follows OpenClaw, a Chinese open-source AI agent that prompted Alibaba, Tencent, and others to double bets on challengers to OpenAI. The $1 billion raise is not yet closed, with Chinese rivals MiniMax and Zhipu already valued at $30-40 billion.
