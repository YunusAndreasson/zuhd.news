South Sudan expelled all UN agencies from Akobo. South Sudan's army then advanced troops into the town — a tactic used in Sudan, Myanmar, and Ethiopia to prevent documentation of civilian casualties before military operations. Thousands are fleeing toward Ethiopia with no international agency remaining inside to document what happens next.
