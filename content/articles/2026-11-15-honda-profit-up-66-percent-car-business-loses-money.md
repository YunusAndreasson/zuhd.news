Tokyo — Honda's profit rose 66% while its cars still lost money.

In [China](country:CN), Honda sold 39.5% fewer cars in 9 months, MarkLines data show.

Motorcycles earned 439 billion yen, and financial services 211 billion, together more than the group's whole operating profit.

Excluding 484.8 billion yen of electric-vehicle charges, the car unit's adjusted operating profit was 479 billion yen.

Honda cut its fiscal-year car sales target from 3.39 million to 3.16 million.
