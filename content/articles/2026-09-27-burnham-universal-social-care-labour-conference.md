Liverpool — Burnham pledged a free, National Health Service-style care system.

It targets a system where some Britons must sell homes and savings to pay for care.

The [UK](country:GB) government also revived Help to Buy: 2.5% deposits and 20% state equity loans on new homes under £600,000.

"How have we let this go on so long?" Burnham asked, weeks after his father died in care.

Burnham won't say how care will be funded until Labour's 2029 manifesto, ahead of the October 28 budget.
