Vatican City — Pope Leo XIV called AI concentration an injustice.

"Magnifica Humanitas" echoes Leo XIII's 1891 labor encyclical, demanding AI gains be distributed from the outset, not eventually.

Leo presented the document alongside Anthropic co-founder Chris Olah — the first such Vatican-tech pairing at an encyclical launch.
