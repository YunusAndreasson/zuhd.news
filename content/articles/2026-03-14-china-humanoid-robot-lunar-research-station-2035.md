China's 2035 lunar base gets a wheeled humanoid robot. The robot pairs a human-like torso for fine manipulation with metal mesh wheels, the same drive design used in China's Yutu lunar rovers. Unlike Artemis, China plans to build its moon station with robots before any crew arrives.
