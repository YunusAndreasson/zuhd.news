Maria Kolesnikova went to prison rather than leave Belarus. Freed in December 2025 under a US-brokered deal, the opposition leader collected her deferred 2022 Charlemagne Prize in Aachen this week. She now calls for EU leaders to negotiate directly with Belarusian President Alexander Lukashenko, saying Europe holds economic leverage over Minsk that has not been used.
