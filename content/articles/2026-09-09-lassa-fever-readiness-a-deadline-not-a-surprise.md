Abuja — [Nigeria](country:NG)'s disease agency published its own readiness gaps.

Health workers keep getting infected because early Lassa symptoms mimic malaria.

Lassa fever spreads from rats and peaks each dry season between November and April, making outbreaks predictable.

The agency's Accelerated Readiness Plan must reach treatment centers before cases rise in November.
