Gaborone — Southern Africa holds most global cobalt, platinum and lithium. Illicit mining networks have outgrown regional law enforcement as global battery demand surges, prompting the 16-member Southern African Development Community to launch a criminal prosecution framework. Member governments must ratify the framework before it can be enforced, a level of cross-border coordination the bloc has never attempted at this scale.
