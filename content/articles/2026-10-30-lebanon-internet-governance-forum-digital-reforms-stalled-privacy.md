Beirut — Lebanon's data protection law remains unenforced as AI rules loom.

Citizens still need intermediaries for simple procedures, and new government platforms keep suffering security breaches.

[Lebanon](country:LB)'s Internet Governance Forum met 6 and 7 October, and officials promised digital transformation and AI governance.

SMEX's Abed Kataya demands mandatory public consultation before any digital law or policy is adopted.
