Tegucigalpa — [Honduras](country:HN) prosecuted 19 mayors in 10 years across 13 departments.

Audits of 170 town halls found 468.7 million lempiras in losses since 2015.

Of 18 mayors in the database, 6 were convicted, 9 had cases dismissed, 2 await trial and 1 is a fugitive.

Pardoned ex-president Juan Orlando Hernández and ex-mayor Alexander Ardón have returned, reactivating local territorial control, the investigation found.
