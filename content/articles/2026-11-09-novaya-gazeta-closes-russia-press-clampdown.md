Moscow — Russia's last major independent newspaper closed its office Monday.

Critical reporting on the war now has no major home inside [Russia](country:RU).

Authorities declared it a foreign agent, seized its printing house, revoked its registration and shut its reader donation system.

Cofounder Dmitry Muratov said peace advocates "have been denied the right to read what they choose."

Editor-in-chief Sergei Sokolov says the paper will return one day.
