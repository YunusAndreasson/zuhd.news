Vilnius — An Italian jet shot down a drone likely carrying explosives.

It is the first drone downed in [Lithuania](country:LT)'s airspace, testing NATO's threshold for a kinetic response.

The drone entered from Belarus and flew 30 minutes over populated areas before interception near Kaunas.

Lithuania's foreign ministry will summon Belarus's ambassador over the incursion.
