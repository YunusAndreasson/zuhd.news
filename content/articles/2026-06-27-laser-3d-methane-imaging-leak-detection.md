Hefei — Laser imaging reveals methane gas cloud sources precisely.

[China](country:CN)'s Academy of Sciences system finds microleaks, locates sources, and measures emission rates in 3D, researchers said.

Scientists rank methane second only to CO₂ in warming impact; no standardized leak-measurement tools have previously existed for enforcement.
