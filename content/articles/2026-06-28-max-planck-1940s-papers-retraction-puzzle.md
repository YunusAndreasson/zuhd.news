Berlin — Springer's editor-in-chief called retraction of 2 Max Planck physics papers "a mistake."

Historians suspect the publisher's algorithm flagged them as duplicates, misapplying modern self-plagiarism rules to pre-digital norms where wide republication was standard.

Springer declined to explain and both papers remain removed.
