Minna — A panel proposes N1.4 billion over 33 custody deaths.

It also clears 18 suspended officers of [Nigeria](country:NG)'s civil defence corps and wants them reinstated.

The committee proposed sanctions for 4 other officers over gross management incompetence, and tamper-proof cloud-backed cameras in cell blocks.

It wants 4 officers who testified protected as key prosecution witnesses.

It also asked Interior Minister Olubunmi Tunji-Ojo for a formal apology to families and survivors.
