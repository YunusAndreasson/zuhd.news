Moscow — A Ukrainian drone struck the Gazpromneft refinery, starting a fire.

[Ukraine](country:UA) said its strikes on [Russia](country:RU)'s refineries doubled in 2026, forcing partial shutdowns; Kyiv calls the campaign "sanctions."

Mayor Sobyanin confirmed no casualties; [Ukraine](country:UA) said the plant supplies 40% of Moscow's gasoline.
