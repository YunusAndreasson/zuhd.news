Islamabad — Undocumented Afghan families face arrest and deportation to Taliban rule starting July 10.

Pakistan's Interior Ministry order covers all provinces; many families have lived in [Pakistan](country:PK) for decades and now face Taliban-ruled [Afghanistan](country:AF).

Provinces must file daily arrest tallies with Islamabad beginning July 11.
