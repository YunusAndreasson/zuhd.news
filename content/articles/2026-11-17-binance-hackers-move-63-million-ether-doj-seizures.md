United States — Binance's thieves moved $63 million in ether Tuesday.

The Federal Bureau of Investigation blames [North Korea](country:KP)'s TraderTraitor group for crypto's largest theft, $6.3 billion.

Bitcoin and ether cannot be frozen by an issuer, so Elliptic counts $3.28 billion still with the attackers.

The three new wallets are not linked to an exchange, and the transfers alone do not show a sale.

No ether had moved again from them when MistTrack and Solid Intel reported Tuesday.
