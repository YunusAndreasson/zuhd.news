Cambridge — AI advice raised savings for people over 30.

MIT Sloan researchers tested GPT-5.2, GPT-5.6, and Gemini 3 Flash on 1,000 adults' own prompts, then again with detailed academic-style prompts.

Models still under-rebalanced portfolios and struggled with shocks like unemployment, the study found.
