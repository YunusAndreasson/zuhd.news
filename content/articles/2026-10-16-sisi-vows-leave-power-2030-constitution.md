Cairo — Sources disagree whether Sisi's 2030 pledge is sincere.

He said [Egypt](country:EG)'s constitution stays untouched while warning against a repeat of 2011's protests.

Three parliamentary sources close to a security agency say he wants to avoid Mubarak's three decades in power.

A judicial source and an opposition source call it a maneuver to defuse public anger.

A parliamentarian said Sisi could appoint a vice president, under a 2019 provision, to smooth a transfer.
