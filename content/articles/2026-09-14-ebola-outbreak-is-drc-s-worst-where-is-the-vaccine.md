Kinshasa — Congo's Ebola outbreak is its worst on record.

No licensed vaccine protects against this outbreak's Bundibugyo strain.

The outbreak in [DR Congo](country:CD) is moving at unprecedented speed while vaccine candidates for the strain remain undeveloped.

Vaccine development for the strain continues, with no timeline yet for approval.
