Frankfurt — In 2022, Deutsche Bank backed €11.4 million for Merkava 4 tank transmissions.

The first delivery was due in September 2024, 11 months into [Israel](country:IL)'s war on Gaza.

The bank's deputy general counsel judged exposure unlikely, saying German law does not class the transmissions as weapons, and approved.

The Renk contract with Israel's defence ministry runs through 2027.
