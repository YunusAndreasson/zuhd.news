Adelaide — A century of dams cut [Australia](country:AU)'s Murray outflow 60%.

The 2022-23 floods delivered a nutrient pulse that briefly restored the marine productivity that river regulation had removed.

Without policy changes, Murray flows will continue at 60% below pre-regulation levels, researchers noted.
