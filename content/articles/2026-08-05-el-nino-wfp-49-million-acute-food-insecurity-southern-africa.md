Lusaka — A record El Nino could push 49 million into acute hunger.

The World Food Programme says the Pacific pattern will peak by December; southern Africa's rain-fed maize belt has thin import cover against the shock.

The World Food Programme and Food and Agriculture Organization need $167 million more to reach 7.6 million people already targeted.
