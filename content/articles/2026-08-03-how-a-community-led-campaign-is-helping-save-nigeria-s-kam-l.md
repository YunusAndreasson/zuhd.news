Bali — Between 8,000 and 11,000 people speak Kam.

No researcher documented it before 2016 in [Nigeria](country:NG), where a Berlin linguist trained 14 community members to preserve it.

Half of Nigeria's 500-plus languages remain undocumented, project linguist Jakob Lesage said.
