Brussels — The tanker MV Midag was sailing under a false flag.

The UK said in July that 700 shadow-fleet vessels carry three quarters of [Russia](country:RU)'s sanctioned oil.

EU Operation Irini forces boarded the ship Friday under Article 110 of the UN sea convention, which confirmed the false flag.

Russia denies the shadow fleet exists.

EU foreign ministers vote Monday on sanctions against banks, buyers and companies supporting the fleet.
