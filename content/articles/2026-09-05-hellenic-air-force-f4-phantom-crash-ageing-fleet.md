Tanagra — A 1958 jet crashed before a crowd.

Both pilots died in front of thousands watching Athens Flying Week, officials said.

[Greece](country:GR) still flies life-extended Phantoms because a budget crisis and an arms race pushed out the successor fleet.

The investigation has not begun, and the pilots' names have not been released.
