Kuwait City — An Iranian drone killed 2 Italian technicians, Kuwait says.

They are the first reported deaths since the [US](country:US) and [Iran](country:IR) resumed exchanging fire.

[Kuwait](country:KW) said debris from an intercepted drone hit them while they serviced a Skyguard air-defence station.

Iran's armed forces spokesman Abolfazl Shekarchi denied any Iranian operation in the region, Iranian state media reported.

Iranian officials have said the no-war, no-peace standoff cannot last and are weighing military options.
