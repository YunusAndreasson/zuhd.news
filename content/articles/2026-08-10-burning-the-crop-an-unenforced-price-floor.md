Swabi — Farmer Tamriaz Khan torched 5 bales of tobacco.

Burning the harvest is the only leverage a grower has against concentrated buyers.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s Tobacco Board sets tobacco at Rs740 per kilogram, but companies pay growers Rs350, he said.

Growers await any Tobacco Board visit to the purchasing points buying below the floor.
