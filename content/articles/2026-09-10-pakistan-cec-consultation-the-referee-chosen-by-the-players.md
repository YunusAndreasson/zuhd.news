Islamabad — PM Shehbaz invited Achakzai to pick [Pakistan](country:PK)'s referee.

The pick decides who referees an election both sides already dispute.

Article 213's PM-opposition consensus rule has left the vote commission without members for months during past deadlocks.

PTI marches on Islamabad September 27 if Achakzai stays silent.
