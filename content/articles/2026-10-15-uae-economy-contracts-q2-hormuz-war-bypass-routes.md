Abu Dhabi — Non-oil output shrank 1.1% in the second quarter.

The [UAE](country:AE)'s total GDP fell 2.1% year on year to $130 billion, the federal statistics centre said.

Economy minister Abdulla bin Touq says east-coast ports at Fujairah and Khor Fakkan and a new pipeline bypass Hormuz.

Across the first half, real GDP still grew 0.4%, with non-oil output up 1.8%.

The International Monetary Fund expects a 3.9% contraction this year and a 7.7% rebound in 2027.
