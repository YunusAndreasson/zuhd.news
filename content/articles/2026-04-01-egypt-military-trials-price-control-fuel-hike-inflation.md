Cairo — Egypt raised fuel 30%, then threatened military trials. Cairo's third fuel hike in a year was blamed on the Iran war; traders who passed costs through face military prosecution. Whether courts accept any referrals will test Egypt's constitutional bar on trying civilians before military judges.
