Oslo — 65 state conflicts in 2025 — most since 1946.

The 5 years since 2021 produced more battle deaths than the two prior decades; 245,000 died in 2025 alone, Oslo researchers said.

Civilian-targeted deaths surged from 14,200 to 76,500 in one year — a trajectory researchers called "shocking."
