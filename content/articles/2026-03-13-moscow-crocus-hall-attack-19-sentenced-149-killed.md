Russia convicted 19 over the 2024 Crocus City massacre that killed 149. Islamic State claimed the attack, but Russia blamed Ukraine without evidence and used that claim to justify military escalation. The trial was held behind closed doors, and several defendants appeared in court visibly beaten — one in a wheelchair — prompting lawyers to allege forced confessions.
