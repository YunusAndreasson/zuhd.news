Washington — Markets now fully price a December Fed rate hike.

Inflation measures stay at 3% or higher, against the [US](country:US) Fed's 2% target.

Oil has climbed about 20% since September to around $114 a barrel, and AI-industry demand adds to price pressure.

The Fed's statement gave no clear signal of a December hike, saying more information will guide its stance.

The Federal Reserve decides again in early December.
