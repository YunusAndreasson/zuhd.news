Washington slashed Italian pasta tariffs from 91.7% to under 7%. Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni's alignment with Trump secured the cut on exports worth €671 million annually — 17% of Italy's pasta trade. European allies gain tariff relief while oil-importing nations absorb the cost of the US-Iran war's Hormuz disruption.
