Russia — Sanctioned Russian crude sells above Brent as China and India buy.

Sanctions have not discounted Russian barrels, and buyers are paying up for them.

Higher shipping costs have Asian buyers looking for supplies closer to home.

The [US](country:US) Energy Information Administration cites Gulf tanker attacks and limited shipping capacity for October's third monthly price rise.

The agency expects Brent near $100 a barrel by year-end and $85.78 on average in 2027.
