Nairobi — A blood draw can now diagnose Burkitt lymphoma.

East African studies found the liquid-biopsy test quick and accurate, replacing invasive tissue biopsies in regions where oncologists are scarce.

Wider rollout depends on the test reaching clinics beyond the study sites.
