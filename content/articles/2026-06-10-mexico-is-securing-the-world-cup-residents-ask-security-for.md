Monterrey — [Mexico](country:MX) deployed 100,000 security forces for FIFA.

Plan Kukulkán adds helicopters and anti-drone systems to the 100,000-strong force; residents call it a tourist security bubble.

No comparable force has been deployed for the chronic violence that disappears Mexico's residents, collectives say.
