Hong Kong — Mussels glue to rock in 30 seconds. Laboratory replication of the same liquid-liquid phase separation takes hours; HKUST's molecular simulation identified a flux pathway explaining nature's speed. The finding opens design space for instant-set biocompatible surgical glues and underwater industrial coatings.
