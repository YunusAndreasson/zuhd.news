Lansing — Nine Michigan systems breached, joining Minnesota's thirty.

Federal agencies say seven states total were hit and decline to name the rest; officials blame Iranian hackers who exploited remote-access tools, not treatment failures.

No contamination has been reported; the FBI has not yet confirmed who is responsible.
