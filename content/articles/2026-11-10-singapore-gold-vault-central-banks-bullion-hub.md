Singapore — The national currency vault can now hold 500 tonnes of foreign central-bank gold.

It challenges London and New York as custodians of reserve gold.

Asia buys roughly 70% of consumer gold, Deputy Prime Minister Gan Kim Yong said, yet its trading infrastructure lags demand.

Hong Kong began a trial gold-clearing system in July.

The Singapore Exchange and six clearing banks plan to start over-the-counter gold clearing in the first quarter of 2027.
