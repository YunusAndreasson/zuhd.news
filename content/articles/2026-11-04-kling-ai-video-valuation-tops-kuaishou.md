Beijing — Kuaishou's AI video unit Kling raised about 19.8 billion yuan.

A private mark of $18 billion now exceeds the group's Hong Kong market value.

Kling's quarterly revenue rose from over 650 million yuan to over 850 million, while group revenue grew 1.4%.

Kling supplies about 2% of first-half group sales, and filings show its net losses widened in 2024 and 2025.

Industry reporting puts break-even in 2028, if compute costs fall and volume absorbs fixed costs.
