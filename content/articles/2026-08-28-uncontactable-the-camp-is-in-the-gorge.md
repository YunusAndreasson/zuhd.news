Kathmandu — 63 workers were rescued from a hydropower camp.

The missing camp lay in the narrowest point of the flood-prone valley.

Migrant workers housed at valley-floor construction camps sit off any national register, so no state can readily count them.

[India](country:IN)'s foreign ministry says 320 nationals remain uncontactable, with no roll to check them against.
