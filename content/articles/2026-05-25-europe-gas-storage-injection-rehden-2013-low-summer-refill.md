Berlin — Rehden, [Germany](country:DE)'s largest gas store, is 4.2% full.

Germany and [France](country:FR) injection rates are the lowest since 2013 at summer season start, Gas Infrastructure Europe data show.

Summer injection determines next winter's supply; the [Netherlands](country:NL)' largest stores emptied entirely last season, one yet to restart.
