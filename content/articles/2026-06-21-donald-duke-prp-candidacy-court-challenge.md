Abuja — Donald Duke's 2027 candidacy is under legal challenge.

The suit alleges Duke joined [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Peoples Redemption Party after INEC's membership cut-off — so his primary win is void.

The suit also cites Gombe: 1,431 votes cast against 348 registered members — over-voting that could invalidate state results.
