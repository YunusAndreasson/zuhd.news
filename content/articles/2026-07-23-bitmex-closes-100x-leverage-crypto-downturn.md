Hong Kong — The exchange that invented 100x bitcoin leverage is closing.

BitMEX will wind down by September 23; its perpetual futures contract, letting traders borrow up to 100 times their stake, became the template rivals like Binance and Bybit later built on.

New account registrations have already stopped as the decade-long crypto downturn drains smaller exchanges.
