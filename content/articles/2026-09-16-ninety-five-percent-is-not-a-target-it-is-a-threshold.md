Lewistown — An unvaccinated 18-year-old died of measles complications.

Pennsylvania's outbreak, now 693 cases, is the state's worst in three decades.

Measles spreads far more easily than most viruses, so herd immunity needs roughly 95% vaccination coverage to hold.

Health Secretary Robert Kennedy Jr. disputes the deaths and moved to exclude them from federal data.
