Washington — Trump halted all Pentagon strikes on Iran for 5 days Monday. Iran had threatened to destroy every power plant in West Asia — including those supplying US military bases — if US attacks continued. The 5-day window expires Saturday with Iran's foreign ministry flatly denying any negotiations took place.
