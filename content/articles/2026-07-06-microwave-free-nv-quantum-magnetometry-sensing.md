London — A diamond sensor now reads magnetic fields without microwaves.

Microwave hardware normally heats samples and leaks stray fields; researchers used Bayesian inference on photoluminescence maps to recover field direction instead.

Dropping the microwave gear opens a path from lab benches to field instruments.
