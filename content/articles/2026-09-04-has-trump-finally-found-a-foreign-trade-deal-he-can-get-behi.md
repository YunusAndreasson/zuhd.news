Washington — Congress extended African trade preferences through 2028.

Renewal protects apparel jobs in [Lesotho](country:LS), Kenya, Madagascar and Ghana from tariff shocks.

Unlike a negotiated deal, the African Growth and Opportunity Act is a revocable [US](country:US) favor Congress renews in two-year increments.

Washington now wants reciprocal tariff cuts, a deal African governments are wary to sign.
