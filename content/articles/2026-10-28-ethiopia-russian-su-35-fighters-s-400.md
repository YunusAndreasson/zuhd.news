Ethiopia — 12 Russian Su-35 fighters arrived a year early, Abiy says.

The deal also covers four of [Russia](country:RU)'s S-400 missile batteries, with no delivery schedule announced.

The Su-35's radar and long-range missiles improve on [Ethiopia](country:ET)'s roughly 10 secondhand Su-27s at detecting and engaging hostile aircraft.

Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has not disclosed the price, the basing locations or whether more jets follow.
