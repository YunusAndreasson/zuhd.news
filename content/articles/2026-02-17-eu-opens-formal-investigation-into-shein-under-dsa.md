Illegal items including child sexual abuse material were listed on Shein's platform, the European Commission found. The regulator opened a formal investigation under the Digital Services Act, also targeting the fast-fashion app's addictive design features. Shein risks fines of up to 6% of global annual revenue if found in breach.
