Beirut — Israeli forces struck 128 medical facilities in Lebanon. The strikes killed 40 healthcare workers, forcing emergency teams to scatter their ambulances far apart so no single attack can wipe out all available staff, leaving over 1 million displaced people without care. Lebanon's Health Ministry has submitted evidence to the UN, but the US has blocked Security Council action.
