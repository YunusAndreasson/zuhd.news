Gilgit — One markhor permit opens at $200,000.

About 80% of that fee funds the village that hosts the herd, not the state.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s Gilgit-Baltistan will auction 3 markhor, 14 blue sheep and 100 ibex permits based on annual wildlife surveys.

Dealers warn repeated price hikes could push foreign hunters away before the September 10 auction.
