Hida — A 7,000-tonne detector sits two kilometers underground here.

The count sets how long Earth's plate-driving heat engine will keep running.

Geoneutrinos are the only direct read on the uranium, thorium and potassium fueling mantle convection.

Scientists call it a tightening measurement, not a closed question, as more data accumulates.
