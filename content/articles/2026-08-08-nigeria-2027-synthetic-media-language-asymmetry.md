Abuja — Artificial intelligence disinformation is flooding Nigeria's 2027 campaign.

Moderation tools built for English leave Hausa, Yoruba and Igbo posts unscreened.

Researchers built a "Democracy Discourse Index" to track the platforms, including TikTok and X, where the disinformation spreads fastest.

[Nigeria](country:NG) votes 16 January 2027, then holds state elections 6 February.
