Russia staffs the nuclear plant just struck in Iran. Rosatom, Russia's state nuclear company, built Bushehr in 2011 and still has 480 engineers on-site — making it the first nuclear facility struck since the US-Israel war began February 28. Rosatom is preparing a 3rd worker evacuation as International Atomic Energy Agency chief Rafael Grossi warned of nuclear accident risk.
