Lake Geneva — AcuRite kills its 10-year-old app May 30, stranding existing device owners.

Internet of Things devices carry no software continuity warranty under [US](country:US) consumer law, a gap that has bricked hardware from 3 prior vendors.

AcuRite NOW, the replacement, lacks sensor renaming and a web dashboard, with no restoration date set.
