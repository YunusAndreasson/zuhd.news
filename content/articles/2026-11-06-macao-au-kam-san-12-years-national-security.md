Macao — A closed-door court gave Au Kam-san 12.5 years.

It is the first public prosecution under Macao's 2009 national security law.

The court found the 69-year-old guilty of subversion by spreading false information to disrupt the 2024 leadership election.

"It's a political trial and it's a groundless persecution," his wife, Virginia Cheang, wrote in a public letter.

Human Rights Watch asked the EU and [Portugal](country:PT) to press for his release.
