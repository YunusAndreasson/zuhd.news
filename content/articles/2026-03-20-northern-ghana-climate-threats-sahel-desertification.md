Bawku West faces drought, heat, and crop collapse. The Sahel's encroaching desert reaches northern Ghana, where local environmental group BEWDA says government drought policies are failing without community enforcement. The group is calling for co-designed adaptation plans, with no government response or funding commitment announced.
