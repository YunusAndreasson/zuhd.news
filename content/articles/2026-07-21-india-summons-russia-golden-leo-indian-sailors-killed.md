New Delhi — Four Indian sailors died aboard a grain ship leaving Odesa.

[Russia](country:RU) hit the Guinea-Bissau-flagged Golden Leo with three cruise missiles Sunday, killing 10; [India](country:IN) summoned Moscow's envoy over the "unacceptable" toll.

Indians and Filipinos crew most merchant ships; Moscow says it will keep striking vessels serving Ukraine.
