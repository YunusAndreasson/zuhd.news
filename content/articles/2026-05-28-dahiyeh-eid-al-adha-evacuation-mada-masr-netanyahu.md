Beirut — Dahiyeh families packed cars at midnight on Eid.

[Israel](country:IL)'s Netanyahu threatened escalation hours after [US](country:US)-[Iran](country:IR) talks neared a deal, sending Dahiyeh residents back onto evacuation roads.

Netanyahu's cabinet approved Hezbollah leadership strikes in Beirut; Lebanese residents have no shelter capacity further north.
