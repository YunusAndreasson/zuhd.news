Barcelona — Humans once spoke up to 75,000 languages.

A computational model built from hunter-gatherer proxies puts the peak 3,000 to 1,000 years ago, declining as multinational empires standardized administrative languages, researchers led by Damián Blasi found.

Colonialism accelerated the collapse; a language without digital training data now risks the same fate.
