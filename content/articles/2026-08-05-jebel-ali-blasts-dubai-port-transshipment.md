Dubai — Seven blasts in twenty minutes hit Jebel Ali port.

Officials in the [United Arab Emirates](country:AE) acknowledged the explosions and a warehouse fire but withheld the cause; the port is the largest transshipment hub between Rotterdam and Singapore.

No perpetrator has been named; casualty and damage figures remain unavailable.
