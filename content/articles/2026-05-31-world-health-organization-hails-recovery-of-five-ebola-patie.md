Bunia — 5 patients recovered from Ebola's untreatable Bundibugyo strain.

Bundibugyo has no approved vaccine; the [DRC](country:CD)'s 17th outbreak reached 1,000 suspected cases faster than any prior eruption, Doctors Without Borders said.

[Uganda](country:UG), which shares hundreds of kilometres of border with [DRC](country:CD), has reported 1 death and 9 cases.
