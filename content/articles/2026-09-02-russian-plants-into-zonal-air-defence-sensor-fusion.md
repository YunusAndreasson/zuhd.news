Vladivostok — Factories are joining Russia's military radar network.

Plant sensor feeds now sit inside a military network, factory operators ceding control.

[Russia](country:RU)'s defence ministry says scattered private radars can't hand off a drone track, so plants now feed one system.

Deputy Prime Minister Denis Manturov did not say which enterprises, at what cost, or who pays.
