Washington — The 30-year mortgage rate reached 6.71%.

Freddie Mac says it's the highest since July 2025, squeezing homebuyer affordability.

Mortgage rates track Treasury yields, which climbed on inflation fears tied to the [Iran](country:IR) war and AI-linked borrowing demand.

The Fed meets 15-16 September to decide whether renewed inflation forces a rate hike.
