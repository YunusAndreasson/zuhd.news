Tokyo — Core prices rose 2.9%, fastest since July 2025.

Core inflation in [Japan](country:JP)'s capital has stayed above the central bank's 2% target for three months.

Ending government utility subsidies drove part of the rise, and prices also climbed for food, daily necessities and hotel charges.

Excluding fresh food and fuel, inflation slowed to 2.5% from 2.6%, and the headline undershot the 3.0% forecast.

The Bank of Japan decides on 18 December whether to lift its 1.25% policy rate.
