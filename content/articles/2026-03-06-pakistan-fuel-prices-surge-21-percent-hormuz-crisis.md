Pakistan raised petrol prices Rs55 a litre. The country imports nearly all its crude through the Strait of Hormuz, closed by the US-Iran conflict, forcing an emergency Saudi supply deal. Pakistan dispatched ships to Saudi and UAE ports and will review prices weekly until supply stabilizes.
