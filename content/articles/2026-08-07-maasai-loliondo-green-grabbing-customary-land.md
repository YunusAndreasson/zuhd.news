Loliondo — 1,500 square kilometers of village land face demarcation.

[Tanzania](country:TZ) cites overgrazing to justify moving Maasai herders resettled here in 1959, while the same land opens to tourism and hunting concessions.

A 2024 decree delisted several villages; residents say consent was never genuinely sought.
