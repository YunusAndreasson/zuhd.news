Paris — French inflation hit 3.0% in September, up from 2.4%.

Energy prices rose 21.2% year-on-year, against 16.7% in August, Insee said.

[France](country:FR) skipped broad fuel-tax cuts for targeted aid that does not lower measured inflation, so oil prices pass through more directly.

The government projects interest costs rising to €91bn in 2027 from €79bn.
