Jakarta — Indonesia's Hercules maintenance deal carries a wartime liability.

Sustaining [US](country:US) military aircraft converts a neutral host into a belligerent under the law of neutrality, Lowy Institute analysts argued.

[Indonesia](country:ID) has not said whether the contract includes carve-outs if conflict erupts over [Taiwan](country:TW).
