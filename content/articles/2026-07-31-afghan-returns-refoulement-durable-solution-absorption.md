Kabul — Millions have been pushed back into an economy still shrinking.

[Iran](country:IR) and [Pakistan](country:PK) are returning Afghans faster than any funded absorption plan exists, blurring voluntary repatriation with deportation.

Non-refoulement binds both states under customary law regardless of treaty status; the UN refugee agency's per-returnee funding remains undisclosed.
