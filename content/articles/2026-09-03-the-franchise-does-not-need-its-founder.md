Trujillo — [Bolivia](country:BO) arrested [Peru](country:PE)'s most-wanted extortion boss.

His capture is unlikely to slow extortion collected street by street.

The Pulpos run a family-based franchise collecting weekly fees from bus drivers and shopkeepers across Trujillo.

It is unclear who succeeds him; his father, the group's founder, was freed in 2025.
