Algiers — Saudi firm Midad won a $5.4 billion Algeria contract. Algeria's overland pipelines supply 33% of Italy's gas, bypassing the Strait of Hormuz now disrupted by war, while the 30-year deal opens the Illizi South basin to exploration. Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni flies to Algiers on March 25 to negotiate additional supply.
