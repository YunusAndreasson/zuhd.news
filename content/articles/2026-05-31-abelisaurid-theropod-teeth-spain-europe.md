Guadalajara — 40 teeth place African predators in Cretaceous Europe.

Cladistic analysis matched the Algora teeth to majungasaurine abelisaurids — a family known from Madagascar — placing the lineage in Cenomanian [Spain](country:ES).

The teeth leave the European entry route open: land bridges across the Tethyan margin remain to be mapped.
