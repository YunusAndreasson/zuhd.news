Tokyo — Toyota, Nissan and Honda will standardise parts from 2027.

[Japan](country:JP)'s 7 automakers face Chinese rivals and want the savings spent on innovation.

They start with common steel quality criteria, plastics and resin protocols, and wiring-harness colours, which customers rarely notice.

"We all share this strong sense of crisis," said Koji Sato, chairman of Japan's automakers' association.

Talks begin with Japanese suppliers, and international suppliers join later.
