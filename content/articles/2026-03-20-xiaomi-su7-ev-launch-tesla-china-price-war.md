Xiaomi's SU7 sedan debuted at $31,900, below pre-sale estimates. Xiaomi, 2 years into carmaking, priced the new model 4,000 yuan above the original despite higher supply chain costs. Citi analysts estimated the model could reach 270,000 monthly units, drawing buyers from Tesla Model 3 and Xpeng P7.
