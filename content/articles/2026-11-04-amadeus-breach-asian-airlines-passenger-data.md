Spain — Six Asian airlines report passenger data stolen via one supplier.

Japan Airlines says 92,000 customers had information stolen.

Cathay Pacific says Amadeus told it attackers used login credentials assigned to its system account.

Amadeus says there is no evidence that payment or financial data was accessed.

Singapore Airlines is contacting passengers with upcoming flights to change their exposed booking numbers.
