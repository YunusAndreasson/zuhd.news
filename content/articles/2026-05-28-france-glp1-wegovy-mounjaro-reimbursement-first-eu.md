Paris — 1 million French patients gain Wegovy coverage.

[France](country:FR)'s public reimbursement covers 65–100% of cost, collapsing the €300/month price barrier, the health minister said.

Other EU states are in pricing talks with Novo Nordisk but have not committed to matching the rate.
