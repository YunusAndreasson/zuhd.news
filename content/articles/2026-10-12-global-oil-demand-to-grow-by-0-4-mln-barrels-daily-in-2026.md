Riyadh — Oil exporters cut 2026 demand growth by 1 million barrels a day.

The Organization of Petroleum Exporting Countries expects the missing demand back in 2027, at 1.7 million barrels a day.

Hormuz traffic averaged 2.7 ships a day to 4 October, 38% below normal, and Brent stood at $105.50 on 9 October.

Polymarket traders give Hormuz traffic a 14% chance of normalising by 31 December, down 6 points in a week.
