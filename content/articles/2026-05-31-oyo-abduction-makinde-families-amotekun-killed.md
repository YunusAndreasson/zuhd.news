Oriire — 39 pupils remain held, 1 teacher killed.

Gunmen raided 3 Oyo schools on May 15; Governor Makinde says nearly 200 Amotekun operatives have died fighting insecurity in 6 years.

Makinde signaled willingness to negotiate; 45 captives are believed held in [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Oyo forests.
