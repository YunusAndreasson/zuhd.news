Uyo — Officials offered Nduke Pious N550,000 for her bungalow.

She spent over N3 million building it before state agents demolished it.

More than a dozen owners rejected offers, and valuers assessed some homes at up to 7 times more.

The state has not answered as construction of the N117.5 billion medical city continues.
