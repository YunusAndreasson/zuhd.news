London — Advocacy outlet Declassified UK says most Labour Friends of Israel directors aren't Labour members.

Groups run mostly by party members must register donations above about £12,000 with the Electoral Commission.

Documents journalist Solomon Hughes obtained under freedom of information show the group told the commission its Labour members are not a majority.

The group denies Israeli government funding and says no director belongs to another party.

Until the Electoral Commission reclassifies the group, its donors stay undisclosed.
