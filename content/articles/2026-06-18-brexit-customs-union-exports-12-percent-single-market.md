London — Leaving the EU single market, not tariffs, caused 10 of Brexit's 12-point export fall, the Centre for European Reform found.

A customs union alone recovers almost nothing — 10 of the 12 lost percentage points trace to single-market exit, researchers said.

Labour has ruled out rejoining the single market; Starmer holds an EU summit next month.
