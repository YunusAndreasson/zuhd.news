778 Indian sailors cannot safely leave the Persian Gulf. 24 of 28 ships sit on the western side of the Strait of Hormuz, where Iran has warned every transiting vessel will be targeted. New Delhi is monitoring all 28 vessels but has not announced an extraction plan.
