Bolsonaro is in ICU with bronchopneumonia and failing kidneys. The jailed former Brazilian president is serving 27 years for organizing the 2023 coup against President Lula; a 2018 stabbing left him prone to lung infections. His son Flávio said he is stable but named no discharge date with October's election approaching.
