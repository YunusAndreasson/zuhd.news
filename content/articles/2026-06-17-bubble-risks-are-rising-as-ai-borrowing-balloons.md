New York — AI infrastructure buildout increasingly runs on debt.

When capital spending is debt-financed, a sector slowdown becomes a debt cascade — unlike equity losses, borrowed capital demands repayment even when demand reverses.

No peak in AI capital spending is in sight; credit markets now hold the sector's downside.
