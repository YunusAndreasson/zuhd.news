German CO2 fell 0.1%, barely clearing the legal limit. The new Christian Democratic Union government under Chancellor Friedrich Merz has weakened home heating laws and plans new gas plants, trading climate commitments for industry support. Germany must cut from 48% to 65% below 1990 levels by 2030, with no coalition agreement on how.
