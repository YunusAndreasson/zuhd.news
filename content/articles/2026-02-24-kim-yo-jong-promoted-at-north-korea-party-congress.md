Kim Yo-jong became a department director at North Korea's first Workers' Party congress in 5 years. The promotion formalizes power she has wielded informally as her brother's closest adviser, and her return to the politburo signals she ranks second in the regime. The congress is expected to set nuclear policy for the next 5-year cycle.
