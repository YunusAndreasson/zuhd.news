Indonesia — MSCI rates 80% of Chinese mining firms laggards on sustainability.

[China](country:CN) leads Indonesia's nickel industry, and the metal goes into electric-vehicle batteries and wind turbines.

The International Institute for Sustainable Development found Chinese nickel processors do well on emissions but weakly on community relations.

The Zhongguancun Green Mine Industry Alliance plans an international standard this year for companies operating outside China.
