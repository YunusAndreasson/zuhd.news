Barkin Ladi — Soldiers arrested three men after Peter Gyang's killing.

The 17-year-old died in Barkin Ladi, the site of Plateau's deadliest recent violence.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s army, not police, made the arrests under a long-running military operation in the region.

The military has not said whether the men are linked to his killing.
