The National Institutes of Health fired its own whistleblower. Jenna Norton, an NIH grants administrator, said research awards are being cut without scientific criteria and with no institutional check on political appointees. No other NIH official has backed Norton's warning, which implicates funding covering a third of US biomedical research.
