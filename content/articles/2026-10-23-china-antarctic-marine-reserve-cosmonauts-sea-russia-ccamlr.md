Hobart — [China](country:CN)'s 127,000km² Antarctic reserve expires in 20 years.

Beijing's rule changes mirror ones [Russia](country:RU) pushed in 2022 and 2023, which failed to win consensus.

The commission works by consensus, so a single member could veto renewal and let the reserve lapse without a vote.

"The very small area apparently proposed by China also raises significant questions," said Karen Scott, a University of Canterbury professor.

Nothing takes effect until all 27 members, [South Africa](country:ZA) included, agree.
