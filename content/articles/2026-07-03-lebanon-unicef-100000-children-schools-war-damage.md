Beirut — 100,000 children risk missing the school year.

A UNICEF-backed assessment found 340 [Lebanon](country:LB) schools damaged and 17 destroyed in fighting between [Israel](country:IL) and Hezbollah, worst in Nabatieh and Bekaa.

Classrooms must be repaired before the new school year opens in September.
