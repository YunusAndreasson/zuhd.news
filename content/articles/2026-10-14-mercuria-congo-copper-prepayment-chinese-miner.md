Congo — Mercuria advances $250 million against Congolese copper.

Trading houses now pay miners in advance to lock up metal before it ships.

Tariff fears have put roughly 70% of the world's visible copper stocks in [US](country:US) warehouses, tightening supply elsewhere.

The price gap that drew metal to the US has shrunk to about $310 a ton, from above $3,000.

Trump has not decided whether to impose a 15% refined-copper tariff from 1 January 2027.
