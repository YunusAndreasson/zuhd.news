New York — 2 belugas passed a test that fish already pass.

The 1970 mirror test is now extended to elephants, dolphins, and fish; critics say it detects visual tracking, not selfhood.

Neuroscientist Anil Seth argues it must be paired with species-specific perception studies to have any meaning.
