San Francisco — A court found Google can't sue over scraping.

Google invoked DMCA anti-circumvention rules to fight SerpApi, but the judge ruled it owns none of scraped content, so it lacked standing.

Google has 21 days to amend its complaint; Reddit's parallel suit against SerpApi awaits the same ruling.
