---
title: "Fungicide Marks Persist 20 Generations"
date: "2026-02-26T00:00:00Z"
source: "Nature"
sourceUrl: "https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-00555-3"
category: "science"
---

20 rat generations show disease from one vinclozolin dose. By rewriting chemical markers on germ cells without altering DNA, the fungicide passes compounding disease risk through sperm to each new generation. Pesticide regulators worldwide test only for single-generation toxicity, a gap the study's authors said must be closed with multigenerational standards.
