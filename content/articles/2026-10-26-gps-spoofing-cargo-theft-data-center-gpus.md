United States — Investigators say thieves took $100 million of data center freight.

Verisk CargoNet's Keith Lewis says stealing the equipment is easier than robbing a bank.

Thieves hijack broker accounts with fake listings, win real loads, and in one case spoofed the tracking coordinates.

States have formed task forces, and lawmakers propose a stronger federal response to overseas thefts.
