San Francisco — Anthropic gave Apple, Amazon, and Microsoft access to Mythos.

Under Project Glasswing, Mythos found a 27-year-old bug in critical internet software that automated tools had missed.

Anthropic has not set a release date, and Glasswing findings will determine Mythos's safety clearance.
