Washington — [US](country:US) diesel averages $6.51 a gallon, AAA says.

Trump is weighing a 90-day export ban, and Europe and [Brazil](country:BR) rely on US diesel.

A ban would fill storage tanks, then refiners would cut output of all fuels, raising prices again, experts say.

Energy Secretary Chris Wright said the administration is not considering a full ban or zero exports.

Congress could legislate a ban, but the House does not return before November's elections.
