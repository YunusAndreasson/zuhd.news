6.5 million Somalis face severe hunger by March. The Horn of Africa's worst drought in 40 years destroyed crops and livestock, compounded by Al-Shabaab blockades and halved international aid funding. The UN warns 500,000 children under 5 face acute malnutrition and even normal rainfall would leave 5.5 million hungry.
