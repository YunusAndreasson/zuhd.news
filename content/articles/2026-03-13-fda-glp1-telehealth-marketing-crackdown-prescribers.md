US regulators warned 70 telehealth companies over unlicensed weight-loss drugs. The US Food and Drug Administration said the firms sold compounded versions of Ozempic and Wegovy without federal safety approval, marketing them as if the drugs had been reviewed. Affiliated prescribers may face separate scrutiny, but the FDA has not said whether investigations will extend to clinicians.
