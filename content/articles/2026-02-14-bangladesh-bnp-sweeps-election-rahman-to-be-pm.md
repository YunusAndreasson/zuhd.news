Bangladesh's BNP won 212 of 299 seats in the first election since the 2024 uprising that ousted Sheikh Hasina. Chairman Tarique Rahman, back from 17 years in exile, will become prime minister. His approach to India, which backed the ousted government, will shape the country's new direction.
