Abuja — A $1 test bundle screens pregnant women for 3 diseases.

Only 12.3% of Nigerian mothers get tested for hepatitis B despite 72% attending antenatal care.

Premier Medical Corporation and Impact Nexus Africa bundled the HIV/syphilis and hepatitis B rapid tests, pricing them 22-46% below buying them separately.

The companies model $215 million in savings by 2035, but call the figure modeled, not confirmed for any single country.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s health ministry has planned free hepatitis B testing for women who test positive.
