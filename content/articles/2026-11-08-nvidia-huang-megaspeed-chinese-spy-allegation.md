Washington — US intelligence reportedly flagged Megaspeed's Alice Huang as Beijing's asset.

She was close to Jensen Huang, who has urged easing chip export limits on [China](country:CN).

Megaspeed, an Nvidia cloud partner in Singapore, buys its chips and rents out computing power to AI developers.

Nvidia says it holds no classified information, and no one has been charged.

Three House chairmen asked Huang to brief them on 18 November or name another date by 11 November.
