Beijing — Chang'e 7 slipped with no new date and no reason given.

Partner states that signed onto a lunar-base timeline, not hardware, now wait on an unexplained delay.

Chang'e 7 was to survey south-pole ice that would decide whether a crewed research station is viable there.

[China](country:CN)'s space agency has not said when, or whether, the mission will fly.
