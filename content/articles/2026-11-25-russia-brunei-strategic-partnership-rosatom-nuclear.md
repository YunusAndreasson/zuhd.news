Moscow — Rosatom is ready to offer [Brunei](country:BN) floating reactors, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

The sultan and Vladimir Putin adopted a strategic partnership declaration, TASS reported.

Rosatom chief Alexey Likhachev said two 55-megawatt reactors suit Brunei, starting with shaping public opinion and building a nuclear workforce.

Brunei has not joined sanctions on [Russia](country:RU) but backed UN resolutions condemning the Ukraine war, TASS says.

Likhachev said Rosatom will make the offer only if Brunei expresses the desire.
