Frankfurt — Euro-zone inflation is 3.8%, nearly double the central bank's target.

The European Central Bank's account says officials judged the rise would still not restrict growth.

The deposit rate stands at 2.50% after two rises this year, with the US-Israeli war with [Iran](country:IR) driving energy prices.

The bank decides on 29 October, and 64 of 73 economists polled expect a rise in December.
