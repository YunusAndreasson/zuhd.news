Hawthorne — Space has no air to carry heat away.

SpaceX plans 1 million satellites, each carrying large radiators to dump GPU waste heat that radiation also degrades over time.

Analysts estimate the constellation could cost $1.45 trillion to $9.8 trillion depending on launch costs.
