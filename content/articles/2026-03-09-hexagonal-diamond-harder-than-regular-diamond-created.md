title: "Scientists Grow Bulk Hexagonal Diamond"
date: "2026-03-04T00:00:00Z"
source: "Nature"
sourceUrl: "https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-00711-9"
category: "science"
---

Scientists grew hexagonal diamond — 58% harder than regular diamond. Previous attempts produced only atom-thin traces; this team grew millimeter-scale lonsdaleite crystals, marking the material's first bulk production. The team says bulk lonsdaleite could enable harder cutting tools and high-pressure experiments impossible with cubic diamond.
