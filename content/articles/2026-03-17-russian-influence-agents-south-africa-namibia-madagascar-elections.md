South Africa's ruling party requested $300,000 from Russian intelligence operatives. Documents from Russia's Foreign Intelligence Service show the network backed incumbents in South Africa, Namibia, and Madagascar with cash, smear campaigns, and fabricated materials. African National Congress Secretary-General Fikile Mbalula made the request, seeking Russian backing through South Africa's 2026 elections ahead of a 2029 presidential run.
