New Delhi — Congress's Rajya Sabha nominee rejected over an unrecognised complaint.

[India](country:IN)'s electoral law mandates disclosure only after a judge frames charges — a stage not reached in Natarajan's case.

Congress threatened court action if the Election Commission did not reverse the decision before Wednesday's withdrawal deadline.
