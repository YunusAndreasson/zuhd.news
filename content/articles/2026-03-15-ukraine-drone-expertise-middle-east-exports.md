Ukraine sent drone experts to 4 Gulf states. Gulf states have exhausted air-defense missiles countering Iran's Shahed drones, the same model Ukraine has defended against every night for 3 years. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said nearly a dozen countries have sought the expertise, with Kyiv demanding payment in cash and technology transfers.
