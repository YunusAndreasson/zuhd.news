London — Space debris quietly drives waves through plasma.

That wake could reveal debris too small for radar to track.

Orbit is not empty but thin ionized gas, and a fast object crossing it creates a shockwave like a jet in air.

The model is analytical only, and no instrument has yet tried to detect this signature.
