London — New printing method places nanowires at record density.

A line-contact roller lowers stress during transfer, printing zinc oxide nanowires at 14/μm on flexible film, a record.

Flexible ultraviolet sensors from the arrays achieved 4,000 A/W and survived 5,000 bends at 10mm, researchers reported.
