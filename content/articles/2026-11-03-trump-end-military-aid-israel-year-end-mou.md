Washington — Trump says $4 billion in Israel aid stops at year-end.

Congress appropriates the money, and a 10-year memorandum signed in 2016 runs through 2028.

The memorandum gives [Israel](country:IL) $3.3bn a year to buy [US](country:US) weapons, plus $500mn for joint air-defence projects such as Iron Dome.

Netanyahu proposed scaling aid back over a decade and has given no public sign he agreed to end it this year.

Netanyahu's office has not responded, and 68% of Republicans favour continuing aid, a Chicago Council poll found.
