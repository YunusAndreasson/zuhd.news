Port Sudan — Houthis sent at least 3 small-arms shipments to [Sudan](country:SD)'s army since mid-2024.

The army-held port sits 500 kilometres north of the Bab el-Mandeb, where the Houthis attack ships.

Reports also place Houthi operatives in the city, acting on [Iran](country:IR)'s behalf.

Researcher Massaab Al-Aloosy calls the ties transactional, aimed at evading pressure rather than forming an alliance.

Polymarket traders give a Saudi-Houthi ceasefire by 31 December a 9% chance, down 15 points in a week.
