Washington — Every state joined a Medicaid drug pricing plan with no published prices.

State health budgets can't act on a discount that names no manufacturers, drugs, or benchmark.

Medicaid already collects statutory rebates, so a "new" discount only matters against that floor, which the announcement omits.

More than two dozen pharma companies have signed on without disclosing individual deal terms.
