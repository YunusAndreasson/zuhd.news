Dubai — Missiles fall on Gulf cities workers built.

Workers from [Bangladesh](country:BD), [Nepal](country:NP), [Pakistan](country:PK), the [Philippines](country:PH) and [Indonesia](country:ID) cannot easily flee war zones.

The kafala system ties workers' visas to employers who hold their passports and travel documents.

Whether Gulf employers will release passports for evacuation remains unresolved.
