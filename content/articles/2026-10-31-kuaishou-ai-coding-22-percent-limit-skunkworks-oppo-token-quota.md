Beijing — Coding 10 times faster speeds delivery only 22%.

Development is roughly 20% of end-to-end delivery, Kuaishou risk-control lead Wang Dongxu said.

Kuaishou removed handoffs with small teams owning one business goal, and reports 133% more throughput and 79% shorter cycles.

OPPO, which once set monthly token quotas, found individual gains did not lift team delivery, its product lead Hao Gang said.

Wang says only more such teams working together can break large-organisation coordination barriers.
