Thimphu — [Bhutan](country:BT) eliminated dog-transmitted rabies, the WHO confirmed.

WHO estimates put rabies deaths at roughly 70,000 a year, mostly children bitten by dogs.

Bhutan vaccinated over 90% of free-roaming dogs and sterilized them all, rather than culling, with free post-exposure treatment nationwide.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s Sindh province, by contrast, still treated 45,000 dog-bite patients in eight months this year.

The WHO and partners aim to end all human rabies deaths worldwide by 2030.
