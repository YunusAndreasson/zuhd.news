India's cooking gas prices spiked as Hormuz closed. India sources more than a third of its oil through the strait, so Prime Minister Narendra Modi privately negotiated with Tehran while publicly backing Washington. Congress leader Sonia Gandhi demanded in parliament that Modi explain his public silence as Iran's supreme leader was killed — his government has not responded.
