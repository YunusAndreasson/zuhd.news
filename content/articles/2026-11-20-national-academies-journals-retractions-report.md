Washington — No one tracks how long journals take to fix errors.

Retraction Watch data show retractions come 2.4 years after publication on average.

The [US](country:US) National Academies report wants journals to disclose when they were alerted and finish most reviews in 12 weeks.

Publication slipped after Science found references with fabricated author first names, a possible sign of language-model use.

Publishers choose whether to adopt the 12-week standard, since the Committee on Publication Ethics' guidance sets no deadline.
