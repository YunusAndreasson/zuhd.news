The same automated line can make appliances or shell casings.

Text-only AI competes with office work, but robotics competes with a nation's manufacturing base.

Countries banking on cheap manufacturing labor, like [Indonesia](country:ID) and [Bangladesh](country:BD), risk losing that rung.
