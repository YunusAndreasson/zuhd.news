Washington — All four election commissioners are now gone.

Trump fired the two Democratic members and the lone Republican resigned, citing a [US](country:US) Supreme Court ruling expanding presidential removal power over independent agencies.

The commission certifies voting systems; the law leaves how Trump refills the seats unclear before November.
