Gaza — Hamas just picked a negotiator to lead it.

Khalil Al-Hayya, who has run ceasefire talks in Egypt and Qatar, succeeds Yahya Sinwar, killed by [Israel](country:IL) in 2024.

He has survived multiple assassination attempts, including a 2025 Doha strike that killed his son.
