Khartoum — A 19-member committee is preparing El Burhan's dialogue.

Women leaders say talks without a ceasefire let the war's stronger side set terms.

El Burhan chairs the Sovereignty Council and commands the army, one party to the war he is convening.

The committee has yet to say who selects participants or verifies its promised guarantees.
