Iran's missile killed 3 Nepali workers in Abu Dhabi. Migrant workers make up more than half of the Gulf's 60 million residents but have no government evacuation priority as the war continues. Nepal has not restricted travel to the Gulf despite worker remittances accounting for 27% of its GDP.
