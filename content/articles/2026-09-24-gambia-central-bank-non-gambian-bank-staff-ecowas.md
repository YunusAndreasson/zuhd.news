Banjul — [Gambia](country:GM)'s central bank ordered banks to shed foreign staff.

Nigerian subsidiaries including Access Bank and Guaranty Trust Bank are among the recipients.

Its 19 September letter targets non-citizens outside approved expatriate quotas, citing the Labour Act 2023 and Guideline 9.

Banks must replace them with qualified Gambians and set succession plans before year-end.
