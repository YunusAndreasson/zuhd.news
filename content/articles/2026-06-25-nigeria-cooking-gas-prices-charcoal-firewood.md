Kaduna — Gas hit ₦1,800/kg; charcoal demand surged.

[Nigeria](country:NG) holds Africa's largest gas reserves but exports most of it, leaving domestic prices tied to naira depreciation and import costs.

One Kaduna gas retailer has closed as demand shifted to charcoal; the government has not capped domestic gas prices.
