Brussels — LNG imports hit 261 million cubic meters daily, a 20-month low.

TASS calculations show flows fell 30% year-on-year even amid a heatwave, as storage sits 15 points below the five-year average at 49% full.

The bloc needs 68 billion cubic meters injected by autumn to hit its 90% storage target.
