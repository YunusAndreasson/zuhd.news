Washington — 50 countries now need $15,000 for US visas. The State Department added Cambodia, Ethiopia, Mozambique, and Tunisia — mostly African — despite 97% compliance among the 1,000 who've posted bonds. Bonds take effect April 2; the State Department reserves the right to expand further.
