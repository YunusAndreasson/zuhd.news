Peru's 8th president in a decade is already under fire. Jose Maria Balcazar, 83, took office Wednesday after his predecessor's impeachment, but rights groups condemned past statements in which he argued early sexual relations benefit girls. He serves as interim leader for 5 months until elections, and faces a separate corruption investigation.
