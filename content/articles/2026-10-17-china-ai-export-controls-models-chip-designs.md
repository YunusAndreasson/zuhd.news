Beijing — The draft list covers 23 AI technologies, including model weights.

Models of 2 trillion-plus parameters trained on "important data," like Kimi K3, would need export licences.

Open-source code already public is exempt, and customers using models through apps or websites are not covered.

[China](country:CN)'s Commerce Ministry says the controls target misuse, not any country, region or entity.

Exporters must register covered deals, including transfers underway, under a temporary rule that took effect Friday.
