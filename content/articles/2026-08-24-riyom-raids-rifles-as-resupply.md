Riyom — Gunmen killed two operatives and took their rifles.

Each captured rifle arms the attackers' next raid on the same farming villages.

The Sunday attack on Tanjol and Tarai follows an August 4 ambush nearby that also ended in stolen service rifles.

Security agencies have deployed reinforcements but not identified who carried out the attack.
