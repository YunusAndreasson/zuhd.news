Batavia — A 25-year physics anomaly has been calculated away.

New lattice calculations of the muon's magnetic moment resolve the one-in-a-million gap once read as a hint of undiscovered particles.

The fix creates a fresh conflict: the new hadronic calculation now disagrees with older, separately measured results.
