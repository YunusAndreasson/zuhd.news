Melbourne — Mitsubishi signed Japan's first major warship export contract. Australia pays A$10B for 10 Mogami frigates, formalizing Tokyo's shift from postwar pacifism to tier-one Indo-Pacific arms supplier. The first 3 ships arrive in 2029; 8 more will be built in Western Australia.
