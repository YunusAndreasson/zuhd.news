Port Harcourt — One oil treaty dispute exposed [Nigeria](country:NG) to over $2 billion, its attorney-general said.

Roundtable participants warn such claims deter Nigeria from forcing oil majors to clean up the Niger Delta.

Investor-state arbitration lets foreign firms challenge regulation under treaties like the 1992 Nigeria-Netherlands agreement.

Participants want the federal government to review its treaties and drop arbitration clauses from new ones.
