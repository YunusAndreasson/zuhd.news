Jenin — Journalist Mujahed al-Saadi says [Israel](country:IL) held him 22 months without charge or trial.

He writes that soldiers beat him in front of his family when they raided his home on 19 September 2024.

Israel held him under administrative detention, which requires no charge, until his release on 14 July 2026.

The independent outlet publishing his account says nearly 40 Palestinian journalists remain jailed, mostly in administrative detention.
