Washington — Supreme Court shields Monsanto from cancer lawsuits, 7-2.

Roundup's Environmental Protection Agency label carries no cancer warning; federal pesticide law preempts state failure-to-warn suits, the court ruled.

Bayer faces thousands of pending Roundup suits and will seek dismissal under Thursday's ruling.
