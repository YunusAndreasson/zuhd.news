San Francisco — GitHub shipped official stacked PR support via gh-stack. Stacked PRs let developers submit a chain of small, dependent changes instead of a single massive diff that reviewers won't read. With 389 upvotes on Hacker News, adoption will pressure teams to establish new code-review norms for dependent chains.
