New Delhi — 8 people are jailed over stolen temple donations.

[India](country:IN)'s Supreme Court ordered a status report from investigators and put the temple trust on notice.

Petitioners want a Central Bureau of Investigation probe and forensic audit of the trust's gold, cash and digital funds.
