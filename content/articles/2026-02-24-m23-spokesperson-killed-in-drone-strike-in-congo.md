Congo killed the March 23 Movement's spokesman by drone. The group, which UN experts say is backed by Rwanda, holds Rubaya's coltan mine — source of roughly 15% of the world's tantalum, a mineral in every smartphone. Strikes hit 3 more positions across Kalehe the same morning, widening an offensive in a war that has displaced 7 million.
