Kronstadt — Ukraine set Russia's Boikiy warship ablaze in Kronstadt dry dock with drone strikes.

[Ukraine](country:UA) said the vessel had been escorting [Russia](country:RU)'s sanctioned oil tankers through the Baltic.

The attack puts the entire Baltic Fleet within Ukraine's demonstrated drone strike range.
