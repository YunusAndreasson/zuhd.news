Kyiv — [Russia](country:RU) struck a Kharkiv power plant after Trump's truce, Zelenskyy says.

[Ukraine](country:UA) faces winter with power cuts every day, Zelenskyy said.

Trump announced it on Truth Social before either side confirmed, and a White House official said it covers only refineries.

Zelenskyy said Russian President Vladimir Putin "doesn't read Truth Social."

Zelenskyy says Ukraine will respond if Russia does not agree to an energy ceasefire this winter.
