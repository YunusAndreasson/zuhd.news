Beijing — Kuaishou's users hit a record as revenue fell 11.1%.

[China](country:CN)'s second-tier internet platforms are losing pricing power over both advertisers and paying users.

ByteDance's free short-drama app Hongguo reached 168 million daily users in July, more than four long-video platforms combined, QuestMobile data show.

Bilibili was the exception, growing revenue 8% as advertising growth slowed from 30% to 20%.

Weibo's chief executive expects last year's delivery price war to keep dragging fourth-quarter comparisons.
