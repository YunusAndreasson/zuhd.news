Kyiv — Ruslan Kravchenko signed charges against his own anti-corruption chief.

The case tests whether Ukraine's watchdogs survive an attack from the prosecutor's own office.

Western donors created Ukraine's anti-corruption bureau and prosecutor's office as loan conditions, shielded by law from Kravchenko's office.

Parliament must still vote on Kravchenko's own resignation, submitted after investigators searched his office.
