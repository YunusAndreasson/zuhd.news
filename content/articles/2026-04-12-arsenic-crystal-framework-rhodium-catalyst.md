Published — Arsenic stabilizes rhodium, a $4,500-per-ounce metal driving industrial synthesis of millions of tons of chemicals annually. Researchers lined metal-organic framework pores with arsenic atoms, locking rhodium in place and blocking deactivation. The technique cuts how much rhodium each reaction cycle requires.
