Islamabad — Pakistan asked Washington for $10 billion in dollars.

The request tests whether mediating the Iran war buys Islamabad relief its economy needs badly.

An Exchange Stabilisation Fund line skips IMF conditions, unlike Pakistan's existing swap with China's central bank.

Past Fed swap lines went to allies, not the neediest, so the price is unwritten.
