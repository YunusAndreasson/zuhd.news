Peñas Blancas — Gangs robbed at least 156 truckloads in [Costa Rica](country:CR) in 2025.

Private security adds up to 25% to the cost of each container.

Thieves smash cab windows, hold pistols or machetes to drivers and strip trucks while they wait at the [Nicaragua](country:NI) border.

"They rob us while we wait," said Irvin Pérez, a Panamanian trucker.

Queues at Peñas Blancas run 15 to 20 kilometres and can take four days to clear.
