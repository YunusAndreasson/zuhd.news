Tehran — Iran threatened to shut off Gulf states' desalination water. Iran's military command Khatam Al-Anbiya warned that US airstrikes on power plants would trigger attacks on energy and desalination infrastructure supplying drinking water to millions across the region. Trump's 48-hour deadline for Iran to fully reopen the Strait of Hormuz expires March 23 — Iran has pledged to keep it closed until any destroyed power plants are rebuilt.
