Anthropic must drop its AI safety limits by Friday. US Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth threatened to invoke the Defense Production Act — a Cold War-era law — if the company refuses unrestricted military use of its models. If the Pentagon compels compliance, every AI company's safety commitments become conditional on government approval.
