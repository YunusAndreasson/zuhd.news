At least 6,000 people died in 3 days. The Rapid Support Forces and allied Janjaweed militias overran Al Fasher, the capital of North Darfur, on October 26 after an 18-month siege. Fighters executed about 600 people, including 50 children, at Al Fasher University facilities and killed 460 patients and staff at the Saudi Maternity Hospital. United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights Volker Türk said investigators found reasonable grounds that the Rapid Support Forces committed war crimes and crimes against humanity. Thousands remain missing.
