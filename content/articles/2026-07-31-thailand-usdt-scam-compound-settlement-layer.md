Bangkok — Scam compounds hold workers trafficked for the job.

[Thailand](country:TH) is moving against Tether, the stablecoin that settles scam proceeds from Myanmar and Cambodian border compounds without touching a bank.

The public blockchain leaves flows traceable, but cash-out brokers remain the enforcement gap regulators haven't closed.
