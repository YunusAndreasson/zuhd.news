Hezbollah's chief called the disarmament plan a grave sin. Lebanon announced a 4-month timeline to dismantle the group's arsenals between the Litani and Awali rivers, extending disarmament 40 kilometers north of the ceasefire zone. The group has not said whether it will comply, and previous deadlines have passed without action.
