Doha — Gulf states are designing a security architecture that includes [Iran](country:IR).

[Qatar](country:QA)'s premier told the Financial Times that any Hormuz management model must include Iran and [Oman](country:OM) — moving beyond the post-1990 framework anchored on [US](country:US) guarantees.

Qatar will not normalize energy operations until Hormuz is secured, the premier said.
