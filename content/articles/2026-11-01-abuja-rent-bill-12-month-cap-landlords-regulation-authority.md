Abuja — Tenants can face upfront bills of millions of naira.

A bill in [Nigeria](country:NG)'s House of Representatives would cap compulsory advance rent at 12 months.

A new authority could order refunds within 30 days, and landlords would face fines of ₦500,000 to ₦5 million.

The bill sets no rent ceiling and no cap on agency fees, which reach 40% of rent for some.

Sponsors Paul Nnamchi and Ahmed Doro passed first reading on 22 October, and the bill still needs further readings.
