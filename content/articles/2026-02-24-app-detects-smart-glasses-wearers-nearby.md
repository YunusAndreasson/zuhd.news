Meta Ray-Bans broadcast a detectable Bluetooth signal. A sociologist built Nearby Glasses, a free app that scans for those signals and alerts you when it finds recording-capable eyewear. The code is open-source on GitHub, inviting anyone to extend detection as smart glasses proliferate.
