London — Robots get a planner-independent task interface.

Labs can swap automated planners through one common API.

UPF4ROS2 plugs the Unified Planning Framework into ROS 2, and was tested in PlanSys2 and MERLIN2.

The accepted paper is an early release and will be replaced by its final version.
