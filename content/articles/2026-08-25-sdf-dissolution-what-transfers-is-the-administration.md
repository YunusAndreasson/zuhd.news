Damascus — The SDF dissolved itself as an independent force.

Its own conscription, courts and prisons across the northeast now transfer to Damascus or cease.

Leader Mazloum Abdi completed a March deal merging tens of thousands of fighters into [Syria](country:SY)'s national army.

The fate of roughly 10,000 ISIS detainees the SDF guarded remains unaddressed.
