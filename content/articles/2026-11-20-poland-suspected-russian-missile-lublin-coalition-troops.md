Chmielek — A suspected Russian projectile burned in a Polish field.

It landed 60 miles from British and allied troops training for a postwar Ukraine mission.

Ukrainian monitors tracked a KH-101 cruise missile toward [Poland](country:PL) minutes before alerts reached residents of two border provinces.

A source in the central administration told broadcaster Onet it was likely a drone.

Poland's military is still checking radar records for a second possible violation near Rudnik nad Sanem.
