United States — Signal blocks screenshots by default on iOS 27.

Journalists, officials and dissidents rely on Signal, and a screen-reading assistant sees messages after decryption.

On Android, Signal switches on Screen Security when it detects an AI assistant reading the screen, and users can switch it off.

Signal says blocking screenshots does not stop agents reading content stored on the device through the filesystem.

Signal is asking operating system makers for a documented way to opt apps out of AI data access.
