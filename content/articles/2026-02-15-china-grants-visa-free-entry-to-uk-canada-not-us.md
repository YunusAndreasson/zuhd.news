US nationals remain banned while allies gain access. Beijing granted 30-day visa-free entry to British and Canadian visitors starting Tuesday, adding them to the 77 countries that already include Australia and New Zealand from the Five Eyes intelligence alliance. Canada dropped its 100% tariff on Chinese electric vehicles before the announcement, isolating Washington from its closest partners on both trade and travel.
