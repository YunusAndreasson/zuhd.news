UK and Canadian citizens enter China visa-free starting Tuesday. Beijing announced both countries' passport holders can stay up to 30 days without a visa, joining Australia and New Zealand who gained the same access in 2024. The US remains the only member of the Five Eyes intelligence alliance requiring a visa, after Canadian and British prime ministers both visited Beijing in recent weeks.
