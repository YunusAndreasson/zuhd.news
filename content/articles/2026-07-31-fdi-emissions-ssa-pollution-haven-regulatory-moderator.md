Accra — Foreign investment raises emissions across 42 countries.

A panel study spanning 2005-2023 finds environmental regulation significantly cuts that effect, strongest in West and East Africa, researchers report; the manuscript is unedited.

The proxy for "regulation" measures stated policy, not enforcement — the gap the finding rests on.
