Delhi — 'Hostel Daze' collapsed Sunday afternoon, killing one student.

Dozens more students from across [India](country:IN) remain trapped beneath the rubble.

Locals say basement construction and a weakened foundation preceded the five-storey building's collapse after days of heavy rain.

Police have identified the building's owner and are searching for him.
