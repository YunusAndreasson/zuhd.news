Cairo — [Egypt](country:EG)'s Interior Ministry admitted holding 6 Matsda2sh journalists.

Their lawyers still do not know where they are held.

The ministry accuses them of Muslim Brotherhood membership, and the Supreme State Security Prosecution, which handles terrorism cases, has not yet seen them.

"Why are they now saying that they are Brotherhood?" asked founder Abdel Rahman Mansour, who calls the claim untrue.

Lawyers from the Egyptian Initiative for Personal Rights wait outside prosecution headquarters, denied entry.
