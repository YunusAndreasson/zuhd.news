Karachi — The State Bank held its rate at 11.5%, as expected.

Governor Jameel Ahmed's committee is betting Gulf-driven inflation is imported, not domestic — a rate that mainly rations investment and services state debt.

The rupee absorbs the cost if that bet is wrong, alongside Fed and Singapore moves this same week.
