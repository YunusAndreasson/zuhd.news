Wuhan — Baidu's AI agent turns residents' group chats into work orders.

The Qiaokou district pilot covers 1 million people, and no report says whether residents consented.

The agent analyses posts in neighbourhood grid chats and sends the resulting work orders to the street office, Baidu said.

[China](country:CN)'s National Data Administration says daily token use passed 140 trillion by March, over 1,000 times early 2024.

Whether residents can opt out of the analysis is unanswered.
