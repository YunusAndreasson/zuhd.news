London — A virtual power plant scheduler raised simulated revenue 18%.

It also cut frequency-regulation tracking deviation 42%, the accepted manuscript reports.

Its quantum circuits ran on classical hardware, using public US grid and household datasets.

The authors concede the model ignores decoherence, gate errors and readout noise of current quantum hardware.
