Shanghai — 4 in 5 dealers refuse a 5-year-old electric car.

Unpriceable used EVs raise the cost of leasing and financing new ones too.

Battery warranties rarely transfer fully to a second owner, and no accepted test certifies a used pack's remaining health.

Chinese used EVs are flowing to Central Asia and Africa, where no replacement pack exists.
