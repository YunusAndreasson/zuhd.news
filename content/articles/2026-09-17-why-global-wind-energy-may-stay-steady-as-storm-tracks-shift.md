London — Storm tracks are shifting toward Earth's poles.

Global wind power investment assumes a resource map that climate warming is now redrawing.

New modeling finds the total global wind-energy resource holds roughly steady even as storms migrate poleward.

Which regions gain or lose wind resource under the model was not detailed.
