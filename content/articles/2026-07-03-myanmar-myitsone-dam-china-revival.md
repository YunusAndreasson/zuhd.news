Myitkyina — [Myanmar](country:MM)'s junta revives a dam its own people killed in 2011.

[China](country:CN) is pushing to resume the $3.6 billion Myitsone project, which would flood Kachin land and send 90% of its power north.

Displaced communities and downstream farmers face the cost as the junta and Beijing finalize financing terms.
