London — 27 studies show gaps in vaccine evidence.

Patients without a spleen face outsized risk of invasive pneumococcal disease, so the review checked whether conjugate vaccines still protect them.

Researchers found no data on optimal vaccine timing around splenectomy, a gap still unresolved.
