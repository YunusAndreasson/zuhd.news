750 vessels are stranded outside the Strait of Hormuz. Iran's Revolutionary Guard declared it will fire on any vessel attempting passage, severing the route that carries 21% of the world's daily oil supply. India and Japan hold roughly 90 days of strategic petroleum reserves — their buffer if Hormuz stays closed.
