Brooklyn — Asif Merchant received 330 months for plotting to kill Donald Trump.

The Justice Department says Iran's Revolutionary Guards recruited the Pakistani national in 2022 and trained him in Tehran.

He paid undercover federal agents posing as hitmen after an acquaintance he approached reported him and became an informant.

[Iran](country:IR) denies plotting against Trump, and Merchant testified he joined the plot unwillingly to protect his family.

The sentence lands as US-Israeli attacks on Iran, begun 28 February, have killed thousands.
