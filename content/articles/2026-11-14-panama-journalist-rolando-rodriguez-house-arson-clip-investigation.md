San Miguelito — An arsonist burned two cars at a reporter's home at 2:30 a.m.

Colleagues call it the first such attack on a journalist in [Panama](country:PA).

Rolando Rodríguez of La Prensa was preparing a new story on businessman Roberto Carretero, whose lawyer wrote the paper Friday objecting.

A neighbour's camera filmed a man carrying a backpack, and no source names a suspect or motive.
