London — Britain's FCA handed Palantir its fraud and money-laundering intelligence. The US firm already holds £500 million in UK public contracts spanning the NHS, military, and police; it was co-founded by Peter Thiel, a major donor to US President Donald Trump. A 3-month trial will determine whether Palantir wins a full contract covering the 42,000 firms the FCA regulates.
