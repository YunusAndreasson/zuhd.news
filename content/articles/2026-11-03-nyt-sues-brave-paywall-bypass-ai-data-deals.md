New York — NYT alleges Brave made $100 million reselling its journalism.

The suit names Anthropic, Perplexity, Mistral, xAI and Cohere as buyers of that content.

The suit says Brave's browser bypasses paywalls by default and its search API sells that content to AI companies.

The Times seeks a permanent injunction, and 3 newspaper companies filed a similar suit Monday.
