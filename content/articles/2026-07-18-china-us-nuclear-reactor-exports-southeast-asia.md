Jakarta — A reactor choice locks a nation in for 50 years.

Washington and Beijing are both courting Southeast Asian states weighing their first nuclear plants, since a vendor pick fixes decades of fuel supply and technical dependency.

[Indonesia](country:ID), [Vietnam](country:VN) and the Philippines must each choose a supplier neither superpower will let them walk back easily.
