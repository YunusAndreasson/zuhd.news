Iran threatened to black out 3 Gulf states. Ali Larijani, secretary of Iran's Supreme National Security Council, warned that a US strike on Iran's power grid would cut electricity to Qatar, UAE, and Bahrain within 30 minutes — US military bases sit in all 3. US President Donald Trump threatened to destroy Iran's electricity "within one hour" and said the US must "finish the job."
