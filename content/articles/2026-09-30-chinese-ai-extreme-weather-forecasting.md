Beijing — AI models missed the flash flood that killed 32.

[China](country:CN)'s Mazu early-warning system already serves over two dozen countries.

Conventional forecasts solve atmospheric physics for hours on supercomputers, while AI models reanalyse existing forecast data in minutes on a thousandth of the compute.

A government report on the Gansu flood said accurate predictions need monitoring in remote mountain blind spots.

Sudden localised storms remain the test: Typhoon White Dolphin broke Henan rainfall records on 9 August.
