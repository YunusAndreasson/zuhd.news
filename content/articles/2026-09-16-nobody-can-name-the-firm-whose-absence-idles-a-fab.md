Vilnius — A Soviet-era physics program now guards Taiwan's chips.

A few small Lithuanian firms could idle chip fabs if they vanished.

The lasers cut and anneal silicon wafers, tasks [Taiwan](country:TW)'s fabs cannot source from many other suppliers.

[China](country:CN) cut [Lithuania](country:LT) from its customs system after Vilnius opened a Taiwan office in 2021.
