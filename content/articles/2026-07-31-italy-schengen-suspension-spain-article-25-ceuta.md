Rome — Italy reimposed border checks against Spain, not migrants.

Giorgia Meloni cited "national security" after 60,000 crossed into Ceuta from Morocco; 41 died breaching the border, officials said.

[Spain](country:ES)'s Pedro Sánchez calls it a treaty breach; France has also tightened checks on its own Spanish border.
