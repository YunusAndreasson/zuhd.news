London — Owning strangulation pornography becomes a crime in England Friday.

The offences also cover family role play and performers who are or pretend to be under 16.

Possession carries 2 years for strangulation or familial content, 3 for under-16 content, and publication up to 5.

Courts will define strangulation and suffocation case by case once the Crime and Policing Act takes effect.
