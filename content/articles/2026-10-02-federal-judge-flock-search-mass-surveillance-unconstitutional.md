Tulsa — A judge called a warrantless Flock plate search unconstitutional.

It is among the first [US](country:US) federal rulings to find Flock searches unconstitutional.

A deputy searched a California plate in Flock's network, then used the driver's travel history to justify searching her car.

Judge Sara Hill ordered all Flock and vehicle-search evidence thrown out, but her ruling sets no binding precedent.

Several other cases nationwide are weighing whether warrantless plate-reader searches are legal.
