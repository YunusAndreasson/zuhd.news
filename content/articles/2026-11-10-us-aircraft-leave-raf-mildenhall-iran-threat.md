Mildenhall — Flight tracking shows 12 [US](country:US) aircraft left RAF Mildenhall Monday.

It is the second British base the US has emptied of aircraft over [Iran](country:IR), after Fairford.

A US official said Mildenhall and Lakenheath moved to Force Protection Condition Charlie, the Pentagon's second-highest threat level.

[Britain](country:GB)'s defence ministry calls the moves routine and declines to comment on other nations' activity.

US officials have not settled what the threat is, and London has not said.
