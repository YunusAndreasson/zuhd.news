Utilities rose 6.8% in Argentina as Milei cut subsidies. Falling overall inflation — from 200% to 33% annually — masked accelerating costs for utilities and food, the essentials poorest households cannot avoid. Milei has forecast monthly inflation below 1% by August, with his government's own data still showing essential costs rising.
