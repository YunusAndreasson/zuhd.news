Washington — Trump calls the midterms dishonest after losing both chambers.

He told The New York Times on election night he would accept the results.

On Truth Social he alleged, without evidence, that large early-voting dumps made Republican victories impossible and blamed voting machines.

Trump conceded last week that the Iran war drove up gasoline prices, a key voter grievance.

Democrats now hold the [US](country:US) Senate and House for the remaining 2 years of his term.
