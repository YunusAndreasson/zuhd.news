Ferrol — SAIC's EU plant is a tariff bypass.

[China](country:CN)'s SAIC Motor chose [Spain](country:ES) to convert EU electric-vehicle tariffs into a domestic-production exemption; the Ferrol factory would create 1,000 jobs.

The €200 million project needs Madrid's foreign-investment approval before a 2027 groundbreaking; operations start in 2028.
