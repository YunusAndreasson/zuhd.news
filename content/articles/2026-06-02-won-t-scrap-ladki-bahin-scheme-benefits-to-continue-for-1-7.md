Mumbai — Maharashtra dropped 7 million women from its welfare rolls.

Government blamed fraud — enrolled men, ineligible incomes — but opposition in [India](country:IN) said a fiscal squeeze drove the 29% cut.

The government said it will not claw back payments from the removed; no reinstatement process was announced.
