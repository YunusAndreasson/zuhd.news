Astana — [Russia](country:RU) will finance 85% of [Kazakhstan](country:KZ)'s first nuclear plant.

Rosatom would build and co-finance the $16.4 billion facility, making Moscow both creditor and constructor of Kazakhstan's power infrastructure.

No formal agreement has been signed and no construction timeline was announced.
