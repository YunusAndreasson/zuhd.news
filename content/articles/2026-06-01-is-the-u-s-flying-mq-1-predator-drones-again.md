Washington — US Central Command struck an Iranian radar site after Iran downed a drone over the Gulf.

The Pentagon declined to confirm the airframe type — whether a retired MQ-1 Predator or an active MQ-9 Reaper.

Dozens of Reapers lost to Iran and Houthis since 2023 have raised questions about whether retired airframes are being reactivated.
