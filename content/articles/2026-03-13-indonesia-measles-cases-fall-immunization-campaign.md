511 measles cases threaten Indonesia's Eid holiday travel season. The health ministry is vaccinating in 50 districts before Eid, when millions travel and 1 case can spread to 18 contacts. If coverage falls short of 95% before Eid, holiday gatherings could trigger a nationwide outbreak.
