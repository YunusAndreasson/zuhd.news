A dust storm made Jerusalem the most polluted city on Earth. The storm from northern Africa pushed the city's air quality index to 777, compared with its usual reading of 56, according to monitoring service IQAir. Particulate matter reached 93 times the World Health Organization's guideline level. Israel ranked as the most polluted country in the world on Saturday as the storm spread across the region.
