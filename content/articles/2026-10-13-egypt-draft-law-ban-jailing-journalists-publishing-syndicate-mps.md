Cairo — 26 journalists are detained in Egypt over their work.

[Egypt](country:EG)'s constitution bars jailing journalists for publishing, but no law enforces it.

Prosecutors use counterterrorism, cybercrime and telecommunications laws instead of content offences, defence lawyer Nabih al-Genady said.

MP Atef Maghawry is gathering support to bring a decade-old draft ban to parliament's general session within weeks.
