Kuala Lumpur — Najib Razak left Kajang prison Friday for house arrest.

[Malaysia](country:MY)'s governing party, the United Malays National Organisation, paid the $12.3 million fine his pardon required.

House arrest covers only his reduced 6-year sentence, not a separate 15-year term he is appealing.

Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim said he disagreed with the party paying, but called it the party's right.

If his appeal on the 15-year sentence fails, Najib returns to prison.
