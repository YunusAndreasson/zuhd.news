London — An activity-recognition model shrank 81.7%.

Wearables can classify motion on a microcontroller, with no cloud connection.

The framework gives high-entropy sensor signals more precision and cuts inference energy from 1.07 to 0.47 millijoules.

The paper is an accepted manuscript, and its final version may change.
