Guatemala City — Ash rose 6,000 metres as Fuego entered a "more explosive phase."

[Guatemala](country:GT) raised its second-highest alert and evacuated villages; the volcano's real danger is fast-moving pyroclastic flows down its ravines, not the slow lava.

The 2018 eruption killed 215 with less warning; monitors now watch which drainages the flows follow.
