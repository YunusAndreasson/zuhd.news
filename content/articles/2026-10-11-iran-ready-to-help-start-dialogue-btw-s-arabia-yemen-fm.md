Tehran — [Iran](country:IR)'s foreign minister phoned his Saudi counterpart, Iranian state agency Mehr reported.

Abbas Araghchi told [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s Faisal bin Farhan Tehran will give "any assistance" to start talks with Sanaa.

In the Sunday-night call, Araghchi said there is no military solution and insisting on one would complicate the situation.

Riyadh's reply to the offer is not in Mehr's report.
