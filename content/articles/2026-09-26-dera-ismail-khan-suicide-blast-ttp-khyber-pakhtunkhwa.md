Dera Ismail Khan — A tractor-trolley bomb killed at least 11 people.

The dead include 2 women and a child, Rescue 1122 said.

A suicide bomber drove the explosives-laden trolley into a joint police and customs checkpost on the Dera Ismail Khan–Quetta road.

Police have not confirmed the Pakistani Taliban's reported claim, and bomb disposal teams are still examining the site.
