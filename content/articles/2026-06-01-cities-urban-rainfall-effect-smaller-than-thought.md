Sydney — New study shrinks cities' role in local flooding.

Urban heat islands and aerosols nudge storm behaviour, but less than the flood-planning models that shaped infrastructure investment assumed.

Flood-risk models using the higher estimates may overstate infrastructure needs in megacities.
