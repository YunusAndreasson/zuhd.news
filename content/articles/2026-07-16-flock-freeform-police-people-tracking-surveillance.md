Washington — Cops searched Flock cameras for "black sweatshirt."

Flock's "FreeForm" tool lets police search camera networks by clothing, tattoos or race, not just license plates, the ACLU says.

Civil liberties groups call it a bait-and-switch: a tool sold to catch stolen cars, now searching for people.
