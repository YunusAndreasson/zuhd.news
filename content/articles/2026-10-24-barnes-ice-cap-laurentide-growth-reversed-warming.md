Baffin Island — Barnes Ice Cap began regrowing 2,000 years ago, a Science study finds.

Warming ended growth by 1960, and none of the ice now sits above the snow line.

Cold-based ice froze onto bedrock, preserving moss that radiocarbon dating shows died as the margin advanced.

A full ice age was still tens of thousands of years off, lead author Nicolás Young says.

Young's team may sample 2,000-year-old moss in 20 years, as the margin retreats.
