Shanghai — A chip fund made its first software investment.

Cardinal Operations raised several hundred million yuan for solvers that schedule power grids and airlines.

Solvers handle discrete choices such as generator start-ups, which language models failed to do reliably in Cardinal's tests.

Its Tianquan solver, which beat Gurobi by 14% in grid tests, now runs [China](country:CN)'s southern power market.
