Abuja — President Tinubu seeks $20.5 billion and €1.04 billion in foreign loans.

The money would fund infrastructure, agriculture, health, education, water, security and jobs.

The loans fall under [Nigeria](country:NG)'s 2026-2027 Supplementary External Borrowing Plan, and Tinubu says technical and economic evaluations selected the projects.

Senate President Godswill Akpabio gave the debt and appropriations committees one week to report back.
