Le Monde found France's nuclear carrier by tracking crew Strava routes. 8 years after Strava exposed secret US military bases globally, militaries still permit crew members to use the fitness app. The Charles de Gaulle is now deployed in the Iran war region; its real-time position is visible to any Strava user.
