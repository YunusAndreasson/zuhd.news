Pensacola — More than 3 million people face 120 mph Isaias.

Mandatory evacuation orders cover Panhandle counties, and Alabama's governor told tourists to leave immediately.

Producers shut in 25% of [US](country:US) Gulf oil output, 511,000 barrels a day, the Marine Minerals Administration said Wednesday.

S&P Global Energy's Jim Burkhard says other hurricanes cut more output, and a direct hit on refineries worries him more.

The National Hurricane Center expects landfall late Friday, then a downgrade to tropical storm by Saturday afternoon.
