Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) summoned [Norway](country:NO)'s envoy over Starlink, state-affiliated Mehr News Agency reported.

Tehran says the [US](country:US) and Israel use Starlink for interventionist actions against it.

Iran says Norway, Starlink's registry state at the International Telecommunication Union, must block transmissions under Radio Regulations Board decisions.

Mehr's report carries no response from Norway or SpaceX to the protest note.
