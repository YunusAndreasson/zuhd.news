10 workers died in a Daejeon auto parts factory fire Friday. 200 kilograms of reactive sodium blocked firefighter entry for 9 hours, trapping workers on the 3rd floor with no exit. Overnight searches resumed after engineers cleared the building; South Korea's workplace safety authority launched an investigation into its 3rd major industrial fire in 5 years.
