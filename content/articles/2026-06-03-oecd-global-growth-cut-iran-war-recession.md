Paris — OECD cut 2026 global growth forecast to 2.8%.

[Iran](country:IR) war disruptions to the Strait of Hormuz drove Asian gas prices up 81% and European gas up 43%, the OECD said.

If disruptions persist into 2027, global growth falls to 1.8%, with some economies in recession, the OECD warned.
