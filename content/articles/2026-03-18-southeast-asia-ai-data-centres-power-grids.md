AI servers may consume 30% of Southeast Asian electricity. Singapore, 1 degree from the equator, hosts 70 facilities and 1.4 gigawatts of data-centre capacity in air that never drops below 25°C. Governments are offering tax breaks and fast permits to major cloud providers but have yet to build matching grid capacity.
