Cairo — Citizen journalists in three states self-censor to survive.

A study of [Egypt](country:EG), [Iran](country:IR) and [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) found vague laws and digital monitoring push reporters to silence themselves before posting.

Researchers call the pattern a governance tool, not a response to unrest.
