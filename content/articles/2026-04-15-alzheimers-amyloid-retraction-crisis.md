Boston — Another amyloid paper retracted, this one from 2011. Multiple retractions now undermine the hypothesis behind 4 decades of Alzheimer's drug development, including FDA approvals that caused fatal brain bleeding. No replacement hypothesis has clinical proof; inflammation, viral, and microbiome theories await larger trials.
