Edwards — A B-52 crashed at Edwards, killing 8.

The aircraft was testing the [US](country:US) Air Force's Radar Modernization Program, already years behind schedule and over budget, Col. James Hayes confirmed.

Boeing confirmed 2 of its employees died; the Air Force has opened an investigation into the crash.
