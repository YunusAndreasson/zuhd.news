London — Cation length governs CO2 fixation in ionic liquids.

Shorter and medium alkyl chains lower activation energy for CO2-to-carbonate conversion, the study found — turning waste gas into industrial feedstock.

The results are computational; the study recommends 2- and 3-carbon imidazolium chains for experimental validation.
