Nigeria began evacuating citizens from Iran. Embassy officials are escorting Nigerians across the Armenian border, joining Pakistan, Singapore, and the Philippines in pulling workers whose remittances sustain families back home. The government reported no casualties but has not disclosed how many citizens remain in Iran.
