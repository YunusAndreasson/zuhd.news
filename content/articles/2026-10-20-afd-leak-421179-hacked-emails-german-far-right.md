Berlin — Hackers published 421,179 internal AfD emails Monday.

The 281 GB archive exposes the internal correspondence of [Germany](country:DE)'s far-right party.

Messages from party supporters include racist slurs, antisemitic conspiracy theories and Nazi salutes, according to an analysis of the archive.

The analysis does not say whether the AfD or prosecutors have verified the emails.
