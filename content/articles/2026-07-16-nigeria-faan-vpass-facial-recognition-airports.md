Lagos — Domestic flyers will register their face to fly.

The Federal Airports Authority is deploying V-Pass, which enrolls passengers via national ID and facial capture and checks them again before boarding, built with Verxid Technologies.

The authority says the data stays off local kiosks, but has not named an independent oversight body.
