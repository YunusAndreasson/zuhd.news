Yongin — SK Hynix's first fab here will stand 150 metres tall.

[South Korea](country:KR)'s memory chipmaker is building a $450 billion, 4-fab complex to keep pace with AI demand.

It will also spend 1.4 trillion won ($1.05 billion) over 5 years backing domestic suppliers, Reuters reported, and build them a testing line.

The 874.6 billion won testing facility, built with the government, opens next year inside the Yongin complex.
