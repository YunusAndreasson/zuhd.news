Lebanon's parliament extended its mandate without a public vote. Lebanon's political class has postponed elections repeatedly since 2013, always citing crises; the current Israeli bombardment is the newest justification. Elections are now deferred until 2028; the same lawmakers who voted for the delay will remain in power.
