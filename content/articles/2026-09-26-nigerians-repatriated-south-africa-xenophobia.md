Lagos — 105 more Nigerians landed from [South Africa](country:ZA) on Thursday.

About 1,640 have been evacuated since xenophobic attacks on Black foreigners began in April.

The Redeemed Christian Church of God paid for 100 of the 105 one-way tickets, the foreign ministry said.

Investigators have made no arrests in any of at least 5 killings of Nigerians since April.
