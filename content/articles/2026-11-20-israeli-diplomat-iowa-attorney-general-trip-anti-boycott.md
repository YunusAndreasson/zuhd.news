Des Moines — [Israel](country:IL) offered Iowa's attorney general a fully paid trip.

Emails released under Iowa's open records law show the offer came first and the request weeks later.

The consul asked Brenna Bird to invoke Iowa's anti-boycott law against Turkey, and she signed a statement denouncing ICC prosecutions.

The emails do not show whether Bird accepted the trip or opened an investigation.

Quincy Institute analyst Ben Freeman says complying would raise Foreign Agents Registration Act concerns.
