Brussels — Trump predicts a "very big" [Russia](country:RU) deal covering all of Europe.

Spokeswoman Paula Pinho said the EU decides matters that affect its own security.

Envoy Jared Kushner says the [US](country:US) is working on a broader European security architecture, with NATO still central to collective defence.

Kushner says Europeans get direct participation only if the Abu Dhabi talks make progress.

The US-mediated Abu Dhabi talks begin next week, after rounds in Miami, Oslo and Brussels.
