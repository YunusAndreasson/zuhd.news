Copenhagen — 10,650 more Europeans died than expected in June.

Mortality monitor EuroMOMO found over 9,000 of the dead were 65 or older, as heat topped 40°C in [Germany](country:DE), France and Spain.

The WHO's European director warned more deadly heat weeks are already forming over the Atlantic.
