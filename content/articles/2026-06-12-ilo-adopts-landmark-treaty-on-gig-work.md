Geneva — International Labour Organization passed its first binding gig-work treaty.

The 406-8 vote bans contractor misclassification and requires platforms to pay minimum wages, targeting the loophole used to deny protections.

The [US](country:US) and [New Zealand](country:NZ) voted no; countries must ratify before individual workers can sue.
