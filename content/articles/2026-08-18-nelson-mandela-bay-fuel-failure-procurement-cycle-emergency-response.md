Gqeberha — Fire engines could not refuel for at least 24 hours.

It was the metro's third fuel-supply failure since May, risking emergency response citywide.

Officials filed for contract renewal just 30 days before expiry, too late for the mandatory tender period.

A new 6-month contract runs to February, but a permanent supply deal remains unresolved.
