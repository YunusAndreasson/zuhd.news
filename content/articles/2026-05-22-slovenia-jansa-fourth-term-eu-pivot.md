Ljubljana — EU leaders welcomed [Slovenia](country:SI)'s Janez Jansa as premier within hours.

His right-wing party finished 2nd in March but liberal leader Robert Golob failed to form a majority, handing Jansa a 4th term.

The outgoing government recognized Palestine in 2024; Jansa's first EU summit is June.
