Tehran — An 88-day blackout was Iran's longest recorded shutdown.

[Iran](country:IR) has cut national internet access during unrest since 2009, escalating from days in 2019 to weeks in 2025 to 88 days this year.

Iranians who spent months offline have no guarantee the government won't sever access again in the next crackdown.
