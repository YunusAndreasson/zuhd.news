Manila — One nurse can now watch 10 intensive-care patients from a screen abroad.

[US](country:US) hospitals hire Filipino nurses for remote monitoring to plug staffing gaps, letting a wealthy health system draw on trained labor without the nurse ever emigrating.

The arrangement deepens the [Philippines](country:PH)' own nursing shortage as clinicians choose overseas screens over local wards.
