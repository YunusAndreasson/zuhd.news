Kenyan AI trainers earn $1 an hour. The workers formed the Data Labelers Association to demand better pay and mental health care from US companies that hire them to annotate pornography and operate AI sex chatbots. No US company has publicly acknowledged the union's demands, leaving NDA-bound workers with no legal avenue to escalate.
