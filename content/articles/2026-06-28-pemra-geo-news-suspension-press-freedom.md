Islamabad — Geo News goes dark for 15 days.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s media regulator cited content "liable to hurt religious sentiments" — a standard with no defined threshold that applies across the country's major broadcasters.

Geo faces a Council of Complaints hearing that could trigger further regulatory action beyond the suspension.
