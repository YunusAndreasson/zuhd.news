London — 3D-printed ceramics absorb 4,000 times more energy.

Researchers 3D-printed interlocking ceramic blocks bonded by a flexible polymer, mimicking mother-of-pearl's stiffness-toughness balance.

A 4cm tibia-shaped prototype tested successfully, signaling potential for bone implants and armor.
