Germany — Four months of talks ended with Mercedes-Benz halting investment.

Labour director Britta Seeger said plant closures are now an option the company must examine.

Mercedes wants 40-hour weeks without extra pay, against the 35-hour standard collectively agreed for about a fifth of German employees.

Production head Michael Schiebe says the company sees no path to a deal and must act now.
