Washington — Just 10 H5N1 particles can infect a cow, a new study finds.

The virus targets mammary glands rather than airways, making milk — not breath — the transmission route and explaining why farm containment measures failed.

[US](country:US) agriculture officials have not updated containment guidance for hundreds of dairy herds still infected.
