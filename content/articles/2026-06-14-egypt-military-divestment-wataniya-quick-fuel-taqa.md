Cairo — [Egypt](country:EG) sold its first military-owned asset: fuel stations.

Under IMF pressure, the army transferred 172 Wataniya stations to TAQA Arabia — a firm the military has held stakes in since 2023.

Gulf sovereign funds walked away in 2021 citing missing title data; Quick Fuel, the new vehicle, faces the same disclosure questions.
