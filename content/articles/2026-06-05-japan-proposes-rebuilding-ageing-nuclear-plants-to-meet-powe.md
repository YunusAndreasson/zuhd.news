Tokyo — Japan's trade ministry proposed 14 new reactors at existing sites.

[Japan](country:JP) shut all 54 reactors after Fukushima; imported fuel covers 60–70% of generation while AI-driven electricity demand climbs, the ministry said.

The existing-sites approach sidesteps siting approvals; utilities await parliamentary passage of the draft energy plan.
