Germany — Rivian booked $354 million from Volkswagen in one quarter.

Volkswagen is buying in the software its CARIAD unit failed to deliver.

Volkswagen's accounts show CARIAD lost €7 billion over 3 years, yet Volkswagen pays 75% of shared development through 2028.

Rivian told [US](country:US) regulators the licence lets Volkswagen use the technology in rival cars.

Rivian says its main Volkswagen development obligations run until mid-2028.
