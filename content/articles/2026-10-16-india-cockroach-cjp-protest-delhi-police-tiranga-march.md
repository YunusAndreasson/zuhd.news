New Delhi — 6,000 police are deployed against a flag march.

Two leaders of the Cockroach Janta Party were jailed for 5 days after entering a barred protest site.

The movement began over exam paper leaks and now demands [India](country:IN)'s election chief Gyanesh Kumar be removed over voter-roll revisions.

The Delhi High Court on Thursday declined to restrain the march, rejecting a trader's petition.

Founder Abhijeet Dipke says he will risk arrest despite a police notice barring Connaught Place.
