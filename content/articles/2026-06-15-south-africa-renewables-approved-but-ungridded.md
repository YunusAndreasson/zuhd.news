Cape Town — [South Africa](country:ZA) approved 4,861 MW of renewables that cannot connect to the grid.

Eskom's 2025 grid assessment found transmission capacity exhausted in [South Africa](country:ZA)'s 3 best renewable zones, where the cleared projects sit.

Developers are moving to Mpumalanga and Gauteng, where grid capacity exists but renewable resources are weaker.
