Islamabad — Intelligence chief Fuad Khan received a 4-year extension.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s PM Shehbaz Sharif approved the deal; the Intelligence Bureau oversees domestic surveillance and counter-terrorism coordination.

Khan's new contract begins July 14; the Establishment Division gave no official rationale for the unusually long term.
