Cairo — [Egypt](country:EG)'s security arrested prisoner-rights organizers at dawn.

Lawyer Aboul Diyar organized a May 13 exhibition demanding the release of political detainees; authorities charged him with terrorism and publishing false news.

Two co-organizers were released on bail; Aboul Diyar's 15-day remand continues.
