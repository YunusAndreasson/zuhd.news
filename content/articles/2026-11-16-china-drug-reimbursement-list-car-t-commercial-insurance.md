Beijing — A 1.29 million yuan CAR-T therapy now costs 288,600 yuan.

[China](country:CN)'s insurer added 107 drugs, a record 51 of them class-1 innovations.

Basic insurance covers essentials while a commercial list of 28 drugs carries costlier therapies, including 7 CAR-T products.

Only adults who failed second-line therapy qualify, and patients' share is estimated at under 100,000 yuan.

The list takes effect 1 January 2027, and regulators will track real-world haemophilia drug use before renewal.
