Iran began laying mines in the Strait of Hormuz. US intelligence detected the preparations and disclosed them publicly — a deterrence move, as the strait carries 20% of global crude oil. Whether the public warning halted Iran's program remains unconfirmed, with Trump threatening unspecified retaliation.
