Imphal — Gunmen killed 2 Kuki community leaders before dawn.

6 mutilated Naga hostage bodies were recovered the day before in [India](country:IN)'s Manipur, where 14 Kuki civilians also remain held.

A Naga civil body, the United Naga Council, called a 24-hour shutdown; the 14 Kuki captives have been held since May 13.
