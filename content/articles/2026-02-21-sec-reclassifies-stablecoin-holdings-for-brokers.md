Stablecoins now count as capital for US broker-dealers. The SEC issued guidance valuing tokens like USDC at 98% of face value, reversing a policy that counted them as worthless. The informal change could reshape crypto trading from Robinhood to Goldman Sachs, but carries no force of law.
