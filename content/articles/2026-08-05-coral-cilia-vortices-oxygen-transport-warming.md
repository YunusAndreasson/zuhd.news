Honolulu — Coral tissue spins microscopic vortices to breathe.

Beating cilia stir the water layer clinging to a coral's surface, pumping oxygen across a diffusion barrier the animal must actively defeat.

Warming water stalls the stirring, adding a mechanical failure mode alongside symbiont loss during bleaching.
