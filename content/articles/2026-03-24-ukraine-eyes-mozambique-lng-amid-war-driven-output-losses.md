Kyiv — Russian strikes hit Ukraine's gas infrastructure more than 1,700 times since 2022, cutting domestic production by half. Kyiv is now in talks to import from Mozambique's TotalEnergies LNG project — itself threatened by an Islamist insurgency in Cabo Delgado. Rwanda's stabilization force could withdraw from Cabo Delgado if EU funding lapses, threatening the project's 2029 launch date.
