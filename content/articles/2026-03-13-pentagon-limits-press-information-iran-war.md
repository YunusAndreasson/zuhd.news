The Pentagon held 5 briefings on the Iran war. In Iraq and Afghanistan the Pentagon held daily briefings with embedded journalists, but the Iran war has had 3 speakers and no embeds. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth said Friday he will not disclose how many Americans are killed or wounded in Iran.
