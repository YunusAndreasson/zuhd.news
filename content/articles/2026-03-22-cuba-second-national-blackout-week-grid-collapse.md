Havana — Cuba blacked out again, cutting power to 10 million. A US fuel blockade and the Hormuz crisis together cut Cuba off from oil, leaving aging power stations without fuel. Cuba's Deputy Foreign Minister said the political system is non-negotiable as US President Donald Trump demands Cuban President Miguel Díaz-Canel's removal.
