Oman will host talks between the US and Iran in Geneva next week, Switzerland confirmed Saturday. Washington wants Tehran to agree to limits on its nuclear program after two earlier rounds in Abu Dhabi produced no breakthrough. Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi met US envoy Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner, President Donald Trump's son-in-law, in Oman on 6 February in what Araghchi called "a good start." Switzerland, which has represented US consular interests in Iran since the 1980 hostage crisis, said it stood ready to facilitate the dialogue.
