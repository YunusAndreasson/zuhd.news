Washington — Senator Blumenthal demands answers on Cantor Fitzgerald's Tether ties.

Cantor manages Tether's [US](country:US) reserves, and its chief executive is the commerce secretary's son.

His Thursday letter, not a subpoena, asks Cantor about sanctions safeguards after his scrutiny of Tether's role in Iran's finances.

The November election decides whether Democrats gain more authority behind such probes.
