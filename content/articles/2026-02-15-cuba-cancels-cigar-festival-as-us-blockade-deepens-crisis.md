Blackouts now last up to 18 hours a day. Cuba postponed the Festival del Habano indefinitely, citing the "complex economic situation" caused by the United States trade embargo. The festival typically draws 1,300 participants from 70 countries and raised $19.5 million last year for Cuba's health care system. The Cuban government said the crisis deepened after the United States detained Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro on January 3, cutting off roughly 35,000 barrels of oil per day. Airlines have suspended service to Cuba over aviation fuel shortages, and the United Kingdom and other governments now warn against non-essential travel there.
