Mumbai — Reliance beat estimates on record refining margins.

Its refining arm captured all-time-high middle-distillate cracks as the US-Iran war disrupted crude supply, profiting from the same volatility raising South Asian fuel costs.

Retail profit fell 14% as e-commerce investment squeezed margins for a third straight quarter.
