Dhaka — A drone model matched rice seedling density at 98.68% accuracy.

The model replaces hand counts with a single drone pass, handling variable spacing that defeated earlier detection approaches, per the study.

Field trials at scale remain unpublished; the model has not been tested outside the study's aerial dataset.
