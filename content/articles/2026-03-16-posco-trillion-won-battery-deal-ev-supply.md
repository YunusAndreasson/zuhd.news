POSCO Future M won a $668 million graphite deal. The South Korean battery materials company will supply synthetic graphite anodes under the 5-year contract starting 2027 — its second deal with the same unnamed automaker, after a $446 million October agreement for natural graphite. POSCO will build a new graphite plant in Vietnam by 2027 to fulfill both contracts.
