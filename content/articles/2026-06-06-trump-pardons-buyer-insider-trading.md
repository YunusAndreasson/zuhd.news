Washington — Trump pardoned former US Representative Stephen Buyer for securities fraud.

Buyer traded Sprint shares after learning the T-Mobile merger would lift them — insider access from his consulting practice, the Securities and Exchange Commission found.

Trump has issued more than 1,600 pardons this term; Buyer had already lost his Supreme Court appeal.
