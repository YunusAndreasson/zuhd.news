London — Ministers' ChatGPT logs are subject to public disclosure.

[Britain](country:GB)'s Information Commissioner's Office confirmed that AI prompts and outputs generated by public-body staff fall under the Freedom of Information Act.

Public bodies previously rejected AI-related requests as vexatious; that defense is now untenable, a data-law expert said.
