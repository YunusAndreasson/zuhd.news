Denver — Hackers likely stole data on nearly 150,000 Coloradans.

The app, still down Tuesday, holds digital IDs and documents for 2.1 million users.

The state technology office found the breach on 18 November, shut the app and won't say what was taken.

Colorado urged residents travelling for Thanksgiving to carry physical IDs, and state services remain open on agency websites.

The office says systems return only once security is restored, with no date given.
