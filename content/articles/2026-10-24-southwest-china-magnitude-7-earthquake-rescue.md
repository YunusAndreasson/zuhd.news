Baqen — A 7.0 quake killed 16, Iran's state-affiliated Mehr News Agency reported.

Local authorities counted 65 injured, and rescuers are still searching for the missing.

The quake struck Baqen County in Nagqu, southwest [China](country:CN), at 10:33 p.m. Thursday, at high altitude and in low temperatures.

Xi Jinping ordered officials to monitor seismic activity and weather to prevent secondary disasters.
