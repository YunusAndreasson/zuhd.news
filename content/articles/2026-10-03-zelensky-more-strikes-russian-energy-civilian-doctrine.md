Kyiv — Zelensky rejected Trump's appeal to spare Russian refineries.

Trump says refinery strikes raise diesel prices, which the American Automobile Association put above $6 a gallon in the [US](country:US).

Zelensky says intelligence documents show Putin approved winter strikes on roads, schools and hospitals, so Kyiv will target refineries.

[Russia](country:RU) says its strikes answer Ukrainian attacks inside Russia and denies deliberately targeting civilians.

Zelensky says he expects the longer-range FP-9 missile this autumn.
