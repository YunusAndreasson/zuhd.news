Washington — Leaked list shows Apple, Nvidia gave $25 million each.

Trump, who promised to pay for the ballroom himself, is not on it.

The list names donors of $330 million, routed through a nonprofit that allows anonymous giving, Public Citizen says.

White House spokesperson Davis Ingle says critics alleging "fake conflicts of interests" would also complain if taxpayers paid.

Representative Jared Huffman demands "a full accounting of who paid and what they were promised."
