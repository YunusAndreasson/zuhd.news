Geneva — AI data centres need water, land and minerals.

No AI governance framework requires free, prior and informed consent from affected communities.

Data protection law protects individual privacy, but Indigenous knowledge and land are held collectively, so it offers no cover.

Ibrahim, an Indigenous rights advocate from [Chad](country:TD), wants African governments to require impact assessments first.
