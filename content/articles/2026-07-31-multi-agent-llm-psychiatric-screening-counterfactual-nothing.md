London — Four AI agents ran 1,350 psychiatric interviews.

Researchers split questioning and diagnosis across separate models, reaching 88.9% sensitivity against rule-based outcomes, a Translational Psychiatry study found.

The study is an unedited early-access manuscript testing simulated interviews; screening is not diagnosis, and clinical outcomes remain untested.
