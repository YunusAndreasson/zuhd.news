Grand Junction — 3 firefighters died in western Colorado's merged wildfire.

The Knowles, Gore and Snyder fires merged and burned 28,000 acres; a warm winter left low snowpack across the Southwest, extending fire season.

Colorado's governor declared a disaster; [Utah](country:US) has 11 large fires burning 208,000 acres, none contained.
