Brasília — Industry takes no hit yet from Brazil's "sin tax".

The tax was meant to charge makers of tobacco, alcohol and sugary drinks for health costs.

Provisional Measure 1,399/26, published Wednesday, sets rates at provisional fiscal neutrality, so [Brazil](country:BR)'s government postponed the protections the reform promised.

Congress, where the opposition holds a majority, now decides the real impact in 2027.
