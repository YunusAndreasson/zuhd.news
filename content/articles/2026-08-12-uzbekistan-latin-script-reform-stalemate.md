Tashkent — A three-decade-old law ordering Latin script still hasn't taken hold.

Every law, land record and textbook in the old script must be reprinted or retrained to switch.

[Uzbekistan](country:UZ)'s alphabet has changed three times in a century, each time chosen by an outside power.

Thirty years on, signage, courts and schools still run on different scripts.
