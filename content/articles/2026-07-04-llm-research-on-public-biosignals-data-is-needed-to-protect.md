London — Medical models need open heart and brain data.

Training language models on open electrocardiogram and electroencephalogram data, rather than proprietary hospital-industry sets, lets outsiders audit errors before they reach patients.

The paper's own authors disclose ties to Philips, Siemens and NVIDIA, among other device makers.
