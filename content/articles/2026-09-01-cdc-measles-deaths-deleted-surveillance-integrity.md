Atlanta — A measles death count went zero, then vanished.

States, not Washington, have always certified US causes of death.

Pennsylvania reported two deaths Aug. 25, but the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention deleted them from its website.

Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. has called the deaths possibly "fabricated," offering no evidence.
