New Delhi — Opposition parties mock-impeached [India](country:IN)'s Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar on Saturday.

The opposition says the voter-roll revision favours Prime Minister Narendra Modi's party, which denies it.

Only Parliament can remove the chief election commissioner, by a special majority, so Saturday's proceeding carried no legal force.

An opposition rally follows in Delhi on Sunday, and Home Minister Amit Shah offers a special parliament session.
