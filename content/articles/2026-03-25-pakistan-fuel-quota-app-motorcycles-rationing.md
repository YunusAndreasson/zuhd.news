Islamabad — Pakistan built a national ID-linked app to ration subsidized fuel for motorcycles and rickshaws.

Holding petrol and diesel prices unchanged has cost Rs70 billion in 2 weeks as kerosene rose 128% since the Hormuz closure.

The Cabinet has not yet decided whether to extend the scheme to small cars or pass the rising costs to consumers.
