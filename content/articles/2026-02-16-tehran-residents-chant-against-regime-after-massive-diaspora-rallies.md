"Death to Khamenei" echoed from balconies across Iran. Residents in Tehran, Shiraz, and Arak chanted anti-government slogans Sunday night, one day after diaspora rallies drew 250,000 people in Munich alone, where exiled crown prince Reza Pahlavi addressed crowds demanding the government's end — the largest opposition turnout since a January crackdown that rights groups say killed thousands and detained nearly 54,000. Whether the balcony protests signal revived internal dissent or an isolated echo of the diaspora movement remains unclear.
