Istanbul — [Turkey](country:TR)'s fertility rate fell to 1.4 children per woman in 2025.

Erdoğan calls the decline an existential threat.

Government packages worth 56 billion liras pay about $100 for a first child and $30 monthly for a second.

"Raising children is everything to do with money," said Kübra Erkol, a mother of two.

Erdoğan's family decade runs through 2035, and most countries below 2.1 children have not reversed course.
