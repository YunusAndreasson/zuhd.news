New Orleans — 153 million licenses surfaced on the dark web.

A license cannot be rotated like a password, so the exposure has no expiry.

The breach hit private scanning services that rental firms and hotels use, not a government database.

The FBI is investigating after the marketplace, called Nexus, went offline within hours.
