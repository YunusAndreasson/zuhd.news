Düsseldorf — Phosphite and palladium could have powered life's first metabolism.

Every cell uses phosphate as energy currency, and its pre-enzyme origin was unexplained.

Düsseldorf biologists report in The FEBS Journal that both occur in hydrogen-producing hydrothermal vents and form an inorganic precursor of adenosine triphosphate.

The claim is the researchers' own, and no independent scientist is quoted.
