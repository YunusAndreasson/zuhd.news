Lviv — The regional council dedicated 2027 to the Ukrainian Insurgent Army.

[Poland](country:PL) holds the army responsible for massacres of Polish civilians in Volhynia and Eastern Galicia.

Prime Minister Donald Tusk warned that opponents of [Ukraine](country:UA)'s EU accession could use the commemoration against Kyiv.

"This is unwise. This is harmful for Ukraine," Tusk told a Warsaw press conference on Wednesday.

Every EU member, Poland included, must approve Ukraine's accession.
