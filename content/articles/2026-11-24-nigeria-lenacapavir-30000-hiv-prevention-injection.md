Abuja — 30,000 Nigerians now take a twice-yearly HIV prevention shot.

One injection replaces 6 months of daily oral prevention pills.

[Nigeria](country:NG) has received nearly 50,000 doses and pilots the drug in 9 states and the capital territory since March.

The drug is reserved for eligible high-risk people, the programme coordinator said, and supply remains pilot-scale.

Another 170,000 doses are due by January 2027, and a domestic HIV financing strategy launches on 1 December.
