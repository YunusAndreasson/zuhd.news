Zhob — At least 40 dead as a bus fell 24 meters into a ravine.

The Quetta-to-Peshawar coach veered off a mountain road in [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Balochistan, where officials say poor road conditions cause frequent bus crashes.

11 injured remain hospitalized; rescue crews cite the rugged terrain that slowed their response.
