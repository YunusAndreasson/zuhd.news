Berlin — Wind and solar generated 44% of electricity, fossil fuels 43%.

[Germany](country:DE) crossed the threshold in 2025 while closing its nuclear fleet, Carbon Brief found — but electricity is only a fifth of final energy, so heating and transport are untouched.

The government will publish a review of its 2038 coal phase-out timeline in August.
