China reportedly sold Iran a Mach 4 anti-ship missile. The CM-302 covers the entire Persian Gulf from Iran's coastline; at Mach 4, US warships would have seconds less warning than against Iran's current subsonic missiles. China denied the Reuters report but has not confirmed whether a deal was completed before hostilities began.
