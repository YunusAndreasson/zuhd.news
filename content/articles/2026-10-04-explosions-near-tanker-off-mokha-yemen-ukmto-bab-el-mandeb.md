Mokha — Blasts erupted beside a tanker off Mokha, [Iran](country:IR)'s state-affiliated Mehr reported.

As of 27 September, 27.1 ships a day crossed Bab el-Mandeb against a normal 29.1.

UKMTO said the crew are safe and reported no environmental impact, but named neither the tanker nor a cause.

At least 3 tankers were struck near Hormuz since Thursday, and the report names no one responsible.
