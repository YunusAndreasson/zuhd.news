Korla — One perch population stands genetically apart.

Researchers used ten microsatellite markers across eight geographic populations, finding the Korla group most distinct while 91% of variation sits within individuals, the study found.

The genetic map now guides which populations breeders select to farm Perca fluviatilis without inbreeding stocks.
