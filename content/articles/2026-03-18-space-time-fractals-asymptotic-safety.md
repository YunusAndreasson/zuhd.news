Physicist Astrid Eichhorn says the universe needs no extra dimensions. Her theory, asymptotic safety, holds that space-time turns fractal past the Planck scale and gravity stabilises — no strings, no supersymmetric particles, nothing beyond what we can observe. If the approach survives its next predictions, string theory faces its first credible rival in decades.
