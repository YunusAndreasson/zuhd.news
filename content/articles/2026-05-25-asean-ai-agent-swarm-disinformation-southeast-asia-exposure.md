Singapore — Southeast Asia lacks AI-swarm disinformation defenses.

AI agents campaign in Bahasa, Tagalog, Thai, and Vietnamese at near-zero cost; platform moderation in [Indonesia](country:ID), the region's largest electorate, is thinnest.

No regional binding framework covers AI agents; existing rules were drafted for human-run troll farms.
