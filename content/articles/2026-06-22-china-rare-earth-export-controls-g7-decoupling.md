Beijing — [China](country:CN) restricted 2 [US](country:US) rare earth producers' exports.

The move came days after the G7 agreed to cut dependence on Chinese rare earth supply; Beijing warned supply chains may fragment.

No duration has been announced; G7 members are considering further measures to reduce Chinese supply dependence.
