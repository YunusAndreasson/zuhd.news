Fort Meade — A Cyber Command unit lost operators to suicide.

Operators worldwide share the same clearance-gated isolation this unit's culture exposed.

Security clearances bar operators from discussing their work with family or most clinicians, so seeking care can risk the clearance.

Cyber Command has not said whether its mental-health screening process will change.
