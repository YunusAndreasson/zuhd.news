Over 1 million Iranians marched worldwide on Saturday. Exiled crown prince Reza Pahlavi addressed 250,000 in Munich, calling it "our final battle," while 350,000 rallied in Toronto and 350,000 in Los Angeles in the largest Iranian diaspora protests on record. Inside Iran, residents in Tehran, Isfahan, and Shiraz defied a government crackdown with synchronized nighttime rooftop chants — the protests coincided with ongoing Geneva nuclear talks where Iranian negotiators are offering diplomatic concessions.
