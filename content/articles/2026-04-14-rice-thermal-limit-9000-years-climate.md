Bangkok — Rice hit its thermal ceiling after 9,000 years. Wild rice spread into China and South Asia during post-ice-age warming; 3 billion people now depend on it. South and Southeast Asia, the zones most dependent on rice, face the steepest losses as temperatures keep rising.
