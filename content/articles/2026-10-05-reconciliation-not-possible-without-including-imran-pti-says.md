Islamabad — Imran Khan's party says there is no reconciliation without him.

Talks between [Pakistan](country:PK)'s government and Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf collapsed over the weekend, Secretary General Salman Akram Raja says.

Federal minister Amir Muqam says the government's side suggested both sides be on the same page about terrorism.

Islamabad opposes governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Muqam said, but claims the provincial government has lost control.
