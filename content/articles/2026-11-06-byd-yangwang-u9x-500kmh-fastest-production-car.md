Abu Dhabi — Yangwang's U9X reached 503.17 km/h on a closed highway.

Yangwang says the International Automobile Federation and Guinness certified it as the fastest production car.

Four motors spinning to 30,000 rpm, a 1,200-volt platform and 30C battery cells produce over 3,000 horsepower.

[BYD](country:CN) will build only 30 U9Xs, so the record shows engineering rather than volume.

Whether the motor, battery and thermal technology reaches cheaper cars is the open question.
