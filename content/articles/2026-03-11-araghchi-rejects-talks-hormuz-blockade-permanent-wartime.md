Iran's Hormuz blockade has no end date. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi told PBS that negotiations are "no longer on the agenda," foreclosing the only visible path to de-escalation. Oil markets, which fell 15% on ceasefire hopes, now face a sustained blockade with no stated conditions for lifting.
