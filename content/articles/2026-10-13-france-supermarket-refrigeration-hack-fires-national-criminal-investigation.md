Paris — Hackers heated supermarket fridges to 50°C across [France](country:FR), starting fires.

Mousquetaires, owner of Intermarché, says 300 of its stores were hit, with losses in the millions of euros.

Attackers reached internet-exposed Danfoss and Copeland controllers overnight and forced defrost cycles, France's CERT-FR said.

Danfoss says most affected controllers ran outdated firmware it had urged stores to update.

The Paris prosecutor's cybercrime section is investigating, and no one has named the attacker.
