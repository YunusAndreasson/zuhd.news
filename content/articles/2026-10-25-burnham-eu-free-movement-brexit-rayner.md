London — Burnham reopened free movement, reversing his May stance.

Brussels calls free movement non-negotiable for single-market access, so any closer deal depends on it.

Oxford's Migration Observatory counts 162,000 more EU nationals leaving [Britain](country:GB) than arriving between mid-2021 and 2025.

Housing Secretary Angela Rayner denies Burnham is reopening Brexit and says he wants only an honest conversation.

Burnham lays out EU options near the 20 November Brussels summit, then a 10-year plan before Christmas.
