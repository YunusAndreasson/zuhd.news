NASA set April 1 for a crewed Moon flyby. After 2 delays, the crew carries Victor Glover, the first Black astronaut to reach the Moon, and Jeremy Hansen, the first Canadian. Artemis 3's Moon landing depends on this flyby; another failure could end the program.
