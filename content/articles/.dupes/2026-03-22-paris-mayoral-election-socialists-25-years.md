Paris — Paris hasn't had a right-wing mayor since 2001. Rachida Dati, a Macron ally, challenges Socialist Emmanuel Grégoire in the last major local vote before the 2027 presidential election. Preliminary results expected Sunday evening will show whether the right reclaims Paris ahead of France's most consequential race.
