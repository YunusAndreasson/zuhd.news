Egypt's Suez Canal earnings have collapsed as ships reroute. Egypt earns $9 billion per year in transit fees, enough to prop up the pound, but the money now stops at the Cape. 100 million Egyptians face rising food prices with no Hormuz ceasefire in sight to restart transit trade.
