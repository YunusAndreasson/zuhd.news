Lima — 600-year-old parrot feathers rewrote pre-Inca trade history. Tomb DNA shows Andes-spanning parrot trade predated the Inca road network by centuries, connecting Pacific coast cities to the Amazon rainforest. The research team will expand isotope testing to other pre-Inca coastal sites to determine whether long-distance trade preceded empire across the Andes.
