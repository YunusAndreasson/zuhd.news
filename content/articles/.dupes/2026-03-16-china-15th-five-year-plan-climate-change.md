China dropped its absolute emissions ceiling for the first time. Analysts say the new carbon intensity formula could allow total emissions to rise 3-6% over 5 years while officially meeting targets. China must submit updated Paris Agreement pledges before COP30 in Belém this year, when the new accounting method will face international scrutiny.
