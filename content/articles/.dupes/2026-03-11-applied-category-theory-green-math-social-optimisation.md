"Abstract nonsense" math is now modeling disease and AI. Category theory uses only arrows and relationships — no numbers — preserving the identity of things where calculus would reduce them to data points. A successful validation would give regulators the first formal method for certifying AI behavior in safety-critical infrastructure.
