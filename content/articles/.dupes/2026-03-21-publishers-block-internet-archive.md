San Francisco — The New York Times blocked the Internet Archive's crawlers. The Archive holds 1 trillion web pages and is not building AI, but the blocks publishers intended for commercial scrapers target it too. If more publishers follow, pages that sites delete or quietly rewrite will disappear from the historical record permanently.
