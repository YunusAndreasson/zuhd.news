UAE told Washington it expects 9 months of war. Saudi Arabia opened a second air base to US forces, giving Washington a logistics hub near Jeddah as ground troops arrive from East Asia. Iran struck Qatar's Ras Laffan refinery this week, causing damage the Qatari government estimates will take 3-5 years to repair.
