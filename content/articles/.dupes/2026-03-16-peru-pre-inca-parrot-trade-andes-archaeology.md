600-year-old Peruvian tomb feathers came from Amazon parrots. Using DNA and isotope analysis, researchers traced feathers from an Ychsma noble's headdress to wild parrots carried hundreds of kilometres across the Andes. The feathers are the oldest material evidence of trans-Andean trade, predating Inca roads by at least a century.
