New York — Quantum cryptography's pioneers won the $1 million Turing Award. Charles Bennett and Gilles Brassard's BB84 protocol makes eavesdropping detectable by altering the quantum state of any intercepted data. The protocol is now deployed by governments and banks as quantum computers threaten today's encryption standards.
