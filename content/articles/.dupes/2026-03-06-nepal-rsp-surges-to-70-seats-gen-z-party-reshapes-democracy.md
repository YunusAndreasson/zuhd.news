Rastriya Swatantra Party leads 70 constituencies, up from 36. The party, born from 2024 Gen Z street protests, ran on anti-corruption under engineer-turned-mayor Balen Shah, 34. The party may hit the 138-seat majority threshold by Friday, ending Nepal's era of coalition-only governments.
