Your DNA's gatekeeper runs on chaos, not order. New imaging reveals nuclear pore proteins have no fixed shape — their disorder is what enables the machine to sort molecules precisely. The structure has resisted imaging for decades; understanding it could explain how cells decide what enters the nucleus.
