A $500 million nonprofit will fix science's data plumbing. The Astera Institute, a science philanthropy, launched Radial to argue that AI's ceiling in research is set by outdated data infrastructure — not the models themselves. Radial has not announced its first grants, and the scientific community has not agreed on what infrastructure to rebuild first.
