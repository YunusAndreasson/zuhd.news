A CEO asked ChatGPT how to void $250 million. Krafton CEO Changhan Kim overrode his lawyers, using an AI-designed scheme to force out a studio founder before a bonus vested. A judge ordered the founder reinstated and Krafton to pay the $250 million it tried to void.
