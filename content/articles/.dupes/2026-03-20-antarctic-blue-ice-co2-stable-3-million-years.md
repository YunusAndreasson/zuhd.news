title: "CO2 Was Stable Until Now"
date: "2026-03-18T00:00:00Z"
source: "Nature"

category: "science"

Antarctic ice cores now span 3 million years of CO2. Levels barely moved through ice ages and warm cycles — until industrialization pushed CO2 50% above any level in the entire record. Drilling deeper at Allan Hills Blue Ice could extend the record to the Pliocene, 5 million years ago.
