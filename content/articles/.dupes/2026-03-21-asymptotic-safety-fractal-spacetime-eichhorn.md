Heidelberg — Space-time has fewer effective dimensions at the smallest scales. Astrid Eichhorn's asymptotic safety framework proposes that physics self-stabilizes at the Planck scale and requires no extra dimensions or undetectable strings. Eichhorn must now derive specific particle mass predictions that distinguish her framework from string theory and can be tested.
