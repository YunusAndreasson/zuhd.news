Nuclear pore complexes work through organized chaos, not precision. Each cell has thousands of these pores, and their disordered central proteins, long considered noise, turn out to be the selective filter controlling all nuclear traffic. The discovery may change how drugs are designed to enter the nucleus — an unsolved problem in cancer treatment.
