China's new five-year plan sets no absolute emissions cap. A carbon intensity target measures emissions per GDP unit, not total output, and analysts project a 3-6% emissions rise over 5 years. The plan promotes "peaking" coal and oil but sets no timeline, contradicting earlier signals that coal would peak by 2027.
