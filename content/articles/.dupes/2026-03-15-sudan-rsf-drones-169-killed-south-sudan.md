RSF-armed militias killed 169 people in South Sudan. Sudan's Rapid Support Forces armed tribal groups as auxiliary fighters, and those militias have now crossed the border independently. The RSF is simultaneously running drone strikes across Kordofan and Blue Nile, forcing displacement on a second front.
