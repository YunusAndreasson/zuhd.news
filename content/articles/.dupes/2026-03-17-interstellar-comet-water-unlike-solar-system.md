3I/ATLAS holds deuterium at 30 times Earth's ocean levels. That ratio — the most extreme ever measured — means the comet formed near absolute zero in another star system. Astronomers have weeks to study the only 3rd interstellar object ever confirmed before it leaves our solar system.
