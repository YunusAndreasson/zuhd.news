A man rammed a Michigan synagogue with his truck. The attacker, Ayman Ghazali, had lost 4 relatives to Israeli airstrikes on Lebanon weeks before the attack and died in a gunfight with police. The FBI is investigating as a targeted attack on the Jewish community but has not established a motive.
