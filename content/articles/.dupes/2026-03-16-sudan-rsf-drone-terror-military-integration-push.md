Rapid Support Forces hit Kordofan markets after losing Bara. Losing ground to Sudan's military, the RSF shifted to drone strikes targeting civilian markets, hospitals, and power stations across 3 towns. Sudan's military is now pressuring armed factions to integrate, which peace-agreement signatories warn will erase their political leverage.
