Britain's last coal plant closed in September 2024. The closure drove a 2.4% drop in total emissions to their lowest since 1872, coal accounting for 60% of the decline. The UK's 9 MtCO2e reduction in 2025 was only 60% of the annual pace needed to reach net zero by 2050.
