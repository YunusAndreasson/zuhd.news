Brasília — Brazil pledged to cut emissions 49–58% by 2035. Brazil's soy industry scrapped its deforestation moratorium in 2026, undercutting private-sector compliance with the new targets. IBAMA, Brazil's environmental enforcement agency, must now sanction agribusiness violations with fewer staff than before Bolsonaro's cuts.
