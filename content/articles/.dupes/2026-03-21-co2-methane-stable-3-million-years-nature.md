Antarctica — Antarctic blue ice reveals CO2 was stable for 3 million years. The study, published in Nature, extends the previous 800,000-year ice core record fourfold, providing the longest baseline yet for measuring the human-era carbon spike. Today's 424 ppm nearly doubles the 3-million-year average; scientists must now project a future the entire geological record cannot map.
