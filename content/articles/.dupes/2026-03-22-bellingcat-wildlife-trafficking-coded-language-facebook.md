Jakarta — Bellingcat found gibbons sold on Facebook across 70,000-member groups. 9 groups used alphanumeric codes to evade Meta's detection while selling protected species including silvery gibbons, with fewer than 2,500 left in the wild. Meta removed all groups only after Bellingcat's contact, while Indonesian enforcement is limited to civil warnings for unlicensed wildlife sales.
