Boulder — 130 universities sued to halt America's climate research shutdown. The National Center for Atmospheric Research runs supercomputers that model hurricanes and droughts, but the White House branded it "a source of climate alarmism" and ordered it closed. The university consortium alleges First Amendment retaliation; a federal court must act before staff dispersal makes the center impossible to reopen.
