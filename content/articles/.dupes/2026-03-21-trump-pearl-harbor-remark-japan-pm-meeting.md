US President Trump invoked Pearl Harbor to pressure Japan. He made the remark to Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi, who has refused to join the US coalition citing Japan's pacifist constitution. Takaichi must now defend that refusal in parliament; Japan imports most of its oil through the Hormuz strait.
