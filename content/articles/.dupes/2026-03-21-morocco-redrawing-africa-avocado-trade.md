Rabat — Morocco became Africa's top avocado exporter in 2025. Its exports rose 90% to 141,000 tonnes — roughly 33% of Africa's total — as Hormuz shipping disruptions rewarded proximity and logistics over farm output. Kenya and other East African exporters now face a structural disadvantage as long as Indian Ocean route instability persists.
