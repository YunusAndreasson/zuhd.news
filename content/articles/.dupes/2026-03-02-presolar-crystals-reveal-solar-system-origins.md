Crystals older than the Sun challenge how ours formed. The crystals carry low iron-60, ruling out a supernova trigger and pointing to a Wolf-Rayet star that exploded before our Sun ignited. Cosmochemist Nan Liu says the findings will help determine whether solar systems capable of hosting life are rare.
