China's new five-year climate plan sets no coal cap. Beijing targets a 17% cut in carbon intensity by 2030 — a metric measured against GDP growth that allows total emissions to rise 3-6%. China could meet 2°C limits by doubling renewables by 2035, but without an absolute emissions cap its targets cannot be independently verified.
