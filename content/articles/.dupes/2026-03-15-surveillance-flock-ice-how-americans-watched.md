Data firm Palantir selects neighborhoods for ICE immigration raids. The analytics company, along with Ring home cameras and Flock license plate readers, built separate products that now form a national surveillance grid no legislature voted to create. No federal law requires a warrant before police access data from Flock's nationwide camera network.
