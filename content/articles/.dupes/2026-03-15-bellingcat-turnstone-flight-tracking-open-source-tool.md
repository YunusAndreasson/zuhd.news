American tanker surges preceded both Israeli strikes on Iran. Bellingcat's open-source Turnstone tool found the pattern in publicly available Atlantic flight data — matching US tanker activity before each of the 2 strikes over the past year. Source code is on GitHub, and journalists can apply for access to Bellingcat's hosted instance.
