70,000 Facebook users joined groups secretly trading protected animals. Traders used letter codes for prices — A for 100,000 Indonesian rupiah, B for 50,000 — with one group posting 200 ads weekly for 5 years. Meta removed all 9 groups after Bellingcat's investigation; Indonesian authorities warned the linked pet shop but filed no charges.
