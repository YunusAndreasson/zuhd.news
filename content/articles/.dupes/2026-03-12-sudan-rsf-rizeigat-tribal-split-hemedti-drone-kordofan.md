Sudan's rebel army is losing its founding tribe. Musa Madibo, head of the Rizeigat tribe, left for Juba after a falling-out with Rapid Support Forces commander Hemedti. A Rizeigat withdrawal would strip Hemedti of his tribal backing in Darfur, the region where his militia was founded.
