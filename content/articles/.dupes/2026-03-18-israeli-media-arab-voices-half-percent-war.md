Arab Israelis filled under 0.5% of Israeli wartime coverage. Of 199 reports on civil protection measures, only 3 covered Arab localities, despite Arabs being 21% of Israel's population and facing the same bombardment. Haaretz, which conducted the study, called the pattern "no coincidence," attributing it to editorial choices across all 3 networks.
