Beijing — China adopted its 2nd comprehensive environmental code since 1949. The law makes 2030 peak-emissions and 2060 carbon-neutrality targets legally binding, authorizing courts to prosecute GDP-first provincial officials. Enforcement regulations must follow before provincial governments finalize development plans under the 15th five-year plan.
