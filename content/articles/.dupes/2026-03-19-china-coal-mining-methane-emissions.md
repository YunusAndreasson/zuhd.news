Coal-mine methane is 80 times more potent than CO₂. China's coal consolidation after 2015 moved to deeper mines, shifting the emissions profile rather than shrinking it. China's methane commitments face scrutiny at COP30 in Belém later this year, where coal emissions are a contested accounting gap.
