Crystals smaller than a bacterium predate the Sun. Found inside meteorites, presolar grains carry isotopic ratios that could settle whether a supernova or a Wolf-Rayet star triggered solar system formation 4.6 billion years ago. Researcher Nan Liu's search for Wolf-Rayet grain signatures could overturn the dominant supernova model that has guided solar science for decades.
