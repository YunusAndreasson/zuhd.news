Peshawar — Khyber Pakhtunkhwa plans courts with anonymous judges.

An official said terrorism witnesses in [Pakistan](country:PK)'s northwest fear coming forward.

An amendment already sent shields prosecutors and judges too, and Rs70 billion would fund counter-terrorism police within a year.

Officials gave no date for the first faceless court to sit.
