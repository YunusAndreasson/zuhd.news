Geneva — 13.5 million infants got zero vaccine doses in 2025.

The WHO and the UN children's agency UNICEF blame aid cuts, conflict and misinformation for the widening coverage gaps.

Gavi's chief warns 600,000 preventable deaths could follow if its funding keeps shrinking.
