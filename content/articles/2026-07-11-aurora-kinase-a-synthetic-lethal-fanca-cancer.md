London — Losing one gene makes tumors kill themselves.

Cancers missing the DNA-repair gene FANCA depend on Aurora kinase A to survive — inhibiting it kills tumor cells while healthy ones live.

Aurora-A inhibitors already exist, raising the question of trials in FANCA-deficient patients next.
