Biała Podlaska — Russian satirist shot dead metres from Belarusian consulate.

Skrepetsky, who caricatured [Russia](country:RU)'s Putin and Lukashenko, was shot 5 times; 2 Belarusian nationals were detained near the consulate.

The gunman remains at large; [Poland](country:PL)'s security bureau warned of potential Russian escalation beyond its borders.
