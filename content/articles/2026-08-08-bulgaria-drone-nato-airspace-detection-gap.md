It blew up 100 metres inside [Bulgaria](country:BG), near a compressor station on the Trans-Balkan gas pipeline.

The drone crossed first from [Romania](country:RO), defence officials said, calling it likely an accidental Ukrainian device.

The North Atlantic Council takes up the incident next week.
