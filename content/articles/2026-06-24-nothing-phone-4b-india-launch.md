London — Nothing Phone 4b launches in [India](country:IN) on July 7 via Flipkart.

Nothing canceled its cheaper 2026 handset due to rising memory costs, co-founder Akis Evangelidis confirmed, opening space for a B-series device priced under ₹33,000, the company said.

Pricing and full specs remain unconfirmed ahead of the July 7 launch.
