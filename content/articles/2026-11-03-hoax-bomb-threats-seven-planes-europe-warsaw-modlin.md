Warsaw — At least 7 flights received bomb threats, none carrying explosives.

Hackers shut supermarket fridges across Europe this week, and some experts see a pattern of cheap disruption.

Threats targeted flights to Warsaw, Sofia, Rome and Murcia, forcing a diversion to Modlin, a runway closure and a fighter escort.

No one has claimed responsibility, and officials have named no suspect, though European officials blame [Russia](country:RU) and [Iran](country:IR) for hybrid attacks.

European authorities will cooperate to determine whether the threats were coordinated.
