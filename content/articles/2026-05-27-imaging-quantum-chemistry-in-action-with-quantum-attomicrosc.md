London — DNA base-pair charge dynamics can now be mapped, a theoretical study finds.

Ab initio simulations reveal attosecond electron dynamics in DNA base pairs — the basis for a proposed 'quantum attomicroscope' instrument.

Building the instrument requires laser pulses precise enough to resolve DNA at attosecond timescales.
