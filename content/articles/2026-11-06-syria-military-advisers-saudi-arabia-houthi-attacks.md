Riyadh — Syrian military trainers are now working inside Saudi Arabia.

Houthi missiles have hit Saudi airports and oil facilities since July.

Two Syrian sources say scores of personnel arrived in recent weeks, under a defence pact [Syria](country:SY) signed with Riyadh on Wednesday.

A senior Syrian diplomat said talk of troop deployments is premature and the mission is training and advice.

Syria's foreign minister says Damascus is studying entry into the Mecca defence pact, and Washington has urged caution.
