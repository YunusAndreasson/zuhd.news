Moscow — Putin admitted Ukrainian drone strikes created a fuel shortage.

[Ukraine](country:UA)'s drones hit refineries 300–700km inside [Russia](country:RU), including Krasnodar's 4-million-tonne plant; Crimea declared a fuel emergency.

Putin ordered more fuel imports and faster anti-aircraft production, calling the deficit "not critical."
