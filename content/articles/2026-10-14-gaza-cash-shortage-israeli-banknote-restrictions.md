Gaza City — One shopkeeper accepts cash only for a 10% commission.

At the war's height, Palestinians paid commissions of up to 50% to withdraw their own money.

[Israel](country:IL) has barred new banknotes since the war began, so worn notes are refused and payment apps spread.

"This financial siege involves preventing cash from entering Gaza," said Ismail al-Thawabta, who directs Gaza's Government Media Office.

With no new notes arriving, wallets like Jawwal Pay are becoming the main way to buy food.
