Yemen — Houthis told Aramco shareholders to sell, [Iran](country:IR)'s state agency Mehr reported.

The warning also told Saudi civilians and oil employees to avoid the kingdom's oil facilities.

An unnamed military source gave the warning to Yemen's Saba News Agency, and Mehr cites no new strike.

The source urged shareholders to sell "before it is too late" and set no date.
