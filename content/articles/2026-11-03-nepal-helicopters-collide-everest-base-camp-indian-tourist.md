Pheriche — Vasanth Kumar Balaramudu died when two helicopters clipped rotors.

The crash site lies about 12km south of Everest Base Camp.

Both aircraft tried to land at the same spot, [Nepal](country:NP)'s Civil Aviation Authority said.

Footage shows clear skies and one helicopter descending faster than the other.

Fishtail Air said the injured passengers and crew were rescued and taken to Kathmandu.
