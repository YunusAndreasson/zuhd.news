Brussels — EU governments seek to revoke Ukrainian men's protection.

Sweden's migration minister cited "strong support" for excluding men 23-60 from temporary protection covering 4.3 million Ukrainian refugees.

Norway already stripped coverage for men arriving after May 5; no EU-wide vote is scheduled.
