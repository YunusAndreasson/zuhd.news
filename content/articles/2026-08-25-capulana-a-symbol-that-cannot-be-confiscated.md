Nampula — Women wore ordinary wrap cloth to protest.

A garment every woman already owns cannot be confiscated or banned as evidence of membership.

Police, unable to distinguish protesters from bystanders wearing the same capulana, dispersed crowds in Nampula and nearby Anchilo.

Organizers now face the same scrutiny online that documents the police response.
