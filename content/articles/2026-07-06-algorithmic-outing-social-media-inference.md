London — Platforms can name a queer identity before its owner does.

New research terms this "algorithmic outing" — recommender systems infer sexual orientation from likes, follows and dwell time, then surface it in feeds.

Researchers warn the same inference becomes a safety threat in states that criminalize LGBTQ+ identity.
