Hangzhou — DeepSeek raised about ¥100 billion ($15 billion), people familiar say.

Battery maker CATL and Tencent are among the largest investors in [China](country:CN)'s biggest AI round.

Funds go to data centres and a Shanghai STAR Market listing, for which it hired CITIC Securities.

Rival Moonshot AI is preparing a Hong Kong listing in the first quarter of 2027.
