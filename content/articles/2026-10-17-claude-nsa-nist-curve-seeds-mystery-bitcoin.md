United States — The National Security Agency's mystery seeds were 1997 timestamps.

Anthropic says that makes a hidden weakness in the [US](country:US) government's curves much less likely.

An Anthropic model matched P-256's published seed at the 1,842nd candidate of lrand48, an old C library random-number generator.

Anthropic's team still cannot explain why the program passed over some usable candidates.

Bitcoin's secp256k1 curve is unaffected, but no one can say when a quantum computer will threaten it.
