Hong Kong — Zhonghui Biotech's shares cannot trade since 1 April.

The 2025 listing drew over 4,000 times oversubscription, and a third director has resigned this year.

Auditor Deloitte questioned HK$272 million in fund purchases and HK$58 million in advisory fees, then resigned on 13 April.

The company says its business remains substantive and viable, and two forensic investigations have not reported.

The exchange's 6 conditions for resuming trading include forensic findings and the overdue 2025 annual report.
