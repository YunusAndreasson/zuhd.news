London — Tottenham Hotspur cut VMware licensing costs by 85%.

The savings measure how much Broadcom's post-acquisition VMware pricing had been extracting.

Tottenham replaced VMware with Hewlett Packard Enterprise's Morpheus software across its stadium's servers, storage and network infrastructure this year.

Tottenham is finishing a broader three-year stadium data-center redesign, its chief technology officer said.
