Mumbai — Landslide shut a tunnel opened two months ago.

The Bhor Ghat rail section runs three parallel tracks, and landslides hit all three, canceling long-distance trains.

Restoration crews race to reopen tracks as the India Meteorological Department forecasts more heavy rain.
