Lahore — Pakistan's Federal Investigation Agency seized Rs20.4M in a hawala sweep.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s currency controls push remittances off-book; the state arrests couriers while the dollar shortage that drives hawala persists.

A nationwide crackdown began in March; the rupee shortage underlying hawala demand has not been addressed.
