Kyiv — Russian attacks killed 5 people, one a child.

Mayor Vitali Klitschko said 43 people were injured across the capital, 25 hospitalised including 2 children.

A daytime drone hit an office building in Solomianskyi district, and 3 bodies were found in its parking lot.

Vladimir Putin blamed Kyiv for the collapse of talks, saying [Ukraine](country:UA) launched 1,600 drones on Russia's election day.

Putin said [Russia](country:RU) will decide on resuming negotiations by its own interests, after weighing its response.
