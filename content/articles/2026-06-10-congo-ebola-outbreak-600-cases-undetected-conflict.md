Bunia — Ebola has killed 115 across 3 [Congo](country:CD) provinces.

The Bundibugyo strain spread undetected for weeks; conflict in Ituri and North Kivu disabled the surveillance systems designed to catch it.

The International Rescue Committee called for urgent funding Tuesday to contain the outbreak in Ituri before it spreads.
