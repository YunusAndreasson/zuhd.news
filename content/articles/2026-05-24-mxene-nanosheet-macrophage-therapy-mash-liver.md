Beijing — A battery material now targets liver inflammation.

Researchers engineered Ti₃C₂ MXene nanosheets — originally developed for energy storage — to deliver anti-inflammatory therapy directly to macrophages in the liver.

In mice, the compound reduced liver fat linked to metabolic steatohepatitis and restored normal function, the study found.
