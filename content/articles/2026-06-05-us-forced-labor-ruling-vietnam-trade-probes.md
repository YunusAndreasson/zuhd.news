Hanoi — [Vietnam](country:VN) now faces 3 simultaneous American trade probes.

A forced-labor designation blocks import entry at US Customs; 3 active probes give [the US](country:US) compounding leverage in ongoing tariff negotiations.

Hanoi says the ruling does not reflect its efforts; its export access is now subject to terms set in Washington.
