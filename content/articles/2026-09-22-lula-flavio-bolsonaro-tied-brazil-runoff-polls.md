Brasília — Flávio Bolsonaro trails Lula by one point.

The Bolsonaro movement transferred its base to a son, keeping [Brazil](country:BR)'s election a coin flip.

Jair Bolsonaro is barred from running, so two separate polls put Flávio and Lula within the 2-point margin of error.

The first round is October 4, with a runoff October 25 if no majority emerges.
