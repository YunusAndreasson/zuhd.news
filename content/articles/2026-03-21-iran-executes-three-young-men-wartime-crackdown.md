Iran hanged wrestler Saleh Mohammadi in Qom on Thursday. All 3 were arrested during January protests and convicted on "waging war against God" charges in trials Amnesty International said used torture-extracted confessions. More than 100 others arrested during the same protests face death sentences, with Amnesty International warning of further mass executions.
