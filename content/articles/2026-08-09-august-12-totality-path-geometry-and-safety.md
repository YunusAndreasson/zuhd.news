Burgos — Totality lasts under 2 minutes here on Wednesday.

Mainland Europe hasn't seen a total eclipse since 1999, and won't again until 2081.

The Moon's shadow tracks a roughly 250km band across Iceland and northern Spain, requiring near-perigee alignment with the lunar node.

Only totality itself is safe to view unfiltered; even 99% coverage requires eclipse glasses.
