Detroit — Michigan's Democratic Senate primary narrows to two.

Mallory McMorrow dropped out, leaving Representative Haley Stevens against progressive Abdul El-Sayed for retiring Senator Gary Peters's seat.

Michigan Democrats vote August 4 on which wing of the party fields the nominee.
