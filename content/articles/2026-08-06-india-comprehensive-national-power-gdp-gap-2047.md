New Delhi — India ranks fourth in GDP, not power.

Comprehensive National Power, the index [China](country:CN) uses to rank states, weighs manufacturing depth and R&D spending that GDP does not capture.

[India](country:IN)'s 2047 "developed nation" target hinges on closing gaps GDP growth alone cannot fix.
