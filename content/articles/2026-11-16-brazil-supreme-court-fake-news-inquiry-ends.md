Brasília — Court president Edson Fachin archived the fake news inquiry on 9 November.

[Brazil](country:BR)'s Supreme Court was both victim and investigator of the attacks it probed.

A São Paulo bar association official calls the closure a rare chance to write clear rules.

She wants a code of conduct, fixed terms and a minimum age for justices.
