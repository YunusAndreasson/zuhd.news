China — BYD sells steer-by-wire on a $28,194 five-seat electric.

BYD claims the system improves response speed by more than 25%.

The system drops the mechanical link to the wheels, but only the $3,000 four-wheel-drive package has the full feature set.

Maximum range on China's test cycle is 850km, but the steer-by-wire four-wheel-drive version is rated at 770km.

BYD says 10% to 97% charging takes nine minutes, but that relies on its dedicated flash-charging network.
