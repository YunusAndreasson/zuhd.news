3 C-17 cargo planes landed in Maiduguri, Borno State. About 200 US troops will deploy to northern Nigeria in an advisory role over the coming weeks, with Nigeria's Defence Headquarters insisting its forces retain full command authority across all operations. The deployment follows President Trump's authorization of US airstrikes in Nigeria on Christmas Day and comes days after gunmen killed 46 people in village raids in neighbouring Niger State.
