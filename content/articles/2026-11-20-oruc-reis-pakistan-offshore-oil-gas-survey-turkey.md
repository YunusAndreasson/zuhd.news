Karachi — A Turkish ship will map 670 square kilometres off the city.

It is [Turkey](country:TR)'s second overseas seismic operation.

The Oruç Reis will gather 3D seismic data in the Indus-C Block, then 2D data over 2,353 kilometres in the Makran Blocks.

The ship works 2 months in [Pakistan](country:PK)'s waters, returning to Karachi port between projects.
