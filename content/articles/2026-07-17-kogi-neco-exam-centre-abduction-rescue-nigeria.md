Dekina — Soldiers freed 4 hostages seized from an exam hall.

Gunmen abducted the principal, a National Examinations Council official and 3 candidates Tuesday; a multi-agency forest sweep by [Nigeria](country:NG)'s army forced captors to flee.

Kogi has logged repeated school and convoy kidnappings since June, with suspects still at large in the forest.
