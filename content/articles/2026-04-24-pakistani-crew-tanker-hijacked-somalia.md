Mogadishu — Pirates seized 11 [Pakistani](country:PK) sailors in the Gulf of Aden. The tanker Owner 25 was boarded April 21; the vessel's [Indonesian](country:ID) captain was also taken hostage, and Pakistan's Maritime Ministry is scrambling to confirm crew details. India's navy freed 19 Pakistani sailors from Somali pirates in 2024; no rescue force has been dispatched.
