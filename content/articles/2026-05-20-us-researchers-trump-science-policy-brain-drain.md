Washington — Trump policies disrupt US research labs.

The [US](country:US) research base drew global talent through stable federal grants; funding reviews and visa restrictions since January 2025 have disrupted that pipeline across institutions.

A multi-university survey documents project cancellations and researcher departures toward European and East Asian institutions.
