Mastung — Motorcycle gunmen seized 20 police recruits heading home.

The recruits, Levies Force men now merged into [Pakistan](country:PK)'s police, were going home for the weekend.

The gunmen took the van and recruits to an undisclosed location but freed the driver and 3 passengers, officials said.

No group has claimed it, and security forces are searching the mountains nearby.
