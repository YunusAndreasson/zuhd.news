Israeli officials privately admit Iran's government may not fall. Israeli PM Benjamin Netanyahu launched the war promising regime change — but new Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei is consolidating power, not fracturing. Netanyahu has shifted to "it is up to them," leaving no plan for how the war ends.
