Saline — A township treasurer quit over Stargate death threats.

Jennifer Zink said she received tar-and-feather threats tied to the Oracle-OpenAI Stargate datacenter in Saline Township, Michigan.

Her resignation takes effect May 29; the Stargate zoning process in Saline Township continues.
