Iraq slashed Basra production by 73% this week. The Hormuz closure, which blocks 20% of global oil, left Iraqi storage full with no tankers able to lift exports. Iraq's oil revenue — over 90% of state income — may not recover until Hormuz reopens, with no timeline set.
