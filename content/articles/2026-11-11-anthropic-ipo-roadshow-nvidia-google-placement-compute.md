United States — Google and Nvidia each buy $10 billion of Anthropic.

Seven suppliers of its chips and cloud capacity are buying $26 billion of shares privately.

Anthropic's filing shows about $895 billion in future compute purchase and lease commitments, far above what the offering raises.

Founders sell up to $1.8 billion of stock yet keep 51.1% of votes through a special F share.

Pricing is set for 18 November, with Nasdaq trading to begin 19 November.
