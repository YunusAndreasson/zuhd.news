Iran's new supreme leader hasn't appeared since taking power. A state TV newsreader read Mojtaba Khamenei's first statement over a still photo, weeks after his father was killed on the first day of the war. Pete Hegseth said he is "wounded and likely disfigured," with Iran's military and clerics watching for a power vacuum.
