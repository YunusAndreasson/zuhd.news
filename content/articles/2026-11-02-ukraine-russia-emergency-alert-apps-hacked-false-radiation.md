Kyiv — [Ukraine](country:UA)'s air alert app sent false radiation warnings Sunday.

[Russia](country:RU)'s Emergencies Ministry reported its own alert app was hit hours later.

The false messages warned of nuclear and chemical threats near Kyiv, street combat and a deployed Oreshnik missile.

Kyiv's separate capital-city alert app kept working normally, city authorities said.

The Kherson truce ended Sunday, and Zelenskyy offers to stop refinery strikes if the [US](country:US) supplies Patriot missiles.
