Washington — A $238 million Navy surveillance drone crashed unannounced. Surveilling Hormuz, the MQ-4C Triton broadcast emergency codes, lost its ground link, and dropped from 50,000 feet on April 9. The Pentagon waited 5 days to confirm the loss; cause and crash site remain classified.
