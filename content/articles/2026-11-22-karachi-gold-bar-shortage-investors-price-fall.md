Karachi — Dealers charge up to Rs30,000 extra per tola for gold bars.

Investors outnumber jewellery buyers, jewellers say, after prices fell Rs208,664 per tola from the record.

Investors pay the premium and wait 1 to 2 days for delivery, a jewellers' association vice president said.

Haseen Qureishi of the Karachi manufacturers' association says half of investors await a further Rs40,000 to Rs50,000 drop.

Wedding demand peaks this winter, and Ramazan begins in early February.
