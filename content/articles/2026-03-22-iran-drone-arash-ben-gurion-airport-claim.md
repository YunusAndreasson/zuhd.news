Tel Aviv — Tehran claims its Arash-2 drone hit Ben Gurion Airport directly. Unlike earlier airport damage caused by intercepted missile debris, a direct hit would mean the drone bypassed Israel's air defenses. Israel has not confirmed the strike; whether the airport remains open will be the first independent verification.
