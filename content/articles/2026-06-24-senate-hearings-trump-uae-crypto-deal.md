Washington — [UAE](country:AE) nationals own 49% of Trump's family crypto venture.

5 Democratic senators demanded hearings, citing [US](country:US) policy decisions favorable to the UAE after the deal closed 4 days before Trump's inauguration, the senators said.

Senate committees have not scheduled hearings; neither World Liberty Financial nor the UAE government has commented.
