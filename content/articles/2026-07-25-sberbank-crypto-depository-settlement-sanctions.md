Sberbank will launch crypto custody by December.

The depository lets [Russia](country:RU) settle crypto trades on a domestic ledger instead of a Western intermediary that can freeze them, Interfax reported.

Public trading stays capped to assets above 5 trillion rubles; crypto payments inside Russia remain banned.
