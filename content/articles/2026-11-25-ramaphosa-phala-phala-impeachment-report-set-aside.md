South Africa — A court voided the report that revived Ramaphosa's impeachment.

Parliament's impeachment proceedings over the $580,000 Phala Phala theft are now at an end.

The court found the panel misconstrued its functions, made errors in law and exceeded its lawful remit.

The African Transformation Movement is consulting lawyers on an appeal, and the impeachment committee is due to meet Thursday.
