Darmstadt — A virus that infects algae carries a potassium channel gene.

Tandem potassium channels steady nerve-cell excitability in humans, yet no bacterial precursor has been found.

Experiments showed the gene encodes a working channel, and its evolutionary relationships suggest viral origin rather than theft from the host.

The same virus already yielded a light-controlled ion channel used in optogenetics.
