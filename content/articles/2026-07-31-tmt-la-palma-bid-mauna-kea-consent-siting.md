La Palma — A second-choice site is offering €1 billion.

[Spain](country:ES)'s package could bring the Thirty Meter Telescope here instead of Mauna Kea, where Native Hawaiian protest and litigation have blocked construction since 2014.

The US National Science Foundation still favors a rival telescope; the consortium's funding case erodes with every year unbuilt.
