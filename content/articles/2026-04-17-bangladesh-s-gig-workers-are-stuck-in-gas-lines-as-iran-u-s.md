Dhaka — Ride-share workers are queuing hours for fuel. Bangladesh imports most fuel from the Gulf; Iran-US war disruptions have turned Dhaka gas lines into multi-kilometer queues for shift-to-shift workers. The country has no domestic fuel buffer; how long the Hormuz disruption lasts will determine who survives it.
