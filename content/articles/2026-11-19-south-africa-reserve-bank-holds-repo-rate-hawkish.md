Pretoria — 3 of 7 rate-setters voted to hike from 7.25%.

Inflation hit 5.2% in October, the highest since May 2024, Statistics South Africa said.

Petrol costs almost R30 a litre against R21 a year ago, and transport drives over a third of inflation, the committee said.

Economist Johann Els sees little evidence fuel increases are feeding into other prices apart from transport.

The [US](country:US) Federal Reserve decides on 9 December, and Polymarket traders put a hike at 92%, up 6 points in a week.
