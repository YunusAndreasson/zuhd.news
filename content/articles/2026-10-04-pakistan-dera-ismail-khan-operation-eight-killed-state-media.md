Dera Ismail Khan — Security sources told state radio forces killed 8 militants.

State radio's account has no independent confirmation of who the 8 dead were.

[Pakistan](country:PK) calls them Fitna al Khawarij, its own term for the banned Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan, and says 3 were commanders.

The Pakistan Institute for Conflict and Security Studies counted 54 attacks in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa in August, up from 36.

State media reported 5 more killed in South Waziristan on Saturday and 7 in North Waziristan Friday.
