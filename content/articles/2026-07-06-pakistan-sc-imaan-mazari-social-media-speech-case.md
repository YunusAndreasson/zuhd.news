Islamabad — Imaan Mazari was sentenced to 17 years for social media posts.

A Peca complaint accused her of "propagating narratives" aligned with banned groups; the Supreme Court adjourned her appeal hearing to July 21.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s top court has not yet ruled on whether to fast-track her challenge to that sentence.
