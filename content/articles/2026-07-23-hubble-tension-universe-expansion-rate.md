Baltimore — Nobel laureate Adam Riess says the mismatch has become a cosmological crisis.

Two rigorous measurement methods disagree on how fast the universe expands, meaning either a measurement is subtly wrong or the standard model of cosmology is incomplete.

Neither side has yet found the flaw that would let the discrepancy resolve.
