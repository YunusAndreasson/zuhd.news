Police strangled children with packing tape over their heads. ICC prosecutors presented these killings and death lists at Rodrigo Duterte's crimes-against-humanity hearing, playing video of the former Philippine president declaring "I am the sole person responsible." Charges cover 76 of up to 30,000 drug war killings — the court must decide whether to proceed to trial.
