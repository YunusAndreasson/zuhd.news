Cambridge — A weak acid unlocks lithium from ordinary rock.

Bypassing the kiln cuts energy costs — Rock Zero projects under $6,000 per metric ton, competitive with brine extraction, per the study's authors.

Rock Zero plans a pilot plant by end-2026 and is in talks with mining partners.
