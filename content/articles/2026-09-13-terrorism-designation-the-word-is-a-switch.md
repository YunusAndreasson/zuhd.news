Washington — Newsrooms now write "gunman," not "terrorist."

A government designation unlocks asset freezes and removal without an ordinary hearing.

[US](country:US) and [UK](country:GB) laws now apply the label to nonviolent protest groups like antifa.

Affected groups must contest such labels after the fact, not before.
