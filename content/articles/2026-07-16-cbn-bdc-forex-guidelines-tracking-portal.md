Abuja — Nigeria will track every forex bureau purchase.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s central bank bans exclusivity deals and referral fees between banks and currency bureaus, tracking every purchase to break cartelized dollar access.

Bureaus face fines or suspended market access if they hold unsold dollars beyond 24 hours.
