Groningen — 41.5% of local colonies died over winter.

Nationwide losses hit 24%, a beekeepers' survey found — the fourth straight year above 20%, against 16.9% in Overijssel.

The survey gives no cause, leaving mite, pesticide and forage pressures unresolved before next winter.
