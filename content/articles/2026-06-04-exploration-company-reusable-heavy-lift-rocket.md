Munich — The Exploration Company unveiled Yrene, a reusable heavy-lift rocket to rival [US](country:US) launchers.

Yrene's 9 Storm engines would produce 180 tonnes of thrust each; no European vehicle currently reaches that lift class.

The Storm engine makes its public debut June 10 at the International Aerospace Exhibition.
