Washington — NOAA gives a historic El Niño 75% odds.

A weakened monsoon threatens food prices across South Asia and southern Africa.

El Niño shifts Pacific heat and rain eastward, a pattern reached only three times since 1950.

NOAA expects the event to peak between October and December this year.
