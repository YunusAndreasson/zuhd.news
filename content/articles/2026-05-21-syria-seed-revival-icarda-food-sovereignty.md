Jaramana — [Syria](country:SY)'s war-erased crop varieties are being revived near Damascus.

A regional seed bank evacuated its Fertile Crescent collection to Morocco and Lebanon in 2012; Jaramana volunteers now recover the landraces lost in the war.

The project runs on volunteer labor alone, with no state support and under international sanctions.
