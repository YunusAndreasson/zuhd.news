Seoul — Tusk names South Korea Poland's top defense partner. Poland's $44.2 billion arms framework covers K2 tanks, FA-50 jets, and artillery—bypassing NATO's procurement channels as Warsaw spends nearly 5% of GDP on defense. The partnership now includes joint production and tech transfers, making Seoul and Warsaw co-manufacturers.
