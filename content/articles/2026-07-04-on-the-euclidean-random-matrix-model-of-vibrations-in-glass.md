London — Glass has resisted full theory since 1971.

Physicists propose the Euclidean random matrix model, treating atoms as randomly placed, to explain glass's anomalous heat and sound behavior.

Researchers report the model already matches measurements on vitreous silica, with tests on more complex glasses planned next.
