London — Weakening EV sales rules could cost drivers £3bn a year.

Fewer electric cars on the road means households keep paying higher petrol running costs longer.

Electric cars cost about £1,100 less a year to run, so slower adoption delays that saving.

[Britain](country:GB)'s prime minister's office is reviewing a consultation on cutting the 2030 target to 50%.
