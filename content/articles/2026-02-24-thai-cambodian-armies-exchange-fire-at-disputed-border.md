A Cambodian grenade round broke the December ceasefire. Clashes along the 800-kilometre border killed dozens and displaced over 1 million people last year before a truce ended 3 weeks of fighting. Thailand accused Cambodia of breaking the truce, a claim Cambodia's defence ministry declined to address.
