Tehran — Brent jumped 3.2% to $86.80 after three tankers were disabled.

[Iran](country:IR)'s Revolutionary Guard says it struck the ships for using an "unauthorised passage," a claim that overrides transit passage rights UNCLOS grants at Hormuz.

No flag state, owner or the US Navy has confirmed the IRGC's account.
