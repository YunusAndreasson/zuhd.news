Ain Arab — Soldiers expelled a Lebanese village door-to-door at gunpoint.

12 days after a ceasefire, [Israel](country:IL) declared Ain Arab inside its unilateral 'yellow line' in [Lebanon](country:LB), giving residents 2 hours to leave.

Over 1.2 million Lebanese have been displaced since March 2, UN figures show; no return date has been given.
