Naha — China closed major ports a full day before Typhoon Dolphin's expected landfall.

Dolphin already injured six people and cut power to 50,000 buildings in [Japan](country:JP)'s Okinawa.

Zhejiang halted port operations and Shanghai's Yangshan port cleared its vessels on forecast data alone.

Landfall near Zhoushan is expected late Sunday into Monday.
