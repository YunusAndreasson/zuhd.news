Giza — 7 dead and 112 injured, the Health Ministry said.

[Egypt](country:EG)'s 110 million people depend heavily on a rail network with repeated deadly accidents.

A Giza-to-Fayoum train rammed the rear of the Cairo-to-Aswan service, derailing carriages onto a countryside road in al-Ayyat.

Al-Ayyat is where more than 370 people died in a 2002 train fire, Egypt's worst rail disaster.

Prosecutors are investigating the cause, and President Abdel Fattah al-Sisi has promised to punish negligence.
