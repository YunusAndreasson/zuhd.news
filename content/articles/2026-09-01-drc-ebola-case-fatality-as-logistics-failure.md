Bunia — Ebola has infected over 6,000 people in Congo.

Congolese government figures put the fatality rate near 48%, health officials said.

Containing it requires tracing contacts within days across six provinces of [DR Congo](country:CD) already strained by conflict.

No vaccine or treatment yet works against this outbreak's Bundibugyo strain.
