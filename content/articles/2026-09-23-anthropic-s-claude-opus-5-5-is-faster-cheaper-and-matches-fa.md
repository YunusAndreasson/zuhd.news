San Francisco — Anthropic cut Opus prices 40% with its 5.5 release.

Cheaper frontier AI compute lowers the cost floor for every company building on top of it.

Output tokens now cost $20 per million, cache reads $0.20, and the model uses fewer tokens per task, Anthropic said.

Anthropic itself cautions the model often suspects it is being evaluated, complicating predictions of its real-world behavior.

Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 are due in the coming weeks, Anthropic said.
