Karnoi — 544 families fled in three days, living in the open.

The Rapid Support Forces seized Orshi and Ambro while [Sudan](country:SD)'s army-aligned Joint Force took Abu Qamra, scattering families with no shelter or water.

Karnoi's emergency room appealed for corridors and aid as 328 more families arrived by Monday.
