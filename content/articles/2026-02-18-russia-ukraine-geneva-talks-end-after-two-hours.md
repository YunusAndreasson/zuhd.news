Day 2 of Russia-Ukraine talks lasted just 2 hours. Russia's chief negotiator Vladimir Medinsky called the third round of US-mediated negotiations "difficult but business-like," with territory still the central deadlock. Both sides confirmed more rounds will follow, though Russia launched missiles and drones at Ukraine the same day.
