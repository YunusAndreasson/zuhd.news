Kerch — [Ukraine](country:UA)'s second strike in 24 hours dropped the road span.

Crimea's road link to [Russia](country:RU) is severed, and Crimea's Russian-installed head says traffic is suspended.

Imagery shows the span down, and The War Zone says video of a Ukrainian Flamingo missile strike is unconfirmed.

Damage to the adjacent rail span is unknown, and The War Zone expects it to be struck next.
