German car exports to China fell another third in 2025, dropping below €14 billion. The German Economic Institute reported shipments have halved since their 2022 peak of €30 billion as Chinese automakers dominate their home market, especially in electric vehicles. Chancellor Friedrich Merz travelled to Beijing this week for his first visit as the trade relationship unravels.
