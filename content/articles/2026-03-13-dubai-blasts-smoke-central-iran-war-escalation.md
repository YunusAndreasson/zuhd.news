Iranian drones struck Dubai's financial district Friday. Iran's strikes had moved from the city's airport and port to Palm Jumeirah before hitting the financial district. Dubai's 3.5 million residents, mostly migrant workers, have no embassy evacuation as Iran's targeting reaches civilian zones.
