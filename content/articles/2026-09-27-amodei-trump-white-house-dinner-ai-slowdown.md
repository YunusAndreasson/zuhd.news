Washington — Trump will host Anthropic's Dario Amodei for dinner Sunday.

The dinner puts Anthropic back in the room after being barred from federal contracts.

A federal appeals court ruled Friday that the Pentagon acted legally in barring Anthropic over a stalled $200 million AI contract.

Trump holds a tech summit Tuesday, and the contract dispute remains unresolved.
