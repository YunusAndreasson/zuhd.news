Maarrat al-Numan — 500 burned tires over a 40% fuel hike.

Diesel fuels ovens, pumps and generators, so the hike shocks bread, water and power.

[Syria](country:SY)'s transitional government raised prices Sunday, inheriting a subsidy system it cannot fund since Bashar al-Assad's 2024 ouster.

Protesters say they still back the state, testing what dissent means without a dictatorship to blame.
