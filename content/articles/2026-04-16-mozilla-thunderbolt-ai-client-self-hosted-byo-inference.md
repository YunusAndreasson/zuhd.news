San Francisco — Mozilla's Thunderbolt runs AI entirely on your hardware. Built on open-source Haystack, it connects to local llama.cpp, Ollama, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint — routing zero prompts through third-party servers. Mozilla is seeking enterprise licensing; the client is under security audit and not yet in production.
