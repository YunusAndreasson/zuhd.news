New York — Attackers drained Foundation's and Citadel21's Lightning nodes.

Lightning nodes must stay hot to route payments; a flaw exposed the credential files that control them.

BTCPay told Lightning node operators to update immediately; the team has not disclosed how much bitcoin was taken.
