Islamabad — Imaan Mazari is being tried for terrorism inside Adiala jail.

Six prosecution witnesses testified while she and her lawyers stayed away in protest, the petition says.

Her lawyer says the police report stayed concealed for 2 years, then police arrested her right after Supreme Court bail.

The Supreme Court is asked to stay the trial the Islamabad High Court left running.
