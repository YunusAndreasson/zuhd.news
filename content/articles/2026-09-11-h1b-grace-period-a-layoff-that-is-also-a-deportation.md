Washington — Homeland Security would erase the 60-day job hunt.

It hands employers leverage over layoffs, since firing now deports workers overnight.

H-1B visas, mostly held by workers from [India](country:IN), currently allow 60 days to find new sponsors.

The proposal heads to public comment before Homeland Security decides whether to finalize it.
