London — A decade of cancer immunotherapy progress has stalled.

Patients with treatment-resistant tumors now wait longer for next-generation drugs.

Researchers spent years copying one success instead of understanding how checkpoint drugs release tumors' brake on T cells.

The review's authors say the correction now costs years the field cannot easily recover.
