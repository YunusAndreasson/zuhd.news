Mumbai — Rs 25,000 fined for leaving a 17-year-old permanently vegetative.

[India](country:IN)'s Railways rejected a Rs 5 crore civil settlement the Bombay High Court recommended; a criminal court then imposed a Rs 25,000 fine — a 2,000-fold gap.

The Bombay High Court has ordered the Railways Ministry to produce its compensation refusal file.
