London — [UK](country:GB) carbon plan shows £865bn net economic benefit.

Investing £880bn over 25 years yields £1,620bn in benefits, the government's impact assessment finds — £445bn from reduced fossil-fuel imports alone.

Parliament must approve the seventh budget before end of June; the government must then publish a delivery plan.
