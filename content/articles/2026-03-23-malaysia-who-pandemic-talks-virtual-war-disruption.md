Kuala Lumpur — Gulf airspace closures have pushed WHO pandemic treaty talks online. Member states have failed to agree on a final pandemic treaty text after 4 years of negotiations. The World Health Assembly convenes in May — continued airspace disruptions could block the in-person sessions where the treaty's contested clauses must be resolved.
