Beijing — A 21-year-old cried daily over her chatbot boyfriend.

New rules on emotionally intelligent chatbots are ending months-long companion relationships overnight.

ByteDance's Doubao is now cutting companion features to comply with [Beijing](country:CN)'s new chatbot rules.

Beijing has not said whether other companion-app makers nationwide must follow the same shutdown.
