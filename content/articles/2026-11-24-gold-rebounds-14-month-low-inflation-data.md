United States — Gold rebounded 2.4% to $3,616.51 an ounce Monday.

It fell about 9% last week, its steepest weekly decline since March.

Capital.com analyst Kyle Rodda says the tech selloff made markets reprice [US](country:US) rates, which supports non-yielding gold.

Pepperstone's Dilin Wu cautions against calling a bottom, citing possible forced liquidation.

US inflation data lands Wednesday, and the Federal Reserve decides on 9 December.
