Egypt's Suez earnings fell when the Iran war began. Egypt earns most of its foreign currency from Suez fees, tourism, and Gulf remittances, all of which the war has disrupted. Egypt's government has offered no emergency measures, leaving 60% of the population to absorb price shocks without a safety net.
