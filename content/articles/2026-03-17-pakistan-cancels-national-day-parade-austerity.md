Pakistan cancelled its March 23 National Day parade, citing the economic crisis caused by the Hormuz shutdown. The government has already cut official fuel allowances by 50% and reduced state-sector salaries by up to 30%. The parade has been held since 1940 to commemorate Pakistan's founding; its cancellation is the starkest sign yet that the Hormuz conflict is restructuring daily life in a country 3,000 km from the fighting.
