Dubai — Shipfinex and ADI Chain tokenize ship debt.

Bank syndicates retreated for years, leaving smaller operators without institutional financing access.

Tokenization splits loan participations into tradable units for new investors, not the vessel's underlying credit risk.

ADI Chain and Shipfinex now target the $680 billion ship lending and leasing market.
