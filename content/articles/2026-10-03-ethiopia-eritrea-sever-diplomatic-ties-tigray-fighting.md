Addis Ababa — [Ethiopia](country:ET) expelled 10 Eritrean diplomats on Thursday.

[Eritrea](country:ER) cut all diplomatic ties in response, as federal forces advance on Tigray's capital Mekelle.

Addis Ababa accuses Eritrea, [Egypt](country:EG) and [Sudan](country:SD) of backing the Tigray People's Liberation Front alliance, and all three deny it.

Eritrea's information minister Yemani Meskel called Ethiopia's move a "declaration of war."

African leaders meet at the Egypt-Africa Summit in Cairo, but no one has offered to broker talks.
