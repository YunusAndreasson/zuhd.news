Abuja — [Nigeria](country:NG) threatened to revoke mining firms' licences.

Minister Dele Alake said mining firms that ignore legally required Community Development Agreements with host communities risk having licences revoked.

Alake gave no enforcement timeline, and no licences have yet been revoked under the policy.
