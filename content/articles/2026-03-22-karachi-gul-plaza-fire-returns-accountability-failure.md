Karachi — Gul Plaza burned again, 2 months after killing 70. Authorities sealed it after the January fire but left it unguarded, letting squatters start a basement blaze that police had warned was coming. 3 are in custody and 8 officers suspended, but no government body has committed to compensating the hundreds of vendors displaced since January or to securing the building against another fire.
