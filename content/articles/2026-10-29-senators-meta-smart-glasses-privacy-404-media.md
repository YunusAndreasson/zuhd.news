Washington — Senators say Meta's own disclosures show glasses footage may train AI.

Ron Wyden and Edward Markey wrote Wednesday that most bystanders filmed never consented.

Their letter cites an investigation into Instagram accounts posting Ray-Ban glasses videos of men soliciting massage parlour workers.

More than 70 people are suing Meta, claiming labelling workers saw their intimate images.
