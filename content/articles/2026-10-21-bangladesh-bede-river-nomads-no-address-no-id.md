Tangail — Estimates of [Bangladesh](country:BD)'s Bede range from 75,000 to 800,000.

Clinics, schools and civil registers assume a fixed address, which boat families lack.

Floods, erosion and drying channels are forcing families off the Jamuna River, according to a May 2026 study of three Tangail settlements.

A Dhaka University study of Munshiganj settlements urges government agencies and relief NGOs to coordinate.
