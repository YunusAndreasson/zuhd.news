China — Huawei shipped 572,000 AI accelerators in six months.

Huawei holds 28% of [China](country:CN)'s data-center AI accelerator market to Nvidia's 34%, research firm IDC says.

[US](country:US) export restrictions constrained Nvidia's supply, and its China shipments fell 44% to 678,000 as 9 of 11 Chinese suppliers grew.

The figures stop at June, so full-year 2026 shares are not yet counted.
