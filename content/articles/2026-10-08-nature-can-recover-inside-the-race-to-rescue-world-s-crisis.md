Bwindi — Average monitored wildlife population shrank 73% in 52 years, WWF says.

Mountain gorillas, once near extinction, now number over 1,100 across [Uganda](country:UG), [Rwanda](country:RW) and [Congo](country:CD).

Tourists at Bwindi pay $800 a visit, and villages around the park earn from lodging, carvings and ranger jobs.

Grauer's gorillas in Congo have fallen an estimated 60% to 6,800.

WWF names the food, energy and finance systems as the drivers it wants transformed.
