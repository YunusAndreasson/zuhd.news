Bogotá — One decree word closes a demobilization path.

Terrorist status bars fighters from surrender deals available to ordinary criminal suspects.

The label also opens the door to foreign military assistance and to lethal force framed as war, not policing.

[Colombia](country:CO)'s courts and rights groups have not yet challenged the president's order.
