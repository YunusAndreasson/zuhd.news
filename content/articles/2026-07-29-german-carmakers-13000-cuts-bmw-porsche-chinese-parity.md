Munich — BMW joins every major German carmaker cutting jobs.

BMW will cut 8,000 desk roles by 2027 and Porsche another 5,000, as Chinese rivals reach price and quality parity.

BMW reports half-year results Thursday in [Germany](country:DE), the first test of CEO Nedeljković's restructuring plan.
