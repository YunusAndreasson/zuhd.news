Tina — Rapid Support Forces drones killed at least 14 in [Sudan](country:SD)'s Eid markets.

Families bought Eid livestock when shrapnel tore through market crowds; a local activist said the strikes targeted supply lines in military-held Darfur.

Army forces advanced to within 5 km of RSF-held Kurmuk on the Blue Nile front by Wednesday.
