Washington — Duffy wants $50 billion after a 4-hour ground stop.

Republican Senate leader John Thune says lawmakers need to know why more money is needed.

The request adds to $12.5 billion approved last year for modernisation, of which Democrats say only $1 billion is spent.

Bernstein estimates the four largest airlines face a $345 million pretax hit.

Speaker Mike Johnson will take up the request in the lame-duck session.
