Abuja — [Nigeria](country:NG)'s rural mothers die at 656 per 100,000 births.

The Northwest and Northeast account for three-quarters of facility deaths, which rose there while falling nationally.

The health ministry's review counts only deaths in health facilities and admits the figures may be under- or over-reported.

Facility-recorded maternal deaths fell from 5,391 in 2024 to 4,455 in 2025.

The national ratio stands at 572 per 100,000, against a 2030 target of fewer than 70.
