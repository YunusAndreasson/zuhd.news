Rasuwagadi — 934 workers are trapped or missing at hydropower sites.

Most of the trapped or missing are migrant dam laborers, not the flood's other missing foreigners.

[Nepal](country:NP) built its hydropower plants in the same steep valleys that glacial lake floods scour.

Nepal has not yet requested [India](country:IN)'s tunnel-rescue experts, who remain on standby.
