Ellesmere Island — A rare epishelf lake drained into the Arctic.

The drainage cannot be reversed, ending a distinct freshwater habitat.

An ice shelf dams meltwater atop denser seawater, and [Canada](country:CA)'s Milne shelf broke apart in 2020.

The lake's ecosystem now exists only in data recorded before 2020.
