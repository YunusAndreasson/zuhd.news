Madinah — An unclaimed attack set Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline ablaze.

It became the kingdom's outlet for crude exports once the US-Israeli war shut the Strait of Hormuz.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s energy ministry said operations were unaffected and no one was hurt.

Iraqi militia drones forced a temporary shutdown in September, and the Houthis claimed a pumping station strike last month.

Polymarket traders give a Saudi-Houthi ceasefire by 31 December a 31% chance, up 8 points in a week.
