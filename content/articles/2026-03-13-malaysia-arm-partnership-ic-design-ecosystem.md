Malaysia assembles chips but does not design them. Malaysia already assembles for Intel and Infineon under Arm's architecture, with chip design — where margins are highest — remaining in the hands of US and European firms. The economy minister said the partnership must produce tangible results for Malaysia's economy and local talent, not just announcements.
