212 seats out of 299. The Bangladesh Nationalist Party won a two-thirds parliamentary majority on February 12, returning to power after 20 years in opposition. Party leader Tarique Rahman will be sworn in as prime minister. Rahman, 60, fled to London in 2008 and was convicted in absentia on corruption charges under former Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina, who was ousted in a 2024 student-led uprising in which security forces killed hundreds of protesters. Jamaat-e-Islami won 77 seats as the largest opposition bloc, giving this parliament more pluralism than any in recent Bangladeshi history.
