San Francisco — OpenAI seeks up to $30 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation.

An Emirati investor co-leads, and OpenAI is negotiating more computing capacity in the [UAE](country:AE).

The target is $150 billion below the roughly $1.4 trillion OpenAI sought in September.

OpenAI, which filed confidentially for its listing in June, has not commented, and terms remain under negotiation.
