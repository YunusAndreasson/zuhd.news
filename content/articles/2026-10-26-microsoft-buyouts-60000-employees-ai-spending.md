Redmond — Microsoft forecasts $175 billion in capital spending this year.

It is offering buyouts to 60,000 employees, more than a quarter of its workforce.

Employees at level 70 and below in the [US](country:US), Canada, UK and India can leave on 31 December.

Microsoft excludes marketing, commercial business and security units, and some data center staff.

Investors get first-quarter results Wednesday, with scrutiny over whether AI returns justify the spending.
