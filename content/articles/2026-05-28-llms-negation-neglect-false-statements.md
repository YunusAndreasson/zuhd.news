San Francisco — LLMs believe false statements even after explicit warnings.

Models fine-tuned on explicitly labeled falsehoods still believed them 88.6% of the time, researchers found — negation is not a reliable safety control.

The fix: embed negation in the same sentence as the claim — broader production applications remain untested.
