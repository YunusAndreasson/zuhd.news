Hormuz — [US](country:US) Central Command says it escorted 1.5 billion barrels of Gulf crude.

[Iran](country:IR) says the strait stays closed until Washington meets seven conditions, including lifting the blockade of its ports.

The command says 3,000 ships transited in six months, over 95% safely, and its forces cleared Iranian-laid mines from main routes.

Traffic still ran 11% below normal, at 3.7 ships a day as of 18 October.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 2 November a 7% chance.
