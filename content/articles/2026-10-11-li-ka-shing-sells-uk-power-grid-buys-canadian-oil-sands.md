Hong Kong — Li Ka-shing's family sold a British grid for HK$110 billion.

The family trust and CK Hutchison hold about 30% of Cenovus, the buyer of Athabasca Oil.

Cenovus pays about C$5.7 billion in cash and shares for Athabasca, whose proven and probable reserves last over 75 years.

Cenovus expects about C$85 million in yearly savings, after the deal's expected December close.
