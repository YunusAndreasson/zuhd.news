Banten — Rescuers found no trace of the missing photojournalists.

Photojournalists, including one from [Turkey](country:TR)'s Anadolu Agency, vanished covering a volcanic eruption in [Indonesia](country:ID).

A 2018 landslide tsunami at Anak Krakatau killed over 400 people with no warning.

The governor says the search could resume if searchers get new leads.
