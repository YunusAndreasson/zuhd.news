Havana — [Cuba](country:CU) suspends Visa and Mastercard transactions from June 6.

The central bank said a foreign intermediary cut card processing after a [US](country:US) executive order on May 1 broadened sanctions, triggering an exodus of hotels, airlines, and shipping firms from the island.

With no alternative payment network, tourists and businesses are limited to cash.
