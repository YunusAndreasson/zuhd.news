Vladivostok — Moscow plans $80 billion in Far East deals.

Deputy Prime Minister Yury Trutnev said the forum totals are signed intentions, not disbursed capital, as [Russia](country:RU) redirects investment east under sanctions.

The Eastern Economic Forum convenes September 1-4, when Trutnev's 6.5 trillion ruble target will be tested.
