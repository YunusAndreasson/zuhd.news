United States — One player's mod hides all chat from Asmongold's guild.

Fans of the streamer flooded the World of Warcraft: Forever beta's public chat with slurs and guild-invite spam.

OlympusMute hides messages from any player with Olympus in their name and auto-declines their group and guild invites.

Solastro, the mod's creator, said the spam "was too pervasive to be able to /ignore each player."

The mod works only on the installing player's screen, so the spam continues for everyone else.
