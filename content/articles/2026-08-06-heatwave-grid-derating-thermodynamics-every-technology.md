Paris — Three of [France](country:FR)'s 57 reactors shut in July.

Thermal plants convert heat to power via a temperature gradient that heatwaves shrink; hot solar panels lose efficiency too.

France's nuclear fleet has now lost output to heat in seven summers since 2003.
