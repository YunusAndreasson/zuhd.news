Bishkek — President Sadyr Japarov says he ordered a Bakiyev-era financier to mine crypto.

30 megawatts of a state hydropower plant now feed a private mining farm in [Kyrgyzstan](country:KG).

Of Kambar-Ata 2's 120 megawatts, 90 reach the grid, and the 30 stranded by weak lines go to mining.

A 2023 cabinet decree grants the firm 10 years, the energy ministry confirmed then, not Japarov's 4 to 5.

Japarov says Alexei Shirshov hands the farm to the state grid operator in two years.
