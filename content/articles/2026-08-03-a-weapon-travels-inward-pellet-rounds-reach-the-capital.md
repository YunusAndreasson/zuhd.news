Delhi — police fired pellet shotguns at protesting students.

Pellet guns, adopted as 'non-lethal' in Kashmir since 2010, have blinded hundreds there and now reached students in Delhi on July 20.

Congress chief Mallikarjun Kharge wants Home Minister Amit Shah to explain the action in Parliament.
