Minna — Alhassan Niworo endured 225 days in captivity.

Bandits demanded ₦150 million for him; other captives' families paid ₦5-10 million each, he said.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s police have not said whether the gunmen who wore their uniforms were identified.
