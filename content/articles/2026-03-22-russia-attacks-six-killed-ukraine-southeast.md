Zaporizhzhia — Russian drones killed 6 Ukrainians on Saturday. A drone struck a residential house in Zaporizhzhia, killing 2 and wounding 8, as Ukrainian and US peace negotiators were meeting in Florida. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky warned Russia must not be rewarded in any deal, while Ukraine's delegation pushes back against what it calls Washington's prioritization of Iran over Ukraine.
