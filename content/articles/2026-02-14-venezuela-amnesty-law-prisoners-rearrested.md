Venezuela freed a political prisoner, then rearrested him hours later. Armed men detained Juan Pablo Guanipa after his release from 8 months in prison and placed him under house arrest for violating unspecified conditions. Acting President Delcy Rodríguez's government has freed 431 political prisoners since January 8 and advanced an amnesty law covering political cases since 1999. Rights group Foro Penal estimates more than 600 people remain jailed for political reasons. The government pledged to close El Helicoide, a Caracas prison documented as a torture center, but the rearrest pattern suggests amnesty as performance.
