Bamako — A drone strike hit Inafarak village in July.

Investigators say the munitions were likely Russian-built, aiding [Mali](country:ML)'s fight against Tuareg rebels.

Satellite images matched to 2024 photos confirm strikes also hit Talahandak, independent of either side's account.

Officials haven't said whether [Russia](country:RU)'s Africa Corps supplied the drones directly.
