Riyadh — [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) plans a Yemen offensive, six people briefed on it say.

Houthi control of Bab el-Mandeb threatens the Red Sea route that replaced closed Hormuz.

Yemeni forces overseen by Riyadh would lead on the ground, backed by Saudi air strikes, with over 100,000 troops possible.

A [Pakistan](country:PK)i official says its 30,000-40,000 troops in Saudi Arabia have no order to fight in Yemen.

Timing ranges from within a week to after the early November US midterms.
