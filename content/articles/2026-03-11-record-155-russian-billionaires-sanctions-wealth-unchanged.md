155 Russians made the Forbes billionaires list — a record. For a 4th consecutive year, the number grew despite EU and US sanctions, two rounds of asset freezes, and an ongoing war, with combined wealth reaching $695.5 billion. The EU and US maintain their sanctions aim to weaken Russia's elite — a claim the Forbes record directly contradicts.
