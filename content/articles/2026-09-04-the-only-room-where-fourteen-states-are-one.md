Taipei — Taiwan accuses China of forum-floor espionage.

The dispute threatens the only body where 14 Pacific states negotiate as one bloc.

[China](country:CN) objected to [Taiwan](country:TW)'s attendance at summit host [Palau](country:PW), then issued Palau a travel warning last month.

Pacific states risk losing climate financing and fisheries terms to the rivalry.
