Seoul — The Federal Bureau of Investigation and [South Korea](country:KR) warned of Gunra ransomware.

Hospitals and utilities behind breached firewalls face the same downtime risk worldwide.

Firewalls sit exposed to the internet by design and run vendor firmware customers cannot inspect or patch.

The advisory does not name which firewall vendors or flaws are exploited.
