United States — Gig workers at both apps would answer to one company.

Instacart workers average about $13 an hour and DoorDash workers about $11, Gridwise data show.

DoorDash plans to keep both apps, cross-list merchants and let workers take restaurant and grocery orders.

"Greater earning opportunities for shoppers," promised CEO Tony Xu, without committing to higher pay.

The $17 billion deal closes only if federal regulators approve it.
