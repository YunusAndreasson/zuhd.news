New York — Fox acquired Roku for $22 billion, gaining the platform used by 100 million streaming households.

Roku earns 90% of revenue from digital advertising; the deal gives Fox a direct layer between viewers and every streaming service.

Shareholder votes from both firms are pending; the deal targets a first-half 2027 close.
