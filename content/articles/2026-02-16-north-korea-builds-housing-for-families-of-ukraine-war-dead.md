Pyongyang built homes for its Ukraine war dead. Kim Jong Un inaugurated Saeppyol Street, a housing district for families of North Korean soldiers killed fighting alongside Russian forces — the government's most explicit acknowledgment yet of combat losses from its troop deployment. A ruling party congress later this month is expected to set 5-year policy goals, with Kim using the "young martyrs" to rally domestic support.
