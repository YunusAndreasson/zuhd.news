Washington — Cairncross fired after outages cancelled 12,462 flights in one day.

The post coordinating [US](country:US) federal cyber defence now has only an acting occupant.

Outages hit Microsoft, air traffic control, Chase, United Airlines and the National Security Agency between 12 and 17 November.

Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton told Congress Wednesday there is no indication outside actors caused the outages.

Kirsten Davies, the Pentagon's chief information officer, takes the post in an acting capacity.
