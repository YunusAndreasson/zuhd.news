San Francisco — A decade of Pokémon Go scans now help military drones navigate.

Niantic Spatial trained on 30 billion scans, then licensed its visual positioning model to Vantor, a contractor with [US](country:US) military and intelligence clients.

Scopely, Saudi-backed and now owning Pokémon Go, controls current scans under new privacy terms.
