Panama ordered the seizure of both canal entrances. Its Supreme Court voided Hong Kong-based CK Hutchison's 29-year concession, ordering occupation of terminals the US had called evidence of Chinese control. CK Hutchison filed for international arbitration as Danish operator APM Terminals takes interim control.
