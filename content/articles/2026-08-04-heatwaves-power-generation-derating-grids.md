London — Three of France's 57 reactors shut down in July's heat, not just wind and solar.

A power plant is a heat engine: hotter cooling water or air narrows the temperature gap it runs on, cutting output from nuclear, gas and solar alike.

Grids in Pakistan, Iraq and Nigeria run mostly on gas turbines with the least reserve margin to absorb it.
