BioNTech's founders stepped down to build their 3rd company. Uğur Şahin and Özlem Türeci, who developed BioNTech's mRNA COVID-19 vaccine, say the technology holds untapped potential against cancer, malaria, and HIV. BioNTech will hold a minority stake in the new venture via patent licenses, with both founders departing by year-end.
