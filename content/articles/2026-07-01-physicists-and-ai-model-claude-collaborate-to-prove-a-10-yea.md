Rome — A decade-old jamming physics problem, solved.

Nobel laureate Giorgio Parisi and Francesco Zamponi say the AI model Claude contributed to finding the proof, published in the Journal of Statistical Mechanics.

Neither physicist has specified which step Claude supplied, leaving open how far AI can verify rigorous math unaided.
