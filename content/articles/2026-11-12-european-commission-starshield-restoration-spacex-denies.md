Brussels — Starshield caps Ukraine's drones at 161 kph, Zelenskyy says.

The European Commission demanded urgent restoration and called the outages unacceptable if confirmed.

[Ukraine](country:UA)'s interceptors cannot reach jet-powered Shahed drones at that speed, Zelenskyy said, and some have already hit.

SpaceX's media office told Ars Technica the claim is false and nothing has changed.

Ukraine's Starshield contract with the [US](country:US) expires next year, and Kyiv is seeking new talks.
