Santa Clara — Intel jumped 11% on a reported Google foundry order.

The Google order, if confirmed, would give Intel Foundry its first major outside customer — 3 million AI chips for 2028 — challenging Taiwan Semiconductor's hold on US AI chip production.

Nvidia is also reportedly evaluating Intel as a backup foundry; no order has been confirmed.
