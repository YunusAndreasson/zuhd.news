Former elite officer Sotheni allegedly shot South Africa's corruption witness dead before his family. The witness, Van der Merwe, had named police officers in torture and murder at the Madlanga anti-corruption commission. Sotheni faces a bail hearing March 25; police have yet to recover the AK-47.
