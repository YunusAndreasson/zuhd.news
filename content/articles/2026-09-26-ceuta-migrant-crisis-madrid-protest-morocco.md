Ceuta — 8,300 migrants sit in shelters while 4,000 sleep outside.

Thousands marched in Madrid on Saturday demanding an early election over the crisis.

[Morocco](country:MA) says it will take them back, but [Spain](country:ES) must first identify each person and hear asylum claims.

Spanish authorities deny migrants' accounts of beatings by uniformed men, saying police respect human rights.

Spain's cabinet is reportedly due Tuesday to approve a decree shortening asylum decisions and speeding returns.
