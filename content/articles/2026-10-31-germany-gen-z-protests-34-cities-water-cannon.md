Berlin — Water cannon met young Germans marching in 34 cities.

Marchers want affordable living, school funding and no conscription from Chancellor Friedrich Merz's government.

Police in Cologne fired water cannon after saying protesters targeted officers with fireworks.

Berlin police counted 5,000 by evening against organisers' 10,000, and 170,000 had registered nationwide.

Berlin is in contact with French and Italian officials over fears that radicals from abroad could join.
