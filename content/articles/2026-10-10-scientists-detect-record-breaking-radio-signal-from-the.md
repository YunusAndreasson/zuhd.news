Sydney — FRB 20240304B crossed 10 billion light years to reach Earth.

It is more than twice as far as the next-farthest known fast radio burst.

[South Africa](country:ZA)'s MeerKAT caught it on 4 March 2024, and James Webb located its host galaxy at redshift 2.148.

Manisha Caleb's team says only that the host galaxy is consistent with a magnetar origin.

Radio telescopes' sensitivity limits have kept bursts beyond redshift 1 scarce, Caleb's team says.
