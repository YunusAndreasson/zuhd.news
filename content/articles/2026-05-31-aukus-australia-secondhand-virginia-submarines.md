Singapore — [Australia](country:AU) drops new-build subs for used [US](country:US) hulls.

American shipyards produce 1.1 Virginia-class submarines per year; AUKUS needs 2.33. Australia's sovereign SSN-AUKUS fleet isn't due until 2042.

Canberra will extend its 30-year-old Collins-class boats by 10 years to bridge the gap to first delivery.
