Moscow — Early ballots open Friday in occupied Ukrainian territory.

The vote treats annexed land as domestic Russian territory, absent international recognition.

Voting runs 20 days across Donbas, Crimea and 7 other regions, with no single day to observe.

The main vote falls September 18-20, and [Ukraine](country:UA) says it will not recognize the result.
