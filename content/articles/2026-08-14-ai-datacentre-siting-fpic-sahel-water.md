N'Djamena — Predictive tools help herders in [Chad](country:TD) dodge drought.

Yet developers rarely consult the communities whose water cools those systems.

Free, prior and informed consent already governs mining and dam projects on Indigenous land, but not datacentre siting.

African governments are still drafting national strategies without requiring environmental impact assessments.
