Nairobi — Fertilizer and wheat exports vanished overnight in 2022.

Import-dependent [Kenya](country:KE) absorbed a food-price shock it did not cause.

Russia's invasion of Ukraine cut global wheat, maize, sunflower oil and fertilizer exports, pushing food prices to records.

Researchers are now tracing exactly how that price shock reached household plates in Kenya.
