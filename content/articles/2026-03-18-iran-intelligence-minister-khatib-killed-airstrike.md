Israel killed Iran's spy chief Esmail Khatib in Tehran. Khatib ran VAJA since 2021 — Iran's domestic surveillance agency that managed informant networks and organized crackdowns on protesters. Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian vowed Iran's intelligence work would continue, leaving Tehran to rebuild its surveillance leadership amid the Nowruz protest season.
