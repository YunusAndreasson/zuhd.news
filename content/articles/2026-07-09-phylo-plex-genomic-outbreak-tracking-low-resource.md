Cambridge — Pathogen genomics no longer needs expensive lab equipment.

Wellcome Sanger Institute scientists built Phylo-Plex, a scalable sequencing method for labs with limited resources, published in Nature Communications.

The tool lets under-resourced labs track antibiotic resistance and detect new variants where outbreaks actually start.
