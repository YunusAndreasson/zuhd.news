Iho, Imo State — A Nigerian court dismissed a $25 million Meta privacy suit.

The ruling shows how hard it is to prove Big Tech misused data it alone controls.

The judge said she failed to show how Meta processed her data, after a regulator's $32.8 million fine was set aside.

Meta argued consent is not the only lawful basis for processing data under [Nigeria](country:NG)'s 2023 law.

The regulator's case against Meta remains settled and set aside, leaving no legal record to build on.
