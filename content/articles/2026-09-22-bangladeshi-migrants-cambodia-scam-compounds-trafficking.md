Phnom Penh — 17,031 migrants from [Bangladesh](country:BD) traveled to [Cambodia](country:KH) for promised jobs.

Recruiters trap graduates in debt, then sell them into forced cyber-scam labor abroad.

Middlemen post fake job ads on Facebook and WhatsApp, seize arriving workers' passports, then sell them to scam compounds.

"Every single promise turns out to be a scam," said Riaz Ahmed, a migration researcher.

Recruiters keep posting new ads, and each graduate who answers risks the same debt-bondage pipeline.
