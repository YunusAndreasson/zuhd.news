Beirut — [Lebanon](country:LB)'s state telecom company has existed on paper since 2005.

Rights groups warn public funds are at risk as telecom assets get valued without governance safeguards.

Cabinet ordered a valuation of ministry and Ogero assets on 8 October, excluding mobile networks, with operator Alfa managing the tender.

Former Touch manager Wissam Mansour says valuing before reform lowers the price, with 1 million subscribers on illegal networks.

Ogero's employees and the assets headed to Liban Telecom remain undefined.
