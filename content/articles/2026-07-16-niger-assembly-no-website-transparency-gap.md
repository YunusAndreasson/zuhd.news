Minna — 27 years of democracy, zero legislative website.

[Nigeria](country:NG) builds digital ID and payment infrastructure nationally, yet Niger State's assembly publishes no bills, votes or committee records online, forcing citizens into physical requests.

The assembly says a website is planned; no funding timeline has been disclosed.
