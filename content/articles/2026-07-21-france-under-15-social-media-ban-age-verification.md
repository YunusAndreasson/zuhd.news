Paris — "If someone is under 15, the account will be closed."

[France](country:FR)'s parliament passed the EU's first blanket social media ban for under-15s; enforcing it by age requires proving age for everyone.

The Constitutional Council must rule before the September rollout, as other European governments watch closely.
