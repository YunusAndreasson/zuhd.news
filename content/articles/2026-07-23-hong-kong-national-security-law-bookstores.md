Hong Kong — Independent bookstores are closing shelf by shelf.

Licensing pressure, landlord harassment and inspections under the National Security Law are squeezing the city's remaining independent booksellers, not arrests.

The attrition leaves readers and small proprietors with a shrinking, self-censoring stock.
