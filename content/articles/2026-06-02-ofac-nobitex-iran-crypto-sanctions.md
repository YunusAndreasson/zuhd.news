Washington — [The US](country:US) blacklisted [Iran](country:IR)'s 4 largest crypto exchanges.

Nobitex, Wallex, Bitpin and Ramzinex are barred from the dollar system, cutting the main channel Iranians use to move money outside state banks.

Treasury Secretary Bessent said the US has seized $1bn in Iranian crypto since the war began.
