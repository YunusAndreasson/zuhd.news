Hormuz disruptions could push oil to $100 a barrel. Pacific islands have no domestic oil, no reserves, and no alternative suppliers — the most exposed nations to a conflict they didn't start. Impacts may reach Pacific consumers within 4 months as higher shipping costs filter through fuel, transport, and tourism prices.
