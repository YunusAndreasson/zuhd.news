Ukraine — Drone fiber costs 6 to 10 times its 2025 price, a manufacturer says.

[Russia](country:RU) bought over 10% of the world's optical fiber in 2025, up from under 1%.

Chinese customs data show [China](country:CN) shipped Russia 328,000 miles in August 2025 and Ukraine 72 miles.

TrendForce projects a 111 million mile global fiber shortfall in 2026, and Ukrainian makers now spool fiber in-house.
