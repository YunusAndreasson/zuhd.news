Honiara — Solomon Islands is quitting its 2022 China security pact, unnamed officials say.

[China](country:CN) ties were a voter concern in May's election that made Matthew Wale prime minister.

The 5-year pact expires in April, and Wale's government has told Beijing it will not renew it.

China's foreign ministry says its Pacific cooperation targets no third party.

Wale meets Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese on Wednesday, where a treaty making [Australia](country:AU) the top security partner is expected.
