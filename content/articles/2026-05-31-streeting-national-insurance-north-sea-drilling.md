London — [UK](country:GB) health secretary backs new North Sea drilling.

Wes Streeting endorsed new North Sea licences, contradicting Labour's 2024 manifesto, and proposed a £3,000-per-hire youth jobs grant for long-term benefit claimants.

Energy secretary Ed Miliband must now rule on the Jackdaw and Rosebank field applications, where a decision is imminent.
