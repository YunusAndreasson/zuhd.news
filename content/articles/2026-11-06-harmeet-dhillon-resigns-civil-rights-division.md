Washington — Over three quarters of Civil Rights Division staff have left.

Harmeet Dhillon resigned Thursday, after Democrats swept Congress, saying she decided weeks earlier.

Under her, the division scrapped police reform settlements and pressed universities into settlements over diversity programs and campus antisemitism.

"Dhillon decimated it," said Stacey Young, a former division attorney.

Representative Robert Garcia, likely the next Oversight chair, has pledged inquiries into shootings by immigration agents and the Epstein files.
