Washington — Nvidia told partners to stop shipping servers to 4 countries.

[Malaysia](country:MY), [Singapore](country:SG), [Thailand](country:TH) and [Indonesia](country:ID) now need US licences for advanced chips.

A Commerce Department rule licenses chip deliveries and remote access, closing the route Chinese developers used through regional data centres.

Operators of existing data centres have about 90 days, until mid-February, to obtain licences.

Nvidia, Dell and Super Micro did not respond to requests for comment, and it is unclear whether the halt is temporary.
