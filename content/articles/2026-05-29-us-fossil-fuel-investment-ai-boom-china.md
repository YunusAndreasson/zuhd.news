Washington — [US](country:US) fossil fuel investment overtakes [China](country:CN)'s in 2026.

AI data centres demand electricity faster than grids supply it; US tech firms are building captive gas plants, International Energy Agency figures show.

Each new gas plant runs 30-plus years, locking in fossil demand through mid-century as AI electricity needs climb.
