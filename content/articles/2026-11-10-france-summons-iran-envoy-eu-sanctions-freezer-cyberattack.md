Paris — [Iran](country:IR)'s Revolutionary Guards heated freezers in 700 French supermarkets, prosecutors say.

[France](country:FR) calls it the first large-scale cyber sabotage on its soil and wants sanctions.

Attackers altered settings on refrigeration units, some running outdated firmware, and a later wave hit blood-bank cold storage.

"We rule nothing out," said Foreign Minister Jean-Noël Barrot, who summoned Iran's ambassador.

Barrot wants EU sanctions, which require all 27 member states to agree.
