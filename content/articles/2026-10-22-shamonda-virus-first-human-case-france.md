Lyon — A Lyon-area man in his 60s died infected with Shamonda.

The virus was known only in animals and reached Europe in late July.

Culicoides midges carry it between cattle, and investigators suspect a midge bite infected the immunocompromised patient in eastern [France](country:FR).

French authorities see no risk to the general population and are investigating whether the virus caused the death.

The European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control is monitoring, and how the man was infected remains unestablished.
