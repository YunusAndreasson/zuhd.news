London — OPEC output hit 22.87 million barrels a day in October.

That is 90% of the pre-war February rate, led by [Saudi Arabia](country:SA), Kuwait and Iraq.

Survey data show output up 1.38 million barrels a day in a month, with [Iran](country:IR)'s exports under a US blockade.

Seven OPEC+ members held output steady, though the war made earlier quota increases impossible to deliver.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 31 December a 36% chance, up 3 points in a week.
