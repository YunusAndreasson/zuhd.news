Tokyo — [Japan](country:JP)'s 10-year bond yield hit a 30-year high.

For years, near-zero Japanese rates funded carry trades that indirectly suppressed global borrowing costs, including the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding bitcoin.

If the Bank of Japan lets yields climb further, the leverage funding bitcoin's rally could unwind.
