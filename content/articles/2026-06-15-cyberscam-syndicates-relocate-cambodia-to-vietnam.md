Hanoi — [Cambodia](country:KH)'s crackdowns are pushing scam networks into [Vietnam](country:VN).

Chinese syndicates staff cyber-fraud compounds with trafficked workers; enforcement in one country relocates the operation, not the crime.

Vietnamese police disrupted new compounds, but regional coordination among Southeast Asian states remains absent.
