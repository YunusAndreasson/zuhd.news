1,431 pages of secret Russian documents leaked to journalists. The pan-African outlet The Continent received the files and shared them with Forbidden Stories, a consortium that verified them over months. The leak reveals a disinformation network called "The Company," supervised by Russia's Foreign Intelligence Service, that paid local journalists between $50 and $10,000 per article to place pro-Kremlin content in legitimate outlets across Africa, Latin America and the Middle East. About 90 operatives in Saint Petersburg spent 7.3 million euros on the campaign over 10 months in 2024.
