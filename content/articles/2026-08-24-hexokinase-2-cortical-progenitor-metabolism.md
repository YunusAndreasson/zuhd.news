London — Deleting one glucose enzyme grew mouse brains larger.

The variable was metabolic — how a cell burns sugar can set neuron count.

Removing hexokinase 2 kept progenitor cells on glycolysis longer, delaying their switch to neurons, researchers reported.

The knockout mice showed autism-like behaviors, a mouse model finding, not a human cause.
