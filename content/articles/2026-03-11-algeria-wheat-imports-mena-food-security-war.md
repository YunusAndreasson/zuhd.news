Algeria bought 200,000 tonnes of wheat Wednesday as global cereal prices spike. Wheat cost $259 per tonne in February; it now costs $291 — the purchase is Algeria's 3rd since January, totalling 1.4 million tonnes. European traders expect a 4th purchase before April as prices continue rising.
