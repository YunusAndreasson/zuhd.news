Dnipro — A Russian drone killed at least 13 on a railway platform, Ukrainian officials say.

[Russia](country:RU)'s Defence Ministry says [Ukraine](country:UA) launched a record 1,214 drones at 20 regions overnight.

Moscow's mayor said 613 drones targeted the capital region, and Vnukovo, Domodedovo and Zhukovsky airports restricted flights.

Moscow and Kyiv are due to resume peace talks next month with [US](country:US) mediation.
