Seoul — South Korea's chip exports hit a record $18.7 billion. AI server demand for chips is unaffected by oil prices, shielding Korea's export revenue from the same Hormuz crisis that is raising energy costs at home. Two economies run inside South Korea: chips at record highs while the stock market fell 4.5 percent Monday.
