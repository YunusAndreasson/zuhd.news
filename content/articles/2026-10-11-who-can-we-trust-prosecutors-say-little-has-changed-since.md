Gqeberha — Justice Minister Kubayi counted 5 prosecutors shot dead in 5 years.

Prosecutors in [South Africa](country:ZA)'s Nelson Mandela Bay still feel unsafe 15 months after Tracy Brown's killing.

Court evidence indicates the 31 July 2025 hit was ordered from St Albans Prison and linked to Buhle Mbini's prosecution.

The justice department says it has enhanced camera surveillance, access control and screening at court entrances.

Six accused, including a Hawks police warrant officer, return to court this month to learn their trial date.
