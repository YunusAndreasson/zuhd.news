Jackson — Markets now favor a September rate hike.

A hike would strengthen the [US](country:US) dollar, raising emerging-market debt costs abroad.

Inflation is largely imported through war-era energy prices, which a policy rate cannot fix by curbing demand.

Delivering a hike before the midterms would trigger a public clash with Trump.
