Iran hanged 3 protesters Thursday, including teenager Saleh Mohammadi. A national-team wrestler, Mohammadi was convicted of "moharebeh" — waging war against God — on a confession rights groups say was extracted under torture. Thousands detained in January's protests face the same charges.
