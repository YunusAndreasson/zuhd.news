A$3.9 billion down payment on a nuclear submarine yard. Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese announced the country's largest defence infrastructure project — a facility at Osborne in Adelaide to support 5,500 jobs and service US Virginia-class submarines arriving in 2027. The spending locks Australia into military alignment with the US and UK in the Indo-Pacific for decades.
