Pakistan struck 6 military targets in Kabul Monday night. The destroyed workshops held drone parts made in India and Israel, part of Operation Ghazab launched after Afghan Taliban border attacks in February. Afghan Taliban forces killed 4 Pakistani civilians in Bajaur with artillery that same night.
