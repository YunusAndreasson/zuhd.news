Finless porpoise numbers rose from 445 to 595. China's 10-year Yangtze fishing ban, now at its midpoint, has tripled fish biomass and lifted species richness 13%, a new study found. The moratorium runs until 2031, testing whether a single decade can reverse 70 years of ecological decline.
