Hong Kong — Refusing police a phone password means prison. City leader John Lee gazetted National Security Law amendments Monday without a legislative vote, setting a year in prison and a HK$100,000 fine for anyone who refuses. Activists in exile warn the change will drive remaining dissidents to destroy devices and flee Hong Kong before arrest.
