Mine-hunting drones could reopen Hormuz, Britain's energy secretary said. Hormuz carries 20% of the world's daily oil; Iran's blockade has driven energy prices higher across importing nations. US President Donald Trump threatened to reopen the strait "one way or another," pressing allies to send warships within days.
