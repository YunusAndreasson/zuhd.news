Paris — OECD reading scores fell 28 points since 2015.

Education ministries worldwide set curriculum policy on PISA's authority and findings.

OECD director Andreas Schleicher blamed AI and phone use, but PISA only measures self-reported use within one cohort.

Governments including the [US](country:US) now weigh new school phone and AI bans.
