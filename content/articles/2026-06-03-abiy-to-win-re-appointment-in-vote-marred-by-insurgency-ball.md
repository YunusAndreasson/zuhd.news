Addis Ababa — 46 constituencies were suspended before [Ethiopia](country:ET)'s national vote.

All 38 Tigray and 8 Amhara constituencies were canceled over civil war displacement, locking out Abiy's strongest opponents.

Abiy's second term will govern 120 million Ethiopians while Fano militias and the Oromo Liberation Army remain active.
