Gas suppliers cut Nigeria's already undersupplied power grid further. Nigeria earns billions exporting LNG while 220 million people suffer blackouts because domestic supply is less profitable than exports. Higher LNG export prices from the Hormuz crisis reduce the incentive to supply domestic plants, threatening further cuts.
