Kabul — 3 million Afghans were pushed home in 2025.

Two-thirds came from [Iran](country:IR) and one-third from [Pakistan](country:PK); the UN refugee agency says there are no good options at either end.

[Afghanistan](country:AF) faces drought, flooding, heatwaves, and Taliban-era restrictions on women's employment; the IRC says no absorption capacity remains.
