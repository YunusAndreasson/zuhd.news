Busan — A Korean ship hauled 737 containers over Russia's Arctic route, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

No shipping company from [South Korea](country:KR) had used the Northern Sea Route since 2016.

The Busan-Rotterdam route is 13,000 km, 35% shorter than via Suez, TASS said, citing the ministry through Yonhap.

The ship carried used cars and spare parts and returned six days behind its original plan.

A ceremony marking its return is set for 13 October.
