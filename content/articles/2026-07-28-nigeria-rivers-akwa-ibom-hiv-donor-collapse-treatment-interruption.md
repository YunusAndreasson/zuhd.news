Uyo — A woman died after her drug delivery stopped.

Donor cuts shut community HIV programmes in Rivers and Akwa Ibom states, forcing patients into clinics that risk exposing their status.

More than 300 community health workers in [Nigeria](country:NG) lost their jobs; state governments have yet to fund replacements.
