London — Scientists are using AI to decode bacteria-killing viruses.

Antibiotic resistance already ranks among the world's leading causes of death.

Each phage usually kills one bacterial strain, so matching patient to phage becomes a search problem machine learning can speed up.

Researchers have not said how soon a matched phage could reach a treated patient.
