Mumbai — A producers' body sets the city's milk price.

Wholesale milk jumps 9 rupees to Rs 102 as festive demand peaks.

The Bombay Milk Producers Association cites rising fodder and transport costs, and locked the new rate for six months.

Retailers and sweet shops are expected to pass most of the increase to customers within days.
