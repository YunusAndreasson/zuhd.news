No Japanese leader has sought Kim talks since 2002. Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi told Trump she intends to meet Kim Jong-un to secure the return of 17 citizens Japan lists as North Korean abductees. Trump and Takaichi discussed specific approaches, with no date or venue set.
