Rome — A 2022 gamma-ray burst may hide two explosions.

It would upend the standard single-collapse model for long gamma-ray bursts entirely.

Researchers say the star's core collapse triggered a second collapse 3.5 seconds later, forming a black hole.

Other astronomers say the data could equally fit a single-collapse model.
