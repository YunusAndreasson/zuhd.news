Brussels — [Ukraine](country:UA) would repay €45 billion only if [Russia](country:RU) pays reparations.

It covers part of a €70 billion funding gap Kyiv faces next year.

The Commission borrows on markets for the EU, with up to €25 billion for arms and €20 billion for budget support.

The European Parliament and EU governments must approve the loan, which Zelensky wants adopted swiftly.
