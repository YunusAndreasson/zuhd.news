Dead Sea — [Jordan](country:JO) pitched $15 billion in projects to European investors.

The International Monetary Fund puts 2025 unemployment at 21%, unchanged, and investors want risk-sharing guarantees.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s feasibility study for the railway link is due by the end of the year.
