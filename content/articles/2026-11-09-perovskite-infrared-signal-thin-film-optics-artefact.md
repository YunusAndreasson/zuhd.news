Okazaki — Light bouncing inside thin films can mimic molecular vibrations.

Researchers have read that infrared signal as molecules responding to light-generated charges in perovskite solar materials.

Jun Nishida of [Japan](country:JP)'s Institute for Molecular Science found charges change how infrared light reflects and transmits within film layers.

Nishida's study is theoretical, a calculation rather than a new measurement.

Which published spectra carry the artefact remains for experimenters to check.
