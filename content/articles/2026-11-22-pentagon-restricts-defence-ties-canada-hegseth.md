Washington — [US](country:US) officials say Hegseth restricted military cooperation with [Canada](country:CA).

The Pentagon says it is refocusing the relationship around American defence priorities.

Spokesman Sean Parnell says the memo leaves intelligence sharing, NATO cooperation and the joint continental air-defence command untouched.

Canadian Defence Minister David McGuinty says he has not seen the memo and sees no change in military cooperation.

Neither the Pentagon nor Ottawa has said which programmes the classified memo restricts.
