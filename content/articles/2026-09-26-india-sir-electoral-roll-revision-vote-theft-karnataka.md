Bengaluru — Two election commissioners reportedly objected on record 14 times.

Congress says the roll revision is deleting voters, and demands the chief commissioner's arrest.

The commission's data put 1.08 crore Karnataka voters in absent, shifted, dead or duplicate categories, and 43.81 lakh got notices.

The commission says all three commissioners approved every decision, including those tied to the revision.

Congress starts district-level protests Monday.
