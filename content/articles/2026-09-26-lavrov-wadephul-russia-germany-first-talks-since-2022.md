United Nations — [Russia](country:RU) and [Germany](country:DE)'s top diplomats spoke for 20 minutes.

It is their first meeting since Russia's 2022 invasion of Ukraine.

Wadephul pressed for a Black Sea grain deal, as Russian and Ukrainian attacks on ports have sharply curtailed exports.

Lavrov's General Assembly speech then attacked Germany and criticised giving it a permanent Security Council seat.

Zelensky says Washington wants technical talks with Russia and Ukraine in the UAE, and Kyiv awaits a date.
