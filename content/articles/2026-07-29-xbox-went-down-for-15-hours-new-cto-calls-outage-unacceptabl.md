Redmond — Even physical disc owners were locked out for 15 hours.

A shared internal Microsoft licensing service failed, breaking the entitlement checks discs require before running, new Xbox CTO Scott Van Vliet said.

Van Vliet has committed to a post-incident review of why one service could take down sign-in and game launches.
