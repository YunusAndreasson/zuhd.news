Jakarta — [Indonesia](country:ID) plans a 500km sea wall along Java's north coast.

Jakarta has sunk for decades from groundwater extraction; a sea wall holds the coastline but concentrates subsidence-driven flood risk behind it.

Every coastal megacity in Southeast Asia faces the hold-or-retreat choice; Indonesia's 500km bet sets the regional precedent.
