Antalya — Nearly 200 countries ended COP31 with no climate pact.

It is the first collapse of a COP's main deal since 2009.

Oil producers including [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) resisted fossil-fuel language, so the EU, [UK](country:GB) and Pacific states rejected a draft silent on it.

Host [Turkey](country:TR) said it secured more than $26bn in investment plans outside the deal.

Delegates still have no agreed way to implement the 2023 Dubai pledge to transition away from fossil fuels.
