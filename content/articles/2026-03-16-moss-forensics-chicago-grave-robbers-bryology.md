Cemetery moss placed suspects at individual Burr Oak graves. Each moss colony grows only on specific substrates in specific conditions, making it a biological fingerprint unique to each plot. Researchers at Chicago's Field Museum want investigators to collect moss samples as routinely as fingerprints.
