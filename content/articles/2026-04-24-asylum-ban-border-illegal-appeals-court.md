Washington — DC Circuit voided Trump's Day 1 asylum ban. The [US](country:US) court found statutory asylum rights under the Immigration and Nationality Act can only be revoked by Congress, not presidential proclamation. The ruling doesn't take effect until the court considers reconsideration; DOJ has announced it will appeal.
