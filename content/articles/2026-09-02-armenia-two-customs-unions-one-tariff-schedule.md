Yerevan — Putin told Pashinyan that Armenia cannot join two customs unions at once.

Russia buys roughly 30% of Armenia's trade, giving Moscow leverage to punish the pivot west.

Bloc membership means one external tariff schedule, making EU accession legally incompatible with Eurasian union membership.

Pashinyan has promised a referendum on EU membership but set no date.
