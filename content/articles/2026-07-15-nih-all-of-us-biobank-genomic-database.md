Bethesda — 747,000 Americans have joined the National Institutes of Health's gene bank.

Launched in 2015, All of Us aims to enroll a million volunteers with deliberately diverse ancestry, correcting genetics' historic European bias.

Researchers worldwide can now draw on the dataset to study the genetic roots of disease.
