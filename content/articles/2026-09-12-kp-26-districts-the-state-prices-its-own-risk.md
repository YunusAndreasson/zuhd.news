Peshawar — Islamabad priced danger at 250% extra pay.

The allowance covers officers only, not residents of the same 26 districts.

The 250% risk premium for police and administrators applies retroactively to July, covering 26 of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's 35 districts.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s Centre for Research and Security Studies says Khyber Pakhtunkhwa fatalities rose to 2,331 last year.
