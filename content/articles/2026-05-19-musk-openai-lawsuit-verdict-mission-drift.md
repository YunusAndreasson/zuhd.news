San Francisco — Musk lost his OpenAI suit on the statute of limitations — not on the merits.

The jury ruled on timing, not on whether OpenAI's nonprofit-to-for-profit conversion breached Musk's $38m charitable trust.

Musk will appeal; whether OpenAI lawfully converted from nonprofit to for-profit remains judicially unresolved.
