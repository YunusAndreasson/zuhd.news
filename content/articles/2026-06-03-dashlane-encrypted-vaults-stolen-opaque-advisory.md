New York — Attackers stole 20 Dashlane encrypted vaults via a two-factor authentication bypass.

The advisory says accounts were "brute-forced" but doesn't explain how 6-digit codes rotating every 45 seconds could be bypassed.

Vaults stay encrypted without the master password, but Dashlane has not clarified the attack method after 48 hours.
