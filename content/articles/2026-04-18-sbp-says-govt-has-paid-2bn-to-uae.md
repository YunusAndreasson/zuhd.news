Karachi — Pakistan returned $2bn to the UAE Saturday. Abu Dhabi sought $3.5bn immediately; the Gulf rollover deposits that propped Pakistan's reserves since 2019 are now being unwound, the State Bank of Pakistan said. Saudi Arabia pledged an offsetting $3bn Thursday; Islamabad is pursuing Eurobonds and sukuk to cover what remains.
