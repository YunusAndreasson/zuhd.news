960 million Africans have mobile coverage but no smartphone. At $40, a 4G phone costs only 10% more than a feature phone, putting smartphones within reach for hundreds of millions locked out of mobile banking and digital services. A GSMA 6-country pilot begins in 2026, but manufacturers have not confirmed they can profitably sell at $40 without subsidy.
