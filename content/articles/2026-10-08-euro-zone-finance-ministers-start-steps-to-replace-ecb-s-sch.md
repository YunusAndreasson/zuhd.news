Brussels — Governments have until 28 October to nominate Isabel Schnabel's replacement.

Schnabel leaves for the IMF on 3 January, and chief economist Philip Lane and Lagarde follow.

The Eurogroup recommends a candidate after the deadline, and EU leaders appoint, most likely at their December summit.

Technically any country can contest the seat, but [Germany](country:DE), [France](country:FR) and [Italy](country:IT) hold de facto permanent seats.

Spain and the Netherlands are also vying for board seats, which points to a package deal.
