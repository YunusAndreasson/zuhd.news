Sydney — [Australia](country:AU)'s nuclear science agency can now target single living cells.

Occupational and medical radiation limits rest on doses too small to measure directly.

The ANTARES facility fires counted ions, from hydrogen to carbon, onto live cell cultures in open air.

Researchers say the setup could next model radiation risk for space and aviation crews.
