Nuseirat — A dry cleaner sold his last machine.

Every business, hospital and water pump in Gaza runs on the same private generators.

Gaza's public grid has not delivered power since October 2023, forcing residents onto generator electricity costing roughly $9 a kilowatt-hour.

A full grid shutdown has not happened yet, but fuel rationing keeps that risk alive.
