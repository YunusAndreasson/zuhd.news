Shenzhen — Tencent's quarterly capital spending jumped 528% to 81.4 billion yuan.

Free cash flow turned negative, and new AI products cut operating profit by 15.2 billion yuan.

Tencent prepaid about 86 billion yuan for compute, and president Martin Lau says the latest chip import curbs cut usable compute.

WorkBuddy passed 10 million daily users by early November, the most used Chinese AI product by tokens, Tencent says.

Lau says the next Hunyuan model may take longer, and more Chinese firms will rely on domestic chips.
