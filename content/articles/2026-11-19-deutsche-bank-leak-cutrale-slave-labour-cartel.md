São Paulo — Deutsche Bank's 1,148-page Cutrale file softened slave-labour allegations.

Cutrale supplies between a quarter and a third of the world's orange juice.

The leaked know-your-customer report records the labour and cartel cases, yet bank staff stressed the family's spotless international reputation.

Deutsche Bank said the issues raised by journalists date back many years.

The leak covers more than 15 million bank documents, and more [Brazil](country:BR) clients are still being reported.
