Apple Daily's Jimmy Lai would leave prison at 98. The media founder declined to appeal his 20-year sentence, handed down by Hong Kong courts under the 2020 national security law for advocating Beijing sanctions through his newspaper. The appeal window closes Monday; the British government has not secured his release despite his citizenship.
