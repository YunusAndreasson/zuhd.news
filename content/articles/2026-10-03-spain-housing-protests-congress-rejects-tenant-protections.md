Madrid — Tens of thousands marched after an 87-year-old's eviction.

Parliament narrowly rejected the government's tenant protections Friday, leaving renters in [Spain](country:ES) without relief.

The decrees would have renewed expiring leases automatically and limited evictions of vulnerable tenants.

Junts, the Catalan separatist party whose votes were essential, said the decrees lacked rigour.

Opposition leader Alberto Núñez Feijóo wants early elections, which are otherwise due in 2027.
