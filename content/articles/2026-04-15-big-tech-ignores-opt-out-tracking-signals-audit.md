San Francisco — 194 ad services ignore California's do-not-track law. A webXray audit of March traffic found they block the Global Privacy Control signal, which California's CPRA made legally mandatory in 2023. California's privacy regulator can fine violators; no enforcement action against any of the 194 services has been filed.
