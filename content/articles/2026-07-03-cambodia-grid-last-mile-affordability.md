Tonsaong — The power line reached this village last year; most homes still burn firewood.

[Cambodia](country:KH) electrified 99% of villages by 2024, but connection fees and wiring costs price out families like Kha Sros's, the energy ministry's own data shows.

Government loan and solar subsidy programs remain too costly for the rural poor to take up.
