Ceuta — 72,000 people crossed into a city of 84,000.

Arrivals have no automatic right to stay or move onward.

A Spanish court ruling limiting summary returns to [Morocco](country:MA) fueled a social-media rumor that triggered the crossing.

Spanish and Moroccan authorities have not agreed on a returns process for those still stranded.
