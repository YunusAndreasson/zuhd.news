Brisbane — [Australia](country:AU)'s second-largest sugar producer went dark mid-harvest.

Sugar harvest runs on a narrow seasonal clock; days offline mean cane left in the field rots before it can be crushed.

The company named no attacker and set no restoration date, leaving harvest losses unquantified.
