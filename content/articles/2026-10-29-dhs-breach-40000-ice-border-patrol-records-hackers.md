Washington — Hackers leaked about 40,000 Homeland Security staff records, sources say.

The data names several undercover Immigration and Customs Enforcement agents, sources confirmed.

A group calling itself Department of Peace claimed the hack, and the leak site Distributed Denial of Secrets published the data.

Its co-founder Emma Best said a search tool would "replace anonymity with accountability."

The department has promised an update but has not said how the data left its systems.
