Newark — An Amtrak crew's cut cable grounded Northeast flights.

FlightAware showed 31% of Newark departures cancelled by 3pm, as UN delegates flew in.

The severed line was a backup, and officials found the break only after the primary radar-control circuit failed.

Verizon, which owns the cable, denies responsibility.

The Federal Aviation Administration's chief, Bryan Bedford, said the repair could take up to 13 hours.
