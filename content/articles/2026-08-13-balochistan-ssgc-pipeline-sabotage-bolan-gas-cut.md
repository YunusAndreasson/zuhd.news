Quetta — One remote detonation cut gas to seven towns.

Households and small industry across Balochistan lose heat and cooking fuel while repairs wait.

Attackers destroyed the KMP-246 valve near Bolan, the fourth such bombing in the province since March.

Crews need security clearance before they can even start repairs.
