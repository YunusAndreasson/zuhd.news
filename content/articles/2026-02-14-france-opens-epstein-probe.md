France opened a probe into Jeffrey Epstein. Paris prosecutors formed a team to examine roughly 3 million documents the United States Department of Justice released earlier this year. The unit will work with the financial crimes office and police to identify suspected crimes involving French nationals. The team will also reopen the case of Jean-Luc Brunel, a French modeling executive charged with raping minors, who was found dead in his Paris prison cell in 2022.
