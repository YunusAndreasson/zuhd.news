Marseille — Seized Russian shadow tanker fined, freed Thursday. The Mozambique-flagged Deyna, boarded March 20 with British help, was charged with absent registration — not with sanctions evasion. Ukraine has urged EU states to pass seizure-enabling laws for shadow fleet cargo; no such legislation exists.
