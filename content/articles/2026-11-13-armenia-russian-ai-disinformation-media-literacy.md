Yerevan — 31 fabricated stories about Pashinyan circulated in a week, NewsGuard found.

[Armenia](country:AM), pursuing European Union membership, faces reported Russian interference since its June election.

The fakes imitated France 24, Politico, Armenpress and CivilNet, with AI-generated videos alongside fake news sites.

Armenian journalists and activists built iVerify, a platform where users can check information.

The groups' media-literacy work runs on Digital Spark grants from the Metamorphosis Foundation and TechSoup.
