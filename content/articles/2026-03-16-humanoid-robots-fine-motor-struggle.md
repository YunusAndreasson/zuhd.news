Tesla's Optimus robot cannot open a door. Engineers cracked locomotion with fast motors and deep learning, but manipulation requires tactile feedback and real-time physics current AI cannot provide. Tesla is already replacing some car production with Optimus units, even as researchers warn widespread deployment requires solving these fundamentals first.
