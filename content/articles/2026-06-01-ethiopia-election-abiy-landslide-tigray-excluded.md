Addis Ababa — Tigray is barred from [Ethiopia](country:ET)'s election.

The electoral board cited "unfavourable conditions" after the 2020–22 civil war; Abiy Ahmed's Prosperity Party faces no meaningful opposition in the region it defeated.

Results are expected by June 11; Tigray's main party warned this month of fresh unrest risk.
