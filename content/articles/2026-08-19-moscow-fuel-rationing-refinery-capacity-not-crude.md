Moscow — Gas stations cap sales at 30 litres.

Rationing has reached the capital, the one place [Russia](country:RU) works hardest to supply.

[Ukraine](country:UA)'s drones have struck refineries that convert crude into fuel, a capacity sanctions make hard to rebuild.

Rosneft and Lukoil warn of longer queues as shortages persist into autumn.
