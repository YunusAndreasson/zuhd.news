Bandar Abbas — [Iran](country:IR) gave [Kazakhstan](country:KZ) 15 hectares at its main Gulf cargo port for 27 years.

Shahid Rajaei is Iran's largest cargo facility, anchoring the North-South Corridor linking the Indian Ocean to Russia and Central Asia.

A 2-year construction phase begins now; Bandar Abbas resumed exports last week as the Strait of Hormuz reopened.
