Islamabad — A court ruling demands third-party corroboration of harassment, lawmakers warn.

Committee members warned complainants in private or digital settings, where witnesses are rare, would lose protection.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s Federal Constitutional Court appeared to overlook the 2022 amendment covering non-sexual gender discrimination, Attorney General Mansoor Usman Awan told the committee.

The committee urged the government to pursue its review petition in Usman Shahid vs Nadia Sarwar.
