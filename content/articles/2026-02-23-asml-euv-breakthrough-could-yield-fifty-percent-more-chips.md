Each machine will produce 50% more chips by 2030. Dutch chipmaking equipment maker ASML raised its extreme ultraviolet light source to 1,000 watts by doubling the tin droplets it fires to 100,000 per second. The advance widens the gap between nations with access to ASML's equipment and those — principally China — without it.
