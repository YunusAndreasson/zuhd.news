Swabi — Traders are offering 350 rupees per kilo of tobacco.

The government's own 740-rupee floor goes unenforced, leaving growers unable to cover production costs.

Only two multinational buyers honor the official rate, and only for growers still under contract.

The [Pakistan](country:PK) Tobacco Board has yet to act on growers' complaints against smaller buyers.
