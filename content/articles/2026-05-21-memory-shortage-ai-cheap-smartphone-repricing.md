New York — AI is pricing the poor out of smartphones.

Hyperscalers pay 3-4× consumer margins for AI memory, crowding out cheap devices; data firm IDC forecast a 13% drop in 2026 smartphone shipments.

Africa and the Middle East face a 20%-plus shipment decline, IDC forecast — the steepest regional fall.
