Abuja — Worshippers booed President Bola Tinubu after Friday prayers at the National Mosque.

Some chanted "Tinubu Must Go" in [Nigeria](country:NG)'s capital, ahead of the January presidential election.

Security operatives sealed the mosque after he arrived at 1:49 p.m. and held worshippers inside until he left, eyewitnesses said.

The imam prayed for Tinubu's wisdom in his sermon, and presidential spokesman Bayo Onanuga did not respond to calls.

Polymarket traders give Tinubu a 72% chance of winning the 2027 election, up 4 points in a week.
