The Pentagon threatened to sever its $200 million contract with Anthropic. The Department of Defense wants 4 AI companies to remove all use restrictions on military-deployed models. Three companies agreed to lift their guardrails, but Anthropic refused to drop 2 red lines: mass surveillance of Americans and fully autonomous weapons. The dispute intensified after reports that the military used Anthropic's Claude model in the operation to capture Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro. A senior administration official told Axios the Pentagon would reevaluate the partnership, framing the standoff as a test of whether companies or governments set the limits on military AI.
