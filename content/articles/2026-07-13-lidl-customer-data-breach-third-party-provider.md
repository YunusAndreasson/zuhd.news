An outside vendor leaked Lidl shopper data.

Attackers breached a third-party database, not Lidl's own platform, exposing customers in [Germany](country:DE), [Belgium](country:BE) and the [Netherlands](country:NL).

Lidl has not named the contractor or said how many records were taken.
