London — Psilocybin's social effects differ by sex and timing.

Researchers found male mice lost dopamine novelty-response at 24 hours; females showed no dopamine effect at the same point.

Most psilocybin trials enroll mixed-sex cohorts at single timepoints, missing the sex-specific windows the study identified.
