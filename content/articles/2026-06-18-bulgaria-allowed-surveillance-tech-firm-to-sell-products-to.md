Sofia — [Bulgaria](country:BG) licensed spyware sales to rights-abusing governments.

The government approved Circles' interception tech for export to abusive states' agencies from 2018 to 2023, per Human Rights Watch export-licensing records.

Whether those approvals violated EU export rules — Bulgaria is an EU member — has not been established.
