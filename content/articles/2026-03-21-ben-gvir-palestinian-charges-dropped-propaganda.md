Israeli police arrested and freed a Palestinian man without charges. National Security Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir aired the 22-year-old's arrest footage on Channel 14 and social media before prosecutors found no evidence of wrongdoing. Ben-Gvir retains his cabinet seat; the man has no legal path to remove his image from public record.
