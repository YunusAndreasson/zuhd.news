Kuala Lumpur — 10,000 Rohingya will leave [Malaysia](country:MY) for [Turkey](country:TR).

The International Court of Justice ruled Thursday that Myanmar's 2017 killings of Rohingya amounted to genocide.

Deputy Prime Minister Ahmad Zahid Hamidi said refugees will get 3 to 6 months of vocational training for Turkish jobs.

Malaysia has also deported more than 10,000 Myanmar nationals since January and does not recognise refugee status.

No start date has been announced, and Turkey's foreign ministry has not responded.
