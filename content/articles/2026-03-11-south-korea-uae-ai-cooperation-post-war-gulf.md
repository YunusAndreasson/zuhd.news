South Korea plans to help build the world's largest AI data centers outside the US. Ha Jung-woo, South Korea's senior presidential AI secretary, said UAE cooperation on data centers, energy grids, and supply chains will accelerate once the conflict ends. Samsung and SK Hynix — South Korea's leading chipmakers — are central to the plan, but no formal agreement has been signed.
