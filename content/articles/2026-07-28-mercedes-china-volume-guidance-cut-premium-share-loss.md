Stuttgart — China car sales fell 30% in a single quarter.

Second-quarter profit rose 22% on cost cuts, but [Germany](country:DE)'s Mercedes-Benz cut its full-year sales outlook as Chinese rivals take share on software and battery cost.

The carmaker is intensifying productivity measures at its German plants through year-end.
