Kaduna — Petrol cost about 1,400 naira a litre in September as Tinubu seeks re-election.

The 16 January vote will be fought over the worst cost-of-living crisis in a generation.

Scrapping fuel subsidies and devaluing the naira improved reserves and investor sentiment, but households paid through pump prices.

"They created the hunger," Tinubu, 74, told the Kaduna crowd, blaming his opponents.

The party's timetable has Tinubu attending rallies in 12 states, closing in Lagos on 9 January.
