Benghazi — [Ukraine](country:UA) says 7 naval drones sank Russian corvette Merkury.

[Libya](country:LY) is not party to the war, yet a harbour in its east was hit.

The corvette and the cargo ship Angara were berthed in eastern Libya under Khalifa Haftar's control, about 1,800 km from Crimea.

Libya's eastern government asked the UN to investigate whether western Libya was used to launch the attack.
