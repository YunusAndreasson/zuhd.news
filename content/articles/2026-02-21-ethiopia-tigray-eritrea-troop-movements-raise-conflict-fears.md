Military convoys moved through northern Ethiopia this week. Ethiopia and Eritrea both deployed forces toward Tigray, threatening a 2022 peace deal that ended a civil war killing an estimated 300,000. Disputed control of western Tigray and unresolved displacement could trigger renewed fighting.
