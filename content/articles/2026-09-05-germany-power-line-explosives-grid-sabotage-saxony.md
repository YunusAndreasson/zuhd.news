Saxony — Police found 12 explosives along power lines.

A regional grid fault can trip protection relays and cut power across eastern Germany.

The charges targeted high-voltage lines to induce a short circuit, not to destroy the pylons themselves.

Authorities are searching for a 48-year-old suspect who claimed the attacks in letters.
