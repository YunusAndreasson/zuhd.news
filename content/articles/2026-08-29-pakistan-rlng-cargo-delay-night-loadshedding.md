Islamabad — Pakistan apologized for night-time blackouts across the grid.

A missed liquefied natural gas cargo left a 3,600-megawatt shortfall during peak night demand.

The [US](country:US)-[Iran](country:IR) war has disrupted liquefied natural gas shipments from Qatar through the Strait of Hormuz since February.

The Power Division says cuts will ease only once the delayed cargoes arrive.
