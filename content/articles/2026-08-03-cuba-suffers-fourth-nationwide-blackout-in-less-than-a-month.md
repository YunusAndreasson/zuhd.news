Havana — 11 million people lost power for a fourth time in a month.

[US](country:US) energy restrictions have cut off fuel and spare parts for Cuba's aging thermal plants, so each restart strains the next component toward failure.

Havana's energy ministry restarted three plants Monday; no timeline exists for a lasting fix.
