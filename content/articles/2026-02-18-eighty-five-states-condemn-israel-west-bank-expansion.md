85 governments condemned Israel's West Bank policies as annexation. The joint UN statement, backed by the European Union and 2 other multilateral blocs, cited land registration as state property and settlement expansion toward Jerusalem. The statement is non-binding; Israel did not respond, and settlement construction has continued.
