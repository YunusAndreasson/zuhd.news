London — TRIP13 blocks apoptosis in venetoclax-resistant leukemia cells.

TRIP13 traps an apoptosis factor in mitochondria; disrupting it restored cell death and reversed resistance in mouse models.

The finding is preclinical; no TRIP13-AIF inhibitor has entered trials for acute myeloid leukemia patients relapsing on venetoclax.
