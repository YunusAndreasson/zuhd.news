London — 9 states warned contractors off [Israel](country:IL)'s E1 settlement bids.

E1 construction east of Jerusalem would bisect the occupied West Bank, severing Palestinian communities over 12 square kilometres, the joint statement warned.

The [US](country:US) did not join; no government has named penalties for contractors that proceed.
