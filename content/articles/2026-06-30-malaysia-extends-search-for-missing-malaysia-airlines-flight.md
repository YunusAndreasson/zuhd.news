Kuala Lumpur — Ocean Infinity gets another year to find MH370.

[Malaysia](country:MY) extended Ocean Infinity's mandate on the Boeing 777 that vanished in 2014; no confirmed wreckage has been found in 12 years.

No new search area or timetable for the 1-year extension has been publicly announced.
