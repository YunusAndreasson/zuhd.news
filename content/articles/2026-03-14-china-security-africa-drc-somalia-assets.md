Terrorist attacks on Chinese diplomats in Somalia triggered China's decision to deploy its own security forces across Africa. Beijing had long relied on host governments for protection, a model that proved inadequate in Somalia and the DRC. African governments have not yet agreed to terms for a permanent Chinese security presence.
