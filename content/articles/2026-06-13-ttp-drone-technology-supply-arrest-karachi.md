Karachi — Retail drone parts reached [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Taliban, investigators alleged.

The Counter Terrorism Department said the suspect bought drone parts including DJI units from Karachi bazaars for a Taliban fighter in Afghanistan.

Forensic tests of recovered explosives, a drone, and a controller are underway; further arrests expected.
