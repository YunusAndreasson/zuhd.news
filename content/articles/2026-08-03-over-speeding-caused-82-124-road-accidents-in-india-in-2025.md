New Delhi — 21 people died on Indian roads every hour in 2025.

Crashes rose 5.3% to 513,563, killing 183,382, the road transport ministry said; officials blame 16% of crashes on over-speeding alone.

[India](country:IN)'s 2019 motor vehicle law promised stricter road engineering that ministry data shows remains unevenly enforced.
