United States — OpenAI's 12-hour cache costs 2.5 times input price to write.

That doubles the 1.25 times rate for the default 30-minute cache, on GPT-5.6 and later models.

OpenAI's documentation says 12 hours only sets billing eligibility for cache reads, not guaranteed reuse or lower latency.

A day earlier, DeepSeek open-sourced an inference stack designed to retain cache for 3 days.

OpenAI has not yet announced details of the permanent cache promised to Codex and Work users.
