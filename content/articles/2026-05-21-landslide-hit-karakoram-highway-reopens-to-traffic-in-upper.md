Mansehra — 3 landslides blocked [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Karakoram Highway overnight.

The highway links Gilgit-Baltistan to Pakistan proper; coaches and freight in both directions were stranded overnight.

Light traffic resumed Wednesday evening; the deputy commissioner said heavy vehicles would clear within 3 to 4 hours.
