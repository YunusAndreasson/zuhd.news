New York — Creditors are organizing claims on [Venezuela](country:VE)'s $150 billion debt.

The government that replaced Maduro after a [US](country:US) raid now picks who gets paid first.

Legal advisers formed a creditor committee last month while the regional development bank's board resists a $2.5 billion loan.

Rodríguez's government has already ceded 100-year concessions over 65 billion barrels of reserves to a US-led venture.

The government and an opposition faction are negotiating moving $4 billion in gold from the Bank of England.
