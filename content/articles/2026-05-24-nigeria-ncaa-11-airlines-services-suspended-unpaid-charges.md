Lagos — Unpaid charges cost 11 [Nigeria](country:NG) airlines their regulatory services.

The Nigerian Civil Aviation Authority funds its operations from airline charges, making it a creditor withholding services from its own debtors.

If the authority holds through Eid, carriers including Air Peace and Ibom Air face operational disruption.
