Athens — A third Greek vessel in three days burned in the Black Sea.

The attacks threaten the tanker fleet Russia relies on to move sanctioned crude.

The Liberian-flagged Anna S was hit by a shell and five drones near Novorossiysk, destroying it by fire.

Drones struck the Elina B hours later, after it rescued the Anna S's 21 crew.
