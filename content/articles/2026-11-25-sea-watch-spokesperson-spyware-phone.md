Italy — Spyware ran on Sea-Watch spokesperson Giorgia Linardi's phone in 2021.

Unknown parties could read her messages as prosecutors in [Italy](country:IT) investigated rescue groups.

The software appears to differ from the state trojans previously found used against sea-rescue activists.

Sea-Watch is filing a criminal complaint and hopes for answers about who installed it.
