Kyiv — One regulator would decide how many crypto exchanges [Ukraine](country:UA) gets.

The National Bank of Ukraine warns that discretion risks market concentration and corruption.

The bank had agreed to cap the market at 3 operators initially, but the final draft leaves the first five years open.

The bill now goes to parliament for a second reading.
