Islamabad — A new rain spell arrives on soil that cannot absorb more.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s Met Department forecasts heavy rain from July 29 to August 5 over ground already flooded, threatening standing cotton and rice in Punjab and Sindh.

The crop losses risk widening the current-account gap under Pakistan's IMF programme.
