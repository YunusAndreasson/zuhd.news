Amsterdam — Vyvgart, Argenx's blockbuster, failed to beat placebo in Sjögren's.

The setback is unusual for a company that has racked up a series of autoimmune wins.

Vyvgart blocks the FcRn receptor so the body clears IgG antibodies faster, which works in antibody-driven diseases.

Argenx shares fell 13% Thursday morning.
