Ibadan — Oyo governor Makinde enters [Nigeria](country:NG)'s 2027 presidential race.

The Allied People's Movement pick challenges Nigeria's two-party grip on a platform of security decentralisation and state oil company reform.

All 36 state chapters backed the nomination; Makinde must now formally register with the Independent National Electoral Commission.
