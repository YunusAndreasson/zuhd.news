Lassa — 80 Borno schoolchildren remain captive after nearly 6 months.

[Nigeria](country:NG) has seen roughly 30 mass school kidnappings, with more than 2,000 students taken.

Insurgents took students at Mussa in May, 44 by the governor's count, and 36 more at Lassa two months later.

"Why have the Oyo children been freed and our own children are still held captive?" asked parents' chairperson Ishaku Wabba.

Parents threaten to take the next protest to Aso Rock if the children are not freed.
