London — Researchers released mAIEnergy, an open dataset for grid AI.

General models pass exams but fail at grid operations because the data was never public.

The corpus harmonizes 50,000 documents, 20,000 images and 25 million time-series records into usable formats.

Grids outside Europe, North America and China remain least represented in the dataset.
