Iowa — Trump unveiled a $15bn steel mill before midterms.

Republicans are trailing in Iowa's Senate race, the seat Democrats need to flip the chamber.

The plant won't produce steel until 2030, using iron ore from a Minnesota mine backed by a $10bn [US](country:US) Export-Import Bank loan.

EXIM spokesman Alec Varsamis says only $770 million of the $10bn loan has been approved so far.

Public Citizen wants EXIM's loan scrutinized over a Trump adviser's lobbying for Mesabi and [India](country:IN).
