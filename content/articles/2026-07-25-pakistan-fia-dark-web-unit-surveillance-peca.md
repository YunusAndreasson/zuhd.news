Islamabad — A new FIA unit will trace "threats to the country's interests."

Real deanonymization comes from exchange records and blockchain forensics, not from breaking Tor, so the unit's actual reach extends to anyone using the same tools, officials said.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s cybercrime law has repeatedly been stretched toward journalists; the warrant regime, not the technology, decides who gets traced.
