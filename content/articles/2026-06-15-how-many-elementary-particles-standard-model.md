Geneva — Particle physics gives 17 to 995 answers.

The count depends on whether color charge, antiparticles, and spin states are tallied separately — the Standard Model's '17' is a convention, not a physical fact.

The disagreement exposes a deeper question: what physicists mean by a 'fundamental' thing.
