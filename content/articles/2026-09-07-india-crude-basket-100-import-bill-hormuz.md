New Delhi — India's crude basket hit $101.07 a barrel Friday.

The country importing 85% of its oil now pays a war premium it played no part in.

Shipping rates from Saudi Arabia to [India](country:IN) rose over 400% since Iran shut the Strait of Hormuz in February, the oil ministry said.

India's July import bill already ran 41% above last year, with September's average still climbing.
