Visakhapatnam — Crane collapse poured molten steel on 8 workers.

[India](country:IN)'s state steelmaker, slated for privatisation, lost an overhead crane during casting; the ladle fell, spilling 1,600°C steel, the Steel Ministry said.

A 3-member external inquiry will probe the failure; unions allege chronic staff shortages drove the risk.
