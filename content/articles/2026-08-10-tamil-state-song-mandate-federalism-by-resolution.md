Chennai — Rival parties voted as one on Monday.

Tamil Nadu's two biggest parties rarely agree, signaling a shared claim to Tamil identity.

Tamil Nadu's assembly made "Tamil Thaai Vazhthu" mandatory at government offices, schools and centrally funded institutions.

Compliance at federally funded bodies is where the mandate will next be tested.
