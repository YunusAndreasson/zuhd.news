Deir al-Balah — A sacrificial sheep now costs 11,000 shekels.

[Israel](country:IL) controls all [Gaza](country:PS) entry points, letting in fewer trucks than needed to lower war-inflated prices, NGOs say.

Pre-war a sheep cost 1,000 shekels; only 15,000 remain for 2.1 million people, the UN food agency found.
