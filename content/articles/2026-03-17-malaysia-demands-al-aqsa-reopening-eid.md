Israel sealed Al-Aqsa Mosque on February 28. The closure coincided with the start of US-Israeli strikes on Iran, barring worshippers during Islam's holiest period. Malaysian Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim called on governments with real influence to apply pressure, with no reopening date set as Eid begins.
