Dhaka — The tribunal's rules allow no constitutional challenge.

That design, built for mass-atrocity trials, offers journalists almost no defense.

Relaxed evidence rules and restricted appeals, both written into [Bangladesh](country:BD)'s 1973 statute, remain unchanged today.

Some accused deny inciting the 2024 killings, and the interim authority controls which forum decides.
