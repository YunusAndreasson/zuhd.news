London — Burnham signed 3 EU deals but left tuition fees open.

They end most post-Brexit food checks and open 100,000 youth visas a year.

Brussels wants EU students charged £9,970, not up to £38,000, and EU officials say the deals may stall until London agrees.

Downing Street says the deals are done, and Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband says legislation arrives next week.

[Britain](country:GB) has six months, until about May 2027, to settle tuition fees.
