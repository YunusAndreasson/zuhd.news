China's rural elderly live on $24 a month in retirement. China's hukou household registration system has maintained separate rural and urban pension tiers since the Mao era, leaving city retirees with benefits that are multiples higher. National People's Congress deputies proposed tripling the rural minimum to 500 yuan over 3 years, but Beijing has not responded.
