36 Iranian missiles hit Israeli territory in 3 weeks. Israel's 3-layer defense intercepted 270 of 365 missiles — cluster warheads scatter submunitions that no intercept system can stop. Iran holds an estimated 1,000 ballistic missiles remaining, designed to exhaust interceptors through sheer volume.
