Beijing — 180 videos on arthritis were scored against a clinical scale.

Researchers using the DISCERN and Global Quality Score instruments found Bilibili videos more complete and higher-quality than TikTok's, despite TikTok's greater engagement.

Both platforms, the unedited study found, left prevention content largely unaddressed.
