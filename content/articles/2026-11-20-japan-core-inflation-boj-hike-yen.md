Tokyo — Core inflation hit 2.2%, below the 2.3% forecast.

It strengthens the case for a December hike to 1.5% from 1.25%.

[Japan](country:JP)'s central bank board mostly agrees on a hike but splits on the neutral rate, which staff place between 1.1% and 2.5%.

The index excluding fresh food and fuel, which the Bank of Japan watches closely, slowed to 2.2% from 2.4%.

The Bank of Japan decides on 18 December, with the yen at 159.6 per dollar on 19 November.
