San Francisco — A language model read a 370-year-old cipher's key from the book itself.

The result argues language models are cryptanalytic tools, not just puzzle-solvers, for text-based historical ciphers.

Vals AI's model spent 44 minutes linking the cipher's 32 numbers to Thomas Urquhart's own 32 "Proquiritations."

Independent scholars have not yet confirmed Vals AI's capability claim.
