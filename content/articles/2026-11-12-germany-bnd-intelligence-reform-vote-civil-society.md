Berlin — 28 organisations urge MPs to reject spy reform Friday.

The reform would blur [Germany](country:DE)'s post-Nazi wall between police and spies.

It widens intelligence access to video surveillance and biometric matching, and strips the federal data protection commissioner of most oversight.

The Bundestag votes on 13 November.
