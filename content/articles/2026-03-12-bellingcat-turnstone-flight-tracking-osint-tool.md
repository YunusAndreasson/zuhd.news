Turnstone tracked US aerial tanker movements heading toward Iran before the February 28 strikes began. The open source tool aggregates transponder signals that most aircraft broadcast voluntarily, visualizing patterns across time and geography — work that previously required expensive commercial subscriptions. Military aircraft routinely disable transponders, so the bombers and fighters operating over the Gulf cannot be tracked by Turnstone.
