Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s FY27 budget allocates Rs8 trillion for interest payments.

Debt servicing consumes 42.8% of the federal budget, Senator Sherry Rehman warned, crowding out development and social spending.

Pakistan's debt doubled to Rs97 trillion in 4 years — the first Rs44 trillion took 74 years, budget data showed.
