Islamabad — 10 Pakistani sailors have waited 100 days for rescue.

No state owes MT Honour 25's crew protection because it flies Palau's flag, not theirs.

Somali pirates seized the tanker off Puntland on April 21, demanding roughly $3 million while captors withheld medicine.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s foreign ministry cites an "ownership crisis" blocking talks, with no release date set.
