Washington — A bill that misses this term dies entirely.

Frontier-AI safety rules now wait for a new Congress, and possibly a new committee chairman.

Energy and Commerce Chairman Brett Guthrie said he won't rush the FRONTIER Act "in a lame duck session."

Until 2027, no binding US rule governs frontier model release beyond voluntary industry commitments.
