Tehran — Two tankers hit mines in Hormuz and burned, [Iran](country:IR)'s Guard says.

Hormuz traffic ran 25% below normal on 22 November, at 2.7 ships a day.

The Guard says the tankers tried to slip through a "fake" route, and warns crews against switching off positioning systems.

The statement names no ship, flag or casualty.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 31 December a 13% chance.
