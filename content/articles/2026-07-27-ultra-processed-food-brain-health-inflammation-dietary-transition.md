London — A study tracked 58,423 adults' brains for years.

Researchers point to two candidate pathways: gut-microbiome disruption and low-grade inflammation reaching the brain — association, not yet proven cause, researchers caution.

Processed-food intake is rising fastest in the Gulf and Southeast Asia, ahead of health systems built for it.
