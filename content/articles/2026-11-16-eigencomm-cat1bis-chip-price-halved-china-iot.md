Shanghai — Eigencomm's Cat.1bis chip prices nearly halved since 2023.

It lost 201 million yuan in three and a half years, profitable only in 2024.

Cat.1bis gross margin fell to 2.8% in the first half of 2026 from 9.4% in 2025, its listing prospectus shows.

Revenue still grew 13.7% a year, and Frost & Sullivan ranks it third globally with 3.9%.

Eigencomm seeks a Hong Kong listing, while five customers still supply over 76% of revenue.
