Paris — [Iran](country:IR)'s Revolutionary Guards hacked 700 French supermarkets, prosecutors say.

The 11 October attack spoiled food and set off about 20 fires, the Paris prosecutor's office said.

Attackers took remote control through web interfaces, ran endless defrost cycles and disabled the temperature alerts.

Iranian authorities did not immediately respond, and no government has linked the Belgian, Luxembourg, Swedish or US incidents to it.

[France](country:FR) summoned Iran's ambassador, and prosecutors seek arrest warrants for the unit's leader and 2 deputies.
