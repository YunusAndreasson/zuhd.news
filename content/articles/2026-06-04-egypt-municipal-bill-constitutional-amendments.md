Cairo — [Egypt](country:EG)'s parliament revived a decade-stalled municipal administration bill.

A government source said the revival could justify constitutional amendments; a 2019 change already extended the presidency to 2030.

Parliament plans to extend committee debate until a green light for a second amendment is secured, the source said.
