4.5 percent is China's growth floor through 2030. China's National People's Congress set the target, the lowest in 30 years, as property deflation and Hormuz oil costs compound. Countries from Southeast Asia to Sub-Saharan Africa that depend on Chinese demand face falling orders and lower commodity prices.
