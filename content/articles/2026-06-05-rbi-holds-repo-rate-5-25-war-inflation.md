Mumbai — War inflation, not domestic demand, froze [India](country:IN)'s benchmark rate.

The Monetary Policy Committee voted unanimously to hold at 5.25%, citing Iran conflict fuel prices — not domestic slowing — as the inflation risk.

No cut timetable given; the stance holds until war's fuel shock or a monsoon failure tips it.
