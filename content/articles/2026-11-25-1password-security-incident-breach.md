Toronto — An attacker downloaded names and email addresses of a subset of 1Password users.

Vault contents and the keys protecting them were not accessed, the company says.

A hijacked employee GitHub account let the attacker push malicious code to backend services and copy private source code.

Emailed invitation and recovery tokens were exposed, but 1Password says they work only with a sign-in or a code.

1Password has not said how many users' records were downloaded.
