New Delhi — India scrapped a forced gas-surrender mandate.

The Hormuz closure cut 90% of [India](country:IN)'s liquefied petroleum gas supply, the ministry said, forcing a piped-gas switch it hadn't planned.

Ministry figures show 59,800 surrendered connections; the government set no pipeline completion date for rural areas.
