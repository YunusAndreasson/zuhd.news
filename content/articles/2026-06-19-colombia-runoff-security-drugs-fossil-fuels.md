Bogotá — [Colombia](country:CO)'s runoff Sunday pits militarization against negotiation.

Official figures show 25 years and $15bn in [US](country:US) aid have not cut Colombia's cocaine output; de la Espriella offers force, Cepeda offers substitution.

The winner also inherits Colombia's oil-export policy: Cepeda favors a phase-out, de la Espriella expansion.
