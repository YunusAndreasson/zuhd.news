Darmstadt — Eight years to travel, because arriving means braking.

Falling toward the Sun leaves a spacecraft too fast to enter orbit without help.

BepiColombo used nine gravity-assist flybys to shed speed before Thursday's final module separation.

The European-Japanese probe enters Mercury orbit on November 21.
