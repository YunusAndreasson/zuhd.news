Islamabad — DNA barcoding now identifies [Pakistan](country:PK)'s cryptic insect species.

The method flags which whitefly biotype carries the virus threatening Pakistan's top export crop.

Barcoding reads a short DNA sequence against a global database that undercounts species outside Europe and North America.

Only 21% of Pakistan's barcoded insect groups match a named species so far.
