United States — Ladybird's from-scratch engine became downloadable on 23 November.

Nearly the entire web runs on 3 engines: Blink, Gecko and WebKit.

Developers optimize for Chromium, so a fourth implementation exposes sites that depend on its behaviour rather than published standards.

Project lead Andreas Kling said "we make no promises about security today."

Windows support is planned, but the alpha runs only on macOS and Linux.
