Chapainawabganj — Shreejib Kumar Singh, 55, has no fingers.

He says guards pushed him into Bangladesh despite his Aadhaar card.

[India](country:IN)'s Border Security Force allegedly bypasses the bilateral verification and repatriation procedure, a practice Bangladesh calls push-in.

A [Bangladesh](country:BD) court will decide his illegal-entry case.
