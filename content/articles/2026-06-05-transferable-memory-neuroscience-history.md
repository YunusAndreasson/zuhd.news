Cambridge — Memory-transfer experiments that shook neuroscience are being retested.

1960s experiments fed trained flatworms to naive ones; naive worms appeared to inherit the memory — a result never replicated.

Harvard researcher Zachary Kelso is re-running the experiments to determine what the original data actually showed.
