Bolivia arrested a $2 million US-wanted cocaine trafficker. The arrest of Uruguayan Sebastián Marset came 6 days after Bolivian President Rodrigo Paz met US President Donald Trump at the Shield of the Americas Summit, ending a break in cooperation that began when Bolivia expelled the DEA in 2008. Paz called it the first of many operations to free Bolivia from "crime, drug trafficking, corruption and impunity."
