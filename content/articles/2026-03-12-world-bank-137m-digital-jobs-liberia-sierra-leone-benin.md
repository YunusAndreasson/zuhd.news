5.2 million West Africans will get broadband access. The World Bank's board approved $137 million for Benin, Liberia, and Sierra Leone, covering digital infrastructure, business regulation, and cross-border services — equivalent to roughly 5% of Liberia's GDP. The program projects 5.4 million active users when fully deployed, testing whether infrastructure spending can drive real digital access in the region.
