85 companies joined Mastercard's blockchain payments network. Partners including Binance, PayPal, and Ripple will link blockchain settlement to Mastercard's 200-country banking infrastructure, targeting cross-border transfers and remittances where fees are highest. Visa is pursuing an identical strategy, setting up a race to carry the backbone of global commerce on blockchain rails.
