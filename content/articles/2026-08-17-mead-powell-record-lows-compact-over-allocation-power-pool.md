Boulder City — Lake Mead and Lake Powell hit record lows.

Federal officials say 40 million people across seven states depend on the river.

The 1920s Colorado River Compact allocated the river using an unusually wet decade as its baseline.

The Bureau of Reclamation's post-2026 management strategy for the reservoirs has not yet been released.
