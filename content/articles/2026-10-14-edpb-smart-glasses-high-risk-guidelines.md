Brussels — EU privacy regulators called camera glasses a high risk to rights.

The glasses can record bystanders who never know their data is being processed.

The European Data Protection Board's first guidelines ask makers to signal recording with bright LEDs, audio signals or distinctive designs.

The board sees the greatest risk in hospitals, schools and workplaces, and more if footage reaches social networks.
