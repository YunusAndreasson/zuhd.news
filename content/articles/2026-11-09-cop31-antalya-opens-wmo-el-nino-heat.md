Antalya — El Niño will add 0.1-0.2C to global heat, the World Meteorological Organization says.

Developing countries need $305 billion a year for adaptation by 2035, the UN Environment Programme estimates.

International public adaptation finance reached $28.2-31.7 billion in 2024, and negotiators fought late Sunday over agenda items on finance.

A senior European Union official said last week there would be no political will for extra funding pledges.

Leaders address the summit on 11-12 November, and the talks close on 20 November.
