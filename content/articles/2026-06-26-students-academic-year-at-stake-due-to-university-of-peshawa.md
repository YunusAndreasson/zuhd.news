Landi Kotal — 350 Khyber students risk losing their academic year.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s Peshawar University cancelled semester exams in February after border clashes with [Afghanistan](country:AF); no new date has been set.

Students have appealed to Chief Minister Afridi — also the university's chancellor — to intervene before July 15 finals.
