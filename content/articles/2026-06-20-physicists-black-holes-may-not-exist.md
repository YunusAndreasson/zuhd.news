New York — Gravastars may replace what we call black holes.

Gravastars are horizonless compact objects that sidestep the singularity and information-loss paradox challenging general relativity.

Gravitational-wave echoes and black-hole shadow imaging could distinguish gravastars from classical black holes, making the claim testable.
