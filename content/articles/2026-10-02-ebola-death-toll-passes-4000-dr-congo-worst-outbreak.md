Bunia — [DR Congo](country:CD)'s Ebola toll reached 4,018, with 8,300 confirmed cases.

The WHO says the outbreak is on track to pass West Africa's 11,000 deaths.

Soldiers burned a transit centre for Ebola patients near Bunia this week, forcing 19,000 people to flee, the UN said.

Africa CDC says confirmed cases are falling, though insecurity and community refusal to cooperate may explain it.

No vaccine or treatment is approved for the Bundibugyo strain, though several are in trials.
