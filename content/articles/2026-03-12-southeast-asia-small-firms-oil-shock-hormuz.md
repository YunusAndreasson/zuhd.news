Southeast Asian food suppliers face 30% price rises. Small and medium enterprises cannot hedge fuel costs or pass price increases to customers without losing business, leaving them fully exposed to an oil shock their governments had no part in causing. The shock hits firms already absorbing US-China tariff losses, with no capital cushion rebuilt and no government relief announced.
