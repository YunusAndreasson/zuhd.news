Karachi — A government inquiry has already suspended officials.

The Sindh High Court gave [Pakistan](country:PK)'s province two weeks to explain the outbreak, tied to reused syringes at a state-run hospital.

Sindh Health Minister Ghani promises lifelong treatment for the 78 confirmed cases, though petitioners put the toll near 200.
