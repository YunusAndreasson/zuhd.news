Hong Kong — 3 security judges alone will decide Chow Hang-tung's fate. The pro-democracy barrister, on trial for subversion, told the court that Beijing's 2018 constitutional changes regressed Hong Kong to one-party rule. A conviction carries up to life in prison under a law used against over 260 people since 2020, most tried without a jury.
