Washington — One committee could reject any of 64,000 yearly National Institutes of Health grants.

The agency's $47 billion budget exceeds that of the next 10 research funders combined.

Under a draft executive order, grants need unanimous sign-off from a panel including budget chief Russell Vought and agency director Jay Bhattacharya.

Congress has already rejected Vought's proposed near-halving of its funding and put his political grant rule on hold.

Courts can intervene only after someone shows enough harm to sue.
