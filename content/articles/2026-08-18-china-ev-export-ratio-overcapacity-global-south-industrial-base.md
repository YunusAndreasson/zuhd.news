Beijing — 540,000 EVs left China last month, a record.

One now leaves [China](country:CN) for every two sold domestically.

Fixed plant costs and provincial jobs targets push the oversupply outward at any price.

Brazil, Thailand and Gulf buyers gain cheap cars but a weaker domestic auto industry.
