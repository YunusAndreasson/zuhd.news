France refused to join the US-led Hormuz coalition. President Emmanuel Macron named Europe's largest warship "Free France," invoking de Gaulle's wartime resistance as a signal of strategic independence. The €10 billion nuclear-powered carrier begins hull construction in 2031 as France assembles a European naval mission outside US command.
