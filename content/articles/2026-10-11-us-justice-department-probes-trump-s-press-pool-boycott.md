Washington — The Justice Department is investigating 5 networks for boycotting Trump.

Press-freedom group the Committee to Protect Journalists says antitrust probes should not intimidate outlets.

ABC, CBS, CNN, NBC and Fox News halted White House coverage on 21 September after the administration barred 3 outlets.

"Group boycotts among commercial competitors can violate the Sherman Act," spokeswoman Emily Covington said.

A judge's reprieve for CNN, MS NOW and Politico runs only until Tuesday.
