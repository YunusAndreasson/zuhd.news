New Delhi — Modi did not mention last month's protests.

The protests demanded jobs and exam integrity, not more coaching seats.

Nearly 40% of graduates aged 20 to 24 are jobless, India's Centre for Monitoring Indian Economy says, yet coaching adds supply, not jobs.

[India](country:IN)'s exam-review panel has not set a deadline or a coaching budget.
