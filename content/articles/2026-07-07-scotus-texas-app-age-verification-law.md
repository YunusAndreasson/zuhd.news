Washington — The Supreme Court let Texas's app age law proceed.

The law forces Apple and Google to verify users' ages at the app store gateway, not per app, pushing identity checks to the entrance of the mobile economy.

A student advocacy group and tech trade group sought an emergency stay and lost; the underlying case continues.
