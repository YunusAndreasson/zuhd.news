Canberra — Australia sued 3M for AU$2B over contaminated defence bases.

"Forever chemicals" that persist in soil and bloodstreams contaminated 28 defence bases; [Australia](country:AU) alleges 3M knew and concealed it.

3M says it will contest the claim; Australia has already spent AU$1B remediating the 28 sites.
