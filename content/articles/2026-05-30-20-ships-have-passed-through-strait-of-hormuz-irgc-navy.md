Tehran — 20 ships transited Hormuz Friday under Iranian navy supervision.

[Iran](country:IR)'s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps said transit requires coordination with its navy; the strait carries roughly 20% of global oil supply.

Military vessels and ships from "warmongering countries" remain barred, the corps said, with any unauthorized entry to be blocked.
