London — Occupied [Palestinian](country:PS) land sold openly in London Sunday.

Despite organiser denials, brochures at the Great Israeli Real Estate Event promoted properties in 7 illegal settlements including Ma'ale Adumim and Givat Ze'ev.

Over 100 [UK](country:GB) Members of Parliament had urged a ban; no such action followed the event.
