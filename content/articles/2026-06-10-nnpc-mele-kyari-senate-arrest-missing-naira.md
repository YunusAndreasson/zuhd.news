Abuja — [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Senate panel threatened Mele Kyari with arrest over ₦210 trillion in missing state oil revenue.

Auditors flagged the gap in Nigerian National Petroleum Corporation accounts from 2017–2023; Kyari said total revenue reached only ₦54.5 trillion.

Kyari is reportedly abroad; senators have given no deadline for his return.
