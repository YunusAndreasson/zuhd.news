Washington — Ex-lawmaker Chris Vieson lobbies for Anduril while advising a Navy panel.

31 ex-lawmakers left armed-services committees for weapons firms since 2015, a Bulletin study found.

Anduril, valued at $61 billion, cannot win the Pentagon's slow process, so it hires people who navigate it.

Seven armed-services members still trade stocks in violation of conflict-of-interest law, the report said.
