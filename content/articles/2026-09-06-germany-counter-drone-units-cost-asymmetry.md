Berlin — [Germany](country:DE) plans mobile counter-drone units.

German intelligence blames [Russia](country:RU), which denies it, for the failed Leipzig attack.

Small drones fly too low and quiet for radar built to track aircraft, forcing new detection methods.

Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt has not confirmed a budget for the program.
