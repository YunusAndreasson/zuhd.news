Lagos — S&P Global flags Africa as most food-inflation exposed.

Households spend over 40% of income on food; fertiliser shocks are still transmitting into consumer prices, S&P Global said.

Food inflation lags fertiliser shocks by months; [Nigeria](country:NG)'s petrol prices already rose 25% since February, Global Petrol Prices data shows.
