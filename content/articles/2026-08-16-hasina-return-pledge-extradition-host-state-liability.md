New Delhi — Sheikh Hasina, sheltering in India, has pledged to return home.

The pledge turns quiet asylum into active interference in a neighbour's politics.

[Bangladesh](country:BD) has sought her extradition, but [India](country:IN) has not surrendered her, so each statement binds its host.

Whether Delhi can still recover ties with Dhaka's interim government now hinges on her.
