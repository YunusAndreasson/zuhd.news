Brussels — [France](country:FR) proposed releasing 100 million barrels of diesel and crude.

The [US](country:US) wants 120 million barrels from Europe and threatens a diesel export ban otherwise.

European countries would release 50 million barrels of diesel, International Energy Agency members 50 million of crude.

Analyst Frederic Schneider expects any release to fall well short of 120 million barrels while the Hormuz closure continues.

The EU's 27 governments are still discussing the proposal, and no EU decision has been announced.
