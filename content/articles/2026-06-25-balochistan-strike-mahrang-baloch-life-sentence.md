Quetta — Rights organiser Dr Mahrang Baloch jailed for life.

An anti-terrorism court convicted Dr Mahrang for peaceful advocacy; [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Balochistan answered with a province-wide general strike.

The Human Rights Commission of Pakistan demanded her immediate release; 4 mainstream parties condemned the ruling.
