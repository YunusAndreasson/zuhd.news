The Gulf war froze one-third of revenue at i2Cool, a Hong Kong cooling-technology company. CEO Martin Zhu says rebuilding in new markets takes a year — meaning today's diversification decisions will outlast any ceasefire. Peter Hui, a Hong Kong manufacturer who planned a Dubai office and an Egyptian factory, has shelved both.
