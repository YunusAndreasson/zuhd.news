Amman — Three men face hanging 23 days after a church massacre.

Gunmen killed 9 and wounded 25 at a Greek Orthodox funeral in Hammoud village.

The State Security Court convicted two brothers and a relative by marriage of terrorist attacks, conspiracy and promoting a terrorist organisation's ideas.

[Jordan](country:JO)'s military statement did not say whether the three can appeal.
