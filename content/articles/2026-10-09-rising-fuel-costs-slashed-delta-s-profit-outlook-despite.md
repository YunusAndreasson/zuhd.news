Atlanta — Delta expects $6 billion more in fuel costs this year.

It cut its earnings forecast to $5.10-$5.60 a share, from July's $6.50-$7.50.

Tensions between the [US](country:US) and [Iran](country:IR) have lifted fuel prices, and Brent stood at $125.40 on 6 October.

CEO Ed Bastian said Delta raised prices roughly 20% this year and could sustain them if fuel falls.

United Airlines reports next, after the market close on 20 October.
