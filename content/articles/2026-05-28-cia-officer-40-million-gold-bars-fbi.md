Alexandria — Authorities seized 303 gold bars worth $40M from Central Intelligence Agency officer David Rush.

Rush listed them as work-related expenses from November to March; the agency could not locate them, court documents say.

Detention hearing is June 5; Director Ratcliffe referred it to the Federal Bureau of Investigation after an internal review.
