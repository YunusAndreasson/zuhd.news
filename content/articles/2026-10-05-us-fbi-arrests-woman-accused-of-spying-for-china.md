Los Angeles — The FBI caught a woman allegedly tracking Taiwan's president's son.

Taiwan's presidential office calls it transnational repression on [US](country:US) soil.

Wanying Zhang, 34, allegedly sent officials in [China](country:CN) his family's photos, video and plates, then headed for a Shanghai flight.

Charged as an unregistered foreign agent, not with espionage, Zhang appears in a Los Angeles federal court Monday.
