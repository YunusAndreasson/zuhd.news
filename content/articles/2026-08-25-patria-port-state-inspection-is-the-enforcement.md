Douala — Patria has shuttled between four ports since March.

Sanctions only work if port states choose to inspect the ships that arrive.

Barred from Western harbors, the sanctioned carrier loiters off [Cameroon](country:CM), [Gabon](country:GA) and [Nigeria](country:NG) with no declared cargo.

None of the three governments has said whether it plans to inspect the vessel.
