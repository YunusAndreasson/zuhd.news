Islamabad — The IMF began [Pakistan](country:PK)'s 4th review of a $7bn loan.

A $1.2bn tranche hinges on reforms to 7 state firms holding $8bn in assets.

Islamabad missed a March deadline to pass sovereign wealth fund rules covering OGDCL, Pakistan Petroleum and 5 other listed state firms.

Brent crude was at $114.90 a barrel on 22 September, up 23.9% in a month, straining Pakistan's import bill.

Pakistan needs IMF board waivers to unlock $1.2bn in tranches due by early November.
