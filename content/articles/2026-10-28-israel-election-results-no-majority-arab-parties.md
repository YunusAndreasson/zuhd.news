Jerusalem — Neither bloc reaches 61 seats without Arab parties.

Ra'am and the Joint List won 17 seats on record Palestinian turnout, and neither bloc has agreed to partner with them.

Netanyahu's bloc has about 54 seats and Eisenkot's 49, with counting unfinished in [Israel](country:IL)'s 120-seat Knesset.

"They want change but do not want to cooperate with Arab parties," said Daniel Bar Tal, a Tel Aviv University political psychologist.

Polymarket traders give 17% to Netanyahu leaving office by year-end, down 25 points in a week.
