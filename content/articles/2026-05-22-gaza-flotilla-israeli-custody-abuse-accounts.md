Istanbul — Israeli forces sexually assaulted at least 15 flotilla detainees, survivors said.

Australian activist Juliet Lamont described 5 men beating her while cable-tied; others reported rubber bullets, sedatives, and broken ribs.

Several governments summoned Israeli ambassadors; none has opened a formal inquiry.
