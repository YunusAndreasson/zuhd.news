Cape Canaveral — 270 rockets launched last year, still not enough.

Satellite operators worldwide now wait years just to book a ride.

Nearly all the added capacity flies SpaceX's own Starlink satellites, while Vulcan and New Glenn both slipped for years.

SpaceX has told [the US](country:US) government it intends to end commercial Falcon 9 flights after 2028.
