Shenzhen — OPPO's 770 million users will get a borrowed AI agent.

[China](country:CN)'s phone shipments fell 15.4% in the third quarter and OPPO's 17.6%, International Data Corporation says.

On 13 October OPPO connected Manus's multi-step task execution to AI Mind Pilot, launching first on the Find X10 with ColorOS 17.

Counterpoint says part of the drop is deliberate, as OPPO merges three brands and cuts low-margin products.

Whether fewer sales bought better margins stays unknown until OPPO reports phone-business profit.
