Tarrana — Egyptian archaeologists found hundreds of coffins and gold.

Officials say preliminary studies suggest the necropolis held middle-class dead.

The graves date from Egypt's Late Period to early Roman rule, beside Wadi el-Natrun, source of the natron used in mummification.

More fieldwork is planned at the site, the ministry says.
