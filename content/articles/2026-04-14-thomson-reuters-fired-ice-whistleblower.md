New York — Thomson Reuters fired its internal ICE watchdog. Billie Little, a 20-year attorney editor, was fired after leading 200 colleagues to challenge CLEAR's use by ICE to track immigrants. Little filed a lawsuit; Thomson Reuters has not named the code-of-conduct violation it used to fire her.
