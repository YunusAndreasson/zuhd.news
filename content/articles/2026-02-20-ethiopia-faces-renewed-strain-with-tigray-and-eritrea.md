Eritrean troops remain in northern Ethiopia 3 years after the Pretoria peace deal required their withdrawal. Amhara and Eritrean forces still control western and southern Tigray, where rights groups document ongoing sexual violence, arbitrary detention, and land seizures. Ethiopia's demand for Eritrea to leave has escalated into open threats, raising fears of a new war in the Horn of Africa.
