Abuja — Zakaria Garba sentenced to death for 2014 bombing.

Garba pleaded guilty to coordinating the Nyanya Motor Park blast that killed 70, court records show; the trial took 12 years.

Co-conspirators remain at large; [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Abuja court sentenced 4 others in the concurrent mass terrorism trial.
