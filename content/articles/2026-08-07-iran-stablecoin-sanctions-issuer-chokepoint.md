Washington — Treasury sanctioned a dollar-token exchange, not a bank.

Treasury says Shelbit and [Iran](country:IR)-based Aban Tether used dollar-pegged stablecoins, marketed as a sanctions hedge, to route Revolutionary Guard funds around sanctioned banks.

Neither exchange has confirmed wallets were frozen; Tether says it is checking for ties to Shelbit.
