Seoul — OKX paid $53m for [South Korea](country:KR)'s Coinone stake.

At 19.6%, OKX gains access to South Korea's tightly regulated market without triggering a control-change review; founder Cha Myunghun retains 27.8%.

The deal requires regulatory approval; Korea Investment & Securities is making an identical $53m investment simultaneously.
