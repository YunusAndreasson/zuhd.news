Dubai — Gold fell 9.2% in a week to $3,515 an ounce.

Buyers who postponed purchases at record prices are returning to Dubai's Gold Souq.

A hawkish Federal Reserve, a stronger dollar and oil-driven inflation pushed prices down, with markets pricing another rate increase next month.

"Don't look to sell tomorrow," said Kaushik Rajpara, senior sales executive at Paris Gems.
