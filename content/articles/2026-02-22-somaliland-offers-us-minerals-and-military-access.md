Somaliland offered the US lithium, coltan, and military bases. Somaliland minister Khadar Hussein Abdi said the unrecognized state wants American recognition in exchange for mineral rights and strategic access. US President Trump hinted last August he was preparing to act, but Washington has not formally shifted its position.
