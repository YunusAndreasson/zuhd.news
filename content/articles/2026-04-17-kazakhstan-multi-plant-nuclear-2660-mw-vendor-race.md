Astana — Kazakhstan will build several nuclear plants by 2032. The world's top uranium producer needs 2,660 MW of new capacity; southern and western regions already face electricity shortages. Rosatom leads the bid; the contract also determines Kazakhstan's fuel-cycle partner for 60 years.
