Vietnam's Communist Party pre-approved 93% of Sunday's ballot. The vote installs replacements for leaders ousted in an anti-corruption purge, held as global attention fixes on the Iran war. In April, the National Assembly will ratify a president and prime minister already chosen by the party in January.
