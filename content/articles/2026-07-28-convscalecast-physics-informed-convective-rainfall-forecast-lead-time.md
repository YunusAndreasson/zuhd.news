Shanghai — Weather models underpredict extreme rain.

ConvScaleCast blends physics constraints with machine learning to forecast downpours 24 hours out, beating Europe's top forecaster over Shanghai.

The system was validated on one city; whether it generalizes to Karachi, Lagos or Dhaka remains untested.
