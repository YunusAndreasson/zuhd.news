Mundafan — A 340 km² lake once filled [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s Empty Quarter.

Humans lived on its shore 125,000 years ago among hippos, extinct buffaloes and ostriches.

Residue on 5,511 stone tools shows toxic plants, possibly used in hunting, the study found.

The study calls the toxic-plant hunting use potential evidence, among the first recorded in Saudi Arabia.

Researchers from King Abdullah University of Science and Technology and Griffith University published the findings in Science.
