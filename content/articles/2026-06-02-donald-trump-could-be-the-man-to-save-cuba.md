Havana — Visa and Mastercard cut Cuba on June 6.

Executive Order 14404 forced the cutoff; [Cuba](country:CU) has also lost Venezuelan oil and seen tourist arrivals fall 48% in early 2026, the government said.

UN rights experts called the combined measures "colonial-era practices"; Cuba retains cash, Mir, and UnionPay transactions.
