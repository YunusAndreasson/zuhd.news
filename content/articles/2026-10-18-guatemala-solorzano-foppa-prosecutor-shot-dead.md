Guatemala City — Gunmen killed ex-tax chief Juan Francisco Solórzano Foppa.

In [Guatemala](country:GT), he prosecuted dozens of powerful business figures while running the tax authority from 2016 to 2018.

Two men shot him and Gustavo Adolfo Aguilar Gamarro at the Campo Marte sports complex on Saturday, prosecutors said.

Police hold 2 suspects, and President Bernardo Arévalo wants those who ordered the killing prosecuted too.
