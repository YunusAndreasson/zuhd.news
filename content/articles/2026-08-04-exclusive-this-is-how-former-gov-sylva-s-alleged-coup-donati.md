Abuja — A currency dealer moved 785 million naira to plotters.

Investigators allege former Bayelsa governor Timipre Sylva financed 2025's failed coup through a company account, and a bureau de change operator paid named officers before the plot collapsed on 29 September.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s prosecution of the alleged financiers and officers is ongoing.
