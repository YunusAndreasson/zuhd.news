Kirkland — Amazon now builds 27 satellites a week, up from 27 in six months.

Starlink has dominated satellite internet for 5 years, and OneWeb offers only limited service.

Amazon's satellites wait on rockets, and the Vulcan's return-to-flight mission will carry the next batch.

Delta Air Lines chose Amazon over Starlink for its planes, which upset Elon Musk.

Commercial service is weeks away, Rajeev Badyal, who leads the project, said.
