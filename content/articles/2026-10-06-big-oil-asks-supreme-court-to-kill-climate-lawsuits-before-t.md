Washington — [US](country:US) justices heard a bid to end 24-plus climate suits.

Plaintiffs seek potentially billions of dollars to cover climate-related extreme weather costs.

ExxonMobil and Suncor argue federal law preempts state claims, so Boulder's 2018 suit should never reach a jury.

"It's about deception," said Kevin Russell, Boulder's lawyer.

A ruling is due by June, and Justice Alito's recusal means a tie would let the case proceed.
