Chancellor Rachel Reeves convened an emergency heating oil meeting. 1.5 million rural UK households are off the gas grid and have no alternative fuel; UK gas prices roughly doubled in early March after Hormuz closed. No specific relief has been announced, and families face another heating month with bills doubled by a war 5,000 kilometers away.
