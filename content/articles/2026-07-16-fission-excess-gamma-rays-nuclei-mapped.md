Grenoble — One experiment mapped gammas across a dozen nuclei.

Fission fragments shed excess energy as gamma radiation; measuring it across many isotopes at once tightens models used for reactor heat accounting.

The result narrows a long-standing gap in fission theory that reactor engineers rely on.
