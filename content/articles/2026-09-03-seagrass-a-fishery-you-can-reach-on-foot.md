Zanzibar City — Millions in extreme poverty rely on seagrass.

Women, children and boatless households glean the one fishery open to them.

Conservation funding has flowed to reefs and mangroves while seagrass value went uncounted in national accounts.

The study is a first attempt to make that value count in policy.
