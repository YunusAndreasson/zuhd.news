Freetown — Sierra Leone declared an opioid national emergency. Trade records show 320 million tapentadol pills reached Sierra Leone and Ghana; over half were in doses unapproved even in India. India's drug regulator has banned only tapentadol-carisoprodol combinations; single-agent exports face no controls.
