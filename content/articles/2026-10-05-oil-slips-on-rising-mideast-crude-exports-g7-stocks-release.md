New York — Brent fell $1.93 to $100.32 despite a G7 stock release.

[US](country:US) reserve stocks fell to 283 million barrels, lowest since 1982, Energy Department data show.

The G7 agreed Friday to release 100 million barrels, though it is unclear how much repeats March's 400 million-barrel release.

Aramco chief Amin Nasser said refilling emergency stockpiles could take two years.

OPEC+ delayed its review of 2027 output quotas, leaving future production capacity uncertain.
