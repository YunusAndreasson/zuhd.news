Suva — [Fiji](country:FJ)'s election season is arriving without fact-checking infrastructure.

Small Pacific media markets lack the moderation systems large platforms built for bigger markets, leaving communities to verify claims themselves.

Community verification networks, not platform moderation, may decide whether Pacific votes hold.
