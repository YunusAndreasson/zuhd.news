Samsung Heavy won a $253 million LNG ship order. The vessel, due in April 2029, will serve Pacific LNG routes made critical by the closure of the Strait of Hormuz. Energy analysts say Pacific corridors will remain competitive even after Hormuz reopens, as importers diversify away from Gulf supply.
