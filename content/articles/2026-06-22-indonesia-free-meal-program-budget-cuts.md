Jakarta — [Indonesia](country:ID) revised a free-meal scheme for 83 million.

Prabowo's flagship program — covering children and pregnant women — faces scrutiny over its high cost; the government cited budget efficiency.

Which recipients lose access and to what extent has not been publicly disclosed.
