Kourou — Upgraded Ariane 64 launches 36 Amazon Kuiper satellites 17 June.

Amazon is paying [France](country:FR)-backed Arianespace to race Starlink; upgraded P160C boosters add 14 tonnes of propellant, lifting payload from 32 to 36 satellites per flight.

The next Ariane 6 launch follows August 28, likely carrying a European weather satellite.
