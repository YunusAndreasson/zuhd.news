Gillingham — Missed fracture cases dropped 72% at one hospital.

Medway Maritime Hospital caught only half its non-ambulatory fragility fractures until it added a mandatory prompt to its admissions system, researchers report.

Identification accuracy reached 89.1%; the hospital is now using the tool in staff induction training.
