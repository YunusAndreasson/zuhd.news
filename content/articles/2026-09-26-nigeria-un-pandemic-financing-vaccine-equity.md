New York — [Nigeria](country:NG)'s health-security evaluation score rose from 39% to 54%.

Minister of State for Health Iziaq Salako told the UN poorer countries need faster pandemic financing.

He wants countries that share pathogens to get timely benefits from resulting vaccines, plus technology transfer to African manufacturers.

Nigeria hosts the fifth global antimicrobial resistance ministerial conference in December, the first held in Africa.
