Zurich — Hackers replaced the hockey federation's homepage with "Scared to face Russia?"

The pro-Russian message mocked [Russia](country:RU)'s exclusion, and the federation's website stayed offline Wednesday.

The Court of Arbitration for Sport ruled for Russia in October, and the federation is resisting immediate reinstatement.

Russia's own hockey federation condemned the hack, and no one has identified the attackers.

The federation says its platforms return once it is confident they are secure.
