The training centre served 16,000 Palestinian refugees across the occupied West Bank.

A 2024 law barring contact with UNRWA, passed by [Israel](country:IL)'s Knesset, lets officials treat its site as unclaimed land.

The agency and the Palestinian presidency have asked the UN Security Council to intervene.
