Iranian drones hit Nakhchivan's airport and a school, injuring 4. Nakhchivan is an Azerbaijani exclave bordering Iran — the first strike on a country with no declared role in the war. Azerbaijan brought its army to full combat readiness and summoned Iran's ambassador; Iran denied responsibility.
