14 Arab and Muslim governments issued a joint condemnation. US Ambassador to Israel Mike Huckabee endorsed Israeli control of land from the Nile to the Euphrates on biblical grounds, prompting Saudi Arabia to call the remarks dangerous and irresponsible. Huckabee later dismissed his remarks as hyperbole.
