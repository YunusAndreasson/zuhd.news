Cape Town — The 23-member planning tribunal declared member qualifications non-mandatory.

Under [South Africa](country:ZA)'s access law, the body refused to disclose members' records; it controls all rezonings in the city.

Residents have no mechanism to verify whether members hold conflicts of interest.
