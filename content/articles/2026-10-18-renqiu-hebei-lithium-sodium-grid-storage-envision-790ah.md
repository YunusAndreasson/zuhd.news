Renqiu — A 400MWh grid battery will mix lithium and sodium.

It is among [China](country:CN)'s first lithium-plus-sodium pilots, and Envision's 790Ah cells won the lithium tender.

A January national rule scales capacity payments by hours of full-power discharge, so 2-hour storage earns less than 4-hour.

Foresight Energy says one tender proves nothing until more projects repeat the choice.
