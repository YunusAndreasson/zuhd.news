Washington — Washington is planning its largest war supplement since Iraq. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth wants the funds for "what we may have to do in the future"; Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent says the US has "plenty of money" for Iran. Neither official has submitted a formal appropriations request to Congress.
