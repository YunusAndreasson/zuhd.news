Lafayette — 130,000 acres of marsh may become SpaceX land.

The Pecan Island tract, freed by an ExxonMobil pollution settlement, would give Starship a hurricane-buffer coastline and a polar-orbit launch path near the Henry Hub gas hub.

The Times-Picayune expects Louisiana Governor Jeff Landry to announce terms this month; neither side has confirmed them.
