Tokyo — [Japan](country:JP)'s Finance Ministry offers 50-60 trillion yen for defence.

Ruling-party conservatives and military planners want 80 trillion yen, and Washington wants 3.5% of GDP.

The Japan Institute for National Fundamentals says imported [US](country:US) equipment and dollar-priced fuel cost more as the yen weakens.

Washington wants over 3 trillion yen for hosting US forces from April 2027, nearly triple today's payments, Japan's public broadcaster reported.
