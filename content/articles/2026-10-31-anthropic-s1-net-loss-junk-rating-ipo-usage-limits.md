San Francisco — Anthropic reports an $80.7 billion first-half loss.

Its 26 October filing shows only $16.3 billion in revenue over the same period.

Anthropic expects a double-B rating after listing, the top junk grade, while Blackstone forecast investment grade by 2027.

The listing is targeted for the week of 16 November, and a junk rating would make borrowing costlier.
