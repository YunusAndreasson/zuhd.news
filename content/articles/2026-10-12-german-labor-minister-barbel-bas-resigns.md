Berlin — Bärbel Bas quit two posts, citing her stepdaughter's death.

The Social Democrats lose [Germany](country:DE)'s welfare minister and one of two leaders at once.

Her ministry holds the pension and working-time bills the coalition wants passed this year, which conservative politicians accused her of delaying.

"The attacks against her during the last few days of her mourning were indecent," said Vice Chancellor Lars Klingbeil.

Alexander Schweitzer is set to take the labor ministry, and the party has not named a co-leader.
