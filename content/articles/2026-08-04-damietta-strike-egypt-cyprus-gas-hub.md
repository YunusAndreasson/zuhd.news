Damietta — Energy vessels were struck days after Europe staked its gas on this port.

TotalEnergies and Eni greenlit piping Cyprus's gas through [Egypt](country:EG)'s Damietta and Zohr plants by 2028 — the two liquefaction terminals are the single point of failure for the whole route.

Analysts say the deal survives one strike; war-risk cover is the open question.
