China — State investors now supply 27% of DeepSeek's funding, up from 2%.

The round totals 105.5 billion yuan ($15.8 billion) from nearly 30 investors, a breakdown shows.

DeepSeek doubled the round midway as an 8-chip Nvidia B300 server rose from 15 million to 19 million yuan in 4 months.

Listed rivals MiniMax and Zhipu have already slid as early investors' shares came unlocked.
