London — Only 87 women got the stronger blood thinners tested.

That's too few to prove the drugs work differently by sex, yet it may shape future guidance.

The trial paired potent P2Y12 inhibitors with a blood thinner but stopped early after a bleeding signal, researchers said.

Researchers call the sex finding hypothesis-generating, not proof, pending a larger trial.
