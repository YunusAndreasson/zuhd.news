Le Bourget — Mélenchon promised 20,000 supporters nationalisations and a NATO exit.

He polls about 18% for the first round in [France](country:FR), within reach of a run-off against Marine Le Pen.

He would convert €600bn of debt held by the European Central Bank into interest-free loans that need never be repaid.

"Borrowing money and deciding not to give it back - that's called theft," said Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu in September.

The election is in April, and Polymarket traders give Le Pen a 45% chance of winning, up 3 points in a week.
