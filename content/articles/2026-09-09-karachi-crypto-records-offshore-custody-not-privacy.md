Karachi — Police obtained a dead man's four-year trading record.

A private individual's complete trading history proves retrievable, years deep, on police request.

[Pakistan](country:PK) has no domestic exchange, so traders use offshore platforms like Binance that retain identity documents and full order histories.

A judicial commission is investigating Mir Raza Ali's death, and no charge has been filed yet.
