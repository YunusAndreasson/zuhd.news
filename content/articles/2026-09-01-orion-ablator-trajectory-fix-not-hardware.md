Mountain View — Artemis II's shield lost only nine char sites.

That's down from over 100 sites on Artemis I, easing Artemis III's timeline.

NASA didn't rebuild the shield — it flew a steeper reentry, cutting the gas-trapping heating time that caused cracking.

Future Artemis missions get a more permeable Avcoat, restoring downrange distance lost this time.
