Johannesburg — Gauteng's taps have run dry for years. South Africa's Human Rights Commission launched a formal inquiry, citing infrastructure failure and criminal tanker networks charging residents for municipal supply. Submissions close April 30; a May 19 hearing could produce legally binding directives against the state.
