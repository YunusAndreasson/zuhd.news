China — Chinese firms got 3 to 8 times more state support than rivals, a 2026 report finds.

Li Yuankun of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences says one interest-rate choice can inflate the estimate.

The Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development benchmarks below-market loans to [China](country:CN)'s Loan Prime Rate, Li says, not comparable interbank or bond yields.

Washington and Brussels are again arguing subsidies over Chinese electric vehicles, solar panels and steel.
