Monrovia — Clinic shelves ran empty of contraceptives.

Health workers fear a rise in unsafe abortions and teenage pregnancies as supplies disappear.

Donor funding gaps, not local procurement, left Liberian women and girls without access, SciDev.Net reports.

How soon shelves restock depends on funders who have not said.
