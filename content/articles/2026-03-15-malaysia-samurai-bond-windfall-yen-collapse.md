The yen fell 28% against Malaysia's ringgit. The crash shrank Malaysia's repayment on a 2019 yen bond from RM7.2 billion to RM5.2 billion, eliminating all RM400 million in interest costs. Any yen recovery before the bond matures in 2029 would reduce the windfall.
