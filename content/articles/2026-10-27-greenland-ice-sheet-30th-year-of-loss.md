Nuuk — Greenland lost ice for the 30th straight year, 135 billion tonnes.

It is the largest present-day source of ice-driven sea level rise, Danish scientists write.

Snowfall still outweighed surface melt by 519 billion tonnes, but iceberg calving and glacier-front melting erased the gain.

It was not an extreme year, the Danish Meteorological Institute scientists said, with a cool start to summer.

A new report finds Greenland's melt processes are increasingly replicated in Antarctica.
