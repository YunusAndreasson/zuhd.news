Durban — Jacob Zuma's MK party won eThekwini's mayoralty, 139 votes to 79.

It is MK's first metro, and ANC deals now threaten KwaZulu-Natal's unity government.

Democratic Alliance insiders said ANC national leaders overruled provincial officials who favoured a deal with the Democratic Alliance and Inkatha Freedom Party.

"We believe handing the keys of eThekwini to MK would be a disaster," said Martin Meyer, a Democratic Alliance provincial minister.

The Democratic Alliance says it will ask its partners what this means for the provincial government.
