New York — Gold, silver, and bitcoin are selling off together.

All 3 are unwinding the same "debasement" bet against paper currency as capital rotates into artificial intelligence equities, analysts say.

Bitcoin has fallen about half from its peak; the artificial intelligence rally shows no sign of reversing.
