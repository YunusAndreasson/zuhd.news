Johannesburg — Herman Mashaba beat Helen Zille 170 votes to 97.

ActionSA and the African National Congress now run all three Gauteng metros, excluding the Democratic Alliance.

The Democratic Alliance's 91 of 270 seats fell short of the 136 needed, and its rivals combined 170 votes.

The uMkhonto weSizwe party's Dali Mpofu denied Mashaba's Thursday claim that it was withdrawing coalition support.

Mayoral committee portfolios in Tshwane are still being finalised.
