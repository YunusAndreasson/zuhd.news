Abakaliki — Liquefied petroleum gas prices doubled; Nigerian families reverted to charcoal.

LPG rose from N1,100 to N2,000 per kilogramme in [Nigeria](country:NG) since April, pricing out millions who had switched from charcoal.

A charcoal paint-bucket sells for N1,500; kerosene hit N2,200 per litre, leaving no affordable clean alternative.
