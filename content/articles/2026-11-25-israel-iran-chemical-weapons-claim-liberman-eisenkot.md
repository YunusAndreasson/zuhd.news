Israel — Avigdor Liberman says Israel has no grounds to strike [Iran](country:IR) now.

Defense Minister Israel Katz alleges Iran is developing chemical weapons but cannot yet attack with them.

Liberman, who joined Gadi Eisenkot's coalition Tuesday, accuses caretaker Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and Katz of a lust for power.

Iran's foreign minister Abbas Araghchi dismissed the claim last week as "rubbish."

Liberman says Eisenkot could present a government by the end of next week.
