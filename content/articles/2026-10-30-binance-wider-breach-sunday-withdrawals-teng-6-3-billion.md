United States — Binance found more breach points than the one laptop it blamed.

Bitcoin fell to a six-week low of $76,368 and customers cannot withdraw while Binance traces the attackers.

Attackers emptied wallets on 10 blockchains in about 20 minutes, and TRM Labs ties them to [North Korea](country:KP)'s TraderTraitor unit.

Binance says it has frozen about $3bn of the $6.3bn, but has not said where the other $3.3bn went.

Co-CEO Yi He says withdrawals should resume by Sunday.
