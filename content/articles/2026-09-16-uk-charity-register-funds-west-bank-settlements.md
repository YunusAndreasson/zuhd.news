London — Six UK charities sent £1.7m to settlements.

Gift aid means British taxpayers help fund construction on occupied Palestinian land.

The Charity Commission certified each group as charitable even as [Britain](country:GB) calls the settlements themselves unlawful.

None of the six charities responded, and the commission won't say if it is investigating them.
