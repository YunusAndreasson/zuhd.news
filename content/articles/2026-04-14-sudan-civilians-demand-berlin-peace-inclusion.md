Khartoum — Sudan's war enters year 3 without civilian voices. 4 civil society blocs representing hundreds of organisations wrote Berlin conference principals demanding inclusion; talks have centred the SAF and RSF, sidelining civilians. The Berlin conference opens April 15; no civil society group is listed as a principal participant.
