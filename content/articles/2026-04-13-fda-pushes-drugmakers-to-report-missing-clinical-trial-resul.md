Washington — FDA reminded 2,200 companies to follow reporting law. An internal analysis found 30% of mandatory-reporting studies withheld results — covering 3,000 trials, some taxpayer-funded. Without results, findings cannot be replicated, treatment decisions are skewed, and costs are hidden from regulators and clinicians.
