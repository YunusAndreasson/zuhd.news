Islamabad — Petrol pumps refused to honor a new subsidy.

Dealers said they lacked the system to give buyers Rs100 off per liter.

The app-based subsidy makes dealers front the discount, with [Pakistan](country:PK)'s central bank promising repayment within 48 hours.

Islamabad has earmarked funds for three months; wider dealer complaints remain unresolved.
