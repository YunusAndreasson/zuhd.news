Both candidates from Congo's last election are serving 20-year prison sentences. Denis Sassou Nguesso, in power for 40 years, ran Sunday with no credible challenger and 2 parties boycotting. French prosecutors are investigating Congolese officials for embezzling oil revenues, which account for 75% of exports while more than half the population lives in poverty.
