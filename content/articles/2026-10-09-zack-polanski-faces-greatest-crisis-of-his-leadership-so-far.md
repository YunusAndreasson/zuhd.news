London — Labour held Starmer's old seat with 45% of the vote.

Sagal Abdi-Wali, a Somali-born former child refugee, now holds one of [Britain](country:GB)'s most watched seats.

Green leader Zack Polanski took 33%, the party's second-best Westminster by-election result, a big swing since the general election.

Prime Minister Andy Burnham still faces a sluggish economy and rising bond yields from the war with Iran.
