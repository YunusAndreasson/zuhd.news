Samut Sakhon — Khieo Lapchanh vanished the night before his asylum interview.

He is at least the third Lao dissident to disappear or die in [Thailand](country:TH) since 2019.

A 2024 Human Rights Watch review found Thailand trading dissidents with neighbors in what it called a "swap mart."

More than 130 UN rights experts urged governments last week to speed asylum protections for people at risk.
