London — Front-leg stride, not hind-leg, flags canine dementia.

Chronological age predicted stride length poorly; cognitive performance drove it — mirroring step-length reduction documented in human dementia, the study found.

Researchers propose front-leg gait measurement as a low-cost, non-invasive early screening tool for veterinarians.
