Islamabad — Islamabad's regulator canceled 2,999 kanals of layout.

Thousands of buyers now hold plots that cannot be legally registered.

The Capital Development Authority says Bahria Town, [Pakistan](country:PK)'s largest developer, missed 2010 approval terms for parks and public land.

Bahria Town called the cancellation illegal and vowed to challenge it in court.
