Karachi — [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) and [UAE](country:AE) together sent $2 billion to [Pakistan](country:PK) in May, a record monthly total of $4.3 billion.

Cumulative fiscal year 2026 remittances reached $38.1 billion, up 9.2%, the State Bank of Pakistan said.

Full-year remittances are on track to top $41 billion, the State Bank said.
