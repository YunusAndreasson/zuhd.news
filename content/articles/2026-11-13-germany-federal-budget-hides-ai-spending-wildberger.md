Berlin — [Germany](country:DE)'s digital ministry budget mentions AI in just 3 of 80-plus lines.

The ministry's AI taskforce aims to make Germany an AI nation by 2030.

Its report says its targets justify no funding decisions and do not prejudge the 2027 budget.

The ministry's press release puts €80 million aside for AI applications in administration and business.

The federal government is still negotiating the 2027 budget, and the ministry has not explained its AI spending.
