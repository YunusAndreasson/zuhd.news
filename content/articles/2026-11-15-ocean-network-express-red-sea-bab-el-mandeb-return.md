Ocean Network Express is expected to step up shipping through the Red Sea.

Cape of Good Hope traffic ran 12% below normal and Bab el-Mandeb 5% below, as of 8 November.

[Yemen](country:YE)'s government pulled forces back from the strait's approaches Sunday, leaving the Houthis in control.
