KC-135 tanker crashed in Iraq Thursday, killing 6 airmen. The 60-year-old aircraft has been flown at elevated intensity during the Iran war, straining airframes designed for Cold War peacetime operations. The crash brought US war deaths to 13; the Islamic Resistance in Iraq, an Iran-backed militia coalition, claimed it shot down the plane.
