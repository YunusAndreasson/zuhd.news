Port Shepstone — SA Lithium earmarked 150 homes for demolition.

The mine's environmental impact assessment was filed by its own consultant; [South Africa](country:ZA)'s law required relocation agreements before mining began.

Lawyers demanded SA Lithium restart the assessment; the first forced relocations were scheduled for August 2026.
