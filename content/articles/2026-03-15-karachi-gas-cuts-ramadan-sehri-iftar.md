Karachi families cannot cook Sehri during Ramadan. Iran's Hormuz blockade halted Qatar's LNG shipments to Pakistan, forcing the gas utility to cut supply to 2 daily windows: 3:30-6am and 3:30-7pm. Pakistan's government has offered no timeline for restoring full supply, and Qatar's LNG shipments will remain suspended for the war's duration.
