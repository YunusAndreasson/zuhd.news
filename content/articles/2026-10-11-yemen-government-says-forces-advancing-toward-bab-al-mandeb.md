Yemen — Government says it took Dhubab and cut the Mocha road.

Bab el-Mandeb averaged 29.6 ships a day to 4 October, 3% above normal.

[Yemen](country:YE)'s prime minister's office said forces are moving to control the strait, and government forces separately reported 8 air strikes near Taiz.

Only the government makes these claims, and no independent confirmation accompanies them.

The Houthis, who struck Riyadh's airport the same weekend, have declared Saudi airspace a target.
