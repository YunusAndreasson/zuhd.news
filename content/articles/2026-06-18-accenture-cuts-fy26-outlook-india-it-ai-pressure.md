Dublin — Infosys fell 4.4% after Accenture cut its outlook.

Accenture cut fiscal 2026 guidance to 3-4% (from 3-5%), and fourth-quarter revenue missed estimates, as AI compresses demand for outsourced [India](country:IN)n information technology work.

Wipro fell 2%; both firms hinge on technology services demand that Accenture's guidance signals is contracting.
