Doha — QatarEnergy will buy up to 3 million tonnes of US liquefied natural gas yearly.

An [Iranian](country:IR) strike knocked out about 17% of Qatar's gas export capacity, and repairs could take up to 5 years.

The 20-year deal covers Woodside's Louisiana project, with volumes starting when its second train runs.

Buyers in [Pakistan](country:PK) and Bangladesh were told force majeure runs to year-end, and Bangladesh is buying spot cargoes.

Woodside targets first exports in 2029, and Qatar expects its first deliveries in 2030.
