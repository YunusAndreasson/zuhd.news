National Rally tested local dominance in Sunday's French vote. Municipal wins would give National Rally leader Marine Le Pen ground-level infrastructure for the 2027 presidential race — open since President Emmanuel Macron cannot seek a third consecutive term. A second round follows next Sunday, with left-wing parties scrambling to build a coalition to block Le Pen.
