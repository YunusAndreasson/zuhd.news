Washington — The US will criminally prosecute Iranian oil buyers. Acting AG Todd Blanche moved beyond Treasury fines — buying sanctioned Iranian crude now carries criminal charges, directly targeting China, India, and Turkey. Trump has also announced a naval blockade of Iran, compounding pressure on the 3 largest crude buyers.
