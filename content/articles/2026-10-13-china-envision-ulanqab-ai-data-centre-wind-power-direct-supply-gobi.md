Ulanqab — [China](country:CN)'s Envision says wind farms feed 80% of its data centre.

Substations take 12-18 months to build, and data-centre customers want capacity in 6.

Envision says dedicated lines carry the wind power, with batteries, forecasting models and 800-volt direct current cutting conversion steps.

Envision's account does not say how the site runs when the wind drops.

Envision plans 5 gigawatts of desert sites by 2030 under Mission Gobi.
