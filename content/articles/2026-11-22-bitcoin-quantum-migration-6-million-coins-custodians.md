United States — 6 million bitcoin sit behind exposed public keys.

That is 31.2% of circulating supply, Glassnode data show as of October.

A powerful enough quantum computer could use exposed keys to seize coins, and exchanges, custodians and bridges must upgrade too.

Developer Jameson Lopp wants to freeze coins that never migrate, while Blockstream CEO Adam Back favours voluntary upgrades.

Bitcoin Improvement Proposals 360 and 361 both remain drafts.
