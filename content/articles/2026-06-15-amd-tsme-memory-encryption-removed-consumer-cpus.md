Santa Clara — AMD removed Transparent Secure Memory Encryption from consumer CPUs, leaving them vulnerable to cold-boot attacks.

The feature encrypted all RAM against physical-access attacks; AMD moved it to PRO-tier chips with no public explanation.

AMD engineers closed a public bug report saying they had "nothing more to share" on the change.
