London — The [UK](country:GB) economy is 4-6% smaller than if it had stayed in the EU.

Trade friction — not sovereignty — drove it: EU goods exports fell 12%, food 30%; a 4% permanent productivity loss, the Office for Budget Responsibility estimates.

Andy Burnham, likely the 7th prime minister in a decade, ruled out rejoining the single market.
