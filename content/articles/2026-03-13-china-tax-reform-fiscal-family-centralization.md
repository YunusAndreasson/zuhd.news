Local governments carry an estimated $13 trillion in debt. Beijing collects most tax revenue while mandating that local governments fund schools, hospitals, and pensions — the structural source of the shortfall. China's 15th five-year plan pledges to increase local "autonomous fiscal resources" without reducing Beijing's dominant role.
