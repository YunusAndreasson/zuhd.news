Gaza — Under-5 mortality more than doubled to 33.4 per 1,000 births.

[Israel](country:IL)'s attacks caused 70.1% of the 24,650 deaths Gaza's Health Ministry registered in 2025.

Excluding war deaths, the ministry puts the under-5 rate at 15.8, still above the 2022 level of 13.6.

No outside body has yet published figures to confirm or contest the ministry's count.
