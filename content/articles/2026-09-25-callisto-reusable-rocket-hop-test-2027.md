Kourou — Europe's reusable rocket demonstrator will not hop before 2027.

[France](country:FR)'s space agency first hinted at the delay in a September 2025 procurement notice.

[Germany](country:DE)'s DLR finished testing the nose cone and completed the fins, while landing legs remain in manufacture.

[Japan](country:JP)'s JAXA has not announced the start of the hot-fire campaign for Callisto's propulsion block.
