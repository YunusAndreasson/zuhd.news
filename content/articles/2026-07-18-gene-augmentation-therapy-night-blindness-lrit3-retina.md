Louisville — Adding one gene restored mice's night vision.

The disorder breaks the synaptic handoff between photoreceptors and downstream neurons; researchers added a working LRIT3 gene instead of editing it.

The approach worked only in mice; human trials for inherited retinal disorders remain years away.
