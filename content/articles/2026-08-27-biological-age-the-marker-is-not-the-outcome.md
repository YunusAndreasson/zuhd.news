A startup offered a reporter unproven rejuvenation injections.

Generation Lab won't disclose the drugs, making its safety and dosing impossible to verify.

Aging is not a registrable drug indication, so longevity products cannot go through a formal approval trial.

Its planned trial will charge participants to enroll, a model researchers call pay-to-play.
