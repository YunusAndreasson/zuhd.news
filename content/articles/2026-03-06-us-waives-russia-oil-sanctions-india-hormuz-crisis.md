US issued India a 30-day Russian oil waiver. The same Iran war that closed Hormuz forced the sanctions reversal: India imports 85% of its oil, and with that route shut, Russia is its only viable supplier. The waiver expires in April, forcing a renewal decision that could trigger a supply crisis for 1.4 billion people.
