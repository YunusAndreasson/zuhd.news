Armed men entered a hospital treating war casualties. Doctors Without Borders suspended non-critical services at Nasser Hospital in Khan Younis on January 20 after staff reported masked gunmen, suspected weapons transfers, and arbitrary arrests of patients inside the facility. The group continues emergency surgery but warned southern Gaza's largest medical center has lost its protected status.
