Beijing — China's 11 million annual graduates can't all find jobs. Beijing is subsidizing single-person companies built on AI platforms like OpenClaw, where one person can replace entire teams. The program has no stated enrollment cap, leaving unresolved whether AI micro-firms can absorb structural unemployment at scale.
