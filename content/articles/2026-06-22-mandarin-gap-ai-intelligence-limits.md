Canberra — AI can surface [China](country:CN)'s strategic communications at scale; too few Mandarin speakers exist to verify what it flags.

Automated collection widens the intelligence aperture but cannot replace the linguistic judgment that validates it.

[Australia](country:AU) has not disclosed how it plans to close the verification gap.
