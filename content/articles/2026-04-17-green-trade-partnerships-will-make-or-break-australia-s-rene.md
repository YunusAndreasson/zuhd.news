Canberra — Australia's renewable superpower targets lack signed trade contracts. The Lowy Institute argues these industries need offtake commitments from Asia-Pacific buyers; without them, the domestic build-out remains aspirational. Whether Japan, South Korea, or India will commit to the volumes Canberra's strategy requires remains unanswered.
