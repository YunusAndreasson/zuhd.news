Ankara — The pact never states whose nuclear deterrent applies.

It is the first mutual-defence pact with a military instrument among Muslim-majority states.

[Turkey](country:TR), [Pakistan](country:PK) and [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) treat an attack on one as an attack on all.

Egypt could join once "technical issues" are resolved, Turkey's foreign minister said.
