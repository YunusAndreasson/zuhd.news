San Francisco — A top AI conference expects 62,000 submissions, up from 490 a decade ago.

The International Conference on Learning Representations caps authors at 20 submissions and newcomers at 1.

Chair Jacob Andreas says authors submit large numbers of usually AI-generated papers, and only authors with an accepted paper may review.

The Association for Computational Linguistics applies its cap of 20 per person at its Budapest conference later this month.
