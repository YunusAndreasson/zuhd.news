New Delhi — Two election commissioners objected 14 times to [India](country:IN)'s voter purge.

The dissent implicates how 130 million names were struck from India's electoral rolls.

India's Election Commission is a three-member body, letting the chief election commissioner override objections and act alone on final decisions.

The commission says all decisions in the revision process, including the objected ones, had unanimous approval from all three officials.

The Supreme Court is reviewing voter notices in Delhi as opposition parties demand the chief's removal.
