Kyiv — Zelensky says Starshield cuts off anything above 161 km/h.

Ukrainian interceptors cannot reach Russian jet-powered drones in time, and some strikes got through.

The military says the limit is systematic, not random, and follows an October cap lifted on 24 October.

Trump has said he did not order the October restrictions, and the Pentagon declined to investigate.

[US](country:US) envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner brief Putin in Moscow, then meet Zelensky in Dublin.
