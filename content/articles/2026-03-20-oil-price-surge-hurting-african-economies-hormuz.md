Oil crossed $100 after Iran closed the Hormuz strait. The International Energy Agency called this the largest oil supply disruption in history — the strait moves 20% of world oil. Africa imports most refined fuel, and with no end to the closure announced, its poorest citizens face the sharpest price shock.
