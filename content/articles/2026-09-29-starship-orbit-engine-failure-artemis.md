Starbase — Starship reached orbit despite losing 1 of its 3 main Raptor engines.

NASA aims to land astronauts by 2028 with a Starship-based lander using the same engine system.

Starship deployed 26 Starlink satellites, then splashed down after 3 of 10 planned hours as a precaution.

"The real pressure is the moon," said Dean Sladen, an aerospace engineer at Accu Components.

The shutdown's cause remains undetermined, and NASA targets Artemis III as early as summer 2027.
