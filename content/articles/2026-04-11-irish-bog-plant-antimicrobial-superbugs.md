Dublin — An Irish bog plant kills antibiotic-resistant bacteria. Researchers identified antimicrobial compounds in a traditionally used folk remedy that remain effective against bacteria resistant to clinical antibiotics. Drug-resistant infections kill 1.27 million people annually; the antibiotic pipeline has yielded no new drug class since 1987.
