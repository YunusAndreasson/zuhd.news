San Francisco — Midjourney licensed Butterfly Network ultrasound hardware for $74M over 5 years to build a body-scan spa.

The devices achieve half-millimeter tissue resolution, but FDA approval is pending before the planned 2027 opening.

Midjourney has not disclosed how it will store, secure, or use the body data it collects.
