Tirana — Police fired rubber bullets at protesters, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

TASS says protesters demand [Albania](country:AL)'s Prime Minister Edi Rama resign and opposition leader Sali Berisha backs them.

TASS, citing a news portal, says unrest began 13 October at the education ministry and spread to parliament and other buildings.

TASS does not say how Rama has responded to the demand that he resign.
