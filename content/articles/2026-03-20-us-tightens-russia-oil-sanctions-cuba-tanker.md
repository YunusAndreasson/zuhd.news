A Russian tanker delivered diesel to Cuba this month. Washington amended its Russian oil waiver to bar Cuba, which imports 60% of its energy and has suffered widespread blackouts. A second Russian tanker, the Anatoly Kolodkin, carrying 730,000 barrels of crude, is now sailing toward Cuba.
