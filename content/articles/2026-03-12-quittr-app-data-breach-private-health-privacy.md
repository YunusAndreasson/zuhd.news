Quittr exposed masturbation habits of hundreds of thousands of users. The leaked data included ages, masturbation frequency, and emotional responses to pornography; some users were minors, and the app had known of the vulnerability for months before 404 Media could name it. HIPAA does not cover most consumer health apps, leaving users of addiction tools with no legal protection against intimate data exposure.
