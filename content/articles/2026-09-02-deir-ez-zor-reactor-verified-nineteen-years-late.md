Vienna — International Atomic Energy Agency inspectors confirmed a reactor at Deir el-Zour.

Confirmation came nineteen years after [Israel](country:IL) bombed the site on suspicion alone.

Inspectors need years of ground access, while a strike needs none, so confirmation can't retroactively justify it.

[Syria](country:SY)'s new government has pledged any nuclear material found stays under international safeguards.
