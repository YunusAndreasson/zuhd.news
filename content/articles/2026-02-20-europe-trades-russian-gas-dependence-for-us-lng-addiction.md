US liquefied natural gas now supplies 57% of EU LNG imports. Shipments quadrupled from 21 billion cubic metres in 2021 to 81 billion in 2025, and long-term contracts could push that to 80% by 2030. The EU banned Russian gas imports in January, but analysts warn the bloc has traded one energy dependence for another with geopolitical strings attached.
