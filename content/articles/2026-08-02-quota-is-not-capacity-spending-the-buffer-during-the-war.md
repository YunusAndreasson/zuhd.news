Vienna — OPEC+ hiked output by 188,000 barrels daily.

Seven members, led by [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) and [Russia](country:RU), agreed to a September increase completing a 1.65-million-barrel cut first made in 2023.

The [UAE](country:AE)'s May exit adds complexity to future quota talks, the group said.
