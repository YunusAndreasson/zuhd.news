US sanctioned 4 Rwandan generals for backing M23 rebels. Rwanda's army helped M23 seize Goma and Bukavu, with the US holding back until investigators found mass graves near Uvira this week. The US spared President Paul Kagame, leaving Rwanda's Congo strategy intact.
