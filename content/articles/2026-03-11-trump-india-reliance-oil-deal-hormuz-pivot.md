Trump claimed a "$300 billion deal" with India's Reliance Industries on Truth Social. Reliance will back a refinery at Port of Brownsville, Texas — the first new US refinery in 50 years, designed to process American shale oil exclusively. Element Fuels, the project developer, estimated construction at $3-4 billion in 2024; no groundbreaking date has been announced.
