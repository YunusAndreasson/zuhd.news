Trenton — $5.5 billion settles 69,000 talc cancer claims.

The deal covers 99.75% of claims in New Jersey court but includes no finding or admission that talc caused cancer, J&J said.

The company sold talc powder abroad until 2023, 3 years after halting [US](country:US) sales.
