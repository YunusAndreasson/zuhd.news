Dhaka — Bangladesh confirmed a deal for [China](country:CN)'s J-10CE jets.

Beijing gains a third South Asian buyer and a live combat reference.

Dhaka cited the jet's reported combat record against Rafales in May's brief [Pakistan](country:PK)-[India](country:IN) clash.

The purchase needs finance-ministry approval this fiscal year, or it rolls into the next budget cycle.
