Bangkok — IMF sees [Kuwait](country:KW) shrinking 16.7% as world grows 3.1%.

Only the Middle East and North Africa is forecast to contract in 2026, by 2%.

The fund assumes the Strait of Hormuz reopens only in March 2027, stranding Gulf exporters' crude until then.

"The economy has been resilient, but that resilience is reaching its limits," said IMF chief economist Silvana Tenreyro.

The IMF's baseline has pre-war shipping conditions returning only in October 2027.
