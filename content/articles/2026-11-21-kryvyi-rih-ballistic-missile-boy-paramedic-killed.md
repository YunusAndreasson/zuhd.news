Kryvyi Rih — Tykhon, 7, and a 20-year-old paramedic were killed.

60 people were wounded, 6 of them children, and 25 apartment blocks damaged, officials said.

[Russia](country:RU) fired 2 ballistic missiles, then 3 more rockets hit doctors and rescuers responding, city head Oleksandr Vilkul wrote.

"Most of his life was lived during a war like this," Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy wrote of Tykhon.

Eight of 28 hospitalised patients remain in serious condition, including a 4-year-old boy.
