Houston — Four volunteers left NASA's Mars habitat after 378 days.

NASA wants to know what it takes for astronauts to stay healthy, productive and connected.

The crew grew crops in simulated Martian soil and handled communication delays that mimic interplanetary lag.

The crew now faces two weeks of post-mission evaluations and debriefing with NASA scientists.
