China — The spaceplane's design nearly matches the [US](country:US) X-37B.

The Defense Intelligence Agency confirmed the long-circulating image in a new unclassified report.

The Chinese craft has payload-bay doors, tricycle landing gear and a tail extension like the X-37B's, but a longer wing chord.

It is unclear when Maxar took the image, and neither it nor the Space Force commented in 2024.
