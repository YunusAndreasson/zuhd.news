Dahshur — Bowed hand bones point to trained archers, not ceremony.

Researchers reexamined five Middle Kingdom royal mummies, finding forearm and palm bone growth consistent with regular bow and dagger use around 3,800 years ago.

Other researchers caution similar bone changes could stem from unrelated repetitive labor, not weapons training.
