Washington — 5 supervision regions will replace 12 reserve bank presidents' oversight.

Bowman, the Fed's top bank regulator, cited examiners' slow action before Silicon Valley Bank collapsed.

Each region gets a leader charged with all supervision of [US](country:US) banks, though Reserve Bank staff still examine.

Later this year the Fed will consider updating the asset thresholds that trigger bank rules.
