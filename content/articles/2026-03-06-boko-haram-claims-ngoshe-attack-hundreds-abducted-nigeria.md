Boko Haram abducted hundreds in Ngoshe, Borno State. Nigeria's military had redeployed troops to Zamfara State to counter bandits, leaving Borno exposed to the 15-year insurgency. The faction threatened attacks on Maiduguri and Abuja, vowing to enslave the captives.
