London — A laser trick doubled the dephasing energy limit.

Conventional accelerators need kilometers of tunnel, which compact plasma versions aim to replace.

Plasma waves normally outrun the electrons they push — a flying-focus pulse moves its focal point to match them instead.

Whether flying-focus pulses scale to higher electron energies remains untested.
