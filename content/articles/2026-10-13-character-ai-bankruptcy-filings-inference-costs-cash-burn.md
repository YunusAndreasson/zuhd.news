United States — Character.ai was losing $8-9 million a month by August.

Subscription revenue peaked at $2.8 million a month, and all revenue at $5.3 million, its bankruptcy filings say.

Compute cost $13.8 million a month in 2024 and $10 million in 2025, and the company added ads and simplified its model.

"Character's impressive technology is expensive to operate," CEO Colin Adams said in an affidavit.

Disney has reportedly offered $20 million to license characters, after sending a cease-and-desist letter last year.
