Johannesburg — Lucky Montana, ex-head of [South Africa](country:ZA)'s Passenger Rail Agency, received R685,000 from a train-deal accounting firm.

Tax records show the firm funnelled R32m from the locomotives deal, with the South African Revenue Service finding the payments undeclared.

Montana calls them bridging loans and is contesting the tax assessment.
