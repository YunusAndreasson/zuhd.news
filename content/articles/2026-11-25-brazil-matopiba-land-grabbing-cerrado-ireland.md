São Desidério — Land grabbers took 7 million hectares of Matopiba, lawyers say.

Farmers and gunmen threaten traditional and quilombola communities across [Brazil](country:BR)'s main farming frontier.

Fraudulent land titles are registered at notary offices, then legalised by state-court rulings that favour large farmers over communities waiting decades.

A case open for more than 50 years in São Desidério's court covers 5.6 million hectares of public land.
