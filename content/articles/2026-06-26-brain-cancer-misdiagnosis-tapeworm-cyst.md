Madrid — CT scans led doctors to diagnose cancer; MRI found Taenia solium tapeworm larvae instead.

His construction coworkers came from Taenia-endemic regions; the patient had not traveled abroad.

Cysticercosis causes more epilepsy than any other single infection; he recovered on antiparasitic drugs.
