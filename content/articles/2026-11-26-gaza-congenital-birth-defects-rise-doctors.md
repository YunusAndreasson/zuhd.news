Gaza — Recorded birth defects rose 75% between 2022 and 2025.

Gaza's health ministry recorded 184 cases in 2022, 322 in 2025 and 260 by September 2026.

Obstetrician Ashraf Qeshta of Nasser Medical Complex attributes the rise to toxic gases, missile remnants, malnutrition and displacement caused by the war.

The ministry says these figures alone do not establish the rate or the causes.

Proving a cause needs advanced testing and specialists the ministry lacks, Qeshta said.
