China threatened confrontation over Taiwan. Foreign Minister Wang Yi told the Munich Security Conference that any US effort to split China through Taiwan would "very likely lead to a confrontation between China and the United States." Wang met US Secretary of State Marco Rubio on the sidelines but neither government disclosed what they discussed. Wang accused Washington of "plotting to split China" while saying Beijing was "prepared to deal with various risks."
