Tehran — Iran's foreign ministry confirms serious debate on quitting the nuclear treaty.

Russian state news agency TASS reported spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei said [Iran](country:IR) remains a treaty party for now.

Baghaei said the debate is in "expert circles" and that Iran "has come under attack."

The foreign ministry will abide by the decision of the competent authorities, which Baghaei did not name.
