Bogotá — Three infant girls died from the practice in 2007.

[Colombia](country:CO)'s new ban grew out of Emberá Chamí communities after that case; the health ministry logged 91 further cases in 2023 alone.

No consolidated national registry exists, leaving enforcement dependent on hospitals reporting future cases.
