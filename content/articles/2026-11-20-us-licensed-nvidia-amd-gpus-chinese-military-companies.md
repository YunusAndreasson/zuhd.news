Washington — Commerce licensed Nvidia and AMD chips to military-listed [Chinese](country:CN) firms.

Alibaba and Tencent, both on the Pentagon's military-company list, were approved.

House chair Brian Mast revealed Commerce approved 879,000 of 3.4 million requested chips, refusing Alibaba's 610,000-chip order.

Many approved orders never shipped, likely because Beijing withheld import approval while its own chipmakers improve.

Alibaba's CEO says its 20-gigawatt data-centre target may shrink under the new ban on remote chip access.
