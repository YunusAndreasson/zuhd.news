Cape Town hit 43°C for a 3rd consecutive day in early March. March is the start of autumn in Cape Town, where historical average highs sit around 26°C; 43°C is roughly 17 degrees above the seasonal norm. Cape Town nearly ran out of water entirely in 2018 — the same climate trajectory now delivers 43°C autumn days.
