United Kingdom — Clips4Sale hid 392 of creator Sophia Truee's 506 videos.

She films herself talking alone, and the platform did not say what to change.

The [UK](country:GB)'s Crime and Policing Act makes possessing or publishing choking porn punishable by up to 5 years.

Clips4Sale says the purge is precautionary and does not mean the clips are prohibited.

Clips4Sale will restore clips to UK buyers only after its own reviewers clear them.
