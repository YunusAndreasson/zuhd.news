Islamabad — Relief begins at midnight Wednesday nationwide.

Motorcycle and rickshaw owners get Rs100 off each liter of fuel.

The subsidy is funded by the same Rs114-per-litre levy [Pakistan](country:PK) charges, capped at 20 to 30 liters monthly.

The central bank must repay fuel stations within 24 hours.
