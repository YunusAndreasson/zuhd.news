Benin City — Gunmen shot 1 man at Peter Obi's rally.

Gunmen suspected to be political thugs killed 6 people at a Nigeria Democratic Congress rally in neighbouring Delta State days earlier.

Police say the gunmen's black Toyota Hilux turned back at officers posted at the airport exit gate, and they fired.

Aimionkhale Micheal took gunshot wounds to the thighs, and Edo police have ordered a criminal investigation without naming a suspect.
