Adiala — Imprisoned ex-premier Imran Khan says his health is deteriorating.

He is appealing to the judiciary for medical treatment, for himself and his wife Bushra Bibi.

Lawyer Salman Safdar, who met him at the jail on 2 and 11 November, shared the letter on X.

"Today, I ask only that my wife and I be treated," Khan wrote.

The letter leaves the next move to three courts: the Federal Constitutional Court, Supreme Court and Islamabad High Court.
