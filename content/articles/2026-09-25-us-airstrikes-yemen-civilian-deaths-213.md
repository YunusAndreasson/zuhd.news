Saada — 3 [US](country:US) airstrikes killed 68 sleeping migrants.

Mwatana for Human Rights documented at least 213 civilians killed in 46 incidents under two presidents.

Seven Biden-era incidents caused 27 casualties, while Trump's seven-week Operation Rough Rider caused 549, including 50 children.

Families have received almost no compensation and no acknowledgement from Washington, the report says.
