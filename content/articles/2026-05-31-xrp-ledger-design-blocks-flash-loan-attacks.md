San Francisco — Flash loan attacks have cost decentralized finance hundreds of millions of dollars.

A flash loan borrows millions, executes a manipulation, and repays — all inside one transaction, with no capital at risk.

A draft XRP Ledger amendment forbids intra-transaction calls, blocking the borrow-manipulate-repay sequence by design.
