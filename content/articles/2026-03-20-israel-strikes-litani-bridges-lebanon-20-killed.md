Israel struck both Litani River bridges, killing 20 Lebanese. The bridges are the primary civilian escape routes from south Lebanon — Israel says they were used by Hezbollah commanders moving between north and south. The dead include an Al-Manar TV journalist and his wife — Hezbollah has not yet announced its response.
