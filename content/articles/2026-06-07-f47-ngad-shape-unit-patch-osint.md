Washington — Boeing hid the F-47 planform in its program patch — the same trick used for the Bird of Prey in the 1990s.

Analysts matched the silhouette to Area 51 footage: canards, swept wings, no tails — a shape that defeats low-frequency radar.

The US Air Force has released no imagery; the demonstrator's dimensions remain classified.
