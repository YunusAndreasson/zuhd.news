Winchester — A jury convicted 4 men of raping a drugged Joanne Young.

It is the first conviction in England for organised drug-facilitated sexual assault.

Her then-husband Philip Young drugged her over 13 years, advertised online, and has pleaded guilty to 48 offences.

The men told the court they believed she consented and was pretending to sleep, when she was heavily sedated.

The National Crime Agency has identified more than 270 people linked to one online forum and its successors.
