Edwards Air Force Base — An F-22 pilot sent a drone new orders mid-flight.

The [US](country:US) Air Force plans at least 500 collaborative combat aircraft by 2032.

The pilot sent an updated mission plan over a tactical data link, and the drone's onboard autonomy flew the maneuvers.

Neither the Air Force nor Anduril said how the F-22 was configured for the test.

The first drones enter operational service around the end of the decade.
