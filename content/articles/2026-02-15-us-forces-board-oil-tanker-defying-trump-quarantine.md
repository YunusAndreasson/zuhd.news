United States forces boarded a sanctioned tanker in the Indian Ocean. The Panamanian-flagged Veronica III carried nearly 2 million barrels of Venezuelan crude and fled port on 3 January, hours after forces captured former President Nicolás Maduro. The boarding is the first enforcement of President Donald Trump's quarantine order, which aimed to cut off Venezuela's oil revenue.
