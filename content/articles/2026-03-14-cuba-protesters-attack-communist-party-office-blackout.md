Cubans torched a Communist Party office over power blackouts. In January, the US cut off Venezuela's oil shipments to Cuba after Trump captured Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro, removing the island's last reliable fuel source. Trump has threatened tariffs on any country that sells Cuba oil, leaving no replacement supplier and no power restoration timeline.
