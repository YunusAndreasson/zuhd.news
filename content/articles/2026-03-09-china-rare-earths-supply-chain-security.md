China's Central Party School now teaches health officials about rare earths. Hundreds of mid-level cadres from unrelated fields are being trained in supply chain security; China refines most of the world's neodymium, required in every electric vehicle motor. As Washington restricts chip exports to China, Beijing is embedding mineral leverage across every ministry that could one day restrict the materials those chips require.
