Mexico — Miguel Ángel Félix Gallardo died at 80 after 37 years in custody.

He founded the Guadalajara Cartel, remembered as [Mexico](country:MX)'s first, and died on 15 November.

The corruption that protected him is still in court, as the [US](country:US) prosecutes Mexico's former security chief.

Crime analysts question how much of the story that he carved Mexico into territories holds up.

A sitting governor is also under indictment, and neither case is closed.
