United States — Over a dozen miners had rewards diverted.

Owners paid for electricity while the rewards went to someone else.

Luxor's Commander software let operators change machines' pool settings remotely, so one compromised tool redirected many customers' hash power to OCEAN.

CEO Nick Hansen called it a "sophisticated AI-enabled vector" but did not say how AI was used.

Luxor will reimburse customers, and the attacker's address held about 0.243 bitcoin, unspent, on Monday morning.
