Ulaanbaatar — About three-quarters of Mongolia's land is degraded.

Herders now face dzud losses that have become structural rather than seasonal.

These desertification summits carry no binding finance instrument, so [Mongolia](country:MN) spent its hosting leverage setting land-restoration targets instead.

Mongolia called the results "mixed," with no financing commitment yet attached to those targets.
