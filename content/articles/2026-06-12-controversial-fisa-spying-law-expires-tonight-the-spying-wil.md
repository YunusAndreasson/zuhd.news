Washington — US spying law lapsed; surveillance continues to 2027.

Existing court certifications issued in March 2026 authorize collection through March 2027 under the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act's transition provision, legal analysts said.

[US](country:US) Congress reconvenes June 23; if the law stays expired past March 2027, agencies revert to Executive Order 12333.
