Copenhagen — [Denmark](country:DK)'s parliament voted 109-0 for a US Greenland deal.

The [US](country:US) may build 2 military bases on an island of 57,000 people.

The pact also bars investors from outside the EU and NATO from strategic sectors, and it survives Greenland's independence.

Greenland's parliament passed it in early November with 16 votes and 7 abstentions.

The US Senate gets no vote, and the deal takes effect once Denmark notifies Washington.
