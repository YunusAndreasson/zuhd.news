New York — AI chiefs asked the Security Council for rules.

The [US](country:US) technology adviser Michael Kratsios told the same meeting Washington rejects global governance of AI.

OpenAI's Sam Altman asked for common standards to measure capabilities, assess risks, verify compliance and report incidents quickly.

Decisions on AI risks "cannot rest with a handful of companies or capitals," said [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar.

[China](country:CN)'s Xi Jinping is expected to discuss whether to regulate AI during a Washington visit this week.
