Glasgow — Chicxulub's hydrothermal system stayed hot for 8 million years.

That is at least 4 times longer than the 2 million years previously thought.

Potassium-argon dating of feldspar from a 1 km drill core showed heat from 66 to 58 million years ago.

The work does not show whether anything lived there, and microbes have colonised only 8 of 70 such craters.

Lead author Annemarie Pickersgill says larger impact basins elsewhere may have held habitable heat even longer.
