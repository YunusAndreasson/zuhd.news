Washington — Democrats won the [US](country:US) Senate, flipping Texas after 38 years.

Democrats courted voters disillusioned by Trump's unpopular war in [Iran](country:IR) and rising prices.

Roy Cooper won North Carolina and Annie Andrews took South Carolina, where Republicans won by nearly 18 points two years ago.

"I wasn't on the Ballot and if I was, we would have won IN A LANDSLIDE!" President Donald Trump wrote.

Keisha Lance Bottoms, the first Black woman elected a US state governor, succeeds Brian Kemp in January.
