OpenAI owes $100 billion with no path to profit. Training costs rise with each new model and most of its 900 million users pay nothing, leaving the company needing 15 times current revenue by 2030. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang called his company's $30 billion investment "the last" before an IPO, signaling private funding may have peaked.
