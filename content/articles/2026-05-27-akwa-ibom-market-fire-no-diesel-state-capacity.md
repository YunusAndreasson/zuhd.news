Uyo — 16 shops burned 106 metres from a fire station that had no diesel.

No diesel reached the station for 2 months, officials confirmed; [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Akwa Ibom state received N397 billion in Q1 2026.

Akwa Ibom has 3 functional fire trucks state-wide; diesel shortages are routine, fire service officials said.
