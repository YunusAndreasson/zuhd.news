Pakistani exporters face Rs50-per-kg surcharges from air cargo handlers. Air freight is Pakistan's only export route since Hormuz sea routes closed, giving cargo handlers monopoly pricing power. Karachi's customs authority has ordered carriers to halt retroactive surcharges on cargo that shipped before the war began.
