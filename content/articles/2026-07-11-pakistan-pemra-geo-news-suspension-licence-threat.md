Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s regulator fined Geo News Rs10 million.

The regulator upheld a 15-day suspension over depicting religious figures during Muharram, warning any repeat could cost Geo its license entirely.

The regulator also ordered all broadcasters to submit in-house editorial monitoring plans.
