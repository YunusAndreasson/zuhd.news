Suzhou — In 192 patients, ear defenders cut nausea or vomiting from 52% to 35%.

Anaesthetised patients appear to process theatre noise, which averaged 66 decibels in the study.

Noise may trigger a stress response that engages brainstem nausea pathways, though the team did not test that mechanism.

All patients also received standard anti-nausea medication, so the headphones worked on top of drugs.

Huayue Liu of Soochow University hopes to run a larger multi-site trial measuring noise exposure against nervous system activity.
