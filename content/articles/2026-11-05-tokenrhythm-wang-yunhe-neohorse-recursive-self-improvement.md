Beijing — TokenRhythm says its new model gained 22% on agent tests.

Founder Wang Yunhe previously led Huawei's Noah's Ark Lab and its Pangu model.

The model, built on Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Flash, trains on gaps between its answers and fused results from several frontier models.

Its computing and maths score fell 11%, from 59.51 to 52.96, in the company's own results.

The preview is in beta testing, and every score so far comes from TokenRhythm itself.
