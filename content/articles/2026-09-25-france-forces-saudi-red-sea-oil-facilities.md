Paris — [France](country:FR) sends soldiers and radars to guard Yanbu.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA) routes millions of barrels a day through Yanbu to avoid the Persian Gulf.

Repeated Houthi missile and drone attacks, plus suspected Iraqi militia strikes, prompted the deployment.

"Not to get us involved in any conflicts, but to protect this site," said President Emmanuel Macron.

Macron did not say how many troops, what equipment or when they arrive.
