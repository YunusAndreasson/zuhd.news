Seoul — [South Korea](country:KR)'s Samsung C&T posted a 74% profit jump.

The construction and trading arm of Samsung Group posted 915.5 billion won in profit, driven by steel and chemicals trading, it said.

Sales rose 19.7% to 11.99 trillion won for the April-June quarter, the company reported.
