Hanoi — [Vietnam](country:VN) becomes BrahMos's 3rd Southeast Asian buyer.

The Indo-Russian supersonic cruise missile now arms [Philippines](country:PH) and [Indonesia](country:ID); all 3 buyers face [China](country:CN)'s competing territorial claims in the South China Sea.

An Indian minister confirmed the deal; no delivery timeline was given.
