Cairo — [Egypt](country:EG)'s industrial power rates rose up to 18%.

The May 13 official tariff table added iron and 3 other heavy industries at 15-18% — excluded from the government's April announcement.

Fertilizer makers are pushing to export more output to offset costs, tightening domestic supply before summer planting.
