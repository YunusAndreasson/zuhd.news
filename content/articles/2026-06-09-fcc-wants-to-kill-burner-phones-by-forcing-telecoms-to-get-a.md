Washington — The Federal Communications Commission proposed mandatory ID for all phone plans.

Telecoms must store customer IDs and home addresses, ending anonymous prepaid phones.

Domestic-abuse survivors and journalists rely on untraced phones; the American Civil Liberties Union said the registry would outlast its anti-fraud purpose.
