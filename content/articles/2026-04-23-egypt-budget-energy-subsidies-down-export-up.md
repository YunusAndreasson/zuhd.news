Cairo — Interest payments consume nearly half of [Egypt](country:EG)'s total spending, Finance Ministry figures show. The draft 2026/27 budget slashes petroleum subsidies and redirects funds to export incentives. Health and education remain below half their constitutional floors, a mandate the government has not addressed.
