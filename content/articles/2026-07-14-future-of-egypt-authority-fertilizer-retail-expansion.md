Cairo — A military-linked body now sets fertilizer prices.

The authority undercuts market rates by up to 39%, after the government slashed subsidized fertilizer for citrus and other crops.

[Egypt](country:EG)'s farm ministry plays no role in the new scheme, as exports claim more of the supply.
