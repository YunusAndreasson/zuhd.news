Moscow — [Russia](country:RU) deported 63,500 foreigners in nine months, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

Bailiff service director Dmitry Aristov told TASS that is 1.5 times the same period of 2025.

Earlier reports counted 16,000 foreigners deported from Moscow alone in six months for violating entry or stay rules.

Nearly 64,000 were deported in all of 2025, and the report names no nationalities, grounds or destinations.
