Havana — Cuba's grid went dark for 30 consecutive hours. Trump's 3-month oil blockade starved a country where oil generates 80% of electricity, cutting neighborhoods to a few hours of daily power. Gasoline sells for $10 a litre on the black market; the blackouts will continue until Cuba finds a new oil supplier.
