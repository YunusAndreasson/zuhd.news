Garden Grove — GKN Aerospace's methyl methacrylate tank nears explosion.

A 7,000-gallon tank at a [US](country:US) aerospace plant overheated Thursday; tens of thousands have been evacuated for 2 consecutive days.

Caltech professor Joseph Shepherd warned of a "catastrophic" fireball; crews have found no way to stop the reaction.
