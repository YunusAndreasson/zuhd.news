Brussels — The Commission charged TikTok's feed design, not one video.

TikTok has 189 million EU users, and the content in question is legal.

Tapping "not interested" leaves diet and body-image videos in the feed, and refreshing helps only briefly, the Commission found.

TikTok called the Commission's earlier addictive-design findings false and meritless, and has not yet commented on this one.

TikTok can change its recommender system to avoid a fine of up to 6% of global turnover.
