United States — MNX lets traders bet on OpenAI's valuation with 3x leverage.

OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek and Moonshot are private, so traders hold futures, not shares.

MNX's contracts expire at the end of 2027 and carry no recurring funding fees, unlike Bitget's perpetual futures.

Larger exchanges OKX and Bitget already offer similar contracts.

MNX does not serve [US](country:US) customers and plans more markets tied to the AI economy.
