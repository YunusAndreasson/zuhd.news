Baniyas — [Syria](country:SY) arrested Ali Kayali, Turkish state broadcaster TRT World reported.

The Syrian Arab News Agency calls him main suspect in 2013 killings of hundreds of civilians.

A UN inquiry found forces shelled and entered al-Bayda on 2 May 2013, then Ras al-Nabaa, with no armed opposition present.

The General Intelligence Directorate says it caught him fleeing across the border, and names no trial court.
