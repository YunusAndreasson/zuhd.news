Scientists solved why some supernovae outshine entire galaxies. A December 2024 supernova revealed a magnetar, a rapidly spinning neutron star that hurled charged particles into the expanding cloud and boosted luminosity 100-fold. Published in Nature on Wednesday, the findings confirm a 2010 hypothesis that had awaited direct evidence for 16 years.
