Uyuk River Valley — Tetrahydrocannabinol survived in a 2,800-year-old Siberian's hair.

It is the earliest direct proof of psychoactive cannabis use, a study in Science reports.

Excavators found over 1,400 cannabis seeds among Iron Age remains in a royal mound ringed by human and horse sacrifices.

"This is ultimately complete luck and chance," said Gino Caspari, the archaeologist who led the study.
