Paris — 164 people were arrested at French high school protests.

The unrest hits a day before a nationwide strike testing Prime Minister Lecornu's fragile government.

Students have blockaded lycées since last week over overcrowded classes and teacher shortages, spreading across [France](country:FR) from Paris to Lille.

Education Minister Edouard Geffray accused La France Insoumise of hijacking protests Mélenchon called a racist crackdown.

Teachers' unions expect 40% of primary staff absent Tuesday as the strike and blockades continue.
