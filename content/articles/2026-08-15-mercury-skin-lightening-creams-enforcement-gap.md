New York — Testers found mercury at 27,000 parts per million.

The creams cause kidney damage and cross the placenta, researchers say.

Nearly one in three products tested from 2009 to 2022 contained mercury, and few labels listed it as an ingredient.

New York pulled over 3,000 units from shelves since 2017, but surveillance remains resource-constrained, officials said.
