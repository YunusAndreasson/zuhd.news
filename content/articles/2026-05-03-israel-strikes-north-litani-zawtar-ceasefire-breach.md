Zawtar — [Israel](country:IL) bombed the Zawtar villages north of the Litani Thursday, past the April ceasefire line, after Hezbollah drones struck Israeli positions on the southern bank.

Israeli forces remain at Taybeh in [Lebanon](country:LB); a retired general said the advance is stalled by drone ambushes.

Displacement orders have sent Nabatieh civilians toward Saida.
