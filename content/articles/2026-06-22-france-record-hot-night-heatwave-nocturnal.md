Paris — The city hit 24.2°C overnight, a near-decade record.

WHO said heat killed over 200,000 Europeans in 4 years; sustained overnight warmth above 23°C leaves no recovery window for the body.

French weather authorities placed 49 departments under red warning as Bordeaux faces 42°C and nights above 23°C through the week.
