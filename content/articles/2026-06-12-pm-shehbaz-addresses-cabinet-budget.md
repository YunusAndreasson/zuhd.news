Islamabad — Pakistan passed its third consecutive IMF-constrained budget.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s inflation fell from 38% to single digits under two years of austerity, PM Shehbaz Sharif said; the US-Iran war pushed prices and the policy rate back up.

The rate, cut from 22.5% to 11%, was raised again; budget targets now determine the next cut.
