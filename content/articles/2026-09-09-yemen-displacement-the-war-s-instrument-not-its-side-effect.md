Sana'a — Some families have fled Yemen's war five times.

They arrive in governorates already sheltering more displaced people than residents.

Saudi-backed forces attacked Ansarallah positions after Ansarallah struck [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s energy facilities, displacing 18,500 people.

Humanitarian agencies have not said whether they can fund shelter for the newly displaced.
