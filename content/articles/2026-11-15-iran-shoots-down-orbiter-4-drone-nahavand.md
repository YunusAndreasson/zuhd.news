Nahavand — [Iran](country:IR) says it downed an Orbiter 4 drone, state-run Mehr News Agency reported.

The Orbiter series is built by [Israel](country:IL)'s Aeronautics Ltd, though Mehr's headline calls this one American.

Iran's army air defence says the drone needs no satellite-navigation guidance, and that downing it shows Iran's electronic warfare capability.

Mehr's report carries no US or Israeli comment, and the army says the wreckage is under examination.
