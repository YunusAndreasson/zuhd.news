Brasília — Land-use emissions fell nearly a third since 2000.

Enforcement policy in [Brazil](country:BR) and [Indonesia](country:ID) mainly drove the global decline, researchers say.

The figure is net — gross clearing minus regrowth — so a country's forests can still shrink.

National inventories and independent estimates of the land sink still disagree, complicating climate negotiations.
