[US](country:US) courts let Israeli-American families sue the Palestinian Authority under an anti-terrorism law; [Israel](country:IL) holds no equivalent terrorism designation.

Abu Siyam's family, whose son was killed in the West Bank in 2026, has no equivalent federal claim.
