Moscow — Russia's crude shipments hit their highest since late July.

[Ukraine](country:UA)'s campaign squeezes Russian fuel supply at home while adding crude to world markets.

With refineries knocked out, producers divert crude meant for them into export streams.

[Russia](country:RU)'s cabinet said Deputy Prime Minister Alexander Novak wants refineries restored fast and fuel imports to continue.
