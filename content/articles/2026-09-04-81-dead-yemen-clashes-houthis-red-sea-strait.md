Mokha — 81 killed in a day of Houthi advances.

The push targets Bab al-Mandeb, the strait now carrying rerouted [Saudi](country:SA) oil exports.

Houthi fighters seized hills above the strait after [Iran](country:IR)'s blockade of Hormuz forced Gulf oil through the Red Sea.

[Yemen](country:YE)'s government has sent reinforcements to Taiz to stop the advance reaching the coast.
