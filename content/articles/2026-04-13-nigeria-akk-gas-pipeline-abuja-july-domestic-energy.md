Abuja — Nigeria's capital may get piped gas by July. The 614-km, $2.8 billion AKK pipeline—conceived in 2008—was delayed by funding gaps and a technically complex River Niger crossing. Nigeria plans to scale to 3-GW export capacity; whether domestic supply reaches northern power plants first remains unresolved.
