Perth — Injecting CO2 into iron-rich rock could yield hydrogen.

The same reaction that produces the fuel also mineralizes the carbon dioxide.

Researchers are testing the process on iron-rich rock formations deep beneath [Australia](country:AU)'s west.

It remains unclear how much of the rock formation is suitable for the process.
