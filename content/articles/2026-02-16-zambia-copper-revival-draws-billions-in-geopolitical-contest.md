$12 billion has poured into Zambia's mining sector since 2022. Africa's second-largest copper producer raised output 8% last year to 890,000 metric tons as demand from electric vehicles and data centres turns the metal into a geopolitical prize — the United States launched a $12 billion critical-minerals initiative while China holds stakes in Zambia's largest mines and smelters. KoBold Metals, backed by Bill Gates and Jeff Bezos, begins sinking shafts this year on a $2.3 billion project designed to produce 300,000 tons annually while over 70% of Zambia's 21 million people remain in poverty.
