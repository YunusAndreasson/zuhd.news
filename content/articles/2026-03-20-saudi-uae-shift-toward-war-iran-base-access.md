Riyadh opened a 2nd air base to US forces. The shift comes after Iran hit UAE and Qatari infrastructure, with the UAE intercepting 338 missiles and 1,740 drones since late February. The UAE told US Secretary of State Marco Rubio it is ready for a 9-month war, putting no pressure on Washington to negotiate.
