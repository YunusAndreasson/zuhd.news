Singapore — Asia tops crypto volumes, US draws institutions.

The 2026 Global Digital Asset Adoption Index ranks Asia first in stablecoin flows, exchange volume, and on-chain activity; US regulatory clarity attracts institutional capital but not transaction volume.

Whether Asian volume or US institutional scale defines the next cycle is unresolved.
