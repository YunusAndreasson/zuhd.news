Kathmandu — Deportees from the US have nowhere to land.

Ethnic-Nepali refugees fled [Bhutan](country:BT)'s ethnic cleansing decades ago and resettled in the US, which now says it has no obligation to take them back.

Deportation manufactures a second statelessness for people the world once called protected.
