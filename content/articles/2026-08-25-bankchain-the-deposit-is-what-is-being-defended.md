Washington — 39 state banking associations announced a chain with no builder.

A stablecoin that pays no interest still drains the cheap deposits bank lending depends on.

The "BankChain Alliance" aims to launch by next year but has yet to hire the technology partner to build it.

The associations still must find a vendor before the network can move a single deposit.
