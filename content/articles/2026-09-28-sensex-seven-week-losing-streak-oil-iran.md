Mumbai — Sensex falls 900+ points, extending a seventh week of declines.

[India](country:IN) imports most of its crude, so pricier oil strains its import bill and rupee.

Brent crude rose 2.27% to $106.70 a barrel after Trump refused to rule out further [US](country:US) strikes on [Iran](country:IR).

Trump also said Sunday he expects the war with Iran to end soon.

This week's US inflation, manufacturing and employment data could add further volatility to markets.
