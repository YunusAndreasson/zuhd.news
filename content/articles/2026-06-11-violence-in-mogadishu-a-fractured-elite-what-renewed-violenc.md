Mogadishu — Government troops stormed opposition leader Khaire's home Wednesday.

[Somalia](country:SO)'s parliament extended the president's term by one year in March; Khaire left the airport compound to rally in his clan stronghold.

Both sides blame the other for firing first; no election date has been set.
