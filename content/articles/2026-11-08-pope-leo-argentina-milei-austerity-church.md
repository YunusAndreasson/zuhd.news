Buenos Aires — Pope Leo lands where 32.3% of Argentines live in poverty.

The Catholic Church has criticised President Javier Milei's austerity, which cut health spending 48% in real terms.

Official data put first-half 2026 poverty at 15 million people, as Milei cuts social spending to reach a budget surplus.

"The pope belongs to all Argentines," said Karina Milei, the president's sister, who calls the visit purely religious.

Leo meets Milei, visits a prison, then leaves for [Peru](country:PE) on Wednesday.
