Washington — Six Chinese firms allegedly copied US AI models.

Three [US](country:US) security agencies name [China](country:CN)'s DeepSeek, Alibaba and Moonshot as culprits.

Distillation, training one model on another's outputs, is common but usually barred only by terms of service.

The advisory names no forensic method that can prove distillation from a model's behavior alone.
