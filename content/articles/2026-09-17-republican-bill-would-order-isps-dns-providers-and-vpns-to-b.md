Washington — A bill would order DNS to block sites.

DNS and VPNs are not content hosts, but tools people use to bypass censorship.

Courts could order any resolver or VPN with 100,000 [US](country:US) users to block a site on a lower evidence standard.

Providers get 14 days to object, but judges can shorten that for live broadcasts.
