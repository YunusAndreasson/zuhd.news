Abuja — [Nigeria](country:NG)'s top court freed FPSO Tamara Tokoni's crude cargo.

A five-judge panel ruled the loan dispute was contractual, not admiralty, so lower courts lacked jurisdiction.

General Hydrocarbons still owes First Bank $112,100 from an October award; AMCON's separate claim was struck out in December.
