London — Swapping two metals' positions changed storage capacity.

In early lab tests, it could replace heavy pressurized tanks with a rechargeable electrode.

Normal hydrogen storage needs 700-bar gas tanks or cryogenic liquid, while this drives hydrogen into an electrode at room pressure.

One material relies on cobalt, mostly mined in the [Democratic Republic of Congo](country:CD).
