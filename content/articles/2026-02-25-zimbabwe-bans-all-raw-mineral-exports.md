Even minerals already in transit must stay in Zimbabwe. The government banned all raw mineral and lithium concentrate exports to force in-country processing of Africa's largest lithium reserves, most mined by Chinese firms. Chinese miners shipped 1.1 million tonnes of concentrate to China last year and must now build full processing capacity locally.
