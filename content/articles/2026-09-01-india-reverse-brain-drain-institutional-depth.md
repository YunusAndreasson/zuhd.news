Bengaluru — [India](country:IN) will pay 120 returning scientists $520,000 each.

Fellowships alone rarely reverse the 73% of emigres who never come back.

Returnees cite lab equipment, reliable procurement and PhD-student quality as the real gap money can't close.

China paired funding with building labs first; India's scheme has not yet.
