Ljubljana — Slovenia rationed fuel on election day Sunday. Cross-border drivers from neighboring states stripped stations dry, exploiting Slovenia's open Schengen borders as Iran war disruptions drove regional hoarding. The government deployed the army to restore supply and opened a criminal inquiry into state distributor Petrol, whose management must now answer for why reserves were not maintained.
