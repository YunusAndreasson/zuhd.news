London — A single chip now reads light's polarization.

That could tell real tissue from plastic, or a real face from a photo.

Ordinary cameras record only intensity through three colour filters, discarding the wavelength and polarization data that reveal material and stress.

The sensor is a lab demonstration, and no commercial product has yet been built from it.
