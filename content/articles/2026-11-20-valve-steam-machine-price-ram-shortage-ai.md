Bellevue — Valve says its 2TB Steam Machine will be especially constrained.

Valve cannot say how many it can build or at what price.

Memory and storage costs keep climbing, and analysts say Valve lacks the supplier priority Nintendo, Sony and Microsoft hold.

Valve says no waitlist remains for the 512GB model, and purchase invites have reached the remaining 2TB reservation holders.

Valve plans a Steam Machine availability update in January.
