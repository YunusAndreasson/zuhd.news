Cairo — Omar Nourain, 21, survived Sudan's war and died in Cairo.

The UN refugee agency cut nonemergency care for [Egypt](country:EG)'s 1.5 million Sudanese refugees in 2025 after receiving under 30% of required funding; a suburban clinic now logs 3-4 suicide attempts per shift.

No donor has committed replacement funding; the clinic runs on volunteers alone.
