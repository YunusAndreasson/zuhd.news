Riyadh — Saudi defences downed 2 Houthi drones aimed at the capital.

The Houthis' July blockade of [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) now reaches the capital, not just tankers.

The coalition also shot down 2 ballistic missiles aimed at Khamis Mushait, home to an air base, its spokesperson said.

Fudan University's Zhang Chuchu says Turkey and Pakistan are unlikely to intervene under the Mecca defence pact.

Houthi politburo member Hezam Alasad calls the attacks a rehearsal for full-scale escalation.
