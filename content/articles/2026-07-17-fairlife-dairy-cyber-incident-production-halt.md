Chicago — A cyber incident stopped Fairlife's production lines.

The billion-dollar dairy brand, which topped $1 billion in retail sales in 2022, suspended output at its Michigan, New York and Arizona plants after the intrusion.

Fairlife has not said when manufacturing will resume or what data or systems were affected.
