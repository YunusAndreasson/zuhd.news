Paris — Europe's €100 billion fighter programme is cancelled.

[France](country:FR) and [Germany](country:DE) each shielded their national champion — Dassault or Airbus — blocking the workshare and intellectual property deal the programme required.

Both countries now develop separate, smaller programmes; no joint European air combat programme remains.
