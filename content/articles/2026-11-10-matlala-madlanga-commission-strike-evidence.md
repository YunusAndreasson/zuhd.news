Pretoria — Matlala confirms paying R550,000 to a police general.

The R228-million Medicare24 police tender led to charges against 12 senior officers.

His lawyer says a March prison interview was confidential, which would remove investigator Bongani Dlamini's notes on payments to Sibiya.

Evidence leader Mahlape Sello said the session was formally meant to secure Matlala's statement.

Matlala testifies again Wednesday, and Chairperson Mbuyiseli Madlanga awaits an affidavit seeking to expunge the notes.
