New Delhi — Congress says 130 million names were deleted from [India](country:IN)'s voter rolls.

It wants Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar removed and eligible voters restored.

Congress plans marches, rallies and village meetings from 10 October to 26 November, with workers identifying deleted genuine voters.

Congress general secretary KC Venugopal called the Election Commission "the cheating commission of India."

Opposition parties rally at Ramlila Maidan on 1 November, and Congress has invited two estranged allies.
