New Haven — A single-payer model saves money and lives together.

Yale researchers project $1.04 trillion cut and 114,174 fewer [US](country:US) deaths yearly.

The model attributes savings to lower drug prices, less billing overhead, and fewer costly emergency-room visits from deferred care.

The preprint has not yet completed peer review.
