Washington — Every departing flight in the [US](country:US) stopped Friday.

Inbound international flights land only case by case, so connections collapse far beyond American airports.

A communication fault hit every Air Route Traffic Control Center, the regional facilities that direct planes between airports.

Two law enforcement officials said preliminary reports indicate this was not a cyberattack.

The Federal Aviation Administration has not named a cause or said when departures resume.
