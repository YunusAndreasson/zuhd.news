Ithaca — Records show prosecutors declined Cornell police's additional evidence.

The district attorney now says the evidence he reviewed made charges impossible.

On 25 November 2024, campus police told his office of further interviews, and staff said charges would not be pursued.

District Attorney Matthew Van Houten says the additional records were "not pertinent to our analysis."

None of the accused men, who deny wrongdoing, has been charged, and Attorney General Letitia James now holds charging power.
