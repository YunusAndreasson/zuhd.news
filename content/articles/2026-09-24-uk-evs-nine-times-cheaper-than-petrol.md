London — Off-peak EV charging costs 2.3p a mile, against 21p for diesel.

Diesel has risen 38% since February to £1.96 a litre, government figures show.

Oil above $100 a barrel lifts pump prices, while the electricity unit rate under the price cap rises under 1% in October.

Only 6% of [Britain](country:GB)'s 35 million cars are battery EVs, and petrol still makes up 55%.

Electricity unit rates under the price cap are expected to jump 20% in January 2027.
