A French soldier died in a drone strike in Iraqi Kurdistan Thursday. France has troops there training Kurdish forces — a coalition targeted by pro-Iranian drones since the US attacked Iran last month. Italian forces withdrew from a nearby Erbil base; French President Emmanuel Macron insists France's role is strictly defensive.
