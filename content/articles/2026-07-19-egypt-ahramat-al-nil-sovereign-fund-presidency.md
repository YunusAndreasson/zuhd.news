Cairo — A fund answers only to the president.

The Ahramat al-Nil fund's assets accrue through presidential decrees transferring [Egypt](country:EG)'s state property, and it answers to Future of Egypt's board, not Parliament.

Lawmakers already stripped a bond-issuance clause to protect the Finance Ministry's authority over debt.
