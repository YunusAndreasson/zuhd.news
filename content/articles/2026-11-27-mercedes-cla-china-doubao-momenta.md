Guangzhou — Mercedes launched a hybrid CLA running ByteDance's Doubao AI.

A German premium brand now sells in [China](country:CN) on a Chinese software stack.

Momenta supplies the city and highway driving assistance, built on its R7 world model, inside Mercedes' MB.OS architecture.

Introductory prices of RMB199,000 to RMB245,600 undercut list prices of RMB229,000 and RMB285,600.
