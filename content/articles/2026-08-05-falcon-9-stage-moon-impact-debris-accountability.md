Washington — Nobody was watching it happen.

A four-ton Falcon 9 upper stage, adrift since a 2025 lunar launch, was expected to strike Einstein Crater at 5,400 mph.

Confirmation waits on telescope images; no agency tracks or claims spent rocket stages.
