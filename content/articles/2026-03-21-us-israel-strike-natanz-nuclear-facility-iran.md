Iran confirmed a joint US-Israeli strike on Natanz. The complex holds the bulk of Iran's centrifuges for uranium enrichment, buried underground and long treated by Washington as a nuclear red line not to be crossed. Iran's Supreme Leader has warned such strikes would trigger a nuclear doctrine response, a decision now before Tehran's leadership.
