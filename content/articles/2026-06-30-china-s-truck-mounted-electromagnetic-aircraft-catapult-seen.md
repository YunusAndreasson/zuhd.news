Beijing — [China](country:CN) filmed a mobile electromagnetic catapult launching drones.

The [US](country:US) uses such catapults only on nuclear carriers; China's 3-truck system deploys the same capability from any airstrip or cargo deck.

It belongs to a containerised weapons family already placed on cargo ships for pre-positioning.
