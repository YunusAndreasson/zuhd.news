Johannesburg — Food inflation hit a 16-year low.

[South Africa](country:ZA)'s maize yields reached 6.4 tonnes per hectare after decades of GMO adoption and precision farming, absorbing an oil shock that usually raises food prices too.

Egypt, Pakistan and Nigeria face the same shock without that yield buffer, Grain SA notes.
