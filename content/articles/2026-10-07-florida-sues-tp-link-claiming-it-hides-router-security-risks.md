Florida — 4 states sued TP-Link over alleged lies about China ties.

The Trump administration exempted Netgear and Asus from the foreign router ban but not TP-Link.

Florida's suit cites TP-Link's own confirmation that all but 0.5% of its Vietnam plant's inputs are imported from [China](country:CN).

TP-Link says its US-sold devices are made in Vietnam and no foreign government controls it.

Florida seeks an injunction, restitution and penalties, while TP-Link's new routers await Federal Communications Commission approval.
