Peshawar — At least 28 students died as a school roof fell.

More than 40 were hurt, and the dead children were all 13 or younger.

The private Al-Khair Education Center has about 350 students, and heavy machinery could not reach its congested neighbourhood.

Authorities in [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province arrested the owner and ordered an investigation into the collapse.
