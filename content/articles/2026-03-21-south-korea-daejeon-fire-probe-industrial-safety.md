14 workers died in a Daejeon car parts factory. South Korea records among the highest workplace fatality rates in the OECD, driven by weak safety enforcement in manufacturing. President Lee Jae-myung pledged an investigation including bereaved families, but South Korea's factory safety record has not improved despite similar probes after previous disasters.
