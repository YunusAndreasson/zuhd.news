Peshawar — Officials discussed direct Peshawar-Mashhad flights.

Any airline flying the route risks [US](country:US) secondary-sanctions exposure.

Mashhad, in [Iran](country:IR), hosts the Imam Reza shrine, and insurance, fuel and payment settlement each need a sanctions-exposed institution's approval.

No date or airline has been named, and [Pakistan](country:PK)'s talks with Tehran remain preliminary.
