Cairo — [Egypt](country:EG) delayed a 2nd bread price hike request in months.

Households already stripped of subsidized loaves would absorb the cost of pricier market bread.

Bakeries want tourist and fino bread prices up 15% as imported wheat prices hit record highs of up to LE17,000 a ton.

The ministry gave no commitment to a specific increase and held prices steady after an August request.

Supply Minister Sherif Farouk will decide within a week whether prices fall enough to avoid a rise.
