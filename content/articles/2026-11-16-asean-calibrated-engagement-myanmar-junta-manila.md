Manila — The Association of Southeast Asian Nations may restore [Myanmar](country:MM)'s full political representation.

Myanmar's leader, the coup general Min Aung Hlaing, stays barred from summits.

Restoration depends on sustained progress on the 2021 five-point plan, which Philippine President Ferdinand Marcos concedes has shown none.

The bloc and [China](country:CN) also failed to conclude a South China Sea code, with about 80% of the draft agreed.

[Singapore](country:SG), which analysts say opposes normalisation, takes the chair from the Philippines after December.
