21,000 free doses of lenacapavir arrived in Kenya Thursday. Lenacapavir, a 6-month HIV-prevention injection named Science magazine's 2024 breakthrough, overcomes barriers that daily PrEP pills cannot — stigma and supply shortages. Kenya is the latest African country to join a Global Fund rollout, with 12,000 more doses arriving in April.
