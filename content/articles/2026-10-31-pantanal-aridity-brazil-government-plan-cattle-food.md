Corumbá — 2 Pantanal towns are now officially classed as dry.

Before, only the Northeast, northern Minas Gerais and northwest Espírito Santo carried that label.

[Brazil](country:BR)'s 2025-2045 desertification plan puts Corumbá and Ladário at 0.5-0.65 on an aridity index, rain divided by evaporative demand, for 1990-2020.

Corumbá's 2.1 million-plus cattle anchor its rural economy, and the plan runs to 2045.
