15 people died when a military rescue helicopter crashed in southern Peru. Over 700 districts declared emergencies as El Niño Costero-driven rains triggered mudslides that buried vehicles and collapsed homes across Arequipa and Ica provinces. Forecasters warned the coastal weather pattern will strengthen next month, threatening heavier rainfall before conditions ease.
