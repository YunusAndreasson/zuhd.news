A convicted terrorist freed from US prison in 2024 killed 1 soldier and wounded 2 at Old Dominion University in Virginia. Army trainees at the university shot him dead — Mohamed Jalloh had been convicted of terrorism support in 2016 before his release. The FBI opened a terrorism investigation, but officials have not explained why a convicted terrorism supporter was freed without supervision to target military personnel.
