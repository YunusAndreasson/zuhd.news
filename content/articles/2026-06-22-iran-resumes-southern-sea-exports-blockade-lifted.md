Tehran — [Iran](country:IR)'s first post-blockade export vessels sailed Sunday.

A trade official said ships left for [Qatar](country:QA) and the [United Arab Emirates](country:AE) after the US blockade was lifted and Hormuz reopened.

Which goods are moving — and at what volumes — has not been disclosed.
