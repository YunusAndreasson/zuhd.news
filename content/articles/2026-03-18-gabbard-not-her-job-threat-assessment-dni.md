Tulsi Gabbard told senators assessing threats isn't her job. Her post as Director of National Intelligence, created after 9/11 to synthesize threat assessments across agencies, had predicted Iran's Hormuz disruption in last year's intelligence assessment. Democrats are demanding to know whether Gabbard briefed US President Donald Trump before he ordered strikes on Iran on February 28.
