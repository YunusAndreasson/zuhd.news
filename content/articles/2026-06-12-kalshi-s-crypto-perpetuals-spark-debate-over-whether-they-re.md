Washington — Kalshi launched crypto perpetuals under federal derivatives rules.

Whether classified as futures or swaps determines which rulebook applies, who can trade them, and how much leverage retail investors can take on.

The [US](country:US) Commodity Futures Trading Commission has not ruled; the outcome could force Kalshi to restrict access or restructure the product.
