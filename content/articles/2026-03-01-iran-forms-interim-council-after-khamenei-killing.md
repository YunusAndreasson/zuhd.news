Iran split Khamenei's 36-year authority among 3 men Sunday. Article 111 of Iran's constitution designates these 3 men as a stopgap — a mechanism designed for incapacitation, not assassination. The Assembly of Experts must appoint Khamenei's successor but has not yet convened.
