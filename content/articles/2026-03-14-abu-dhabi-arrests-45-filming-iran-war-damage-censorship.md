Abu Dhabi arrested 45 for filming ongoing Iranian strikes. Police charged them with spreading "inaccurate and misleading information," the label Gulf states apply to citizen journalism during wartime. The same week, Bahrain arrested 5 Pakistani workers on identical charges.
