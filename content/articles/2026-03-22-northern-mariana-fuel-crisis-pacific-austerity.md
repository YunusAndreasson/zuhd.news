Saipan — Diesel hit $9.99 a gallon on Tinian island. The 47,000-person US territory buys all its fuel in small volumes at prices set by global markets now disrupted by the Iran war. The territory's delegate has asked the US Energy Secretary to stabilize Pacific fuel supply; the government has already enacted austerity cuts to sustain essential services.
