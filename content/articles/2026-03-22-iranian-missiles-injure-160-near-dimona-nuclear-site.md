Dimona — 2 Iranian warheads hit 13km from Dimona. Iron Dome failed to intercept the missiles, injuring 160 people near the Shimon Peres nuclear research center, which Israel has never officially declared holds weapons. Israel launched an urgent investigation into why its air defenses failed — a gap Iran has now proven it can exploit.
