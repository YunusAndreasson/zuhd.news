The Hague — [Myanmar](country:MM)'s forces killed some 10,000 Rohingya in 2017.

[The Gambia](country:GM), a small Muslim-majority state, asks the court for its first genocide finding.

Genocide requires proof of intent to destroy a group, and the court has never found a state guilty of it.

Myanmar calls the offensive legitimate counterterrorism and says no direct evidence shows intent to destroy the Rohingya.

Only the UN Security Council can enforce the ruling, and 1.2 million Rohingya wait in [Bangladesh](country:BD)'s camps.
