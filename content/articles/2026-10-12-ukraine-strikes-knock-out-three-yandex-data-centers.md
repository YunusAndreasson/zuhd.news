Kyiv — Ukrainian drones have hit 3 of Yandex's 6 Russian data centers.

Payments, email, ride-hailing and food delivery apps failed across [Russia](country:RU).

Ukraine moved state data to foreign clouds, while Russia's sovereign internet law blocks Yandex from backups abroad, Russian bloggers argue.

Yandex has not said whether any of its 3 supercomputers were damaged.

Yandex says it cannot give a restoration timeline, and an unnamed technology expert says replacement could take 6 months.
