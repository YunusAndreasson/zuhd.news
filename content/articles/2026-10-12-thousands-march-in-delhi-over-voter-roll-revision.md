New Delhi — 133 million names have left India's voter rolls since last year, media reports say.

Thousands marched in [India](country:IN)'s capital Monday, demanding Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar resign.

Officials say the revision removes duplicate, dead and fraudulent names, but critics say it favours Modi's party and hurts Muslims most.

Police detained thousands over the weekend, and the Supreme Court ordered that peaceful protesters not be held illegally.

The youth-led Cockroach Janta Party says it will not stop until Kumar resigns.
