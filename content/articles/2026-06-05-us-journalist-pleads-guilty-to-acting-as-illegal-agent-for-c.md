Washington — Thomas Pauken II admitted unregistered work for Beijing.

Pauken received $100,000 from a [China](country:CN) handler over six years and offered $10,000 for [US](country:US) government reports for Xi Jinping, prosecutors said.

Sentencing is September 1; he faces up to 10 years in prison.
