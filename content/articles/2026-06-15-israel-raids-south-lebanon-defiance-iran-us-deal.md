Nabatieh — [Israel](country:IL) struck 7 south Lebanon towns hours after the ceasefire text.

The US-Iran memorandum, finalized Sunday, halted war on all fronts including [Lebanon](country:LB); signing in Switzerland is set for June 19.

No casualties were reported; Lebanon's Civil Defense urged residents to leave affected towns.
