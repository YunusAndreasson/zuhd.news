The Bangladesh Nationalist Party won a majority. The party returns to power after 20 years in opposition, with Tarique Rahman set to become prime minister. Jamaat-e-Islami, the main Islamist party, conceded and said it would form the opposition with 77 seats. Newly elected members of parliament will take their oath on February 17. The election follows mass protests in 2024 that toppled the previous government led by Sheikh Hasina.
