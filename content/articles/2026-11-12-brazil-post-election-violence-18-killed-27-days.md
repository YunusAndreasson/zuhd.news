Brasília — One person died every 36 hours after [Brazil](country:BR)'s runoff.

Researchers counted 379 incidents in 198 municipalities between 5 and 31 October, and 4 of the 18 dead were Indigenous.

3 Kaingang died on 13 October in a dispute that began when workers were fired for joining a Lula rally.

Terra de Direitos and Justiça Global logged 15 official calls for firings or boycotts, which they call a strategy, not isolated clashes.
