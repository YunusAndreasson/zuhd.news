Lahore — 95% of Punjab's stone-crushing workers exposed to crystalline silica die from it, the court found.

In [Pakistan](country:PK), unregistered workers fall outside labour law — no employer accountability, no medical surveillance, no compensation pathway.

Deputy commissioners of 3 districts must submit compliance reports or appear before the court.
