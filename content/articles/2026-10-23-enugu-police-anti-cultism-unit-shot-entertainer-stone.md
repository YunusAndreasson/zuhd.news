Enugu — Izuchukwu Nnaji was arrested Wednesday and found dead Thursday.

Hundreds of youths protested, 18 months after an Enugu police officer killed musician Chikezie Mba.

Police spokesperson Daniel Ndukwe says Nnaji fled a hotel raid and the firearm discharged as he and an operative struggled.

Commissioner Bitrus Giwa ordered the state intelligence department to investigate and promised discipline for any culpable officer.
