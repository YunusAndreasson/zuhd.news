Qalyubiya — Meter readers face dismissal for refusing quota contracts.

[Egypt](country:EG)'s Qalyubiya Drinking Water and Sanitation Company tied contract renewal to each collector's bill-redemption rate, replacing yearly terms some workers held for 8 years.

Workers demand permanent employment; the company has cut data access for those who refuse to sign.
