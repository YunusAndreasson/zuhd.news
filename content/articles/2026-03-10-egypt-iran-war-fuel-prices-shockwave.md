Egyptian petrol prices jumped up to 30% this week. Egypt depends on imported oil and Suez Canal fees, both now collapsing as shippers reroute away from the Mediterranean. The Suez Canal will not recover fee revenue until the Strait of Hormuz reopens — a timeline Iran controls.
