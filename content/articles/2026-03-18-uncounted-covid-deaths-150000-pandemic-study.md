Elected coroners missed 155,000 Covid deaths. Deaths fell hardest on Hispanic communities, where elected coroners lacked testing resources, training, and sometimes the will to list Covid as cause. The death investigation system, run by elected and sometimes partisan coroners, has not changed since these deaths were missed.
