Obra Dinn renders in just 1 bit per pixel. Spherical mapping anchors dither patterns to the view direction rather than screen coordinates, eliminating the banding that standard 3D dithering produces as perspective shifts. Game developer Lucas Pope posted the full technical breakdown on TIGSource, making the algorithm available for others to adapt.
