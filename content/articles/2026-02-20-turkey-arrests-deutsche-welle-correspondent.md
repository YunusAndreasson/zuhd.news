30 police officers detained Deutsche Welle correspondent Alican Uludağ in Ankara on Thursday. Prosecutors charged the award-winning journalist with insulting the president and spreading misleading information, citing a social media post from 2024 about released Islamic State suspects. DW Director General Barbara Massing called the charges baseless and demanded his immediate release.
