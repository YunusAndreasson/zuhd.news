United States — House committees ask Jensen Huang for a closed briefing 18 November.

Anonymous [US](country:US) intelligence officials link him to a [Singapore](country:SG) executive, but no court has ruled.

A committee of independent directors, assisted by outside counsel, will review all available information while Huang stays chief executive.

The board says it has no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised.

Huang's reply to the committees is due 11 November, and Nvidia reports earnings 17 November.
