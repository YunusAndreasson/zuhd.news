Beijing — [China](country:CN) consumes a third of world hydrogen.

Beijing named hydrogen a "future industry," applying the electric vehicle playbook of subsidies and infrastructure that eliminated Western solar competitors.

Only 1% of China's output is currently green; which country makes the electrolysers is being decided now.
