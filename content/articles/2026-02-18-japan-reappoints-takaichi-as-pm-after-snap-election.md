Japan's first woman premier won a second term. Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi's Liberal Democratic Party secured a two-thirds supermajority in a snap election, winning 316 of 465 lower house seats. The margin gives her the votes to propose constitutional amendments, including lifting Japan's postwar military ban.
