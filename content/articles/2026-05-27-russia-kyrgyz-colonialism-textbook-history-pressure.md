Bishkek — Russian historians told Kyrgyz peers: drop "colonialism."

The request came in joint historian sessions — a channel [Russia](country:RU) has used to contest colonial-era labels in neighboring states.

[Kyrgyzstan](country:KG)'s education ministry has not confirmed whether the term will be removed from next year's curriculum.
