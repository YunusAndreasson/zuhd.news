Iraq — The Kirkuk-Ceyhan pipeline pumps crude again after a decade.

It gives [Iraq](country:IQ) an export route besides the Kurdistan region's pipeline.

Flows to [Turkey](country:TR) start at 120,000 barrels a day, with 500,000 planned, the oil minister said.

The minister gave no date for reaching 500,000 barrels a day.
