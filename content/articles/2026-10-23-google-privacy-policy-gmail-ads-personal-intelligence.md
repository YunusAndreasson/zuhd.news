Mountain View — Google softened its Gmail ad-targeting promise on 16 October.

Google says ads in AI search could draw on data users connect through Personal Intelligence.

Personal Intelligence, released in early 2026, passes data from Gmail and Photos to Gemini, AI Overviews and AI Mode.

Google says the feature is opt-in and ads will not draw directly on Gmail inboxes or Photos libraries.

The Gemini app currently carries no ads, but AI search tools can show sponsored content.
