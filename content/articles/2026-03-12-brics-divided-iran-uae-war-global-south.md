Iran and the UAE, both BRICS members, are now on opposite sides of an active war. Built as an alternative to Western-led institutions, BRICS cannot issue a joint statement: China and Russia back Iran while the UAE aligns with the Gulf coalition, and India — one of the 5 founding members — has remained pivotal but equivocal. Saudi Arabia, a BRICS membership applicant, now holds the only major oil pipeline bypassing the blocked Strait of Hormuz.
