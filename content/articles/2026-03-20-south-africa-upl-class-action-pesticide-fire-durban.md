UPL's 2021 pesticide fire sent toxic fumes across Durban. South Africa has no established class action mechanism — lawyers are asking Judge Henriques to certify the suit against UPL, a Mumbai-based agrochemical company, in what would be one of the country's first. UPL is contesting certification, leaving hundreds of affected residents without a legal path for damages.
