Lyon — Infections were linked to 2.3 million new cancers in 2024.

That is 1 in 8 cases, and researchers say many were preventable by vaccination, screening or treatment.

The International Agency for Research on Cancer, in The Lancet Oncology, linked 12 infections to cases, 5 driving nearly all.

The authors say they counted only proven carcinogens, so the total may be underestimated.

Researcher Harriet Rumgay says vaccination and screening progress still leaves low- and middle-income countries carrying the burden.
