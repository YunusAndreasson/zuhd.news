London — A court ordered Nirav Modi to repay $10.7M to Bank of [India](country:IN).

Bank of India invoked Modi's 2012 personal guarantee after his Firestar Group defaulted following the Punjab National Bank fraud investigations.

Modi remains in [UK](country:GB) custody fighting extradition, pursuing relief at the European Court of Human Rights.
