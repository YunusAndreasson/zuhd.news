Jakarta — Stocks fell 11.6% Friday, the worst day since 1998.

Index provider MSCI proposed demoting [Indonesia](country:ID) to standalone status, alongside Argentina, Lebanon, Palestine, Ukraine and Zimbabwe.

Global funds tracking MSCI indexes would drop Indonesian stocks, potentially triggering billions of dollars in outflows.

MSCI says reforms, including a 15% minimum free float, have not stopped suspected coordinated trading.

MSCI takes feedback until the end of April 2027 and announces its decision by 30 June.
