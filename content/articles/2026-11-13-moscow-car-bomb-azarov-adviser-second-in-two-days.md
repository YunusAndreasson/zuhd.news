Moscow — A car bomb injured Mykola Azarov's former adviser, Sergei Zavorotny.

A similar blast killed a Russian colonel in Saint Petersburg a day earlier.

[Russia](country:RU) calls the bombings the latest in a series of attacks on pro-Kremlin figures, and investigators are testing for explosives.

Zavorotny was driving when the bomb exploded on Friday morning and was taken to hospital.
