Cochabamba — Madidi National Park holds 68% of [Bolivia](country:BO)'s known bat species.

7 species are new to Bolivia, raising its total to 146.

Acoustic recorders detected 14 species that nets, traps and roost searches missed across 12 sites from 198 to 4,814 metres.

The authors caution the true total is probably higher, because high-elevation habitats were sampled at few sites.

Further surveys across seasons are needed, while illegal mining and land conversion press on the park.
