Copenhagen — Novo Nordisk stopped two heart trials for futility.

The result undercuts a theory that cooling inflammation, not cholesterol alone, prevents heart attacks.

A monitoring committee found HERMES and ATHENA, its ziltivekimab trials, unlikely to succeed after ZEUS also failed.

Novo has not said whether it will pursue the inflammation approach in other diseases.
