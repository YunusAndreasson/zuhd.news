London — Honey-made silver particles cut biofilms up to 86%.

Vancomycin-resistant enterococci have "limited treatment options," researchers wrote; the honey-synthesized nanoparticles killed the bacteria in lab dishes at low doses.

The study was in-vitro only — clinical trials in patients have not begun.
