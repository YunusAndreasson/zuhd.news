Moscow — Eastern Arctic sea ice still freezes 1.5 meters thick. Russia's Arctic and Antarctic Research Institute says the western sector is already navigable year-round, leaving the eastern stretch as the only remaining barrier. Rosatom, Russia's state nuclear corporation, is testing year-round escort convoys — a functional Northern Sea Route would bypass the Suez Canal and reorder global shipping.
