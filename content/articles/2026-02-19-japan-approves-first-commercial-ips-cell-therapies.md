2 therapies made from reprogrammed stem cells won approval in Japan. ReHeart treats heart failure with lab-grown muscle sheets, and Amchepry delivers dopamine-producing neurons to Parkinson's patients — both world firsts using induced pluripotent stem cells. The approvals arrive 14 years after Shinya Yamanaka won the Nobel Prize for the underlying discovery.
