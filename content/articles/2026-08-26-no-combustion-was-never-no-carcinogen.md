London — Vaping's smoke-free promise obscured a carcinogen risk.

Forty million adolescents use these products, most having never smoked a cigarette.

Heating propylene glycol and glycerin without flame still yields formaldehyde and acrolein, carcinogens in tobacco smoke.

Researchers are now measuring DNA-adduct and epigenetic markers in users, not yet cancer incidence.
