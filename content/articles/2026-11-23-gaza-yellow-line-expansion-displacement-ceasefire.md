Gaza City — [Israel](country:IL) moved the yellow line again on 25 October.

The ceasefire boundary now displaces families every few weeks.

Residents say Israeli forces advance yellow concrete blocks hundreds of metres at a time, leaving families east of them nowhere to go but west.

"Every day, we hear that a bullet has struck someone's tent," said Walid Sakhr, a displaced Shuja'iyya resident.

Polymarket traders give the ceasefire a 5% chance of being cancelled by 31 December.
