Washington — Homeland Security ordered DeepSeek removed from civilian agencies' devices.

Sensitive data could reach [China](country:CN) through staff or vendors, the department warned.

The ban also covers third-party tools built on DeepSeek, but the department cannot direct the military or intelligence services.

[US](country:US) and Chinese leaders meet in Shenzhen next month, where an AI-incident communication channel is expected to come up.
