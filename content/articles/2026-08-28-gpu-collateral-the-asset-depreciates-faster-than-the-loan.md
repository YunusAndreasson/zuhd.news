New York — Bullish is lending $100 million against GPUs.

Depositors chasing AI yield now hold debt on chips that depreciate fast.

Banks won't underwrite compute with no resale price or repossessable location, so lenders price risk through crypto instead.

Bullish will list the yield token itself, though it is CoinDesk's parent company.
