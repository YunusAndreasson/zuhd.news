Muscat — An unknown projectile hit a ship's engine room, killing one crew member.

It turns a pricing risk into a physical one, repricing every hull crossing [Oman](country:OM)'s strait.

UK Maritime Trade Operations said the outbound vessel's Company Security Officer reported the strike as Omani coastguard assisted survivors.

Authorities are investigating; no attribution or environmental damage has been confirmed yet.
