Enerhodar — Zaporizhzhia lost external power; diesel took over. Specialists restored supply via the single remaining 330kV Ferrosplavnaya-1 line; the 750kV Dnepropetrovskaya line has been offline since March 24. The IAEA is in talks with Russia and Ukraine on a local ceasefire to protect the plant.
