Guwahati — Assam bars adult Aadhaar enrollment without state approval.

[India](country:IN)'s universal ID now requires deputy commissioner sign-off for adults in Assam; the state government said the rule targets Bangladeshi migrants.

No timeline was given; those who fail verification cannot enroll in the national ID system used for welfare and banking.
