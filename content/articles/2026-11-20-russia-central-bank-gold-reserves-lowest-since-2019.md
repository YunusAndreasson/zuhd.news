Moscow — [Russia](country:RU)'s gold reserves hit a 7-year low, state news agency TASS reported.

The central bank's holdings fell 0.3 million troy ounces in October, the tenth straight monthly decline.

Regulator data put the remaining 72.5 million ounces at $291.958 billion on 1 November, the lowest since October 2019.

The central bank's November data will show whether the decline reaches an 11th month.
