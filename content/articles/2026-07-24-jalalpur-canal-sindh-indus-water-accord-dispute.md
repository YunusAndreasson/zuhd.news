Larkana — Sindh has called federal water diversions unconstitutional.

Pakistan Peoples Party Sindh chief Nisar Khuhro says releases into the Jalalpur and link canals violate [Pakistan](country:PK)'s 1991 Water Apportionment Accord.

Province-wide protests are planned Friday, with Khuhro demanding an emergency session of the Council of Common Interests.
