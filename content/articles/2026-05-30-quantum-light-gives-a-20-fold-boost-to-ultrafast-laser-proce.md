London — Squeezed quantum light boosted laser-matter interactions 20-fold.

Higher laser intensity drives stronger nonlinear interactions but destroys fragile samples; quantum light buys the signal gain without raising intensity.

The approach targets ultrafast spectroscopy of biological samples, where conventional lasers would destroy them.
