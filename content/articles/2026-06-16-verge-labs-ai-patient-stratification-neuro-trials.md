San Francisco — Verge's failed amyotrophic lateral sclerosis trial is now a benchmark.

Most neurological trials fail on patient heterogeneity; Verge Labs is converting its dropout data into an AI cohort-selection tool.

The company has not said when the AI benchmark will be available for external researchers.
