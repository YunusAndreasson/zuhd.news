Abu Dhabi — The [UAE](country:AE) launched its first retail Treasury sukuk programme.

Retail sukuk lets households hold Shariah-compliant sovereign debt, opening a government funding channel previously limited to institutional investors.

The debut places the UAE among the small number of sovereigns with retail-accessible Islamic debt markets.
