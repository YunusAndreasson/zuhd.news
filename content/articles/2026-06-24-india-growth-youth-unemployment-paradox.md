New Delhi — A 'Cockroaches' party spotlights India's graduate unemployment.

[India](country:IN) is the world's fastest-growing major economy, yet formal jobs for graduates remain scarce despite headline growth.

Whether the 'Cockroaches' movement translates frustration into seats will test how the ruling coalition addresses the formal-jobs gap.
