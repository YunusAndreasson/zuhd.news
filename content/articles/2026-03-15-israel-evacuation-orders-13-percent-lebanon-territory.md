Israel's evacuation order covers 13% of Lebanon's territory. Israeli forces ordered all residents south of the Zahrani River — 40 kilometers into Lebanon — to leave, with 820,000 already displaced. Lebanese officials have given no timeline for return as fighting continues south of the Zahrani line.
