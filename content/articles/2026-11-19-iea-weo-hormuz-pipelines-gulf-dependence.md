Paris — 2 million barrels a day would still cross Hormuz in 2035.

Importers can cut reliance on the strait, but the International Energy Agency says the Middle East cannot.

Bypass pipelines of 7 million barrels a day would cost $40 billion, and Qatari gas has no route around the strait.

The agency also warns a post-war glut could push oil and gas prices down hard.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 31 December a 14% chance, down 17 points in a week.
