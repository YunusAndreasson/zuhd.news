Islamabad — One notice now binds every Pakistani degree.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s Higher Education Commission (HEC) requires it for 250 universities from Fall 2026.

The three-credit mandate needs thousands of instructors, but computer-science faculty already leave for industry and abroad.

Fall 2026 rollout tests whether HEC funds the faculty it will need.
