Mackay — Ransomware halted sugar harvesting and milling in [Australia](country:AU).

Mackay Sugar was hit mid-harvest — when downtime means spoiling crop — by a group not yet identified.

No restoration timeline has been given; each day idle mid-harvest risks crop spoilage with no recovery.
