Starbase — SpaceX's tower caught Ship 42 after 14 orbits.

Ship 42 came home intact and could, in theory, fly again.

It deployed 26 Starlink V3 satellites, relit one Raptor to deorbit, then lit three for a landing flip.

Super Heavy Booster 22 missed its catch after relighting 32 of 33 engines, and SpaceX is investigating.

SpaceX president Gwynne Shotwell said the company aims to refly a second stage by early next year.
