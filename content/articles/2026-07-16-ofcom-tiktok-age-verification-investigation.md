London — Ofcom calls age checks a "cornerstone."

The UK's Online Safety Act requires robust age checks, forcing platforms to choose between identity verification and a self-declared birthday box.

Ofcom's investigation into TikTok is among the first real tests of whether the law has enforcement teeth.
