Baghdad — [Iraq](country:IQ)'s oil ministry says October crude revenue hit $8.8 billion.

Exports rose to 3.6 million barrels a day from 200,000 in May, the ministry said.

Most crude ships through the Strait of Hormuz, where traffic ran 25% below normal as of 22 November.

The ministry did not split the revenue by region after Baghdad ordered it to take control of Kurdistan's oilfields.

Iraq asks oil exporters for a higher quota on Sunday, though the group is likely to hold targets through March.
