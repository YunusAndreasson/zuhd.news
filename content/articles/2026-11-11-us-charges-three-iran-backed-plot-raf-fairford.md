New York — Prosecutors link 3 alleged Iran-backed operatives to nearly 20 attacks.

Prosecutors say retaliation for the Iran war reached synagogues, Jewish schools and a British base.

Prosecutors say Iraqi Mohammad Ali al-Hassani sent vans with fuel barrels to RAF Fairford and paid for shrapnel metal.

[Iran](country:IR) has strongly denied any involvement in the Fairford incident.

Prosecutors have not disclosed where Mohsen Mohajeri and Mohammad Ali al-Hassani are.
