United Nations — Russian state news agency TASS: Moscow may let Turkey move its S-400s.

Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov said so at a news conference closing the General Assembly's high-level week, about systems now in [Turkey](country:TR).

Lavrov set one condition: the destination must be responsible and friendly to [Russia](country:RU).

Lavrov said Russia will approve a transfer only when a request arrives, and none has.
