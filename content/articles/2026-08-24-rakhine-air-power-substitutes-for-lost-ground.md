Sittwe — Military defectors say the bombing will intensify.

Civilians absorb the strikes meant to hold townships troops can no longer defend.

Having lost the ground war in [Myanmar](country:MM)'s Rakhine State, the military substitutes aircraft for infantry it no longer has.

The defectors' account is the only source naming intent behind the targeting.
