Uduere — Ovie Omo-Agege's campaign says gunmen killed 4 supporters.

[Nigeria](country:NG) votes on 16 January 2027, and campaigning is turning deadly.

Police describe the attackers as suspected political thugs, and the state government imposed a curfew on Ughelli North.

"Seeking the votes of the people should not be a death sentence," said Sunny Areh, the campaign's media adviser.

The campaign wants Governor Sheriff Oborevwori to condemn the attack and order arrests.
