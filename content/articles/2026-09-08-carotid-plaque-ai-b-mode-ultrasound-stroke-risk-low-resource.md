London — An AI grades carotid plaque from routine ultrasound.

The tool works on machines most hospitals worldwide already own.

Stroke risk depends on whether plaque ruptures, not on how narrow the artery is, and texture reveals that.

The model scored 0.88 out of 1 for accuracy in external testing, the authors reported.
