Swat — Police arrested about 140 opposition workers in Swabi on Monday.

The arrests follow the emergency and governor's rule imposed in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

A Swat police complaint names 2 opposition National Assembly members and 4 provincial legislators over a 28 September rally.

The complaint accuses them of threatening speeches against the federal government and blocking Mingora-Murghazar Road.

Police raided opposition lawmakers' homes in Battagram, Mohmand and Charsadda, and found none of them.
