Hillsides collapsed onto homes in Juiz de Fora. Decades of iron ore mining left Minas Gerais slopes bare of vegetation; the city's rainiest February on record turned them into mudslides, killing 28. 40 people remain missing as rescue teams warn continued rain could trigger further collapses.
