Motorola will engineer future devices with GrapheneOS compatibility. GrapheneOS — previously available only by manually loading it on Pixel phones — strips out Google telemetry, hardens memory, and verifies the boot chain. Motorola and GrapheneOS will publish joint research and reveal compatible hardware in the coming months.
