Kurukshetra — Attackers pelted a bus of about 40 Kashmiris with bricks.

The Haryana dhaba attack injured an elderly Kashmiri man and terrified women and children.

The owner alleges attackers argued with the driver, left, returned on motorcycles and threatened to bar Kashmir-registered vehicles from Haryana.

Jammu and Kashmir Chief Minister Omar Abdullah asked Haryana's chief minister and police to catch the culprits.
