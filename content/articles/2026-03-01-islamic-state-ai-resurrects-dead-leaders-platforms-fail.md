Dead since 2019, al-Baghdadi now delivers AI-generated ISIS speeches. The Institute for Strategic Dialogue, a London-based research group, tracked 350 such accounts on platforms that have gutted their trust-and-safety teams. Facebook and 2 other platforms have not announced a coordinated response despite one account reaching 77,000 views.
