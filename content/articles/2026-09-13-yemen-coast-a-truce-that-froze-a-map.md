Sanaa — Houthi missiles hit four Saudi cities Sept. 8.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA) reportedly asked Washington to strike back, a request declined so far.

A 2022 truce quieted the war but left [Yemen](country:YE)'s forces split among rival commands and foreign patrons.

Yemen's government has ordered offensives on five fronts without a unified command.
