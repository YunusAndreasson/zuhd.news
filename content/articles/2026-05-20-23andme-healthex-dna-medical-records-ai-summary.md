San Francisco — Post-bankruptcy 23andMe merges DNA with medical records.

The company acquired from bankruptcy is launching an AI-generated Health Summary combining genome data with imported medical records via HealthEx.

The new product merges 2 of the most sensitive data categories; the consent users gave the pre-bankruptcy company may not cover the expanded use.
