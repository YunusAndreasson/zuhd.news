Bogotá — [Colombia](country:CO)'s armed-conflict deaths rose 80% in 100 days.

Cauca and Norte de Santander bear it, the Armed Conflict Location and Event Data monitor found.

Against Gustavo Petro's last 100 days, clashes involving security forces rose 33% under President Abelardo de la Espriella.

The Ideas for Peace Foundation found homicides fell 10.7% and kidnappings 66.3% against the same period of 2025.

The government proposes cutting the budget of the Special Jurisdiction for Peace, the 2016 accord's transitional court.
