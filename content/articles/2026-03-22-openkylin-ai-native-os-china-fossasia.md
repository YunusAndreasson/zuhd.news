Beijing — China's openKylin embeds AI into the operating system itself. The three-layer architecture decouples AI models from hardware, letting apps run AI locally or in the cloud without managing underlying infrastructure. openKylin positions itself as the AI-native OS for China and developing nations seeking an alternative to Microsoft and Google.
