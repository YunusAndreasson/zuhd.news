Israel purchased 3 drone-mounted tear gas systems. The devices, called "Surprise Egg," attach to Matrice-type drones and release gas capsules from the air to disperse Palestinian gatherings in the occupied West Bank. Tender documents marked the purchase "urgent," citing preparations for Ramadan. Israeli police already operate 19 similar systems across the West Bank. The military plans to deploy an additional commando brigade on top of the 22 battalions already stationed there.
