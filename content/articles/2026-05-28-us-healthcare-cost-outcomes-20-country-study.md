New York — [US](country:US) healthcare ranked last across 20 countries again.

Private-payer fragmentation means 0.3 primary care doctors per 1,000 people — one-third the peer average — and no universal coverage, the Commonwealth Fund found.

Only the US and [Mexico](country:MX) lack universal coverage among the 20; Mexico targets 2027.
