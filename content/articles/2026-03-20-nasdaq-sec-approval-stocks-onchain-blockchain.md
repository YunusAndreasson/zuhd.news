SEC approved Nasdaq's framework to trade stocks on blockchain. The system opens 24/7 global access to US stocks for the first time but keeps all clearing inside the Wall Street-controlled DTCC, the same intermediary as before. Critics argue the move proves Wall Street can adopt blockchain's branding without surrendering control over markets.
