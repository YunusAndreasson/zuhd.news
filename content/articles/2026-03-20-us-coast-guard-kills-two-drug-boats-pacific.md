US forces killed 2 suspected smugglers off Costa Rica. Operations like this have killed over 145 people this year in the eastern Pacific, all suspected traffickers with no trial or judicial review. The Coast Guard transferred the survivor to Costa Rica while Congress has yet to investigate who authorized the lethal operations.
