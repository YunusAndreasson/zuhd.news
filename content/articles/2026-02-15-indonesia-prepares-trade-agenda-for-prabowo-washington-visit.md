Prabowo called an emergency trade meeting. Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto summoned finance and trade ministers on Saturday to prepare for his February 19 Washington visit, where he will negotiate a reciprocal trade deal after US tariffs on Indonesian exports dropped from 32% to 19%. Final terms remain unresolved, and Southeast Asia's largest economy must balance domestic manufacturing goals against American demands.
