Rangpur — [India](country:IN)'s Gajaldoba barrage decides the river's fate here.

The Teesta crosses that barrage before reaching 21 million people, so China's talked-up upstream dredging cannot restore dry-season flow.

A water-sharing treaty has sat unsigned since 2011, blocked by West Bengal's own state government.
