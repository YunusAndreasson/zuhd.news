Paris — Prosecutors want 4 years for [France](country:FR)'s ex-justice minister Rachida Dati.

A former justice minister faces prison over paid lobbying for a carmaker while in office.

Prosecutors say Dati took 900,000 euros from a Renault-Nissan subsidiary for consulting work she never performed as an EU lawmaker.

Co-defendant Carlos Ghosn, tried in absentia, also faces a requested 4-year term as the trial continues.
