Uganda owes $35 billion — roughly $800 for every citizen. The debt finances roads, hydropower dams, and an East African crude oil pipeline intended to generate the export revenue that would repay it. Uganda's debt grew $650 million in a single quarter, with the Hormuz closure now blocking the oil exports the repayment plan depends on.
