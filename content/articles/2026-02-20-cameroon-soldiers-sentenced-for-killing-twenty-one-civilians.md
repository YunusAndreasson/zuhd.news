A Cameroon military court handed down rare convictions. 3 soldiers received 5-to-10-year sentences for a 2020 raid on Ngarbuh village that killed 21 in the Anglophone region. Victims' lawyers called the terms insignificant, noting the legal minimum for murder is 10 years.
