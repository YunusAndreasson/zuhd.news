San Francisco — 50 workflows trained OpenAI's new biology-specific model. GPT-Rosalind maps genotype to phenotype through known pathways and infers protein function — tasks general models fail at as biology's datasets scale. The model targets drug discovery and integrates public databases; domain-specific tuning, not scale, is now OpenAI's biology bet.
