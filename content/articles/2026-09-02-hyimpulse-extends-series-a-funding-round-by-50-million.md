Neuenstadt am Kocher — HyImpulse raised another €50 million for rockets.

That funds Europe's bid for a sovereign small-launch alternative to [US](country:US) and Indian rockets.

Its rockets burn solid paraffin wax with liquid oxygen, a mix that cannot detonate and needs no cryogenic handling.

SL1's first orbital launch attempt still has no set date.
