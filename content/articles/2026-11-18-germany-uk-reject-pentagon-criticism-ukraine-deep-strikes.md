Brussels — Pentagon's Elbridge Colby says [Ukraine](country:UA)'s deep strikes have failed.

[Germany](country:DE) and [Britain](country:GB) publicly rejected him, opening a split among Ukraine's backers.

Colby told the Ukraine Defense Contact Group the strikes neither end the war nor blunt Russian advances, and could harden [Russian](country:RU) support.

German Defense Minister Boris Pistorius called the criticism "quite a stupid thing" and cited Russia's nightly strikes on Ukrainian heating.

Europe and Canada now finance new deliveries, including American-made weapons, as talks turn to air defense.
