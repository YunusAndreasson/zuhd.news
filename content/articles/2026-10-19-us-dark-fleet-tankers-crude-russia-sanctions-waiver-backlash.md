Washington — Washington is letting sanctioned tankers begin shipping crude legitimately.

It is the latest White House bid to lower oil prices, with Brent settling at $109.30 Monday.

Investors would buy and refurbish idled sanctioned ships, which Energy Secretary Chris Wright says will stop carrying sanctioned oil.

Analytics firm Kpler estimates only 24 of about 160 [US](country:US)-sanctioned vessels could return to mainstream trade.

Polymarket traders give a Trump-Putin-Xi meeting before 2027 an 86% chance.
