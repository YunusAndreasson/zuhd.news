Ukrainian negotiators fly to Washington Saturday for Russia talks. Peace talks stalled February 28 when the US-Israel war on Iran began — Russia has since demanded Ukraine cede the 20% of Donetsk it still occupies. A senior Kremlin official said Friday the pause is "temporary" and a new round will likely happen soon.
