Abuja — State-run stations cut petrol ₦66 a litre until 31 October.

Hundreds of private stations across [Nigeria](country:NG) are excluded and still charge market prices.

Finance minister Taiwo Oyedele says the retail arm funds it from its own margin, not the budget.

At Abuja stations, app users paid ₦1,339 a litre and pump payers ₦1,405.

Atiku Abubakar's campaign proposes cheaper crude for refiners instead and has not published its cost.
