An AI beat doctors across 2,919 rare conditions. DeepRare, published in Nature, uses 40 specialized tools to generate diagnoses with traceable reasoning that experts verified 95% of the time. 300 million people with rare diseases typically endure 5+ years of misdiagnosis before getting answers.
