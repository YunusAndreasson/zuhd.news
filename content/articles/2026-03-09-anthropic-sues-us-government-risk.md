Anthropic sued the Pentagon for calling it a security risk. The Defense Department designated the AI company a supply chain risk after it refused to drop safety restrictions for military use — restrictions its competitor OpenAI agreed to remove. The case will determine whether AI developers can legally refuse Pentagon demands to override safety restrictions, setting a precedent for every AI company that follows.
