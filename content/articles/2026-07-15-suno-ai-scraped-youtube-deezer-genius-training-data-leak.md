Cambridge — A hack revealed 113,879 hours of scraped YouTube Music alone.

Leaked source code shows AI music generator Suno pulled decades of songs from YouTube, Deezer, Genius and other catalogs to train its model, per the hacker's files.

The Recording Industry Association's lawsuits continue; one has already settled with Suno claiming fair use.
