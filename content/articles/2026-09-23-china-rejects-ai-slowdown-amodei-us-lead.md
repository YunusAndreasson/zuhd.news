Beijing — China is rejecting Dario Amodei's call to slow AI.

It signals Beijing will not cede any ground in the global AI race to Washington.

Amodei urged the global AI industry to slow down; Chinese officials read it as an attempt to lock in the [US](country:US)'s lead.

Beijing has given no indication it will heed Western calls to pause AI development.
