Shanidar Cave — A spear killed a Neanderthal 75,000 years ago.

If intentional, it is one of the earliest direct examples of violence between hominins.

A puncture in the shoulder blade shows a sharpened wooden spear entered through the chest and heart, exiting toward the back.

Researchers cannot tell whether another Neanderthal, a Homo sapiens or a hunting accident caused the wound.

A partly healed face injury shows kin cared for Shanidar A before the fatal wound.
