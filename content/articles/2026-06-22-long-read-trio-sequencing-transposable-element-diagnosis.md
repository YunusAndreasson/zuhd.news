Tokyo — Long-read sequencing cracked a case exome testing missed.

The workflow found an AluY insertion in GPC3 — a jumping gene invisible to short reads — that caused Simpson-Golabi-Behmel syndrome.

Broader clinical uptake requires long-read trio sequencing to become routine; it remains limited to specialist centres.
