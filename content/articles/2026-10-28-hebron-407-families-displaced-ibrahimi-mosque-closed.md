Hebron — Israeli soldiers gave 407 families one hour to leave.

The municipality has registered 2,649 displaced people, 1,109 of them children.

[Israel](country:IL)'s army sealed Hebron's exits after a shooting killed 2 soldiers, and closed the Ibrahimi Mosque to Muslims.

"I don't know how long things will stay like this," said Majda al-Ja'ba, a displaced widow.

The army has announced no return date, and 168 families have no safe place to stay.
