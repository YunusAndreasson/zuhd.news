Sudan — Petrol stations have become campsites as fuel prices skyrocket.

An October ban on fava bean imports, a daily staple, is drawing fierce criticism from government sources and traders.

Government sources say authorities will tighten fuel allocations and seek new supplies, with Abdel Fattah al-Burhan negotiating with [Saudi Arabia](country:SA).

No end date exists until external agreements and financial guarantees are finalised, a government source says.
