Healthcare company Nabla will test LeCun's world-model AI clinically. LeCun's startup AMI raised $1 billion for a system that, unlike large language models, learns causal representations of environments — reasoning about drug interactions rather than autocompleting them. Success would validate LeCun's claim that large language models cannot reason reliably, opening a regulatory path for autonomous clinical AI.
