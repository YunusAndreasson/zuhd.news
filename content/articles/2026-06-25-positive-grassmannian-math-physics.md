Cambridge — One algebraic shape governs physics, waves, and traffic.

The positive Grassmannian, an object where all coordinates are non-negative, appears in quantum scattering, shallow-water waves, and traffic models, Lauren Williams found.

Williams, a Harvard mathematician, is investigating why one pure-math object keeps surfacing in unrelated physical systems.
