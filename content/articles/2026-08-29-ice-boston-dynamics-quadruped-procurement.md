Washington — Immigration and Customs Enforcement will buy robot dogs.

Immigration raids gain a new surveillance device with no rules on what it records.

The Homeland Security Department frames the purchase as improving officer safety, the usual route military hardware enters policing.

Boston Dynamics bars weaponizing its robots but says little about surveillance use.
