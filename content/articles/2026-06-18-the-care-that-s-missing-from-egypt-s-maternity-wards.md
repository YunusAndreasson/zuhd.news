Alexandria — [Egypt](country:EG) arrested the doctor who exposed maternity ward abuse.

Omnia Sweidan's viral testimony triggered mass corroboration; Alexandria University's lawyers filed a complaint against her before any investigation began.

No state health authority has opened a formal inquiry as accounts from across Egypt's public hospitals accumulate.
