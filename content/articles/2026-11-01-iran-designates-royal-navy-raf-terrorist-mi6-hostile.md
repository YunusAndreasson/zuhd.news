Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) declared [Britain](country:GB)'s navy and air force terrorist groups.

MI6 now falls under Iran's espionage law as a hostile group.

Iran's Supreme National Security Council ordered it, citing Britain's 13 July designation of the Revolutionary Guards.

The foreign ministry accuses Britain of joining the US-Israeli war by lending bases and logistical facilities.

The ministry warned Britain will bear responsibility for the consequences, with the measures effective from 1 November.
