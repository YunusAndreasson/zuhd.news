Lagos — 960 million Africans have coverage but no affordable smartphone. The GSMA, a global mobile industry body, is piloting $40 4G smartphones in 6 African countries with Airtel, Vodafone, and Orange — priced just 10% above a basic feature phone. Every comparable India experiment failed on quality, leaving 2026 results as the first real test of whether $40 can hold up.
