Pretoria — [South Africa](country:ZA) loses $400 million in HIV funding.

The [US](country:US) ended the President's Emergency Plan for AIDS Relief citing a "white genocide" claim; researchers estimate 519,000–712,000 deaths over 20 years.

Most services end by September; access to lenacapavir, a new injectable HIV drug, is uncertain.
