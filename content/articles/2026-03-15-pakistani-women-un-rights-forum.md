Education activist Malala Yousafzai called Afghanistan a 'gender apartheid' state. Pakistan cited 480 specialized courts for gender violence, but UN reviewers found most women hesitate to use them under social pressure. The UN Commission on the Status of Women continues this week, with delegates warning of global pushback that could spread Afghanistan's model further.
