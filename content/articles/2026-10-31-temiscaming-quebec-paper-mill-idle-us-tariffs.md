Témiscaming — Most of the paper mill's 360 workers are idle.

The mill is the town's main employer, and property values fell almost overnight.

[US](country:US) duties of 50% hit [Canada](country:CA)'s pulp and paper in August, and the mill ships about 75% of its output south.

"We feel we got into a war with kid's water guns to fight back," said Mayor Alain Gauthier.

Workers want to buy the mill back from its American owner before winter freezes idle equipment.
