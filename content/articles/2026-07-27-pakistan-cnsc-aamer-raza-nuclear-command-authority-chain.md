Rawalpindi — A new general now heads [Pakistan](country:PK)'s nuclear command.

The post sits inside the National Command Authority, nominally civilian-chaired, but appointment now rests solely with the Chief of Defence Forces.

The 27th Amendment bars any court from reviewing that appointment, extension or dismissal.
