Manchester — Panel finds Manchester City guilty on 114 of 115 charges.

Sheikh Mansour bin Zayed, brother of the [United Arab Emirates](country:AE)' president, owns the club.

The charges cover 2009 to 2018 and stem from leaked financial documents published by Der Spiegel.

Manchester City maintains its innocence and will appeal, and penalties range from a fine to stripped titles and relegation.

UAE officials privately warned a harsh verdict could damage [UK](country:GB) ties and threaten billions in Emirati investment.
