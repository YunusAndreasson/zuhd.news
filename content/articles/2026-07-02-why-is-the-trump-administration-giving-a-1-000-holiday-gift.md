Washington — Every July 4 baby gets $1,000 in the stock market.

The Treasury Department seeds a "Trump Account" per child, automatically invested in an S&P 500 index fund once parents contribute, per a senior Treasury official.

More than 6 million accounts have already been requested ahead of Saturday's launch.
