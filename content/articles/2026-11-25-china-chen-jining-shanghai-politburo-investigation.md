Shanghai — Party secretary Chen Jining, a Politburo member, is under investigation.

He is the fourth Politburo member pushed out in two years.

[China](country:CN)'s discipline watchdog cited serious violations of discipline and law, usually code for corruption, and gave no details.

"The takedown again shows that Xi Jinping's anticorruption campaign has no limits," said Mingda Qiu of Eurasia Group.

Xi is expected to clinch a fourth term at the party congress in the second half of 2027.
