Wolfsburg — Volkswagen will cut 50,000 more jobs worldwide.

It follows a 2024 deal that already cuts 35,000 jobs in [Germany](country:DE) by 2030.

Volkswagen says its European plants have 500,000 more vehicles of capacity than demand requires, citing Chinese competition.

Four German plants face uncertain futures from 2031 through 2034, Volkswagen said.
