Iran's navy vowed "deadly retaliation" for 87 sailors killed. A US submarine sank the IRIS Dena off Sri Lanka on March 4, the first Iranian warship lost since the Iran-Iraq war. Iran's drone-and-mine arsenal makes Gulf energy facilities the most likely targets for the promised counterstrike.
