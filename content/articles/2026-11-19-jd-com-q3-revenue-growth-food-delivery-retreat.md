Beijing — JD.com reported a 5.2 billion yuan quarterly operating profit.

Revenue rose 3.5% to 309.6 billion yuan as JD.com cut spending on food delivery.

Marketing spend fell 26.2% to 15.5 billion yuan, and losses at new businesses including delivery narrowed to 9.64 billion.

Electronics and appliance revenue still fell 0.7% as trade-in subsidies faded.

JD.com closed its 1.335 billion euro Ceconomy takeover on 12 November, and integration is the next test.
