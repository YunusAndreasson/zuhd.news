The Ocean Census catalogued 1,121 new marine species in 2025, while 2.2 million remain unnamed.

Nations pledged to protect 30% of oceans by 2030, but taxonomy is underfunded and shrinking as a profession.

Deep-sea mining and warming advance against a baseline that does not yet exist.
