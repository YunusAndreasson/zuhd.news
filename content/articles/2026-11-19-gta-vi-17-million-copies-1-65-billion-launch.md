New York — Grand Theft Auto VI has sold 17 million copies.

Sensor Tower puts revenue at $1.65 billion, lifting Take-Two, Sony and Microsoft shares.

PlayStation 5 accounts for about 75% of sales, and the game cost an estimated $1 billion to make.

Take-Two shares remain down 15.4% in 2026, Yahoo Finance data show.

Sensor Tower's figures are estimates, and the game's online mode has yet to launch.
