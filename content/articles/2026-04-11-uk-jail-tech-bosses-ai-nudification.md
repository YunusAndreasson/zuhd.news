London — Ofcom threatened jail for tech executives Friday. Grok circulated millions of non-consensual AI nude images; Ofcom will now hold platform executives personally criminally liable for failing to prevent them. No compliance threshold has been published; executives face arrest if platforms spread such material without adequate countermeasures.
