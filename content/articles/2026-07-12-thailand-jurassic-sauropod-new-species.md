Kalasin — A 20-meter, long-necked herbivore gets a name.

Uragasaurus kalasinensis lived 150 million years ago; a CT-scanned vertebra placed it in the Mamenchisauridae family, previously known almost only from [China](country:CN).

Mahasarakham University's team published the find in Nature this week, the first of its kind in Thailand.
