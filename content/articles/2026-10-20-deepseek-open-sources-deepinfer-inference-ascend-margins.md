China — DeepSeek says one cluster serves 155 trillion tokens a day.

Revenue works out to about $38 per card per hour at off-peak prices, DeepSeek said.

It open-sourced DeepInfer, the serving framework behind V4.1 Flash, which runs on Nvidia and Huawei Ascend chips.

DeepSeek did not release its cache-management and load-balancing code, which is tied to internal infrastructure.

DeepSeek says inference code for its new models will keep landing in DeepInfer.
