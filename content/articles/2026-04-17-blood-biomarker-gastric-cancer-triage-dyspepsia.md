Hong Kong — A blood test cuts endoscopy need 6-fold. Trained on 20 years of hospital data, a machine-learning model reduces the number-needed-to-scope from 44 to 7 using only standard blood panels. Gastric cancer kills disproportionately in East Asia and Latin America, where the tool requires no specialist lab.
