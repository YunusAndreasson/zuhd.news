Nigeria — Shell is selling its gas distribution arm, which serves more than 130 customers.

Shell has piped gas to [Nigeria](country:NG)'s factories and businesses for more than 25 years.

Vaaris Energy, a private Nigerian firm, takes 170 kilometres of pipeline across Ogun, Abia and Rivers states, and neither side disclosed a price.

Regulators must approve the deal, and Shell keeps its deepwater and Nigeria LNG stakes.
