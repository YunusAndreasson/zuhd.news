South Korea imports 70% of its crude through Hormuz. Iran is selectively blocking the strait — allowing some vessels through while denying passage to adversary-linked ships, turning a right of transit into a privilege. Seoul must choose between joining Washington's naval escort and negotiating directly with Tehran — each option threatens the other alliance.
