New Delhi — Rights groups documented 500 facial-recognition cameras at [India](country:IN)'s AI summit with no legal basis. The same systems run at railways and police precincts nationwide; no law requires impact assessments. Amnesty International said they target marginalized communities; India's data-protection bill remains unenacted.
