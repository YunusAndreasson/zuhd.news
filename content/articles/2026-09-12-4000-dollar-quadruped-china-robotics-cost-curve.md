Hangzhou — A capable robot dog now costs $4,000.

Police forces and farms can now treat quadruped robots as consumables, not capital.

[China](country:CN)'s Unitree builds the same motors and batteries at drone and EV volume that once cost six figures.

Unitree's stock has quintupled since its August public offering, valuing the firm near $34bn.
