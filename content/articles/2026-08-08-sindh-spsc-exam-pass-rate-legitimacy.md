Hyderabad — Only 70 of 4,300 candidates passed Sindh's exam.

Marks certificates showed a single 100-mark paper listed as 200, prompting Sindh's High Court to suspend the results.

A federal court has lifted the suspension, and interviews of the 70 successful candidates proceed as protests continue.
