New York — Crypto exchanges now offer tokenized stock futures.

OKX and Kraken added synthetic [US](country:US) stock futures as centralised crypto trading volumes fell 11% to $4.61 trillion, April exchange data show.

Tokenised equities lack the custody and settlement protections of standard brokerages; long-term success depends on filling those gaps.
