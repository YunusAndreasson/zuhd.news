Geelong — 60-metre flames engulfed Australia's Viva refinery overnight.

Viva produces 10% of domestic fuel; Australia holds just 38 days of petrol reserve, against the IEA minimum of 90.

Canberra secured 100 million litres of diesel from Brunei and South Korea; the damaged section produced high-octane petrol.
