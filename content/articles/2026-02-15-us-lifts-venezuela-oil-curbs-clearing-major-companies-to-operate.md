5 oil companies can now drill in Venezuela again. The US Treasury issued 2 general licences authorizing Chevron, BP, Eni, Shell, and Repsol to resume operations — the broadest sanctions rollback since Washington helped oust President Nicolas Maduro. All payments must route through a US-controlled fund, and the licences bar any dealings with Russian, Iranian, or Chinese entities, tying Venezuela's oil revival to American oversight.
