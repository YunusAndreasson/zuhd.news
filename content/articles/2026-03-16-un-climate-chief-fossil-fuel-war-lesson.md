European gas prices jumped 50% since the war began. UN climate secretary Simon Stiell told EU officials in Brussels that fossil fuel dependence "rips away national security" and that no enemy can blockade the sun. Italy and Hungary are pressing Brussels to weaken climate rules for short-term cost relief, a move Stiell called "completely delusional."
