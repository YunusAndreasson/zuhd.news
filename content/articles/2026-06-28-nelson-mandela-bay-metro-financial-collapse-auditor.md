Nelson Mandela Bay — [South Africa](country:ZA)'s Auditor-General found R28 billion in irregular metro spending, highest among all municipalities.

Missing wastewater licences are actively harming residents; the city's senior-management posts stand 67% vacant.

The metro faced a Tuesday deadline to halve irregular expenditure it has so far not reduced.
