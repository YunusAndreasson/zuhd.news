Silver Spring — The FDA approved the first Sanfilippo treatment Thursday.

Sanfilippo syndrome destroys children's brains and kills before adulthood, with no prior approved drug.

Called Fayuvi, the Ultragenyx therapy replaces the missing enzyme whose absence lets toxic sugar build up in cells.

Ultragenyx has not disclosed a price for the ultra-rare disease treatment.
