Dubai — Gulf solar-panel imports have fallen up to 90%.

The war disrupting shipping now stalls the region's own energy-transition equipment.

Solar panels are cheap per watt but bulky per container, making shipments unusually exposed to corridor disruption.

Utility-scale solar projects in [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) and the [UAE](country:AE) now face delays or costlier freight alternatives.
