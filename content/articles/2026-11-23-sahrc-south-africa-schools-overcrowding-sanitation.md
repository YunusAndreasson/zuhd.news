South Africa — 101 learners share one Limpopo classroom.

The Human Rights Commission found unsafe, overcrowded schools in all nine provinces.

At Van Coller Primary in the Eastern Cape, 2 of 30 toilets work, and Limpopo's mobile-classroom requests since 2022 went unanswered.

"The repeated identification of similar challenges from one monitoring cycle to another demands more than acknowledgement," said Commissioner Tshepo Madlingozi.

The commission wants a binding national plan from the Department of Basic Education within 60 days.
