Warsaw — Researchers found 1,655 ghost-authored records on one repository.

Fabricated findings, once cited by real papers, cannot be un-published.

Language models repeatedly invent the same names, and peer review checks method and argument, never whether an author exists.

Publishers and indexers still do not verify an author's identity before indexing a paper.
