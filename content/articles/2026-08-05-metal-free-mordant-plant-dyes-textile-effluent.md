London — A new process fixes plant dyes without metal.

Natural dyes have always needed a metal mordant to bond to fibre, so the effluent leaving dye mills carries chromium, copper or tin regardless of the colourant.

The finding is a single journal paper; industrial-scale testing has not been reported.
