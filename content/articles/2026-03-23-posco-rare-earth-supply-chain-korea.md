Seoul — POSCO, South Korea's largest steelmaker, is breaking China's near-total monopoly on rare earth magnets. Dysprosium and terbium, essential for EV motors, are produced almost entirely in China, giving Beijing leverage over the global EV supply chain. POSCO's US processing plant opens in 2027, its magnet factory in 2028 — both beyond China's reach.
