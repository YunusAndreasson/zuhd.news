Frankfurt — Euro zone inflation rose to 3.2% in May, Eurostat said.

Energy costs surged 10.9%; the European Central Bank is set to raise rates June 11 to contain the acceleration.

Markets price in 2-3 more hikes through autumn as the Iran conflict keeps energy elevated.
