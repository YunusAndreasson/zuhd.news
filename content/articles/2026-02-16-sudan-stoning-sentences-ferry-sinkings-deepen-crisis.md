2 women sit in Omdurman prison without lawyers, awaiting stoning. Courts in Khartoum and El Gedaref convicted them under Sudan's 1991 Criminal Act — prompting European Union envoy Wolfram Vetter to warn the sentences marked a "dangerous return to extremism" in a justice system collapsing under civil war. Rights lawyers reported thousands more detainees held in secret across army and Rapid Support Forces-controlled prisons as the country's displacement crisis surpasses 11 million people.
