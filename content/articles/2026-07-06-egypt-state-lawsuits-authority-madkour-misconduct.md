Cairo — No honors marked Hussein Madkour's exit.

Judicial sources in [Egypt](country:EG) say Madkour deducted roughly 75 million pounds from judges' salaries for commemorative events and an unapproved sports club.

Judges have filed complaints with the public prosecutor and justice ministry; no response has followed.
