Strait of Hormuz — At least 2 [Indian](country:IN) crew are missing after 6 ships were hit, India says.

Six hits is the war's most in a day, and Brent closed at $105.6 on 12 October.

A [US](country:US) jet disabled an Iran-linked tanker that officials say tried to run the blockade, and ships were hit across the strait.

The Revolutionary Guards say a 750,000-barrel tanker struck a mine on an unauthorized route.

[Abu Dhabi](country:AE)'s state oil company has not confirmed the Guards' claim that a hit supertanker was its own.
