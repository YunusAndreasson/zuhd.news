Khartoum — The pound fell below 8,000 per dollar.

Import costs, already inflated by war, now price out ordinary households.

The minister blames import demand, but gold, [Sudan](country:SD)'s main export, largely leaves the country informally, priced near $4,350 an ounce.

Fuel queues persist as the ministry blames insurance costs from Red Sea disruption.
