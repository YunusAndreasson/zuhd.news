Moscow — Hardware wallet sales doubled in a quarter.

Buyers are quietly moving crypto off exchanges before new withdrawal limits bind.

[Russia](country:RU) bars withdrawals from regulated depositories to personal wallets, though a transition runs to mid-2027.

A hardware wallet won't bypass an exchange's own withdrawal restrictions, lawyers told Russia's RBC.
