Chicago — A ransomware negotiator got 70 months for betrayal.

Angelo Martino leaked clients' insurance limits and negotiating positions to BlackCat hackers, helping inflate ransoms that prosecutors say totaled over $75 million.

Martino must forfeit his assets and pay 10% of future earnings to victims.
