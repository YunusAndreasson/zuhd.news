Turgutlu — A student shot 8 classmates outside a Turkish school.

It is [Turkey](country:TR)'s third school shooting since April, alarming educators about rising campus violence.

Officials say the child suspect used a rifle stored by his grandfather, with 8 to 11 students hospitalized.

Ankara had already tightened school gun controls after April's attacks, before Tuesday's shooting.

Investigators are still determining how the suspect's family stored the grandfather's weapon.
