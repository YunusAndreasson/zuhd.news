Dandong — A 3,026-metre bridge opened to North Korea 12 years late.

[China](country:CN) is widening trade with [North Korea](country:KP) despite sanctions it backed in 2017.

The four-lane crossing supersedes a narrow ageing bridge, and a Kyungnam University analyst expects cargo traffic to triple or quadruple.

North Korea has not resumed tourist visas for Chinese citizens, who once made up most of its visitors.

Seoul says it is monitoring the crossing, a day after Pyongyang's submarine cruise-missile tests.
