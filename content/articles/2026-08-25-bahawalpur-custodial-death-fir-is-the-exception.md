Bahawalpur — Daud drowned after constables Muhammad Khan and Aurangzeb allegedly beat him at a canal bridge.

A murder case against police is rare because the same station controls the evidence and the report.

Police say Daud jumped in while fleeing arrest as a suspected drug peddler, and the two constables remain unarrested.

Whether [Pakistan](country:PK)'s courts convict, not dismiss, the case will test that rarity.
