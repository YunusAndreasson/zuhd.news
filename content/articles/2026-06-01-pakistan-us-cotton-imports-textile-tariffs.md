Lahore — [Pakistan](country:PK) bought 68,030 US cotton bales last week, leading all nations.

Domestic mills import US cotton because energy tariffs make local cotton uncompetitive; 500 ginning factories have shut or cut output, industry bodies said.

Industry bodies warned of widespread bankruptcies without relief on energy tariffs and interest rates.
