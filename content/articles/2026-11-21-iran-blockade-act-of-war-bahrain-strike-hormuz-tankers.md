Manama — [Bahrain](country:BH) says an Iranian strike set an industrial site ablaze.

Two tankers were also hit in Hormuz, which carried a fifth of world oil before the war.

[Iran](country:IR)'s Revolutionary Guards said they hit [US](country:US) bases at Sheikh Isa and Juffair.

Iran's army says a tanker hit a mine, but the US military says it cleared the main routes.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 31 December an 11% chance, down 11 points in a week.
