Abuja — Nigeria's anti-trafficking agency rescued more than 180 children.

Traffickers posed as charities, using real registration papers to gain families' trust.

Displaced Benue families were offered free schooling, but children were sold through orphanages as fake adoptions.

The agency has begun a manhunt for suspects, including a named charity executive.
