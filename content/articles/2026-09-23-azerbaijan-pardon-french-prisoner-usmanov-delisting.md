Baku — Aliyev pardoned 20 prisoners shortly after the EU delisted Alisher Usmanov.

Martin Ryan, a Frenchman jailed in [Azerbaijan](country:AZ) on espionage charges, was among them.

The pardons followed a broader diplomatic deal struck after [France](country:FR) lobbied for Usmanov's delisting.

The EU Council extended sanctions on roughly 3,000 others for 3 years, and also delisted Mikhail Fridman.
