Washington — Boeing now holds both US next-generation fighter programmes.

US military officials had argued the [US](country:US) industrial base could not support two next-generation fighters at once.

Boeing can draw on F-47 design work, production experience and supply chains from its Air Force win.

Air Force Gen. Dale White said two weeks ago that building both jets at once still strains industrial capacity.

The Navy also ordered 30 drone ships averaging $40 million each, with deliveries starting as early as fiscal 2027's fourth quarter.
