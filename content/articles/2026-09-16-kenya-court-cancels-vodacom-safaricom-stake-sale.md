Nairobi — A court unwound an already-completed $1.6 billion deal.

Safaricom runs M-Pesa, the mobile money system most Kenyan households depend on.

The High Court found [South Africa](country:ZA)'s Vodacom concealed material facts about its Safaricom purchase from [Kenya](country:KE)'s public.

Vodacom plans to appeal, while the 15% stake reverts to the government for now.
