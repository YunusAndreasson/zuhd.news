title: "Trial Cuts Nigeria Child Marriage"
date: "2026-03-11T00:00:00Z"
source: "Nature"
sourceUrl: "https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-00720-8"
category: "science"
---

A trial cut child marriage from 86% to 21%. Single-focus interventions — cash alone, schooling alone — failed where a combined programme of cash, girls' schooling, and community engagement succeeded. Policymakers have not committed to national scaling despite Nigeria having the world's highest number of child brides.
