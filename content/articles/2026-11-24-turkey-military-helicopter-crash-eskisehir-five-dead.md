Eskisehir — 5 crew died when an AS-532 helicopter crashed.

[Turkey](country:TR) fields NATO's second-largest army, and its defence ministry has explained neither crash.

The Air Force helicopter went down on a training flight shortly after taking off from Eskisehir.

On 12 November an army Sikorsky S-70 crashed near Malatya, killing 4 crew.

The Air Force is investigating, but no findings have been announced.
