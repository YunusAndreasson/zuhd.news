Hayya — Drones set oil tanks ablaze, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

The pipeline through Hayya carries [South Sudan](country:SS)'s oil to the Marsa Bashayer export terminal.

The Sudan Tribune, citing local sources, said a drone also hit a nearly finished Hayya substation meant to power Port Sudan.

TASS said Sudanese authorities accuse neighbouring Ethiopia of aiding the rebels, and quoted no response.
