Washington — Trump revived tariffs via Section 301.

The US Trade Representative proposed 10–12.5% duties on 60 economies — [India](country:IN), [China](country:CN), [Pakistan](country:PK) among them — after courts struck down Trump's emergency-powers tariffs in February.

Public hearings open July 7; a separate manufacturing-capacity probe may stack additional duties on top.
