Deportations from the [US](country:US) cut the remittances feeding families in [Guatemala](country:GT)'s Dry Corridor.

Erratic rains ruin subsistence crops and farm jobs, and deported workers return to villages without income.

Some Maya Ch'orti' communities still harvest abundantly with drought adaptation they organised themselves.

That adaptation work is running out of funding.
