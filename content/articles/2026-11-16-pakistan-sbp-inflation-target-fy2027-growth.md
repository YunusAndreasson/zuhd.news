Karachi — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s central bank sees inflation returning to 5-7% in the 2027 fiscal year.

Inflation has run in double digits since April, after an energy price shock.

The bank assumes remittances hold the current account deficit within 1% of GDP and $3 billion in Eurobond proceeds build reserves.

Worsening El Niño conditions threaten farm output, the bank says, though early data show higher kharif crop output.

The bank aims for reserves of four months of import cover.
