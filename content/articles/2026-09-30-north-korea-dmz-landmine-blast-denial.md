Seoul — Landmines wounded 3 South Korean soldiers in the Demilitarized Zone.

[South Korea](country:KR) demanded an apology and vowed staged countermeasures over what it calls an armistice violation.

The UN Command, which investigated on site, identified an active North Korean anti-personnel mine on South Korea's side of the border.

[North Korea](country:KP) denies it, and leader Kim Jong Un's sister Kim Yo Jong calls the claim groundless.

The UN Command says it will handle the armistice violation through established armistice mechanisms.
