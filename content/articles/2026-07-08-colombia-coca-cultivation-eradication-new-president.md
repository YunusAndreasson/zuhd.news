Bogotá — [Colombia](country:CO)'s coca cultivation rose 3.5% in 2024, UN data show.

Nearly half of Colombia's coca now grows in just 10 municipalities, UN data show, concentrating what eradication could target.

President-elect Abelardo De la Espriella takes office in August vowing industrial-scale eradication.
