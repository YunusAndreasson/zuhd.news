Amsterdam — Hackers breached Basic-Fit and stole member data. Europe's largest gym chain, with 3.5 million members in 5 countries, confirmed an unauthorized download of personal data from its systems. GDPR triggers simultaneous notification to 5 national regulators; Basic-Fit has not named the attacker or data types stolen.
