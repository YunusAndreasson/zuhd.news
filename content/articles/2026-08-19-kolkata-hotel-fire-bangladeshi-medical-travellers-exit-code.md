Kolkata — Nine died before dawn, five confirmed Bangladeshi nationals.

Four belonged to one family seeking treatment unavailable in [Bangladesh](country:BD).

Cross-border patients fill Kolkata's unlicensed guesthouses near hospitals, buildings with one staircase and no alarm system.

Bangladesh's high commission in Kolkata has opened a 24-hour helpline as the toll may rise.
