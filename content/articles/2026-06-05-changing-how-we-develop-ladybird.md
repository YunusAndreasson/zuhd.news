Ladybird, the only browser engine independent of Chromium or WebKit, closed all public contributions Saturday.

AI tools collapse the effort signal that made large patches a proxy for good faith, the project said, making external contributions unsafe.

Whether the maintainer-only model can sustain pace toward a first alpha is unproven.
