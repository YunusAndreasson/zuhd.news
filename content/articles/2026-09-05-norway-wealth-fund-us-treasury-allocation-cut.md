The world's biggest sovereign investor is treating [US](country:US) debt as one bond among many.

Norges Bank proposes cutting government bonds from 70% to 50% of its portfolio, favoring higher-yield US mortgage debt.

Norway's Finance Ministry reviews the plan before an expert report due 25 January 2027.
