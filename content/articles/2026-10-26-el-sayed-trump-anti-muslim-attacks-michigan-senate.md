Detroit — Trump wants a metal detector for Senate candidate El-Sayed.

The Michigan race against Republican Mike Rogers could decide Senate control for two years.

Trump accused El-Sayed of wanting Sharia law, and Rogers links him to the Muslim Brotherhood, Hamas and Hezbollah without evidence.

"My name and my faith are not the reason you can't afford your gas," El-Sayed said at a Detroit news conference.

Michigan voters decide the seat on 3 November.
