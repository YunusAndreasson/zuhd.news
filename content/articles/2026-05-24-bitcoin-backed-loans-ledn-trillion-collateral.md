New York — Bitcoin-backed consumer loans could reach $1 trillion within a decade, Ledn projects.

88% of surveyed holders say they would borrow against bitcoin; only 14% do — a gap Ledn calls a trust deficit, not a demand problem.

No [US](country:US) or EU regulation covers bitcoin-backed lending; the market is growing without rules.
