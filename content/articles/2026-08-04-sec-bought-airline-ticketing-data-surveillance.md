Washington — Over a billion ticketing records, sold by the airlines' own broker.

ARC, owned by Delta, United and American, sold the SEC names, card numbers and itineraries for flights worldwide — a purchase needs no warrant a demand would require.

The airlines' broker stopped selling after prior reporting; the SEC's stash of already-bought records remains.
