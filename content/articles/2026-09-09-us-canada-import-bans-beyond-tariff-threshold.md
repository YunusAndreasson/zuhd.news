Ottawa — The US banned Canadian dairy, alcohol and motorcycles outright.

A ban removes the market entirely, unlike a tariff that still lets trade happen at a price.

The US move follows [Canada](country:CA)'s retaliatory tariffs on $20 billion of American goods, extending an 18-month trade war beyond August's 50% duties.

The ban takes effect September 29, days before Ottawa's Carney addresses the European Parliament.
