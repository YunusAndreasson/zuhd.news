Mecklenburg-Vorpommern — A coalition rejecting Palantir still plans automated police data analysis.

Data police may review by hand today could be analysed automatically in future.

The Social Democrats, Left and Greens bar Palantir's software but promise automated analysis compliant with EU law and the constitution.

It also promotes open-source software and names no further expansion of video surveillance.

The agreement calls data sovereignty and data security mandatory conditions before the system arrives.
