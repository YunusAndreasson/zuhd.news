Manila — [Iran](country:IR) is exploring a Washington dirty bomb, unnamed officials say.

The report offers no evidence, and the White House sees no credible threat to the homeland.

A dirty bomb packs radioactive material around conventional explosives, contaminating a few city blocks without a nuclear blast.

Iran's foreign ministry calls the allegation baseless, and US agencies reportedly disagree on whether Tehran has the personnel.

Trump says the [US](country:US) takes the claim seriously as a third carrier arrives in the region.
