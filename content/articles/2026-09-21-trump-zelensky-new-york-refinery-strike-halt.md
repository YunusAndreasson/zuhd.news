Kyiv — Trump repeatedly asked [Ukraine](country:UA) to stop striking refineries.

Brent crude stood at $130.80 a barrel on 15 September, up 27.9% in a week.

Strikes on [Russia](country:RU)'s refineries cut diesel supply, and a source on the call says Trump kept returning to diesel.

Trump announced on 14 September that both sides agreed to spare energy infrastructure, yet strikes resumed within hours.

Zelensky and Trump are expected to meet 22 September, the day Trump addresses the UN General Assembly.
