San Francisco — A "small subset" of Coinbase customers' contact details leaked.

Exposed emails and phone numbers feed phishing and social-engineering scams.

The breach began at analytics vendor Amplitude, and Coinbase says its own systems saw no unauthorized access.

Coinbase says customer funds are safe but has not said how many customers were affected.
