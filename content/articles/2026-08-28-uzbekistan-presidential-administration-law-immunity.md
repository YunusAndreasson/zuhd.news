Tashkent — Saida Mirziyoyeva's office now has legal immunity.

The president's daughter holds a legally shielded post, not an informal role.

[Uzbekistan](country:UZ)'s law writes succession into the constitutional order her father Shavkat Mirziyoyev built on reform promises.

The immunity clause leaves no legal route to challenge the office while she holds it.
