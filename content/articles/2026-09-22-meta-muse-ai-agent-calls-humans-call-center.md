Muse hands its calls to a trained human agent.

Callers cannot tell whether an AI or a person is actually speaking to them.

Meta pitched Muse as autonomous, but a human agent places the call and files a transcript afterward.

An internal Meta message-board post warned the feature "could portray us as their AI is not good enough."

Meta has not disclosed how often Muse calls are human, and employee privacy concerns remain unresolved.
