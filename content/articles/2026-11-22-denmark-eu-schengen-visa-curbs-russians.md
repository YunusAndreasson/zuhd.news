Copenhagen — [Denmark](country:DK) wants the EU to halt tourist visas for Russians.

Danish security officials say [Russia](country:RU)'s intelligence services planned to kill a Ukrainian rapper in September.

Its note also asks Schengen states to stop recognising non-biometric Russian passports and to issue more limited visas barring wider Schengen travel.

The Russian embassy in Copenhagen says Denmark's accusations are not backed with proof.

Denmark presents the proposals to EU ambassadors in Brussels on Monday.
