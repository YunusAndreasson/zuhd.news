Lassa — 35 students still missing after a Borno school raid.

Disguised as market traders, gunmen abducted students sitting national exams in Lassa; 8 have been rescued, the ward councillor said.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s Borno State introduced no new school security after a similar mass abduction 6 weeks prior, the councillor confirmed.
