Osnabrück — Volkswagen workers face a weapons ultimatum.

Volkswagen and Israel's Rafael Advanced Systems agreed to build Iron Dome parts at a plant threatened with closure, after management said arms work or the factory shuts.

Over 20 workers petitioned against the shift; hours there were already cut to four days.
