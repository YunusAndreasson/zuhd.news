Imran Khan lost 85% vision in his right eye. A court-appointed lawyer confirmed the former prime minister, 73, suffers from central retinal vein occlusion after months in Rawalpindi's Adiala Jail. Opposition lawmakers staged an overnight sit-in at Parliament House demanding Khan's transfer to Shifa International Hospital. Parliamentary Affairs Minister Tariq Fazal Chaudhry announced Saturday the government would move Khan to a hospital and form a medical board. Khan's supporters won the most seats as independents in the 2024 election but were blocked from forming a government, and his deteriorating health in detention has become the sharpest confrontation between the opposition and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif's administration.
