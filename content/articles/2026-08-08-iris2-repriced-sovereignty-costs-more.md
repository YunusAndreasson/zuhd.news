Brussels — IRIS2's bill just rose to €15.6bn.

The overrun tests EU appetite for paying extra for orbital independence from US providers.

The constellation grew from 290 to 348 satellites, since continuous coverage requires a fixed number in orbit.

Commissioner Kubilius says initial service could begin in 2029, a year ahead of the original plan.
