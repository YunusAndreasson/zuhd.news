Belgium sheltered Ecuador's ex-president from extradition in 2022. Rafael Correa, sentenced to 8 years for corruption, used that asylum status to travel freely to Uruguay this week. The opposition demanded an explanation after Uruguay's foreign minister acknowledged no law barred his entry.
