Tokyo — [Japan](country:JP)'s Sony will halt PlayStation game discs by 2028.

A digital purchase is a revocable license, not property — the same shift already remade music and now targets film and software.

Modders behind the Omnidrive project are racing to preserve disk-era libraries before physical copies vanish.
