Kourou — Workers in Kourou demanding a 1.6% wage rise blocked gates to [France](country:FR)'s sole Ariane 6 launch site.

Arianespace chief David Cavaillolès said the base is fully operational and the strike won't delay the scheduled launch.

The Ariane 6 window opens 17 June at 12:53 Central European Time.
