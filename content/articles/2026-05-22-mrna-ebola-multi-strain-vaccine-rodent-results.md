Wuhan — mRNA shot covers 3 Ebola strains in mice.

No approved vaccine covers Bundibugyo, now spreading in [DR Congo](country:CD); this shot encodes all 3 strains' glycoproteins in one nanoparticle.

Primate trials must precede human testing; researchers caution that licensing a multivalent vaccine is harder than a single-strain approval.
