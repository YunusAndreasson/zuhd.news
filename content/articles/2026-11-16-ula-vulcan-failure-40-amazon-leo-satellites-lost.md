Cape Canaveral — United Launch Alliance's 100% success record ended Sunday, 10 minutes after liftoff.

Amazon Leo has 398 satellites up, 12% of the 3,236 it needs.

Vulcan failed to deliver all 40 satellites to orbit, and the company says it has no data on the cause.

Investigators could ground Vulcan for months, and the Space Force has yet to clear it for security launches.
