Lagos — Barricades sealed Air Peace's terminals in two cities.

Stranded passengers now sit at the center of a dispute that exposes the regulator itself.

Unions accuse the airline of blocking unionisation and withholding a 5% ticket charge that funds [Nigeria](country:NG)'s aviation agencies.

The aviation minister's return is expected to reopen stalled talks between the parties.
