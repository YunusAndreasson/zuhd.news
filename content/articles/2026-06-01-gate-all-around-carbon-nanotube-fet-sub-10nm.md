London — Below 10nm, silicon leaks; nanotube transistors handle it.

A gate-all-around carbon-nanotube design with high-k dielectric gains electrostatic control, cutting leakage 50% and improving switching ratio 84%, simulations show.

The device is simulation-only; fabrication and real-world testing remain the next threshold.
