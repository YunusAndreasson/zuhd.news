Stanford — Researchers tracked 35,012 diabetes patients over 3.5 years; those on semaglutide had 794 fractures versus 1,045 on other drugs, a 15% reduction.

Whether the drug or its weight-loss effect protects bone is unresolved.

Results are not peer-reviewed.
