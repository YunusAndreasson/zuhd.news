Washington — US visa bonds hit 50 countries, topping $15,000. 97% of ~1,000 current bond-holders complied with travel terms, contradicting the administration's stated overstay rationale. State added 12 mostly-African nations Wednesday, with rules taking effect April 2 and the department reserving the right to expand further.
