No Friday prayers at Al-Aqsa this Ramadan. Israel extended the closure through Eid and beyond, citing the Iran war — the first Ramadan closure since it occupied East Jerusalem in 1967. Waqf officials say Israeli authorities placed cameras inside the Dome of the Rock, surveillance they warn may outlast any ceasefire.
