New Delhi — [India](country:IN) last counted castes in 1931.

Caste data will determine which groups qualify for job and education quotas.

Congress says the questionnaire lacks a pre-set caste list, risking answers too scattered to tabulate.

Self-enumeration opens August 17, with snow-bound regions counted first from September 1.
