London — An autonomous lab produced growth rules a scientist can read, not just a recipe.

That lets the discovery transfer to new labs instead of staying locked in one machine.

The system tuned sputtering runs on gallium oxide films, cutting a key defect score below prior published results.

A human then applied those rules and beat the machine's own best result.
