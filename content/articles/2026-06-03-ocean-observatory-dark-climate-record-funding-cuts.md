Newport, Oregon — Decades of Pacific climate records end this month under funding cuts.

The ocean monitoring network took decades to build; continuous data, once severed, cannot be recovered retroactively.

The retrieval vessel departs the Oregon coast this month to recover the buoy, with no replacement announced.
