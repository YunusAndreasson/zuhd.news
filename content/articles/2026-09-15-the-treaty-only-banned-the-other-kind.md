Washington — The US confirmed it has weapons already in orbit.

It is the first government admission of orbital arms, a precedent [China](country:CN) and [Russia](country:RU) can now cite.

Air Force Secretary Troy Meink gave no details, citing a treaty that bans only mass-destruction weapons in orbit.

Meink would not say whether the systems are kinetic, leaving allies and rivals to guess.
