Pohang — An X-ray laser filmed azobenzene flipping in methanol solution.

The experiment addresses a long-standing debate over how azobenzene converts from its trans to cis form.

A 400 nm laser triggered the switch, and X-ray pulses recorded molecular structure at 140 time delays up to 100 picoseconds.

The authors say the method could reveal mechanisms of other organic reactions in solution.
