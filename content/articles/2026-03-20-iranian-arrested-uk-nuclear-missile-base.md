An Iranian tried breaching Britain's nuclear submarine base Thursday. Faslane houses Britain's entire nuclear deterrent and had been named an Iranian target since the Hormuz strikes began. Investigators have not determined whether the 2 suspects acted on state orders — a finding that would mark the first confirmed Iranian state operation on British soil.
