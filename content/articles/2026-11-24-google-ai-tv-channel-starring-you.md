United States — Google filed a patent for a 24/7 AI channel called "My Channel."

It would build videos from your demographic data and watch history.

A prompt in the filing casts the user as protagonist, and an advertising server inserts ads into the result.

The patent is a filing, not a product, and no launch has been announced.
