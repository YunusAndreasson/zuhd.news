Beijing — Sinopec earned 13.36 billion yuan in the third quarter.

Its nine-month fuel sales fell 11.4%, as [China](country:CN)'s fuel consumption fell 8.6%, Sinopec said.

Refining earnings before interest and tax reached 30.1 billion yuan in nine months, against 7 billion a year earlier.

Its chemicals segment still lost 1.69 billion yuan, and ethylene output fell 19%.

Fourth-quarter results will show whether refining margins hold as demand keeps falling.
