London — Half of clinical E. coli globally resists sulfamethoxazole.

Class 1 integrons carry the resistance cassettes between unrelated bacteria in farm runoff and sewage — agriculture drives hospital resistance, not the reverse.

Constructed wetlands remove 86–99% of the drug but leave the resistance gene; sul4 remains untracked globally.
