Karachi — Sindh cut lady health worker funding 75%, the union says.

Lady health workers deliver vaccines, prenatal care and family planning door-to-door where clinics don't exist; the union says Sindh has moved their funding to a private company with no governing board.

The union plans province-wide protests starting next week in Karachi.
