Chicago — Pancreatic cancer drug doubled survival in 500-person trial.

RAS proteins drive cancer growth but their smooth surface resisted drugs for decades; daraxonrasib now shuts off all 3 family members.

The [US](country:US)-developed drug awaits regulatory review; trials combining it with single-mutation KRAS inhibitors could extend survival further.
