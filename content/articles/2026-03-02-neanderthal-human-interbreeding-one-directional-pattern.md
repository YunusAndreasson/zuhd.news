---
title: "Neanderthal Men Joined Human Groups"
date: "2026-02-26T00:00:00Z"
source: "Nature"
sourceUrl: "https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-00583-z"
category: "science"
---

Neanderthal men, not women, fathered children with early humans. Researchers found that Neanderthal X chromosomes carry 62% more human DNA than other chromosomes — meaning human mothers, not human fathers, drove ancient cross-species reproduction. Study co-author Alexander Platt says no study has yet searched for the reciprocal pattern in Neanderthal genomes.
