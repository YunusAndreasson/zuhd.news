Karachi — Sindh sells state wheat at Rs12,500 per 100kg from 15 October.

Karachi wheat trades near Rs13,200 and flour at Rs165 to Rs170 a kg, both up since 1 October.

The province holds 346,414 tonnes, and 190,000 from a state storage agency plus 300,000 imported will lift releasable stock to 836,414.

The chief minister ordered the food department to monitor the market and pass the subsidised price to consumers.
