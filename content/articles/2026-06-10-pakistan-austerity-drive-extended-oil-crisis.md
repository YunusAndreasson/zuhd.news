Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s austerity measures stretch into their 4th month.

The Middle East war forced 4-day government work weeks and fuel rationing for official vehicles in March; Pakistan imports most crude oil.

The Intelligence Bureau is auditing compliance; results are due before June 30.
