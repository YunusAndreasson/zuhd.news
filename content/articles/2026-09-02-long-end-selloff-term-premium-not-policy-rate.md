New York — The 10-year Treasury yield hit 4.81%.

Emerging-market borrowers price debt off this curve and buffer none of the shock.

Unlike 2022's rate-hike shock, this selloff is concentrated at the long end, pricing government borrowing, not policy rates.

Traders now watch whether the yield breaches 5%, a threshold that could unsettle stocks further.
