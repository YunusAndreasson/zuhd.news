Abu Dhabi — [Ukraine](country:UA), Russia and the US meet on 10 November.

Europe supplies most of Kyiv's support, yet no European officials are expected at the table.

Rustem Umerov leads Kyiv's team, lawmaker David Arakhamia confirmed, after media wrongly reported Kyrylo Budanov would.

Moscow has rejected a full ceasefire and still demands Ukraine cede Donbas.

EU leaders discuss on 13 November how the bloc is represented at peace talks.
