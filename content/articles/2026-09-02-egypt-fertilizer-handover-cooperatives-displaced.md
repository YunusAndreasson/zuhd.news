Cairo — Five governorates lost their fertilizer cooperatives this month.

Control of subsidized fertilizer means control of what farmers can afford to plant.

The Future of Egypt authority now allocates fertilizer directly to villages in [Egypt](country:EG), checking it against census records the cooperatives used to run.

A nationwide takeover is due in October, an agriculture ministry official said.
