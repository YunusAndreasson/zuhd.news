Lagos — The naira fell 3.6% this month, to 1,403.50 per dollar.

Food inflation is 19.13% and households feel little relief, the government acknowledged.

Investors are leaving [Nigeria](country:NG) over next year's election and expectations of higher [US](country:US) interest rates.

Reserves held $54.87 billion on 12 November and inflation fell for a 5th month, Finance Minister Taiwo Oyedele said.

The Federal Reserve decides on 9 December, and Polymarket traders give Tinubu a 70% chance of winning in 2027.
