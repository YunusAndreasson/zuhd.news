Paris — Henon launches to watch the Sun in 2027.

The CubeSat will use electric propulsion to reach a solar orbit ahead of Earth, warning of storms that can disable satellites.

It launches as a secondary payload alongside the European Space Agency's exoplanet-hunting PLATO telescope in early 2027.
