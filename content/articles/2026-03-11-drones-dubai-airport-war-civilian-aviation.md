2 drones fell near Dubai International Airport Wednesday, injuring 4 people. Dubai handles 90 million passengers a year and connects Asia, Africa, and Europe; air traffic continued operating normally after the incident. No party has claimed responsibility, and UAE authorities have not confirmed the drones' origin or whether they were intercepted.
