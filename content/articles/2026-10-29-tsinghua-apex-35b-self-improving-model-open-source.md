Beijing — A 27-year-old professor's startup open-sourced Apex-35B, built partly by AI.

Apex-35B scored 60.9 on PaperBench, above 753-billion-parameter GLM-5.3's 42.5, the company reports.

Its automated research system generated training data, designed experiments and tuned training, lifting throughput 45% to 60%, the company says.

All benchmark scores come from the company's own release.

Weights are public under Apache 2.0, so outside labs can now test the claims.
