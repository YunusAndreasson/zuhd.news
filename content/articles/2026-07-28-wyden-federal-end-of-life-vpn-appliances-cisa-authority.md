Washington — Sen. Wyden wants obsolete VPNs purged from agencies.

An internet-facing VPN box terminates encryption inside a network's perimeter, so one unpatched appliance becomes a credentialled foothold for intruders.

The senator asked three federal agencies to lead the effort; none has confirmed authority to compel removal.
