AI images showed 500,000 at a rally; police counted 5,000. Parties across the spectrum used AI tools to flood Facebook and TikTok ahead of Thursday's first vote since last year's protests toppled the government. Police have handled 150 deepfake cases; polling opens Thursday with no platform accountability in place.
