Islamabad — Diesel rose Re0.31 a litre while petrol fell Rs2.71.

Diesel mainly fuels heavy transport and power plants, so its price reaches the public at large.

From Thursday [Pakistan](country:PK)'s petrol sells at Rs379.31 and diesel at Rs390.11 a litre, including Rs114 and Rs100 in taxes and duties.

Diesel peaked at Rs520.35 on April 3, after the [US](country:US)-Iran war began February 28.

The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority revises prices daily, and markets must close by 9pm under reinstated austerity.
