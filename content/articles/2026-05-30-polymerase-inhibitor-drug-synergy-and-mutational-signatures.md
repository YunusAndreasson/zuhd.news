London — Drug pairs showed synergy against respiratory syncytial virus and parainfluenza type 3 in lab tests.

Combining 2 polymerase inhibitors exposed mutational signatures — fingerprints that reveal resistance before it takes hold.

Researchers said findings could guide clinical trials for respiratory viruses burdening millions, especially children.
