Abuja — 7 properties, $2 million, forfeited permanently.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s Supreme Court restored a lower court's non-conviction forfeiture order against ex-central bank governor Godwin Emefiele, overturning an appeals court reversal.

The ruling adds to 753 housing units already seized from Emefiele; he still faces separate corruption trials in Lagos and Abuja.
