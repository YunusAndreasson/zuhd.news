New York — Nvidia fell 6%, AMD 12% and Micron 11% on Thursday.

Midterm winners pledged moratoriums on the AI data centres these three chipmakers supply.

The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped more than 8%, and the Nasdaq Composite fell over 5% before paring losses.

IG analyst Chris Beauchamp warned: "If this goes south, this market action turns from rotation to general panic."

Democrats take control of Congress in January, when the pledged data centre moratoriums face their first test.
