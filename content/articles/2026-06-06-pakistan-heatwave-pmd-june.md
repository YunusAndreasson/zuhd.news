Islamabad — Sindh forecast 48-51°C for 6 days.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s meteorological department issued a heatwave warning for Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Punjab and Sindh; temperatures run 4-6°C above normal through June 12.

The department warned electricity demand will spike; it advised vulnerable groups to avoid sun between 10am and 4pm.
