Linköping — Chemotherapy kills everywhere to treat one place. Researchers combined ion pumps with click chemistry so drugs activate only where ion gradients trigger the reaction — at the tumor site, not systemically. The approach has not yet entered clinical trials; it was demonstrated in a lab setting.
