40 Japanese entities lost access to Chinese dual-use exports overnight. Beijing banned ships, aircraft, radar, and missile sales to Mitsubishi Heavy Industries and other defence contractors — retaliation after Tokyo suggested a Taiwan attack could warrant a Japanese military response. Japan's defence industry must now find alternative suppliers or accept production gaps.
