Managua — Nicaragua severed relations with Italy over a 1978 killing.

[Nicaragua](country:NI) has sheltered Red Brigades fugitive Alessio Casimirri, convicted in premier Aldo Moro's murder, since 1983; its constitution bars extraditing citizens.

Rome's foreign minister keeps demanding Casimirri's extradition, with no bilateral treaty to compel it.
