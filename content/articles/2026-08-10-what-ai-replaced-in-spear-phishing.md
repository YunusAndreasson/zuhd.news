Seoul — Kimsuky now writes phishing bait with artificial intelligence.

A tactic once reserved for elite targets can now scale to hundreds.

Genians, a South Korean firm, says the [North Korea](country:KP)-linked group runs offline AI tools to avoid detection while forging documents.

Genians has not said how many campaigns already used the AI tools undetected.
