Phnom Penh — A year later, the shock still hasn't faded.

Development workers describe the sudden 2025 collapse of the [US](country:US) aid agency as feeling "like an earthquake" across Cambodia's aid-dependent sector.

Programs once funded by Washington remain without a replacement backer.
