London — "Biodegradable" guarantees nothing without specifying conditions and timeframe.

Researchers say most biodegradable items require industrial composting — heat and microbes landfills and oceans lack.

Ask three questions of any biodegradable label: degrades into what, under what conditions, in what timeframe.
