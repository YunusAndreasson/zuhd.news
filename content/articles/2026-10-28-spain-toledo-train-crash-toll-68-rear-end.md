Toledo — At least 68 people died when one train hit another.

It is [Spain](country:ES)'s third fatal rail crash this year, after 46 died in January.

After a signalling failure halted traffic, a controller cleared the Renfe train past a red light at about 200km/h.

Transport minister Óscar Puente promised a full investigation into why the controller cleared the train.
