Brussels — Europol and Microsoft seized more than 300 servers supporting 3 malware operations.

Microsoft framed the action as targeting the cybercrime "supply chain" — shared infrastructure rented by unskilled operators.

No arrests were announced; Microsoft described the supply-chain approach as its new standard for enforcement.
