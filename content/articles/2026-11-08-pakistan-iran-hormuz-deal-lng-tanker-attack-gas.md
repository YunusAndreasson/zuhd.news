Islamabad — Gas rationing and nighttime power cuts already grip [Pakistan](country:PK).

An attack on a Pakistan-bound gas tanker has put its safe-passage diplomacy with [Iran](country:IR) in doubt.

Reduced liquefied natural gas deliveries since the US-Israeli attacks on Iran disrupted daily life and business, and the Hormuz route is the chokepoint.

Pakistan now faces urgent pressure to diversify its gas suppliers and supply routes.
