Zagreb — Tens of thousands marched in [Croatia](country:HR)'s capital Saturday.

More than 30,000 tonnes of hazardous waste sits near Gospić's drinking-water supply.

Waste arrived from elsewhere in the EU since 2023 onto karst terrain that spreads contamination fast, complicating any cleanup.

Organisers gave the government 20 days for a cleanup plan, 3 months to remove all waste.
