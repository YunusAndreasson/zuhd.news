Attackers set 2 tankers ablaze off Iraq's coast overnight. Al-Faw, Iraq's main oil export terminal, shut port operations, extending the energy blockade into waters Iraq cannot defend. Iraq's Ministry of Oil called for "protection of maritime routes" with no named responder.
