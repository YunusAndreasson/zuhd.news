Qamishli — Rojava's autonomous administration dissolved on August 25.

Fadia Ismail, whose son died fighting ISIS, calls it "the imposition of defeat."

A January government offensive forced the Syrian Democratic Forces into integrating with [Syria](country:SY)'s state under Ahmed al-Sharaa.

SDF commander Mazloum Abdi now serves as special adviser to the president.
