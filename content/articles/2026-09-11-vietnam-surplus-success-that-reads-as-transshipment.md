It makes Hanoi a target for tariffs meant to catch relabeled Chinese goods.

Factories moved from [China](country:CN) into Vietnam to dodge US tariffs, but many components still originate there.

Washington has not signaled whether Vietnam will face new tariffs or transshipment probes.
