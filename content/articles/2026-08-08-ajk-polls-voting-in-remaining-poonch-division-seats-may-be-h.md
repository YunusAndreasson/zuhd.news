Muzaffarabad — Kashmir splits its election into phases for security.

460,000 voters cast ballots Monday while 1.15 million more still wait.

Security concerns delayed the remaining Poonch constituencies to an expected Aug 20-21 vote, the election chief said.

The election commission will announce the Poonch and Sudhnoti dates within a week.
