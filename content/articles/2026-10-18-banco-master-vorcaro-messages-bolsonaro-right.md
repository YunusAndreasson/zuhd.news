Brazil — Vorcaro told a pastor "we support the right," intercepts show.

In September, 34% told Datafolha the scandal involves more left-wing politicians, 27% more right-wing.

Vorcaro, who owns Banco Master, protested in April 2024 after congressman Nikolas Ferreira reposted a story about a Master-organised event.

In a March 2025 audio, Ferreira apologised, saying he did not know Master was the bank behind the event.

He then asked Vorcaro for help, the messages show.
