Kampala — Zero new Ebola cases for nearly three weeks.

[Russia](country:RU)'s Rospotrebnadzor withdrew its field team after the Sudan-strain outbreak stabilized, crediting donated mobile labs and Russian-trained Ugandan staff.

Rospotrebnadzor says it stays in contact with [Uganda](country:UG)'s health service should cases resurface.
