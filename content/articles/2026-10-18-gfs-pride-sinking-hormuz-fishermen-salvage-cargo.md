Iran — Hundreds of fishermen are hauling iPhones from the sea.

The GFS Pride is the third ship sunk since February, the second in 24 hours.

[Singapore](country:SG), the flag state, says a fire sank it, while the Revolutionary Guards' navy says it struck a mine.

[Iran](country:IR)'s deputy cyber police chief warned that salvaged phones may be scams or corroded by seawater.

Polymarket traders give an Iran-Oman Hormuz agreement by 31 October a 7% chance, down 7 points in a week.
