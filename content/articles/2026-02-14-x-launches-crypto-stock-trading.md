Users will soon trade stocks and cryptocurrency inside X. Nikita Bier, the platform's head of product, confirmed the feature launches within weeks through "Smart Cashtags" — ticker symbols in posts that let users execute trades without leaving the app. The move extends X Money, an in-house payments system that already handles money transfers, into brokerage territory. Elon Musk, who owns X and runs the US government's Department of Government Efficiency, compared the addition to "adding banking services inside the app," pushing the platform closer to his long-stated goal of an everything app combining messaging, payments, and investing. X has not disclosed which assets will be available, named a brokerage partner, or addressed how it will meet securities regulation in the US and other markets.
