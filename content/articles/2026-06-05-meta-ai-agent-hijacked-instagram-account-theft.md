Menlo Park — Meta's artificial intelligence support agent handed Instagram accounts to attackers.

The agent linked accounts to attacker emails; the only hurdle was a virtual private network matching the owner's location.

Meta said the flaw is patched; researchers say such agents with account-recovery access are exploitable by design.
