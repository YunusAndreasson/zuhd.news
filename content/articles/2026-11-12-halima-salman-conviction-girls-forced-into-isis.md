Brooklyn — A federal jury convicted Halima Salman, married into ISIS, foreign policy magazine Inkstick reported.

She is the first American woman tried over involvement with ISIS in Syria.

Prosecutors relied on her husband's phone: a training document, a seconds-long AK-47 video and a selfie before an ISIS flag.

Her defence called the training document fake and said no witness saw her train.

Letta Tayler, formerly of Human Rights Watch, says prosecutors ignored thousands of girls taken to Syria and forced to marry.
