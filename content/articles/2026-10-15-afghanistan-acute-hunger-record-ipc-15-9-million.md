Kabul — 15.9 million people face acute food insecurity, the highest on record.

Winter will lift [Afghanistan](country:AF)'s count to 19.2 million by April, the hunger monitor projects.

Falling humanitarian aid is eroding household coping, the Integrated Food Security Phase Classification found, even as rain and harvests improved food supply.

UN food agencies seek urgent funding for the 4.04 million children needing treatment in 2027.
