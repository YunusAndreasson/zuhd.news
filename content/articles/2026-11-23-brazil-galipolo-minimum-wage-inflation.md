Brasília — A Lula appointee says the minimum wage feeds inflation.

Many benefits and pensions in [Brazil](country:BR) are indexed to the minimum wage.

Central bank governor Gabriel Galipolo said spending rules tied to revenue growth create feedback loops that push inflation higher.

Galipolo argued social programs and benefits can be preserved while the rules are revisited.

The Chamber of Deputies votes this week on Lula's fiscal adjustment.
