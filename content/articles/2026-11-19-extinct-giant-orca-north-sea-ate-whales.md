Rotterdam — Fossils suggest a 7.7-metre orca swam the North Sea.

Orcas may have hunted marine mammals more than 2 million years ago, earlier than scientists thought.

Its teeth sat in deep, bone-reinforced sockets and its head was mobile, traits of today's mammal-hunting orca ecotypes.

The remains are too fragmented to name a new species, and no chemical analysis has yet confirmed the diet.

Olivier Lambert says dating the extinction of whale-eating sperm whales would test whether orcas replaced them.
