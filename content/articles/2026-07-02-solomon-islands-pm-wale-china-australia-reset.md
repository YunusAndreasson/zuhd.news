Honiara — A new premier is unwinding [Solomon Islands](country:SB)'s tilt toward Beijing.

Matthew Wale took office May 15 after a no-confidence vote, inheriting a 2022 pact letting Chinese forces respond to unrest.

He has pledged to review that pact and opened treaty talks with [Australia](country:AU), his first stop as premier.
