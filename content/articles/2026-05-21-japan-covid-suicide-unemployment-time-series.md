Tokyo — Women and youth bore Japan's pandemic suicide surge, a study found.

A peer-reviewed study counted roughly 11,000 excess suicides; unemployment explains less than 10%, using pre-pandemic baselines.

Excess deaths concentrated among women and younger generations; the dominant driver remains unidentified.
