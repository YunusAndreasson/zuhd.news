Serbia operates Chinese supersonic cruise missiles on Russian jets. Serbian President Aleksandar Vucic confirmed his country's MiG-29s carry China's CM-400AKG missile — Serbia is not in NATO but is surrounded by alliance members. Vucic said Serbia "will have even more" of the missiles and declined to name what other Chinese weapons his country has acquired.
