New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s Supreme Court closed over 100 protest cases.

It ends legal risk for thousands who joined youth-led protests against unemployment.

The Cockroach Janata Party movement grew from a meme mocking jobless youth into mass protests over exam fraud and corruption.

The government reserved the right to file fresh cases against nearly 2,900 people.
