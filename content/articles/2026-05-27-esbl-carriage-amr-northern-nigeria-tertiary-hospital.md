Kano — 87% of northern [Nigeria](country:NG) inpatients carried resistant bacteria.

Extended-spectrum beta-lactamase bacteria defeat front-line antibiotics; carriage precedes infection and drives ward outbreaks before surveillance detects spread.

Authors called for surveillance before harder-to-treat resistant strains spread undetected.
