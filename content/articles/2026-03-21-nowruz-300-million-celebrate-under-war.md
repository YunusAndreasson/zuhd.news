Khamenei's Nowruz audio declared victory while Tehran absorbed airstrikes. Nowruz, which marks the start of spring for 300 million people from Iran to Central Asia, falls in its 4th week of US-Israeli airstrikes. Iran's Assembly of Experts has named no successor to Khamenei, whose survival the ongoing strikes have put in question.
