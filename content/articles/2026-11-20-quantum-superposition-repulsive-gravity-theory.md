Oxford — Gravity could push instead of pull, two theory papers show.

A repulsive result would show gravity is quantum, which no experiment has yet demonstrated.

A probe measuring an object in superposition, with all but some outcomes discarded, would feel repulsion, the teams' equations show.

Sougato Bose and Lev Vaidman calculate the push at under a quadrillionth of Earth's gravitational acceleration.

Vlatko Vedral says his Oxford team is already working with experimentalists who specialise in large quantum objects.
