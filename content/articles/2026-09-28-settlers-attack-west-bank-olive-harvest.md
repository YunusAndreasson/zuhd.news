West Bank — Israeli settlers attacked Palestinian olive pickers Monday during a closure.

The olive harvest is the yearly income many Palestinian families in the West Bank depend on.

The attack came during a closure of the occupied West Bank that already restricts Palestinian farmers' access to their groves.

The closure blocking Palestinian access to the groves stays in force as the harvest is just beginning.
