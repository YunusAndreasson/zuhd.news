Muscat — [France](country:FR)'s Latitude will launch first rocket from Oman.

The deal makes [Oman](country:OM) the second Gulf site to land a European rocket firm, after PLD Space signed with Etlaq in 2025.

Neither company has disclosed how the spaceport's buildout will be funded ahead of the 2027 flight.
