Washington — Maj.

Gen. Niemi approved requirements calling drones expendable Wednesday.

The [US](country:US) Air Force will replace the $30 million MQ-9 Reaper with a cheaper platform built for mass production and attrition, Niemi told senators.

The shift favors mass producers over General Atomics, which has supplied Reapers for 2 decades.
