Washington — [US](country:US) inflation hit 3.8% in October, the Bureau of Economic Analysis said.

Core inflation rose to 3.3%, so the Federal Reserve is expected to keep raising rates.

St. Louis Federal Reserve staff blame supply shocks for 90% of excess headline inflation, yet several policymakers see demand.

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent argues the Fed should not chase inflation that will abate.
