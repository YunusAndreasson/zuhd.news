Geneva — Measles killed an estimated 72,000 people in 2025, a record low.

[Bolivia](country:BO), [Mexico](country:MX) and the [US](country:US) lost measles elimination status this month, the World Health Organization said.

Coverage in the Americas fell from above 90% before COVID-19, and outbreaks in unvaccinated clusters spread, the report says.

Southeast Asia has 96% first-dose coverage yet nearly half of all cases, the report says.

WHO says defending elimination requires two doses for every child, and 29 million children missed them in 2025.
