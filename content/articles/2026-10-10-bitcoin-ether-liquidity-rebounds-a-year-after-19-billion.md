New York — Bitcoin order books are deeper than on crash day.

Last 10 October, $19 billion in leveraged positions liquidated after Donald Trump announced 100% China tariffs.

Depth is the value of orders resting near the price, and the deeper the book, the less a big trade moves it.

Weekly spot volume is still down nearly two-thirds from crash week, CoinDesk Research data show.

Tokens outside bitcoin and ether have not rebuilt that depth, CoinDesk Research found.
