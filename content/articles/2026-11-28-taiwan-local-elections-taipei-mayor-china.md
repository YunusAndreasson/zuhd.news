Taipei — A candidate wanted by Chinese police contests the mayoralty.

The vote gauges party support before 2028's presidential race, though it won't change policy toward [China](country:CN).

The Kuomintang won 14 of 22 cities and counties four years ago, and Chiang Wan-an won Taipei by double digits.

Results were due early Saturday evening, and the Kuomintang has no 2028 candidate yet.
