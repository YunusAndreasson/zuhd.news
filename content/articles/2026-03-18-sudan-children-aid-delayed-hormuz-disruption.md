Medicines for 400,000 Sudanese children are stuck in Dubai. Gulf ports are Africa's main humanitarian logistics hub, and Iran's Hormuz closure has severed supplies to 90 of Sudan's primary healthcare facilities. Those facilities face running out of essential medicines with no alternative supply route in place.
