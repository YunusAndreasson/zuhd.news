Japan releases 80 million emergency oil barrels. The country imports over 90% of its oil through the Hormuz strait — more than any other G7 economy — and begins the drawdown on March 16. Analysts warn dwindling reserves give China leverage over Japan amid their ongoing dispute over Taiwan.
