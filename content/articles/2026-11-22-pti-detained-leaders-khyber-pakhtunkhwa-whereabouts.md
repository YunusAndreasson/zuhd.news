Islamabad — PTI says its detained chief minister's whereabouts are undisclosed.

President Asif Ali Zardari's ordinance stripped provincial lawmakers of protection against preventive detention.

Police have held Sohail Afridi and Speaker Babar Saleem Swati in preventive detention since governor's rule began on 11 October.

Minister of State Tallal Chaudhry said last month that detainees with cases would be tried and the rest released.

PTI is pressing the Pakistan Peoples Party not to vote for extending governor's rule in the National Assembly.
