San Francisco — Uber is cutting 3,300 corporate jobs, about 10% of staff.

Drivers, who are contractors rather than employees, sit outside this cut entirely.

The reduction returns Uber's payroll to 2021 levels even as revenue and valuation run far above that year.

Uber is redirecting the estimated $2bn saved toward robotaxi partnerships, Wedbush analysts said.
