Ogun Waterside — Ogun's 28km coastal road is promised this year.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s 700km Lagos-Calabar Coastal Highway would give a planned Ogun deep-sea port its first coastal road access, replacing a route 35km inland.

Governor Abiodun set no contractor milestone; the full 700km route has no confirmed federal completion date.
