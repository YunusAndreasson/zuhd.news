South Korea builds 86% of the world's LNG carriers. Japan is reviving domestic production after Iran's Hormuz blockade exposed its dependence on foreign-built ships. Japan's 2 largest shipbuilders, Imabari and Oshima, are in early talks to cooperate, though domestic capacity would take years to develop.
