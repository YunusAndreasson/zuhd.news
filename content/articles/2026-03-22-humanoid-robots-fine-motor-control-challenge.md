New York — Boston Dynamics' Atlas still cannot reliably grasp a small object. The human hand's 27 degrees of freedom require tactile feedback loops that current sensors cannot yet replicate. Researchers say solving fine motor control requires new sensor designs, not scaled-up versions of existing ones.
