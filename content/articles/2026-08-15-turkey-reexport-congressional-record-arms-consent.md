Moscow — 70 long-range US missiles head to [Ukraine](country:UA) via [Turkey](country:TR).

The shipment would arm Kyiv with weapons Ankara insists it never supplies directly.

Re-exporting [US](country:US)-made arms requires State Department consent, filed to Congress on Aug. 6.

Moscow says it has asked Washington and Ankara to clarify the shipment's timeline.
