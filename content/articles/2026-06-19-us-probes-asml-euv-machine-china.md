Washington — The [US](country:US) Commerce Secretary said he has evidence a restricted extreme ultraviolet lithography machine reached [China](country:CN).

ASML, which makes all such machines, monitors all 314 remotely and says none has reached China.

Lutnick has not shared his evidence with ASML; shares fell 2% Friday.
