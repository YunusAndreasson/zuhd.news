Cape Town — Shell's Wild Coast right is cancelled for good.

Fishing communities' customary claims now outrank a supermajor's renewal bid.

[South Africa](country:ZA)'s court ruled that consultation during a renewal cannot cure a failure to consult before the original grant.

The exploration block stays closed, with no new attempt possible.
