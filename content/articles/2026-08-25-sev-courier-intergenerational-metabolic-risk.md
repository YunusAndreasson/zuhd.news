Nanjing — Researchers identified the physical courier for inherited metabolic dysfunction.

Small extracellular vesicles carry regulatory RNA that rewrites a fetus's epigenome without changing its DNA sequence, the study found.

A mother's metabolic state reaches the fetus through membrane-wrapped RNA parcels her tissue releases and the fetus absorbs.

The finding is mouse and cell work, with human relevance still unproven.
