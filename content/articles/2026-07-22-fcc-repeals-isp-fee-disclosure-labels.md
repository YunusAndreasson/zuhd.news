Washington — The FCC voted 3-0 to stop requiring itemized fee labels.

Providers had to list every "passthrough" fee separately; now they can show one aggregate "up to" figure instead, hiding the true price.

Carriers get to advertise lower headline prices 30 days after the order publishes.
