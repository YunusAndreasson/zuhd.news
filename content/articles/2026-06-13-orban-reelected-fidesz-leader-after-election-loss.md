Budapest — 729 of 737 delegates reelected Orban, running unopposed.

Fidesz fell from 39% to 17% in April's election, losing [Hungary](country:HU) to a pro-Western supermajority; Orban retains the opposition machine intact.

The new government resumes [Ukraine](country:UA)'s EU accession talks in Luxembourg next week — a process Orban vetoed for years.
