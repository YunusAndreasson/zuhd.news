Poonch — 3 [Indian](country:IN) TV channels branded a slain religious teacher a top militant.

All 3 used a condolence photo of Maulana Qari Mohammad Iqbal, killed by Pakistani artillery; Poonch Police confirmed he had no links to any armed group.

A rights group filed a formal complaint with the broadcasting regulator; no channel has corrected the record.
