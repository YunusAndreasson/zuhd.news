London — Isomorphic Labs has raised $7.7 billion with no drug yet in human trials.

Its oncology and immunology programmes show positive preclinical results only, the company says.

Greenoaks and Sequoia led the $5 billion round, with Alphabet, Abu Dhabi's MGX and Temasek returning.

Isomorphic says it is preparing its first candidates for clinical trials.
