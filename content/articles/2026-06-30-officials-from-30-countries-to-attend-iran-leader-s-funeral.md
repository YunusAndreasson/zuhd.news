Tehran — Officials from 30 nations to attend Khamenei's funeral.

[Iran](country:IR) named Mojtaba Khamenei the new Supreme Leader; the government said officials from 30 countries confirmed attendance at July 5 ceremonies.

[Iraq](country:IQ) formed a national committee to receive the body and coordinate ceremonies with Iran before the Tehran burial.
