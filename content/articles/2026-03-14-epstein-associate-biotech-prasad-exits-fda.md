Vinay Prasad left the FDA for a second time. As director of biologics evaluation, Prasad publicly challenged COVID vaccine mandates and clinical trial standards — drawing a congressional investigation into his division's own approvals. No replacement has been named, leaving the office without a permanent director as the investigation continues.
