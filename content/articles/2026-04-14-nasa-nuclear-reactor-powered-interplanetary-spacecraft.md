Washington — NASA is building its first fission reactor-powered spacecraft. Nuclear fission fuel is orders of magnitude denser than chemical propellants, enabling missions that would take years in months. The last US space reactor ran for 43 days in 1965; this one targets Artemis deep-space missions.
