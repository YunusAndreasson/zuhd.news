Abuja — Kidnappers tasered a woman, then drained her account.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s Firearms Act defines weapons by barrel and cartridge, so a device that injures by electric shock falls outside the law entirely.

The National Assembly has not amended the statute; sellers in Abuja and Lagos operate openly.
