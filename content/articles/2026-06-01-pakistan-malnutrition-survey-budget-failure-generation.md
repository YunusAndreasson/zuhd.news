Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s stunted children of 2018 are teenagers now.

The 2018 National Nutrition Survey found 40% of children stunted, wasting above WHO emergency threshold — the 2026 budget holds no nutrition line.

Finance Minister Aurangzeb tables Rs17.1 trillion in spending; no nutrition allocation appears in the development schedule.
