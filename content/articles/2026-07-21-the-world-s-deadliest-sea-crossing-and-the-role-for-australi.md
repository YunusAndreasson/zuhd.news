Cox's Bazar — Hundreds drowned crossing the Andaman Sea last week.

Most were Rohingya fleeing [Myanmar](country:MM) and [Bangladesh](country:BD)'s camps on the world's deadliest sea route.

ASEAN members already hold search-and-rescue tools they have not used, the report says.
