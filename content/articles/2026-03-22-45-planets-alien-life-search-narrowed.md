Ithaca — Researchers narrowed 6,000 exoplanets to 45 potential life hosts. All 45 are rocky, no bigger than twice Earth, and orbit within the habitable zone — the range where liquid water can exist. 4 of the 45 orbit TRAPPIST-1 — 40 light years away — making it the nearest cluster for James Webb to study.
