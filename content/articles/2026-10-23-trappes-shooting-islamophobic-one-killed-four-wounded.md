Trappes — A gunman killed 1 and wounded 4, including a 12-year-old girl.

[France](country:FR)'s antiterrorism prosecutors are investigating a racial or religious motive.

Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez said the gunman fired from a car at a playground, a fuel station and a medical centre.

"This was a terrorist attack with racist, likely Islamophobic, overtones," said Ali Rabeh, Trappes's mayor.

Nuñez will deploy 70,000 officers across the country Saturday for cost-of-living protests.
