Donetsk — Ukrainian strikes cut power to 279,000, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

Andrey Chertkov, the Russian-installed official quoted, said the outages compounded damage from earlier attacks.

Several facilities were hit at once overnight across 12 municipal districts, Chertkov wrote on Telegram.

Chertkov gave no date for restoring power.
