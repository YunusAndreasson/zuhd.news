6 people died in Israeli strikes on central Beirut. The strikes hit densely populated neighbourhoods near government headquarters; Israeli forces gave no evacuation warning, unlike previous raids on the same area. Israel also ordered evacuations in Tyre, including Palestinian refugee camps — signaling a wider offensive across Lebanon.
