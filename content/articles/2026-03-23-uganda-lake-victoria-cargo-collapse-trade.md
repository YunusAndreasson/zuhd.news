Kampala — Lake Victoria now carries 1% of Uganda's trade. Cargo's share collapsed from 30% in the early 2000s as infrastructure decayed and investment dried up, removing a regional route that once served landlocked communities across East Africa. The Hormuz crisis is pushing overland freight costs higher; Uganda's government must now decide whether to fund a waterway revival or leave landlocked communities with fewer trade options.
