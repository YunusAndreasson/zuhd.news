Singapore — [Singapore](country:SG)'s central bank tightened its currency band again.

The Monetary Authority of Singapore, or MAS, sets no interest rate; it manages the S$NEER exchange band, the lever an economy whose trade exceeds its GDP relies on.

Core inflation is projected to keep rising from July before easing around mid-2027, MAS said.
