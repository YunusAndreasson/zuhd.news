Brasília — A Bolsonaro documentary declared a production cost of R$4,500.

[Brazil](country:BR)'s film agency Ancine opened a review, saying the figure does not match the cost of a feature.

Entities tied to the film received R$1.5 million, including R$860,000 in amendments from Mario Frias, Marcos Pollon and Eduardo Bolsonaro.

Producer Dori Filmes told Ancine all technicians worked as volunteers, calling it a project without money.

Ancine has asked Dori Filmes to confirm the financing, and the film's executive producer is deputy Mario Frias.
