Paris — AC refrigerants warm climate thousands of times faster than CO₂.

Cooling stocks may triple by 2050, UN Environment Programme projects; the growth concentrates in the Global South, where refrigerant disposal rules are weakest.

Which refrigerant chemistry fills the coming equipment boom is not yet governed by any global standard.
