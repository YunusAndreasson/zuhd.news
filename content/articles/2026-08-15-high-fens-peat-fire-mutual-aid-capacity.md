Malmedy — 2,700 hectares of peat bog have burned.

Peat stores decades of carbon and can smoulder underground for weeks.

[Belgium](country:BE) has no wildfire fleet of its own, so it is borrowing helicopters from neighbours fighting their own fires.

Officials have not said when the blaze, still uncontained, will be extinguished.
