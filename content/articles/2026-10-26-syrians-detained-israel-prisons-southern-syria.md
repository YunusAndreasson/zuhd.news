Quneitra — Israeli soldiers took Saddam Hassan Ahmed, 16, from his father's farm.

He is one of 40 Syrians still held in [Israel](country:IL) after a cross-border forcible transfer, considered a war crime.

Since moving into the 1974 buffer zone, Israeli forces raid Quneitra and Daraa and take civilians into Israel.

"I told them it wasn't Israel's land, it was mine," said Hassan Saadeddin Mohammed, Saddam's father.

He has not seen his son since April 2024.
