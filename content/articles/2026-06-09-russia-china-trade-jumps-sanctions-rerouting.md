Beijing — [Russia](country:RU)-[China](country:CN) trade hit $109.5bn in 5 months.

Chinese customs data showed exports to Russia up 26.4% to $49bn — cars, machinery, electronics — while Russian oil and gas went east.

Full-year 2025 trade slipped 6.9%; whether the 2026 pace holds will show in year-end customs data.
