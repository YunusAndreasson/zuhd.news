114 Rafale jets in a deal worth $40 billion. French President Emmanuel Macron arrived in India for a 3-day visit centered on the fighter purchase and an artificial intelligence cooperation pact. The deal would make India France's largest defence customer, shifting New Delhi further from its dependence on Russian arms.
