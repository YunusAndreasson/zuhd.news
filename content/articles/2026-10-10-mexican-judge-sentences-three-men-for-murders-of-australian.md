Ensenada — A Mexican judge convicted 3 men of killing 3 surfers.

Callum Robinson, 30, Jake Robinson, 33, and Carter Rhoad, 30, were killed by gunshots to the head.

The ex-partner of accused gunman Jesus Gerardo Garcia testified he confessed to arriving at their tent with a 9mm pistol.

"No outcome can ever undo this tragedy," said Martin Robinson, father of Callum and Jake.

A judge in [Mexico](country:MX) announces final sentences on 16 October, and prosecutors want 210 years each.
