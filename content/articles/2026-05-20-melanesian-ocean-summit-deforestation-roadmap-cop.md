Port Moresby — Papua New Guinea's zone bans all fishing, not just industrial.

[Papua New Guinea](country:PG), [Fiji](country:FJ), and Vanuatu created 5 joint enforcement zones spanning the Melanesian Pacific.

Vanuatu's prime minister called it "solidarity over colonial rule"; no great-power ratification is required.
