New York — 85.7% of injected volunteers made antibodies against H7 flu.

Side effects were mostly mild, but phase 1 measures safety and antibody response, not protection.

BPL-1357 contains 4 inactivated low-pathogenicity avian flu viruses, given by injection or nasal spray to 45 volunteers, 28 days apart.

Nasal doses produced only subtle responses, and injections produced no mucosal antibody response.

The authors say other dose levels and efficacy against infection still need testing.
