Nothing can prevent the nuclear harm, the WHO warns. US and Israeli strikes hit 3 of Iran's nuclear sites, though the International Atomic Energy Agency called damage to the Bushehr reactor "not significant." The WHO is briefing governments on emergency response but won't say which populations near active reactors are being warned.
