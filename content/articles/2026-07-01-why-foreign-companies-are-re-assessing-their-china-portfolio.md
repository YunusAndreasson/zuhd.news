Beijing — [China](country:CN)'s foreign investment fell 39%, UNCTAD says.

Two new State Council decrees since 2022 impose criminal liability on executives who cut ties with Chinese suppliers.

Beijing has not said whether the decrees will ease if the investment slide continues.
