Each of your cells has thousands of DNA gatekeepers. Each nuclear pore complex, assembled from hundreds of proteins, controls which molecules enter the nucleus through a permanently disordered, shifting core. The selectivity mechanism remains unsolved, with implications for understanding how viruses hijack nuclear transport to replicate.
