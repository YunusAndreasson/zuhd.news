Abuja — NNPC picked two Chinese firms after screening 50 bidders.

[Nigeria](country:NG) still imports fuel despite billions spent repairing state refineries, now leaning on [China](country:CN).

Sanjiang Chemical and Xinganchen signed an MOU for a technical equity partnership, not yet finalized, to revive Port Harcourt and Warri refineries.

"We need a partner that has a genuine stake in the success and sustainability of the refinery," Ojulari said.

NNPC has not yet signed a final agreement, and Ojulari said other bidders were rejected for seeking equity or control instead.
