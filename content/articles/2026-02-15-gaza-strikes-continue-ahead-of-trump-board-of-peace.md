Israeli forces killed 10 Palestinians on Saturday. Strikes on displacement camps and residential areas brought the death toll since October's ceasefire to 601, according to Palestinian health officials, with documented ceasefire violations surpassing 1,600. US President Donald Trump's newly formed peace council holds its first meeting Wednesday with no Palestinian representation, while Israel's military operates across 58% of Gaza.
