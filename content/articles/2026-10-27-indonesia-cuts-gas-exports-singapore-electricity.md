Jakarta — Gas generates up to 95% of [Singapore](country:SG)'s electricity.

[Indonesia](country:ID), its largest supplier, cut pipeline gas by about 8% on Saturday.

The cut is under 2% of daily demand, and Indonesia's energy minister says supply still meets the contract minimum.

"The cost to Indonesia will far, far outlive any twinge we may feel," said veteran Singaporean diplomat Bilahari Kausikan.

Indonesia's foreign ministry hosts Singapore Foreign Minister Vivian Balakrishnan on Tuesday.
