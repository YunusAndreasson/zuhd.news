Brussels — The draft covers AI chatbots and video games.

Under-15s would need parental consent to open an account at all.

Enforcing it means verifying every user's age, via an eIDAS wallet or on-device estimation, each with a privacy cost.

The Commission publishes the bill Thursday, open to two years of amendment.
