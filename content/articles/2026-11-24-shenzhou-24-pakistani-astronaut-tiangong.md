Jiuquan — A Pakistani becomes the first foreigner aboard China's space station.

It is the first joint flight of Chinese and foreign astronauts in China's manned space history.

Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, 40, a former air force pilot, will run 4 biotechnology and materials experiments as payload specialist.

"Look up at the sky and remember that I am up there with your dreams," said astronaut Ali.

[China](country:CN) launches Shenzhou 24 from Jiuquan on Wednesday morning, and [Pakistan](country:PK)'s astronaut returns aboard Shenzhou 23.
