Anchorage — Trump wants Peltola prosecuted over a second Dan Sullivan.

Text messages show Mary Peltola's campaign discussed recruiting a namesake of Republican incumbent Dan Sullivan.

Alaska's ranked-choice ballot lists four candidates, so a same-name rival can pull votes from the incumbent.

Peltola's campaign and the second Sullivan, a retired schoolteacher, both deny any coordination.

Alaskans vote 3 November, and the [US](country:US) Justice Department has sued to block certifying a Peltola win.
