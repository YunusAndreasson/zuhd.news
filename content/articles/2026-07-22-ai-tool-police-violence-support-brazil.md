Rio de Janeiro — 122 people died in one police raid, and public support for it held.

Researchers built an AI chatbot interviewer to find out why [Brazil](country:BR)'s public backs raids despite no evidence they cut crime, work grassroots groups couldn't otherwise afford.

The findings are meant to guide counter-messaging groups still testing.
