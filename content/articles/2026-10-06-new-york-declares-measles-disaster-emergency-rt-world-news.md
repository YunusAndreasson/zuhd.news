New York — 92 of the state's 108 measles cases came since 15 July.

Neighbouring Pennsylvania passed 1,000 cases, the largest [US](country:US) outbreak in three decades.

Hochul's order through 4 November widens who can give measles-mumps-rubella vaccines, after coverage fell below the 95% experts say outbreaks require.

The Pan American Health Organization meets in November to decide whether the US and Mexico keep measles-free status.
