Islamabad — Pakistan spent Rs69 billion to hold fuel prices. Since the Iran war began, oil has risen from $72 to $158 a barrel — and the blocked diesel hike alone would have doubled pump prices for Pakistani consumers. Pakistani Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif acknowledged the subsidies are unsustainable and announced means-testing to restrict fuel relief to lower-income households.
