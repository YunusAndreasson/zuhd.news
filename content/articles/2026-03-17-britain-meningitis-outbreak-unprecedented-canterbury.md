2 people died and 13 others were infected after bacterial meningitis was traced to Club Chemistry nightclub in Canterbury. 4 cases are confirmed as Meningitis B, a strain that kills roughly 1 in 10 people it infects; UK Health Minister Wes Streeting called the cluster "unprecedented." A French case has emerged in a University of Kent student, and the UK Health Security Agency has urged everyone who visited the venue between March 5 and 7 to seek preventative antibiotics.
