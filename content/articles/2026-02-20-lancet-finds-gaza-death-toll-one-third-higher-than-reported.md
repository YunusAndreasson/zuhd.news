75,200 people died violently in 16 months. A peer-reviewed Lancet study surveying 2,000 Gaza households estimated the war death toll a third above the Palestinian health ministry's 49,090 figure. Women, children, and elderly people made up 56% of the dead — the first population survey conducted during the war.
