United States — Core consumer prices rose 0.3% in October.

At that pace, annual core inflation in the [US](country:US) tops 4%, double the Fed's 2% target.

Dallas Fed President Lorie Logan wants at least 2 more quarter-point hikes, possibly faster than this fall's pace.

Governor Christopher Waller said a 0.3% core reading would justify considering a half-point hike.

The Federal Reserve's next rate decision comes on 9 December.
