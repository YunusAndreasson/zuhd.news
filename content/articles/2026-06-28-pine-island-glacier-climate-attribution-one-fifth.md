London — Climate caused a fifth of Pine Island's retreat.

A study in The Cryosphere — the first Antarctic glacier attribution research — separated human-caused warming from natural variability in 80 years of melt.

Pine Island and Thwaites together drive nearly half of Antarctic ice-sheet sea-level rise, researchers say.
