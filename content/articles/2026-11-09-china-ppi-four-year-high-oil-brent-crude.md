Beijing — Producer prices hit 5.7%, the highest since June 2022.

Consumer prices in [China](country:CN) rose only 0.7%, so upstream oil costs have yet to reach shoppers.

Higher oil prices lifted costs in mining, extraction and chemicals, and weak household demand blocks passing them on.

Core inflation eased to 0.8% from 1.0%, the National Bureau of Statistics said.

Brent crude closed at $98.32 on 6 November, down 16.4% in a week.
