Stuttgart — Six decade-old wallets moved $40 million in Bitcoin.

These aging keys are the assets most exposed to future quantum attacks.

Early wallets expose their public key on-chain, the exact data a quantum computer could harvest now to decrypt later.

Nobody has confirmed who moved the coins, or whether they were re-keyed for safety.
