San Francisco — Judges ruled sports bets aren't "swaps."

It undercuts prediction markets' bet that one federal license beats fifty state regulators.

Kalshi's bets clear through an exchange regulated by the Commodity Futures Trading Commission, but the panel ruled that doesn't make a sports bet a commodity swap.

The ruling splits with an earlier appeals court, pointing toward a Supreme Court test.
