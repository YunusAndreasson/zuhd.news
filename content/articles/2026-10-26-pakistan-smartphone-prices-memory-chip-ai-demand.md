Karachi — Dealers now quote Rs40,000 for phones that cost Rs22,000.

Buyers are shifting to used and refurbished phones, and shopkeepers report sales falling sharply.

The Pakistan Mobile Phone Manufacturers Association says AI demand for memory chips has outrun production, raising prices.

The Karachi Electronics Dealers Association president says taxes have not risen significantly.

Consumers want [Pakistan](country:PK)'s government to keep basic handsets affordable, but shopkeepers report shortages and swinging prices.
