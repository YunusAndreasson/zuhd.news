Stockholm — Light now switches individual neurons on and off in living brains.

Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel share 12m Swedish kronor.

Hegemann and Nagel showed microbial light-sensing proteins act as light-controlled ion channels, and Deisseroth adapted them for neurons in living animals.

The Nobel season opened Monday, with the remaining prizes announced through the week.
