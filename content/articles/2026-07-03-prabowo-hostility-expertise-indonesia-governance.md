Jakarta — Indonesia's government is excluding its own experts.

Analysts say sidelining academics, think tanks and technical bodies from policymaking is degrading governance in [Indonesia](country:ID), the world's largest Muslim-majority democracy.

Commentators want technical institutions restored to a central role in policymaking.
