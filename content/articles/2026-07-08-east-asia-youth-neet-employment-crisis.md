Seoul — NEET youth exit the labor force instead of registering as jobless.

That loophole masks the true scale of a growing share of young East Asians who have stopped job-hunting entirely, unemployment data shows.

Japan, South Korea and China face pressure to rebuild the link between education and stable work.
