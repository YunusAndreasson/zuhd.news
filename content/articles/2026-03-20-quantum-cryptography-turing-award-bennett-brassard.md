Quantum encryption was invented poolside in 1979. Researchers Charles Bennett and Gilles Brassard won the 2025 Turing Award for a quantum protocol that makes any eavesdropper mathematically detectable. Quantum-secure networks in China and Europe now run on their BB84 protocol as quantum computers near the threshold for breaking classical encryption.
