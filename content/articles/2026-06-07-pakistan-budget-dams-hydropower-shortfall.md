Islamabad — Pakistan allocates Rs179bn for dams needing Rs500bn.

Water authority officials warned [Pakistan](country:PK)'s two largest dams, Diamer-Bhasha and Dasu, may miss timelines; the Rs321bn shortfall compounds IMF fiscal constraints.

[India](country:IN) is advancing upstream Chenab projects; Pakistan cannot yet fund the storage to compensate.
