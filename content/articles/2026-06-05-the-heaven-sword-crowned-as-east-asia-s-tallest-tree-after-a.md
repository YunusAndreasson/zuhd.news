Hualien — Heaven Sword, a Taiwan cypress, tops East Asia.

A team of climbers, ecologists and remote-sensing specialists searched [Taiwan](country:TW)'s rugged interior since 2014 for specimens over 80 metres.

Most of Earth's giant trees remain unmapped; Taiwan's survey is one of few systematic efforts to locate them.
