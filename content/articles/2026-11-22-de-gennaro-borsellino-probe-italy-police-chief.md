Rome — Ex-police chief Gianni De Gennaro, 78, took his own life.

He denied wrongdoing after prosecutors named him in a probe of obstructing the Borsellino inquiry.

Prosecutors allege concealed evidence, including Borsellino's vanished diary, and sought his house arrest, which a judge refused.

"Another piece of the truth may have gone (with him)," said Manfredi Borsellino, the murdered magistrate's son.

A hearing on prosecutors' appeal against the house-arrest refusal had been set for 1 December.
