Dhaka — Bangladesh wants India's stance before Hasina speaks.

Ousted leader Sheikh Hasina, convicted in absentia and living in exile in [India](country:IN), will deliver a virtual "homecoming" speech Wednesday; Dhaka says hosting a broadcasting fugitive is itself a policy choice.

[Bangladesh](country:BD)'s extradition request under the 2013 treaty remains under Indian consideration.
