A US-built iPhone hacking toolkit leaked to Russian spies. Google traced Coruna, a toolkit from a US surveillance vendor, through a Russian spy group to profit-focused hackers who infected 42,000 devices. Apple patched the vulnerabilities in iOS 26, leaving anyone on iOS 17.2.1 or earlier exposed.
