The first New World screwworm in [US](country:US) livestock was confirmed June 3 in a Texas calf, federal officials said.

The parasite, eradicated in 1966, entered via cattle smuggled from Central America through [Mexico](country:MX) on falsified certificates.

A 20-kilometer quarantine is in place with sterile fly releases; containment depends on stopping the smuggling route.
