Washington — Treasury named nearly 60 entities Monday.

Banks in [Pakistan](country:PK) and [India](country:IN) must now choose between Tehran and dollar access.

Sanctions bite when banks fear secondary penalties, so compliance officers abroad, not Washington, enforce the list.

Treasury has not published the full roster but teased a bank designation by week's end.
