Athens — Researchers genotyped 243 loggerhead turtles across 11 beaches.

The genomes show a major genetic break separating Greek nesting sites, with a second dividing Libya from Levantine beaches.

Conservation funding remains limited even as the study finds each rookery holds adaptive diversity found nowhere else.
