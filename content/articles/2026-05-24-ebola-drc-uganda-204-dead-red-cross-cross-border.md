Bunia — Ebola killed 3 Red Cross volunteers in eastern [DR Congo](country:CD).

The outbreak, caused by the Bundibugyo strain with no approved vaccine, has spread from Ituri across the border into [Uganda](country:UG), which confirmed 5 cases.

Africa Centres for Disease Control listed 10 countries at risk; WHO declared a health emergency and the [US](country:US) pledged $23 million.
