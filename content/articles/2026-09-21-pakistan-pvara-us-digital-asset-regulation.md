Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s new crypto regulator briefed a [US](country:US) envoy Sunday.

The initiative aims to align Pakistan's virtual asset market with international standards.

Minister of State Bilal Bin Saqib briefed chargé d'affaires Natalie Baker on efforts to build a transparent regulatory framework.

The readout named no licensing rules or timetable.
