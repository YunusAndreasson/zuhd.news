Mangystau — 14 Kazakh servicemen drowned during a Caspian naval exercise.

Prosecutors identified officials suspected of negligence for not securing troops in poor weather.

[Kazakhstan](country:KZ)'s Defense Ministry said sudden winds swept 17 servicemen into the sea and 3 were rescued.

Deputy Prime Minister Kanat Bozumbayev's commission is investigating, and a criminal case is open.
