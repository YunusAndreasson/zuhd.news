Berlin — German firms are turning to AI rather than new hires to fill the gap from mass retirement.

Unlike economies where automation threatens jobs, [Germany](country:DE)'s shrinking workforce makes AI a replacement, not a threat.

38% of German financial firms lack in-house AI expertise, a 2026 Seismic wealth-management survey found.
