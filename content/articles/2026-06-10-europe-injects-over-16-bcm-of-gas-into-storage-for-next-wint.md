Brussels — Europe's gas stores sit 14 points below average.

Severed from [Russia](country:RU)'s pipelines, Europe now buys summer liquefied natural gas in competition with Asian markets; EU rules require 90% storage before winter.

Filling lags last year by 21%; Europe must inject 68 billion cubic metres by October, per Gas Infrastructure Europe data.
