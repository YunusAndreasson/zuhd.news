Florida — The sister of [Cuba](country:CU)'s military conglomerate GAESA chief is in US immigration custody.

GAESA controls about 40% of the Cuban economy, US figures show; Washington sanctioned it May 7 under a Trump decree targeting foreign firms.

Adys Lastres Morera, arrested for managing Florida real estate while aiding Havana, faces deportation.
