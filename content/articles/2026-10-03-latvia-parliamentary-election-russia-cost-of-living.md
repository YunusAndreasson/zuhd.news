Riga — 1 in 5 Latvian voters entered election day undecided.

Prime Minister Andris Kulbergs's United List leads with over 14%, the SKDS institute found, but his coalition is expected to lose power.

14 lists contest 100 seats and only 7 are likely to clear the 5% threshold, so coalition talks decide who governs.

Sovereign Power polls above 9% and opposes Ukraine aid, but most major parties rule out working with it.

Preliminary official results are due Sunday.
