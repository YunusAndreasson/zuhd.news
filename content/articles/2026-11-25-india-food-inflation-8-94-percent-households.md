New Delhi — Food inflation hit 8.94% in October, 9.09% in rural areas.

[India](country:IN)'s poorest rural fifth faces 6.9% inflation, the richest 6.5%, Crisil calculates.

The monsoon ran 13% below normal, and rice, pulses and edible oil are up 8% to 13% in a year.

Government cereal stocks sit well above minimum levels, and a free-food programme reaches nearly 800 million people.

Economist Rajeswari Sengupta warns that wages lagging food costs can start a wage-price spiral.
