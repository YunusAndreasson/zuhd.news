Helsinki — [Qatar](country:QA)'s sovereign wealth fund joined a €450 million round valuing [Finland](country:FI)'s ICEYE at €10 billion.

The firm's synthetic-aperture radar sees through clouds and night; [Ukraine](country:UA)'s war created demand for persistent satellite coverage that ICEYE had already scaled to meet.

ICEYE targets 100 satellites a year by 2028.
