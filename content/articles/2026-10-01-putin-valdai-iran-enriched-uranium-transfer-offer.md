Moscow — Russian state news agency TASS reported President Vladimir Putin says Moscow's offer to take Iran's uranium stands.

Enriched uranium is central to the dispute between the [US](country:US) and [Iran](country:IR).

The offer would move some of Iran's enriched uranium onto Russian soil, TASS reported.

"Everyone initially agreed," [Russia](country:RU)'s president told the Valdai forum, in TASS's account, naming no one.

Putin did not say who walked away, and TASS carries no response from Tehran or Washington.
