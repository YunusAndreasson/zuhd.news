London — The eye has flagged disease since the 1800s.

A new review proposes extending that window into psychiatry, which has no biological test.

The retina is embryologically brain tissue, so its vessels and neurons already track vascular, metabolic and neurological disease.

Whether any signal is sharp enough to diagnose one patient remains untested.
