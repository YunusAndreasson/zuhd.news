Tehran — Destroying 20,000 Iranian buildings released 2.4 million CO2-equivalent tonnes. The total from 2 weeks of US and Israeli strikes — 5 million tonnes — equals Iceland's full annual output, but wartime carbon appears in no country's official climate accounts. Researchers warn emissions will climb as strikes target more oil facilities, exposing surrounding populations to toxic pollution.
