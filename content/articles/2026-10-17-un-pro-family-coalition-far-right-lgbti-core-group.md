New York — Activists plan a rival to the UN's 42-state LGBTQ+ group.

Trump administration officials attended the forum, and the [US](country:US) recently left the existing group.

Core Groups are informal cross-regional coalitions of states, not official UN bodies, that steer resolutions on topics such as extrajudicial executions.

Organisers of the Global Forum for Life, Family and Freedoms agreed to draw up plans within months.
