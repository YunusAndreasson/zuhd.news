Islamabad — National Resources Limited says one hole hit 295 metres of mineralisation.

Eight sites across Chagai now carry copper and gold, from nearly 25,000 metres of drilling.

That hole averaged 0.43% copper and 0.13 grams of gold a tonne, with a 132-metre core at 0.83% copper.

The company is withholding its consultant's preliminary resource estimates until they meet international reporting standards.

Environmental and metallurgical studies begin in 2027, with a bankable feasibility study due by decade's end.
