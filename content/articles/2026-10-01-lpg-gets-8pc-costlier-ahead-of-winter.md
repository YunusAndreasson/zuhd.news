Islamabad — A household LPG cylinder now costs Rs3,296.23.

Households in [Pakistan](country:PK) off the piped network pay 8% more as winter demand begins.

The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority's formula tracks the Saudi Aramco contract price, which rose 9.9% from September.

The new price holds until 31 October.
