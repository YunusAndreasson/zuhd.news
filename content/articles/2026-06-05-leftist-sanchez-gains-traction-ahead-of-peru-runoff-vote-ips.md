Lima — 0.6 points separate Peru's two runoff candidates.

Sanchez — ally of jailed ex-president Castillo — leads Fujimori 43.8% to 43.2%, within a ±2.1% margin of error, Ipsos found.

Publishing polls is illegal in [Peru](country:PE)'s final week; 13% of voters remain blank, enough to decide the outcome.
