Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) claimed toll rights over Strait of Hormuz undersea cables.

FALCON and Gulf Bridge cables run through Iranian territorial waters; repair ships have avoided the strait since February, leaving faults unrepaired.

Tech firms face paying protection fees or accepting permanent cable degradation, maritime intelligence firm Windward said.
