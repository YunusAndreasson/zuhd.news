Brussels — Freezers in dozens of European supermarkets hit 50°C overnight.

A Belgian butcher says the apparent attack ruined €15,000 of meat.

Sabcobel's chief executive says attackers switched evaporators to defrost mode, and [Sweden](country:SE)'s cyber agency flags exposed controllers.

Carrefour Belgium says only 15 of its 700 stores were affected.

No one has claimed responsibility, and Sweden's cybersecurity centre is still working several incidents.
