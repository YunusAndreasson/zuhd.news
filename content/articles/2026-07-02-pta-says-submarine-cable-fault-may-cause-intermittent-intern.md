Islamabad — One undersea cable fault, nationwide slowdown.

A fault in the SEA-ME-WE 5 line is degrading service across [Pakistan](country:PK), whose international traffic depends on a handful of subsea cables run by one private operator.

Transworld Associates is rerouting traffic while it works with the consortium to find the fault.
