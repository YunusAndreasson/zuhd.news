Karachi — Summer temperatures in [Pakistan](country:PK) regularly exceed 40°C.

Aga Khan University's Jai Das is testing reflective coatings and ventilation as low-cost alternatives to air conditioning for poor households.

Pakistan faces glacial floods, droughts and extreme heat; Das is searching for solutions affordable and scalable beyond Karachi.
