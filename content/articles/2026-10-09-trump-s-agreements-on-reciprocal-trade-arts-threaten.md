Washington — 10 countries signed [US](country:US) trade deals limiting their telecom suppliers.

Over 100,000 Indonesian schools lack internet, and most signatories have wide digital divides.

Each deal bars signatories from ICT suppliers deemed a security risk, and 4 must consult Washington on which suppliers qualify.

The digital-rights group EngageMedia argues the clause lets the US exclude Huawei and ZTE and favour Cisco and Hewlett Packard.

The US has framework deals in place with several more countries.
