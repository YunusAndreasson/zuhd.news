London — One RAF tanker will refuel Saudi jets fighting Houthi forces.

The deal draws [Britain](country:GB) back into a war that has killed thousands of Yemeni civilians.

Houthi fighters seized the port of Mocha and Red Sea islands, threatening the Bab al-Mandeb chokepoint that carries 12% of world trade.

UK officials say the deployment stays defensive and excludes support for strikes against the Houthis.

The agreement remains under review, with no end date set as the fighting continues.
