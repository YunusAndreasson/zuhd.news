Bishkek — Authorities named a blogger detained in [Vietnam](country:VN) an extremist.

The label covers the opposition movement Shumkar and 3 other named critics.

Those named are ecologist Samat Kushbek, analyst Aidar Raev and Tengiz Bolturuk, the Kumtor gold mine's former external manager.

The announcement names no penalty for membership or support.
