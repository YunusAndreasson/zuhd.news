San Francisco — Aptoide sued Google for monopolizing Android app distribution. The Portuguese store, with 200 million annual users, claims Google steers developers exclusively to Play Store and blocks rivals from access to top content. The suit seeks triple damages and an injunction that would force open Android's only viable distribution channel.
