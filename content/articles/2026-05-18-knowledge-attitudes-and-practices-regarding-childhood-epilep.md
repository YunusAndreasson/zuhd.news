Zibo — 7 in 10 epilepsy caregivers predict career stigma for their child.

A 385-caregiver study in [China](country:CN) found attitude predicts practice more than knowledge; some still insert objects into a seizing child's mouth.

The study recommends addressing attitudes, not just knowledge, to change caregiving practice.
