London — 2026 has 67% odds of record warmth, Carbon Brief says.

Datasets disagree: record odds range from 97% at Berkeley Earth to under 20% at Copernicus.

A strengthening El Niño adds heat to human-caused warming, with January-September around 1.45C above pre-industrial.

The analysis projects 2027 at 1.7C above pre-industrial, with 96% odds of a record.
