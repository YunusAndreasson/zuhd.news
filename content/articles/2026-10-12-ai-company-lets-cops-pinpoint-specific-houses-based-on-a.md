United States — One washer-dryer photo led Raven to a specific Airbnb.

The Los Angeles Police Department and Miami-Dade Sheriff's Office have bought access.

Raven matches distinctive features to listing photos on sites like Airbnb and Vrbo, without needing location data or metadata.

Graylark CEO Daniel Heinen wrote of "the ability to geolocate interior images across millions of short-term rental units worldwide."

Heinen pitches it for trafficking and exploitation cases, where investigators verify the location lead themselves.
