Ennedi — Sudanese drones struck a Rapid Support Forces convoy inside [Chad](country:TD).

The strike crossed into Chad for the first time, risking a wider regional war.

Washington's Darfur-only arms embargo on [Sudan](country:SD) left the Chad border open, making it the paramilitary's resupply route from Libya.

The US wants the embargo extended nationwide and to drones before its September expiry.
