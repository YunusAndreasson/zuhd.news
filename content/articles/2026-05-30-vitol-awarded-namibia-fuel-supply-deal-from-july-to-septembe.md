Windhoek — [Namibia](country:NA) hired Vitol to guarantee fuel through September.

African fuel importers with no storage buffer absorbed [Iran](country:IR)'s Gulf disruption directly; Vitol offered the Basic Fuel Price with no premium, no public funds required, the energy minister said.

If markets don't stabilize, the minister said a second tender will follow.
