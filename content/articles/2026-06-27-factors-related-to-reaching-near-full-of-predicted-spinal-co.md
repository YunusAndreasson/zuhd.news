Charleston — Spinal cord injury survivors who worked lived longer.

A 1,570-person cohort tracked from 2003 to 2019 found those who reached expected lifespans were more employed and socially active.

Disability insurers and rehabilitation programs — not just hospitals — determine who survives, the researchers conclude.
