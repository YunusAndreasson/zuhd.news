Moscow — A drone strike halted the refinery supplying 40% of city fuel.

Gazprom Neft stations halved the per-purchase fuel limit from 60 litres to 30.

[Ukraine](country:UA)'s September 20 attack set both primary crude distillation units alight, halting processing, three industry sources said.

After June's strikes, the plant restarted sooner than those sources predicted.

Industry sources say repairs could take several weeks.
