8,683 anti-Muslim complaints were recorded in 2025. The Council on American-Islamic Relations called it the highest volume since its reports began in 1996, as the Iran war drove online content describing Muslims as pests and vermin. The council expects 2026 numbers to be worse, with Minnesota already reporting a 96% rise in complaints.
