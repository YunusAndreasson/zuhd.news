Venezuela replaced its entire military command in 24 hours. Delcy Rodriguez, Venezuela's interim president installed after a US raid ousted Nicolás Maduro in January, is dismantling the military structure that kept him in power. Trump has said he effectively runs Venezuela and will keep Rodriguez in power only while she follows his orders.
