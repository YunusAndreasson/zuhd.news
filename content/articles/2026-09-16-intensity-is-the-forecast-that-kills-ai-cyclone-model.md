London — WeatherNext Cyclones gained forecasters a full extra day.

Rapid intensification, not track, is what kills, and it barely improved in thirty years.

The AI model maps past atmospheric states to outcomes directly, skipping the physics grids limiting resolution near a storm's eye.

It already guides the US National Hurricane Center, though performance on an unprecedented storm is untested.
