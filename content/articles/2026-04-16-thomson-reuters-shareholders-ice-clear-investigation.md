Toronto — Thomson Reuters shareholders demanded an ICE audit. Canada's BCGEU union fund demands an independent audit of CLEAR, the data product ICE uses to build deportation-target maps. The company fired the protest organizer; the resolution demands a public report on CLEAR's human rights impacts.
