Over 100 litres of oil pour hourly into Niger Delta mangroves. The Trans Niger Pipeline ruptured near Freetown in Rivers State, sending crude into creeks fishing communities depend on. Tidal currents are carrying the oil into neighbouring waterways. Nigeria's National Oil Spill Detection and Response Agency said it received no formal notification hours after the spill began. Renaissance Africa Energy Company, which acquired the pipeline from Shell, has not explained the cause or its containment plan.
