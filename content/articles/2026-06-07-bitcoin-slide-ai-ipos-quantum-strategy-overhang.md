New York — Bitcoin touched a fresh cycle low below $60,000.

New York Digital Investment Group attributes the drop to capital leaving for artificial intelligence and initial public offering stocks, plus a potential Strategy forced sale.

Whether institutional demand changed bitcoin's cycle or delayed a deeper reset is unresolved, the group said.
