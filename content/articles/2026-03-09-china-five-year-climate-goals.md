China's new 5-year climate plan may allow emissions to rise. Beijing's 17% carbon intensity target lets total output grow 3-6% while meeting the stated goal; analysts describe the 2026 interim target as "alarmingly lax." A doubling of non-fossil energy within 10 years could align with 2°C limits — analysts will test both claims against actual 2026 emissions data due late this year.
