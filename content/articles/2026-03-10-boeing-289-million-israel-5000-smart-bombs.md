Boeing sold Israel 5,000 smart bombs for $289 million. The deal closed as Israel struck Tehran and Isfahan, 4 days after a separate $151 million arms sale also bypassed Congress. Neither contract has faced Congressional review, leaving the question of legislative oversight unresolved.
