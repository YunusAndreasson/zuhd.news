Bosaso — A drone killed 4 relatives, including a pregnant 17-year-old.

Four anonymous Puntland officials say the [UAE](country:AE) flew the drone from its Bosaso airport base.

The January 2025 strike hit a hut in the Al-Miskaad mountains, where no one keeps official civilian casualty counts.

"Even the livestock were ripped to pieces," said Khadra Abdi Ismael, who lost her sister and nephew.

The UAE embassy and foreign ministry did not respond to questions about the base or the strike.
