Oslo — [Norway](country:NO)'s government is Telenor's controlling shareholder.

That raises doubt on whether a state can credibly prosecute its own company.

Authorities are examining whether Telenor's dealings with [Myanmar](country:MM)'s military government, which seized power in 2021, enabled crimes against humanity.

Investigators have not said which sanctions statutes or agencies are involved.
