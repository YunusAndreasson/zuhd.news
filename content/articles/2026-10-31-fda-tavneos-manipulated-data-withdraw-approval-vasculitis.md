Washington — Tavneos was approved on manipulated data, US regulators say.

Tavneos, meant to reduce steroid use, sold nearly $500 million in fiscal 2025.

A shareholder suit's report says employees unblinded the data, then reassessed chosen patients, and ChemoCentryx did not tell the regulator.

Amgen told the regulator in July that the totality of the evidence supports continued availability.

The regulator's April withdrawal proposal, citing liver injuries, awaits a decision, and the EU has pulled the drug.
