Russia exported $1.3 billion in pharmaceuticals in 2025.

The 30% year-on-year rise was driven by import substitution under Western sanctions — the blockade designed to create dependency instead accelerated domestic production.

Russia's domestic pharma market hit $38.6 billion; Western governments have not adjusted sanctions.
