Armavir — Russian officials said drones set an 800-square-meter blaze.

The strike is part of a wider [Ukraine](country:UA) campaign hitting [Russia](country:RU)'s refining and logistics network, not its territory.

Officials said nine fire crews responded; Moscow has not disclosed how much fuel capacity the strikes have degraded.
