Atlanta — 48% of Delta flights ran late Sunday, FlightAware shows.

It hit the world's busiest airport days before Thanksgiving travel peaks.

Delta's automated weight-and-balance system failed, so crews used manual workarounds while the Federal Aviation Administration warned 3,500 flights were at risk.

The agency said the delays were unrelated to its air traffic systems, which failed on 13 November.

The ground delay programme, averaging 57-minute waits, was due to run until 12:50 pm Eastern.
