Washington — Trump told the Justice Department Monday to expedite federal executions.

The [US](country:US) executed 47 people in 2025, the most since 2009.

Trump signed the directive while attacking Gov. Gavin Newsom, who commuted all 548 California death sentences.

"When victims are this divided, the death penalty shouldn't be the answer," said Jess Nichol, whose sister was murdered.

A coalition including about 30 county district attorneys has asked the California Supreme Court to halt the commutations.
