Kenge — A military plane hit a residential plot, killing 13-14.

Two lieutenant generals, including the army's auditor general, were among the dead.

The plane developed a technical fault, and Kwango's deputy governor said Kenge had no landing strip.

Civil Aviation Authority chairman Tryphon Kin-Kiey Mulumba said the cause is unknown.

The military opened an investigation and has not said when it will report.
