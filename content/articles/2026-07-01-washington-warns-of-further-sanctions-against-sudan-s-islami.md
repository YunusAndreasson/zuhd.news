Washington — More sanctions loom over an Iran-linked militia.

The [US](country:US) says fighters from [Sudan](country:SD)'s Islamist movement, designated a terrorist group in March, train with Iran's Revolutionary Guard, official Massad Boulos said.

Washington is weighing further designations while a Senate bill would block Sudan from World Bank and IMF loans.
