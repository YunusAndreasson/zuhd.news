United States — Hydrogen for nitro reduction emits 10 to 12 kg of CO2 per kilogram.

Nitro reduction underlies medicines, agrochemicals and dyes, so electrifying it would cut emissions across fine chemicals.

Phenazines built for flow batteries carry electrons from saltwater to an organic solvent, handing the charge to the reactant at the liquid boundary.

Harvard chemists Richard Liu and Michael Aziz published the metal-free method in Nature Catalysis.
