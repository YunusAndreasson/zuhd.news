New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s diesel export duty falls to 16 rupees a litre from 20.

The government imposed it 27 March to keep fuel at home during the West Asia war.

The finance ministry resets export duties every fortnight on oil prices, and cut jet fuel to 10.5 rupees from 15.

Duties on petrol exports stay at 0.5 rupees, and domestic fuel excise is unchanged.

The finance ministry revises the rates again in about two weeks.
