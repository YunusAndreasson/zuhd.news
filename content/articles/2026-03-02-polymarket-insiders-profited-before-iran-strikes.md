6 accounts made $1.2 million betting on Iran strikes. Prediction markets like Polymarket operate on blockchain, giving no regulator real-time visibility — the CFTC, America's futures regulator, warned of exactly this risk months earlier. Rival Kalshi fined 2 users for insider trading the same week; Polymarket has announced no charges.
