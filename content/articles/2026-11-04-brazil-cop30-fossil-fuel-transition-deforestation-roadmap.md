Brazil — [Brazil](country:BR)'s fossil-fuel exit roadmap binds no country.

85 countries wanted it in COP30's outcome, but opponents kept it outside the UN process.

Countries should diagnose their own readiness, then pursue tailored levers, with efforts culminating by 2033.

COP31 opens next week in [Turkey](country:TR), where countries will debate the roadmap.
