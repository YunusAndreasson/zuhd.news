Moscow — [Russia](country:RU) just banned bitcoin mining until 2032.

Miners will now compete for power elsewhere, straining grids the ban didn't reach.

A miner is an electricity buyer wearing a computing costume, bidding up to a coin's value for scarce grid power.

[Kazakhstan](country:KZ) and [Paraguay](country:PY) are courting the same miners the ban is displacing.
