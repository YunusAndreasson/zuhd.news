New York — [US](country:US) hacker group ShinyHunters published internal Madison Square Garden data on celebrity visitors.

Files include home addresses, profile tags, and talent fees for Knicks-linked personalities.

ShinyHunters says Madison Square Garden can pay to delete the data; the hack occurred June 5, days before the Knicks won the title.
