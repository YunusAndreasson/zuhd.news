Washington — Trump proposed $1.5 trillion in defense spending for FY2027.

The budget doubles military funding while cutting the State Department 30%, NASA 23%, and HUD by $10.7 billion — the Iran war paid for by gutting domestic programs.

Congress must pass the budget; Senate Democrats called it "rotten to the core" and vowed to block it.
