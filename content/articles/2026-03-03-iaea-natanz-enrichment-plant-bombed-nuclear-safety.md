The IAEA confirmed bombs struck Natanz's uranium enrichment plant. Natanz's centrifuges spin uranium hexafluoride at supersonic speeds; a breach releases radioactive gas, but the IAEA expected no radiation release from this strike. Wartime damage to Natanz is now confirmed twice in 9 months; no arms treaty governs attacks on enrichment plants.
