Milan — Police tear-gassed stone-throwing protesters outside the Duomo.

[Italy](country:IT)'s protests in Milan, Bologna and Turin target inflation, low wages and underfunded healthcare.

Hooded groups broke from the march, and in Bologna police used a water cannon and made 7 arrests.

Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni said thousands marched peacefully, including a rally called by the Italian General Confederation of Labour.

Meloni, facing an election next year, demanded opposition parties condemn the violence without ambiguity.
