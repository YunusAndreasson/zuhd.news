Palestinians returning from Umrah slept at the Jordanian border. Israel sealed all West Bank and Gaza crossings as it escalated strikes on Iran, locking 2 million Palestinians inside occupied territory. Israel's territorial administration, COGAT, announced closures would last "until further notice," leaving dialysis patients cut off from hospitals and pilgrims stranded at the border.
