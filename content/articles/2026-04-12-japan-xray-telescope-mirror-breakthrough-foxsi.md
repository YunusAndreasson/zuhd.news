Tokyo — New X-ray optics resolve 3.5mm objects from 1km. Focusing X-rays requires grazing-incidence mirrors; the team also built a first-of-its-kind ground simulator to verify optics before launch. The telescope flies on the US-Japan FOXSI rocket; success would enable high-resolution X-ray science on small satellites.
