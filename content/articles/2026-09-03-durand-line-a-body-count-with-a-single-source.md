Miranshah — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s military says it killed 15 fighters.

The claim rests on one military statement, with no independent verification.

The Inter-Services Public Relations says the men crossed from [Afghanistan](country:AF) over the Durand Line, a border Kabul has never recognized.

No independent source has corroborated the statement or the death toll.
