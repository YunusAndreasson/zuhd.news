Iranian missiles severely damaged Al Udeid's Combined Air Operations Center, [Qatar](country:QA)'s main US air base, early in the US-[Iran](country:IR) conflict.

The center ran US campaigns in Afghanistan, Iraq, Syria, and Yemen; personnel had evacuated to South Carolina before the strikes.

Whether to rebuild the facility, now within Iranian missile range, remains unresolved.
