New Delhi — "Agitation cannot justify police excesses," the court said.

Chief Justice Surya Kant's bench will hear pleas Tuesday over baton and teargas use on students protesting exam-paper leaks in [India](country:IN)'s capital.

The court wants uniform protocol across states; Tuesday's hearing decides whether any record of the force is ordered.
