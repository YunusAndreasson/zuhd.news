Anchorage — Nine flipped seats give Democrats 56 in the Senate.

That still falls short of the two-thirds needed to override a Trump veto.

Peltola won 50.1% of first-choice votes to Sullivan's 45.8%, vote counts show, so no ranked-choice rounds were needed.

The [US](country:US) Justice Department is still investigating whether her campaign recruited a namesake spoiler, which she denies.

The department says it will appeal the dismissal of its suit over the second Sullivan.
