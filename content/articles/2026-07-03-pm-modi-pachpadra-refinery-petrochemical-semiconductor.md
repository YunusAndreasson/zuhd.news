Balotra — A ₹79,459 crore refinery came online Saturday.

The plant refines 9 million tonnes of crude yearly into fuel and petrochemicals, cutting [India](country:IN)'s import reliance.

The Sanand chip plant, opened the same day, targets 5 billion semiconductors annually once fully scaled.
