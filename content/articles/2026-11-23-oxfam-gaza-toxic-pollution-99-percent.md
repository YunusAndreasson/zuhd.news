Gaza City — Contamination sources cover more than 99% of Gaza, Oxfam found.

Residents absorb it through air, water, food and soil, and clearing rubble releases more.

[Israel](country:IL)'s bombardment destroyed all 6 wastewater plants, so 40,000 cubic metres of raw sewage enter the sea daily, Oxfam estimates.

Oxfam wants a full independent investigation and a five-stage clean-up plan before rebuilding begins.
