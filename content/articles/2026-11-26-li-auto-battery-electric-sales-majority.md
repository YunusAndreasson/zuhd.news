Beijing — Battery-electric cars passed 50% of Li Auto sales.

[China](country:CN)'s Li Auto lost 310 million yuan in the third quarter, down 50.4% from a year earlier.

Li Auto's president disclosed the share on the earnings call, and the company does not report electric models' profit separately.

Vehicle gross margin recovered to 15.3% from 9.4%, still below 2024's 19.8%.

Li Auto expects fourth-quarter deliveries of 110,000 to 115,000 and a return to profit.
