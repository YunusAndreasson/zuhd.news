Sanaa — Houthis say they fired 11 missiles at [Saudi](country:SA) forces, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

The claimed targets lie south of Bab el-Mandeb, the Red Sea's southern gateway.

Houthi spokesman Yahya Saree said the strikes on Al-Suqia, Ras al-Arah and Al-Mudaraba killed or wounded dozens, including commanders.

Coalition spokesman Turki al-Maliki said forces destroyed a launcher in Sanaa used in a recent attack on Riyadh.

TASS carried no coalition confirmation of any casualties or damage.
