Paris — France's economy shrank 0.1% before Fitch cut its outlook.

Fitch is now the most pessimistic of the 3 major agencies rating [France](country:FR).

10-year yields hover near 5%, debt nears 120% of GDP, and the German spread is widest since 2011.

Fitch kept the A+ rating and cited a wealthy, diversified economy and resilient banks.

Finance minister Roland Lescure wants a budget cutting the deficit to 5% by year-end.
