Marjayoun — Mortar shells killed a UN peacekeeper in [Lebanon](country:LB).

Shells hit a UN Interim Force in Lebanon position near Marjayoun Wednesday; the wounded peacekeeper was airlifted to Beirut and died, 2 others injured.

The force warned of rising numbers of impacts and trajectories across southern Lebanon.
