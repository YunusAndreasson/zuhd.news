Miami — Alex Saab pleaded guilty to money laundering.

Saab ran Maduro's sanctions-evasion finances for years, laundering food-import and oil-contract proceeds.

Saab was a Colombian businessman who managed sanctions-era deals for [Venezuela](country:VE)'s government.

A sentencing date for Saab has not yet been set.
