China's energy emissions fell for 21 consecutive months. The new five-year plan targets a 17% cut in carbon intensity by 2030 — a metric that allows absolute emissions to still rise 3-6%. Experts described the immediate 2026 annual target as "alarmingly lax" and inconsistent with a 1.5°C pathway.
