Dallas — Texas police now hold suspects for ICE.

Senate Bill 4 forces police to hold suspects 48 hours for ICE pickup, a quieter tactic after fatal Minneapolis raids.

The new Homeland Security secretary has vowed to keep the tactic out of headlines within six months.
