$7.25 billion is on the table for 100,000 Roundup cancer claims. German pharmaceutical company Bayer proposed the settlement after its glyphosate weedkiller was linked to non-Hodgkin lymphoma — the company has already paid $11 billion since acquiring Monsanto in 2018. The deal needs court approval while the Supreme Court weighs Bayer's separate appeal to shield companies from future lawsuits.
