Drones struck Fujairah's oil hub, 150km east of Dubai. Fujairah holds one of the world's largest oil storage hubs, east of the Strait of Hormuz where tanker traffic has been blocked. The strike was the 2nd drone attack on UAE energy infrastructure in 48 hours; no group has claimed responsibility.
