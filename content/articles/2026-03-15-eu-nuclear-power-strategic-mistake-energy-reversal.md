Europe's nuclear power share halved since 1990. European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen called the phase-out a mistake, pointing to the Strait of Hormuz closure as proof of the cost of fossil fuel dependency. A €200 million innovation fund and a 2030 target for small modular reactors now signal the reversal.
