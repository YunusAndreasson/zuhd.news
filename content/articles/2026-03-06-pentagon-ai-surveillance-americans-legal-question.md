No US law bars military AI profiling of Americans. Because the Fourth Amendment was written for wiretaps, agencies can legally buy location data and browsing records without a warrant. US Senator Ron Wyden announced bipartisan legislation to restrict commercial data purchases, reviving a bill that has stalled in Congress since 2021.
