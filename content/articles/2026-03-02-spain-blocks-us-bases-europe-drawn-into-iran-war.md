Spain alone refuses US base access for Iran strikes. Spain's Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez called the strikes "unjustified" after Britain reversed course Sunday and authorized US base access, splitting NATO between collective defense and collective offense. 7 aircraft that departed Spanish bases this weekend landed at Germany's Ramstein — Washington's fallback if Spain holds its position.
