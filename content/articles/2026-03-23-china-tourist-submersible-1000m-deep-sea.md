Wuxi — Titan's lethal depth is now a Chinese tourist target. China Ship Scientific Research Centre is engineering a hull for 1,000m dives, where pressure reaches 100 atmospheres — the same force that imploded Titan in 2023, killing 5. A prototype is planned this year, with 4-passenger commercial dives by 2030.
