Australia committed AU$3.9 billion to a submarine facility near Adelaide. The initial outlay is part of a deal with the United States and Britain under the AUKUS pact that could cost up to $235 billion over 30 years, with the first submarines expected by 2032. The program accelerates a military build-up in the Indo-Pacific, where China has expanded its naval presence in contested waters.
