[Spain](country:ES)'s High Court has never investigated a former prime minister for corruption; the probe centers on the 2021 Plus Ultra airline bailout.

Prosecutors are seeking Zapatero's court appearances every 2 weeks; Prime Minister Sánchez's wife faces a separate trial decision within days.
