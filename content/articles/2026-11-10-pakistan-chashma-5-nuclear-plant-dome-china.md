Mianwali — Chashma-5's dome is up, 1,200 megawatts pending.

Nuclear supplies 16.6% of [Pakistan](country:PK)'s electricity from 8.6% of capacity, the atomic energy commission says.

The sealed dome ends civil works, and installation begins on Pakistan's seventh nuclear plant built with help from [China](country:CN).

"Power that does not rise and fall with the price of imported fuel," said Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif.

The government expects first electricity in September 2030.
