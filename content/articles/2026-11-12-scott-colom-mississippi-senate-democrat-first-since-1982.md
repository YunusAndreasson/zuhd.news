Jackson — No Democrat had won Mississippi's Senate seat since 1982.

Scott Colom will be the state's first Black senator in more than 140 years.

Black residents are 37% of Mississippi, the highest share of any state, and joined white voters angry about prices.

Only a few thousand votes separated Colom from Senator Cindy Hyde-Smith, and counting took more than a week.

Colom is sworn in next January, giving Democrats 55 seats in the [US](country:US) Senate with Alaska uncalled.
