Turkey offered to extend the Kirkuk-Ceyhan pipeline to Basra. The Kirkuk-Ceyhan pipeline has sat idle since ISIS attacked it in 2014 but could carry 1.5 million barrels daily, bypassing Hormuz. Iraq expects oil flows to restart within weeks, though Turkey's proposed Basra extension would take years to complete.
