Jazan — Houthi strikes shut Aramco's 400,000-bpd Jazan refinery.

Crude is fungible and easily rerouted; refined fuel is not, so the outage tightens diesel and petrol across the Red Sea basin.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s Aramco says the refinery restarts by 15 August, its only public damage estimate.
