Cape Town — Woolworths Foods turnover grew 1.9%, concession sales 0.1%.

[South Africa](country:ZA)'s consumer shares fell, and Woolworths' in-store inflation runs above rival chains'.

Rand Swiss analyst Viv Govender says shoppers leaving Woolworths are changing the food they buy for their children.

Clothing groups fell hardest, and the next trading updates will show whether the squeeze spreads.
