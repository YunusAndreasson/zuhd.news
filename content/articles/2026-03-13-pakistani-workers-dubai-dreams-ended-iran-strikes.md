Intercepted Iranian missile debris killed Muzaffar Ali, 27, in Dubai. He is one of 3 Pakistani workers killed in Gulf states since Iran began retaliatory strikes 2 weeks ago — more than 5.5 million Pakistanis work across the region, sending home remittances worth 3-5% of Pakistan's GDP. Capital Economics warned the conflict could collapse that flow, while Ali's family in Jamshoro has received no compensation from the UAE or Pakistani governments.
