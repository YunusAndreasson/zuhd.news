Cairo — A court fined Lina Attalah LE3 million and closed Mada Masr.

[Egypt](country:EG) loses its last major independent newsroom by court order, and its editor goes to custody.

The New Cairo Misdemeanor Court convicted her of managing an unlicensed website, then ordered its closure and the confiscation of its property.

The judge appointed a lawyer Attalah rejected and refused to record her objection, said rights lawyer Hossam Bahgat.

Attalah remains in custody at the courthouse.
