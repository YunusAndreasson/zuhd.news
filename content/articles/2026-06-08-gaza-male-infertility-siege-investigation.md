Gaza — Siege destroyed all 9 of Gaza's fertility clinics.

Malnutrition, toxic rubble, and chronic stress from the blockade degrade sperm motility — harm reaching a generation not yet conceived.

Fertility treatment now requires leaving Gaza; most men cannot make the crossing.
