Seattle — Microsoft dropped legal threats against vulnerability researchers.

Public backlash followed contract language that appeared to let Microsoft threaten researchers who disclosed vulnerabilities without permission.

The statement is not a binding safe-harbour policy; Microsoft has made no changes to its legal terms.
