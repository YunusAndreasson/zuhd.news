Niamey — Niger's generals thanked Africa Corps in person.

The claim reframes overnight gunfire at the airport as an internal army revolt.

Ambassador Viktor Voropayev, the account's only source, says [Niger](country:NE) hired [Russia](country:RU)'s Africa Corps after expelling French and US troops.

Niamey has not confirmed a mutiny occurred or that Russian forces intervened.
