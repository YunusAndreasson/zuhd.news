Tokyo — SoftBank booked over $3 billion in losses on turbine deposits.

That is over half of its $7.1 billion quarterly loss, announced Tuesday.

SoftBank reportedly put nonrefundable deposits on 13 gigawatts of turbines, almost 50% more than needed, as order queues run into the early 2030s.

Brent Nelson of Ascend Analytics says most data centres would do better with a smaller grid connection plus batteries.

Winter curtailments, when gas supply dips and demand spikes, remain the risk Nelson flags for gas-reliant data centres.
