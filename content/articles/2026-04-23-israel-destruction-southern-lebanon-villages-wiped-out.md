South Lebanon — [Israel](country:IL) emptied a 40-kilometer corridor between the Litani and Zahrani Rivers, killing 2,294 and displacing a million, Lebanese health ministry figures show. The evacuation followed a systematic bombardment of towns and villages, many now destroyed. A ceasefire took effect April 17; thousands of displaced residents remain barred from returning.
