Seattle — Shayan Oveis Gharan won the Abacus Medal.

He cracked decades-old algorithm problems, including the traveling salesperson problem, a routing puzzle with wide industrial use.

The prize goes once every four years, only to mathematicians under 40, with the next award in 2030.
