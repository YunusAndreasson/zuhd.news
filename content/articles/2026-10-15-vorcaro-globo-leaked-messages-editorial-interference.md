Rio de Janeiro — Globo fired a 29-year executive over messages to Daniel Vorcaro.

Police intercepts show the jailed Banco Master owner complained to Globo about its editorial line.

[Brazil](country:BR)'s federal police intercepted the messages from Vorcaro's phone, covering October 2023 to July 2025.

Globo says the messages show an inappropriate relationship with an advertiser and affirms its journalism's independence.

Globo says it will name Frederic Kachar's successor at Editora Globo and its radio system soon.
