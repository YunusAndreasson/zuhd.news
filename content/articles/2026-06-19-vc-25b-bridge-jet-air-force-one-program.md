Andrews AFB — Boeing's presidential jet replacements remain years overdue.

A [Qatar](country:QA)-gifted 747-8i, modified in 10 months, fills the gap while Boeing's official VC-25B replacements stall.

Boeing's 2 replacement jets have no confirmed delivery date; the bridge aircraft enters service once commissioning flights clear.
