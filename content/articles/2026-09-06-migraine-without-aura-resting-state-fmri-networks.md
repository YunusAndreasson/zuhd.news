London — Migraine without aura carries no warning sign.

Patients, mostly women, are diagnosed on description alone and are routinely dismissed or under-treated.

Resting-state scans measure which brain networks activate together when a patient is not having an attack, isolating a baseline signature.

Researchers call the finding preliminary, pending larger independent confirmation.
