Eurozone — inflation has topped 2% for eight straight months.

Surging oil and gas prices are eroding household spending power across 21 countries.

Eurostat's flash estimate hit 4% in October, up from 3.8%, as the [US](country:US)-Iran war's energy shock reached household bills.

The Bank of England's new sticky-inflation gauge shows little sign of building UK price pressure.

Traders see roughly 70% odds the European Central Bank raises rates a quarter point on 17 December.
