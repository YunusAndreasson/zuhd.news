Netherlands — [China](country:CN)'s share of ASML's sales jumped from 14% to 23%.

The [US](country:US) is pressing the Netherlands to restrict exports of the less advanced machines China buys.

Sales to China rose 109% from the prior quarter, and ASML expects China to account for about 20% of 2026 sales.

Prime Minister Rob Jetten says he expects a deal with Washington on the proposed Match Act.
