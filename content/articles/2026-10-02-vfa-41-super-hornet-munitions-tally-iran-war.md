San Diego — VFA-41 dropped 173 500-pound JDAMs during Operation Epic Fury.

The Pentagon has released little official data on munitions expended against [Iran](country:IR) this year.

A photographed squadron board also lists 82 2,000-pound JDAMs, 41 glide bombs, 33 Sidewinders and 10 cruise missiles.

The USS Abraham Lincoln, the squadron's carrier, returns to San Diego within days.
