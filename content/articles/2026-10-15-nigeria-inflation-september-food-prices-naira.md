Abuja — Food inflation rose to 19.59% as headline inflation fell.

Food takes a substantial share of lower-income households' spending.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s headline inflation fell to 14.63% from 15.39% in August, and core inflation to 12.95%, the statistics bureau said.

Food inflation was 20.16% a year earlier, so the annual pace has moderated.

Households are still absorbing fuel-subsidy removal, the naira's devaluation and tight monetary policy.
