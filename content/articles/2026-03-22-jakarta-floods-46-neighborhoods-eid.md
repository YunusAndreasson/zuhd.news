Jakarta — 80cm of water flooded 46 Jakarta neighborhoods on Sunday. Jakarta sinks up to 25cm per year from groundwater extraction, meaning each heavy rain finds a city incrementally lower and more flood-prone. Indonesia's replacement capital, Nusantara, lags behind schedule as millions of Jakartans remain in the flood zone.
