London — A [UK](country:GB) jury acquitted ex-oil minister Diezani Alison-Madueke of bribery.

A Nigerian civil-society watchdog said domestic institutions failed to build the case; frozen assets linked to her tenure remain in legal limbo.

The watchdog urged Nigeria's anti-corruption commission to pursue all outstanding criminal and civil cases.
