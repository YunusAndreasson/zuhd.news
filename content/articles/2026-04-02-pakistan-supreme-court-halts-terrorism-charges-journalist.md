Islamabad — Pakistan's Supreme Court blocked terrorism charges against journalist Matiullah Jan.

Anti-terrorism courts apply harsher procedures than civilian courts; prosecutors must prove intent to spread terror — a threshold Jan's lawyers say the complaint never meets.

The case returns to Islamabad High Court, yet to rule on jurisdiction.
