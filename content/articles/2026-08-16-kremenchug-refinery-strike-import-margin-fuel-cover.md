A country that cannot refine pays a premium on every drop of imported fuel.

The claim comes from Russia's defense ministry alone, and no Ukrainian or neutral source has confirmed the damage.

Storage tanks at Kremenchuk kept handling imported fuel after the strike, Russia's own ministry said.
