Rob Jetten commands just 66 of 150 parliamentary seats. The 38-year-old D66 leader became the Netherlands' youngest prime minister after Geert Wilders' far-right coalition collapsed over asylum policy. Every bill needs opposition support to pass, with parties across the spectrum already opposing the coalition's defense and welfare plans.
