Mumbai — Jio Platforms shares rose 21.4% after [India](country:IN)'s largest initial public offering.

Investors oversubscribed the 302 billion rupee ($3.2 billion) offering 22 times.

Shares opened at ₹1,357 against an upper price band of ₹1,119, as Reliance Industries moves beyond oil refining revenue.

The Nifty 50 fell 0.32% and the Sensex 0.47% as of 09:15am.
