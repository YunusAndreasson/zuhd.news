Singapore — Middle East crude exports peaked at 22.5 million barrels daily, Kpler data show.

That beats the 18 million pre-war average, even as at least 7 tanker attacks hit Hormuz.

Shipping intelligence firm Marisks says Iranian missiles may hit a fixed engagement zone, so presence there, not the ship, is the exposure.

The 7-day average was 18.5 million barrels a day on 1 October.

The UK maritime trade agency has logged at least one attack a day since 2 October.
