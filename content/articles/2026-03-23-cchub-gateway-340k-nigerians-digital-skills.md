Lagos — Co-Creation Hub (CcHUB) will train 340,000 Nigerians for global freelance platforms. The 5-year GATEWAY program covers 4 digital skills tracks across 10 states, sorting participants into beginner or advanced cohorts before connecting them with international clients. Whether that pipeline converts training into sustained global income — not just platform enrollment — is the program's central test.
