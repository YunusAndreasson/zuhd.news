Taliban claimed to capture a Pakistani military post. Pakistan's information ministry denied the claim, but the exchange escalates a conflict that has moved from drone strikes to ground-level territorial fighting. Pakistan's 313 Corps now faces Taliban drone capabilities it did not expect, strained by its own airstrikes on Kabul.
