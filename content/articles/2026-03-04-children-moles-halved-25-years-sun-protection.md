Children have 47% fewer moles than 25 years ago. Queensland's Slip Slop Slap campaigns cut UV exposure before age 12, when most moles develop, physically altering children's skin. Because mole count predicts melanoma risk, researchers say this generation will have lower skin cancer rates than any before it.
