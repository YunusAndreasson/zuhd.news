Washington — Every member of Kennedy's preventive services panel got conflict waivers.

The [US](country:US) Preventive Services Task Force decides which screenings insurers must cover free.

Waiver documents show two members own private practices that could profit from the care the panel recommends.

A top federal official signed the waivers, though Kennedy frequently decries corporate influence in health care.

Others hold drug and device stock, and the newest members carry the most extensive conflicts.
