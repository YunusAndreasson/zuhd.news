Washington — A bet is as good as its judge.

The Commodity Futures Trading Commission told Kalshi, Polymarket, Coinbase and Crypto.com to stop filing broad template contracts and detail how each resolves.

The regulator's advisory does not name a deadline for firms to revise their filings.
