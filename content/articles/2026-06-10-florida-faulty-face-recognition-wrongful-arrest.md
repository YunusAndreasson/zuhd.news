Jacksonville Beach — Facial recognition jailed Robert Dillon 300 miles from the crime scene.

Police had license plate records showing he was never in the county but omitted them from the arrest warrant, the lawsuit alleges.

He is 1 of 15 known wrongful-arrest cases; the American Civil Liberties Union suit demands limits on police use of facial recognition.
