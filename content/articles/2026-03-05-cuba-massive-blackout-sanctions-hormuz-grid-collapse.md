All of Havana lost power again Thursday. US sanctions blocked Venezuelan oil and the Hormuz shutdown cut alternative supplies, stranding a Soviet-era grid that needs both to function. The outage will last days, threatening hospitals and water systems serving Cuba's 11 million people.
