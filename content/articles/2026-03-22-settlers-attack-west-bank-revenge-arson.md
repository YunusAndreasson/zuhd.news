West Bank — Settlers burned Palestinian homes after a fatal road collision. Right-wing groups posted calls for revenge on social media within hours, in an episode Israeli human rights groups say reflects near-total impunity for settler violence. Israeli forces made no arrests; with settler convictions below 7 percent, the Palestinian families face displacement while those responsible face no legal consequence.
