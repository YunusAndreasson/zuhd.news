Tokyo — [Japan](country:JP) bought 720,000 Sakhalin barrels, a ruling party official said.

Japan banned Russian crude in 2022 but kept a Sakhalin-2 exception; Gulf war disruption to the Strait of Hormuz prompted the government request.

Japan's Petroleum Association head named [Russia](country:RU) as a possible further supplier; the government has not confirmed.
