New Delhi — Police reportedly detained 400-500 people before Saturday's protest.

They closed roads and metro stations and cancelled trains to block a rally against the election chief.

Protesters demand Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar resign over alleged voter-list manipulation, which [India](country:IN)'s Election Commission denies.

"If you want to jail us or even shoot us we are prepared," said Abhijeet Dipke, the movement's founder.

The Cockroach Janata Party says it will march to Jantar Mantar on Saturday morning regardless.
