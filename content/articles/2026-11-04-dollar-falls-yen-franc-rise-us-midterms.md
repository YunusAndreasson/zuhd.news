Hong Kong — The franc rose almost 1% and the yen 0.7%.

Democratic wins in early [US](country:US) results gave the dollar its worst day in a month.

Brent crude fell more than 7% Monday after Tehran said US contacts continued, and the dollar index hit a one-month low.

OCBC strategist Sim Moh Siong wrote that AI-driven stock losses and election risks still support the dollar.

Economists polled expect the Bank of England to raise Bank Rate to 4.0% from 3.75% this week.
