Pishin — Gunmen shot dead four road workers Tuesday.

The attack raises the security cost of every project along the corridor.

Armed men on motorcycles targeted unarmed migrant labourers rather than their security escort, the area's second attack in three days.

Police have opened an investigation, but the gunmen remain unidentified.
