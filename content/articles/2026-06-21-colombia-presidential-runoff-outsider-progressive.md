Bogota — [Colombia](country:CO)'s oil moratorium hangs on Sunday's runoff.

Petro's heir Cepeda backs the ban on new exploration; outsider De La Espriella, who led the first round 44%-41%, would reverse it.

[US](country:US) President Trump endorsed De La Espriella; results expected hours after polls close at 4 p.m. local time.
