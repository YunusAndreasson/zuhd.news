Mandi Bahauddin — Gunmen shot an Ahmadi man near Eidgah Road.

[Pakistan](country:PK) legally classifies Ahmadis as non-Muslim, making the minority identifiable to attackers.

The constitution bars Ahmadis from Islamic religious practice and lists them separately on the electoral roll.

Police say the killing appears targeted, with no arrests made yet.
