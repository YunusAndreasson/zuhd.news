Seoul — 78 skeletons confirmed Silla royals practiced systematic cousin marriage. DNA from the Imdang-Joyeong site revealed the pattern across burial strata — rank consolidation that extended to individuals selected for sacrifice. Researchers say the method can now recover marriage networks wherever skeletons survive and written records do not.
