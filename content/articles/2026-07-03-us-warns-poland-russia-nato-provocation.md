Warsaw — Washington reportedly warned [Poland](country:PL) of a coming Russian provocation.

Media reports say [Russia](country:RU) is planning an armed test of NATO's resolve, calibrated to stay below the threshold that triggers collective defense.

The prospect remains months away; neither US nor Polish officials have confirmed the reported warning.
