Beijing — [China](country:CN)'s property slump has likely bottomed, think tank CF40 says.

Its report says the current-account surplus-to-GDP ratio may peak, after property's slump widened it.

CF40 tracked 33 core cities, where prices and home sales have stabilised, while most third- and fourth-tier cities stay weak.

The report says further adjustment remains possible but another sharp contraction appears unlikely.

If new starts keep falling sharply, CF40 says excess housing inventory could turn into a shortage.
