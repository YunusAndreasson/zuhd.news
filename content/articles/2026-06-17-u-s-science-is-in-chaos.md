Washington — The Department of Government Efficiency cut roughly a fifth of NASA's workforce.

Federal grants fund 40% of all [US](country:US) basic research; agencies are now aligning budgets to presidential priorities before Congress acts, bypassing the postwar autonomy model.

Displaced researchers are leaving — Asian and Gulf institutions are hiring what American labs cannot retain.
