Zhengzhou — Apple's factories run without warehouses or inventory.

That efficiency shifted the cost of demand swings onto factory workers.

Components arrive as they're consumed, so surges are absorbed by longer shifts rather than stockpiles.

Two decades of dependency make leaving [China](country:CN) a geopolitical choice, not a business one.
