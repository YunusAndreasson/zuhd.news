Hong Kong — GOGOX agreed to sell its mainland business for 1 yuan.

The unit lost RMB49.3 million after tax on RMB44 million revenue in early 2026.

After years of heavy spending, Kuaigou held 0.5% of intra-city freight against Lalamove's 61% in 2025, one estimate shows.

GOGOX says only ownership changes, and Kuaigou Dache keeps operating under its new owner.

The sale still needs shareholder approval, and GOGOX would rename itself GOGO Technology.
