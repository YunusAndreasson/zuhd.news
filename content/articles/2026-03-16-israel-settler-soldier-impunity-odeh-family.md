Israeli forces shot the Odeh family dead in Tammun. They were 2 parents and 2 children, killed under rules of engagement the Netanyahu government loosened during the Iran war emergency. No soldiers face charges under attorney general guidelines that Haaretz says make prosecution in the West Bank effectively impossible.
