Ouagadougou — [Burkina Faso](country:BF) summoned [Nigeria](country:NG)'s top diplomat.

Niger, Mali and Burkina Faso quit the Economic Community of West African States in 2025, ending intelligence-sharing that once tracked cross-border jihadist groups.

Nigeria has not yet responded; Defence Minister Christopher Musa has called for renewed coordination between the two blocs.
