48 shell companies exported $90 billion in sanctioned Russian crude. A Financial Times investigation traced the network through a shared email server to traders linked to Azerbaijan and Dubai, handling over 80% of Rosneft's seaborne exports. The scale reveals how far sanctions evasion has outpaced enforcement.
