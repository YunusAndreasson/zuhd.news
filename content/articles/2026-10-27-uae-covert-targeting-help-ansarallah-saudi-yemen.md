Sanaa — [UAE](country:AE)-aligned Yemeni forces give Ansarallah targeting help, four sources say.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA) sidelined the Emirates in Yemen earlier this year.

Two sources say Abu Dhabi itself passes Ansarallah intelligence, limited to targets inside Yemen.

One source says a senior US official told Abu Dhabi to stop, but the sharing continues.

Polymarket traders give a Saudi-Houthi ceasefire by 31 December a 24% chance, down 7 points in a week.
