Berlin — Police arrested seven suspects across two continents at once.

Coordinated timing matters because any single arrest can alert the rest of a crew to destroy evidence.

Germany's federal police charged three suspects with fraud while Brazil's federal police arrested four more.

Neither agency has named the banks or systems breached.
