Meta blocked rival AI chatbots from WhatsApp in October. The Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa opened an antitrust probe, accusing Meta of reserving WhatsApp Business API access for its own chatbot. WhatsApp dominates messaging across the bloc's 21 member states, making the API a gateway for AI access in the region.
