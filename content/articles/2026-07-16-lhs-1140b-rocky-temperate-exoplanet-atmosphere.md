Cambridge — A planet 50 light-years away has an atmosphere.

Harvard researchers detected escaping helium in starlight passing through LHS 1140b's atmosphere as it orbits a cool dwarf star.

The helium signal vanished in a follow-up observation a year later, and researchers still can't explain why.
