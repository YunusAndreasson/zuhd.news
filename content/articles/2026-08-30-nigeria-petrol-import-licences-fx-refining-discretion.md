Abuja — Petrol imports hit 43% of supply in July.

Unpublished import approvals shape [Nigeria](country:NG)'s exchange rate and its refining industry's future.

The regulator can approve import cargoes without disclosing what shortfall they fill, a business lobby says.

The Nigerian Midstream and Downstream Petroleum Regulatory Authority has not said whether it will publish supply-gap data before approving imports.
