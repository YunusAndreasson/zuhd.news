China — Xinzhansu began mass-producing the country's first PCIe 6.0 solid-state drive.

Faster storage stops AI chips idling while they wait for data.

PCIe 6.0 doubles bandwidth to about 28 gigabytes a second, halving cache reload time on eight-card servers, TMTPost reported.

Some market voices call PCIe 6.0 more performance than buyers need over PCIe 5.0.

Industry data show 2026 high-bandwidth memory capacity sold out and 2027 prices forecast up 121%.
