Washington — A US trade group wants Chinese cars banned.

The Alliance wants a permanent law, not a tariff a president can waive.

The group's letter cites data collection and industrial policy from [China](country:CN) that it says undercuts other automakers.

The Alliance urged Congress to pass the ban before adjourning this year.
