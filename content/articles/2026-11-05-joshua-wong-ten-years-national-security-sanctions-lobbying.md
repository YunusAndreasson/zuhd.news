Hong Kong — Joshua Wong's release moves from January 2027 to November 2036.

Lobbying foreign governments for sanctions carries a 10-year minimum when a court calls it grave.

Judge William Tam set 13 years as the starting point, then cut 3 for Wong's September guilty plea.

Police say they will discuss next steps with the Department of Justice, while Nathan Law remains in [Britain](country:GB).
