Honolulu — Red Hill fuel tanks contaminated 93,000 people's drinking water.

Military leases price "security" but not contamination — making the [US](country:US) military's 250,000-acre Hawaiʻi footprint appear to break even, an Institute for Policy Studies report found.

46,000 acres of leases expire around 2029, the first renegotiation window in a generation.
