Paris — [France](country:FR)'s finance minister told [Britain](country:GB) to "come back, anytime."

Prime Minister Andy Burnham signalled a second Brexit referendum was "possible" in a future Labour manifesto.

Burnham warns the EU's planned Made In Europe scheme, which favours bloc companies for public contracts, would hurt British industry.

"Come back, and you'll be at the table, and we discuss all these things together," Roland Lescure said.

Burnham has not said whether Labour's manifesto will include a second referendum.
