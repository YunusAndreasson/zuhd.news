Syria and the US pledged to uphold Syrian sovereignty. Syrian Foreign Minister Asaad al-Shaibani met United States Secretary of State Marco Rubio at the Munich Security Conference. Mazloum Abdi, commander of the Syrian Democratic Forces, joined the talks to discuss a recent integration agreement with the Syrian government. Rubio said Washington supports "a Syria that is stable, at peace with its neighbors, and protects the rights of all its ethnic and religious minority groups."
