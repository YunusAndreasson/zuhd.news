5 of 7 Iranian footballers dropped Australian asylum bids. Zahra Ghanbari, the team captain, flew to Iran Sunday after rights activists reported the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps threatened players' families. The 2 remaining players face continued IRGC pressure on their families in Iran, rights groups warn.
