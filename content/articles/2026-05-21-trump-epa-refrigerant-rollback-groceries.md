Washington — Trump halted the [US](country:US) Environmental Protection Agency's ban on hydrofluorocarbon refrigerants.

The gases are potent greenhouse compounds; the agency's own analysis found compliance represents a small share of grocery operating costs.

The agency cited $2.4 billion in savings but named no mechanism linking the rollback to grocery shelf prices.
