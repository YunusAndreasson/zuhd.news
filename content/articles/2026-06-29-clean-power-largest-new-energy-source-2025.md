London — Wind and solar grew 18.3% and met all new electricity demand in 2025, the Energy Institute found.

Coal simultaneously set a new record; fossil fuels still supply 86% of primary energy, the agency said.

Electricity demand is rising faster than total energy use, keeping fossil fuels central to the global mix.
