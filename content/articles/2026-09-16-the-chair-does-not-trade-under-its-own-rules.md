Brasília — Coordinated carbon prices could cut emissions sevenfold.

[Brazil](country:BR) now chairs the body deciding which carbon credits the world's buyers will accept.

A Harvard-MIT model found that gain requires 21 countries and the European Union coordinating their carbon prices together.

Brazil hopes [China](country:CN) will start buying its carbon credits, aiming for a deal by November's COP31.
