Shipping lines are abandoning West Africa-to-Europe routes. Hormuz and Red Sea closures force rerouting around the Cape of Good Hope, adding 10-14 days and making West African freight uncompetitive in Europe. The Shipping Association of Nigeria warned the disruption will worsen domestic inflation if the conflict persists.
