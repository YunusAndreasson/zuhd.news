Minnetonka — UnitedHealthcare drops prior authorization on 1,700 procedure codes.

Only about 120 codes apply to Medicare Advantage, where it denies the most claims.

The 1,700 figure bundles 5 separate business lines, and commercial, ACA and Oxford plans got the largest cuts.

KFF found UnitedHealth denied 17% of standard Medicare Advantage requests in 2025, the highest of 6 insurers studied.

UnitedHealthcare has pledged to cut 30% of prior-authorization volume by year's end, with no code list released.
