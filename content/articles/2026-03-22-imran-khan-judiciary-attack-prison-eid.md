Rawalpindi — Imran Khan accused Pakistan's judges of selling their integrity. Khan, imprisoned since 2023, relayed the Eid statement through his son Kasim; his wife Bushra Bibi is separately held in isolation with visits rarely granted. Khan's former wife Jemima Goldsmith alleges the government is pushing his sons to surrender British consular documents, severing his last line of outside support.
