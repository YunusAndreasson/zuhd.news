Sweden Democrats propose fuel tax cuts to offset war-driven energy price spikes. The nationalist party is Sweden's largest in the governing coalition; leader Jimmie Åkesson said preparations for cuts are needed now if the Iran war proves prolonged. The Green Party opposes the plan, arguing it deepens fossil fuel dependence rather than accelerating Sweden's energy transition.
