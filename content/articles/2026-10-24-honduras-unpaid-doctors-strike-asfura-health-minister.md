Tegucigalpa — 727 doctors remain unpaid after 47 days of protest.

[Honduras](country:HN)'s president, Nasry Asfura, who is also health minister, left for [Italy](country:IT) on 20 October.

At Hospital Escuela, renal patient Javier Ordóñez says supplies almost never arrive, and some patients wait three months for appointments.

The Honduran Medical Association says the government already broke an April pay agreement.

Vice Minister José Miguel Castillo promised payment on Friday 23 October.
