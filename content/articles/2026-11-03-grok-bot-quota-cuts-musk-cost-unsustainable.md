United States — Elon Musk says Grok Bot's serving costs outrun its revenue.

Some users saw weekly quotas jump from 50% to 100% without any use.

Grok Bot runs multi-step tasks, and each step can add inference and compute cost the subscription must cover.

SpaceXSI's AI unit made about $7.5bn of $14.6bn third-quarter revenue and $431mn profit, filings show.

Musk has yet to say how Grok Bot's quotas will change.
