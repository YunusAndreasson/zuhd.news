Bissau — Nine days before polling, the vote moved to November 2028.

The army that seized power in [Guinea-Bissau](country:GW) in 2025 was tasked with a one-year transition.

Transitional president Major-General Horta Inta-a's decree cites insufficient technical, material and financial conditions for a vote.

The December charter barred Inta-a from running, but an August vote expanded presidential powers.

The Economic Community of West African States had demanded a short, transparent transition.
