Shetland — A German rocket could launch from British soil.

It could be the first vertical rocket launch ever from [UK](country:GB) soil.

SaxaVord sells location and permits, while the hybrid paraffin-fueled rocket itself belongs to [Germany](country:DE)'s HyImpulse.

The launch window, set by UK aviation notices, runs 5 to 18 October.
