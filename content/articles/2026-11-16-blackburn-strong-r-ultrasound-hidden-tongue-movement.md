Blackburn — Ultrasound caught the town's strong R fading inside speakers' mouths.

East Lancashire is one of the last places in [England](country:GB) still pronouncing R after vowels.

Lancaster University researchers watched speakers' tongues and found younger people made weaker movements for the same traditional R.

"We can see that change happening right before our eyes in people's mouths," said Danielle Turton, the study's lead researcher.

The paper, "Rhoticity in Decline?", was funded by the Leverhulme Trust.
