Dead-star crystals are hiding inside common meteorites. Presolar grains carry isotopes only possible inside ancient stars, fingerprinting the stellar event that seeded the sun. If the grains confirm a stellar-wind origin, they overturn the supernova model dominant since the Allende meteorite landed in 1969.
