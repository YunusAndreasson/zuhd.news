Scientists extracted a 228-metre rock core from Antarctic ice. An international team drilled 523 metres through ice at Crary Ice Rise in West Antarctica, recovering sediment spanning 23 million years. The core will help predict retreat of the West Antarctic Ice Sheet, whose collapse could raise sea levels 5 metres.
