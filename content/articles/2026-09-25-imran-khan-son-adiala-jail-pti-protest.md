Rawalpindi — Imran Khan's son fears a crowd could endanger him.

Kasim Khan says sources told him the establishment may use the protest as pretext to raid Adiala jail.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s Punjab government banned gatherings of 5 or more from 23 September to 5 October.

Authorities cite an imminent threat from militant groups and hostile intelligence agencies.

The Lahore High Court is weighing the 30-day detention of Khan's sisters Uzma and Noreen.
