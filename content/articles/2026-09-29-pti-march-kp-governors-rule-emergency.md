Peshawar — Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's chief minister vows to march on Islamabad 4 October.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s federal government threatens to remove his elected provincial government through governor's rule or an emergency.

Adviser Rana Sanaullah says the Centre can take control of the province and appoint an administrator.

"Let them first cross Swabi," said Governor Faisal Karim Kundi.

Chief Minister Sohail Afridi says marchers reach Swabi, on the Punjab border, in the first phase.
