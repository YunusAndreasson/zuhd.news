Oxford — OpenAI released more than 350 mathematical findings in one day.

OpenAI claims a proof of the quasi-Riemann hypothesis, a conjecture related to a Millennium Prize Problem.

OpenAI withdrew 3 papers and fixed several others by Thursday in its public repository.

"Peer review is gone in this process," said Bryna Kra, a Northwestern mathematician and former American Mathematical Society president.

Rumours among mathematicians say further releases are coming soon.
