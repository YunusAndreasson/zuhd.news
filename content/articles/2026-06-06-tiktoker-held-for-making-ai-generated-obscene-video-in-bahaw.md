[Pakistan](country:PK) police arrested a TikToker for posting an AI-generated sexual video of a female rival and threatening to kill her.

Generative tools enable fabricated sexual content targeting named individuals; Pakistani law had no synthetic media provision before the arrest.

Pakistan's Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act covers the case; platforms face no liability standard.
