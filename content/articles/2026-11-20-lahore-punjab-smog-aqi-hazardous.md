Lahore — Gujranwala's air quality index hit 661 on Friday.

Children, the elderly and people with lung or heart disease face [Pakistan](country:PK)'s worst air.

Punjab inspectors demolished 1,429 brick kilns and sealed 968 since January, department figures show, yet air quality still worsened.

Punjab's environment department is re-inspecting kilns that resume polluting after earlier enforcement.
