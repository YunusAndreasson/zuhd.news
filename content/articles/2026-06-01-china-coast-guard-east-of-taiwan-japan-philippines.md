Taipei — [China](country:CN) sent Coast Guard east of Taiwan as [Japan](country:JP) and [Philippines](country:PH) held maritime boundary talks.

Beijing denounced the talks as a sovereignty infringement and deployed Coast Guard rather than naval ships.

China's Coast Guard said it will continue monitoring and take all necessary measures.
