London — 45% of thalassemia-trait blood tests were potentially low-yield.

Researchers studied 12,622 anemia patients; iron deficiency and post-transfusion samples most often triggered unneeded confirmatory testing, the study found.

Rechecking iron levels first could redirect scarce lab capacity, the authors said.
