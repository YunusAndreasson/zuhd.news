Khan Younis — Gaza's health ministry counted 272 meningitis cases in October.

It declared an epidemic alert across the Strip on 24 November after 15 deaths this year.

Cases normally run 30 to 50 a month, and the ministry blames Israel's destruction of infrastructure, crowded shelters and winter flooding.

"We live alongside insects, mice, and rodents," said Islam al-Ajili, whose 5-month-old is treated at Nasser hospital.

Ministry teams are tracking cases in every governorate as winter sets in.
