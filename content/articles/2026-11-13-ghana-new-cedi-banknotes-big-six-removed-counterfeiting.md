Accra — Only Nkrumah remains of the Big Six on [Ghana](country:GH)'s new notes.

Some designs are 20 years old while counterfeiting technology has evolved, the central bank said.

The smaller notes use reinforced cotton paper and add tactile markings for people with visual impairments.

"Nobody owes anybody any permanent fixture on the currency," said Felix Kwakye Ofosu, minister of state for government communications.

The Heritage Series enters circulation on 1 December alongside old notes, which remain legal tender.
