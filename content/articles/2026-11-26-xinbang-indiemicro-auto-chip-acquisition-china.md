Wuxi — A 2.59bn-yuan automation firm plans to buy a 2.86bn-yuan chip designer.

Indiemicro's largest shareholder, a unit of US-listed indie Semiconductor, would exit with 961 million yuan in cash.

Xinbang's draft pays 1.693bn yuan in shares and 1.163bn in cash, and seeks up to 1.313bn yuan more from investors.

The deal would add 2.108bn yuan of goodwill, at risk if Indiemicro misses its roughly 122 million yuan annual profit pledge.

The Shenzhen Stock Exchange must approve the deal, then [China](country:CN)'s securities regulator must register it.
