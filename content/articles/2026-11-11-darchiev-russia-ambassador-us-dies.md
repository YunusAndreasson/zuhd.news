Moscow — Alexander Darchiev, 66, had been ambassador since March 2025.

He represented [Russia](country:RU) in Washington throughout the warming between the Kremlin and the White House.

He twice directed the foreign ministry's North America department and was ambassador to [Canada](country:CA) from 2014 to 2021.

Ministry spokeswoman Maria Zakharova cited a chronic illness, though the ministry's statement said only that he died suddenly.

The statements published so far name no successor.
