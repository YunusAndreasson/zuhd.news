Nyala — Over 13,000 fled South Darfur's Kubum locality in 3 days, the International Organization for Migration said.

This is the 3rd Beni Halba-Salamat eruption since [Sudan](country:SD)'s 2023 war began; 3 successive ceasefire deals have collapsed.

Rapid Support Forces commander Hemedti brokered the last deal in July 2025; it collapsed within a year.
