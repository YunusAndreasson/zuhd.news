London — Losing HAT1 sped tumor growth in mouse models.

Median glioblastoma survival has stayed near 15 months since 2005, unchanged by new drugs.

HAT1 loss triggered ALDH2, driving the lipid synthesis a dividing cell needs to build new membrane.

Blood-brain barrier delivery remains untested before any HAT1 drug could reach patients.
