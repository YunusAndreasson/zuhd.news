Space-time may be fractal at its smallest scales. Physicist Astrid Eichhorn's theory, asymptotic safety, predicts a quantum fixed point where physics stabilizes — unifying gravity and quantum mechanics without string theory's extra dimensions. Eichhorn is now calculating how matter shapes fractal space-time — territory asymptotic safety has barely begun to chart.
