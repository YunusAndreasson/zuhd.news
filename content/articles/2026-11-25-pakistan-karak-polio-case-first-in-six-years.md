Karak — A 14-month-old girl is [Pakistan](country:PK)'s 10th polio case of 2026.

It is Karak's first case in 6 years and the 8th of the year from Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

The national emergency operations centre says the virus is genetically linked to intense circulation in southern Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

The programme says Pakistan has cut cases 99.8% since 1994, from an estimated 20,000 to 31 in 2025.

Bannu, which borders Karak, has found wild poliovirus in sewage for 8 months.
