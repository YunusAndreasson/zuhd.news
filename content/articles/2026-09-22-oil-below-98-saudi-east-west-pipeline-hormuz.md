Riyadh — Brent crude fell below $98, a two-week low.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s pipeline restart offers Hormuz a bypass Houthi attacks can still contest.

The 1,200km East-West pipeline can carry 7 million barrels daily to the Red Sea port of Yanbu, bypassing the Strait of Hormuz.

Only 2 commercial vessels crossed Hormuz Monday, versus about 125 a day before the war, shipping data show.

[Iran](country:IR) says Hormuz could reopen within a week if Washington lifts its port blockade.
