Johannesburg — Petrol and diesel cost 42.9% more than a year ago.

Food inflation is 1.4%, so the headline hides what low-income households pay to reach work.

Transport rose 13.4% and paraffin 7.9%, and food is bought only after essentials are paid, said Mervyn Abrahams of the Pietermaritzburg Economic Justice and Dignity Group.

Maverick Citizen's food basket costs R390.86, R20.86 more than the R370 relief grant.

The Institute for Economic Justice wants the grant made permanent and tied to a nutrition poverty line.
