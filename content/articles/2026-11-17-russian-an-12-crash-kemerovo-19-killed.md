Yagunovo — All 19 aboard the Defence Ministry's An-12 died.

The An-12 is a Soviet-era transport design still flying for [Russia](country:RU)'s military.

The ministry cites a technical malfunction as the preliminary cause, and the plane burned in an unpopulated area.

Investigators have not established why the aircraft went down.
