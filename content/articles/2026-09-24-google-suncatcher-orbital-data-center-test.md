Mountain View — Google's orbital AI test satellite runs on 1 kilowatt.

Google is pitching orbit as an alternative to divisive terrestrial data centers.

Its 4 AI chips shed heat through radiators that allow only about 15 minutes of operation before shutdown.

The team expects years before Suncatcher becomes a product, and the test satellite will operate only months.

The satellite launches October 1 on a SpaceX Falcon 9 rideshare.
