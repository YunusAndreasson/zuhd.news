United States — Bitcoin fell seven straight weeks, losing 29% since early October.

Spot bitcoin funds lost $1.79 billion last week, their fourth straight weekly outflow.

Bitcoin dropped from about $86,500 to $61,100 by 22 November, TradingView data show, touching $55,500 on Saturday.

Bloomberg Intelligence strategist Mike McGlone sees bitcoin falling toward $10,000.

Analyst Michael Terpin puts the chance of a drop to the low $40,000s at about 10%.
