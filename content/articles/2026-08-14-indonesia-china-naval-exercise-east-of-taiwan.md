Taipei — Indonesian warships drilled with China's navy east of Taiwan.

Taipei wants an explanation, since that water is the US Navy's approach route from Guam.

[Indonesia](country:ID) has long exercised with the US, Australia and China alike under its non-alignment doctrine.

Jakarta's foreign ministry has yet to publicly explain the drill's location.
