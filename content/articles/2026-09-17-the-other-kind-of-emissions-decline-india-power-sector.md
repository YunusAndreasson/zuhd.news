New Delhi — Coal power added zero growth in two years.

That breaks a 50-year pattern in [India](country:IN)'s coal-heavy grid.

Centre for Research on Energy and Clean Air analysts found clean energy met all of India's 7% demand growth over two years, adding 63 terawatt-hours.

India's total emissions still rose 3.7% in the same period as steel and cement output kept climbing.
