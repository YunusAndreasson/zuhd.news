Bangkok — Two bacteria strains raised Jasmine rice yields.

It could cut fertilizer costs for [Thailand](country:TH)'s smallholders, exposed to volatile gas prices.

The strains fix nitrogen and produce a growth hormone, an early-access manuscript reports, substituting biology for imported urea.

Field trials are still needed, since greenhouse microbes often lose to native soil microbes.
