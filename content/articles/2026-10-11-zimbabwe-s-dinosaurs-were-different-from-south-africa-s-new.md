Johannesburg — A 4.5-metre, 220-kilogram Triassic dinosaur walked Zimbabwe on two legs.

[Zimbabwe](country:ZW)'s dinosaurs are not an extension of [South Africa](country:ZA)'s but include species unique to the region.

The incomplete skeleton of Musango matusadonaensis came from the Nyamasango River bank, and bone growth marks show a subadult.

The skull was not preserved, so its diet is unknown.

The researchers suspect environmental conditions explain the difference and are working to test that.
