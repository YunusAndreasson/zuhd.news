Jind — A fuel-cell train now runs Jind to Sonepat emitting only water.

[India](country:IN)'s 10-coach, 3,200-horsepower train beats hydrogen trains elsewhere in coach count, but its climate benefit depends on hydrogen still mostly made from fossil gas.

Indian Railways says the launch is foundational to expanding hydrogen mobility on lightly used routes.
