France — Servier's $1.55 billion drug failed to slow Becker muscular dystrophy.

Patients with Becker muscular dystrophy still have no approved treatment.

Sevasemten showed promise in a smaller randomized study, but the pivotal Phase 3 trial did not align, Servier said.

Servier is analysing the full dataset, with about $1 billion in potential milestone payments still attached to the deal.
