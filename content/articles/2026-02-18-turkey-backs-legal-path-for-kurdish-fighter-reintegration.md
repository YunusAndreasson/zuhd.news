48 of 50 commissioners backed the plan. Turkey's cross-party parliamentary commission recommended revising terrorism laws to reintegrate former Kurdistan Workers' Party fighters after the group renounced violence in January. Legislative debate begins after Ramadan, with the scope of prisoner releases and the exclusion of amnesty as the central disputes.
