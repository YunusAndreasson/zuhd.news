Oxford — Rock erosion can release CO2, amplifying climate warming.

Eroding organic carbon locked in sediments oxidizes into CO2; Oxford researchers found warming that drives erosion also intensifies the carbon release.

Oxford geochemist Hilton estimated the effect reduces the carbon budget over centuries, smaller than permafrost feedbacks.
