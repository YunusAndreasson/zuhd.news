Iran's Supreme Leader claimed victory without appearing in public. Mojtaba Khamenei has governed since a US-Israeli airstrike killed his father and predecessor, Ali Khamenei, on February 28, communicating only through written statements. US strikes are in their 29th day and Hormuz remains closed, contradicting his claim.
