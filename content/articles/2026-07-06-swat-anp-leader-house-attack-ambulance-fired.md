Swat — Gunmen attacked Awami National Party leader Malik Mumtaz Khan's home.

Gunmen then fired on his family's fleeing ambulance; he has lost seven relatives to militant attacks in [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Swat Valley.

Matta police have opened an investigation; no group has claimed responsibility.
