China warned US military AI risks a "Terminator" future. China's Defense Ministry — itself a major military AI investor — warned that assigning "algorithms authority over life-and-death determinations" risks technology exceeding human control. US media report the Pentagon cleared xAI's Grok for classified military use while blocking Anthropic, whose Claude declined autonomous weapons requests.
