Buenos Aires — Washington approved a $103m Black Hawk helicopter sale.

It follows Trump doubting [Britain](country:GB) could defend the Falklands militarily.

The Black Hawks carry 11 troops and fly 370 miles, and the deal includes radar systems and spare parts for [Argentina](country:AR)'s military.

Britain calls Falklands sovereignty "non-negotiable" after Trump, in Dublin, floated sanctioning oil firms there.
