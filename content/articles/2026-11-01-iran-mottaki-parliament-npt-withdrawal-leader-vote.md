Tehran — [Iran](country:IR)'s parliament would unanimously quit the nuclear treaty, Iranian state news agency Mehr reported.

Manouchehr Mottaki, a lawmaker, said on national television that the decision rests with the Leader.

One lawmaker's statement is not a bill, and Mottaki said MPs receive about 200 text messages a day urging withdrawal.

Mehr's report carries no response from the Leader, and it mentions no withdrawal bill.
