Thiruvananthapuram — India's monsoon arrived 4 days late in Kerala.

First miss beyond the error margin since 2015; the weather agency forecasts 90% of average rainfall this season, citing El Niño.

El Niño's suppressive effect peaks July-August, when [India](country:IN)'s rain-fed kharif crop needs moisture most.
