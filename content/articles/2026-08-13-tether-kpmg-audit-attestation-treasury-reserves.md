New York — KPMG counted Tether's gold bars by hand.

Full audits check a year of books, unlike past attestations covering one date.

KPMG issued an unqualified opinion, finding reserves exceeded liabilities by $6.814 billion at end-2025.

Tether has not said whether it will release KPMG's full findings.
