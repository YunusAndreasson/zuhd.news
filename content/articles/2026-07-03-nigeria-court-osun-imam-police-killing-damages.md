Judge Peter Lifu ruled police unlawfully killed imam Kabiru Babai in [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Osun State in 2021, awarding damages against police leadership.

Inspector-General of Police must now prosecute Sergeant Moses Samuel; no deadline was specified.
