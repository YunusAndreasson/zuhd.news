United States Central Command hit more than 30 targets in Syria. American aircraft conducted 10 strikes against the Islamic State, designated a terrorist organization by the United States and European Union, between February 3 and 12. The campaign began after an Islamic State ambush killed 2 American soldiers and an interpreter in Palmyra on December 13. Central Command said at least 50 fighters have been killed or captured and more than 100 targets struck since then. The strikes came as the United States affirmed Syria's sovereignty at the Munich Security Conference.
