Washington — Trump says [China](country:CN) will catch up on warheads within 5 years.

He says the [US](country:US) is negotiating denuclearisation with Moscow and Beijing.

With the Strategic Arms Reduction Treaty expired, nothing caps the 4,000-plus warheads each that the US and Russia hold.

Trump concedes Beijing does not seem willing to disarm, though Stockholm's peace research institute counts only 620 warheads.

Trump reportedly spoke with Xi Jinping about it, but no terms, dates or negotiators have been disclosed.
