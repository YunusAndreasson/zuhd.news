Khartoum — The grid served Khartoum and Port Sudan; Darfur and Kordofan had almost none.

Former minister Abdelrahman argues [Sudan](country:SD)'s peripheral electricity deficit was a structural precondition for conflict — not just a casualty of it.

His paper calls for 40% hydro and 30% solar by 2040, binding any peace settlement to equitable grid distribution.
