Brussels — Meta's updated business terms let it use content from linked websites to develop its products.

Only EU and UK users can object.

European data protection law gives them a right to object, and users file a form in Instagram's privacy center.

Meta also dropped the word train from its list of uses and limited them to advertising and business products.

The terms now take effect on 20 November, not 30 October.
