Evanston — 17,500 antibodies carried manipulated marketing images.

A wrong antibody produces a clean, publishable, wrong result, not an obvious error.

The vendor's own image is usually a buyer's only evidence, and no regulator audits research reagents.

Vendor responses ranged from "we'll look into it" to flat denial.
