Greenbelt — Bolton shared 1,000 classified diary pages with relatives while writing a Trump memoir, prosecutors said.

He pleaded guilty to retaining classified notes; the [US](country:US) investigation began under Biden and continued after Trump returned.

Sentencing is October 28; he faces up to 5 years in prison and a $2.25 million fine.
