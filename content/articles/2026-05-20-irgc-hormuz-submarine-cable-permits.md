Tehran — [Iran](country:IR)'s Revolutionary Guards announced cable-permit demands for Hormuz.

Legal experts say Iran has no right under maritime law; cables run through shallow Hormuz waters where the Guards can block maintenance vessels.

Only one repair vessel serves the Gulf; [Qatar](country:QA), which hosts the AAE-1 terminal, faces the greatest disruption risk.
