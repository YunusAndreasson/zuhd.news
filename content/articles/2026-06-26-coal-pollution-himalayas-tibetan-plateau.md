Lhasa — The Himalayas do not block South Asian coal soot from [China](country:CN)'s Tibetan Plateau, a study found.

Geophysical Research Letters confirmed coal aerosols cross the range unimpeded.

Industrial soot darkens glacier ice and accelerates melt; study authors link South Asian coal to Himalayan glacier retreat.
