Wadi al-Mollak — Two open-bed trucks collided head-on before dawn.

Survivors return to the same road tomorrow because it is their only route to work.

[Egypt](country:EG) classifies such deaths as traffic accidents, not workplace ones, waiving employer liability and inspection.

No compensation has been announced for the 12 dead from Dawoudiya alone.
