Bengaluru — India's launches cost less than Western rivals.

Recipients get imagery, weather and communications but not the launcher or ground-segment control — dependency built for less than a Chinese infrastructure loan.

[Pakistan](country:PK) remains the conspicuous exclusion, buying Chinese satellites instead, while [Bangladesh](country:BD) has since sought other suppliers.
