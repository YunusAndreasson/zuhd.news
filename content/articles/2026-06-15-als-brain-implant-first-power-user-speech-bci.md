Davis — Casey Harrell reads to his daughter through electrodes in his brain.

Harrell has amyotrophic lateral sclerosis and logged 3,800 device-hours over 22 months at 97.5% accuracy, University of California Davis researchers reported in Nature Medicine.

The team is developing a system to restore speech with natural cadence and intonation.
