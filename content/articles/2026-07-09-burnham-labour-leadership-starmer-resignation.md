London — 81 lawmakers, not voters, can install the next prime minister.

Labour's rulebook needs only 20% of its 403 lawmakers to nominate Andy Burnham; Wes Streeting dropped out and endorsed him after Keir Starmer resigned.

Burnham could enter [Britain](country:GB)'s Downing Street by 20 July, with no election required before 2029.
