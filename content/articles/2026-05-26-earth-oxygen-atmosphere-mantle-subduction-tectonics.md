Chengdu — Cold subduction, not just photosynthesis, built breathable air.

Cooler-temperature subduction carries carbon and sulfur into the mantle, hiding oxygen's chemical enemies before they return via volcanoes.

The team's model implies Venus-like stagnant-lid planets cannot sustain breathable atmospheres regardless of their biology.
