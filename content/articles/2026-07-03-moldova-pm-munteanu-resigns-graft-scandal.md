Chișinău — Alexandru Munteanu quit after 8 months as prime minister.

A corruption scandal at [Moldova](country:MD)'s state air-traffic-management company toppled his government, exposing the fragility of institutions underpinning its EU accession push.

Munteanu gave no reason; his resignation triggers a full government collapse under Moldovan law.
