Brasília — Police probe centers on film financing.

The probe threatens Flávio Bolsonaro weeks before [Brazil](country:BR)'s tightest presidential race.

Prosecutors allege banker Daniel Vorcaro funded the elder Bolsonaro's biopic through the collapsed Banco Master.

The Supreme Court's full bench meets Tuesday to review a justice's own ties to the banker.
