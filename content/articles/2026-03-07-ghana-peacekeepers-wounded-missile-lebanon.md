A missile critically wounded 2 Ghanaian UN peacekeepers in Lebanon. Israel's military campaign in southern Lebanon, launched Monday after Hezbollah fired rockets, has put United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon positions in repeated crossfire — the Ghanaian battalion's officers' mess was hit and burned down. Ghana protested to the UN as the offensive continues to threaten the force's peacekeepers across the south.
