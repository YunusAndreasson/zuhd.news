Jaipur — Congress won 27 of Rajasthan's 37 district councils.

The Bharatiya Janata Party won 6, and its state president conceded.

Congress also took 269 of 419 block-level Panchayat Samitis to the Bharatiya Janata Party's 73.

Counting in Kota, Sri Ganganagar, Baran and Karauli takes place on 17 December.
