New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s chief justice wants to know who altered Form 6.

Form 6 is how new voters enter the electoral roll during the Special Intensive Revision.

Petitioners allege Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar altered Form 6 without amending the 1960 rules, over two commissioners' objections.

The Election Commission says differences of opinion are normal and all its orders are legally valid.

The bench issued notices and will review the commission's records before deciding whether to undo any decision.
