Washington — Indus Basin sustains 250 million Pakistanis.

[India](country:IN) suspended treaty machinery in 2025, leaving water sharing legally unresolved.

India calls this "abeyance," not withdrawal — suspending data-sharing and arbitration while claiming the treaty stays binding.

[Pakistan](country:PK) is pursuing arbitration at The Hague over Kishenganga and Ratle hydropower projects.
