Wilmington — North Carolina Ports switched to manual processing after a breach.

Terminal software tracks which container sits where and which trucks may pass; losing it doesn't stop cranes, it erases the yard's index.

Officials have not attributed the intrusion; recovery time, not the breach itself, determines the backlog.
