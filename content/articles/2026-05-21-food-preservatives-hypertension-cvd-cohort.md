Paris — Researchers linked 8 preservatives to 29% higher hypertension risk.

French researchers tracked 112,400 people over 8 years; potassium sorbate, sodium nitrite, and ascorbic acid were among the 8 named compounds.

Lead researcher Mathilde Touvier called for food safety regulators in [France](country:FR) and the [US](country:US) to re-evaluate these additives' risk classification.
