Pakistan ordered a nationwide hawala and hundi crackdown. The Hormuz closure has squeezed Pakistan's foreign exchange reserves, making every dollar moving through informal channels a dollar lost from the banking system. The government ordered a task force from the State Bank of Pakistan and the Federal Investigation Agency but set no enforcement deadline.
