Wyoming — Payward spent billions in 2 years to outgrow Kraken.

Co-CEO Arjun Sethi wants trading, banking and asset management on one ledger.

Payward bought into futures and derivatives, pushed into tokenized stocks and pursued banking capabilities in the [US](country:US) and Europe.

Coinbase and Binance are building similar single platforms spanning trading, payments, investing and derivatives.

Sethi says Payward is profitable and in no rush to go public.
