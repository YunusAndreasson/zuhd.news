UK emissions fell 2.4% in 2025. The UK closed Ratcliffe-on-Soar, its last coal plant, in September 2024, pushing annual coal use to its lowest since the 1600s. Emissions need to fall nearly twice as fast each year to meet the government's 2050 net-zero target.
