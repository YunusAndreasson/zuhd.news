Indigenous communities occupied Cargill's Santarém port for over a month and won. Brazil revoked the decree permitting dredging of the Tapajós River for soy exports, after communities argued the waterway is vital to their survival. The reversal comes days after Brazil's soy industry abandoned its Amazon deforestation moratorium, intensifying pressure from other directions.
