Samsun — One crew member died, three were injured.

A drone struck the MV Reyhan Sari in international waters while it carried Russian coal to [Turkey](country:TR); the vessel's owner says it reached Samsun despite disabled communications.

One crew member remains unreachable; the cabin stays inaccessible as damage assessment continues.
