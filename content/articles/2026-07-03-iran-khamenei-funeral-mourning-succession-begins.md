Tehran — Six days of mourning open in [Tehran](country:IR).

Delegations from about 100 countries arrived as riot police locked down the capital before the procession to Khamenei's coffin at the Mosalla complex.

Successor Mojtaba Khamenei has not appeared publicly since the war; who leads Sunday's funeral prayers is unclear.
