Beijing — Nearly every African import now enters duty-free.

Yet it likely won't narrow [China](country:CN)'s trade surplus with the continent.

Tariffs aren't the real barrier — certification labs, cold chains and port capacity are, and those take years to build, not a signature.

Whether processing moves onto African soil, rather than staying raw-commodity export, will decide if the offer changes anything.
