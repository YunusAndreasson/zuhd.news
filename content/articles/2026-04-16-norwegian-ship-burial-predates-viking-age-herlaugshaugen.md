Leka — Norway's oldest ship burial predates the Viking Age. Dated to ~700 CE, the vessel under Herlaugshaugen mound is a century older than any previously known ship burial. Further excavation may identify the buried individual and reveal what pre-Viking Norse maritime society looked like.
