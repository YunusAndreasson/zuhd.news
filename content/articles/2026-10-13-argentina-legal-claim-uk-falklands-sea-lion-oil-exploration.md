Buenos Aires — [Argentina](country:AR) asked a tribunal to halt new Falklands exploration permits.

Sea Lion, off the Falkland Islands (Malvinas), holds an estimated 1.7 billion barrels.

Argentina says licences the [UK](country:GB) authorised unilaterally violate its sovereign rights over its continental shelf.

The UK says Sea Lion is lawful and lies on the Falklands shelf.

First oil is expected in 2028, and Brent closed at $105.6 on 12 October.
