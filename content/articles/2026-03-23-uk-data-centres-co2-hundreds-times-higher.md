London — UK data centres emit 70 megatons of CO2 annually. The government model assumes nearly carbon-free electricity while the UK grid will still emit 50g CO2/kWh in 2030. At 70 megatons, data centres alone would account for one-fifth of the UK's projected 2035 emissions.
