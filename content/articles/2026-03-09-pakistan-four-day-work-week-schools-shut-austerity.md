Pakistan cut its national work week to 4 days. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif also closed all schools for 2 weeks, moved universities online, and grounded 60% of government vehicles; Pakistan imports nearly all its oil through Gulf routes now blocked by the war. With Hormuz closed and crude above $100, the government has no alternative supply and the crisis deepens daily.
