An autonomous drone debuted aboard China's Type 075 assault ship. The People's Liberation Army Navy drone, designated AR-2000, extends the carrier's surveillance and anti-submarine reach without risking pilots. It could expand the ship's patrol range into contested waters near Taiwan.
