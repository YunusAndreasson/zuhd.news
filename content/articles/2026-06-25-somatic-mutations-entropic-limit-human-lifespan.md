London — No medicine can extend human life past 194 years, a neuroscience model finds.

Neurons, unlike liver cells, cannot self-replace; accumulated somatic mutations make the ceiling irreversible.

The model predicts 146-194 years as the maximum even if every other aging mechanism were cured.
