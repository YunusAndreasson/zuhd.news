Syria asked to prosecute its former ruler. Foreign Minister Asaad al-Shaibani met International Criminal Court President Tomoko Akane at the Munich Security Conference — the first meeting between the two institutions. The discussion centered on holding ousted leader Bashar al-Assad and senior figures of his former government accountable for war crimes committed during the civil war. Syria is not an ICC member state, which limits the court's jurisdiction unless the UN Security Council issues a referral. Al-Shaibani's government, which took power after Assad's fall in December 2024, has not joined the ICC but is seeking the court's cooperation without formal membership.
