Albany — Parts of the eastern Pacific sit 40 centimetres above normal sea level.

Coastal flooding and erosion could hit shores from Alaska to [Chile](country:CL), researcher Daniel Swain warns.

Wind bursts push warm water east as Kelvin waves, which take about 60 days to cross the Pacific.

Oxford researcher Thomas Monahan calculates [Peru](country:PE) and [Ecuador](country:EC) could see rises of 19 to 33 centimetres, peaking late November or December.

California's highest tides are expected in Thanksgiving week and Christmas week, Monahan said.
