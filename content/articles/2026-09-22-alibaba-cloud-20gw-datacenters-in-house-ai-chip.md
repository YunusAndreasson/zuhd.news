Hangzhou — Alibaba targets 20 gigawatts of datacenters, 20 reactors' worth.

US export controls on Nvidia forced [China](country:CN)'s hyperscalers to design their own chips.

CEO Eddie Wu unveiled the Zhenwu V900 chip, tripling its predecessor's performance and scaling to clusters of 500,000 units.

Rivals build faster: Meta plans one 5-gigawatt campus, and Stargate has pledged 10 gigawatts by 2029.

The announcement lands two days before Xi meets Trump in Washington, with the chip's production timeline still unannounced.
