Berlin — [Germany](country:DE)'s draft budget trims sovereignty lines to 129.7 million euros.

The federal administration spent 481.4 million euros on Microsoft licences in 2025, Agora Digitale Transformation found.

The lines, down from 137.9 million, fund the openDesk workplace and the Sovereign Tech Agency.

Chancellor Friedrich Merz called Google's proprietary cloud a truly sovereign solution at November's digital summit.

The Bundestag still has to pass the 2027 draft.
