Sokoto — Islamic State claimed 19 killed in [Nigeria](country:NG).

The Islamic State Sahel Province had not operated in Nigeria before; the north-west had been controlled by bandit militias, not jihadist groups.

Nigeria's military has not confirmed the deaths; the region's bandit-jihadist boundary is now disputed.
