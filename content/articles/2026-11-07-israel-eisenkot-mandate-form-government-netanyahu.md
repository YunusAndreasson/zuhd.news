Jerusalem — 67 of 120 Knesset members recommend Eisenkot for prime minister.

The former army chief is all but certain Sunday to get the mandate to unseat Benjamin Netanyahu.

The recommending parties span left, right, centrist and predominantly Arab factions, and most must break campaign promises to join one coalition.

Failure could send [Israel](country:IL) to another election, with Netanyahu staying prime minister in the meantime.
