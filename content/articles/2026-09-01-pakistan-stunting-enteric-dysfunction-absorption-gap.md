An estimated 70% drink contaminated water in [Pakistan](country:PK).

Around 25 million people practice open defecation, exposing children to repeated infection.

Repeated diarrhea damages the gut lining, so children absorb fewer nutrients from every feeding program calorie.

Health and water ministries still fund nutrition and sanitation from separate budgets.
