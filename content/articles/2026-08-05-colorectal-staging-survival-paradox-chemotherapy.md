London — Lower-stage patients survived worse, a study found.

Stage III triggers chemotherapy because it shows lymph node spread; deep-invading stage II tumors often skip it and go untreated.

Researchers say the current staging rule, set by the American Joint Committee on Cancer and used worldwide, may need reassessment.
