El Hierro — 3,300 arrivals since 1 November, on an island of 11,400.

The island council declared an emergency and asked [Spain](country:ES)'s government for help with housing and a strained hospital.

Patrols off Senegal, Mauritania and Morocco pushed departures south to The Gambia, Guinea-Bissau and Guinea, making crossings longer.

Spain's interior ministry counts 13,327 sea arrivals from January to October, 5.5% fewer than last year.

Officials are still seeking temporary accommodation for the new arrivals.
