London — Symmetry once ruled out this effect.

Theorists find magnetic spin- and orbital-Hall effects inside centrosymmetric ferromagnets, materials symmetry was thought to forbid; orbital angular position, not spin, drives the orbital version.

The result widens which materials engineers can test for spin-based, low-power memory chips.
