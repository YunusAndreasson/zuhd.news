Dilling — Strikes destroyed food for 5,800 people, WFP says.

Ten Western governments and the EU condemned the attack but named no attacker.

WFP had notified all parties in [Sudan](country:SD)'s war of the convoy's route, under standard aid deconfliction rules.

WFP has recorded eight aerial attacks on its operations this year alone.
