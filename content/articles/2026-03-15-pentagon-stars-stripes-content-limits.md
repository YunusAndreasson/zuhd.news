Pentagon banned Stars and Stripes reporters from FOIA requests. The memo also cuts wire service access, removing the 2 main tools Stars and Stripes uses to report beyond official releases. Stars and Stripes editors warn the restrictions will shrink what troops can read about the war they are fighting.
