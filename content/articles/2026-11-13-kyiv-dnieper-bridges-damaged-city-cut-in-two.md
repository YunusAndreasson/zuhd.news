Kyiv — Kyiv's bridges are badly damaged, Russian state agency TASS reported.

Dmitry Razumkov of [Ukraine](country:UA)'s parliament says nobody can cross between Kyiv's banks.

Traffic has repeatedly jammed since late October, TASS says, leaving drivers stuck for hours and some commuters crossing on foot.

TASS names no cause for the damage and quotes no Ukrainian authority.

Left-bank residents fear another failure would cut their districts off from the right bank's business centre.
