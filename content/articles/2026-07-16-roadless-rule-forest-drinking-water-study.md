Washington — 25 million Americans drink water filtered by roadless forest.

Undeveloped national forest land supplies part of the drinking water for 90% of the US population, a new study found, because intact watersheds filter and store runoff roads would disrupt.

The administration's proposed rollback of the Roadless Rule would reopen that land to development.
