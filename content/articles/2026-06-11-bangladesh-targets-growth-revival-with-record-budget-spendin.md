Dhaka — [Bangladesh](country:BD)'s first post-Hasina budget targets 6.5% GDP growth.

The government will fund record spending by automating tax collection and introducing a flat-rate tax, targeting 7.5% inflation.

The budget still requires parliamentary approval from a transitional government with no elected mandate.
