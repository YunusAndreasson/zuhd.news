Mumbai — Chandrasekaran got reappointed weeks after vowing to quit.

Charitable trusts, not shareholders, hold 66% of [India](country:IN)'s largest conglomerate and now dispute control.

Trustee Noel Tata cast the lone board vote against it, calling the move a "legal nullity."

Tata Sons calls the vote valid, while a $2.6 billion stake sale and listing remain unresolved.
