Iran — The UN maritime agency counts 123 ship attacks near Hormuz.

Agency head Arsenio Dominguez says owners hide attacks and offers no estimate of how many.

Shadow tankers go dark by switching off tracking transponders, and some sail under false flags or fraudulent registries.

Dominguez cannot confirm that mines laid by [Iran](country:IR) are cleared and still tells ships to stay away.
