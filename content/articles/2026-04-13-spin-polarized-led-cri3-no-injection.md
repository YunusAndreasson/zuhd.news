London — Monolayer CrI₃ emits spin-polarized light without external injection. Sandwiching it between graphene contacts yields 20% circular polarization, reversible at 0.17 tesla, eliminating separate spin injectors and GaAs epitaxy. The design integrates into van der Waals heterostructures, a step toward on-chip spin-optoelectronics.
