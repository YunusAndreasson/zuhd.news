Jakarta — [India](country:IN) will supply Indonesia BrahMos missiles.

Modi finalized the long-pending sale during his Jakarta visit, alongside Astra air-to-air missiles.

The sale marks India's entry into a market long dominated by [Russia](country:RU) and the West.
