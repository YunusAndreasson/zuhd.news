Nigeria — Senator Orji Kalu admits selling guns to any buyer.

Kalu is a former Abia State governor and now sits in the Senate for Abia North.

He sold country to country to buyers in Congo and Namibia, and declined to name his suppliers.

Kalu denies working for US or Israeli intelligence but says arms trading made him know people linked to both.

He did not say when he stopped trading or whether Nigerian authorities ever asked about it.
