Brussels — EU negotiators relaunched banned message-scanning at Monday's closed trilogue.

Client-side scanning breaks encryption by design; detecting targeted content requires scanning all messages, creating a backdoor for every user.

The draft seeks to impose warrantless scanning orders despite Parliament's March veto.
