Washington — Kalshi reclassified sports bets as federal commodities contracts.

A Commodity Futures Trading Commission carve-out for "event contracts" let Kalshi offer sports wagers in the [US](country:US), bypassing state gambling bans.

The boundary between sports betting and prediction markets is before the commission, with no ruling date set.
