Manama — [Bahrain](country:BH) cut ties with Iran a decade ago.

That rift will keep it out of Monday's Hormuz security talks in Muscat.

Oman alone has kept channels to [Iran](country:IR) open, which is why it hosts the talks.

Iran's foreign ministry doesn't expect a signed deal from Monday's session.
