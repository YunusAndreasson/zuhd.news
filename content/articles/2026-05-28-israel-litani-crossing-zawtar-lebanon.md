South Lebanon — Israeli tanks crossed the Litani, entering Zawtar's outskirts.

UN resolution 1701 set the Litani as Hezbollah's southern limit; [Israel](country:IL) crossed it to pressure [US](country:US)-[Iran](country:IR) ceasefire talks, a retired officer said.

Israel is excluded from the US-Iran talks; no ceasefire framework has addressed Israel's northern border demands.
