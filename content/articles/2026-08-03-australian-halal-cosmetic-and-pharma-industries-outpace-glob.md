Sydney — Halal-certified cosmetics and pharma are growing faster than the global sector here.

[Australia](country:AU)'s Muslim population is roughly 3%, so the growth tracks export demand from Indonesia, Malaysia and Gulf markets requiring certification, not domestic buying.

Certification requires tracing gelatin, enzymes and alcohol carriers to standards set by the importing country's own regulator.
