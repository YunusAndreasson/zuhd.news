Washington — The FBI seized a host accused of ignoring child-abuse complaints.

After complaints from Germany, Canada and Australia in January, Virtualine helped a forum's operators build a new site.

Bulletproof hosts rent servers on a promise to ignore abuse complaints, and Virtualine advertised hosting for illegal activity on cybercrime forums.

The Justice Department has named no operators and filed no charges.
