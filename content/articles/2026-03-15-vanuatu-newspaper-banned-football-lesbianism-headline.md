Vanuatu's football federation banned its only daily newspaper. Federation president Lambert Matlock ordered the ban after the paper ran a headline blaming players' same-sex relationships for 3 consecutive World Cup qualifier losses. Reporters have received no match invitations since last Wednesday, with no appeal process announced by the federation.
