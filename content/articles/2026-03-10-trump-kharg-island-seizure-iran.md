Trump is reportedly planning to seize Kharg Island. The island carries 90% of Iran's crude exports — a plan Trump first proposed in 1988, saying he would "do a number on Kharg Island" after any attack on US forces. Iran's new Supreme Leader has pledged to fight indefinitely against any attempt to seize the island, which would violate the UN Charter.
