Baltic Sea — Seawater microbes can replace a chemistry instrument suite.

Microbial DNA sequences from a water sample predicted temperature, salinity, and alkalinity with direct-sensor accuracy, a Nature study on Baltic Sea data found.

The authors said the method can extend to pollutant and toxin monitoring from a single sample.
