Sudan's army retook Bara from the Rapid Support Forces. Armed movement leaders backed the army in exchange for postwar political autonomy — the military now demands to absorb their fighters into state command. No faction has agreed to join the unified command; RSF attacks on Kosti and Dalang continue.
