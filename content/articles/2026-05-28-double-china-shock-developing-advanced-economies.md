Beijing — [China](country:CN) now dominates both ends of the value chain.

Low-wage exporters built growth by starting cheap and moving upmarket — China now occupies both rungs, closing the ladder for late industrialisers.

Manufacturing hubs in [Bangladesh](country:BD), [Nigeria](country:NG), and [Vietnam](country:VN) face Chinese competition at every price point simultaneously.
