Europe wants its own security guarantee. European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen told the Munich Security Conference that the EU must activate Article 42.7, the bloc's dormant mutual defence clause, calling it "an obligation within our own treaty" rather than an optional commitment. No EU member has invoked the clause since France did after the 2015 Paris attacks, and no timeline for collective implementation exists.
