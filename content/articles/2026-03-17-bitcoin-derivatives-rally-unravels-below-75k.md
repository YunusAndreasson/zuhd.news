Bitcoin touched $75,912 and fell within hours. The rally came from traders closing $60,000 put options, forcing market makers to buy Bitcoin to rebalance — not from investors buying on conviction. Bitcoin has failed twice to hold above $74,400, a former support from April 2025 that now acts as a ceiling.
