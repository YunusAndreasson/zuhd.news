Nanjing — Roughly 2,000 lakes tracked since 2000 are increasingly synchronised.

Synchrony erases the diversity that once buffered local disturbances.

Heatwaves, droughts and urban development push lakes the same way, the study says, confirmed in sediment records from 119 lakes.

Coincident algal blooms could trigger parallel lake collapses across continents, the researchers warn.

Lakes are managed locally, and the study says that approach will have to change.
