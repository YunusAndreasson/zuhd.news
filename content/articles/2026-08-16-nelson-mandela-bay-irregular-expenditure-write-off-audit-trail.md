Gqeberha — A Democratic Alliance councillor walked out of the vote.

The vote erased R23 billion in spending from the audit trail.

[South Africa](country:ZA)'s finance law requires each item investigated and recovered before any write-off, which an African National Congress-Economic Freedom Fighters majority bypassed.

The council votes on the write-off Monday, with the opposition asking Treasury to intervene first.
