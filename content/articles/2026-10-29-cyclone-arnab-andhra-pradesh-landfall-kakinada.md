Kakinada — Cyclone Arnab killed 5 people, Chief Minister Naidu said.

Floods damaged crops on 224,489 hectares and hit 345,000 farmers, Naidu said.

[India](country:IN)'s weather department recorded 125-135 km/h winds at landfall, after officials told about 98,000 people to leave the coast.

Odisha's chief minister said the state recorded zero deaths despite landslides.

Nine Odisha districts remain under orange rain alerts on Thursday.
