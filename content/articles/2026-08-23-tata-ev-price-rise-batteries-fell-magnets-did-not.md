New Delhi — Tata is raising electric car prices too.

Battery costs keep falling, so buyers financing at the margin pay more anyway.

Traction motors and power electronics rely on rare-earth magnets and imported parts, now pricier under export curbs and duties.

It is Tata's third price rise this year, effective September 1, days before festive-season buying.
