Russia poisoned Alexei Navalny, 5 European governments said. The UK and 4 European allies announced that tissue samples from Navalny's body confirmed epibatidine, a lethal toxin found in South American dart frogs. The countries said Russia had the means, motive and opportunity to administer the poison while Navalny was imprisoned. They are now reporting Russia to the Organisation for the Prohibition of Chemical Weapons for breaching the Chemical Weapons Convention. Navalny, Russia's most prominent opposition leader, died in an Arctic penal colony in February 2024 at age 47.
