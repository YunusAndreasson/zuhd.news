Bujumbura — 5 dead in Burundi; Ebola, Marburg, all cleared. The cluster emerged March 30 in Mpanda District with household spread; lab tests ruled out 5 viral hemorrhagic fever suspects. A multisectoral rapid response team is deployed; the pathogen remains unidentified as investigators apply a One Health approach.
