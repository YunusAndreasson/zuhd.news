Mumbai — Tata Consultancy Services' quarterly revenue rose 11% to ₹73,188 crore.

[India](country:IN)'s biggest software firm annualizes AI revenue at $3.1 billion, up nearly 20% in a quarter.

Net profit rose 15% to ₹13,884 crore, with AI passing 10% of revenue.

Analysts at Choice Institutional Equities say deflationary pricing pressure remains the main headwind to growth.

The [US](country:US) suspended it from the Permanent Labor Certification programme, and the company expects no material impact.
