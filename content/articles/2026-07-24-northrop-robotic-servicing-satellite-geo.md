Cape Canaveral — A dual-armed satellite is now reaching geosynchronous orbit.

Northrop Grumman's Mission Robotic Vehicle can dock and refuel other satellites — a capability DARPA spent $420 million developing over two decades.

The Space Force will take over the decade-long mission once the first servicing call concludes.
