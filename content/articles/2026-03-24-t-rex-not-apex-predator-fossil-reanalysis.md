Raleigh — "Manteo," a fossil tyrannosaur, was fully mature at 700kg — one-tenth of T. rex's weight — with longer arms than an 8-tonne adult, proving it a distinct species rather than a juvenile.

Growth rings inside its leg bones confirmed maturity; the Hell Creek Formation now holds 3 recognized tyrannosaurid species, not one.

Steve Brusatte at Edinburgh says most researchers have reversed their view; Thomas Carr at Carthage College disputes the growth-ring methodology.
