Australia is sending missiles to the UAE. Prime Minister Anthony Albanese confirmed no ground troops in Iran but deployed a P-8A Poseidon surveillance aircraft, signaling Australia expects Hormuz mine operations to define the conflict. Albanese has not sought a parliamentary vote and has set no end date for the deployment.
