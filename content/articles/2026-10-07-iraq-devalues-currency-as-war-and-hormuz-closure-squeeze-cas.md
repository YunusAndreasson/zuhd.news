Baghdad — [Iraq](country:IQ) devalued the dinar to 1,500 per dollar, a central bank directive shows.

Nearly 8 million Iraqis paid in dinars now face dearer imported food and medicine.

Devaluing from 1,320 gives Baghdad about 13% more dinars per oil dollar as the Hormuz war cuts dollar income.

The 2020 devaluation set off inflation and protests in southern provinces.
