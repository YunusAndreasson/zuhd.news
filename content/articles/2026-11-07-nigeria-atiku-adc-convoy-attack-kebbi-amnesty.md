Birnin Kebbi — Armed gangs beat opposition members and smashed journalists' cameras Thursday, Amnesty International says.

Suspected thugs attacked Atiku Abubakar's African Democratic Congress convoy, days after a similar Sokoto attack.

Amnesty analysed video of gangs with knives and machetes blocking roads and wrecking vehicles.

Amnesty urges [Nigeria](country:NG)'s authorities to prosecute the attackers and their sponsors.
