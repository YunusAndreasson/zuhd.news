Honolulu — Researchers predicted El Niño from ocean data alone. [US](country:US) University of Hawai'i at Mānoa used only ocean surface temperature and height, with no atmospheric model, to achieve 15-month ENSO prediction. Whether operational forecasters adopt the ocean-only approach over established coupled models is now a practical decision.
