Gilgit — A landslide near Kohistan severed [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Karakoram Highway for 20 hours Sunday, stranding thousands. The highway is Gilgit-Baltistan's only land route and the main artery for China-Pakistan Economic Corridor freight. Astore Valley road remains blocked; Pakistan's meteorological department warned of continued landslides.
