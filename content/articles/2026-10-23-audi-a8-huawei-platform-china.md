Changchun — Huawei would do key development work on Audi's next A8.

Audi would build the flagship in [China](country:CN) rather than import it from Germany.

Talks also cover a sport utility vehicle and other premium models, with production at Changchun targeted before the end of 2028.

The companies have not finalized an agreement.
