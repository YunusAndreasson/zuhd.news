London — A model predicts EV demand at neighbourhood level.

Standard grid planning assumes uniform uptake; clustered real-world adoption overloads low-voltage infrastructure before utilities can pre-position capacity.

Validated across 1,294 English and Welsh neighbourhoods, uptake already clusters faster than utilities can upgrade transformers.
