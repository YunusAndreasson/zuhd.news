100 US soldiers landed at Bauchi in northeastern Nigeria. The troops will provide intelligence and training to Nigerian forces fighting Boko Haram and Islamic State West Africa Province under Nigerian command. The deployment follows a week in which gunmen killed 46 people across 3 northern villages.
