Houston — Seismic waves travel differently through buried ice.

Orbital instruments detect hydrogen at the lunar south pole but can't see depth or purity; a seismometer network could map structure, researchers propose.

South-polar ice is the resource crewed lunar programs plan around, and no treaty yet governs who may extract it.
