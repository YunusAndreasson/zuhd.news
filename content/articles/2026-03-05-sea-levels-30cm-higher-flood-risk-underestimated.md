Flood maps worldwide are 30cm too low. The bias is systematic across 90% of assessments, meaning flood maps, insurance models, and climate adaptation plans everywhere use the wrong baseline. The error puts millions more in risk zones than governments currently plan for, from Jakarta to Miami.
