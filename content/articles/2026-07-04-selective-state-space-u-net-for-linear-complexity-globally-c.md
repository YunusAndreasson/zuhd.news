London — A new image model scales linearly, not quadratically.

Transformers scan images at cost that grows quadratically with resolution; the new state-space design keeps full-image context while scaling linearly instead.

Researchers report 94.2% accuracy on a polyp-detection benchmark, opening full-resolution use on medical and edge devices.
