Águas Claras — One office room registered 90 companies, 25 named in pension-fraud probes.

Company records place a firm of runoff candidate Flávio Bolsonaro in the same room.

The link is accounting firm Voga, whose partner Alexandre Caetano dos Reis, jailed since December, laundered money for the scheme, federal police say.

The Supreme Court is still investigating Flávio Bolsonaro and lawyer Willer Tomaz, who defends Voga, for betting-linked laundering.
