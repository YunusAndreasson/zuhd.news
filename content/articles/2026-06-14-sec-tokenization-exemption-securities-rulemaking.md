Washington — The Securities and Exchange Commission chose exemption, not rulemaking, for tokenization.

Exemptive authority lets the agency fast-track tokenized securities without Congress — but the next chair can revoke it unilaterally.

Agency leaders called the policy narrow and time-limited; no rulemaking timeline has been set.
