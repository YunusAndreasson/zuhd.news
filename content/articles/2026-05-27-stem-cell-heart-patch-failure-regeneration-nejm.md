Boston — A stem cell patch reversed heart-attack scarring.

After a heart attack, damaged muscle heals as scar; BioVAT grafts stem-cell tissue onto the damaged wall to restore pumping force.

A larger trial will determine who benefits most and how durable the graft is, researchers said.
