Manila — Sara Duterte's Senate trial opens Monday; she won't attend.

The [Philippines](country:PH)' Vice President faces four impeachment articles, including threats against President Marcos Jr.; conviction needs 16 of 24 senator-judges.

Two absent senators have prosecutors floating a lower vote threshold to convict.
