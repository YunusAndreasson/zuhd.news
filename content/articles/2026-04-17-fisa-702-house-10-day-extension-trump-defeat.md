Washington — House gave FISA 702 a 10-day extension. Section 702 compels tech companies to produce foreign-intelligence data without warrants; Trump lobbied for clean reauthorization and lost. The clock expires in 10 days; surveillance-reform amendments Congress has held back for years now have leverage.
