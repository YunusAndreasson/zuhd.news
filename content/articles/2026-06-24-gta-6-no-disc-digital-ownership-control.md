New York — Rockstar's $80 GTA 6 box converts a game purchase into a revocable license.

The discless format — a download code instead of physical media — ends resale and blocks pre-launch leaks.

Pre-orders open Wednesday; box art will not disclose the absent disc before buyers commit.
