Novartis paid $2 billion for an early-trial cancer drug. SNV4818 targets only the mutated form of the PI3Kα enzyme; earlier drugs hit the healthy version too, causing widespread toxic side effects. Phase 2 data are expected later this year for HR-positive, HER2-negative breast cancer — the most common form of the disease.
