Washington — Federal agents seized Ilhan Omar's phone and passport.

The Justice Department is investigating her immigration history, which her lawyers call politically motivated.

Lawyers say agents gave House counsel no notice and ignored a magistrate's instructions meant to protect her legislative files.

Justice Department spokesperson Emily Covington says attorneys "complied with all court orders and legal obligations."

Prosecutors have issued over a dozen grand jury subpoenas, some to Omar's relatives, but the scope is unclear.
