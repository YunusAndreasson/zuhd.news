London — Dependency cooldowns make companies free-riders on open source. The policy delays security patches and lets corporations consume open-source work without contributing, shifting costs entirely to unpaid maintainers. No payment mechanism exists for open-source supply chains; companies consume maintainer time until a critical project breaks.
