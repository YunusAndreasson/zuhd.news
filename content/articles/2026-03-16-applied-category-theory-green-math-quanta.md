Abstract math from 1945 is now modeling epidemics. Category theory maps relationships between structures rather than numbers, reaching into epidemiology and AI safety where traditional models fail. Mathematical physicist John Baez launched the applied movement in 2011; its founding goal — modeling climate systems — remains unachieved.
