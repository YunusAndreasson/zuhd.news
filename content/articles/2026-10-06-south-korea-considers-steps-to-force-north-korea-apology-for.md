Seoul — Investigators trace 2 landmines to North Korean forces.

3 soldiers were wounded on 21 September, and Seoul says it rules out no further measures.

Seoul and the UN Command differ on roughly 60% of border coordinates, South Korea's unification minister says.

[North Korea](country:KP) denies responsibility, and Kim Yo Jong said it will define its border by force, not negotiation.

[South Korea](country:KR)'s military pledged to keep clearing mines south of the line unless Pyongyang removes the rest.
