London — Retailers must stop inflating fake "was" prices.

The government projects the ban will save consumers about £400m a year.

Retailers set an inflated reference price, then advertise a discount calculated off that inflated figure.

Prime Minister Burnham's government will consult this autumn before writing the ban into law.
