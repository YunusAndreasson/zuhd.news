Anthropic's Claude helped plan the Caracas raid. The Wall Street Journal reported that Pentagon officials used the AI system to support the operation that captured Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro, the first confirmed deployment of a frontier AI model in a US military operation of this scale. Neither Anthropic nor the Pentagon has clarified what role the system played or what limits governed its use.
