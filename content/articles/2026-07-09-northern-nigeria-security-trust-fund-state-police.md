Kaduna — 19 states will pay ₦1 billion monthly for security.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s northern governors launched the fund and endorsed state police, a response to banditry and terrorism federal forces have not contained.

The fund's board of trustees must now decide how the money gets spent.
