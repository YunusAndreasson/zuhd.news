Dallas — One paper puts $700 billion capacity at risk.

Dallas Fed economists say tokenized deposits threaten funding banks rely on for long-term loans.

Banks profit from slow, sticky deposits; instant money lets savers chase yield in seconds, [Brazil](country:BR)'s Pix data already show.

[United States](country:US) banks are already piloting tokenized deposits, with no funding-stability rules yet written.
