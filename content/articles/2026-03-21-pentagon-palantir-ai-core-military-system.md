The Pentagon made Palantir its official targeting system. Palantir's Maven AI platform fuses 9 intelligence feeds into one interface, cutting targeting decisions from hours to minutes for 20,000 US military users. A US investigation is examining whether Maven recommended the strike that killed over 170 students at an Iranian girls' school.
