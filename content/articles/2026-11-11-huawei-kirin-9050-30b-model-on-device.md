China — Huawei's Kirin 9050 runs a 30-billion-parameter AI model offline.

Inference stays on the handset, with no internet connection or data centre needed.

A mixture-of-experts design activates about 2 billion parameters at a time, and the Da Vinci processor uses low-bit quantization.

Huawei's account gives no speed or battery figures for the model.
