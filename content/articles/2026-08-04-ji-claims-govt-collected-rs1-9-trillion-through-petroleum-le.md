Peshawar — Jamaat-i-Islami claims Rs1.9tn was collected via fuel levy.

The party says [Pakistan](country:PK)'s 30 million motorcyclists alone pay Rs124 per litre in levy daily.

Jamaat-i-Islami plans to block the Indus Highway and Bab-i-Khyber crossing nationwide Friday.
