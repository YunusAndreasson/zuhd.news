Delhi — Stubble accounts for at most 23% of smog.

Secondary particulates and traffic actually rank higher, government told the Rajya Sabha, citing a Supreme Court-mandated study of pollution sources.

Farm fires have already fallen over 50% in two years, even as the smog persists.
