United States — Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom eye up to $5 billion of Meta stock.

Meta plans to raise $95 billion in new equity against $135 billion to $145 billion of 2026 capital spending.

$35 billion sells now, then $60 billion in non-voting Class C shares, which leave Mark Zuckerberg's voting control undiluted.

Elon Musk said 200+ neocloud firms face "guppy-level death and consolidation" as debt markets shut.

Meta starts the $60 billion offering once Class C shares begin trading.
