Washington — Bowman addressed bankers the day after a rate vote.

She is the Fed's top bank supervisor, and its inspector general is checking whether she broke blackout rules, two people familiar say.

The rules bar officials from discussing monetary policy from the second Saturday before a meeting until the day after it ends.

Bowman says she revealed no non-public information and referred to earlier public statements.

Elizabeth Warren, who led senators asking for the review, will chair the Senate banking committee next Congress.
