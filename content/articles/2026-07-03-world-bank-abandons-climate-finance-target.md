Washington — The World Bank scrapped its 45% climate finance target.

The bank, which gave developing countries $39.2bn in climate funding in 2025, dropped the goal after [US](country:US) pressure.

Its "action plan" survives; whether shareholders hold future climate flows to Turkey, India and Nigeria remains unresolved.
