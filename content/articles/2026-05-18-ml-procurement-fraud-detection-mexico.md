Mexico City — [Mexico](country:MX)'s sanctioned-supplier list trained a fraud-detection model.

Training on the government's banned-firm list solves machine learning's key problem: no confirmed "clean" examples exist.

Shared directors and bid-clustering were the top predictors; researchers say the method works on any national debarment dataset.
