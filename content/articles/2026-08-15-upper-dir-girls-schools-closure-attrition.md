Upper Dir — Only girls' schools made the closure list.

Repeated closures rarely reverse — girls' enrollment in border districts seldom recovers after one.

A district education officer, not security forces, ordered the closure by letter Saturday.

The deputy commissioner has not approved the Aug. 15-22 closure of the 18 schools.
