Asian spot LNG prices have doubled to 3-year highs since the Hormuz blockade halted Qatari shipments. Utilities across 5 Asian economies — including Bangladesh, South Korea, and Japan — are now substituting coal, with Bangladesh's Summit Group chairman warning the shock is "breaking the backbone of the economies of poorer countries." Most LNG contracts are linked to oil prices on a 3-month lag, meaning the full cost hits buyers in June.
