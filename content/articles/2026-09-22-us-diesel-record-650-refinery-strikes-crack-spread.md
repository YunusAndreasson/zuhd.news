Washington — US diesel hit a record $6.51 a gallon.

Diesel powers freight and farm machinery, so higher prices feed straight into food costs.

Middle East diesel exports fell by half since March as refineries worldwide already run near full capacity, the International Energy Agency said.

"Any further disruption to Red Sea flows risks tightening an already stretched market," said Kpler analyst George Shaw.

Diesel prices are likely to keep climbing unless Hormuz reopens or refiners add capacity.
