Khartoum — Five Sudanese civil society coalitions rejected army-led dialogue.

The dialogue lets [Sudan](country:SD)'s army claim a civilian mandate without real consent.

One warring party convenes this dialogue on its own turf and picks its own guest list.

The coalitions want the Quintet mechanism to withhold legitimacy from any army-run process.
