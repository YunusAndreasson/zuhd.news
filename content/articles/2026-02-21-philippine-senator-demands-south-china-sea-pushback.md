China calls Thitu Island's 400 Filipino residents illegal occupants. Philippine Senator Risa Hontiveros landed on the island Saturday and demanded continuous pushback against China. She urged joint patrols with allied navies as Chinese coast guard and navy vessels continue operating near the outpost in the South China Sea.
