United States — Bank of America's unrealized bond losses rose to $109 billion.

Fifth Third delayed a share buyback in September after rates rose.

Bond prices fall as yields rise, and the [US](country:US) 10-year yield posted its sharpest rise since 1994 in the third quarter.

Bank of America says the bulk of those securities are held to maturity and not for sale.

The Federal Reserve announces its rate decision on 28 October.
