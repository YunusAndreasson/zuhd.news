France offered to station nuclear-armed aircraft in allied nations. French President Emmanuel Macron declared "to be free, we have to be feared," citing US distraction in Iran as proof Europe must build its own deterrent. Allied governments must now decide whether to accept French nuclear coverage, a choice that would reshape NATO's reliance on US power.
