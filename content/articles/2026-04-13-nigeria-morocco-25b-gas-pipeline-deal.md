Abuja — Nigeria and Morocco plan a $25 billion pipeline. The 6,900 km African Atlantic Gas Pipeline will carry 30 billion cubic meters a year, with half allocated to Morocco and European export markets. Signing is due before year-end; Phase 1, connecting Morocco to West African fields, is expected in 2031.
