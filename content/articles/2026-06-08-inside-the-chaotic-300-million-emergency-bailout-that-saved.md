Paris — A KelpDAO bridge exploit drained $123.7 million from Aave and triggered an $8.45 billion deposit run.

LlamaRisk said hackers minted fake collateral to drain funds; shared liquidity pools spread the contagion instantly across the protocol.

Aave's V4 hub-and-spoke redesign, which localises risk per pool, has not yet launched.
