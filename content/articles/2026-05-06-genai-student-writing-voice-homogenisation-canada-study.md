Toronto — A Canadian study found AI use collapses the stylistic diversity that distinguishes one student's writing from another. [Canada](country:CA)'s universities focus the policy debate on plagiarism detection; researchers say the deeper loss is voice and identity. No institution is yet measuring how quickly students' own prose styles are being displaced.
