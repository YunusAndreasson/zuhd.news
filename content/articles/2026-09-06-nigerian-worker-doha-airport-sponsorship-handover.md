Doha — Saratu Yusuf vanished after landing from Lagos.

Nigerian aviation officials have raised fears she was trafficked into forced labor.

Airport CCTV in [Qatar](country:QA) shows she collected her luggage and left the terminal alone.

A manhunt begins if she is confirmed to have never reached her booked hotel.
