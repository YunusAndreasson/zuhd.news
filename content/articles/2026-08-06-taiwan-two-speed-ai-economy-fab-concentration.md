Taipei — Chipmaking employs few of Taiwan's workers.

The industry sells almost entirely abroad, so its gains rarely reach smaller domestic businesses, an analysis found.

President Trump has called [Taiwan](country:TW) a "negotiating chip" — the boom complicates that framing, the analysis said.
