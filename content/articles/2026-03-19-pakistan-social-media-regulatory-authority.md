Pakistan's new authority can block any social media platform outright. Born from a cybercrime law Amnesty International calls repressive, the authority requires platforms to register and comply with its orders or face suspension. Chairman Ayaz Shaukat, Islamabad's Advocate General, takes office after Eid, with 3 of 8 board seats still unfilled.
