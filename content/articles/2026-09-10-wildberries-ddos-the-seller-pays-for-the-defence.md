Moscow — A distributed denial-of-service attack froze Wildberries seller payments.

Small sellers across [Russia](country:RU) and Central Asia bore the cash-flow hit.

The delay came from Wildberries' own defensive lockdown of its withdrawal systems, not the attack itself.

No group has yet claimed responsibility, though pro-Ukrainian hackers have hit Russian platforms before.
