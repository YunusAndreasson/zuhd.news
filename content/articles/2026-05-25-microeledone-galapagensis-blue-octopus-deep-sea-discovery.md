Galápagos — A cerulean octopus found 1,800 metres down.

Classified this month via CT scan after a 2015 sighting off [Ecuador](country:EC)'s Galápagos, where deep-sea remotely operated vehicle surveys remain rare.

Its nearest relative lives off Uruguay; researchers have no second specimen and cannot dissect the first.
