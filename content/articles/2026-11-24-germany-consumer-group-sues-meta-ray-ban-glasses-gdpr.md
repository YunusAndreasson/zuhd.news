Germany — [Germany](country:DE)'s consumer federation sues Meta over its camera glasses.

The Verbraucherzentrale Bundesverband says wearing them in public can hardly comply with EU data protection law.

The federation says a white light a few millimetres wide cannot tell bystanders they are being filmed.

Courts will now rule on injunction suits against both companies, filed after Ray-Ban ignored the federation's warning letter.
