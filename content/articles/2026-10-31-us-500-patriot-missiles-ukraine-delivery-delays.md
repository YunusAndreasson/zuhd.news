Kyiv — [Ukraine](country:UA)'s 500 Patriot missiles may arrive years from now.

Interceptors are scarce while [Russia](country:RU) hit energy sites over 30 times in two weeks, Zelensky said.

Lockheed Martin has yet to build them, two briefed people say, and [Canada](country:CA), [Germany](country:DE) and [Norway](country:NO) fund the $2.1 billion order.

NATO's top commander, General Alexus Grynkewich, said Friday that Patriot deliveries to Ukraine are ongoing, including one last week.

Congress reviews the sale next, and the State Department gave no delivery date.
