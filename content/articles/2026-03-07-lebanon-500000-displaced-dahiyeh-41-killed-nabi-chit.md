Israel ordered 500,000 people to evacuate Dahiyeh, southern Beirut. The Israeli military has applied to Beirut the same evacuation tactics used in Gaza, widening its offensive to include new cities. Similar evacuation orders reached Tyre this week, leaving Lebanon's 300,000 displaced with no announced shelter plan.
