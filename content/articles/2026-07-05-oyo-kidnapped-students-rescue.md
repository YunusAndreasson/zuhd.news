Oyo — 39 pupils and 7 teachers remain missing since May 15.

Army chief Waidi Shaibu says troops are making "tremendous progress" toward freeing them from three schools seized in Orire.

No rescue date has been set; the abduction is [Nigeria](country:NG)'s only unresolved incident in an otherwise calmer southwest.
