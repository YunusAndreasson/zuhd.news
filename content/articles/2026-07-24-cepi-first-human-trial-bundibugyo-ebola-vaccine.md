Oxford — More than 1,000 have died in [Congo](country:CD)'s Ebola outbreak.

Licensed vaccines protect only against Ebola's Zaire strain; Bundibugyo has had none, despite outbreaks stretching back nearly two decades.

The Serum Institute of India has stockpiled 620,000 doses; a further Uganda trial still awaits regulatory approval.
