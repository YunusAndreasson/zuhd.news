Seattle — Amazon says AI agents found an unpatched flaw themselves.

A China-linked operator then reused the flaw to breach a second university, Amazon said.

Given network addresses and a goal of shell access, Cairn's agents planned, executed and adjusted without the operator choosing each step.

Most attempts failed, including 148 actions over 16 hours and over 30,000 credential guesses against 80 usernames.

Amazon declined to name the universities or their country.
