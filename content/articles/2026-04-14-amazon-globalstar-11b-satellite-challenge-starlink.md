Seattle — Amazon agreed to buy Globalstar for $11.57 billion. The acquisition adds existing satellite infrastructure as Amazon races to deploy 3,200 LEO satellites by 2029 against Starlink's 10,000-strong network. Amazon and SpaceX will each control a parallel orbital layer, both backed by large US government contracts.
