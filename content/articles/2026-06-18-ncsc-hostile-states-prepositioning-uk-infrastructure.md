London — Hostile states are "prepositioning" in [UK](country:GB) infrastructure for future conflict, the National Cyber Security Centre said.

Intrusions are target-package building for future conflicts, not espionage, centre chief Richard Horne warned.

Horne has not named responsible states or announced new defensive measures.
