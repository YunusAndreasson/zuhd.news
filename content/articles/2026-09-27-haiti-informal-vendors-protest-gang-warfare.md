Port-au-Prince — Gang tolls have cut off Port-au-Prince's food supply routes.

Most Haitians live off informal trade, and gangs have made trading in the capital deadly.

Viv Ansanm gang factions run illegal checkpoints on the two roads that supply [Haiti](country:HT)'s capital, extorting drivers and driving up food prices.

Vendors have protested outside the prime minister's office since August, with no government response yet announced.
