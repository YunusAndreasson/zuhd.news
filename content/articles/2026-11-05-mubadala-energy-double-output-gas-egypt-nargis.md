Abu Dhabi — Mubadala Energy plans to double its 450,000 barrels of oil equivalent a day.

Gas would be about 70% of output within a decade, the acting chief executive told a state news agency.

It bought 15% of [Egypt](country:EG)'s Nargis offshore area, adding to Zohr and Nour stakes.

Next comes converting discoveries into production, including Indonesia's Tangkulo deepwater gas development, which the company is accelerating.
