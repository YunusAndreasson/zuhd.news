166 million rials is now Iran's monthly minimum wage. Iran's interim governing council approved a 60-percent raise from 103 million rials, as sanctions-driven inflation erodes workers' purchasing power. The next annual review falls in March 2027, when wartime inflation will test whether the raise held its value.
