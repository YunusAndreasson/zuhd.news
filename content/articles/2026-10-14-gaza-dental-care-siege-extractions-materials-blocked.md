Deir al-Balah — A root canal costs 300 shekels, an extraction 50.

Families living on aid, displaced repeatedly, cannot afford the treatment that saves the tooth.

Three years of attacks by [Israel](country:IL) and its siege on medical supplies have all but destroyed Gaza's dental care.

"I can't pay 300 shekels," said Nour Abo Shamala, 22, who has lived with the pain since February.

Her tooth is still salvageable, but she waits for the root canal price to fall.
