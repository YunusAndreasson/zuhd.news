Mumbai — Services PMI dropped to 53.1, HSBC's flash survey found.

[India](country:IN)'s private-sector activity slowed to its weakest pace in over 4 years as the Middle East war lifted oil prices and dented demand.

HSBC's survey ties the slowdown to the war, with no ceasefire yet to ease oil costs.
