Shanghai — China's military says a stealth drone landed on a warship.

[China](country:CN)'s navy appears on track to be first to operate stealth combat drones from a ship.

The Type 076 Sichuan carries an electromagnetic catapult, and the GJ-21 differs from the land-based GJ-11 by a catapult bar on its nose gear.

Neither the military nor state media has released video, dates or launch counts.
