Iran struck Al-Kharj air base in Saudi Arabia. The base stores US F-35s and fuel tankers supplying American air raids on Iran, making Saudi Arabia both a logistics hub and now a target. Saudi Arabia must decide whether to expel US forces or absorb further Iranian strikes.
