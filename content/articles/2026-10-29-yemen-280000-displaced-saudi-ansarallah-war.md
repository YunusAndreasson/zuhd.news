Sanaa — 281,000 Yemenis have been displaced since August.

Nearly 1,700 people have died in the renewed Saudi–Ansarallah war over the same period.

Ansarallah counts 101 airstrikes by [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)-backed forces, and Human Rights Watch says its forces deprive Taiz civilians of food and medicine.

Polymarket traders give a Saudi-Houthi ceasefire by 31 December a 24% chance, down 7 points in a week.
