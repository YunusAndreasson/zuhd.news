Taipei — A Taiwanese engineer took a job at an undeclared Chinese chip lab.

Export controls police machines, but engineers can simply change employers.

For six years [Taiwan](country:TW) has investigated Chinese firms operating undeclared on the island to recruit semiconductor talent.

Government data show few of those investigations end in conviction.
