San Francisco — OpenAI fired 2 safety researchers and a program manager.

OpenAI alleges some of the information went to an outside organization that evaluates AI systems.

The company says its investigation found they broke policies on accessing and handling confidential information, plus other misconduct it has not described.

OpenAI says none were dismissed for raising AI safety concerns.

OpenAI has not said whether the material concerned its recent agent incidents or exposed any user information.
