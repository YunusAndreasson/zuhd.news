Anyone can now rotate a 5,000-year-old Egyptian sculpture. The Metropolitan Museum of Art published 140 artworks as free 3D scans, ranging from ancient sculptures to 19th-century paintings. War damage across the Middle East is destroying heritage faster than institutions can preserve it, making digital archives like this one more urgent.
