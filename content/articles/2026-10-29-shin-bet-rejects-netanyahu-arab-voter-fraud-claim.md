Israel — Shin Bet says its probe does not implicate Arab voters, who voted legally.

Arab parties now hold the balance of power, and Likud calls their votes stolen.

Likud alleges fraud and foreign funding after Arab turnout rose from about 50% to 80%, Israeli reports say.

[Qatar](country:QA) denied any involvement, and Israeli police said they hold no evidence for Likud's claims.

Polymarket traders price Netanyahu leaving office by end of 2026 at 17%, down 25 points in a week.
