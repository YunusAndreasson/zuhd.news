Changwon — Researchers hid Teflon nanoparticles inside silver plating.

A [South Korea](country:KR) team dispersed PTFE nanoparticles in a cyanide-free bath, making silver coatings harder and more wear-resistant than standard plating.

The cyanide-free composite could replace toxic baths used in electronics and connector plating.
