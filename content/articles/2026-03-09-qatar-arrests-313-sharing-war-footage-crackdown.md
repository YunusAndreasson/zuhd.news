Qatar arrested 313 people for circulating attack footage. Gulf monarchies use wartime security to restrict information: Bahrain arrested 4, Kuwait 3, and the UAE threatened prosecution for similar posts. Qatar's Interior Ministry announced no charges but accused detainees of "stirring public opinion," without disclosing their fate.
