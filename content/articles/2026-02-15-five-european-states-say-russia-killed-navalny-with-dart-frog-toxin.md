Dart frog toxin killed Alexei Navalny. The United Kingdom and 4 European allies confirmed epibatidine in tissue samples from the Russian opposition leader, who died in an Arctic penal colony in February 2024 — the neurotoxin occurs naturally only in South American frogs and is not found in Russia. The 5 governments issued a joint statement declaring Russia "had the means, motive and opportunity" and reported Moscow to the Organisation for the Prohibition of Chemical Weapons.
