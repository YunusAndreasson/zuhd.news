Tehran — A 2-million-barrel supertanker burned after hitting a Hormuz mine, Russian state news agency TASS and [Iran](country:IR)'s state Mehr agency reported.

Hormuz traffic averaged 3.7 ships a day to 18 October, 10% below its 90-day normal.

The Revolutionary Guards say 3 tankers hit mines on routes Tehran calls illegal, with radar and navigation switched off.

The Guards warn owners and crews on unauthorized routes will be held responsible and face a firm response.
