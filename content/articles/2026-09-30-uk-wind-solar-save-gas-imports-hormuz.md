London — Wind and solar spared the [UK](country:GB) more than 100 liquefied natural gas tanker deliveries.

Carbon Brief's analysis values the avoided gas imports at £5.9bn since the February US-Iran war began.

Record wind and solar, up 14% year on year, cut gas-fired power nearly 10% while gas averaged 189p a therm in September.

Typical household gas bills will be 33% higher than in April from Thursday, Nesta says, against 4% for electricity.

European gas stocks are low, so Europe competes with Asia for winter LNG cargoes.
