Reykjavik — The corona is a million times fainter.

The US space agency's WB-57 jet will chase the moon's shadow at 460 mph, stretching totality to nearly 3 minutes.

The eclipse crosses Greenland, Iceland and Spain on August 12; only aircraft reach the innermost corona.
