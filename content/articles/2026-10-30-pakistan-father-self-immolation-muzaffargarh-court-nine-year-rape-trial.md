Muzaffargarh — A father set himself on fire after a nine-year trial.

Allah Bakhshaya says his daughter's husband divorced her over the alleged rape.

A court acquitted the accused Thursday for lack of evidence, and doctors put Bakhshaya's burns at 45.5%, Rescue 1122 said.

Bakhshaya remains in critical condition at the district headquarters hospital.
