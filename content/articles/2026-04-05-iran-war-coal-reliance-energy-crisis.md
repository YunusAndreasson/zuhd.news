London — Italy extended coal plant closures by 13 years. Japan, India, Germany, and 6 other nations are restarting coal plants as the Hormuz blockade cuts 20% of global gas supply. Coal already accounts for 28% of global energy, up from 23% in 2000; war-driven inflation raises renewable financing costs.
