London — Reciting poetry, not reading it, rewires the brain.

A 4-day trial of 70 participants found only rhythmic recitation, not semantic study or transcription, strengthened motor-planning and sensory brain connections.

The gains transferred to unpracticed verses, researchers say — evidence that how a text is voiced changes the brain.
