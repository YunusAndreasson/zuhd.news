80% of Madagascar's main port is damaged. Cyclone Gezani struck Toamasina on Tuesday with gusts up to 250 km/h, 10 days after Cyclone Fytia hit the same region. The city has 5% electricity and no running water. At least 38 people died, 374 were injured, and 400,000 need immediate humanitarian aid. The World Food Programme's warehouse and office were destroyed, crippling the relief operation just as 1.57 million Malagasy were already facing food insecurity. The WFP called the destruction "overwhelming" and requested $18 million over 6 months. Madagascar, one of the world's poorest nations and among the least responsible for carbon emissions, now faces its worst climate disaster in years.
