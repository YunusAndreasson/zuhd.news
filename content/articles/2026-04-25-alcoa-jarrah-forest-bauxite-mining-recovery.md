Perth — 2,000 jarrah hectares cost Alcoa $55 million.

The fine earmarks $40 million for "permanent offsets"; researchers say jarrah soil microbiomes take centuries to recover after mining.

Whether [Australia](country:AU)'s jarrah forests can recover at mining scale is unsettled; the penalty assumes they will.
