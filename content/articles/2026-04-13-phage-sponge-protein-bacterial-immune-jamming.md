London — A phage protein sequesters bacterial alarm signals mid-cascade. TadIV-1 absorbs N7-cADPR — the Thoeris Caspase trigger — before it can activate immune defenses, a sequestration mechanism new to phage biology. The discovery expands the phage anti-defense arsenal and opens a new engineering pathway for targeted antimicrobials.
