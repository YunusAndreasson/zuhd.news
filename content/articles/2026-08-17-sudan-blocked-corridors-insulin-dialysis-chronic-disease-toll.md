Wad Madani — For 39 years, her insulin arrived on time.

The war did not take her medicine, only the roads that carried it.

Most aid and commercial imports now funnel through Port Sudan, the sole gateway across [Sudan](country:SD)'s fractured front lines.

Millions like her now wake up unsure if insulin will reach them today.
