Paris — [France](country:FR) secured military bandwidth on a European constellation.

France's defence procurement agency awarded Eutelsat €138m for OneWeb constellation access, bridging a gap before IRIS2 arrives in the 2030s.

Whether other European governments will fund similar alternatives before IRIS2 arrives in the 2030s remains unresolved.
