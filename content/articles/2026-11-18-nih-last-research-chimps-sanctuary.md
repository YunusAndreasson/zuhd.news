Bastrop — The last government-owned lab chimpanzees leave Texas next year.

NIH Director Jay Bhattacharya said the transfers fulfil a commitment made in November 2015.

Nearly 3 dozen move to Chimp Haven, a 200-acre federal sanctuary in Louisiana, the only one in the [US](country:US).

Transfers begin early next year.
