No cattle appeared at the Paris Agriculture Fair for the first time in its history. A lumpy skin disease outbreak forced government-ordered herd culls across France, the EU's largest agricultural producer, compounding what former EU trade chief Demarty called "15 years of failure" in farm policy. Farmers who protested in Paris last month face a squeeze between cheap Mercosur imports and EU environmental rules with no relief in sight.
