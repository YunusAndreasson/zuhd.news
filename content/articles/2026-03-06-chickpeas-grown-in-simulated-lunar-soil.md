Scientists grew chickpeas in soil 75% simulated moon rock. Chickpeas fix nitrogen in soil rather than depleting it, making them ideal for establishing agriculture on barren surfaces. The US and China each plan long-term lunar bases, requiring astronauts to grow food from local materials.
