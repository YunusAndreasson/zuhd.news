Berlin — [Germany](country:DE) ties Ukraine's €70bn gap to the EU budget.

Hungary, Slovakia, Czechia and Bulgaria say they will stay out of any new Ukraine loan.

Germany, the largest net payer, wants Ukraine funding decided by qualified majority so no single government can block it.

"What the chancellor is doing is typical blackmail, and I will not give in to it," said Slovak Prime Minister Robert Fico.

EU leaders discuss Ukraine funding in Dublin on Friday and at a budget summit on 26-27 November.
