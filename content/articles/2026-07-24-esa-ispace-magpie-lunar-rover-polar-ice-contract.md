Paris — €65 million buys an entire moon mission.

The European Space Agency is buying a finished mission from ispace-Europe rather than building it, though ispace's parent has failed two lunar landings.

MAGPIE is due to land at the lunar south pole in 2029, hunting water ice for future crews.
