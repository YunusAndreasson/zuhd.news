London — Lloyds drew 2,297 crypto-transfer complaints, more than any other bank.

Advocacy group Stand With Crypto logged 10,092 complaints from all 650 [UK](country:GB) constituencies.

Barclays caps crypto transfers at £2,500 per payment and £10,000 over 30 days, while Chase, Starling and TSB ban them.

Complaints came from people choosing the group's online tool, so the sample is not representative.

The Financial Conduct Authority and government are still drafting the regime meant to attract crypto firms.
