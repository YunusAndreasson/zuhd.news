TotalEnergies cut 15 percent of its global production. The 360,000 barrels a day now offline make TotalEnergies the first major Western company to cut production rather than evacuate staff. When the 5th-largest oil major deems production too risky, insurers and smaller operators follow — that cascade has not yet begun.
