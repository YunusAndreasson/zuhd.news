Washington — Cockroach eggs carry 4,900 bacterial DNA segments into inheritable cells.

Gut bacteria pass to offspring through eggs, transferring microbial genes directly into animal chromosomes, researchers found.

The Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences study found transfer rates exceed prior estimates across animal genomes.
