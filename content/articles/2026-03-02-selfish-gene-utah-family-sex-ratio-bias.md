60 sons, 29 daughters — one Utah family defies chance. Researchers believe a Y-chromosome gene biases sperm to favor sons — a mechanism found in mice and flies but never before confirmed in humans. The study is a preprint; population geneticist Wynn Meyer says IVF embryo selection hasn't been ruled out.
