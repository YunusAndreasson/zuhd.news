Mountain View — Your phone's modem is hackable from the internet. Google rewrote the Pixel 10's modem DNS parser in Rust, grafting memory-safe code onto legacy C/C++ firmware without a full rewrite — adding just 371 KB. Pixel 10 ships first; Google wants other platforms to adopt the approach for broader modem hardening.
