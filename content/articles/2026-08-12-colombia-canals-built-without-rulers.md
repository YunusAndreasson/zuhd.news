La Mojana — 1,600 workers built [Colombia](country:CO)'s ancient canals in weeks.

The find challenges the idea that monumental works require a ruling elite.

Satellite mapping and dating of a 1,200-acre patch show the 2,000-square-mile network could rise in six weeks.

Cambridge researcher Jasmine Vieri says the finding reshapes how archaeology reads other ancient sites.
