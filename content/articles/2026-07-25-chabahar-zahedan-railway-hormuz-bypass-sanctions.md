Tehran — [Iran](country:IR)'s Chabahar-Zahedan rail line nears completion.

Chabahar is Iran's only deep-water port reachable without crossing the Strait of Hormuz, and this line links it to Central Asia.

The project has slipped for over a decade; the minister set no completion date, only "soon."
