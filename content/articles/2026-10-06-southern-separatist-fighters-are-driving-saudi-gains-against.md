Mokha — Yemeni forces say they retook ground near Bab al-Mandab.

Bab el-Mandeb traffic averaged 29.6 ships a day to 4 October, 2% above normal.

The advance relies on the Giants Brigades and Saudi-led air strikes, after a Saudi-Emirati meeting in Riyadh on 29 September.

The Houthis say they repelled the offensive near Al Wazi'iyah and fired 10 missiles.

Yemen's government says its offensive continues and offers Houthis amnesty if they lay down weapons.
