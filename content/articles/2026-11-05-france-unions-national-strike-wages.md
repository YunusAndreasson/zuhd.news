Paris — Authorities expect up to 700,000 marchers on Thursday.

Five unions want a higher minimum wage, currently €12.31 an hour, and protected public services.

Student protests across [France](country:FR) since September spread to workers, and violence on 17 October left more than 2,000 stores looted.

The government released 10 million barrels of diesel from strategic reserves to cut fuel costs.

Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez has deployed 70,000 police and warned of black blocs, looting and wildcat marches.
