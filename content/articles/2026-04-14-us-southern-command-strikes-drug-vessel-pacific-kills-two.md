Washington — SOUTHCOM killed 2 people on a suspected drug vessel. The US military struck the boat in the Eastern Pacific based on intelligence that it was on narco-trafficking routes, without arrest or trial. The identities of those killed have not been released; no legal framework governs lethal maritime drug interdiction.
