3 suicide bombers struck Maiduguri on Monday evening, killing 23 people and wounding 108. Boko Haram and Islamic State West Africa Province have escalated attacks in Borno State since Nigeria redeployed counter-insurgency troops south to fight banditry, thinning its northeastern defences. Their 16-year insurgency has killed more than 40,000 people; the night before, bombers also struck a military post in the area.
