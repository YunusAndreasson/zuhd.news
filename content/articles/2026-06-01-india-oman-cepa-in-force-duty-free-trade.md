New Delhi — 99% of Indian exports enter [Oman](country:OM) duty-free.

[India](country:IN) and Oman's new trade pact raises duty-free access from 15% to 99% of exports, covering professional mobility and 127 services sub-sectors.

The government projects gems and jewellery exports rising from $26 million to $150 million within 3 years.
