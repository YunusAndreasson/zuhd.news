Washington — A White House aide read speeches early.

Early script access let a staffer trade on the president's exact words.

Gabriel Perez won $107,500 betting on Trump's words on Kalshi, the Commodity Futures Trading Commission said.

The three-year ban and $65,000 fine leave open whether other prediction-market staffers face the same scrutiny.
