Saudi Arabia downed 2 ballistic missiles over its eastern oil fields. Riyadh received its first-ever air raid alerts, underlining the war's spread despite Saudi Arabia's efforts to stay neutral. Saudi Arabia hosted regional de-escalation talks last week and must now decide whether to retaliate or absorb the strikes.
