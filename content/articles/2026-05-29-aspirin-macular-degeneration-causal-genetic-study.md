London — Aspirin's macular degeneration risk runs through LDL reduction.

Genetic instruments established cause rather than correlation; the risk is fully mediated by lipid changes, with no direct aspirin-to-retina pathway.

Whether stopping aspirin reverses the risk, or whether the wet form is affected, remains to be tested.
