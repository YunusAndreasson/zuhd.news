Israeli forces arrested over 100 Palestinians in 5 days since Ramadan began, roughly 20 per day. The Palestinian Prisoner's Society reported severe beatings, home destruction, and a starvation policy lasting 2.5 years across a prison population of 9,300, including 350 children. Authorities also banned Ramadan decorations in Jerusalem and barred thousands of permit-holding worshippers from Al-Aqsa Mosque.
