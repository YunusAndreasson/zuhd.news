Moscow — Ukrainian drones cut Russian refining 45%, industry sources estimate.

Several Russian regions now restrict retail petrol and diesel sales, local authorities say.

Putin told oil executives [Russia](country:RU)'s retaliation is inevitable because [Ukraine](country:UA) ignored the [US](country:US) president's request to stop striking refineries.

Putin said petrol prices have stabilised since peaking in October, though the fuel market remains difficult.

Kyiv says it will stop striking Russian energy targets only when Moscow does the same.
