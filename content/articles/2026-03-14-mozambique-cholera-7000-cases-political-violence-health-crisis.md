Mozambique's cholera epidemic has killed 82 and infected 7,326. Mozambique's post-election conflict in late 2024 destroyed water and sanitation across the hardest-hit northern provinces, where cholera spreads without treatment infrastructure. 63 new cases appeared in 24 hours, with Nampula province — half the national total — showing no sign of slowing.
