3 Gulf airports have closed, cutting cancer drug routes. The Middle East normally handles over a fifth of global air cargo, with no viable overland substitute for cold-chain cancer drugs. Gulf hospitals could run out of chemotherapy and critical medicines within weeks if new cold-chain routes are not established.
