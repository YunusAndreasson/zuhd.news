Ohio — Household power bills rose over 20% in two years, federal data show.

Nationwide bills are up over 10% since 2024, and 9 state utility commissions face voters.

Utilities recover grid costs via rate increases, and requests are on track to match last year's record $31 billion, PowerLines found.

Utility advocates say freezing rates and cutting guaranteed returns will not meaningfully lower bills but could cut grid investment.

Ohio voters pick between Democrat Amy Acton, who backs a data centre moratorium, and Republican Vivek Ramaswamy.
