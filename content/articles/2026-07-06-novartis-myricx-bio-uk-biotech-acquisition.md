London — Novartis will pay $1.1bn upfront for Myricx.

Myricx's next-generation antibody-drug conjugates target cancer cells directly — research spun from [Britain](country:GB)'s Imperial College and the Francis Crick Institute in 2019.

[Switzerland](country:CH)'s Novartis owes a further $400 million if development milestones are met.
