Washington — 1 FDA-approved C. diff drug costs $19,680. The FDA shut down OpenBiome, a nonprofit that shipped donor material to hospitals at low cost, after approving the branded drugs. Neither drug is cleared for children or the immunocompromised — groups that face the highest C. diff mortality.
