Washington — US consumer prices rose 0.4% in August.

Petrol, up 27% on the year, drove a third of the gain.

Brent crude traded near $109 a barrel as of Sept. 9, driven by Hormuz and Red Sea disruptions.

The Fed's FedWatch tool puts 87% odds on a rate hike next week.
