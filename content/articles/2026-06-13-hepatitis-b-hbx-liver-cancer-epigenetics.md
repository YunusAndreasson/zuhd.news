London — HBx reprograms liver gene regulation — not just mutations.

Hepatitis B's cancer burden falls heaviest on Asia and Africa; HBx silences tumor-suppressor genes through methylation, creating a switch that epigenetic drugs can target.

Histone deacetylase inhibitors show preclinical promise; none has reached clinical approval.
