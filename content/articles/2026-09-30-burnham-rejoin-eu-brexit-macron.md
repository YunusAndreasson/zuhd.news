London — [Britain](country:GB)'s prime minister put EU re-entry on the table.

It breaks Labour's 2024 manifesto, which ruled out the customs union and single market.

Burnham listed staying put, a customs union, the single market or full membership, and promised options before a summit with the EU's 27 members.

Anand Menon of the UK in a Changing Europe think-tank says Burnham has promised only a conversation.

An EU official says the UK-EU summit is planned for November, though the date is unconfirmed.
