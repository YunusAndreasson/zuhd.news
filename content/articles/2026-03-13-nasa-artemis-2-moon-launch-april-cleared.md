4 astronauts launch for the Moon on April 1. Artemis 2 will be the first crewed Moon flyby in 53 years, testing the Orion capsule before Artemis 3 attempts a landing. Final hardware checks remain before the launch window, and the mission's lead official warned it "is not without risk."
