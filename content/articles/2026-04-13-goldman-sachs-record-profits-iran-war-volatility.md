New York — Goldman equity traders broke their own record. Iran war volatility drove equities revenue to $5.33 billion — $1 billion above Goldman's own previous all-time high — as hedge funds borrowed heavily to bet on oil. Overall profit rose 19% to $5.6 billion but shares fell 3% as war uncertainty dampened corporate deal flow.
