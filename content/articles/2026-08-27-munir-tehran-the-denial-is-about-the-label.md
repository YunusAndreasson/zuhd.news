Islamabad — Pakistan denies Munir went to Tehran for Trump.

At stake is whether Islamabad is seen as an independent mediator or a US proxy.

Army chief Asim Munir met Iranian leaders on August 24, seeking to end the US-[Iran](country:IR) stalemate, the Foreign Office said.

Spokesperson Tahir Andrabi did not say whether talks produced any next steps.
