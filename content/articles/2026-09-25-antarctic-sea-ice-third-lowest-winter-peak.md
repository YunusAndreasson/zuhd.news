Boulder — Antarctic sea ice peaked at 17.59 million square kilometres.

It is the third-smallest peak in 48 years of satellite records.

All 5 lowest July extents occurred since 2022 and all 4 lowest August extents since 2023, researcher Clare Eayrs says.

Lettie Roach of the Alfred Wegener Institute says scientists cannot yet separate human warming from natural variability.

The [US](country:US) National Snow and Ice Data Center calls the 14 September peak preliminary.
