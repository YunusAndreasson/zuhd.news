Sanaa — Athir al-Amri, 14, died on her school bus.

Shrapnel from a coalition airstrike killed her after her last exam, and 2 classmates were wounded, witnesses and relatives said.

The coalition says 100 jets destroyed 324 military targets on 5 October, while Ansarallah counts 1,798 strikes since the escalation.

"She was planning to spend the holiday playing as all children do during school breaks," said her cousin Mohammed Al-Lahida.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s offensive to retake Mokha continues, and Ansarallah is answering with missiles.
