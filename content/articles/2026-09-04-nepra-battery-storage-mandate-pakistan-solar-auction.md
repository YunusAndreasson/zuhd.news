Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s power regulator will require batteries in an 800MW solar and wind auction.

The mandate prices grid stability in now, instead of paying later in curtailment and shutdowns.

Bidders must pair at least 10% battery capacity with output, addressing Pakistan's midday surplus and evening surge.

The shift raises winning tariffs, a trade the auction settles in two months.
