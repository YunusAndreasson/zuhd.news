Pennsylvania — Slugs forced soybean farmers to replant 3 times. A warm winter allowed slugs to lay a second egg cycle; twice as many hatched in spring 2024, eating seedlings wholesale before crops could establish. Breeders are now developing varieties resilient to pest pressures that didn't exist a decade ago.
