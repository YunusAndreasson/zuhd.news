Frankfurt — [Germany](country:DE) vetoed UniCredit's €39 billion Commerzbank takeover bid.

Berlin holds a 12% stake in Commerzbank, giving it a shareholder veto; the finance ministry cited an inadequate premium and UniCredit's "aggressive approach."

[Italy](country:IT)'s UniCredit has not said whether it will revise its offer.
