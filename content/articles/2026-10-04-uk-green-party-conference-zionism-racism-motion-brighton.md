Brighton — Greens voted 1,022 to 813 to treat Zionism as racism.

The [UK](country:GB) party also backs one Palestinian state, and leader Zack Polanski skipped the vote.

Any member can table a motion, and Greens for Palestine recruited supporters to join and pass it as membership tripled.

The Movement for Progressive Judaism, which represents over 80 synagogues, calls it antisemitic.

Polanski, whose amendments failed, faces Thursday's Holborn and St Pancras by-election.
