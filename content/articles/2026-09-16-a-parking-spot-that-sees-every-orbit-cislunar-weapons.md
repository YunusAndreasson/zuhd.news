Washington — The disclosure came first, the doctrine second.

Days after confirming orbital weapons, the [US](country:US) told troops to prepare for combat around the moon.

Cislunar space holds Lagrange points where an object can loiter unseen, then descend on any orbit at will.

The 1967 treaty bars nuclear arms in space but not conventional ones, a gap [Russia](country:RU) and [China](country:CN) both protested this week.
