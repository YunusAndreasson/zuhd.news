Kyiv — [Ukraine](country:UA) has ordered 16 Gripen E fighters.

The jet is built to fly from short, temporary runways and roads, letting squadrons disperse and survive under sustained missile threat.

Saab's contract with [Sweden](country:SE)'s FMV, valued near $2.5 billion, delivers the jets to Ukraine only in 2029-2030.
