Washington — A report says Alice Huang kept a Chinese intelligence contact.

Her firm Megaspeed once accounted for billions in Nvidia chip sales.

The report says she was in touch with a Ministry of State Security officer from spring 2025 until this fall, citing people with knowledge of intelligence.

Attorney General Todd Blanche says the Justice Department has not concluded she spied for [China](country:CN).

Lawmakers from both parties are questioning whether export-control enforcement on AI chips has been too lax.
