São Paulo — Meta admits its AI generated images favouring Flávio Bolsonaro.

Meta earned the most from boosted campaign posts: R$282 million.

Meta deactivated the Lula campaign's Instagram ad account on 9 October, and says it was a mistake lasting just over an hour.

Meta employees complained on internal channels that US-based developers were slow to restore the account.

[Brazil](country:BR)'s runoff between Lula and Flávio Bolsonaro takes place Sunday.
