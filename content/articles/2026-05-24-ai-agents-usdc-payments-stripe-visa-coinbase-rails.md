New York — 98.6% of AI agent payments settle in USD Coin, a Keyrock report found.

Card rails carry a 30-cent minimum fee; 76% of agent micropayments fall below that floor, making it the only viable settlement layer.

MiCA and the [US](country:US) GENIUS Act take effect mid-2026 but neither addresses machine-to-machine payments or agent liability.
