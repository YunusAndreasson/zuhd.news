Istanbul — The Makkah pact's first committee met in Istanbul.

A standing secretariat would turn a mutual-defence pledge into a real institution.

Signed August 7, the pact treats an attack on [Pakistan](country:PK), [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) or [Turkey](country:TR) as an attack on all three.

Turkish media say the secretariat's framework and roadmap are still to be outlined.
