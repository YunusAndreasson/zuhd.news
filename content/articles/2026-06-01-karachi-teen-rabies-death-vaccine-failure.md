Karachi — Rabies killed a vaccinated 13-year-old in [Pakistan](country:PK).

He completed a full vaccine course after a stray dog bite but died three months later, Jinnah Postgraduate Medical Centre said.

Whether cold-chain failure, incomplete regimen, or absent immunoglobulin caused the breakthrough has not been established.
