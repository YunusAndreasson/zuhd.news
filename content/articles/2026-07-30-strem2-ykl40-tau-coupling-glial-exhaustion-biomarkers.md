London — The blockbuster Alzheimer's drugs target the wrong signal.

sTREM2 marks microglia and YKL-40 marks astrocytes; both track tau pathology, and their signal weakens as disease advances.

The authors call the weakening "glial exhaustion," but caution the finding is association, not proof of causation.
