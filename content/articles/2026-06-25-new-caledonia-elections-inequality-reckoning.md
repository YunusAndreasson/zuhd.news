Nouméa — New Caledonia held its first vote since 2019.

The election ends a seven-year suspension but leaves intact the Kanak-settler economic inequality that drove 2024 unrest in France's Pacific territory.

[France](country:FR) has set no timeline for talks on the territory's political status.
