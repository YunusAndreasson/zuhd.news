Cairo — Parliament voted behind closed doors to defend [Saudi Arabia](country:SA).

No force size, mission or duration was presented to lawmakers, a parliamentary source said.

[Egypt](country:EG) relies on Saudi investment, and Houthi attacks near Bab al-Mandeb have cut Suez Canal revenue.

Twelve Social Democratic MPs stayed seated, and Sisi says Egypt will not strike Yemen.

Riyadh has made no statement, and Polymarket traders price a Saudi-Houthi ceasefire by 31 December at 24%, down 4 points.
