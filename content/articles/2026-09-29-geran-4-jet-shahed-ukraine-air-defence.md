Ukraine — 79 of 165 Russian drones in one night were jet-powered.

A teardown of a captured drone found the Geran-4 flies up to 8 km, twice as high as propeller-driven Shaheds.

[Ukraine](country:UA)'s defence intelligence says Russia built a new airframe because the Shahed-136 structure could not survive high-speed flight.

Overnight into Monday, air defences downed or suppressed 112 of the 165 drones, the Air Force said.

Hits were recorded at 14 locations.
