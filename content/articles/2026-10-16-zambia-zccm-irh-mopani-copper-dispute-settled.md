Abu Dhabi — International Resources Holding paid $620 million for 51% of Mopani.

[Zambia](country:ZM)'s state miner accused the Abu Dhabi firm of withholding equity funding and taking on unapproved debt.

A meeting between President Hakainde Hichilema and the firm's CEO in Abu Dhabi produced a truce, not an answer on funding.

The partners' statement skipped the charges and cited new joint-exploration projects instead.

The firm aims to lift Mopani output from 66,369 tons in 2025 to 300,000 tons by 2029.
