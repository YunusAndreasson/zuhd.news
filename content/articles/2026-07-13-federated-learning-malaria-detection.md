London — A new AI diagnoses malaria without sharing images.

The model trains locally at each clinic and shares only algorithm updates, letting resource-poor facilities avoid sending patient images to any server.

Researchers report 97% accuracy, but the manuscript remains an early, unedited release ahead of clinical deployment.
