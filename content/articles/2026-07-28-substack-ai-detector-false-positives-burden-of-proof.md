San Francisco — Substack built in an AI-detection tool, Pangram.

Detectors flag low-perplexity prose — the same statistical signature as plain, clear writing, which is why non-native English writers get flagged most.

Pangram's own CEO puts its false-positive rate at 1 in 10,000; no independent audit confirms it.
