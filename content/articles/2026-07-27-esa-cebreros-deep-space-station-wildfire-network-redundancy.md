Cebreros — A 35-metre dish resumes deep-space tracking Monday.

Wildfires forced its evacuation days earlier — one of only three deep-space stations, so its outage shifted probe coverage onto Australia and Argentina.

Director General Josef Aschbacher cleared the antenna after inspections found no fire damage.
