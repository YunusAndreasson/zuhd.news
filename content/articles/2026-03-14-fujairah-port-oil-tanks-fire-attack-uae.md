Fujairah's oil storage tanks caught fire after an attack. Fujairah is the UAE's main export hub on the Gulf of Oman, built to bypass the Strait of Hormuz. Iran has threatened to reduce US-linked facilities to ash, leaving the Gulf without a safe export route.
