London — 8 adults, 20 days, one pilot trial of black seed oil.

It is the first prospective test of a remedy used in hadith literature for 14 centuries, not a retrospective claim.

Researchers found no microbiome shift, but detected thymoquinone in urine, confirming it reaches circulation.

A placebo-controlled phase II trial must confirm any effect before doctors act on it.
