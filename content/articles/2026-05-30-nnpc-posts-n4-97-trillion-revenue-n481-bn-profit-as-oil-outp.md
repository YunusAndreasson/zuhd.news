Abuja — Nigeria's state oil company posted N481bn April profit on record output.

Crude and condensate production hit 1.68m barrels per day, a 12-month high per its monthly report; retail fuel availability stood at only 54%.

The company said it expects to deliver gas to Abuja via a new cross-country pipeline by end of 2026.
