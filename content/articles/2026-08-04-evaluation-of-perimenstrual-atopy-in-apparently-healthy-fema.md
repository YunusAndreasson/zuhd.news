London — A third of healthy women showed cyclical allergy sensitisation.

Hormones and autonomic nerve balance shifted with menstrual phase in 103 women with no allergy diagnosis, meaning the same allergen dose can read differently two weeks apart.

Authors call the manuscript unedited and early access, with limitations still to be resolved.
