London — Hackers keep breaching the suppliers that hold police data.

Most [UK](country:GB) police supplier contracts include no ongoing cybersecurity assurance, inspectors found.

The Police Digital Service logged 252 cyber incidents and 796 near misses in the first three months of this year.

The report does not say how many attacks occurred, which suppliers were hit, or whether data was stolen.

The Cyber Security and Resilience Bill, still in Parliament, omits policing, and no official could say why.
