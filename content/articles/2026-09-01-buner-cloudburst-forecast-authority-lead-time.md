Freelance forecasters now rival [Pakistan](country:PK)'s weather agency.

Evacuation decisions increasingly rely on unverified social media warnings, not official alerts.

Cloudbursts strike within minutes across areas smaller than 10 kilometers, faster than the Meteorological Department's radar network can warn.

The department has no funded plan to close its rural radar gaps.
