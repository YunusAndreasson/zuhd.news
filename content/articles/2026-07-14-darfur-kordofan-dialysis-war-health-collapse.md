Nyala — 35 dialysis patients died there in two months.

Only 4 of Nyala's 12 dialysis machines still work, after [Sudan](country:SD)'s 2023 fighting wrecked maintenance and supply chains.

Officials have pledged funds to repair five machines within weeks, raising capacity to eight.
