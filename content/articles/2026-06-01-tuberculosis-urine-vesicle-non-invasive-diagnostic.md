London — Urine detects active tuberculosis with 94% sensitivity.

Urinary vesicles carry tuberculosis proteins even when patients can't produce sputum — the barrier that blocks diagnosis in children and people with HIV.

The study enrolled 162 participants; larger, diverse trials are needed before deployment in TB-endemic settings, authors said.
