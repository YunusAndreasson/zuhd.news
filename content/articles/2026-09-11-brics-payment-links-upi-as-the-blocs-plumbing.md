New Delhi — India's instant-payment network handles over 250 billion transfers yearly.

Linking payment systems lets sanctioned members like [Russia](country:RU) and [Iran](country:IR) bypass the Belgium-based SWIFT bank-messaging system.

Commerce minister Piyush Goyal urged members to trade in local currencies, settling bilaterally instead of through dollar correspondent banks.

Russia also proposed a BRICS grain exchange and a joint space council at the summit.
