London — A 226 GHz wireless link spanned 1,400 meters. Plasmonic photonic modulators achieved 214 Gbit·s·km — the highest sub-THz rate-distance product recorded — suggesting fiber-equivalent backhaul without fiber trenching. If the result holds at scale, 6G cell-site density falls and rural backhaul becomes economically viable.
