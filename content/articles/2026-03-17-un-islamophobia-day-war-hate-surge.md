The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation told a UN event that online hate speech targeting Muslims has reached "unprecedented" levels since the war on Iran began. The International Day to Combat Islamophobia, established in 2022 on Pakistan's initiative to honour 51 worshippers killed in the Christchurch attacks, falls this year as Al-Aqsa Mosque remains sealed through Eid and Iran is under active bombardment. UN Secretary-General António Guterres warned that "subtle biases" are now shaping "who is seen as belonging."
