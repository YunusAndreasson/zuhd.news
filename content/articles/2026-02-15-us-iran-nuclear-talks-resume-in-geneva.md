A second round of nuclear talks begins next week in Geneva. The Swiss Foreign Ministry confirmed that Oman will again mediate between US and Iranian negotiators, following a first round of indirect talks on 6 February. The Trump administration insists Iran must abandon all uranium enrichment, while Tehran wants negotiations limited to the nuclear programme alone. Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu pressed Washington this week to expand any deal to cover Iran's ballistic missiles and its funding of Hamas and Hezbollah. Trump warned Tehran after the first round that failure to reach an agreement would be "very traumatic," even as the Pentagon prepares weeks-long military operations against Iranian infrastructure.
