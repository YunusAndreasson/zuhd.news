Algeria outspends most EU members in EU research. The government committed €6 million for 2025–2027 to PRIMA, the EU's Mediterranean programme targeting water scarcity and food security — Algeria has backed 99 of its 269 projects since 2018, more than most member states contribute. Whether the science reaches farmers before North Africa's freshwater crisis deepens is the test.
