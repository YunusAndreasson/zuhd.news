Changzhi — 82 miners killed, 9 missing after a Shanxi coal mine blast.

Shanxi produces a third of [China](country:CN)'s coal on migrant labour; safety violations were cited and the mine operator detained.

Xi Jinping ordered an investigation and "accountability under the law"; rescue teams continue to search.
