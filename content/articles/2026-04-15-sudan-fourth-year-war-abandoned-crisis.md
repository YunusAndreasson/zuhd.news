Khartoum — 59,000 dead as Sudan enters its fourth year. Iran war shipping disruptions pushed Sudan's fuel prices up 24%, tightening supply chains feeding 34 million people who depend on international aid. The UN's top Sudan official calls it an "abandoned crisis"; no ceasefire is in sight.
