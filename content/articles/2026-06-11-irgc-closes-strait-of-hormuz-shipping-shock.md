Tehran — Iran's Revolutionary Guard Navy sealed the Strait of Hormuz indefinitely.

The strait carries roughly 20% of globally traded oil — a declared closure transmits fuel and freight shocks worldwide within hours.

The Revolutionary Guard said approaching the strait counts as enemy cooperation; no reopening date was set.
