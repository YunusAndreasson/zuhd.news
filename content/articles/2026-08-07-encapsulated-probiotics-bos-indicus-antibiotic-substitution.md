London — Encapsulated probiotics cut E. coli in calves.

Growth-promoting antibiotics are livestock's largest-volume antibiotic use and a driver of resistance; this trial used Bos indicus, the breed most farmers keep.

The trial used 40 calves; researchers say wider replication is needed before adoption.
