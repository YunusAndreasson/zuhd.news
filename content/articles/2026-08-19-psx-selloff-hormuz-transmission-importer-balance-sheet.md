Karachi — KSE-100 fell 2,547 points on Tuesday.

[Pakistan](country:PK) imports nearly all its oil, priced in dollars under an IMF programme.

Brent crude was up 32% over the month to August 11 amid the Hormuz stalemate, straining reserves.

A fuel-adjustment surcharge and looming IMF review threaten household electricity and transport bills.
