Port Moresby — Volcanic pumice has blanketed [Papua New Guinea](country:PG)'s coast.

The Titan Ridge eruption has buried coastal waters in meters of pumice, severing subsistence communities from fishing and transport.

Pumice rafts persist for months and can cross oceans; no timeline for clearing has been announced.
