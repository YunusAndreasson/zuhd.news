New York — [Cuba](country:CU) accuses Washington of engineering a humanitarian crisis.

Since the [US](country:US) imposed an oil blockade this year, Cuba has lived with fuel shortages and blackouts.

Cuban officials blame the blockade for shortages of food and other basic needs on top of the blackouts.

"Cuba is not a threat. The blockade is a threat," Foreign Minister Bruno Rodríguez told the General Assembly.

Trump predicts a deal and rules out the military, but Havana demands talks without preconditions.
