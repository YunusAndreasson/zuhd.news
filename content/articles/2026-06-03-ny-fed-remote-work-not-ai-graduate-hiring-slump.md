New York — Remote work, not AI, is freezing graduate hiring.

The New York Fed links 64% of rising graduate unemployment to remote work — junior roles need in-person mentoring firms can't provide.

[US](country:US) entry-level hiring is down roughly 29% from pre-pandemic; [India](country:IN) shed 44% of tech openings year-on-year, Xpheno said.
