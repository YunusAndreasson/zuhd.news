A 7.1-magnitude earthquake struck 628 kilometers underground. At that depth, seismic energy dissipates before reaching the surface — residents across 3 countries reported only faint vibrations. A shallow 2015 quake killed 18 on Mount Kinabalu — the next one may not strike so deep.
