Ballistic missiles struck Tel Aviv, Iran's Revolutionary Guards said. The Guards said the attack was revenge for Israel's killing of Ali Larijani, a former parliament speaker, in Tehran. Iran said the strikes exposed a collapse in Israel's air defences; Israel has not announced ceasefire negotiations.
