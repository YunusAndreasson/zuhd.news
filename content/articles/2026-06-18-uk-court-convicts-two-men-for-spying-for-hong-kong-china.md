London — A serving UK immigration officer spied for Hong Kong.

Chi Leung Wai queried [Britain](country:GB)'s immigration database to track Hong Kong pro-democracy exiles — the first [China](country:CN)-spy convictions on British soil.

Wai received 10 years; Chung Biu Yuen 8 — the judge warned modern espionage now targets dissidents, not military secrets.
