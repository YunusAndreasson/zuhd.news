Brussels — De Wever offered to quit over a €10 billion gap.

[Belgium](country:BE) has a prime minister who has neither left nor settled its 2027 budget.

De Wever's Flemish nationalists and the Reformist Movement want spending cuts, while partners push higher taxes on wealthier households.

The king consults majority party leaders Tuesday and meets De Wever again Friday.
