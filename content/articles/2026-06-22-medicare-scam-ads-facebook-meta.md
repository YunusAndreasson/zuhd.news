Washington — 215 million Medicare scam ads ran on Facebook last year, the Center for Countering Digital Hate found.

Meta's tools directed 73% of those impressions at users over 65; Meta earns more the longer a fraudulent ad runs, the watchdog said.

Meta's internal projections put scam-and-fraud ad revenue at $16 billion annually.
