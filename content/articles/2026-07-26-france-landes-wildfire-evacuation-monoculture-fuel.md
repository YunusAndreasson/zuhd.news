Bordeaux — More than 220,000 people have fled Bordeaux.

The fire is burning across [France](country:FR)'s largest planted pine forest, a monoculture whose uniform fuel spreads flame fast under drought.

Interior Minister Laurent Nunez said containment will take a long time; evacuees cannot yet return.
