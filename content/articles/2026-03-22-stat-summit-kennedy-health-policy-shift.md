Washington — An Oregon judge will void Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr.'s gender-affirming care declaration. Kennedy issued the order without a public comment period, exceeding the authority federal law grants him. Once lifted, clinics in states that support gender-affirming care will no longer face federal pressure to end treatments.
