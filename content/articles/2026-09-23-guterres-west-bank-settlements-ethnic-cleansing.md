New York — Guterres said [Israel](country:IL)'s settlements raise "the spectre of ethnic cleansing."

The UN chief's own words name the occupying power driving displacement in the West Bank.

Guterres cited settlement expansion, forced displacement and settler violence as bulldozing the two-state solution's remaining path to peace.

No member state has yet attached a resolution or sanction to his language.
