The International Energy Agency declared history's worst energy security threat. The energy body issued 10 behavioral measures — work from home, drive slower, restrict air conditioning — after releasing 400 million barrels from emergency reserves. IEA director Fatih Birol warned a further reserve release may follow if prices keep rising.
