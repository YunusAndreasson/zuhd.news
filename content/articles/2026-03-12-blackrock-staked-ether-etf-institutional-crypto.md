Previous ether exchange-traded funds offered no staking yield. BlackRock listed ETHB on Nasdaq, becoming the first regulated fund to pay ether holders who lock tokens to validate the network. The test is whether ETHB can replicate the success of BlackRock's bitcoin fund IBIT, which accumulated $55 billion by drawing crypto investors into regulated markets.
