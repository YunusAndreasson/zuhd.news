Sivasagar — 70 to 80 people remain missing.

More than 721,000 people across 13 [India](country:IN)n districts are affected, officials said, as upstream rain from neighboring hill states floods the Brahmaputra plains again.

Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma has pledged talks with neighboring states on flood coordination.
