Claremore — Bodycam shows farmer Blanchard handcuffed at a data-center hearing.

Claremore police charged Blanchard with criminal trespass after he exceeded public-comment time opposing a data center approved under city non-disclosure agreements.

Blanchard's legal team filed a motion to dismiss, calling his arrest retaliation for protected speech.
