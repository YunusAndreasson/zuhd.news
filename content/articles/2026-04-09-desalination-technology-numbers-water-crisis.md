Doha — Qatar gets nearly all drinking water from desalination. 4,897 of the world's 17,910 desalination plants sit in the Middle East, consuming electricity equivalent to 60 million households by 2035. The region's $25 billion plant expansion through 2028 depends on electricity the Iran conflict has made scarce.
