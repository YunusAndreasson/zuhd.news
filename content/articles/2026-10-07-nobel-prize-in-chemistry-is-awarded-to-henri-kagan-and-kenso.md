Stockholm — Kagan and Soai won for asymmetric organic synthesis discoveries.

A drug's two mirror-image forms can act differently in the body.

Their cited work: a slightly lopsided catalyst yields a far more lopsided product, and Soai's product catalyses itself.

The Swedish Academy announces the literature prize on Thursday, and Oslo the peace prize on Friday.
