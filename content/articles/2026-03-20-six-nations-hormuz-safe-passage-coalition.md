6 allied nations pledged Thursday to help reopen Hormuz. Iran has effectively blockaded the strait — the route for one-fifth of the world's oil — leaving 20,000 seafarers stranded on 3,200 vessels. No country named a ship, a timeline, or a route — Iran says the strait is open only to non-enemies.
