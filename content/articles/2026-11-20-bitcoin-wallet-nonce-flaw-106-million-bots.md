Liège — Bots drained University of Liège researchers' bait bitcoin within seconds.

Addresses behind 3,620 recovered keys peaked at about $106 million at November 2024 prices.

A wallet that signs two payments with the same random nonce lets anyone compute its private key.

The researchers did not establish how much was actually stolen.

Most remaining vulnerable funds sit at one bitcoin address the paper does not publish.
