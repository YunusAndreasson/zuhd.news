Hong Kong — The exchange's dollar-gold futures have hit record volume since July.

Hong Kong is the offshore hub where [China](country:CN)'s non-convertible currency can be held, traded and hedged.

Chief executive Bonnie Chan wrote that the exchange will also seek yuan counters in southbound Stock Connect and add more bond futures.

Nothing has launched, and Chan gave no date for the yuan gold contract.

The exchange launches 12 more single-stock option classes in November, each with weekly and monthly expiries.
