Marianhill — A group in party clothing held election officials hostage, the electoral commission said.

The confrontation disrupted 2 of the warehouse's 24 voting stations a day before [South Africa](country:ZA)'s local elections.

They demanded special-vote ballot boxes be moved, saying the building was insecure, at a site sharing an entrance with a library.

Chief executive Sy Mamabolo called them alleged uMkhonto weSizwe supporters, but the party's Willies Mchunu doubts they were members.

Polls open Wednesday with 165 KwaZulu-Natal stations rated high-risk by police.
