London — Liquefied natural gas costs triple its pre-crisis price.

[Britain](country:GB)'s system operators say supply will cover demand, but only by outbidding neighbouring markets.

Britain holds some of Europe's smallest gas stores, so it competes for liquefied gas cargoes forecast up 5% on last winter.

The newly approved Jackdaw gasfield, not fully counted in the forecasts, is due to start up next month.

National Gas director Glenn Bryn-Jacobsen warns of tighter supply margins in high-demand spells.
