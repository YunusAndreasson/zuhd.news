Every air-to-fuel startup before Carbonology needed government subsidies to survive. The Shanghai firm claims it can convert air and water into commercial-scale petrol; direct air capture has never turned a profit without state backing. China's Hormuz fuel crisis has made energy independence a state priority — Carbonology says its first large-scale plants are ready.
