Lahore — Punjab's assembly unanimously demanded harsher blasphemy penalties.

The assembly cannot amend the federal Penal Code, so it urged [Pakistan](country:PK)'s government to act.

Section 298-A punishes defiling the Prophet's wives, family or companions with up to 3 years in prison.

Pakistan's rights commission says blasphemy complaints often stem from personal vendettas or far-right clerics' pressure on police.

The last attempt, a 10-year minimum passed in 2023, stalled when then-president Arif Alvi returned the bill.
