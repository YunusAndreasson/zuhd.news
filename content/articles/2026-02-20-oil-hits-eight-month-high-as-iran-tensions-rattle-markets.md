Brent crude topped $72 for the first time since July. April futures rose 2.3% as Trump's nuclear deadline for Iran and Strait of Hormuz disruptions tightened supply expectations. West Texas Intermediate climbed 2.6% in parallel — the strait carries roughly a fifth of global oil trade.
