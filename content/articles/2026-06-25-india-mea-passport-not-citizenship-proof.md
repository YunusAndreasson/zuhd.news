New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s foreign ministry: passports don't prove citizenship.

Under the 1967 Passports Act, passports can be issued to non-citizens, making them travel documents abroad, not citizenship proof at home.

Opposition leaders warn a booth officer can now use the clarification to strike voters from electoral rolls.
