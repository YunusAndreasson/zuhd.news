London — Iron-coated biochar boosts a nitrogen-saving pathway 3.2-fold.

The iron strengthens biochar's electron-shuttling, favoring ammonium retention over nitrogen lost as gas, researchers said.

The technique could cut synthetic fertilizer costs and emissions if scaled to working rice paddies.
