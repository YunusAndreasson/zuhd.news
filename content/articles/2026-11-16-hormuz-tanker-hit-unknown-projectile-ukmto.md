Tehran — Tanker hit leaving Hormuz, [Iran](country:IR)'s state news agency Mehr reported.

No casualties were reported, but damage and environmental impact are unknown.

An unknown projectile started an engine-room fire as the tanker transited outbound, UK Maritime Trade Operations said, and authorities are investigating.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 31 December 17% odds, down 18 points in a week.
