Brooklyn — Jurors found Huawei guilty on 10 of 12 counts.

Victims included Cisco, Motorola Solutions, T-Mobile, Quintel USA and Fujitsu, court records show.

Prosecutors said Huawei paid staff bonuses for rivals' secrets, and Meng Wanzhou's 2018 arrest in [Canada](country:CA) made the case public.

Huawei's lawyer Brian Heberlig called the racketeering acquittal vindication and said the company will appeal.

Huawei is sentenced next September and could face billions of dollars in penalties.
