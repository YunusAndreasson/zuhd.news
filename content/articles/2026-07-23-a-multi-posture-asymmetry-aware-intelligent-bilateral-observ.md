London — 1,810 people, both hands, three postures.

Researchers built MAIBO, a wearable-ring dataset pairing bilateral pulse readings with blood pressure and heart rate, to fix how posture skews existing single-hand sensors.

The dataset, spanning 7,478 sessions, is now open for teams building next-generation cardiovascular wearables.
