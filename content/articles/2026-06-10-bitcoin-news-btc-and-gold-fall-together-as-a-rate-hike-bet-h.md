New York — Bitcoin fell 6.9% on the week; gold dropped below $4,200.

Both pay no yield, so rate-hike bets punish both; a hot [US](country:US) inflation print Wednesday could force further selling, traders said.

If gold steadies while bitcoin falls further, traders say the case for bitcoin as a macro hedge weakens.
