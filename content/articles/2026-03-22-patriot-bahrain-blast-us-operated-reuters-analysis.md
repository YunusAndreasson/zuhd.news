Manama — A US Patriot missile struck Bahrain on March 9. Bahrain and US Central Command blamed an Iranian drone for 32 civilian injuries until Reuters analysis by academic researchers identified the likely culprit as a US-operated Patriot battery on Sitra island. Neither the Pentagon nor Bahrain has called for a public investigation.
