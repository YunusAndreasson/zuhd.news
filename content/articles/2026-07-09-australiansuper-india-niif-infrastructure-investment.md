Melbourne — Retirees' savings will now build Indian roads and ports.

AustralianSuper is adding A$500 million to [India](country:IN)'s National Investment and Infrastructure Fund, lifting its total Indian holdings to A$3.3 billion.

The fund called its earlier NIIF bet one of its best-performing infrastructure investments.
