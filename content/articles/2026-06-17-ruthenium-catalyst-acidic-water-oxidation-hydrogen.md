London — New water-splitting catalyst runs 1,000 hours without degrading.

Anchored ruthenium atoms prevent the dissolution that kills efficient catalysts by offloading oxidative charge to a metal-oxide host.

It uses 80% less precious metal than standard iridium-oxide anodes, though commercial validation remains ahead.
