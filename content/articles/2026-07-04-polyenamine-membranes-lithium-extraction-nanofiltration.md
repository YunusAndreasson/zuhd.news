London — A new membrane separates lithium from magnesium 309 times over.

Conventional polyamide filters degrade through hydrolysis; the click-chemistry polyenamine membrane resists breakdown, cutting the cost of pulling battery-grade lithium from brine.

Module-scale tests point toward cheaper lithium recovery from both salt lakes and spent batteries.
