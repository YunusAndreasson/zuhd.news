Prague — Common iron ore pyrrhotite confirmed as altermagnet. Theorized only in 2022, altermagnetism merges antiferromagnet speed with ferromagnet readability; pyrrhotite fills mining waste heaps worldwide. Spintronics researchers can now prototype altermagnetic devices — from wireless chips to quantum memory — using cheap, abundant material.
