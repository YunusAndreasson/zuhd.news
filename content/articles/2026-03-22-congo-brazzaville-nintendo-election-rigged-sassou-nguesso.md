Brazzaville — Congo's biometric voting devices came pre-loaded with results. President Denis Sassou-Nguesso has held power for 40 of the last 47 years; the devices he sold as democratic infrastructure gave him 94.82% of the vote. Civil society group CAPGED has filed a legal challenge, but overturning the result would require Sassou-Nguesso's own judiciary to rule against him.
