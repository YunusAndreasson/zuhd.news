Israel — The US Army seeks concrete bunkers for troops in 3 countries.

Converted shipping containers now serve as shelters, and Iranian drones killed 6 soldiers at a base in Kuwait.

Each bunker is 3 precast concrete culvert sections with steel doors and a sandbag roof, planned for Israel, Lebanon and Saudi Arabia.

The 13 November notice only canvasses builders, and the Army has named no contract or cost.
