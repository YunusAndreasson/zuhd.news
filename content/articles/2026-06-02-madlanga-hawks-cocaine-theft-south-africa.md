Durban — The cocaine thieves were Hawks managers, a witness testified.

Warrant Officer Karl Sander told the Madlanga Commission that suspects who stole R200m of seized cocaine from [South Africa](country:ZA)'s Hawks unit in 2021 were linked to its management.

Sander confirmed the links but named no managers; the commission continues.
