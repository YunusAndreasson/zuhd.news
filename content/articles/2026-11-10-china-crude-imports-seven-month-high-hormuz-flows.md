Beijing — China imported nearly 42 million tons of crude in October.

Imports are still 13% below a year ago, nine months into the [Iran](country:IR) war.

Iraqi cargoes led the recovery, and the Gulf now loads about 3 million barrels a day of refined products, mostly exiting Hormuz.

Polymarket traders give Hormuz traffic a 10% chance of normalising by 31 December, down 8 points in a month.
