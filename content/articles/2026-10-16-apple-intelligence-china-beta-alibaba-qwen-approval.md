Shanghai — Apple's China AI runs on Alibaba's Qwen models.

[China](country:CN)'s regulators approved both the features and the models, executive chairman Tim Cook said.

The first batch has live translation, writing tools and photo cleanup, but excludes the redesigned Siri assistant.

The public rollout is expected in December.
