Balasore — About 515,000 people are already affected by flooding.

A new red alert warns of 20 more centimeters onto already-saturated ground.

The Baitarani and Brahmani rivers rose above danger levels in eastern [India](country:IN), breaching an embankment in Jajpur.

The India Meteorological Department expects the rain to intensify Monday before easing Tuesday.
