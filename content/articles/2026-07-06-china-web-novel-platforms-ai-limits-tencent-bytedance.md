Shenzhen — A novelist used DeepSeek to outline his book.

Tencent, ByteDance and Baidu, [China](country:CN)'s biggest web-fiction platforms, added daily word-count limits and quality gates after unrestrained AI writing collapsed story values.

The platforms, not regulators, are now rationing the very tool they promoted to writers.
