Canberra — One Nation's real migration number nearly matches Labor's.

The party's anti-migration brand rests on which arrivals get counted, not the total.

MP David Farley wants 130,000 counted as migration plus 100,000 farm and care workers counted separately.

Pauline Hanson has already counselled Farley once over the same admission.
