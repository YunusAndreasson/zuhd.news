Lagos — 7 water facilities face 20-year private concessions.

Campaigners say prepaid meters could cut off [Nigeria](country:NG)'s low-income households when credit runs out.

Corporate Accountability and Public Participation Africa says the framework sets cost-reflective tariffs, currency protections for investors and a 5% royalty.

The Lagos State government has presented partnerships as a way to attract investment and expand reliable supply.

The water corporation received 23 bids in May and has not named preferred bidders or published agreements.
