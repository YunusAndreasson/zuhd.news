Singapore — The prime minister's pay rises 64% to S$3.6 million.

The world's highest-paid leader earns roughly 52 times the median worker's income here.

Law pegs ministerial pay to a discounted benchmark drawn from the country's top 1,000 earners, meant to recruit talent and deter graft.

Parliament debates the formula Thursday, first raised since a 2012 pay cut.
