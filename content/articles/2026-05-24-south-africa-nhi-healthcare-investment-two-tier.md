Johannesburg — [South Africa](country:ZA) signed its National Health Insurance law in 2024; it has never operated.

Court challenges from private hospitals, insurers, and medical associations have blocked enforcement.

Private providers are expanding two-tier services as the law stalls; no implementation date is set.
