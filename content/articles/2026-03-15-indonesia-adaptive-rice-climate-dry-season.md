Drought forced Indonesia to import rice in 2025. Indonesia's agriculture ministry ordered 6 drought-resistant varieties to avoid a repeat as dry seasons arrive earlier across Java, Bali, and Sumatra. A production shortfall would again pressure global rice markets, as Indonesia's 280 million people depend on domestic supply.
