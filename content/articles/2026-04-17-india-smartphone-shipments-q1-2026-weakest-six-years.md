Mumbai — India's smartphone market hit a 6-year low. Memory prices, inflated by AI server demand for NAND and DRAM, pushed average handset prices up Rs 1,500, squeezing first-time buyers in the sub-Rs 15,000 segment hardest. Analysts project a 10% full-year decline, with budget buyers the most exposed.
