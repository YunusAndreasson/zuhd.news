Delhi — Delhi compressed natural gas prices surged Rs 4 in 10 days.

The fuel powers [India](country:IN)'s auto-rickshaws and city buses, passing hikes directly to fares.

State oil companies lose about Rs 1,600 crore daily; no fare relief for auto-rickshaw commuters has been announced.
