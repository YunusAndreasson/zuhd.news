Karama, Sudan — Rains reach displacement camps built over flood channels.

Thousands driven from Kurmuk and Geisan by fighting survive on 1 meal a day, unsafe water and makeshift shelters as disease spreads.

Volunteers urge a move to higher ground before rains intensify; neither [Sudan](country:SD)'s Rapid Support Forces nor the army has acted.
