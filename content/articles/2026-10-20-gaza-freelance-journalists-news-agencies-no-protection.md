Gaza — The Palestinian Journalists Syndicate counts over 260 reporters killed.

[Israel](country:IL) bars foreign reporters from Gaza, so outlets depend on Palestinian freelancers paid per story.

Paid per story, freelancers rarely get a safety net, so they cover their own treatment and replacement cameras.

"We carry full responsibility for our lives," said Salma al-Qadoumi, a freelancer wounded in Khan Younis in 2024.

Nearly half of the Rory Peck Trust's 2025 crisis grants went to Palestinian journalists in Gaza.
