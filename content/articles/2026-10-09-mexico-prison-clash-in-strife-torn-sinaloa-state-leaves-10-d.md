Mazatlán — A prison fight killed 9 inmates and a minor visitor.

State authorities say 16 more inmates were hurt and moved for medical care.

The Sinaloa Public Security Secretariat said an inter-institutional group of security forces took control of El Castillo prison.

Officials have not said whether the fight is tied to Sinaloa's cartel war, and prison visits stay suspended.
