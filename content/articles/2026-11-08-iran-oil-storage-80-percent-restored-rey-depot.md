Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) says 80% of bombed oil storage is rebuilt, Russian state agency TASS reported.

Mohammad Meshkinfam, who runs the state oil engineering company, says Tehran's fuel supply returned within 2 weeks.

He put petroleum-product pumping facilities at about 60% complete, in figures carried by Iranian state television.

He expects full restoration by the end of Aban, in late November.
