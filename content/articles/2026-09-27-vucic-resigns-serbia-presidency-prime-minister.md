Belgrade — Aleksandar Vucic resigned as [Serbia](country:RS)'s president Sunday.

It extends 14 years of one-man rule past a constitutional limit meant to end it.

Serbia's constitution bars a 3rd presidential term but not a return to the premiership, a post Vucic held twice before.

Serbia's Progressive Party is expected to win the Oct. 25 election, and Vucic takes over as premier.
