Congo basin — Rainforest loss nearly matched the Amazon's for the first time.

Global loss fell 15% to 7 million hectares, still short of the 2030 pledge.

Latin America's loss fell 33%, driven by [Brazil](country:BR)'s Amazon enforcement, the Forest Declaration Assessment found.

"Losing such a size of forest every year is a disaster," said Cameroonian lawyer Sam Nguiffo.

Brazil's runoff between Lula and Flavio Bolsonaro is on 25 October.
