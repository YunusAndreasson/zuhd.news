Damascus — A cafe bombing killed nine, mostly lawyers.

A second blast struck during President Macron's visit days later, striking at symbols of [Syria](country:SY)'s return to civilian courts and diplomacy.

Investigators have named no suspect; officials call the cafe blast an improvised device.
