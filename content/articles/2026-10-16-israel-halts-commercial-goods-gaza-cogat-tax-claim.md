Gaza — Israel says no commercial goods have entered for 2 days.

Merchants import the fruit and meat that aid groups do not bring.

[Israel](country:IL)'s military coordinator says merchants halted deliveries over a 10,000-shekel Hamas fee per truck, which Gaza's government media office denies.

"The markets need to be functioning," said Shaina Low, spokesperson for the Norwegian Refugee Council.

The US-mediated deal requires 600 aid trucks a day, and diplomats say far fewer arrive.
