Bogor — Between 23 and 26 miners remain trapped, and 3 are dead.

Informal mining gives thousands of people in [Indonesia](country:ID) a livelihood with a high risk of death.

Heavy rain made the Ciseureuh River flood a shaft about 100 metres deep, and police heard 16 hours later.

"The problem was that nobody reported it and nobody wanted to report it because the activity was illegal," said M. Adam Hamdani, Bogor's disaster agency emergency chief.

Floodwater has not receded, and rescuers expect draining the shaft to take a long time.
