Sana'a — 36% of Sana'a inpatients face lethal drug combinations.

A study of 2 [Yemen](country:YE) psychiatric hospitals found 36% of inpatients on antipsychotic combinations that trigger lethal cardiac arrhythmias.

Only 1 in 4 patients had electrocardiogram monitoring; the war-damaged system cannot provide the surveillance these prescriptions require.
