Belgrade — A police officer shot dead a knife-wielding minor outside Israel's embassy.

[Serbia](country:RS) raised its terror threat level to red, its highest.

The minor charged the guard with two knives and hit his left cheek with one, prosecutors said.

Interior Minister Ivica Dačić said security agencies had been monitoring the attacker, who displayed an Islamic State flag on Instagram.

Serbia's caretaker government commands the police until a new one forms after Sunday's election.
