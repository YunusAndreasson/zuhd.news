Hanoi — [Vietnam](country:VN) moved HIV and TB treatment onto national insurance before donors left.

Prevention outreach has no billing code, so it loses funding just as treatment survives.

Insurance reimburses defined care for enrolled patients, not peer workers finding people who don't yet know they're positive.

Indonesia, Nigeria and Pakistan face the same donor drawdown with no comparable fix in sight.
