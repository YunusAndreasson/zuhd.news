Heidelberg — Gunther von Hagens, 'Doctor Death,' has died.

His Body Worlds exhibitions drew 58 million visitors, but investigators in [China](country:CN), Kyrgyzstan and [Russia](country:RU) questioned where some bodies came from.

Von Hagens asked that his own body be plastinated after death; his family says it will be.
