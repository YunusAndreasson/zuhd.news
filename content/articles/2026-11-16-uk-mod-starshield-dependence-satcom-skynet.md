London — [UK](country:GB) defence holds a substantial number of Starshield terminals.

SpaceX restricted [Ukraine](country:UA)'s satellite access, so MPs asked what happens if Britain loses it.

The Ministry of Defence pairs military and commercial satellite links, minister Luke Pollard told Parliament, to avoid dependence on one provider.

Pollard said Starlink is not used for UK military operations.

Pollard refused to say what Starshield does for the military, leaving the dependence question unanswered.
