Washington — Democrats won 258 House seats, matching 1992.

They gain power to investigate Trump's administration for his last two years.

Chuck Hubbard unseated 11-term Republican Virginia Foxx by 330 votes after a recount, the race that took Democrats to 258.

Trump keeps his veto, and has questioned the vote's honesty without giving specific evidence.

Four House races remain uncalled, and Hakeem Jeffries is positioned for the speakership in January.
