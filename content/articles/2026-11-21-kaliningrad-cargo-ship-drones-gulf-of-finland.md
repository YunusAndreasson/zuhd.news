Helsinki — The burning Sparta II drifted into Finnish waters after 2 drone strikes.

[Finland](country:FI)'s prime minister said the war is now spilling into his country's immediate vicinity.

The ship left St Petersburg for Kaliningrad, and Finland's Border Guard is preparing for a possible oil spill.

Nobody has claimed the attack, and Kaliningrad's governor said supplies to the exclave face no threat.

Finland has restricted air and sea traffic in the eastern gulf in case more drones need intercepting.
