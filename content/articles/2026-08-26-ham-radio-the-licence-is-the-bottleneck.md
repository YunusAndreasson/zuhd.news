Islamabad — Two hams walked into Azad Kashmir after the 2005 quake cut every road.

They sent 3,000 survival messages when landlines failed, work the Pakistan Amateur Radio Society still does today.

High-frequency radio needs no tower or power grid, which is why the Telecommunication Authority licenses and vets every operator.

Around 160 licensed operators remain, each renewing every five years.
