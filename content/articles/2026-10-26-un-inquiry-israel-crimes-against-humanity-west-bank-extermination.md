Geneva — UN investigators found [Israel](country:IL) committed extermination in the West Bank.

It follows the commission's September 2025 finding of genocide in Gaza.

UN humanitarian office figures show 1,143 Palestinians killed in the West Bank since October 2023, at least 250 of them children.

Israel's Geneva mission called the report misinformation and demanded the commission's dissolution.

Francesca Albanese presents a UN report on almost 300 killed Palestinian journalists to the Human Rights Council.
