Ottawa — [Canada](country:CA) vowed 'dollar for dollar' tariff retaliation.

Canada sends 70% of exports to the [US](country:US), so retaliation strains its own economy.

New 50% US tariffs took effect Saturday under a Depression-era law, taxing vehicles at every border crossing.

Automakers face no exemption yet, as vehicles cross the border and get taxed repeatedly.
