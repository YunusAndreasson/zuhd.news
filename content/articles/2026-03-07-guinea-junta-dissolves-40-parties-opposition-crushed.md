Guinea's military junta dissolved 40 political parties. Junta leader Mamadi Doumbouya signed an overnight decree dissolving all 3 main opposition parties and 37 others, reversing his 2021 coup promise of democratic transition. With no election timeline announced, 13 million Guineans have no legal route to political change.
