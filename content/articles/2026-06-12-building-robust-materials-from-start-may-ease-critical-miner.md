Berlin — German lab: recyclable design cuts critical mineral dependence.

[Germany](country:DE)'s federal materials research institute argues batteries, turbines and electronics should be designed for recyclability from the start, not built around mined inputs.

[EU](country:EU) critical mineral strategy targets supply diversification; the institute's perspective paper targets design instead.
