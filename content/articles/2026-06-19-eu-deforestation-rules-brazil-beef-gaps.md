Manchester — [Brazil](country:BR)'s beef supply chains can circumvent EU deforestation rules, researchers found.

The EU regulation requires supply chain traceability; Brazilian cattle cross through indirect suppliers where deforestation cannot be verified, researchers found.

The [UK](country:GB) faces identical enforcement gaps if it adopts its own import restriction.
