San Francisco — Meta's Oversight Board received over 750 public submissions on account-disabling practices it said lack due process.

Accounts can be suspended without explanation, leaving harassed users no meaningful recourse under current enforcement rules.

The Board urged binding enforcement rules and an independent appeals process; its policy recommendations are advisory only.
