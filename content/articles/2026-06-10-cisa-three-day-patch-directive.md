Washington — The [US](country:US) Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency now requires federal agencies to patch flagged bugs within 3 days.

The mandate phases in over 180 days; attackers now weaponize disclosed bugs within hours of public release.

Which vulnerability classes trigger the rule and whether waivers apply has not been specified.
