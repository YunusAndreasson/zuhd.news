Mountain View — Mobile-first markets drove Google's traffic past 50% IPv6.

[India](country:IN) and [Vietnam](country:VN) adopted early as IPv4 exhaustion hit mobile carriers; fixed-line networks in richer economies are the long tail.

An Asia-Pacific internet registry puts global capability at 42%, not 50% — reflecting whose traffic is measured.
