Russia's FSB arrested a regional health minister, accusing him of accumulating 57 properties worth $12.1 million across 5 cities while in office. Yevgeny Filippov has led health services in Krasnodar since 2013; his arrest follows prosecution of 2 former deputy governors in the same region over the past year, a pattern that analysts read as factional pressure rather than a systematic anti-corruption drive. Russian officials are typically arrested not when they steal, but when they lose their political protector — leaving other Krasnodar officials exposed.
