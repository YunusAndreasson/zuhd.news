Lawmakers gave Africa's trade deal one more year. The African Growth and Opportunity Act has moved $500 billion in African goods duty-free to the US since 2000, sustaining more than 1 million African jobs and 460,000 American ones. Program architect Rosa Whitaker stepped down as co-chair as advocates push for a permanent, modernized successor agreement.
