Bishkek — 2 Kyrgyz state banks dropped 131 clients.

The move responds to EU concerns about goods re-exported to [Russia](country:RU) through [Kyrgyzstan](country:KG), circumventing Western sanctions.

The excluded companies haven't been publicly named; the move is designed to show Brussels that Bishkek is acting.
