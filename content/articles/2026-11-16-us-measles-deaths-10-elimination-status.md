Pennsylvania — Measles has killed 10 people in the [US](country:US) this year.

At least 4 were infants, and cases are the highest since 1991.

Status is lost when one strain's transmission chain runs 12 months unbroken, and the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention counts nearly 5,500 cases this year.

"We've gotten comfortable letting children be stopped by a deadly disease and die from it," said committee chair Noel Brewer.

The Pan American Health Organization announces its elimination ruling Tuesday, 17 November.
