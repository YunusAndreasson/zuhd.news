Berlin — Organisers of [Germany](country:DE)'s digital summit sidelined civil-society critics of AI.

Digital minister Karsten Wildberger says AI can be the country's comeback.

Industry wants public administration as its market, and Wildberger offers funds for computing power and looser EU AI rules.

Chancellor Friedrich Merz said sovereignty means shaping power, but did not define what that looks like.

The coalition agreement sets 2030 as the deadline for Germany to become an AI nation.
