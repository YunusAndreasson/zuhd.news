Perth — Earth's oldest continental rock is only 4 billion years old.

Curtin University modeling shows thousands of asteroid strikes kept the young crust under 5km thick and molten, melting away everything that formed before, geologist Tim Johnson said.

Impacts eased by 3.5 billion years ago, letting crust thicken to 30km and plate tectonics begin.
