Seoul — Seoul named Shin Hyun-song to manage the won crisis. Shin, a Bank for International Settlements researcher, brings experience in global central bank coordination as the Strait of Hormuz closure cuts off South Korea's near-total oil imports. He replaces outgoing Governor Rhee Chang-yong in April as South Korea weighs its response to the energy shock.
