Tehran — Russian state agency TASS reported [Iran](country:IR)'s proposed Hormuz ship ban.

Every other state's vessels would need a permit from the armed forces General Staff.

Parliament security committee spokesman Hassan Ghashghavi told Tasnim the bill bars ships of [Israel](country:IL), hostile states and any carrying military cargo.

The Majlis has not voted on the bill, and the Guardian Council must review it afterwards.
