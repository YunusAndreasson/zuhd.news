Hasina faces execution as Bangladesh's new parliament convenes. A 2024 uprising killed 1,400 people, ended her 15-year rule, and produced February elections the Bangladesh Nationalist Party won. The parliament's first test is an Iran war oil shock already forcing university closures and fuel rationing, with no relief announced.
