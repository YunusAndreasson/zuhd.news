Manila — Five Southeast Asian states have hosted Min Aung Hlaing this year.

Leaders meeting here from 15 November will review a five-year ban on [Myanmar](country:MM)'s leaders.

The ban rests on Myanmar's failure to implement the Five-Point Consensus, the peace plan agreed after the 2021 coup.

"Political acceptance should follow progress, not precede it," said Simon Tay, chairman of the Singapore Institute of International Affairs.

[Thailand](country:TH) wants Myanmar's foreign minister readmitted to ministerial talks, with a decision by 2028 at the latest.
