Brussels — The European Consumer Organisation filed scam-ad complaints against Google, Meta, and TikTok.

Groups reported 900 scam ads over 4 months; platforms removed 27%; Digital Services Act fines reach 6% of global turnover.

Google and Meta denied the complaints; regulators in 27 European countries must now decide whether to investigate.
