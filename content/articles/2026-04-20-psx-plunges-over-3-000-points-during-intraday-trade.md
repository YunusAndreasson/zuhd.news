Karachi — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s benchmark index fell 1.81% intraday. Traders cited uncertainty over whether [US](country:US)-[Iran](country:IR) talks will proceed in Islamabad, reversing the previous week's 4% recovery. The index sits 15,227 points below its January 2026 high; the Islamabad round has not been scheduled.
