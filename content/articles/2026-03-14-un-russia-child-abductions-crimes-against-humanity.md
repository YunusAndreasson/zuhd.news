The UN declared Russia's child abductions crimes against humanity. A UN human rights commission identified around 1,200 children taken from occupied Ukraine in 2022 alone — placed in Russian families and orphanages, with 80% unreturned. The finding builds on 2023 ICC arrest warrants against Putin for the same abductions, with no prosecution yet.
