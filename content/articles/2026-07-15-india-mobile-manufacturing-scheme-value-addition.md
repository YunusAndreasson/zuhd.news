New Delhi — 99.2% of Indian phones are made at home.

The Cabinet approved a Rs 62,500-crore scheme paying manufacturers extra for sourcing components domestically, pushing [India](country:IN) past assembly toward chips and design.

The five-year program targets Rs 39 lakh crore in output and 60,000 jobs through 2031.
