Mexico City — A new backdoor is hitting Latin American governments.

Citizens' immigration, tax and diplomatic records reportedly sit inside those same government networks.

Researchers dubbed the implant "SparroWocky" and linked it to FamousSparrow, a group tied to [China](country:CN).

Neither the researchers nor named governments have confirmed which agencies were compromised.
