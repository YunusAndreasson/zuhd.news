Cambridge — Drained peatlands drive 2.7% of human-caused warming, researchers estimate.

Peatlands hold 30% of the world's soil carbon on 3% to 4% of its land.

Draining exposes peat to oxygen, so microbes release carbon dioxide for hundreds of years even as methane falls.

The Nature study from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology finds methane suppression does not offset the carbon dioxide released.

Co-author César Terrer says the second priority is restoring drained peatlands by raising their water table.
