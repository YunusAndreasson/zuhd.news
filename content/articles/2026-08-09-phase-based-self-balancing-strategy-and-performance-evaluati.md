Beijing — A new mixer cured concrete to 53.9 MPa strength in 80 seconds.

That is 31.5% stronger than a conventional twin-shaft mixer, engineers report.

A self-balancing gear design lets the machine vibrate at 75 Hz without the structural fatigue that once limited high-frequency mixing.

The design remains a lab prototype; no construction site trial has been reported.
