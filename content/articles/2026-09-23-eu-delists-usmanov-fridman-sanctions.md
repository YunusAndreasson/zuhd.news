Brussels — The EU delisted Alisher Usmanov and Mikhail Fridman Tuesday.

It buys freedom for two billionaires so sanctions on 3,000 others survive three more years.

Renewal required unanimous approval from all 27 states every six months, so [France](country:FR) and Slovakia held the list hostage until Latvia yielded, winning a pledge of extra French troops in return.

Latvia alone has pledged national sanctions on both men, with no other EU state following yet.
