Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) says 2 supertankers carrying 4.5 million barrels hit mines.

Iran's Revolutionary Guards say ships taking an unauthorized southern route through the strait risk mines.

Fires disabled both engine rooms and crews abandoned ship, the Guards said, and no one has independently verified it.

A [US](country:US) Central Command spokesperson said forces cleared the shipping lanes of mines and 95% of vessels get through.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 2 November an 11% chance, up 4 points in a week.
