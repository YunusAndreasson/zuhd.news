Paranal — A planet 100 times fainter, finally caught.

Astronomers spotted Beta Pictoris d with the Very Large Telescope, then found its trace in a decade of archived images.

Direct imaging, unlike transit dips, captures a planet's own light — the method needed to study exoplanet atmospheres.
