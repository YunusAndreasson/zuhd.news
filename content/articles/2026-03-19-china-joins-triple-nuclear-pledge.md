Beijing — China joined a global pledge to triple nuclear power capacity by 2050. Beijing signed after the Hormuz crisis exposed its dependence on Middle Eastern oil — the government had pre-positioned 1.4 billion barrels as a buffer while accelerating domestic nuclear investment. Analysts expect China to miss the 2030 target of 110 GW nuclear capacity.
