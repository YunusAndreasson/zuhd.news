Itanagar — Amit Shah launched 199 border projects as dam protests continue.

Shah says people are returning to all 40 villages along the 1,126-km [China](country:CN) border.

He ordered the army and Indo-Tibetan Border Police to buy produce from village cooperatives, not from Tezpur or Guwahati.

Union Minister Kiren Rijiju said land-ownership limits deter investors and called for a balance with indigenous rights.

The proposed dam remains the open question, and neither minister's reported remarks addressed it.
