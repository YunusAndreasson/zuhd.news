Pucallpa — Groups count 35 to 57 Indigenous defenders murdered in [Peru](country:PE).

Pope Leo XIV demanded justice for them Sunday before a largely Indigenous crowd.

Illegal logging and mining press Amazon communities, and Peru lost about 200,000 hectares of forest in 2025, Global Nature Watch says.

Courts jailed four people for up to 35 years in August for the 2023 killing of leader Quinto Inuma Alvarado.

Leo left for Rome Monday, and Indigenous leader Fernando Carteiro hopes his message reaches the world.
