London — [Britain](country:GB)'s Ofcom fined Virgin Media O2 £28 million.

The telecom split its retention staff into tiers so only senior agents could process cancellations, dropping calls to delay switches.

Ofcom called it its largest-ever consumer-protection penalty, its second against Virgin Media since 2018.
