Beijing — China's trade surplus is not just overcapacity, Chen Deming says.

The former commerce minister urged Beijing to secure investment agreements with other countries.

Chen gave [China](country:CN)'s responsibility as a major country as the first reason firms should invest abroad, sharing know-how.

The transcript comes from a Center for China and Globalization forum recording, which Chen has not reviewed.
