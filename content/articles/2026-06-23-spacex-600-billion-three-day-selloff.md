New York — SpaceX shares fell 16% Monday following a $20 billion bond offering announcement, JonesTrading said.

The company floated only 4.2% of shares at IPO; 3 days of selling erased $600 billion in market value.

Shares remain 15% above the $135 offer price, still placing SpaceX sixth-largest globally.
