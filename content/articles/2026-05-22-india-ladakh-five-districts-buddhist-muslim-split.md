Leh — [India](country:IN) created 5 new districts in Ladakh.

Observers say the reorganization is designed to separate Buddhist and Muslim communities into administrative silos, breaking their solidarity against central control.

Ladakh has had no elected legislature since India revoked Article 370 in 2019; no restoration date has been set.
