Tehran — Parliament approved a sanctioned missile builder as defence minister.

Mehrdad Akhlaghi Ketabchi replaces Aziz Nasirzadeh, killed on the first day of the US-Israel war.

He led [Iran](country:IR)'s Aerospace Industries Organization and now must rebuild the military-industrial base US and Israeli strikes targeted.

"The US president must think of getting his forces out of our region in one piece," Akhlaghi told reporters.

Trump this month threatened a heavy-bomb strike on Iran's nuclear and energy infrastructure.
