Miami — Immigration and Customs Enforcement custody deaths topped the pandemic peak.

Detainees now face less medical oversight than at any point on record.

[US](country:US) detention rose 77% in a year while deaths in ICE custody tripled, researchers found.

One death report took ICE a full year to publish, despite its own 30-day rule.
