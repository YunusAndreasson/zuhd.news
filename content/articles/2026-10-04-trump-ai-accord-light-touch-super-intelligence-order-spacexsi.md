Washington — Six executives signed a one-page accord with no legal force.

Congress has passed no legislation setting guardrails on the AI industry.

The accord says companies should set internal risk controls and hire an external auditor, while the Federal Trade Commission plans to compel testimony.

Anthony Aguirre of the Future of Life Institute says the industry cannot be trusted to regulate itself.

Trump says he will name an AI czar within a week, and Musk says SpaceXAI becomes SpaceXSI.
