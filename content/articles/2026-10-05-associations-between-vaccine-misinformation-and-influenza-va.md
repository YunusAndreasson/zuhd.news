Zhejiang — Weekly flu vaccinations flipped from 7.3% growth to 9.0% decline.

[China](country:CN)'s records, not surveys, show uptake falling after two vaccine-safety events, npj Digital Public Health reports.

The paper compares weekly trends before and after three misinformation events, finding Sinovac rumours slowed growth from 4.8% to 1.8% weekly.

The authors say they lack individual-level exposure data, so the association cannot prove cause.

The authors urge health systems to build early-warning and rapid-response communication before each vaccination season.
