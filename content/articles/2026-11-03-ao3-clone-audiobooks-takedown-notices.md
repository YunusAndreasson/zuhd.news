United States — ao3.life received 2,000 takedown requests in a single day.

It sold AI-narrated audiobooks of fanfiction without asking the authors.

The creator says AI agents on 40 virtual machines scraped works while Archive of Our Own sat offline.

Authors who had archive-locked their works reported them appearing anyway.

Archive of Our Own's owner, the Organization for Transformative Works, expects up to four weeks of outage.
