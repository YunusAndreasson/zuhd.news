Stanley — [Argentina](country:AR) gave [Britain](country:GB) 2 weeks to halt Falklands' oil project.

The $2.2bn project could triple the territory's economy and end its dependence on fishing.

Argentina cannot contest sovereignty by force, so it restricts flights, shipping and suppliers to squeeze the islands' economy instead.

"We're going to take matters into our own hands," said Argentina's President Javier Milei.

Argentina's Congress is still reviewing a tougher sanctions bill targeting the oilfield's foreign suppliers.
