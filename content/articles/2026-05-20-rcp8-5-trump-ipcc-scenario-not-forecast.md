Washington — Trump called IPCC's stress-test scenario a disproven forecast.

RCP8.5 is a reference pathway, not a forecast; the world remains on course for 2.5–3°C warming, scenario authors confirmed.

New climate scenarios no longer include RCP8.5 but show limiting warming to 1.5°C now requires significant overshoot.
