Moscow — Putin approved 4 new deputy prime ministers, Siluanov among them.

Siluanov keeps the finance portfolio of [Russia](country:RU), held since 2011, while joining the deputy premiership.

Nearly half of Mishustin's deputies change, independent journalist Farida Rustamova said, including government chief of staff Dmitry Grigorenko.

Mishustin's spokesman said Grigorenko requested the transfer himself.

The State Duma must still schedule confirmation votes on every nominee.
