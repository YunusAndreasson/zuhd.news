Eindhoven — Researchers moved energy millimetres without radiating it away.

Near-field transfer was assumed to die within nanometres; vibrating gold nanorods channel energy without the radiative leakage that caps efficiency.

The result pushes a nanometre-range constraint to millimetres, rewriting limits for chip photonics and energy harvesting.
