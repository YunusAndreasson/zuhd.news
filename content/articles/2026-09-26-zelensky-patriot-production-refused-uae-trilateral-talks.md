Kyiv — Trump declined to confirm Zelensky's Patriot licence claim.

A licence gives [Ukraine](country:UA) no Patriots this winter, as production takes at least a year.

Few weapons stop Russian ballistic missiles, and Patriot supplies have run short since the Iran war began on 28 February.

Zelensky said Trump told him Ukraine "will receive a license to manufacture Patriot missiles."

Zelensky says the [US](country:US) proposed technical talks with [Russia](country:RU) in the UAE, and Kyiv awaits a date.
