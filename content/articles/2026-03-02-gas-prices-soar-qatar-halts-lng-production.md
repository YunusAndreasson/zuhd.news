European gas prices nearly doubled Monday. QatarEnergy, the world's largest liquefied natural gas producer, halted all output after Iranian strikes, cutting Europe's main alternative to Russian pipeline gas. Oil prices hit $82 a barrel with the Strait of Hormuz still blocked and no restart date announced.
