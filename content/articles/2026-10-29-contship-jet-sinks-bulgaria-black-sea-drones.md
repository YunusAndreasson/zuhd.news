Sofia — All 18 crew survived 3 drone hits on the Contship Jet.

It was the fifth drone attack in NATO member [Bulgaria](country:BG)'s exclusive economic zone this month.

The Greek-managed ship carried hazardous cargo, including lithium-ion batteries, from Haifa to Constanța, and navy samples found no pollution.

[Russia](country:RU) has not claimed the attacks, and Prime Minister Rumen Radev said "we cannot prove it."

Bulgaria, Romania and Turkey agreed to share more intelligence, but none has named who launched the drones.
