Washington — The man who wrote the 39% cap into law says the FCC can't erase it.

Tom DeLay says Congress, not the agency, set the limit on how many households one broadcaster may reach; the FCC votes Thursday to repeal it anyway.

Litigation is expected to test whether an agency may undo a statute by rulemaking.
