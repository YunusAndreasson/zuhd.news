Paris — Raphaël Glucksmann won 66% of the primary.

The Socialist Party will field no candidate of its own for the first time since 1969.

Jean-Luc Mélenchon's party boycotted the primary, and Glucksmann refuses to work with it in [France](country:FR).

Polls put Marine Le Pen above 30%, Mélenchon near 15% and Glucksmann at 9% to 14%.

Glucksmann now seeks to unify the left before the first round in April 2027.
