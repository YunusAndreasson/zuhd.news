Islamabad — Energas asked Pakistan's prime minister to refund $2 million.

[Pakistan](country:PK) loses planned import capacity while the Hormuz closure has suspended [Qatar](country:QA)'s liquefied natural gas production.

Energas says the government's March 2025 energy study found no new gas terminals necessary for five years.

The Port Qasim Authority says the $2 million is a forfeitable concession fee, not a refundable advance.

The authority will cancel the letter of intent unless it gets unconditional acceptance by 30 November.
