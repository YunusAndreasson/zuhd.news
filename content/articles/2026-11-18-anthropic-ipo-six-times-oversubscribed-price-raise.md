New York — Anthropic's share sale could price at $83-85 a share.

That values the company near $1.5 trillion, above the $1.4 trillion target, Anthropic's advisers told investors.

Orders reached at least 6 times the 825 million shares offered, so Anthropic can raise the price or add shares.

Pricing is due 19 November, with Google and Nvidia each buying $10 billion in a parallel placement.
