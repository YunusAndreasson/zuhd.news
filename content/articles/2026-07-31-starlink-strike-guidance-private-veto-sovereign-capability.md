Washington — One businessman can veto Ukrainian strikes inside Russia.

Volodymyr Zelenskyy asked Donald Trump to secure Elon Musk's permission for Starlink to guide targeting there; [Ukraine](country:UA) has no substitute constellation at that scale.

Musk has not responded publicly; [Russia](country:RU)'s intelligence services reportedly suspect an anti-satellite weapon is being built against Starlink.
