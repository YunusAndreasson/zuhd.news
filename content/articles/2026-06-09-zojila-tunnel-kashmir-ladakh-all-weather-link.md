Ganderbal — [India](country:IN) opened year-round road link to Ladakh.

The 13km Zojila tunnel bypasses a pass snow-blocked half the year, securing military supply lines to the Chinese and Pakistani frontiers.

Until a second tunnel at Shinku La opens, this route handles all winter military traffic to the region.
