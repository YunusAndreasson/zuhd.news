Lahore — Punjab launched free cardiac surgery for Pakistan's poor. The Rs3 billion annual programme covers all adult cardiac procedures at Rs1 million per patient across 9 public and 15 private hospitals in the province. Whether Rs3 billion a year covers the province's cardiac surgery backlog will determine how many Pakistanis reach the operating table.
