Tehran — Zero vessels transited Hormuz on Sunday, Kpler data show.

A fifth of the world's oil moves through this strait, mostly to Asian buyers.

Insurers and charterers stopped fixing tonnage after tanker attacks, so the strait closed commercially before any naval blockade.

[Iran](country:IR)'s Araghchi says Washington must meet Tehran's conditions before shipping resumes.
