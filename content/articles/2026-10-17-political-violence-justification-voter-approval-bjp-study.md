Göttingen — Bharatiya Janata Party supporters disliked peaceful responses to justified violence.

Framing violence as a response to out-group threat raised approval of the violence itself.

Ursula Daxecker and Neeraj Prasad tested violence presented as a justified response to threat or injustice from an out-group.

Legitimizing violence also raised approval of the parties involved, the study found.
