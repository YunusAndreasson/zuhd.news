Athens — Binance withdrew its licensing bid days before a deadline.

The exchange pulled its application under the Markets in Crypto-Assets regime after months of talks, forcing it to suspend some services across the bloc.

Binance says it remains committed to a new license as regulators weigh excluding its largest exchange.
