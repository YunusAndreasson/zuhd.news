Riyadh — OPEC+ left quotas unchanged as Brent nears $96.

A quota only limits producers who have spare capacity to exceed it.

Gulf war outages already limit supply, and prices are up 10% in a month.

Oil-importing states from [Pakistan](country:PK) to Kenya absorb the war-driven price and cannot negotiate it.
