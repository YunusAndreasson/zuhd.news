Britain sanctioned him in December 2025, saying Rostec funds him and he works with Russian intelligence officers.

Rybar says it broke no Montenegrin law and expects all five detainees released.

[Montenegro](country:ME)'s Special State Prosecutor's Office has not said what charges the five face.
