Tanegashima — A Japanese probe lifts off Tuesday to fetch Phobos dirt.

The samples could settle whether Mars's moons are captured asteroids or debris from a giant impact.

Circular orbits above Mars's equator point to an impact, while dark red surfaces resemble captured D-type asteroids.

"This will be a first-time activity, which is very complex," said Yasuhiro Kawakatsu, project manager at Japan's space agency.

[Japan](country:JP)'s Martian Moons eXploration probe reaches Mars orbit in August 2027, lands on Phobos in 2030 and returns samples in 2031.
