Gaborone — Criminal networks strip Southern Africa's cobalt through porous borders. UNODC trained Southern African law enforcement and prosecutors in Gaborone; Japan funded the workshop because its clean energy industry depends on regional cobalt and lithium. The SADC Ministerial Committee must approve the action plan before regional enforcement can begin.
