New Delhi — Police detained 260 people, including Rahul and Priyanka Gandhi, Tuesday.

The opposition wants [India](country:IN)'s Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar removed over the voter-roll revision.

Police filed a first information report after a complaint that MPs entered Akashvani Bhawan without permission once barricades stopped their march.

The Election Commission rejects the allegations and says its electoral-roll revision is lawful.

The Aam Aadmi Party joins a new protest outside Delhi's chief electoral officer's office on Wednesday.
