Chennai — Stalin demands Tamil Nadu's 7.18% Lok Sabha share never fall.

[India](country:IN)'s government may reintroduce a constitutional amendment on delimitation when Parliament sits 30 November.

Stalin also wants each state's share, set by the 1971 Census, frozen for another 25 years.

Congress MP Manoj Kumar says some DMK MPs told him they could back the government on conditions.

Mallikarjun Kharge hosts opposition floor leaders at 11:30am on 28 November, with the DMK invited.
