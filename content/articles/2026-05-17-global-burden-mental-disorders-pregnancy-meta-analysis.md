London — Mental illness strikes 1 in 3 pregnant women.

Intimate partner violence triples the risk, a meta-analysis found; prevalence ranges from 22% in Asia to 40% in South America.

Most low-income maternal health systems lack routine mental health screening — where the burden is highest.
