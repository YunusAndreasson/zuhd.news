Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK) has recorded 7 wild polio cases this year.

It is one of two countries, with [Afghanistan](country:AF), where wild poliovirus remains endemic.

Five cases are in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and two in Sindh, the Pakistan Medical Association said, which it reads as ongoing local transmission.

Attackers killed 2 police constables guarding a polio team in Balochistan in September, the association said.

The association demands provinces place vaccination teams under high-security protection.
