A US-Israeli airstrike killed Supreme Leader Khamenei Saturday. The strike also killed Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps commander and defense minister, 2 days after nuclear talks with Washington had reported progress in Geneva. Iran's Assembly of Experts must now convene to name a successor, with a planned Vienna nuclear follow-up now in doubt.
