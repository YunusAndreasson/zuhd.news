Paris — EV costs fell globally, rose in the [US](country:US).

1 in 4 cars sold globally is now electric; [US](country:US) tariffs on [Chinese](country:CN) models and cut subsidies kept domestic prices elevated.

China supplies the cheap models that drove global price declines, the International Energy Agency said; markets blocked from them miss the drop.
