Kasibu — More than 30 cases target anti-mining leaders here.

Land defenders, not the mining firms, are the ones now facing prison.

Law requires Indigenous consent before mining, but companies fund and the state runs the process certifying it.

Zesirie Enggo and other named leaders await trial while barricades still block the mine roads.
