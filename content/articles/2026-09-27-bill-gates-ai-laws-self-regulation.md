United States — Bill Gates says AI needs laws, not company promises.

Voluntary safety pledges have no enforcement when a lab's incentives point elsewhere.

Gates wants Congress to require monitoring of AI systems as a legal condition, not a voluntary company choice.

President Donald Trump and other opponents say stricter rules could hand [China](country:CN) an edge in the AI race.

The [US](country:US) and China agreed September 26 to open a new AI dialogue at the government level.
