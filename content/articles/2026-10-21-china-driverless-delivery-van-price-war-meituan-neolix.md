China — [China](country:CN)'s Neolix announced nearly $400 million for driverless vans.

Total funding now exceeds 10 billion yuan, with an Alibaba-affiliated fund joining the round.

Industry sources say vans stall at gates and absent customers, so Meituan shifted to short branch-line transport.

A person close to Meituan said van-courier trials in dense districts ran slower than couriers alone.

Meituan has pushed this year's promotion reviews to year-end so staff can count its new van launch.
