It reverses Trump's rejection of an Iran deal days earlier, reopening nuclear negotiations.

Iran's state-run Mehr News Agency said Araghchi met Qatari mediators in New York over the US message exchange.

Iranian officials have repeatedly said they are not seeking a nuclear weapon.

Trump gave no timetable, and the Strait of Hormuz stays contested territory as the talks continue.
