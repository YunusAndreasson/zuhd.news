Cairo — Nile island residents blocked cement trucks for days.

The state denies residents repair permits while building towers meant to replace them.

Security officials, not a municipality or court, negotiated the truce, showing who controls Cairo's Nile-front land.

Residents meet Friday to decide whether to resume the sit-in.
