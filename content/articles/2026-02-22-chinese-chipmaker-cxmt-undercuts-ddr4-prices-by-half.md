CXMT sells DDR4 chips at half the market price. The state-subsidized Chinese manufacturer is undercutting Samsung and SK Hynix, whose legacy DRAM still accounts for over half their production capacity. HP and Dell are testing CXMT products as Korean firms shift to next-generation chips.
