Gaza City — One woman buried 10 relatives recovered after 3 years.

Israeli bombing killed the 75 inside their homes early in the war, Civil Defence said.

Euro-Med estimates 68 million tonnes of rubble cover Gaza, and [Israel](country:IL) still blocks heavy machinery despite the October 2025 ceasefire.

"The rubble formed a barrier between us and giving the dead a proper farewell," said Kamal Hijazi, who lost relatives.

Civil Defence believes 8,000 more people lie under the rubble and it lacks heavy machinery to reach them.
