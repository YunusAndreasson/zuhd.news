Seoul — Users 'buy' $44,860 watches that never ship.

It substitutes for real spending in [South Korea](country:KR), squeezed by debt and job scarcity.

Infinite scroll, wishlists and delivery tracking are variable-reward designs where anticipation itself, not the purchase, is the payoff.

Platforms have not said whether the trend helps or hurts actual sales.
