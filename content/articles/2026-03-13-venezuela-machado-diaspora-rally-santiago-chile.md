17,000 Venezuelans rallied in central Santiago, Chile on Thursday. Chile hosts 600,000 Venezuelan migrants — South America's largest diaspora — giving Venezuelan opposition leader María Corina Machado a base to organize from exile. Machado left Venezuela in late 2025 and said her return would be coordinated with international allies, naming no date.
