Addis Ababa — Amhara and Tigray forces are allies now.

It unites two armed movements capable of unseating [Ethiopia](country:ET)'s government.

Ethiopia's ethnic-federal system ties territory to identity, so former enemies over Welkait and Raya can regroup as partners.

Tigray's regional government has warned the fighting could widen into "another catastrophic war."
