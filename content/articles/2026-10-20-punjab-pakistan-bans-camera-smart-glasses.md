Lahore — Punjab's ban covers disguised pens and keychains with hidden cameras.

The 30-day order cites privacy risks to women, children, airports, courts and exam centres.

The Home Department issued it under section 144 of the 1898 Code of Criminal Procedure, and law enforcement personnel are exempt.

Meta has not officially listed [Pakistan](country:PK) as a market, yet a petitioner's counsel says its glasses sell openly in Lahore.

The Lahore High Court told the federal government to send the cabinet a summary on unregulated imports.
