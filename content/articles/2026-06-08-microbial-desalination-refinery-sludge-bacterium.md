Isfahan — Refinery-waste bacteria desalinated brine and produced bioplastic precursors.

The strain removed 70-78% of minerals from refinery brine in 15 days while simultaneously generating electricity, the study found.

Researchers said the process suits water-stressed oil-producing regions but gave no field trial timeline.
