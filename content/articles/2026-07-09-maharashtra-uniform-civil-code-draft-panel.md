Mumbai — A single code could replace separate Muslim, Christian and tribal family law.

Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis gave a 7-member panel led by retired judge Ranjana Desai six months to draft rules on marriage, inheritance and adoption for [India](country:IN)'s second-most-populous state.

Fadnavis wants the bill introduced at Nagpur's winter legislative session.
