Tokyo — The Bank of Japan's 1.25% rate is a 31-year high.

[Japan](country:JP) is easing household costs with a food tax cut while its central bank fights inflation.

Finance Minister Satsuki Katayama told parliament import-cost inflation is nearing a shift to wage-driven inflation.

Katayama conceded tax cuts generally push inflation up, but says she sees no gap with the central bank.

The Bank of Japan decides on rates on 30 October.
