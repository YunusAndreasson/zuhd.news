Washington — The [US](country:US) Mint sells a $1 Trump coin.

It is the first living president on US currency in a century.

A 1866 law bars deceased-only portraits, but a 2020 anniversary statute permits new $1 coin designs.

Bessent says a $250 Trump banknote is also in preparation, pending congressional approval.
