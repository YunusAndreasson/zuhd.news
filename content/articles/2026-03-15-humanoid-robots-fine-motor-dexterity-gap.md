Boston Dynamics' Atlas can't reliably climb stairs in 2026. Despite advances in large language models, motors, and computer vision, former Boston Dynamics researcher Scott Kuindersma says variable terrain remains unsolved. If Atlas can't navigate stairs, Tesla's timeline to deploy Optimus in homes is further off than advertised.
