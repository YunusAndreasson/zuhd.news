Kuala Lumpur — Siti Hasmah, [Malaysia](country:MY)'s former first lady, has died at 100.

Her death closes a chapter for Malaysia's founding political generation, leaving Mahathir, 101, alone.

She trained as one of the first Malay women doctors and led rural maternal and public-health programs for decades.

Malaysia's government has not yet announced whether it will hold a state funeral.
