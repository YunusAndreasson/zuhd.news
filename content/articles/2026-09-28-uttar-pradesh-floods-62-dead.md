Lucknow — At least 62 people have died in [India](country:IN)'s Uttar Pradesh floods.

More than 1,000 homes were damaged, mostly among rural families with the least ability to rebuild.

Three days of rain from a Bay of Bengal weather system struck as the monsoon should already be withdrawing.

Meteorologists expect rain to ease Monday, but forecast light rain in parts of the state through Tuesday.
