Redmond — Chinese state hackers now run an AI intrusion tool at machine speed.

Microsoft says Salt Typhoon and Crystal Typhoon use Artex, as do North Korean groups, and a Chinese company sells access.

Artex lets operators set goals and AI agents move from reconnaissance to privileged access in minutes, Microsoft said.

Microsoft adds that Artex introduces no new techniques, so defenders can spot its bursts of machine-speed requests.

Microsoft's report followed press reports of Artex attacks on the Central Intelligence Agency and two other agencies.
