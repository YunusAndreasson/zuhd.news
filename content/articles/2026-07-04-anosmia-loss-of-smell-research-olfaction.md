San Francisco — Up to 22% of people live with impaired smell, researchers estimate.

Covid's mass smell loss finally drew research funding, revealing olfactory neurons regenerate after weeks and that smell loss can precede Parkinson's by years.

Scientists are now testing whether repeated scent training can rewire recovery in lasting anosmia cases.
