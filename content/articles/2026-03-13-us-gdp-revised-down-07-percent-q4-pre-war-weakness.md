US Q4 growth was halved to 0.7%. The revision means the US entered the Iran war with an economy already stalling — before Hormuz fuel costs began driving inflation higher. The Federal Reserve now faces a dilemma: raise rates to fight oil-driven inflation, or hold and risk deeper contraction.
