10 US F-16s triggered a standoff with Chinese fighters over the Yellow Sea. United States Forces Korea flew large-scale drills from Osan Air Base on Feb 18-19 without notifying Seoul, prompting China to scramble jets into overlapping air defence zones. Defence Minister Ahn Gyu-back demanded an explanation, and commander Xavier Brunson apologized and halted the exercise early.
