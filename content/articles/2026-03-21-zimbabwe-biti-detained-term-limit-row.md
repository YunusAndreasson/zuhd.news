Harare — Zimbabwe police arrested former Finance Minister Tendai Biti Saturday. Zimbabwean President Emmerson Mnangagwa, due to step down in 2028, is pushing legislation extending terms to 7 years and handing presidential selection to parliament rather than voters. Constitutional experts say any term-limit change requires a public referendum, and legal challenges are expected.
