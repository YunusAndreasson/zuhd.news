source: "Nature"

category: "science"

Nigeria girls' marriage rate fell from 86% to 21%. A randomised trial in northern Nigeria found the reduction required all 3 interventions simultaneously: cash transfers to families, school enrollment support, and mentoring. The UN's 2030 child marriage target now has a proven method — if governments fund all 3 components together.
