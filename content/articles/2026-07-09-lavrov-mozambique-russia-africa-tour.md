Maputo — Sergey Lavrov met Mozambique's foreign minister, Maria Manuela dos Santos Lucas.

The [Russian](country:RU) minister's Africa tour has stopped in Ethiopia and Niger before Mozambique, a year after his last talks with dos Santos Lucas in Moscow.

The tour continues a push for diplomatic and security ties across the continent.
