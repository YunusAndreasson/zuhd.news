Bengaluru — Attackers could forge certificates for websites they do not control.

[India](country:IN)'s national cybersecurity agency warned the compromised keys also enable impersonation and interception of encrypted traffic.

eMudhra said a flaw created weak keys alongside the strong originals, on 8 intermediate certificates, and its Indian signature business is unaffected.

The IT Ministry says the impact on Indian sites is very limited and accuses some accounts of fear-mongering.

The ministry has formed a special team to probe the incident.
