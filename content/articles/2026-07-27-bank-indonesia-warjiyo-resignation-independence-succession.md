Jakarta — Perry Warjiyo quit mid-term, no reason given.

Bank Indonesia's law gives the governor a fixed term and named removal grounds; a president can only accept a resignation, not order one.

Deputy Destry Damayanti is acting governor; [Indonesia](country:ID)'s president now picks a permanent successor.
