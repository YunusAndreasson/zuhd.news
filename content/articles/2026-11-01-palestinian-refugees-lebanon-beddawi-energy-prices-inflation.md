Tripoli — Osama Najjar closed his aluminium shop in 2024.

Palestinians in [Lebanon](country:LB) are barred from medicine, law, engineering and pharmacy, so shops are lifelines.

The state provides a few hours of electricity a day, and the World Bank projects 17.5% inflation in 2026.

The UN agency for Palestinian refugees expects a deficit of at least $200 million between the end of 2026 and early 2027.
