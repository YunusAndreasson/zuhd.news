GLP-1 drugs halved addiction deaths in a 600,000-person study. The drugs mimic a hormone governing reward pathways — the same brain circuits that drive drug cravings as well as appetite. Randomized controlled trials are still needed; pharmaceutical companies already struggle to meet global demand for the drugs.
