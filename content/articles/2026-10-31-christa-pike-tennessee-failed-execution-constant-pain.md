Nashville — Two lethal doses on September 30 failed to kill Christa Pike.

Pike, who killed Colleen Slemmer in 1995, still faces death and lives in constant pain, she says.

Her lawyers say the drug pooled in her arms instead of entering her bloodstream, leaving them burned and blistered.

"I'm deeply hurt by the incompetence that has put so many people in this terrible position," Pike said.

Governor Bill Lee suspects her sentence will stand unless courts decide otherwise, and an independent review is pending.
