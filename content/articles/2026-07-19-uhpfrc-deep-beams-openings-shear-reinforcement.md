London — An unreinforced opening cut shear capacity 40%.

Engineers tested ultra-high-performance fiber concrete beams with holes for ducts, finding diagonal cross-bar reinforcement recovered 79% of solid-beam strength.

The technique lets architects thread service openings through load-bearing beams without bulking up the structure.
