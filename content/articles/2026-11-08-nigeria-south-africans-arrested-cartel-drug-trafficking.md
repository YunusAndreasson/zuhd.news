Abuja — A South African mother got 25 years in Nigeria for heroin.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s drug agency says South Africans now help cartels move drugs, reversing a South African claim about Nigerians.

Couriers from Cambodia and Thailand route through Doha, and one arrested with 36.65kg of cocaine was promised R20,000, the agency said.

[South Africa](country:ZA)'s government says more than 500 Nigerians passed through its criminal courts by 7 July.

Nigeria's parliament banned official visits to South Africa in September, and neither government has dropped its claim.
