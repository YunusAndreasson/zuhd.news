Islamabad — Women and children now lead [Pakistan](country:PK)'s HIV caseload.

Unsafe medical practices — not sexual transmission — are the primary vector, health experts warned.

Health experts called for mandatory clinical audits; [Pakistan](country:PK)'s government has not announced a reform timeline.
