Karim Lamido — Between 57 and 60 people died in a market attack.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s House heard the district has no access road, so security forces arrive after attackers leave.

Gunmen killed 10 at Binnari market on 23 October, and others jumped into a river, lawmaker Mohammed Audu said.

"They will leave, and then we start reacting. It's like medicine after death," said Minority Leader Fred Agbedi.

Three House committees now weigh the abandoned Wase-Karim Lamido road and relief for survivors.
