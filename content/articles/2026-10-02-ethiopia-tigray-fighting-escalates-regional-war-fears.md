Mekelle — Tigrayan fighters seized 3 federal-held airports on 23 September.

[Eritrea](country:ER) and [Ethiopia](country:ET) expelled each other's diplomats Thursday, raising fears of a regional war.

Eritrea has moved toward Tigray to block Ethiopia's Red Sea ambitions, and its allies Egypt and Saudi Arabia face Ethiopia's close partner, the United Arab Emirates.

Tigrayan leaders say the African Union, which is mediating, favors the federal government.

Analysts warn a renewed war could merge with Sudan's civil war along the shared border.
