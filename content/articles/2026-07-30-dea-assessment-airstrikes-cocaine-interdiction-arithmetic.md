Washington — Airstrikes haven't cut cocaine's price or purity.

The US Drug Enforcement Administration and Pentagon's own analysis found the campaign hasn't reduced cocaine reaching the [United States](country:US), only pushed traffickers to new routes.

Smugglers have shifted to Guyana, the Amazon and semi-submersibles as the strikes continue killing crews.
