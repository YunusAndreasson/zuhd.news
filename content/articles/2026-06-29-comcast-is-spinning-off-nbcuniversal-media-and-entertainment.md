Philadelphia — Comcast is shedding its 15-year Hollywood bet.

NBC, Telemundo, Peacock and Universal move to a new listed company; Comcast retains broadband and wireless as cable TV subscribers declined.

Comcast had explored a Warner Bros. Discovery merger before choosing the spinoff to avoid additional debt.
