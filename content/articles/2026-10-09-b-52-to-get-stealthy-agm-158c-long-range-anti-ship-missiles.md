Washington — The [US](country:US) Navy plans stealthy anti-ship missiles for B-52 bombers.

It adds a long-range maritime strike option against adversary navies, especially in the Indo-Pacific.

Lockheed Martin would integrate a missile sized like the cruise missiles a B-52 already carries, up to 20.

The October 7 notice gives no schedule and no missile count, and no contract is signed yet.
