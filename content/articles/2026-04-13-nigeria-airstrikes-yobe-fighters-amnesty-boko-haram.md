Yobe — Over 100 died in Nigeria's Jilli airstrike. Nigeria's 17-year counterinsurgency has repeatedly killed civilians in disputed strikes; Amnesty and local witnesses say Saturday's dead were traders at Jilli market, not ISWAP fighters. Amnesty called for an inquiry; neither the military nor government has confirmed opening one.
