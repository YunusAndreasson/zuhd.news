Churches pay no property tax, even on rented buildings, and face little oversight.

Federal Police intercepted Pastor André Valadão proposing a fund that pools congregations' rent to buy properties leased back to churches.

Jurist Francisco Tenório says some leaders hide luxury assets in a church's name to evade taxes.

Lagoinha already lists 14 properties worth R$38.2 million, and the messages reopened Brazil's immunity debate.
