Cupertino — Apple's M6 MacBook Pro claims 24 hours of battery, from $1,999.

Apple says the M6 runs on-device AI tasks up to 25% faster than the M5.

Apple says graphics gain up to 30% over the M5, and the iMac's on-device AI runs 5 times faster than the M4's.

Apple's 28 October event is expected to bring its first touchscreen MacBook Pro.
