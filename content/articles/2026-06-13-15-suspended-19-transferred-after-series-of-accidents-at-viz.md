Visakhapatnam — 9 workers died when a ladle fell.

[India](country:IN)'s state-owned Rashtriya Ispat Nigam steel plant suspended 15 employees and transferred 19 after inspections found operational lapses and alleged substandard materials.

A Steel Ministry expert committee is investigating; further suspensions are expected as the inquiry continues.
