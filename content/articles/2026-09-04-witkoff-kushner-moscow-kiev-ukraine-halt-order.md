Kyiv — [Ukraine](country:UA) ordered a battlefield halt, Sept. 5-8.

The three-day pause is Kyiv's biggest concession yet to US-led mediation.

Witkoff and Kushner, Trump's envoys with no diplomatic portfolio, go to [Russia](country:RU) before Kyiv, their first-ever Ukraine trip.

Zelensky pledged no Ukrainian strikes on Russia during the envoys' Sept. 6-8 visit.
