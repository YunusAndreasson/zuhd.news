Cape Town — BPaLM outpaced standard drug-resistant TB therapy by 20%.

Drug-resistant TB needed months of injections; BPaLM's oral format improves access in high-burden settings like [South Africa](country:ZA), the TB-PRACTECAL trial found.

Findings reinforce WHO's recommendation for BPaLM as preferred adult regimen, the study said.
