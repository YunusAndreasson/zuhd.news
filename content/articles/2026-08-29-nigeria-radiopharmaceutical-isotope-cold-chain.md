Lagos — Cancer isotopes decay within hours of production.

That decay forces patients to fly abroad for scans a domestic reactor could otherwise supply.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s health minister called for reactors, fast customs and an unbroken cold chain, not just funding, to build the therapy locally.

The health ministry seeks international partners to build that supply chain next.
