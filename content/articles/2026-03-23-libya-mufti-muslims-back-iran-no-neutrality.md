Tripoli — Libya's grand mufti called supporting Iran a religious duty. Most Muslim-majority governments have backed the US-Israeli campaign or stayed silent, making al-Ghariani's declaration the first from a national religious authority. Saudi state clerics must now reconcile his ruling with Riyadh's public backing for the campaign.
