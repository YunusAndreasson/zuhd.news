Dilling — Two marked UN food-aid trucks were destroyed nearby.

The strike cut rations for 5,800 people in a besieged region for a month.

The trucks carried UN banners and flags, the marking meant to protect convoys from attack.

The World Food Programme says this is the eighth aerial attack on its [Sudan](country:SD) operations this year.
