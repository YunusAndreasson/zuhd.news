Lagos — Uber shut down in [Nigeria](country:NG) with no notice.

Thousands of driver-contractors lose income overnight, with vehicle loans still outstanding.

Nigeria and [Uganda](country:UG) drivers work as contractors, so Uber can exit without severance or notice.

Uber's help centre closes September 23, after 12 years operating in Lagos.
