Geneva — Europium-doped nickelates regain superconductivity after losing it.

Europium ions introduce paramagnetism that suppresses, then restores, superconductivity — mapping how magnetism and superconductivity compete in nickelates.

Nickelates are a candidate for high-temperature superconductivity; the finding maps how re-entrant phases arise.
