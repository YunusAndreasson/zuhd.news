Astana — Turnout reached 72.15%, an 18-point jump from 2023.

A single chamber now holds all law-making power once split between two houses.

March's referendum abolished the Senate, leaving seven parties to compete for 145 seats under [Kazakhstan](country:KZ)'s new charter.

The Central Election Commission has yet to publish final seat allocations.
