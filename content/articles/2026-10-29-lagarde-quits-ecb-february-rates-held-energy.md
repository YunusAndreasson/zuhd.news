Frankfurt — Christine Lagarde leaves the European Central Bank on 7 February 2027.

Leaders pick her successor before a French election that polls say Marine Le Pen is favoured to win.

European Council President Antonio Costa opened the appointment process Thursday, with Klaas Knot and Pablo Hernández de Cos seen as frontrunners.

The bank held its deposit rate Thursday, and markets barely moved on the resignation.

It decides on rates again on 17 December, with a hike in play.
