United States — Meta's emissions jumped nearly 40% in 2025.

Meta is now reconsidering its 2030 net-zero goal, unlike Amazon, Google and Microsoft.

Capital goods such as data center servers drove the rise, the steepest among 4 major [US](country:US) tech firms.

Demand for AI infrastructure "has grown faster than almost anyone projected," wrote Meta's Rachel Peterson and Blair Swedeen.

Meta has given no new date for net zero.
