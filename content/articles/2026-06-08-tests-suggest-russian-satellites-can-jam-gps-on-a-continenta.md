Brussels — Russian satellites caused continental GPS blackouts from orbit.

University of Texas researchers traced 75 interference days to [Russia](country:RU)'s Kosmos 2546 early-warning satellite via raw signal timing, a June preprint found.

Whether the bursts are weapons tests or communications remains disputed; Russia's embassy declined to comment.
