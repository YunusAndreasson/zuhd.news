London — Gold is falling while a war drives inflation. Russia, Turkey, and Poland are selling central bank gold; bond yields and dollar strength are pulling capital away from the safe-haven trade. The 20% correction tests whether gold's safe-haven status survives a crisis big enough to force holders to sell.
