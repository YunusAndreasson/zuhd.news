Miami — Fugitive illegal-mining boss is a registered Florida Republican.

A Brazilian first-instance court gave Rodrigo Cataratas 16 years for illegal mining on Yanomami land.

He left X One Charters, a Miami yacht-rental company he owned, in January, a month after the arrest warrant.

The company website's contact number still belongs to him.

An unserved warrant says he helped Alexandre Ramagem flee, and the Supreme Court denied his habeas corpus plea 19 October.
