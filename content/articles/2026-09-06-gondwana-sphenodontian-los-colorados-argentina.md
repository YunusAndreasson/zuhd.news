London — A new skull pushes the family tree south.

It challenges the view that the tuatara's lineage arose entirely in the northern supercontinent.

"Earliest-diverging" marks a branch's position on the family tree, not simply the oldest bone found.

Researchers named the species from a single partial skull unearthed in northwestern [Argentina](country:AR).
