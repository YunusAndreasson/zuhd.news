Yerevan — Armenia detained ex-president Kocharyan and five others.

He is Prime Minister Nikol Pashinyan's chief political rival.

Investigators allege state property in [Armenia](country:AM) was sold below market price during his presidency, a valuation only the state set.

Armenia's National Security Service has not detailed which specific properties are involved.
