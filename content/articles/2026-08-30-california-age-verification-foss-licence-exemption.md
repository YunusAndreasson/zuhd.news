Sacramento — California nearly outlawed distributing Debian legally.

The exemption now keeps free operating systems legal to distribute in the state.

Age-verification laws regulate operating systems as products with an owner, and free software has none.

The fix applies only in California, leaving other states' maintainers unprotected before January 2027.
