New York — $15 million in weapons research funding went unreported.

The money from [Israel](country:IL)'s defense ministry reaches Columbia, Cornell, Harvard and dozens of schools.

Grants routed through foundations and subawards skip the foreign-gift disclosure that Section 117 otherwise requires, Drop Site News found.

How much of two decades of transfers still went undetected remains unconfirmed.
