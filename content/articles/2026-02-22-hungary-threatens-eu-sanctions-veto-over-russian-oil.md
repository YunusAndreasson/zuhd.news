The EU's 20th Russia sanctions package stalled. Hungary's Foreign Minister Péter Szijjártó demanded Russian oil resume flowing through the Druzhba pipeline before Budapest would approve the measures. He also threatened to block a 90-billion-euro loan for Ukraine ahead of Monday's foreign ministers meeting.
