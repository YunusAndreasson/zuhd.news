France placed 81 departments on flood alert. Storm Nils battered the southwest with gusts over 180 km/h, killing at least 2 people and cutting power to 850,000 homes. France activated simultaneous Red Alerts for wind, flooding, and avalanches for the first time in its history. The storm caps a month of relentless flooding that has pushed France's alert system past every threshold, with water levels in Langon doubling from 3.80 meters to over 7 in 48 hours.
