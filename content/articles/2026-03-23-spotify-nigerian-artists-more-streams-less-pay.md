Lagos — Nigerian streaming grew 140%, but per-stream payouts are falling. Spotify divides a fixed Nigerian revenue pool by total streams, so more listeners dilute each payout rather than growing it. Artists must push Spotify to raise Nigerian subscription prices, or growing popularity will continue to shrink their earnings.
