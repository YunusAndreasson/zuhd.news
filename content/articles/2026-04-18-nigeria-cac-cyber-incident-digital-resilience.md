Abuja — Nigeria's corporate registry was breached on April 15. CAC holds ownership records for every Nigerian company; the breach came 6 days after NITDA's new resilience strategy was unveiled at GITEX Africa. The full scope remains unclear; multiple agencies are reviewing systems without a disclosed timeline.
