Madrid — 400 officers cleared 334 people from Puerta del Sol overnight.

The camp began after an 87-year-old's eviction and spread to Barcelona, Seville and Valencia.

Rents in Madrid often consume more than half of a modest household's wage, and the Bank of Spain counts 700,000 missing homes.

Organisers say police hit people and used tear gas, injuring several, while the government delegation reports no violence.

Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez faces a general election on 29 November after parliament rejected his two housing decrees.
