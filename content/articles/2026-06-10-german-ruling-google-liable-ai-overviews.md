Munich — [Germany](country:DE) held Google liable for false AI search answers.

Search engines index third-party content and escape liability; AI overviews rewrite it — the Munich court held Google is the author, not a conduit, ending the search-engine liability shield.

Google faces a temporary injunction barring the false summaries; no appeal has been filed.
