Five Micronesian states are pooling negotiating leverage.

A unified bloc changes what Washington and Beijing must each offer alone.

[Palau](country:PW), the Federated States of Micronesia and the Marshall Islands trade defense compacts for aid, each negotiating alone until now.

How far the forum's shared position should bind each state remains unresolved.
