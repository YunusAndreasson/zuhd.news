Wilmington — A judge blocked a rival app from using "Twitter," but not "tweet" or the bird logo.

The split sets how much of an old brand a firm keeps after walking away from it.

X still lists "formerly known as Twitter" on its App Store page, though Musk said he cut the logo off the building.

The rival app, Operation Bluebird, has rebranded to "Tweet.App" while litigation continues.
