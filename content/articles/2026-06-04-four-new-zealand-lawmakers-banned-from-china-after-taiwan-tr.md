Wellington — 4 [New Zealand](country:NZ) MPs banned from entering [China](country:CN).

Beijing's first ban on New Zealand parliamentarians followed their May meeting with [Taiwan](country:TW)'s vice president — sanctioning individuals, not governments, to enforce the One China line.

Beijing offered to lift the ban if MPs apologize; all 4 refused.
