Tartus — Ghazal Imad Rustum, 20, now trains for tourists.

Nearly 15,000 [Syria](country:SY) women applied, seeking the first stable income since 2011.

Post-Assad Syria trains women as tourism police to reassure foreign visitors and attract hard currency.

Instructors have not said what authority these officers hold if tourists stay away.
