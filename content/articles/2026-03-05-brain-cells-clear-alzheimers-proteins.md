Scientists found why aging brains accumulate Alzheimer's plaques. Astrocytes — cells that clear toxic proteins from the brain — decline with age, which researchers say explains why dementia, affecting 55 million people worldwide, typically strikes in later life. Scientists will now test whether boosting astrocyte activity can prevent plaque buildup before it starts, bypassing drugs that only attack existing deposits.
