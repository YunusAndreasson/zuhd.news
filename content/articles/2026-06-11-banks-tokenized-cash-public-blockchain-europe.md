Zurich — Banks sidelined Europe's central-bank digital currency model.

Major banks want tokenized deposits, stablecoins, and money-market funds on one shared public blockchain rail.

No regulator has set rules; the contest between bank-issued and central-bank digital money for institutional settlement is unresolved.
