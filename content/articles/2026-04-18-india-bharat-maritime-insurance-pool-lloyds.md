New Delhi — India created a state-backed maritime insurer on Saturday. The Rs 12,980 crore pool backstops London-linked P&I clubs whose war-risk premiums surged after Hormuz disruptions, the cabinet said. The pool runs 10 years, building domestic underwriting capacity to reduce India's foreign-exchange outflows on premiums.
