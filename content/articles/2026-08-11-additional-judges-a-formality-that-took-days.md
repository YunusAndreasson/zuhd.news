Islamabad — Zardari approved judges after a three-week delay.

The pause left five high courts short a judge while cases sat unresolved.

Additional judges serve probation before confirmation, so [Pakistan](country:PK)'s executive holds leverage over their rulings.

The Judicial Commission's next nomination round will test whether such delays recur again.
