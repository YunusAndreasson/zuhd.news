Mumbai — BYD registered 6,755 electric cars in [India](country:IN) this year.

Tata and Mahindra took over 60% of India's 300,000 electric car sales, which rose 66%.

Since 2020, New Delhi has vetted investment from land-border countries, so MG's Chinese owner sold 51% of MG India to local investors.

About 70% of MG India's goods purchases still come from that owner's affiliates, its 2026 filings show.

JSW MG Motor plans 160,000 cars of capacity by March 2027 and 220,000 by January 2028.
