Washington — Biotech lobbies sued Friday to halt a Medicare drug-price pilot.

Expected to have little impact, the pilot still sets a precedent the industry wants stopped.

The pilot ties Medicare drug prices to what peer countries pay, and the complaint argues it exceeds Medicare's statutory authority.

The [US](country:US) District Court for Northern California has not ruled, and Medicare's administrator has not commented.
