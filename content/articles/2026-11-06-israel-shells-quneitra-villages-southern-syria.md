Quneitra — Israeli shells hit 3 villages, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

Syrian state television said several people were hurt and homes were damaged.

The channel said Syrian authorities logged more than 200 violations by [Israel](country:IL)'s forces in southern governorates in October, including raids and detentions.

Local authorities said they were still verifying the casualty count.
