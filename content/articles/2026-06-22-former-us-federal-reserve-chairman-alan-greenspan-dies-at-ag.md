Washington — Alan Greenspan died Monday at 100.

His 18-year [US](country:US) Federal Reserve tenure established the 'Greenspan put': the assumption markets would always be backstopped.

Critics link that doctrine to the 2008 crash; Greenspan told Congress he made a mistake trusting banks to self-regulate.
