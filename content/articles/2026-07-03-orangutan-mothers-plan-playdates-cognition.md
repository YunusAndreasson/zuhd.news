Borneo — Orangutan mothers detour miles to find peer playmates.

A 15-year Max Planck study found mothers of similarly aged young travel farther and eat less to meet, regardless of fruit supply.

Researchers say the tradeoff shows future-oriented planning previously undocumented in wild orangutans.
