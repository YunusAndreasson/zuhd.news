New York — [Germany](country:DE) lost its Security Council seat to [Portugal](country:PT) and [Austria](country:AT), 104 votes to their 134 and 131.

Germany's foreign minister said [Russia](country:RU) lobbied against Berlin over its Ukraine support; the 5 rotating seats carry speaking rights but no veto power.

All 5 new members join January 1 on a council deadlocked over Ukraine, Gaza, and Iran.
