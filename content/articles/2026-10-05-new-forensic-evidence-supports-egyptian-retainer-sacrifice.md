Abydos — Over a third of skulls near First Dynasty tombs are fractured.

The bones suggest servants and officials were killed to accompany [Egypt](country:EG)'s first kings.

Purdue's Roselyn Campbell found linear fractures in 18 of 46 Abydos skulls, published in Frontiers in Environmental Archaeology.

"It is economically very costly to kill off hundreds of people every 20 to 40 years," said Campbell.

Petrie reburied 96 people's long bones mixed in one pit, making matches to skulls extremely difficult.
