San Francisco — WhatsApp filed contempt charges against NSO Group.

NSO, an [Israeli](country:IL) spyware maker, allegedly kept attacking WhatsApp users in direct violation of a permanent injunction barring it from doing so.

The contempt motion tests whether civil court judgments can actually bind a mercenary foreign surveillance firm.
