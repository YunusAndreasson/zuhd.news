San Francisco — Hacker breached a16z-funded AI phone farm Doublespeed. Doublespeed uses AI to create fake influencers and flood TikTok; the attacker exfiltrated 47MB and queued posts to 573 accounts. No posts were published; it is Doublespeed's second breach in 4 months, with no customer notification issued.
