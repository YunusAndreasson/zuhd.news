Boston — A gene-edited pig kidney kept a 66-year-old off dialysis for 271 days.

It worked as a bridge, not a cure, buying time where dialysis capacity limits care.

Mass General removed the kidney after microvascular injury and gave him a human donor organ, The Lancet study said.

Researchers stress this remains a single case, needing lifelong immunosuppression few hospitals can provide.
