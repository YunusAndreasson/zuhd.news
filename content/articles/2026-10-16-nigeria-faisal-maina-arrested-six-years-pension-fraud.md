Abuja — Faisal Maina was reportedly caught after 6 years on the run.

A court convicted him in absentia of laundering N58.1 million through a fictitious bank account.

A source says State Security Service operatives arrested him in Karsana after intelligence that he had secretly returned.

Neither the security service nor the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission has responded.

His father, Abdulrasheed Maina, released this year, is wanted over alleged receiving of stolen property.
