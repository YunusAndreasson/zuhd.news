Washington — US Border Patrol data show seizures fell 3%.

That undercuts President Trump's claim that strikes cut trafficking by 98%.

Traffickers now use larger boats and shifted routes toward Guyana and Suriname to dodge strikes.

Washington has not released crew names or provided evidence of drug cargo.
