Johannesburg — 260,000 women die yearly in pregnancy and birth.

Global health aid falls 5.8% in 2026 after a 23.1% drop in 2025, the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development projects; maternal care is cut first.

Nigeria added $200 million to offset lost US aid; most of 18 African states made no comparable move.
