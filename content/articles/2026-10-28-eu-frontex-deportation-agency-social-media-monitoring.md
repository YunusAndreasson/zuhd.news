Brussels — Frontex would scan social media for migrants' crossing advice.

Two draft laws from the European Commission make the border agency the EU's central deportation body.

A monitoring centre would watch public posts about crossing borders and flag them to platforms with priority.

Frontex will apply for trusted-flagger status, and Commission Vice-President Henna Virkkunen expects approval.
