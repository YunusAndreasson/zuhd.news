Frontera — 51 fishing families now live 12 km from the sea.

El Bosque became the first community in [Mexico](country:MX) recognised as displaced by climate change, in 2023.

Erosion and storm surges had damaged or destroyed more than half its roughly 100 homes before families moved inland in 2024.

Viviana Velázquez, 38, says she is mostly housebound because the beach, where women once gathered, is too far.

Governments owe displaced communities help well before the water takes their homes, the reporting concludes.
