BYD's China sales fell 35.8% in January and February. China replaced a 10% electric vehicle purchase tax exemption with a 5% levy, hitting BYD's pure-electric lineup far harder than Geely's multi-fuel range. Geely sold 476,327 units to BYD's 400,241; BYD plans a 24% export increase this year to offset domestic losses.
