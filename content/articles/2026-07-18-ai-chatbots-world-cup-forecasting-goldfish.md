Toronto — A tank goldfish keeps beating chatbots at World Cup calls.

Large language models pattern-match plausible narratives about players like Kylian Mbappé and Lionel Messi rather than model actual probability, so a random pick can outscore them.

The gap exposes how "sounds right" diverges from "is right" in AI forecasting tools.
