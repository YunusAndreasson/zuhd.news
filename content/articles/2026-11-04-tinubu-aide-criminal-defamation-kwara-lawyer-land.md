Ilorin — Court orders arrest of lawyer who accused Tinubu's aide.

Lt-Col Nurudeen Yusuf, the aide, is accused of using military rank to seize land.

Yusuf's 28 October criminal defamation complaint under Section 391 of [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Penal Code won an arrest warrant a day later.

Court documents do not establish who owns the disputed land or the merits of either side's claims.

The summons orders Nuruddin Abdulsalam before the Upper Area Court in Ilorin on 8 November.
