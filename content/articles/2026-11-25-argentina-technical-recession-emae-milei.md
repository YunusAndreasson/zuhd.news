Buenos Aires — [Argentina](country:AR) appears to have entered recession after two shrinking quarters.

The sectors that employ the most people are the ones shrinking.

Emerald Capital's Elena Donoso says private wages sit 3.5% below late 2023 and manufacturing is down 8%.

Economy Minister Luis Caputo called the technical-recession definition almost arbitrary and a poor gauge of severity.

The national statistics institute publishes official third-quarter GDP on 16 December.
