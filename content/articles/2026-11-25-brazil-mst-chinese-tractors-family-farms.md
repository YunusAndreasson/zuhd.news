Brazil — The tractor market still favours big producers over family farms.

The Landless Rural Workers' Movement says about 450,000 families gained land through its occupations.

It is testing smaller, cheaper machines with [Chinese](country:CN) manufacturers and universities, adapting designs built for China's small plots.

"We realise that we need to shape the industry to meet the needs of family farming," said co-founder João Pedro Stedile.

The machines are still in testing and adaptation for Brazilian smallholders.
