Los Angeles — Past communication disruptions took a century to settle.

Algorithm-driven social media is still in its chaotic opening phase, Macario Schettino argues.

The printing press fractured Christendom before slowly building the Enlightenment, the same technology resolving what it broke.

Schettino does not say what public feedback mechanism could speed the reckoning.
