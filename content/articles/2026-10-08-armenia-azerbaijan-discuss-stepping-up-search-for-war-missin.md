Baku — [Azerbaijan](country:AZ) says 4,010 of its citizens remain missing.

None of 50 DNA profiles [Armenia](country:AM) sent matched remains Azerbaijan recovered, its commission head said.

Azerbaijan gave Armenia a list of 834 citizens it believes were taken prisoner in the 1990s, with detail on 104.

The peace deal initialled in August 2025 is still unsigned, and Article 9 binds both sides to exchange information and return remains.
