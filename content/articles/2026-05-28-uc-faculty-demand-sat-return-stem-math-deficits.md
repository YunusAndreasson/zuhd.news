Berkeley — 600 UC professors want the SAT back.

At least 20% of Berkeley's first-semester calculus students can't pass a basic diagnostic after 6 test-free years, the letter says.

The UC Academic Senate board meets within days; the letter asks for mandatory SAT from fall 2027.
