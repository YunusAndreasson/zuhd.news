Las Palmas — 1,317 people died reaching [Spain](country:ES) in 5 months.

Caminando Fronteras counted 27 boats lost with all aboard; longer Atlantic routes have replaced the shorter Mauritanian crossing as patrols tightened.

Spain logged 3,090 route deaths in all of 2025; the 2026 pace is on track to exceed it.
