London — Seawater chemistry doubles as a planetary record.

Researchers reframe ocean acidification beyond reefs and shellfish: the same carbon-driven pH shift also appears in rock layers marking past mass extinctions.

Reading today's acidification against that deep-time record is what makes scientists call the current pace alarming.
