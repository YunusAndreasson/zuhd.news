London — Scientists described 1,121 new ocean species in 2025.

Estimates put total ocean species at 2.2 million; the 30%-by-2030 protection pledge requires knowing what lives there before it can be conserved.

Deep-sea mining advances faster than taxonomy; uncharted species cannot qualify for any protection treaty.
