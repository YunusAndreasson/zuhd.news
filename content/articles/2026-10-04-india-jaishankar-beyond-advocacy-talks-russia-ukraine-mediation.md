New Delhi — [India](country:IN) discusses grain, energy and non-attack steps with both sides.

External affairs minister S Jaishankar said Sunday that India has moved beyond advocating dialogue.

Putin and [Ukraine](country:UA)'s foreign minister each said this week India has offered ideas, while the [US](country:US) criticises its Russian crude purchases.

The talks are ongoing, Jaishankar said, and no Russian or Ukrainian response to his remarks has been reported.
