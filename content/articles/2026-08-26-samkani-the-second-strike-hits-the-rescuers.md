Villagers who ran to help became casualties themselves, UN investigators found.

[Pakistan](country:PK) struck Samkani in June, hitting militant camps it says; the UN counted 22 dead, 5 of them children.

The UN calls its 22-dead count preliminary; no cross-border authority tracks the toll.
