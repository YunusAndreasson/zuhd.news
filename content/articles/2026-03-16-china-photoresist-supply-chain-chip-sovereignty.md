Japan controls the chemical that etches every chip. Photoresist — a light-sensitive compound applied like film before circuits are burned into silicon — has been dominated by Japanese producers and used as a US export-control lever against China. Xuzhou B&C Chemical aims to mass-produce advanced photoresist grades within 5 years, closing the last foreign gap in China's chip supply chain.
