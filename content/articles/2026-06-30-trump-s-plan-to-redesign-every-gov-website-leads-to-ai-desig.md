Washington — Most federal agencies are refusing to adopt a White House redesign studio after it deployed trackers on 4 .gov sites with no required privacy filings.

The National Design Studio — tasked by executive order with overhauling 27,000 .gov sites — used software configured to bypass privacy tools.

Who holds data collected while the trackers ran has not been disclosed.
