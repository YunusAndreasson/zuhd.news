Toronto — Ads infected at least 45 Al Jazeera devices with spyware, Citizen Lab found.

Seeing the banner is enough to infect a device, with no click needed.

Israeli firm Discover-Tech bid in ad auctions and used ad-profile data to pick targets, the researchers say, delivering Candiru spyware.

Al Jazeera, based in [Qatar](country:QA), had not commented, and Discover-Tech did not answer press requests.

Discover-Tech held Google's certified-vendor status until recently, and researchers urge users to block ads.
