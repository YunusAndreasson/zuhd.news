Dubai — Fuel costs rose 37.52% in a year.

Dubai's statistics office put September inflation at 5.03%, below August's 5.54% and June's 5.7% peak.

The [UAE](country:AE) lifted fuel subsidies in 2015 and resets pump prices monthly against world markets, so residents pay the war premium.

S&P Global's survey put Dubai's purchasing managers' index at 54.5, its fastest growth pace of 2026.

Brent crude was $105.7 on 12 October, and Hormuz traffic ran 38% below normal as of 4 October.
