Chicago — Agents like OpenAI's dots can no longer book United flights.

United says handing credentials to AI agents risks unintended purchases and spent MileagePlus miles.

United will restrict unauthorized third-party agents on its own platforms and, indirectly, through travel agencies and aggregators.

Southwest has instead opened its flight inventory to ChatGPT users, and Kayak and Google Flights still sell United fares.

United is rewriting its booking and ticketing policies to reflect the ban.
