Qyzylsay — Women in [Kazakhstan](country:KZ)'s south spend hours a day securing water.

Their labour goes unpaid and unnoticed, and remoteness limits their access to government services.

Activist Nazgul Aidabossynova's 2026 project trains them to use eGov services, cost their labour and products, and press authorities together.

A planned cooperative would pool their advocacy and seek funding for shared irrigation, but none exists yet.
