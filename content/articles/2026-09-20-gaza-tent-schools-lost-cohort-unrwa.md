Gaza City — 541,000 students returned to school, the ministry said.

About 700,000 children missed nearly three straight school years, UNICEF said.

UNICEF says 98% of school buildings are damaged, so classes run in tents without desks or textbooks.

Director Inaam al-Batreekhi says her tent school cannot hold classes when rain comes.
