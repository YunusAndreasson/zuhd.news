Kathmandu — Ice and rock collapse, not rain, triggered this flood.

Downstream valleys had an alert that never became an evacuation before the water arrived.

Early warning runs four stages — detect, predict, communicate, act — and [Nepal](country:NP) failed at the last two, said Saswata Sanyal of the International Centre for Integrated Mountain Development.

Millions still live below glacial lakes across Nepal, India, Pakistan and Bhutan.
