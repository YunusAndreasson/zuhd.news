Oslo — A new memorial names all 77 victims.

Neo-Nazi Anders Breivik killed 8 with a car bomb in Oslo and shot 69 more, mostly teenagers, on Utoya island in 2011.

The attack's 15th anniversary lands as far-right, anti-immigrant politics hardens across Europe.
