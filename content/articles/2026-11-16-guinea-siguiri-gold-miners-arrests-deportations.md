Siguiri — [Guinea](country:GN)'s army says 1,263 miners were arrested in Siguiri.

[Burkina Faso](country:BF)'s security minister alleges deportees suffered brutality, injuries and deaths.

Guinea cites environmental harm, while its new Conakry refinery aims to process 30% of the country's 70 tons of gold.

Guinea's mining committee chair says the ban hits millions of Guineans, not just foreigners.

Mining expert Ibrahim Diallo says the crackdown may be meant to feed the refinery.
