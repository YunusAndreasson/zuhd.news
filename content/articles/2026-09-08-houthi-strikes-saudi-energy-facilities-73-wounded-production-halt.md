Jazan — Houthi strikes wounded 73 civilians in [Saudi Arabia](country:SA).

It marks [Yemen](country:YE)'s war reaching the kingdom's oil production, not just shipping lanes.

The strikes hit facilities across four southern cities, including Aramco assets near Jazan, halting some operations.

Houthi spokesperson Yahya Saree says a broader operation into Saudi territory is coming.
