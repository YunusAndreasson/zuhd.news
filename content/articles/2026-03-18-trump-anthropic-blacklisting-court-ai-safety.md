The Pentagon blacklisted Anthropic for refusing autonomous weapons use. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth declared Anthropic, an AI safety company, a national security risk on March 3 after it refused to remove safety limits on autonomous weapons and domestic surveillance. Anthropic is challenging the designation in a California court, arguing the government punished protected speech rather than a business decision.
