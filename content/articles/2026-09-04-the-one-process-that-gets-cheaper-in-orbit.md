Toulouse — ArcSpace raised €2 million to weld in orbit.

Satellites launched today must survive as one rigid piece, with no repairs.

Electron-beam welding needs a vacuum, expensive on Earth but free in orbit, making metal-joining cheaper there.

A 12-kilogram payload demonstrates the welder autonomously aboard a partner spacecraft in 2027.
