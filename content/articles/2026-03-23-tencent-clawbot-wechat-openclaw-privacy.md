Beijing — ClawBot gives WeChat's 1 billion users an AI task-agent. OpenClaw, built by Austrian developer Peter Steinberger, lets an AI act autonomously through chat apps — sending emails, organising files, and running tasks on users' behalf. Authorities have warned that ClawBot's autonomous access creates data harvesting risks yet to be regulated.
