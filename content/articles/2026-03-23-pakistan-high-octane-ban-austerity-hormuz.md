Islamabad — Pakistan banned premium fuel in all government vehicles. The ban came as oil import costs — Pakistan imports 85% of its supply — pushed fuel spending above budget, prompting Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif to act. Pakistan has now imposed 3 emergency fuel measures in a week, and the import bill remains unsustainable.
