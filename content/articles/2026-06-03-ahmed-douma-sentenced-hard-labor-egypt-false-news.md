Cairo — Poet Ahmed Douma drew 1 year hard labor for writing about prison conditions.

[Egypt](country:EG)'s "false news" statute targets writing that "disturbs public order" — Douma has faced prosecutors 7 times since a 2023 pardon that ended a decade in prison.

His defense team will appeal; Douma was barred from attending his own sentencing.
