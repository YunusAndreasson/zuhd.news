Portland — A fur seal tested positive for H5N1 Sunday.

Seal-to-seal spread would show the virus adapting to mammals, a step toward human risk.

Elephant seals recognise each other by touching noses, the same contact through which flu passes between birds and seals.

None of [Australia](country:AU)'s more than 100 reported marine mammal cases in Victoria has been an elephant seal yet.
