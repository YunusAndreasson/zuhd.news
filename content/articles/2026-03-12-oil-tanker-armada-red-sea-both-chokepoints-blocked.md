30 tankers rushed to Saudi Arabia's Red Sea coast. Saudi Arabia activated its East-West pipeline after Iran closed Hormuz, leaving Asia-bound tankers to face the Houthi-controlled Bab el-Mandeb strait. If Houthis re-enter the war, both seaborne oil routes will be simultaneously under attack.
