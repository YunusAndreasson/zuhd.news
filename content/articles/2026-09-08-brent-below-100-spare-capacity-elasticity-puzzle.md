A war across two chokepoints has not produced the price shock past ones did.

Spare Saudi and Emirati capacity, bypass pipelines and rising shale output are absorbing the shortfall markets bet is temporary.

Brent sat near $96 as of September 1, but every buffer holding it there is finite.
