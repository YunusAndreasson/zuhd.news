North Kordofan — Sudan's Rapid Support Forces shot 12 civilians in Bara. The RSF accused the civilians of informing for the army; the group retook Bara 10 days after Sudanese forces recaptured it. Without air cover, neither side can hold the town; Bara has now changed hands twice this month.
