London — Extreme heat now bleeds into spring and autumn.

Heat outside peak summer catches unprepared people off guard.

The study compares 1980s and 2015-24 reanalysis data to track how heat has shifted into two-month shoulder periods.

Heat-health warning systems still use fixed seasonal windows the findings now challenge.
