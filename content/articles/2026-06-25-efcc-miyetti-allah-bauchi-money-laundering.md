Abuja — Herder chief Bello Bodejo charged with $2.53m laundering.

Bauchi State paid the sum in physical cash bypassing banking records — logged as "security commitments" — [Nigeria](country:NG)'s anti-graft agency alleged.

Bodejo's plea is pending; the state's finance commissioner faces separate terrorism-financing charges over the same disbursements.
