Vladivostok — Russia handed Tehran a small-reactor draft mid-war.

A civil nuclear deal ties Iran to Russian fuel and operations for decades, a bet no strike removes.

Rosatom CEO Alexey Likhachev said [Iran](country:IR) would develop its nuclear program "as Iran returns to peaceful life."

No Iranian official confirmed the draft agreement.
