Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) moved orphanage infants to private homes.

At least 1 orphanage was reportedly struck, forcing Iran's decade-long adoption bureaucracy to place newborns in private care within days.

Whether emergency placements become permanent remains unresolved as courts handle guardianship cases under active bombardment.
