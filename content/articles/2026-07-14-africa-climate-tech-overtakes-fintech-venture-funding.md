Lagos — Climate tech drew 40% of Africa's venture funding in 2025.

That's up from 13% in 2016, Briter's report found, as investors shifted from payments apps toward adaptation and energy across [Nigeria](country:NG), Kenya and South Africa.

Nigeria and Kenya are now competing for the deals that once went to fintech startups.
