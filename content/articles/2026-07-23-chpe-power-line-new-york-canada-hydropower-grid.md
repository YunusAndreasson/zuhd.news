New York City — A heat wave pulled 9% of the state's power from [Canada](country:CA).

The $6 billion Champlain Hudson line, buried 339 miles from Quebec, could supply 20% of the city's electricity with hydropower, but has been down for most of July after cable damage.

Repairs are due by the weekend; drought in Quebec still threatens the hydropower feeding it.
