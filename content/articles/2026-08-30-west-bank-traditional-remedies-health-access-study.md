Nablus — 35 adults describe treating illness before seeing doctors.

Their substitutions map where formal medical care fails them.

Movement restrictions, cost and distrust push patients to herbs and home remedies first, researchers found.

Most never tell their doctors, the study's authors said, urging routine screening for herb-drug interactions.
