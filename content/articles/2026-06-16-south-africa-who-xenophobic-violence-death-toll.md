Johannesburg — WHO said 10 migrants died in protests; Pretoria said no.

WHO chief Tedros cited 5 Ethiopians and 5 Mozambicans killed; [South Africa](country:ZA)'s police confirmed 2 Mozambican deaths and linked Ethiopian killings to organised crime.

7,000 Malawians await repatriation in Durban; anti-immigration groups plan a June 30 national shutdown.
