Washington — A price floor, not just a tariff, targets polysilicon.

[China](country:CN) makes 90% of the world's supply; the floor bars underselling it, a tool aimed squarely at China's lower production costs.

Solar buyers from Pakistan to the Gulf source the same Chinese supply chain the December tariffs target.
