Marseille — Dockworkers at 110 ports in 22 countries stopped work.

Weapons for [Israel](country:IL) move through commercial ports, where the workers who load them can refuse.

Campaign group No Harbour for Genocide tracked 315 Elbit Systems shipments through 56 ports since August 2025, all expected to strike.

Only 5 countries called 24-hour strikes, and Morocco and Cyprus stop for up to 2 hours.

Union leader Markos Bekris still faces prosecution for blocking 21 tonnes of ammunition bound for Israel.
