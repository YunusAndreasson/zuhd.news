32 people died in coordinated dawn attacks. Armed groups struck 3 communities in Niger state's Borgu area on Saturday, killing 26 in Konkoso and 6 in Tunga-Makeri while abducting an unknown number of villagers. Konkoso resident Abdullahi Adamu told the Associated Press the attackers operated freely without any security presence. The raids follow a February attack in neighboring Kwara state that killed 162 people, part of a wider crisis in north-central Nigeria where armed gangs and Islamist militants operate with impunity. Niger state police confirmed joint security teams have been deployed, but no arrests have been reported.
