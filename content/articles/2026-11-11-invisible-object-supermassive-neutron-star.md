China — A dark companion in J0008+0128 weighs up to 2.8 Suns.

Few remnants weigh 2.5 to 5 Suns, and this one may sit in that gap.

Published in Science Advances, the study found it only through its pull on a red giant companion in a 1,218-day orbit.

Thousands of simulations by the researchers could not reproduce the object's orbit and mass.

The neutron-star equation of state, linking pressure to density, remains unsolved, co-author Song Wang said.
