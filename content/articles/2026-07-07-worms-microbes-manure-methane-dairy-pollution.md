Hickman — Worms are cutting methane from California dairy manure.

Eight vermifiltration systems already operate on [US](country:US) dairies, funded by $18 million in state grants versus $1 billion for costlier digesters.

California's 2030 deadline requires cutting dairy methane 40% below 2013 levels.
