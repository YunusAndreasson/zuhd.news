London — Standard foundation theory fails on coral sand islands.

Coral grains shatter under load, shrinking volume instead of expanding — so traditional stone-column calculations underpredict lateral pressure on reclaimed islands.

Gulf and South China Sea islands rest on coral substrate; the model still needs prospective validation.
