Villa de Leyva — Jurassic survivors and Cretaceous newcomers shared one sea.

Researchers at McGill and [Colombia](country:CO)'s Centro de Investigaciones Paleontológicas call the pattern a biotic inflection.

The Paja Formation holds Jurassic survivors beside new Cretaceous lineages, in greenhouse seas newly linking the Pacific and early Atlantic.

Further sampling, dating and global comparisons will show whether other eras saw a biotic inflection, the authors say.
