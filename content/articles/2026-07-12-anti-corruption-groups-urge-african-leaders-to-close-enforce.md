Addis Ababa — Africa loses $88.6 billion yearly to graft.

UNCTAD puts the loss at 3.7% of GDP; civil-society groups say weak enforcement, not missing law, is the problem.

Groups renewed calls for an International Anti-Corruption Court to prosecute cases national courts won't pursue.
