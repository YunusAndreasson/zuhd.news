Woro — Villagers seized six months ago are free.

A Boko Haram faction abducted about 176 people from this Kaiama farming community in February; [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Kwara state announced the release on Facebook.

Authorities have not said how many were freed or whether any ransom was paid.
