Bhubaneswar — $11.5 billion, one MoU, 53,500 jobs.

Adani and Abu Dhabi's International Holding Company will build a refinery, smelter and power plant in [India](country:IN)'s bauxite-rich Odisha, which produces 54% of national aluminium.

[United Arab Emirates](country:AE) capital now backs India's push to sextuple domestic output by 2047, easing import dependence.
