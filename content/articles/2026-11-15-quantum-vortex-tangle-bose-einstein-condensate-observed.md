Cambridge — Physicists watched a quantum vortex tangle unravel in 3D.

The decay matched superfluid helium's rate, though the two fluids differ utterly at microscopic scale.

Sebastian Morris's team, in Physical Review Letters, scrambled a potassium condensate, let it relax and photographed slices to count vortices.

Later the decay slowed, as vortices were lost at the trap's walls.

The test covered one gas, potassium, in one cylinder-shaped trap.
