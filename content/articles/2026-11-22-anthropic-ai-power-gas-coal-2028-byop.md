United States — Gas will power 87% of Anthropic's 2028 compute, analysts estimate.

The Better Data Center Project puts coal at 11%, and says Anthropic has no US renewable commitments.

It counts on-site gas plus the marginal grid plants likely to meet Anthropic's added load, which are fossil.

It is an outside estimate, not a company disclosure, built on 5.9 to 9.6 gigawatts tied to named sites.

Anthropic has bought commitments for 1.7 gigawatts of heavy-duty gas generators for $3.4bn, the analysis notes.
