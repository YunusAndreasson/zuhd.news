Adaptation funding follows these attribution claims, so overstating them is not harmless.

Seismic data timed the collapse, satellites measured its volume, and only a separate warming-attribution study addresses climate change.

No formal attribution study for [Nepal](country:NP)'s flood has yet been officially completed.
