Cyclone Gezani killed 4 people in Mozambique on Saturday. The storm struck Inhambane province with winds reaching 215 km/h after killing 41 and injuring 427 in Madagascar earlier this week, where 75% of the port city Toamasina was destroyed. Madagascar declared a national emergency with 16,300 people displaced and damage estimated at $142 million. Mozambique was already reeling from severe flooding that affected over 700,000 people and damaged more than 170,000 homes. The World Food Programme reported its Madagascar office and warehouse completely destroyed, warning that the scale of devastation is overwhelming relief capacity.
