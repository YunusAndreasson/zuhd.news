Washington — FBI blames [North Korea](country:KP)'s TraderTraitor for $6.3 billion Binance theft.

Bitcoin fell about 5% after the theft, and the stolen funds are moving into Ethereum.

Attackers shift funds across wallets, networks and swap services, the FBI said, in a statement the Turkish aggregator Haberler.com relayed.

President Donald Trump said, "We don't know. We are investigating."

The FBI asked exchanges and analytics firms to block transactions linked to the suspect wallets.
