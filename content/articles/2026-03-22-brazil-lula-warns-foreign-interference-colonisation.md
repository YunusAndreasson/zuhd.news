Bogotá — Washington demanded access to Brazil's rare earth deposits. Brazilian President Lula called the demand colonial at a Bogotá summit, citing Washington's blockade of Cuba and abduction of Venezuela's president since Trump took office. Trump imposed 50 percent tariffs on Brazil last year; Lula is set to travel to Washington to press for their removal.
