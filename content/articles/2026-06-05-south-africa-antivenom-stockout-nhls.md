Johannesburg — Snake, scorpion, and spider antivenom is exhausted at public hospitals.

The National Health Laboratory Service confirmed [South Africa](country:ZA)'s polyvalent stock returns July 15; only boomslang antivenom remains.

Researchers say fixing recurring shortages requires a public-private partnership; no government deadline exists.
