Hong Kong — Hong Kong team edits RNA without touching DNA.

Editing the transient RNA message rather than the genome is reversible — a different safety class from CRISPR, targeting neurodegenerative disease.

The technique could treat disorders where permanent DNA edits are too risky; clinical applications remain years away.
