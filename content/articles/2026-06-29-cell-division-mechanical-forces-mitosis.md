New York — Breaking a cell's scaffolding during division reinforces it.

Mitosis pulls chromosomes apart under extreme mechanical tension; the forces that fracture the cell's internal scaffold also trigger its reinforcement.

The mechanism may explain why cell division so rarely goes wrong despite the physical stresses involved.
