Hot Springs — The sinkhole is now dated to 160,000 years.

That is six times older than the accepted age, changing what era it documents.

Radiocarbon dating fails past 50,000 years, so [US](country:US) scientists measured light-trapped electrons in buried quartz grains.

Any human-association claim tied to the old date now needs review, researchers say.
