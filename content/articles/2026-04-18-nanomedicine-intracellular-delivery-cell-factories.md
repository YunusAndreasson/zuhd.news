Toronto — Nanoparticles now ferry mRNA directly into human cells. Lipid carriers deliver mRNA into cells that produce therapy on site, extending the mRNA vaccine platform to chronic disease. No intracellular therapy for common chronic conditions has cleared human trials yet.
