A gunman rammed a Michigan synagogue and opened fire. The FBI confirmed the assault at Temple Israel in West Bloomfield; police killed the assailant, and no other casualties were reported. The attack occurred during Ramadan, following Israel's closure of Al-Aqsa Mosque; investigators have not established a motive.
