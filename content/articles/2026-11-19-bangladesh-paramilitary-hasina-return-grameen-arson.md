Dhaka — Arsonists hit 37 Grameen Bank branches since 10 November.

The bank's founder, Muhammad Yunus, led the interim government that replaced Sheikh Hasina.

Hasina, exiled in [India](country:IN) since August 2024, requested a passport to return in December, and the government refused.

Police have not identified suspects in Gopalganj, though the government blames Hasina's banned Awami League.

The Awami League's nationwide lockdown runs Thursday, with hundreds of paramilitary troops deployed.
