Russia imported $125 million in drones from Thailand in 11 months — an eightfold increase. Bloomberg traced the supply chain to Chinese manufacturers shipping through Thai intermediaries, with one firm sanctioned by Britain now rebranding as Lanto Global Logistics. The dual-use drones, marketed as civilian, end up on the Ukrainian front line.
