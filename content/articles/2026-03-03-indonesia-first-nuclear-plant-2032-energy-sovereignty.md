Indonesia approved nuclear power on the Ring of Fire. The government chose Bangka Belitung island for its low seismicity — a rare stable site in an archipelago where electricity demand grows 5% annually. ThorCon will build the 500-megawatt plant by 2032, with Indonesia targeting 44 gigawatts of nuclear capacity by 2060.
