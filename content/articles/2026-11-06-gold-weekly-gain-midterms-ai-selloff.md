United States — Gold gained 4% this week, near $4,063 an ounce.

The dollar and Treasury yields fell after the [US](country:US) midterms, lifting the metal.

Gold pays no interest, so oil-driven inflation worries that keep Fed rates high raise the cost of holding it.

Gold eased 0.6% Friday, and Goldman Sachs cut its end-2026 forecast to $4,150 from $4,650.

Traders see 68% odds of a December Fed hike, with US inflation data due 10 November.
