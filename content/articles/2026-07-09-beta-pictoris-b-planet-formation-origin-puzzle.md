Grenoble — An 11-Jupiter-mass planet won't say how it formed.

Beta Pictoris b's chemistry fits neither of astronomers' two models — slow core accretion or rapid gravitational collapse — for building giant planets.

Researchers must now measure more young worlds to adjudicate between the two mechanisms.
