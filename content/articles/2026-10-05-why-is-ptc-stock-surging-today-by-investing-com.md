Paris — [France](country:FR)'s Schneider Electric lost nearly €15 billion in value Monday.

It is paying a 42.3% premium for [US](country:US) software maker PTC, its biggest acquisition.

Schneider will fund the $22.6 billion cash deal with up to €17 billion in new debt and up to €6 billion in new shares.

AI disruption fears let Schneider buy PTC at a decade-low valuation, Jefferies said, but could still weigh on Schneider afterward.

Closing is expected by the third quarter of 2027, pending PTC shareholder and regulatory approval.
