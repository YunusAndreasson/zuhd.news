Hyderabad — Paclobutrazol raised rice culm strength from 0.39 to 0.60.

Plant height fell up to 9.8% at the recommended nitrogen dose, without affecting grain yield.

The Institute of Rice Research tested 4 paclobutrazol doses at 2 nitrogen levels in the 2022 Kharif season.

The strongest culms came at 90 grams of active ingredient per hectare.
