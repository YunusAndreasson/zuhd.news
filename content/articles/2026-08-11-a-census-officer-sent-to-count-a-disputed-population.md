Jammu — Census commissioner Narayan arrived to study demographic change.

Its report could decide who legally must prove residency in Jammu.

[India](country:IN)'s home ministry ordered the panel after Assam's 2019 count excluded 1.9 million people.

The panel meets Jammu officials and civil-society groups, then reports to New Delhi within a year.
