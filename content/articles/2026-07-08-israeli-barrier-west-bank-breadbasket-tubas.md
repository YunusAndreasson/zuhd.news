Tubas — [Israel](country:IL)'s military cut water to Al-Forat farm.

The first 22km segment of a planned 500km barrier is sealing the Jordan Valley off from the rest of the West Bank.

The barrier's first phase covers Tubas; 19 more phases are planned across the eastern West Bank.
