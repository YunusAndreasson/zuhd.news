Zug — Ethereum's co-founder says the network of 2030 won't just be a blockchain.

The shift could let Ethereum handle far more users without new computers slowing verification.

Today every computer re-checks the same transactions to stay honest, so adding more computers doesn't add capacity.

Buterin says the shift depends on developers making cryptographic proofs cheaper and coordinating parallel client work securely.

The transformation accelerates after Ethereum's planned Hegotá upgrade, whose date Buterin hasn't specified.
