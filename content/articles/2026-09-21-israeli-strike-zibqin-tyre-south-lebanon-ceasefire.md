Zibqin — [Israel](country:IL)'s military bombed the Tyre district town from the air.

Israeli forces also shelled al-Mansouri and Majdal Zoun, the [Lebanon](country:LB) state news agency reported.

Houses in al-Mansouri were blown up.

Thursday's shelling of both towns came during a broader advance toward border towns that wounded at least 2 people.

The state news agency, the report's only source, has given no casualty figure for the latest strikes.
