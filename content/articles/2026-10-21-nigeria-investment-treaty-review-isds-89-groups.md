Nigeria — [Nigeria](country:NG)'s investment treaty review, begun in November 2024, remains unpublished.

89 civil society groups, Amnesty among them, warn arbitration claims could drain public funds.

Investor-state dispute settlement lets foreign investors challenge government measures before international tribunals, which can chill new environmental regulation.

The groups note the 3 petroleum cases they cite have not established Nigeria's liability.

A Korea National Oil Corporation case and a Jupiter Lithium claim over Kaduna mining remain pending.
