Reisterstown — Research suggests 9% of new [US](country:US) mothers have eating disorders.

Eating disorders are linked to miscarriage and low birthweight, though studies do not prove cause.

The American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists' guidance on perinatal mental health omits eating disorders, and no screening tool is widely used.

"Providers don't see it because we're not looking for it," said Danielle Prentice, a maternal-fetal medicine specialist.

Massachusetts' expanded screening excludes eating disorders because the state follows that guidance.
