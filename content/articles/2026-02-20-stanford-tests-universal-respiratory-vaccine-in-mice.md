A nasal spray protected mice against Covid, flu, staph infections, and allergic asthma. Stanford Medicine researchers published in Science a vaccine that activates lung T cells to fight broad threats rather than targeting specific pathogens. Human trials could begin within years, though the leap from mice remains the hardest step.
