New Delhi — India just bought production rights, not weapons.

It hands Russian exporters' market share to [Belgium](country:BE)'s defence firms in exchange for factory access.

Decades as the world's top arms importer taught New Delhi that queue position, not price, was the real constraint.

Belgian Prime Minister Bart De Wever signed the pact; named technology transfers are still unpublished.
