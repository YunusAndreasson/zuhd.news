Phnom Penh — Cambodia ranks eighth in ASEAN by GDP.

Yet it became the first Southeast Asian country to meet every global HIV target, showing delivery design can outperform wealth on epidemic control.

Richer ASEAN neighbours now face the same question Cambodia already answered on testing and treatment access.
