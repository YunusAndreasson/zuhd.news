Khartoum — [Sudan](country:SD)'s anti-war coalition rejected African Union backing for talks.

The dialogue's convenor, Gen. El Burhan, is also a party to the war.

The groups demand a ceasefire first and exclusion of parties they blame for starting the war.

The coalition asked the bloc to explain its endorsement and reaffirm a ceasefire-first stance.
