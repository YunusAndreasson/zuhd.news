Cairo — Bulldozers reached Warraq's only ferry dock Wednesday.

Every sack of cement arrives by boat, so a moved dock can halt repairs.

Residents rejected the same relocation two days earlier in talks that ended their sit-in against security measures.

Nine years into a redevelopment push, no return date exists for the negotiated ferry site.
