New York — Nvidia has lost $2.74 trillion in value since 6 October.

The Philadelphia semiconductor index fell 10.7% Friday, 43% below its June peak.

Two people familiar say the US Securities and Exchange Commission is investigating Nvidia's accounting, after Jensen Huang resigned as chief executive.

Nvidia's latest quarter beat forecasts, yet that did not stop the selling.

The Federal Reserve decides on rates on 9 December, with investors fearing further hikes.
