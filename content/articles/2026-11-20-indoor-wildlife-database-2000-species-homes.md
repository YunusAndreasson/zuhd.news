Raleigh — Researchers logged nearly 2,100 species in homes, including an alligator.

Scientists know little about man-made indoor ecosystems, said lead author Bradley Allf.

Volunteers photographed wild organisms on iNaturalist, and experts verified 23,782 records for the study in Ecology.

The dataset is public on Zenodo, so researchers can now ask why these species live indoors.
