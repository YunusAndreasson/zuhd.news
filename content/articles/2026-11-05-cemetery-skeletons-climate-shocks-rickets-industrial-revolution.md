England — Rickets was commonplace among children between roughly 1760 and 1840.

Urbanization, latitude and socioeconomic status have long been named the main drivers of the disease.

Skeletons in a centuries-old cemetery link climate shocks to rickets, which stops a growing skeleton mineralizing properly.

Whether climate outweighed pollution, housing and limited sunlight is the question the cemetery data must now answer.
