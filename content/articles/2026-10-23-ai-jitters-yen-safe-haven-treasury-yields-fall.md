Singapore — The yen hit a one-month high at 156.28 per dollar.

AI spending doubts pushed the 10-year Treasury yield to a near one-month low of 4.953%.

[Japan](country:JP)'s core inflation topped 2% in September, first time in 9 months, strengthening the case for a rate hike.

The gain proved fleeting, as the yen slid back toward 157 per dollar.

The Bank of Japan decides on 30 October, and most economists polled expect a hold.
