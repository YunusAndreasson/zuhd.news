Budapest — [Hungary](country:HU)'s first anti-Kremlin act in 16 years.

It strips Moscow of the EU's most dependable veto on [Russia](country:RU) sanctions.

Diplomats can be expelled under the Vienna Convention without formal charges, the standard method against suspected intelligence officers.

Russia promised a "harsh and painful" response Tuesday, giving no timeline.
