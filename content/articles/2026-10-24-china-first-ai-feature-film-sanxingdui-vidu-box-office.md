China — An AI-made feature grossed 301,500 yuan on opening day.

[China](country:CN)'s first licensed AI film cost roughly 90% less than comparable live-action science fiction.

Bona Film's team used Shengshu's Vidu model to generate over 1.2 million base images across two years and 100 versions.

Hand-made animation Niu Lai took 34.2 million yuan on its first day in August.

Box-office tracker Lighthouse projects just 2.078 million yuan in total.
