Geneva — One in 6 confirmed bacterial infections resisted antibiotics in 2023.

WHO warns resistance could cause 39 million deaths in 25 years and endanger routine surgery.

Only 42 of 183 countries have a national plan that is financed, implemented and monitored, WHO officials said.

Drug-resistant infections "have been taking people's lives for years without us noticing it," said Jean-Pierre Nyemazi, WHO's acting antimicrobial resistance director.

WHO released 2 diagnostic tools, including a list of 73 laboratory procedures, for the 2030 targets.
