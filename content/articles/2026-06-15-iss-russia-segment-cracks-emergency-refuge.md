Houston — 16 cracks threatened catastrophic International Space Station failure, NASA said.

[Russia](country:RU) approached the cracking PrK module with a saw, sharing no safety analysis; 5 astronauts took refuge in Crew Dragon on June 5.

Russia will now decommission the PrK module, removing what NASA warned was a sudden depressurization risk.
