Geneva — Sulphur hexafluoride warms 24,000 times more than CO2 over a century.

Grids use it as a spark suppressant, so its levels climb with electrification.

The World Meteorological Organization says CO2 hit a record 426.8 parts per million in 2025, 53% above pre-industrial levels.

COP31 opens in Turkey in November, and the UN says emissions are on track for 2.8°C of warming.
