Nairobi — Ebola killed 2 of [Kenya](country:KE)'s 9 confirmed patients.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s visa suspension on 30 October hit hundreds of Kenyan Gulf workers.

One traveller from [DR Congo](country:CD) infected 4 relatives and 4 health workers, and the ministry traced 135 contacts.

Next door the outbreak worsens: the DR Congo government counts 11,042 infections and 5,387 deaths.

Health Minister Aden Duale says prevention measures continue until 23 December.
