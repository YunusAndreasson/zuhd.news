Seychelles — Hacker moves $83 million of XRP Ripple cannot freeze.

Bitget lost $351.6 million and froze withdrawals on a platform it says has 120 million users.

Attackers compromised a backend system, spoofed transaction data and triggered authorization, Bitget's preliminary investigation found, ruling out stolen private keys.

CEO Gracy Chen says the exchange's own funds cover the loss and customers face no shortfall.

Another $75 million still sits in wallets that only a receiving exchange can restrict.
