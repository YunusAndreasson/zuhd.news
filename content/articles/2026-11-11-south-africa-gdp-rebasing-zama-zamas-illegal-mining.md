South Africa — Counting illegal miners raised [South Africa](country:ZA)'s mining output 12.5%.

Treasury now puts peak debt at 74.6% of GDP, down from 78.4% in October's budget statement.

Statistics South Africa rebased to 2022 and raised that year's GDP 4.9% to R6.99 trillion, counting illegal mining.

Treasury says revenue, spending and debt estimates are unchanged.

Treasury projects a 3.5% main budget deficit in 2026/27, down from 3.7%.
