Beijing — Climate now has its own five-year plan.

[China](country:CN)'s Ministry of Ecology and Environment, with 18 other departments, issued the first plan built as a full target system covering emissions, carbon markets and adaptation together.

Officials call it the main policy instrument through 2030; which targets bind and which merely guide remains unclear.
