New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s medical retest faces fresh paper-leak claims.

The National Testing Agency cancelled May's exam after teachers sold question papers for ₹10 lakh; the Central Bureau of Investigation is prosecuting.

The retest is June 21; the agency filed criminal complaints over the new fake-paper circulation.
