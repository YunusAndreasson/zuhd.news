Beijing — [China](country:CN) aided West Africa's 2014 Ebola outbreak.

[China](country:CN) has not filled the gap since the [US](country:US) dismantled USAID.

Outbreak response relies on a handful of donors funding labs, tracing and vaccines for affected countries.

No other donor has stepped forward to replace the withdrawn US funding.
