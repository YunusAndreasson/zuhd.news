Beijing — Bank of China now lends against AI token bills.

Chinese AI startups own little to pledge, and equity rounds now cluster around a few winners.

Inference costs grow with every customer, so banks read usage records and purchase contracts as proof of real activity.

Industrial and Commercial Bank of China offers a similar product, but disbursements stay in the low millions of yuan.

Venture investors still weigh paid demand, gross margin and retention above raw token volume.
