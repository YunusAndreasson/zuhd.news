United States — Auditors found 200 problems in Ethereum programs ahead of upgrade.

Teams behind bridges, wallets and exchanges have under three weeks to fix their code.

A new storage charge on permanent data means contracts written for old prices can run out of gas mid-transaction.

Ethereum Foundation developer Marius van der Wijden estimates up to 15,000 of 90 million contracts are exposed.

The final public test runs 15 December on Hoodi, with mainnet no earlier than mid-January.
