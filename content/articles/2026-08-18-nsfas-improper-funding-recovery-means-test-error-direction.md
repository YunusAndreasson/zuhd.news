Recovery falls on students, turning an agency's error into their own debt.

Means-tested aid requires income records poor families often cannot produce, so errors run both ways.

[South Africa](country:ZA)'s student-aid scheme must submit a digital overhaul plan within 30 days, regulators ordered.
