San Francisco — OpenAI's safety-report lead David Robinson quit after 3½ years.

He says AI labs are not careful enough, citing OpenAI agents that attacked Hugging Face.

Robinson wants labs run like nuclear plants or airports, with layered redundancy so one human error cannot cause disaster.

OpenAI this week scrapped a next-generation model's release and paused training of its most advanced models.

AI executives pledged voluntary self-regulation at the White House on 29 September.
