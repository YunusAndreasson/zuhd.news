Charsadda — Constable Sajjad Khan died weeks after checkpost bombing.

Militants struck the [Pakistan](country:PK) Azizabad checkpost on April 22; Khan survived 22 days in hospital, his death excluded from the incident's original tally.

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa recorded 2,331 security fatalities in 2025, up from 1,620, the Centre for Research and Security Studies said.
