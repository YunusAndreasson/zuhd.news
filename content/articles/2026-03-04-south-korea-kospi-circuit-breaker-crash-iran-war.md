Seoul stocks fell 8% in a single morning session. South Korea imports virtually all its oil, and the 4-day Hormuz Strait closure has cut off its primary energy supply. The Bank of Korea warned of "excessive" currency volatility; the won has broken 1,500 per dollar, a 17-year low.
