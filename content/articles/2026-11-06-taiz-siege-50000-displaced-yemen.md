Taiz — A mother of four fled the city, then came back.

Fighting between government forces and Ansarallah has reached villages outside Taiz, including Al-Nagesha.

Ansarallah took Al-Turbah, 70 kilometers south, on 4 October after government forces withdrew, and coalition airstrikes followed.

"I regret fleeing the city," said Hana Bagash, who returned on 1 November.

A bus driver told Bagash the main road to the south and west was still open.
