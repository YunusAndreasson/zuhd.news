United States — Pioneer Labs designs a 20-tonne habitat grower for Mars.

The paper says it needs no Earth inputs after landing and fits Starship's payload.

A 40 kW reactor melts ice, and a perchlorate-tolerant strain of Cupriavidus necator grows in Martian regolith nutrients.

The bacterium has been tested only in regolith simulant on Earth.

Mars Seed would deliver one full habitat roughly every 4 Earth years once running.
