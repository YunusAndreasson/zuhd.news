Manila — Philippine senator Jinggoy Estrada surrendered on plunder charges.

The Sandiganbayan anti-graft court ordered his arrest; Estrada frames it as fallout from the Marcos-Duterte dynastic feud, not independent prosecution.

Estrada claims political motivation drove the charges; the Sandiganbayan has yet to set trial dates.
