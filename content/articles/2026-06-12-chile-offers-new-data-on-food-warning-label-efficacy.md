Santiago — Chile's black warning labels cut girls' obesity risk 2.9%.

[Chile](country:CL) introduced octagonal black labels in 2016; a Lancet study of 300,000 children confirmed the drop within 18 months of rollout.

Researchers expect stricter 2018 rules to show larger effects; dozens of countries have since adopted the model.
