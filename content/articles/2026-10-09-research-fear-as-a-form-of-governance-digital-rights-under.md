Beirut — [Tunisia](country:TN)'s Decree-Law 54 anchors a digital crackdown, SMEX finds.

SMEX says vague laws let governments decide retrospectively what online speech counts as a crime.

Smear campaigns often precede arrests and raids in all three countries, SMEX says, based on 20 interviews and a review of laws.

[Egypt](country:EG) runs the region's most centralised model, while [Iraq](country:IQ)'s divided authority leaves some room but little safety.

The report feeds a SMEX-led initiative to draft a regional digital rights strategy for civil society.
