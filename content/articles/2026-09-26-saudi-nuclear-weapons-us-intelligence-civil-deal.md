Washington — A classified US assessment says [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) has not ruled out nuclear weapons, Russian state outlet RT reported.

The assessment alarmed senators reviewing the July civilian nuclear deal.

The deal allows enrichment up to 20% later and skips the International Atomic Energy Agency's Additional Protocol.

The State Department says Washington will not support a Saudi weapons programme.

Congress's 90-day review began 24 August.
