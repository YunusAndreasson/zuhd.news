Orange County — Police were warned about Flock hearings despite no credible threat.

The bulletin lists emotional confrontations, large turnouts and media presence as public safety risks at council meetings.

The Orange County Intelligence Assessment Center, a government fusion center, sent it to California police in July, and a Freedom of Information Act request released it.

The bulletin says the Los Angeles Police Department's decision to end its Flock contract may raise turnout at other councils.
