Afrin — She lit candles for a bulldozed shrine.

Returning after eight years, Yazidi families find sacred sites erased, not merely abandoned.

[Turkey](country:TR)'s 2018 offensive emptied [Syria](country:SY)'s Afrin of Kurds and Yazidis, destroying shrines and reallocating their land.

Damascus's new government has not said whether returnees can reclaim shrine sites or homes.
