Japan imports 95% of its oil through the now-closed Hormuz. US Interior Secretary Doug Burgum offered to replace that supply at a 17-nation Tokyo energy forum where participants expect $30 billion in deals. South Korea's Hanwha signed a 1.5-million-ton LNG deal with Venture Global, a US exporter, with Japan and others expected to follow.
