Dubai — Rights monitors documented 375 Abu Dhabi arrests for filming Iranian strikes.

Since February, the [UAE](country:AE), [Kuwait](country:KW), Qatar, and Bahrain used cybercrime laws to prosecute hundreds who shared the footage.

Bahrain sentenced photographer Sayed Baqer Al-Kamel to 10 years; more than 100 regional social media accounts remain geo-blocked.
