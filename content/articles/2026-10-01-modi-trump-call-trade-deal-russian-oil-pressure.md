New Delhi — Modi and Trump spoke Wednesday amid a Russian-oil sanctions threat.

Russian state media RT says a new [US](country:US) law allows tariffs up to 100% on Russian-crude buyers.

RT puts Russian crude shipments to [India](country:IN) at about 2.1 million barrels a day in August, expected to fall to 1.75 million in September.

RT says the Indian readout omitted the law, though Foreign Minister Jaishankar raised it with Secretary of State Rubio last week.

Trade talks on the first phase of the bilateral agreement, framed in February, have stretched for months.
