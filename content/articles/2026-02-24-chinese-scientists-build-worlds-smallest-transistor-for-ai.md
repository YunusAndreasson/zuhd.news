Peking University shrank a transistor gate to 1 nanometre. Its ferroelectric design computes and stores data in one component, using 1/10th the power of existing chips by eliminating data shuttling. The team filed Chinese patents for the full process, sidestepping Western export controls aimed at curbing China's chip development.
