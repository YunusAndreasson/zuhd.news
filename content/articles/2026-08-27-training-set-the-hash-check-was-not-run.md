A lawsuit alleges xAI trained Grok on child sexual abuse material.

It could force courts to treat model weights as possession of banned material.

The National Center for Missing & Exploited Children's hash lists can flag known abuse images without human review, and the suit says xAI skipped that check.

Doe is asking the court to order xAI to destroy all Grok-generated abuse material.
