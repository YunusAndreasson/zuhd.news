Durban — Gunmen killed at least 11 people at a house party, including a pregnant woman.

Soldiers joined police in violent hotspots in March, and Cyril Ramaphosa calls crime among [South Africa](country:ZA)'s biggest threats.

Attackers entered shortly before 11pm Tuesday, forced everyone into one room and opened fire, then burned a victim's car.

Police say some victims were on Crime Intelligence's radar and a feud between rival groupings cannot be ruled out.

Premier Thamsanqa Ntuli said 2 people survived and the motive is not yet clear.
