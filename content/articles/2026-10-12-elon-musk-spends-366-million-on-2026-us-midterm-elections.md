Washington — Musk has spent $366 million this cycle on Republicans.

It tops his roughly $290 million in 2024 and likely makes him this year's largest donor.

Of the $276 million since July, $158 million went to the Senate Leadership Fund and $76 million to his own America PAC.

"Republicans are hoping to buy these elections," said Lauren French, spokesperson for Democrats' Senate Majority PAC.

Polymarket traders put a Republican Senate with a Democratic House at 28% before the 3 November vote.
