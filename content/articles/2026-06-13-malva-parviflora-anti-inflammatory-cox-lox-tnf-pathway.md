Cairo — Egyptian mallow yields 2 anti-inflammatory compounds, study finds.

Cassipourol and β-sitosterol, from the hexane fraction, inhibit cyclooxygenase and lipoxygenase enzymes and the tumor necrosis factor cascade.

The study used molecular docking and in vitro validation; clinical trials have not begun.
