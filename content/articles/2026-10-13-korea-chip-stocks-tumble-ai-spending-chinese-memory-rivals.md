Seoul — Samsung and SK Hynix dragged [South Korea](country:KR)'s benchmark stock index down 5.5%.

[Japan](country:JP)'s Nikkei 225 and Taiwanese shares fell nearly 2%, and SoftBank lost more than 8%.

Chinese media reported [China](country:CN)'s ChangXin Memory Technologies unveiled a design breakthrough that would let memory chips store more data without growing.

Investors now watch whether the company ships the design, which so far rests on Chinese media reports.
