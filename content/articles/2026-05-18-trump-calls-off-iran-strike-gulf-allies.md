Washington — Gulf capitals halted Trump's Tuesday airstrike on [Iran](country:IR).

[Qatar](country:QA), [Saudi Arabia](country:SA), and the [United Arab Emirates](country:AE) — the 3 Gulf states that host US military bases — persuaded Washington to stand down.

Trump ordered the military ready "on a moment's notice" if negotiations fail.
