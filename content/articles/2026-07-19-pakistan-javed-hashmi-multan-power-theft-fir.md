Multan — Police booked a veteran politician.

Multan Electric Power Company accuses Javed Hashmi and his three sons-in-law of looping meters, alleging losses near Rs990,000 combined.

Hashmi says he won't seek bail, testing whether [Pakistan](country:PK)'s power-theft crackdown reaches the influential too.
