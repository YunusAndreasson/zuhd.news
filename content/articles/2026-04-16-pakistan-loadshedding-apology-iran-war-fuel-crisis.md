Islamabad — Pakistan's power minister apologized for uncontrollable electricity blackouts. A 2,500-megawatt shortfall has 2 causes: the Iran war cut fuel imports, and simultaneous drought has reduced hydropower generation. No alternative fuel route exists while the war continues; IMF austerity constraints rule out emergency import financing.
