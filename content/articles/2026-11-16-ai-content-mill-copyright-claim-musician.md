United States — A mill running 500 AI channels copyright-struck a human musician.

Her cover drew 125,000 views, and DJs sampled it into tracks, one with 4 million.

Media Nov Records struck her 50-second cover of a public domain folk song, and YouTube took it down.

"I can't get in touch with a human being," said Samantha Shokin, a writer and musician.

Shokin's cover remains off YouTube while the claim stands.
