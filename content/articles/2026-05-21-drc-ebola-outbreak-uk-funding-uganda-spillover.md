Kinshasa — Ebola has crossed from Congo into Uganda.

[Congo](country:CD)'s outbreak stands at 600 suspected cases and 139 suspected deaths — 51 lab-confirmed — and WHO warns it will keep growing.

[Britain](country:GB) committed $26.87M — the largest single donor pledge so far — to fund surveillance and frontline worker protection.
