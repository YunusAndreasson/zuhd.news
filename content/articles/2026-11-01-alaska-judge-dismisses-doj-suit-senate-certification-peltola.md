Anchorage — Alaska's commingled ballots sank the Justice Department's certification suit.

It leaves Alaska free to certify its result even if Republican Senator Dan Sullivan loses.

Judge Sharon Gleason found the government offered no plan to segregate ballots cast for namesake candidate Daniel J. Sullivan without breaching secrecy.

The [US](country:US) Justice Department said it will appeal, while Senator Sullivan said he will accept the results.

Alaska votes Tuesday in one of seven Senate toss-ups that decide control of the chamber.
