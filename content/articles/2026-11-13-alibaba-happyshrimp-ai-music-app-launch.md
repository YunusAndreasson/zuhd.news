China — Alibaba's app composes and sings a 3-minute song in 1 minute.

HappyShrimp, now on iOS and Android, searches current events when writing lyrics.

Users describe a mood, story or style, and the AI writes lyrics, composes, arranges and sings.

A hands-on test found the vocals steady but synthetic, without a human voice's layers.

A "make the same" feature lets users remix a published song with their own details.
