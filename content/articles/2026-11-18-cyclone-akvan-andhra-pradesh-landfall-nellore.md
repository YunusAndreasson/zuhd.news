Nellore — Cyclone Akvan is forecast to hit Andhra Pradesh on 20 November with 145 km/h gusts.

It is the 3rd Bay of Bengal cyclone in under a month, after Arnab and Murasu.

[India](country:IN)'s weather department forecasts 120-130 km/h sustained winds and over 21 cm of rain in north coastal Andhra Pradesh.

Odisha's Rayagada district will evacuate 55 landslide-prone villages, and Tamil Nadu has ordered fishermen off the sea.
