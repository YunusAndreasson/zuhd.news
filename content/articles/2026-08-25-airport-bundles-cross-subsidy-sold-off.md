New Delhi — [India](country:IN) will privatise 11 airports in five paired bundles.

Small-airport service now hinges on a concession contract, not a ministry mandate.

Bidders earn from retail, parking and fuel at the profitable airport, cross-subsidising the smaller one for 50 years, minutes show.

The aviation ministry must still set tariff rules and a cap on bundles per bidder.
