Gombe — Armed men blocked Isa Pantami's convoy with burning tyres.

Amnesty calls it a deliberate bid to keep the opposition candidate out before [Nigeria](country:NG)'s 2027 elections.

Amnesty says video shows thugs smashing windscreens on Jekadafari Road, and suspects they work for the ruling All Progressives Congress.

Amnesty sees no sign of arrests as campaigning for the 2027 general election intensifies.
