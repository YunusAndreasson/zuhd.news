Washington — Up to 2,000 82nd Airborne soldiers are mobilising. New fighter jets and attack aircraft have already arrived in the Middle East, even as US-Iran ceasefire talks proceed in Pakistan. 2 carrier strike groups — USS George H.W. Bush and USS Boxer — are in transit, weeks from arrival.
