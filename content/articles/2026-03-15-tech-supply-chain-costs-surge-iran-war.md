Samsung's raw material costs rose 8.8% last year. The Iran war is pushing oil and logistics costs higher, compounding a memory price surge already driven by the AI boom. With LG Electronics also up 5.6%, analysts say costs will climb further in Q2 as Hormuz stays closed.
