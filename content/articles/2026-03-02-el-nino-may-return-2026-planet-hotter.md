2026 may set a new global temperature record. The US National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration puts El Niño odds at 60% from July, which could add 0.2°C to a planet that already passed 1.5°C warming last year. Southeast Asia, East Africa, and the Pacific face drought if it develops, with peak warming expected in 2027.
