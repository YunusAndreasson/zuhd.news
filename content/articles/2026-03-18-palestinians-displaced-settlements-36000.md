Israeli settlements displaced 36,000 West Bank Palestinians last year. Settler attacks rose to 1,732 incidents as Israel accelerated West Bank annexation, a UN review of October 2024 to October 2025 found. Israel's Geneva mission promised a response to the UN finding of potential ethnic cleansing, with no timeline given.
