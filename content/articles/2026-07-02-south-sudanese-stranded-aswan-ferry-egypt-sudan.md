Aswan — 76 people, seven days, one anchored ferry.

The group boarded [Sudan](country:SD)'s "voluntary return" trip meant to pass through to South Sudan, but Sudan barred entry and [Egypt](country:EG) refused re-entry.

Three governments are negotiating; no disembarkation date has been set for those still aboard.
