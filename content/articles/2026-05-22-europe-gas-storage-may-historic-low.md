Brussels — 36.99% — EU gas stores set 12-year May low.

European buyers replaced [Russia](country:RU)'s pipeline gas with liquefied natural gas competing for Asian cargoes; the Iran war made injection uneconomic, Gas Infrastructure Europe data shows.

EU rules require 90% by November; 68bn cubic meters must be injected, against 55bn last year.
