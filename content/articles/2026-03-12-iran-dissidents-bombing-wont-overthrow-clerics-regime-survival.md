Bombing tightened Iran's clerical grip, not loosened it. The National Council of Resistance of Iran — Iran's main exile opposition — said 12 days of strikes have consolidated the Revolutionary Guards rather than weakened them. The council predicted protests bloodily suppressed in January will resume once bombing stops, calling popular pressure the only path to change in Tehran.
