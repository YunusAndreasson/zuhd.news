Brussels — Hungary's veto froze €6.6 billion for Ukraine since 2023.

[Ukraine](country:UA) faces a $27 billion defence budget shortfall that threatens drone deliveries due early 2027.

The Peace Facility reimburses states for donated weapons, and one member's veto froze it until Hungary's April change of government.

Not every state backs Germany's demand that the €4.7 billion reimbursement pot go entirely to Ukraine.

EU defence ministers take the final decision Monday, and Zelenskyy wants the budget gap closed by November.
