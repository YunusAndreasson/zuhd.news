Abuja — Pilgrims must pay 26 September, nine months early.

Committing naira to a riyal-priced package this early shifts exchange risk onto pilgrims.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA) sets the registration calendar that [Nigeria](country:NG)'s commission merely relays to pilgrims.

The naira has slid 1.7% this week, with a remittance deadline still due in December.
