New Delhi — A voice vote passed with no recorded ayes or nays.

Mineral-rich states lose the power to tax mining rights, reversing where royalty revenue lands.

This bill legislates away a 2024 Supreme Court ruling that let states levy such taxes 8-1.

The bill now moves to the Rajya Sabha, where opposition states will press for changes.
