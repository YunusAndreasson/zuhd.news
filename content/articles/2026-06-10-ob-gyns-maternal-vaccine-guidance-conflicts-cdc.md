Washington — 13 medical bodies restored vaccines the CDC cut.

The American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists led the effort, restoring COVID-19 and flu shots dropped under Kennedy's CDC.

A pediatric association lawsuit won a March injunction reversing some CDC changes; that case remains ongoing.
