Osogbo — Ten days from the vote, rivals dispute a killings count.

Governor Ademola Adeleke's council says seven Accord members died and 45 more in 2022; the ruling All Progressives Congress disputes his statement, not the deaths.

Osun votes August 15, a rehearsal for [Nigeria](country:NG)'s 2027 general election.
