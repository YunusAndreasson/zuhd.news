Israel killed 26 Lebanese paramedics in 12 days. Lebanon's health ministry accused Israel of targeting ambulance crews during rescue operations, a practice prohibited under international humanitarian law. Israel dismissed the claim, accusing Hezbollah of using ambulances militarily, with no independent investigation announced.
