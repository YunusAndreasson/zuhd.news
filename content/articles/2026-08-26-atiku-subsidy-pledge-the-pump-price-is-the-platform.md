Abuja — Atiku Abubakar pledges to restore [Nigeria](country:NG)'s petrol subsidy in 2027.

It reverses the policy that freed the naira but tripled living costs since 2023.

President Tinubu ended the subsidy on his first day in office, floating the naira alongside it.

Atiku, once a subsidy-removal advocate himself, calls his reversal a "targeted" restoration.
