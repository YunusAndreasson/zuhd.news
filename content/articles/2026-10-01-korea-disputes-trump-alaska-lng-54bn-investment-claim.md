Seoul — [South Korea](country:KR) says it never pledged $54bn to an Alaska gas project.

The [US](country:US) counted that sum inside $200bn of Korean energy investment it announced Wednesday.

Seoul's trade ministry says no decision has been made on whether to invest or how much, and any stake depends on commercial viability.

Seoul did confirm a $22.3bn gas-fired power plant in Texas, supplying co-located data centres.

South Korea will back the 1,300km pipeline only if Glenfarne Group's long-stalled project proves viable.
