Zhuhai — Amicro says 8 of 10 top robot-vacuum makers use it.

Its chips sit inside machines from Xiaomi, iRobot, Ecovacs, Philips, SEB and Anker.

The chips run mapping software that lets a robot build a floor plan while locating itself inside it.

Amicro's Hong Kong shares are expected to begin trading on 12 November.
