Ukraine has deployed 201 anti-drone specialists across Gulf states. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy is marketing the expertise — developed fighting Russian drones in Donbas — to Gulf states seeking protection from Iranian drones. Ukraine says it produces 2,000 drone interceptors daily, with half available to Gulf allies once its own front line is covered.
