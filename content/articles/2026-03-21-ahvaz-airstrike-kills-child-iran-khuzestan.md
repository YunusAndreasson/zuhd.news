An airstrike on Nowruz killed a child in Ahvaz. Khuzestan province produces 90% of Iran's oil and is home to an Arab-majority population that Tehran has long marginalised. Iranian authorities have not acknowledged civilian deaths in the province as Israeli strikes are set to intensify next week.
