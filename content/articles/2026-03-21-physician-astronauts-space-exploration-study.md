On deep-space missions, communication delays make Earth-based medical care impossible. Physician-astronauts hold all spaceflight endurance records, a pattern a Royal Society of Medicine study attributes to their high-stakes decision-making training. A Lancet study argues physician-astronauts will become mission-critical as space agencies plan crewed missions beyond the Moon.
