Warsaw — [Poland](country:PL) will pay Palantir about $300 million over 5 years.

Polish commanders will weigh battlefield decisions using a [US](country:US) company's software.

The system, called Saturn, fuses drone, satellite and radar data and flags objects of military interest.

The contract limits Palantir's access to confidential military information.

Palantir has not disclosed when it will book the revenue or what profit the deal carries.
