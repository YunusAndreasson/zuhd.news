Hanoi — [Azerbaijan](country:AZ) and [Vietnam](country:VN) pledged oil and gas cooperation.

Vietnam's prime minister called bilateral trade, which exceeded $150 million in 2025, relatively low.

Both governments pledged rail freight links from Vietnam to Azerbaijan across Central Asia via the Trans-Caspian corridor.

Vietnam will open an embassy in Baku, and both sides plan events for the 2027 anniversary of ties.
