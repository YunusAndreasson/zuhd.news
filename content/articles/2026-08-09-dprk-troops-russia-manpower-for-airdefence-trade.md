Kyiv — Zelensky claims up to 50,000 North Korean troops are coming.

He says Pyongyang trades soldiers for missile know-how, technology that could later threaten Seoul and Tokyo.

Zelensky did not cite a source for the figure, up from his 30,000 estimate in July, under [North Korea](country:KP)'s 2024 pact with [Russia](country:RU).

He is pressing South Korea for air-defence support as talks with Seoul continue.
