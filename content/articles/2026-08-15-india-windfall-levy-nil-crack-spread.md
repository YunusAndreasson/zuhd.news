New Delhi — Petrol's export tax fell from ₹3.5 to nil.

Domestic pump prices are unchanged — only exported fuel loses the levy.

[India](country:IN) imposed the tax in March, resetting it every two weeks against Brent, at $93.26 as of Aug. 11.

Diesel and jet-fuel duties, still positive, face the same review within two weeks.
