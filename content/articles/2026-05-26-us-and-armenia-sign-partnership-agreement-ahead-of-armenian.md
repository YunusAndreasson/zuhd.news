Yerevan — [US](country:US) and [Armenia](country:AM) signed a strategic partnership Tuesday.

Washington also agreed on critical minerals and a 43-km transit corridor through southern Armenia, bypassing [Russia](country:RU) and [Iran](country:IR) to reach [Turkey](country:TR).

Russia threatened to raise Armenia's gas price; Armenians vote June 7 with Pashinyan's pro-West party facing pro-Russian rivals.
