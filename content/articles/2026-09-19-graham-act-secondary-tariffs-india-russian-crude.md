New Delhi — Oil from [Russia](country:RU) made up over half [India](country:IN)'s July imports.

A new [US](country:US) law threatens 100% tariffs on countries buying that crude.

The tariff, not a direct sanction, would tax unrelated Indian exports like textiles over refiners' oil purchases.

New Delhi says it will protect its energy security and trade interests.
