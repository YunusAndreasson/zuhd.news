Beirut — Israeli forces entered Lebanon, opening a 3rd front. Israeli airstrikes killed more than 1,000 Lebanese and displaced a million over 18 days, targeting bridges and medical facilities systematically. Israeli ground units are advancing into southern Lebanese villages, the first ground incursion since the 2006 war.
