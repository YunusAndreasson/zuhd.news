Tbilisi — Life on Europa may already be ours.

Researcher Zaza Osmanov calculated that trillions of Earth dust grains, each potentially carrying bacteria, could reach Europa's surface and migrate through ice cracks to its ocean.

Europa missions face a detection problem: alien life found there may prove to be Earth microbes' descendants.
