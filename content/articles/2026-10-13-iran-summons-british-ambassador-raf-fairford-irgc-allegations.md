Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) threatened to reconsider relations with Britain, Iranian state news agency Mehr reported.

Assistant foreign minister Alireza Yousefi summoned ambassador Hugo Shorter and demanded evidence for Britain's allegations over Royal Air Force Fairford, Mehr said.

Yousefi called the allegations baseless and said [Britain](country:GB) bears responsibility for joining [US](country:US) and Israeli attacks on Iran.

Shorter said he would convey Iran's position to London, Mehr said.
