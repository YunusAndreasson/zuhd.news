Venezuela ended 7 years of broken US relations. Acting President Delcy Rodriguez cited "mutual respect," but Venezuela's oil has become a US energy lifeline as Iran's war chokes global supply. Rodriguez has governed since Maduro's removal and must now define that mutual respect in concrete terms with Washington.
