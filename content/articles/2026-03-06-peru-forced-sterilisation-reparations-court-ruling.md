An Inter-American court ordered Peru $340,000 in reparations. The ruling covers one of 314,000 women the government of then-President Alberto Fujimori forcibly sterilized in the 1990s, targeting the poor and Indigenous. His daughter Keiko Fujimori is running in Peru's April 2026 presidential election.
