Sochi — Ukrainian sea drones hit a vessel in Sochi.

It is the first known drone attack on Putin's Black Sea resort city.

The support vessel Nefrit serviced [Russia](country:RU)'s offshore oil and gas operations, part of Kyiv's campaign against Moscow's energy infrastructure.

Kyiv says it will keep targeting vessels it links to Russia's war economy.
