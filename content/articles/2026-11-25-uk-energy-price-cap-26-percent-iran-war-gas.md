London — Typical bills rise £443 to £2,166 a year from January.

[Britain](country:GB)'s households now pay more per unit of gas than in the 2022 crisis, with less state support.

Ofgem passes wholesale costs into the cap quarterly, and wholesale gas rose about 33% in three months on [Iran](country:IR) war disruption and cold.

The government offers a £100 rebate, only to households in council tax bands A to D.

At £2,166, typical bills sit £334 below the £2,500 level at which the government intervened in 2022.
