Khasab — Projectiles hit tankers on consecutive days off [Oman](country:OM).

[Iran](country:IR)'s navy says one tanker caught fire after striking a naval mine east of the strait.

Hormuz traffic averaged 2.3 ships a day as of 15 November, 39% below its 90-day normal of 3.7.

The UK maritime agency called both Saturday projectiles unidentified and could not say whether they hit one vessel.

Polymarket traders give a [US](country:US)-Iran Hormuz agreement by 31 December an 11% chance, down 11 points in a week.
