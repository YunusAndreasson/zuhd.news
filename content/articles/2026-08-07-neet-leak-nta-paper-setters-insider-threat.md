New Delhi — No one frisked experts entering the confidential unit.

Three National Testing Agency subject experts allegedly memorized exam questions and copied them onto chits to evade detection, a Central Bureau of Investigation chargesheet says.

All 13 accused remain in judicial custody as the chargesheet's allegations await trial in Delhi.
