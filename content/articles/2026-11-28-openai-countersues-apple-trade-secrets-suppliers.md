San Jose — OpenAI alleges Apple pressured its hardware suppliers.

OpenAI says its AI devices threaten to outcompete Apple on Apple's own turf.

One supplier shifted OpenAI's metal finishing overnight after Apple objected, and another halted planned work over threats, the filing says.

Apple denies suppressing competition and says a court already ordered OpenAI to stop using Apple's confidential information.

OpenAI seeks damages, court orders and a jury trial.
