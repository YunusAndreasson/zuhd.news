The task force lacks a legal framework, which members have until September 2027 to settle, including who prosecutes captured pirates.

The Houthis told ships on Friday to evacuate Jeddah port before an imminent attack.

Polymarket traders give a Saudi-Houthi ceasefire by 31 December an 11% chance, down 11 points in a week.
