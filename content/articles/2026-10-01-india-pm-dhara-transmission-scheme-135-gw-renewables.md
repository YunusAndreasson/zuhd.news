New Delhi — [India](country:IN) approved ₹1.86 lakh crore for transmission lines.

It lets states move 135 GW of renewable power, toward a 500 GW target for 2030.

₹50,000 crore buys 50 GWh of battery storage, which holds surplus solar and wind output until generation falls.

Power Grid shares fell 1.4% and NTPC 1.1% Thursday despite the approval.

The scheme is due to be set up by the 2032-33 fiscal year.
