Visakhapatnam — 75% of INS Mahendragiri was built at home.

The Nilgiri-class frigate is [India](country:IN)'s sixth Project 17A warship, built by Mazagon Dock to cut reliance on Russian and Western naval suppliers.

5 sister ships have entered service since January 2025, with further Indo-Pacific deployments planned.
