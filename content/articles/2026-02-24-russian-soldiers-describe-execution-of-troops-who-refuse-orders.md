1 of 79 mobilised Russian soldiers survived. 4 ex-soldiers told the BBC they saw commanders shoot troops at point-blank range for refusing human-wave assaults that trade lives for meters of ground. Moscow denied the claims; the soldiers remain wanted fugitives for fleeing a war they say executes its own.
