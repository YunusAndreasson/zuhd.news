Bolzano — 5,300-year-old yeasts still grow on Ötzi's body.

Researchers at Eurac confirmed via DNA damage patterns that the yeasts predate modern access; cold-adapted relatives grow in alpine glaciers nearby.

Whether the yeasts survived 5 millennia dormant or colonized Ötzi post-exhumation from the same glacier remains unresolved.
