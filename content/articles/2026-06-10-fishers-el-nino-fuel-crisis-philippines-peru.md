Lima — Peru's artisanal fishers have grounded their boats.

El Niño scatters fish stocks while fuel costs have risen over 25%, fishers say, closing the margin on every trip.

[The Philippines](country:PH) and [Peru](country:PE) have no announced relief programs for fishers absorbing both shocks simultaneously.
