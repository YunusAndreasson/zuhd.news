Kyiv — Drones killed 1 person and shut the Southern Bridge.

It is 1 of 8 Dnipro crossings linking the capital's east and west banks.

[Russia](country:RU)'s fast-moving, jet-powered drones hit the bridge repeatedly, and authorities restricted the Paton Bridge to one direction, gridlocking commuters.

"The enemy is tearing Kyiv apart," said Mayor Vitali Klitschko.

President Volodymyr Zelensky is pressing allies for tougher sanctions on drone components as winter threatens power and heating.
