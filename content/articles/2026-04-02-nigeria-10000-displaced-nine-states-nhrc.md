Abuja — 10,884 Nigerians displaced in 2 months.

Benue absorbed 4,178 from herder raids on farming communities; Borno took 3,411 from insurgent attacks, both drivers active for years without resolution.

6,003 displaced are children; Nigeria's human rights commission dropped 2 states from monitoring for lack of funding, understating the true count.
