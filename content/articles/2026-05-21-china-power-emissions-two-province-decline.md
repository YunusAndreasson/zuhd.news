Beijing — [China](country:CN)'s power-sector emissions fell in only 2 provinces last year.

Inner Mongolia and Shandong cut fossil output by 42 TWh, exceeding the national net decline; 13 others recorded increases, China Electricity Council data show.

Transmission bottlenecks wasted roughly 13% of potential wind and solar output, capping the national gain.
