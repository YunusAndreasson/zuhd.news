Israel destroyed or damaged 1,160 of Gaza's 1,244 mosques. The targeting is systematic: 93% of Gaza's mosques hit, Al-Aqsa periodically sealed, and Israeli settlers raiding West Bank mosques during Ramadan. Israel's parliament will vote on National Security Minister Itamar Ben Gvir's legislation to silence the call to prayer.
