Nyala — Darfur sheep fetch up to SDG 450,000 at source.

The same animal sells for above SDG 2 million in central [Sudan](country:SD) — war roads trap supply in the south, livestock expert Bakht said.

Traders are pressing authorities to reopen war-blocked routes before Eid demand peaks elsewhere.
