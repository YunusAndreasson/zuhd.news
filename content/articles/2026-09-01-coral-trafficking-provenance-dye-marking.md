The wildlife trade treaty CITES logged 1.3 million coral exports worldwide.

A quarter of [US](country:US) coral imports were wild-caught from reefs in [Indonesia](country:ID) already bleaching.

Farmed and poached coral look identical, so treaty paperwork is the only difference traffickers can forge.

Experts propose dyeing farmed coral, though no treaty member has adopted the marking rule.
