London — An enzyme helps lung tumors dodge cell death.

USP32 stabilizes MAPK12, blocking ferroptosis and autophagy — the self-destruct pathways that make tumors sensitive to therapy, researchers found.

Blocking the USP32-MAPK12 axis restored cell death in mouse tumors, a candidate route to new targeted drugs.
