Colombo — Petrol costs 25% more in Colombo as of Sunday. Sri Lanka imports nearly all its fuel through Iran war-disrupted routes, with an IMF recovery plan that offers no buffer for energy shocks. President Dissanayake faces demands for fuel subsidies from unions that toppled former President Rajapaksa in 2022 through mass protests.
