Jamtara — Cyber scams have become normal household income.

The scams target victims in the [US](country:US) and the West, cross-border reporting shows.

Jamtara in [India](country:IN)'s Jharkhand state and Mewat on the Haryana-Rajasthan border are among the areas involved.

Local labor markets offer few alternatives as paid work, leaving the practice likely to continue.
