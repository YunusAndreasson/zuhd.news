London — Antibiotic-surviving bacteria aren't dormant — they're metabolically reprogrammed. Persister cells survive antibiotics not through resistance but by rewiring metabolism — a finding that invalidates the "slow them down" treatment model. New treatments must target active metabolic pathways, not the dormancy state current drugs assume.
