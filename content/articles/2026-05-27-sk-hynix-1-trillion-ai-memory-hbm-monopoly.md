Seoul — SK Hynix holds 57% of global HBM revenue, Counterpoint Research data show.

Each HBM generation demands deeper NVIDIA co-design, widening [South Korea](country:KR)'s yield lead; Samsung trails at 22% and Micron at 21%.

SK Hynix filed for a US ADR this year; it projects HBM demand will exceed supply through 2027.
