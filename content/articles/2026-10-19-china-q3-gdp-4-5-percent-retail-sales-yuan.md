Beijing — Retail sales rose 1.1%, more than double the 0.5% forecast.

Third-quarter GDP grew 4.5%, the statistics bureau said, and Japan's Nikkei rose 1.8%.

[China](country:CN) is the world's factory and a major importer of industrial commodities, so strong data tends to encourage risk-taking.

The Federal Reserve is expected to hold its 4.75% to 5% rate this month.
