Paris — Police arrested 219 people across [France](country:FR) on Saturday.

Turnout is falling, yet unions have called another strike for Tuesday.

The Interior Ministry counted 106,000 marchers, down from 146,000 a week earlier, while student unions claim 200,000.

The government offered a 0.6% minimum wage rise, which unions rejected as crumbs.

Tuesday is the eighth anniversary of the Yellow Vest movement's first roundabout blockades.
