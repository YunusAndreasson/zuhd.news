Cape Canaveral — 2 lunar landers are racing to Shackleton Crater. Both target water ice in Shackleton's permanent shadow — frozen deposits that could supply rocket fuel and water for a lunar base. If both landers reach the rim, only the Outer Space Treaty's untested "due regard" clause applies.
