Seoul — Two Chanel bags bought a channel into the presidency.

The verdict marks the first time [South Korea](country:KR)'s courts confirmed a religious group bought political influence.

A court sentenced Unification Church leader Han Hak-ja, 83, to two years for gifting ex-first lady Kim Keon Hee luxury goods and funneling illegal donations.

Han's church plans to appeal, and a $19 million cash stash from her safe remains unexplained.
