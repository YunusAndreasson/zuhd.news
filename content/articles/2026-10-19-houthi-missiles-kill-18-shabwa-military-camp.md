Shabwa — Houthi missiles killed at least 18 recruits at a training camp.

Fighting in [Yemen](country:YE) has escalated since the Houthis seized Red Sea coast territory last month.

Three ballistic missiles hit the al-Alam camp during a drill, and officials counted more than 30 wounded.

Houthi spokesperson Yahya Saree says any base with a Saudi-backed presence is now a legitimate target.

Polymarket traders give a Houthi–[Saudi](country:SA) ceasefire by 31 October an 11% chance, down 3 points in a week.
