Bethesda — The National Institutes of Health will stop sharing exact grant scores.

Losing the score data makes bias by institution or career stage harder to detect.

Proposals will instead be sorted into 3 tiers, based on peer review plus agency health priorities.

Agency official Jenna Norton warns hidden scores could shield political interference in funding decisions.
