China disciplined nearly 1 million officials in 2025 alone. President Xi Jinping launched the campaign in 2012; analysts now say it functions as a loyalty purge, not a corruption cleanup. The party's anti-graft body purged 10 senior officials in January 2026 and indicated further reviews are under way.
