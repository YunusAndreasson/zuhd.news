Shehbaz ordered remittances 100% digitised Tuesday.

[Pakistan](country:PK) leans on diaspora remittances as its top dollar source, much of it moving through untracked informal hawala networks.

Officials have not said how millions of workers using informal channels will be moved onto digital rails.
