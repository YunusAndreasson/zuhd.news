Washington — SHEIN will pay $100 million to settle review-suppression claims.

The US Federal Trade Commission alleges the retailer buried bad reviews and made unsupported cotton-origin claims.

The complaint says SHEIN shipped nearly 1 million US packages daily with only 5 phone agents as of September 2025.

SHEIN says the deal is no admission and disagrees with aspects of the regulator's characterization.

A federal judge must approve the settlement before the money reaches shoppers.
