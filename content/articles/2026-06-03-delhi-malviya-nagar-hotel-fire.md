New Delhi — Delhi hotel fire killed 21, including 17 foreigners.

Flourish Stay ran 25 rooms on a 6-room licence, its single staircase and sealed windows acting as a chimney, fire officers said.

Most victims came from Africa and [Bangladesh](country:BD); the owner faces culpable homicide charges.
