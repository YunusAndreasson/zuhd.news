Yerevan — [Armenia](country:AM) opened rail trade with [Turkey](country:TR) via Georgia for the first time.

The Akhalkalaki–Kars corridor, built in 2017, was barred to Armenia until the post-2020 Azerbaijan peace process unlocked normalization.

Pashinyan said direct links to [Azerbaijan](country:AZ) and [Iran](country:IR) via Nakhichevan are planned under a regional connectivity framework.
