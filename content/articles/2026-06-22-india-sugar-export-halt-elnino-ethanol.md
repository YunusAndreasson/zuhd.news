New Delhi — Exports banned; mill stocks to hit a 30-year low.

[India](country:IN) averaged 6.8 million tonnes in exports through 2023; El Niño is cutting cane planting while an ethanol mandate pulls supply into fuel, industry estimates show.

Traders warn India could become a net importer if El Niño cuts the 2027-28 cane crop further.
