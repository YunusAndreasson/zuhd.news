Sweden boarded a 2nd shadow fleet vessel this week. Shadow fleet tankers obscure ownership to carry Russian crude in defiance of Western oil sanctions, and Sweden is one of the few states that boards them. Sweden's 2 boardings in a week are testing whether Baltic neighbours will enforce the same rules.
