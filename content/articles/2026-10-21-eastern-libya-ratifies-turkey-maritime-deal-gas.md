Tobruk — [Libya](country:LY)'s eastern parliament voted unanimously for [Turkey](country:TR)'s 2019 maritime deal.

Both rival Libyan governments now back maritime boundaries [Greece](country:GR) and [Cyprus](country:CY) call unlawful.

The same parliament rejected it in 2020, but military contacts with Ankara since last year, including a July deal, reversed that.

"Some countries may be upset by this agreement, but what matters to us is our national interest," said lawmaker Tarek al-Jeroushi.

Turkey's foreign ministry says ratification moves it closer to expanding eastern Mediterranean exploration.
