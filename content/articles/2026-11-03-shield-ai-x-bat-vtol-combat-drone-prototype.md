Frisco — Shield AI unveiled a stealth drone that launches tail-down.

The [US](country:US) Navy hopes X-BAT becomes an operational capability on a very aggressive timeline.

Its thrust-vectoring nozzle controls pitch and yaw, and bleed air routed through wing thrusters will control roll, Shield AI has said.

X-BAT 001, the first of 2 planned prototypes, is moving to Shield AI's Newton, Kansas, flight test facility.
