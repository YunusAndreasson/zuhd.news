New York — Bitcoin recovered to $63,500 on Iran de-escalation.

The [Iran](country:IR) war premium was priced into oil; de-escalation news triggered a cross-market rally lifting bitcoin from a below-$60,000 low.

Analysts say a durable recovery requires fresh exchange-traded fund inflows; bitcoin is 50% below its October 2025 peak.
