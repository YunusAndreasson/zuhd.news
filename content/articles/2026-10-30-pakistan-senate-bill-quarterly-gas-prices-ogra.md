Islamabad — A Senate bill would end public hearings on gas price revisions.

It would also reset gas prices every quarter.

The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority reviews each licensee's quarterly costs, then [Pakistan](country:PK)'s government has 30 days to notify consumer prices.

Minister of State Tallal Chaudhry's bill says quarterly pricing tracks rupee-dollar parity and curbs circular debt.

If the Senate passes it, the law takes effect at once.
