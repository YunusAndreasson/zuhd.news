Manila — [China](country:CN) sanctioned [Philippines](country:PH)' defense secretary Gilberto Teodoro.

Manila called the sanctions 'an unfriendly act'; Teodoro has led the Philippines' confrontational posture in the South China Sea.

Teodoro said the Philippines will defend its national interests but gave no specific countermeasures.
