Washington — The ban covers robot vacuum cleaners.

The Federal Communications Commission now requires radio equipment authorisation for any market entry, sweeping in Chinese, Japanese, Korean and German hardware over 4.4 pounds.

Existing models stay legal; the Defense Department can still exempt specific foreign robots from the new list.
