Mahahual — $1 billion cruise park threatens the Mesoamerican Reef. Royal Caribbean's Perfect Day Mexico would send 15,000 daily cruise visitors through a 2,800-person village whose reef the development would partially destroy. Quintana Roo's governor endorsed the project; opponents must fight it through permits that the state controls.
