San Francisco — A bug in XRP Ledger's new upgrade could drain balances.

It's the feature banks need to split payments from compliance without exposing full account keys.

The fix lets an account delegate up to 10 permissions, and needs 28 of 35 validators' support to activate 5 October.

The countdown resets if validator support drops below 80% before the 5 October activation.
