Brussels — EU leaders struggled Thursday to agree a $1.76 trillion budget.

[Ukraine](country:UA)'s 2027 budget faces an estimated €70 billion hole, the European Commission says.

The EU would borrow €45 billion against its budget headroom, repayable by Kyiv only once [Russia](country:RU) pays reparations.

[Slovakia](country:SK), the [Czech Republic](country:CZ), [Hungary](country:HU) and [Bulgaria](country:BG) object to contributing, but Germany makes participation a condition of any budget deal.

Leaders target a deal on 17 and 18 December, before elections in France and Italy complicate talks.
