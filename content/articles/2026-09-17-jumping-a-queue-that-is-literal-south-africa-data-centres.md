Johannesburg — [South Africa](country:ZA)'s groups protest new data centers.

It is one of the first coordinated African pushbacks against AI infrastructure.

Data centers consume electricity and water permanently, while the local jobs they create largely end after construction.

Neither the companies involved nor South African officials have detailed a public response yet.
