After exile leader Reza Pahlavi's last protest call, 7,000 Iranians died, rights groups say. Now calling for celebrations as US airstrikes kill Iranians, Pahlavi is the son of a shah whose CIA-backed restoration in 1953 shaped Iran's modern history. His call for Chaharshanbe Suri marches on Tuesday has drawn backlash from Iranians who say he is exploiting their deaths for political gain.
