Tirana — Hundreds of women have travelled for unproven three-parent IVF.

Research reviewed by investigators shows it fails most older women, whose infertility has other causes.

Clinics put a couple's DNA into nucleus-stripped donor eggs, and broker International Fertility Group claims over 50% pregnancy success without substantiation.

The broker says it is not a clinic and promises patients no pregnancy, live birth or outcome.

It has paused new patients and removed all mention of the program from its website.
