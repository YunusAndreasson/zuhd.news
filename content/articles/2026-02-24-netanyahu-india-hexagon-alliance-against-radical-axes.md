India buys more Israeli weapons than any other country. Netanyahu unveiled a 6-nation alliance during Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi's visit, linking Israel, India, and unnamed Arab and Asian states against what he called "radical axes." Netanyahu signed a defense pact with Modi in November; the alliance's 4 other members remain unnamed.
