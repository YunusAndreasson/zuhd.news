Doha — Qatar Airways moved 20 wide-body jets to Spanish storage. Qatar's Al Udeid Air Base is the US military's largest regional facility, making Doha a potential strike target. After Trump's Hormuz ultimatum expires Monday, Qatar must decide whether to return 20 jets to an airspace that may be contested.
