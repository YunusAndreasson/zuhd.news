Guangzhou — Ant Group open-sourced a 560-billion-parameter medical AI model.

Ant's own benchmarks put it ahead of GPT-6 Astra on HealthBench Professional, 65.72 to 64.18.

The 124-billion-parameter sibling reads medical images and reports, while the 560-billion model handles clinical reasoning.

Ant's health technology chief Gu Jinjie concedes the vision model's resolution limits it, so complex skin conditions resist single photos.

Ant's Afu app will keep steering doubtful cases to human doctors, technology chief He Zhengyu said.
