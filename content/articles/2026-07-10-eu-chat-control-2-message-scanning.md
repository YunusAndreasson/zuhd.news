Brussels — Google, Meta and Microsoft can now scan private messages.

The European Parliament passed "Chat Control 2.0," letting platforms scan user messages for child-abuse material — the bloc's biggest concession yet against end-to-end encryption.

Platforms must decide whether to build scanning through client-side or server-side breaks in encryption.
