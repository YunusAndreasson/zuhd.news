Port Nolloth — A High Court judgment on TotalEnergies' drilling is due soon.

The Aukotowa fishing co-operative argues the company's assessment covered small-scale fishers in only one section.

TotalEnergies has authorisation to drill a well 188km off [South Africa](country:ZA)'s west coast, plus up to nine more.

TotalEnergies puts the blowout probability at 0.0143%, and the state told the court 358 offshore wells were drilled without one.

Port Nolloth's small-boat fishers were still waiting in mid-November for rock lobster permits.
