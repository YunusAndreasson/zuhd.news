Berlin — [Germany](country:DE)'s intelligence reform says nothing on buying advertising data.

App location data sold for advertising includes movement profiles of officials and soldiers.

Security agencies in the US, Hungary and Austria already use it, and at least one German state criminal police office has.

The Bundestag is still debating the bill, which runs to hundreds of pages.
