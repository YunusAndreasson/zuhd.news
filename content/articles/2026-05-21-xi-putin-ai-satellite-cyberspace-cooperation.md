Moscow — [Russia](country:RU) and [China](country:CN) pledged joint satellite internet development.

A joint statement named satellite internet and open-source repositories as shared ventures — infrastructure outside the reach of US export controls.

Neither government gave a launch date; the stated target markets are countries currently excluded from US-controlled platforms.
