Israel has executed 1 person in 78 years. The Knesset voted 39-16 to advance a bill mandating death for Palestinians convicted of killings but life imprisonment for Israelis convicted of the same crime. UN human rights experts demanded Israel withdraw the bill, which requires 2 more readings to become law.
