Seoul — AI-written peer reviews got 497 ICML papers rejected.

Organizers caught 506 violators by embedding hidden trigger prompts in paper drafts that caused LLMs to insert telltale phrases — a technique that catches only the most careless cheaters.

ICLR deploys GPTZero on all April submissions in Rio as more than half of researchers admit using AI in reviews despite the bans.
