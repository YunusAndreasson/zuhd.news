Redmond — Xbox raises console prices $100-$150 from August 1.

Memory prices rose 2.5x from AI data-center demand, Microsoft said; consoles are sold below cost and cannot absorb the gap.

Microsoft projects memory costs to double again by fall 2027, with no timeline for reversing the August hike.
