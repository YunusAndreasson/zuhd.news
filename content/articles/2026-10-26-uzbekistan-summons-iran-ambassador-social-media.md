Tashkent — Iranian embassy posts about Samarkand drew a formal protest note.

Tashkent says it values ties with [Iran](country:IR) but rejects one-sided readings of Samarkand's heritage.

The posts answered [US](country:US) Secretary of State Marco Rubio's Acropolis speech, which recalled Persia's destruction of the site in 480 BC.

Iran's embassies in Armenia, China and Zimbabwe deleted posts, but its Islamic Cooperation mission kept its own up.

Tashkent has demanded Iran remove every post it flagged.
