Pyongyang — Kim Yo-jong denies [North Korea](country:KP) stole $6.3bn from Binance.

The US Federal Bureau of Investigation blames Pyongyang's TraderTraitor group for the record crypto theft.

TraderTraitor, also called Lazarus, stole $1.5bn from Bybit in 2025, and US investigators asked exchanges to block flagged wallets.

"We don't know if it's North Korea," [US](country:US) President Donald Trump told reporters in Florida.

Kim said Pyongyang has issues to reconsider about Washington, while US investigators have yet to publish their forensics.
