BNP won 209 of 297 parliamentary seats. Party chair Tarique Rahman will be sworn in as prime minister by February 18 in Bangladesh's first election since a student-led uprising toppled former Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina in 2024 — Rahman returned from 17 years of UK exile in December. Jamaat-e-Islami secured 68 seats, its highest tally ever, positioning itself as the main opposition in a parliament where the governing alliance holds a two-thirds majority.
