Michoacan — Tens of millions of monarchs winter in one sacred fir forest.

The butterflies complete a 3,000-mile migration from Canada each year, and some locals guarding the oyamel groves have died defending them.

Cartels running illegal logging for cash crops keep eating the sanctuary the migration depends on.
