London — Hospitals must secure themselves, AI vendors need not.

Firms that train the models carry no legal duty under [Britain](country:GB)'s new cyber bill.

The government rejected Lords amendments bringing AI vendors into scope, arguing regulation should target deployers, not developers.

The bill's Grand Committee resumes scrutiny Thursday.
