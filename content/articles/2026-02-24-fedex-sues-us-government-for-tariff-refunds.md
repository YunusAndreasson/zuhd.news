FedEx filed the first corporate lawsuit demanding a full tariff refund. The Supreme Court ruled 6-3 last week that Trump exceeded his authority under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act, putting $175 billion in collected duties in legal limbo. Hundreds of importers are expected to follow, even as Trump threatens new levies.
