Washington — Federal efficiency buyouts took 4,000 NASA employees — 1 in 5.

Federal grants fund about 40% of [US](country:US) basic science research; one space telescope team lost 20 engineers, including the mirror inventor.

Congress has not yet appropriated the budget — programs zeroed in Trump's request remain at risk until it does.
