Karachi — A crude line burst under residents' homes.

Volatilising crude releases benzene, a carcinogen residents breathe before any evacuation is ordered.

Pipelines built through open land get built over as cities grow, leaving no setback or evacuation plan.

[Pakistan](country:PK) has no mandatory pipeline-inspection disclosure, so the line's age remains unknown to residents.
