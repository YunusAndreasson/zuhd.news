Houston — Orion survived 2,760°C re-entry and splashed down in the Pacific on April 10. Rice University materials research underpinned the heat shield — Artemis II's only critical failure point, never tested at crewed lunar return speeds. NASA's heat shield inspection and Artemis III reuse decision are pending.
