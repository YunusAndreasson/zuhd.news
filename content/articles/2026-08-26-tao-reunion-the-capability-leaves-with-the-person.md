Fort Meade — The NSA is inviting back hundreds of former hackers.

Their departure has drained the agency's most sensitive offensive hacking unit.

Commercial spyware firms and Gulf-funded contractors pay exploit developers several times a federal salary.

The agency has not detailed what its newly rebranded unit will do differently.
