The US offered $10 million for Iran's new leader. The Rewards for Justice program, which has never before targeted a sitting head of state, extended the offer to Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei and 5 of his security officials. The US said the program is intended to pressure Iran's leadership from within as the war continues.
