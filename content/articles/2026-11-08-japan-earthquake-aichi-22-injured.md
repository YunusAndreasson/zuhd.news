Hamamatsu — A magnitude 5.7 quake injured at least 22 people.

Shaking reached Kyoto, Nara and Yamanashi, where Mount Fuji stands, but not Tokyo.

The quake struck at 1:04am, 40km deep, and some of the injured fell on stairs fleeing buildings.

Chubu Electric Power said [Japan](country:JP)'s Hamaoka nuclear plant in Shizuoka showed no abnormalities.

The Meteorological Agency warns strong quakes could follow within 2 to 3 days.
