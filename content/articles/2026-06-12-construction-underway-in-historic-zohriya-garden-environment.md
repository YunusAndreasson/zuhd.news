Cairo — Concrete poured inside historic Zohriya Garden, plans undisclosed.

[Egypt](country:EG)'s state developer has proceeded with the botanical park's redevelopment without publishing plans, prompting a lawsuit before a July 5 court hearing.

A court-appointed expert may inspect ongoing work, but rare trees have reportedly already been removed.
