Kostiantynivka — [Ukraine](country:UA) says its amphibious robot ran a 40 km supply mission.

Rivers and wetlands bisect much of the front, where attack drones make human movement too dangerous.

Its commander, call sign Electric, says the 93rd Brigade's unit converted one of its own ground robots, and also tried a commercial amphibious model.

Electric says development to battle deployment took under 3 months, and Brave1 is developing them at scale.
