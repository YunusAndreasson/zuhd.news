Mykolaiv — Russia says it struck a grain-port bulk carrier.

[Russia](country:RU)'s defence ministry, the sole source for this account, calls the vessels military cargo; Kyiv has not confirmed or disputed the claim.

Kyiv has not said whether it will dispute Russia's account of the strike on [Ukraine](country:UA)'s grain-export route.
