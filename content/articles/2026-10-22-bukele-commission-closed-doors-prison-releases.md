San Salvador — [El Salvador](country:SV) may free about 10,000 prisoners early.

For four years the government said its state of exception jails only gang members and their helpers.

Bukele said on 13 October that he is weighing conditional early release for detainees forced to work with gangs.

Expert panel member Santiago Cantón says the government has not said who decides cases or on what basis.

Which of the 10,000 go free, and by what criteria, remains unpublished.
