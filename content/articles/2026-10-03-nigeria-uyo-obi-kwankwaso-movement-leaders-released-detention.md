Uyo — Police freed 9 opposition organisers after more than 24 hours.

Police ordered 1 October rallies rescheduled, citing security, as campaigning for [Nigeria](country:NG)'s 2027 election builds.

Elijah Noah says he agreed to reschedule, then went to tell supporters who had gathered, and police fired tear gas.

Police deny using tear gas or guns and reject any political motive.

Organisers say they will march on a date agreed with police.
