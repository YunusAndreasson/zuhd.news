Nimba County — Beoyoolar clinic's only midwife has no fistula training.

[Liberia](country:LR)'s health ministry estimates more than 100 new cases a year, mostly among younger women.

Obstructed labour cuts off tissue blood supply, leaving a leak of urine or faeces, and staff here have no screening guide.

Fistula is rarely covered in the periodic training the clinic's nurses, midwives and doctors receive.
