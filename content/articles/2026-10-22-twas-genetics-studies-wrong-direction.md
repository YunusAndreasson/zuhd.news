United Kingdom — Common gene-mapping method flips direction for 23% of plasma-protein genes.

Direction decides whether drug-target discovery should boost or block a gene.

Transcriptome-wide association studies infer gene activity from DNA variants, so researchers checked 1,543 proteins whose levels should rise with activity.

Filtering for shared causal variants helped, but gene-level false sign rates stayed above 10% at the strictest threshold.

The authors say the results raise questions about how these methods behave as commonly understood.
