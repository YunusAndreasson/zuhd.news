Muscat — [Oman](country:OM)'s main oil export terminal halted loading after an explosion Thursday.

The Mina al-Fahal berths are Oman's sole crude transfer point; officials gave no cause, though reports cited a drone strike near the Strait of Hormuz.

Operations resumed Friday with no group claiming responsibility.
