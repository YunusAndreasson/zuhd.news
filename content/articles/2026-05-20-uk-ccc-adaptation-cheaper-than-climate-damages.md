London — UK climate advisors: adaptation costs less than damages.

The Climate Change Committee called for £11bn in extra annual investment — mostly private — on flood defenses, cooling, and water supply.

The report feeds into the [UK](country:GB)'s fourth climate risk assessment, due in 2027; the government has not yet responded.
