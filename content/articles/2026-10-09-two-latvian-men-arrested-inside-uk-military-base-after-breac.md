Molesworth — Police found 2 Latvian men inside a US intelligence base.

The base houses [US](country:US) intelligence centres for Europe and Africa and a NATO hub.

A patrol spotted an abandoned vehicle Wednesday night, and police found a small fence breach before arresting both men.

Commander Helen Flanagan of Counter Terrorism Policing London says nothing suggests a link to the 27 September Fairford incident.

Police have not disclosed the men's motives, and enquiries will now focus on why they were there.
