Beirut — [Lebanon](country:LB)'s parliament shelved an artificial-intelligence ministry bill.

One government office designs, procures and oversees Lebanon's digital reforms alone, rights group SMEX says, leaving no body to flag a failed system.

Without a ministry, no statutory owner exists that parliament can interrogate, audit or vote out.
