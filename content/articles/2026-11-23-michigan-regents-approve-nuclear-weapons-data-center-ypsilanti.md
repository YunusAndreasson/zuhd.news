Ypsilanti Township — Michigan regents approved a $1.25 billion nuclear-weapons data center, 7-1.

Township residents opposed it, and a student told the board Los Alamos gets 90% of capacity.

The university called it an AI research facility in 2024, now a research computing center, with construction due late 2027.

The electricians' union backs the project, saying it will create more than 300 local jobs.

Township leaders met hours after the vote to plan how to stop it.
