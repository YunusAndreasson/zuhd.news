South Darfur — Tribes backed by [Sudan](country:SD)'s Rapid Support Forces shelled rival villages.

Salamat and Beni Halba fighters, both militia-armed, traded artillery fire after a contested arrest inflamed communities.

Rapid Support Forces commander Hemedti's advisor Fares al-Nour defected to [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) — the 2nd senior figure to flee in a month.
