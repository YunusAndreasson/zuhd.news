Shanghai — MiniMax's AI recovered 94 McEliece challenge keys.

55 of the 94 instances had no previously published solution, MiniMax reports.

MiniMax says its internal model found the attacks and its researchers verified them, with speedups up to 43,400 times.

The work does not break mainstream encryption standards, only experimental schemes and old competition candidates.

Papers and code, released 20 November, are public for outside cryptographers to check.
