Trump said he'll have "the honour of taking Cuba." Cuba's national grid collapsed under a US oil blockade in effect since January 9, with 9.6 million people losing power 20 hours daily. Cuba's deputy prime minister announced US investors may now own businesses on the island — an offer Trump has not acknowledged.
