Dearborn — 1.4 million F-150 pickups recalled for spontaneous downshifts. Heat erodes 6R80 sensor connectors over time, causing the control module to misread gear position and downshift unpredictably. Dealers update PCM software from April 15; owner remedy letters ship July 13, complete by July 17.
