Cape Town — [South Africa](country:ZA) is short of about 50 critical public medicines.

Some chronic patients have gone 3 months without supplies, and epilepsy patients are having seizures.

Imported medicines need 3 to 4 weeks of quality testing, and port delays at Durban and supplier back-orders slow stock further.

"I can't go on like this much longer," said Graham Linet, a 45-year-old epilepsy patient.

Internal health department reports show new shortages emerging faster than existing ones are resolved.
