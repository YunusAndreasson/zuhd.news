Karachi — Meerdali Bugti fled Dera Bugti twenty years ago.

Displacement lasting two decades is now a settled arrangement, not an aftermath.

Displaced families hold no land title back home and no tenancy in Karachi, leaving them ineligible for aid.

No official displacement registry exists, so families like theirs remain uncounted twenty years on.
