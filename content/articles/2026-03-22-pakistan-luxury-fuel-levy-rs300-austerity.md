Islamabad — Pakistan is taxing luxury cars to shield ordinary drivers. The government tripled the premium fuel levy to Rs300 per litre on Sunday, targeting only high-octane fuel and saving Rs9 billion a month. It also rejected broader fuel hikes on Friday, but ordinary prices hold only as long as the Hormuz Strait stays open.
