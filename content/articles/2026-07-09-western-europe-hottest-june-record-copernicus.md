Brussels — 4,700 excess deaths followed June's heatwave, national authorities reported.

Average Western European temperatures hit 20.74°C, over 3°C above the 1991-2020 norm, in [France](country:FR), Belgium, Spain and the Netherlands.

Spain and Portugal are already in the grip of a fourth heatwave this week.
