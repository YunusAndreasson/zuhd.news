Tehran — Iran's state-affiliated Mehr says Saman satellites will add liquid fuel.

Space centre head Nader Mokari says [Iran](country:IR) wants cheaper, more reliable access to orbit.

Mokari says two 3D simulation programs will test spacecraft on the ground before launch, to reduce mission failures.

Mehr's report gives no dates for the Saman launches.
