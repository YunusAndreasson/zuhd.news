Pretoria — Eleven years, seven buses, zero delivered.

Centurion Bus Manufacturers took R26 million to convert buses into mobile vet clinics; the Auditor-General found officials certified payments without required insurance or guarantees.

Litigation between the company and [South Africa](country:ZA)'s agriculture department remains pending.
