Madrid — Evicted from her home of 71 years, Maricarmen Abascal died.

Her eviction on 23 September set off protests across [Spain](country:ES) and a snap election.

Urbagestión bought the property in 2018 and raised her rent from about €500 to €2,650, the tenants' union says.

"Maricarmen only wanted to die in her home," said Eugenio Melchor, 22, a protester.

Spain votes 29 November, and Polymarket traders give Sánchez 23% to be next prime minister, up 9 points in a week.
