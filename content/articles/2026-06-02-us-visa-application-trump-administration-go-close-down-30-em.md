Washington — 30 US Africa visa posts will close by June.

[US](country:US) Secretary of State Rubio's directive consolidates processing in 20 hubs; nationals from the 30 closed countries must travel internationally to apply.

The State Department memo names no specific closing date but sets June for implementation.
