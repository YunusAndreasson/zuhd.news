Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s regulator says it cannot bar under-16s from social media.

Waqas Nasir's petition asks the Islamabad High Court to direct the government to legislate for under-16s.

The Pakistan Telecommunication Authority says it cannot create age verification by administrative action, and web encryption makes blocking single URLs nearly impossible.

It says a new body, the Social Media Protection and Regulatory Authority, will replace it once notified.

The authority asked the court to dismiss the petition insofar as it exceeds its mandate.
