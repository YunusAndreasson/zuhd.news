Washington — Trump submitted the Saudi nuclear deal to Congress this week.

Unlike the 2009 United Arab Emirates pact, it lets [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) keep an enrichment path open.

The "gold standard" United Arab Emirates deal required renouncing enrichment outright, while this one relies on a bilateral safeguards pact instead.

The 90-day review lapses into approval unless Congress passes a rejection resolution.
