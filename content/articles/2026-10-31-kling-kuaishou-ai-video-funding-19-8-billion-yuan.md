Beijing — Kuaishou's Kling raised 19.8 billion yuan for AI video.

Zhidx calls it AI video's largest round, with Alibaba, Tencent and Baidu among investors.

Kuaishou's Lucky Labs keeps 66.68% of equity, while CEO Gai Kun's special shares give him 23.72% of votes on 3.02%.

Two investors missed payment deadlines and quit, cancelling 100 million yuan and $80 million of commitments.

Three late-joining investors, including Kingdee and MetaX, hold buyback rights on their shares.
