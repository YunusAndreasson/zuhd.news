United States — Colette Kress, Nvidia's finance chief since 2013, now runs it.

Jensen Huang quit as chief executive, president and director after more than three decades.

Reports linked Huang to a businesswoman [US](country:US) authorities reportedly suspect of Chinese intelligence ties, and the board opened an independent investigation.

Nvidia said it had no reason to believe national security or its proprietary information was compromised.

Kress addresses staff on 18 November as the board searches inside and outside for a successor.
