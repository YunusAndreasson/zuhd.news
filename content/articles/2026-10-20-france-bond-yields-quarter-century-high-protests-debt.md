Paris — [France](country:FR)'s 10-year bond yield reached 4.96%, highest since 2002.

Higher borrowing costs leave the government less money for pensions, schools and health.

Investors doubt a fragmented National Assembly can pass a budget with 54 billion euros in savings, so they charge more.

The US, Japan and Italy owe more, but a reserve currency, domestic holders and a smaller deficit shield them.

Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu may invoke a constitutional clause to pass the budget without a parliamentary vote.
