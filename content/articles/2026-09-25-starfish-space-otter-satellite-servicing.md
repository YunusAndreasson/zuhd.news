Kent — Starfish Space's first Otter launches next month on Falcon 9.

It is the first commercial debris inspection mission the US space agency has funded, at $15 million.

Otter will approach defunct [US](country:US) satellites to within hundreds of metres, gathering data on spin rates and condition.

The 2023 Otter Pup 1 lost a thruster after its launch vehicle ejected it spinning at 330 degrees per second.

Five more Otters are in production to demonstrate docking, de-orbiting and life extension.
