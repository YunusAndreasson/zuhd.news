New Delhi — The country's best-known bottled water failed a safety test.

Poor water quality pushes Indians toward packaged water, a $3.6 billion market in 2025.

A Goa sample showed 12 micrograms of bromate per litre against a limit of 10, and a referral laboratory then detected PAHs, toxic compounds.

Bisleri says independent tests it commissioned found bromate within limits.

[India](country:IN)'s food regulator has sanctioned prosecution and plans to test samples from every brand, a government source said.
