Istanbul — Some vessels bound for Novorossiysk were turned back.

Under the 1936 Montreux Convention Turkey must keep the straits open to merchant ships, so a coast guard denial is treaty brinkmanship.

Turkish authorities have not confirmed the restriction; Bloomberg's sourcing remains anonymous and unverified.
