New York — Mangione's lawyers will mount an extreme emotional disturbance defense to reduce murder to manslaughter.

New York law requires a disturbance and a reasonable explanation; Mangione's 5-day flight complicates the claim, legal experts said.

Judge Carro rules on the defense before jury instructions; trial begins September.
