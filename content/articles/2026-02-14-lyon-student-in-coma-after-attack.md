A 23-year-old student is in a coma after being attacked at a far-right protest in Lyon, France, on Thursday. The man had joined a demonstration organized by Nemesis, a far-right collective, when he was beaten by assailants that fellow protesters identified as members of an antifascist group linked to La France Insoumise, the left-wing party led by Jean-Luc Mélenchon. French authorities have opened an investigation. Other activists present at the protest said they identified the attackers, according to France 24.
