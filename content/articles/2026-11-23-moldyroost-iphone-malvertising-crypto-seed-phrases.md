Hong Kong — One ad tap infects older iPhones, stealing crypto seed phrases.

Seed phrases cannot be reset, so anyone holding one can move the wallet's funds.

Confiant researchers tied more than 480 ad domains to MoldyRoost, whose pages read the iOS version and run a matching exploit kit.

iPhones on iOS 26 appear safe: researchers found no working exploit code for it.

Confiant says the operators may rent parts of the operation to affiliates, which remains undetermined.
