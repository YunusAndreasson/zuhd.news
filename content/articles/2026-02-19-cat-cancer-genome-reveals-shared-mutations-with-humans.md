Researchers sequenced 493 feline tumour samples across 13 cancer types. The study found TP53 mutations in 33% of cat tumours, nearly identical to the 34% rate in humans, with feline mammary cancers sharing genetic drivers with aggressive human breast cancer subtypes. The findings open a path for veterinary and human oncology to advance treatments together.
