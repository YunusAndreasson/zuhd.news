Bushehr — [Iran](country:IR)'s nuclear plant passed 80 billion kWh lifetime.

Iran's sole operating reactor generates electricity equivalent to burning 131 million barrels of oil, the Atomic Energy Organization of Iran said.

[US](country:US) and [Israel](country:IL) struck areas surrounding the plant in March; it continued operating throughout.
