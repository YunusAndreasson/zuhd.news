Washington — US immigration services ended at-home green card applications Friday.

US Citizenship and Immigration Services reclassified adjustment of status as extraordinary; H-1B and student visa holders must now apply at consulates abroad.

For applicants from [India](country:IN) and [Pakistan](country:PK) in decade-long backlogs, this means years abroad with no reentry guarantee.
