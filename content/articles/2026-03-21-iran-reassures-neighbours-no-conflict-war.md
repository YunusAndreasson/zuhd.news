Iran hit a Western base, then pledged Gulf peace. Iran offered Japan safe Hormuz passage last month after Tokyo refused US naval escort duty, now extending the same guarantee to Gulf states hosting US bases. Saudi Arabia, the UAE, and Qatar have not responded to Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian's offer as US-Israeli strikes escalate.
