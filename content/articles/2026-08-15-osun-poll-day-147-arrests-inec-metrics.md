Ede — Voters queued calmly across Osun's governorship vote.

The result decides who runs [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Osun State for the next term.

Fifteen parties are on the ballot, security escorted elderly and disabled voters first, and observers reported no disorder.

The Independent National Electoral Commission has not yet said when it will announce the result.
