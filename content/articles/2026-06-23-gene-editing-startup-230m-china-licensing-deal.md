Boston — Serapha Bio launched with $230 million built on a Chinese drugmaker's gene-editing patents.

The deal routes [China](country:CN)-originated therapy into a [US](country:US) shell via reverse merger, separating patent origin from the commercial vehicle to skirt US-China decoupling rules.

The therapy targets Alpha-1 Antitrypsin Deficiency, a liver-and-lung disease with no curative treatment.
