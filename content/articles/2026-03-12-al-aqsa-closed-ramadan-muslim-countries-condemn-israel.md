8 governments called Israel's Al-Aqsa closure illegal. Israel shut the mosque citing the Iran war, blocking tens of thousands of worshippers from Islam's 3rd-holiest site during Ramadan. Iran's supreme leader has named Al-Aqsa as justification for the war, with no reopening date announced.
