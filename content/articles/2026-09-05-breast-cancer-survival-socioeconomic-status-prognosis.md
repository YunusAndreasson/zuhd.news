London — Low-income patients faced nearly triple the metastasis risk.

A 109-patient study found poverty carries prognostic weight alongside tumor biology in metastatic breast cancer.

Later diagnosis, interrupted treatment and distance to care each convert a social fact into a clinical outcome.

Researchers call the findings early access, subject to revision.
