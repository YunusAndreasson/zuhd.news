Lahore — Farmers will block roads nationwide on August 7.

The Kissan Board and Jamaat-i-Islami are protesting the petroleum levy, a per-litre charge that raises tubewell diesel costs for growers.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s government has yet to set a wheat support price farmers have long demanded.
