It signals judicial accountability now reaches [Malaysia](country:MY)'s highest former office, not just its opposition.

The anti-corruption law criminalizes failing to declare assets, so prosecutors need not trace the money's origin.

Ismail Sabri returns to court September 29 and faces up to five years in prison.
