Newark — 2 jets nearly collided on crossing runways at Newark. The Federal Aviation Administration's air traffic control system has been understaffed for years — Congress flagged the problem in 2023 after multiple near-misses. The National Transportation Safety Board must now determine whether the controller shortage caused this one.
