London — Voltage-controlled films cycle green, blue, and purple.

Metal atom chemistry controls color-switching speed in 2D frameworks; zinc and nickel lasted 20,000 cycles versus copper's fewer than 300, researchers report.

Target applications include smart windows, camouflage, and wearable displays; no commercial timeline is announced.
