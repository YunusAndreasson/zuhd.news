Hangzhou — DeepSeek is quietly designing its own chips.

US export bans cut Nvidia out of [China](country:CN)'s market, and DeepSeek wants inference chips that cut reliance on Huawei too, three people told Reuters.

No launch date exists; DeepSeek has spent about a year hiring engineers and courting hardware partners.
