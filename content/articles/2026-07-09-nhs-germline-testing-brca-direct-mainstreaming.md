London — Family-history rules missed half of gene carriers.

A North Thames pilot tested 3,515 unselected breast cancer patients, finding old criteria caught only 49% of high-risk mutations.

Researchers say simplified rules could lift detection to 81% while nearly doubling how many patients get tested.
