The Asian Development Bank bypasses Pakistan's energy crisis. Its $10 billion, 5-year strategy is the bank's largest Pakistan commitment, targeting structural reform while household energy bills spike. Pakistan must convince investors the program survives a military campaign that has already consumed billions in reserves.
