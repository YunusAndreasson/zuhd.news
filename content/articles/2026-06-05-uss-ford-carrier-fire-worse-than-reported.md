Washington — Pentagon called USS Ford fire "contained"; footage showed otherwise.

Sources said the fire-suppression system failed; the [US](country:US) Navy cited 2 minor injuries and declared the carrier operational, but footage showed more than 600 crew had lost their berths.

No Navy explanation for the gap between its statement and the footage has been given.
