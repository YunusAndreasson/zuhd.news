Pyongyang — North Korea's fissile material output doubled in 5 years, Kim Jong Un announced.

Kim inspected a new production plant and ordered further increases, calling fissile material the warhead bottleneck.

[North Korea](country:KP) set a sequenced timetable for what Kim called exponential growth in arsenal capability.
