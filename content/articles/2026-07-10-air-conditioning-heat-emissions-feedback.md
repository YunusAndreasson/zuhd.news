London — Only 4% of English households have air conditioning.

Days above 30C have surged across [Britain](country:GB), France and Germany since 2000, but wider AC adoption would add electricity and refrigerant emissions that deepen the warming.

Wider AC adoption will need cleaner power grids to avoid deepening the warming it responds to.
