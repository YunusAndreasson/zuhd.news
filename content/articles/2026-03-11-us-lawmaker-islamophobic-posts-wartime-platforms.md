Andy Ogles posted that Muslims "don't belong in American society" on X. The Tennessee Republican posted as the US wages war on a Muslim-majority country; the Council on American-Islamic Relations recorded 8,683 anti-Muslim complaints in 2025, a record high. House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries condemned the posts; X has not removed them and no Republican colleague has publicly criticized Ogles.
