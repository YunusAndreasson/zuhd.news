Bern — [Switzerland](country:CH)'s parliament voted 100-98 to allow new nuclear plants.

The vote reverses a post-Fukushima ban, driven by energy-security pressure; a national referendum will give voters the final word.

No referendum date is set; Switzerland's 4 existing reactors supply about a third of the country's electricity.
