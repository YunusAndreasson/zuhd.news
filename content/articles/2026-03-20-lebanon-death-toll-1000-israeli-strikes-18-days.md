Israeli strikes killed 1,001 in Lebanon in 18 days. The toll includes 118 children, 79 women, and 40 healthcare workers as strikes expand from southern Lebanon to Beirut, Baalbek, and Sidon. Israel has announced no ceasefire and has issued no statement on limiting its target geography.
