Britain's only Gulf minehunter left Bahrain on March 1. Iran deployed about a dozen mines in Hormuz in the days after, while its boats set 2 tankers ablaze in an Iraqi port on Thursday. Defence minister John Healey said Britain is "considering additional options" to deploy alongside allies, with no vessels or timeline named.
