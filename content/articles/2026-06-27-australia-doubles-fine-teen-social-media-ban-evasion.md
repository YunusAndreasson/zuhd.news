Canberra — [Australia](country:AU)'s teen social-media fine rose to A$99 million for systemic violations.

A British Medical Journal study found widespread circumvention 3 months after the ban took effect, prompting the government to double the cap.

Facebook and 4 other platforms are under active investigation by the eSafety Commissioner for non-compliance.
