Islamabad — Pakistan now leads Syria and Somalia in terrorism deaths. The Taliban's 2021 takeover gave Tehrik-i-Taliban Pakistan, a domestic militant group, a cross-border sanctuary, reversing a decade of declining attacks. Pakistan's army is escalating operations in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa while TTP commanders remain sheltered in Afghanistan.
