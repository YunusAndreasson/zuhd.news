London — Sun-reflecting clouds evaporate faster than models assume.

26 of 32 observed stratocumulus profiles showed "super-adiabatic" evaporation, driven by dry air mixing in at cloud edges, researchers found.

Researchers say remote sensing and atmospheric models need to modify their adiabatic assumption to match.
