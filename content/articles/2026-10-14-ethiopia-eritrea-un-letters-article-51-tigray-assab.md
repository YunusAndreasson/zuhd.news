Addis Ababa — [Ethiopia](country:ET) cited UN Charter Article 51 against [Eritrea](country:ER) on Friday.

Eritrea's foreign minister replied on Saturday that the accusation amounts to a declaration of war.

Ethiopia says Eritrean troops advanced up to 38 miles into Tigray, Responsible Statecraft, the Quincy Institute's magazine, reported.

Eritrea's letter quotes Ethiopia's deputy chief of staff saying the war's aim was to invade the port of Assab.

Ethiopian drones reportedly hit Eritrean forces near Adigrat on Wednesday and Thursday.
