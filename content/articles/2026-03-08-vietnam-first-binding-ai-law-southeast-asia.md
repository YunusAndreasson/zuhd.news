Vietnam passed the first binding AI law in Southeast Asia. Indonesia, Thailand, and the Philippines rely on voluntary guidelines; Vietnam chose enforceable regulation while competing for the same semiconductor investment. No ASEAN-wide AI framework exists; Vietnam's law sets a regional precedent that neighboring governments must now decide whether to match.
