Lahore — Jamaat-e-Islami says 510 protests hit [Pakistan](country:PK) in a day.

The party will now stage sit-ins in all four provincial capitals at once.

Protesters want petrol at 225 rupees a litre, accusing officials of keeping free fuel and aircraft perks.

Simultaneous sit-ins begin August 16 outside the chief minister's and three governors' residences.
