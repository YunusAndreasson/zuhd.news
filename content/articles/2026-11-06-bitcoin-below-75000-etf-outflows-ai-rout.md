New York — $1.26 billion in leveraged crypto bets were liquidated, CoinGlass says.

Bitcoin dropped below $75,000, the first time since 15 September, Binance data show.

Spot bitcoin ETFs in the [US](country:US) lost $430.2 million Thursday as the Nasdaq fell 5.5%, SoSoValue data show.

Simon-Peter Massabni of broker XS.com said asset managers "liquidate alternative holdings to meet margin calls and deleverage portfolios."

Bitcoin recovered to about $76,000 Friday, though it traded at a discount on Coinbase to Binance, a sign of weak US buying.
