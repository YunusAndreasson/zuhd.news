Freetown — [Nigeria](country:NG)'s vice president lands for the 69th ECOWAS summit.

Mali, Niger and Burkina Faso quit the bloc for their own alliance, stripping ECOWAS of its Sahel security core ahead of talks on trade and free movement.

Leaders meeting at the Julius Maada Bio center face the question of what economic integration survives without them.
