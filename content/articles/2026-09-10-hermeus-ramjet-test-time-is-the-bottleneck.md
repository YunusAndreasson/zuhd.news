Atlanta — Hermeus unveiled an air-launched ramjet test craft.

It targets the scarcity of hypersonic flight-test time, not a lack of ideas.

Released at Mach 3 from the reusable Quarterhorse drone, the ramjet can sustain hypersonic flight for minutes, not seconds.

Hermeus has not disclosed Ramjet-X's cost per flight or a first-flight date.
