Mountain View — Google spent $44.9 billion on AI in one quarter.

Operating cash of $39.1 billion couldn't cover it, leaving free cash flow at -$5.8 billion for the first time since Google went public, despite $119.8 billion in revenue.

Google now plans $205 billion in 2026 capital spending, up from $91 billion last year.
