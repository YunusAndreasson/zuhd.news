New York — The US and China refused to sign a new AI pact.

20 other signatories want global oversight, but AI's two leading powers kept out.

The pact would create a body to convene states when AI capabilities cross set thresholds, with [Germany](country:DE) and [South Africa](country:ZA) among signatories.

OpenAI countered that the US, not a global body, should set the technical standards for AI.

Trump and Xi meet Thursday in Washington, where AI dominance, not oversight, tops the agenda.
