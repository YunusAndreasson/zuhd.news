Cairo — Six journalists remain in 15-day remand after several alleged torture.

The Journalists Syndicate filed a complaint, and [Egypt](country:EG)'s Interior Ministry denies any torture or mistreatment.

The Supreme State Security Prosecution holds the six and largely repeated its first questions on the platform's editorial policy.

Prosecutors ordered a forensic examination of one journalist, but he has not been examined.

One lawyer says the delay may have let visible signs of torture fade beyond forensic recognition.
