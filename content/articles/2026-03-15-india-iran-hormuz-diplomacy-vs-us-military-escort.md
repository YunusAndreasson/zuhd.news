2 Indian-flagged ships passed through Hormuz safely on Saturday. Indian Foreign Minister Subrahmanyam Jaishankar credited direct talks with Tehran for the passage. India imports 85 percent of its oil through Hormuz and has not joined US President Donald Trump's naval escort coalition, staking its energy supply on diplomacy holding.
