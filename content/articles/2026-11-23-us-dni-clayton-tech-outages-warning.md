Washington — The [US](country:US) intelligence chief says more outages are coming.

A nationwide flight halt, bank glitch, Microsoft outage and National Security Agency network disruption hit this month.

Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton blames upgrades of ageing systems, not coordinated or state-sponsored attacks.

Clayton declined to discuss reported cyberattacks on the Federal Bureau of Investigation and Department of Agriculture.

No date has been set for investigators' findings on those two attacks.
