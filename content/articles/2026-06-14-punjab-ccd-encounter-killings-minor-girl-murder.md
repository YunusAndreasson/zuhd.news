Chakwal — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Crime Control Department shot 9-year-old Hania Ahmed dead.

The unit fired on the family's car mistaking it for a robbery target; the same unit killed the 2 actual suspects in a staged encounter the following night.

The officer who shot Hania faces murder charges; the 2 suspects killed in the encounter received no trial.
