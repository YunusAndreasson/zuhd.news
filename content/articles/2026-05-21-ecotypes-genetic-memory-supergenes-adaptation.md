Stockholm — Small populations may hold more adaptive capacity than their census size suggests.

Chromosomal inversions bundle alleles into supergenes, locking solutions that reactivate when past conditions return without speciation.

This shifts conservation biology: a species' resilience is stored in gene structure, not headcount.
