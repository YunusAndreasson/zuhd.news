Brasília — Right-wing top vote-getters spent R$5.3 million on Meta ads.

The 10 most-voted left-wing deputies spent R$2.6 million on Meta advertising in the same 90 days.

André Fernandes, a Liberal Party deputy from Ceará, spent about R$1.8 million, the most of [Brazil](country:BR)'s 20 top vote-getters.

The Liberal Party took the largest share of the electoral fund and elected the most federal deputies.
