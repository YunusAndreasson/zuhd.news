Washington — Deportation agents are now screening passengers at US airports. A government shutdown cut the Transportation Security Administration's budget, prompting the Department of Homeland Security to replace trained aviation screeners with Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) agents whose mandate is deportation and arrest. Congress must pass a new budget to restore TSA — each day it delays, passengers face agents trained for arrests rather than aviation security.
