Mumbai — Prudential bought 75% of Bharti Life for $365m.

[India](country:IN)'s middle class is largely uninsured, and Prudential gains Bharti's distribution network rather than building one from scratch.

Prudential will cut its ICICI Prudential Life stake below 10% as part of the restructure.
