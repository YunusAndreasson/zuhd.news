Kumamoto — A magnitude 7.1 quake struck Kyushu.

The Japan Meteorological Agency issued a 1-metre tsunami advisory, its lowest tier, for a region with [Japan](country:JP)'s Sendai and Genkai nuclear plants.

No casualties or nuclear irregularities reported yet; officials warn residents to watch for aftershocks.
