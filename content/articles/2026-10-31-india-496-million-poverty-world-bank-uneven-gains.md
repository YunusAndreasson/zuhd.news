New Delhi — 496 million Indians rose above $4.20 a day.

The World Bank says it is among the fastest declines recorded, but gains remain uneven.

The share of people in [India](country:IN) below that line fell from 57.7% in 2011, the World Bank said.

Bharatiya Janata Party MP Sudhanshu Trivedi said only 2.6% remain in extreme poverty and credited Prime Minister Narendra Modi.
