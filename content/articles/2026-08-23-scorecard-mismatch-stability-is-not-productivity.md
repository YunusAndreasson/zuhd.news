Abuja — "Macroeconomic stability is a means, not an end," Nigeria's Centre for the Promotion of Private Enterprise said.

Reserves and the naira improved, but wages and jobs have not followed for [Nigeria](country:NG)'s households.

The naira has fallen 0.8% in a week as electricity output shrank 15.3% even as manufacturing grew.

The government's own scorecard admits poverty recovery remains unclaimed.
