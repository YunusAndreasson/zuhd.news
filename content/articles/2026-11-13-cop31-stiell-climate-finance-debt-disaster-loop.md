Antalya — 70% of 2024 climate finance arrived as loans, UN Women found.

UN climate chief Simon Stiell says a $300 billion target does not always meet needs.

Sub-Saharan African countries paid over twice as much in debt service as they received in climate finance, a Heinrich Böll analysis found.

The African Group of Negotiators lost a bid Monday for a separate agenda item on tripling adaptation finance.

The dialogue's conclusions go to COP32 in [Ethiopia](country:ET) next year.
