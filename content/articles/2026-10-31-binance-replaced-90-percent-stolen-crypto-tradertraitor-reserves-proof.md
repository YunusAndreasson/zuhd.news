Washington — Binance says it has refilled 90% of $6.3 billion stolen.

Customers must trust a snapshot Binance published itself until cryptographic proof arrives 3 November.

Binance says bitcoin, ether and 8 other tokens are fully backed, funded from its treasury and insurance fund.

Binance's earlier reserve disclosures listed wallets drained in the attack as backing users' holdings.

Withdrawals reopen Sunday 1 November, and the investigation into additional breach points remains open.
