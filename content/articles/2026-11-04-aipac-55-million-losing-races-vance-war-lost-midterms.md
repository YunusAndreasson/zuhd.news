Washington — The pro-Israel lobby spent $55.3 million on losing races, Responsible Statecraft reports.

Only about a third of its $84.1 million went to winners, against 87% in 2024.

The Quincy Institute's magazine counts $31.47 million spent failing to stop Abdul El-Sayed's Michigan Senate win.

"The reason we lost is the war," Vice President JD Vance wrote on X.

President Donald Trump announced the [US](country:US) will stop military aid to [Israel](country:IL) at the end of 2026.
