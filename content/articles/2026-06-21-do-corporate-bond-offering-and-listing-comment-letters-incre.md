Beijing — A regulator query about bonds signals default risk to equity investors, raising their cost of capital, a study finds.

In [China](country:CN)'s capital markets, bond comment letters reveal credit risk that equity holders had not priced in.

The study found no tested mitigation; whether the effect holds in other markets was not examined.
