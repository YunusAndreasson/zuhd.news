Dunyapur — Six-year-old Arsalan burned to death in a kiln.

He was at the kiln with his father, not in school.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s brick kilns often run on debt advances that bind whole families, sending children to work sites banned since 1992.

It's unclear whether the kiln was registered or any case was filed over the boy's death.
