Berlin — One vitamin can decide which microbes dominate.

Researcher Erika Freeman argues in Trends in Ecology & Evolution that scarce "keystone molecules" organize communities because most organisms lack cofactors.

The claim is a proposed framework, not an experimental result — testing it means tracing molecules through whole communities.
