Canada — [Canada](country:CA)'s price for a Cohere stake: 1,000 new tech jobs.

Cohere must build at least C$1.7 billion of computing in Canada and stay headquartered there.

Ottawa would invest up to C$1.6 billion in stages, in a round led by [Germany](country:DE)'s Schwarz Group targeting C$7 billion.

The Aleph Alpha merger, signed in September, has not closed.
