New Delhi — [Nepal](country:NP) reportedly seeks $2 billion from [India](country:IN), half in grants.

Balen Shah, 36, chose New Delhi for his first bilateral foreign visit, before Beijing.

India is Nepal's largest trading partner and main transit route, and Nepal exports hydropower to India.

Shah meets Modi on Monday, when India wants to adopt a joint vision document on energy and transit.
