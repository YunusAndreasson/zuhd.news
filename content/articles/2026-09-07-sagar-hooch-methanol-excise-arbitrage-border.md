Sagar — Eleven died from fake 'Sagar Gold' whisky.

Cross-border liquor smuggling has now killed dozens in Bundelkhand twice in six years.

Uttar Pradesh's lower liquor taxes create a smuggling margin filled by unregulated industrial alcohol.

Madhya Pradesh has suspended three excise officials as police pursue arrests in the case.
