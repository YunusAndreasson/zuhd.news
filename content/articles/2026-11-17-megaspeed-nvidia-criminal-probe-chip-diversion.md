Singapore — US investigators began watching Megaspeed in 2024.

[US](country:US) authorities suspect Southeast Asian data centres of routing Nvidia chips to Chinese developers.

Commerce requested Nvidia's sales records in May 2025, Nvidia blacklisted Megaspeed a month later, and Justice took the lead.

Megaspeed says it conducts lawful business and has hired outside counsel to investigate internally.

Singapore police continue investigating Megaspeed and six individuals, and the government agreed to keep talking with Washington.
