Online clinics prescribed Ozempic without examining patients. The US Food and Drug Administration moved against their advertising after GLP-1 weight-loss drugs surged from specialty prescriptions to mass-market demand. Health advocates warn the crackdown will restrict access for patients who cannot afford specialist care.
