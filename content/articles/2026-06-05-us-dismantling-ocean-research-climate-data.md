Washington — A $368m ocean observation system is being dismantled.

The [US](country:US) administration is shutting deep-ocean monitors that track climate currents and greenhouse gas absorption.

A court temporarily blocked National Center for Atmospheric Research closure; ocean monitoring has no similar legal protection.
