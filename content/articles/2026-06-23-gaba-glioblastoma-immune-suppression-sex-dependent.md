London — Current immunotherapy ignores a sex-specific immune brake.

GABA amplifies immune-suppressing cells in female glioblastoma tumors only — a sex-specific effect current protocols miss, researchers found.

If the pathway holds clinically, glioblastoma treatment may need sex-differentiated dosing.
