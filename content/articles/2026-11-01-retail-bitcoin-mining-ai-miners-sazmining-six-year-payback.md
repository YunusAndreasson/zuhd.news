United States — A $4,187 mining board needs over six years to repay.

Sazmining claims its customers' bitcoin will cost at least 20% less than exchange prices.

On Sazmining's estimate of 0.035 bitcoin a year at $83,000, a board nets about $633 after costs and fees.

"Receiving bitcoin regularly and recovering the full investment are different milestones," said Mark Zalan, GoMining's chief executive.

The next halving, in about 18 months, cuts the block reward 50% and lengthens that payback.
