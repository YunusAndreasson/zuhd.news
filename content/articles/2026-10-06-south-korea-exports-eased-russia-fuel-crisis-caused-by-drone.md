Seoul — [Russia](country:RU) imported over 176,000 tons of South Korean fuel, [Ukraine](country:UA) says.

That was 31% of Russian fuel imports in August, Ukraine's sanctions chief says.

Drone strikes cut Russian refining, and 7 tankers, some under UK and EU sanctions, loaded the replacement in South Korea.

[South Korea](country:KR)'s Foreign Ministry says it strictly enforces export controls against Russia.

South Korea's president has threatened unspecified measures against Ukraine over two transferred North Korean prisoners.
