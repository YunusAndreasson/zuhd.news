Pakistan bombed camps inside Afghanistan this week. Pakistani President Asif Ali Zardari declared Afghanistan had crossed a red line, citing the Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan insurgency, which Pakistan says shelters in those camps and has killed hundreds of its soldiers. China and Turkey have both attempted to broker a ceasefire since late February with no agreement reached.
