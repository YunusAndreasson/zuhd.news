New York — Bootstrap proofs suggest string theory is mathematically inevitable. String theory has dominated physics for 58 years but predicted structures too small to detect by any known experiment. Cliff Cheung at Caltech says the results justify, for the first time, asking whether string theory actually describes the universe.
