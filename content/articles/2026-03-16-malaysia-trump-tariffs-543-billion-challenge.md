Malaysia demands US tariff refunds after a court ruling. Former Finance Minister Lim Guan Eng urged the government to pursue claims after the US Supreme Court ruled Trump's $166 billion in tariffs illegal. Trump has proposed a replacement 15% global tariff facing the same constitutional challenge.
