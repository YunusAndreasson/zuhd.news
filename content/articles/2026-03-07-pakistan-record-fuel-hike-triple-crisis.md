Pakistan raised petrol 17%, its largest fuel increase ever. Pakistan faces 3 simultaneous crises: a Hormuz blockade cutting oil imports, an Afghan border war, and International Monetary Fund fiscal demands. The opposition demands a reversal, calling it an "inflation bomb" that 240 million Pakistanis will pay for.
