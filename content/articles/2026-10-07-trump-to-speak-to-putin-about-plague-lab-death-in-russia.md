Irkutsk — Trump will speak to Putin about a plague-lab worker's death.

The WHO chief has demanded transparent information from [Russia](country:RU) to settle conflicting reports.

Russia's health watchdog says the 28-year-old died of undetermined pneumonia and her contacts tested negative.

Russia told the WHO Irkutsk city has no plague cases, while local media counted about 200 people quarantined.

Trump says he will probably report on his talks with Putin on Thursday.
