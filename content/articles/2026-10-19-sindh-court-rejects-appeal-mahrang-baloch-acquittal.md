Karachi — Mahrang Baloch's sedition acquittal stands.

She remains in Quetta prison, serving 2 life sentences handed down in June in a separate case.

A two-judge Sindh High Court bench called the appeal non-maintainable and asked why prosecutors, not the private complainant, were appealing.

Prosecutors argued the anti-terrorism court should not have acquitted her before charges were framed.

The Balochistan High Court has yet to rule on her challenge to the life sentences in [Pakistan](country:PK).
