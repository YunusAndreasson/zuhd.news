East London — Julius Malema sentenced to 5 years in prison. South Africa's constitution bars any MP sentenced over 12 months — the ruling strips Malema's parliamentary seat and silences the EFF's land redistribution platform. His lawyers will appeal; if upheld, the EFF faces the next election without its founding figurehead.
