Nawa — 9 men died stopping an Israeli advance on 3 April 2025.

Villagers in southwestern [Syria](country:SY) face Israeli troops with no command structure and no government support.

Nawa residents say men on motorbikes with hunting rifles halted troops 6 kilometres away, under drone, helicopter and artillery fire.

Israeli troops have reportedly not returned to any town that saw armed firefights as of August 2026.

Unarmed street protests against [Israel](country:IL)'s military are growing across the area.
