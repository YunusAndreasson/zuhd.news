Singapore — Half of Bitget's 125 million users are Asian.

BlackRock needs Bitget's accounts and local licensing to reach those retail holders.

52% of Bitget's users hold both crypto and traditional stocks, the overlap Wall Street firms want access to.

BlackRock confirmed talks with exchanges but declined to detail any expansion plans.
