Abuja — 70% of Nigerian health facilities lack steady power.

Cold chains, oxygen concentrators and theatre lights fail whenever electricity does.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s Minister Iziaq Salako set a 30% target for steady facility power by 2027, calling electricity "a clinical input."

Reaching even that target now depends on private capital the government lacks.
