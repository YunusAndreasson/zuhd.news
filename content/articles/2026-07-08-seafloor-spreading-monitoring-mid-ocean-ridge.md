Amsterdam Island — The seafloor spread 4.2 meters in six days.

Sensors recorded a magma reservoir draining beneath the ridge, producing roughly 150 million cubic meters of new seafloor.

Some spreading left no seismic trace, researchers said, complicating future forecasts.
