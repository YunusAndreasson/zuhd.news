Mexico City — Meta, TikTok and X have 1 month to comply.

[Mexico](country:MX)'s decree follows two 18-year-olds' attack on their former high school in Coahuila.

Platforms must by design restrict violent, harassing and sexual content and immediately remove risky material from minors' profiles.

President Claudia Sheinbaum says a law with penalties follows if platforms make no progress.
