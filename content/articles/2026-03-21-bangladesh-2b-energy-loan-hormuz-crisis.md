Dhaka — Bangladesh seeks $2 billion to hold fuel prices steady. Bangladesh imports 95% of its oil but has not raised consumer prices, shutting fertilizer factories and asking multilateral lenders to cover the gap. If the blockade outlasts the loan, the government must choose between default and fuel price hikes.
