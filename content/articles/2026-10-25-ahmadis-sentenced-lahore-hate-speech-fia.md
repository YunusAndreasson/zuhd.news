Lahore — Ahmadi man gets 7 years for administering a WhatsApp group.

A second Ahmadi man got 3 years though he was not a group member.

The judge held the administrator liable for banned books members shared, an offence under [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act.

The judge acquitted both men of defiling the Quran, saying no witness described such an act.

A third accused, identified as M, has been declared a proclaimed offender.
