The strait it was sailing through normally carries one-fifth of global oil.

Owner Velos Tankers said an unidentified projectile hit the port quarter on Thursday as the ship sailed to Bahrain.

[Iran](country:IR)'s Revolutionary Guards say 2 more tankers hit mines Friday after switching off tracking and exiting with US military support.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 31 December a 37% chance, up 3 points in a week.
