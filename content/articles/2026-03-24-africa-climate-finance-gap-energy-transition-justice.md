Johannesburg — Africa receives 3% of global climate finance.

The continent emits less than 4% of historical carbon, yet G20 pledges and COP resolutions have repeatedly failed to channel money to the most climate-vulnerable populations.

G20 governments must agree binding disbursement rules before the $100 billion annual pledge moves from political commitment to cash transfer.
