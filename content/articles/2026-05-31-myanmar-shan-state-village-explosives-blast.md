Namkham — 46 dead, 6 children, from a gelignite blast.

Ta'ang rebels stored quarrying gelignite in a building 3 km from [China](country:CN)'s border; the cause is under investigation.

Rescue operations continue in [Myanmar](country:MM)'s Shan State; local media put the toll at 46–55.
