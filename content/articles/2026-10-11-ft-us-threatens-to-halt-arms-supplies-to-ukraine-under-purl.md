Kyiv — US threatens to halt Europe-funded Ukraine arms, European officials say.

The threats also cover cutting intelligence sharing unless Kyiv stops striking Russian refineries.

Under the Prioritised Ukraine Requirements List, Europeans pay for [US](country:US) weapons, while Trump blames refinery strikes for fuel prices.

A US official denied making direct threats and said negotiators only reminded Kyiv of American support.

Moscow has not confirmed the energy ceasefire Trump announced Sunday, which Zelensky called news to him.
