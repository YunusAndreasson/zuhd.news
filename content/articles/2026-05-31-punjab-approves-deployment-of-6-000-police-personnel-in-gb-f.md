Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s opposition expelled lawmakers from Gilgit-Baltistan mid-campaign.

Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf alleges systematic pre-poll rigging; the disputed territory has no full constitutional status and its elections are managed from the centre.

6,000 Punjab police were deployed ahead of the June 7 vote, which the party says proceeds under conditions of fraud.
