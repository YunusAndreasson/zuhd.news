Medicare Advantage insurers overcharge the US government $76 billion annually. Insurers inflate patient risk scores to claim payments 14% above what traditional Medicare costs, in a program now covering 30 million Americans. Insurers are now funding a campaign to eliminate MedPAC, the congressional advisory commission whose overpayment findings Congress has consistently ignored.
