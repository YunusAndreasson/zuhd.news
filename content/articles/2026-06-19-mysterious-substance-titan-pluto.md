Paris — The James Webb Space Telescope found an identical light-absorbing compound on Titan and Pluto that matches no known substance.

Both worlds share nitrogen-methane atmospheres; Paris Observatory researchers say no lab spectrum fits the signal.

NASA's Dragonfly lands on Titan in 2034 and could identify it directly from the surface.
