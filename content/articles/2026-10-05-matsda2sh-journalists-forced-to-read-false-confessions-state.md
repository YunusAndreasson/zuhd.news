Cairo — Lawyers say Matsda2sh journalists were forced to film false confessions.

[Egypt](country:EG) disappeared six fact-checkers for about a week before they saw a prosecutor.

The Egyptian Initiative for Personal Rights says National Security Agency officers filmed the confessions to manufacture evidence for conviction.

Prosecutors have yet to issue detention orders for five of the six.
