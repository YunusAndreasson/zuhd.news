Osogbo — Civil society groups tallied 56 incidents, 14 deaths.

Lawmakers now demand a federal probe into the Osun election violence.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s electoral body runs the vote, with federal security forces deciding which polling units stay reachable.

Lawmakers also want Senator Francis Fadahunsi investigated over a circulated video urging violence.
