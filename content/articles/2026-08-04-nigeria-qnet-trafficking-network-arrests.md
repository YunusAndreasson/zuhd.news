Lagos — Twelve foreigners recruited victims through fake job offers, [Nigeria](country:NG)'s immigration service alleges.

The QNET/IGNITE ring charged an upfront registration fee and promised income tied to recruiting others — the same structure as multi-level marketing.

Investigators say more members and victims remain unidentified across the country.
