US intelligence says war cannot topple Iran's government. The National Intelligence Council concluded Iran's clerical-military system would survive its leaders' deaths, with the fragmented opposition deemed "unlikely" to take control. The White House has not publicly addressed the assessment as Khamenei's successor enters his second week in power.
