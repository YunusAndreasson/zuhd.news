Rights groups named journalists, students, and activist Lyle Prijoles among the dead; the [Philippines](country:PH) armed forces characterized all as communist fighters. [The US](country:US) funds and trains the Philippine military under the Mutual Defense Treaty and has not commented on the deaths.
