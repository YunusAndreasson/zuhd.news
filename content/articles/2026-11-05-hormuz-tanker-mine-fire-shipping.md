Tehran — Another tanker hit a mine east of Hormuz, [Iran](country:IR)'s navy says.

Hormuz normally carries one-fifth of global oil consumption, and Iran calls it closed to uncoordinated ships.

The navy said the tanker had switched off its tracking systems, like other vessels mined in recent days.

[US](country:US) Central Command says it has helped Gulf states export 1.5 billion barrels through Hormuz since summer.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 31 December a 36% chance, up 4 points in a week.
