Riga — French Rafale downed [Latvia](country:LV)'s first intercepted drone Monday.

Latvia's army said it entered from [Russia](country:RU) via electronic warfare — the jamming that has repeatedly deflected Ukrainian drones into allied airspace.

NATO has no settled rule for deniable drone incursions; Latvia's government collapsed over the last one in May.
