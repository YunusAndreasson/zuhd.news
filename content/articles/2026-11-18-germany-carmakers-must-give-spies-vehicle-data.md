Berlin — [Germany](country:DE)'s Bundestag voted Friday to let spies demand carmakers' driver data.

Unlike a smartphone, a car's electronics cannot be switched off, expert Erich Möchel says.

Foreign and domestic intelligence agencies can now secretly demand telemetry, sensor and behaviour data through standardised wiretap interfaces.

Which data counts remains undefined, and carmakers' associations are asking for legal clarity.
