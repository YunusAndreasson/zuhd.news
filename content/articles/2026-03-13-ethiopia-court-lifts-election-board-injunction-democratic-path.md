Ethiopia's court cleared 5 war-disputed constituencies to hold elections. Contested between Amhara and Tigray since their 2022 war, the 5 seats will now elect MPs outside Tigray's regional jurisdiction. Opposition groups that boycotted Ethiopia's 2021 election have not said whether they will contest these seats.
