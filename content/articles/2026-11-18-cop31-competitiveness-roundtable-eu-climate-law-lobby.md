Antalya — 41 people from EU climate rollback lobbies registered at COP31.

The Roundtable, which gutted the EU's due-diligence law, includes Chevron, ExxonMobil and TotalEnergies.

Teneo, the public relations firm that coordinated the group, holds a $3.9 million contract handling communications for host [Turkey](country:TR).

None of the 7 companies or Teneo answered Drop Site, which reviewed the UN participants list.
