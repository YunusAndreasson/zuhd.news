Heraklion — [Greece](country:GR) arrested a Palestinian on bombing suspicion.

Police say the suspect, a Crete-based electrician, traveled to [Malaysia](country:MY) with 4 Palestinian men detained in [Cyprus](country:CY) for explosives training using commercial chemicals.

The suspect faces a magistrate; no formal charges have been filed and the investigation continues.
