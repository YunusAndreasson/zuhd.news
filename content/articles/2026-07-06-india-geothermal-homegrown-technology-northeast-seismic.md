Dirang — Scientists drilled [India](country:IN)'s first northeast geothermal well.

Arunachal Pradesh sits on the seismically active Main Central Thrust fault, the same geology that yields its estimated 2 gigawatts of heat.

A planned Tawang project could generate up to 1 megawatt, pending community consent.
