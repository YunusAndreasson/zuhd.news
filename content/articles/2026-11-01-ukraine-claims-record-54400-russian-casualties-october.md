Kyiv — [Ukraine](country:UA)'s General Staff counts 54,400 Russian casualties in October, a monthly record.

The figure combines killed, wounded and missing soldiers and comes from one belligerent counting the other.

Unmanned Systems Forces commander Robert Brovdi said his pilots hit a Russian soldier every 2.5 minutes.

President Zelensky said Ukraine retook 37 square miles, against 33 square miles [Russia](country:RU) reportedly took.

Zelensky said only a Russian ceasefire would stop the losses.
