Médecins Sans Frontières suspended most operations at Gaza's largest functioning hospital. Armed men entered Nasser Hospital in Khan Younis repeatedly, intimidating staff and moving suspected weapons through the facility. MSF pulled out of pediatrics, maternity, neonatal intensive care, and outpatient services on January 20 but still runs surgical and critical inpatient wards. Israel, whose military campaign the International Court of Justice has called plausibly genocidal, ordered MSF to hand over staff lists or halt operations. MSF refused without safety guarantees, leaving hundreds of patients with nowhere to go.
