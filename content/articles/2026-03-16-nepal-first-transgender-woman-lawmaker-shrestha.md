Nepal's LGBTQ rights exist on paper but not yet in statute. Bhumika Shrestha was seated as Nepal's first transgender MP on Monday, in a country that legalized same-sex marriage in 2023 and constitutionally recognizes a third gender — among the most progressive frameworks in South Asia. Shrestha says her mandate is to convert those constitutional provisions into enforceable law.
