Nabatieh — [Israel](country:IL) killed 6 Lebanese paramedics in 48 hours.

4 died overnight in Hannawiyah when strikes destroyed a Health Authority center and ambulance station; a nominal ceasefire holds since April 16.

Field reporting counts over 120 rescue workers killed since March 2, 16 hospitals damaged, and 130 ambulances targeted.
