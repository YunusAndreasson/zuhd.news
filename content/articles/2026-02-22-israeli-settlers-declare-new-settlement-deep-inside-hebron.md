Israeli soldiers arrested dozens before settlers moved in. The settlers seized 5 Palestinian-owned buildings in Jabal Jalles, a densely populated neighborhood in Hebron, declaring it a new settlement. They vowed not to wait for government approval, and the takeover's legality remains contested.
