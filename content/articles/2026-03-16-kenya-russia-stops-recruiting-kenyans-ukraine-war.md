More than 1,000 Kenyans fought for Russia in Ukraine. Kenyan officials say brokers recruited young men with promises of regular work, delivering them into Russian army contracts that exploited high youth unemployment. Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov declined to confirm the recruitment-halt deal and said the Defense Ministry was reviewing cases.
