London — New Linux users routinely assume sudo has crashed. The 1980 sudo design shows nothing during password entry to hide length from shoulder surfers; Ubuntu 26.04, shipping April 23, will show asterisks instead. If Debian and Fedora follow, the 1980 behavior disappears from Linux entirely.
