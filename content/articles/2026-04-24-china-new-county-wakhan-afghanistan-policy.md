Beijing — [China](country:CN) gazetted a county in the Wakhan Corridor. Administrative designation in Chinese practice precedes infrastructure licensing — the sequence that opened Xinjiang's trade corridors before construction followed. No investment timeline with Taliban-governed [Afghanistan](country:AF) has been announced; the designation establishes China's legal basis to negotiate one.
