Israeli strikes killed 10 in southern Lebanon on Sunday. The raids also wounded 13 across Qatrani, Majdal Selm, and Aitaat — the same day Israeli officials announced plans for ceasefire talks with Beirut. The Lebanese death toll has passed 826 since the war began March 2, with no ceasefire in effect.
