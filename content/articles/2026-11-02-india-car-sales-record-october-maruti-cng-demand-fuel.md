New Delhi — Carmakers dispatched an estimated 525,000 cars to dealers in October.

[India](country:IN)'s October figures count dealer shipments, not buyer purchases.

Maruti Suzuki led with 212,000 domestic dispatches, up 20%, as makers stocked dealers ahead of Diwali.

Mobility Global's Gaurav Vangaal expects October registrations to fall as festive deliveries shift into November.
