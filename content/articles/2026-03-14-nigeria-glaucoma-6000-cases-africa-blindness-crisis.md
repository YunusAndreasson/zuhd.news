Nigerian state Abia logged 6,381 glaucoma cases in 18 months. Africa carries the highest global burden of the disease but has far less treatment infrastructure than wealthier regions, making early detection the only defense against irreversible blindness. State health officials estimate thousands more remain undiagnosed and face permanent blindness without screening.
