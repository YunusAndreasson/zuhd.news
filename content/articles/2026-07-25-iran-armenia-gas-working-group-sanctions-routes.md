Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) and Armenia agreed to a joint energy working group.

Their existing gas-for-electricity swap is barter, not sale — how sanctioned energy trade moves without dollar settlement, oil minister Mohsen Paknejad said.

[Armenia](country:AM) is landlocked between two neighbors it distrusts; any expansion runs through one pipeline and one border.
