Bengaluru — Spot gold hit $3,757 an ounce, its lowest since September 2025.

Goldman Sachs says central bank buying is now gold's main remaining support.

Higher [US](country:US) rates raise the opportunity cost of holding gold, which pays no yield.

Polymarket traders give a 25-basis-point Federal Reserve hike after the December meeting a 94% chance, up 8 points in a week.
