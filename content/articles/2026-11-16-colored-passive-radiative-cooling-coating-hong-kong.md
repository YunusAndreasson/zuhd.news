Hong Kong — A vividly coloured coating reflects 97% of light.

Until now, passive daytime cooling meant white or mirror-like surfaces.

A top layer makes colour by thin-film interference, above a thicker reflective layer, in an ethyl cellulose material system.

Whether the ethyl cellulose films scale up cheaply is the open question.
