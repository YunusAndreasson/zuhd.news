Antalya — Countries get their first guide to leaving fossil fuels.

The pledge made 3 years ago carried no details on how to do it.

[Brazil](country:BR)'s road map offers different levers, starting with halting new fossil fuel plants and cutting fossil fuel subsidies.

Brazil's Ana Toni, who ran last year's talks, declined to comment on her country's oil exports.

Brazil also presents road maps on deforestation and on $1.3 trillion in climate finance for poorer nations.
