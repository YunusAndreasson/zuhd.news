Michigan — Mike Rogers once chaired MITRE, which now coordinates Hegseth's Project Meridian.

Tax filings show he earned over $420,000 on MITRE's board in six years through 2023.

Project Meridian convenes weapons makers and tech leaders, including Elon Musk, to map the future of [US](country:US) warfare in [Iran](country:IR) and beyond.

Rogers left in 2023 to run for Senate, and his campaign did not respond to requests for comment.

Michigan votes Tuesday, with a pollster finding Democrat Abdul El-Sayed narrowly ahead in late August.
