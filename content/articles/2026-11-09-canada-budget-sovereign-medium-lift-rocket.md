Canada — The budget backs medium-lift rockets from at least 2 spaceports.

SpaceX is retiring Falcon 9, opening a medium-lift gap Canadian companies want to fill.

[Canada](country:CA)'s defence department awarded C$200 million over 10 years to Spaceport Nova Scotia and C$20 million to NordSpace.

The budget sets no clear timeline for the medium-lift upgrade.

Small sovereign rockets are expected to fly from the two spaceports in 2028.
