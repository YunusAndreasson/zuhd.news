Obeid — Five towns fell in a 300 sq km triangle.

[Sudan](country:SD)'s army cut the Saderat Road, the Rapid Support Forces' supply line to Omdurman, in its largest offensive since retaking the capital.

Both sides now compile detainee lists for a UN exchange; the RSF says the offensive ends any basis for a truce.
