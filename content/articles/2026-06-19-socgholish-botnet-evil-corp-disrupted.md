Washington — An international operation disrupted SocGholish, fake-browser-update malware linked to [Russia](country:RU)'s Evil Corp.

SocGholish posed as browser updates to install malware, then sold victim access to ransomware operators.

No arrests were made; Evil Corp has not faced extradition despite US, UK, and EU indictments.
