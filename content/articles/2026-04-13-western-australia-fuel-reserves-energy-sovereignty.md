Perth — Western Australia is building its own diesel stockpile. During the fuel crisis, private suppliers Viva and Ampol held WA's national fuel obligation stocks in Queensland — 3,500 kilometers away. Queensland's Taroom Trough began producing 200 barrels daily — Australia's first new oil field since the 1970s.
