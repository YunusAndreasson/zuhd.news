Jerusalem — Gadi Eisenkot has one coalition partner and 48 seats.

Netanyahu's bloc holds 53, so neither side reaches the 61 needed.

Eisenkot's 48 plus the Arab parties' 19 would reach 67, but Lieberman has opposed relying on them.

Eisenkot is also negotiating a unity deal with Likud, so the Democrats suspended talks with him.

His mandate expires 6 December, extendable by 14 days, then Netanyahu gets a turn before a fifth election.
