10 Solomon Islands ministers quit, threatening the government's survival. Solomon Islands switched recognition from Taiwan to China in 2019, making its government the Pacific's most geopolitically contested seat. Defection leader Kologeto says he commands 28 parliamentary votes and plans to file a no-confidence motion.
