Washington — One Indiana search's stated reason: "LMAO."

Every driver logged by Flock's 19,000 cameras depends on that box for oversight.

Flock's defense against misuse is a free-text reason field that nobody reviews, the Electronic Frontier Foundation found.

The audit covers 2023 through late 2025, when Flock changed the reason field.
