Stockholm — About 100 trillion neutrinos cross your body each second.

Francis Halzen, 82, won the 2026 physics Nobel for detecting the cosmic kind.

Buried sensors in a cubic kilometre of South Pole ice record the flash when a neutrino strikes a nucleus.

"I am working on a proposal, and I hope that this prize will help getting it approved," said Halzen.

Hundreds built the observatory, but Halzen alone receives the prize at the 10 December ceremony.
