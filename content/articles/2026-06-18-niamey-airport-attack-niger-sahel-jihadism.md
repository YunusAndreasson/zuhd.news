Niamey — Gunmen breached the perimeter of Diori Hamani airport before dawn Thursday.

[Niger](country:NE)'s junta justified its 2023 coup on restoring security; a January Islamic State-linked attack on the same airport was also repelled, with [Russia](country:RU)n help.

No group claimed Thursday's assault; Niger's military has not issued a statement.
