Washington — Nobody legally owns an international waterway's name.

[Iran](country:IR) and [Oman](country:OM) control the strait's actual waters and shipping lanes.

Hydrographic names are set by each country's own charting agency, not decreed by executive order.

A precedent already exists: the Persian Gulf naming dispute has run unresolved for 70 years.
