India — Apple Pay launches on Axis Bank cards only.

Apple Pay cannot scan a Unified Payments Interface QR code, the rail behind Google Pay's Indian payments.

It stores Axis Bank Visa and Mastercard credit cards for contactless taps on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

Apple's Jennifer Bailey discussed expanding bank support beyond Axis, but no other issuer is live.
