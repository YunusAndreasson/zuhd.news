Berlin — [Germany](country:DE) is buying 40% of Leopard-maker KMW+Nexter Defense Systems.

[France](country:FR) already holds 50%; both governments described the deal as securing arms production as strategic infrastructure, not a tradable asset.

Both governments plan to reduce their combined stakes to around 30% after a near-term stock market listing.
