London — Linux's kernel project patched over 1,000 vulnerabilities last month.

[Britain](country:GB)'s National Cyber Security Centre warns of an unprecedented rise in cyber incidents in coming months.

Chief executive Richard Horne says AI lets attackers exploit long-tolerated weaknesses at a scale once unimaginable.

Officials concede experts have not yet seen a matching rise in exploitation.

The agency rates AI-powered ransomware against critical infrastructure by 2028 as highly likely.
