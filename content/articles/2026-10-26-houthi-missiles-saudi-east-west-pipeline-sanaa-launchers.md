Riyadh — [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s air defences intercepted 10 ballistic missiles Sunday.

The Houthis claim they hit the East-West pipeline, Riyadh's crude export route avoiding Hormuz.

Debris from two intercepted missiles injured 2 people and damaged 3 homes in Khamis Mushait, Civil Defense said.

Saudi Arabia had not confirmed any hit, and the coalition says it destroyed two launchers in Sanaa.

Polymarket traders give a Saudi-Houthi ceasefire by 31 December 21% odds, down 10 points in a week.
