Gaza — 186 aid workers were killed there in 2025.

That is over half the 350 aid workers killed worldwide, the UN says.

The Aid Worker Security Database recorded 907 total killed, injured or kidnapped, most of them local staff.

UNRWA's former chief has sought a panel to investigate the deaths of its staff.
