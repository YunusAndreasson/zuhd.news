Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) says commandos retook 2 tankers, state-run Mehr reported.

Navy commander Rear Admiral Shahram Irani said the Strait of Hormuz stays closed on the Supreme Leader's order.

Ship traffic through Hormuz averaged 2.7 ships a day to 22 November, 24% below its 90-day normal.

No outside source has confirmed the recovery of the tankers.
