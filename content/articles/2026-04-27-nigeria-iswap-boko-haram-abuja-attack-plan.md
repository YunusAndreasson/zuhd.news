Abuja — A leaked memo names 3 Abuja sites as jihadist targets.

Nnamdi Azikiwe Airport, a military detention centre, and a prison are listed as targets for Islamic State's West Africa branch and Boko Haram.

[Nigeria](country:NG) has neither confirmed the alert nor denied it; security forces are deployed at all 3 sites.
