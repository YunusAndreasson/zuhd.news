Niamey — Niger declared Africa's largest gas pipeline "imminent" on Sunday. The 4,000 km pipeline would carry 30 billion m³ of Nigerian gas to Europe; it has been on hold since Niger's 2023 coup froze ties with Algeria. Algeria's state energy company Sonatrach surveyed the route this month, with construction set to begin after Ramadan 2026.
