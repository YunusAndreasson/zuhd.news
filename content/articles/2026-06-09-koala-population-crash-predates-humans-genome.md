Sydney — Koalas crashed 100,000 years before humans reached [Australia](country:AU).

A genomic study found the species already genetically depleted before human contact, carrying less diversity to absorb today's pressures.

Researchers said conservation strategies premised on a pre-human genetic baseline need revision.
