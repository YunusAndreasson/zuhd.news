Guangzhou — Nanopore caught rare thalassemia variants PCR panels missed.

35 hospitals in southern [China](country:CN) used a portable MinION sequencer completing each run in 3 hours — cheap enough for clinic-level deployment.

Thalassemia is endemic in South Asia and the Middle East; researchers say the method adapts to any regional variant panel.
