Milot — 30 died at Haiti's symbol of revolution Saturday. Built to repel French reinvasion after independence, the fortress was packed for its annual anniversary when rain triggered a stampede at the entrance. Visits are suspended and an investigation opened; Haiti has no crowd management infrastructure for major public gatherings.
