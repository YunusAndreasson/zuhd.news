Carnarvon — The world's largest radio telescope has two hosts.

Karoo communities in [South Africa](country:ZA) and Wajarri communities in [Australia](country:AU) host the array.

Big science usually treats a site as empty, but this project pairs the array with residents' own sky records.

Whether host communities count as collaborators or the displaced is now a design choice.
