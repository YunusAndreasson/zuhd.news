Antarctica's Larsen C ice shelf could collapse by 2100. A study in Frontiers in Environmental Science modelled warming scenarios and found sea ice could fall 20%, triggering irreversible glacier retreat. The changes would raise sea levels worldwide with no way to reverse them on any human timescale.
