Atlanta — 67,984 US adolescents died with a mental disorder listed as a cause since 1999.

American Indian and Alaska Native youth carried the highest burden; the rate climbed in two waves, 1999-2006 and 2009-2021, per federal death-certificate data.

The figures rest on what a coroner wrote, researchers noted — a gap that undercounts more where psychiatric coding is thinner still.
