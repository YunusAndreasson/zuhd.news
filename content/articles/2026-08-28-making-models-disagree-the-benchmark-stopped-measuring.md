London — Rival brain models often fit data equally well.

A benchmark all models pass has stopped distinguishing which explanation is right.

Researchers say experiments should be designed so competing models predict different outcomes, not just fit the same data better.

The review proposes disagreement-maximizing tests, not yet standard practice in neuroscience labs.
