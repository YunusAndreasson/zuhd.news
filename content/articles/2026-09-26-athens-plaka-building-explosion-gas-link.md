Athens — An explosion killed 1 woman and left 5 missing.

The blast destroyed a building in Plaka, injured 3 people and damaged nearby homes, shops and cars.

Authorities suspect a gas leak, but the sequence of events is unclear and investigators are examining gas infrastructure.

Rescuers are still searching for 5 missing people, and authorities have not confirmed their nationalities.
