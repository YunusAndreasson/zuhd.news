Ypsilanti, population 20,000, may host America's nuclear weapons datacenter. The township's attorney warned that the facility's ties to Los Alamos National Laboratory — birthplace of the atomic bomb — make Ypsilanti a drone-strike target during the Iran war. Ypsilanti's trustees must now decide whether to formally oppose the $1.25 billion project.
