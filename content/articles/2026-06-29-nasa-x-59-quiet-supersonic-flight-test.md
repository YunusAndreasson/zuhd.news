Edwards — The sonic boom's replacement just flew supersonic.

The X-59 spaces shockwaves apart so the atmosphere weakens each before merging, trading Concorde's 105-decibel boom for a 75-decibel thump, the US space agency said.

A [US](country:US) national tour will collect public feedback Congress needs to consider lifting a 1973 overland supersonic ban.
