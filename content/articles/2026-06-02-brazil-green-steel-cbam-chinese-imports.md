São Paulo — Chinese steel imports into [Brazil](country:BR) rose 25.9% last year, the industry association said.

The EU's carbon border tariff will penalize coal-made exports; Brazil has 2 green hydrogen pilots and an $18bn investment pipeline.

Anti-dumping duties on Chinese steel took effect in early 2026; the EU carbon deadline remains.
