Atlanta — Rubio hosted Quad ministers to plan a Manila leaders' summit next month.

It would be Trump's first Quad summit, after he cancelled a 2025 India trip over tariffs.

[US](country:US) Secretary of State Marco Rubio hosted [Australia](country:AU), [India](country:IN) and [Japan](country:JP) on Saturday, then closed a G20 session Lavrov attended.

India's foreign minister Subrahmanyam Jaishankar told the G20 that deliberate attacks on energy infrastructure are a strategy to hold prices hostage.

G20 leaders meet at Trump's Doral resort near Miami on 14 December.
