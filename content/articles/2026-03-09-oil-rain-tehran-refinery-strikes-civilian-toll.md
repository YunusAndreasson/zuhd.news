Israeli airstrikes left Tehran residents using headlights at noon. Strikes hit 5 oil facilities near the capital, igniting fires that filled the air with burning hydrocarbons; Iran's Red Crescent warned of chemical burns and lung damage. With the facilities destroyed, Iran faces fuel shortages for a population already cut off from international markets by sanctions.
