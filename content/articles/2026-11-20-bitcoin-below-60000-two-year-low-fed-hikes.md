United States — Bitcoin erased its $87,000 rebound, hitting $57,716.

The Nasdaq dropped 3.8% and gold fell 2% toward $3,500 the same day.

Exchanges liquidated about $1.5 billion in leveraged crypto positions in 24 hours, CoinGlass data show, as falling prices exhausted collateral.

"The place to hide is cash," said Maxime Seiler, CEO of trading firm STS Digital.

Polymarket traders give a 25-basis-point Fed hike in December a 91% chance, up 3 points in a week.
