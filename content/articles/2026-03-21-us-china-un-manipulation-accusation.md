A House report accused China of subverting the UN. The report says China fills the vacuum created by US withdrawal from 31 UN entities and $6.7 billion in unpaid dues. The committee recommended new US hiring in lower-level UN roles, warning that without re-engagement, China will continue filling positions Washington has vacated.
