Aralsk — The Kok-Aral Dam restored [Kazakhstan](country:KZ)'s North Aral Sea.

Soviet irrigation diverted Aral rivers, drying 90% of the lake; the dam refilled the northern section over 20 years.

The southern half, on [Uzbekistan](country:UZ)'s side, stays desert; 80 million tons of toxic dust disperse yearly.
