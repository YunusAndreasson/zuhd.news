Jiuquan — Muhammad Zeeshan Ali is the first foreigner aboard Tiangong.

The US has barred [China](country:CN) from the International Space Station since 2011, so Beijing is recruiting partners such as [Pakistan](country:PK).

Ali, a payload specialist, trained for months in China and returns aboard Shenzhou-23.

His stay of 5 to 10 days is far short of the usual six-month Chinese tours.

China aims to land astronauts on the Moon by 2030 and build a lunar base with Russia by 2035.
