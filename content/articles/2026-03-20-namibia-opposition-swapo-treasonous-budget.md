Namibia's monthly pension will rise by N$100, roughly US$5. Opposition lawmakers accuse Swapo, the ruling party, of breaking a promise to shield the poor from inflation in one of the world's most unequal countries. The budget faces a final parliamentary vote that Swapo's majority is expected to carry without amendment.
