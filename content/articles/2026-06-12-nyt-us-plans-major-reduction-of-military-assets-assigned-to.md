Brussels — Washington withdraws all 8 NATO refuelling tankers.

NATO's long-range strike posture has depended on [US](country:US) refuelling for 75 years; fighters and bombers also face reduction, European officials said.

No European member has committed to fill the gap; the document circulated this month named no replacement timeline.
