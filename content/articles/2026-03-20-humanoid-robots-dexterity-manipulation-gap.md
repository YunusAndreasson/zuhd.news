Humanoid robots backflip but cannot grasp a dime. Fine manipulation remains unsolved because scientists lack a complete model of human hand sensation and proprioception, despite deep learning having cracked bipedal walking. Tesla is preselling humanoid robots for households, a bet that the manipulation gap will close before delivery.
