Bucharest — Dozens of Facebook pages posted one message within 15 minutes.

The grievance was real: farmers demanded compensation for culled animals and drought losses.

Funky Citizens, a Romanian NGO, analysed 1,186 Facebook posts and 100 TikTok videos, tracking near-identical wording moving between pages seconds apart.

Several hundred to over a thousand people attended on 15 September, against the message's claim of almost 200,000.

The EU ban on live sheep and goat exports runs to the end of 2026.
