Cambridge — AI systems now run thousands of lab experiments without human sign-off. Governance frameworks were built for human-paced research; no biosafety authority has jurisdiction over AI-generated experimental designs. The gap between capability and oversight grows as these systems scale into drug discovery and pathogen research.
