London — A 22-year-old is the 7th arrested over RAF Fairford.

Police arrested him on suspicion of preparation of terrorist acts near a base hosting [US](country:US) warplanes.

He is linked to the 27 September incident, when three vans carrying gasoline were stopped but no explosives were found.

[Iran](country:IR) denies involvement, and all six earlier suspects have been released on bail.

Prime Minister Andy Burnham cites strong indications of Iranian involvement but has given no details.
