New York — WebinarTV scraped and posted 200,000+ private Zoom meetings without consent. Targets included 12-step recovery and chronic illness groups where participants used their real names. Zoom's architecture does not prevent scraping of public webinars; regulators have not acted on the disclosed incidents.
