Vladimir Oblast — [Ukraine](country:UA) struck Moscow's oil hub a 2nd time this month.

The Vtorovo depot, run by Transneft, supplies Moscow's 3 major airports and regional oil exports.

Ukraine's Security Service said the strike is part of a 40-day campaign against [Russia](country:RU)'s energy infrastructure.
