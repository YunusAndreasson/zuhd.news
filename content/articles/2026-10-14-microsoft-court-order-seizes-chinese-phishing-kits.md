United States — A court order seized domains behind 750 million phishing messages.

The kits impersonated 75,000 organizations in more than 150 countries, Microsoft said.

Iron Man rents 2,300 pre-built lure pages, while YAM Store sells compromised Microsoft and Google accounts to host them.

Microsoft is still removing infrastructure and blocking more than 100,000 impersonation domains.
