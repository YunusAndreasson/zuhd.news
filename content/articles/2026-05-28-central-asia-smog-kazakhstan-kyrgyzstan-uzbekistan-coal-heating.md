Bishkek — [Uzbekistan](country:UZ) ranked 10th most polluted country on Earth.

Soviet coal heating that keeps -25°C winters survivable is also the dominant source of the city-choking smog, IQAir's 2025 rankings show.

[Kazakhstan](country:KZ), [Kyrgyzstan](country:KG) and Uzbekistan have begun reforms, but replacing coal requires gas and grid investments each has postponed.
