Montreal — Alzheimer's goes undiagnosed 2.7 years longer in women.

A McGill University study found women score normally on memory tests despite the same brain pathology, masking disease progression.

Standard screening cutoffs may need sex-specific thresholds; new Alzheimer's drugs only work in early-stage disease.
