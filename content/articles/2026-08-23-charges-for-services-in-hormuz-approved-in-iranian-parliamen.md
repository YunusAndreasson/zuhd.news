Tehran — Parliament approved fees on at least five services.

Brent crude has risen 33% amid [Iran](country:IR)'s Hormuz standoff, as of Aug. 18.

International law bars fees for mere passage but allows charges for services such as insurance and safety.

Parliament has not yet set an effective date for the new charges.
