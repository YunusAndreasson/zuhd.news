London — Half-strength BECLIN1 leaves the gut lining vulnerable.

BECLIN1 governs autophagy; mice with one working copy developed fewer mucus-making goblet cells and worse colitis under chemical stress.

The findings link autophagy gene dosage to inflammatory bowel disease susceptibility; no therapeutic target was identified.
