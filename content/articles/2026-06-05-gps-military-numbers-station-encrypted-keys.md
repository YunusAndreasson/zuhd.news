London — Every GPS satellite carries hidden Pentagon encryption traffic.

University College London's Steven Murdoch identified a 176-bit GPS field as the [US](country:US) military's cryptographic key-distribution channel, in use since 2007.

The military has not confirmed the channel; Murdoch found 3,994 unique transmissions in 12 million archived signals.
