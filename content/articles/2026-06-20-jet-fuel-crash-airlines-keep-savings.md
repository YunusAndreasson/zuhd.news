New York — Jet fuel dropped 45%; airlines kept elevated fares.

Airlines lost $1 billion in Q1, Bureau of Transportation Statistics figures show, and cite unbroken demand to justify holding fares up.

United's CEO said the carrier would retain 20% of price increases even if conditions return to pre-war normal.
