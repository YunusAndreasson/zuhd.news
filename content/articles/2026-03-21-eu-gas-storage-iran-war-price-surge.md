Brussels — EU gas prices jumped 30% since Iran's war began. Iran bombed Qatar's Ras Laffan complex, which supplies 20% of global LNG, knocking out 17% of Qatar's export capacity for up to 5 years. EU Energy Commissioner Jorgensen cut the winter storage target from 90% to 80% and urged member states to begin refilling now.
