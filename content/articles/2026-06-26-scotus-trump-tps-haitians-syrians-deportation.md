Washington — The Supreme Court ruled executives can revoke Temporary Protected Status by nationality, ending deportation protection for Haitians and Syrians.

The status barred removal of nationals from war-torn states; the Court held no individual review is required.

The ruling applies to holders from other countries the administration is reviewing.
