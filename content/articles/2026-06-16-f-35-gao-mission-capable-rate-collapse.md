Washington — F-35 full readiness fell from 38% to 25% since 2021.

Spare-parts shortages, software debt, repair backlogs, and corrosion compounded across the [US](country:US)'s $2 trillion F-35 program, the Government Accountability Office found.

The Pentagon requested $21 billion for 85 more F-35s in fiscal 2027, nearly double the prior year's 47.
