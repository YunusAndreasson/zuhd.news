Bandar Abbas — [Iran](country:IR)'s judiciary counts at least 420 fishing vessels hit by US strikes.

The city's wholesale market, the country's largest seafood loading site, handles an estimated 800,000 tons a year.

Wholesaler Elias Rahimi says sales fell 30% as inflation and the [US](country:US) naval siege keep fishers from deep water.

Strikes on 8 and 9 July destroyed more than 30 boats at the wharf.

Polymarket traders give Hormuz traffic a 19% chance of normalising by 31 December.
