Washington — 1.8 million educators were urged to boycott Target.

The union is pricing Target's silence on Immigration and Customs Enforcement raids in Minnesota.

The union sent Target's chief executive letters in February and March before launching this week's boycott across the [US](country:US).

Target has not yet responded to the union's letters or its boycott call.
