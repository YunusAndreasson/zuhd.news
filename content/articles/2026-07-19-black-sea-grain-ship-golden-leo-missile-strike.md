Odessa — Five died aboard a flagged grain ship.

The Guinea-Bissau-flagged, Turkish-owned Golden Leo was hit by three cruise missiles from [Russia](country:RU) while leaving [Ukraine](country:UA)'s grain export corridor.

Five crew remain missing as Kyiv notifies the International Maritime Organization and the home governments of Syria and India.
