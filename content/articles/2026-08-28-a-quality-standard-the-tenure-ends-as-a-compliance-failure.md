Urumqi — A cotton-quality rule now tracks every bale to a named farmer.

Growers who miss its seed or acreage specifications lose the right to sell their land.

The July 1 regulation ties barcoded traceability records, kept five years, to planting, land plots and processing across [China](country:CN)'s Uyghur region.

About 7 million people farm the region's cotton, most of them Uyghur, Xinhua reported.
