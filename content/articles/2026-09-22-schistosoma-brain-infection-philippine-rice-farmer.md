Half her body convulsed for a minute, then stopped.

The rice farmer's seizure, in the [Philippines](country:PH), traced to a neglected tropical disease of paddy labor.

Schistosoma japonicum larvae travel from paddy water through skin and blood to the brain, confirmed by a 1950s precipitin test.

Treated with antiparasitic and anti-seizure drugs, she had no further seizures three months later, a brain scan clear.
