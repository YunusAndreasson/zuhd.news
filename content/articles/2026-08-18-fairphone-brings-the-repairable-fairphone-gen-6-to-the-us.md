Amsterdam — Two screws open the Fairphone Gen 6+'s back panel.

Modular parts let owners replace worn components instead of buying a new phone.

The $650 phone ships with a removable battery and 12 purchasable components, from an $8 SIM tray to a $90 screen.

Fairphone promises seven years of software support as it sells directly in the US.
