Vienna — 5 European states will send rejected asylum seekers to Rwanda.

[Rwanda](country:RW) would receive European funds to build and run centres in Kigali from 2027.

[Austria](country:AT), [Denmark](country:DK), [Germany](country:DE), [Greece](country:GR) and the [Netherlands](country:NL) will start with single men whose visas or asylum claims were refused.

Human Rights Watch cites Rwanda's record of arbitrary detention and mistreatment in detention centres.

The centre is due to open in 2027, under an EU rule change permitting offshore sites.
