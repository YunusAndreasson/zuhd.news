United States — Downdetector logged 130,000 Outlook outage reports at the peak.

Outlook, Teams and Copilot failed in 74 countries within an hour, Downdetector data show.

Servers failing automated health checks went offline, and Microsoft says the strain on the rest, not extra traffic, caused the overload.

Microsoft has not found a recent change that coincided with the start of the outage.

Microsoft is bypassing the failing health-check component and has not said when service fully returns.
