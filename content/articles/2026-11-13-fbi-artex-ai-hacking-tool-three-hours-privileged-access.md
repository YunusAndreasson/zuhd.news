Washington — Some Artex intrusions reached privileged access in under 3 hours.

Cybercriminals and nation-states are using it against governments and critical industries worldwide, the Federal Bureau of Investigation said.

Artex turns any large language model into a hacking agent that converts plain-language goals into machine commands.

Its developer pulled the GitHub repository, but forks, archives and a commercial version keep circulating, the bureau said.

Censys found over 1,600 exposed Artex consoles, and the bureau says real deployments run higher.
