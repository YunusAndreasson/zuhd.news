Lagos — Blackouts have interrupted clinical services at [Nigeria](country:NG)'s federal hospitals.

The government is contracting private firms to finance and operate hospital power under the Nigeria Power for Health Initiative.

The first phase covers federal tertiary hospitals; expansion to primary care has no set timeline.
