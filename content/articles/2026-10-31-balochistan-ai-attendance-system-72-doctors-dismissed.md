Quetta — Balochistan dismissed 72 doctors for prolonged absence from duty.

An AI-based attendance system monitors staff, and health department dismissals now total 200.

Notices went to 11,000 employees, and officials say Rs410 million has been deducted from salaries over absenteeism.

Health Minister Bakht Muhammad Kakar said the doctors ignored repeated notices to report for duty.

Departmental action is under way against another 800 employees, the notification said.
