New Delhi — Sachet-sized soap and tea sales just hit a 13-quarter high.

Hindustan Unilever's stock fell 7% anyway, erasing ₹35,620 crore, as investors read 10% sales growth as margin bought with price cuts and promotion spending.

The company expects commodity inflation to keep pressuring margins into FY27.
