Cambridge — New chips trap light for millions of oscillation cycles. Researchers solved the fabrication problem for van der Waals photonic chips, long too fragile for standard nanofabrication despite decades of promise. Millions of cycles without energy loss is the threshold for practical optical computing and on-chip communications.
