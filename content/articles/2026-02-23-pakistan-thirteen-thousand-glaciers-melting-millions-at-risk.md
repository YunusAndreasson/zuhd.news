Pakistan holds 13,000 glaciers — 10,000 are retreating. February temperatures in Gilgit-Baltistan ran 3-5°C above normal, collapsing the nighttime refreezing that normally prevents glacial lakes from bursting through their dams. Pakistan's meteorological department warned 7.1 million people face likely glacial outburst floods if warming persists.
