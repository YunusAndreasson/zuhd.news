Baghdad — [Iraq](country:IQ) was placed on the Financial Action Task Force grey list.

The designation flags money-laundering and terror-financing gaps; grey-listed economies face higher borrowing costs as global banks pull back.

[Kuwait](country:KW) remains under the task force's scrutiny, with no exit timeline given for either country.
