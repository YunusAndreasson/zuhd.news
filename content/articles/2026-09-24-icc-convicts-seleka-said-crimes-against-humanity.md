The Hague — Mahamat Said Abdel Kani was found guilty on 4 counts of crimes against humanity.

He is the first Seleka member the court has convicted over [Central African Republic](country:CF) violence.

He and others beat and tortured prisoners in a plan targeting perceived supporters of ousted president François Bozizé, judges found.

Judges dismissed several war crimes charges, finding the armed conflict had already ended.

A separate hearing will decide his sentence, and he denied all charges.
