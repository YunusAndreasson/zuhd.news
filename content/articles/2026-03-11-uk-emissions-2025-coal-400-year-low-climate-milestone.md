UK coal demand fell to its lowest since 1600. Overall greenhouse gas emissions fell 2.4% to 364 MtCO2e — 54% below 1990 levels — as the last UK coal power plant closed in September 2024. Decarbonising heating — where gas still dominates — is now the primary obstacle to meeting UK climate law targets.
