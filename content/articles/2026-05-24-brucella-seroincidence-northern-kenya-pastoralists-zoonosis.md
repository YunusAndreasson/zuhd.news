Isiolo — Unpasteurised milk transmits Brucella from herd to herder.

A [Kenya](country:KE) study found 25 human seroconversions in 435 person-years; placenta removal and household livestock infection were key risk factors.

The study argues health spending targeting only humans or only livestock misses most transmission.
