Helsinki — Putin spoke with 2 Nordic leaders for 90 minutes.

It is the first European leaders' contact with him since July 2025.

[Finland](country:FI)'s Stubb and [Norway](country:NO)'s Støre called to influence US-brokered Ukraine talks, set for Abu Dhabi in November.

Danish Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen said "It hasn't been co-ordinated with any of the rest of us."

The three agreed to keep talking by phone, the Kremlin said.
