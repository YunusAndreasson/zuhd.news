New York — Tidal will pay no royalties on wholly AI-generated tracks.

Streaming royalties are pro-rated across all tracks; AI catalogs dilute human artists' share without any human performance or composition.

Tidal will label AI tracks but not remove them; the policy covers only 100% AI-generated uploads.
