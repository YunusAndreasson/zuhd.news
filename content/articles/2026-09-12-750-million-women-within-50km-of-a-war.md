Oslo — 756 million women lived near war in 2025.

That's nearly 1 in 5 women worldwide facing collapsing health care and lost schooling.

Peace Research Institute Oslo counted conflict events within 50 kilometers, a tally that has tripled since 1990.

Sub-Saharan Africa's exposure has grown fivefold since 2010, as new UN peacekeeping missions grow rarer.
