Washington — FCC exempted Netgear but won't say why. The FCC banned foreign-made consumer routers in March; virtually all brands manufacture abroad, so whoever controls exemptions controls market access. Chinese-origin manufacturers like TP-Link may face presumptive denial; Netgear's exemption expires in October 2027.
