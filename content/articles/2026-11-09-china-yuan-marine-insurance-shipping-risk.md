Beijing — 12 London clubs insure 87% of ocean-going tonnage.

[China](country:CN)'s new plan aims to settle marine insurance in yuan and price shipping risk itself.

Seven agencies urged yuan settlement of claims and shared data, and a Chinese shipowners' mutual applied last month to join the London group.

The International Group will assess that application against its admission criteria over coming months.
