Turbat — Traders, labourers and students rallied against the [Iran](country:IR) border closure.

Rally leader Fida Jan Buledi said cross-border trade has historically been a major source of local livelihoods.

Protesters demanded removal of check-posts between Nasirabad and Hoshab and withdrawal of taxes on Iranian food items.

Speakers demanded reopened commercial and fuel crossings and an end to closing markets, courts and hospitals.
