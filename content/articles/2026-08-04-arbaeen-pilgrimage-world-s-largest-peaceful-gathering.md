Karbala — Tens of millions walk 50 miles from Najaf, free of charge.

Unpaid roadside mawakib run by Iraqi households feed and house the pilgrims marking Imam Hussein's martyrdom, making Arbaeen the largest annual gathering with no ministry or ticketing behind it.

This year's walk crosses an [Iraq](country:IQ) still absorbing US and Saudi strikes on militia targets.
