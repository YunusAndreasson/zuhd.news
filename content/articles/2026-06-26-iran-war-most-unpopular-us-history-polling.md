Washington — 60% of Americans oppose the Iran war, the most opposed any [US](country:US) war has polled from the start.

Analysis of 153 polls across 7 US wars finds this conflict began with more opponents than backers — now at negative 32% net support, Economist/YouGov surveys show.

65% support a rapid peace deal; Congress has not scheduled a vote.
