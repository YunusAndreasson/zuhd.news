New York — Bitcoin at $62,500; production costs $78,000, JPMorgan said.

Publicly traded miners sold more than 32,000 bitcoin in Q1 — more than all of 2025 combined — to cover operating costs, the bank said.

Mining difficulty fell 10% in early June; JPMorgan expects larger resets as long as bitcoin stays underwater.
