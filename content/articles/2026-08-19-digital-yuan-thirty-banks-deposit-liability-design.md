Beijing — [China](country:CN) tripled digital yuan banks to 30.

Since January, e-CNY held in wallets counts as a bank deposit, paying interest.

An interest-free digital currency would drain bank deposits at app speed in a crisis, so Beijing keeps e-CNY on banks' books.

Beijing plans further expansion as cross-border use through mBridge remains limited.
