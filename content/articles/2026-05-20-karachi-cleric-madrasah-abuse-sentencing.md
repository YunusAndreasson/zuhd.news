Karachi — Madrasah cleric Qari Anwar sentenced to 15 years.

A sessions court convicted him on 2 counts — abusing students aged 13 and 17 — in a rare [Pakistan](country:PK) madrasah prosecution.

Judge Naseer Noor Khan ordered Rs1 million fines per count, half payable to each victim's family.
