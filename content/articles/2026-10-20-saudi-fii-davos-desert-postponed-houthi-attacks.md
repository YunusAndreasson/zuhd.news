Riyadh — [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) postponed its investor summit, due 26 October, to 2027.

The kingdom needs billions in foreign capital to move its economy beyond oil.

Houthi strikes across the kingdom, including Riyadh's airport, killed at least 18 people, and Riyadh agencies ordered remote work.

Polymarket traders give a Houthi-Saudi ceasefire by 31 October a 10% chance, down 4 points in a week.
