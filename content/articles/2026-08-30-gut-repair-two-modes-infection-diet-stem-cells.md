Ithaca — Infection kills gut cells outright; diet just reshapes the ones already there.

Cheaper repair without stem-cell division could mean lower long-term cancer risk.

Cornell researchers found the fruit fly intestine picks its rebuilding strategy by what damaged it.

The Drosophila finding is a hypothesis about mammal guts, not yet tested in one.
