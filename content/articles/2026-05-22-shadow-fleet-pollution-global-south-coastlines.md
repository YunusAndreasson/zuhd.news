Valletta — The Arctic Metagaz, a crewless Russian tanker, drifts off [Libya](country:LY).

Crew abandoned the ship after a suspected drone strike near Malta in March; it carries 60,000 tonnes of LNG.

Shadow fleet ships carry no insurance; when they spill, coastal states bear uncompensated cleanup costs that flag-of-convenience registries will not cover.
