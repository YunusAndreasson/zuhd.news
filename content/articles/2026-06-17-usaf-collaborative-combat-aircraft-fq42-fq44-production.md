Washington — 2 rival semi-autonomous combat drones enter production.

The [US](country:US) Air Force split the contract between General Atomics and Anduril to hedge development risk, targeting 150 units by decade's end.

The Air Force requested nearly $1 billion in its 2027 budget for initial procurement.
