China's chip leaders admitted their industry cannot match ASML. ASML's extreme ultraviolet lithography machines — the only technology for fabricating chips below 7nm — contain 100,000 components sourced from 5,000 suppliers. Executives called for national coordination through 2030 and a government fast-track for mergers to consolidate the fragmented industry.
