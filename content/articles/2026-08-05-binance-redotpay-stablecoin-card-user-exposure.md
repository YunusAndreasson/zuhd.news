Hong Kong — Binance sued RedotPay for $470 million Wednesday.

RedotPay's stablecoin card stacks an issuer, processor, network and bank — any dispute can freeze user spending without recourse.

RedotPay says it will defend itself "vigorously"; the dispute leaves cardholders' payment access unresolved.
