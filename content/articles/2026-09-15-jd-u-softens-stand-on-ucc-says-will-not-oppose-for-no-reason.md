Patna — A Bihar ally may back [India](country:IN)'s civil code.

The party's vote has long blocked the code, which would replace Muslims' personal law nationwide.

The proposed code would replace personal-law systems governing marriage, divorce and inheritance for Muslims and other minorities.

Janata Dal (United) leaders plan talks with Home Minister Amit Shah before deciding.
