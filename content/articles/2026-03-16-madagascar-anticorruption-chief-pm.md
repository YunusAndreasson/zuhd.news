Madagascar's anticorruption chief became prime minister Monday. Coup leader Colonel Randrianirina appointed Mamitiana Rajaonarison — head of the national anticorruption agency — to lead the government his own office is meant to oversee. Presidential elections are scheduled for late 2027, when voters will judge whether the appointment was reform or co-optation.
