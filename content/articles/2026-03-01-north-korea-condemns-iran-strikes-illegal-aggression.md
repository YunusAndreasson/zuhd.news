North Korea watched Iraq and Libya disarm and collapse. Pyongyang called US-Israeli strikes on Iran "illegal aggression," pointing to both countries as governments overthrown after giving up nuclear programs. US President Donald Trump has threatened further strikes unless Iran disarms, an ultimatum Pyongyang says confirms nuclear weapons are non-negotiable.
