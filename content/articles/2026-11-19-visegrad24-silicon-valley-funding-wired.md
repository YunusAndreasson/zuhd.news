Warsaw — Over $1 million reached Visegrad24, an investigation found.

Its founder wrote that he chose a pro-Israel line to fundraise.

Much came through donor-advised funds, which hide donors' identities, including $175,000 from DonorsTrust.

Founder Stefan Tompson answered the documents with a blanket denial and a lawyer's email.

Named donors declined to comment or said they held no records, so no one has confirmed the gifts.
