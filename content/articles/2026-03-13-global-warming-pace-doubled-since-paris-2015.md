Earth now warms at 0.4°C per decade. A study in Geophysical Research Letters isolated human-caused warming by controlling for natural climate variability and found the rate doubled from 0.2°C per decade before 2015 to 0.4°C after. At this pace, the Paris Agreement's 1.5°C limit will be breached between 2026 and 2029.
