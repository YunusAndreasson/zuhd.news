Jakarta — Indonesia hasn't grown 8% since Suharto. Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto's plan rests on 30,000 school kitchens, 80,000 village cooperatives, and 1 million new homes to stimulate domestic demand. The government's own Q1 projection is 5.5%, and Prabowo has not said how the programs close the remaining 2.5 percentage points.
