Bishkek — [Iran](country:IR) proposed a sanctions-proof bank for the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation.

Iran's central bank designed it with member currencies and a settlement system outside SWIFT, the interbank network.

Member states referred the proposal to the secretariat; no adoption timeline has been set.
