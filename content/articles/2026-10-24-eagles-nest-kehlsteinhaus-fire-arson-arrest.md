Berchtesgaden — Police detained a 33-year-old as the Eagle's Nest burned.

The Nazi-era guest house drew 350,000 visitors a year, Bavarian authorities say, and has held a Nazi-history exhibition since 1952.

About 200 firefighters fought the blaze from 9:50 pm Friday through rain, snow and wind, and nobody has entered the building.

Prosecutor Rainer Vietze says arson looks likely and the man is provisionally held in psychiatric care.

Bavaria's interior minister, Joachim Herrmann, says the site will not be left in ruins.
