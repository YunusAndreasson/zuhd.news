IBM lost 13.2% in its worst trading day since 2000. Anthropic demonstrated that its Claude Code tool can modernize COBOL systems in quarters rather than years, threatening a mainframe business built on the language being too risky to migrate. Banks and governments running COBOL face a choice that could redirect billions from IBM's contracts.
