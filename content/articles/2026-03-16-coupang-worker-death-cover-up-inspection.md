Coupang allegedly pressured a dead worker's family. South Korea's labor ministry launched an inspection into Coupang, the country's largest e-commerce retailer, alleging the company covered up a delivery worker's death in 2024. Coupang now faces 3 simultaneous probes over the cover-up, a worker blacklist, and a data breach affecting 33 million accounts.
