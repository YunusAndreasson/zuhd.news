Chelyabinsk — A 1957 tank blast dumped 20 million curies.

That waste still sits in soil now threatened by encroaching wildfire.

Fire does not split atoms, but it re-releases cesium and strontium already bound in burning peat and brush.

[Russia](country:RU)'s wartime-thinned fire service is what now stands between the ash and the wind.
