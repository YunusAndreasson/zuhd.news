Argentina's lower house voted 135-115 to pass President Javier Milei's labour reform early Friday. The bill loosens hiring, firing, and collective bargaining rules, and triggered a nationwide strike that shut down buses, flights, and hospitals on Thursday. The Senate must give final approval after Milei stripped a contested sick-pay clause to secure earlier support there.
