Durban — 62 people go on trial Monday.

Prosecutors allege WhatsApp groups coordinated looting and arson after Jacob Zuma's 2021 jailing, in violence that killed 354 people across [South Africa](country:ZA).

The trial testing whether prosecutors can prove orchestration runs through September 18, with about 125 witnesses called.
