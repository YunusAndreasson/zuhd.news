Abuja — Ebola has killed more than 4,600 people and reached [Nigeria](country:NG).

No proven vaccine exists for the Bundibugyo strain, and Nigeria is Africa's most populous country.

Africa's disease agency left Nigeria off its initial list of 15 at-risk countries, its director-general said.

Uganda rejects links to the case, and WHO says the source of the Nigerian patient's infection remains unknown.

Health, finance and transport ministers meet next week, with Africa's disease agency arguing against border closures.
