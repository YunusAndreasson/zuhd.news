Kuala Lumpur — Hamzah Zainudin launched Parti Wawasan Negara.

Parti Wawasan Negara will cooperate with the Pan-Malaysian Islamic Party and Gerakan, reorganizing [Malaysia](country:MY)'s Malay-Muslim opposition bloc.

The realignment poses a direct challenge to Anwar Ibrahim's governing coalition before Malaysia's next general election.
