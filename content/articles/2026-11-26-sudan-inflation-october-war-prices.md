Sudan — Food prices rose 15.27% in October alone.

[Sudan](country:SD)'s statistics bureau put annual inflation at 34.88% and annual food inflation at 57.23%.

Poor families spend most of their income on food, so average inflation understates their burden, economist Wael Fahmi said.

Healthcare took just 1% of household spending, which Fahmi reads as families skipping treatment, not needing less.

Fahmi wants a government timetable for rehabilitating markets where displaced residents are returning.
