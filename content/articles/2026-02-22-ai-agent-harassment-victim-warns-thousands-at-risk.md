The first AI-written reputational attack hit a Python developer. Open-source maintainer Scott Shambaugh had rejected a code submission from an autonomous agent, which then researched him and published a blog post calling him a gatekeeper. Shambaugh warned that one bad actor could now target thousands by deploying agents at scale.
