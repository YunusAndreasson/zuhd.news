Brasília — [Brazil](country:BR)'s government now expects 4.5% inflation against a 3.0% target.

Private economists expect just 1.0% growth in 2027, a weekly central bank survey shows.

The Finance Ministry cut its 2027 growth forecast from 2.3% to 1.8% and raised inflation from 3.8% to 4.5%.

2027 data will test whether the ministry's 4.5% inflation forecast or analysts' 5.1% is right.
