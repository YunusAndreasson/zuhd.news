Global — Phishers targeted an exiled activist's Telegram account.

For an exile from [Belarus](country:BY), the account is the address book of everyone still inside — taking it over reaches them too.

Researchers also found targets in [Russia](country:RU) and [Kazakhstan](country:KZ); the only real defence is a cloud password.
