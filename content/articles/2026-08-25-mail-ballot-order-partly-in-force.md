Washington — The Supreme Court lifted part of a block on Trump's mail-ballot order.

A separate injunction still stops the Postal Service piece, so the order is only partly active before midterms.

Election procedure is a state power under the Constitution, yet the 6-3 ruling let the order operate mid-litigation.

States can sue again as November's midterms near, the Court said, leaving the merits undecided.
