Buenos Aires — 58 arrested, 196 identified, one network dismantled.

The infrastructure enabling West African fraud rings was sold from Latin America, not run from Nigeria.

Domain registrars, hosting resellers and money mules spanned separate jurisdictions until Interpol coordinated simultaneous arrests.

It remains unclear how many of the 196 identified suspects are still at large.
