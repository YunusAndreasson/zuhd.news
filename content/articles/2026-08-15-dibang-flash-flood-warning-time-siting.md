Pasu Pani — Five soldiers vanished when floodwaters swept their shelters.

A same-day landslide nearby killed four more, showing the risk spans northeastern [India](country:IN).

A mountain stream swelled suddenly at 4:30 pm, sweeping the shelters before soldiers could react.

State, army and federal rescue teams are searching, and damage to the camp is not yet assessed.
