East Darfur — Fighters attacked a hospital in East Darfur, killing 64 people. WHO confirmed the facility is now unusable; armed groups have killed over 2,000 people in attacks on Sudan's medical facilities since the war began in April 2023. Sudan's war enters its 3rd year as the UN Security Council has imposed no ceasefire and peace talks remain unscheduled.
