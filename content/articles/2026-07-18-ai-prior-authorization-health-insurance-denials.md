Washington — One in five insured [US](country:US) adults faced denials.

The Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services is piloting AI-driven prior authorization in six states through 2031; vendors earn a cut of "averted expenditures," incentivizing denials.

Lawmakers have moved to block funding for the pilot, called WISeR, citing threats to patient access.
