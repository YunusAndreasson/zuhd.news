Bengaluru — Gold fell to $3,830.74 an ounce, down 31.5% since January.

It is on course for its worst year since 2015.

Gold pays no interest, so rate-hike expectations and a [US](country:US) dollar near its highest since April 2025 make holding it costlier.

Physically backed gold funds still took in 211 metric tons in the third quarter.

The Federal Reserve decides on 9 December, and Polymarket traders give a 25-basis-point hike 88%.
