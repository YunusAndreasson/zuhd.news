Shenzhen — Vanke asks bondholders for another 3 years.

Bondholders may get far less upfront cash than the 40% agreed in the first extension, sources familiar said.

The bond has about 1.2 billion yuan ($180 million) outstanding as debt deferred earlier this year falls due again.

Bondholders vote on 8 December.
