Cairo — A court ordered Mada Masr shut, fining it 3 million Egyptian pounds.

Police carried editor Lina Attalah out of court before she heard the verdict.

Attalah learned in 2022 that authorities had rejected its licence under [Egypt](country:EG)'s 2018 media law, with no notice or reasons.

"Just like that, 13 years of journalism practice had ended in a five-minute judicial performance," Attalah wrote.

Authorities have not answered its resubmitted licence application since 2023, Attalah wrote.
