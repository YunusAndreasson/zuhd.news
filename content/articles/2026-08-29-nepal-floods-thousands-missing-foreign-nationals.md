Kathmandu — Almost 2,500 remain unaccounted for, including 41 Australians.

Search teams cannot yet confirm whether pilgrims and trekkers survived the border floods.

The missing traveled routes through the same glacial valleys the flood drained, per a Red Cross tally of 93,000 affected.

Families are traveling to [Nepal](country:NP) to search as foreign ministries press for access to the gorges.
