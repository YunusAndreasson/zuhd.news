Europe's largest research programme excludes Chinese labs. Horizon Europe's new rules bar Chinese institutions from AI, quantum computing, and semiconductor projects — fields in which China had been a top participant. The restrictions follow US policy on Chinese technology access and signal further limits on China's research ties with Western institutions.
