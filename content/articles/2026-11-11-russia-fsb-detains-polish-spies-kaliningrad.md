Kaliningrad — [Russia](country:RU) says it detained 2 Polish intelligence agents.

Its Federal Security Service also cautioned 7 Poles and 3 Russians over espionage and treason.

It alleges they gathered data on military sites and migration policy for Polish military intelligence.

A court has jailed both agents while espionage cases proceed.
