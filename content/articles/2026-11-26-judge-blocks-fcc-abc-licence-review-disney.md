Washington — Judge blocks early licence reviews of 8 ABC stations.

The Federal Communications Commission had not ordered one in over 50 years.

Chair Brendan Carr ordered the reviews a day after [US](country:US) President Donald Trump urged ABC to fire Jimmy Kimmel.

"This is not a close case," US District Judge Loren AliKhan wrote, saying the commission appeared to run a retaliatory campaign.

The commission called the ruling wrong on the law and says it will keep using its authority.
