Fairfield — Gunfire killed 1 and wounded 6 at a Halloween store.

At least 3 of the wounded were in critical condition, police said.

It began about 8:30 p.m. Friday at a Spirit Halloween in Solano Town Center.

Chief Dan Marshall said it is not yet clear whether there was a single suspect.

Investigators are expected to review mall surveillance footage and witness video.
