Karachi — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s first Chinese-built Hangor submarine fired an anti-ship missile.

The test shifts the undersea balance with [India](country:IN) as tensions ripple across a contested Arabian Sea.

The class is named for a 1971-war submarine that sank an Indian frigate, the first subsurface kill since World War Two.

Seven more Hangor-class submarines are still due for delivery under the program.
