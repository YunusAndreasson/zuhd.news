Trump plans a March 31 summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping. Economic officials from both countries met in Paris last weekend to prepare; Trump has since opened trade investigations into 60 economies. Trump has said he may delay the visit if Beijing does not help reopen the Strait of Hormuz.
