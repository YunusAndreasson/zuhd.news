Paris — Villepin, who opposed the Iraq war, is running for president.

Polls put him at 1% to 3%, far behind Marine Le Pen's 32% to 36%.

He still needs 500 endorsements from elected officials to qualify, and his team says it holds about 300.

His condemnation of Israel's Gaza war has won echo among young voters and admiration on the left.

[France](country:FR) votes on 18 April 2027, with a runoff on 2 May.
