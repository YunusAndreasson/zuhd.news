US aid cuts halted free HIV drugs.

Some patients now pay faith healers for cures instead of drugs.

Stopping antiretrovirals mid-course, unlike never starting them, can breed drug-resistant HIV strains.

It remains unclear how many patients across [Nigeria](country:NG) have abandoned treatment.
