Rawalpindi — 3 killed, 189 hurt in 167 Eid crashes.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s Rawalpindi district recorded 167 road accidents over Eid al-Adha's 3 days; 84 victims were critically injured, Rescue 1122 said.

Traffic police attributed most crashes to speeding; 75,000 vehicles had entered the Murree resort area, Rescue 1122 records showed.
