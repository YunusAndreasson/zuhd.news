Deir al-Balah — Haitham Ftayir climbed 10 palms this season, not 100-plus.

Climbing, pruning and pollinating jobs that fed families all year disappeared with the palms.

[Israel](country:IL)'s army destroyed an estimated 4,500 dunams of date palms, many inside the so-called yellow line.

Harvester Abdullah Baraka fears there will be no harvest at all within 3 years.
