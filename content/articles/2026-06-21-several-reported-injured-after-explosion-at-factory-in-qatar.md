Ras Laffan — An explosion hit [Qatar](country:QA)'s Barzan gas facility Sunday.

Ras Laffan hosts Qatar's core liquefied natural gas operations, supplying Europe and Asia; QatarEnergy said the fire was contained with no leak.

The ministry gave no casualty count or cause; the start-up incident remains under investigation.
