Edmonton — Polls put separatist support near 1 in 5 Albertans.

A yes vote would not make Alberta independent but opens the path to a binding referendum.

Viewpoint Alberta data show two-thirds of referendum supporters want to send Ottawa a message, not leave [Canada](country:CA).

Even a No will not end it, said Cory Morgan, founder of Pathway to Independence: "That doesn't mean I'll stop."

Premier Danielle Smith, who opposes separation, wants a yes on nine other questions to strengthen her hand with Ottawa.
