Redmond — A weather widget can eat 1.2GB of memory.

That is 5 times heavier than Apple's equivalent, and hits oldest hardware hardest.

Windows 11's Weather app runs on WebView2, bundling its own Chromium browser engine instead of native code.

Whether Microsoft rebuilds Weather as a native app remains a pending decision.
