Beijing — Chinese robot beat surgeons at brain angiography by 29%. The procedure requires threading a guide-wire from the thigh to the brain under X-ray, exposing surgeons to cumulative radiation damage over their careers. Study authors called the findings preliminary, but the radiation risk creates pressure to deploy before larger randomized trials are complete.
