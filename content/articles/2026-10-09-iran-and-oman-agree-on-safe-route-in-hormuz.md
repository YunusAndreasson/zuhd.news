Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) says [Oman](country:OM) agreed coordinates for safe Hormuz lanes.

Hormuz traffic averaged 2.7 ships a day to 4 October, against a normal 4.5.

Foreign ministry spokesman Esmail Baghaei said the two states will announce the coordinates to international authorities.

Trump says the [US](country:US) will not attack Iran before 3 November, but the blockade stays "in full force and effect."

Polymarket traders give Hormuz traffic a 19% chance of returning to normal by 31 December.
