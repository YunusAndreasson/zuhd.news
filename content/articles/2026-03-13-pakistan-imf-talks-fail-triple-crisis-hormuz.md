The Hormuz blockade is pushing Pakistan toward default. The International Monetary Fund ended talks without a deal — it demanded fuel price increases that Pakistan cannot impose after the blockade drove energy costs up 22%. Talks are set to continue, with a successful review unlocking $1.2 billion by end of April — Pakistan's last buffer against default.
