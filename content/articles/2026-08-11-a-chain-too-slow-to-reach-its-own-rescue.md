San Francisco — A breakaway chain cannot yet fix itself.

Block times stretch from minutes to hours, stalling routine transactions indefinitely.

Bitcoin resets mining difficulty only every 2,016 blocks, assuming hashrate departs gradually, not almost entirely at once.

Any faster fix requires an emergency difficulty change, itself another divisive hard fork.
