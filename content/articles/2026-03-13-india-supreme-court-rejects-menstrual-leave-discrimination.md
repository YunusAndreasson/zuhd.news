India's top court refused menstrual leave, citing hiring bias. Judges acknowledged menstrual pain as real but said legally mandating leave would prompt private employers to stop hiring women altogether. The court asked the government to consult stakeholders and design a voluntary policy, with no timeline or binding requirement.
