Russia — [Ukraine](country:UA) struck 4 Russian data centers in a week.

Two of Yandex's six data centers went offline, cutting email, taxi and bus apps for Russians.

Three strikes hit Yandex's supercomputers, and Wednesday's fourth hit a data center run by Sberbank, [Russia](country:RU)'s largest bank.

Sberbank's outage effects are unknown, and researcher ChrisO_wiki says Yandex's outage blocks firms logging incoming goods.
