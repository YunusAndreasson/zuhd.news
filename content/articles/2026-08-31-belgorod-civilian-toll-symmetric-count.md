Belgorod absorbs strikes neither [Russia](country:RU) nor [Ukraine](country:UA) calls deliberate.

Officials say the missile struck warehouses in a commercial district, a claim that has not been independently verified.

Belgorod official Shuvaev says the region absorbed 138 strikes in 24 hours, with nightly fire continuing.
