Brussels — A 10% margin cap made Hungary a defendant.

The Commission is suing at the EU's top court, arguing [Hungary](country:HU)'s margin cap on 30 food staples falls mainly on foreign retailers.

The case will set how far any EU state can shield consumers by squeezing foreign firms.
