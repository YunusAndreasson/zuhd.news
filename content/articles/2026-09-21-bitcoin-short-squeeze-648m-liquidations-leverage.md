London — $647.9 million of short bets were liquidated in 24 hours.

Bitcoin traded at $84,984 Monday, up 5.4% in a day and above its 4 September high.

Forced closures of shorts create forced buying, and Coinglass figures put bitcoin shorts at $277.5 million of the total.

Open interest still rose 7.59% to $156 billion, so traders are replacing positions rather than retreating.

Volume rose 39% to $224 billion.
