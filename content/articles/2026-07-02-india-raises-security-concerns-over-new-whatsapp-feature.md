New Delhi — A privacy feature just got blocked as a fraud risk.

WhatsApp's new usernames let 850 million users in [India](country:IN) hide phone numbers, the trait officials say scammers exploit to impersonate police and banks.

WhatsApp has 3 days to respond; the rollout stays frozen until consultations end.
