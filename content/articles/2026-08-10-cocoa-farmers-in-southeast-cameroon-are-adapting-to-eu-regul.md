Sangmélima — [Cameroon](country:CM) grew a record 309,518 tons of cocoa.

Cocoa prices have crashed since early 2025, even as Brussels demands costly new certification.

The EU's deforestation law binds importers by law, but importers push the paperwork cost onto smallholders instead.

The regulation takes effect next year, and farmers still don't know if the price will rise.
