London — Glioblastoma kills most patients within 15 months. Researchers found that 2 proteins, BIRC3 and CAV1, mark chemotherapy-resistant tumors and identify a subset where the drug AZD5582 can restore treatment response. Patients where both proteins are elevated have less than 7% 24-month survival; AZD5582 clinical trials are the next step.
