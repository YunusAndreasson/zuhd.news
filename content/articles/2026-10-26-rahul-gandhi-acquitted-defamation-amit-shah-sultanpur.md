Sultanpur — Rahul Gandhi spent 8 years in court over one remark.

A local Bharatiya Janata Party politician sued [India](country:IN)'s opposition leader, and the court found he lacked standing, Gandhi's lawyer said.

Gandhi called Amit Shah a murder accused in 2018, though a Mumbai court had discharged Shah in that case in 2014.

Gandhi's lawyers have yet to read the full judgment, which will set out the court's grounds.
