Kazan — Leaders of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations met Putin in Kazan Tuesday.

It was [Russia](country:RU)'s first such summit since its Ukraine invasion; leaders adopted a 2026-2030 plan on energy, trade, investment, and agriculture.

No member state has joined Western sanctions against Russia; the summit formalizes energy and trade ties for 5 years.
