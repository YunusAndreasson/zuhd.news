Caracas — [Venezuela](country:VE)'s criminal economy didn't fall with Maduro.

Drug routes, mines and prison gangs still run without him, InSight Crime says.

The networks were never centered on one man — they're embedded in security forces, prisons and armed groups, the group says.

InSight Crime says dismantling those networks, not removing leaders, is what would weaken them.
