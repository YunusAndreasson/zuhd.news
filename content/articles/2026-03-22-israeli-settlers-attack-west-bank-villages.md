West Bank — Settlers burned homes across 4 Palestinian villages on Saturday. Settler violence has surged since the US and Israel attacked Iran, with 6 Palestinians killed by settlers since March 1, according to the United Nations. Israeli police are investigating the killing of a Palestinian during the attacks; no settlers have been arrested.
