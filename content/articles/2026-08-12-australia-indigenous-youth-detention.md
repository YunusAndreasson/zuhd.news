Canberra — Indigenous children wait 57 days in detention unsentenced.

No court found them guilty, yet Indigenous youth fill [Australia](country:AU)'s cells.

Government figures show bail laws presuming detention, and a missed curfew becomes a new chargeable offence.

The 2031 target to cut detention by 30% shows no progress yet, NATSILS says.
