Panama Canal hit maximum capacity at 38 daily vessels. LNG tankers rerouting from the blocked Strait of Hormuz drove the surge; canal chief Ricaurte Vasquez said dedicated LNG slots jumped from 4 a month to 1 a day. The canal has no capacity buffer left — every additional vessel rerouted from Hormuz will face a queue.
