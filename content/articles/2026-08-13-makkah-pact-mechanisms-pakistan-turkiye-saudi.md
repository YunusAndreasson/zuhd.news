Ankara — Defence chiefs, foreign ministers and military heads now form a standing bloc.

[Turkey](country:TR), [Pakistan](country:PK) and [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) are converting a defence pact into a permanent command structure.

The Makkah Joint Defence Agreement, signed last week, adds joint exercises and shared weapons production.

Erdogan wants Egypt and other states to join; Cairo has not yet responded.
