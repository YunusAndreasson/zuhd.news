$1.7 trillion in private credit hit record defaults in 2025. Fitch, a credit rating agency, put the default rate at 9.2% — highest ever for a market that finances mid-market companies banks rejected, making the failures structural rather than cyclical. Unlike bank deposits, private credit carries no federal insurance backstop, leaving investors exposed if defaults spread to larger lenders.
