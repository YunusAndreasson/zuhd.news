Brussels — Member states agreed personal data may train AI.

Unions and consumer groups call the package the biggest setback for digital fundamental rights.

The Council rejected browser-level cookie choices, but exempts service, audience-measurement and device-security cookies from consent.

[Germany](country:DE), Austria and Estonia voted against the position, and Germany wanted an impact assessment first.

Trilogue talks with Parliament begin once it adopts its own position, expected in February.
