Abuja — Nigeria lost 3% of economic output to multiple exchange rates.

The gap let politically connected buyers get cheap dollars while ordinary citizens absorbed the inflation.

Central Bank of [Nigeria](country:NG) governor Olayemi Cardoso said multiple windows enabled arbitrage until the bank unified rates into one willing-buyer, willing-seller market in 2023.

The bank says it will hold further rate decisions until inflation comes down.
