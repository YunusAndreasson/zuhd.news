Sanaa — Houthis say Saudi-led strikes killed 27 villagers.

Most of the dead in al-Shariya were women and children, the Houthi health ministry said.

[Yemen](country:YE)'s government forces say they carried out 875 attacks on Houthi positions Saturday under Operation Dawn of Yemen.

[Syria](country:SY) denies sending troops to Yemen, but says its [Saudi](country:SA) pact could become a mutual defence agreement.

Polymarket traders give a Saudi-Houthi ceasefire by 31 December a 23% chance.
