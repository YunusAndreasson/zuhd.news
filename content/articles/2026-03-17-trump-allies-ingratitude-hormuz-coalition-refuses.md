3 NATO allies refused Trump's request for Hormuz warships. Trump launched strikes on Iran without consulting allies, then demanded military support — a sequence analysts say makes coalition-building impossible. Without allied escorts, Hormuz stays shut and oil remains above $106 per barrel as Trump calls partners ungrateful.
