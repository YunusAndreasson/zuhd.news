Ithaca — Surface chemistry can extinguish gallium nitride transistors' electron layer.

Gallium nitride powers fast chargers via a 2D electron layer; momentum-space imaging revealed that surface molecules quench or restore it.

The observation maps which surface treatments preserve the electron layer in next-generation power-chip design.
