Primorsk — 40,000 tonnes of Russian diesel are bound for New York.

It is the first Russian diesel bound for the [US](country:US) since the 2022 import ban.

The 298,000-barrel cargo covers about 7.8% of one day's US demand and, LSEG data show, arrives 7 November, four days after the midterms.

The Kremlin confirmed the tanker sailed but did not say whether the cargo is bound for the US market.

[Russia](country:RU)'s Deputy Prime Minister Alexander Novak says shipments rise to 500,000 tonnes in November and 1 million in December.
