Pacifica — Waves destroyed part of a 350-metre pier Wednesday.

6 California tide gauges tied or set all-time flood records the same day.

An El Niño Kelvin wave, which can raise seas 15 to 30 centimetres, stacked on the king tide and 9-metre waves.

The pier had been closed since summer after cracks appeared.

The US ocean and weather agency warns above-normal tides may return 22 to 26 December across the [US](country:US) West Coast.
