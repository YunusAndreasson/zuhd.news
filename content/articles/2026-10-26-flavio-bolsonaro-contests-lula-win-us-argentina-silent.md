São Paulo — Flávio Bolsonaro refuses to concede a 4.4 million-vote loss.

The [US](country:US) and [Argentina](country:AR) withheld congratulations that five right-leaning South American governments sent.

Bolsonaro says a Supreme Court order extending voting by two hours invalidated the 51.79% result.

Lula's Workers' Party says right-run cities skipped court-ordered free buses that carry poorer voters.

Lula is inaugurated on 5 January 2027 before an opposition-controlled Congress.
