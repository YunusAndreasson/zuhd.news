Tehran — Iran's Moscow envoy floated a Hormuz passage toll.

[Iran](country:IR) has never ratified the UN convention barring transit-passage fees; [Oman](country:OM) has ratified it but hasn't confirmed the plan.

Any levy would raise war-risk insurance costs already dominating Gulf shipping, before either foreign ministry weighs in.
