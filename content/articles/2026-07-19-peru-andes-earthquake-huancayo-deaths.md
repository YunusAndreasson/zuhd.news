Huancayo — Five died as adobe homes collapsed.

The magnitude 5.5 quake near Sicaya displaced 300 residents; officials say traditional adobe construction worsened the damage across [Peru](country:PE).

Search teams are still counting the missing — the 2007 Pisco quake, in comparison, killed nearly 600.
