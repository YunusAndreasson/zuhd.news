Jakarta — Gojek's co-founder jailed 10 years for graft.

[Indonesia](country:ID)'s anti-corruption court found Makarim steered a COVID school-laptop contract toward Google, which had backed Gojek before he became education minister.

He will appeal; a former Google executive who testified warned the verdict will chill tech investment in Indonesia.
