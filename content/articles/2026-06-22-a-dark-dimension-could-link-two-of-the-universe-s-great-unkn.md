New York — Theorists propose a 'dark dimension' linking dark energy and dark matter.

Dark energy comprises roughly 70% of the cosmos and dark matter 25%; the model proposes both arise from a single hidden dimension.

Whether the dimension exists hinges on galaxy surveys not yet complete; dark energy's stability is the key observable.
