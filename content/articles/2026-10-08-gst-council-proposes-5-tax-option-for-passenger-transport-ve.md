New Delhi — Goods and Services Tax officials lose the power to arrest.

The prosecution threshold rises fivefold, from Rs 1 crore to Rs 5 crore, [India](country:IN)'s finance minister said.

Refunds become largely automated, the general penalty falls from Rs 25,000 to Rs 10,000, and no notices go out below Rs 10,000.

The changes start 1 April 2027, and the 5% rate for electric-vehicle transport is still a proposal.
