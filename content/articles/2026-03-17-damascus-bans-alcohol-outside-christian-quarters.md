Damascus banned alcohol in all but 3 Christian quarters. Syrian President Ahmad al-Sharaa's government, which emerged from an al-Qaeda affiliate, passed the ban to consolidate conservative Sunni support as economic recovery stalls. Businesses have 3 months to comply; analyst Jihad Yazigi warns the decree reverses a century of urban integration.
