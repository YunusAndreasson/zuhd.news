London — MLHnet scores its own brain-scan heatmap explanations.

The system assigns each brain scan two quantitative scores — shape and faithfulness — so radiologists audit reasoning rather than trust a picture.

Expert radiologists confirmed 87.64% of explanations correct across 7,023 images; the 12% gap remains unaddressed, the study said.
