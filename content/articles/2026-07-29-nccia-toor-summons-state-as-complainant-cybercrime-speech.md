Islamabad — The state itself is Asad Ali Toor's accuser.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s cybercrime agency summoned the journalist under a law against "false and fake information," with the state itself named as complainant.

Toor must appear Friday or the inquiry proceeds without him; press groups have demanded the notice be withdrawn.
