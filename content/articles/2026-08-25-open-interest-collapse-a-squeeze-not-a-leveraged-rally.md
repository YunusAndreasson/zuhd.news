New York — Bitcoin surged to $80,020, up 23.7% this week.

The rally is driven by short covering, not new buying.

Futures open interest fell instead of rising, the signature of traders closing short bets rather than opening new ones.

Whether the rally holds now depends on positioning, not price alone.
