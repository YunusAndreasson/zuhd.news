Ulaanbaatar — Seoul will drop tariffs on Mongolian copper and rare earths.

Landlocked and reliant on [China](country:CN) and Russia for market access, Mongolia trades mineral access for capital under its second-ever free trade pact, following Japan's 2016 deal.

Technical talks continue before the Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement is finalized.
