Rawalakot — At least 32 protesters were killed here in two days.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s defence minister Khawaja Asif called Azad Kashmir demonstrators "enemies like India," NDTV reported; the region elects no seats in parliament.

No Pakistani outlet had confirmed or disputed the quote as of Wednesday.
