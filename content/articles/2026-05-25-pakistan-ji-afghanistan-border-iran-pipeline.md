Quetta — Pakistan's Afghan crossings strangle Balochistan's traders.

Jamaat-i-Islami chief Hafiz Naeemur Rehman proposed joint tribal committees to reopen Chaman and legalise petroleum trade with [Iran](country:IR).

Rehman warned of a nationwide post-Eidul Azha strike if the crossings stay shut and fuel hikes continue.
