Seoul — CrowdStrike says a likely China-based 26-year-old hit [South Korea](country:KR)'s banks.

At least 9 banks have been targeted since late September, and police opened a probe this week.

ARTEX automates penetration testing through outside models such as ChatGPT, Claude and DeepSeek, and the suspect also used Claude Code.

The developer, using the handle Autumn-27, says ARTEX was built for security testing and disowns any illegal use.

ARTEX's GitHub page is down, and the developer says no further versions will reach the public.
