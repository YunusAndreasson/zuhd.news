Beijing — A startup with no data centres raised 2.3 billion yuan.

Chinese tech outlet Zhidx calls it China's largest AI infrastructure round, bringing the total raised to 4.3 billion yuan.

Infinigence sells software scheduling mixed chips and says a 2024 run on 4 Chinese and 2 foreign processors hit 97.6% utilisation.

Foreign media report the company is preparing an initial public offering.
