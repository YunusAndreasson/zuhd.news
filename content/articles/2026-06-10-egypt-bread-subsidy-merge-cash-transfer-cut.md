Cairo — [Egypt](country:EG) merges century-old bread subsidies into cash transfers.

The government will cut 10 million of 70 million recipients; IMF reforms drove the shift, exposing beneficiaries directly to price swings.

No date is set; a party official warned recipients will wake up one morning to find they're removed.
