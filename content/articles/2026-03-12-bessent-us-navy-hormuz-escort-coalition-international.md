A US finance minister set military conditions for Hormuz escorts. Scott Bessent told Sky News the mission requires "complete control of the skies" over the strait and Iran's missiles "completely degraded," but set no timeline. Iran's Revolutionary Guard has threatened to block all Gulf oil exports until US-Israeli strikes cease.
