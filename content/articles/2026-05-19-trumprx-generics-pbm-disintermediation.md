Washington — TrumpRx added generic medicines to its catalogue.

The [US](country:US) government portal had covered only branded drugs under negotiated discounts; generics are the first category added to the direct-purchase channel.

The expansion bypasses pharmacy benefit managers, intermediaries that dominate US drug pricing.
