East Jerusalem — [Israel](country:IL) ordered 20 British diplomats out of the country.

Palestinians say the consulate reported abuses to London that the Tel Aviv embassy cannot.

Israel is retaliating for [Britain](country:GB)'s settlement-trade ban and allows only 7 diplomats to stay in the building.

"We will work in the coming months to build back up our presence," said Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband.

Former consul general Vincent Fean expects London to wait for Israel's 27 October election result.
