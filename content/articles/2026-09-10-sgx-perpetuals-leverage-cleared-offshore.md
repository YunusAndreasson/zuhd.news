It moves leveraged crypto trading offshore, under Singapore's oversight instead of Washington's.

A perpetual future never expires, tracking spot price through a funding rate that longs and shorts pay each other.

The Singapore Exchange has processed $5.8 billion in volume since its November 2025 launch.
