Kuala Lumpur — New medicines are outpacing halal certification rules.

A drug halal-certified in one Muslim country can be barred in another.

Istihalah, the ruling that transformed substances lose their origin, decides if a pork-derived ingredient is permissible.

Malaysian, Gulf and international halal certifiers have still not harmonized rulings on new biologics.
