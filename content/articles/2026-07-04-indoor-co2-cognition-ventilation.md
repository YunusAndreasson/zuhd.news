Berkeley — Meeting-room CO2 tops 2,000 ppm within an hour.

Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory found decision-making scores fall on six of nine measures once indoor CO2 passes 1,000 parts per million.

Few offices monitor indoor air quality, and no [US](country:US) ventilation standard requires it.
