A second Iranian strike hit Ras Laffan Wednesday. The hub produces a fifth of global LNG — strikes wiped out 17% of its capacity, causing $26 billion in damage. QatarEnergy may declare force majeure for up to 5 years on contracts with buyers in Europe and Asia.
