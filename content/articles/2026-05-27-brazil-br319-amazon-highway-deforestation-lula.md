Brasília — [Brazil](country:BR) will pave a $75M Amazon highway.

The BR-319, unpaved since its 1976 inauguration, is the strongest infrastructure predictor of Amazon deforestation, researchers say.

Critics say the environmental protection plan lacks safeguards to offset the deforestation the pavement will enable.
