San Mateo — 551 titles vanish from PlayStation libraries September 1.

A "buy" button on a digital storefront conveys a revocable license, not ownership; Sony says its licensing deal with distributor StudioCanal lapsed, and offers no refunds.

It is the third such mass deletion since 2022, after prior lapses with StudioCanal and Discovery.
