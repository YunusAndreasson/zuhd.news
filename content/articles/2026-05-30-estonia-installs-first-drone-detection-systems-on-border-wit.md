Tallinn — [Estonia](country:EE)'s first fixed drone-detection arrays are live.

3 southeastern sections of the Russian border now carry permanent sensor infrastructure, replacing ad-hoc response after a NATO jet downed a suspected drone over Estonian airspace on May 19.

Full eastern border coverage is targeted by year-end, interior minister Igor Taro said.
