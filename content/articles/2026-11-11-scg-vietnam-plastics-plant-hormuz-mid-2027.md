Ho Chi Minh City — Siam Cement's core Vietnam plastics plant stays shut until mid-2027.

Closing the Strait of Hormuz cut the plant's supply of naphtha, the oil-derived plastics feedstock.

CEO Thammasak Sethaudom said the group is halving Middle East supply and making plastic from [US](country:US) ethane.

The group says the restart follows its switch to non-Middle East feedstock.
