Ankara — The same chatbot scored differently months apart.

A hospital validating a chatbot today validates a version that may vanish.

Researchers tested 3 chatbots on 179 dental exam questions from [Turkey](country:TR) in April and July, and accuracy shifted for two.

The study gives no changelog for what providers quietly retrain behind an unchanged name.
