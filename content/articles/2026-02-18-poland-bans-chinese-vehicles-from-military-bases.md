Onboard sensors on Chinese cars triggered a military ban. Poland barred all Chinese-made vehicles from entering military bases, citing risks that cameras, GPS, and microphones could feed data to Beijing. China warned Poland to stop "abusing national security" even as Chinese car sales there quadrupled last year.
