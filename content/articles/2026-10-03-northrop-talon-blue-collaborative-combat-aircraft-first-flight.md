Mojave — Northrop's Talon Blue completed its first fully autonomous flight.

It joins Anduril and General Atomics in competing for the [US](country:US) Air Force's next hundreds of jets.

Northrop Grumman said the company-funded jet taxied, took off, manoeuvred and landed itself, with a lower part count to cut cost.

Increment 2, the Air Force's next purchase of hundreds of aircraft, still has undefined requirements.
