London — A textbook chip model just got an exact solution.

Engineers using fitted tight-binding tables can now derive those numbers instead of guessing them.

Physicists solved the Kronig-Penney band model with harmonic-oscillator wells, replacing its old unphysical square wells with a closed form.

The closed form can now be differentiated to derive new device parameters directly.
