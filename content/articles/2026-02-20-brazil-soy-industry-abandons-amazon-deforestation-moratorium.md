Cargill, ADM, and Bunge are abandoning the Amazon Soy Moratorium. The 2006 pact barred traders from buying soy grown on newly cleared Amazon land and drove a 70% drop in deforestation across monitored regions. Without it, analysts estimate Amazon deforestation could rise 30% by 2045.
