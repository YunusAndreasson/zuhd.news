Nepal holds its first post-uprising election March 5. Security forces killed 76 protesters in September's uprising, putting 4 million first-time voters aged 18-24 at the center of this election. Former Prime Minister KP Sharma Oli is running again, testing whether protest movements can translate street power into parliamentary seats.
