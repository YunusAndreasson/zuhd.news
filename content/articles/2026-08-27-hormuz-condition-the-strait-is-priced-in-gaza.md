[Iran](country:IR)'s Rezaei opened a temporary Hormuz corridor.

He named ending the Gaza and Lebanon wars as the top condition for shipping.

A strait closes through insurance, not warships — war-risk premiums and reinsurance pullouts are what actually stop tankers.

Tehran is drafting its formal list of conditions for [US](country:US) mediators.
