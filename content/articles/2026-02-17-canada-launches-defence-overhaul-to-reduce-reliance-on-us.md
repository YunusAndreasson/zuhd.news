Canada announced over $500 billion in defence and security investment. Prime Minister Mark Carney's plan aims to award 70% of military contracts to Canadian firms within a decade, up from 43% today. 75 cents of every federal defence dollar currently goes to US suppliers — a dependency Carney called untenable under Trump.
