Beijing — A three-year-old humanoid robot maker has reportedly filed to list.

Noetix's consumer humanoid Bumi starts at 9,998 yuan.

The filing could raise several hundred million dollars and follows about RMB 1 billion raised in March, led by a fund tied to battery maker CATL.

The listing could come as early as 2027, though amount and timetable are not finalised.
