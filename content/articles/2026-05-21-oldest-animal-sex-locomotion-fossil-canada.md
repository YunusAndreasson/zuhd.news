Northwest Territories — Fossils in [Canada](country:CA)'s Mackenzie Mountains push animal sex origins back 7 million years.

Funisia colonies and Dickinsonia trackways shift both the earliest sexual reproduction and locomotion records, researchers reported in Science Advances.

The site is the first Ediacaran assemblage found outside Russia, Asia, and Australia.
