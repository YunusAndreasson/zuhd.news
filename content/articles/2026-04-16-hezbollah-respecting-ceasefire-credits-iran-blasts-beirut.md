Beirut — Iran brokered the Lebanon ceasefire, not Washington. MP Ibrahim al-Moussawi said the 10-day pause rests on the Islamabad Iran-US deal — not the separate process US officials claimed. He demanded full Israeli troop withdrawal — including border areas — as the condition for the ceasefire to last.
