Nairobi — Kenya spends $1 billion yearly servicing China's railway loans. China built the Standard Gauge Railway between 2013 and 2019 with Uganda as the intended endpoint, but has refused further loans until Kenya clears its existing debt. Kenyan President William Ruto and Ugandan President Yoweri Museveni opened the latest segment Saturday; without debt relief, the Uganda extension will remain unbuilt.
