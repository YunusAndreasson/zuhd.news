Manila — Super typhoon Champi, at 185 km/h, enters Philippine waters Tuesday.

The Philippine weather bureau may raise Signal No. 3 or 4 over northern Luzon and Bicol from Monday evening.

It expects a 205 km/h peak, with a likelier track toward [Taiwan](country:TW) and heavy rain over Batanes.

Gusts will reach most of Luzon and the Visayas from Tuesday, with very rough seas from Wednesday.
