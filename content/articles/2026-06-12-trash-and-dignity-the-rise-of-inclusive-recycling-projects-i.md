Buenos Aires — 2 million Latin Americans earn from informal recycling.

[Argentina](country:AR)'s cartoneros, born from the 2001 crisis, inspire 50 similar projects and offer workers cooperative ownership.

[Colombia](country:CO)'s Amazoniko is scaling the model; whether governments formalize the sector determines those livelihoods.
