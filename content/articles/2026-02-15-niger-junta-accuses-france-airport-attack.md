Niger's military ruler accused France of orchestrating a January airport attack. General Abdourahamane Tiani, who seized power in a July 2023 coup, called the 29 January assault on Niamey airport part of a French "destabilization" campaign. A French military spokesperson dismissed the accusation as "clearly information warfare." Islamic State in the Sahel claimed responsibility for the attack; Russia reportedly helped Niger repel it. The confrontation reflects the collapse of French influence across West Africa: Niger, Mali, and Burkina Faso have all expelled French forces since 2022 and turned to Moscow for security assistance. Tiani offered to return roughly 100 tonnes of uranium extracted by French firm Orano before the coup, a pointed reminder that Niger controls a resource France depends on for its nuclear power fleet.
