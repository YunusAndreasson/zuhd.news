220 drones hit Russia in a single night. Ukraine launched its heaviest strikes of the war against Russian energy infrastructure just as negotiators departed for a 3rd round of trilateral talks with Russia and the United States in Geneva. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy urged the talks to be "serious and substantive," but Russian forces claimed 12 settlements captured in eastern Ukraine this month and the two sides remain far apart on occupied territory.
