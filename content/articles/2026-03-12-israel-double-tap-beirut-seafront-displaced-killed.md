Israel killed 8 displaced civilians sheltering at Beirut's seafront. The attack used a double-tap method: 2 strikes in sequence to kill rescuers who rush to the first impact. The strike wounded 31 more, with rescue teams still clearing a 4-storey building struck in a separate nearby attack.
