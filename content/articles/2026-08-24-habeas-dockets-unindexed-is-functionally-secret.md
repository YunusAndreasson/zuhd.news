Chicago — A 2007 rule hides 50,000 immigration filings.

It leaves attorneys, journalists and researchers unable to track detention patterns nationwide.

Habeas Dockets, built by 400 volunteers, scrapes and republishes petitions once readable only at a courthouse kiosk.

The rule remains in place, so volunteers keep filling the gap one filing at a time.
