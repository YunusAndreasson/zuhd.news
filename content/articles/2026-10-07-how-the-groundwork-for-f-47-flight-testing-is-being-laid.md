Edwards Air Force Base — The F-47 fighter is not expected to fly until 2028.

The [US](country:US) Air Force wants first production F-47s in service in the early 2030s.

The Air Force Test Center will run developmental and operational tests together, as it did on the F-15EX.

Brig. Gen. Mark Massaro, who heads the test center, deferred timelines to the F-47 program office.
