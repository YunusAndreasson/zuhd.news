New Delhi — The seat-expansion Bill needs about 360 Lok Sabha votes.

It would raise [India](country:IN)'s Lok Sabha ceiling from 543 to 850 seats.

The amendment adds 50% to every state's seats, reserves one-third for women and freezes boundaries until 2051.

The INDIA opposition bloc will oppose it, but two opposition parties with 30 MPs have indicated support.

The government introduces it Monday, after an April version fell 54 votes short.
