Stockport — A family day out costs £120 in northern England. Businesses cite National Insurance rises and energy costs as the driver, passing increases directly to consumers through food and activity prices. A BBC Panorama investigation found households earning the national average now expect to cut leisure further as costs show no sign of falling.
