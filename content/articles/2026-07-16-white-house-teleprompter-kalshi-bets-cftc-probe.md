Washington — Trump's teleprompter operator made $100,000 betting on him.

Gabriel Perez had advance sight of the president's scripted remarks and wagered on Kalshi's mention markets, which pay out on words spoken.

The Commodity Futures Trading Commission is in settlement talks with Perez, who has been placed on unpaid leave.
