The White House credits [US](country:US) first lady Melania Trump with brokering it.

[Ukraine](country:UA)'s and [Russia](country:RU)'s human rights commissioners met at the White House on Thursday, alongside Putin envoy Kirill Dmitriev.

The truce lasted six days, and Melania Trump's letters to both presidents remain undisclosed.

Officials from the US, Ukraine and Russia meet in Abu Dhabi on 10 and 11 November.
