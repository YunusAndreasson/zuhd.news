London — Dry mouth can end a cancer patient's ability to eat.

Radiotherapy, Sjögren's syndrome and diabetes all damage the glands that produce saliva.

Researchers now trace the failure to sustained cell stress rather than dead tissue, a target current drugs don't treat.

If the stress pathway holds up, it points toward reversing gland failure instead of just masking it.
