President Tinubu gave Nigeria's appointees a March 31 resignation deadline. Tinubu is rebuilding patronage networks after the ruling All Progressives Congress lost governorship races in 2025, 18 months before the next election. Officials who miss the deadline face disqualification under the 2026 Electoral Act.
