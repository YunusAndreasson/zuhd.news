Boston — DT120 more than doubled placebo's depression score improvement.

Patients on DT120 showed 13.3-point clinical depression improvement versus 5.2 on placebo, Definium said — effects held at 12 weeks.

Definium must navigate the blinding problem: participants can typically detect an active dose, complicating the comparison.
