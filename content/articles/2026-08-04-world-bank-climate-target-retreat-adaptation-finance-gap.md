New Delhi — Village water systems and cyclone shelters can't find lenders.

Development banks lend against revenue a project generates; a solar farm produces one, an embankment or shelter produces only avoided deaths.

The World Bank's retreat from climate-lending targets removes the one tool forcing that allocation.
