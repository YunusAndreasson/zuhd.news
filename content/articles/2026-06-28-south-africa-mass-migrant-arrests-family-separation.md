Johannesburg — Children are being detained during mass migrant arrests.

Civil society groups said [South Africa](country:ZA)'s provincial police launched coordinated mass arrests targeting African migrants to reassure anti-immigrant constituencies.

More than 20 organisations demanded Justice, Home Affairs, and Police ministries suspend operations immediately.
