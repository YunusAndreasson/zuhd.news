Abuja — [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Special Seats Bill would reserve 37 constituencies for women-only contests.

At 4.5% female representation in the National Assembly, no party has delivered parity voluntarily.

Passage requires a constitutional amendment and a two-thirds majority; previous reforms cleared advocacy and died in the chamber.
