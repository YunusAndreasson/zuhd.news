London — Burnham is the first [UK](country:GB) prime minister to say [Russia](country:RU) interfered.

He makes the claim as he argues for closer ties with the EU.

A 2020 parliamentary intelligence report found the government never fully assessed it, and Burnham offered no evidence.

Reform leader Nigel Farage called him a liar, telling a press conference "there was no Russian interference."

Burnham addresses MPs on Parliament's return, and has called a referendum pledge in a future Labour manifesto "possible".
