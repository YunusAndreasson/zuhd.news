New Delhi — India mandates 273 of 816 Parliament seats for women. Parliament votes April 16–18 on amendments bypassing the delimitation clause that would have delayed implementation from 2023 to 2034. The quota covers every state assembly and federal chamber, touching 900 million voters in the world's largest democracy.
