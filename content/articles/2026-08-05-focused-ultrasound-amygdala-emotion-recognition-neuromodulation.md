London — Sound waves through the skull altered emotion recognition.

Unlike magnetic or electrical stimulation, focused ultrasound reaches structures deep in the brain, putting the amygdala within reach for the first time.

Focused ultrasound is already in PTSD trials, and this result extends its reach to social perception.
