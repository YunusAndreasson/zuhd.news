Manaus — [Brazil](country:BR) approved $75 million to pave the BR-319 highway through intact Amazon.

Access roads are the primary deforestation driver, opening forest to settlement and farming behind paving crews.

A supreme court ruling simultaneously advanced a 965km Amazon railway, compounding the forest-loss risk.
