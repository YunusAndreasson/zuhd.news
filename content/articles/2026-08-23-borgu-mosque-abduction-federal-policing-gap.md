New Bussa — Gunmen struck a mosque during Friday prayers.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s states control no police, so governors can only appeal to Abuja.

The federal government alone commands armed forces, leaving Niger State's response limited to a statement two days later.

Security forces have not disclosed how many worshippers remain missing or when they might return.
