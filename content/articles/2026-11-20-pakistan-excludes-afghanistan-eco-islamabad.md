Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK) did not invite [Afghanistan](country:AF) to a regional ministerial.

Pakistan now chairs the Economic Cooperation Organisation for two years, with transit connectivity among its priorities.

Pakistan does not recognise the Taliban government, and the organisation's secretariat had suspended Afghanistan's membership, the foreign office said.

The foreign office says Afghanistan's return depends on it addressing terrorism threats from its soil.
