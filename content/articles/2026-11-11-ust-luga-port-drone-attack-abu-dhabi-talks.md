Ust-Luga — Drones hit the port, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

The attack came on the second day of US-mediated Russia-Ukraine talks in Abu Dhabi.

Governor Alexander Drozdenko said air defences downed 150 drones, 3 workers were wounded and a transformer substation blacked out.

Drozdenko said officials are still clarifying the damage at the substation.
