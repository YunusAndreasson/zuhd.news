Mountain View — Errors just taught a quantum chip to fix itself.

Google engineers reused error-correction data to drive reinforcement learning across 40,000 control parameters, catching calibration drift mid-calculation for a 20% error-detection gain.

Researchers say the method only holds if hardware drift stays slow enough between corrections.
