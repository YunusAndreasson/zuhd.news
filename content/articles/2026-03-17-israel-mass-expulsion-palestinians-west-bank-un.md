Israel expelled 36,000 West Bank Palestinians in a year. A UN report documented 1,732 settler attacks — a 24% rise — that Israeli authorities directed, enabled, or participated in, alongside military expulsion orders. Israeli forces killed a family of 4, including 2 children, in the West Bank on Sunday.
