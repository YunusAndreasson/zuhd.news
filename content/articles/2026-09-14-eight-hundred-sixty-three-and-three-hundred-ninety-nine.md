Kyiv — [Russia](country:RU) launched 863 drones at [Ukraine](country:UA) overnight.

Four civilians died in Ukraine as strikes edged toward NATO's Poland border.

Kyiv counted its incoming drones and Moscow its intercepted ones, each reporting only the war aimed at it.

The Kremlin floated three-way talks with Washington and Kyiv for October, unconfirmed.
