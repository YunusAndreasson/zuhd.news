Helsinki — [Finland](country:FI) will order about 1 million anti-personnel mines.

Mines maim civilians long after wars end, and Finland has left the Ottawa treaty banning them.

Production of a tripwire tube mine and a pressure-activated step mine is due to start in 2028, Col. Riku Mikkonen said.

Finland signs the contract soon, and the army may later buy cluster-type mines.
