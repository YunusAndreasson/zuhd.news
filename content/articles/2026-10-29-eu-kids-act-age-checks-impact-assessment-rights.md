Brussels — Age checks would apply to every adult, not only children.

Platforms would treat users as minors until they prove their age with an identity document or a biometric face scan.

An analysis of the Commission's 83-page assessment finds it skips milder options, such as parental-control software on children's devices.

The Commission says it took an evidence-based approach from the start.

Child-protection, digital-rights and parent groups oppose the proposal, which is not yet law.
