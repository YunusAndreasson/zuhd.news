London — TOPAS-nBio maps cerium-134's DNA damage per cell.

The simulation tracks low-energy electrons through virtual tissue, modelling DNA double-strand breaks at cell scale before patient treatment.

Cerium-134 also images actinium therapy; the authors call for further therapeutic trials.
