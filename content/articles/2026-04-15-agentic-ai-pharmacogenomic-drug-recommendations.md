London — Thousands of gene-drug pairs have no clinical guidelines. The pipeline reads biomedical papers and FDA labels, generating CPIC-style dosing guidelines at 91.9% accuracy for pairs experts haven't curated. It outperformed GPT-5, Claude, and Grok in expert evaluation — and most humans have never had pharmacogenomics applied to their care.
