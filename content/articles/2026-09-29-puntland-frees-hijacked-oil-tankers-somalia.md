Puntland — Its maritime police retook 2 hijacked tankers off [Somalia](country:SO).

5 crew of Honour 25 died, the force said, blaming the pirates.

Pirates moved fuel ashore in small boats, and a land network sold it on the black market, Puntland officials said.

Officials declined to name the local fuel importers who bought the barrels, citing an ongoing investigation.
