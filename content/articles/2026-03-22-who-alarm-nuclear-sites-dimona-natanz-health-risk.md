Geneva — Missiles struck both Dimona and Natanz, 2 nuclear facilities. The IAEA detected no abnormal off-site radiation, but Natanz holds enriched uranium stockpiles that would contaminate the region if dispersed in a direct strike. WHO Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus warned the conflict has entered a phase where nuclear safety cannot be guaranteed; the IAEA investigation of both sites is ongoing.
