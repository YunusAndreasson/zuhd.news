60 percent of the Shalimar Express ran without brakes. The initial inquiry blamed the driver; Pakistan Railways has been chronically underfunded — successive governments built motorways instead of maintaining rail. Railway officials want maintenance supervisors punished; the government has ordered a separate safety review.
