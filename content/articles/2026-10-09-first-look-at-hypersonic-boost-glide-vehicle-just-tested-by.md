Seoul — [South Korea](country:KR) flew its first domestic hypersonic glide vehicle.

Such a weapon would give Seoul a new way to hold North Korean targets at risk.

A rocket lofts the glider, which then manoeuvres at hypersonic speed inside the atmosphere, flying a flatter path than a ballistic missile.

The government has not named the missile, and low-resolution images leave the vehicle's design unclear.

Boost-glide vehicles have proven hard to turn into operational weapons, even for countries with long experience.
