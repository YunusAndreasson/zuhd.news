Kathmandu — 20 megawatts have stalled three governments for months.

Whoever owns the transmission lines can veto cross-border trade, and [India](country:IN) owns these.

[Nepal](country:NP)'s monsoon hydropower surplus can only reach [Bangladesh](country:BD) via Indian lines, which a state trading firm says lack capacity.

Nepal's late-August floods damaged 360 MW of that capacity, clouding the export outlook.
