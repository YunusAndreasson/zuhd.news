Baltimore — Anduril won up to $2.9bn in Navy submarine work.

Co-founder Palmer Luckey co-leads the Pentagon's 120-day Project Meridian war study with Elon Musk.

The $3.7bn plant will make torpedo tubes and hull parts while Pentagon reports put yard output near 1.2 boats yearly.

An industry official says it will be years before any Anduril component reaches a submarine.

The [US](country:US) Navy still needs 31 more Virginia-class boats to reach its plan of 59.
