Gilgit — Lawmakers want Islamabad to pay for melting glaciers.

The resolution demands water royalties and compensation, arguing [Pakistan](country:PK)'s water towers get no return despite feeding the country's rivers.

Opposition leader Hafeezur Rehman demands a corruption probe into existing climate funds before any new money is approved.
