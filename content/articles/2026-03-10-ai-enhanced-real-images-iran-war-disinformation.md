AFP found AI-altered real Iran war photos online. Unlike fabricated imagery, the underlying events are genuine, making AI-enhanced photos nearly impossible to debunk without SynthID watermarks or satellite footage comparison. Experts warn the technique could become undetectable at scale as the war continues.
