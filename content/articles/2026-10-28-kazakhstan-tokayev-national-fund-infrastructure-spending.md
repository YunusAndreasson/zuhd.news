Astana — Tokayev would spend 6.5 trillion tenge of oil savings.

That is over 21% of the fund and scraps a $100 billion-by-2030 target.

The $14.4 billion would fund roads, rail, energy and logistics through 2029, drawing on a fund of about $66.7 billion.

Of the total, 1.5 trillion tenge ($3.3 billion) goes to social projects through February 2027.
