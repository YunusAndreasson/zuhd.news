Jakarta — 138 oil and gas blocks go to Russia.

It is the largest OIC energy opening to [Russia](country:RU) since Ukraine sanctions began.

Western sanctions have left [Indonesia](country:ID)'s blocks underbid for years, opening a lane for Russian bidders.

Minister Bahlil Lahadalia is due to release technical bidding details for the blocks.
