India's LPG shortage is closing restaurants nationwide. India rerouted crude oil imports away from Hormuz, but LPG for cooking stoves follows a separate supply chain that could not adapt, leaving cylinders scarce since March 8. State-controlled cylinders are vanishing into black markets, and the government's emergency production increase has yet to reach shelves.
