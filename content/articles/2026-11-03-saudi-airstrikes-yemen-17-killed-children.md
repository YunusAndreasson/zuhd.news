Yemen — Saudi strikes killed 17 civilians, Iranian state agency Mehr reported.

[Yemen](country:YE)'s health ministry said 59 more were wounded, most of them children.

Armed forces spokesman Yahya Saree said [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s warplanes carried out 109 airstrikes over the previous 24 hours.

Polymarket traders give a Saudi-Houthi ceasefire by 31 December a 25% chance, up 8 points in a week.
