Police arrested 14 Aurat March activists in Islamabad. Police invoked Section 144, a ban on public gatherings, after Lal Masjid — a prominent Islamabad mosque — threatened to block the procession; detainees included rights activist Dr Farzana Bari. Pakistani police also detained lawyers who tried to secure their release; authorities have banned the march every year since 2023.
