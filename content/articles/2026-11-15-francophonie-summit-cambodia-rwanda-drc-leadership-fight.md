Phnom Penh — [Congo](country:CD) threatens to quit the Francophonie over Rwanda's candidate.

Congo, with over 110 million people, is the most populous country where French is official.

UN experts say [Rwanda](country:RW) backs the M23 rebels in eastern Congo, and Kinshasa now backs Romanian Dacian Ciolos instead.

Rwanda says Macron asked President Kagame to withdraw its candidate, Louise Mushikiwabo, and Kigali refused.

Leaders choose the secretary-general on Monday, and Macron's office says most members want a consensus candidate.
