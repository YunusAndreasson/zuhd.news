The US lifted Russia's oil sanctions for 30 days. Washington said the waiver eases prices spiking from its own Iran war — Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky called it a direct revenue lifeline for Moscow's Ukraine campaign. Zelensky flew to Paris on Friday to demand French President Emmanuel Macron press Washington to revoke the relief.
