Washington — Circle can now hold its own stablecoin reserves.

The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency approved a national trust charter, putting USDC's $73.2 billion backing under federal bank oversight.

Circle joins Ripple, Paxos and BitGo in the agency's crypto-custody line, deepening tokens' place in [US](country:US) banking.
