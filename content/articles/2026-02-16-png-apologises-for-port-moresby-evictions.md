2 people died in police-led evictions across Port Moresby. Prime Minister James Marape apologised to families displaced from informal settlements across the capital after clashes at the 2-Mile settlement turned fatal and a National Court halted demolitions. Marape pledged a permanent relocation plan by mid-2026, acknowledging that many residents are second- and third-generation Port Moresby citizens entitled to secure land tenure.
