Israeli police fractured a CNN producer's wrist in Jerusalem. Officers attacked journalists documenting Ramadan prayers outside Al-Aqsa, which Israel has barred to Muslim worshippers since the war began. The Foreign Press Association demanded the officers be suspended, as Israel holds Al-Aqsa closed through Eid al-Fitr.
