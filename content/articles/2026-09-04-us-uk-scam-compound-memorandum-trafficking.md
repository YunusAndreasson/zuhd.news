Washington — The [US](country:US) and [UK](country:GB) signed a memorandum on Southeast Asian scam compounds.

The deal targets prosecutions, not the debt-bonded workers trafficked into running scams.

The compounds sit in Myanmar and Cambodia border zones outside state control.

The memorandum coordinates cases, not extradition, reaching neither the compounds nor their operators.
