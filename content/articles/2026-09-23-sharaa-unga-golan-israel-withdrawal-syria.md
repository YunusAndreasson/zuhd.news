New York — [Syria](country:SY)'s president alleges 1,200 Israeli incursions and 300 detentions.

He says 500 air strikes endanger a recovery that returned 3.5 million displaced Syrians.

The [US](country:US) recognised [Israel](country:IL)'s Golan sovereignty in Trump's first term, but Security Council Resolution 497 calls the 1981 annexation void.

"Why don't you give New Jersey to the Israelis?" al-Sharaa said he asked the US president.

Al-Sharaa urged countries that abstained on December's 123-vote Golan resolution to reconsider.
