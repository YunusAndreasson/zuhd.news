Paris — Long-term French yields rose from 4% in August to above 5%.

Goldman Sachs estimates over a third of companies in [France](country:FR) borrow below the state.

M&G's Richard Woolnough finds the spread between 7- to 10-year government and investment-grade corporate debt has fallen below zero.

That is an index comparison, so not every French company can borrow more cheaply than the state.

Investor risk appetite and liquidity can switch rapidly, and corporate markets may struggle to absorb heavy selling.
