Oil jumped 5% on news Israel struck South Pars. South Pars, the world's largest natural gas field, is shared with Qatar and supplies a fifth of global liquefied natural gas. Iran cut gas exports to Iraq and threatened to strike energy infrastructure in countries hosting the attackers.
