London — Russia's uranium share could reach 36% by 2040.

Countries building reactors for decarbonization risk tying fuel supply to a sanctioned state.

The Financial Times, citing the Center for Strategic Advantage, says Western mines face decade-long permitting delays.

Washington's ban on Russian enriched uranium carries waivers letting purchases continue through January 2028.
