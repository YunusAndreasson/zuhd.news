Yanbu — Non-Iranian Hormuz crude flows hit the pre-war 13.5 million barrels a day, Kpler says.

Brent still trades near $102 as new US deployments keep the risk premium alive, Emirates NBD says.

More tankers run the strait under [US](country:US) escort, while [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) and the [UAE](country:AE) pipe crude past it.

Chatham House's Sanam Vakil said "higher exports today should not be mistaken for a new normal."

OPEC+ meets Sunday and is expected to hold November output targets steady.
