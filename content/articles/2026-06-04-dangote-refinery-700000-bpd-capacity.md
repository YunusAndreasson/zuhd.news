Lagos — Dangote's refinery hit 700,000 barrels per day, above nameplate.

[Nigeria](country:NG) has long exported crude and imported refined fuel; Dangote now exports jet fuel to Europe, the [US](country:US), and [Saudi Arabia](country:SA), S&P Global data shows.

Dangote Industries said expansion to 1.4 million bpd by 2028 is planned, with construction contracts being awarded.
