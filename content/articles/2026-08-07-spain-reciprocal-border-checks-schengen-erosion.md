Madrid — Two Schengen allies now police each other's border.

[Spain](country:ES) will check travellers from [Italy](country:IT) from midnight Saturday, retaliating after Rome kept its own checks past Spain's Sunday deadline.

At least 1,342 children remain in Ceuta as neither government sets a date to lift the controls.
