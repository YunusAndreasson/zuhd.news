Washington — Consumer routers, not government systems, were the entry point.

A hacked home router inside the target country defeats geographic detection filters entirely.

The Justice Department says it disrupted QScan and QTRouter, alleged [China](country:CN)-backed tools that reached the Federal Reserve, DOJ and Senate.

The DOJ has not detailed how affected device owners will be notified.
