Oleshky — Up to 2,000 people, mostly elderly, are promised a ceasefire.

Melania Trump's office announced it, saying residents lack medicine, food and water on mined roads.

Her office says the pause will halt all military action so aid can enter and civilians can leave by a safe passage.

Neither [Russia](country:RU) nor [Ukraine](country:UA) has confirmed the deal, though Ukrainian officials indicated a tentative agreement.

The pause begins Thursday, 29 October, with Newsmax set to document Russian and Ukrainian teams.
