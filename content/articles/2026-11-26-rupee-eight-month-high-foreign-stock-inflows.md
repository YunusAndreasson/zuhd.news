Mumbai — Foreign investors put nearly $15 billion into Indian stocks in November.

The rupee settled at 92.23 per dollar on Wednesday, an eight-month high.

Selling in AI-linked stocks sent investors toward [India](country:IN), a market many had largely shunned for two years.

Brent crude climbed 3.5% to $94.90 a barrel on Middle East supply fears, pressuring the rupee.

India faces a 17% [US](country:US) tariff if a trade deal is finalised and 25% if talks fail.
