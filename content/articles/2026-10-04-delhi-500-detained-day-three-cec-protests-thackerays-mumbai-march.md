New Delhi — Police detained nearly 500 on Ashoka Road, including a journalist.

Protests against Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar spread to Mumbai, where about 6,000 marched Sunday.

The Special Intensive Revision of [India](country:IN)'s voter rolls drops names, and Uddhav Thackeray claims 13 crore were deleted nationwide.

The Election Commission says the revision cleans the rolls and offers claims and objections.

Mumbai police have filed a case against the march's organisers at Azad Maidan station.
