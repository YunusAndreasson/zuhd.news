Chiba — 350mm of rain fell here in 12 hours.

At least 5 people died and thousands were stranded overnight near Tokyo.

It was Chiba's heaviest 12-hour rainfall since 1976, triggering [Japan](country:JP)'s highest warning level for the first time.

Officials say the toll could still rise as flooding continues into the weekend.
