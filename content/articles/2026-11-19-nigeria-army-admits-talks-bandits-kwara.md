Kwara — Army says talks with gunmen freed 10 hostages.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s military has repeatedly said it does not directly facilitate dialogue with armed groups.

Troops and State Security Service officers met armed men who the army says chose surrender over being killed.

Some Nigerians accused officers of fraternising with the gunmen, and the army calls that interpretation misleading.

Surrendered fighters face screening and legal procedures, spokesman Ibrahim Shittu said, but the framework behind such talks is unclear.
