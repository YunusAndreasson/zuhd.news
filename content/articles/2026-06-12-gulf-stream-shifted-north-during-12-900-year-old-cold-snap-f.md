London — 12,900-year cold snap shifted the Gulf Stream north.

University College London found the first direct evidence Atlantic circulation can reorganize abruptly — the failure mode scientists warn of today.

The same reorganization could recur as greenhouse warming weakens Atlantic circulation, the researchers said.
