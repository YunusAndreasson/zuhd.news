Abuja — Energy expert Dan Kunle urged Tinubu to sell all four state refineries.

The plants have combined capacity of 445,000 barrels daily but stayed largely idle for decades despite billions in public spending, Kunle said.

He wants a transparent bidding process completed within one year.
