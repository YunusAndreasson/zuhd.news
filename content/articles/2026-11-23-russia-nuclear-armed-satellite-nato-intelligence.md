Moscow — German media say [Russia](country:RU)'s nuclear satellite awaits launch.

The [US](country:US) Space Command chief says one blast would probably disable a third of low-orbit satellites.

Sputnik-S, assembled years ago, is stored near Moscow, intelligence shared by Germany's foreign intelligence service and armed forces reportedly shows.

Russia's embassy in [Germany](country:DE) called the accusations unfounded propaganda and said it has no information.

The Bundeswehr reportedly told Defence Minister Boris Pistorius a launch could come within weeks.
