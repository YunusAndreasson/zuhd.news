Islamabad — Rs56bn in power relief reaches [Pakistan](country:PK) consumers.

A Rs1.99/unit quarterly cut was offset by a Rs1.19/unit fuel surcharge, netting just 80 paisa relief in June.

The relief expires August; capacity payments and transmission costs keep Pakistan's power sector in structural deficit.
