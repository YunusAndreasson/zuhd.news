Herat — A market trip now means weighing arrest odds.

Women across [Afghanistan](country:AF)'s most educated city are restricting movement no rule explicitly requires.

At least 30 women were detained 6 June for allegedly breaking an unwritten dress code, residents said.

Whether any remain in custody or have been charged is still unconfirmed.
