Tehran — 2 tankers burn after hitting mines, [Iran](country:IR)'s Revolutionary Guards say.

The Guards warn that any ship leaving Iran's authorised route will strike a mine.

One tanker burns east of the strait and one inside it, both with tracking systems switched off, the Guards said.

The Guards named neither ship, and no outside party has confirmed the mines.

Polymarket traders price a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 31 December at 36%.
