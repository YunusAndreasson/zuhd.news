Khasab — Over 30 attacks hit ships near Hormuz in October.

That is more than double September's total and tops March's 30, UK Maritime Trade Operations data show.

Over 10,000 [US](country:US) troops enforce a blockade of [Iran](country:IR)'s oil terminals, and Brent stood at $117.60 on 30 October.

US Central Command says over 95% of ships transit safely and that American forces control the waterway.

Trump has not decided on a new campaign, Israeli media report, while [Israel](country:IL) updates its Iran target lists.
