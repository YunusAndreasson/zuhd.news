Washington — A fully US-made smartphone would cost 152% more, a trade group says.

Reshoring 10 device categories by 2031 would cost $185-230 billion, the Consumer Technology Association estimates.

It would need 555,000-668,000 more workers, more than double the existing [US](country:US) computer and electronics manufacturing workforce.

The group proposes reshoring assembly first for $16-19 billion and dropping tariffs on allied components.
