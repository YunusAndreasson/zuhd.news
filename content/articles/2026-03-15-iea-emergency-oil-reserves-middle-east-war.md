271.7 million barrels of emergency oil enter global markets. The International Energy Agency authorized the release, calling it the largest in its history, after Iran's supply disruptions outpaced what OPEC+ could replace. Asia releases immediately while Europe and the Americas follow at end of March, leaving non-IEA nations with no buffer.
