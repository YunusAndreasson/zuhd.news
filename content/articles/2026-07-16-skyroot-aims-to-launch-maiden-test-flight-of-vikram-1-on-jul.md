Hyderabad — Skyroot's orbital rocket debuts on July 18.

Vikram-1 is an all-carbon composite rocket with 3D-printed engines, aiming to reach a 450km orbit carrying small satellites.

The flight follows Vikram-S's 2022 suborbital debut, and a success would let Skyroot pursue regular launch cadence.
