Beijing — [China](country:CN)'s Zhuque-2E rocket shed 100–150 fragments near the Starlink constellation.

3 of 4 worst orbital breakups on record involve Chinese rockets; the debris field grew 150% in 5 years, Space Domain Awareness data shows.

Most fragments will reenter within months; Space Force found no immediate threat to crewed craft.
