Lahore — Imran Khan's 3 sisters face a second 30-day detention.

Noreen Niazi was moved to a cardiology institute after her health deteriorated in Kot Lakhpat jail.

Punjab holds them under its 1960 public-order ordinance, and a Lahore deputy commissioner's notification extended the first 30 days.

Their lawyer calls the extension unlawful, while adviser Rana Sanaullah told the Senate that Noreen was fine after treatment.

Justice Abher Gul Khan ordered the Home Department's reports by 2pm and will examine the matter after reading them.
