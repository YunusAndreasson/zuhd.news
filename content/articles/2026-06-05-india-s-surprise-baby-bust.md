[India](country:IN)'s fertility has fallen faster than projected, closing the demographic-dividend window before the country can fund pensions.

Rich countries aged after building welfare states; India ages with thin social infrastructure and a shrinking labor force.

Much of the Global South faces the same trap, with fertility falling at lower incomes than in East Asia.
