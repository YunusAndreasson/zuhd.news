China has over 300 EV brands. Most are losing money and no longer rely on mall showrooms for brand awareness, freeing prime retail space for robotics startups. Robotics startups are paying premium Beijing mall rent, replicating Tesla's 2019 strategy of building consumer trust before the category matures.
