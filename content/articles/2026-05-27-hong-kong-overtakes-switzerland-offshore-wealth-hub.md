Hong Kong — [Hong Kong](country:HK) surpasses [Switzerland](country:CH) as top offshore wealth hub.

Cross-border assets rose 10.7% to $2.95 trillion in 2025, Boston Consulting Group found, driven by mainland capital inflows and an IPO surge.

The firm says capital is choosing [China](country:CN)'s perimeter over neutral [Switzerland](country:CH) as the safer store of global wealth.
