New Delhi — Opposition calls laws passed by stolen votes illegal.

Rahul Gandhi and Akhilesh Yadav demand that [India](country:IN)'s Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar resign.

A newspaper investigation found 2 election commissioners recorded objections to decisions taken in the commission's name at least 14 times.

The Election Commission says such comments are normal during discussion of the voter-list revision.

A new party has given Kumar 48 hours to resign or face nationwide agitation.
