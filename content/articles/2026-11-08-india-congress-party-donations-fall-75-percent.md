New Delhi — Congress donations fell 75% to Rs 130.69 crore.

It is the steepest annual fall in a decade in filings to [India](country:IN)'s Election Commission.

Prudent Electoral Trust gave Rs 25 crore, down from Rs 216.34 crore, accounting for much of the drop.

The report excludes coupon sales and smaller gifts, and the party's audited 2025-26 account is not yet filed.

The Election Commission has yet to publish the Bharatiya Janata Party's 2025-26 report, after Rs 6,074.02 crore in 2024-25.
