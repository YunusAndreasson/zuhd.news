London — HistoCAM digitizes only what pathologists actually view.

It could bring AI diagnostics to hospitals that can't afford whole-slide scanners.

A 31-megapixel camera streams the pathologist's eyepiece view, capturing magnification, search paths and dwell times.

The paper remains an accepted manuscript, still ahead of its formal version of record.
