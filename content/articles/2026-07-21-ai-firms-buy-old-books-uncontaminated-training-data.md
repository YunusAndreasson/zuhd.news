San Francisco — Old paperbacks are now a scarce AI commodity.

Broker ISBNdb sells AI labs up to 1 million printed books at a time, prized because pre-2022 print predates AI-generated text and "model collapse."

Authors get no payment as firms scan and destroy the books; ISBNdb signs an NDA to keep each buyer's identity secret.
