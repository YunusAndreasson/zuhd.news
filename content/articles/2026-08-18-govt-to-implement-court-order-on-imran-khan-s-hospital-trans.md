Islamabad — A court had to order Imran Khan's hospital transfer.

The government resisted the transfer until the court forced its hand.

The Supreme Court directed the government to move [Pakistan](country:PK)'s jailed former premier to Shifa International Hospital within two days.

A medical board including a cardiologist and eye specialist must now examine him.
