Boise — Micron says memory demand will exceed supply for at least 2 years.

PC, phone and console makers already pay more as AI customers take priority.

Samsung executive Kim Taewoo said high-bandwidth memory will take almost 30% of DRAM wafer capacity in 2027, up from 20%.

Micron plans new clean rooms in 2028, but CEO Sanjay Mehrotra expects supply to stay limited as production ramps slowly.

75% of Micron's 2027 output is already accounted for, and most sales talks now concern 2028.
