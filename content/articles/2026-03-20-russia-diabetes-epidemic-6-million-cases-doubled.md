Russia's adult diabetes cases more than doubled in 20 years. Deputy Prime Minister Tatiana Golikova warned Friday of an epidemic, with 65,000 children and over 6 million adults now diagnosed. Health Minister Mikhail Murashko said half of Russian adults could be overweight by 2030 without dietary change.
