Duki — Three brothers died crossing a streambed toward farmland.

Roadside bombs in Balochistan select civilians by design, not combatants.

A remote-controlled IED destroyed their pick-up; a fourth relative survived, police said.

Balochistan's civilian deaths rose 93% in July, the security-studies institute PICSS reported.
