Reykjavik — Shallow sea volcanoes can explode, not just flow.

At shallow depths, magma meeting seawater fragments explosively — overturning the assumption that pressure muffled submarine eruptions near [Iceland](country:IS).

The discovery demands new tsunami and aerosol-climate risk calculations for North Atlantic coastlines.
