London — Researchers built a test blind to fake coupling.

Every EEG biomarker depends on telling a real neural link from a filtering artefact.

This measure vanishes unless the lag between rhythms is genuine, unlike ordinary correlation.

The single-subject proof of concept carries no clinical claim, an early-access manuscript still under further editing.
