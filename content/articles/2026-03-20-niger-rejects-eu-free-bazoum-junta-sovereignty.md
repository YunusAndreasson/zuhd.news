Niger summoned the EU ambassador for demanding Bazoum's release. The EU Parliament set April 2 — the expiry date of ousted President Mohamed Bazoum's constitutional mandate — as its deadline for his release and democratic elections. Niger's junta shows no sign of reversing course, with the EU yet to announce sanctions.
