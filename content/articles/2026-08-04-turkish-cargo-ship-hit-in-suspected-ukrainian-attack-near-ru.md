Novorossiysk — Drones struck the Nadezhda, a Turkish-operated ship, Monday.

The Cameroon-flagged vessel, carrying 22 crew, was sailing to Samsun when hit; [Türkiye](country:TR)'s authority says three were injured, per RT.

Ukraine has not claimed the strike; insurers are expected to reprice Black Sea war risk.
