Pretoria — Rights lawyers challenged [South Africa](country:ZA)'s [US](country:US) arms sales.

Rights lawyers filed in the Pretoria high court, arguing R279m in 2025 permits violated national arms law given [US](country:US) support for Israel's war in [Gaza](country:PS).

The Pretoria court must decide whether the permits stand while South Africa leads the ICJ Gaza case.
