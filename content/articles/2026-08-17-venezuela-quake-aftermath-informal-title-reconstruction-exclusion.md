Morón — 40 of 60 affected families lost their homes.

International attention went to La Guaira, leaving [Venezuela](country:VE)'s smaller towns without support.

Boca de Aroa also logged 252 displaced residents and 128 homes declared uninhabitable.

No rebuilding date has been set for the smaller towns, residents said.
