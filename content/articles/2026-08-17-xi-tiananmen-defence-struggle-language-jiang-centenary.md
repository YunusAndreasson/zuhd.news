Beijing — Xi defended the 1989 Tiananmen crackdown at a state ceremony.

Naming the suppression approvingly, rather than erasing it, signals a mobilisation doctrine ahead.

Xi spoke marking Jiang Zemin's birth centenary, calling for "indomitable fighting spirit" as [China](country:CN) faces mounting pressure.

Party language tracks "fighting spirit" as a signal preceding discipline and mobilisation campaigns.
