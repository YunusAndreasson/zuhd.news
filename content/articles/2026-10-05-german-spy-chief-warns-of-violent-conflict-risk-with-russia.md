Berlin — Germany's spy chief says Russia poses its most direct threat.

Martin Jaeger told lawmakers [Germany](country:DE) risks being drawn into violent conflict with [Russia](country:RU).

He cited an explosives-laden drone at Leipzig airport in August, though Moscow denies waging any hybrid campaign.

Jaeger said his service sees "no indication" of a major Russian attack on a NATO member.

He warned that low-level Russian military activity could occur in the Baltic states.
