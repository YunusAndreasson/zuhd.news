Washington — Hackers are breaking into accounts specifically to steal and sell intimate photos.

Victims worldwide face a US-based takedown process many cannot easily reach.

The FBI says attackers combine leaked passwords, social engineering and spoofed login pages to reach personal accounts.

The bureau has not said how many victims or arrests the operation involves.
