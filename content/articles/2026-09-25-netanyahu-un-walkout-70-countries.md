New York — Nearly 70 delegations left as Netanyahu spoke.

Netanyahu addressed the Assembly under an International Criminal Court arrest warrant issued in November 2024 over Gaza.

[Türkiye](country:TR), [Saudi Arabia](country:SA), [Pakistan](country:PK), [Spain](country:ES), [Brazil](country:BR) and [Nigeria](country:NG) were among those that walked out or stayed away.

Netanyahu called the departing delegates "moral cowards."

Israelis vote on 27 October, weeks after the speech.
