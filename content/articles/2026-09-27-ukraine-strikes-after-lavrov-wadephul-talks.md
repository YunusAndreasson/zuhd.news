Kyiv — Strikes killed 6 in [Ukraine](country:UA) and 1 in [Russia](country:RU) overnight.

The exchange followed Moscow's first face-to-face talks with [Germany](country:DE)'s foreign minister since the 2022 invasion.

Russia's defence ministry says it struck a Kyiv data centre and an Odesa logistics depot it calls military-linked.

Ukraine's president says the same strikes wounded two children and an adult at that data centre.

Lavrov says Russia is negotiating Black Sea navigation with India, Egypt and Turkey, with Kyiv talks still pending.
