Cairo — Moody's upgrade leaves Egypt 6 notches below investment grade.

The upgrade to B3 moves [Egypt](country:EG) out of Moody's substantial-risk tier, easing access to capital markets.

Moody's credits adjusted domestic fuel and electricity prices, a floated currency and spending controls rather than reversed energy reforms.

Capmas data show inflation still ran 13.9% in September, driven by energy costs.

Moody's positive outlook points to a further upgrade if revenue collection and growth strengthen.
