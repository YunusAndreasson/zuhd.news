Washington — A Pentagon official's family-firm clients won up to $3.5 billion.

Michael Dodd's office writes technology roadmaps that shape what the military buys.

His nominee disclosure lists the clients, and the Pentagon solicited the contracts after his September 2025 Senate confirmation.

Dodd pledged to sell his stake in Dioltas but still holds $1 million to $5 million, his filings show.

Neither Dodd nor the firms have said whether Dioltas helped win the awards.
