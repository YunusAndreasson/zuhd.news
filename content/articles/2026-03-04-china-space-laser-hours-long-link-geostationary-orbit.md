China's space laser hit 1 Gbps to geostationary orbit. The Chinese Academy of Sciences, China's state research body, sustained the link for 3 hours using a 1.8-meter telescope, 10 to 100 times faster than conventional microwave radio. The technology could enable real-time contact with probes at the Moon and Mars.
