San Francisco — Visa runs the infrastructure beneath Stripe's Tempo blockchain. Tempo, backed by Stripe and Paradigm, launched last month to let AI agents pay autonomously; Visa spent 6 months integrating its infrastructure. Mastercard spent $1.8 billion on stablecoin firm BVNK this year; Visa has not decided whether to issue one.
