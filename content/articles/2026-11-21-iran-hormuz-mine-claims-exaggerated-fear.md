Strait of Hormuz — None of the 30-plus claimed mine hits has been verified.

[Iran](country:IR)'s Revolutionary Guards made the claims, yet crossings stay 80% below prewar levels, Lloyd's List Intelligence says.

Five experts say the claims do not add up, and no shipping company or maritime agency has confirmed a blast.

"You can detonate the psychological warhead with a press release," said Chris O'Flaherty, a former Royal Navy captain.

Allied forces have cleared 27 mines, and a former Royal Navy captain says Iran can lay more.
