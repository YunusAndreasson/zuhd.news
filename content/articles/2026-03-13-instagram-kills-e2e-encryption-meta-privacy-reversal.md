Instagram DMs will lose end-to-end encryption on May 8. Governments in the US, UK, and EU have pressured Meta to scan DMs for child sexual abuse content, which encryption prevents. Meta encrypted Messenger for 2 billion users in December 2023 and has not said whether Messenger is next.
