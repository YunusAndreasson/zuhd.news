Spain announced aid shipments to Cuba through the UN. A US fuel blockade left only 44 of Havana's 106 garbage trucks running, piling waste across the capital for over 10 days. Mexico sent 800 tonnes of supplies last week as the UN warned of a deepening humanitarian emergency.
