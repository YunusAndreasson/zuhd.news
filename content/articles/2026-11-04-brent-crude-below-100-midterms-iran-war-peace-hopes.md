Washington — Brent crude fell 8.7% to $94.60, a two-month low.

Traders priced the Democratic midterm sweep as pressure on Donald Trump to end the [Iran](country:IR) war.

Brent is down about 17% since last week on peace-deal reports, and US benchmark crude fell 8% to about $88.40.

Joel Hancock, a Natixis analyst, warns volatility is so high that few traders will place big bets.

Without a Washington–Tehran peace deal, Hancock expects Brent back above $100 on shipping costs.
