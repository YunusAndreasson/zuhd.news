Lahore — AI-fabricated images cost a Faisalabad woman Rs2 million.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s cyber agency formally elevated women's harassment cases to highest priority; it arrested 10 across 3 Punjab cities in a single day.

A large backlog of harassment complaints remains pending; a ruling party legislator's case is before the courts on bail.
