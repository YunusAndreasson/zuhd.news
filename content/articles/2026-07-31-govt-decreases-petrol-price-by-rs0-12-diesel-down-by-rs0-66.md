Islamabad — Diesel has fallen from Rs520 to Rs392 since April.

[Pakistan](country:PK) cut petrol 12 paisa and diesel 66 paisa per litre for three days, after prices spiked when the US-Iran war began in February.

Ogra now sets prices daily instead of weekly; dealers are weighing a protest against the shift.
