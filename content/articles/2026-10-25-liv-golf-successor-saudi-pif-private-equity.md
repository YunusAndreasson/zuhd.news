Saudi Arabia — 15 golfers have tentatively joined a $300mn LIV Golf successor.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s Public Investment Fund stopped at $5.5bn, and LIV filed for bankruptcy in September.

BC Partners wants 30 to 40 players, and its term sheet requires each to settle LIV claims with the fund.

Jon Rahm and 3 others say they will not join, and the DP World Tour bars dual membership.

The fund will seek to wind down LIV if no deal closes by 30 October.
