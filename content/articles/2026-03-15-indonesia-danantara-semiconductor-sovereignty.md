Indonesia has nickel and tin but exports them raw. Danantara, Indonesia's sovereign wealth fund, is partnering with the education ministry to develop domestic chip design and fabrication capacity for the first time. The initiative targets AI chip development, where Danantara says new entrants can still compete if they move now.
