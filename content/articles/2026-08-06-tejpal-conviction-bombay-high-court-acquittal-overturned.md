Goa — A 2021 acquittal did not survive appeal.

The Bombay High Court convicted former Tehelka editor Tarun Tejpal of raping a junior colleague in a hotel lift in 2013, overturning the lower court's finding.

Tejpal says he will appeal to the Supreme Court; his sentence was due later Thursday.
