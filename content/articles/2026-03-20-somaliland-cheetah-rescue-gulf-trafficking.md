Somaliland holds 127 cheetahs seized from Gulf-bound smugglers. Only 7,000 cheetahs survive in the wild, with around 300 smuggled from east Africa each year — a rate the Cheetah Conservation Fund warns risks virtual extinction. Cheetahs still appear on Gulf social media despite formal bans in the UAE and Saudi Arabia, trafficking experts say.
