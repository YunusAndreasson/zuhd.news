San Francisco — Reddit's AI wrongly deleted decade-old posts.

Generating AI content is cheap and unbounded, but classifying it is expensive and error-prone, so unpaid moderators absorb the false positives.

Discord says it reinstated about 8,400 accounts wrongly banned by its AI system between May and July.
