Mexico City — A severed limb regrows to its exact original size.

The axolotl, native to lakes near the city, rebuilds lost limbs from a blastema — cells that revert to an embryonic state — rather than sealing the wound with scar tissue as mammals do.

Researchers are still working out what tells the regrowing cells where they are.
