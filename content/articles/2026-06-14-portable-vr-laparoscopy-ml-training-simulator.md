London — A portable rig now scores laparoscopic camera skill objectively.

Its machine-learning layer replaces a human evaluator for laparoscope training — existing high-fidelity alternatives cost far more, excluding under-resourced hospitals.

The system was validated across 38 participants; deployment in low-resource settings has not been tested.
