LA's Olympics chief faces a city probe over reported ties to Jeffrey Epstein's network. The city council voted 12-0 to investigate Casey Wasserman; the LA28 board had reviewed and retained him last month, calling the resolution non-binding. Wasserman is selling his talent agency as clients departed — the 2028 Games are 3 years away with his role unresolved.
