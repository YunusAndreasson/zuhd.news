Kuala Lumpur — Most foreign spouses in Malaysia cannot work legally. Spouse visas require employer sponsorship, a deliberately high barrier; only 1 in 10 have sought work authorisation as Malaysia's fertility rate falls. The government has announced no reforms to the employer-sponsorship requirement.
