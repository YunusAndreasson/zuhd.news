Jacobabad — 39 flooded farmers are suing 2 German companies.

A ruling billing firms in proportion to their emissions would be a breakthrough, lawyers say.

Plaintiffs put RWE's share of global industrial emissions at 0.68% and Heidelberg Materials' at 0.12%, and argue both ignored known climate risks.

RWE calls the suit an attempt to shift climate policy demands to German courtrooms.

The civil case in [Germany](country:DE) will likely take years, and a Peruvian farmer's similar claim against RWE failed last year.
