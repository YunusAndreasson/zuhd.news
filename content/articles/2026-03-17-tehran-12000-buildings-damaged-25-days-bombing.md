US-Israeli strikes damaged or destroyed 12,000 homes in Tehran. The count covers only Tehran's 9 million residents; strikes also hit Isfahan, Arak, and Shiraz. Iran's Health Ministry counted 1,444 civilians killed and 18,551 wounded, with no ceasefire talks announced.
