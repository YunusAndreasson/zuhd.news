New York City — Fossil plankton shells reverse their spiral worldwide.

Scientists have used that switch for decades as an ocean-temperature proxy.

Researchers now propose the flips mark one hidden species replacing another, not a temperature shift.

If lineage, not heat, drives the flips, parts of the climate record need re-checking.
