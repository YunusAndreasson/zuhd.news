London — Burial location tracks who holds authority over death.

Archaeologists find a near-universal shift from the house floor to dedicated ground — authority passing from family to institution to bureaucracy.

Urban land scarcity now presses on Islamic burial's shared, unpurchased ground in Cairo, Karachi and Jakarta.
