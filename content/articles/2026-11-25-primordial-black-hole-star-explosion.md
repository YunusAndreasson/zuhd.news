Cambridge — Black holes with an asteroid's mass could destroy whole stars.

Such black holes could make up some of dark matter, and the blasts would reveal them.

Simulations show a slow-spinning star fading as the hole swallows it, while a fast spinner's jets blast it apart.

Primordial black holes remain hypothetical, and none has been observed.

Ore Gottlieb's team is now modelling how to tell these blasts from other stellar explosions.
