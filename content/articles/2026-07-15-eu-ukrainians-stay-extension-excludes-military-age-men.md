Brussels — Kyiv asked to bar its own refugees.

Draft-age men barred from leaving [Ukraine](country:UA) lose eligibility for the EU's protection status from March 2027, at Kyiv's request.

The 4.4 million already sheltered keep their status; only new arrivals face the March 2027 cutoff.
