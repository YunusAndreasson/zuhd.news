United States — A NASA adviser cites 22 refuelling flights for Artemis IV.

NASA is still working toward a crewed lunar landing in 2028.

SpaceX must launch a depot ship, then tankers to fill it with liquid oxygen and methane before the lander leaves orbit.

"Actual number will be more like 6 to 8," said SpaceX founder Elon Musk.

SpaceX needs about three months to fuel one depot, even at twice-weekly launches by the end of 2027.
