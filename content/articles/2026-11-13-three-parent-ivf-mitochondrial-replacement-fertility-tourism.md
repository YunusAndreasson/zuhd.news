Chicago — 1 of 19 older women gave birth after three-parent in vitro fertilisation.

Unpublished Kyiv clinic data show no benefit, yet clinics still market it to women over 40.

It combines parental genes with a third person's mitochondrial DNA, and the [UK](country:GB) permits it only to prevent mitochondrial disease.

The same data show 9 live births among 10 patients under 35.

Ghent University researchers expect first results next year from a small study in younger women.
