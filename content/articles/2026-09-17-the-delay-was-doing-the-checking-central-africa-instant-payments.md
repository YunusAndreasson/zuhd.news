Money launderers now have less time to be flagged before funds move.

Central Africa's central bank joined the continental payment system in July, linking 30 countries and 200 banks instantly.

[Cameroon](country:CM) remains under enhanced Financial Action Task Force monitoring as transaction volumes keep rising fast.
