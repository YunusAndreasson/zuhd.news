Tehran — Revolutionary Guards say 3 tankers exploded on mines within 24 hours.

Hormuz traffic was already 20% below normal, at 3.3 ships a day as of 25 October.

[Iran](country:IR)'s Guards say the ships switched off radar and ignored warnings on an unauthorised route south of the strait.

The Guards did not say what happened to the crews, and Brent stood at $110.20 on 28 October.
