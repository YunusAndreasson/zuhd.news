Monrovia — Clinic shelves are running empty.

Clinicians expect the shortage to surface next as a rise in unsafe abortions.

[Liberia](country:LR)'s contraceptive supply depends almost entirely on donor funding now being withdrawn.

The health ministry has not said whether it can cover the gap donors leave.
