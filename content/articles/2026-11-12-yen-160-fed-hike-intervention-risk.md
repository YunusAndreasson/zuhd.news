Tokyo — The yen traded at 159.58 per dollar Thursday.

Wholesale prices in [Japan](country:JP) rose 7.1% in the year to October, the Bank of Japan said.

Traders expect a Federal Reserve hike after hot October inflation, widening the gap with Japan's 1.25% borrowing costs.

Many analysts polled expect the Bank of Japan to raise rates as soon as next month.

The Federal Reserve decides on 9 December, with officials leaving a 50-basis-point hike open.
