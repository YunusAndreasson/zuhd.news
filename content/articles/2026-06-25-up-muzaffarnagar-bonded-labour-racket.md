Muzaffarnagar — Dogs, whips, and unpaid wages: 12 workers freed.

Factory owner Ankit Balyan held migrant workers from 8 states and Nepal under promise of ₹12,000 monthly wages never paid, [India](country:IN)'s police said.

Balyan is absconding; his father and supervisor are arrested on human trafficking, murder and child labour charges.
