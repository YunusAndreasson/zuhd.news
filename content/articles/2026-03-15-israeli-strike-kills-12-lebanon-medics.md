Israel struck a Lebanese medical team mid-rescue on Sunday. Lebanon's health ministry said 31 medics have been killed since March 2, with crews targeted during active rescue operations; Israel says the struck sites stored Hezbollah rockets. France proposed Paris for direct Israel-Lebanon talks, but Lebanon has not agreed while the bombing continues.
