Magdeburg — Researchers confirmed the skeleton of Otto I, founder of the Holy Roman Empire, after 1,053 years. DNA matched the Magdeburg Cathedral bones to a sample from Heinrich II, last Ottonian emperor and a direct descendant, while physical analysis confirmed a man of 60 with combat injuries and an elite diet. A small possibility remains that the bones belong to an unrecorded blood relative rather than Otto himself.
