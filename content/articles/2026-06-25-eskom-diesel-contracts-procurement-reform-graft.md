Johannesburg — Suspect firms won all 3 of Eskom's diesel contracts, each worth R4 billion.

One recipient is under bribery investigation; [South Africa](country:ZA)'s new Public Procurement Act, built to stop this, hasn't yet taken effect.

Public comment on the Act's regulations closes July 15; a Constitutional Court challenge could force Parliament to restart.
