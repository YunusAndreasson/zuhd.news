Islamabad — An 11.8kg gas cylinder now costs Rs3,589.

Pakistan's OGRA raised LPG 35% for April, adding Rs924 to the standard household cylinder price as Saudi Aramco contract prices jumped 44%.

The war's energy shock has reached Pakistan's household kitchens; the new price holds until April 30.
