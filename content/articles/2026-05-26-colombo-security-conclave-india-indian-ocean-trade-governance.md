Colombo — [India](country:IN)'s new ocean security body met in Colombo.

The Colombo Security Conclave coordinates undersea cables, port governance, and maritime awareness on Gulf-to-Asia oil and gas shipping lanes.

[Australia](country:AU) depends on those lanes but has no observer seat and no plan to seek one.
