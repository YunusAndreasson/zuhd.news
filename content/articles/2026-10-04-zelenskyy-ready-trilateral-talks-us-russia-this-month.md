Kyiv — Zelenskyy says Washington wants technical-level talks by October's end.

[US](country:US) ceasefire efforts have paused since the war on [Iran](country:IR) began.

Kyiv would meet in the United Arab Emirates or another country Washington proposes, and Washington has contacted Moscow, Zelenskyy said.

Both sides say they will intensify long-range strikes, and [Russia](country:RU) says it targets Ukraine's energy sector.

No meeting is agreed, and no Russian reply to the offer has been reported.
