Abuja — Prosecutors rest their case over a $6 billion unfinished dam.

A Paris tribunal found bribery tainted the contract and ruled for [Nigeria](country:NG).

Former power minister Olu Agunloye faces 7 charges, including forgery and disobeying a presidential directive in the 2003 award.

Agunloye denies all 7 charges and says he is a scapegoat for others.

Judge Jude Onwuegbuzie hears arguments on the no-case submission on 3 February.
