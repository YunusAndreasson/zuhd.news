Hartford — Connecticut has logged 54 mosquito species.

Warming lets tropical species like the Asian tiger mosquito establish in New England, carrying dengue and Zika into a region with no statewide monitoring in most states, researchers say.

New York's legislature is weighing a bill to replace its county-by-county surveillance patchwork before the next outbreak.
