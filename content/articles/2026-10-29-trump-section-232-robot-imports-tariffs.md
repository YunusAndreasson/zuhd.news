Washington — Trump set a 180-day clock on foreign robot imports.

Any tariff would fall on [US](country:US) factories, which have no domestic source of heavy-duty robotic arms.

The "big four" arm makers — FANUC, ABB, Yaskawa and KUKA — sit in [Japan](country:JP), [Switzerland](country:CH) and [Germany](country:DE).

Major US companies can still win waivers, as Intel recently did.

Without trade agreements by the deadline, Trump may impose tariffs, duties, quotas and fees.
