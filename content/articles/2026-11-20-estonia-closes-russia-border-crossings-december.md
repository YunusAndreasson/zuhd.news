Tallinn — All vehicle crossings into Russia close on 1 December.

[Estonia](country:EE) cites a failed arson attack on a military-industrial firm, which it blames on Russian intelligence.

Interior Minister Igor Taro told residents not to expect the old crossing rules to return.

Russian foreign ministry spokeswoman Maria Zakharova called the closure provocative, and Moscow has warned of a response.

Prime Minister Kristen Michal's government will reassess the closure in six months.
