New York — Two-thirds of the ocean got a shared rulebook.

The treaty, in force since January, brings marine protected areas and impact assessments to waters beyond any nation's jurisdiction.

The [US](country:US) has signed but not ratified it; [Russia](country:RU) has not signed at all.
