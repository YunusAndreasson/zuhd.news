South Korea imports 70% of its oil through Hormuz. US President Donald Trump's demand for warships forces Seoul to choose between US tariff threats and Iran's potential retaliation against Korean ships. Seoul is waiting for the outcome of Trump's March 19 summit with Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi before deciding on deployment.
