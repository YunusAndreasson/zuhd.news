Jakarta — Sugarcane scraps grew into a solid panel.

The residue is normally burned instead, driving the seasonal haze that closes regional schools.

Ganoderma lucidum fungus digests the waste and its threads bind it, reaching 0.63 MPa strength with cassava-pulp supplement.

The unedited study leaves moisture sensitivity and building-code certification unresolved before real use.
