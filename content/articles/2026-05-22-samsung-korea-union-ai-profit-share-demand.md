Seoul — Samsung averted a walkout by 48,000 workers.

[South Korea](country:KR)'s largest chipmaker union demanded a share of AI-driven profit gains, linking labor terms directly to the company's technology windfall.

The tentative deal covers bonus payments; the union's demand for a structural AI profit share remains unresolved.
