Iran called 97 people arrested Thursday "soldiers of Israel." The sweep followed Israel's killing of intelligence chief Esmaeil Khatib 2 days earlier, a strike Israel said targeted spy networks inside Iran. All 97 face the death penalty, and human rights monitors have been denied access to the 200-plus people Iran has detained since the war began.
