Washington — US strikes on Iran began February 28 without congressional authorization. Congressional Democrats filed a privileged resolution and discharge petition to force a War Powers Act vote — the first such challenge since the strikes began. Democrats need only 5 Republican defections in the Senate to pass the measure.
