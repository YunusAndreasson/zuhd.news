Berlin — A blood-sugar watch read a banana like a wrist.

EU rules ban the claim, and users who trust the readings face mortal danger.

No wearable measures glucose optically through skin, and [Germany](country:DE)'s consumer federation caught one simulating values.

eBay and Mediamarkt.de listed them, and Mediamarkt's hits fell from 108 to 7 only after netzpolitik.org asked.
