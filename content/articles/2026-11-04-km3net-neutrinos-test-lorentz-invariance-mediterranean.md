Toulon — Researchers tested relativity on 5,828 neutrino events.

Some theories reconciling gravity with quantum physics suggest Lorentz invariance might not always hold.

The KM3NeT/ORCA telescope, a lattice of light sensors 2,450 metres under the Mediterranean, records atmospheric neutrinos.

A violation, if found, would break the principle at the heart of Einstein's relativity.
