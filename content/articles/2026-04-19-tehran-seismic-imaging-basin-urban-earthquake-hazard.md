Tehran — 3 fault-bounded sub-basins discovered beneath [Iran](country:IR)'s capital. Southern Tehran's sub-basin sediments amplify earthquake shaking — a risk invisible to existing low-resolution hazard maps for 9 million residents. The study identifies likely blind faults under the Pardisan system, enabling district-level seismic retrofit targeting.
