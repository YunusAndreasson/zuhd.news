New York — Physicists produced the first equation linking a quantum property to space-time curvature.

Entanglement builds space-time; gravity tracks non-stabilizerness — how far a quantum system exceeds classical simulation.

The finding covers holographic toy models; whether it extends to realistic quantum gravity is unresolved.
