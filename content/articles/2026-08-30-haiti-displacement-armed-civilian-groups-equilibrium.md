Mirebalais — Two allied gangs overran a once-peaceful region.

Residents now depend on civilian militias, not the state, for protection.

Police and the armed forces now share territorial control with self-defense militias, turning displacement into a permanent condition.

Jeff Larose and Wilson Joseph's Viv Ansanm coalition still controls routes into the region.
