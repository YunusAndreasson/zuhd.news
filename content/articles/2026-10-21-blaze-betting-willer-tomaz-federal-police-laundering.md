Curitiba — Blaze's operator shared an address with Willer Tomaz's companies.

[Brazil](country:BR)'s Federal Police suspect Tomaz used the betting platform to circulate money and recover it.

Police say Tomaz made repeated R$100,000 transfers to Foggo Entertainment, Blaze's Brazilian operator, and logged R$40.83 million in losses.

Tomaz has denied being a partner or shareholder in companies tied to betting operations.

Police are tracing whether the losses returned to his group through intermediary companies and casino withdrawals abroad.
