Cairo — Prosecutors summoned Lina Attalah without naming a charge.

Case 9072/2026 is the sixth against Mada Masr in six years, the outlet said.

In August 2025 [Egypt](country:EG)'s security prosecution released Attalah on 30,000 Egyptian pounds bail after questioning over a story on prison abuse.

The Supreme State Security Prosecution questions her on Wednesday 21 October.
