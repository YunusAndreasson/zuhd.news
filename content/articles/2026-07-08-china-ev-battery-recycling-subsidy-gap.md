Beijing — [China](country:CN)'s EV batteries retire at 4M tonnes yearly.

The China Electronics Energy Saving Technology Association projects the surge from 2028; over 80% of batteries use low-value lithium iron phosphate cells.

The author's research finds subsidies alone won't fix a market informal recyclers already dominate.
