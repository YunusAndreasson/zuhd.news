London — 72 studies trained AI to dose sepsis drugs.

Reinforcement learning models learned vasopressor and fluid policies from retrospective ICU records, mostly the MIMIC database, often beating clinicians on paper.

Researchers say evaluation methods remain too unreliable to deploy the models on real patients.
