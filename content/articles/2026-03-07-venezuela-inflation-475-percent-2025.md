Food prices rose 532% in Venezuela last year. Tightening US sanctions before former President Nicolás Maduro's January ouster drove the surge, which Venezuela's central bank suppressed for over a year. Economists forecast inflation near 100% in 2026 after Venezuela and the US vowed to jointly develop oil and mineral reserves.
