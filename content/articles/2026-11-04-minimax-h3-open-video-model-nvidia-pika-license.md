China — Nvidia and Pika now build on MiniMax's Chinese video model.

H3's free licence excludes the [US](country:US), EU, UK and [South Korea](country:KR), so firms there must negotiate.

H3 has over 24 million downloads since August, but users whose products earn over $20 million a year need MiniMax's written permission.

Whether H3 becomes a standard base depends on MiniMax's updates, licensing and partnerships holding up.
