Fort Worth — 19 million eggs are recalled over salmonella risk.

A 2025 outbreak sickened 134 before recall; the FDA requires no pre-market pathogen test for shell eggs.

Affected cartons carry sell-by dates through August 17; the producer reports no illnesses so far.
