London — Oil emissions fell 1.9% as the [Iran](country:IR) war priced out demand.

Total CO2 still hits a record 43.3 billion tonnes as land-use emissions rise.

The Global Carbon Project projects fossil CO2 down 0.4% to 38.2 billion tonnes, with Hormuz disruption raising prices and curbing oil demand.

The project's own range runs from a 1.5% fall to a 0.6% rise, so an increase cannot be ruled out.

Emissions climbed again after the 2009 and 2020 dips, once economies recovered.
