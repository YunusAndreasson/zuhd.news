San Francisco — Mirendil says one researcher trained a model for under $10 million.

It raised a $1 billion Series A on 13 November, reportedly at a $5 billion valuation.

The company says agents wrote over 99% of the code in 3.5 months, while humans set direction and judged results.

The cost and coding figures are Mirendil's own, and no outside party has verified them.

Mirendil says Mirendil-Base-Large is 80% done and the larger XL model 20%.
