New Delhi — Indian textiles enter [Britain](country:GB) duty-free from Wednesday.

The pact scraps tariffs on 99% of exports each way, aiming to nearly double two-way trade to $100bn by 2030.

[India](country:IN)'s firms gain a 5-year exemption from UK payroll levies, saving an estimated $600 million.
