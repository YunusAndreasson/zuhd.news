Lahore — Punjab's wheat procurement reached 160,000 tonnes against a 2.5 million target.

Farmers bear price risk alone since [Pakistan](country:PK) scrapped the wheat support price in April 2025.

Banks questioned the private purchasing scheme's viability, and crackdowns on hoarders failed to lift volumes before the price crashed.

Punjab, which grows over 76% of the wheat, refused in October to commit to its federal target.
