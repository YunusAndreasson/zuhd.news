Abuja — A court order lasted one day.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s anti-graft agency froze Osun State's accounts on August 5 over an alleged N11bn infraction; President Tinubu ordered it reversed a day later, days before the state's governorship election.

The EFCC must now return to court to vacate its own order, with no new filing date set.
