Gaza City — A widow of 6 cannot reach $5,000 in her husband's account.

Without functioning courts, widows cannot obtain the certificates that unlock inheritance.

[Israel](country:IL)'s military blew up the Palace of Justice on 4 December 2023, and banks demand a court-issued inheritance certificate.

"My husband's brother seized my husband's car without any legal right," said Salma Abdel Hadi, a widow in Nuseirat.

She estimates the car could fetch $20,000, enough to move her children out of a tent.
