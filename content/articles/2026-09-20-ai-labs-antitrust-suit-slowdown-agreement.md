San Francisco — 4 paying subscribers sued 4 AI labs.

They allege a pact to slow development illegally reduces what subscribers get.

The complaint says coordination occurred Sept. 12, when Dario Amodei urged a slowdown and rivals publicly agreed.

The labs have not answered, and Amodei had asked Washington for a narrow antitrust waiver.
