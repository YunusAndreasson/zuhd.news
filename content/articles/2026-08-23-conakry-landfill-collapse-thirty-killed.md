Conakry — A landfill set to close that day collapsed instead.

Waste-pickers who lived at the dump lost their homes, income and lives.

Overnight rain destabilized the uncompacted mound at [Guinea](country:GN)'s largest dump, burying holdouts who had ignored eviction notices.

The government has not set a date to relocate survivors or the waste itself.
