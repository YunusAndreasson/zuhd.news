Christchurch — Leaves yield protein when staple crops fail.

David Denkenberger's group at Canterbury is testing leaf-protein extraction for the scenario where nuclear winter or climate shock collapses global farming.

The group has spent a decade on food-resilience research; this strand has not yet produced published yield figures.
