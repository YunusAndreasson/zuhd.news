New York — Spot bitcoin ETFs shed $5.4 billion in November, a record.

Bitcoin fell from above $77,500 to $55,122 on 21 November, SoSoValue data show.

Stablecoin supply shrank $13.9 billion in three weeks, and treasury companies trading below asset value find it harder to fund purchases.

The bounce to about $61,600 came in thin holiday trading, driven by traders closing short bets.

The Federal Reserve decides 9 December, with CME FedWatch pricing a quarter-point hike above 90% as of 25 November.
