London — European gas rose 36% this spring, ICE data show.

Europe replaced [Russia](country:RU)'s pipeline gas with LNG, now priced by the Strait of Hormuz closure that cut one-fifth of global supply.

Storage sits at 39%, down from 47% a year ago, against the EU's 90% November fill target.
