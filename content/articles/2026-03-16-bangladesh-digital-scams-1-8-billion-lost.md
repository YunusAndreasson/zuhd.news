An 1860 colonial law is Bangladesh's primary legal tool against digital fraud. Bangladesh lost $1.8 billion to scams as mobile money and e-commerce platforms expanded faster than fraud protections could follow. Home Minister Salahuddin meets Britain's Home Office minister Tuesday to push cross-border enforcement and modernize the law.
