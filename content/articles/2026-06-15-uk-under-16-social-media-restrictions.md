London — [Britain](country:GB) will restrict under-16s on major social platforms.

Following [Australia](country:AU)'s December ban, Britain's bill requires platforms to verify users' ages or block under-16 access.

Privacy advocates argue age verification creates data risks; enforcement details are still being drafted.
