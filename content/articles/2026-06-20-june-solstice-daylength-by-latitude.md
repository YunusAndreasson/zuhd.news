Manila — Manila logs 13 hours of solstice daylight; Boston logs 15.

Earth's 23.5-degree axial tilt produces the effect: the Philippine weather bureau measured 12h59m in Manila versus 13h50m in the [UAE](country:AE) and 15h17m in Boston.

From Monday, all Northern Hemisphere cities lose daylight until December's solstice.
