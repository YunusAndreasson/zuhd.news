title: "Tropical Insects Cannot Withstand Warming"
date: "2026-03-05T12:00:00Z"
source: "Nature"
sourceUrl: "https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-00377-3"
category: "science"
---

Half of Amazonian insect species face deadly heat stress. Unlike Arctic species accustomed to extreme swings, tropical insects evolved in stable temperatures and cannot adjust their protein architecture to cope with warming. Their loss would cascade through food systems in Africa, Southeast Asia, and Latin America.
