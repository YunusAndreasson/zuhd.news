Kamchatka — Rope now shields billion-dollar ballistic-missile submarines.

Cheap drones have made [Russia](country:RU)'s sea-based nuclear deterrent vulnerable at home.

Satellite images show nets draped over Borei-class submarines at Rybachiy since May, plus similar screens on Black Sea boats.

Whether the netting holds against faster or explosive-tipped drones remains untested.
