Sensors too weak for real encryption get protected by shaping radio waves instead.

Reinforcement learning steers reflectors so legitimate receivers get a stronger channel than eavesdroppers, an information-theoretic guarantee.

The unedited manuscript secures one room, not a network, as 6G standards and patents accumulate.
