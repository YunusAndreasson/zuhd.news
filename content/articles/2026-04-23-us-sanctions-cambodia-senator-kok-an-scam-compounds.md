Phnom Penh — [US](country:US) sanctioned a Cambodian senator running scam compounds. Treasury blacklisted senator Kok An and 28 associates for operating pig-butchering compounds that traffic workers and run crypto fraud against foreign victims. Kok An is a sitting senator in [Cambodia](country:KH) — Treasury's designations name a lawmaker, not just operators.
