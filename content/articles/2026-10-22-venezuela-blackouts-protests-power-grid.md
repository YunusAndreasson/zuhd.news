Maracaibo — Blackouts now last 6 to 15 hours daily outside Caracas.

Residents in [Venezuela](country:VE) have blocked roads at night in Turmero and Valencia since August.

A turbine at the Guri hydroelectric plant that generated 770MW collapsed, and the government blames El Niño drought.

"Yesterday we didn't have electricity for 11 hours," said Yanira, a 33-year-old designer in Maracaibo.

Authorities answered with shorter public-sector hours and a deal with shopping malls to cut consumption.
