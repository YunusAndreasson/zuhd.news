London — DeepMind's Gemini now reasons about physical space. Gemini Robotics-ER 1.6 applies transformer reasoning to physical environments, letting robots infer tasks from context rather than follow predefined scripts. If language models disrupted knowledge work, embodied reasoning models are the next test for physical labor.
