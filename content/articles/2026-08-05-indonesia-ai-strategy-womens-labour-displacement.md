Jakarta — Indonesia's AI policy names chips, not jobs.

Data-center and chip investment dominate the debate while no plan addresses who absorbs the jobs AI displaces first, the Lowy Institute said.

The Lowy Institute warns other developing economies are already copying Indonesia's approach.
