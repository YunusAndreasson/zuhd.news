Mumbai — Maharashtra may sell tokenized claims on its grid.

It would raise infrastructure funds without adding to state debt.

The draft Digital Economy Ledger Technology Act would make it the first state in [India](country:IN) with a tokenization law.

No sale date or pricing has been set for the tokenized claims yet.
