Seoul — SK hynix cancelled a Solidigm listing worth up to $15 billion.

Its US shares rose 5.1% in pre-market trading after it kept Solidigm's growth inside the company.

SK hynix cited market conditions, after [South Korea](country:KR)'s financial regulators questioned a dual listing's effect on existing shareholders.

SK hynix reports third-quarter earnings later this month, where analysts expect higher shareholder returns.
