London — [Britain](country:GB)'s court classed a protest group with ISIS.

The Court of Appeal upheld Palestine Action's 2025 proscription as lawful Monday; police arrested 58 supporters outside for displaying signs.

Co-founder Huda Ammori will seek a Supreme Court appeal; membership now carries up to 14 years' imprisonment.
