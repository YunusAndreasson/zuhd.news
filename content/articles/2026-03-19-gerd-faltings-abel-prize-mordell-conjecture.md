Mathematician Gerd Faltings, 71, won the 2026 Abel Prize. His 1983 proof of the Mordell conjecture showed that polynomial equations of a certain type have only finitely many rational solutions, linking number theory to algebraic geometry. He will receive the prize in Oslo in May, 43 years after the proof that earned it.
