Budapest — Hungary's parliament stripped its own PM's immunity.

Péter Magyar requested it himself, testing whether his graft probes look like justice or revenge.

Magyar's Tisza party won in April vowing to investigate 16 years of alleged corruption under [Hungary](country:HU)'s former leader Viktor Orbán.

Former minister Balázs Hankó, accused of embezzling over €40 million, denies wrongdoing.

Orbán's Fidesz party boycotted the vote, calling the probes an attempt to cripple it legally.
