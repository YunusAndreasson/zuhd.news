Iran has struck all 6 of China's Gulf allies. China brokered the 2023 Saudi-Iran détente but has issued no ceasefire call as Iranian strikes hit the Gulf states supplying China's energy. Beijing is ramping up mediation, but analysts say it lacks the leverage to stop the strikes.
