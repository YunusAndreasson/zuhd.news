Bamako — JNIM, al-Qaeda's Sahel affiliate, killed [Mali](country:ML)'s defence minister and blockaded the capital on April 25.

The group has targeted fuel and power since September 2025; the siege cuts the Dakar trade corridor, causing shortages in Bamako.

The junta that expelled France in 2022 has no security partner and no plan to reopen blocked routes.
