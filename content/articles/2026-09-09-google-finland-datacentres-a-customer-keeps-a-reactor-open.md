Hamina — Google will invest €13 billion in Finland.

A 22-year power deal keeps a Finnish nuclear plant running to 2050.

The Loviisa plant supplies 10% of [Finland](country:FI)'s power, and Google's purchase funds the upgrade that keeps it open.

Google's contract lets it draw up to half the plant's output from 2030.
