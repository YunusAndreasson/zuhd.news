San Francisco — 1.7 million bitcoin sit in quantum-vulnerable wallets.

A Coinbase-convened cryptography panel split on whether to freeze exposed addresses or push network-wide migration to quantum-safe standards.

Bitcoin has no central authority; any migration requires near-universal network adoption.
