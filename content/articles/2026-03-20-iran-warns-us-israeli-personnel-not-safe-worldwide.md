Iran threatened US and Israeli personnel at global tourist sites. The warning followed US-Israeli strikes that killed Iran's intelligence minister and the Revolutionary Guard's spokesman — a direct hit on Iran's command structure. No government has formally raised travel alerts, with Iran's ability to execute attacks while under bombardment unconfirmed.
