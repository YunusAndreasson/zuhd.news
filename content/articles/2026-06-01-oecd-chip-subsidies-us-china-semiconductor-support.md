Paris — [China](country:CN) edges all regions in chip revenue support.

The Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development found China's share exceeded 10% while [US](country:US) firms led in absolute dollars.

The trade body valued the chip market at $631 billion in 2024; both countries' programs are still expanding.
