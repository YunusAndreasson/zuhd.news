Cambridge — Juvenile mice developed liver tumors from NDMA doses harmless to adults. A Nature study found cell proliferation rate — not dose — determines cancer risk: fast-dividing juvenile cells accumulate mutations adult cells repair, explaining why Zantac-era childhood exposure posed greater danger. Thousands of Zantac lawsuits remain pending.
