Cupertino — Apple's iPhone Duo starts at $1,999, or ₹299,900 in [India](country:IN).

That's a rounding error for [US](country:US) buyers and a month's salary elsewhere.

US carriers hide the real price in 36-month bill credits, a subsidy few other markets offer.

Preorders open October 16, with the foldable shipping worldwide October 23.
