Pretoria — [Nigeria](country:NG) counts 8-10 extrajudicial killings of citizens in [South Africa](country:ZA) since February.

Four came in direct contact with security personnel, Nigeria's deputy high commissioner says.

Nigeria sent notes on each death to South Africa's foreign ministry and is still waiting for investigation updates.

South Africa's foreign minister cited more than 500 Nigerians processed through its courts by 7 July, citing organised crime and fraud.

Nigeria wants the African Union's January summit to debate xenophobia, while Pretoria wants migration discussed continent-wide.
