Tehran — Satellites measured SO₂ over Tehran no agency disclosed.

Researchers used Sentinel-5P and TROPOMI open satellite data to reconstruct the SO₂ plume from March 7 oil storage explosions in [Iran](country:IR) — no ground measurements needed.

The same method works for any strike or siege; satellite sensing is now the de facto wartime air-quality monitor.
