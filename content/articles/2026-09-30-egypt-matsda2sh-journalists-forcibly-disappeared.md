Cairo — Security forces arrested all 6 Matsda2sh journalists within two days.

No one has told their families or lawyers where they are held.

[Egypt](country:EG)'s security personnel raided editors' homes Monday night, seized phones and laptops, then arrested four more reporters Tuesday.

Rights group director Hossam Bahgat says no Egyptian newsroom has been arrested whole in two centuries of journalism.

No authority has said who is investigating them or on what charge.
