Kandahar — Radio can no longer even report a cricket result.

Cricket is the one major sport the Taliban government lets men and boys play.

The provincial Information and Culture Department phoned stations on Saturday and banned commentary, news and results, two broadcasters said.

[Afghanistan](country:AF)'s government hosted cricket matches in August, and the men's team is playing Zimbabwe in Sharjah now.

Officials gave no reason, and the national cricket board has not commented.
