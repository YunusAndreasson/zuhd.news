Beijing — 125 Chinese cities studied over 12 years.

Coastal [China](country:CN) clusters high-value gains while the Northwest and Northeast stay cold spots.

An early-access Nature paper used a genetic algorithm so data, not a committee, weight livability's indicators.

The authors urge policymakers to abandon uniform renewal for context-specific plans instead.
