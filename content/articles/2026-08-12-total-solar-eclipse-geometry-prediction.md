Zaragoza — A cosmic coincidence made Wednesday's totality possible.

The Moon drifts away yearly, so total eclipses will eventually stop occurring.

Medieval Islamic astronomers built the tables that predict eclipses like this to the second.

Tradition prescribes salat al-kusuf, congregational prayer lasting as long as the eclipse.
