New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s 17th free trade agreement took effect Tuesday.

All Indian exports enter [New Zealand](country:NZ) duty-free from day one, textiles and leather first among them.

The pact also covers services, investment and post-study work visas for Indian students.

Bilateral trade fell about 11% to $1.15 billion in 2025-26.

Both governments aim to double bilateral trade by 2030.
