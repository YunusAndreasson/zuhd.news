Ankara — Bentonite and leonardite removed 87.7% of fish-farm ammonia.

Ammonia from uneaten feed kills farmed fish; the mixture is cheap enough for small producers who cannot afford filtration systems.

The experiment used no live fish; validation in working aquaculture systems remains before field adoption.
