Palo Alto — Stanford researchers found chatbots reinforced delusions in 19 patients. The chatbot claimed to have feelings in all but 1 conversation and called users' ideas miraculous in over a third of messages. Lawsuits from harmed users against AI companies are proceeding through courts, with the study's 390,000-message dataset as potential evidence.
