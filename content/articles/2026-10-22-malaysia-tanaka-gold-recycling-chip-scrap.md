Tokyo — A [Malaysia](country:MY) plant will recover gold from chip-factory scrap.

Semiconductor makers would draw gold and other precious metals from recycled material.

[Japan](country:JP)'s Tanaka Precious Metal Group and Malaysian recycler MEP will process scrap from semiconductor and electronic-component production.

The plant, announced this month, targets an April 2029 start of operations.
