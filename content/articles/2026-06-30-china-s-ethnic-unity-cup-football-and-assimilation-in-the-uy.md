Urumqi — A football tournament doubles as a propaganda set.

[China](country:CN)'s government brands the matches "ethnic unity," using sport to launder mass detention and assimilation dismantling Uyghur identity across Xinjiang.

Analysts say the spectacle recasts detainees as grateful participants, masking abuses the tournament was never meant to resolve.
