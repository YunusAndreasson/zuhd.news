Panama faces international arbitration over a revoked gas plant licence. Singapore's Sinolam International filed the claim after the government cancelled its 441-megawatt plant permit in 2024, using the Panama-Singapore Free Trade Agreement to bypass Panama's domestic courts. Panama already faces a separate port arbitration from CK Hutchison, making this its second active international investment claim.
