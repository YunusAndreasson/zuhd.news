Berlin — A new model reports 99.78% German accuracy.

Emissions forecasts like this shape carbon budgets and national climate targets.

Its "interpretable" label comes from attribution scores on [Germany](country:DE)'s data, describing what moved output, not what caused emissions to fall.

The authors acknowledge accuracy collapses across structural breaks — the exact moments forecasts matter most.
