Binzhou — [China](country:CN)'s 19-seat electric airliner flew 16 minutes 38 seconds.

AutoFlight's Matrix, another Chinese electric aircraft, carries 10 passengers.

Electric ducted fans drive the 10-tonne aircraft, developed with battery maker CATL, over a designed range of 400 kilometres.

The flight covered 60.4 kilometres, state broadcaster CCTV said, against that 400-kilometre design range.

The Civil Aviation Administration has accepted the Commercial Aircraft Corporation of China's type certification application, the step before regulatory approval.
