San Francisco — 7 million 23andMe genetic profiles were stolen and posted to the dark web.

The company went bankrupt before meaningful redress; a court approved $7-per-person settlement for the 2023 breach.

Unlike passwords, genetic data cannot be reset, leaving victims with no further recourse.
