Pyongyang — Kim's aide Jo Yong-won now chairs North Korea's parliament. North Korea's parliament always convenes after a party congress to legislate its decisions into law, keeping both bodies under Kim's inner circle. The session will enact February's congress decisions into law; Kim has not commented publicly on North Korea's position toward the Iran war.
