Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) says [US](country:US) jets struck its southern coast.

Its General Staff vowed "crushing" revenge — a claim only Tehran confirms.

The reported strikes hit Chabahar and Konarak, in Sistan-Baluchestan and Hormozgan provinces, the General Staff said.

Revolutionary Guards spokesman Hossein Mohebbi says "severe punishment" awaits, without saying when or how.
