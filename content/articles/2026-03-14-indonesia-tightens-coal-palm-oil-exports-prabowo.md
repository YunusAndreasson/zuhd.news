Jakarta ordered coal firms to supply Indonesia before exporting. Indonesia, the world's largest palm oil exporter and a top coal supplier, faces surging demand since Hormuz closed. Energy Minister Bahlil Lahadalia is preparing a decree formalizing domestic-first rules, threatening supply chains from India to Japan.
