Dhaka — Bangladeshi students are back in the streets.

The same Gen Z cohort that toppled the government in 2024 is mobilizing again over grading disputes in Higher Secondary Certificate exams.

Interim authorities have not said whether they will revise the Higher Secondary Certificate results.
