PM Anwar Ibrahim warned hostile actors are targeting Malaysia's unusual stability. Malaysia, which imports Middle East oil, called its National Security Council to tighten borders as regional currencies weaken around it. Anwar told police to arrest without hesitation if needed, with security forces on daily monitoring until the conflict eases.
