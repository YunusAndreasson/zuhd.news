Bishkek — Reporters found an Ilan Shor-linked fund bought Kolbaev's seized resort.

President Sadyr Japarov of [Kyrgyzstan](country:KG) had named Alexei Shirshov, a Maxim Bakiyev associate, as the buyer.

Bars, confiscated from slain crime boss Kamchy Kolbaev, sold at twice its price in Japarov's account, which investigative reporters call untrue.

A Bishkek court sentenced Baktybek Moldaliev to 3 years on Wednesday for sharing Kloop's reporting, resting on one linguist's opinion alone.
