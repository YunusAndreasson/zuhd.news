Borrowing money in Nigeria now costs 26.5% a year. The Central Bank of Nigeria cut from 27%, calling the half-point reduction a response to inflation falling to 15.1% in January. Banks must still hold 45% of deposits in reserve, leaving businesses and consumers competing for a shrinking pool of credit.
