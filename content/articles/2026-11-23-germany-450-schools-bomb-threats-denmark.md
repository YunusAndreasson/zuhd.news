Freiburg — More than 450 schools received identical emailed bomb threats.

Police say the threats are not credible, but a few schools sent pupils home.

The emails carry identical wording, and police in [Denmark](country:DK) are investigating similar threats against several schools.

No one has identified the sender, or shown the earlier hoaxes in [Italy](country:IT) and [Britain](country:GB) are linked.
