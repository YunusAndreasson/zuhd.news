Tegucigalpa — More than 6,000 patients wait for surgery at [Honduras](country:HN)'s largest public hospital.

Doctors' 49-day assemblies left more than 1,000 elective operations undone, hospital figures show.

A 500-million-lempira trust pays private hospitals up to 110,000 lempiras an operation and had funded 405 by July.

Critics say the trust's spending lacks transparency.
