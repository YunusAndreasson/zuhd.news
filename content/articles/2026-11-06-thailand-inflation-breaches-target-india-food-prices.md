Bangkok — Thai inflation hit 3.9%, above the central bank's ceiling.

It is the first breach of [Thailand](country:TH)'s target in over 3 years.

Higher oil prices and flood-related food costs pushed the annual rate up from 2.82% in September.

Brent crude fell 14.4% in the week to 5 November, to $98.29 a barrel.

A Reuters poll of economists forecasts [India](country:IN)'s October inflation at 5.93%, due 12 November.
