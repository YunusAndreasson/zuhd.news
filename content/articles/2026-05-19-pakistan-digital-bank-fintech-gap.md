Karachi — Half of [Pakistan](country:PK)'s 240 million adults remain unbanked.

Raast processes 88% of retail transactions, the State Bank of Pakistan said, but formal credit reaches just 2.4% of adults.

Bangladesh and Indonesia have pulled ahead on digital credit while Pakistan's 5 licensed digital banks remain subscale.
