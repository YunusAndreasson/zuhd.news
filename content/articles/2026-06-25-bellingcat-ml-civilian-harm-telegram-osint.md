Amsterdam — Bellingcat ranked 54,000 Telegram posts by civilian-harm likelihood.

Bellingcat built the model on 2,500 verified incidents from [Russia](country:RU)'s invasion, cutting search time so researchers can verify rather than hunt.

Bellingcat is releasing the code for others documenting conflicts in [Sudan](country:SD) and the Middle East.
