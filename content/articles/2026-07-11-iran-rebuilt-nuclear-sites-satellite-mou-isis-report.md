Tehran — Satellite images show repairs at bombed nuclear sites.

A memorandum between the [US](country:US) and [Iran](country:IR) required Tehran to freeze its nuclear program; repairs began while it was still in force.

Washington and Tehran have not said whether the memorandum still stands.
