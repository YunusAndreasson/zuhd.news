962 measles cases have hit South Carolina since October. Outbreaks are active in 16 US states as kindergarten vaccination rates fall below the 95% threshold needed to prevent spread. Epidemiologists warn declining immunisation could revive mumps, hepatitis B, and other diseases held in check for decades.
