San Francisco — SpaceX agreed $60bn all-stock deal for Cursor.

Exercised days after SpaceX's IPO, the deal gives Musk an AI code editor and resolves Cursor's compute bottleneck via xAI.

The deal closes Q3 2026, SpaceX said; Cursor's revenue grew 100-fold in under 2 years.
