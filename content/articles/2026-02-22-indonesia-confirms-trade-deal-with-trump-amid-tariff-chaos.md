Indonesia sealed a $38.4 billion deal with the US. President Prabowo agreed to remove tariffs on 99% of US goods in exchange for exemptions on 1,819 Indonesian export lines at a 19% reciprocal rate. Other Southeast Asian nations face Trump's tariffs with no bilateral deals of their own.
