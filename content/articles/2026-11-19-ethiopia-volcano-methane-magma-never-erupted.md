Bristol — [Ethiopia](country:ET)'s Fentale volcano released 38 kilotonnes of methane without erupting.

It is the first confirmed methane outburst of this size from a volcano.

A sheet of magma forced through the crust and broke an impermeable cap holding methane in hydrocarbon-rich sediments.

"The observation of a methane outburst from a volcano was a complete surprise," said Dr. Edna Dualeh, lead author.

Researchers will explore whether methane from continental rifting drove rapid warming in Earth's past.
