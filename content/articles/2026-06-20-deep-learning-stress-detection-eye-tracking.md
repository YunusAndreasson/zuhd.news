Erlangen — Eye trackers detect stress in real-world conditions.

Long short-term memory models classified stress from raw gaze signals without lab calibration, the study found — the barrier that blocked consumer deployment.

Who receives the stress data — insurer, employer, advertiser — has no regulatory answer in any jurisdiction yet.
