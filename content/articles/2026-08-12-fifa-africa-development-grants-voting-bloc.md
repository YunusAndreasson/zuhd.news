Rabat — 54 African federations hold FIFA's largest voting bloc.

Their loyalty to president Gianni Infantino tracks the money that keeps national teams running.

Sponsorship and broadcast revenue rarely cover league costs, so federations depend on FIFA's fixed annual grants.

Europe, Asia and North America's confederations have condemned Infantino, but Africa's has not.
