Jakarta — Myanmar's summit return remains far off, envoy said.

Envoy Ma. Theresa Lazaro said the bloc wants more progress on the 2021 Five-Point Consensus, ASEAN's roadmap to end [Myanmar](country:MM)'s civil war.

The junta stays barred from summits until it shows credible steps toward the plan.
