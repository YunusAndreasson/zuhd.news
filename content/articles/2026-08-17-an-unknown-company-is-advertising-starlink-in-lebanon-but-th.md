Beirut — A firm registered a month ago is pre-selling Starlink in [Lebanon](country:LB).

Customers paid pre-orders to a company whose telecom license nobody can confirm.

The unlicensed firm, registered by one owner, vanished from public view soon after advertising Starlink plans.

Lebanon's telecom ministry has offered no roadmap for when Starlink will actually launch.
