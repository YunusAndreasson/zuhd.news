United Kingdom — Rockstar fired 31 unionising [UK](country:GB) staff before Grand Theft Auto VI.

The Independent Workers of Great Britain called it the games industry's most unscrupulous union busting.

Crunch, weeks of extra hours before release, is common practice, researcher Jonas Ferdinand says, and often neither capped nor paid.

Rockstar denies union busting, saying the fired workers shared confidential information on Discord.

Employees at Rockstar's UK sites vote on formal union recognition as the game releases on 19 November.
