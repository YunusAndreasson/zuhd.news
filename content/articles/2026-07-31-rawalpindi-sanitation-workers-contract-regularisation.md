Rawalpindi — Maryam Nawaz's Rs10,000 Eid bonus went unpaid.

More than 300 sanitation workers in [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Punjab struck, saying years on renewable contracts left them without pensions or job security.

Regularisation power was delegated to the corporation in 2021; the union now threatens an indefinite strike.
