New York — A quarter of these loans lasted 39 days.

Elsewhere in lending, a single price tick can wipe a borrower out instantly.

704 Curve loans sold collateral gradually across a price band, reversing the sale if prices recovered.

Fees and rebalancing losses accrue throughout, and a deep enough drop still forces liquidation.
