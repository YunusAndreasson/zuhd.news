Manang — An avalanche killed 2 Nepali guides fixing a mountain route.

About 10 more local guides are missing, carrying the risk of a foreign-client climbing industry.

Guides were sent ahead to fix ropes and prepare the camp before foreign climbers arrived at the 7,126-metre Himlung Himal peak.

"The chance of survival remains high because of fresh snow," said Bibhuti Chand Thakur, head of a Nepali trekking company.

Helicopters and rescue teams remain grounded by weather, with no confirmed count of survivors yet.
