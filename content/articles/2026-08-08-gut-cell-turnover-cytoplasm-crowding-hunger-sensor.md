Kobe — Fly gut cells renew faster when starved.

Scarce nutrients mean fewer proteins, so the cytoplasm turns watery — a physical crowding change cells appear to read, without any hormone receptor.

The same crowding physics is now being probed in aging and in how the gut lining resists cancer.
