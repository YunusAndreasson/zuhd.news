Washington — 453 Boeing jets may have seats installed wrong.

Loose seats could block the aisle in an emergency, the [US](country:US) aviation regulator said, proposing inspections rather than ordering them.

Nearly 2,300 737 MAX jets fly worldwide; other regulators often follow such orders with a lag.
