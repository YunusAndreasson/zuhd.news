Washington — The bill missed its pre-recess vote window.

The bill decides whether a token is a market security or a tradable commodity, and whether stablecoins may pay interest like deposits.

Dollar stablecoins serve as savings tools in [Nigeria](country:NG), [Turkey](country:TR) and [Pakistan](country:PK); a September vote would set their rules.
