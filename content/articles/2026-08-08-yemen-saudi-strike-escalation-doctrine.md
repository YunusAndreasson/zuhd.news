Sanaa — [Yemen](country:YE) said its forces struck a Saudi military site.

Spokesman Yahya Saree said Yemen would answer any Saudi mobilization with "escalation for escalation," citing Riyadh's years-long blockade of Yemen.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA) has not confirmed the strike, and no independent assessment of the claim exists yet.
