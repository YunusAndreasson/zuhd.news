Astana — Two off-topic questions now cost reporters parliament access for 6 months.

About 40 accredited journalists asked [Kazakhstan](country:KZ)'s parliament chair to rewrite the rules.

Since late August, deputies speak to reporters only in the press center, on the Kurultai's agenda, a deputy chief of staff said.

The appeal now sits with chair Aibek Dadebay, who headed Tokayev's presidential administration until 5 May.
