The US merchandise trade deficit widened to $1.24 trillion in 2025. Companies front-loaded imports of chips and tech goods before tariffs took effect, driving the goods gap to a record even as the deficit with China fell 32%. The result undercuts the central rationale of the highest US tariff regime in 8 decades.
