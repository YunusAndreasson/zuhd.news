Tokyo — Japan imports 90% of its crude oil through Hormuz. Tokyo is considering sending minesweepers after a ceasefire, framing the mission as non-combat to sidestep its pacifist constitution. Foreign Minister Motegi made no commitment, leaving any deployment dependent on a ceasefire Japan cannot compel.
