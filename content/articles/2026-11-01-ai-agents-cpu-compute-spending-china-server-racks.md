Beijing — Server processor shipments will rise 31% in 2026, TrendForce forecasts.

AI agents are shifting data-centre budgets from accelerator cards alone toward whole racks.

Intel's chief executive says agents push the processor-to-graphics-chip ratio from 1:8 toward 1:1, as processors handle planning and tool calls.

Chinese tech outlet TMTPost cautions the processor boom remains expectation, not yet earnings.

TrendForce expects 2027 shipments to rise over 40%, with x86's dominance possibly changing.
