Washington — The US ended in-country green-card adjustment for employer-sponsored immigrants.

"Adjustment of status" lets applicants stay in the [US](country:US) while awaiting a green card; new immigration agency guidance eliminates it for most.

Homeland Security called it a restatement; lawyers warn of new evidence burdens or forced departure.
