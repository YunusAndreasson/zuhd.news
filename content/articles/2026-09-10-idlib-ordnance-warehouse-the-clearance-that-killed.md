Sarmada — A weapons-depot blast killed at least four.

Syrian state television put the toll as high as 14, with 11 hurt.

Collection points gather unexploded shells and cluster munitions from fields, sitting unlicensed in towns after 14 years of war.

No authority has named who operated the depot or why it sat near homes.
