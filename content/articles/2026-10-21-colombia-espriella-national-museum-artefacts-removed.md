Bogotá — Dozens of artworks left [Colombia](country:CO)'s National Museum on Friday.

They covered LGBTQ+ history, feminism, student movements and the armed conflict.

President Abelardo de la Espriella announced a fight against what he calls wokism the same day and vowed priority for Catholic symbols.

Protesters and arts faculties at Universidad de los Andes and Javeriana call it political censorship.

Judge César Enrique Osorio on Tuesday barred destroying, altering or moving the works out of the museum.
