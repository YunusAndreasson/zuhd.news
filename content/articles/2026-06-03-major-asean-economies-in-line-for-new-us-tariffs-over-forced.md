Washington — [US](country:US) recommended forced-labor tariffs on 60 partners.

Trade Representative Jamieson Greer proposed 10 to 12.5 percent levies; major Southeast Asian economies are among the 60 targeted trading partners.

The Trade Representative's recommendation moves to interagency review before taking effect; no timeline was given.
