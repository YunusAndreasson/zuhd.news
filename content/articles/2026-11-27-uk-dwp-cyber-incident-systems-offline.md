London — Millions of [UK](country:GB) claimants lost online access to benefits and pensions services.

The Department for Work and Pensions handles benefits and pensions for tens of millions of people.

It took digital services down as a precaution after detecting a cyber incident, and says payments will go out as normal.

Downing Street has not ruled out a hack but says there is currently no evidence personal data was lost.

An investigation is under way, and no one has said when services return.
