4 astronauts docked at the International Space Station on Friday. The SpaceX Dragon capsule carried crew from NASA, the European Space Agency, and Roscosmos after 34 hours in transit, restoring the station to 7 people after a month at minimum staffing. The team begins an 8-month science mission as NASA prepares to decommission the station around 2030.
