Wangaratta — A car hit MP Helen Haines outside her office.

Police recorded over 1,000 threats against Australian politicians in 2025-26, Home Affairs Minister Tony Burke said.

Police arrested a 43-year-old driver at the scene and seized a knife from his Toyota Land Cruiser.

Police are still investigating whether the act was intentional, and Prime Minister Anthony Albanese says it is too early to establish a motive.

[Australia](country:AU)'s counter-terrorism taskforce is investigating, and police expect to interview the driver.
