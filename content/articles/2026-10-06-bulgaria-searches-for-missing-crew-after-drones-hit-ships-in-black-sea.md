Varna — Drones sank the Togo-flagged Alfa Watan, leaving its crew missing.

Prime Minister Rumen Radev said it was the first reported attack on shipping inside [Bulgaria](country:BG)'s economic zone.

Sea and air drones hit both ships off Byala, and 18 rescued Able crew included 7 Indians.

Bulgaria has blamed no one, though Ukraine blamed Russia for Monday's sinking off Romania.

Authorities have not said how many people were aboard the Alfa Watan, or what it carried.
