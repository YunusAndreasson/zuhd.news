Muzaffarabad — Protesters call the vote a sham.

The unrest tests Pakistan's claim to speak for Kashmiri self-determination.

Candidates must swear allegiance to Kashmir's accession to [Pakistan](country:PK) before standing; an Islamabad council controls the territory's finances.

Islamabad has yet to say whether it will ease the crackdown or address protesters' grievances.
