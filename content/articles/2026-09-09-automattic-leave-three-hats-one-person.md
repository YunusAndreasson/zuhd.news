San Francisco — WordPress co-founder Matt Mullenweg is on leave.

He alone controls WordPress's company, its foundation, and its update-distribution site.

The board voted Mark Davies interim CEO while Mullenweg is away.

Automattic has not said whether Mullenweg keeps control of wordpress.org or the trademark.
