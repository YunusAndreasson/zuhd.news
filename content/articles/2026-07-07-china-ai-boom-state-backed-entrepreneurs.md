Beijing — "Neijuan," or involution, entered everyday Chinese speech in 2020.

Borrowed from anthropologist Clifford Geertz, the term describes intensifying competition without real gain — now the backdrop [China](country:CN)'s AI workers are building around.

Cheap AI tools are letting state-backed workers route around the capital constraints that fuel that grind.
