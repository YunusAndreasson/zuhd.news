Johannesburg — Nokuthula Mguni died after the hospital demanded upfront payment.

[South Africa](country:ZA) law bars upfront payment for emergency care, but Charlotte Maxeke staff told the [Zimbabwe](country:ZW) permit holder all foreigners must pay first.

The Gauteng health department did not respond to comment requests; Mguni's daughters carried her home to Zimbabwe.
