Jaipur — Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw pledged a rebuilt rail ticketing site after a student's captcha plea.

[India](country:IN)'s state rail booking monopoly serves millions daily; persistent captcha failures, payment crashes and bot-driven seat theft forced the rebuild.

The new platform must handle last-minute booking surges; launch is set for July 15.
