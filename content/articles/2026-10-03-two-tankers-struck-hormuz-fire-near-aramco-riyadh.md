Riyadh — UKMTO says 2 tankers were hit in Hormuz.

A witness told Reuters of fire near an Aramco facility, and no one has claimed either attack.

Hormuz traffic averaged 3.1 ships a day as of 27 September, against a normal 5.7.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA) said last week it intercepted six Houthi missiles near Yanbu and reported no damage to Aramco.

Polymarket traders give Hormuz traffic a 21% chance of normalising by 31 December, down 7 points in a week.
