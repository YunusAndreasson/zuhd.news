Mashhad — Iran's top steelmaker offered investment in Afghan mines.

Both states are cut off from Western finance; the summit linked [Iran](country:IR)'s industrial base to [Afghanistan](country:AF)'s minerals via proposed rail corridors.

No deal was signed; both governments described the rail-and-mining framework as the foundation of future bilateral ties.
