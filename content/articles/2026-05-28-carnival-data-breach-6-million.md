Miami — 1 compromised employee account exposed 6 million Carnival customers' data.

The attacker gained access in April, then copied personal data before the company detected the intrusion by month's end, Carnival said.

Cruise lines retain passport and payment records for years; [US](country:US) disclosure arrived over a month after the April breach.
