New York — Bitcoin crash insurance cost traders $685 million last month. Bitcoin's put/call ratio peaked at 0.84, the highest since China's 2021 mining ban, as the 30-day price fell 19%. US asset manager VanEck found that similar fear readings in the past 6 years preceded average Bitcoin gains of 13% over 90 days.
