Cairo — Ahmed Douma is back in prison for describing prison. [Egypt](country:EG) charged him with 'false news' for a personal essay about his post-release life, 3 years after completing a decade-long sentence. A Badr court rejected his appeal Thursday; defense lawyers say the 2025 criminal code's detention conditions don't apply to his case.
