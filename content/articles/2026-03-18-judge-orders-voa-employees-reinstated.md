A judge ordered 1,042 Voice of America employees reinstated. Kari Lake, Trump's appointee to head the US Agency for Global Media, cut VOA's staff last year in violation of a firewall law barring White House editorial control. The Trump administration's appeal, if successful, would silence broadcasts to audiences in 49 languages.
