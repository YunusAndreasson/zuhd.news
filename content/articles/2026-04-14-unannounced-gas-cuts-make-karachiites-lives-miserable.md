Karachi — Gas vanished from household kitchens without warning for 2 weeks. SSGC guarantees supply until 10:30pm but quietly diverted gas to power plants and factories, leaving families queuing at restaurants during meal hours. PTI and Muttahida demand accountability; SSGC has set no restoration timeline.
