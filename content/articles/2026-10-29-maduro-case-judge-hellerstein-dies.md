New York — Judge Alvin Hellerstein, 92, who handled Maduro's case, has died.

[Venezuela](country:VE)'s deposed president now needs a new judge on his drug trafficking charges.

Hellerstein handled the case for over a decade, including the conviction of Venezuela's former intelligence chief, Hugo Carvajal.

Questions had been raised about whether a 92-year-old should preside over a prolonged trial.

[US](country:US) forces seized Maduro in January, and his trial now waits on a successor.
