Burao — Somaliland's dryland vegetable farmers apply pesticides with no residue testing or safety oversight.

A Nature study found 74% of consumers buy from informal markets where withdrawal periods go unobserved.

86% lacked food safety information; no regional residue standard exists for Somaliland vegetables.
