Indonesia's president prayed Eid with flood survivors in Aceh. Floods stranded thousands of Aceh Tamiang residents in shelters through Eid, Indonesia's biggest family homecoming. Displaced families face weeks more in emergency housing; Aceh Tamiang's regional government must now secure funding and begin reconstruction.
