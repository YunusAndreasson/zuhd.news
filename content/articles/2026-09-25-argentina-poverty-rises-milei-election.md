Buenos Aires — Poverty jumped 4.1 points to 32.3% in six months.

Household income per person rose 11.5% while the poverty basket's cost rose nearly 20%, the statistics agency INDEC said.

President Javier Milei says he lifted 14 million people out of poverty, measured from the 2024 peak of 52.9%.

Catholic University researchers forecast [Argentina](country:AR)'s rate could reach 35% by year-end.
