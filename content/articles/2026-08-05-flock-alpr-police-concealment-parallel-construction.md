Ottumwa — "DO NOT MENTION ALPR USAGE," a police policy reads.

Wapello County's Flock camera guidance tells officers to call the license-plate database "county resources" in reports, so defendants cannot challenge evidence they never learn exists.

The Federal Bureau of Investigation and Justice Department have circulated similar vagueness guidance to police departments elsewhere.
