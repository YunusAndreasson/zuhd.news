Tokyo — AI helps [China](country:CN)-linked operators write fluent Japanese disinformation.

The same barrier once protected Urdu, Bahasa, Farsi and Arabic speakers too.

Detection has relied on translation errors as a tell, a signal generative models now remove.

Platforms have not disclosed how many AI-generated posts they have taken down.
