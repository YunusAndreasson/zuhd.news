Bern — Swiss homes get 25 Gbit fiber; US homes often get 1.

[Switzerland](country:CH) treats fiber as a natural monopoly, mandating open access so rival ISPs share one line, unlike [US](country:US) networks where one firm owns both.

Blogger Stefan Schueller argues the gap persists until regulators separate line ownership from retail service.
