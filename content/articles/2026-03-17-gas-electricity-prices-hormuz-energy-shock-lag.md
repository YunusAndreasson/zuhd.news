UK energy bills fall 7% on April 1. Ofgem, the UK energy regulator, calculates the price cap using wholesale prices locked in before the Iran war, so the Hormuz shock has not yet reached consumer bills. Ofgem announces the next cap on May 27 — if oil stays above $106, bills will spike in Q3 2026.
