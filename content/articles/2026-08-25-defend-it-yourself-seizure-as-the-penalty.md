Moscow — Undefended factories can now be seized by the state.

Owners who fail to buy their own air defense risk losing the facility outright.

[Russia](country:RU)'s military already lacks enough air defense to cover every refinery drones have hit, so Putin's decree shifts the cost onto private owners.

Officials have not specified what protection standard a facility must meet to keep it.
