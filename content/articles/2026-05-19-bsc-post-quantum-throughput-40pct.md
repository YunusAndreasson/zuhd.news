Singapore — BNB Chain's quantum hardening test dropped throughput 40%.

Lattice signatures bloated transactions from 110 bytes to 2.5 KB, BNB Chain's test found; the bottleneck was bandwidth, not verification speed.

Bitcoin and Ethereum are pursuing different upgrade paths; no chain has shipped a quantum-resistant mainnet.
