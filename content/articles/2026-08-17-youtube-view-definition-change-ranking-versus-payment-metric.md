Mountain View — A view now counts from the first frame.

Every channel's count jumps overnight, though creator pay stays tied to watch time.

Long videos once required 30 seconds of viewing, but now a single click counts, splitting ranking from pay.

The change takes effect August 24, aligning YouTube's count with TikTok and Instagram.
