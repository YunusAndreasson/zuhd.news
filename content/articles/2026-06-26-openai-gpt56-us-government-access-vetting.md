San Francisco — [US](country:US) government will vet who gets access to OpenAI's GPT-5.6.

Vetting commercial AI access follows the export-control model used for weapons — a first for consumer software, OpenAI said.

OpenAI has not published vetting criteria; developers outside US-aligned jurisdictions face being locked out of frontier capability.
