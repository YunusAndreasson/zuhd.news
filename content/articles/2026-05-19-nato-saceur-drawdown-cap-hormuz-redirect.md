Brussels — NATO capped [US](country:US) Europe withdrawals at 5,000.

The 5,000 covers 4,000 armored troops no longer deploying to [Poland](country:PL) and 1,000 missile specialists halted from [Germany](country:DE).

General Grynkewich, NATO's Supreme Allied Commander Europe, said he is "thinking about" a NATO role in the Strait of Hormuz; no formal planning has begun.
