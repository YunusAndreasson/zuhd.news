Crude oil surpassed $100 as Iran's Hormuz threats escalated. Iran's new supreme leader, Ayatollah Mojtaba Khamenei, declared the Strait of Hormuz would stay closed, reversing fuel cuts announced days earlier. Nigeria's fuel retailers cut petrol prices this week but may reverse those cuts if crude holds above $100.
