The Ethereum Foundation vowed to build nothing. The foundation published a 38-page mandate positioning itself as a neutral steward while rival chains compete aggressively for institutional money flowing into blockchain. Former Ethereum researcher Dankrad Feist warned that few core developers prioritize real-world adoption — a gap the new mandate does not address.
