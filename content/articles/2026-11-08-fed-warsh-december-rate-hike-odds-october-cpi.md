Washington — Traders see 78% odds of a December Fed hike.

Fed Chair Kevin Warsh would raise rates into a stock market 5.6% below its October high.

Inflation by the Fed's preferred measure ran 3.6% in September against a 2% target, and two dissenters wanted a hike in October.

The Bureau of Labor Statistics publishes October consumer prices Tuesday, and the Fed decides on 9 December.
