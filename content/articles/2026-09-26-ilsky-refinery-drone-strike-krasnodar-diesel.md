Ilsky — Drones reportedly hit a refinery producing 6.6 million tons yearly.

Brent crude stood at $114.90 on 22 September, up 23.9% in 30 days.

[Ukraine](country:UA) targets refineries to cut fuel supplies to [Russia](country:RU)'s military and the revenues financing its war.

Governor Veniamin Kondratiev reported fatalities and 1 seriously injured, and fires at 2 enterprises he did not name.

Whether Ilsky is one of the 2 burning sites awaits confirmation from Ukraine's General Staff or the governor.
