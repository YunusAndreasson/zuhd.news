Washington — [US](country:US) will not renew the US-Mexico-Canada Agreement before June 30.

Non-renewal triggers 10 annual reviews; Washington wants rules rewritten to require higher US content, disadvantaging [Canada](country:CA).

Trade Representative Greer said the US will push content rules at the first review in July.
