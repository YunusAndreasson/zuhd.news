Jerusalem — CIA report warns Israel risks civil war, Drop Site says.

Two US sources described the CIA Red Cell's August assessment to Drop Site News.

It names economic strain, veterans returning from war, ultra-Orthodox draft exemptions, settler violence and Netanyahu's fight to stay in power.

The CIA did not respond, and Drop Site says the assessment appears to omit [Israel](country:IL)'s Palestinian citizens.

Israelis vote on 27 October, the moment the report flags.
