Dhaka — Russia holds [Bangladesh](country:BD)'s only nuclear fuel supply.

The Rooppur plant ties [Bangladesh](country:BD) to [Russia](country:RU) financially, technically and institutionally — Moscow provided the loans, engineers and retains fuel control.

No alternative supplier, spare-parts chain or financing path exists while the reactor operates.
