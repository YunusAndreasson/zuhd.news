Washington — 149 scientists filed a climate report the [US](country:US) won't write.

The report is legally required, and its absence leaves planners without their standard climate guide.

The Trump administration cut funding and dismissed authors, so the American Geophysical Union and American Meteorological Society now publish papers instead.

Researchers plan a stream of papers on AI data-center demand and regional impacts instead of one full report.
