London — London handles 70% of gold trading, the Financial Conduct Authority says.

Britain's regulator may exempt gold tokens from fund rules meant to protect pooled investors.

The change hinges on whether a token means an allocated bar or just unsecured credit against the issuer.

The FCA has set no consultation timetable, and the bullion market's trade group hasn't stated a position.
