The US approved 12,000 bombs for Israel, bypassing Congress. Marco Rubio, Secretary of State, invoked emergency provisions letting the executive skip the 30-day notification window — the only legislative check on arms transfers. No member of Congress has announced a challenge, even as Congress retains the legal power to block the sale.
