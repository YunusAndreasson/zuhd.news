850,000 Lebanese are displaced, with no ceasefire date agreed. Lebanon banned Hezbollah's military activities this month — the group rejected the order and fought on while Beirut sought a negotiated exit. Lebanon demands a full ceasefire before talks begin, a condition Israel has not accepted as 826 people lie dead.
