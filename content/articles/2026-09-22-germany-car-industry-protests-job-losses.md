Wolfsburg — Volkswagen wrote down $11.5 billion, mostly on Porsche.

Factory closures loom across [Germany](country:DE)'s carmakers, threatening jobs that anchor the national economy.

European car sales fell from 18 million in 2019 to 13 million in 2025 amid Chinese EV competition and 25% US tariffs.

"We have absolutely no time to lose," Volkswagen brand head Thomas Schaefer told staff.

Mercedes-Benz says it may close one German assembly plant and one powertrain plant unless it cuts costs.
