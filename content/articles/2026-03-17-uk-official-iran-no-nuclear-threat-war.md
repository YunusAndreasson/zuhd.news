Iran's offers at Geneva surprised Britain's negotiators. UK envoy Powell attended the final pre-war talks, where Britain concluded Iran posed no imminent nuclear threat and further talks were set for March 2. Prime Minister Keir Starmer is under US pressure to send warships to the Hormuz escort operation but has not committed.
