London — A drone model spots cracks at 82.9% accuracy.

Road condition surveys usually need a specialist vehicle few authorities can afford.

Cracks are a few pixels wide and share texture with shadows, so false positives are the harder problem.

The model is untested on unpaved roads, the majority of South Asia's network.
