Colombia sent ministers after Venezuela canceled the border summit. Colombian President Gustavo Petro had restored ties with Venezuela in 2022 — the cancellation signals Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro is losing even close regional allies. Both governments set no new date, requiring preparatory talks in Caracas before any presidential meeting.
