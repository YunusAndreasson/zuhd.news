South Korea — Glucose metabolism makes hospital superbug Acinetobacter baumannii shed membrane vesicles.

Those vesicles carried the resistance enzyme Bla and the cell-clumping adhesin Ata.

Strains that oxidise glucose acidify their surroundings, which stresses the cell envelope and boosts vesicle release.

The abstract reports only laboratory experiments, with no animal or patient testing.
