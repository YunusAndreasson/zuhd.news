London — Euro zone output held at 51.7 as [Germany](country:DE) surged.

[France](country:FR) contracted at its sharpest in six months, the widest gap with Germany in six years.

German factories grew fastest in five years while French firms hesitate before the 2027 presidential election, S&P Global said.

Business confidence fell for a fourth month, to its lowest since May.

The European Central Bank decides on rates on 17 December.
