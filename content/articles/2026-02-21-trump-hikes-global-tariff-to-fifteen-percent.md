15% tariffs replaced the 10% rate overnight. US President Donald Trump invoked a 1974 trade law — after the Supreme Court voided his emergency tariffs 6-3 — to push global tariffs to their legal ceiling. The authority expires in 150 days, forcing a congressional vote by July.
