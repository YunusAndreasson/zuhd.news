One Giza grocery lost roughly 200 subsidized customers, none restored.

Families losing food subsidies face mounting price pressure with no working remedy in sight.

[Egypt](country:EG)'s new "social justice" system flags eligibility by data-matching, excluding many in error, with appeals unresolved since July 14.

Lawmakers are pressing for a swift decision and compensation for the wrongful suspensions.
