Berlin — [Germany](country:DE)'s federal police reportedly track phones through advertising data.

No legal basis for the programme is clear, and the Interior Ministry refuses to say.

Penlink's Webloc follows selected phones in near real time using location data tracking firms collect, supposedly only for advertising.

The ministry says disclosing software use would reveal police capabilities and weaken investigations.

The federal police and the Federal Criminal Police Office have not answered whether either uses Webloc.
