Islamabad — Petrol jumped to Rs331.52 a litre overnight.

Renewed fighting in the Persian Gulf pushed the increase straight through to [Pakistan](country:PK)'s pumps, now under daily rather than fortnightly price-setting.

Fuel dealers are weighing a protest against the new daily-pricing rule this week.
