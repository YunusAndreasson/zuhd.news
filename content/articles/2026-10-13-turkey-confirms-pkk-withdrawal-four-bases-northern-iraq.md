Ankara — [Turkey](country:TR)'s intelligence service confirms Kurdish militants left 4 [Iraq](country:IQ) bases.

August's amnesty law requires Turkish intelligence to verify disarmament before any militant returns home.

The militants left the Hebat, Yajk, Sabri and Sason hideouts in Metina on 8 October, security sources said.

The group says Ankara is pursuing the process with too little sincerity and speed.

Abdullah Ocalan joins a disarmament sub-committee, but an Erdogan adviser says he will not be freed.
