Beijing — Chinese Premier Li Qiang told executives protectionism "solves nothing." Hours earlier, Beijing released a five-year plan elevating commodity stockpiling to national security, contradicting Li's open-markets message. Beijing is now offering executives from Hormuz-disrupted economies at the China Development Forum a path to realign supply chains away from the US.
