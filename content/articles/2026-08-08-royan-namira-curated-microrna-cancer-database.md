Tehran — Researchers manually checked 9,983 cancer studies.

Most microRNA-disease databases are text-mined and inherit inflated claims; each microRNA can silence many genes at once, making it a biomarker candidate.

The database, built with [Germany](country:DE)'s University of Duisburg-Essen, shows research collaboration surviving sanctions when the output is data.
