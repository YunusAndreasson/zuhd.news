Muzaffarabad — 7 of 11 Kashmir seats went unpolled.

The assembly can seat a government before its most unrestful districts vote.

Kashmir's home secretary postponed Poonch and Sudhnoti's polls after clashes killed more than 50 on July 27.

Authorities have set no new poll date, and [Pakistan](country:PK)'s ruling party plans to govern regardless.
