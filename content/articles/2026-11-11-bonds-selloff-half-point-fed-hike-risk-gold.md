Sydney — Two-year Treasury yields jumped 18 basis points overnight.

Hot core inflation raised the risk of a half-point Federal Reserve hike in December.

Core prices rose 0.3% in October, the reading Governor Christopher Waller said would make him weigh a 50-basis-point hike.

Goldman's chief US economist David Mericle expects 25 basis points, since most Fed voters would hesitate at 50.

The Federal Reserve decides on 9 December, after October producer prices print on Friday.
