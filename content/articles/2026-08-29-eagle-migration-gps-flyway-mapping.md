Konstanz — GPS tags now map exact eagle migration routes.

Precisely mapped flyways let planners site wind farms and power lines without guessing.

Since 1899, researchers tracked migration only through recaptured leg rings, leaving the journey between points unknown.

Many species' precise flyways remain undescribed despite the new tracking technology.
