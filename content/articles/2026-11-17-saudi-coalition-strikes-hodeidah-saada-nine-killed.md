Hodeidah — Saudi-led strikes killed at least 9 people in Yemen, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

One strike hit a foam-rubber factory in northern Hodeidah, killing 7 and wounding 9, the Houthi health ministry said.

Another strike in Saada's Kitaf district killed 2 and wounded 4, according to the Houthi-run news agency.

Polymarket traders give a Saudi-Houthi ceasefire by 31 December a 13% chance, down 17 points in a week.
