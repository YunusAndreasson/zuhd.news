Canberra — [Australia](country:AU) signed its largest-ever defense export deal.

[Canada](country:CA) will pay A$2.5 billion for the Jindalee radar, which bounces signals off the ionosphere to see 3,000km beyond line-of-sight.

Australia is also in talks with [Germany](country:DE) over its Ghost Bat drone, extending the middle-power export push.
