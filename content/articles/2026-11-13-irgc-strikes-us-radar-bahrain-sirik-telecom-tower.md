Manama — [Iran](country:IR)'s Guards destroyed a US radar in [Bahrain](country:BH), state agency Mehr reported.

The Guards say it answered a [US](country:US) air attack on a telecommunications tower in Sirik.

Guards statement No. 22 demands an end to US aerial violations and what it calls oil smuggling in the Strait of Hormuz.

The Guards say any repeat brings a limitless response.
