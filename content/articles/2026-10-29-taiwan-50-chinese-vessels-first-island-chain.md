Taipei — Taiwan's map shows 53 Chinese ships, 34 of them warships.

They sit off [Japan](country:JP), Taiwan and the [Philippines](country:PH) at once, security chief Joseph Wu said.

The carrier Liaoning's group sits east of the Philippines, with formations off Taiwan's east and southeast and three off southern Japan.

[China](country:CN)'s Defence Ministry said this month the Liaoning's drills are routine and not aimed at any country.

A Taiwanese security official says a Liaoning move north would signal pressure on Japan's defence posture.
