Islamabad — Virtual schools now face formal oversight rules.

The Private Educational Institutions Regulatory Authority set teacher-training, cybersecurity and curriculum standards to reach [Pakistan](country:PK)'s out-of-school children and students in remote areas.

Other provinces may adopt the framework as a model, the regulator's chairman said.
