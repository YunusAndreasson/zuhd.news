Kunming — China arrested an American Myanmar analyst on espionage charges.

Min Zin, a dual [US](country:US)-Myanmar citizen, disappeared in Kunming on June 3; [China](country:CN) cited national security and notified the US consulate in Guangzhou.

[Myanmar](country:MM)'s military leader visits China June 15–19, days after Beijing confirmed the arrest.
