Menlo Park — Meta launched a paid AI platform for businesses Monday.

Salesforce, ServiceNow and Snowflake all fell more than 4% within minutes of the announcement.

Meta gave its AI models away free for years, and now sells them to fund $130-145bn in 2026 capex.

Meta's own July earnings call never named Salesforce, ServiceNow or Snowflake as targets.

MongoDB holds an investor meeting Tuesday, its first since CEO Chirantan Desai's abrupt departure to Meta.
