Nairobi — Russian recruiters tricked 252 Kenyans into signing military contracts. Promised civilian work, the men signed Russian-language documents they could not read; Russia then deployed them to the front. Kenya has secured amnesty for returnees and Russia's pledge to halt recruitment, but 160 Kenyans remain at the front.
