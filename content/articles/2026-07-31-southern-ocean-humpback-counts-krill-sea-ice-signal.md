Perth — Humpback sightings fell 60% below normal.

Watchers in southwest [Australia](country:AU) counted far fewer whales in late June; scientists debate whether the animals shifted range or numbers.

Whether this is a range shift or a population decline hinges on krill and sea-ice data.
