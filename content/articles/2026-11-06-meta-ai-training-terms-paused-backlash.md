United States — Meta shelved terms claiming training rights over its customers' websites.

Business accounts would have had to let Meta use content from their domains to develop its products.

Meta moved the 30 October start to 20 November, after outcry from journalists, then paused it.

A Norwegian court granted a temporary injunction, and the UK Competition and Markets Authority will investigate.

Meta says it will update after consulting stakeholders, and existing terms stay in force.
