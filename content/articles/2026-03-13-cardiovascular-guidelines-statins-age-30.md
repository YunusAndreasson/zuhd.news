LDL above 160 now means statins at 30. The shift from 10-year to lifetime risk assessment means younger people with LDL cholesterol above 160 mg/dL get treatment decades earlier than previous thresholds allowed. Whether insurers will cover statin prescriptions for 30-year-olds remains for individual payers to decide.
