Abuja — [Nigeria](country:NG) moves to legalize state police forces.

Nigeria's single federal force covers 220 million people; state governors would control the new forces, with powers and accountability still undefined.

Chief of Staff Gbajabiamila said the constitutional amendment is imminent; enabling legislation follows.
