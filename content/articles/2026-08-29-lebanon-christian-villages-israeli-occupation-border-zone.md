Ain Ebel — Israeli soldiers turned back a Catholic aid convoy.

Thousands of residents depend on this aid for basic survival.

[Israel](country:IL) has razed dozens of villages in [Lebanon](country:LB)'s buffer zone, leaving only a few Christian towns standing.

The next round of Israel-Lebanon talks will decide whether access to the villages is restored.
