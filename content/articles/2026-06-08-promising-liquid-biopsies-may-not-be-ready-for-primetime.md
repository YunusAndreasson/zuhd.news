Washington — Physicians doubt cancer blood tests for routine use.

Multi-cancer detection tests identify cancer from a blood draw; physicians warn routine use in healthy individuals risks invasive follow-ups without proven survival benefit.

Whether early detection in low-risk populations reduces mortality remains unproven, they say.
