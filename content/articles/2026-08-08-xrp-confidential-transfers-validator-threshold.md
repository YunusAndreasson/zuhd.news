San Francisco — Validators must approve XRP Ledger's Confidential Transfers.

The amendment would encrypt balances and payment amounts, letting institutions settle real-world assets without publishing position sizes to rivals.

Activation needs 80% validator support sustained for two weeks on a $530 million ledger.
