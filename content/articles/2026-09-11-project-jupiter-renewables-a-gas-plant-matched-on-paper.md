Santa Teresa — Project Jupiter will still burn natural gas.

Oracle's 2-gigawatt renewable pledge won't cut a single hour of that combustion.

Analyst Michael Thomas calls the matching "synthetic" since the renewables won't power the site directly.

New Mexico's Supreme Court has paused Jupiter's air permit over two legal challenges.
