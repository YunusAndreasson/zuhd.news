New Delhi — Police demand a Rs 10,000 peace bond from a student leader.

She was detained at a march demanding Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar resign.

Police invoked Section 126 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, asking a magistrate to bind her over with one surety.

"Whose peace am I being asked to keep?" asked Neha Bora, president of the All India Students' Association.

She will not sign before the magistrate, a day after the High Court dismissed her challenge.
