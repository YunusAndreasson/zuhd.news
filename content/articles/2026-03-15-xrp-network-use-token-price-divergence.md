XRP transactions hit records while the price fell 62%. Ripple's stablecoin uses XRP as a 3-second payment bridge and immediately sells it, leaving each transaction with no lasting impact on token demand. Whether XRP's $84 billion market cap is sustainable without sustained token demand remains unresolved.
