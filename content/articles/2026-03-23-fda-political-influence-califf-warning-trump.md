Washington — Politics now drives US drug approvals, former FDA Commissioner Robert Califf warned. Because dozens of countries treat FDA decisions as their reference standard, political interference in Washington affects drug safety globally. Patients waiting for rare-disease gene therapies now depend on an FDA where politics, not science, governs approvals.
