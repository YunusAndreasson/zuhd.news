Kunshan — Kunshan once made a third of all global laptops. Foxconn built its first factory there in the 1990s and drew hundreds of thousands of migrant workers for three decades, but today's young workers are rejecting assembly-line jobs for higher wages elsewhere. Kunshan's government is recruiting AI and semiconductor firms, but retraining hundreds of thousands of assembly-line workers for chip design and software is a challenge no Chinese city has solved.
