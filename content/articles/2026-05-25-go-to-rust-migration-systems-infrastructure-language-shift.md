San Francisco — A Go-to-Rust guide drew 316 Hacker News votes Monday.

Teams move to Rust for memory safety and runtime predictability; Go's garbage collector creates pauses that latency-sensitive infrastructure cannot absorb.

JetBrains surveys show Go holding at 17-19% while Rust grows; the guide's author says migration runs one-directional.
