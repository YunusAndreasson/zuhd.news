London — A nickelate study narrows superconductivity theories, researchers find.

Different nickelate compounds all share 1 electronic spectroscopic signature — the common pattern constrains which high-temperature superconductivity theories can be correct.

Confirmation would focus materials engineering for power grids and quantum processors.
