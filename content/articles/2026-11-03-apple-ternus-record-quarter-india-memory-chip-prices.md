Cupertino — Apple expects gross margin of 45-46%, down from 47.9%.

It already raised iPhone 17 prices $100 in the [US](country:US) and Rs17,000 in [India](country:IN).

AI infrastructure demand is bidding up memory chips, and chief financial officer Kevan Parekh said costs keep rising into next year.

Revenue still hit a record $114.5 billion, up 12%, with iPhone revenue up 17% to $57.58 billion.

Apple will cap server-based Siri AI and photo editing, and is weighing higher iCloud+ prices.
