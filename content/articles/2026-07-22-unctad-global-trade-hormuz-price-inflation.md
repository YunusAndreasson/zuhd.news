Geneva — Global trade rose 12.5% to $13.7 trillion.

UNCTAD says a significant share reflects higher shipping and energy prices from Strait of Hormuz disruptions, not more goods moving.

Import-dependent economies keep paying those higher costs as the shipping disruptions continue unresolved.
