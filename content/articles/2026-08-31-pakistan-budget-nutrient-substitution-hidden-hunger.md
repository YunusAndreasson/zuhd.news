Karachi — Full stomachs can still mean failing nutrition.

Households squeezed by [Pakistan](country:PK)'s budget cut protein and micronutrients first, keeping cheap calories up.

That split hides malnutrition from calorie-based food-security counts even as hunger rises.

Humanitarian leaders and officials meet in Islamabad on September 2 to confront the gap.
