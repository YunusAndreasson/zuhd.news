Stockholm — 78,633 international peacekeepers deployed — the lowest since 2000.

A $2 billion UN shortfall forced 25% cuts; all 10 top troop-contributors are from the Global South, the Stockholm peace institute found.

The UN force in [Lebanon](country:LB) ends its mandate this year under [US](country:US) pressure; no new UN mission has been approved since 2014.
