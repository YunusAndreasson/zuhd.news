New Delhi — Penguin India withdrew from publishing Sonia Gandhi's memoir over cuts she refused.

It is the third such withdrawal, each removing a book on Hindu nationalism or communal violence from Indian shelves.

[India](country:IN)'s defamation and hurt-sentiment laws make the publisher, not the state, absorb the legal risk of a ban.

HarperCollins India will publish the memoir instead, on November 10.
