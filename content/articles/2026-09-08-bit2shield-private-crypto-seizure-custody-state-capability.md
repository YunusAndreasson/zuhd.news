Madrid — [Spain](country:ES)'s Bit2Me spun off crypto-seizure firm Bit2Shield.

European police lack the custody infrastructure to seize crypto on their own.

Bit2Shield traces and stores seized crypto wallets, while a separate MiCA-licensed Bit2Me arm converts them into euros.

Bit2Shield will now handle wallet seizures, forensic reports and court-ordered crypto sales.
