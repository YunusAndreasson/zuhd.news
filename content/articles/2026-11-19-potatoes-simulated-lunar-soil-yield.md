Corvallis — Potatoes completed their full life cycle in simulated lunar soil.

Aymeric Goyer of Oregon State University calls it a step toward food systems for long-term Moon exploration.

The plants matched Earth-grown yields once researchers added organic matter, and the potatoes were safe to eat.

Simulant is not real regolith, so no one has yet grown potatoes in actual lunar soil.
