Hong Kong — Hong Kong jailed vigil organizers for seven years.

The ruling criminalizes commemorating Tiananmen as subversion against the state.

Judges ruled that calling for an end to one-party rule alone met the security law [China](country:CN) imposed in 2020.

Organizer Lee Cheuk-yan's wife says he will appeal, as the disbanded Hong Kong Alliance faces a $191,000 fine.
