Rawalpindi — Oil tankers and grain trucks have been blocked from Islamabad and Rawalpindi since April 19. [Pakistan](country:PK)'s government imposed road closures for undisclosed security reasons, emptying grain markets and halting airport fuel. Punjab government must authorize resumption; traders warn shortages reach Kashmir within 2 days.
