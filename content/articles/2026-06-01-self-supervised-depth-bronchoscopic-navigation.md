London — A depth-aware AI pinpoints bronchoscopes inside branching airways.

Standard endoscope tracking drifts without depth data; DaAPNet learns depth self-supervised, cutting translation error 36% in phantom tests.

The method targets hybrid bronchoscopic navigation systems; human trial data have not been reported.
