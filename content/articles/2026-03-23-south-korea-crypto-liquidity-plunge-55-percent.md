Seoul — Korean crypto stablecoin balances crashed 55% in 8 months. When the won broke 1,500 per dollar for the first time since 2008, traders sold dollar stablecoins for domestic equities, aided by a government tax exemption on overseas assets reinvested domestically. Whether the reallocation is permanent will determine whether South Korean crypto exchanges survive the liquidity drought.
