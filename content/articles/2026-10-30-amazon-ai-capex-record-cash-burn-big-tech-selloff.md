United States — Amazon's 12-month cash burn hit a record $42.4 billion.

Amazon fell 7.3% in extended trading after revenue and earnings missed analyst estimates.

Amazon filled the gap with debt: long-term borrowing reached about $173 billion, up from $66 billion at the end of 2025.

"We can fund this plan," said chief executive Andy Jassy, as cloud revenue grew 39% to $46 billion.

Amazon expects about $220 billion in capital spending this year and says 2027 will be higher.
