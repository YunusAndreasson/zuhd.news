Brasília — Flavio Bolsonaro led Lula 49.45% to 41.97% in early returns.

Only 2.4% of votes were counted, and polls had Lula ahead in the first round.

Without more than 50% of valid votes, the top two candidates meet in a runoff on 25 October.

The same ballot fills all 513 lower house seats and two-thirds of the Senate.

Lula's allies warn the Trump administration may refuse to recognise a Lula win.
