Kyiv — 550,000 households lost power overnight.

Air raid alerts and fear of new strikes are slowing repair crews, the Energy Ministry said.

Zelensky said [Russia](country:RU) fired 16 ballistic missiles and over 200 drones, hitting 28 substations and other energy sites across 11 oblasts.

[Ukraine](country:UA) is short of Patriot interceptors, and Zelensky says US supply deliveries are delayed.
