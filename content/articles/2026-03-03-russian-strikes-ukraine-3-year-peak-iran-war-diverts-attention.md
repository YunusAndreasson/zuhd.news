Russia hit a Ukrainian passenger train; 8 died. February saw 288 Russian overnight missiles — more than double January's 135 and the highest monthly count in 3 years — while the Iran war consumed international attention. Ukraine Railways' CEO warned the strikes aim to sever entire regions from the national network.
