London — Canada Rocket Company will build a $30 million engine test facility.

[Canada](country:CA) is reassessing its reliance on US rockets as relations with Washington sour.

Ottawa's first Launch the North awards totalled $8.33 million CAD, split among 3 companies building light-lift rockets.

Canada Rocket Company skipped light lift for the 12.5-tonne R-2, which its chief executive says could launch by 2032.

The company expects to start testing its E-1 engine at the site in about 2 years.
