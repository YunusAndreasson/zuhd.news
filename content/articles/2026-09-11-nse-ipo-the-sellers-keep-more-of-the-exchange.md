Mumbai — Sellers cut the NSE's stake sale to 5.1%.

It signals sellers think [India](country:IN)'s largest exchange is worth more than planned.

The listing is entirely secondary shares, so NSE itself receives none of the sellers' proceeds.

Shares open for subscription September 17 and list on the BSE September 24.
