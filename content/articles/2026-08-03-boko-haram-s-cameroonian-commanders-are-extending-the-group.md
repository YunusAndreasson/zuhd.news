Maroua — Five Cameroonian commanders lead 1,000 fighters, researchers say.

Boko Haram promotes local qaids for their language and terrain knowledge, researchers found; these leaders drove 714 attacks in 2025.

[Cameroon](country:CM)'s disarmament program lacks incentives for these commanders to defect, researchers said.
