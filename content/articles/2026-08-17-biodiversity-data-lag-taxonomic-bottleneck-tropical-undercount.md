London — Conservation decisions run on years-old species data.

Funding gets allocated on evidence that systematically under-counts the world's richest habitats.

Researchers trace the worst delay to taxonomic identification, which depends on a shrinking pool of specialists, not sensors.

The Congo Basin, Borneo and Amazonia remain the least-observed regions the study names.
