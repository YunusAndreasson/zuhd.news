A Saudi complaint letter about the UAE reached Washington. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman accused the Emirates of arming Sudan's Rapid Support Forces and Yemeni separatists. Saudi Arabia bombed a UAE arms delivery in Yemen in December, deepening a rift between the Gulf allies.
