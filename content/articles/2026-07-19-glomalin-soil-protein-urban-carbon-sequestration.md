Nanchang — Cities strip a hidden soil-carbon glue.

Glomalin, a fungal protein that binds carbon in soil, declined faster than bulk soil carbon across 184 samples in urbanizing Nanchang.

Researchers now propose glomalin as a routine indicator for grading urban soils' carbon vulnerability.
