Moscow — Russia orders oil firms to rush fuel to its own regions.

Deputy Prime Minister Alexander Novak said panic buying pushed domestic demand up 20-30%, straining supply most in Irkutsk and Trans-Baikal.

Novak floated a months-long diesel export ban to replenish stocks while logistics realign.
