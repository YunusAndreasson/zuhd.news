Vilyuchinsk — Nuclear submarines now sit under nets.

The base is homeport to most of [Russia](country:RU)'s Borei-class ballistic-missile submarines.

The nets counter drone strikes the same way netting already protects targets inside [Ukraine](country:UA).

A similar setup appeared on Black Sea submarines in June, with anti-drone screens over their towers.
