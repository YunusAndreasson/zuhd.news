Maseru — [Lesotho](country:LS) signed a $6.2 billion energy deal.

A minister holds a stake in a likely local partner to the deal.

The hydropower-and-data-centre project rivals Lesotho's entire economy, leaving no arm's-length counterparty for a minister's holding.

Convalt Energy's feasibility study, due within a year, will set the project's financing terms.
