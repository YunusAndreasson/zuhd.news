London — The sodium-potassium ratio drives insulin resistance.

Type 2 diabetes is rising fastest in the Gulf, South Asia and Egypt.

An immune sensor called NLRP3 fires under high sodium, releasing a protein that blocks insulin signalling.

Researchers tested mice and 60 healthy adults, and supplementation risks anyone with impaired kidneys.
