Boston — Most patients in a pancreatic cancer trial responded.

Combining an epigenetic blocker with a cancer-mutation drug produced durable responses in most patients, company data show; survival benefit is unconfirmed.

Phase 3 trials have not begun; response rate and long-term survival are separate endpoints.
