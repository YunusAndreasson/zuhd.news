Washington — US Supreme Court blocked Alabama's scheduled nitrogen execution.

Federal courts found nitrogen hypoxia likely causes severe pre-death distress, violating the [US](country:US) Constitution's ban on cruel and unusual punishment.

3 justices dissented; the unsigned order leaves the constitutional question formally unresolved.
