Bogotá — Alluvial gold mining grew 4.5% to 118,410 hectares.

In [Colombia](country:CO), 61% of the growth fell on Afro-Colombian communities' land, a UN report found.

The report puts 75% of output as illegal, and Mines Minister Maria Nohemi Arboleda says nearly half the area also has coca.

Arboleda wants gold-buying countries to require traceability systems proving the metal is legally sourced.
