Taipei — Nvidia launched a PC chip to challenge Intel.

RTX Spark runs large AI models locally on Arm, made by TSMC with [Taiwan](country:TW)'s MediaTek — shifting inference off the cloud.

First devices target premium laptops this autumn; subsequent versions will expand to broader price ranges, Nvidia said.
