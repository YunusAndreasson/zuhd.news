The investigators quit. Members of a government-appointed committee examining unauthorized police use of spyware, including tools made by NSO Group, resigned and accused law enforcement of coordinating efforts to obstruct their work. In a letter to Justice Minister Yariv Levin, the departing members wrote they could no longer fulfill their mandate. Israel's High Court then proposed barring the panel from reviewing active criminal cases, including those involving Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu. The committee's collapse leaves no independent body investigating whether Israeli police deployed surveillance technology against citizens without legal authorization.
