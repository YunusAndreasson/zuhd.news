Bengaluru — [India](country:IN)'s eMudhra quits a line it says earns 1% of revenue.

eMudhra will replace affected customers' certificates free through partners during the transition.

eMudhra says its G1 RSA roots keep serving customers while it works with browser programs on the transition.

eMudhra says it revoked the affected authorities within 14 hours and issued no end-entity certificates from them.

eMudhra still owes browser root programs a full root-cause report.
