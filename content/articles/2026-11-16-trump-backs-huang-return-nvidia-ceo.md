Washington — 177,000 people signed a petition to reinstate Nvidia's Jensen Huang.

[US](country:US) President Donald Trump told reporters Monday he would welcome Huang's return.

Analysts estimate Nvidia could lose 25% to 30% of its value without him, and its stock is down 25.7% in a month.

The Federal Bureau of Investigation has not concluded Alice Huang spied for China, Attorney General Todd Blanche said last week.

Nvidia's board, with interim CEO Colette Kress in place, is still searching for a successor.
