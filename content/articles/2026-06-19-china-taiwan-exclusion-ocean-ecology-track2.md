Beijing — [China](country:CN) excluded [Taiwan](country:TW) from an ocean conservation forum.

The exclusion targeted a Track 2 maritime-ecology platform, an informal channel where Beijing can apply political pressure without formal exclusion rights.

Scientists warn the exclusion creates a template for politicizing other informal scientific cooperation tracks.
