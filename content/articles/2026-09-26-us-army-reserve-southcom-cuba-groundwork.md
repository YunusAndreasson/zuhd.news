Washington — The Army Reserve is checking whether it can supply Southern Command with military police and medics within 120 days.

[US](country:US) officials link the request to Cuba planning, though the internal message never names Cuba.

Six formations would fall under Southern Command, including a military police brigade and a theatre-level logistics command.

The document shows no deployment orders, and Southern Command declined to discuss planning.

Subordinate commands' replies were due 25 September, with units possibly needed in 90-120 days.
