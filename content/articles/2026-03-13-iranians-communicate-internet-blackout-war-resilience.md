Iran's government cut internet to 1% of capacity. Amsterdam-based Radio Zamaneh broadcasts shortwave Farsi news Iran cannot jam; VPN use has collapsed from 6 million to under 100,000 daily users. Amnesty International warned the blackout suppresses both dissent and documentation of the war itself.
