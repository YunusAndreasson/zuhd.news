New Delhi — A court jailed Tahir Hussain for life.

A former Aam Aadmi Party councillor, he was convicted of murdering officer Ankit Sharma in riots that killed 53 people, mostly Muslim.

Hussain's lawyers plan to appeal; a separate Unlawful Activities Prevention Act conspiracy case against student organisers remains pretrial.
