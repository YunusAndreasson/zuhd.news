A Russian tanker sank between Libya and Malta. Ukrainian naval drones sank the Arctic Metagaz, a sanctioned vessel ferrying Russian liquefied natural gas to China, extending Ukraine's strike range into the central Mediterranean. All 30 crew escaped by life raft, with neither Ukraine nor Russia officially acknowledging the strike.
