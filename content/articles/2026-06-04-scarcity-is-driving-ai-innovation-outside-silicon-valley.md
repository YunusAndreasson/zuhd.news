New York — AI development is shifting toward compute-scarce regions.

Developers without hyperscale cloud access are building AI for low-resource environments, treating GPU scarcity as an engineering condition rather than a barrier.

Several Global South deployments are live; no agreed benchmark for low-resource AI performance exists.
