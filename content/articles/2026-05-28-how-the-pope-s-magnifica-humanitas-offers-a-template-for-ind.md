Rome — Pope Leo XIV's encyclical Magnifica Humanitas rules technology is never neutral.

The document argues AI is a commercial product concentrating power, calling for oversight wherever it touches fundamental rights.

Once OpenAI, Anthropic and xAI go public, shareholder accountability campaigns could apply the encyclical's framework directly.
