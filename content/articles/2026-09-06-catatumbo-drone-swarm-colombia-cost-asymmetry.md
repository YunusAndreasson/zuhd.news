Ocaña — Drones killed three soldiers at an army post.

It marks [Colombia](country:CO)'s worst wave of violence in a decade.

The military blames the National Liberation Army for the 20-drone assault, part of its fight with rival dissidents for the coca-growing region.

President Abelardo de la Espriella has vowed intensified operations against the group's leadership.
