London — Bone-cell drug screens failed over unreliable stem-cell yields.

A macrophage colony-stimulating factor priming step during differentiation raised consistent osteoclast yield above 70% across 3 independent cell lines.

Primed cells showed dose-dependent responses to alendronate and zoledronate, drugs used clinically for osteoporosis.
