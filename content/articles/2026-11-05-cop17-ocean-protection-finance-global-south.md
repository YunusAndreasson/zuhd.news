Yerevan — Just 3.5% of the ocean is effectively protected, experts say.

Global South ministers blame rich countries for withholding promised biodiversity finance.

Donors pledged $20 billion a year by 2025 and gave $18.9 billion in 2024, the latest available data.

Governments have 4 years left to protect 30% of the ocean, and 10% is covered.
