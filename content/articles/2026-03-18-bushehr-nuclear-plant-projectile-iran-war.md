A projectile struck near Iran's Bushehr nuclear plant Tuesday. Iran's atomic energy authority reported no damage, though international law prohibits strikes on nuclear facilities. Who fired remains unknown — Gulf winds would carry any radiation release toward US-allied Gulf states.
