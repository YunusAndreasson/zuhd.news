Beijing — Pandemic lockdowns cut global fire detections 12.3%.

Forest fires fell 27.7% and grassland fires 8.4%, the study found.

A Cell Reports Sustainability study compared satellite fire detections in 2020 lockdown weeks with 2013-2019, adjusting for weather.

"The 12.3 percent is a net effect, not the share of fires caused by humans," said lead author Jianghao Wang.

Fires rose 31.4% in Colombia and 25.4% in India, which the authors infer, without field data, reflects weaker suppression.
