Abuja — Families now cook smaller portions to cope.

Cooking jollof cost 29,578 naira in June, up 14.6% in 11 months, SBM Intelligence found across 13 [Nigeria](country:NG) markets.

Lagos posted the steepest regional surge, 49.6%, as naira weakness raises import costs at its ports.
