6 African countries run space programs on Chinese-built infrastructure. From ground stations in Namibia to satellite factories in Egypt, Beijing funded and built the hardware African governments rely on for orbital operations. African partners must return to Beijing for every upgrade and repair, with no affordable alternative yet in place.
