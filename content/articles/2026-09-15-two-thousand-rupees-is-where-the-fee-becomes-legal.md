New Delhi — Unified Payments Interface fees start above Rs 2,000.

Small merchants above that line may face a fee they cannot legally pass to customers.

A notification exempting payments under Rs 2,000 is the first rule to acknowledge fees exist, opening room for a merchant charge above it.

[India](country:IN)'s payments committee has yet to set the rate above the threshold.
