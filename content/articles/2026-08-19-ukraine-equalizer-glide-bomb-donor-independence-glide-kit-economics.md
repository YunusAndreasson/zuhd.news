Kyiv — Ukraine's new glide bomb needs no foreign supplier.

It frees Kyiv from the delivery schedules and conditions donors attach to weapons.

The Equalizer bolts wings and guidance onto existing bombs, a kit a mid-size industrial base can mass-produce.

[Ukraine](country:UA) is adding propulsion aiming for a 125-mile range.
