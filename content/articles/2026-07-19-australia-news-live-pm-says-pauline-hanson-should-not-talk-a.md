Darwin — 20 nations sent warplanes to Australia.

The biennial Exercise Pitch Black draws 100 aircraft and 2,500 personnel from 20 countries to bases across northern [Australia](country:AU).

Prime Minister Albanese calls the three-week drills proof of regional security partnerships as Indo-Pacific competition intensifies.
