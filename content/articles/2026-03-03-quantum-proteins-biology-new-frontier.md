Quantum tunneling speeds up enzyme reactions, studies show. Protein scaffolds reduce quantum tunneling barriers rather than destroying quantum states, overturning the assumption that molecular noise blocks quantum effects in biology. Quantum-aware drug design could unlock reaction mechanisms conventional chemistry cannot model.
