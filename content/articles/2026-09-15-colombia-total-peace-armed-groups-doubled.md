Bogotá — [Colombia](country:CO)'s armed groups nearly doubled during peace talks.

The expansion undercuts Total Peace, President Petro's signature negotiating strategy.

Defense Ministry data show groups gained territory and recruits while sitting at Petro's negotiating table.

President de la Espriella has shifted to a militarized approach as the talks stall.
