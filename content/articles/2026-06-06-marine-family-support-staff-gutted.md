Washington — Marine family counselors cut 87% mid-war.

Civilian coordinators who guide Marine families through deployment crises fall from 151 to 19 by 2029, replaced by troops carrying other duties.

4,500 [US](country:US) Marines remain in the Middle East; the Corps has not paused the drawdown.
