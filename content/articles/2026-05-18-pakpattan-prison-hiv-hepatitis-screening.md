Pakpattan — All 6 HIV positives were Pakpattan prison inmates.

[Pakistan](country:PK) screened 7,336 citizens at 7 sites; all HIV cases clustered in the 900-inmate jail, which has no mandatory intake screening.

13 Hepatitis C cases were also found among inmates; no follow-up testing schedule for the prison was announced.
