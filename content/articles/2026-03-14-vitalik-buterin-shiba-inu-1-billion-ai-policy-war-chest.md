A dog-coin joke became a $1 billion AI fund. Ethereum co-founder Vitalik Buterin received the tokens unsolicited from Shiba Inu's creators in 2021 and donated them to the Future of Life Institute, expecting a $10-25 million exit. FLI liquidated roughly $500 million, pivoted to AI political lobbying, and has not responded to Buterin's public challenge.
