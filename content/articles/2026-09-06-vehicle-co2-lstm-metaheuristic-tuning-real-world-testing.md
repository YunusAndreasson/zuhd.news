Ottawa — An AI model predicts emissions at 98.71% accuracy.

It could let regulators audit real-world emissions without lab dynamometer testing.

Two nature-inspired search algorithms, mimicking fireflies and ants, tuned the network using [Canada](country:CA)'s vehicle registry data.

The model leans on fuel-economy figures and awaits final peer review.
