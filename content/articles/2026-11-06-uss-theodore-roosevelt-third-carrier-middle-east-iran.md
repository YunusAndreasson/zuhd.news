Singapore — A third US carrier strike group is one port call from the Iran war.

Two carrier groups are already prepared should Trump renew strikes on [Iran](country:IR).

The Theodore Roosevelt group left Yokosuka on 19 October and crosses the Strait of Malacca next.

Trump called Monday's unannounced strike on Qeshm Island a minor attack on an anti-aircraft facility.

A US official says the group will not reach the region before mid-November.
