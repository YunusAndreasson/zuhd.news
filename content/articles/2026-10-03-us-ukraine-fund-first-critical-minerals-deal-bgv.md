Kyiv — The first US-Ukraine minerals deal is worth about $30 million.

It is the first minerals project from the April 2025 deal between [Ukraine](country:UA) and the [US](country:US).

Conor Coleman of the US Development Finance Corporation said the platform with BGV Group targets rare earths, beryllium and zirconium.

US military aid may count as contributions to a fund that reinvests all profits in Ukraine for 10 years.

The fund is still seeking more capital, and its press release did not specify the investment's terms.
