Iran threatened US bases in Bahrain and Qatar. Mojtaba Khamenei, Iran's new supreme leader, named the US Fifth Fleet in Bahrain and 10,000 troops in Qatar as targets in his first public statement. Bahrain and Qatar must now choose: expel US forces or absorb Iranian strikes.
