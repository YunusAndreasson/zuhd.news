118,000 people fled in 7 days of Pakistan-Afghanistan fighting. Pakistan accuses the Taliban of sheltering militants behind the disputed colonial Durand Line, a 2,600-km border the Taliban government does not recognize. Pakistan rejected Turkey's mediation offer, leaving no diplomatic path forward.
