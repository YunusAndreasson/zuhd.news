Brent traded at $88.90 a barrel as of 3 August, pressuring [Pakistan](country:PK)'s import bill.

Pakistan buys nearly all its crude oil and gas through the Strait of Hormuz, paying in dollars it does not earn.

Traders now watch a US-Iran deal on Hormuz that remains distant.
