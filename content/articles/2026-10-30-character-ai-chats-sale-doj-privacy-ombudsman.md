Washington — Character.AI's bankruptcy sale could include chats of millions of users.

The [US](country:US) Justice Department's bankruptcy office says the chats are often sensitive and sometimes involve minors.

The company's privacy policy permits transferring personal data in a sale, and it changed on 1 July while Character sought buyers.

Character's chatbot tells users their conversations stay private, the filing says.

The bankruptcy judge can restrict or block the sale of user data.
