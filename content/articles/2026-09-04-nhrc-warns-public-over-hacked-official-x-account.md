Abuja — The rights commission lost its X account.

Anyone can now post fake findings in [Nigeria](country:NG)'s top human-rights body's name.

Recovery runs through X's support queue, not a legal process, so a state agency waits like any user.

The commission says it will confirm its return only through other verified channels.
