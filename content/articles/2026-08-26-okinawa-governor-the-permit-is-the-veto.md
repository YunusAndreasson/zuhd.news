Naha — Governor Denny Tamaki seeks re-election on an anti-base platform.

His office controls the landfill permits [Japan](country:JP) needs to relocate US forces within Okinawa.

Okinawa holds 0.6% of Japan's land yet hosts most of its US military footprint.

Tokyo has repeatedly overridden the governor's permit refusals, and this vote decides who holds that veto next.
