Caracas — Opposition leader Machado says the [US](country:US) proposed 2027 presidential elections.

Interim president Delcy Rodríguez has promised a vote but set no date.

Washington backs talks between Rodríguez's government and opposition groups, excluding Machado's party, on overhauling the electoral council and top court.

Rodríguez says her government should oversee a "transition of this magnitude," which Machado dismissed as illogical.

Machado will not join the talks, and Rodríguez has not invited her to meet.
