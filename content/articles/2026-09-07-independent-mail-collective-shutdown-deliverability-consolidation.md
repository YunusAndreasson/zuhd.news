Milan — Autistici/Inventati is shutting down after 25 years.

Activists, unions and journalists lose a mailbox built to hold minimal logs by design.

The collective says it was designated a "global terrorist organization," making continued operation a legal risk to its users.

Backup instructions are coming, but the collective warns its domain could vanish without further notice.
