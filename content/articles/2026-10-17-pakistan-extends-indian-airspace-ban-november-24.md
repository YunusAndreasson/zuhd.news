Pakistan — Air India estimated the ban costs it $455 million a year in profit.

[Pakistan](country:PK) extended its ban on Indian aircraft, military flights included, until 24 November.

Pakistan closed its airspace in April 2025 after the Pahalgam attack and has renewed the ban several times.

The ban lapses on 24 November unless Pakistan's airports authority renews it again.
