Berlin — Ilse Aigner, 61, would be [Germany](country:DE)'s first woman president.

Merz's Christian Democrats ended weeks of strain with their Bavarian sister party, German media report.

The Bundestag and delegates from 16 states form the Federal Convention that elects the president, reportedly on 30 January.

"I don't have the killer instinct," said Aigner, speaker of Bavaria's parliament.

Party committees meet Monday to confirm her nomination before Steinmeier's term ends in March 2027.
