Butembo — A beekeeper of 30 years found all 10 hives empty this season.

Drought and irregular rainfall kill nectar-producing flowers; a litre of honey that sold for $7 in 2025 now costs $10-12 as production collapsed.

No government program covers beehive collapse; families relying on honey for both income and nutrition have no fallback.
