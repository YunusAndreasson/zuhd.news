The FCC threatened US broadcast licenses over Iran war coverage. Federal Communications Commission chairman Brendan Carr controls all US TV and radio license renewals and warned outlets to "correct course." US network ABC suspended a late-night show after his last warning, with dozens of network renewals now pending.
