Tehran — [Iran](country:IR)'s Guard says 2 tankers hit mines.

Ships outside the lane Iran authorises now face mines, the Guard warns.

The Guard says one supertanker carrying over 2 million barrels sank, but no one has independently confirmed either strike.

The [US](country:US) military recently claimed it had cleared all mines from the main routes.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 31 December a 16% chance, down 20 points in a week.
