London — [Britain](country:GB)'s motor fuel sales fell 10% in April.

The Office for National Statistics reported retail volumes fell 1.3% — the sharpest annual drop — after the [Iran](country:IR) war raised fuel prices.

The Bank of England faces its June decision: hold rates despite inflation or cut into a slowing economy.
