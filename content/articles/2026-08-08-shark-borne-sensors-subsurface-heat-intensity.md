Newark — Sharks now carry the sensors hurricane forecasters lack.

Storm-intensity forecasts have barely improved while track forecasts advanced for decades.

University of Delaware researchers tag mako, blue and hammerhead sharks with depth-and-temperature sensors to capture subsurface heat data.

Satellites cannot see that heat, leaving intensity forecasts dependent on the shark data.
