Abuja — Petrol costs about ₦1,400 a litre in Abuja and Lagos, reports say, against a ₦70,000 wage floor.

Eight public-sector unions in [Nigeria](country:NG) want petrol at ₦500 a litre and a higher minimum wage.

Petrol sold at about ₦195 before Bola Tinubu ended the subsidy in 2023, and market pricing has driven it up since.

They will strike for 3 days from 2 October unless Tinubu answers in Thursday's Independence Day broadcast.
