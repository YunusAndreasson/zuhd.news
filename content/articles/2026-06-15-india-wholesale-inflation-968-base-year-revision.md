New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s Commerce Ministry revised the Wholesale Price Index base year to 2022-23 as May wholesale inflation hit 9.68%.

Fuel and power rose 30.33% year-on-year, the ministry said.

A new Producer Price Index will phase out the Wholesale Price Index by 2031, with May the first dual-series release.
