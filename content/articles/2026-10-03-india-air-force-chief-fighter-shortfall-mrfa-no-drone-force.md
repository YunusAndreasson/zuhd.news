New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s air force has about 29 squadrons against 42.5 authorised.

Air Chief Marshal AP Singh says Tejas Mk-1A deliveries are far later than planned.

India is negotiating with [France](country:FR) for 114 Rafales, estimated at around Rs 3.25 lakh crore.

"Drones are not revolutionary to the Indian Air Force, but evolutionary," said Singh, ruling out a separate drone force.

Singh says the Rafale contract could be signed this financial year if France agrees.
