Seville — Solar panels above tomatoes cut water use. University of Seville and Madrid researchers found solar-shaded tomato crops needed less irrigation water, despite lower yields. Arid-region farms face water scarcity and energy costs simultaneously — agrivoltaics address both without claiming new land.
