Berlin — Hamburg regulators found Ray-Ban Meta's recording light hard to see.

[Germany](country:DE)'s states want camera glasses that film unnoticed restricted, which would probably ban many popular models.

Current law bans only devices designed for covert recording, and the Federal Network Agency accepts the Ray-Ban Meta light as sufficient.

The Bundesrat adopted this position Friday and asks Berlin to press for EU-wide technical minimum standards.
