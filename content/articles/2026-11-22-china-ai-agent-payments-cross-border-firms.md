Singapore — LianLian completed Asia-Pacific's first real agent-paid B2B purchase.

Chinese payment firms are competing to define which AI agents qualify to pay.

A Visa payment token carried the office-supplies order, and Ant International wants one-time customer checks replaced by continuous agent checks.

PingPong skips the front door, embedding its payment network in banks that keep the customer.

Ant, Visa and Mastercard are drafting shared agent-liability standards on [Singapore](country:SG)'s BuildFin.ai platform.
