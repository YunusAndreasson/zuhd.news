St Petersburg — Fire broke out at the Vysotsk oil terminal overnight.

[Ukraine](country:UA)'s drones reached 900 km past the front line to a Baltic port handling oil, coal and gas, part of a campaign targeting the revenue funding [Russia](country:RU)'s war.

Governor Alexander Drozdenko said 72 drones were shot down over the surrounding Leningrad region.
