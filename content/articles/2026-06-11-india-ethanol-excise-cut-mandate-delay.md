New Delhi — [India](country:IN) zeroed excise on petrol blends above E20.

Most vehicles on the road predate E20 certification, making a mandate for higher blends risky for engines.

Adoption beyond E20 remains undecided; E25 vehicle trials are underway but time-consuming, officials said.
