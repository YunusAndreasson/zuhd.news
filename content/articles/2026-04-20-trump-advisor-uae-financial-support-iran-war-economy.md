Washington, D.C. — [UAE](country:AE) threatened yuan payments to secure a dollar lifeline. Iran war damage prompted Abu Dhabi to warn [US](country:US) officials it could switch to yuan, winning a Treasury backing pledge. No formal swap line requested; any package would need Treasury rather than Fed approval, officials said.
