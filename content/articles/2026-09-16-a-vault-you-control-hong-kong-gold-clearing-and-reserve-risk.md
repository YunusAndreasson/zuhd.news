Hong Kong — John Lee may grow the city's gold reserves.

Adding bullion, not just trading it, hedges against foreign payment freezes.

Vaulted gold cannot be frozen by a foreign decision, the lesson reserve managers drew from 2022 asset seizures.

The plan slots into Beijing's five-year strategy, with the city's own share unclear.
