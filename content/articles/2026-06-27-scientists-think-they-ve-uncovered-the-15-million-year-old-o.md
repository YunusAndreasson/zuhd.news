Coventry — The "ha-ha-ha" rhythm appears in all great apes.

Acoustic analysis of 4 great ape species and human children traced the rhythm to a common ancestor 15 million years ago.

More closely related apes show more complex, varied laughs, tracing the evolution of human vocal range.
