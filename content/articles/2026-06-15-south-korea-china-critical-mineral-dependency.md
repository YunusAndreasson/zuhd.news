Seoul — [China](country:CN) holds up to 94% of some Korean critical mineral imports.

For 6 strategic minerals, China's share of [South Korea](country:KR)'s imports spans 14.8%–94.2%; diversification has cut the low end but not the top.

Refining capacity, not mining, is concentrated in China; ore diversification has not reduced South Korea's deepest dependencies.
