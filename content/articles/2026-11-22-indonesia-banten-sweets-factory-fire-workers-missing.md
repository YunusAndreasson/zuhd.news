Serang — 36 to 50 workers are missing after a factory fire.

Families face 2 to 3 weeks of DNA matching before the dead are identified, police said.

The fire began Saturday at the three-storey Fonusa Agung Mulia confectionery factory in [Indonesia](country:ID), still burning 12 hours later.

The owner says 300 people worked the shift, and 50 remain unaccounted for.

Police have not named a cause, and officials warn parts of the building may collapse.
