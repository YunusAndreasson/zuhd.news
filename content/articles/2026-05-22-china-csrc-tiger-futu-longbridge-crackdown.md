Beijing — [China](country:CN) sanctioned 3 offshore brokers serving mainland investors.

The China Securities Regulatory Commission named Tiger, Futu and Longbridge for operating without licences; existing accounts enter a 2-year sell-only wind-down.

Eight agencies including China's central bank and forex regulator are coordinating the enforcement campaign.
