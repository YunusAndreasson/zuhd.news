Hargeisa — [Israel](country:IL) was the first state to recognize Somaliland.

Somaliland holds roughly 500 miles of Gulf of Aden coastline, giving the deal strategic weight beyond development aid.

Regional bodies including [Somalia](country:SO) condemned the recognition; no military basing agreement has been announced.
