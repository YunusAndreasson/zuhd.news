Beijing — Factories built the economy; design didn't.

As manufacturing's share of [China](country:CN)'s output shrinks, policymakers are turning toward higher-margin "producer services" — design, branding, intellectual property.

Whether Beijing can climb that ladder shapes the next phase of its economic contest with the [US](country:US).
