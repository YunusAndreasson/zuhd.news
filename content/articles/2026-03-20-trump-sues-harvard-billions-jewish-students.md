The US sued Harvard for billions over campus antisemitism. The Justice Department alleges Harvard was "deliberately indifferent" to Jewish students — a legal standard that allows the government to recover federal funding. Harvard has not responded to a suit that could set precedent for how far civil rights law constrains campus speech.
