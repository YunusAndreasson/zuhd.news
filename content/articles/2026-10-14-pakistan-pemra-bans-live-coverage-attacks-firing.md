Pakistan — The media regulator banned live television coverage of blasts and shootings.

Channels may air only information the relevant law enforcement agency has verified.

The 13 October order also requires a broadcast time delay and an independent editorial board at every satellite channel.

Violators face legal action under sections 29-A, 30 and 33 of the Pemra Ordinance 2002.
