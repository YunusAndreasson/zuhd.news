Yorito — Gunmen killed 12 people, among them Tolupán leader Delma Olvera.

She helped make Yorito a mining-free municipality in 2019.

Four armed attacks struck before dawn Wednesday, [Honduras](country:HN)'s Security Ministry said, killing her with husband Santos Pérez and daughter Madelin.

Security Minister Gerzon Velásquez declared Yoro department a high-risk zone.
