Bitdeer sold its entire bitcoin treasury on February 20. The Singapore-based miner is converting crypto facilities in the US, Europe, and Malaysia to AI data centers, funded by $369 million in fresh capital. 3 other mining companies are making the same shift, signaling an industry-wide move from bitcoin to AI compute.
