New York — Ex-Anthropic engineer tells council humans likely lose control.

Trump signed an agreement late last month that largely lets AI companies self-police.

The Council is weighing mandatory disclosure, third-party approval of AI systems in the city, and giving whistleblowers a share of fines.

Council Speaker Julie Menin called OpenAI's refusal to quantify the worst-case risk "flippant at best."

Governor Kathy Hochul separately moved to impose AI reporting standards, but state power is limited.
