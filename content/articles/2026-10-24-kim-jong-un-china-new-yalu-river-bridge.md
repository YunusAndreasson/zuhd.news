Dandong — Kim Jong Un's first known trip abroad in a year.

The bridge he crossed opened this month and is the two countries' main land crossing.

[China](country:CN) finished it in 2014, but [North Korea](country:KP) had not built connecting roads and customs facilities, South Korean reports say.

Lawmaker Youn Kun-young expects a very short visit, since Xi Jinping attends a party meeting in Beijing from Monday.

Xi hosts a regional summit in Shenzhen on 18-19 November, but officials see little chance of a Trump-Kim meeting.
