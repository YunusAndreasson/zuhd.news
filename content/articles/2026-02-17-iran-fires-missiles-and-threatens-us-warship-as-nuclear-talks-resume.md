Iran fired missiles into the Strait of Hormuz during live drills. Supreme Leader Khamenei warned he possesses "the weapon capable of sinking" a US warship, as a second carrier group steamed toward the Gulf. Indirect nuclear talks opened hours later in Geneva, with neither side signalling flexibility.
