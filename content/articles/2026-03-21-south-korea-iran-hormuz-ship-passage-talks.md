All Hormuz tanker lanes run through Iranian waters. South Korea signed a Western statement condemning Iran's Hormuz closure but is also negotiating directly with Tehran for passage exemptions. Iran told Japan it would allow its ships through after appropriate consultations — Seoul is seeking the same deal.
