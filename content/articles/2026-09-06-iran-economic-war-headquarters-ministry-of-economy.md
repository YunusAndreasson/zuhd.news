It signals Tehran now treats the blockade as permanent, not a crisis to outlast.

Spokesman Morteza Zamanian says it will fast-track fixes for firms, trade and financing, sending harder cases to the cabinet.

Whether the task force eases shortages for ordinary Iranians remains untested.
