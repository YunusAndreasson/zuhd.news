Tehran — Khamenei funeral opens July 4, burial July 9.

[Iran](country:IR) delayed the March burial until ceasefire; his son Mojtaba, unseen since February, must appear at Mashhad to confirm the succession.

His appearance July 9 would be his first confirmed sighting since the [US](country:US)-Israeli strike that killed Khamenei.
