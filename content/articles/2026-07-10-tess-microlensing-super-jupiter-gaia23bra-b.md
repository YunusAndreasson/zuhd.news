Albuquerque — NASA's TESS was never built to find this planet.

Gaia23bra b, 1.6 times Jupiter's mass, appeared through gravitational microlensing, a method the star-transit satellite wasn't designed to catch, researchers said.

The find suggests more distant giant planets sit unnoticed in TESS's archived data.
