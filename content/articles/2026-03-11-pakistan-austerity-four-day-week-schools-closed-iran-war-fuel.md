Pakistan closed schools and declared a 4-day work week. Pakistan imports all its oil and has no fuel hedges, leaving it fully exposed to the war's supply shock. Pakistan's PM spokesperson pledged support for Saudi Arabia "no matter what, no matter when" — a nuclear state declaring wartime alignment.
