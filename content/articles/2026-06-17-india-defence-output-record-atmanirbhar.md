New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s defence output hit Rs 1.78 lakh crore in FY2026, up 110% in 5 years, the Defence Ministry said.

Growth followed India's Aatmanirbhar self-reliance drive; private-sector share reached an all-time high of 24%.

Defence exports also set a record at Rs 38,424 crore, the Ministry said.
