Iran and the US will talk in Geneva next week. Oman is mediating, and Switzerland confirmed it would host the negotiations. Washington is pushing Tehran to limit its nuclear programme. Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi held indirect talks with his US counterpart in Oman on February 6. Geneva will also host US-brokered talks between Russia and Ukraine the same week.
