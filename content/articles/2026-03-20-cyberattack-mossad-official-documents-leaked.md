Hackers claimed 100,000 emails stolen from a Mossad official. The Hanzala cyber group published the alleged files via Tasnim, an Iranian state news agency, without naming the official or providing proof of authenticity. Israel has neither confirmed nor denied the breach, leaving the documents unverified.
