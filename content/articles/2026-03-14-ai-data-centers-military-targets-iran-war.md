Iran struck 3 Amazon data centres in the Gulf. Iran declared tech infrastructure a legitimate target, citing US military AI targeting systems hosted in Gulf data centres. Google, Microsoft and a planned $700 billion OpenAI data centre in Abu Dhabi now sit in Iran's target category.
