India opened a 5-day AI summit in New Delhi. Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated the event alongside 20 heads of state and tech executives including Google's Sundar Pichai and OpenAI's Sam Altman, while the United Kingdom announced a £58 million programme including an African Language Hub to bring AI capabilities to 40 languages spoken by 700 million people. No binding agreements are expected, but India is positioning itself as a bridge between Silicon Valley and the Global South on AI governance.
