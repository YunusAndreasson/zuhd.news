Vienna — [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s December quota stays at 10.478 million barrels a day.

Most members already pump below quota because the Iran war blocks their exports.

Gulf exporters shipped about 81% of pre-war volumes in September, and Brent stood at $113.4 on 30 October.

The seven producers meet again on 29 November, with a capacity review due before 2027 quotas.
