Tehran — Neither claim to Hormuz holds under maritime law.

The dispute helps determine who insures tankers and how much fuel costs.

Under the UN Convention on the Law of the Sea, transit passage lets any flag state cross the strait, and no coastal state can suspend it.

[Iran](country:IR)'s deputy foreign minister said Tehran controls the strait, and Washington has not clarified Trump's claim.
