Bern — [Switzerland](country:CH) will hold a binding population cap referendum.

Federal data show 1 in 3 workers is foreign-born; passing the initiative forces parliament to cap immigration once population exceeds 10 million.

Swiss officials warn a yes vote would conflict with EU bilateral treaties; no vote date is set.
