A new text-generation method produces answers up to 14.5 times faster. Researchers from Seoul National University, UC Berkeley, and Together AI built consistency diffusion language models that decode entire blocks of tokens at once instead of one at a time. The technique matched standard accuracy on maths and coding benchmarks, potentially slashing the cost of running large language models.
