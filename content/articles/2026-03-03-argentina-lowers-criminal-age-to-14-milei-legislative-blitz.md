Argentina's Senate voted 44–27 to make 14-year-olds criminally liable. The bill is Milei's 2nd law this week, replacing Argentina's 1980 juvenile code. Milei will sign within days, though critics warn detention centres lack capacity for the newly eligible prisoners.
