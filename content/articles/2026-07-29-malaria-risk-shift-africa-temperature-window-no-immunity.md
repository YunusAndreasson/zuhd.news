London — Cooler highlands are gaining malaria, not losing it.

A Nature study finds warming added 1 case per 1,000 since 1900, raising risk in cooler zones while easing it elsewhere.

Bed-net and treatment stocks are allocated against old maps, leaving newly at-risk districts with no acquired immunity.
