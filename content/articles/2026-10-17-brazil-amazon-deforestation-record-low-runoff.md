Brasília — [Brazil](country:BR) cleared 3,335 square kilometres of Amazon in a year.

Clearance has fallen every year since 2022, down 71.2% over four years.

Lula tightened monitoring and penalties for illegal logging, reversing Bolsonaro-era losses of 11,600 square kilometres a year.

Lula faces Flávio Bolsonaro in the run-off on 25 October, with polls showing them neck and neck.
