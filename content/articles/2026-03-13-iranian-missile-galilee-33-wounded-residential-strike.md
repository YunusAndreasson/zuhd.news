An Iranian missile hit a Galilee building, wounding 33. The strike penetrated Israel's layered air defense to reach a residential area — a shift from Iran's earlier pattern of targeting military sites. Mojtaba Khamenei, son of Iran's Supreme Leader and its rising power figure, declared the Hormuz blockade permanent, signaling Iran intends to keep striking Israeli territory.
