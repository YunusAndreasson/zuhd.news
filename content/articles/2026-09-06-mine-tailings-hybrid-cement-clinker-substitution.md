Brumadinho — Researchers replaced 30% of cement with mine waste.

Cement production causes roughly 8% of global CO2 emissions, the study notes.

The recycled tailings react with cement byproducts, avoiding the limestone-burning step that releases most of that CO2.

The results are lab-scale mortar tests, well short of a construction standard.
