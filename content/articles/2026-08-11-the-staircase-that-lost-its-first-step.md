New Delhi — 1.5 million engineers graduate each year in India.

AI now automates the testing and support tasks that once built juniors into seniors.

[India](country:IN)'s IT services model has long relied on billable juniors to do that same work.

Colleges are rewriting curricula, but placement data will show whether the ladder still holds.
