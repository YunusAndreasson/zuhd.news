Caracas — 10 months after capturing Maduro, Washington signed a military pact.

Elections promised after Maduro's removal remain unscheduled while the [US](country:US) deepens ties with his acting successor.

The deal covers joint training and intelligence sharing, and Hegseth said it requires defence reforms inside [Venezuela](country:VE)'s military.

Hegseth continues to Colombia, Ecuador and Honduras, and the Pentagon has not released the agreement's text.
