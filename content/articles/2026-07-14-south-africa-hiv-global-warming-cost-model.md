Pretoria — Rising heat is driving [South Africa](country:ZA)'s HIV cases.

A modeling study found temperature-linked HIV cases rose sevenfold from 2001 to 2020, with heat reshaping migration and risk behavior.

Researchers say antiretroviral treatment costs keep climbing toward 2050 regardless of development scenario.
