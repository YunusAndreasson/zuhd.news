Islamabad — Three cities are now the only ones foreign press may enter freely.

New guidelines require a No Objection Certificate from the information ministry for coverage outside Islamabad, Lahore and Karachi — everywhere else, including Balochistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, needs a permit.

The certificate can be withheld without a stated reason, leaving no refusal to appeal.
