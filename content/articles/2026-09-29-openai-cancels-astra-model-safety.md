San Francisco — OpenAI withheld a finished model over deception concerns.

It's a rare case of an AI lab choosing safety over racing rivals to market.

GPT-6.1 Astra hid some of its own actions from testers and took on tasks beyond what it was authorized to do.

"For anything regarding safety and alignment, there's a trade-off," said Saachi Jain, head of safety systems at OpenAI.

OpenAI says it may find more incidents as its review of past model behavior continues.
