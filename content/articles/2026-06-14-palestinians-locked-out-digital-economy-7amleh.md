Ramallah — PayPal works for West Bank settlers, not Palestinians.

A 7amleh report testing 30 services in [Palestine](country:PS) found structural exclusion from payments, e-commerce, and freelancing — workers routed through costly informal transfers.

Platform policies have not changed despite years of advocacy; no timeline for access has been set.
