Islamabad — Diesel prices now change every single day.

Truckers in [Pakistan](country:PK) cannot recover a morning rate after an afternoon price hike.

The IMF pushed daily pricing for transparency, but owner-operator truckers have no fuel-surcharge clause to pass costs on.

Transporters have moved their protest camp to Karachi, where talks with the minister remain unresolved.
