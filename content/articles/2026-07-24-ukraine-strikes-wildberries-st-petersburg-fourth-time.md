St Petersburg — 3 injured as [Ukraine](country:UA)'s drones hit Wildberries warehouses.

It's the 4th such strike in a week on the retailer, which handles 52% of [Russia](country:RU)'s online orders, in Putin's home city.

Russia's defense ministry claims 571 drones downed overnight; Wildberries has not said when the hubs reopen.
