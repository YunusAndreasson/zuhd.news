Sydney — Smallpox killed up to 220,000 First Nations people.

New epidemiological modelling traces the 1789 outbreak to the First Fleet, reconstructing spread across trade networks no record captured.

[Australia](country:AU) has never treated the outbreak's colonial origin as a settled question.
