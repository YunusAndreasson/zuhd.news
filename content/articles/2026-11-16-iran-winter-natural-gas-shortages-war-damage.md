Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) forecasts a 430-million-cubic-metre daily winter gas deficit.

11 of 31 provinces face shortages, and the government warns cuts could reach Tehran.

The gas company says strikes by the [US](country:US) and [Israel](country:IL) on 18 March cut 28% of production capacity.

Full repair could last until 2028, and power plants will keep burning polluting mazut.
