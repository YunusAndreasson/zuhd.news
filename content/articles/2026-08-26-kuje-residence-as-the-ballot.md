Kuje — A council chairman told voters to back the All Progressives Congress or leave.

Amnesty International warns the threat could incite violence against those who don't.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s ruling party already controls most of Abuja's six area councils, including Kuje.

Amnesty has urged security agencies to act, but none have responded publicly.
