Pretoria — [South Africa](country:ZA) has not vetted 119,000 teachers against the sex-offenders register.

Vetting began in 2023; police clearances take 1–4 months each, and provinces cite understaffing at the South African Police Service.

Authorities found 59 teachers guilty of misconduct last year; no department has set a clearance deadline.
