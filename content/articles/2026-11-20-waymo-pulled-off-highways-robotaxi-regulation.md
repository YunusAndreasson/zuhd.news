San Francisco — Three driverless Waymos entered a closed work zone.

Waymo then suspended paid highway rides in every market it serves.

The next day another Waymo tried to bypass a California Highway Patrol car directing traffic on Route 280, regulators said.

City-street robotaxi service continues, and highway testing may continue with safety specialists aboard.

The restriction lasts until Waymo shows its fixes work, after an earlier recall failed.
