Toronto — Canadians could pledge $190,000 in bitcoin for a $475,000 home.

A bitcoin crash could force borrowers to add collateral or lose pledged coins.

APX Lending says a 40% bitcoin drop lifts a 50% loan-to-value loan to 83%.

"Selling bitcoin collateral and enforcing a mortgage against a home are distinct actions," said Andrei Poliakov, APX's chief executive.

APX plans to launch in early 2027 but has not said when it will publish terms.
