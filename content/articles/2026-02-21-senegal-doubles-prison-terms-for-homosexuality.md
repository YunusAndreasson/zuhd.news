Senegal doubled prison terms for homosexuality to 10 years. The cabinet-approved bill from the ruling Pastef party also bans media promotion of homosexuality and raises fines to 10 million CFA francs. Parliament must still vote, but Human Rights Watch warned the measure could shut down HIV/AIDS organizations.
