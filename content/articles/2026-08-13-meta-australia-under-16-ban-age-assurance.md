Canberra — Meta removed 750,000 under-16 accounts.

The count shows how many children the old self-declared birthday check let onto its platforms.

[Australia](country:AU)'s minimum-age law forced Meta to estimate every user's age from behaviour, not just the young ones.

Regulators haven't independently verified Meta's own number, and other platforms report far less.
