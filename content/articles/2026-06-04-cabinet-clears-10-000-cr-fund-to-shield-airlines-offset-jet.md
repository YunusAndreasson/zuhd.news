New Delhi — Jet fuel costs rose 2.5 times since March.

[India](country:IN)'s cabinet approved ₹10,000 crore to let state oil firms supply jet fuel at fixed prices, shielding airlines from Iran-war price spikes.

Airlines must use state suppliers for 3 years while the subsidy is recovered as international prices fall.
