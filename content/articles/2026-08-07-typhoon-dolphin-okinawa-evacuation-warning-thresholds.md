Naha — [Japan](country:JP) evacuated 260,000 for a Category 1 storm.

The wind category undersells the risk: the Japan Meteorological Agency says most regional typhoon deaths come from rainfall-triggered landslides, not wind speed.

Kumamoto, still housing 6,700 people displaced by last week's earthquake, now faces the same storm.
