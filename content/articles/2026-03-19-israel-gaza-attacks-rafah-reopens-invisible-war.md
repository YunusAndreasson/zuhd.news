Rafah crossing reopened Thursday for 8 medical patients. Israel sealed the crossing 19 days ago when it began bombing Iran, cutting Gaza's only medical evacuation route. US envoys meeting Hamas in Cairo aim to salvage a ceasefire Israel has violated almost daily since October.
