New Delhi — Heatwaves joined a 14-item disaster list on 4 August.

States can now draw national relief funds for heat instead of capping use at 10%.

[India](country:IN)'s Home Ministry unlocked roughly $30 billion in combined disaster funds for 2026-2031, per the new guidelines.

Attribution rules for counting heat deaths are still being written.
