Lucknow — Police detained state chief Aradhana Mishra on Constitution Day.

Congress says [India](country:IN)'s roll revision deleted 13 crore votes, singling out Dalits and other marginalised groups.

Police briefly held Mishra, Rajendra Pal Gautam and Imran Masood for marching to Ambedkar Crossing without permission, then released them.

"We will not wait for permission," said Aradhana Mishra, the Uttar Pradesh Congress president.

Uttar Pradesh holds assembly elections in 2027, and Congress has made the voter rolls its campaign.
