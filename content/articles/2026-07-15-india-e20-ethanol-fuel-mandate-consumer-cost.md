New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s pumps now default to a 20% ethanol blend.

Transport Minister Nitin Gadkari says buyers wanting pure petrol must pay extra, after the blending target was hit early.

Gadkari denies any conflict from his sons' ethanol business, which he says holds under 0.5% market share.
