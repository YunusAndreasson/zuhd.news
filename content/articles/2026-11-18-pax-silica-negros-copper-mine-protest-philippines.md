Bacolod — Protesters target a copper mine regulators have no record of.

More than 200 youths linked the proposed Hinoba-an project to Pax Silica, a [US](country:US) critical-minerals initiative.

Activists say Cooper Metals seeks the Don Jose deposit, but regional officials say Philex and Maricalum hold the titles.

Maricalum Mining "left Sipalay with scarred farmlands," said Rusty Biñas, founder of the Green Alert Network.

The Mines and Geosciences Bureau will check whether Cooper filed any application, the environmental bureau said.
