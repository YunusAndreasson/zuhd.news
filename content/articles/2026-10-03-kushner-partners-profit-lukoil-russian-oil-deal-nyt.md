Washington — Kushner's business partners sit in the group bidding for Lukoil's assets.

The New York Times reported he helped broker the multibillion-dollar sale while negotiating on ending the war in Ukraine.

The group includes a [Qatar](country:QA)-based conglomerate that partners with Kushner in Albania and a fund controlled by Emirati adviser Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed.

The Times found no sign that Kushner or Witkoff would personally profit.

Kushner called a separate CNN report on his business dealings misleading Friday and vowed to rebut coverage.
