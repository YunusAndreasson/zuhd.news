Rabah — Bandits killed at least 7 people in Rabah.

It came a week after the army said it repelled a nearby advance.

Armed groups are mobile and villages in northwestern [Nigeria](country:NG) are not, so a blocked raid often lands nearby days later.

Reports put abductions between 27 and 40, with final tolls still unconfirmed.
