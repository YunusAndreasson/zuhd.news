Addis Ababa — Countries have failed 6 times to schedule the UN climate panel's next reports.

Chair Jim Skea warned the reports miss the 2028 stocktake without a deal by the end of 2026.

Skea suspended the week-long session on Friday with no decision, and delegates also left the panel's budget unagreed.

The panel will reconvene at the earliest opportunity but has given no date.
