Islamabad — A third of [Pakistan](country:PK)'s petrol price is tax.

The levy funds IMF-mandated revenue without a tax administration, falling equally on rickshaw drivers and importers alike.

Diesel fell Rs2 as petrol rose Rs4.45 — shifting cost from freight onto private transport.
