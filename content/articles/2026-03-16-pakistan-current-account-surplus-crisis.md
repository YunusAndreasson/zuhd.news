Pakistan posted a $479m surplus as imports collapsed. Pakistan's economy grew only 3% in FY25 because the country cannot afford to buy goods abroad — the same mechanism producing the apparent surplus. March figures will capture the oil shock of the regional conflicts, which pushed crude prices up 80% since February 28.
