Rome — Avio finished integrating its FD1 rocket demonstrator.

It tests the relight capability that makes rockets reusable and cheap.

The methane-fueled demonstrator will fly from an Italian defense ministry range in Sardinia, funded by a €181.6 million recovery-plan contract.

Avio has completed ground-test prep but set no date for the actual flight.
