Bengaluru — The [US](country:US) suspended green-card sponsorship for 8 firms, including Infosys.

Tata Consultancy Services, Wipro, Cognizant and Microsoft are also suspended.

Suspended firms cannot file new labor-certification applications or have pending ones processed, though H-1B visa holders appear unaffected.

Infosys says it expects no material impact and will answer Department of Labor questions.
