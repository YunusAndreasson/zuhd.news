Bamako — [Mali](country:ML)'s supply routes are under jihadist blockade.

Jama'at Nusrat al-Islam wal Muslimin's blockade of Mali's Dakar-Bamako corridor costs Dakar's port $26.5 million monthly, the Institute for Security Studies said.

The tactic is spreading toward [Côte d'Ivoire](country:CI) and [Senegal](country:SN); regional bodies have not coordinated a response.
