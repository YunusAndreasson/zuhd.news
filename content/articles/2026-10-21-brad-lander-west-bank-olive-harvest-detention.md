Nahalin — Several dozen olive harvesters remain in Israeli custody, Lander says.

US House nominee Brad Lander went free with a warning, and says his profile explains why.

Soldiers arrived two hours into the harvest, declared a closed military zone and took about 80 volunteers to a police station, Rabbis for Human Rights said.

The army said an uncoordinated harvest near an Israeli community posed a security risk, and named no danger.

[Israel](country:IL)'s army has not said when the remaining detainees will be released.
