Argentina faces its 4th general strike under President Milei. The Confederación General del Trabajo called a 24-hour walkout for Thursday, when the lower house votes on reforms allowing 12-hour workdays and reduced severance. The Senate passed the bill 42-30 last week, and Milei wants final approval before his March address.
