Finland asked Seoul for quantum and 6G research access. Nokia's 6G research in Helsinki and South Korea's manufacturing scale together yield capabilities neither country could develop quickly alone. A quantum standards assembly in Finland in May will be the first joint setting for both countries' researchers.
