Washington — Pentagon faces $25,000/month Starlink bills per LUCAS drone.

SpaceX argued [US](country:US) military LUCAS drones used an aviation-tier Starlink service during February's Iran campaign, up from $5,000/month; no LEO broadband rival is at parity.

The Pentagon disputed the figure; AI-swarming upgrades planned for LUCAS would raise connectivity demands further.
