Vatican will not join the Board of Peace. Cardinal Pietro Parolin, the Vatican's top diplomat, said the initiative left the Holy See "perplexed" — the Vatican backs the UN as the primary body for managing global conflicts. 3 European nations also declined on the board's opening day, leaving the US-led body without major allies from the outset.
