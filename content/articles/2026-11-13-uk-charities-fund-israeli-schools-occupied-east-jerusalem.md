East Jerusalem — Charities raised over £276,000 for Jerusalem yeshivas, advocacy outlet Declassified UK reported.

Legal experts told it that [UK](country:GB) charities cannot fund activities that facilitate breaches of international law.

Hakotel pupils combine Torah study with Israeli army service, and some donations were tax-exempt, so taxpayers helped fund both schools, it says.

The Charity Commission is investigating UK charities operating in settlements to decide if regulatory action is required.
