Vereeniging — 56% of Emfuleni's workers have no job.

[South Africa](country:ZA) shed 560,000 manufacturing jobs since 2008; ANC-era liberalization was not matched by reinvestment, leaving the Vaal Triangle towns to collapse.

The 3 Vaal Triangle towns have no announced rebuilding plan; youth unemployment runs far above the national headline rate.
