Baqen — 16 dead, 32 hurt, 300 houses down, authorities say.

The quake struck [China](country:CN)'s Tibet 390km from Lhasa, and power and telecoms are down in the county seat, Xinhua said.

The 7.0 quake struck at 10km depth, the US Geological Survey said, shallow enough to concentrate shaking near the epicentre.

Officials are still counting damage, and January 2025's 6.8 quake in Tingri killed at least 126.
