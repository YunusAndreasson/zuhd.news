Madurai — Tamil Nadu lit the festival flame, but not where the court ordered.

[India](country:IN)'s Supreme Court has let the original petitioners file a contempt petition against the state government.

The state filed a review petition on 21 November, but the Madras High Court held that pending review is no ground for non-compliance.

"This is how we celebrate Karthigai Deepam," said journalist Tharasu Shyam, who comes from Thirupparankundram.

On 25 November the state told the Supreme Court it could not comply with the order.
