Sunshine 100 defaulted on $205 million in debt. The Chinese developer is opposing a Hong Kong winding-up petition — a tactic Chinese real-estate firms now routinely use to buy time while homebuyers who pre-paid for apartments wait. Hong Kong's High Court hears the case on May 20 — a ruling that could determine whether pre-paid homebuyers ever see their apartments.
