Shivpuri — Adani Defence laid the foundation for a ₹2,500-crore missile plant.

The facility will integrate systems and produce critical materials, letting [India](country:IN)'s military induct homegrown missiles at scale, the company said.

Madhya Pradesh's chief minister touted jobs; no start date for production was given.
