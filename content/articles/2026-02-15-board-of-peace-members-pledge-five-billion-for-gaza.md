25 countries pledged over $5 billion for Gaza reconstruction. President Trump announced the commitments ahead of Thursday's inaugural Board of Peace summit in Washington, where member states including the United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, and Jordan will also deploy thousands of personnel to a UN-authorized stabilization force. The UN estimates Gaza needs $70 billion to rebuild, and no Palestinian representatives have seats on the board.
