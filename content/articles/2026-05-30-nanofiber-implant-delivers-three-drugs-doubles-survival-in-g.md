Cincinnati — A nanofiber implant delivering 3 drugs doubled glioblastoma survival in mice.

The blood-brain barrier blocks drug delivery to brain tumors; the implant bypasses it, releasing 3 agents at the site with immediate and sustained dosing, Cincinnati and Hopkins researchers said.

Results are preclinical; human trials have not been announced.
