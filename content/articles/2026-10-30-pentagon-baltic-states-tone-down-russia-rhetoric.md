Estonia — Pentagon officials asked 3 Baltic states to soften their [Russia](country:RU) warnings.

The states bordering Russia are being told to speak less by the power guaranteeing their defence.

Four anonymous sources say policy chief Elbridge Colby delivered it privately while the [US](country:US) reviews its troop levels in Europe.

One European official called the advice baffling, since Baltic intelligence services have played down the chance Russia tests NATO.

The Pentagon's review of force posture in Europe could end in a sharp troop drawdown.
