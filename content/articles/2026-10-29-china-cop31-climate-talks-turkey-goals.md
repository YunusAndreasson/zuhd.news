Beijing — [China](country:CN) helped secure COP30's two-year climate finance work programme.

Delegates at COP31 in [Turkey](country:TR) are watching what Beijing demands next.

China also won a dialogue on unilateral trade measures and co-led a methane summit with [Brazil](country:BR) and the [UK](country:GB).

Environment vice-minister Li Gao wants COP31 to signal that countries are united on climate action.
