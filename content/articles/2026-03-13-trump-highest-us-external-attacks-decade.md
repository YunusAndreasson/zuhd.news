663 US military strikes hit foreign countries in 2025. Conflict-tracking group ACLED found the total rose 50% over 2024 to its highest since 2011 — all under US President Donald Trump, who ran on ending foreign wars. More than 1,000 people died in strikes that lacked congressional authorization for the majority of targets.
