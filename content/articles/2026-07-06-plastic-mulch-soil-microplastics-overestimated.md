Kunming — Farm plastic film explains just 8% of soil microplastics.

In Shandong province, compost and airborne fallout together contributed over half of soil microplastics — more than the film mulch regulators target.

Researchers say mitigation aimed only at farm plastic may be pulling the wrong lever.
