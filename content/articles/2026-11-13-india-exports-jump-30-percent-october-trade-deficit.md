New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s October trade deficit narrowed to $20.09 billion.

Imports still rose to $110.07 billion from $94.42 billion a year earlier.

Exports of goods and services hit $89.99 billion, up from $69.27 billion a year earlier, government data show.

Polymarket traders give a Reserve Bank of India hold in December a 17% chance, up 3 points in a week.
