Kazan — Putin and Southeast Asian leaders adopted the Kazan Declaration Wednesday.

The 2026-2030 action plan covers energy, liquefied natural gas, transport, and artificial intelligence; the declaration names [Russia](country:RU) a strategic partner of Southeast Asian nations.

The association pledged ties with 2 Russian-led regional blocs.
