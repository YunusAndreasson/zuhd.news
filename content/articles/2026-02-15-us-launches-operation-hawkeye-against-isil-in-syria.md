Operation Hawkeye has struck about 100 ISIS infrastructure targets over 2 months. US Central Command carried out precision strikes against more than 30 weapons storage facilities and infrastructure sites between February 3 and 12, killing or capturing more than 50 fighters since the operation began. The campaign is retaliation for a December ISIS attack near Palmyra that killed 2 US soldiers and 1 American civilian interpreter. The US military also completed the transfer of thousands of ISIS detainees from Syria to Iraq for trial, while Syria's new government took control of the al-Tanf base previously run by US forces. The strikes continue as post-Assad Syria works to extend authority across the country, with Washington distancing itself from its former Kurdish-led allies.
