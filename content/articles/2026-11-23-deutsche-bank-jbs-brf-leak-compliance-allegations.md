Brazil — Deutsche Bank kept meatpacker BRF as client despite rating it high-risk.

A leak of 15 million bank documents shows repeated ignored warnings, the Inside Deutsche Bank investigation says.

A June 2017 internal intelligence report accepted BRF's mitigation measures and said police mismanagement lessened the gravity of the charges.

Agência Pública, the project's only Brazilian partner, is publishing further reports from the leaked files.
