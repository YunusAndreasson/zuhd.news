A gang seized Cape Town's R600 million rail project. Ralph Stanfield, alleged boss of the 28s prison gang, drove contractors off Central Line worksites before Prasa — South Africa's passenger rail agency — steered the work to his companies. Stanfield faces trial in October 2026 as communities in Khayelitsha still wait for trains that a gang was paid to restore.
