United States — Nvidia added nearly $1 trillion Monday while Anthropic slipped 1.4%.

Anthropic, two days after listing, missed the Nasdaq's biggest gain of the year.

Trump said he convinced Nvidia's board to reinstate Huang, a champion of free open-weight models that undercut Anthropic's paid ones.

Nvidia agreed to invest $10 billion in Anthropic's $26 billion placement, Anthropic's filings show.

Polymarket traders put 16% odds on Anthropic reaching a $1.75 trillion valuation by 31 December, down 34 points in a week.
