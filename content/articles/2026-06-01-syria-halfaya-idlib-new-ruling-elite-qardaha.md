Damascus — Syria's new ministries fill with Halfaya's fighters.

18 months after Assad's fall, men from Halfaya and rural Idlib hold [Syria](country:SY)'s cabinet seats and security posts, displacing the Alawite patronage network of Qardaha.

Whether the shift broadens power or merely moves patronage from Alawite hills to revolutionary towns remains open.
