United States — World Labs generates explorable 3D scenes at up to 1440p and 15 frames a second.

Google DeepMind's Genie 3 runs faster, at 24 frames a second, but at 720p.

Frameboy streams frames from cloud graphics processors, so a new frame arrives about every 67 milliseconds.

Running it costs more than local rendering.

Its Viewfinder web app is in closed beta with a waitlist.
