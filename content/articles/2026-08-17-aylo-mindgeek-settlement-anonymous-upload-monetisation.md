Montreal — Aylo will pay $120 million, admitting no wrongdoing.

The sum settles claims from people whose childhood abuse was uploaded and monetised.

Anonymous uploads let the platform's ad and subscription systems distribute and profit from the material at scale.

Aylo still must verify every uploader's age and report abuse material under the deal's terms.
