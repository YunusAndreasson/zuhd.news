San Francisco — A brain implant decodes speech and gestures together.

Gestures carry hesitation and emphasis that speech-only interfaces cannot yet convey.

Two paralysed participants' gestures and speech were each predicted with 66 to 88 percent accuracy, researchers said.

Researchers aim next for a synthetic voice, without the avatar in between.
