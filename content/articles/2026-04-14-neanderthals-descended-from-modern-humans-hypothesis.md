Burgos — Ancient DNA may rewrite who Neanderthals descended from. A new hypothesis places early Homo sapiens as Neanderthal ancestors, not cousins — flipping the standard evolutionary model that placed them as a parallel branch. The hypothesis hinges on Homo antecessor DNA; no viable sample has survived at Atapuerca despite decades of excavation.
