US-Israeli strikes have damaged 56 museums and historic sites in Iran, including UNESCO-listed Golestan Palace in Tehran. The 1954 Hague Convention prohibits targeting cultural property in wartime. Iran's Cultural Heritage Ministry has not finished cataloguing losses as strikes enter their 15th day.
