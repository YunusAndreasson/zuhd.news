Beijing — Embodied-AI funding hit 4.93 billion yuan in 15 days.

Peak monthly funding in [China](country:CN)'s robot sector reached 33.98 billion yuan, IT Juzi data show.

Unitree shares fell over 63% in 36 trading days after listing, erasing more than 280 billion yuan of value.

Robot funding still totalled 124.51 billion yuan in the first eight months, twice 2025's full year, IT Juzi data show.

Investors cited in the report expect financing pressure to peak after November.
