Paris — France ordered internet providers to block Polymarket entirely.

Trading from [France](country:FR) has been disabled since November 2024, but the gambling regulator's order also blocks users who visit only to view market odds, not to trade.

Polymarket will contest the order in French courts; Ukraine, Argentina and Spain have issued similar blocks.
