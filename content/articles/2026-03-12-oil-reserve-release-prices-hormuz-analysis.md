400 million barrels of oil failed to cool markets. The International Energy Agency coordinated the release, but oil still rose 15% as traders priced future Hormuz supply cuts over today's inventory. Nations without strategic reserves face months of $100 crude, with South Asia and Pacific Islands most exposed.
