New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s top court froze a cow slaughter ban.

The Madras High Court ordered a blanket ban beyond a 1958 law permitting slaughter of unfit, aged cattle.

The Supreme Court has issued notice and will hear the state's full challenge next.
