Washington — Congress froze Trump battleship contracts, demanding weapons proof.

The FY2027 defense bill bars contracts until the [US](country:US) Navy certifies railguns, hypersonics and laser weapons are "sufficiently mature" — none are yet deployed.

The first 3 ships cost $43.5bn combined; analysts warn it mirrors the failed Zumwalt.
