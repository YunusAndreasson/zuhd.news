Ras Laffan — More Qatari LNG tankers cleared Hormuz over the weekend.

Each cargo transits a strait under security risk from the [US](country:US)-Israeli war on [Iran](country:IR).

Cargoes loaded at Ras Laffan reappeared outside the strait after transiting it, extending a recent rise in [Qatar](country:QA)'s LNG traffic.

Overall Hormuz traffic still averaged 3.1 ships a day to 27 September, 39% below normal.
