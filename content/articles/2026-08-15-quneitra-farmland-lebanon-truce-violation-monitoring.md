Quneitra — Israeli strikes burned farmland in [Syria](country:SY)'s Quneitra countryside.

The fire cost harvest-season families a year's income, with no deaths to tally.

The same day, jets also struck six towns in southern [Lebanon](country:LB), including Hula, using phosphorus shells, Al-Manar said.

No group has claimed the fire, and [Israel](country:IL) has not commented on either strike.
