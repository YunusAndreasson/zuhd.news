Jakarta — A Malacca toll would breach the same treaty that granted [Indonesia](country:ID) its archipelagic status. The UN Convention on the Law of the Sea bars transit levies and guarantees the strait's free passage. Jakarta has not specified a rate, enforcement mechanism, or legal defence.
