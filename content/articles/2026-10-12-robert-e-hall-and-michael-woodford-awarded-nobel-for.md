Stockholm — Two Americans won the economics Nobel for modelling economic shocks.

Their models now guide central banks and governments through energy crises, the prize committee said.

The 1970s oil crises broke the Keynesian models, so Robert Hall and Michael Woodford built new ones from households' and firms' behaviour.

The Federal Reserve decides on 28 October, and Polymarket traders price an 84% chance of no change.
