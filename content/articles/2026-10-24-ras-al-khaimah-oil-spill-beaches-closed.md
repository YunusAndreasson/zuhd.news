Ras Al Khaimah — Oil from a tanker has closed beaches in 3 emirates.

The environment ministry urged fishermen and other sea users to avoid affected areas.

The [UAE](country:AE) has not named the tanker or the cause, and the ministry is still assessing environmental risks.

The ministry says odours reported in Sharjah, Ajman and Umm Al Quwain pose no health risks.

Ras Al Khaimah's beaches stay closed until further notice.
