China summoned 7 tech companies to end AI price wars. The State Administration for Market Regulation called in Alibaba, ByteDance, Baidu, Tencent, and 3 others to stop what it calls "involutionary competition" — price undercutting triggered by DeepSeek's aggressive discounting that forced rivals into a race to the bottom. The regulator ordered the companies to curb promotions but specified no penalties, intervening to prevent market collapse while encouraging rapid AI development.
