Kabul — Global policy has shifted from protection to return.

International law defines a durable solution as voluntary repatriation, local integration or resettlement — not return under pressure, The Diplomat notes.

[Pakistan](country:PK) and [Iran](country:IR) continue deportations while the UN refugee agency presses for that standard to be met.
