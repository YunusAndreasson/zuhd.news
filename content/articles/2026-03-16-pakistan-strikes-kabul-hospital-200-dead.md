Pakistan struck a Kabul hospital, killing 200 patients. Pakistan's military has long targeted the Pakistani Taliban's Afghan sanctuaries; this strike came while US forces were fighting in Iran. No independent observers have reached the site, leaving both the death toll and any Taliban retaliation unconfirmed.
