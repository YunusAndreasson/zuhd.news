Caracas — 226,696 people have received care since June's earthquake, Assembly Speaker Jorge Rodríguez said.

That ratio against 6,509 dead shows a health system still absorbing a mass-casualty event weeks later.

More than 25,000 homes were damaged and 1,500 aftershocks recorded, Rodríguez's office said.

[Venezuela](country:VE)'s government remains the only source of the count, which has been revised upward repeatedly.
