Delhi — Delhi e-waste workers earn 4 rupees per kilogram.

[India](country:IN)'s informal sector is 45% of GDP but absent from just-transition policy; Seelampur's 50,000 recyclers work without contracts or safety gear.

India targets 47% lower emissions intensity by 2035; no plan funds retraining for the displaced.
