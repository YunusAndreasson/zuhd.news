Guangzhou — CATL supplied 75.8% of Yihong Intelligent's 2025 revenue, its prospectus shows.

The battery-equipment supplier seeks a Hong Kong listing with inventory 1.23 times a full year's sales.

Its gross margin fell from 30.9% to 26.2% in 2025, and the prospectus concedes battery giants hold strong bargaining power.

It still ranks first globally in wide-width roll-press slitting machines, with a 42.1% share by revenue.

The Hong Kong exchange has yet to rule on the filing.
