Lahore — An unpaid traffic camera fine could now block your national ID card.

That blocks bank accounts, SIM registration and travel, since daily life runs through the same ID.

Lahore's traffic chief proposed linking e-challan recovery to the national ID card, passport and vehicle-sale services.

A law firm plans to challenge the proposal at the Lahore High Court as unconstitutional.
