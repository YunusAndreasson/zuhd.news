Bloemfontein — The fine is a tenth of the hidden donation.

Non-disclosure stays profitable when penalties fall far below the concealed sum.

The Electoral Court found [South Africa](country:ZA)'s Good party deliberately hid a R900,000 donation before 2021 elections, fining it R90,000.

Good has said it will appeal the ruling.
