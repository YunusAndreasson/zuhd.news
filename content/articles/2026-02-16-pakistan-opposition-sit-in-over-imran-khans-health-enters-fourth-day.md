The opposition alliance Tehreek Tahaffuz-e-Aiyeen Pakistan has camped outside Parliament House for 4 days demanding the jailed former prime minister be transferred to a hospital, after the Supreme Court ordered a medical examination by February 16. The government sent a 5-member team to examine Khan in Adiala Jail on Sunday, but his party rejected the process because neither family nor personal physicians were present.
