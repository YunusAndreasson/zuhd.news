United States — Developers urge moving $300 million out of Zcash's Sapling.

Forged proofs could mint Zcash coins from nothing, and hidden amounts would mask the counterfeiting.

Sapling checks proofs with pairings, a technique a mathematical breakthrough or a large quantum computer could break.

No one has demonstrated a working attack, and Ironwood, which holds about 85% of private Zcash coins, does not use pairings.

No activation date is set for the upgrade that would stop new deposits into Sapling.
