Moscow — 40,000 tonnes of [Russian](country:RU) diesel head for the [US](country:US) Thursday.

US diesel averages $6.32 a gallon, nearly 75% above last year, AAA data show.

Ukrainian drone strikes cut Russian diesel exports 75% to 141,000 barrels a day in September, Kpler data show.

Kpler estimates the market needs at least 400,000 extra barrels a day, far above the deal's 75,000 for October.

Deputy Prime Minister Alexander Novak says more tankers will follow, within what Russian refineries can supply.
