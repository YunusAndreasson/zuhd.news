Nakhon Si Thammarat — 3D-printed pods anchor seedlings until they root.

Murdoch University and Walailak University researchers designed the dissolving bioplastic pods to solve restoration's hardest step: seedling survival through the first weeks.

Seagrass meadows sequester carbon faster than rainforest per hectare and nurse [Thailand](country:TH)'s coastal fisheries.
