Dhaka — The Arakan Army is killing Bangladeshi citizens.

[Bangladesh](country:BD) has no formal channel to the Arakan Army, which controls [Myanmar](country:MM)'s border but cannot be recognised as a government.

Dhaka has issued no formal protest; the conflict lacks a state interlocutor to receive one.
