Baghdad — [Iran](country:IR) mourns Supreme Leader Khamenei; Foreign Minister Araghchi arrived in Baghdad.

Araghchi met [Iraq](country:IQ)'s Fuad Hussein to consult on security and coordinate Khamenei's mourning rites at Iraq's holy shrines.

No successor has been announced; Iran's leadership transition overlaps with active Gulf conflict.
