New York — 38.3% of Pakistanis are multidimensionally poor, versus 16.4% in India.

The UN Development Programme counts 1.2 billion poor worldwide, the first rise in 15 years.

The index counts overlapping deprivations in nutrition, schooling, water and electricity rather than income, with [Bangladesh](country:BD) at 14.9%.

The surveys behind each country's figure date from different years, so the comparison is not strictly contemporaneous.

Another 12.9% of Pakistanis are classed as vulnerable to falling into it.
