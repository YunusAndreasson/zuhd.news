United States — A claimed proof of the Riemann hypothesis runs 250,000 pages.

If it holds, it settles the Clay Institute's $1 million prime-number problem after 167 years.

Anthropic's Levent Alpöge says he built it with Claude, and its Lean translation runs 17 million lines.

Cornell's Steven Strogatz says no one has independently verified the claimed proof.

A computer check of the Lean code comes first, and Scott Aaronson says full understanding may take years.
