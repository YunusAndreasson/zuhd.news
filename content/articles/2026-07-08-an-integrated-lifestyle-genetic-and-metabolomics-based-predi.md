New York — Diabetes outranked age and genetics in predicting decline.

A study of Hispanic and Latino adults found diabetes and a blood marker called ribitol beat standard genetic risk scores at forecasting mild cognitive impairment.

Researchers say screening tools should add diabetes status rather than rely on genetic markers alone.
