Islamabad — EY will advise on outsourcing Karachi and Lahore airports.

Private operators would run both under a concession or management contract.

Open bidding replaced government-to-government talks this year, and the Privatisation Commission expects over $500 million in fresh investment at each airport.

Islamabad airport, slated for outsourcing since December 2022, is not covered by this agreement.
