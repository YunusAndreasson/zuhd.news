Akure — Ondo's civil servants now get paid through an AI system.

Personnel records, payroll and pension migration for every state worker now sit on one platform.

Ondo AI alerts the government as workers near retirement and moves them onto pensions, developer SmartApps IT Limited said.

Governor Lucky Aiyedatiwa told staff who enter records to ensure accuracy, since payroll now depends on them.
