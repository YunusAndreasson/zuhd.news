Patients now travel days through conflict to reach overwhelmed hospitals.

Funding cuts hit peripheral clinics first in [Sudan](country:SD)'s war, since referral hospitals absorb the load until they too fail.

The UN's $2.87 billion response plan remains just 41% funded.
