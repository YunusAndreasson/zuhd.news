London — People underestimate how much peers support climate action.

Researchers surveyed over 5,000 people in [Germany](country:DE) and the [US](country:US) and found correcting that misperception did not increase real donations or actions.

The finding challenges norm-correction campaigns many climate and health programs already rely on worldwide.
