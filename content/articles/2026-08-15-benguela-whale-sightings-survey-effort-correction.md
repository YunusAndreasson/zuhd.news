Walvis Bay — Blue and fin whales are returning.

Their numbers remain low, but sightings are climbing four decades after whaling ended.

Researchers logged 95% of 60 years of sightings and strandings off [Namibia](country:NA) and [South Africa](country:ZA) since 2012 alone.

The study doesn't yet say whether the rise reflects recovery or just better observation.
