Geneva — Fallen debris cannot be traced to its operator.

Stronger spacecraft materials prevent reentry burnup, routing surviving debris toward populations living below high-traffic orbital paths.

The 1972 Outer Space Liability Convention assigns fault but provides no mechanism to identify which operator's debris caused damage.
