Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK) called the EU's compliance report unbalanced.

Duty-free access to European markets under the GSP+ scheme hinges on meeting 27 rights and governance conventions; Brussels found Pakistan had regressed on several.

The Foreign Office disputes the finding but says GSP+ remains central to its exports.
