New York — Bitcoin ETFs shed $4.5 billion in June.

BlackRock's IBIT alone lost $3.55 billion over a 9-day outflow streak, following the Fed's shift toward rate hikes under new chair Kevin Warsh.

Total fund assets have dropped to $71 billion from $83 billion at the month's start.
