Iran's strikes on UAE soil ended Dubai's reputation as a regional safe haven. Property transactions fell 49% month-on-month in early March, with Goldman Sachs estimating a 37% year-on-year drop. Citi forecasts prices falling 7% annually through 2028 if wealthy investors keep leaving for safer markets.
