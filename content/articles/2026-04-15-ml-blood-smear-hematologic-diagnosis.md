London — CAREMIL reads a blood smear like a hematologist. Existing tools classify individual cells; CAREMIL reads the whole slide's population, detecting AML even when circulating blasts are absent. The framework extends to bone marrow aspirates and cytology, shifting blood cancer diagnosis toward quantitative morphology-based reads.
