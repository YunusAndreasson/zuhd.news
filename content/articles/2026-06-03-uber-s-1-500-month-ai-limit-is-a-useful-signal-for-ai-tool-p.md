San Francisco — Uber burned its 2026 AI budget by April.

Uber caps each AI coding tool at $1,500 per engineer monthly — about 11% of median [US](country:US) software-engineer pay annually, the author calculates.

Individual subscribers pay ~$100 monthly for equivalent tokens; that subsidy cannot survive at enterprise scale.
