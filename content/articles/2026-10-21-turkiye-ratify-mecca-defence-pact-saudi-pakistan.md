Ankara — A 16-article pact would bind [Turkey](country:TR) to defend Riyadh.

An attack on [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) or [Pakistan](country:PK) counts as an attack on all three.

Article 2 lets each state set its support, and Turkey's constitution requires separate parliamentary approval to deploy troops abroad.

Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan says Turkey will not send troops to attack another country's territory.

Parliament's foreign affairs commission could take up the pact as early as Friday.
