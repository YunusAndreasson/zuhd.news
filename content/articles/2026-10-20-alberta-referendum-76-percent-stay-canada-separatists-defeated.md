Edmonton — Albertans voted 76% to stay in [Canada](country:CA), early results show.

The loss tests Premier Danielle Smith, who lowered the petition threshold that put the question on the ballot.

A yes would not have meant independence, only a path toward a later separation vote.

Smith also put 9 questions on more autonomy from Ottawa to voters, and early results suggest many pass.

Smith addresses the province Tuesday evening on what comes next.
