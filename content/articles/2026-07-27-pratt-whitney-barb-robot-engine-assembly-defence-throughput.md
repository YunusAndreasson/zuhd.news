Middletown — BARB now assembles three bearings on Pratt & Whitney's engine line.

Aero-engine work resisted automation because every step must trace to a validated process; qualifying the robot's cameras to read new, shiny parts took over a year.

The $20 million system covers one station of the line; the rest, in both commercial and military engine assembly, stays manual.
