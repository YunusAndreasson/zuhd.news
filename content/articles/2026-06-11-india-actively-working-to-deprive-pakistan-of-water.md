New Delhi — [India](country:IN) pledged zero river flow into Pakistan.

Minister CR Patil said India is "actively working" on blocking the Indus after Modi's directive; new diversions cannot begin before mid-2027, an official said.

[Pakistan](country:PK) called any diversion an act of war and has urged the UN Security Council to act.
