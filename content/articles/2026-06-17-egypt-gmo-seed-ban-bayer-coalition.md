Cairo — Bayer and [Egypt](country:EG)'s Future of Egypt authority are co-lobbying to lift a 14-year ban on genetically modified seeds.

Legalization would let Bayer sell patented seed that farmers cannot save and replant.

Future of Egypt stands to gain seed-import privileges and a joint manufacturing facility if the ban falls.
