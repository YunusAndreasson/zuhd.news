Beijing — A [China](country:CN) probe is closing on a quasi-moon.

The target is one of eight known asteroids that orbit the sun in near-lockstep with Earth; the mission will test whether it is lunar-impact debris.

Matching orbit with and sampling such a small, fast body is a demanding navigation feat.
