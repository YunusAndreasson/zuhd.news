Half the US is in drought. US plants treat 33 billion gallons of sewage daily but recover only 7% for reuse, a gap now forcing states to upgrade treatment infrastructure. A survey found voters prefer higher bills over water restrictions, giving state legislators new room to mandate potable reuse programs.
