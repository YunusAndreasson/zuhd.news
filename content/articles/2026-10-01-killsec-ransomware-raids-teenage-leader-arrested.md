Hamburg — Police in [Spain](country:ES) arrested KillSec's suspected 16-year-old leader.

KillSec launched around 1,000 attacks since 2024, at least half successful, authorities said.

Its cheap ransomware-as-a-service panel let low-skill criminals exploit cloud-storage flaws and extort victims through a leak site.

Police identified at least 4 suspected members and are still investigating others.

Dutch suspect Fouad Eltibrizi, indicted in Puerto Rico on 16 September, awaits extradition from Britain to the US.
