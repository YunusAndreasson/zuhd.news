Washington — Trump named intelligence director Jay Clayton to lead a new task force.

Trump's post gives the "Super Intelligence Force" a coordinating role, not power over companies.

Trade commission chairman Andrew Ferguson, Pentagon technology chief Emil Michael and personnel chief Scott Kupor join, reporting to Trump and Susie Wiles.

A separate White House accord asks AI companies for voluntary outside safety audits, and says laws may come later.

The accord's companies will meet regularly to set safety standards, but no date was announced.
