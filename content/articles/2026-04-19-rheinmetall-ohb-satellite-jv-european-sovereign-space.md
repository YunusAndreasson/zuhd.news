Berlin — [Germany](country:DE) cleared Rheinmetall-OHB to build military satellites.

Germany has committed €35 billion to space defense by 2030, building sovereign constellations for surveillance, reconnaissance, and communications.

The new joint venture bids for Germany's communications constellation contract; the European Space Agency's director-general has warned against national program fragmentation.
