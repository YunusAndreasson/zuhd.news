The man who ordered Villavicencio's killing is in custody. Fernando Villavicencio was a lawmaker shot dead at a campaign rally in 2023 after naming politicians linked to narco networks. Aguilar Morales, alias Lobo Menor, was caught living under a false Colombian identity — Ecuador has not set an extradition date.
