Astana — The pipeline still fills, but the tankers can't leave.

Drone strikes on the Caspian Pipeline Consortium's Novorossiysk loading fleet, reported through Russian state media, have not stopped intake, the Kazakh Energy Ministry said, so storage is filling toward capacity.

Kazakhstan ships roughly 80% of its crude through this Russian route; no alternative can absorb a shut-in.
