Washington — House committee killed an amendment blocking US-Israel defense-data fusion.

Section 224 embeds $3.8 billion in [US](country:US) annual aid into Pentagon-[Israel](country:IL) co-production, fusing defense data and AI with limited congressional oversight.

The defense bill heads to a House vote; critics say Section 224 bypasses a direct vote on aid.
