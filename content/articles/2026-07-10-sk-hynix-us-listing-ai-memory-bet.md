Seoul — SK Hynix raised $26.5 billion on Wall Street.

The chipmaker supplies the high-bandwidth memory that gates Nvidia's AI accelerators; the Nasdaq offering was oversubscribed more than sevenfold.

Proceeds go to new fabs and packaging lines as [South Korea](country:KR)'s memory giant races to match AI demand.
