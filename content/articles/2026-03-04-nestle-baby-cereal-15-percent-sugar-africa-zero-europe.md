Nestlé's African baby cereal is up to 15% sugar. The same Cerelac sold in Europe contains no added sugar; WHO recommends zero for children under 2, but African regulators cannot enforce it. Public Eye is calling on Nestlé to apply its zero-sugar European standard globally.
