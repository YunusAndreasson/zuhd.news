Mumbai — Amazon wants a 49-acre campus near Mumbai. The 6-building complex will draw 473 MW of electricity daily and discharge 8 kiloliters of mineral-rich cooling wastewater; it requires formal environmental clearance before construction. Amazon plans to scale India to 3 GW total; Mumbai already holds 49% of the country's data centre capacity.
