Global — Debian's developers voted to permit, not ban, generative AI in its packages.

The choice sets a de facto template for every downstream that inherits Debian's policy.

Debian's guarantee is provenance, tracing each package to a maintainer, so the vote turned on licensing, not code quality.

Contributors must still review and test any AI-assisted work before it enters the archive.
