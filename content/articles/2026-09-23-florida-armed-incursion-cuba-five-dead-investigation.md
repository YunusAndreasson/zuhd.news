Havana — Five men died in a raid launched from Florida.

Ten Cuban exiles armed and launched from [US](country:US) soil aiming to topple a foreign government.

Recruited via TikTok, the ten men crossed from the Florida Keys planning to seize a police station and spark an uprising.

"They were looking for people who had the courage to join an armed uprising," said survivor Rodríguez Castelló, 33.

No one has confirmed whether US authorities knew of the plot beforehand or will charge its organizers.
