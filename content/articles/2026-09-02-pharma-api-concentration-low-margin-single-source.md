Washington — Common antibiotics and blood thinners depend on [China](country:CN).

Analysts warn American patients would have no reliable alternative if Chinese supply broke.

Active-ingredient manufacturing migrated to China over decades because production there costs far less.

No US or allied manufacturer has been named as a backup source.
