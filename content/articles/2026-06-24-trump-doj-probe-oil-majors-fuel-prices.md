Washington — [US](country:US) pump prices lag crude's $120-to-$76 drop.

Trump directed the Justice Department to probe oil majors after retail pump prices failed to track crude's fall from May's $120 peak.

The American Automobile Association put the average at $3.93 a gallon; a former energy secretary predicted $3 by Labor Day.
