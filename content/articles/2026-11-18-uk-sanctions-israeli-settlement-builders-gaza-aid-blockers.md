London — [Britain](country:GB) froze the assets of 8 Israeli settlement builders.

Tzav 9, a settler group blocking food, water and fuel trucks into Gaza, was also sanctioned.

Har Hevron Development, Hemdat Shomron and SAG Construction build West Bank homes, and the freezes landed before final tenders for E1, which would bisect the territory.

Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband told bidders for E1: "do not bid, do not build, and do not profit from the destruction of the two-state solution."
