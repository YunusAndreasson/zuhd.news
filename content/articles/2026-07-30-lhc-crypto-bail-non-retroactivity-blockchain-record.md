Lahore — A blockchain never forgets a transaction the law later criminalised.

Justice Tariq Saleem Sheikh confirmed bail for three peer-to-peer traders, ruling [Pakistan](country:PK)'s 2026 Virtual Assets Act cannot reach 2021-23 trades made before it existed.

The case continues on fraud claims, but the non-retroactivity ruling now shields millions of pre-law crypto users.
