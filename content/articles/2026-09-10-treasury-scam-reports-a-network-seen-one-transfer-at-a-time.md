Washington — Cyber scams have cost $13 billion since 2023.

Victims lose savings while trafficked workers forced to run scams go uncounted.

Scam money moves through ordinary transfers into mule accounts, then crypto, and only a shared reporting key reveals the pattern.

Treasury wants banks to flag these transfers so FinCEN can trace scam networks.
