Culiacán — Two of El Chapo's sons remain at large.

[US](country:US) investigators raised bounties on Iván Archivaldo and Jesús Alfredo Guzmán after their two brothers were extradited in 2023 and 2024.

[Mexico](country:MX)'s Sinaloa governor, indicted in April for taking their bribes, still awaits trial.
