Nairobi — No African nation has emergency oil reserves. The International Energy Agency holds 1.4 billion barrels for its 31 mostly wealthy member states — none of them African — as Hormuz-driven price spikes hit the continent. Nigeria's rising oil revenues bypass citizens facing fuel shortages, while Ethiopia has begun subsidizing pump prices as a stopgap.
