Rome — Two Russian military attachés have 3 days to leave.

[Italy](country:IT) expelled them after arresting two former intelligence officers accused of feeding [Russia](country:RU) military-aid secrets through a diplomatically immune handler.

Moscow's foreign ministry says it will give an "appropriate response" to the expulsions.
