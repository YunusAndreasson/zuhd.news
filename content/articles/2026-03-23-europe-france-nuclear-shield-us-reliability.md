Brussels — 8 European nations are seeking France's nuclear protection. Trump's Iran strikes, launched without a congressional vote, shattered European confidence in US security guarantees, pushing allies toward France's nuclear deterrent despite Paris ruling out explicit commitments. Denmark, which administers Greenland, is among the 8, making French nuclear doctrine directly relevant to Trump's annexation threats.
