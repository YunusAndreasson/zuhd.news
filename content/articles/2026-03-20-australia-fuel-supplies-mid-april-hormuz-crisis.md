Petrol stations are already limiting fuel purchases across Australia. Australia closed its last major refinery years ago, making it entirely dependent on imported fuel that now travels a Hormuz-blocked route. The government says reserves last until mid-April, after which supply depends entirely on how long the Hormuz closure continues.
