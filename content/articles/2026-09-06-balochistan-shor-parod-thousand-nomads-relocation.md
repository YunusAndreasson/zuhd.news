Quetta — 1,000 nomads face relocation with no return date.

A security operation against militants proceeds by clearing a pastoral community not party to the fighting.

Chief Minister Sarfraz Bugti called it temporary, needed for forces' "enabling environment" in Shor Parod.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s assembly got no destination, compensation figure, or return timeline.
