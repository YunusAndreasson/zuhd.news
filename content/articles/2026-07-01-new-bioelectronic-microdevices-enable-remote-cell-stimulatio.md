Barcelona — Wireless chips now stimulate single living cells.

Researchers at the Autonomous University of Barcelona built piezoelectric microdevices that convert ultrasound into electrical signals, activating cells without wires or implants.

The approach opens a path toward noninvasive, targeted therapies that skip surgery entirely.
