Abu Dhabi — The United Arab Emirates downed an Iranian drone offshore.

It is the second Gulf state in two days forced to fire on stray ordnance.

[US](country:US) forces struck Iran's Larak Island a day earlier, and Iran said it retaliated by hitting bases in Jordan.

[Iran](country:IR)'s foreign ministry vowed a "decisive" response to any further aggression.
