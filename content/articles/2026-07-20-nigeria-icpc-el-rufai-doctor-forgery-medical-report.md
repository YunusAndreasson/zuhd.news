Abuja — An oncologist faces 4 forgery counts over a bail report.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s anti-graft commission says the report backed former governor Nasir El-Rufai's bail bid; the hospital says he was never a registered patient.

The doctor faces trial alongside El-Rufai's separate corruption cases in Kaduna State High Court.
