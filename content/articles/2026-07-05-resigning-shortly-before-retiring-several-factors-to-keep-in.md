Johannesburg — Resigning weeks early can trade a guaranteed pension for market risk.

Financial advisers warn workers who swap an 80%-of-salary defined benefit for a living annuity take on inflation, drawdown and longevity risk the fund previously absorbed.

Advisers say resigning early rarely justifies giving up a lifetime income unless the numbers overwhelmingly favor it.
