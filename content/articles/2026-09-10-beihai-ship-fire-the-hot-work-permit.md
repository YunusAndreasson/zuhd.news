Qingdao — 20 dead, 5 missing, on a ship docked for repairs, not at sea.

Ship repair means welding inside enclosed holds still carrying fuel residue and solvent fumes.

The Liberian-flagged Ocean Melody, at [China](country:CN)'s Beihai Shipbuilding yard, had 42 people aboard when fire broke out Thursday.

Xi Jinping ordered an investigation into who bears responsibility for the blaze.
