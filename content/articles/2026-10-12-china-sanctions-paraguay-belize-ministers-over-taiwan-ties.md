Beijing — [China](country:CN) sanctioned Paraguay's communications minister and Belize's foreign minister.

[Paraguay](country:PY) and [Belize](country:BZ) are 2 of the 12 states with formal ties to Taiwan.

Gustavo Villate and Francis Fonseca face frozen Chinese assets, Hong Kong and Macau visa bans, and a ban on Chinese dealings with them.

"Paraguay reaffirms its relationship of friendship and cooperation," Villate wrote on Taiwan's National Day.

Beijing named no one in Guatemala or Haiti, Taiwan's other allies in the Americas.
