Kyiv — Zelensky fired the minister who cut Ukraine's arms bill.

Mykhailo Fedorov had forced competitive tenders on defense suppliers, replacing inflated no-bid contracts that padded politically connected firms' margins.

[Ukraine](country:UA)'s acting security chief Yevhenii Khmara now runs the ministry Fedorov spent two years reforming.
