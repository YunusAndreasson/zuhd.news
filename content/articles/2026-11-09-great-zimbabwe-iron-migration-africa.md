Great Zimbabwe — Its iron furnaces needed no bellows.

Bantu-speaking migrants carried metalworking across southern Africa, and 350 million Africans speak their languages today.

Fused clay tuyeres drew air in and out through a chimney, feeding the 1,150°C that iron smelting needs.

Linguists, archaeologists and geneticists still debate the migration's route and timing.
