London — Frail candidates faced triple the removal risk.

Removal from a transplant waitlist is a committee decision, not a diagnosis.

In 279 candidates followed 22.8 months, 25.6% of frail patients died or were delisted, versus 8.1% of robust ones.

The authors do not say whether the scale should inform decisions or gatekeep them.
