US-Israeli strikes now target Hormuz refineries and oil tankers. Gaza's war already generated 33 million tonnes of CO2 — one-tenth of Kuwait's 1991 oil well fires, which released 400 million tonnes. No international treaty requires states to account for war's carbon emissions or remediate contaminated soil and groundwater.
