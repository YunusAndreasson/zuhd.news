Damascus — Syria's sole rail line runs on Soviet locomotives.

[Syria](country:SY) is cast as a Gulf-to-Mediterranean rail hub; its last working freight line takes a full day on a once-4-hour route.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA), [Turkey](country:TR), and [Jordan](country:JO) signed a rail accord with Damascus but have confirmed no timeline or funding.
