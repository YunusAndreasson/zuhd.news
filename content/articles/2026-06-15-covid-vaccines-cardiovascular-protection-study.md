Washington — Updated COVID shots cut major cardiac events 38%, a 1-million-patient study found.

Benefits concentrate in adults over 75; only 17.5% of [US](country:US) adults got the 2025–2026 booster, federal data shows.

Shots also cut COVID hospitalization 35%; Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. has opposed vaccination.
