Singapore — Nvidia stopped server shipments to [Malaysia](country:MY), [Singapore](country:SG), [Thailand](country:TH) and [Indonesia](country:ID).

Chinese firms rent that computing power, and billions of dollars of data centre projects sit under construction.

The [US](country:US) Commerce Department's rule would require licences for operators supplying major computing power to firms based in [China](country:CN), Russia or Iran.

Dell and Supermicro told buyers in the four countries that Nvidia would not allow their orders to ship.

The Commerce Department is still reviewing the rule, and it is unclear whether the halt is temporary.
