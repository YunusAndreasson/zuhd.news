Wana — Unidentified gunmen killed Muhammad Khalid near a cemetery in Doag.

No group has claimed the killing, leaving [Pakistan](country:PK)'s decade-old "good Taliban" strategy unaccounted for.

Khalid once aided Mullah Nazir's faction, armed by the state as a counterweight before US drones killed Nazir in 2013.

Police have opened an investigation but named no suspects.
