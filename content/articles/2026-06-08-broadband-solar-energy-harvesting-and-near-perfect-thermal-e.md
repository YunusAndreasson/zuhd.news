London — Titanium nitride absorbs 99.27% of solar radiation.

Noble metals degrade at high temperatures; refractory titanium nitride achieves 99.27% absorptivity across 150–3,000 nm with near-perfect thermal emission to 1,500 K.

The design targets solar thermophotovoltaics in heat-stressed regions where conventional absorbers fail.
