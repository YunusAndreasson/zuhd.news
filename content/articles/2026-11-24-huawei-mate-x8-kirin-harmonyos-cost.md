Shanghai — Huawei says each Mate X8 costs 2,000 yuan more to build.

The price holds at 12,999 yuan, so Huawei carries the rise, executive Richard Yu said.

The phone runs Huawei's own Kirin 9050 Pro chip and HarmonyOS, which Yu said passed 100 million devices.

Yu said Huawei holds 70% of China's foldable market and more than 12 million large foldables shipped.
