Brasília — Lula overturned a first-round deficit with 4.4 million votes to spare.

No first-round loser had won [Brazil](country:BR)'s runoff since direct elections resumed in 1989.

Abstention fell from 21% to 19%, and judges added 2 hours of voting after Lula's campaign alleged bus shortages.

Flávio Bolsonaro is seeking annulment, but the Supreme Court president has confirmed the result.

Lula, 81 on Tuesday, faces a Congress dominated by the right.
