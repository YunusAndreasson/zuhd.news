Kyiv — A September vote could hand Russia 300,000 more troops, Zelenskyy said.

An occupied, displaced electorate cannot produce a legitimate roll, so [Ukraine](country:UA)'s vote stays frozen.

Fired minister Mykhailo Fedorov had pushed for elections, drawing Zelenskyy's "tsunami" warning that a vote would split the country.

Allies weigh conditions for a safe wartime vote as [Russia](country:RU) builds a winter offensive.
