Dahshur — Bone strain shows princesses fired real bows.

Bioarchaeologists studied muscle attachments and bone stress on 4,000-year-old mummies, finding signs of repetitive, high-intensity weapon use, not symbolic burial goods.

The findings challenge a century of assumptions about gender and power in ancient [Egypt](country:EG).
