Balisan — Drones targeted Komala's Iraq base, [Iran](country:IR)'s state-run Mehr News Agency reported.

Balisan lies in [Iraq](country:IQ), so the claimed strike crosses an international border.

The Revolutionary Guard's ground forces flew the drones overnight, Mehr said, calling Komala a terrorist and separatist group.

Mehr claimed significant damage and casualties but gave no figures, saying they remain under review.
