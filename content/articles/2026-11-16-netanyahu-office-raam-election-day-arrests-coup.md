Jerusalem — Police arrested 3 Arab-party members on unfounded information.

Arab parties won a record number of seats, and neither bloc holds 61.

Netanyahu's chief of staff reportedly pointed police to a former office employee, whose information led to the arrests.

Netanyahu's office denies it, saying Ido Norden only passed on criminal information and never spoke with police intelligence.

Gadi Eisenkot says he will seek a commission of inquiry while negotiating a coalition.
