Mediterranean — Eastern and western sperm whale groups use distinct click dialects.

Dialect is transmitted socially, not genetically; eastern whales evolved a faster version after splitting from the west 20,000 years ago, researchers said.

The Mediterranean population of a few thousand gives the first snapshot of a dialect forming in real time.
