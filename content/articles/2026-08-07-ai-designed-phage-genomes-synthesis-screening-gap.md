Stanford — A model designed 16 working virus genomes.

The genomes were small enough for the model to hold whole and target only bacteria, not humans, researchers said.

DNA synthesis vendors screen voluntarily against known agents; no international rule governs AI-generated sequences, Johns Hopkins researchers warned.
