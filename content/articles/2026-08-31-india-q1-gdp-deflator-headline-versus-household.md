New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s economy grew 7.8% in the June quarter.

The beat eases pressure on Modi's government after months of slowdown warnings.

Nominal GDP rose 10.3%, faster than the 7% real growth the central bank had forecast.

The ministry has not yet released the private-consumption breakdown behind the headline number.
