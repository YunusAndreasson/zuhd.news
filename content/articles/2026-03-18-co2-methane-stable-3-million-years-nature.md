---
title: "CO2 Was Stable for Millennia"
date: "2026-03-18T00:00:00Z"
source: "Nature"
sourceUrl: "https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-10032-y"
category: "science"
---

Modern CO2 levels are unprecedented over 3 million years. Ice cores from Antarctica's Allan Hills show CO2 held within 10 ppm for that entire span — today's rise of 170 ppm exceeds the full natural range of the record. The data extends ice core records to the Pliocene, giving scientists the oldest direct atmospheric sample of a warming world.
