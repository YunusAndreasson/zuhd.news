Protesters fought police as People Power turned 40. The 1986 revolution toppled dictator Ferdinand Marcos Sr. over corruption; his son Ferdinand Marcos Jr., now president, faces the same accusation over flood-control funds. The Marcos government has opened no investigation into the allegations.
