Los Angeles — Newsom commuted all 548 California death sentences Thursday.

California's death row, the Western Hemisphere's largest, held roughly a quarter of US sentences.

Every inmate now faces life in prison, Newsom wrote in a three-page letter as his second term nears its end.

Victims' advocates say they will challenge the commutations in court.

Newsom, expected to seek the Democratic presidential nomination in 2028, is in his final weeks as governor.
