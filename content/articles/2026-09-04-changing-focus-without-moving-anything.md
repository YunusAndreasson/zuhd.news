Daejeon — A flat lens now changes focus electrically.

It could bring retinal-vessel imaging within reach of routine, low-cost checkups.

Photoacoustic imaging fires a laser and listens for tissue's ultrasound echo, trading depth for resolution until this switchable lens.

Diabetic retinopathy screening could shift from bulky scanners to a single probe.
