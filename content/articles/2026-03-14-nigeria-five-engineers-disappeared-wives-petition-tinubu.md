5 engineers vanished, their killers named but never charged. Ebonyi State's former governor publicly identified the perpetrators of the 2021 abduction without prompting any arrest in the 5 years since. Their wives petitioned President Tinubu and parliament to force prosecution, warning they will go public if ignored.
