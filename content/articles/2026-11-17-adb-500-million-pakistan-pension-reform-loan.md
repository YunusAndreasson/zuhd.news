Pakistan — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s federal pension bill now exceeds Rs1 trillion a year.

The Asian Development Bank lent $500 million after the bill grew nearly fivefold since 2016.

The unfunded scheme is paid from annual budget allocations, and staff hired from July 2024 must join a contribution scheme.

The loan funds only the first phase of the government's Pension Reform Implementation Plan.
