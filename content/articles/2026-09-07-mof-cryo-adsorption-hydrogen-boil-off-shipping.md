London — A material packs near-liquid hydrogen density in tanks.

Hydrogen export plans for the Gulf and Australia depend on solving this.

The framework adsorbs gas onto internal surfaces rather than compressing it, letting tanks run warmer and safer.

Researchers demonstrated the material in lab tanks, not yet a working ship.
