Utilities surged 6.8% in February. President Javier Milei's austerity removed electricity and gas subsidies, making utilities the biggest driver of Argentina's 33% annual inflation. The statistics agency chief resigned this week over methodology disputes, leaving Milei's August 1%-monthly inflation target unverifiable.
