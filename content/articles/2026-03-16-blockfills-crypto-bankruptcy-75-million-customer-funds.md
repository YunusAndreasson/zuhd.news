BlockFills filed for bankruptcy after losing $75 million. A court froze its assets after customers alleged misappropriation of funds — the same absence of fund-segregation rules that toppled FTX. BlockFills reports $50-100 million in assets against liabilities up to $500 million, leaving creditors to compete in Delaware bankruptcy court.
