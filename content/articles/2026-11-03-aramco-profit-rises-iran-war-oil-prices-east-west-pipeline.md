Dhahran — Aramco's third-quarter profit hit $31.2 billion, up 9%.

A 35% higher average selling price outweighed output still below prewar levels.

Aramco is expanding its East-West pipeline, which carries up to 7 million barrels a day and bypasses the Strait of Hormuz.

Aramco's shares have still lagged majors that operate outside the Gulf.

CEO Amin Nasser says refilling depleted global stocks would take two years even if the war ended today.
