Delhi — Planners reopened land once ruled unbuildable.

Seventy peripheral villages gain commercial development rights, while flood-zone protection narrows.

An appointed authority, not an elected council, split the Yamuna floodplain into a protected core and a buildable "outer buffer."

The plan now goes to the housing ministry for final approval before it takes force.
