Islamabad — Jazz allegedly charged customers Rs5.83 billion above approved tariffs.

The audit objection says [Pakistan](country:PK)'s regulator allowed a 15% Jazz tariff increase against a 3% limit.

The regulator says it vets packages before launch and monitors compliance with approved tariffs.

Regulator chairman Hafeezur Rehman said he would resign if even one rupee of overcharging is established.

Jazz says it will present its view if called before any relevant forum.
