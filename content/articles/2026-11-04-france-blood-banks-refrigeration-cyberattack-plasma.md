Paris — Attackers tried to raise plasma storage temperatures past boiling.

The French Blood Establishment says no blood products were lost and distribution never stopped.

Attackers hit a refrigeration provider's control systems at 3 cold-storage sites, and monitoring alarms flagged the anomalies.

Coolworld, the equipment supplier, blames a third-party component outside its direct control.

[France](country:FR)'s cybersecurity agency and Health Ministry are investigating and have named no suspect.
