Kampala — Bobi Wine fled Uganda, surfacing at the US Capitol. Wine, Uganda's main opposition leader, lost the 2021 presidential race to Yoweri Museveni, who has held power since 1986. Wine said he will meet American officials this week to seek pressure on Museveni to allow his return.
