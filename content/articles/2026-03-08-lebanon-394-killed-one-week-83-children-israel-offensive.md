Israel killed 83 children in Lebanon since March 2. Israeli forces advanced tanks into southern Lebanon and struck Beirut, bringing the week's death toll to 394; 2 Israeli soldiers also died in ground combat. The offensive has displaced 454,000 people; Israeli tanks are pushing toward Christian villages that refused to evacuate.
