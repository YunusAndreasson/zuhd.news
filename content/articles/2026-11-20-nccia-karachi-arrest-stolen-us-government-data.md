Karachi — Pakistani agents arrested a man accused of selling FBI data.

The agency says the data also covered the CIA, State Department, Pentagon and [US](country:US) police departments.

Under the alias DP BRUCE, the suspect allegedly advertised databases on LeakBase and Telegram and took Bitcoin payments, the agency said.

Its preliminary examination says only that seized files appear to contain sensitive US government information.

The case is registered under 5 sections of Pakistan's cybercrime law, including electronic fraud and forgery.
