Iran fired 2 ballistic missiles into Turkish airspace this week. Both flew near Kürecik — NATO's early-warning radar in Malatya that gives the alliance 15 minutes' warning of launches toward Europe. Turkey has not invoked Article 5 despite NATO rushing PAC-3 Patriot batteries from Germany to Malatya.
