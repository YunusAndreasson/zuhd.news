Nigeria signed a £746m deal for Lagos ports. Akwa Ibom Governor Umo Eno joined the ruling APC in June 2025 on a federal promise to fund his state's deep seaport — a project that has not broken ground. Whether Eno secured Akwa Ibom's inclusion will determine if his defection from the People's Democratic Party cost his state nothing.
