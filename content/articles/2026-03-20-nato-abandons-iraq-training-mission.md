Several hundred NATO trainers left Iraq on Friday. The Iran war forced the redeployment, ending a 10-year mission that trained Iraqi security forces against ISIS remnants. Iraqi forces in contested areas confront ISIS without on-the-ground advisers for the first time in a decade.
