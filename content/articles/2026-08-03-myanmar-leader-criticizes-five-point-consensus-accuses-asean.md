Naypyidaw — Min Aung Hlaing will pursue his own "solution."

On his 100th day as president, he told parliament he is discarding the Five-Point Consensus the Association of Southeast Asian Nations has used since 2021.

The bloc's non-interference rule leaves it no other instrument to respond with.
