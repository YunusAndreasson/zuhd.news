Soyuz MS-29 docked with the space station as [Russia](country:RU) and the [US](country:US) agreed to coordinate satellite tracking, since shared life-support systems force cooperation that politics blocks elsewhere.

The crew's 261-day mission runs as broader plans, like Russian involvement in Artemis, remain stalled.
