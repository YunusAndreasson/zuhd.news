Washington — Justice Department won't reopen its Jerome Powell probe.

Powell, the former Federal Reserve chair, can keep his board seat until early 2028.

The Fed's inspector general found management deficiencies in the $2.5bn renovation but no grounds to suspect a federal crime.

Trump says Powell should be forced to resign immediately over the overrun.

Attorney General Todd Blanche has not ruled out action if evidence of wrongdoing emerges.
