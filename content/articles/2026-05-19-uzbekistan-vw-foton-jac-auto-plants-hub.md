Tashkent — Volkswagen, Foton, and JAC will all open [Uzbekistan](country:UZ) assembly plants by year-end.

Volkswagen begins in Tashkent then scales to the Angren free economic zone; Foton and JAC launch simultaneously, the presidential press service said.

President Mirziyoyev approved an export phase; no target countries or volumes have been disclosed.
