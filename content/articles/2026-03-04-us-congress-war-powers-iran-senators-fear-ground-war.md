Democratic senators fear Trump plans a ground invasion of Iran. Senator Tim Kaine invoked the 1973 War Powers Resolution, which would require Trump to cease operations within 60 days without congressional authorization. A veto override requires two-thirds of both chambers; the resolution has not yet been scheduled for a Senate floor vote.
