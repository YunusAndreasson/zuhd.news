London — Sun-blindness research still relies on case reports.

New imaging — optical coherence tomography, its angiography variant, adaptive optics — finds subtle retinal damage that routine exams miss, researchers said.

A multicentre research group is calling for a shared registry to replace decades of scattered case reports.
