Berlin — Almost every plastic and cosmetic runs on petrochemicals.

[Germany](country:DE)'s Charité is engineering bacteria to convert methanol into the same base chemicals, replacing the fossil feedstock rather than just the factory's energy source.

CarboNcare has not reported yields competitive with petroleum; commercial viability is unproven.
