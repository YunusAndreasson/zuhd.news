Caracas — Thousands marched in 5 cities for free elections.

[Venezuela](country:VE) has set no election date since [US](country:US) forces captured Maduro in January.

Interim president Delcy Rodríguez governs with Trump's backing, and US-backed talks on remaking the electoral council exclude Machado's movement.

"I'm even less afraid today because I want my voice to be heard," said Leosmary Hernandez, an 18-year-old student.

Opposition leader María Corina Machado demands elections by July 2027.
