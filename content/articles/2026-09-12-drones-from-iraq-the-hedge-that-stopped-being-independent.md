Riyadh — Drones from Iraq struck the Hormuz-bypass pipeline.

That pipeline was Saudi Arabia's hedge against a single chokepoint failing.

The hedge assumed its two export routes could not fail together, but one launch site can now reach both.

[Iraq](country:IQ) sacked Maysan's security chiefs as Brent held 22% higher as of Sept 9.
