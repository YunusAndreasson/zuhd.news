Pretoria — The Public Investment Corporation manages over R3-trillion in pensions.

The board suspended CEO Patrick Dlamini over whistleblower claims he bypassed board approval on the Lanseria Airport probe.

An independent investigation will determine whether Dlamini returns as [South Africa](country:ZA)'s pension-fund steward.
