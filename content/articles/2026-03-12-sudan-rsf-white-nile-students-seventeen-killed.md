Rapid Support Forces drones killed 17 at a school in Sudan's White Nile State. The militia lost Khartoum in March 2025 and shifted to drone strikes on schools and civilian facilities in territory it can no longer hold. Sudan's war enters its 3rd year with no ceasefire talks underway and both sides under International Criminal Court investigation for war crimes.
