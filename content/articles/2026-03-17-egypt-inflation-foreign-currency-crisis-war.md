Egypt's 3 hard-currency sources collapsed in the same war. Suez tolls, Gulf remittances, and tourism were each severed by the Iran conflict, leaving the government with no foreign-exchange buffer. With Ramadan food prices spiking, Egypt's 105 million people absorb the full inflation shock.
