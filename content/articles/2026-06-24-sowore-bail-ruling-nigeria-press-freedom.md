Abuja — [Nigeria](country:NG) jailed publisher Sowore over social media posts.

The State Security Service charged Sowore for posts calling Tinubu a criminal; bail was revoked after he missed a June 16 hearing.

A federal court set 30 June for the bail restoration ruling; Sowore remains in Kuje prison.
