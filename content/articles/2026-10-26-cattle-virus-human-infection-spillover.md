Lyon — The first human Shamonda virus patient was severely immunocompromised and later died.

French officials believe a midge bite infected him, and he showed neurological symptoms.

Midges spread the virus, which has circulated among cattle in Africa and Japan since the 1960s and was documented in Europe this year.

The World Health Organization says broader transmission is unlikely.

Investigators are studying whether the virus contributed to his death.
