Singapore — Measles is resurging across Southeast Asia.

COVID-19 disrupted routine vaccination across the region; measles, which requires near-universal coverage to suppress, exposes how far immunity has eroded.

Whether routine immunization campaigns resume fast enough will determine if the region faces wider preventable-disease outbreaks.
