Aden — Residents who fled Houthi rule once prepare to flee again.

Aden has housed [Yemen](country:YE)'s Saudi-backed government since 2015, and over 350,000 people have been displaced since August.

Houthi troops massing north of Kirish and in al-Bayda could cut the northern and eastern roads into the city.

The Houthis have not announced any intent to take the city.

Polymarket traders give a Saudi-Houthi ceasefire by 31 December an 11% chance.
