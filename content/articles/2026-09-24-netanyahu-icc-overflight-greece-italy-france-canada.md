New York — [Greece](country:GR), [Italy](country:IT), [France](country:FR) and [Canada](country:CA) let Netanyahu overfly.

All four are ICC members, and the court has no enforcement power beyond their will.

The court issued the warrant in November 2024 over starvation as a method of warfare and crimes against humanity in Gaza.

Mayor Zohran Mamdani says New York City cannot arrest him and urges the [US](country:US) government to act.

Netanyahu addresses the General Assembly Thursday and leaves before Sukkot, with no Trump meeting scheduled.
