Antalya — [Australia](country:AU) leads COP31's negotiations while [Turkey](country:TR) hosts the summit.

Delegates must settle climate finance for developing countries and the details of a just transition mechanism.

The Belém-Antalya mechanism came from COP30, but parties still disagree on its scope and whether it is tied to finance.

Civil society groups call getting the mechanism running a key expected outcome of COP31.
