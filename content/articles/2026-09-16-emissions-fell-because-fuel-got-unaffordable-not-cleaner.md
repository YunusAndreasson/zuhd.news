London — 0.5% fewer emissions, all from unaffordable fuel.

The drop reverses once supply returns, unlike shutting a coal plant for good.

Carbon Brief attributes it to oil and gas priced out of reach by the Hormuz crisis.

Importers rationing diesel cut emissions through poverty, counted the same at the next UN climate summit.
