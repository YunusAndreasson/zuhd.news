Berlin — More than 30 organisations warn against AI in asylum decisions.

Under [Germany](country:DE)'s draft, asylum seekers could not see or challenge a machine's role.

The draft lets AI process data on persecution, sexual orientation and religion, without transparency or evaluation duties.

The coalition says the law will make procedures more efficient, uniform and secure.

Signatories, including Amnesty International and Pro Asyl, say the draft breaches fundamental rights and EU law.
