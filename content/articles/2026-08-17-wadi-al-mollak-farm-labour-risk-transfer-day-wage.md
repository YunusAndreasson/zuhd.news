Zagazig — A truck crash killed 19 farm laborers.

Survivors return to the same road for lack of any other wage.

Contractors in [Egypt](country:EG) pay day laborers to ride open trucks along a narrow unlit road with no contract.

Wadi al-Mollak's laborers have no other road to the farms that pay them.
