Maputo — Tires abandoned on Maputo's streets become furniture.

In [Mozambique](country:MZ), cities lack functioning waste systems; activists like Vania Gonçalo build recycling economies that employ youth where the state has failed.

Whether Maputo's municipality will formally integrate these grassroots recycling networks is unresolved.
