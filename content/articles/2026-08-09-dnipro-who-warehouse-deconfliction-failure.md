Dnipro — 170 of 300 pallets of medical aid burned in one strike.

One warehouse resupplies hospitals region-wide, so the front line loses months of stock at once.

[Russia](country:RU)'s Aug. 7 attack hit the WHO facility; staff evacuated less than half the pallets first, the agency said.

WHO chief Tedros condemned the strike without naming Russia, a pattern UN diplomats are now questioning.
