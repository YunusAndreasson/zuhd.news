London — Ostrich eggshells measure past radiation doses precisely. Radiation permanently alters the calcite crystal structure in eggshells; EPR spectroscopy reads the shift, detecting doses as low as 0.21 gray. Shells are cheap, stable, and UV-resistant; field deployment for nuclear accident dosimetry is the next step.
