Vladivostok — VTB switched on QR payments in two new countries.

Sanctioned Russian banking now reaches consumers in [Indonesia](country:ID) and the [Philippines](country:PH).

QR schemes run on domestic switches and never touch Visa, Mastercard or SWIFT, VTB said, which is why sanctions do not bite here.

VTB did not name which local switch operator approved the interconnection.
