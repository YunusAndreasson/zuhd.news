Tokyo — Only 3.2 billion yen of JPYC circulates, a quarter of target.

Its issuer earns interest on the reserves backing each token, so less circulation means less income.

Holders have redeemed most of the 12.1 billion yen issued, and a 1 million yen per-request cap slows corporate buyers.

JPYC chief executive Noritaka Okabe said "Frankly, I don't think it's growing."

Okabe wants tenfold growth in year two, and a [Japan](country:JP) Financial Services Agency stablecoin roadmap is expected in early 2027.
