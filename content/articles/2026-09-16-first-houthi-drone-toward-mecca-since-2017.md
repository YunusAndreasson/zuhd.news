Mecca — [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s defenses intercepted a drone near Mecca.

It is the first attempt against Islam's holiest city since a 2017 missile attack.

A one-way attack drone costs a fraction of the interceptor used to destroy it, an imbalance neither side can sustain.

[Yemen](country:YE)'s Houthis denied targeting Mecca as the coalition warned of further deterrent strikes.
