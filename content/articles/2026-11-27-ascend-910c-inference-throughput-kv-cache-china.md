Hefei — Zhongke Leinao says it lifted GLM-5.1 decode speed 60%.

Gartner expects 2026 inference spending of $23.3 billion to pass training's $19 billion.

Pooling memory for attention data across an Ascend supernode raised concurrency from 2 to 8 and decode from 39.41 to 63.01 tokens a second.

The figures come from the company's own test on Huawei's Ascend 910C, at 64K context.

Gartner projects inference's share of AI infrastructure spending rises from 55% to 59% in 2027.
