Nigeria — Muslim women allege hijab harassment at Bar exams.

No law graduate is called to the Nigerian Bar without passing these examinations.

The school calls the claims false and says a candidate was allegedly found with an earpiece in her hijab on 29 October.

Muslim Rights Concern argues candidates should not face collective punishment for one student's alleged misconduct.

The school is still investigating the 29 October incident and asks the public to await its findings.
