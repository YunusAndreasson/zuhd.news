Brussels — Bart De Wever arrived at the Royal Palace expected to resign.

His five-party coalition again failed to agree a budget while [Belgium](country:BE)'s bond yields climb.

The prime minister's N-VA and MR want cuts including healthcare, while partners demand higher wealth taxes to close a €10 billion gap.

De Tijd cited sources saying the king may refuse the resignation and send De Wever back to negotiate.

If King Philippe accepts, he could ask other parties to form a coalition or call elections.
