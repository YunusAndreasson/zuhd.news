United States — Hirundo edits Chinese AI weights to remove censorship, it says.

Chinese open models trail US closed systems by 3% on benchmarks and lead Hugging Face downloads.

Hirundo's chief executive Ben Luria says it edits open weights to remove state-aligned bias and dangerous knowledge, work he calls neurosurgery.

"We are bringing it up to speed on safety and to the standard of mainstream Western AI," said Luria.

Europe's OpenEuroLLM sovereign model is under development on Alibaba's Qwen.
