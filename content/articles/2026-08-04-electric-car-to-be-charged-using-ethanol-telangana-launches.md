Hyderabad — Telangana launched a 150kW ethanol EV charger.

The system reforms ethanol into power onsite in [India](country:IN), skipping grid links and years-long utility approval queues.

The charger can be installed in 72 hours, cutting setup costs by up to half, developer Keto Motors said.
