Moscow — [Iran](country:IR)'s president left for Moscow, Iranian state news agency Mehr reported.

He left hours after meeting senior security officials from Turkey, Pakistan and Saudi Arabia.

Kremlin aide Yury Ushakov forecast gas demand rising 28% to 5.4 trillion cubic metres by 2055, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

Mehr said Pezeshkian may meet other attending leaders on the summit's sidelines.
