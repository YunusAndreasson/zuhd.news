Google's $32 billion Wiz purchase set a cybersecurity acquisition record. The Israeli cloud security firm covers 45% of Fortune 100 customers and stays available across AWS, Azure, and Oracle, giving Google a multi-cloud weapon against Microsoft. Google pledged to preserve Wiz's multi-cloud neutrality but named no timeline for product integration into Google Cloud.
