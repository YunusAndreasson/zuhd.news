Ecuador launched US-backed military operations against its criminal economy. Guayaquil, Ecuador's Pacific port and a top global banana export hub, is also a primary cocaine transshipment point for European and US markets. Curfews in 4 provinces begin March 15 through March 30, as officials promise operations of "greater magnitude."
