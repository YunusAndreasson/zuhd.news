Islamabad — Rs114 of every Rs379.25 litre of petrol is tax.

Petrol carries the middle and lower-middle classes, while diesel moves freight and power plants.

[Pakistan](country:PK) reprices fuel daily since July, and Tuesday's rises add Rs1.54 to petrol and Rs2.16 to diesel.

Petrol peaked at Rs458.41 and diesel at Rs520.35 on 3 April.
