London — A $1.1 trillion manager tokenizes emerging-market bonds on Solana.

Tokenization now reaches debt tied to developing countries' credit and currencies, not just cash-like funds.

Investors hold tokenized shares in a feeder fund that invests in Schroders' Emerging Market Bond Fund, with Centrifuge running the infrastructure.

US rival Federated Hermes plans its first tokenized fund shares in early 2027.
