New York — Quantum jamming protects data if physics fails. Unlike post-quantum cryptography, jamming-based security needs no assumptions about the rules of quantum mechanics — securing messages against attacks that don't yet exist. The approach, recently rediscovered, is not yet standardized — a gap that matters as post-quantum algorithms reach deployment.
