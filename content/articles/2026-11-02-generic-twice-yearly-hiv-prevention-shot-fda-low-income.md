South Africa — Generic lenacapavir is expected to cost $40 a year, not $28,218.

It opens supply to 120 low- and middle-income countries, where demand outstrips Gilead's supply.

[India](country:IN)'s Dr. Reddy's Laboratories makes it under a Unitaid-brokered deal, and US Food and Drug Administration approval can speed other countries' sign-off.

The approval is tentative, and generics from the other licensed makers still await it.

Generic doses could reach some countries within weeks, partners say, but others require national approval first.
