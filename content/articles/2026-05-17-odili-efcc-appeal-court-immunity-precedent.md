Port Harcourt — Nigerian governors gain immunity precedent via Odili ruling.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s Court of Appeal held an unappealed constitutional injunction permanently bars the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission from prosecuting Odili.

Legal scholar Chidi Odinkalu warned other governors will now invoke the same shield.
