Suva — Bilateral pacts are multiplying faster than island consensus.

[Australia](country:AU) has stacked security agreements across the Pacific, treating small island states as contested terrain rather than as authors of their own peace framework.

Analysts say the region's own peace priorities keep losing ground to great-power partnership deals.
