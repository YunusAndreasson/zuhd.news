San Francisco — Four nonprofits want next-generation AI models frozen by court order.

Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI and SpaceX are the five defendants.

Plaintiffs claim the labs violated California's unfair competition law by endangering public health and interfering with property such as computer systems.

The groups seek an immediate emergency injunction, to lift only once companies prove human control.
