Mexico City — El Mencho died; Mexico's meth market didn't. CJNG dominated global meth production for 20 years under Mencho, expanding as far as Australia; analysts find the cartel's network too distributed to collapse with its founder. Whether CJNG fractured or consolidated remains the test of whether Mexico's decapitation strategy ever worked.
