New York — ShinyHunters stole 45 million records from McGraw Hill. ShinyHunters accessed McGraw Hill's Salesforce through a misconfiguration, not an exploit, while serving millions of students whose educational records were exposed. The ransom deadline passed April 14; if McGraw Hill didn't pay, ShinyHunters threatened to leak the full dataset.
