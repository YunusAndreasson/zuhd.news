London — Models trained on Western photos misjudge heritage art.

A low score can push a manuscript to the back of a digitization queue.

Researchers tested 5 image models on a Western photography benchmark, then transferred them to Indian and Indonesian heritage datasets.

Museums and archives across the Global South are already being sold these cataloguing tools.
