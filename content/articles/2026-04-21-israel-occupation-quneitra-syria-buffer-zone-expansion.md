Quneitra — [Israel](country:IL) crossed [Syria](country:SY)'s disengagement buffer in Quneitra. After Assad fell in December 2024, Israel declared the UN disengagement agreement void and crossed the Bravo Line into Syrian territory. The UN peacekeeping force remains in the buffer zone with no mechanism to halt raids or enforce withdrawal.
