26 unknown microbial species emerged from 38,000-year-old Alaskan permafrost. The Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency funded the extraction to develop frostbite treatments, antifreeze compounds, and de-icing technology for Arctic operations. Researchers at the Army's Cold Regions lab are now screening the organisms for usable biochemistry.
