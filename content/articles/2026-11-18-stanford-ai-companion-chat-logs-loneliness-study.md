Stanford — Those confiding most in AI companions felt worst.

The effect was strongest for people with small offline social networks, the study found.

Diyi Yang's team analysed 460,000 Character.AI messages donated by 244 of 1,131 users, published in Nature Human Behaviour.

[China](country:CN)'s first AI-companion rules took effect 15 July, and 2 major platforms shut emotional-dependence characters.
