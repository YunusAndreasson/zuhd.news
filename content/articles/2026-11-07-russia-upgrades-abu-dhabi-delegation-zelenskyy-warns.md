Abu Dhabi — [Russia](country:RU) sends 5 senior officials to the 10 November talks.

The Kremlin has not signalled any shift in its negotiating position.

Aide Yuri Ushakov oversees the talks, joined by Vladimir Medinsky, two deputy foreign ministers and two general staff officers.

The [US](country:US)-brokered talks with [Ukraine](country:UA) run 10 and 11 November.
