Quetta — Balochistan's government says it killed 10 armed men in Barkhan.

No one outside the provincial government has identified the dead or confirmed its account.

The Counter-Terrorism Department says the men belonged to Fitna al-Hindustan, [Pakistan](country:PK)'s label for Balochistan-based groups, and seized arms and explosives.

Officials have not named the dead, whose bodies lie at Rara Sham district hospital.
