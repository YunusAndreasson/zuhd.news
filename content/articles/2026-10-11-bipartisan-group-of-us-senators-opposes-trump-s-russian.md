Washington — 4 Republicans joined 3 Democrats against Trump's diesel deal.

The senators say allowing Russian diesel imports breaks Congress's 2022 ban on Russian energy imports.

Trump announced 300,000 tonnes of Russian diesel on Friday, and Treasury then issued a general license for imports.

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent argues Trump acted within his legal mandate.

The license runs through April 2027 unless Trump reverses course, as the senators demand.
