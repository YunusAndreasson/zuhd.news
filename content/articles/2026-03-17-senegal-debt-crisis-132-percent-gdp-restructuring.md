Senegal owes $9.1 billion in debt payments this year. The IMF estimates the country's debt at 132% of GDP, driven by Eurobond borrowing at punitive rates and volatile commodity revenues. Prime Minister Ousmane Sonko rejected restructuring, choosing to close 19 agencies that will save less than 2% of this year's debt bill.
