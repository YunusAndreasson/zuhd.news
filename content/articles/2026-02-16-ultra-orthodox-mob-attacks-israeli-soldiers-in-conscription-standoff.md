A mob chased 2 female soldiers through Bnei Brak. Hundreds of ultra-Orthodox men attacked the women during a routine conscription home visit, flipped a police car, and injured 3 officers before police fired stun grenades and arrested 23 — the worst violence yet over a June 2024 Supreme Court ruling that ended decades of military exemptions for ultra-Orthodox men. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu dismissed the attackers as "an extreme minority," but the conscription standoff is deepening as the Gaza war drives demand for recruits from a community that makes up 15% of Israel's Jewish population.
