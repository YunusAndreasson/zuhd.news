Paris — Sterilized soil kept respiring for 6 years.

French biochemist Sébastien Fontaine gamma-irradiated sealed soil jars to eliminate all life — CO₂ kept coming from mineral reactions, not biology.

If mineral chemistry sustains metabolic-like reactions without life, it reshapes the leading theory of how biology first began.
