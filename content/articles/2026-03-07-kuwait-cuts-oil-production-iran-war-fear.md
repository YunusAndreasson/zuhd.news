Kuwait cut oil production as a war precaution. Kuwait Petroleum Corporation reduced output from 2.6 million barrels a day; Iran has struck Gulf infrastructure and Kuwait itself hosts major US military bases. Qatar's energy minister warned exports from all Gulf producers could halt within weeks if oil reaches $150.
