London — Climate graphs assumed a technology that doesn't work. A new Princeton model shows BECCS takes 150 years to deliver net CO2 removal, triples electricity costs, and increases emissions during the critical first decades. Drax, the flagship project, shelved carbon capture; IPCC scenarios built on BECCS have not been revised.
