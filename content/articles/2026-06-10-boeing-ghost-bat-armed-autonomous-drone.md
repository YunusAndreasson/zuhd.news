Berlin — Boeing's Block 3 Ghost Bat carries AIM-120 air-to-air missiles in internal bays.

Hiding weapons inside preserves stealth that external pylons would compromise.

[Australia](country:AU) operates 8 Block 1 Ghost Bats; Boeing is marketing Block 3 to [Germany](country:DE) and wider European buyers.
