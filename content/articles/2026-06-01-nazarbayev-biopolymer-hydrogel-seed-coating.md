Astana — Natural-polymer seed coatings matched synthetic hydrogels without leaving microplastics in soil.

[Kazakhstan](country:KZ)'s Nazarbayev University showed the coatings retain moisture during germination and biodegrade completely, unlike synthetics that persist in arid soil.

The paper appears in Scientific Reports; field trials at scale have not been conducted.
