Hai Phong — VinGroup scrapped its $6.7 billion LNG plant for renewables.

[Vietnam](country:VN) targeted 22.5 GW of gas power by 2030, but lenders won't underwrite contracts that guarantee developers too little of a plant's output.

With 4 years left, only 1.62 GW of LNG capacity is operating.
