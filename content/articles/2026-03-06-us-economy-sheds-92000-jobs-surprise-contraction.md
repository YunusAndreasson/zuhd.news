The US shed 92,000 jobs in February. The Hormuz blockade's energy shock and President Donald Trump's federal workforce cuts pushed unemployment to 4.4%, reversing forecasts of modest growth. The Federal Reserve meets March 19, when rate cuts are back on the agenda.
