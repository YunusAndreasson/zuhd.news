Mumbai — IndiGo will move 12 daily international departures to Navi Mumbai.

Adani Airport Holdings runs both airports, so one operator sets the terms of Mumbai's capacity.

The move clears room to rebuild Terminal 1, lifting its capacity from 15 million to 20 million passengers a year.

The civil aviation ministry intervened after airlines resisted the operator's 1 October plan to move 265 departure slots.

Terminal 1 redevelopment is due to begin in March 2027, a source said, with transfer dates pending approvals.
