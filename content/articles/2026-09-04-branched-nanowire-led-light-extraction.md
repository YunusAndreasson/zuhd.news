Lund — Branched nanowires let trapped LED light escape.

Lighting uses a fifth of global electricity, so cheaper efficient LEDs matter widely.

Conventional LEDs trap photons deep inside via total internal reflection, but this design generates light near the surface.

The Lund team has not yet built a commercial device, only tested one.
