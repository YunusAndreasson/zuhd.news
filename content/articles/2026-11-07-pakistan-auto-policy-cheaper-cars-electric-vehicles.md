Islamabad — New auto policy targets middle-class car buyers.

It rests on 6 pillars, including localisation, exports, vehicle safety and electric vehicles.

Industries adviser Haroon Akhtar Khan said a new tariff policy aims to cut production costs and raw-material import bills.

Saturday's announcement, as reported, gave no tariff rates or start date.
