Ars Technica retracted an article containing fabricated quotes. Author Benj Edwards used ChatGPT to paraphrase a matplotlib contributor's blog post, creating direct quotations that Scott Shambaugh confirmed he never wrote. Editor-in-chief Ken Fisher called it "a serious failure of our standards" and reiterated that the publication bans AI-generated content unless clearly labelled.
