Paris — Grothendieck's scheme theory made intractable 1950s problems routine.

He recast algebraic geometry around functions, not points — removing the structural constraints that had blocked the discipline for decades.

Quanta credits the strategy — redefine the object, not the problem — with shaping ongoing work in topology and analysis.
