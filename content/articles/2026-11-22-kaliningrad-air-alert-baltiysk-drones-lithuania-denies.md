Kaliningrad — Air-raid sirens sounded for the first time since 2022, local media said.

The exclave sits between NATO members [Lithuania](country:LT) and [Poland](country:PL).

The governor blamed an object from the Lithuanian side, then called it harmless, while unverified posts showed drones over Baltiysk.

Lithuania's foreign minister says it never authorised such strikes, and its authorities recorded no drones overnight.

Moscow has not confirmed a strike, and Finnish crews still fought the Sparta II cargo-ship fire Sunday.
