title: "Floods Kill 100 in Ethiopia"
date: "2026-03-12T00:00:00Z"
source: "BBC World"
sourceUrl: "https://www.bbc.com/news/world-africa"
category: "politics"
---

Floods and landslides killed more than 100 Ethiopians. The storms struck highland communities hardest, where emergency services were already strained by the aftermath of Ethiopia's 2022 Tigray war ceasefire. Search teams have not reached all flooded areas, and the death toll is expected to rise.
