Rawalpindi — [Pakistan](country:PK) detained 6,668 Afghans; 6,641 already deported.

The crackdown followed a government voluntary-return deadline; of those detained, 1,700 are children and 817 were released after verification, authorities said.

27 individuals including 5 children remain at the Dhoke Syedan centre awaiting [Afghanistan](country:AF) repatriation.
