Gaza hospitals have no medicine for women giving birth. Israel barred Doctors Without Borders from entering Gaza, cutting off medical staff from hospitals with no surgical supplies or anaesthetics for women in labour. Israel has not responded to Amnesty International's March 9 demand that it lift the ban.
