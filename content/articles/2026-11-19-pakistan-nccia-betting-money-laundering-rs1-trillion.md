Karachi — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s cybercrime agency alleges Rs1 trillion in betting flows.

Its report alleges bank officials kept suspicious accounts open despite anti-money-laundering alerts.

Gambling payments allegedly flowed through mobile wallets into rented "mule" accounts and fictitious businesses, hiding who owned the money.

The Rs1 trillion is an estimated exposure, and the itemised accounts total about Rs8 billion.

The first information report names 40 people and companies, including 32 chief executives, and 5 suspects are in custody.
