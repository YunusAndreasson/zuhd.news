Israel's cabinet approved registering unregistered West Bank land as state property. Finance Minister Bezalel Smotrich declared the move would "bury the idea of a Palestinian state," exploiting a 1968 freeze that blocked Palestinians from formally recording land ownership. Jordan, Qatar, Turkey, and the Palestinian presidency condemned the decision as de facto annexation.
