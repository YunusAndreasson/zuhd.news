Trade through Khunjerab Pass hit records as Hormuz closed. The Karakoram Highway links Pakistan to China through a 4,700-meter mountain pass, providing the only major trade route not threatened by Hormuz closure. Local traders await a tax exemption for Khunjerab imports promised by Pakistan's government but not yet implemented.
