Seoul — [Japan](country:JP) and [South Korea](country:KR) held their first joint naval exercise in 9 years.

The search-and-rescue drills restored maritime coordination after historical tensions between the two states froze cooperation in East Asia's most contested waters.

No missile-defense coordination has been announced; underlying historical disputes remain unresolved.
