Capua — Engineers deliberately cracked Space Rider's heat shield.

Italy's aerospace lab subjected a pre-damaged sample to 1,200°C plasma for 600 seconds; the impact hole held unchanged, validating ISiComp ceramic composite for debris-struck reentry.

Space Rider's demonstration flight on Vega C is scheduled for 2028, with 18 customers signed on.
