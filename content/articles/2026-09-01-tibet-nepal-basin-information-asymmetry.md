Lhasa — The same flood produced two different records.

Tibetan flood deaths go uncounted while [Nepal](country:NP)'s are recorded and aided.

Flood forecasting in a shared river basin depends on upstream data that [China](country:CN)'s state media alone controls.

How much of Tibet's toll is documented depends entirely on what Beijing's state media publishes next.
