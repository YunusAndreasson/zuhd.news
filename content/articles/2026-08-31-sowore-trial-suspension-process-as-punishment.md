Abuja — Nigeria's secret police asked to pause its own prosecution.

A lawyer says the agency could simply withdraw the case instead of leaving Sowore in limbo.

The security service cites election-rights conventions to justify the delay.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s Federal High Court resumes 14 September, but the case itself waits until after 2027 elections.
