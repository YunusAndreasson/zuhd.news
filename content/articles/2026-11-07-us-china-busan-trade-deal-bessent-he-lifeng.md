Washington — Treasury urged Beijing to implement its Busan commitments "fully and immediately."

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent raised concerns about recent Chinese actions with Vice Premier He Lifeng.

Busan eased tariffs and tech curbs, and Beijing agreed to buy $17bn of US farm goods yearly, the White House said.

[China](country:CN)'s commerce ministry says more US farm purchases depend on both sides implementing the agreed reciprocal tariff cuts.

The [US](country:US) says it will closely assess China's implementation, and progress depends on concrete results.
