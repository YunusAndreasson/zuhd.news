Washington — A rival exchange's objection froze a bitcoin launch.

The SEC suspended Nasdaq's approved cash-settled bitcoin options after CME Group argued the CFTC, not the SEC, holds jurisdiction; comments close August 24.

CME already runs the bitcoin futures market Nasdaq's options would have competed against.
