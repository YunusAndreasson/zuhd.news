Cairo — [Iran](country:IR) offered to reopen Hormuz shipping, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

Hormuz traffic ran 11% below normal as of 18 October, and Brent reached $116.70 on 23 October.

Unnamed sources say Tehran asked Pakistani mediators to trade shipping for an end to the [US](country:US) blockade of its ports.

Iran has not commented, TASS said, though it reportedly told Pakistan it is ready to resume talks.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 2 November a 4% chance.
