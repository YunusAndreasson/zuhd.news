Hualien — Grinding rock alone can split water.

It ties the fuel supply of deep microbial life to tectonic stress, not biology.

Fracturing at [Taiwan](country:TW)'s Milun Fault broke silicate bonds, leaving unpaired electrons that split water into hydrogen.

The unedited study calls this one hydrogen source, not evidence of an extractable deposit.
