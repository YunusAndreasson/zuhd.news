Canso — Isar Aerospace signed a $112.5 million launch deal.

The German rocket maker will pay Maritime Launch Services $3.75 million a quarter for a dedicated Spectrum pad, targeting a first launch in 2028.

The pad must be handed over by November 1, 2026 — Isar's only Spectrum flight to date failed shortly after liftoff.
