London — A new index scores biofilm carbon recycling.

Wastewater and carbon-capture systems have run on unmeasured carbon claims.

Algae reabsorb bacterial CO2 inside the biofilm's two layers, and the model separates recycling from real drawdown.

The loop closes only within a narrow 300-500 micrometre thickness window, researchers found.
