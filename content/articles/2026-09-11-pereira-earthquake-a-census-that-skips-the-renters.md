Pereira — Some survivors paid rent by the day.

Without a lease in their name, they cannot register as official victims.

The 7.4-magnitude quake affected 400,000 people in [Colombia](country:CO), but relief is allocated by proof of tenancy.

Nearly 400 people now shelter in a park while volunteers press for an official census.
