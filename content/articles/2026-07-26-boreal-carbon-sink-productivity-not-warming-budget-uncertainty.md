Moscow — Northern Eurasia holds a fifth of Earth's forests.

Researchers find tree productivity, not the region's rapid warming, governs how much carbon it absorbs, since warming raises fire, drought and respiration.

Most of that sink lies in [Russia](country:RU) and Canada, land global climate accounting still struggles to measure directly.
