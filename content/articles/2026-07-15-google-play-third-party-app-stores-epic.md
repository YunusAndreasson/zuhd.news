San Francisco — Rival app stores arrive on Play July 22.

Google withdrew a softer settlement after a judge's skepticism, defaulting to a harsher remedy: automatic Play catalog access for rival stores.

Developers can opt out, but Google must justify any move blocking competing store clients.
