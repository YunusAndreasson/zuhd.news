New York — The [US](country:US) and [China](country:CN) failed to extend their trade truce.

Xi Jinping's state visit to Washington begins 23 September, 3 days after the talks.

Washington wants a 6-month extension, and Beijing wants the truce to run through the rest of Trump's term.

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said both sides want to keep the past year's trade stability.

US sources say China delivers only about two-thirds of its rare earth supply commitments.
