Tallinn — Estonia downed a drone it attributed to [Ukraine](country:UA).

[Russia](country:RU)-origin media report Defense Minister Pevkur's claim; the incident, over Lake Võrtsjärv, has no independent verification outside Russian state sources.

No NATO statement has addressed whether intercept rules extend to allied unmanned aerial vehicles.
