Mecca — A Saudi-led coalition says it shot down a Houthi drone Friday.

No independent source has confirmed the target, which would place Islam's holiest city in the war.

Spokesman Turki al-Malki also reported a cruise missile downed over western [Saudi Arabia](country:SA), a day after 2 missiles aimed at Riyadh.

Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree denied it, saying the group targets only Saudi oil facilities and military bases.

Polymarket traders give a Saudi-Houthi ceasefire by 31 December a 24% chance, down 7 points in a week.
