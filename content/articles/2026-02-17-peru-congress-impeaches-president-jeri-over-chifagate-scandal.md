Peru's Congress voted 75-24 to remove President Jose Jeri. Security footage showed him in secret meetings with Chinese contractor Zhihua Yang, whose companies supply the state — a scandal dubbed "Chifagate." Peru's 8th president in 8 years will be chosen by lawmakers, with a general election set for April.
