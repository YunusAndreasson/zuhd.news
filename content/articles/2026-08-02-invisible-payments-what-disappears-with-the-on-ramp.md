New York — On-ramps and bridges will vanish, Fun's CEO says.

An on-ramp is where a transaction is screened and taxed; embedding it hides the rate a user is quoted.

Fun, which processes over $3 billion monthly for Polymarket and Aave, did not say which regulators approve it.
