Tehran — [Iran](country:IR)'s Guards say a third tanker was hit in 2 days.

Ships in the strait now face Iranian fire and a [US](country:US) blockade enforced with live fire.

The Guards' statement named no ship, flag or weapon, saying only that it had switched off navigation and ignored warnings.

UK Maritime Trade Operations logged an outbound tanker hit by an unknown projectile Saturday and said its crew was safe.

The Guards warned that any ship cooperating with the US military faces a decisive response.
