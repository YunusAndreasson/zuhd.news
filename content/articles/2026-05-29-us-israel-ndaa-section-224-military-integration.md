Washington — Section 224 of the House defence bill would permanently embed joint US-Israeli R&D, AI, cyber, and data programmes.

Section 224 replaces annual aid votes with permanent statutory programmes bundled into the defence authorisation act.

The Senate must clear the provision as polls show 16% of Americans support unconditional arms to [Israel](country:IL).
