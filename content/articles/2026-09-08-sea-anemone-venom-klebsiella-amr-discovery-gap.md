São Sebastião — Brazilian researchers found a superbug-killing venom compound.

Klebsiella pneumoniae is a WHO critical-priority pathogen with few treatments left in hospitals.

Venom disrupts cell membranes, a mechanism bacteria resist evolving around, unlike enzyme-blocking antibiotics.

This lab-stage compound must still clear the trials where most antimicrobial candidates fail.
