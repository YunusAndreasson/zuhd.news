---
title: "Chemists Build First Half-Möbius Molecule"
date: "2026-03-05T00:00:00Z"
source: "Nature"
sourceUrl: "https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-00682-x"
category: "science"
---

Chemists built a carbon ring that encodes half-Möbius geometry. A Möbius strip has 1 side; in this 13-carbon ring, a 90° twist forces electrons through 4 laps instead of 2. Researchers say the half-Möbius topology may influence how electrons respond to magnetic fields — a property sought for molecular computing.
