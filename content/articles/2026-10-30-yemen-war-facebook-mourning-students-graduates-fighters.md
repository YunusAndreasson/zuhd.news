Taiz — Obituaries have filled Yemeni Facebook feeds since early September.

In government-held [Yemen](country:YE), families post the dead's names and photographs, while Houthi areas announce deaths mainly through official funerals.

Houthi fighters launched an offensive on Sept. 3, seized six districts within a week, then cut Taiz's last government-held road.

Government forces launched Operation Dawn of Yemen to retake lost districts, and the siege of Taiz continues.
