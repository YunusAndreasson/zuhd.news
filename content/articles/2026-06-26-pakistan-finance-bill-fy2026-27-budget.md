Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Rs17.57 trillion budget is now law.

An IMF-constrained budget, it cuts income tax for high earners while preserving broad consumption taxes.

Implementation begins July 1; the IMF's next review of Pakistan's fiscal trajectory is the first test.
