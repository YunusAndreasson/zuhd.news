Johannesburg — Africa Bitcoin Corporation's entire bitcoin treasury is 5.5331 coins, disclosures show.

[South Africa](country:ZA)'s financial regulator debarred three insiders for 20 years in August over 2022 market manipulation.

Its core business is small-business lending, funded by overseas loans at under 4% secured against its bitcoin.

Warren Wheatley and Akshay Karan are contesting the debarment while a second probe, into April and May 2026 trading, is open.
