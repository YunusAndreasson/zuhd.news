Brussels — US foreign aid funded 90% of the world's only comprehensive disaster database.

The Emergency Events Database's 27,000-disaster archive underpins UN climate assessments; Global South governments rely on it, lacking national records.

Over 4,000 scientists signed a letter calling on governments and foundations to replace US funding.
