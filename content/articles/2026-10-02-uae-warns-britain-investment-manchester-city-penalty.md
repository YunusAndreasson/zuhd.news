Emirati investment in British defence, science and technology topped £30 billion in 5 years, figures in the report show.

Penalties range from a fine to relegation or exclusion, for breaches worth over €1 billion between 2009 and 2018.

Downing Street said no one is above the law, and Manchester City has lodged an appeal.

The final verdict is expected by January, and a UK-UAE summit is planned for October.
