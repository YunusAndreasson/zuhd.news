Beijing — [China](country:CN)'s Moonshot published a 2.8-trillion-parameter model's full weights.

Unlike an API, downloaded weights can be run, inspected and fine-tuned offline — and can't be withdrawn once released.

The leading open-weight models are now mostly Chinese, a direct result of [US](country:US) export controls.
