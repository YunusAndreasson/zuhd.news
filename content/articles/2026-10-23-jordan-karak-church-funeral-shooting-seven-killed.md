Al-Karak — 7 killed at church funeral, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

More than 150 people had gathered inside the church the gunmen did not enter, TASS said.

A Jordanian security source told Al Mamlaka television that about 10 more were wounded, some critically.

Two masked gunmen fled, and security forces in [Jordan](country:JO) have blocked every road into Karak, TASS said.
