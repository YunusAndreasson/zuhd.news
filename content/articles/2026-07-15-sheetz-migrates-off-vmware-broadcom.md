Altoona — Sheetz is pulling VMware from 838 stores.

Broadcom scrapped perpetual licenses for a mandatory five-year subscription bundle, pushing the chain to StorMagic's rival platform instead.

Sheetz expects to finish migrating roughly 11,000 virtual machines within four months.
