Alaska — The suit applies only if the Republican loses.

Both parties see the seat as possibly deciding Senate control, and expect a narrow result.

The [US](country:US) Justice Department asks a court to bar certifying Democrat Mary Peltola's win while namesake Daniel J. Sullivan stays on the ballot.

"The statute simply doesn't let the Department of Justice enjoin certification," said Derek Muller, a Notre Dame law professor.

Alaska votes 3 November, and ranked-choice counting may take weeks to name a winner.
