Canberra — Australia raised fees on its graduate visa.

The graduate visa converts a paid degree into local work rights; the increase falls hardest on middle-income South Asian families.

[India](country:IN) is [Australia](country:AU)'s second-largest source of foreign students and treats mobility as a core diplomatic ask.
