Minna — 37 mining suspects died in one cell.

None had been convicted, and the agency holding them has no custodial mandate.

A survivor said 65 detainees, including minors, were packed into one poorly ventilated cell.

Amnesty International wants an independent probe as the suspended commandant faces the corps's own inquiry.
