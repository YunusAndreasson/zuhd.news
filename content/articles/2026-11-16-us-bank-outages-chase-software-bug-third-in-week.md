New York — Nearly 5,000 Chase customers reported outages Monday, Downdetector counted.

It is the third major [US](country:US) system failure in a week, after Outlook and a nationwide flight ground stop.

Chase blamed a software bug in an on-premise hardware system, not a cyberattack.

JPMorgan says other banks slowed only on transactions that involved Chase.

Microsoft's final report on its Outlook outage, caused by a defect dormant since July, is due 20 November.
