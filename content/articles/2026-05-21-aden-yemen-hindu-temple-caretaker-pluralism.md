Aden — [Yemen](country:YE)'s last Hindu temple survived the civil war intact.

Built 160 years ago for Gujarati traders, it closed in 2015 when fighting intensified; Abdul Jalil, 70, guards it alone.

Jalil says if he leaves, the temple will be "looted brick by brick."
