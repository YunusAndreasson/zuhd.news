Karachi — Pakistan's 240 million smartphone users gained their first authorized Apple channel.

GNext, Apple's local distributor, now offers official warranty and zero-percent instalment plans through Daraz in a market dominated by grey imports.

Apple has not confirmed a physical store in [Pakistan](country:PK).
