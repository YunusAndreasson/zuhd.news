Chennai — 4 Tamil Nadu seats face by-elections after 3 defections.

Joseph Vijay's Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam governs Tamil Nadu through coalition, absorbing All India Anna Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam legislators rather than winning seats at elections.

17 rebel members remain undecided; a further round of by-elections is possible.
