Washington — The Pentagon dropped 'Indo' from US Pacific Command's title on Tuesday.

'Indo' was added in 2018 to counter China; the command still covers [India](country:IN)'s coast to America's Pacific shore.

New Delhi has not commented; the Pentagon said the command's operational posture is unchanged.
