Shenzhen — Etian shares hit their 20% limit before a takeover announcement.

Display equipment is nearly 90% of Etian's revenue, and net profit fell 50% in nine months.

Etian will buy 37% of Aichen and, with two allied shareholders, hold 51.85%, giving it control.

Only a framework agreement exists, and Aichen's 192 million yuan of 2025 revenue is self-reported and unaudited.

Etian and Aichen's owners still must finish due diligence and negotiate a price.
