Sudan's army retook Bara in North Kordofan. General Yasser al-Atta, the army's operational commander, called for absorbing all allied militias into the military — control of post-war Sudan's security structure is at stake. Rapid Support Forces shelled towns in White Nile and South Kordofan; civilians in both regions face fire from both sides.
