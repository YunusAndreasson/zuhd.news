La Guaira — A robot snake searched 20 feet into rubble.

The 4-foot, camera-tipped robots reach voids that dogs and rescue crews cannot, extending searchers' visual reach into [Venezuela](country:VE)'s collapsed buildings.

The World Bank estimates $19.6 billion in earthquake damage as recovery continues.
