Washington — Doha is reviving Iran-US talks, Turkish state outlet TRT World reported.

[Qatar](country:QA)'s prime minister said the region cannot bear fresh escalation between Washington and Tehran.

A June memorandum signed in Islamabad stalled over sanctions, the [US](country:US) naval blockade and Hormuz security.

[Iran](country:IR)'s parliament speaker Mohammed Bagher Ghalibaf said Washington wants a "no war, no peace" stalemate.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 31 December an 11% chance, down 6 points in a week.
