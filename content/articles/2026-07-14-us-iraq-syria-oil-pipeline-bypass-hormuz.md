Baghdad — 90% of Iraq's oil transits one strait.

Washington is advancing a pipeline routing [Iraq](country:IQ)'s crude to [Syria](country:SY)'s coast, letting Baghdad's exports skip Hormuz entirely as Iran's closure cut output to 1.45 million barrels a day, producer-group data show.

Chevron and Qatar's UCC are expected to sign the Basra-to-Syria route before al-Zaidi's Washington visit ends.
