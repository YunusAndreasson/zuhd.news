Vienna — Austrian judges rejected 4 of Dushanbe's extradition names.

The finding shields four dissidents [Tajikistan](country:TJ) wanted prosecuted abroad.

States file extradition and Interpol requests that freeze bank accounts and travel bans even when courts later refuse them.

Fourteen of Dushanbe's eighteen names still await a ruling in Austrian courts.
