San Diego — Weather satellites now map ocean currents hourly. GOFLOW trains deep learning on thermal images from geostationary weather satellites to infer surface current velocity from existing, unmodified hardware. Higher resolution than radar or buoys at zero hardware cost, the approach opens ocean monitoring to data-poor regions.
