Rio de Janeiro — Maré residents were sent up to 7 kilometres to vote.

Abstention hit 28.46% in Maré's electoral zone, against 21.08% nationally, a survey found.

The state electoral court cited gang control to move polling stations, affecting over half of Maré's first-round voters.

Redes da Maré founder Eliana Sousa Silva says the court never explained why some sections moved and others stayed.

Maré voters return to the polls in [Brazil](country:BR)'s presidential runoff.
