Hargeisa — Israel's foreign minister announced recognition of Somaliland on December 26. Berbera port gives [Israel](country:IL) its first Horn of Africa foothold; the deal followed Red Sea shipping disruptions tied to the Gaza war. [Somalia](country:SO)'s president Hassan Sheikh Mohamud called it the greatest sovereignty violation against his country.
