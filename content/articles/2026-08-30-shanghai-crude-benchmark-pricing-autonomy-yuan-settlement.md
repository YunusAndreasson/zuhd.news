Shanghai — A new study measures Shanghai crude's price independence.

A mature yuan benchmark would let sanctioned buyers clear crude outside dollar clearing.

Researchers call a benchmark mature when its price moves first and others follow, not the reverse.

Whether it displaces dollar-priced Brent as Asia's benchmark remains untested beyond 2025.
