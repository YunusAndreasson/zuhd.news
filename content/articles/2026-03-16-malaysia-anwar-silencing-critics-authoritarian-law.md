Malaysian police questioned activists under a 20-year jail law. Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim, once imprisoned for 6 years on political charges, now faces accusations of using the same law against his critics. Civil society groups demanded the government explain which criticisms it considers a threat to parliamentary democracy.
