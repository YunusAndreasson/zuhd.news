Taiz — Nearly 20,000 Yemenis fled fighting in a week.

Civilians, including children, are among at least 500 killed this week.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA) bombed five governorates more than 50 times in 12 hours as Ansarallah forces seized Mokha's coast.

Ansarallah's push toward Bab al-Mandab threatens more displacement as the fight for Taiz continues.
