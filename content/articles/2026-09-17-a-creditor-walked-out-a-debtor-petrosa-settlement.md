Cape Town — A R227m claim became a R1.4bn loss.

The reversal threatens PetroSA with liquidation, risking [South Africa](country:ZA)'s fuel supply security.

PetroSA signed an acknowledgment of debt that was later ceded to trader Nako Energy's own creditors.

Nako has asked the Western Cape High Court to place PetroSA under provisional liquidation.
