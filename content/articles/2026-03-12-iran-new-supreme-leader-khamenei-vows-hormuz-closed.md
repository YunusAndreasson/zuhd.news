Iran's new supreme leader rejected any ceasefire. A son of the assassinated Ali Khamenei, he took power 19 days into US-Israeli bombardment with closer ties to the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, making Hormuz's closure stated policy rather than a tactical measure. He called on regional governments to expel US military bases, stripping Gulf states of neutral ground.
