Xiaomi pledged 60 billion yuan for AI over 3 years. Unlike Baidu and Alibaba, Xiaomi is embedding AI into its cars, smart home devices and phones rather than building a standalone foundation model. DeepSeek's V4, expected in April, will be the first public test of whether Xiaomi's embedded approach can compete with foundation model rivals.
