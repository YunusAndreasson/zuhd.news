Lahore — Pakistan's premier cricket league will play to empty stadiums. The government cut the Pakistan Super League from 6 host cities to 2 and banned spectators under fuel austerity rules imposed after Iran closed the Strait of Hormuz. Pakistan has also imposed a 4-day work week and 50% remote-work mandate; the cricket season could run to its end under empty stands if Hormuz stays shut.
