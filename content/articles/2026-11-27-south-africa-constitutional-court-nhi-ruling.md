Johannesburg — A single vote saved [South Africa](country:ZA)'s National Health Insurance Act.

The scheme is set to become the country's main healthcare system once the law takes full effect.

Both rulings settled only whether Parliament consulted the public properly, not whether the law is rational, clear or lawful.

"We disagree with the centralisation of healthcare," said Western Cape Premier Alan Winde, who lost the 6-5 case.

Pending cases on the act's substance continue, including a bid to overturn the president's assent.
