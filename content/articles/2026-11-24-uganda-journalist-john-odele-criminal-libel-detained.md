Soroti — Journalist John Odele has been detained since 10 November.

[Uganda](country:UG) is prosecuting him under a libel law its Constitutional Court struck down in March.

His report covered the arrest of presidential adviser Peter Elwelu, and the charge says it implied Gen. Samuel Okiding shirked his duties.

Police and the presidency minister did not reply to the Committee to Protect Journalists' requests for comment.

A Soroti court remanded him until 3 December.
