Enugu — Prepaid meters across [Nigeria](country:NG)'s south-east went dark Friday.

The Enugu electricity distributor holds a 2023-granted regional monopoly and shut its only vending platform for a system upgrade with no backup.

5 states face no payment option until the platform resumes Sunday midnight.
