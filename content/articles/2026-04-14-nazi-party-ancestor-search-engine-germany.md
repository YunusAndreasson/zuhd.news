Berlin — A new tool finds Nazi ancestors in seconds. It indexes millions of NSDAP membership cards — physical records previously requiring in-person archive visits — and is already surfacing results across German and Austrian families. The creators plan to expand coverage; comparable archives of complicity remain analog in dozens of countries.
