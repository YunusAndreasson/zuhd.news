Frankfurt — Lufthansa's flagship airline lost €180 million in nine months.

Management will cut 1,000 full-time jobs at the Munich and Frankfurt hubs.

A management letter blames uncompetitive costs and productivity, and fuel prices raised by the US-Israeli war with [Iran](country:IR).

Brent crude stood at $91.98 on 19 November, down 4% in 7 days.

Lufthansa has not said when the cuts start or whether they add to the 4,000 announced in 2025.
