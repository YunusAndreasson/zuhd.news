Yerevan — Organisers announced $2 billion against a $200 billion annual target.

Leaders conceded they will miss the 2030 target to protect a third of land and seas.

The Yerevan Pathway seeks more aid for developing countries, but a voluntary fund fed by firms using genetic material is largely empty.

The [Netherlands](country:NL) pledged $500 million to Brazil's Tropical Forest Forever Facility, which pays countries to keep forests standing.

Governments reconvene in Ecuador in two years to review progress under the Yerevan Pathway.
