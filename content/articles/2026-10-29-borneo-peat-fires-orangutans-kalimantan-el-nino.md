Palangka Raya — Fruit production fell for 2.5 years after Borneo's 2015 fires.

[Indonesia](country:ID) faces one of its worst wildfire seasons in over a decade, with Kalimantan's peatlands smoldering.

Orangutans lost lean body mass as females rested more, travelled less and ate lower-quality fallback foods.

"You hear them coughing," said Maria van Noordwijk, a Max Planck research fellow who helped establish Tuanan.

Tuanan's researchers, monitoring since 2003, can trace this year's fires the same way.
