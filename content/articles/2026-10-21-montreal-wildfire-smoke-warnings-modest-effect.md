Montreal — Smoke advisories cut bikeshare trips just 5.8%.

Recreational running, the activity warnings target most, showed no statistically significant change.

McGill researchers compared actual trips on 27 advisory days with a model forecast of travel without advisories.

"Advisories may need to be paired with practical measures that make compliance easier," said Daniel Romm, the study's co-author.

The team plans similar analyses in other cities and will compare the results.
