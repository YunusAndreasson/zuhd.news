Qafzeh Cave — Healed jaw wound found in 90,000-year-old human skull.

Scans confirmed bone remodeling at the jaw cut — proof of survival — and deliberate burial at one of the earliest known grave sites.

The imaging method distinguishes wounds from post-mortem damage and could revise interpretations of other Paleolithic remains.
