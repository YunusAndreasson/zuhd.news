San Francisco — A judge dismissed both LinkedIn browser-scanning lawsuits.

Invisible scans left plaintiffs unable to prove any harm.

A page script silently tests thousands of extension IDs to fingerprint each user.

Plaintiffs can still file an amended complaint or turn to state court.
