Havana — Cuba suffered its second national blackout in a week. Puerto Rican activists flew medicine to Havana as Cuba's hospitals run short of supplies the US blockade restricts. Cuba rejected a US offer of diesel conditioned on political concessions, leaving the power crisis unresolved.
