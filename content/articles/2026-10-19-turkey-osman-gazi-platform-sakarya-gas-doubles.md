Filyos — Turkey's first floating gas platform will double Sakarya field output.

[Turkey](country:TR) produces about 300,000 barrels of oil and gas a day and uses about 2 million, Energy Minister Alparslan Bayraktar said.

Ten drilled wells connect to the Osman Gazi platform within weeks, and processed gas then reaches shore by pipe.

Bayraktar says halving import dependence needs 1 million barrels a day and new discoveries.

Bayraktar plans to launch the National Energy 2040 plan on 5 November.
