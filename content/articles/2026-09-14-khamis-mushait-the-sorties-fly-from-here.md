Khamis Mushait — Yahya Saree claimed dozens of missiles and drones hit King Khalid Air Base.

The base flies the sorties bombing Yemen, so the war has closed its loop.

Houthi forces said the strikes answered over 300 [Saudi](country:SA) raids across Yemen in five days, though Riyadh has not confirmed damage.

Civil Defence lifted its Khamis Mushait alert, but the Houthis vowed broader attacks if strikes continue.
