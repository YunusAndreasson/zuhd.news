Dhaka — Sheikh Hasina plans to return December 16, her spokesperson says.

Awami League protests demanding her return have turned deadly, especially in Gopalganj.

A tribunal sentenced her to death over the 2024 crackdown, so [Bangladesh](country:BD) says only extradition from [India](country:IN) can bring her back.

"Bangladesh will not entertain any such delusional request," said Humaiun Kobir, minister of state for foreign affairs.

India's foreign ministry said in April it was examining Dhaka's extradition request, and it declined comment Tuesday.
