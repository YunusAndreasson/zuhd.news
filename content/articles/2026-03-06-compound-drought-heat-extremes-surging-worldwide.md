Heatwaves, not droughts, are fueling compound climate disasters. By accelerating evaporation, heatwaves have more than doubled the land area struck by extreme heat and drought simultaneously since 2001. Researchers say severe impacts are preventable if preparedness systems treat heat and drought as a single combined event.
