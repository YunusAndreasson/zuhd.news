Beirut — Platforms retain facial geometry from AI photo uploads under terms with no deletion path, a Social Media Exchange analyst warned.

Extracted facial geometry trains the model — the same data, the group said, has been deployed in military targeting systems.

[Lebanon](country:LB) and other conflict zones face sharpest risk from biometric harvesting at commercial scale.
