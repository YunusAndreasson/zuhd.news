St. Petersburg — [Tanzania](country:TZ)'s President Hassan said the country holds uranium reserves and opened nuclear energy talks with [Russia](country:RU)'s Rosatom.

Tanzania has drafted a national nuclear roadmap for small reactors.

No deal was signed at the St. Petersburg International Economic Forum; fuel supply terms remain unconfirmed.
