Mumbai — Jio filed for an initial public offering with India's market regulator.

Jio runs [India](country:IN)'s largest wireless network with 500 million subscribers; media reports estimate the offering at $4 billion.

The regulator must clear the prospectus before shares trade; Jefferies estimated Jio's value at $180 billion in November.
