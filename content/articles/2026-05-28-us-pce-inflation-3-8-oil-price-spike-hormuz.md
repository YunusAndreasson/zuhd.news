Washington — [US](country:US) inflation rose to 3.8%, a 3-year high.

Mideast war drove pump prices to $4.50 a gallon, with gasoline spending up 28.8%, locking the Federal Reserve from cutting rates.

Some Federal Reserve members have proposed rate hikes with inflation nearly double the 2% target.
