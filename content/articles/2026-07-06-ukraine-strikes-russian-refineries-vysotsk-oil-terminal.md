Yaroslavl — Drones hit 3 refineries and the Vysotsk terminal.

[Ukraine](country:UA) targeted the Slavneft-YANOS plant, which refines 15 million tons yearly, part of a campaign to starve [Russia](country:RU)'s war of hard currency.

Russian authorities reported a Sevastopol blackout; no restart timeline has been given for Vysotsk.
