Rheinmetall CEO called the Middle East war an "opportunity." Germany's largest arms maker, under CEO Armin Papperger, forecast €14.5 billion in 2026 sales — a 45% increase — citing direct demand from the conflict for its air defence systems. Its order backlog has risen 36% to a record €63.8 billion, targeting €42 billion in annual sales by 2030.
