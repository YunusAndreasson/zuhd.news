London — NAT10 enzyme hijacks cholesterol to fuel gallbladder cancer. By acetylating PCSK9 mRNA to prevent its breakdown, NAT10 floods tumor cells with cholesterol and activates a known growth pathway. PCSK9 inhibitors — already approved for heart disease — blocked tumor growth in models, creating an immediate clinical hypothesis.
