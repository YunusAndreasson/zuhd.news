Beijing — [China](country:CN) raised its research share 22% in 2025.

[South Korea](country:KR) and [Japan](country:JP) each grew about 10% while the [US](country:US) and European powers shed 6-7% adjusted share, the Index found.

Harvard yielded its top university rank to China's Zhejiang University in the 2026 Nature Index.
