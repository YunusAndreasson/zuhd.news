Zaporizhzhia — [Russia](country:RU) paints trucks to defeat AI drone targeting.

WWI dazzle camouflage confused human eyes; the same high-contrast geometry now attacks the object-detection classifiers on [Ukraine](country:UA)'s autonomous drone seekers.

Whether it defeats targeting or merely forces classifier retraining is untested in combat.
