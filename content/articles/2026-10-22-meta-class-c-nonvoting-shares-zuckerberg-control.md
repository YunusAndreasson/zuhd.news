Palo Alto — Zuckerberg can sell 20% of his new nonvoting shares freely.

Meta can raise capital for AI without diluting his roughly 60.6% voting interest.

Existing holders get one Class C share per share held from January 2027, and he commits to eight years in California.

JPMorgan analyst Doug Anmuth says issuing the shares sooner would remove the overhang.

California votes on Proposition 40, the billionaire tax, on 3 November.
