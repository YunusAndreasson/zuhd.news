North Korea may constitutionally declare South Korea a permanent enemy. The 687-seat Supreme People's Assembly convened for its first session, tasked with abandoning the 70-year reunification doctrine in North Korea's founding charter. The constitutional language will signal whether Kim Jong Un's priority is confrontation or diplomacy with Seoul.
