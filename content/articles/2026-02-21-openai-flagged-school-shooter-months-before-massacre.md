An 18-year-old killed 8 at a British Columbia school. OpenAI flagged the shooter's account in June 2025 for violent content but determined it did not meet the threshold for a police referral. The company contacted the Royal Canadian Mounted Police only after learning of the attack and has not announced any change to its referral threshold.
