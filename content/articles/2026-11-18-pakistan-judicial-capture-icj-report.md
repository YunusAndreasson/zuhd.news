Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s top courts no longer check government, jurists find.

Rights victims' avenue for redress is seriously impaired, the International Commission of Jurists says.

The 26th Amendment let a commission without a judge majority pick constitutional benches, and the 27th made them a separate court.

The government argues the changes check judicial overreach and cut case backlogs.

The report asks Islamabad to open a full review and invite the UN rapporteur on judges.
