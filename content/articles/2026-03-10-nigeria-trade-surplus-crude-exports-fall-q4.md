Nigeria's trade surplus topped ₦1.71 trillion despite falling crude. Refined petroleum product exports offset the crude decline, but total exports still fell 5.25% to ₦18.96 trillion from ₦20.01 trillion a year earlier. The Iran war's oil shock now threatens Africa's largest oil producer with either a revenue windfall or a volume collapse as buyers seek stable supplies.
