Washington — FDA advisers endorsed Moderna's mRNA flu vaccine unanimously.

An FDA official had earlier refused to accept the shot for review; the advisory committee voted unanimously to recommend it.

FDA staff signaled a traditional approval pathway for adults 50-64 and an accelerated pathway for those 65 and older.
