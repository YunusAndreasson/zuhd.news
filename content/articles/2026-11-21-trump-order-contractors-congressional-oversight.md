Washington — Firms that answer Congress without permission can lose federal contracts.

Democrats, who take both chambers next year, plan to investigate no-bid contracts and alleged pay-to-play schemes.

The order tells contractors to coordinate replies with the White House and withhold anything it deems confidential or privileged.

Representative Robert Garcia has sent a dozen preservation notices to officials, including Vice President JD Vance.
