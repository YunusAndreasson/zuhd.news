Manaus — Forecasters see the drought coming four months out.

Advance notice turns the fire season into a policy choice, not a surprise.

Warmer Pacific waters suppress Amazon rainfall, drying the canopy that then burns and pushes the forest toward collapse.

Scientists say this El Niño may bring the most severe Amazon drought yet recorded.
