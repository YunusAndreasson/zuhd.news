Giza — Flames engulfed cars on Cairo's Ring Road, killing at least 30.

It is one of [Egypt](country:EG)'s deadliest road accidents in years, in the working-class district of Al-Warraq.

State media reported a fallen high-voltage cable made the driver lose control, and the tanker crossed the median and overturned.

The regional public prosecutor opened an investigation and ordered DNA samples taken from the bodies.
