Harare — Deportation cuts off HIV treatment mid-course.

[Zimbabwe](country:ZW) pairs telehealth with case managers to reconnect migrants deported from host countries to the antiretroviral regimen they lost crossing the border.

The system targets a gap where every deportation risks a treatment relapse.
