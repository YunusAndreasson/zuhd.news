Washington — 4 Republicans handed Democrats a 215-208 war powers win.

The House directed Trump to end hostilities with [Iran](country:IR) — the first time it defied him on the war — after the 60-day War Powers Resolution deadline lapsed May 1 with no authorization vote.

The Senate must still pass the measure and survive a near-certain presidential veto.
