Tokyo — [Japan](country:JP) will assemble US air-to-air missiles costing over $1 million each.

The [US](country:US) wants a second production node as demand rises across American and allied forces.

Raytheon targets at least 1,900 missiles a year under a contract worth up to $20.7 billion, and Japan's plant will make a share.

The plant's location, timeline, capacity and Japanese industrial partner remain undisclosed.
