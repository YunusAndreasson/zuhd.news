Milwaukee — A Tesla's security protocols stopped agents planting a tracker.

Agents wanted to follow a suspected drug trafficker's car when they could not watch it.

The filing does not name the protocol, but Sentry Mode records through 8 cameras and alerts the owner's phone.

Agents arrested the suspect and 17 others in May, and he pleaded not guilty and awaits trial on bond.
