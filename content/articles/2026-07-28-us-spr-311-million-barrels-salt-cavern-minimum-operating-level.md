Washington — The Strategic Petroleum Reserve dropped to 311.4 million barrels.

Drawing crude below the 300-million floor forces fresh water into salt caverns, eroding the walls, the Government Accountability Office warns.

Trump said the [US](country:US) reserve could run out "in about four weeks" as strikes threaten Gulf shipping chokepoints.
