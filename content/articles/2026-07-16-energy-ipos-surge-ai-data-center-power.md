New York — Energy initial public offerings raised $12.6 billion this year.

Dealogic says that's the fastest pace since the dot-com bubble, as investors chase the electricity AI data centers consume.

Nearly two-thirds of this year's energy offerings already trade below their offer price, Dealogic data show.
