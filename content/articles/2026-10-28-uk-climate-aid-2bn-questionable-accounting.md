London — Freedom of information data show £2.1bn of climate aid was relabelled.

Without it, the [UK](country:GB) would have missed its £11.6bn target by nearly £1bn.

In 2023 the government loosened its definition of climate spending, so existing development-bank, private-sector, Afghanistan and Sudan aid counted.

The government says it met the target but has not published the full breakdown.

Labour has kept the looser rules while pledging deeper aid cuts.
