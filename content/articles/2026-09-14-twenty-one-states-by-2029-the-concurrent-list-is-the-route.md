Home Minister Amit Shah's Uniform Civil Code push could override Muslim, Christian and tribal personal law.

Personal law sits on India's concurrent list, letting the ruling alliance pass it state by state.

[India](country:IN)'s remaining alliance-run states face pressure to adopt the code before the next election.
