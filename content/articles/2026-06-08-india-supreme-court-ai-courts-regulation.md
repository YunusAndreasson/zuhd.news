New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s top court drafted rules banning algorithmic rulings.

The draft bars artificial intelligence from bail risk and recidivism scoring, prohibiting black-box systems in any matter affecting personal liberty.

Public consultation follows before implementation, making India one of the first jurisdictions to regulate artificial intelligence in courts.
