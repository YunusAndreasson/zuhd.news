Nuuk — Greenland's parliament unanimously asked for a deep-sea mining ban.

It came a day after [US](country:US) President Donald Trump said exploration was about to begin.

The ban would run to at least 2029, shielding fisheries and hunting, while minerals are seen as a path to independence from [Denmark](country:DK).

"There's no Greenlandic barrels of oil on ground in any near future," said Naaja Nathanielsen, Greenland's member of Denmark's parliament.

The government drafts the bill and may extend the moratorium in 2-year increments.
