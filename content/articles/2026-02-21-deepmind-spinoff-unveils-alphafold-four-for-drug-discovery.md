AlphaFold 4 doubles its predecessor's drug-discovery accuracy. Isomorphic Labs, DeepMind's drug discovery spinoff, released the model to predict how proteins bind to candidate drugs. The company's first AI-designed cancer drug enters Phase 1 trials this year, with the model remaining proprietary.
