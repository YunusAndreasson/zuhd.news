London — War-crimes inquiry hides 2 SAS commanders' identities.

Inquiry chair Haddon-Cave accepted a national security argument shielding officers who commanded units during [Afghanistan](country:AF) killings in 2010–2013.

The chair may revisit anonymity orders once the inquiry ends, though no timeline was given.
