Satellite imagery revealed a 110-meter vessel at Bohai Shipyard. Defense analysts identified it as China's first Type 095 nuclear attack submarine, designed to carry vertical missile launchers and approach US Virginia-class capabilities. The design narrows an undersea gap that has favored Western navies for decades.
