Seoul — Coupang exposed 33 million users' data, [South Korea](country:KR)'s regulator found.

The Personal Information Protection Commission fined the e-commerce firm a record $408M for lax authentication and delayed breach notifications.

Coupang says only 3,000 records were exposed and plans to challenge the ruling in court.
