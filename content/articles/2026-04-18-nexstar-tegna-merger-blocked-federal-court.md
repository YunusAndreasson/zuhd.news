Sacramento — A federal judge blocked Nexstar's FCC-approved Tegna merger. The $3.5B deal would concentrate 228 broadcast stations in 132 markets — 80% of US households — under 1 company Trump backed. No trial date is set; Nexstar plans to appeal, and California will keep fighting.
