London — [Britain](country:GB) cracked Google's AI grip on publishers.

Google controls 90%+ of UK search; publishers can now exit AI Overviews without losing regular search rankings, the Competition and Markets Authority said.

Google is testing the opt-out control; sites that exit will lose AI Overview traffic but retain organic search placement.
