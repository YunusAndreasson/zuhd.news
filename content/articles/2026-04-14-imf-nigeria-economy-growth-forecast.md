Abuja — Nigeria grows 4.1% as global growth falters. IMF's 2026 World Economic Outlook projects global growth at 3.1%, blaming the Middle East war; Nigeria outpaces the US at 2.3%, Germany at 0.8%, and France at 0.9%. The 4.1% forecast holds only if the IMF's own caveat — conflict "limited in duration and scope" — holds.
