Kyiv — [Ukraine](country:UA) needs $154 billion from West by 2030.

Its Finance Ministry estimates $46.4 billion is needed in 2026 alone, via frozen Russian assets, Western loans, and IMF transfers.

The 2027 requirement is the highest single year at $47.7 billion; all figures are preliminary.
