N'Djamena — 8 opposition leaders jailed for planning a march.

[Chad](country:TD)'s Supreme Court dissolved the main opposition coalition the day before sentencing, leaving President Déby's party unchallenged.

The African Union and UN have urged dialogue; the government has not responded and no election is scheduled.
