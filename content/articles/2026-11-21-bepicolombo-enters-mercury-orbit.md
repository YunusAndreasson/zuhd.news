Leicester — Mercury has an orbiter for the first time since 2015.

Its two probes will study Mercury's magnetic field and how it interacts with solar wind.

The craft launched in October 2018 and had to lower itself toward the Sun without gaining speed and overshooting.

Japan's Mio probe reaches its orbit on 9 December, and the European orbiter settles by March 2027.
