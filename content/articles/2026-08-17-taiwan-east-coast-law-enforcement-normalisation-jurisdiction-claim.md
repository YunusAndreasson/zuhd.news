Taipei — [China](country:CN)'s coast guard now patrols east of Taiwan.

It erodes Taiwan's only fallback route if the Strait is closed, analysts say.

Beijing frames the patrols as routine law enforcement, not military activity, the outlet said.

An incident between patrol vessels could force Taipei to define the activity as military.
