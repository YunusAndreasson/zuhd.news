London — Soil fungi stole bacterial DNA to trigger rain. Microbes carry ice-nucleating proteins — genes horizontally transferred from bacteria — that seed clouds and pull rainfall down from the atmosphere. How widely this mechanism operates across ecosystems and whether land-use change is disrupting it remains unquantified.
