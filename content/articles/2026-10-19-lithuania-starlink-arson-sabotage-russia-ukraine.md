Vilnius — 3 NATO states have now seen Starlink sites attacked.

Starlink connects [Ukraine](country:UA)'s armed forces, and Lithuanian residents, businesses and troops.

After fires in [Poland](country:PL) and [Bulgaria](country:BG), Molotov cocktails hit antennas and a control unit at a Lithuanian firm renting space to SpaceX.

Prime Minister Mindaugas Sinkevičius blames [Russia](country:RU) but offered no evidence, and suspects are still being sought.

[Lithuania](country:LT) will raise the alert level at more than 15 critical infrastructure sites, the prime minister said.
