London — One cheap hologram now replaces a fluorescence microscope.

Labs without confocal microscopes or dye budgets gain a route to cell analysis.

A neural network reconstructs fluorescence-equivalent images from a single low-cost exposure, no dyes or stains needed.

The network infers these images rather than measuring them, so diagnostic use still needs validation.
