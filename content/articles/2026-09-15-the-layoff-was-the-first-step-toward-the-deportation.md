Giza — 33 men were sleeping in a public park.

[Sudan](country:SD)'s collapsing economy cost them the jobs anchoring their residency in [Egypt](country:EG).

Sudanese firms in Cairo stopped paying wages, then housing, pushing workers into the Giza park where police found them.

All 33 now face deportation to a country still at war.
