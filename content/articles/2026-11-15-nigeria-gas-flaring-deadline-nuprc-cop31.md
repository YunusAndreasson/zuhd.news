Antalya — [Nigeria](country:NG) flared 12% more gas in 2024, the World Bank says.

Its flaring intensity reached 12 cubic metres per barrel, more than twice the global average.

An official of Nigeria's upstream regulator says the energy transition is constraining funding for gas-capture projects.

The law lets the regulator take flare gas free, yet its 28 commercialisation permits date from December 2025.

Nigeria already missed a 2020 deadline and now faces the World Bank's 2030 target.
