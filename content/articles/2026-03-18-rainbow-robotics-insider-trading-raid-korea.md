Korean prosecutors raided Rainbow Robotics and a Samsung compound. 16 suspects, including 2 executives, allegedly made 3-4 billion won trading ahead of Samsung acquiring a controlling stake in the robot arm maker. Prosecutors have filed no charges, though South Korea's financial regulator referred the case to them last month.
