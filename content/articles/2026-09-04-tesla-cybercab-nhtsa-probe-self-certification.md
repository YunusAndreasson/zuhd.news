Austin — Regulators opened a probe into 1,000 Tesla Cybercabs a day after launch.

Tesla shares fell more than 6%, and the same rule governs every robotaxi rival.

Tesla self-certified the Cybercab, which has no steering wheel or pedals, against standards written for cars that have them.

The road-safety agency's audit could force a court fight over whether that certification holds.
