Brasília — [Brazil](country:BR) and [Argentina](country:AR) will supply [US](country:US) beef.

The move sends grocery-price relief abroad while domestic ranchers absorb the loss.

US cattle herds sit at their lowest count since 1951, leaving imports as the only quick fix.

Kansas State economist Glynn Tonsor says the odds of lower prices are near zero.
