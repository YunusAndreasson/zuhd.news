Israeli settlers shot 3 Palestinians in Mukhmas, killing a teenager. Witnesses described soldiers smoking nearby while the attack unfolded — part of a documented pattern where the Israeli military protects settlers through deliberate inaction. No arrests have been made, including for last week's settler killing of a Palestinian-American.
