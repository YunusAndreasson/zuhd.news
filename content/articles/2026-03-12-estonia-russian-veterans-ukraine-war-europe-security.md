Russia has 1 million combat veterans from Ukraine. Estonian Foreign Minister Margus Tsahkna warned that Schengen's open borders give those veterans a path to any EU capital and that some are recruitable for Kremlin-backed sabotage. Estonia is pressing the EU to ban Russian war veterans from the Schengen zone.
