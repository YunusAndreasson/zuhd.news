Washington — Universities would pay $100,000 per international graduate who works.

About 420,000 students, a third of the international total, used the work programme in 2025-2026.

The fee is $70,000 for the first year of Optional Practical Training, plus $30,000 for the science and engineering extension.

The Department of Homeland Security projects enrolment would fall by up to 44%.

Public comment on the proposal in the [US](country:US) closes 9 November.
