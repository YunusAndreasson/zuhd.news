Belgrade — Apple's alert exposed spyware on a dozen phones.

Activists in [Serbia](country:RS) learned they were surveilled from a phone vendor, not a court.

Forensic analysis found Pegasus and NoviSpy-style malware on devices, sold to states as a licensed service.

A rights lawyer says Serbian law requires a court order that none exists.
