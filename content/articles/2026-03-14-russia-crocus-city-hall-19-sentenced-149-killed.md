Russia convicted 19 for the Crocus City Hall massacre. 15 received life terms after a closed-door trial where Russia blamed Ukraine, contradicting ISIS's claim of responsibility for the attack that killed 149. The secret proceedings left open who financed and directed the attack inside Russia.
