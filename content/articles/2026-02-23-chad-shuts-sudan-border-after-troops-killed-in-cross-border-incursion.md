Cross-border fire killed 8 in Chad's border town Tina. Chad sealed its entire eastern frontier, closing the main escape route for nearly 1 million Sudanese refugees sheltering on its soil. Aid groups warned the closure traps civilians fleeing famine on the wrong side of the border.
