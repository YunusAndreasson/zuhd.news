Berlin — The federal government cannot say how much GovTech Platforms awards.

[Germany](country:DE)'s federal digital stack gets a rival built by an association led by politicians.

The government's reply to a Greens inquiry says GovTech Platforms has awarded work to 114 firms since 2024, 4 outside the EU.

Public contracts went out without tender, the government's answers to the Greens confirm.
