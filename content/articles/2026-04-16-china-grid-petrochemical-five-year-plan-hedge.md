Beijing — China's grid operators spent $24 billion in Q1. Solar utilization fell to 90.8% — the grid can't absorb existing renewables — while Beijing approved a new petrochemicals five-year plan. The $146 billion annual grid plan runs through 2030; the petrochemical buildout targets serve a separate plastics-export economy.
