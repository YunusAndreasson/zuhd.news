Paris — [France](country:FR) detained 144 protesters, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

Police counted 82,500 marchers across 112 rallies, while organizers say 150,000.

Students began blockading schools in late September, and unions joined in October demanding wage rises and no more austerity.

The interior ministry says both rallies and turnout are declining.

The ministry has not said why the 144 were detained.
