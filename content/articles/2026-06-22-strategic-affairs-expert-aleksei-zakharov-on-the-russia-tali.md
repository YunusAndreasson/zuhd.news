Kabul — [Russia](country:RU) is reportedly moving toward a defense deal with the Taliban.

The deal includes Soviet-era equipment and possibly air defense systems and drones the Taliban could use against [Pakistan](country:PK), analysts say.

Analysts warn a Russia-armed Kabul would leave Pakistan between 2 adversaries, with [India](country:IN) to the east.
