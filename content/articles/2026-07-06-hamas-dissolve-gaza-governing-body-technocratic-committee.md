Gaza — Hamas dissolved its 20-year governing body.

A technocratic committee says it's ready to govern, but [Israel](country:IL)'s Board of Peace demands Hamas surrender its weapons first, per UN Resolution 2803.

The Board has not said when committee members, long blocked from entering Gaza, can take up authority.
