A Ukrainian drone struck Russia's Taman oil port. Krasnodar Governor Veniamin Kondratyev confirmed fires at a storage tank, a warehouse, and shipping terminals, with 2 people wounded and over 100 firefighters deployed. US-brokered talks between Russian and Ukrainian envoys begin Tuesday in Geneva, days before the 4th anniversary of Russia's full-scale invasion.
