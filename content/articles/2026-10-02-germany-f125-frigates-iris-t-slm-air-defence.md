Berlin — Each of 4 F125 frigates gets 32 air-defense missiles.

[Germany](country:DE)'s F125 class has long drawn criticism for little combat power relative to its size.

The Budget Committee approved adapting the land-based IRIS-T SLM on 23 September, after Baden-Württemberg fired 3 live missiles in Norway in October 2025.

Diehl Defense will build prototypes and run test firings to validate integration before any fleet fit-out.
