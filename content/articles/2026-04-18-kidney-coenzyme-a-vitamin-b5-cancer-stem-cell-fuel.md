London — Kidneys produce the body's coenzyme A, not livers. Coenzyme A from vitamin B5 fuels stem cell division; Myc-driven overproduction in the kidney directly feeds tumor growth, per Drosophila research. The CoA-isoprenoid synthesis pathway is now a druggable target for cancers where MYC is overexpressed.
