Khasab — 2 limpet mines disabled a tanker in Hormuz Thursday.

[US](country:US) Central Command said Friday that the strait's primary routes are cleared of mines.

A small boat attached two mines to the hull, setting the engine room ablaze, then withdrew, three maritime security sources assessed.

[Iran](country:IR)'s Revolutionary Guards say 3 tankers on unauthorised routes met incidents, and nobody has named who placed the mines.

The US military, which coaches tankers through the strait, has not confirmed the attack.
