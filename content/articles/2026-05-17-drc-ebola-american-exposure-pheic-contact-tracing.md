Kinshasa — WHO declared [DR Congo](country:CD)'s Ebola a global health emergency.

246 cases and 80 deaths from Bundibugyo ebolavirus — a strain with no licensed vaccine — triggered the ruling without an expert panel, an unprecedented step.

The US is evacuating exposed Americans, possibly to a military base in [Germany](country:DE), for a 21-day monitoring window.
