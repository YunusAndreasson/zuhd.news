Tehran — US-Israeli strikes damaged 81,000 homes across Iran. Iran's Red Crescent said 23 days of attacks also hit hospitals, schools, and ambulances, calling the damage a violation of international humanitarian law. The Red Crescent has called for an emergency international response, and no ceasefire has been announced.
