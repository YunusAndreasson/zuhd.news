Santa Bárbara de Iscuandé — 14 people died in a landslide at an unlicensed gold pit.

Armed groups control gold in Nariño, in [Colombia](country:CO)'s southwest, and hampered rescuers, the governor's office said.

Informal pits lack structural reinforcement, and a similar collapse killed 13 people in Policarpa, also in Nariño, in August.

Governor Luis Alfonso Escobar says rescue is over, but a disaster official counts 12 to 15 miners still missing.

Escobar asked Bogotá and the National Mining Agency to speed formalisation of small-scale miners.
