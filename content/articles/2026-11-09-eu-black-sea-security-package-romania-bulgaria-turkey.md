Bucharest — Drones reportedly hit at least 7 Black Sea ships last month.

2 sailors died and at least 25 are unaccounted for.

Ursula von der Leyen pledged 60 million euros, 10 million for surveillance and rescue and 50 million for a Security Hub.

[Romania](country:RO) blames [Russia](country:RU), but Bulgaria says only that the drones came from the east.

Von der Leyen meets Bulgaria's prime minister in Varna on Tuesday.
