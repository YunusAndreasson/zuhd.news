New York — IBM's Nanostack chip hits a 0.7nm node, projecting 50% compute or 70% energy efficiency gains.

The design stacks transistors vertically, doubling density without expanding chip area.

IBM does not manufacture chips; [Japan](country:JP)'s Rapidus is among partners targeting commercialization within a decade.
