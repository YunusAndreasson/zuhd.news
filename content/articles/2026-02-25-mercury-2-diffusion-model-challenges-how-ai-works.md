Mercury 2 generates 1,009 tokens per second. Inception Labs, an AI startup, built it on diffusion, the technique behind image generators, refining many words in parallel instead of predicting one at a time. At one-quarter the price of Google's Gemini Flash, it undercuts the cost model the AI industry is built on.
