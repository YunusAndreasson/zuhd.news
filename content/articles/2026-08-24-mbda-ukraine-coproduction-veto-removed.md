Kyiv — [Britain](country:GB) will let MBDA share SCALP missile component data with [France](country:FR) and [Ukraine](country:UA).

That moves Kyiv from receiving finished missiles to co-producing them, ending London's per-transfer veto.

Weapons transfers are reversible, but a missile assembly line inside a war zone is not — and becomes a target itself.

MBDA's shareholder governments must now decide how to police usage without that veto.
