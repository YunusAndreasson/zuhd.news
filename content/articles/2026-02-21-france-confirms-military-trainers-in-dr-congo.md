France acknowledged training Congo's military after photos surfaced online. French personnel have trained 4 jungle battalions of about 800 soldiers each in Kisangani under a 2021 cooperation agreement. Those battalions are now fighting M23, which Congo and the UN accuse Rwanda of backing.
