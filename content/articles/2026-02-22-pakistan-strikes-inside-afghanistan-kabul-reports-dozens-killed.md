Airstrikes killed dozens in 2 Afghan provinces. Pakistan targeted Pakistani Taliban camps in Nangarhar and Paktika; Afghanistan reported the strikes hit a school and homes, killing civilians including children. Kabul promised a "measured response," threatening to collapse a fragile ceasefire from October.
