Dubai — Airlines shed $53 billion in value since war began. The 3 Gulf mega-carriers built their empires on Gulf geography as the Europe-Asia corridor — that corridor is now a war zone. Jet fuel doubled since late February as airlines warned of shortages that will ground more flights unless the Strait of Hormuz reopens.
