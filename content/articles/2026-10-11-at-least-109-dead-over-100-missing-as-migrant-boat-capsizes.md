Obock — 109 migrants, 15 of them children, drowned off [Djibouti](country:DJ).

Over 116 are missing and all 80-plus survivors are Ethiopian, the International Organization for Migration said.

More than 500,000 people attempted the crossing to [Yemen](country:YE) last year, and at least 900 died or went missing, the agency says.

Djiboutian authorities are still searching for the missing and arranging burials for the dead.
