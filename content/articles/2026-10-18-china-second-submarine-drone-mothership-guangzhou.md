Guangzhou — China's second submarine-drone mothership is a third smaller than the first.

The [US](country:US) Navy has no comparable launch-and-recovery ship in its inventory.

[China](country:CN)'s hulls launch vehicles through a stern garage door and well deck, with six lift towers on this one.

Two hulls may still be experimental, and rumours of a larger programme remain unconfirmed.
