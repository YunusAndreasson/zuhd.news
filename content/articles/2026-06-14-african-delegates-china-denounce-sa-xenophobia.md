Beijing — 5 delegations condemned South Africa's xenophobic attacks on migrants.

African delegates at [China](country:CN) Commerce Ministry seminars called the violence a misdirection of economic grievance, invoking pan-African solidarity.

The African Union faces calls to enforce cross-border migrant protections across member states.
