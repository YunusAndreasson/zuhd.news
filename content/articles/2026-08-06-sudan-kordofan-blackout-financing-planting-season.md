Khartoum — [Sudan](country:SD)'s grid has failed 3 times in a week.

A fault at Merowe Dam cascaded nationwide because war damage disabled voltage-control equipment there, and unpaid repair bills have stalled fixes for months.

North Kordofan's planting season, already strained by fighting, now depends on money the electricity sector hasn't received.
