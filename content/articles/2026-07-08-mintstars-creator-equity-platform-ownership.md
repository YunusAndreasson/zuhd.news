Boston — A platform co-founder gave away her entire equity stake.

Jessica Van Meir set aside 20% of MintStars for a creator-ownership pool and donated her remaining 3% to a sex-worker support nonprofit.

Creators will earn ownership points tied to revenue, referrals and tenure on the platform.
