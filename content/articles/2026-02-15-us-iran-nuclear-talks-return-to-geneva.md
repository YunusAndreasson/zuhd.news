Geneva will host a 2nd round of indirect nuclear talks this week. The Swiss Foreign Ministry confirmed the session, following an initial meeting in Oman on 6 February between Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and US envoy Steve Witkoff. Washington demands Iran halt all uranium enrichment; Tehran insists the talks remain limited to the nuclear program. Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu pressed US President Donald Trump to expand any deal to cover Iran's ballistic missiles and its funding of Hamas and Hezbollah. Trump warned Tehran after the first round that failure to reach agreement would be "very traumatic."
