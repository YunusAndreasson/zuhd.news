Brussels — The EU strategy calls for an undersea cable linking Greenland to Europe.

The first update since 2021 follows Trump's attempt to acquire Greenland, a [Danish](country:DK) territory.

Melting ice opens shipping routes, and the plan adds satellite surveillance, an Arctic cable hub and help buying icebreakers.

The EU will not compete with NATO, Commissioner Jozef Sikela said, since member states hold the budgets.

The strategy puts no price tag on any of it.
