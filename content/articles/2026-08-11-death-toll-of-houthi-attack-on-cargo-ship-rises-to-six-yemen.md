Aden — Six sailors died in a Bab al-Mandeb strike.

Three Pakistanis and an Indonesian were among the dead crew of a shipping war.

The coastguard says the strike also hit rescuers answering the distress call, which it called a violation of maritime law.

[Yemen](country:YE)'s coastguard blames the Houthis, who have not yet responded to the claim.
