Rajouri — A cloudburst left one woman missing Saturday.

The India Meteorological Department's forecast of heavy rain led authorities to halt the Amarnath and Vaishno Devi pilgrimages, which draw 370,000 people.

Officials say the routes reopen only after weather clears and a fresh safety assessment, with no date set.
