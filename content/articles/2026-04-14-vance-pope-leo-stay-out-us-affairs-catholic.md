Washington — JD Vance told Pope Leo to stay out of US affairs. Vance, a 2019 Catholic convert and the government's highest-ranking Catholic, rejected Leo's authority on immigration and war while publishing a book about his own faith. Leo, traveling Africa, said he intends to keep speaking; Trump called him "weak on crime."
