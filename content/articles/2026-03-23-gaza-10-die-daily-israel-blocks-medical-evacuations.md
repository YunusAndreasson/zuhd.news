Gaza — 10 Palestinians die daily waiting for medical transfer. Israel seized the Rafah crossing in May 2024 — Gaza's only border with Egypt — and has allowed just 24 patients through in 4 days. Gaza's health ministry says 400 daily evacuations are needed; 1,971 critical patients will deteriorate within weeks if Israel keeps the crossing closed.
