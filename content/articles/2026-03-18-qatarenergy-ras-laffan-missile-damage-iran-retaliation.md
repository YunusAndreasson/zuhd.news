Iranian missiles struck Qatar's Ras Laffan gas terminal Wednesday. Qatar is the world's second-largest LNG exporter, with Ras Laffan supplying gas to customers from Japan to Germany. QatarEnergy confirmed extensive damage, evacuated all personnel, and gave no timeline for restoring exports.
