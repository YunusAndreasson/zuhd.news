Kyiv — [Russia](country:RU) says it hit an Ukrsibbank data center, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

The Defense Ministry says the Ukrainian army used the data center to store intelligence.

The same ministry statement claims it also hit a dry cargo ship carrying military goods to Odesa.

No Ukrainian or independent confirmation has appeared, and damage and casualties are unreported.
