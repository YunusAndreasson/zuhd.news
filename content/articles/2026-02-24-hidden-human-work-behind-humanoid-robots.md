Robotics companies employ people to remotely operate robots marketed as autonomous. Startup 1X plans to ship its $20,000 humanoid Neo to homes this year without committing to any level of autonomy, while other firms use manual labourers wearing motion sensors as hidden data collectors. The pattern mirrors self-driving cars: conceal the humans, claim the machine.
