Isle of Wight — Europe's first megaraptoran is a 5-metre, tiger-weight hunter.

It fits the hypothesis that megaraptorans spread from Asia through Europe to Africa and South America.

A fragmented braincase sat unnoticed in the Natural History Museum's drawers from 1924 until researchers named it Invictibellatrix amberina.

Paleontologists still debate whether megaraptorans are tyrannosaur relatives, and this braincase is Europe's only evidence.
