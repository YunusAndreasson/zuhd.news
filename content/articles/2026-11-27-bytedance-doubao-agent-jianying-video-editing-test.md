Beijing — Doubao now edits video in Jianying from one sentence.

Zhidx's tests produced a usable talking-head cut but failed on a 52-minute video.

ByteDance's agent drives its own editing app through an official plugin, leaving a Jianying draft for manual changes.

Zhidx's tests used 1,100 Jianying credits, and the plugin is not free.

Zhidx says full automation from import to delivery still needs work.
