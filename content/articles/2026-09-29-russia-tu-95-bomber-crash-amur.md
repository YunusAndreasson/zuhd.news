Amur — A Tu-95 nuclear-capable bomber crashed, killing six crew.

It shrinks the bomber fleet that forms the air leg of [Russia](country:RU)'s nuclear triad.

Designed in 1952, the Tu-95 still flies cruise-missile strikes on [Ukraine](country:UA), and Ukrainian drone raids on bomber bases have thinned the fleet.

The defence ministry says the plane carried no ammunition and was on a routine training flight, not combat.

Russian investigators have not yet said what caused the crash.
