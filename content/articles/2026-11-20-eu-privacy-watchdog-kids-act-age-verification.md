Brussels — A 27-page opinion lists ten objections to Kids Act age checks.

Verifying age means identifying every user, adults included, on social media, video platforms, chatbots and games.

European Data Protection Supervisor Wojciech Wiewiórowski says checks are easily bypassed with VPNs, and biometric face scans are problematic.

He backs EU-wide protection for minors and recommends fewer age barriers and more safe features for everyone.

The European Parliament and Council of ministers now negotiate their positions on the Kids Act.
