77 bodies in a Serbian mass grave overturned assumptions about prehistoric violence. 40 were children under 12, and 87% of the adults were female — reversing the male-dominated pattern found in every known conflict burial from the period. The 2,800-year-old site at Gomolava shows the killers then buried their victims with offerings in a deliberate ceremony.
