400MW data centre claims 25% of Durban's electricity. A South Korean consortium plans it beside the 2Africa submarine cable, drawn by South Africa's cheap electricity despite state utility Eskom's inability to meet existing demand. Targeted for 2028, it would process overseas AI workloads while Durban's residents absorb the grid strain.
