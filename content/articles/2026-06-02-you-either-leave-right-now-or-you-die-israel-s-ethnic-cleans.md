Ain Arab — Israeli soldiers emptied the village at gunpoint.

[Israel](country:IL) declared a unilateral "yellow line" buffer zone in southern [Lebanon](country:LB), ordering villagers out in 2 hours; soldiers told residents "leave or die."

Displaced families shelter in schools with no return date; Israel has not announced withdrawal from the zone.
