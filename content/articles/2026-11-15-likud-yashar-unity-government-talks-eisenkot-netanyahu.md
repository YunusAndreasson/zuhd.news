Jerusalem — Eisenkot is negotiating with the man he says must go.

Neither bloc reached 61 seats, and Arab-led parties hold a record 19.

Eisenkot's bloc and both Arab-led factions hold 67 seats, but Avigdor Lieberman and Naftali Bennett reject relying on them.

"Nice try, but these tricks don't work on me," Eisenkot said of Netanyahu's unity offer.

[Israel](country:IL)'s president Isaac Herzog gave Eisenkot until 6 December to form a government, extendable to 20 December.
