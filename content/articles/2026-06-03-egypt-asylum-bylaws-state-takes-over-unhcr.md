Cairo — [Egypt](country:EG) gave itself 9 months to replace UNHCR as sole arbiter of refugee status.

Bylaws issued a year late transfer asylum to a state committee, displacing the UN agency serving millions of Sudanese and Syrian applicants.

A refugee-law specialist warned the timeline is too short to protect refugees from arrest and deportation.
