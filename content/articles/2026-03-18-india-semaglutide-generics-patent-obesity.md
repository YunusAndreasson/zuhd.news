India's semaglutide patent expires Friday, halving monthly costs. India's generic drug industry slashed HIV treatment costs in the 2000s and will now produce at least 50 semaglutide versions for under $55 monthly. Novo Nordisk and Eli Lilly face price pressure as Indian generics compete for developing-country markets.
