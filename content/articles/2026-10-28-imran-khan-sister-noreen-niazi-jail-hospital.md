Lahore — Detained Noreen Niazi entered a cardiology hospital with high blood pressure, her lawyer said.

Authorities have held all 3 of Imran Khan's sisters under preventive detention since 21 September.

The Lahore deputy commissioner ordered 30-day detentions under a 1960 public-order ordinance, then extended them on 7 October.

A senior jail officer denies the lawyer's claim that the sisters are being denied medicines.

Their lawyer's petitions for family and legal visits remain unanswered by the deputy commissioner.
