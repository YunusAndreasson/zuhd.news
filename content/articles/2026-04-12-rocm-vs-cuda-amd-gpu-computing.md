Santa Clara — AMD narrows the gap with Nvidia's CUDA lock-in. ROCm, AMD's open GPU compute stack, now runs more AI workloads natively, but operator gaps keep production clusters on Nvidia hardware. Whether ROCm achieves parity will determine if Nvidia maintains its pricing monopoly through the next GPU generation.
