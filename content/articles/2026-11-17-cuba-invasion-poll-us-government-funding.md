Havana — Cuba poll co-owner's outlet received $2.3 million in US grants.

The Vanderbilt-backed September survey says Cubans do not oppose a [US](country:US) military intervention.

Vanderbilt's Latin American Public Opinion Project contracted CubaData, a cell-phone pollster whose co-owner founded Diario de Cuba, which took the grants from 2013 to 2021, annual reports show.

PBS NewsHour and Foreign Affairs have cited the poll without examining that funding, the investigation says.
