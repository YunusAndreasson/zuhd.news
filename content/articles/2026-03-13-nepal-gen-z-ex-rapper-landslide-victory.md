Shah's Rastriya Swatantra Party won 182 of 275 seats. Gen Z voters who toppled the last PM over corruption handed Nepal a parliamentary majority its fractured politics rarely produce. Shah takes office at 35, promising anti-corruption prosecutions and jobs after decades of dynastic rule.
