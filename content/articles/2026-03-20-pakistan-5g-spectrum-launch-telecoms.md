Pakistan sold 480MHz of 5G spectrum for $507 million. 3 telecoms now serve Islamabad and Pakistan's 4 provincial capitals — the world's 5th most populous country to reach 5G, years behind India and Bangladesh. Ufone is in the final phase of acquiring Telenor — the combined company would hold Pakistan's largest spectrum portfolio.
