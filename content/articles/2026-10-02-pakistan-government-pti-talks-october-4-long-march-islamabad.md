Islamabad — Talks with Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf began over its 4 October march.

Federal ministers warn of governor's rule or emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa if the march proceeds.

The party is marching to demand founder Imran Khan's release and warns of dire consequences if the Centre imposes either measure.

Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi, Rana Sanaullah and Sherry Rehman are expected to meet party chairman Gohar Ali Khan, with no agreement reported.
