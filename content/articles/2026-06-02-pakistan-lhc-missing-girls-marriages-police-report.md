Lahore — Punjab Police: 80% of recovered girls had married.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s Lahore High Court heard the claim as grounds for dropped kidnapping charges; the chief justice called non-recoveries "poor performance."

The court ordered a progress report by June 18; 1,853 of 3,258 reported-missing girls remain untraced.
