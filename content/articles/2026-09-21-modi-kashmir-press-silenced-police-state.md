Jammu — Police raided a closed newspaper office and claimed live AK-47 cartridges.

Since 2018, only 5 journalists have faced India's anti-terror law, and trials rarely start.

[India](country:IN) revoked Jammu and Kashmir's autonomy in 2019, and by 2020 three of the Kashmir Times' four editions had shut.

Police called the 19 November 2025 raid an anti-terror operation, though the offices had been shut since 2022.

Its editors revived the paper as a digital newsroom in exile in the US in November 2023.
