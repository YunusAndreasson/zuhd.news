Pune — Eight dead, one still buried under garbage.

A heap at the Moshi dump collapsed onto a Waste-to-Energy office run by the Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation, trapping 23 people on unstable landfill.

Rescuers with excavators and NDRF dog squads keep searching for the last missing worker.
