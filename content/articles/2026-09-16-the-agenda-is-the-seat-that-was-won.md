Vienna — [Iran](country:IR)'s mission claimed a General Committee seat.

The seat lets Tehran help set the agenda on its own nuclear file.

The General Committee decides the conference's agenda and order of business, a small but real procedural power.

Iran's mission said member states rejected [US](country:US) pressure but did not disclose the vote count.
