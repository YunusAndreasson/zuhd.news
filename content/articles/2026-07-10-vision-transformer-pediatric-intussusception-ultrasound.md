Wuhu — A trained model beat senior doctors.

The vision transformer, trained on 5,602 [China](country:CN) cases, predicted treatment failure with 93.7% accuracy versus 74.7% for senior doctors, the study found.

The tool still needs validation beyond its 190-patient trial before hospitals can rely on it.
