New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s Supreme Court seized a dowry death probe.

Chief Justice Surya Kant will examine a delayed first information report, disputed autopsy, and alleged probe interference in Twisha Sharma's death.

Madhya Pradesh sought Central Bureau of Investigation takeover; Giribala, an accused in the case, faces an anticipatory bail challenge.
