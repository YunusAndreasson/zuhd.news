United States — United Launch Alliance blames Vulcan's fuel supply, not Blue Origin's engines.

Amazon Leo lost dozens of satellites in the Sunday launch failure.

Low booster thrust came from inadequate fuel flow from the vehicle to the BE-4 engines, which burn liquid oxygen and methane.

ULA calls the findings preliminary and says work remains to determine cause.

Blue Origin's New Glenn, which uses a similar BE-4, could return to flight as soon as January.
