Iran struck Bahrain, Qatar, and Saudi Arabia on Monday. Trump told CBS the war was "very complete" and the US "very far" ahead of its stated 4-5 week timeline, while the Strait of Hormuz remained closed and Iran continued attacking under new Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei. The Pentagon confirmed no drawdown; 10 vessels near Hormuz came under attack the same day.
