Uyo — Oil activity damaged farms in Ibeno, Obolo.

Nigerian banks fund the extraction communities blame for the harm.

Civil society groups argue banks enable extraction, so accountability should extend from drillers to financiers.

The coalition wants [Nigeria](country:NG)'s central bank to tighten sustainable-finance rules for lenders.
