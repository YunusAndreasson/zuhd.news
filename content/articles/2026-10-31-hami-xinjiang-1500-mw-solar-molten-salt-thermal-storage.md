Hami — [China](country:CN) is building a 1,500 MW solar project with heat storage, Iranian state agency Mehr reported.

Xinhua, republished by Mehr, says it will cut about 2.26 million tonnes of CO2 a year.

Molten salt stores solar heat, which turbines can turn into power after dark.

The report gives no completion date, storage hours or cost.
