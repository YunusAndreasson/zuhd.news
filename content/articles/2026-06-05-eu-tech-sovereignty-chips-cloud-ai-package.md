Brussels — Europe makes neither leading chips nor competitive cloud at scale today.

EU's Chips Act 2.0 pairs with a Cloud and AI Development Act to cut reliance on [US](country:US) and [Chinese](country:CN) semiconductors.

Both laws set targets, not timetables, leaving the dependency gap open.
