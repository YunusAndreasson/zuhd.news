Washington — The [US](country:US) now owns nearly 8% of GlobalFoundries.

It is the government's latest of 35 corporate stakes, from critical minerals to quantum computing.

A vehicle co-owned by the US and [Japan](country:JP) will fund two fabs, up to $21 billion, and own them while GlobalFoundries operates for about $3 billion.

The Cato Institute launched a project this week to unwind state ownership.

The first plant is due online in 2029, the second in 2031.
