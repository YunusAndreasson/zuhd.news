New York — 21 major banks are forming one stablecoin issuer.

Competitors are pooling issuance because none believes a solo bank coin wins alone.

A stablecoin is a fully-reserved, no-lending instrument, complying with new [US](country:US) and EU stablecoin laws.

The dollar token launches in early 2027, with a euro version planned to follow.
