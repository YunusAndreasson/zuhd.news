Hebron — [Israel](country:IL)'s Netanyahu ordered Hebron cordoned after 5 Israelis were wounded.

Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir demanded the Palestinian Authority's dismantling, though no evidence links it to the attack.

Netanyahu also ordered preparations for a full closure of the entire West Bank, effective Friday.

Israeli forces killed 2 men, named by Palestinian media as Mohammad and Firas Jaaba, and say they planned a larger attack.

Israelis vote on 27 October, and Polymarket traders give Netanyahu 28% to be next prime minister, down 3 points in a week.
