Dubai — Iranian watermelons still fill Dubai's largest wholesale market.

It shows how hard a full trade ban is to enforce at a port.

The [UAE](country:AE) vowed to halt all trade with [Iran](country:IR) last month, yet a trader estimates 90% of market produce is Iranian.

The UAE has not said whether enforcement of the ban will tighten.
