Washington — [Iran](country:IR)'s defenses downed dozens of [US](country:US) Reapers.

Production ended before the war; General Atomics has fewer than 10 unused units left, and an MQ-9 successor is years from delivery.

The Air Force has funds to begin buying back available units this fiscal year; the fleet stands at 135, down from 231 two years ago.
