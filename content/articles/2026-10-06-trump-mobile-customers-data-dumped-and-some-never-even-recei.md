United States — BYOD says it leaked 3,615 Trump Mobile customers' names and addresses.

One leaked customer says he paid a $100 deposit for the gold T1 phone and never got it.

Ransomware group BYOD says it infected a Liberty Mobile employee with an infostealer, then entered Trump Mobile through the carrier.

Neither the Trump Organization nor Liberty Mobile answered questions, and the dump holds no Trump family data, reporters found.

BYOD claims it still has access to Trump Mobile's systems.
