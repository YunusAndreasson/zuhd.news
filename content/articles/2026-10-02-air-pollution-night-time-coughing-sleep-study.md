Cambridge — Wildfire smoke raised Los Angeles night-time coughing 13%.

Coughing also rose with fine-particle pollution below WHO guidelines, even in Melbourne and Sydney.

A sleep app counted coughs for 18 months, averaging 98,000 users a night in 32 cities, and researchers tested for flu season.

The researchers say source-specific data are needed to confirm London's fireworks nights caused the cough rise.

Lead author Cecilia Mascolo says chronic effects of long-term low-level particle exposure warrant attention.
