Formula 1's Gulf season fell to Iranian missiles. Bahrain and Saudi Arabia spent billions positioning themselves as global sports hubs to diversify from oil — now under Iranian drone and missile attack. The next Grand Prix is Japan on March 27, with no Gulf races scheduled before May.
