Sydney — KPMG is cutting 27 partners and 360 staff.

Shedding owners, not just employees, reprices the firm's partnership during a whistleblower scandal.

Advisory revenue fell 16.9% this year, and consulting has long subsidized audit work sold to the same clients.

[Australia](country:AU)'s parliamentary inquiry into KPMG's conduct continues, with senators citing further whistleblower contacts.
