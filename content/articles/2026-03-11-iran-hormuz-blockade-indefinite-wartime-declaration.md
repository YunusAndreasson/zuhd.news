Iran's Revolutionary Guards declared Hormuz closed until the war ends. The strait carries one-fifth of global fossil fuels; Iran's shift from tactical threat to stated wartime condition removes the expectation of a diplomatic off-ramp. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi confirmed Iran has taken negotiations off the table entirely.
