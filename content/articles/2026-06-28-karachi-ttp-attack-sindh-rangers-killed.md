Karachi — Gulistan-e-Jauhar gun battle: 4 Rangers dead, 6 attackers killed.

Jamaat-ul-Ahrar, a banned Taliban faction, struck [Pakistan](country:PK)'s largest city — far outside its northwest stronghold — in a 90-minute firefight joined by anti-terrorism commandos.

1 wounded attacker was captured; no further operations have been announced.
