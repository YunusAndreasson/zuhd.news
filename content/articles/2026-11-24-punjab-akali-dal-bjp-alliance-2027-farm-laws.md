New Delhi — Akali Dal returns to the alliance it quit over farm laws.

Akali Dal and the Bharatiya Janata Party will face the ruling Aam Aadmi Party in Punjab's 2027 election.

They won 3 and 2 of 117 seats apart in 2022, and one analysis says combined votes flip six.

Chief Minister Bhagwant Mann says the two reunited out of temptation for power after attacking each other over farm laws.

Home Minister Amit Shah says the National Democratic Alliance will form Punjab's next government.
