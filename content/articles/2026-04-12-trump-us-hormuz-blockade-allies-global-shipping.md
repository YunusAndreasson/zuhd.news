Washington — Trump blockaded Hormuz Sunday after Pakistan nuclear talks collapsed. The U.S. joined Iran in blockading the strait, halting all shipping—including to treaty ally Philippines, which routes 98% of its energy through Hormuz. Quincy Institute analysts warn oil could reach $200 a barrel if Houthis restart Red Sea attacks in solidarity.
