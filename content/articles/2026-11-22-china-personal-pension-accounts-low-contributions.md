China — About 79% of [China](country:CN)'s 150 million pension accounts are empty.

Savers hold large deposits but cannot judge the 1,228 listed products.

Savings, insurance and funds each need different tests, so many account holders leave money in current accounts.

The commentary urges independent ratings and promotes Ji'an Jinxin's own rating system, version 3.0.

Tax incentives, account conversion and saver education remain unfixed, the commentary adds.
