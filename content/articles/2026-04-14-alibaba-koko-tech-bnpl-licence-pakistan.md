Islamabad — Pakistan licensed Alibaba's BNPL unit Tuesday. SECP granted Alibaba-owned Koko Tech a BNPL licence for AI-driven consumer credit in a market where most young users, freelancers, and small businesses lack formal bank access. No other foreign firm holds a BNPL licence; Alibaba now controls the gateway into Pakistan's digital economy.
