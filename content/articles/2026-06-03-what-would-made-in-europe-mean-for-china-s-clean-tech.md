Brussels — EU proposed binding local-content rules for clean tech.

[China](country:CN) supplies 80% of global solar and battery output; the Industrial Accelerator Act would cap non-EU content at 50% of any clean-tech product's value.

China's commerce ministry called the Act "serious institutional discrimination"; EU member states must still vote to adopt it.
