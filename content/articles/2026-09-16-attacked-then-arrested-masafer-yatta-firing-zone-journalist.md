Bethlehem — Soldiers held Naqaa Hamed, then seized her devices.

The US embassy in Jerusalem has not commented on her detention.

Hamed was reporting on settler attacks in Masafer Yatta, a zone [Israel](country:IL) designates a military firing range.

She remains in custody, one of nearly 40 Palestinian journalists Israeli authorities are holding.
