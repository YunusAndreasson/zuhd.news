Prime Minister Netanyahu's Likud holds steady at 26 seats. War leaders almost always gain in polls, but neither Netanyahu's bloc (51 seats) nor the opposition (59) can form a government without Arab parties. With neither side reaching the 61-seat threshold, a snap election would produce the same deadlock.
