Washington — Homo floresiensis likely scavenged Komodo dragon kills raw.

Bone-mark analysis at Liang Bua cave finds the 3.5-foot hominins' build was unsuited to running down or throwing at big game like Stegodon.

Charred bones at the site were likely left by modern humans arriving 50,000 years later, researchers say.
