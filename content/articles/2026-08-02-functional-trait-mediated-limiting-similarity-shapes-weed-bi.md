London — Soybean cut weed biomass by 25%.

Weeds sharing traits with their crop competitor lost more biomass and seed output, reducing their competitive edge, researchers found.

The manuscript is unedited and early-access; authors propose trait-matching as a non-chemical weed-management strategy for crop rotations.
