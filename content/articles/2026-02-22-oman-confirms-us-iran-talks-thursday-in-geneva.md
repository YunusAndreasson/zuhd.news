Oman's foreign minister confirmed Geneva nuclear talks for Thursday, pledging "a positive push to go the extra mile." Iran's foreign minister Abbas Araghchi stated Tehran is negotiating "only nuclear" issues and offered full monitoring in exchange for sanctions relief. The 3rd round follows a US military buildup in the region that has left both sides preparing for either a deal or a confrontation.
