Gaza City — [Germany](country:DE) gave a Gaza student a scholarship, not an exit.

Other Gaza students admitted to German universities cannot leave either.

Germany's Cairo visa office requires in-person applications, and the Foreign Office backs departures only for German nationals and close family.

"We have had to flee six times during the war," said Ranin Al-Madhoun, 23, a Konrad Adenauer Foundation scholar.

Her master's place at Niederrhein University of Applied Sciences waits as the winter semester begins.
