Daejeon — 5 dead in Hanwha Aerospace's 3rd fatal propellant blast.

Cleaning explosive residue triggered the explosion, Hanwha said; the Daejeon plant killed workers in propellant blasts in 2018 and 2019.

[South Korea](country:KR)'s President Lee ordered a probe; national security laws blocked investigators from obtaining the plant's layout.
