London — [Britain](country:GB) charged two men, one with assisting Iranian intelligence.

Pentagon commanders evacuated about a dozen B-1 bombers from RAF Fairford in October.

Prosecutors say Mohmed Ahmad Abdulkarim, 24, arranged for five men to drive vans to the base for a handler called Ali.

[Iran](country:IR) denies involvement, and police found gasoline in the vans but no explosives.

Six other men remain on bail as the investigation continues.
