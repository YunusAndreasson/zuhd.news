Islamabad — Awaam Pakistan lost its ballot symbol overnight.

Without a symbol, low-literacy voters cannot recognize a party on the ballot.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s election body cited unheld internal elections despite the party's April filing of results from 12,846 voting members.

Shahid Khaqan Abbasi's party has 30 days to appeal to the Supreme Court.
