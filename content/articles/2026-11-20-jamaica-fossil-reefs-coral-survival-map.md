Austin — Fossil reefs lie metres from living Caribbean corals.

Caribbean reefs have declined sharply over the past 70 years.

Two University of Texas at Austin papers identify which species dominated ancient reefs in northeastern [Jamaica](country:JM).

The researchers say that record could show where corals might survive warming and guide reef rehabilitation.
