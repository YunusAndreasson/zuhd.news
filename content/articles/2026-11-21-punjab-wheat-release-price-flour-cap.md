Lahore — A 10kg bag of flour will retail at Rs1,275 in Punjab.

State wheat costs licensed mills Rs4,500 per 40kg, paid in advance.

Mills must upload daily purchase, grinding and sales records to a state inventory system.

Mills may keep grinding private wheat if they upload daily records of it too.
