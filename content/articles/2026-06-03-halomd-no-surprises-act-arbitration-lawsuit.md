Pittsburgh — 4 health insurers have now sued arbitration firm HaloMD.

Highmark Health alleges HaloMD used a "sham letter" to win $3.9 million through 450 ineligible disputes under the No Surprises Act, which was designed to protect patients from unexpected medical bills.

Highmark is seeking repayment; inflated awards push up insurance premiums.
