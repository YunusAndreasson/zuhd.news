Israeli strikes killed Basij commander Gholamreza Soleimani. Soleimani commanded the Basij, a 20-million-member paramilitary embedded across Iran; national security chief Ali Larijani was also killed. Israel declared it will target Iran's next Supreme Leader, turning assassination from war tactic into standing policy.
