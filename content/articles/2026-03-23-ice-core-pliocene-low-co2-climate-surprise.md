Antarctica — First Pliocene ice cores show unexpectedly low CO2. The finding contradicts climate models that attributed the Pliocene's 2-4°C warmth — a period 3 million years ago — to elevated CO2. Researchers must now identify what other mechanism drove that era's heat, and whether it is absent from current climate forecasts.
