Lancaster — Cloudflare supplied the "lion's share of services" to 88 deepfake abuse sites, a study found.

These providers' own terms often prohibit illegal content, and such imagery is a [US](country:US) federal crime.

Google supplied certificates and ads, Namecheap domains, WordPress publishing tools and Proton mail servers, across 88 sites found in six weeks.

Cloudflare, Proton and Namecheap have not answered requests for comment on the findings.
