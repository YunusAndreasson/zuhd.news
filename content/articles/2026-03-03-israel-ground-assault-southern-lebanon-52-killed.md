Israeli airstrikes killed 52 in Lebanon on Monday. Israel deployed troops deeper into southern Lebanon, targeting territory it held before its 2000 withdrawal, after Hezbollah struck Israeli bases in solidarity with Iran. Lebanon's president called Hezbollah's disarmament "irreversible"; Israel has ordered troops to seize additional strategic positions.
