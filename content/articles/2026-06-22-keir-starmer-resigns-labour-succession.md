London — Andy Burnham entered Parliament hours after Starmer quit.

[Britain](country:GB)'s next prime minister must secure one-fifth of Labour MPs' backing before a wider member vote on a ranked ballot.

Nominations open July 9; if Burnham runs uncontested, he becomes prime minister before the summer recess.
