Gaza — 72,993 Palestinians killed since October 7, 2023.

[Gaza](country:PS)'s health ministry says 983 more died after the October 11 ceasefire; bodies remain under rubble that rescue teams cannot enter.

The ministry reported 173,230 wounded; an unknown number remain on streets beyond rescue teams' access.
