Brussels — Nearly 3,000 [Russia](country:RU) sanctions listings lapse Tuesday without a deal.

One holdout among 27 members can let every asset freeze and travel ban expire.

The compromise delists Alisher Usmanov and Mikhail Fridman and renews the rest for 36 months, not the usual 6 or 12.

Sectoral sanctions on Russia's economy are unaffected.

Ambassadors reconvene at 8 a.m. Tuesday, with [Latvia](country:LV) the last holdout, three diplomats said.
