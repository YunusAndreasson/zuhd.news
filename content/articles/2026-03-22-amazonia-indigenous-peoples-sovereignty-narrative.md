Brasília — Leaders at Brasília rejected being cast as passive nature-guardians. Western portrayals of Amazonian peoples as environmental symbols have shaped land policy for decades, stripping indigenous communities of authority over a biome covering 40% of South America. They demanded legal control over how their communities are represented in research, media, and advocacy — rights no international framework currently recognizes.
