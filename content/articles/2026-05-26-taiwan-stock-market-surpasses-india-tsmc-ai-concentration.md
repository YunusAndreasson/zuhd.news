Mumbai — [India](country:IN)'s market regulator called [Taiwan](country:TW)'s $4.95 trillion market cap a "one stock story."

Taiwan's value tracks TSMC and AI supply chains rather than broad sectors; its lead over India shifts with each quarterly ranking.

India's Securities and Exchange Board chief called diversification an advantage over concentration risk.
