Neuquén — Fracking waste piles sit beside homes in Neuquén's plateau.

[Argentina](country:AR) injects over 95% of Vaca Muerta produced water underground; solid waste near Neuquén homes has no enforced disposal standard.

Experts say inadequate treatment is contaminating soil and water; no regulatory enforcement deadline has been set.
