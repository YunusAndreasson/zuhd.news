Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) now calls 2 Argentine armed services terrorist organisations.

It is the 2nd such listing in 10 days, after Britain's Royal Navy and Royal Air Force.

The foreign ministry cited a retaliation law written against the [US](country:US) designation of its Revolutionary Guards, applying it to [Argentina](country:AR)'s March listing.

Argentina had already expelled Iran's chargé d'affaires, and Tehran says Buenos Aires owns the consequences.
