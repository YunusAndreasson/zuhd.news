Berlin — Meta and Amazon paid under 9% tax in 2025, a study found.

The Fair Tax Foundation puts big tech's 10-year tax gap at €278bn, a third of it in Europe.

Apple and Microsoft declared about 90% of their EU taxes in [Ireland](country:IE), where corporate rates historically run under half Germany's.

[Germany](country:DE)'s culture minister Wolfram Weimer promised a key-points paper on a digital levy last year and has not delivered.
