Jayapura — Indonesia recovered an American pilot's body.

The West Papua National Liberation Army killed him; the group has a history of targeting aircraft serving [Indonesia](country:ID)'s remote Papuan highlands.

Those highland communities remain reachable only by the same air routes the group has struck before.
