Damascus — A court convicted Hassoun of words, not bombs.

The verdict names incitement, leaving the barrel bombs he blessed untried and unpunished.

[Syria](country:SY)'s Fourth Criminal Court found the former grand mufti guilty of inciting sectarian violence, the 10th Assad-era official sentenced.

Ordinary courts, not a special tribunal, will try every regime figure still in the country.
