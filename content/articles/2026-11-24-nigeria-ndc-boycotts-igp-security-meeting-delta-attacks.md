Abuja — The Nigeria Democratic Congress boycotts police security talks after a Delta attack.

The opposition party says police cannot protect its members ahead of [Nigeria](country:NG)'s 2027 elections.

An attack on supporters of senatorial candidate Ovie Omo-Agege in Ughelli North on Monday prompted a dusk-to-dawn curfew.

The campaign counts four dead, the party six, and the local council chairman says three of his own supporters died.

Delta police ordered a full investigation, while the party alleges officers were present and did nothing.
