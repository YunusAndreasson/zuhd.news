Cancer cells expose src kinases on their outer surface. Researchers had assumed these cancer-driving proteins acted only inside cells; their surface presence could allow immune therapies to target tumors that currently lack surface markers — the prerequisite for most immunotherapy to work. If the finding holds across tumor types, it could extend treatment to patients currently ineligible for immunotherapy.
