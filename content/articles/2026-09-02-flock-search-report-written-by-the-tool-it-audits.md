Cleburne — Deputies searched 80,000 Flock cameras nationwide for a woman who had an abortion.

A sergeant then used Axon's Draft One AI tool to write the incident report.

The tool summarized deputies' discussion of "legal implications," a discussion no camera or officer independently recorded.

The sheriff's office has refused to release body camera footage from the case.
