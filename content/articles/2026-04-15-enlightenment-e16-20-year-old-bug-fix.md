San Francisco — A 20-year-old Enlightenment E16 bug is fixed. The fix required reading 2005-era X11 code, reconstructing window management assumptions no longer documented, and isolating one wrong state transition. E16 runs on aging Linux systems globally; the patch now needs distribution maintainers to push it downstream.
