Kyiv — Almost every ballistic missile in the last strike got through.

[Ukraine](country:UA) has run out of Patriot interceptors, a source familiar with the situation said.

Patriots are among the few weapons that can down ballistic missiles, and new interceptors take up to 36 months to build.

A senior NATO official says European-funded deliveries of air defence munitions, including Patriot interceptors, continue.

Zelenskyy says he could halt refinery strikes only in exchange for more Patriot interceptors.
