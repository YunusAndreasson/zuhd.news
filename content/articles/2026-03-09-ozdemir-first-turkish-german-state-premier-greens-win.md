Cem Özdemir will become Germany's first Turkish-origin state premier. His father came to Stuttgart as a Gastarbeiter and never left — a "guest" worker Germany recruited to rebuild itself after the war. Christian Democrat leader Friedrich Merz is forming a federal government on immigration restrictions opposed by Germany's 3 million Turkish-origin residents.
