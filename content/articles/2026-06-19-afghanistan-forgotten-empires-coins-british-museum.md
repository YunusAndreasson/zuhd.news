London — 25 coins disprove [Afghanistan](country:AF)'s backwater label.

East India Company agent Alexander Burnes collected them in Kabul and Balkh in the 1830s; they bear Graeco-Bactrian, Kushan and Sasanian script from a trade crossroads.

The Burnes collection sits in the British Museum; the empires they document remain footnotes in accounts of the country.
