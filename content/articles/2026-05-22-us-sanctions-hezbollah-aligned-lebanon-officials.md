Beirut — [US](country:US) Treasury sanctioned 9 [Lebanon](country:LB) officials for embedding Hezbollah in state institutions.

Named targets include 2 lawmakers, the security directorate chief, and a brigadier general — the first US package targeting uniformed officers.

Secretary Scott Bessent offered $10 million for information on Hezbollah's finances.
