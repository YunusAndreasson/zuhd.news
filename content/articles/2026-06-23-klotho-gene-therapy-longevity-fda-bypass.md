Austin — Minicircle will sell klotho gene therapy at offshore clinics within 6 months, bypassing the Food and Drug Administration.

Evidence covers 24 people with no control group; mice with excess klotho developed bone loss, and trial data is unpublished.

No regulator covers the Bahamas, Honduras, or Panama clinics.
