Britain — A UK telescope array will photograph satellites every 10 seconds.

[Britain](country:GB)'s military is working with civilian astronomers to close its spy-satellite tracking gap.

Narrow beams mean eavesdropping requires flying close, so astronomers read reflected light from the ground to infer a satellite's structure.

"It's a very murky pool," said James Blake, a University of Warwick astronomer, of defence funding.

Intelligence agencies want to discuss how academics and observatories can supply a sovereign capability, researcher Mark Norris says.
