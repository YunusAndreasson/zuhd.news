New York — A cheese importer paid over $520,000 in duties, its complaint says.

Gellert Global Group is suing to void Trump's 50% tariffs on goods from [Canada](country:CA).

Trump invoked Section 338 of the 1930 Smoot-Hawley Act, never used before this year, to tariff Canada without Congress.

The suit says the administration waited 7 days, not the required 30, before collecting duties on cheese.

Gellert wants the tariffs declared illegal and refunds with interest, and the White House has not responded.
