Oleshky — Hundreds left on Friday inside a four-hour window.

The White House counted up to 2,000 residents in a town without power, heat or water.

[Russia](country:RU)'s rights commissioner said 25 buses stood ready and two people died demining the roads.

Evacuations were set to end at 2pm local time, and the six-day [US](country:US)-brokered truce is due to expire.
