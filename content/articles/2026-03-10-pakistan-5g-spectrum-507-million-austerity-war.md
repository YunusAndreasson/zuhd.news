Pakistan raised $507 million from telecoms as schools close. Spectrum fees enter state revenue as the same government cuts school hours and reduces civil service to a 4-day work week. Jazz, Pakistan's leading telecom, committed to 5G at 180 towers by Wednesday with rural expansion via 700MHz spectrum.
