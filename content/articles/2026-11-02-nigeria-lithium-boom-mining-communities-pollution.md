Kangimi — Promised a clinic, the lithium village got broken taps.

Niger State's mines inspectors, four staff and one vehicle, supervise every site.

[Nigeria](country:NG) bans raw lithium exports, and Chinese firms have put $1.3 billion into processing plants.

A broken dam wall still lets plant runoff seep toward Kaduna's reservoir, where authorities report no contamination.
