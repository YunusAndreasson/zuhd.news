Raleigh — A 1880s design got its first real rival.

Conventional transformers take years to order, a bottleneck now rationing grid upgrades worldwide.

Solid-state versions use semiconductor switching instead of copper coils, so they can route power and convert AC to DC directly.

Three startups have raised over $280 million to commercialize the swap.
