Tehran — [UAE](country:AE) permits [Iran](country:IR)ian airlines to restart direct flights July 1.

Iranian carriers open first; Jebel Ali Port cargo clearance is also resuming after wartime backlog, the Civil Aviation Organisation said.

Iran says trade will return to pre-war levels gradually; a full route resumption remains under regulatory review.
