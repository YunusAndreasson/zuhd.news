Berlin — Experts told the Bundestag the intelligence reform is unconstitutional.

The bill would let [Germany](country:DE)'s foreign and domestic spy agencies break into computer systems and hack back.

Mainz law professor Matthias Bäcker said permanent, secret, police-style intrusions by intelligence services contradict rule-of-law principles.

Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt says Russia's shadow war requires Germany to rearm.

The interior committee now weighs the bill after hearing 8 experts for 4 hours Monday.
