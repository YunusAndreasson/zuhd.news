Tegucigalpa — A 45-year cocaine sentence ended in a pardon, not an appeal.

Former president Juan Orlando Hernández, convicted in New York of running [Honduras](country:HN)'s state apparatus for traffickers, was freed by a US presidential pardon; the conviction still stands as fact.

Honduras's Supreme Court has suspended a separate corruption warrant against him.
