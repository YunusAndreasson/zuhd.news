Berlin — Researchers crossed the cell membrane with a nanobody.

The Charité team used it to refold the defective protein causing cystic fibrosis — a barrier antibodies cannot normally cross.

Published in Nature Chemical Biology, researchers say the approach could target other misfolded-protein diseases.
