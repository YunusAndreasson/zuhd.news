Gold fell below $5,000 Monday. Gold first crossed $5,000 when the Strait of Hormuz closed on February 28, driven by safe-haven demand. The retreat signals traders may be pricing in a ceasefire, with the Fed expected to hold rates at 3.5-3.75% this week.
