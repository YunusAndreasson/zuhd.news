Palestinian towns inside Israel have no missile shelters. Iranian missiles have killed 15 people inside Israel, but the Israeli government never extended to Arab towns the alert systems and shelters it built in Jewish communities decades ago. Palestinian rights groups are demanding equal protection, with no Israeli government response as missiles continue to hit.
