Burhan offered dialogue participants temporary immunity.

Suspending Sumud leaders' charges opens Sudan's first war-era political talks.

The immunity expires with the dialogue, leaving participants prosecutable again once talks end.

An African Union delegation in Khartoum is still assessing before backing the process.
