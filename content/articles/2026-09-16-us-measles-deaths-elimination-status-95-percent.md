Washington — Four Americans have died of measles this year.

That is the most measles deaths since 1992, before the US even declared elimination.

Measles spreads so easily that herd immunity needs roughly 95% vaccination coverage, with almost no margin.

Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. disputes the official death toll.
