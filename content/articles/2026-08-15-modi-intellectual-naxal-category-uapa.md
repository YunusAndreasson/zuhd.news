New Delhi — Modi declared the decades-old armed insurgency finished.

The law built for that rebellion now points at government critics, not fighters.

Modi's "dimagi Naxal" — intellectual Naxal — invokes the Unlawful Activities Prevention Act and sedition charges previously used in the Bhima Koregaon case in [India](country:IN).

No criteria defining an "intellectual Naxal" have been published yet.
