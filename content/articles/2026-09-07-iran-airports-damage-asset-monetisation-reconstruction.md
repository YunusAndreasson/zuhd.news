Tehran — 27 airports suffered damage, [Iran](country:IR)'s aviation CEO said.

Rebuilding will sell public assets to Iran's least accountable domestic buyers.

Sanctions block foreign loans and insurance, so Tehran sells assets to state-linked foundations instead, CEO Morteza Dehghan said.

A radar system is due at a high-traffic Iranian airport within a week.
