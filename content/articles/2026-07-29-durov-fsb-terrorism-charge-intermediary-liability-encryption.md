Moscow — Russia charged Pavel Durov with aiding terrorism.

[Russia](country:RU)'s Federal Security Service accuses Telegram of not removing channels used by Ukrainian services; [France](country:FR) detained Durov on similar grounds in 2024.

Durov, who holds Emirati and French citizenship, has not commented on the charge.
