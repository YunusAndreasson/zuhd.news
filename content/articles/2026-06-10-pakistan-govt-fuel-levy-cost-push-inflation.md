Karachi — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s own fuel levy is driving double-digit inflation.

A research paper found the levy drove inflation to 11.7% in May; rate hikes then raise debt costs without curing the cause.

IMF revenue targets prevent cuts to the levy, leaving fiscal and monetary policy working against each other.
