Maiduguri — Six states raised ₦10.8 billion for a regional airline.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s north-east is recovering from over a decade of insurgency and displacement.

Adamawa, Bauchi, Borno, Gombe, Taraba and Yobe put up counterpart funds for a North East Air Shuttle.

Borno governor Babagana Zulum warned that security remains the prerequisite for economic recovery.

Zulum says the remaining counterpart funds arrive before the end of October.
