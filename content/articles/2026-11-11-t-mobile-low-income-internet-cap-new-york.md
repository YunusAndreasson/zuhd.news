New York — New York says T-Mobile throttled low-income internet to 600 Kbps.

A typical customer could reach the 50-gigabyte cap in about 4 days, New York says.

The Affordable Broadband Act requires 25 Mbps for $15 a month, and T-Mobile offered the plan only where it had 5G.

T-Mobile neither admitted nor denied the findings and says it is updating its plan to meet the state's requirements.

T-Mobile has 120 days to scrap the speed limits and expand the plans.
