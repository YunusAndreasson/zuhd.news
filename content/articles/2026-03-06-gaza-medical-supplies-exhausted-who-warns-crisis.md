Gauze and needles have run out in Gaza's hospitals. WHO regional director Hanan Balkhy said only 200 of the 600 required daily trucks are entering even after Israel reopened the Kerem Shalom crossing Tuesday. 18,000 Palestinians await medical evacuation as the Rafah crossing remains shut.
