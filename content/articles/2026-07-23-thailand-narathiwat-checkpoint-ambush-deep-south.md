Narathiwat — Ten gunmen hit a checkpoint with rifles and pipe bombs.

Five paramilitary troops died and 6 civilians were hurt in [Thailand](country:TH)'s deep south, where a Malay-Muslim insurgency has simmered for two decades.

Security forces are hunting the attackers, who fled with stolen rifles and vests.
