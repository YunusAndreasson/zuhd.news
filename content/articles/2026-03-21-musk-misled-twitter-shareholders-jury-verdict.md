A jury found Musk liable for misleading Twitter shareholders. 2 Musk tweets in May 2022, one calling the acquisition "temporarily on hold," drove down Twitter's stock and cost shareholders who sold. The jury set damages at $2.1 billion; Musk has not said whether he will appeal.
