Geneva — 61 more companies joined the UN settlement database.

Airbnb, Booking.com, Motorola Solutions and TripAdvisor stay listed, alongside firms from 11 countries.

The list names firms in any of 10 activities, including construction, surveillance and demolitions, in West Bank settlements illegal under international law.

The list is incomplete: the UN office has reviewed 381 of 596 companies submitted, and submissions closed in June 2024.

[Israel](country:IL) rejects the list as a political tool, and the office has updated it only three times.
