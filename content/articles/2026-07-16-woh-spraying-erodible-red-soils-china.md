Changsha — W-OH treatment seals soil to 3cm deep.

CT scans show the spray, used across [China](country:CN)'s erosion-prone red-soil belt, lowers porosity and pore connectivity while raising pore circularity — denser, but less permeable.

Researchers say the imaging clarifies dosing before wider soil-conservation rollout.
