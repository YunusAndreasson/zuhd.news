London — Cancer cells lose long-range gene coordination. Analysis of 8,772 samples across 15 tissues showed that only cancer — not other chronic diseases — breaks chromosomal co-expression strength with distance. The pattern holds across all 15 tissues tested, suggesting the hallmarks of cancer share a single network failure mechanism.
