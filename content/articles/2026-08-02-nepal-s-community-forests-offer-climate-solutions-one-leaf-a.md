Kathmandu — 22 women turn sal leaves into biodegradable plates.

[Nepal](country:NP) handed forest rights to local user groups from the late 1970s, and forest cover has since roughly doubled nationwide, reversing prior deforestation.

One Pyuthan forest of 488 hectares can supply over 250 quintals of sal leaves a year.
