Jagdalpur — A verdict arrived 13 years after the ambush.

The ruling closes one path to accountability while leaving the alleged wider conspiracy untouched.

A special National Investigation Agency court convicted the 10 surviving defendants of 11 originally charged.

Sentencing is set for September 16, but opposition leaders in [India](country:IN) say security failures were never examined.
