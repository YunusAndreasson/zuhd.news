Ibiza — 1 of 17 crew died as the Med Emre sank.

The ship may have carried up to 7,000 tonnes of diesel, the Balearic interior department said.

[Spain](country:ES)'s merchant marine authority activated its pollution contingency plan at level 2, the second-highest, and sent the oil-spill vessel Clara Campoamor.

No leak has been confirmed, and officials have disclosed neither a cause nor the dead sailor's nationality.

A survey aircraft is checking waters that hold Posidonia seagrass and Balearic shearwaters.
