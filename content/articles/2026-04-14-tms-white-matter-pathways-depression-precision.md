London — Researchers mapped the brain pathways that make TMS work. The therapy reaches the subgenual cingulate via polysynaptic white matter fibers; connectome modeling in 49 patients identified specific routes. The map could enable patient-specific targeting for the 30% of depression patients who don't respond to medication.
