Sheskharis is the main endpoint of Russia's state-run Transneft pipeline, halting tanker loadings within hours of any disruption.

Ukraine's Defense Ministry counted 11 Russian oil facilities struck this month, though Novorossiysk's fire scale is unconfirmed.
