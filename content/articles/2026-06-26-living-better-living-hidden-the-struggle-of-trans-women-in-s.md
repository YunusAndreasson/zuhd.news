Damascus — Cross-dressing law in [Syria](country:SY) predates independence.

The fall of Assad brought new street attacks on transgender women — but no legal change; the 1949 Penal Code remains intact.

The new government has signaled no legal reform; Syria's first LGBTQ+ support group says attacks are intensifying.
