Moscow — Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov confirms energy talks with [US](country:US) negotiators.

The Kremlin declined to comment on a weekend report of a multibillion-dollar oil deal.

The report says the deal would benefit Middle Eastern executives tied to US negotiators Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner.

Both envoys have travelled to Moscow multiple times to meet President Vladimir Putin.
