Stockholm — [Sweden](country:SE) ordered 4 French frigates from Naval Group.

[France](country:FR)'s Defence and Intervention Frigate design was chosen because it is already in production — new NATO members now skip custom designs for wartime urgency.

Sweden expects the frigates to expand its role in NATO's Baltic maritime defense.
