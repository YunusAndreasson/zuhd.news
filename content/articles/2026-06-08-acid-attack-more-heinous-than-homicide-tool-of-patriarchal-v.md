Islamabad — Pakistan's Supreme Court upheld a Faisalabad life sentence for acid attack, ruling the crime graver than homicide.

Justice Kakar noted survivors endure lifelong disfigurement, unlike homicide victims who suffer one final harm.

The court recommended a nationwide acid sale ban and a survivors' rehabilitation fund.
