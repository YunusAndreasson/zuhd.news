New York — The nuclear non-proliferation treaty's third consecutive review collapsed without agreement.

[Iran](country:IR) rejected being named as permanently barred from nuclear weapons; the [US](country:US) refused to condemn strikes on Iranian nuclear facilities.

No US-Russian nuclear arms ceiling remains after the arms-reduction treaty expired in February without a successor.
