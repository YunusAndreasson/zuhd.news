Karanpur — Modi says armed Maoists are gone and ideological ones remain.

He calls them dimagi Naxals, meaning brain Naxals, and says they have infiltrated parties and organisations.

The government declared [India](country:IN) free of Naxalism on 31 March after senior leaders were killed and hundreds surrendered.

"We must identify and defeat them and secure the country," Modi said at a paramilitary camp in Bastar.

Modi named no party or organisation and gave no method for identifying them.
