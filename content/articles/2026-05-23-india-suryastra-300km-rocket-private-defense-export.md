Shirdi — India's first private 300-km rocket rolled out Saturday.

[India](country:IN)'s private defence share is 25-30%, Defence Minister Rajnath Singh said; at 300 km, Suryastra competes with Chinese and Turkish export systems.

India targets 50% private defence production; Nibe's Shirdi complex will also build missiles and satellite components.
