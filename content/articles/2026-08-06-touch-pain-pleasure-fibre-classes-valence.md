New York — The same pressure can soothe or repel; the skin isn't why.

Separate nerve fibers carry pressure, damage and gentle stroking to the spinal cord, Columbia neuroscientist Ishmail Abdus-Saboor's lab finds; the brain assigns meaning afterward from context.

The findings, still tested in mice, point toward pain treatment beyond opioids.
