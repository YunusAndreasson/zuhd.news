Mountain View — Google Maps now shows one lake under two names by country.

A platform's per-territory renderings overwrite [Canada](country:CA)'s sovereign toponym with no vote or appeal.

Google follows each government's official names database, so Trump's GNIS order became "Lake America" for US users.

The company took the same approach after the 2025 Gulf of Mexico renaming.
