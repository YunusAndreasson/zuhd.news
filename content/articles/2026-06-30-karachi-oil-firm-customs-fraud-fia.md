Karachi — 32,000 tonnes vanished from [Pakistan](country:PK)'s bonded fuel depot.

Bonded petroleum must clear customs before sale; Pakistan's Federal Investigation Agency alleged GO Petroleum sold thousands of tonnes of non-duty-paid fuel without filing customs declarations.

GO Petroleum said all dues are paid and it is cooperating fully with authorities.
