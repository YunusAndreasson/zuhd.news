Redmond — Hackers gained privileged cloud access in under three hours, Microsoft says.

Chinese government-aligned group Storm-0940 hit ConnectWise and is targeting hundreds more organisations.

The open-source ARTEX tool coordinates AI agents that run a penetration test from reconnaissance through data theft with little human involvement.

[China](country:CN)'s foreign ministry spokesperson Mao Ning denied any knowledge of the incidents.

ARTEX's developer is closing its source code, so the public tool gets no further versions.
