London — Cell secretions rebuilt bone in osteoporotic rats.

The therapy may not need living cells, only their signalling fluid.

Periosteal cells cultured in simulated microgravity, or their secretome, matched bisphosphonate on mineralized tissue in 60 rats over 42 days.

Rats are the only subjects so far, and the paper is an accepted manuscript.
