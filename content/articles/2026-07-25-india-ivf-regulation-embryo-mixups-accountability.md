New Delhi — Embryo mix-ups can give a child the wrong parents.

Each embryo passes retrieval, fertilization, freezing and transfer, and any step can mislabel it, so [India](country:IN)'s new ART Act mandates clinic registration and audits.

A National Registry now tracks clinics, but inspectors remain too few for a market couples fund themselves.
