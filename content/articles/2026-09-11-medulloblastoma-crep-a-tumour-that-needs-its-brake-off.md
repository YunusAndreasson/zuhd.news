Paris — A brain tumor depends on its own brake.

It offers a way to selectively kill the tumor's own runaway cells.

Researchers found blocking CReP raises the stress brake, eIF2α phosphorylation, that curbs the tumor's protein-making overdrive.

The work is preclinical, and no drug reaching CReP has been tested in patients yet.
