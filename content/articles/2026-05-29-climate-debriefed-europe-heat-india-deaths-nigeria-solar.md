Hyderabad — Over 100 died in southern India's 3-day heatwave.

Record heat pushed [India](country:IN)'s electricity demand to an all-time high, triggering power cuts that compounded heat exposure for millions without home cooling.

Andhra Pradesh and Telangana bore the heaviest toll; authorities have not declared the emergency over.
