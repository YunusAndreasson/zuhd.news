Beijing — Beijing ranked critical commodities equal to food and energy. The 15th five-year plan targets semiconductor equipment, industrial software, and aero-engines — sectors where US export controls have cut Chinese access. The plan gives Beijing until 2030 to reduce foreign dependence as the US tightens export controls on the same sectors.
