Brussels — Air traffic control stops for 14 hours across [Belgium](country:BE).

Charleroi's 3,400 airport workers and thousands of passengers lose the day.

Skeyes said controllers joining the union strike against Prime Minister Bart De Wever's €10 billion savings plan left staffing too thin for safe traffic.

The Board of Airline Representatives counts this as the 11th strike hitting Belgian airspace in 11 months.

De Wever faces a Monday deadline for a coalition budget deal after King Philippe refused his resignation.
