Orlando — Homeland Security says Alexandre Ramagem is in the [US](country:US) illegally.

[Brazil](country:BR)'s Supreme Court sentenced the ex-intelligence chief to 16 years for attempting a coup.

Immigration agents arrested him in Orlando in April for overstaying his visa, then freed him 2 days later without a judge.

The State Department says Brazil's federal police manipulated the immigration system to pursue political persecution.

Homeland Security says his asylum request does not shield him from detention or deportation.
