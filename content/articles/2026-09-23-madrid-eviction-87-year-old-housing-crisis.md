Madrid — Police evicted Maricarmen Abascal, 87, after 71 years.

Her €1,350 monthly pension could not cover even the reduced €1,650 rent.

Urbagestión, the investment firm that bought the flat in 2018, cited a legal glitch to lift rent from €500 to €2,650.

"There is a police deployment as if this were a military operation," said her lawyer, Beatriz Duro.

[Spain](country:ES)'s coalition government says it cannot pass wider rent controls while parliament stays divided.
