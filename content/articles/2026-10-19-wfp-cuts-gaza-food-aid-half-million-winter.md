Gaza — 500,000 people lose food aid as winter nears.

The World Food Programme says 1.4 million people, 67% of Gaza, face crisis-level hunger or worse.

Donor funding fell by more than half from 2025 levels, and [Israel](country:IL) barred commercial goods on 14 October.

"I used the last of our flour yesterday," said Nesreen Muqayed, who lives with four children and two grandchildren.

The agency says it needs $450 million to run Gaza and West Bank operations through March 2027.
