Dhaka — Bangladesh plans two new LNG terminals by 2030.

It locks in decades of gas imports exposed to the Strait of Hormuz.

State-run QatarEnergy halved deliveries after Iran-war damage, forcing pricier spot-market cargo purchases.

[Bangladesh](country:BD) needs 17-18 million tonnes more LNG yearly between 2031 and 2040, the ministry said.
