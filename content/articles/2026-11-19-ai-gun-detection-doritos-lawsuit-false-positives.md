Baltimore — Police handcuffed 4 Black students over a Doritos bag.

An inspector general's report says over 99% of the county's AI gun alerts were false positives.

Officials cancelled the alert within 2 minutes, but the principal missed the cancellation and texted police 15 minutes later.

Omnilert says the school's delegation of authority failed, not its technology, and a cancelled alert must stay cancelled.

The students ask a court to bar Omnilert's use until staff and police are trained.
