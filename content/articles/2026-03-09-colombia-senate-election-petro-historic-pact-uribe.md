Colombia's centrists collapsed in Sunday's legislative vote. The ruling leftist coalition, led by President Gustavo Petro, took the most seats with 22.84%, leading a congress split 24 ways. Former president Álvaro Uribe's party placed 2nd at 15.7%; Petro cannot contest May's presidential election.
