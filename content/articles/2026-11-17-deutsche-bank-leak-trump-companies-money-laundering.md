Frankfurt — A May 2020 Deutsche Bank memo tied two Trump companies to laundromats.

A senior bank lawyer had told [US](country:US) authorities it knew of no Russia link in Trump's business.

Payment checks found the two companies transacting with entities allegedly linked to the [Russian](country:RU) and [Azerbaijani](country:AZ) laundromats, which moved billions of dollars.

Journalists in the Inside Deutsche Bank project are still working through more than 15 million leaked documents.
