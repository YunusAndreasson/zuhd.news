White phosphorus burns at 800°C and sticks to skin. Human Rights Watch confirmed Israeli forces used it over residential Yohmor, Lebanon — repeating a pattern first documented in Gaza in 2023 that drew no legal consequences. No government supplying Israel with weapons has conditioned arms transfers on halting its use.
