Hong Kong — Samsung lists its fridges and washing machines as sold out.

Samsung held 15% of Hong Kong's refrigerator market last year, an industry estimate says.

Japanese and American brands hold the premium segment while Chinese brands take the mid-range and low end, the same report says.

Samsung's Hong Kong office did not confirm an exit, saying it is focusing on televisions and mobile devices.

Samsung says warranty and repair support for appliances already sold stays fully active.
