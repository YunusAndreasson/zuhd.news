London — Britain suspended its Chagos handover Saturday. Trump called the 2024 deal "an act of great stupidity" in January; Diego Garcia served as a US-British base for Iran strikes. International courts have undermined Britain's claim, but Washington's required sign-off — which Trump has withheld — leaves the deal suspended.
