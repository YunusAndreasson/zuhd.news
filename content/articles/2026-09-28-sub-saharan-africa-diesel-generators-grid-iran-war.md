Lagos — Generators outproduce the grid in half of sub-Saharan Africa.

A Gulf war now sets the electricity bill for clinics and shops across the region.

Diesel capacity exceeds grid power in 26 sub-Saharan markets, so blackouts turn households and firms into retail fuel buyers.

Trump is weighing a [US](country:US) diesel export ban, which industry groups warn would tighten global supply further.
