London — Digital exclusion tracks worse health in older adults.

Once portals replace phone lines, exclusion becomes a barrier to care, not just an inconvenience.

A review of studies on adults over 60 found exclusion compounds with socioeconomic and health vulnerabilities, researchers said.

The review calls for affordable access and non-digital alternatives, not just design fixes.
