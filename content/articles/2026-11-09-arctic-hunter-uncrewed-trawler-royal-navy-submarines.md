Norway — A converted 184-foot trawler would hunt submarines without a crew.

[Britain](country:GB)'s Royal Navy wants autonomous vessels hunting Russian submarines persistently across the North Atlantic and Arctic.

Kongsberg Maritime plans to strip the wheelhouse and fish-processing factory and tow sonar where nets hung, using commercial shipyards.

Type 92 is one of four uncrewed vessel types in the Royal Navy's Hybrid Navy plan.
