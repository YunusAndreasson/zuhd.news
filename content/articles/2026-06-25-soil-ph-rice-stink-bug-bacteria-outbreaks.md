Tokyo — Soil pH governs rice-pest outbreaks through gut bacteria.

Japanese researchers found soil acidity governs gut bacteria that rice stink bugs need to survive — a non-chemical lever for Asian farmers.

The findings, published in Microbiome, require field trials before farmers can apply them.
