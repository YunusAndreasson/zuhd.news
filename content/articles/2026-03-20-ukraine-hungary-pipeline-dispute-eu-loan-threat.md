A €90bn EU loan to Ukraine is frozen. A Russian strike hit the Druzhba pipeline hub at Brody on 27 January, cutting oil to Hungary and Slovakia, who then blocked the EU vote. Ukraine says repairs need 6 more weeks, while Zelensky has signaled he may simply not restore a Russian oil route.
