London — New memristors compute AI uncertainty natively in hardware.

The devices embed mean and variance as separate conductance channels, enabling uncertainty estimation without shuttling data to a central processing unit.

In hardware tests, the chip hit 79% accuracy while reliably flagging unseen activities as anomalies, the study found.
