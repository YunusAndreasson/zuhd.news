Washington — [US](country:US) Jones Act waived for 90 more days.

The 1920 law bars foreign-flagged tankers from domestic routes; the [Iran](country:IR) war exposed a gap the US merchant fleet cannot fill.

The domestic shipping lobby says gas prices have risen regardless; the extension runs through mid-August.
