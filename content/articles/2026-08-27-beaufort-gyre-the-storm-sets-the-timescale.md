Utqiagvik — Single storms, not seasons, redirect Arctic freshwater.

The Beaufort Gyre holds meltwater that could stall Atlantic Ocean circulation if released.

Researchers tracked 380 wind-driven upwelling events reshaping the gyre within months, faster than the seasonal winds assumed to drive it.

The finding means the release is governed by storms current climate models resolve poorly.
