Iran is acquiring China's CM-302 supersonic cruise missile, designed to sink aircraft carriers. The deal with state-owned China Aerospace Science and Industry Corporation gives Tehran 290-kilometre strike range against the 2 US carrier groups now deployed to the region. 6 sources confirmed the negotiations as Washington assembles its largest Middle East naval presence since 2003.
