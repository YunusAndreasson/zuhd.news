Pakistan slashed state salaries over Iran war fuel costs. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif announced cuts of up to 30%, choosing fiscal solvency over fuel subsidies as oil prices soared. Cabinet ministers will forfeit 2 months' pay, with a third-party audit of the measures due in 2 months but no timeline for reversing them.
