Somalia's 2 largest federal states refused its new constitution. Puntland and Jubaland boycotted the ratification vote after President Mohamud broke his inauguration pledge to delay constitutional work, invoking a veto that Somalia's federal compact grants peripheral states. Puntland may deepen its de facto independence in response, fracturing the federal design that holds Somalia together.
