Crystals inside meteorites are older than the Sun itself — formed inside stars that died before our solar system existed. New isotopic analysis suggests a Wolf-Rayet star, not a supernova, seeded the material that built our solar system 4.6 billion years ago; supernovas would have left far more iron-60 than researchers find in early solar system samples. Cosmochemist Nan Liu is scanning meteorites with nanoprobe microscopy to find the grains that would confirm the Wolf-Rayet origin.
