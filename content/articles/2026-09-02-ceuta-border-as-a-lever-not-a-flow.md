Protesters nationwide demanded [Spain](country:ES)'s Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez resign over his response.

A police report found [Morocco](country:MA)'s forces showed "total permissiveness" as migrants crossed the border.

Between 5,000 and 10,000 migrants remain in Ceuta, with redistribution talks still deadlocked.
