Villavicencio — A car bomb destroyed the Meta governor's office.

About 20 people were hurt on the eve of the 10th anniversary of the FARC peace accord.

Violence has surged for two years as remaining guerrilla factions and cartels compete for cocaine, illegal gold mines and extortion rackets.

[Colombia](country:CO)'s President Abelardo de la Espriella, in office since August, vowed to hunt down those he calls narco-terrorists, though no group has claimed the bombing.
