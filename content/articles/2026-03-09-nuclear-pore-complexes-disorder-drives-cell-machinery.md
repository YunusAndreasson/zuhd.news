Cell gates filter molecules through disorder, not rigid locks. Rockefeller researchers imaged nuclear pores at millisecond resolution; each gate's proteins continuously rearrange to sort what enters the nucleus. The findings, published in Nature Cell Biology, open a drug target for blocking viral entry and treating cancer.
