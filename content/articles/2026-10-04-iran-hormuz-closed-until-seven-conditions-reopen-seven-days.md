Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) says Hormuz stays shut until 7 conditions are met.

Traffic through the strait ran 42% below normal, at 3.1 ships a day, as of 27 September.

Chief negotiator Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf ties the conditions to the Islamabad Memorandum and says Washington replied through a mediator.

TASS, Russian state media, quotes foreign minister Abbas Araghchi: the strait reopens within 7 days if Washington accepts Iran's plan.
