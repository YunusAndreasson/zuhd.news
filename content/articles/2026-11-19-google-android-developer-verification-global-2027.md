Mountain View — Android will block apps from unregistered developers from January 2027.

Developers in sanctioned nations cannot verify their identity, so Google locks them out of distribution.

Registration needs a government identity document and $25, and a verifier on certified Android 8-plus devices blocks unregistered installs.

Google says registered apps already cover over 99% of installs, and users can opt out after a 24-hour wait.

Google expects verification fully online by 1 March 2027.
