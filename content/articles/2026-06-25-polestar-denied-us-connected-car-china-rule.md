Washington — Geely-owned Polestar lost US sales from 2027.

A connected-car ban targeting [China](country:CN)-linked automakers disqualified Geely-owned Polestar despite its Swedish branding; sister brand Volvo was separately cleared.

Polestar will sell existing [US](country:US) stock but is pivoting to Europe, Southeast Asia, and Canada.
