Islamabad — Diesel rises Rs6.47 to Rs390.42 a litre.

Diesel runs freight, tube wells and tractors, so the hike reaches food prices soon.

[Pakistan](country:PK) now sets pump prices daily, tracking global crude after the Hormuz blockade cut supply routes.

The dealers' association is weighing a protest against daily pricing.
