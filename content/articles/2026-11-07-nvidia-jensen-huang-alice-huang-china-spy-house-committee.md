Washington — [US](country:US) officials believe Chinese intelligence targeted Nvidia's chief Jensen Huang.

Nvidia's chips sit at the centre of export controls on [China](country:CN).

Officials say China's Ministry of State Security recruited Alice Huang, a Megaspeed executive, to gather information on him.

Nvidia says Huang meets thousands of customer executives yearly and shares no classified information with customers.

The House Select Committee on China has asked Huang for a briefing on his contacts with her.
