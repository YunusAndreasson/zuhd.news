Lagos — Washington lifted a 12-year shipping restriction on Nigeria.

The restriction had added security costs to every ship from [Nigeria](country:NG) calling [US](country:US) ports.

Under the Condition of Entry, ships from flagged ports faced extra boarding and inspections, a cost shipowners passed to exporters.

Cameroon, Guinea-Bissau and Liberia remain under the same Coast Guard grading system.
