Quetta — 13,044 crossed from Iran into Balochistan. IOM tracked 2 crossings averaging 296 daily — mostly Pakistani returnees in the first 2 weeks, then Iranian nationals increasing after Eid. The ceasefire is slowing outflows, but Balochistan — Pakistan's poorest province — now absorbs 3 simultaneous displaced populations.
