Beijing — JD.com ended deposit pre-sales for its 18th Singles' Day.

[China](country:CN)'s services retail sales grew 5.3% in the first half, against 1.1% for goods, national statistics show.

JD opened spot sales on 12 October with direct discounts, plus half-price cleaning, car maintenance and elder care.

JD plans to train 1 million more service workers over the next 3 years.
