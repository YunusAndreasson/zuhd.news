20 Iranians shared military and security locations with Israel. Iran arrested all 20 in West Azerbaijan, a province home to Iran's largest Kurdish and Azerbaijani communities, on espionage charges. Prosecutors described "networks of mercenaries" but have not said how suspects were recruited or what charges they formally face.
