Faisalabad — Punjab researchers mapped 11 rice leaf nutrients with drones.

The model used neural architecture search on multispectral imagery, achieving mean absolute errors of 0.06–0.16 across all nutrients.

Lab-free nutrient mapping is now within reach for smallholder rice farmers if the model reaches field deployment.
