Antalya — A draft dropped the $120 billion adaptation target.

100 civil society groups warn the summit could be among the most disappointing ever.

The 19 November draft also removed both fossil-fuel transition formulations, while keeping carbon capture and carbon dioxide removal provisions.

The presidencies say the talks must secure an agreement countries can collectively support, not restate existing commitments.

Talks ran past their 20 November deadline, and the groups warn [Ethiopia](country:ET), COP32's host, will inherit the gaps.
