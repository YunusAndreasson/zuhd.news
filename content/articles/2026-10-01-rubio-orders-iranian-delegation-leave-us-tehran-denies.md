New York — Rubio expelled [Iran](country:IR)'s delegation, a US official says.

Indirect talks on ending the war and reopening the Strait of Hormuz had produced no breakthrough.

The US mission told Iran's mission Monday evening to leave immediately, and Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi flew to Doha early Tuesday.

Iran's UN mission says it left on a schedule it gave the State Department on 17 September.

Qatari mediators are still working on a compromise proposal between the two sides.
