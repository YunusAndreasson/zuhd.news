Kofu — Cut the Y chromosome, and a mouse embryo defaults female.

The technique gives researchers genetically identical mice differing only by sex, a missing control in biology.

Japanese scientists cloned male mice, then deleted the Y chromosome with CRISPR, yielding healthy, fertile female offspring.

The same edit in human embryos stays banned in nearly every jurisdiction.
