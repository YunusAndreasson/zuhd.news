Jakarta — Stocks plunged 10% to a six-year low.

Index-tracking funds must sell, and the rupiah fell 0.6% to 18,000 per dollar.

Reforms on ownership opacity and trading behaviour fell short, so MSCI proposes standalone status for [Indonesia](country:ID), worse than frontier.

The Indonesia Stock Exchange says it has taken concrete steps on transparency and wants clarification.

MSCI consults investors until April 2027, then reclassifies unless governance clearly improves.
