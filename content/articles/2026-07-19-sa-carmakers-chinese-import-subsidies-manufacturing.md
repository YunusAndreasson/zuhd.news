Pretoria — Local content in South African cars fell to 39%.

[China](country:CN)'s state subsidies slash automakers' capital, material and energy costs, letting them underprice a [South Africa](country:ZA) industry with no comparable war chest.

Pretoria is rewriting incentive rules to reward local manufacturing over assembly, hoping to reverse the slide.
