Sanaa — [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s strikes killed or wounded 1,039 people since 13 July, Iranian state agency Mehr reported.

[Yemen](country:YE)'s Sanaa-based health minister said a Saudi strike on homes in Bani Hushaysh killed 32 and wounded 38.

Rescuers still dig bodies from the rubble, and the minister said Saudi jets also targeted medical teams evacuating the wounded.

He called the Bani Hushaysh figures preliminary and reported 30 dead in a Taiz market strike, also non-final.

Polymarket traders price a Saudi-Houthi ceasefire by 31 December at 23%.
