Moscow — [Russia](country:RU) expects 2.15 million tons of diesel exports in November.

The government's ban on producers' diesel exports now runs through January, with exemptions for selected suppliers.

Moscow eased limits this month after Trump said he was working with Putin to return Russian diesel to world markets.

Four tankers of Russian diesel had left Baltic ports by late October, Deputy Prime Minister Alexander Novak said.

The decree is unpublished, and Novak says [Ukraine](country:UA)'s refinery strikes are unlikely to cut exports significantly.
