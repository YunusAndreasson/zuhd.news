Brighton — Green members voted 1,022 to 813 to call Zionism racism.

The first major [UK](country:GB) party to do so also backs one democratic state across historic Palestine.

The UN General Assembly passed the same finding in 1975 by 72 to 35, and revoked it 111 to 25 in 1991.

Four of its five MPs opposed it, and [Israel](country:IL) banned leader Zack Polanski, who is Jewish, from entering.

Polanski faces voters Tuesday in the by-election for Keir Starmer's former London seat.
