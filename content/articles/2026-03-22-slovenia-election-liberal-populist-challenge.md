Ljubljana — Slovenians vote today on whether to reverse Palestine recognition. Janša, Slovenia's former prime minister, has pledged to revoke the country's Palestinian statehood recognition and end its ICC cooperation. A Janša victory would make Slovenia the first EU member to revoke Palestinian recognition, siding with Hungary and Slovakia against the ICC's arrest warrants for Israeli leaders.
