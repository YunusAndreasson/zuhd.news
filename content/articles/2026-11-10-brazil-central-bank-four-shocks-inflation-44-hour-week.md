Brasília — Inflation has passed the top of [Brazil](country:BR)'s target range while growth slows.

The central bank keeps policy restrictive, so borrowing costs stay high for households and firms.

The minutes name 4 supply shocks: oil, El Niño, the end of the 44-hour work week and a weaker real.

Weaker demand could contain spillover into broader inflation, the bank said.

ABC Brasil expects the Selic rate to stay at 13.75% through at least the end of 2027.
