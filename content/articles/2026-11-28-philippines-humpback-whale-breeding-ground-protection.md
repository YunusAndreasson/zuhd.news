Calayan — Two reserves cover 1,677 hectares of a 1.7 million-hectare whale corridor.

The Western North Pacific humpbacks that breed there are endangered under [US](country:US) law, at about 1,000 whales in 2016.

Researchers lack data from inside the reserves, so cannot tell whether they overlap with where whales breed in the [Philippines](country:PH).

Whales "require large areas even when they're breeding," said Jo Marie Acebes, founder of cetacean group Balyena.org.
