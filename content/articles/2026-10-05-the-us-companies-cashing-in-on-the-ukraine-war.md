Kyiv — Palantir is worth about $450 billion, advocacy outlet Inkstick reported.

Palantir first offered Ukraine its services free and was worth under $25 billion in 2022, Inkstick says.

Inkstick says Palantir's AI now sifts battlefield intelligence, plans deep strikes, controls drone swarms and identifies targets across Ukrainian ministries.

Microsoft says it will extend technology support to Ukraine through the end of 2027.
