Calabar — A federal court awarded 100 million naira over Onyekachi Mba's killing.

Police shot the 22-year-old at Cross River's Government House after he insisted on delivering [Nigeria](country:NG)'s governor a message; he died of his wounds.

Judge Ojukwu called it "executive recklessness"; the police force and four named officers must now pay.
