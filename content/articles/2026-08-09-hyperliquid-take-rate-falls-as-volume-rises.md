New York — Hyperliquid trades more, keeps less of it.

HYPE's value comes from fee buybacks, so a shrinking share threatens the token.

Since October, anyone staking 500,000 HYPE can launch a market and keep half its fees, per DefiLlama data.

How much further the take rate falls determines HYPE's next buyback size.
