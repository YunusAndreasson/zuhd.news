Oslo — [South Africa](country:ZA)'s judge who defended Mandela won the Peace Prize.

The committee said she held states and leaders accountable and brought hope to victims.

She chaired the UN inquiry that concluded last year [Israel](country:IL) committed genocide in Gaza, a finding Israel dismissed.

She receives the 12 million crown prize in Oslo on 10 December.
