Rawalakot — Azad Kashmir removed its elected mayor by notification, not court order.

The same day, it cancelled the registrations of 14 private schools in Poonch.

Abdul Naeem Khan, elected in 2023, was among at least 28 names listed as proscribed Joint Awami Action Committee activists under the Anti-Terrorism Act.

The assembly offered Wednesday to reopen talks with the committee if armed groups disband and suspects surrender.
