Gilgit — 3,044 glacial lakes now fill [Pakistan](country:PK)'s northern mountains.

When an ice dam breaks, a glacial lake releases millions of cubic meters within hours; 33 of these lakes are classified highly vulnerable.

The UN Development Programme installed 284 sensors after outbursts rose from 18 in 2021 to 83 in 2023.
