Brussels — Six bodies were pulled from a single elevator.

Fire on the Oxy Tower's second floor raced up the lift shaft during renovation work, trapping workers before crews could force the car open, prosecutors said.

A second lift still awaits inspection for further victims at the De Brouckère Square site.
