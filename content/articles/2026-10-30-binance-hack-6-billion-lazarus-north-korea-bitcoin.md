Singapore — Chainalysis has confirmed at least $5.9 billion stolen from Binance.

Binance held about $144 billion before the breach and says it will cover all losses.

Stablecoin issuers Tether and Circle froze over $2.3 billion, but bitcoin has no issuer to freeze it.

Binance says the breach began on a DevOps engineer's laptop, giving the attacker access to a service container.

Deposits and withdrawals stay suspended until Binance finishes its security checks.
