Kyiv — Drones lost Starshield links above 160 km/h for 10 days.

[Ukraine](country:UA)'s defence against Russian Geran drones runs on a network it does not control.

Terminals dropped connections on equipment moving faster than about 160 km/h after 14 October, forcing operators to slow drones.

Defence Minister Yevhenii Khmara said Saturday that speeds above 400 km/h are stable again.

Nokia and ICEYE, Finland's satellite and telecoms firms, have signed only a letter of intent with Ukraine's defence ministry.
