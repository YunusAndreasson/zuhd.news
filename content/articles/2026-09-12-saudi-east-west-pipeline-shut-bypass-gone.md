Riyadh — Drones from [Iraq](country:IQ)'s Maysan province hit [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s bypass pipeline Thursday.

Houthi control of the Red Sea route leaves Hormuz as Saudi's only option.

The pipeline can carry 7 million barrels daily around Hormuz.

Saudi Arabia has not said when pumping will resume.
