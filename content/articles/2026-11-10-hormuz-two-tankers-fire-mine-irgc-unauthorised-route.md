Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) says 2 tankers burned on its banned Hormuz route, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

The Guards' navy warns any ship using the route with [US](country:US) cover will meet the same fate.

The Guards' navy says a supertanker hit a mine and a second tanker, escorted by US fighter jets, was abandoned burning.

Polymarket traders give a US-Iran Hormuz agreement by 31 December 34% odds, down 5 points in a week.
