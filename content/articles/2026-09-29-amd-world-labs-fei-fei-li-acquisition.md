San Francisco — AMD is paying $8.2 billion for Fei-Fei Li's World Labs.

Owning a model lab is how AMD tries to break Nvidia's software grip on AI computing.

World Labs builds spatial-intelligence models that reconstruct and simulate 3D environments, work AMD wants to steer its chip roadmaps.

The startup raised $1 billion earlier this year, a valuation Reuters did not tie to any disclosed revenue.

Li joins AMD as chief scientist, reporting to CEO Lisa Su, once the deal closes by year's end.
