Beirut — Hezbollah will honour the 10-day Lebanon ceasefire. Al-Moussawi told Drop Site the deal derives from the Islamabad US-Iran agreement, contradicting Trump's claim Lebanon was excluded. Hezbollah conditions permanence on full Israeli withdrawal from occupied Lebanese territory; Israel has not committed to leaving.
