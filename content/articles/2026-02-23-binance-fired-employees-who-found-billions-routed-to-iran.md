$1.7 billion reached Iran through crypto exchange Binance. Binance fired at least 4 compliance investigators within weeks of the finding, calling it 'unauthorized disclosure of client information.' The money flowed between March 2024 and August 2025, entirely after Binance's $4.3 billion guilty plea for sanctions violations.
