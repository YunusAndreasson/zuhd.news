Lagos — Syringes are washing up on West Africa's beaches.

Biomedical waste management across the region has broken down, the investigation found.

Improperly discarded needles can carry hepatitis B and HIV to anyone who touches them.

SciDev.Net has not named which authorities are responsible for the gap.
