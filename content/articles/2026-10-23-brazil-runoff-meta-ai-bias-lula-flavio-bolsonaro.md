Brazil — Lula's campaign says Meta AI refused his posters, made Flávio's.

Lula's campaign also accuses Meta of suspending its advertising account and suppressing posts.

The campaign gave the electoral court screenshots showing Meta AI assigning Bolsonaro's number, 22, to fictional politicians.

Meta says its systems blocked the names Lula and Bolsonaro, but not the first names Luiz or Flávio.

[Brazil](country:BR)'s electoral prosecutors say Meta failed to fully comply with court orders before the 25 October runoff.
