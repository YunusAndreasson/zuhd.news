Muzaffarabad — Kashmir's internet blackout entered its 8th day.

[Pakistan](country:PK) authorities raided homes and businesses targeting the banned Joint Awami Action Committee; protests entered a 4th day.

Students are crossing into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa to access the internet; no restoration timeline has been set.
