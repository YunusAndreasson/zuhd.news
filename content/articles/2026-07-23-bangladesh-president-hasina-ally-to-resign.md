Dhaka — Mohammed Shahabuddin, 76, will resign Friday.

Elected unopposed in 2023 as an Awami League nominee, he is Hasina's last ally in high office as she returns from exile.

Parliament speaker Hafiz Uddin Ahmad may serve as interim president until a permanent successor is elected.
