One in 5 Australian teens still uses banned TikTok. Unlike alcohol or tobacco, social media platforms run inside every smartphone, giving minors VPN workarounds that no age-gating system has yet blocked. The EU, UK, and several US states are considering similar bans, with Australia's data now their clearest warning.
