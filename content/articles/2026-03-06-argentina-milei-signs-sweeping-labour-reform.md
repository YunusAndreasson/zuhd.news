source: "Buenos Aires Times"
sourceUrl: "https://www.batimes.com.ar/news/economy/milei-signs-labour-reform-introducing-new-rules-on-contracts-strikes-and-severance.phtml"

---

Argentine workers must now maintain 75% operations during strikes. President Javier Milei signed the law, ending Peronist labour protections held for 70 years and capping union dues at 2% of wages. The unions that staged a general strike in February against his government must now comply with the same law if they strike again.
