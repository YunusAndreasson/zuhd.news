Sochi — 23 Indian sailors were rescued from a burning crude tanker.

Sailors from India carry the risk as both sides target Black Sea shipping.

[Russia](country:RU)'s transport ministry says naval drones hit the Liberian-flagged tanker and crude oil is burning openly on the water.

Kyiv has not commented, though [Ukraine](country:UA) says strikes on Russian oil infrastructure answer Moscow's nightly barrages.

Firefighters will approach once the fire eases, and officials have restricted access to Sochi's coast.
