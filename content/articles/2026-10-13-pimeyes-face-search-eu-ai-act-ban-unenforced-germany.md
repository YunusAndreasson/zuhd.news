Berlin — PimEyes still searches faces, 20 months into the EU ban.

The EU AI Act prohibits building facial-recognition databases by untargeted scraping of images.

[Germany](country:DE)'s Federal Network Agency became the enforcer only this summer, and PimEyes sells 75 searches a day for €32.90 a month.

The agency will not say what action it is taking.
