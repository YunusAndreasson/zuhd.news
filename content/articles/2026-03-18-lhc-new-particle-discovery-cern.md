CERN's particle smasher found its 80th particle. Xi-cc-plus has 2 heavy charm quarks instead of a proton's up quarks, making it 4 times heavier and only the 2nd double-charm baryon ever confirmed. CERN's planned Future Circular Collider is designed to find more, promising the strongest test yet of the strong force that binds all matter.
