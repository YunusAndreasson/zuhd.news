London — Researchers mapped potato genes for iron and zinc.

Genome-wide analysis of 288 varieties found 16 micronutrient markers in potato, the world's most-eaten non-grain crop.

Breeders can now target specific chromosomal regions to develop iron- and zinc-rich potato varieties without supplements.
