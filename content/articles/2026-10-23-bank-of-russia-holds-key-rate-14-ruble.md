Moscow — [Russia](country:RU) kept its key rate at 14%, state agency TASS reported.

Russian stocks fell on the decision, the Moscow Exchange index losing 0.45% to 2,418.68 points.

The ruble's rise slowed against the yuan, which traded at 12.05 rubles, according to the trading platform.

The ruble stood at 81.89 per dollar on 23 October, down 2.4% in 7 days.
