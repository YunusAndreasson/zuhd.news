Dubai — Swissaid counts 591 tonnes of illicit gold imported by the [UAE](country:AE).

Sudan's Rapid Support Forces, through their trading firm al-Junaid, smuggle gold from areas they hold into Dubai.

Gold crosses into Egypt, Ethiopia or Chad, then almost always ships to the UAE as an official export.

Abu Dhabi says its involvement in [Sudan](country:SD) is purely humanitarian.

Switzerland, the world's largest gold hub, is the main importer of the UAE's gold.
