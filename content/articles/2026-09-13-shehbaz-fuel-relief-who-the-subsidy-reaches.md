Islamabad — Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif unveiled a motorcycle fuel subsidy.

It targets motorcycles and small vehicles that [Pakistan](country:PK)'s lower-income commuters actually drive.

Brent crude has climbed 22% in a week to $109.50 a barrel as of September 9, driving import costs.

The government has not detailed the subsidy's cost or how it will be delivered.
