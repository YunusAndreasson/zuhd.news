Donetsk — A truck driver died on the Urzuf-Yalta highway.

Russian-installed regional head Denis Pushilin said Ukrainian drones killed one civilian and wounded 15, most while driving; nine were hurt when a bus was hit near Starobeshevo.

Pushilin reported five homes and five trucks damaged, with no word yet on further strikes.
