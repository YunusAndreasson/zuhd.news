Southeast Asian financial firms are collateral in Iran's cyberwar. They share cloud providers, payment processors, and supply chains with US and Israeli companies Iran is actively hitting. Iran has threatened to strike all US-linked economic interests across the region, leaving no stated limit on what qualifies as a target.
