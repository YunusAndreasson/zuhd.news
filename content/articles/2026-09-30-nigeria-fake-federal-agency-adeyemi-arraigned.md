Abuja — Adeniyi Adeyemi faced court over a fake federal agency.

Police traced 34 bank accounts to him, 9 opened in the names of purported government agencies.

Prosecutors say he forged a presidential appointment letter and State House letterheads to launch the Presidential Foreign Intervention Promotion Council.

Adeyemi has denied the allegations.

Two co-defendants, identified as Femi and Anu, remain at large after police charged 3 people in November 2025.
