Athens — About 10,000 people marched through the capital over school funding.

Primary teachers across [Greece](country:GR) struck for 24 hours, and students occupied 18 schools in Thessaloniki.

Protesters demand filled teaching vacancies, smaller classes, safer buildings, lower student rents and bigger housing allowances.

The government says hundreds of millions of euros are going into student housing and education upgrades.

Police detained 21 people in Athens and questioned 10 in Thessaloniki after clashes near Omonia.
