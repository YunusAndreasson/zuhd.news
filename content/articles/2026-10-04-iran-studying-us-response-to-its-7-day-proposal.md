Tehran — The [US](country:US) answered Iran's 7-day plan through [Qatar](country:QA).

Hormuz stays shut while Tehran reviews, and it has published none of the reply's terms.

[Iran](country:IR)'s plan: 7 days of ceasefire, at least $12 billion in frozen assets, a lifted blockade, then Hormuz reopens.

Foreign ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baghaei said the US counterproposals were "more or less in line with their previous positions."

Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi said Iran will give its final position after the internal review, with no date.
