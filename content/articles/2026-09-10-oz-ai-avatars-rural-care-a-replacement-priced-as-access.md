Washington — Medicare and Medicaid chief Mehmet Oz is promoting AI avatars as rural care.

An AI front end is cheaper than staff, risking replacement where clinics have no alternative left.

Rural hospitals face $1 trillion in Medicaid cuts over the next decade, and AI tools falter on subjective calls like frailty exemptions.

Rural health leaders are pushing back, questioning whether AI investment will widen the urban-rural care gap.
