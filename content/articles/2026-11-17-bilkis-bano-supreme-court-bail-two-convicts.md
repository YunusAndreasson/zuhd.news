New Delhi — A three-page order freed two of Bilkis Bano's convicted rapists.

[India](country:IN)'s Supreme Court sent them back to jail in 2024, after Gujarat freed all 11 convicts in 2022.

A 28 October order suspended their sentences, citing 17 years in custody and one man's severe disability.

"She has to restart her battle for justice," said Shobha Gupta, Bano's lawyer.

Bano vowed to fight the order as the men's challenge to a 2017 ruling proceeds.
