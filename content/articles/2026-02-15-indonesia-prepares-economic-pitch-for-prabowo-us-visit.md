Indonesia cut export tariffs from 32% to 19%. President Prabowo convened economic ministers at his Bogor residence on Sunday to finalize negotiating priorities before his February 19 Washington visit, where he will sign a reciprocal trade agreement with President Trump. As the world's largest nickel producer and 4th most-populous country, Indonesia is testing whether offering concessions earns relief from Trump's broader tariff campaign.
