New Delhi — BRICS leaders adopted a joint declaration Saturday.

It formally demands the IMF, World Bank and World Trade Organization be restructured.

IMF quota reform needs an 85% supermajority that the [US](country:US) alone can block with its 15% stake.

The declaration condemns "aggression" without naming the US, exposing the bloc's Iran-UAE divide.
