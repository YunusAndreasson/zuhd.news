Beijing — [China](country:CN)'s new export orders fell to 48.7 in October.

The statistics bureau's manufacturing index fell to 49.3 from 50.1, reversing September's rebound.

Factories ship abroad to offset weak property-hit demand, and the EU says its deficit with China hit $1.1 billion a day in 2025.

Li Qiang told [Germany](country:DE)'s president Frank-Walter Steinmeier that China is willing to import more German goods.

[US](country:US) President Donald Trump is expected to meet Xi in mid-November at the Shenzhen summit.
