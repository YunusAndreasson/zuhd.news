Israel used rape and starvation against detainees, a UN rapporteur found. UN Special Rapporteur Francesca Albanese drew on 300 testimonies to describe the abuse as "collective vengeance" applied to 18,500 arrested Palestinians, including 1,500 children. Albanese presents findings to the UN Human Rights Council this month, where states will decide whether to pursue accountability.
