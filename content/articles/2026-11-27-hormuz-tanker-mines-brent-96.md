Iran — Brent rose $1.64 to $96.25 a barrel Thursday.

[Iran](country:IR) says mines hit tankers in the strait Asian importers depend on.

Hormuz ship traffic averaged 2.7 a day to 22 November, 24% below its 90-day normal of 3.6.

The Houthis say they targeted [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s largest refinery, and Brent is still 18.9% below 30 days ago.

Polymarket traders give a Saudi-Houthi ceasefire by 31 December a 7% chance.
