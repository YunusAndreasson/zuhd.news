Hyderabad — Protesters stormed Meta's office over 2 withheld opposition Instagram accounts.

Meta's disclosures show 5.4 million restrictions in India in 2026's first half, versus 70,000 in 2025.

[India](country:IN)'s government cut the takedown deadline from 36 hours to 3 in February and issued nearly 195,000 blocking orders from March to July.

The government says it orders takedowns only for content that breaches local law or threatens public safety.

The Youth Congress gave Meta seven days to restore accounts, or face more protests.
