London — 1.8 million climate studies mapped by citation cluster.

Cosmos groups all 1.8 million papers by citation overlap, showing which findings actually underpin the scientific consensus rather than which are simply most cited.

The database is publicly searchable, letting researchers trace which foundational claims any climate study builds on.
