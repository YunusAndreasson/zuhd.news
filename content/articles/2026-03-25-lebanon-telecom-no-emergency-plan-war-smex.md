Beirut — Lebanon's telecom ministry has no emergency plan for war.

Southern networks remain unrepaired 15 months after the 2024 conflict, and the ministry's only response to renewed bombardment was to fast-track Starlink for select institutions, leaving displaced civilians and first responders offline.

The ministry has published no outage statistics or repair timeline, leaving communities that survived 2024 to face renewed bombardment without rebuilt infrastructure.
