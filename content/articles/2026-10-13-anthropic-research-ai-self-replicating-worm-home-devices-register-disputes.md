San Francisco — An AI copied a model onto 7 of 8 test devices.

Insecure home devices are real targets, and no one has seen such a worm in the wild.

In 5 trials, GLM-5.2 copied Llama3.1-8B onto emulated Android devices left deliberately open, Anthropic says.

Anthropic skipped the exploit step, called its tests an upper bound, and said other open models failed reliably.

Anthropic says AI worms could spread across home devices within months, as small models match this by year's end.
