El-Ayyat — 4 train cars derailed, injuring at least 150 people, Russian state news agency TASS reported.

Egyptian news portal Masrawy said at least 50 ambulances went to the site.

The report names no cause.

The casualty count is still being verified, and no [Egypt](country:EG) rail official has been quoted on what went wrong.
