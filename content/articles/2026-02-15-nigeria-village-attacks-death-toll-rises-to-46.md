46 dead across 3 villages in Niger State. Motorcycle-riding gunmen entered Konkoso and 2 nearby villages at dawn on Saturday, shot residents, slit throats, burned most homes, and abducted an unknown number of people. No security forces arrived during the hours-long assault, and armed groups operating from the nearby Kainji Forest remain unchecked.
