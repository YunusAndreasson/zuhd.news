Armed convoys hit 3 Nigerian villages at dawn Saturday. The gunmen killed 46 people across the Borgu communities in Niger State, with 38 dying in Konkoso alone. Community leaders called on President Bola Tinubu to station troops near Kainji Forest, a staging ground for armed groups that has fuelled repeated attacks in the region.
