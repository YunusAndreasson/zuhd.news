Weekend prices came from crypto traders reacting to each other, because no share market was open.

CoinGlass data show leveraged positions were force-closed when losses exceeded deposits, and the forced selling pushed prices lower.

Quotes return when [US](country:US) markets open Monday, showing how far Saturday's prices strayed from real share prices.
