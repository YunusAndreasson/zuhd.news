New Delhi — Telegram has 15 days to explain itself.

[India](country:IN)'s broadcasting ministry demanded an action report on pirated films after earlier ordering Telegram to disable message-editing during exam season over leak fears.

The ministry has already acted against 3,000 piracy channels; incomplete compliance risks "further examination" under copyright law.
