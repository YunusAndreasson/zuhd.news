London — [Britain](country:GB) banned Cenk Uygur and Hasan Piker.

The Home Office cancelled travel authorisation for the 2 [US](country:US) commentators, citing "public good"; both say they were barred for criticising [Israel](country:IL).

Both were billed at SXSW London and Oxford; no charge was filed and no appeal mechanism exists.
