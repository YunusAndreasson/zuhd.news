Chinese scientists achieved 98.3% purity pairing photons on demand. The device, smaller than a grain of sand, generates entangled pairs that double imaging resolution and form the basis of unbreakable quantum networks. No competing lab has replicated the result, giving Chinese teams a measurable lead in the race for functional quantum communication.
