New York — Meta readies a cross-border investment-grade bond sale.

Investors have grown sceptical about how fast AI infrastructure companies are spending.

Separately, Marvell agreed to buy Lumentum in an all-stock deal valued at $115 billion including debt, about $80 billion in equity.

The deals are separate, though Nvidia invested $2 billion in each company in March.

The Marvell deal is expected to close in the first half of 2028, pending shareholder and regulator approval.
