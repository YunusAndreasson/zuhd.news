Washington — "The end of my life," one Haitian beneficiary said.

The [US](country:US) Supreme Court voted 6-3 to let Temporary Protected Status expire, exposing 350,000 Haitians to deportation into armed-gang violence.

The same ruling strips protection from Syrian nationals; no new deportation date has been set.
