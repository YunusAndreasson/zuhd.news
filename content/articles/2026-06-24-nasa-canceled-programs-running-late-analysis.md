Washington — Artemis contracts more than doubled from $2.8 billion to $5.9 billion before NASA canceled 4 programs.

Delivery dates slipped up to 7 years and further overruns were projected, an inspector general analysis found.

NASA asked Northrop Grumman to halt the Lunar Gateway module, projected to miss its 2032 target.
