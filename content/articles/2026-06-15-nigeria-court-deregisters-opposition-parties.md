Abuja — A judge ordered 5 opposition parties removed from [Nigeria](country:NG)'s ballot.

The constitution bars parties that fail to win any seat or 25% of the vote; the ruling disqualifies Atiku Abubakar of the African Democratic Congress from the 2027 race.

The judge ruled despite an appeals-court stay order; Osun's governorship election is set for August.
