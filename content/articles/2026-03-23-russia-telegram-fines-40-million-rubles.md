Moscow — Russia fined Telegram 10.5M rubles for hosting extremist content. Telegram, whose founder Pavel Durov was arrested in France last year over content moderation, now owes Russia more than 40M rubles in fines it has not paid. Roskomnadzor, Russia's state media regulator, warned in February it would block access until Telegram complied — a threat Russia has carried out against other platforms before.
