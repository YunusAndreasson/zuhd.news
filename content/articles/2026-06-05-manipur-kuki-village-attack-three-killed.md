Kangpokpi — Gunmen killed 3 Kuki-Zo villagers; 7 homes burned.

The attack continues a 2-year ethnic conflict [India](country:IN)'s central forces have not contained; Kuki-Zo groups blamed Naga armed groups, and police said the matter is under investigation.

14 Kuki villagers remain held by Naga groups after 3 weeks, with no government intervention announced.
