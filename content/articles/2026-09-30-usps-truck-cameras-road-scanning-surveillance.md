Washington — 100 Postal Service trucks in the capital area now carry scanning cameras.

The [US](country:US) Postal Service wants to use the nationwide reach of its fleet to collect street data.

A letter to the letter carriers' union says forward-facing Next Base dashcams will map roads, signs and sidewalks, without audio.

The cameras run no plate-reader software, though Next Base advertises 4K capture of every licence plate.

The pilot runs several months, and the Postal Service says findings will inform any future decisions.
