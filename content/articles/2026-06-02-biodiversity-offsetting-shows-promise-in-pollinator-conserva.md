Amsterdam — Dutch researchers found created grassland can host wild pollinators.

The [Netherlands](country:NL) study supports biodiversity offsetting, a policy letting developers destroy habitat if they create replacement land.

Researchers warn the finding holds only if planning authorities enforce quality conditions.
