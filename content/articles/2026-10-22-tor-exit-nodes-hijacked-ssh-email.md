Switzerland — Attackers hijacked 333 Tor exit nodes for 3 days.

Tor hides who you are, not what you send, so unencrypted traffic leaving it is exposed.

Attackers blocked encrypted email ports to force plaintext downgrades and sent remote-login traffic on port 22 to 12 interception servers.

Tor volunteers suspect a financially motivated crime group but call that speculation.

They still do not know how the attackers reached so many servers.
