Washington — [US](country:US) utility-scale solar produced 31 TWh in April; coal produced 40, federal energy data show.

Rooftop solar's 9.8 TWh — consumed on-site, never reaching the grid — was the only margin that pushed total solar above coal.

Solar is projected to lead coal through the full summer and remain ahead indefinitely, federal energy data indicate.
