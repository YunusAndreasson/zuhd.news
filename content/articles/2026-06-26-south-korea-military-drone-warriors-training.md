Seoul — [South Korea](country:KR) to arm 450,000 troops with drones.

[North Korea](country:KP) fields 1.2M troops to the South's 450,000; drones are the asymmetric answer, modeled on [Ukraine](country:UA).

Starting with 11,000 training drones this year, the military aims for 60,000 deployed by 2029.
