Bogotá — Sound waves removed 74.9% of toxic algae.

Chemical algaecides rupture cells and can release the toxins they contain.

The early-access study found ultrasound collapses the gas pockets algae use to float, sinking colonies before they bloom.

Toxins resisted removal even so; scaling the method needs further testing, researchers said.
