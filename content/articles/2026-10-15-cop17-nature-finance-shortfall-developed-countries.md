Yerevan — Nature finance averaged under $50 billion a year, against a $200 billion goal.

A special biodiversity fund drew under $140 million from developed countries in 2 years.

The UN biodiversity convention's report says the 2025 target of $20 billion a year in public aid to developing countries was likely missed.

Negotiators meet 19 October at COP17, where finance is likely a key dispute.
