London — North London's Jewish community lost its free ambulances overnight. Arsonists burned all 4 Hatzalah vehicles — the charity provides free emergency medical care — in what monitoring groups say is part of a surge in antisemitic attacks since the Iran-Israel war began. The 3 suspects remain at large as Community Security Trust, a Jewish safety charity, assists police.
