Havana — The US embassy asked Cuba's blockaded government for diesel. Cuba's entire grid went dark twice last week as the US blockade cut the fuel that hospitals and homes depend on. Cuba's foreign ministry rejected the request in writing; Trump has threatened to "take" Cuba if its government does not change.
