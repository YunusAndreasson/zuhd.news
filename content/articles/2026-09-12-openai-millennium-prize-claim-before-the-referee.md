San Francisco — OpenAI says its model solved Navier-Stokes.

The Clay Institute requires 2 years of review before any prize is awarded.

OpenAI used 10,000 autonomous agents at an estimated $15m cost, building on published work by Diego Córdoba and Luis Martinez-Zoroa.

Mathematician James Robinson called it "immature playground boasting" as peer review of the claim remains pending.
