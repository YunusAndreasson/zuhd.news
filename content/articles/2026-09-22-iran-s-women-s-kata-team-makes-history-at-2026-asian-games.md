Toyohashi — Iran's women's kata team won its first-ever Asian Games medal.

The silver marks a milestone for women's karate in [Iran](country:IR) at this level of competition.

Coached by Fatemeh Baghlanipour, the team beat Thailand and the Philippines en route to the final before finishing runners-up.

Karate at the Games continues through September 30 at the Toyohashi venue.
