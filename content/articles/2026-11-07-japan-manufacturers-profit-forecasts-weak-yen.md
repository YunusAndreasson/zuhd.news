Tokyo — Nearly half of [Japan](country:JP)'s manufacturers raised profit forecasts.

Toyota and Komatsu are among the beneficiaries of foreign-exchange trends and US growth.

Heavy machinery makers gain from US data centre construction, and the yen stood at 158.5 to the dollar on 7 November.

The Nikkei 225 index fell 9.3% in the week to 6 November, to 57,430.

The forecasts will be tested when the fiscal year ends in March 2027.
