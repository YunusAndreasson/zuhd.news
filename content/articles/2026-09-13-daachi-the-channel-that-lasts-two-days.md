Karachi — 200 free stalls let artisans skip the middleman.

It's a rare direct sale for artisans, mostly women, working from home.

The Daachi Foundation gives stalls free, but exhibitions across [Pakistan](country:PK) run just two days a year.

Sandeep Chauhan hopes to sell all 200 of his handmade khussas by Sunday.
