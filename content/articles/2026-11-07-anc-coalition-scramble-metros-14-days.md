Johannesburg — African National Congress support fell to 20.25%.

The Democratic Alliance now leads Johannesburg, Tshwane and Ekurhuleni, cities the party held in 2021.

Under the Municipal Structures Act, councils must hold first meetings within 14 days of declaration to elect mayors.

Secretary-general Fikile Mbalula says the party would rather sit in opposition than sign unfavourable deals.

The party's national executive committee meets Thursday, 12 November, to set its coalition strategy.
