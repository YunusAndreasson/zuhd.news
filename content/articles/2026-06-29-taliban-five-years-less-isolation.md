Kabul — A Herat shooting went without international rebuke.

Five years on, the Taliban face less isolation than at their 2021 return — domestic repression in [Afghanistan](country:AF) continues unchanged even as sanctions have eased.

No state has formally recognized the Taliban government; trade and diplomatic engagement have expanded regardless.
