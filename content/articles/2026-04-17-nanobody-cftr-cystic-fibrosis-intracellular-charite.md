Berlin — A nanobody repairs CFTR from inside human cells. Charité and Leibniz-FMP teams published the first cell-penetrating nanobody to correct the CFTR chloride channel mutation in Nature Chemical Biology. CFTR is proof of concept; nanobodies may now reach disease targets that small molecules and antibodies cannot.
