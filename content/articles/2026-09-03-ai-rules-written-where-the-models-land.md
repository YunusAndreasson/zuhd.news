Jakarta — Two rival superpowers stalled global AI rules.

Most countries only get to shape how AI is deployed, not built.

Regional blocs like ASEAN can bind the firms that sell and deploy models, even without touching how they're trained.

The analysis names no case where a regional rule changed a shipped product.
