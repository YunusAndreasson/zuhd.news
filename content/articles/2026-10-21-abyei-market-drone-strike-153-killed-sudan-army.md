Abyei — A drone strike killed at least 153 at Amiet Market.

Fires still burned Tuesday and the toll will rise, Abyei's information minister said.

Diplomats say Abyei, claimed by [Sudan](country:SD) and [South Sudan](country:SS), is a fuel route for the Rapid Support Forces.

An unnamed Sudanese officer says only fighters and smugglers died, but the UN counts women and children among the dead.

UN peacekeepers are still verifying casualties, and South Sudan has summoned Sudan's ambassador.
