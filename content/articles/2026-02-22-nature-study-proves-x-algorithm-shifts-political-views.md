4,965 users shifted rightward after 7 weeks. A Nature experiment assigned them randomly to X's algorithmic or chronological feed and found shifts in views on policy, Trump, and Ukraine. The effect persisted after switching back — the algorithm had already nudged users to follow right-leaning accounts.
