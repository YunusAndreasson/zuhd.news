Istanbul — [Turkey](country:TR)'s Russian gas deal extends 3 years, state-run TASS reported.

Deputy Prime Minister Alexander Novak said Gazprom and Botas kept volumes and terms unchanged.

Novak said Novatek will also ship liquefied natural gas to Turkey from 1 January 2027, in small volumes at first.

Novak said Akkuyu's first nuclear unit goes online in December.
