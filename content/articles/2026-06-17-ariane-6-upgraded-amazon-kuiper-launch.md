Kourou — Ariane 64's upgraded boosters set a 22-tonne payload record.

New P160C boosters — 1 metre longer, 14 tonnes more propellant — lifted 36 Amazon Project Kuiper satellites on Ariane 6's 3rd flight of 2026.

The European Space Agency is studying raising annual launch cadence from 9 to as many as 20; the next Ariane 6 flight is 28 August.
