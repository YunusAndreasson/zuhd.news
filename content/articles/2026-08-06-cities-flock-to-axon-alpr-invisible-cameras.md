Denver — Cameras that beat privacy campaigns just moved to streetlamps.

Cities that cancelled Flock license-plate contracts after public backlash are signing with Axon, whose cameras mount on existing poles and are built to blend in.

Axon already sells police body cameras and evidence software, consolidating the surveillance chain the campaigns thought they'd broken.
