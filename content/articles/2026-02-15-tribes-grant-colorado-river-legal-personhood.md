The Colorado River is now a legal person. The Colorado River Indian Tribes voted unanimously to grant personhood to the 1,450-mile waterway sustaining 40 million people across 7 US states. Under the resolution, the river gains the right to legal protection. Tribal leaders must consider its needs in all future decisions. The vote arrives as basin states negotiate new management rules during the river's worst drought in 1,200 years.
