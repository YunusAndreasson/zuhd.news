Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) says it replaced 2,000 sanctioned parts.

Sanctions do not remove a part; they raise its smuggled cost until building it locally is cheaper, an Iranian research body said.

The research body is now drafting a self-sufficiency roadmap for the entire petrochemical sector, officials say.
