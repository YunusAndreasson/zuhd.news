Spokane — 65,000 evacuated as fire reduced homes to cinders.

Drought and extreme heat drove the blaze into the city itself, not just its outskirts — under these conditions embers can travel over a kilometer, igniting structures that then become the dominant fuel.

Firefighters were still working Monday night to contain the flames.
