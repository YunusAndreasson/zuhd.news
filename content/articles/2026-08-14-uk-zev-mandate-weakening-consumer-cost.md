London — Weakening [UK](country:GB) EV rules could cost drivers £3bn.

Petrol drivers already pay three to four times more per mile than EV owners.

The ZEV mandate forces automakers to discount EVs to hit sales quotas, Carbon Brief found.

Downing Street is reviewing a consultation on cutting the 2030 target to 50%.
