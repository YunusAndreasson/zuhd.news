Islamabad — 148 used cars entered [Pakistan](country:PK) in April.

Tariff barriers cut monthly imports 96% from last year by closing loopholes in overseas-Pakistani gift and baggage schemes used for commercial imports.

Parts maker association Paapam says the Rs 60 billion annual revenue loss from used imports is now reversing as local output rises.
