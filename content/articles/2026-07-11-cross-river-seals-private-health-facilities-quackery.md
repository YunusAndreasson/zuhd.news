Calabar — A teacher with no medical training ran a sealed clinic.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s Cross River government shut 3 unregistered facilities after inspectors found unlicensed staff and, at one, a June death following surgery.

The health ministry gave no timeline for further inspections of the state's unregistered private clinics.
