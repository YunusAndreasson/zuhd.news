Dakar — Adult chimpanzees in [Senegal](country:SN) hand tools to youngsters.

The finding challenges the idea that only humans teach their young.

Teaching requires a cost to the giver and faster learning for the receiver, criteria long thought uniquely human.

Researchers have not yet confirmed the youngsters learned the skill faster.
