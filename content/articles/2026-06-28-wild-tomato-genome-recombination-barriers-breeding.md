Cologne — Wild tomato repeat DNA blocks breeders' gene transfer.

Structural variants suppress the recombination needed to move disease and drought resistance from wild relatives into cultivated crops, genome assemblies show.

As long-read sequencing makes such assemblies routine, the bottleneck shifts from sequencing to genome architecture.
