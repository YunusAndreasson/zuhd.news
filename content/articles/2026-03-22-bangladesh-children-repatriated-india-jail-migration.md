Dhaka — India jailed 33 Bangladeshis, including children, for crossing the border. Justice and Care, a Bangladeshi rights NGO, said the group were trafficking victims transported by paid brokers, not migrants who chose to cross. The trafficking networks that brought them across remain active, with no brokers charged.
