Umeå — Blocking one transport system makes resistance collapse.

It could revive cheap old antibiotics where newer drugs are unaffordable.

Slowing transport of wall-building blocks stops bacteria from rebuilding faster than drugs damage them.

Umeå researchers say the transport protein is now a target for new drug design.
