Honolulu — 5,500 Oahu residents fled as floodwaters reached rooftops. A 120-year-old dam is at risk of failure after rain fell on soil already saturated from last week's storm. Governor Josh Green warned total damage could exceed $1 billion with more rain forecast this weekend.
