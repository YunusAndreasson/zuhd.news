Naypyidaw — [Myanmar](country:MM)'s independent press is defunding ahead of junta elections.

Myanmar's junta frames the coup as a civilian transition; a propaganda apparatus manufactures legitimacy as independent outlets close.

No replacement funding exists; adversarial coverage may not survive the election campaign.
