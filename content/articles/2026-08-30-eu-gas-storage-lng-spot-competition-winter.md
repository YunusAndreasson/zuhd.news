Brussels — EU gas storage sits near 64%.

Without Russian pipeline gas, the EU bids against Asia for the same LNG cargoes.

ENTSOG data put that below the 80% seasonal norm, after Hormuz disruption cut Qatari LNG into the shared spot market.

A cold winter could push European bids above buyers in Pakistan and Bangladesh.
