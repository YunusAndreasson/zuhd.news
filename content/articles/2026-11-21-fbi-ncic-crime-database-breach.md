Washington — Hackers reportedly reached the database US police query for warrants.

It also holds records on crime victims, missing people and people under court protection.

The National Crime Information Center links federal, state and local agencies through 21 files, 14 on people and 7 on property.

This is the second reported breach in months, after ShinyHunters claimed it stole 2 terabytes of employee data in September.

Investigators have not said whether records were copied or altered, nor who is responsible.
