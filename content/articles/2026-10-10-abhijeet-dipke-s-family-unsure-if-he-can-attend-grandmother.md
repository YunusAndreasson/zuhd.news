New Delhi — Police pulled Abhijeet Dipke off a plane.

His family says it cannot reach him, and his party says even lawyers were refused.

Police call it preventive detention before an unpermitted Jantar Mantar protest demanding the resignation of [India](country:IN)'s Chief Election Commissioner.

"Under what law was my son detained from the aircraft?" asked his father, Bhagwanrao Dipke.

The family does not know if he reaches Hingoli for his grandmother's Sunday rites.
