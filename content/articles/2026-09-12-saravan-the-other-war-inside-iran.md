The clash exposes an internal insurgency inside Iran's poorest, Sunni-majority border province.

The Revolutionary Guards, the operation's only source, said five alleged fighters also died in the raid.

No group has claimed responsibility, and [Pakistan](country:PK) has not corroborated the account.
