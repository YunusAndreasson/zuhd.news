Munich — CATL launched a sodium battery system in Munich.

[China](country:CN) refines 74% of the world's lithium, a dependence sodium is meant to break.

Sodium sits below lithium on the periodic table but forms a heavier ion, storing less energy per kilogram.

Which company builds Europe's sodium plants, and who owns the patents, remains undecided.
