Beijing — The Pinglu Canal cuts a 560km detour.

It gives landlocked southwestern provinces a direct route to Southeast Asian markets.

Guangxi authorities say the 134km waterway cuts logistics costs 18 to 30 percent, saving about $700m yearly.

[China](country:CN) will charge vessels by the tonne after free transit ends December 31.
