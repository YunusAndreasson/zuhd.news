Every Israeli missile hitting Iran is American, Trump confirmed. He said the two countries have "similar" but not identical war objectives, naming US planes, missiles, and Patriot batteries as tools of Israel's campaign. Trump is weighing deploying the US Navy to Hormuz to escort commercial shipping through Iran's blockade.
