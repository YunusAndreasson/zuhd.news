Brussels — The EU's cloud draft does not bar [US](country:US) firms from building data centres.

Critics including the European Pirate Party say it entrenches big US firms rather than alternative providers.

The Cloud and AI Development Act speeds permits and eases investment to triple EU computing capacity by 2035.

Member states in the Council and the Parliament are still drafting their positions on the act.
