Helsinki — Treatable infections predict dementia onset within years. A Finnish study of hundreds of thousands found cystitis, pneumonia, and tooth decay correlating with dementia — implicating systemic inflammation, not just amyloid plaques. Researchers must run randomized trials to establish causality before the finding can change clinical treatment guidelines.
