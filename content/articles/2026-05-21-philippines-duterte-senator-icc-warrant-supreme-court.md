Manila — Court upheld ICC warrant against a sitting senator.

The [Philippines](country:PH) withdrew from the Rome Statute in 2019; the court ruled withdrawal cannot shield crimes committed under prior membership.

The fugitive senator — a Duterte-era police chief — remains at large with no domestic legal barrier remaining.
