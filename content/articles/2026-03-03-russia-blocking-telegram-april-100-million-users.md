Kremlin sources say Russia will block Telegram in April. Telegram founder Pavel Durov's 2024 arrest and cooperation with European authorities gave Moscow a security pretext to act against Russia's last independent news channel. Russia's 90 million Telegram users face losing access on April 1, with telecom carriers already notified.
