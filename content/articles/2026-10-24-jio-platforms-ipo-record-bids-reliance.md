Mumbai — Jio's record share offer filled in 100 minutes, the company says.

Foreign investors sold $33 billion of [India](country:IN)'s stocks this year, the company's presentation noted.

The company says bids reached Rs 7.4 lakh crore against a Rs 30,188 crore issue, 22.19 times subscribed.

Retail investors subscribed 2.72 times, against 70.55 times for qualified institutions.

Jio lists on India's two main exchanges on 28 October, grey-market prices implying a 22% gain.
