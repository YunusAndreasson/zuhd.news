Panama — Harlequin toads persist only where chytrid is seasonally weak.

In [Panama](country:PA), releases of captive-bred toads have suffered high losses, so release sites need ranking.

Chytrid severity swings with temperature and humidity, and most species persist only where over 80% of seasons are unsuitable for it.

Only the variable harlequin toad lives at chytrid-suitable sites, which the researchers say fits evolved resistance.

The models also flag places where wild populations, unseen for years, might remain.
