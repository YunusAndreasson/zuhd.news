London — Advocacy outlet Declassified UK reports BP and Shell signed Venezuelan agreements since January.

They followed the US seizure of Nicolás Maduro, which UN experts called a deliberate violation of international law.

Declassified says Foreign Office documents show high-level executive meetings, while interim president Delcy Rodríguez welcomed foreign companies and softened hydrocarbon law.

[Britain](country:GB) and [Venezuela](country:VE) planned this month to upgrade diplomatic representation to ambassador level.
