Islamabad — Pakistan tagged disaster spending for the first time.

Officials mapped over 5,000 cost centres across 4 disaster categories, enabling auditable climate spending.

Disaster-responsive allocations are 4% of the budget; a new dashboard links ministries to the tracking system.
