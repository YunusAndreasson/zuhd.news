Lahore — Protest caravans leave for Islamabad on Sunday.

Party chief Hafiz Naeemur Rehman refuses to cancel unless [Pakistan](country:PK)'s government abolishes the petroleum levy.

Petrol carries Rs114 per litre in taxes and duties, diesel Rs100, and the party rejected the relief package.

An Islamabad High Court ruling bars authorities from blocking the marchers' movement.
