Islamabad — Moody's raised Pakistan two notches to B3.

The rating sets the coupon Islamabad pays abroad, competing against subsidy budgets.

B3 remains deep speculative grade, and Moody's credited reserves and rollovers under the IMF programme, not new export earnings.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s next eurobond sale will show whether lenders price the upgrade in.
