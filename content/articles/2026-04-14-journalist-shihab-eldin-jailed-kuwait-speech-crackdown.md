Kuwait City — Kuwait has held Ahmed Shihab-Eldin for 6 weeks. The Palestinian-American journalist, arrested March 3, faces a new tribunal created by decree to prosecute speech crimes related to the Iran war. Kuwait banned reporting on unverified war news after March 1; dozens of others face the same charges.
