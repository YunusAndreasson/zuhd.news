Delhi — 3.3 million voters got election roll notices.

LK Advani, 98, and Arvind Kejriwal are among those flagged.

Most notices cite no matching record in the 2002 electoral roll, so voters must supply documents.

Voters must reply by October 29, before the November 4 final roll.
