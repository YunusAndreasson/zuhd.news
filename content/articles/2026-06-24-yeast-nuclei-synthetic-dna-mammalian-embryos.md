London — Scientists put synthetic chromosomes into mouse embryos using yeast nuclei.

The method delivers intact synthetic chromosomes into embryos at 100% efficiency by isolating yeast nuclei that carry them.

Whole synthetic chromosomes can now enter mammalian embryos; no governance framework covers this class of embryo editing.
