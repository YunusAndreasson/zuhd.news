Rome — [Italy](country:IT) wants €5.9 billion from its banks next year.

Intesa's chief warns the levies could push the European Central Bank to make lenders shed government bonds.

Under ECB rules, Carlo Messina said, spreading €20 billion of renovation tax credits over 10 years counts as forbearance.

Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni called the higher taxation "a matter of justice" given rising living costs.

Italy expects a critical ECB legal opinion on the budget, people familiar with the matter said.
