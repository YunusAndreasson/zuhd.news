Tokyo — Toyota's quarterly profit hit $9.4 billion.

A weaker yen added $2.2 billion of that gain on its own — an exporter earns in dollars and pays costs in yen, so currency alone inflates profit without an extra car sold.

[Japan](country:JP)'s automakers still face Hormuz-driven shipping costs Toyota says it is working to route around.
