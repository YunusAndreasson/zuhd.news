Cairo — 15,000 public questions on education went unpublished before the Egypt Forum.

Education Minister Mohamed Abdel Latif opened the forum Monday by citing huge improvements in school attendance.

Part of the rise stems from compulsory weekly assessments, which MPs say burn out scarce teachers without improving learning, a review of government data found.

MPs have filed formal briefing requests accusing the ministry of running an evaluation regime that does not improve learning.
