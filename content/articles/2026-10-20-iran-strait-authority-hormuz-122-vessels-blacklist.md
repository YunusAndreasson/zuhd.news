Tehran — Iran's strait authority says 122 ships are blacklisted.

[Iran](country:IR) runs the Persian Gulf Strait Authority to police passage through the Strait of Hormuz.

Iran's state-run Mehr agency quoted the authority: more than 40 vessels dealt with, 19 negotiating, 2 removed.

Traffic through the strait ran at 4.1 ships a day, level with normal, as of 11 October.

Polymarket traders give an Iran-Oman Hormuz agreement by 31 October a 10% chance, down 7 points in a week.
