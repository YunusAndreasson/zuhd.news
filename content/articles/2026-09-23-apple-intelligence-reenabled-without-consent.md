Cupertino — Apple restored Apple Intelligence after a user disabled it.

The upgrade removed the same opt-out switch Apple had offered since February 2025.

macOS 27 dropped the dedicated Apple Intelligence toggle, re-enabled Siri processes, and buried remaining controls inside Screen Time parental settings.

Apple has not said whether it will restore the missing opt-out control for Apple Intelligence.
