The Ministry of Defence holds the surveillance footage and won't say whether it shows the convoy site.

[Israel](country:IL) launched 3 drone strikes about 4 minutes apart on a World Central Kitchen convoy on 1 April 2024, killing 7.

The coroner will add Israel's military and Colonel Nochi Mendel as interested persons, and families want the Ministry of Defence too.
