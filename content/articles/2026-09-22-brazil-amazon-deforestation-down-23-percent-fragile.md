Brasília — Amazon deforestation fell 23.5% in [Brazil](country:BR) last year.

It pulled national clear-cutting to its lowest level since 2016.

The Amazon accounts for most of Brazil's deforestation, so its drop drove most of the national decline.

The gains face a test in Brazil's presidential runoff between Lula and Flávio Bolsonaro.
