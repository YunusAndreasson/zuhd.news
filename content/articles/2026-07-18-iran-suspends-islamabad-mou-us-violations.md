Tehran — Iran's one de-escalation channel with Washington just collapsed.

Iranian deputy foreign minister Kazem Gharibabadi said [Iran](country:IR) suspended all 14 points of the Pakistan-mediated pact covering Hormuz reopening and a US naval blockade lift.

Gharibabadi said Washington had already nullified the deal by striking; no new talks are scheduled.
