Kolwezi — 27 residents sued a Chinese-controlled cobalt mine for $6.3 million.

Eight claimants are children, and the suit alleges pollution harmed their health.

Under [Congo](country:CD)'s 2018 mining code, companies are liable for environmental damage unless they prove unrelated causes.

COMMUS, 67%-owned by Zijin Mining, says it will contest the allegations.

The suit also names Congo's state and environmental regulator, which did not immediately respond.
