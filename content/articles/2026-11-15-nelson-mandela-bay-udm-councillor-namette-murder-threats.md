Motherwell — A councillor's wife says party leaders knew of his death threat.

Luxolo Namette was shot dead on 7 November, the 4th Nelson Mandela Bay councillor killed since 2022.

An anonymous text message two months before the vote said two United Democratic Movement members wanted him removed from the candidate list.

Mandisa Namette, his wife, says the secretary-general told him "these things happen and he should just ignore it."

Secretary-general Bulelani Bobotyane says Namette never formally reported the threats, and police have made no arrests.
