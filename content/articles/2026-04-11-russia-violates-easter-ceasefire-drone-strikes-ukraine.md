Kyiv — Russia's Easter ceasefire collapsed within hours. Russia sent 160 drones at Ukraine before the ceasefire opened, killing civilians in Odesa and Kherson, and continued striking after 4pm. Ukraine replied fire for fire; Russia's stated priority remains comprehensive peace terms, not the ceasefire it declared.
