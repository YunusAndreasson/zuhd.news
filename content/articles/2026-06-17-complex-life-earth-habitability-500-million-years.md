Washington — Plants tolerate carbon dioxide 10-fold lower than models predicted.

New simulations find plants can scavenge CO2 at 1 part per million, extending Earth's life window to 1.8 billion years.

The revision raises estimated odds of complex biospheres on other planets, researchers say.
