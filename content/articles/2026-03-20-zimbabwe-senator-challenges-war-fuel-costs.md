Zimbabwe's fuel costs kept rising despite local ethanol supplies. Senator Jerry Gotora of the ruling Zanu PF party said the country's ethanol blending program should offset import costs, demanding the government explain why pump prices continue to rise. If the government cannot account for the gap, distributors may be exploiting the crisis under state cover.
