Colombo offered to repatriate 89,000 Tamil refugees from India. The offer inverts 4 decades of refugee flow — Sri Lanka sent over 100,000 Tamils to India during its civil war. India has yet to offer the refugees citizenship, despite Tamil Nadu Chief Minister M.K. Stalin's petition to Prime Minister Narendra Modi in February.
