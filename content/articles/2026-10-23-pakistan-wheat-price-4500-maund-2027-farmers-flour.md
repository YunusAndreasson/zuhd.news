Islamabad — Farmers get Rs4,500 per 40kg maund, Rs1,000 above last year.

Pakistan Kissan Ittehad demanded Rs5,000 and warned farmers may skip sowing wheat.

The prime minister's office cited rising production and fuel costs, and noted [Pakistan](country:PK) has imported wheat for two years.

Sindh has released government wheat at Rs12,500 per 100kg bag since 15 October to steady flour prices.

The statement does not say whether the state will buy wheat at that price.
