US strikes killed 3 Iranian officials but left the government standing. US spy chief Tulsi Gabbard told the Senate that Iran was "largely degraded" by Operation Epic Fury but its government "appears to be intact." Gabbard warned Iran would spend years rebuilding its missile and drone forces.
