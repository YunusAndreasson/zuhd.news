Doha — A Qatari gas tanker is crossing the Red Sea toward [Poland](country:PL).

At least 20 more cargoes have left through the Strait of Hormuz since early September.

One tanker from [Qatar](country:QA) transferred its cargo ship-to-ship in the Gulf of Oman, using hoses built for gas chilled to minus 162 C.

QatarEnergy restored September exports to about a third of prewar levels.

Polymarket traders put 93% on no Hormuz agreement, up 4 points in a week.
