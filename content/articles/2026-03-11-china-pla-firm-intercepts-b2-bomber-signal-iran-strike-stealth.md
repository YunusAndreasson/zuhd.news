A Chinese firm claims it tracked 4 US bombers. Jingan Technology — a PLA-linked firm in Hangzhou — says its system found that stealth aircraft hide from radar but still emit radio signals the system can intercept. If genuine, the capability would reshape US air power calculations across the Taiwan Strait and every future conflict zone.
