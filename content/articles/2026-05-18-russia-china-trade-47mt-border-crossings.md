Harbin — [Russia](country:RU)-[China](country:CN) border traffic hit 790,000 crossings in 2025, up 20%.

Tonnage grew only 3%; vehicle throughput rose 7× faster — reflecting new road corridors opened under sanction-era re-routing.

Carrier permits rose 46% to 217,000; Moscow and Beijing have not yet published 2026 corridor targets.
