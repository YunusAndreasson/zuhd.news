Rawalpindi — [Pakistan](country:PK)'s army said "everything that is necessary will be done" against terrorism from Afghanistan.

The spokesman named no groups, attacks or timeline when asked about Afghanistan.

Lt Gen Ahmed Sharif Chaudhry spoke at a briefing called to rebut Indian claims of killing a Pahalgam attack suspect.

The fight against terrorism "cannot be sacrificed at the altar of anyone's politics," Chaudhry said.

He declined to answer a question on the planned 4 October march on Islamabad by the opposition PTI party.
