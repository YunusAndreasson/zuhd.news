New Delhi — PDP won't join Sunday's rally without Article 370's return.

The party and AIP are boycotting the National Conference-led statehood protest at Jantar Mantar, demanding [India](country:IN) also free political prisoners.

Jailed MP Er Rashid says he'll walk barefoot to Parliament July 21, snubbing the rally too.
