New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s chief justice told Rahul Gandhi to file a petition.

The opposition wants Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar removed and the voter-roll revision halted.

Sources said he cited pending cases, ruled out administrative orders and offered to hear a petition Tuesday alongside existing revision cases.

The Election Commission and Modi's party deny manipulation, saying deletions stem from deaths, migration and untraceable voters.

Opposition parties march to the Supreme Court on Monday, gathering at Connaught Place at 1:30pm.
