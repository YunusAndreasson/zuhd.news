Bucharest — 2 crew died when a cargo ship sank.

[Romania](country:RO)'s coast guard and a helicopter rescued 11 crew inside its exclusive economic zone.

[Ukraine](country:UA)'s president says [Russia](country:RU) hit the Turkish-owned Royad Mammadov, carrying corn, with 2 drones.

Romania has named no cause, and Russia did not immediately comment.

Turkey and the UN are arranging new Russia-Ukraine talks on a Black Sea ceasefire.
