Moscow — Yuki Imai, a Hokkaido Shimbun reporter, faces up to 20 years for espionage.

[Russia](country:RU) has jailed foreign reporters on spy charges before, including Evan Gershkovich, sentenced to 16 years.

Russia's Federal Security Service says Imai gathered defence information for Japanese intelligence, and a Moscow court ordered his detention.

Imai stays jailed until 12 December while [Japan](country:JP) seeks "fair and impartial treatment" for him.
