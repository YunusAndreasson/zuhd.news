Mumbai — The train carried 360 containers at once.

Cheaper freight could pull cargo off trucks and onto [India](country:IN)'s rail network.

Double-stacking needs roughly 7.45 meters of overhead clearance, well above what most electrified lines carry.

The run covers only part of the Western Dedicated Freight Corridor toward Delhi.
