Israeli soldiers fired 910 bullets in under 6 minutes. Forensic Architecture, a UK research group, found they killed 15 medics in Tal al-Sultan last March, firing on marked aid vehicles before shooting survivors at close range. Satellite imagery confirmed the Israeli military bulldozed the wreckage; Israel's own inquiry recommended no prosecution.
