Japan — Switch 2 sales fell 41% to 2.67 million units.

Operating profit fell 16% to 73.9 billion yen, short of the 88 billion yen forecast.

Nintendo raised Switch 2 prices in the US, Canada and Japan as memory-chip costs surged, and buyers pulled back.

President Shuntaro Furukawa says Nintendo has enough memory for 16.5 million units and will not raise prices again.

Nintendo briefs investors Friday on how weaker Switch 2 profitability affects earnings.
