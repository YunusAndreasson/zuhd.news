Strait of Hormuz — Oil exports fell 42% below prewar levels, Kpler says.

Kpler said flows had recovered to prewar levels in September, about 15.5 million barrels a day.

The 7-day average hit 9.9 million barrels a day on 7 October as Kpler recorded intensified attacks on vessels.

Energy analyst Rory Johnston says [Iran](country:IR)'s shipping attacks cut flow 3-4 million barrels a day after 24 September.

Brent crude stood at $125.40 on 6 October, up 10.1% in 7 days.
