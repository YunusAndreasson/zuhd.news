Lahore — Police promised to treat a minister's relative like "any other criminal."

Four of five suspects arrested over the alleged abduction and assault of two foreign women were remanded for five days; one is a close relative of a senior political figure.

The pledge itself signals the impunity [Pakistan](country:PK)'s police chief felt he had to publicly deny.
