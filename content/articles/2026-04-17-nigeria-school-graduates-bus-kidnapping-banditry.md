Otukpo — Armed men seized 18 graduates on a highway. Benue State sits in Nigeria's southeast, where banditry now spreads despite 20 years of counter-terrorism and US troops deployed since December. Security forces launched a search operation; no group has claimed the abduction and the students' location is unknown.
