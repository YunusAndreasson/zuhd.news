Islamabad — Deputy PM Ishaq Dar privately assured the Pakistan People's Party its tax proposals would enter the budget before parliament sees a draft.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s coalition negotiates fiscal terms through pre-budget deals rather than debate, constrained by IMF targets.

A final coalition meeting is set Monday; the budget is presented Wednesday.
