Johannesburg — Petrol rises R1.34 a litre on Tuesday.

The increase sets minibus-taxi fares, the commute cost for South Africans without cars.

[South Africa](country:ZA)'s fuel-price formula converts global oil costs and the exchange rate directly into pump prices.

The state's fuel-price deficit tops R9.5 billion, triggering a higher levy from Tuesday.
