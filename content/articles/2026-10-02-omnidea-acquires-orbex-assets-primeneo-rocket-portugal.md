Forres — [Portugal](country:PT)'s Omnidea bought Orbex's rocket designs and brand.

Orbex collapsed in February, and its Prime launcher now passes to a Portuguese owner.

Omnidea is simplifying Prime into PrimeNEO for easier manufacture, and is dropping Orbex's medium-lift Proxima rocket.

Omnidea says it is too early for a launch date or a choice between Scotland and the Azores.

The space agencies that backed Orbex's Prime bid are in talks over whether earmarked funding transfers.
