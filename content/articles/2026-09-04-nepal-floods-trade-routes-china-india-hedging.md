Kathmandu — The August 26 floods washed away border trade routes.

[Nepal](country:NP)'s thin trade link to [China](country:CN) just got thinner, pushing it back onto [India](country:IN)'s ports.

A landlocked state's foreign policy runs on which mountain passes stay open, and the monsoon closed several.

Importers now face longer routes as global food prices sit near a four-year high.
