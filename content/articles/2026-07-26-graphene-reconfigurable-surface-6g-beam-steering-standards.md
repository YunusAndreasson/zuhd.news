London — A graphene panel bends 6G signals around walls.

The prototype tunes graphene's conductivity by voltage to steer a beam over 266-332 GHz, the study reports, with no moving parts.

It is a bench-tested unit cell, not a deployed panel, while 6G's standards and patents are being written.
