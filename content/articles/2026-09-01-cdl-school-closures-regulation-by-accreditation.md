Washington — The [US](country:US) ordered 110 truck-driving schools closed immediately.

About 5,000 enrolled students lose their licensing pathway without individual findings against them.

The department cited failed English-proficiency tests and decertified the schools directly, ending enrollment for students who might have passed.

Officials say another 160 schools face closure for noncompliance next.
