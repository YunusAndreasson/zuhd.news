Seattle — Masked immigration agents arrested 2 people on a boarding jet bridge.

Immigration and Customs Enforcement gave Alaska Airlines no notice, and Flight 1306 left nearly 3 hours late.

The agency says it sought Ricardo Larrondo Mendoza for a visa overstay and will charge Sofia Hernandez Mejia with assaulting an officer.

"ICE is completely and totally out of control," said Washington Governor Bob Ferguson.

Ferguson is investigating whether the agents broke the state law on law-enforcement face coverings that he signed in March.
