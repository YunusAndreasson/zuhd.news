Owerri — Soldiers now offer ₦30 million for a man they once said they caught.

Residents told the threat had ended now learn the military's own capture claim was wrong.

The Army announced Ifeanyi Eze's capture in October, then he resurfaced on video in January, alive and free.

The [Nigerian](country:NG) Army has not said how the false claim was verified before release.
