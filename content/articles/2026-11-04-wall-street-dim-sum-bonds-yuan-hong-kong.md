Beijing — [China](country:CN)'s trade surplus hit a record $938.1 billion through September.

Wall Street firms are now borrowing yuan in Hong Kong's offshore dim sum bond market.

The central bank raises the foreign-exchange reserve ratio 2 points to 6% on 15 November, locking up $24 billion, Citic Securities estimates.

Moody's Ratings says the measure should moderate, not reverse, the yuan's rise.

Exporters settle payables, wages and taxes in December, when yuan gains hurt most.
