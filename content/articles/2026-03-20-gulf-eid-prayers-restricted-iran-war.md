3 Gulf states banned outdoor Eid al-Fitr prayers this Friday. Qatar, Kuwait and the UAE cited Iran war tensions for the bans, affecting governments that received $16 billion in US arms this month. Authorities offered no timeline, leaving 60 million Gulf residents under the restrictions through the 3-day holiday.
