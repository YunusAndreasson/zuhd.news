Surab — This town has been under curfew since Monday.

Nightly restrictions block hospital access for residents across the district.

A colonial-era law lets [Pakistan](country:PK)'s district magistrates ban movement by notification alone, with no court order.

The Surab curfew, also imposed in South Waziristan and Tank, runs until August 15.
