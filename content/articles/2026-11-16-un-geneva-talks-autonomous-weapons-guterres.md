Geneva — 128 countries debate opening talks on killer-robot rules.

Guterres wants binding rules, including clear prohibitions, on weapons that kill without human intervention.

The convention needs full consensus, so a handful of states with the technology can block any decision.

[Russia](country:RU)'s representative forced a suspension by objecting to observers, which campaigners called an attempt to dodge criticism over Ukraine.

The [US](country:US) proposes a 5-year non-binding process, while the Red Cross and NGOs want binding rules in 1 year.
