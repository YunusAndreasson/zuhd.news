Suicide bombers killed several people in Maiduguri, Nigeria. Boko Haram and its rival the Islamic State's West Africa Province have traded escalating attacks in Nigeria's northeast since splitting in 2021, targeting civilians to prove territorial control. Maiduguri residents waited Monday night to learn which group carried out the attack and whether more were planned.
