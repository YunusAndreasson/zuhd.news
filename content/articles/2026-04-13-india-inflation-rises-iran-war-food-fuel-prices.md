New Delhi — Iran war raised India's March inflation to 3.4%. Hormuz disruption pushed oil prices higher, lifting transport costs and food prices — India's food inflation reached 3.87% in March, up from 3.47% in February. India's CPI stays below RBI's 4% target, but if oil holds, the bank's rate-cut plans stall.
