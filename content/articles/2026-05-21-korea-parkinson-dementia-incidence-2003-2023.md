Seoul — Korean dementia nearly tripled over 21 years but has fallen since 2018, a study shows.

A national insurance dataset covering nearly all of [South Korea](country:KR)'s population shows Parkinson's incidence declined steadily across the same period.

The researchers called for equitable health strategies targeting rural and low-income groups, who faced the worst survival outcomes.
