New York — Digital credit suffered its sharpest selloff ever.

Leveraged investors hit margin calls and were forced to sell, compounding the drop, Strive Asset Management's CEO said.

STRC recovered to $89 and SATA to $97 by session's end, but the underlying leverage concentration remains.
