Dallas — Colossal claimed a synthetic eggshell for moa embryos.

No hatching data were released; experts say the yolk, a single giant cell, cannot be enlarged without rupturing.

Independent researchers say the likely outcome is a gene-edited emu marketed as moa — which [New Zealand](country:NZ) Māori communities widely oppose.
