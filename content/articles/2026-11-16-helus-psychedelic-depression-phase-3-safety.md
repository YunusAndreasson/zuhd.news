United States — Psychedelic drops depression scores 19.5 points, placebo 11.9.

Helus Pharma reported the Phase 3 results Monday, and a number of serious adverse events raise safety questions.

At 12 weeks the gap held: 16.9 points against 9.9 on placebo, with patients also on standard antidepressants.

The company announced the results itself, and no peer-reviewed paper has yet confirmed them.
