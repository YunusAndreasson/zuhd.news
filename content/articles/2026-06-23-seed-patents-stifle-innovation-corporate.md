Washington — 2 firms control 70% of [US](country:US) corn and soy seed.

The Justice Department said seed patents block competition; the Agriculture Department found prices rose 463% since 1990, while crop prices rose only 56%.

A pending court ruling on whether deposited patent sequences can be independently analyzed could open research to competitors.
