Pyongyang — [North Korea](country:KP) says a submarine fired two types of cruise missiles.

If the Hero Kim Kun Ok launched them, it would be that boat's first confirmed weapons trial.

[South Korea](country:KR)'s Joint Chiefs of Staff tracked both launches, one from a vertical launch system, one from a torpedo tube.

State media did not name the submarine or give the missiles' ranges, so the vessel's role is unconfirmed.

Pyongyang has not said when the submarine enters service.
