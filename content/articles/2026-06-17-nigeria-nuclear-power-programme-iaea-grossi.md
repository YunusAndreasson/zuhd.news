Abuja — [Nigeria](country:NG) exploring nuclear power, IAEA chief Grossi confirmed.

Nigeria, a major oil exporter with chronic grid failures, has not yet committed; Grossi called it 'a pre-decision to explore the alternative.'

The IAEA's 2024-2029 cooperation plan with Nigeria covers energy planning; Grossi gave no timeline for a formal decision.
