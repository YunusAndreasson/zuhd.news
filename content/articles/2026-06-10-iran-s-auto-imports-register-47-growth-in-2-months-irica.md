Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) imported 4,538 cars in 2 months via a [Russia](country:RU)-led trade corridor, customs data show.

A 2025 free-trade deal with the Eurasian Economic Union opened the route; customs figures show a 47% growth in auto imports year-on-year.

Russia-linked trade is now Tehran's main channel around Western export controls.
