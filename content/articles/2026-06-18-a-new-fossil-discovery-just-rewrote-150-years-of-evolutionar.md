Chicago — Early land vertebrates did not metamorphose like tadpoles.

Field Museum researchers found no aquatic larval phase in 310M-year-old tetrapod hatchlings, falsifying a 150-year assumption about the fin-to-limb transition.

The study, published in Science, does not explain how early tetrapods managed land transition without a larval stage.
