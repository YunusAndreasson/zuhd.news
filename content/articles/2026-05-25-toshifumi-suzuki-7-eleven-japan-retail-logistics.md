Tokyo — 7-Eleven Japan founder Toshifumi Suzuki died at 93.

Suzuki licensed the failing [US](country:US) chain in 1973, rebuilt it on fresh-food logistics, then bought the bankrupt American parent in 1990.

The company repelled a [Canada](country:CA)-based Couche-Tard takeover bid in 2025 and runs 85,000 stores.
