Bandar Abbas — Iran's Revolutionary Guards fired on tanker Jag Arnav. The ship carried 2 million barrels of crude; Iran had exempted India from its Hormuz shipping blockade, and the attack directly contradicts that status. India's foreign ministry summoned Iran's envoy; the Indian Navy has no vessel in the strait to respond.
