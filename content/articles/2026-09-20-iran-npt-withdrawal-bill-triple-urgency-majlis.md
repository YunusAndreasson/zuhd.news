Tehran — A Non-Proliferation Treaty exit bill awaits the speaker.

Withdrawal would end [Iran](country:IR)'s treaty inspection obligations.

Lawmaker Hossein Ali Haji Deligani cited the International Atomic Energy Agency board's referral of Iran's file to the Security Council.

No vote has occurred, and the speaker has not scheduled an open session.
