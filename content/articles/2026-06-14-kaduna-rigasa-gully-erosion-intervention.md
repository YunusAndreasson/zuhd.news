Kaduna — [Nigeria](country:NG)'s Rigasa gullies get a N34 billion fix.

Unmanaged stormwater carved a 17-kilometre gully through urban-poor Kaduna communities, destroying homes and farmland for 30 years without state intervention.

Groundbreaking is expected within 2 weeks; the 18-month project is projected to protect 2 million residents.
