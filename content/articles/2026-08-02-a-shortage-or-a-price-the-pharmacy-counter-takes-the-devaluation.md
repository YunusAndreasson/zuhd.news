Cairo — A drug pricing freeze becomes an empty shelf.

[Egypt](country:EG)'s pound now floats daily against the dollar; drug prices are fixed by decree, unchanged since 2012's threshold rule.

The Drug Authority is studying a formula tying prices to the exchange rate and inflation, officials said.
