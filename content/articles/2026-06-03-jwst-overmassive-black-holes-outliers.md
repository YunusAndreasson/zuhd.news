Baltimore — James Webb Space Telescope found black holes too massive to exist.

Models predict insufficient growth time for black holes this massive at 2 billion years old — researchers now ask if JWST simply over-sampled rare statistical outliers.

If these are ordinary tail events, early-universe cosmology needs no new physics to account for them.
