Beirut — 4,387 dead in [Lebanon](country:LB), Russian state news agency TASS reported.

The health ministry counts 12,424 wounded since 2 March, and at least 1 civilian died Sunday.

Israeli troops continue operations against Hezbollah in the south, where airstrikes hit Kfar Tibnit and Tallousah in Nabatieh province on Sunday.

Artillery shelled Beit Lif, Majal Zoun and Yater, and the ministry has no casualty reports yet.
