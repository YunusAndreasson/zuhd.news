114,410 crypto wallets lost money after Argentina's president promoted a token. On 14 February 2025, President Javier Milei posted a link to the $Libra token on X, then deleted it as the price spiked and collapsed within hours. A parliamentary commission concluded the promotion used the authority of the presidential office, while a small number of wallets posted multimillion-dollar gains. Milei dismissed the losses, comparing investors to "casino gamblers" and denying any role in developing the project. Criminal investigations continue in Argentina alongside a class-action lawsuit in New York, with no charges filed against Milei himself.
