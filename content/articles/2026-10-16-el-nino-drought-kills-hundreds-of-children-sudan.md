Darfur — Hundreds are dead, mostly children, a UN-backed monitor said.

Almost 29 million people, 62% of [Sudan](country:SD)'s population, are eating less or going hungry.

El Niño makes air sink over the Sahel, cutting rain, and 90% of Sudan's farming depends on rain.

"The gap means right now we are unable to keep people alive," said Manon Radosta of CARE.

Families cannot store food from a spotty harvest before the rains return in June.
