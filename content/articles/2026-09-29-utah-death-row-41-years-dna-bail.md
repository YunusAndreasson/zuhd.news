Provo — Douglas Stewart Carter spent 41 years on death row before bail on Monday.

A state kept a man awaiting execution for decades on a confession he says was coerced.

DNA testing this month excluded Carter from blood on the door and genetic material on the knife handle, prosecutors said.

Eva Olesen's daughter-in-law told the bond hearing the family wanted him jailed, saying a DNA mismatch "doesn't mean he wasn't there."

Carter's attorney is seeking dismissal ahead of the retrial Utah's Supreme Court ordered last year.
