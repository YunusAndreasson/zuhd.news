Ottawa — Canada barred travelers who visited [Congo](country:CD).

The WHO opposes such bans as counterproductive amid a WHO-tracked outbreak of over 2,100 cases and 828 deaths.

The measure takes effect Monday; returning citizens face quarantine instead of a ban.
