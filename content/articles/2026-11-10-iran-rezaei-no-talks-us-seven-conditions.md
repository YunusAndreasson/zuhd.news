Tehran — [Iran](country:IR) has 7 conditions and no answer from Washington.

The Strait of Hormuz stays closed until the [US](country:US) meets them, Iran says.

Mohsen Rezaei, Iran's top security official, told Al-Mayadeen the US has not given the clear response Tehran requested.

After meeting mediator [Qatar](country:QA) on Monday, Donald Trump played down renewed war but still rejected Iran's truce plan.

Rezaei said Washington could still respond, but gave no date.
