Kirillovka — Eight people, including two children, were killed overnight.

Russian-installed governor Yevgeny Balitsky said Ukrainian forces struck tourist centers on the occupied Azov coast; the toll and the target's civilian character are uncorroborated.

[Russia](country:RU)'s foreign ministry says it will compile evidence for international bodies whose jurisdiction it rejects elsewhere.
