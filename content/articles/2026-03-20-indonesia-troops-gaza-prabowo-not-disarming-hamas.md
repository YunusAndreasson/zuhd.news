Indonesia offered 8,000 peacekeepers, refusing to disarm Hamas. President Prabowo invoked national caveats — each country's right under peacekeeping rules to refuse orders conflicting with its own principles — barring any military action against Hamas. The deployment awaits a UN mandate and consent from 7 Muslim-majority states, with no timeline set.
