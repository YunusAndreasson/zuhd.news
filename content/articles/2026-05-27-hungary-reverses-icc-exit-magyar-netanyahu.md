Budapest — [Hungary](country:HU) killed its ICC exit Wednesday, 133-37.

Orbán launched the withdrawal in April 2025 after the ICC issued a war crimes warrant for [Israel](country:IL)'s Netanyahu.

New PM Magyar says [Hungary](country:HU) will now execute all ICC warrants, including against Netanyahu.
