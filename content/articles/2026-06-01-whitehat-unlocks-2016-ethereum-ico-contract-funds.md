New York — 9-year-old Ethereum bug disgorged $2 million Monday.

Ethereum contracts are immutable; 0xflorent found an integer-overflow flaw exploitable via the contract's own admin key, which HongCoin's team signed to execute.

48 original investors are eligible to reclaim; 2 have so far claimed 96.5 ETH worth $193,000, the researcher said.
