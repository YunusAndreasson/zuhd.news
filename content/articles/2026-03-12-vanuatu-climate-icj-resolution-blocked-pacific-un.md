The US is lobbying against Vanuatu's UN climate resolution. The State Department told embassies it poses "a major threat to US industry," despite 132 co-sponsoring countries. Vanuatu's climate minister Ralph Regenvanu says a successful vote would create binding international legal obligations for fossil fuel phase-out.
