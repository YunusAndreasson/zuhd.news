Aba — Alaoji's 450MW plant back online after 3-year shutdown.

The Niger Delta Power Holding Company cleared TotalEnergies debts from a metering dispute that had blocked gas supply since 2023.

375MW is now dispatchable; the company said the plant could eventually expand to 1,100MW.
