Washington — Trump wants Canada to pay for its own smoke.

[Canada](country:CA)'s wildfires pushed hazardous air from Minnesota to North Carolina; Trump called it "willful negligence" and demanded new tariffs on top of existing ones over forest management.

Republican lawmakers are pushing a bill setting a 25% import fee tied to the smoke.
