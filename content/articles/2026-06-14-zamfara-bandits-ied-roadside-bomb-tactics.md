Gusau — Bandits planted a roadside bomb in Zamfara.

North-west [Nigeria](country:NG)'s bandit gangs, long defined by kidnapping and cattle raids, are now deploying improvised explosive devices against commuters.

Police destroyed the device before detonation; the Explosive Ordnance Disposal Unit is investigating who planted it.
