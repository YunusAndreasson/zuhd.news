Tokyo — Olivine shatters as it transforms 500 km underground. Pressure forces olivine into denser poirierite in subducting slabs; the transformation fractures rather than compresses, generating quakes at anomalous depth. Pacific Rim seismic models can now incorporate deep-focus rupture; the highest-risk zones are Indonesia and Japan.
