London — [Britain](country:GB)'s inflation will peak at 4.9%, its central bank forecasts.

The Bank of England lifted Bank Rate to 4%, its first rise since August 2023.

Energy costs from the [Iran](country:IR) war drove an 8-1 vote, with one member demanding an immediate 4.25%.

Governor Andrew Bailey said the rise "shouldn't be seen as the first in an inevitable long sequence of hikes."

The Monetary Policy Committee votes again on 17 December.
