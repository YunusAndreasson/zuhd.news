South Africa's energy regulator Nersa approved an 8.76% electricity price increase from April 1 — higher than the 5.36% initially announced after the regulator corrected calculation errors. Municipalities will add a further 9.01% from July, meaning households that buy power through local councils face compounding increases across a single year. For the millions of South Africans on prepaid meters in townships, where every rand is metered in advance, both rounds of increases take effect before winter.
