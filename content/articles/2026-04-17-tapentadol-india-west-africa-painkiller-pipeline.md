Freetown — 320 million tapentadol pills reached West Africa. Licensed Indian manufacturers exploited regulatory arbitrage: after India tightened tramadol controls in 2018, traffic shifted to tapentadol, which remains unscheduled globally. India's drug regulator has not acted, and Sierra Leone declared a national drug emergency in 2024.
