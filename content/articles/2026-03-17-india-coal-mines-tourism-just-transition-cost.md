Coal India converts exhausted mines into tourist lakes. Pannelal Rajak, a displaced miner, guards one such lake built on his former land without any guarantee his community will share the tourism income. India has hundreds of similar sites to repurpose and no national framework to distribute proceeds to displaced communities.
