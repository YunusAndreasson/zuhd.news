Washington — Patrick Witt is the 3rd White House crypto adviser out.

No successor is named while regulators keep writing crypto rules.

Witt ran the President's Council of Advisors for Digital Assets, the White House's liaison on crypto legislation.

A White House official said crypto and the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act remain a focus for President Donald Trump.

If Democrats flip the House in next month's midterms, 2027 crypto legislation faces a potentially hostile chamber.
