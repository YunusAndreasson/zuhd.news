Moscow — Russia drafted a Moscow crypto ban from July.

65 Moscow-area data centers drawing 734 MW triggered the ban, [Russia](country:RU)'s Energy Ministry said.

The ban would run through 2032, extending restrictions that already cover North Caucasus, Siberia, and occupied Ukrainian territories.
