Oleshky — Up to 2,000 civilians trapped by mines and drones get a truce.

[Russia](country:RU) and [Ukraine](country:UA) halted fighting around the town, and Russia paused strikes on Kherson across the river.

[US](country:US) First Lady Melania Trump brokered the local truce with Pope Leo's involvement, and Russia's Defence Ministry put the start at 01:00 Greenwich Mean Time.

The truce runs through Saturday, and doctors, food and water arrive before evacuation corridors open.
