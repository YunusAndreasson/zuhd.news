United States — Anyone with a link could open 43,601 Grok Build apps.

Security firm Claroc found medical exams, a defense contractor's roster and 24 SpaceXSI internal apps among them.

Grok Build puts the random words before the domain, so public domain-name records list every link.

Cursor says apps are private by default, but Claroc's Asaf Aprozper found links default to anyone-with-the-link.

Claroc is notifying affected companies, and SpaceXSI has not said whether it will notify customers.
