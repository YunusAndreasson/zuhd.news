Tallahassee — One officer's saved login opened Florida's driver database.

ShinyHunters claims the breach, extending its pattern of credential-based mass theft.

Thousands of officers can reach the Department of Motor Vehicles database. One weak device exposes it all.

Florida has not said which records were exposed or whether multifactor login was required.
