Kyiv — One launcher now fires four missile types.

It lets [Ukraine](country:UA) fire whatever missile stock its allies happen to send.

The open-architecture system already fires Soviet R-73s and US-made AIM-9 and ASRAAM missiles from one launcher.

Developers are still testing compatibility with the Soviet-era R-60 missile before wider production.
