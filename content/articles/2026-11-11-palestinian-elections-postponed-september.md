West Bank — Palestinians will not vote before September 2027.

The delay extends more than 20 years without national elections.

Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas's 10 October decree cites occupation obstacles and demands voting access in Jerusalem, the West Bank and Gaza.

Fadi al-Sheikh Youssef, who heads a youth-led electoral list, called the delay an unjust denial of Palestinians' democratic right.

The decree promises a national dialogue, and Hamas says it is ready to join it.
