West African monitors reached the Guinea-Sierra Leone border Friday. The Economic Community of West African States sent the mission after Guinea's 2021 military junta grew assertive along borders disputed since wars that left hundreds of thousands dead. West Africa's security apparatus is focused on Sahel coups, leaving the border without a permanent monitoring mission.
