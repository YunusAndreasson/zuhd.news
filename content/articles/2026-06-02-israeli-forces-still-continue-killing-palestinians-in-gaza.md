Gaza — 933 Palestinians killed since the October ceasefire began, the Gaza health ministry said.

[Israel](country:IL) has killed 72,942 Palestinians since October 2023, the ministry said; 781 more bodies have been recovered from rubble since the truce took effect.

Rescue teams remain unable to reach victims still trapped under rubble, the ministry said.
