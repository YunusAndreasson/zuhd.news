Geneva — Industry-designed substitutes are eroding the ozone treaty. The Montreal Protocol bans ozone-depleting chemicals by class; industry engineered substitutes outside those classes that still deplete ozone. Scientists have documented the loophole's effect; closing it requires consensus from all 197 treaty parties.
