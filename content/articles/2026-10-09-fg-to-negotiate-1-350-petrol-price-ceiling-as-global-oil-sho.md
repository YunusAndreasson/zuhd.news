Abuja — Petrol rose from ₦830 to ₦1,400 a litre since February.

Finance minister Taiwo Oyedele says Brent trades above $100, almost 50% over its pre-war level.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s government is negotiating a ₦1,350-a-litre ceiling on landing cost, with refiners and importers carrying any excess and recovering it later.

Oyedele says the arrangement is neither a subsidy nor a price control, and figures would be published monthly.

The government is also weighing an excess profit tax on energy operators and forward crude sales to domestic refineries.
