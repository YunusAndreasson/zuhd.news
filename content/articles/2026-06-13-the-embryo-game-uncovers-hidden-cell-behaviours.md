London — Researchers modeled embryo cells as strategic agents.

The study applies game theory to early cell divisions, revealing how cells coordinate during first cleavages — patterns invisible to standard imaging alone.

The manuscript awaits final publication; the approach could improve synthetic embryo design, the authors say.
