Ramallah — [Israel](country:IL) erected a new checkpoint near Ni'lin.

The occupied Palestinian territory now holds more than 916 military checkpoints and gates, 243 of them erected since October 2023.

Soldiers checked residents' identification and caused severe traffic congestion; no date for the checkpoint's removal has been set.
