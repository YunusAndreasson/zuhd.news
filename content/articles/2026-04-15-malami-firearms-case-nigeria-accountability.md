Abuja — Nigeria's ex-justice minister is on trial. Malami, Buhari's attorney-general for 8 years, pleaded not guilty to 5 firearms counts after weapons were seized at his Kebbi home. Trial begins May 26; Malami, now aligned with a rival party, calls the prosecution politically motivated.
