India signed an EU free trade deal in January. India wants European technology and market access while the EU, decoupling from Russia and a volatile Washington, needs India's 1.4 billion consumers. The 2-day visit will test whether New Delhi can deepen trade ties with Brussels without condemning the US-Israel war.
