Vienna — OPEC+ ministers meet Sunday to check members' compliance with quotas.

Russian state news agency TASS reported the committee cannot set output but can recommend it.

The agreement caps participants at 36.206 million barrels a day, yet Hormuz traffic ran 44% below normal as of 27 September.

Seven members, including [Russia](country:RU) and [Saudi Arabia](country:SA), also meet Sunday, as they do monthly.
