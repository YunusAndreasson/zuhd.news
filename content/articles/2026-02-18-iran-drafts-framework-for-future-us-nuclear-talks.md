Iran and the US agreed on nuclear "guiding principles." Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi confirmed Tehran is drafting a framework for future rounds after the second session of indirect talks in Geneva. Iran insists on sanctions relief before any concessions, leaving enrichment as the unresolved divide.
