Islamabad — Rs114 of every petrol litre is tax, not crude cost.

Petrol hits Rs391.22 a litre as [Pakistan](country:PK) collects revenue on a war-driven price shock.

A promised fuel discount for motorbikes and rickshaws requires dealers to sell below cost first and wait to be repaid.

Dealers say no reimbursement mechanism yet exists for Shehbaz Sharif's relief scheme.
