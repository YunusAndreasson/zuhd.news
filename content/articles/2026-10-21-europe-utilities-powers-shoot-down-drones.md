Brétigny-sur-Orge — [France](country:FR) lets approved infrastructure operators shoot down drones.

Eurelectric's secretary general Kristian Ruby calls heating outages this winter from hybrid attacks a possibility.

France plans to give its state power utility and railway a catalogue of approved jammers and interceptor drones.

Bringing down a drone could injure people or damage property, and the right to order a response remains legally unclear.

A senior French official says vital sites should be equipped by 2027.
