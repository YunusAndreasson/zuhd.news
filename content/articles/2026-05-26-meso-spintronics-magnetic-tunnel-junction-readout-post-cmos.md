London — Spintronics readout improved 100-fold, enabling circuit integration.

A magnetic tunnel junction boosts spin-orbit readout voltage 100-fold to 1.5 mV — the threshold where magneto-electric logic can cascade like transistors.

The approach uses roughly 7× less energy than silicon transistors; chip-scale cascade integration is the remaining test.
