Riyadh — [Saudi Arabia](country:SA)'s output sank to a record 6.24 million barrels daily.

Exports still rose 7.9% in August to 4.451 million barrels a day, official data show.

Refinery throughput fell 181,000 barrels a day, and UBS analyst Giovanni Staunovo says terminals on both coasts carried the crude.

Houthi forces attacked Aramco's Dhahran headquarters last week, causing a limited fire, the energy ministry said.

Staunovo expects the export recovery to have continued through September and October.
