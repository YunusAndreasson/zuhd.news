Brazil filed its Paris pledge with COP30 8 months away. The submission tests whether President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva's reversal of Bolsonaro-era Amazon deforestation is durable policy, as the Iran war drives a global fossil fuel surge against it. Brazil hosts COP30 in Belém in November, and its NDC credibility is the summit's defining question.
