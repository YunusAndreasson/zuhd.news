Canberra — AI risks extracting Indigenous knowledge again.

As [Australia](country:AU) marks 50 years of NAIDOC Week, researchers warn AI could ingest Indigenous knowledge without consent, credit or return.

Researchers want data sovereignty and consent built into model pipelines, not bolted on later.
