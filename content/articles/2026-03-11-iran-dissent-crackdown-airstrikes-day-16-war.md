Iran arrested dozens accused of espionage since the war began. Police Chief Ahmadreza Radan warned dissidents would be treated as enemy combatants after Trump publicly called for Iranians to overthrow the government. The Intelligence Ministry detained foreign nationals as well; no charge details or consular access have been confirmed.
