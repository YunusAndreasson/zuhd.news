A pre-Inca kingdom traded live parrots across the Andes. DNA from feathers in a noble's tomb showed the birds were born wild in the Amazon, then transported alive to Peru's coast. Researchers say the Inca roads may have followed Ychsma trade routes centuries older than the empire.
