Berlin — Authorities found an explosive-laden drone at Leipzig/Halle Airport.

[Germany](country:DE) calls it part of daily foreign sabotage, espionage and cyberattacks.

The interior ministry plans to double its anti-drone unit to 300 experts and add four more bases.

Investigators have not said who sent the drone, as [Russia](country:RU) denies involvement.
