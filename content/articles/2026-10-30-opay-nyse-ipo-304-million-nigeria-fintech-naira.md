Nigeria — OPay raised $304 million in a New York listing.

It is the first major Nigeria-focused fintech listed on a US exchange.

It sold 16 million American Depositary Shares at $19, each representing 10 ordinary shares, for a market value above $4 billion.

The offering closes 2 November, with proceeds earmarked for technology and expansion in Nigeria and other African markets.
