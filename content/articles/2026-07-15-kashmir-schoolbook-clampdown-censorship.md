Srinagar — Every school must now screen its books.

Authorities ordered schools across Indian-administered Kashmir to flag "objectionable" material, six years after Delhi revoked the region's semi-autonomous status.

Academics and opposition parties call it an attempt to erase Kashmir's history from classrooms.
