The Pentagon declared Anthropic a "supply chain risk." The designation came after Anthropic refused a military demand to strip safety restrictions from its Claude AI models. Anthropic has threatened to sue; if the ruling holds, federal contractors could be barred from using Claude, cutting the company off from government revenue.
