Moscow — Cardinal Zuppi says he met Ukrainian prisoners in a Russian detention facility.

Russian state news agency TASS reported Zuppi hopes the visit speeds prisoner exchanges between [Russia](country:RU) and [Ukraine](country:UA).

He also met presidential aide Yury Ushakov and both ombudswomen, discussing the missing, civilian detainees and family reunifications, TASS reported.

TASS reported no date for the next exchange.
