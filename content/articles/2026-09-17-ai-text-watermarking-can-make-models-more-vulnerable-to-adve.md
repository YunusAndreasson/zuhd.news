San Francisco — A safety watermark makes AI easier to jailbreak.

Researchers found watermarked models more often obey harmful prompts under prompt-injection attacks.

SynthID-Text nudges word choice with a secret key so output can be traced, a bias attackers can learn to exploit.

Anthropic plans SynthID-Text for future Claude models, though the tests covered other systems, not Claude.
