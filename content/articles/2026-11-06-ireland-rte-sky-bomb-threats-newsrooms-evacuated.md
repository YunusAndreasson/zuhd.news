Dublin — RTÉ evacuated its newsroom after a bomb threat Friday.

[Ireland](country:IE) hosts 50 European leaders, including Volodymyr Zelenskyy, for two summits a week later.

The email, signed Mikhail Andropov, claims the same author as this week's airline hoaxes, and nobody has verified that.

Taoiseach Micheál Martin says it is unclear whether individuals or a co-ordinated attack sent the threats.

Martin vowed to identify those responsible, and says Ireland can secure the summits with its largest security operation.
