Guangxi — Homo erectus outlasted its known mainland record by 60,000 years.

Four teeth from southern [China](country:CN) place it in dense rainforest, once thought sapiens-only.

Researchers writing in Nature dated the cave breccia to 324,000 to 184,000 years ago using luminescence dating and uranium isotopes.

Porcupines probably carried the teeth into the caves, from within an area under 100 square kilometres.

A ninth tooth from Baxian Cave probably belonged to a Denisovan, though its proteins match no known sequence.
