Belgian diplomat Davignon, 93, faces trial for Lumumba's murder. Belgium's parliament found the state "morally responsible" for the 1961 killing of Patrice Lumumba, Congo's first prime minister, but charged no individual until his family filed suit in 2011. If convicted of war crimes, Davignon would be the first Belgian official jailed for a colonial killing.
