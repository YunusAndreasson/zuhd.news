Brasília — [Brazil](country:BR)'s Senate gave child sexual abuse victims 20 years to sue.

The bill grew from reporting on exploitation allegations against Casas Bahia founder Samuel Klein.

The 20 years run from the victim's 18th birthday, so suits can be filed until age 38, for civil damages only.

President Lula has 15 working days from receipt to sign it.
