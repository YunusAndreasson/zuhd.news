Morocco's avocado exports nearly doubled to 141,000 tonnes in 2025. Morocco's proximity to Europe and stronger port infrastructure gave it a logistics edge East African farms couldn't match despite similar output. Kenyan and Tanzanian farmers who built avocado plantations for European markets now face a structural disadvantage they cannot easily reverse.
