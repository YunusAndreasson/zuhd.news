Beijing — China approved its first brain-computer interface for paralysis. Neuracle's NEO sits on the dura mater rather than penetrating cortex; 1 trial patient recovered bilateral hand function after 9 months. China is now a parallel BCI regulatory track; Neuracle and Tsinghua must next demonstrate safety at scale.
