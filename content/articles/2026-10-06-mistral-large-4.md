Paris — Mistral says Large 4 has 1 trillion parameters, 49 billion active.

Mistral claims it significantly outperforms any open-weight model developed in the [US](country:US) or Europe.

It was trained on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in Mistral's own European datacentres, which also serve the preview API.

Those performance claims are Mistral's own, and no independent evaluation has been reported.

Mistral publishes the weights by the end of October, red-teaming it with vetted partners and state authorities until then.
