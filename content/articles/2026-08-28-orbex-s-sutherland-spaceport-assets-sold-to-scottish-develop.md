Sutherland — A Scottish agency now owns the launch site.

Public money built it, and public money just bought it back.

Orbex collapsed into administration after a rescue deal fell through, and liquidators then sold the site's assets.

Highlands and Islands Enterprise has not said whether any rocket will ever launch there.
