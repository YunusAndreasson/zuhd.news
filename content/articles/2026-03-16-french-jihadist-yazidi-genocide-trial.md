France opened its first Yazidi genocide trial Monday. Sabri Essid is presumed dead since 2018 and faces an in-absentia trial for buying Yazidi women as slaves at ISIS markets in Iraq. 3 Yazidi survivors are set to testify before the trial closes Friday in what UN investigators have classified as genocide.
