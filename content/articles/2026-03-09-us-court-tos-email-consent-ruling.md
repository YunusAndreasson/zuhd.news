An unread terms of service email still binds users, a US court ruled. Tile, a tracking device company, added mandatory arbitration to its terms by email in 2023; both plaintiffs — stalking victims suing Tile for enabling their attackers — continued using the app, which the Ninth Circuit counted as binding consent. The unpublished ruling extends a judicial pattern treating continued app use as acceptance of any terms an email notification contained.
