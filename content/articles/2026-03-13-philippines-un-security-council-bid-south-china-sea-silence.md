Manila seeks a UN Security Council seat without mentioning China. Philippine President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. has not raised Manila's 2016 legal ruling invalidating China's South China Sea claims before the General Assembly. The Assembly votes on council seats in June, and analysts say the diplomatic silence may outlast the campaign.
