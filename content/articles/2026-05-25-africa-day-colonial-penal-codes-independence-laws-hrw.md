Johannesburg — HRW found [Mali](country:ML) and [Ghana](country:GH) expanded colonial criminal codes post-independence.

Independence transferred sovereignty but not capacity to rewrite penal codes; HRW's Africa Day report documents colonial laws active across most of Africa.

Waves of arrests continue; no African Union mechanism compels member states to repeal inherited colonial law.
