Ninh Thuan — [Vietnam](country:VN)'s nuclear revival is displacing 2,000 farming families. The government is closing Ninh Thuan snail farms for a plant promised in 2009, shelved in 2016, and reactivated in 2025. A resettlement site broke ground in January but construction has not started and no date has been given.
