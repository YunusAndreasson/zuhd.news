Hangzhou — Nineteen family members described three distinct grief phases.

Most caregivers get no formal support for a burden clinical records don't capture.

Researchers tracked caregivers across two Zhejiang hospitals, tracing memory, present strain and imagined future loss.

The authors call for support built around caregivers' shifting sense of time, not just symptoms.
