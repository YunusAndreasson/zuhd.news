Santa Clara — Intel beat revenue guidance and still lost 23% after hours.

CEO Lip-Bu Tan forecasts the personal computer chip market will shrink 12% next year.

Tan blamed memory prices for computer makers cutting orders, while Intel lacks wafers and substrates for server demand.

"We will likely not fully meet what they require," said chief financial officer Dave Zinsner.

Fourth-quarter revenue guidance is $15 billion to $16 billion, against analysts' $17.05 billion estimate.
