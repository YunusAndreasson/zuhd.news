San Francisco — Several driverless Waymos entered a US Route 101 construction zone this month.

California's motor vehicles department banned its driverless taxis from state freeways on 11 November.

The department cited recurring incursions into zones with workers and equipment, and the ban lasts until Waymo shows effective corrective action.

Waymo says no one was hurt, and its website shows 95% fewer serious-injury crashes than human drivers.

Waymo also suspended freeway rides nationwide, including Los Angeles and Miami, while it reviews performance.
