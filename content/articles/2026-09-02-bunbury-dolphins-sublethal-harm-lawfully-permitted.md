Bunbury — A model forecasts this dolphin population falling from 350 to 8.

The projected collapse spans a century, but it starts with dolphins alive under licensed tours today.

Hand-feeding tourism, not boats or nets, is the modeled cause, altering how females forage and raise calves.

Researchers say protective management, not a ban on all tourism, changes the forecast.
