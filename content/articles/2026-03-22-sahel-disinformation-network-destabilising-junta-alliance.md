Bamako — Fake media accounts targeted 3 military-led African states. Pages named "Scoop Africa" and "La Voix du Faso" mimicked regional outlets to spread disinformation against Burkina Faso, Mali, and Niger — 3 governments that expelled French forces and aligned with Russia. RFI and France 24 have traced the network but have not yet publicly named who is behind it.
