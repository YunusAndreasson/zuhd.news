Warsaw — [Poland](country:PL) is paying €656 million for 12 satellites.

The MEO-and-LEO constellation is Europe's answer to relying on Starlink, a private [US](country:US) network, for secure military links.

A Warsaw ground station will link the satellites to Poland's government fiber network.
