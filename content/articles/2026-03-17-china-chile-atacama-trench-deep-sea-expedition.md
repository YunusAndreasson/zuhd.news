China and Chile reached the Pacific's deepest trench floor. Washington had revoked Chilean diplomats' visas over a China-funded submarine cable, warning scientific cooperation could serve military purposes. Chile's navy approved the expedition before departure from Valparaíso, but Washington has not withdrawn its demand that Chile limit cooperation with China.
