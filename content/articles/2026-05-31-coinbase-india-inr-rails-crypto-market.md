Mumbai — A 2022 payment block on Coinbase in [India](country:IN) ends Monday with direct rupee bank rails.

India's payments body had barred Coinbase's instant-payment service; direct rupee transfers now bypass the peer-to-peer workarounds users needed.

Chainalysis ranks India first in global crypto adoption; Coinbase calls the $3 billion market its long-term target.
