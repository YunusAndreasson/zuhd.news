Pakistan bombed Afghan homes in Kabul, killing 4. Islamabad declared war on Afghanistan on Feb 27, accusing the Taliban of sheltering Tehrik-i-Taliban Pakistan militants responsible for attacks inside the country. Strikes have expanded to Kandahar and Paktia, displacing 66,000 Afghans, as China attempts to broker a ceasefire.
