United States — Google says Gemini 4 Carbon resisted every prompt-injection attack.

Google is withholding it from the public, citing its strength in coding and cybersecurity.

Carbon rolls out through Google's Fairwinds cyber defense program after testing under the [US](country:US) government's voluntary framework.

Google's own benchmarks put Carbon only a few points ahead of rival models.

Google gave no wide-release date, and has not said when Carbon's discounted developer rate ends.
