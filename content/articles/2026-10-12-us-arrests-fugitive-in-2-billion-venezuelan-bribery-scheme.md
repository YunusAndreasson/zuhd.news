Miami — Álvaro Pulido Vargas, indicted in 2019, is in US custody.

Prosecutors say he profited from [Venezuela](country:VE)'s state food programme.

Prosecutors allege $1.6 billion in contracts won by bribes carried inflated prices, and about $180 million moved through [US](country:US) banks.

Prosecutors did not say when or where he was arrested.

He appears in a South Florida federal court on Tuesday, 13 October.
