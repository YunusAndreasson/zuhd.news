Moscow — [Russia](country:RU) says Trump's nuclear test order threatens the moratorium.

Foreign Ministry spokeswoman Maria Zakharova says the moratorium has kept the world out of another arms race.

The test-ban treaty opened for signature 30 years ago but never took effect, so only restraint holds the moratorium.

Zakharova marked the anniversary by citing Trump's 29 October 2025 announcement that the [US](country:US) would begin testing.
