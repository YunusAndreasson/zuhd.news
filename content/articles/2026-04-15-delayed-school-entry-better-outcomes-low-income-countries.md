Boston — Waiting to start school buys more total years. Boston University researchers found delayed enrollment in LMICs reduces teen pregnancy, HIV rates in men, and child mortality among women. The intervention costs nothing; policymakers in LMICs where early enrollment is valued now have evidence to delay.
