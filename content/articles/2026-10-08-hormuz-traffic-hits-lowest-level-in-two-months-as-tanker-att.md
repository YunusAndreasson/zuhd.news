Dubai — Seven ships crossed Hormuz Tuesday, fewest since 23 July.

The strait carried a fifth of global oil and gas before the [Iran](country:IR) war.

Crude flows fell 27% to at least 10.1 million barrels a day, Kpler said, after the war's worst week of tanker attacks.

Exports from the Gulf of Oman and Red Sea rose to 6.7 million barrels a day, Kpler said.

Projectiles hit another tanker north of [Qatar](country:QA)'s Madinat ash Shamal on Wednesday, with casualties reported.
