Washington — The US cyber agency has no confirmed director.

Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin told Congress the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency's 600-hire rebuild cannot proceed without a confirmed director.

The White House has named no nominee; the vacancy leaves coordinated cyber incident response without an accountable lead.
