China — Its 2026 clean-tech exports will avoid 450m tonnes of CO2, [Brazil](country:BR)'s yearly emissions.

Over their lifetimes they avoid 8,400m tonnes, the [US](country:US) and [India](country:IN) combined, the Carbon Brief analysis finds.

76% of that lands in the global south, 52% in south and south-east Asia, where Chinese solar displaces high-carbon grids.

A continued domestic solar slowdown could exacerbate trade tensions, the analysis says.
