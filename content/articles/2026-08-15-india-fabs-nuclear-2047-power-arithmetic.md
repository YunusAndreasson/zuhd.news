New Delhi — Modi promised eight more chip plants.

The pledge marks [India](country:IN)'s push to build fabs at home, not import them.

He tied the plants to a seven-point "Shakti Ki Saptadhara" plan spanning manufacturing, tech, farming and defence.

The roadmap targets a "Viksit Bharat," or developed India, by the 2047 independence centenary.
