Venezuela transferred 80 million oil barrels to the US. Trump seized Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro in January and installed an interim government that opened Venezuela's nationalised oil sector to foreign investors. Trump warned interim president Delcy Rodríguez she would "pay a bigger price than Maduro" if she refused to comply.
