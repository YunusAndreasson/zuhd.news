Islamabad — Autopsy found 20 injuries on a 13-year-old domestic worker.

Fatima Asghar died at the official residence of a senior Federal Investigation Agency officer.

The autopsy says repeated assaults caused a fatal blood clot, and it records old burn scars.

Her father says he was told she had suddenly fallen ill.

Police hold the officer's wife and a servant on murder charges while forensic tests on her organs continue.
