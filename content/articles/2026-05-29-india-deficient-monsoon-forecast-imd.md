New Delhi — [India](country:IN)'s weather agency puts 60% odds on drought.

Over half of India's cultivated land is rain-fed; El Niño is forecast to grow from weak in June to strong by September.

The agency warned a deficient season would delay sowing across India's rain-fed farmland, which feeds half the country.
