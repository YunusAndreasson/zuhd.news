Paris — [France](country:FR)'s interior ministry counts more than 500 arrests.

It is the third weekend of unrest over living costs, fuel prices and poor public services.

Largely peaceful rallies gave way to riots in which protesters threw paving stones and petrol bombs at police.

Whether protests continue into a fourth weekend is unknown.
