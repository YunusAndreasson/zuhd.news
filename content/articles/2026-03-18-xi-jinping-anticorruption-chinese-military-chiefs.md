China is investigating General Zhang Youxia, its 2nd-ranked military officer. Chinese President Xi Jinping's anticorruption sweep now covers all 5 People's Liberation Army theatre commands — broader than any previous round. The army marks its centenary in August 2027; analysts warn the purge could leave it without its most experienced commanders.
