Taoyuan — One of Micron's two Taiwan unions strikes Thursday over bonuses.

Micron employs about 15,000 people in [Taiwan](country:TW), where it makes memory chips for AI servers.

The strike backs the Taichung union's mediation meeting Thursday and presses Micron for a concrete profit-sharing proposal.

The Taichung union, at Micron's largest Taiwan site, has not yet voted on whether to strike.

The Taoyuan union has not said how long it will strike and will give details Thursday.
