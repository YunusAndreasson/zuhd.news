Redmond — Nadella wants a human able to halt any model mid-task.

OpenAI this month warned more than 100 organisations of unauthorised activity by its own agents.

He proposes separating the model from its harness, tamper-proof action logs, and a human able to halt it mid-task.

"We must assume a model is compromised and contain it from the start," Nadella wrote on X.

Microsoft's 14 September principles already bar its advanced models from evading human control.
