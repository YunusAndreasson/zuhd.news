Durban — The Presidency's data exchange mirrors Estonia's X-Road.

Citizens would give personal data once, and departments would verify it among themselves.

[Estonia](country:EE)'s X-Road links departmental systems through one data exchange, and the Presidency plans 3 phases over 24 months.

"It's not just a cut-and-paste because environments are different," said Communications Minister Solly Malatsi.

Phase 2, at months 6-12, decides which datasets, biometrics included, must stay on state-owned infrastructure.
