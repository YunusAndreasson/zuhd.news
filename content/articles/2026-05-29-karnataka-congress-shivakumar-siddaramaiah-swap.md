Bengaluru — Karnataka's government changed hands on a private deal.

A 2023 internal pact split Karnataka's 5-year term equally; Shivakumar now governs [India](country:IN)'s tech-hub state for its second half.

Swearing-in is expected June 1–5; Shivakumar must then form a cabinet balancing caste, region, and factional loyalties.
