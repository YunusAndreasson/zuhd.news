Berlin — A separate European army would add weeks of delay, Rutte says.

Russian missiles reach Lisbon or Rome only 5 minutes later than Warsaw, he told NATO lawmakers.

He says a second command layer would need 2 or 3 weeks to agree who does what.

Rutte says he does not oppose a European military if national ones are abolished.

He meets Donald Trump in Palm Beach, Florida, on Wednesday, the White House said.
