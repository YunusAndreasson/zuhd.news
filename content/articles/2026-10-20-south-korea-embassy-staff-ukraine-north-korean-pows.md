Seoul — [South Korea](country:KR) will withdraw more embassy staff from Kyiv after receiving no "constructive response."

South Korea's constitution counts every North Korean as a citizen, and defectors are generally allowed to stay.

[Ukraine](country:UA)'s Zelenskyy announced the transfer of 2 North Korean prisoners at the UN General Assembly, though Seoul wanted it secret to protect the soldiers' families in [North Korea](country:KP).

Kyiv considers the situation resolved and says diplomacy must prevail over emotions.

A chargé d'affaires and consular staff stay in Kyiv, and Seoul has not said how many diplomats leave.
