H5N1 has spread across 900 kilometres of Antarctic coastline. Migratory birds carried the strain south after it devastated wildlife on every other continent since 2021 — researchers found it can kill 100% of a colony within days. Tens of thousands of penguins, cormorants, and seals remain exposed, with no treatment available.
