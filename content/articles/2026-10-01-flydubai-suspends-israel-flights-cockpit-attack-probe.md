Dubai — flydubai says its copilot stabbed the captain mid-flight.

The airline has suspended every flight to and from [Israel](country:IL) while investigators work.

Flight FZ1073 dropped from 34,000 to 15,600 feet before Captain Smit Machchhar opened the cockpit door and passengers overpowered the attacker.

flydubai gave no end date and says it will review the suspension as the investigation produces information.
