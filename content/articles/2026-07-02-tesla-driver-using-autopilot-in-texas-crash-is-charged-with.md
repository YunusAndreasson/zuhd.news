Katy, Texas — Martha Avila, 76, died in her own living room.

Michael Butler's Tesla was using Autopilot at 73 miles per hour when it left the road and struck her house, a Tesla executive said on X.

Butler faces a manslaughter charge; federal safety regulators opened their own investigation last month.
