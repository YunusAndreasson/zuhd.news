Vatican City — Pope Leo XIV's lung nodule was found early and is "circumscribed".

He announced surgery Sunday, the first major health event of his 17-month pontificate.

Benign and cancerous nodules can look alike on imaging, so removal gives a definitive diagnosis, said Dr. Stephen Liu of Georgetown.

Leo says he still plans the 6 November trip to Uruguay, Argentina and Peru.
