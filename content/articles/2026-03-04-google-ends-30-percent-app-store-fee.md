Google's 30% app store fee ends June 30. The Epic Games antitrust verdict and the EU Digital Markets Act forced the change, with developers set to pay 15-20% and gain access to rival billing systems. Apple's App Store still charges 30%, while regulators in the EU and US have already opened probes.
