Kennedy Space Center — NASA's new X-70 carries a pilot and 2,000 pounds of experiments.

It gives NASA a crewed platform to test flight-control and maneuverability technologies in real flight.

NASA says it weighs 10,000 pounds at takeoff, reaches 45,000 feet and ranges about 1,500 nautical miles.

NASA released only a generated concept image, with much of the aircraft hidden in clouds.

Administrator Jared Isaacman expects first flight under the NASA banner within two years.
