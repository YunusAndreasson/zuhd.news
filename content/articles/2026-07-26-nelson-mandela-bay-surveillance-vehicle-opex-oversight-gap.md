Gqeberha — A R6-million surveillance van sat idle five years.

The metro has spent R885,846 repairing it since a camera fault, while its gunshot-detection system stays offline over a separate contract dispute.

A replacement camera is due in August; officers need training, and the gunshot system has no return date.
