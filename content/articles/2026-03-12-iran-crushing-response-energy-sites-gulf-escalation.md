Iran threatened to set Gulf oil fields on fire. Khatam al-Anbiya, Iran's main military command, warned even a "slight" attack on its oil, gas or port facilities would trigger "crushing" regional retaliation. Gulf states are the intended audience: Iranian missiles can reach every major oil facility in the region.
