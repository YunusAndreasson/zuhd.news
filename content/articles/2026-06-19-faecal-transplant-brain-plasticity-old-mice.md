Pisa — Gut transplants from young mice restored brain plasticity in old adults, curing a condition irreversible after childhood.

Pisa researchers found gut bacteria producing fatty acids reopened plasticity windows normally closed in adolescence.

Specific microbial strains are unidentified; human extrapolation remains premature, researchers said.
