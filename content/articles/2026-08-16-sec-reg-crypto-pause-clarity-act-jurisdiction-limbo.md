Washington — [America](country:US)'s Securities and Exchange Commission scrapped Friday's Reg Crypto vote.

Nobody yet governs digital assets in America, so firms rationally wait.

Congress's Clarity Act could overwrite the rule the commission was writing, so it paused instead.

The delayed "innovation exemption," letting firms skip registration meanwhile, still has no announced date or scope.
