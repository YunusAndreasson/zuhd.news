London — SpaceX and Blue Origin run mainly on government funding, not billionaire wealth.

That funding decides who reaches orbit, since one company now launches most of the world's payload mass.

NASA shifted from cost-reimbursement to fixed-price contracts, letting contractors keep the vehicles they build.

Bezos separately sells $1 billion yearly in Amazon stock to fund Blue Origin.
