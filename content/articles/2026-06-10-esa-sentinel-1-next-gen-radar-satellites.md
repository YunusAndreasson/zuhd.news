Berlin — Europe contracted €700M for next-generation Earth radar.

[Italy](country:IT)'s Thales Alenia Space will build 2 satellites; synthetic aperture radar monitors ice loss, floods, and ground shifts through cloud, improving resolution 4-fold to 5m.

A contract delay has pushed the 2032 launch target back; Thales Alenia has not given a revised date.
