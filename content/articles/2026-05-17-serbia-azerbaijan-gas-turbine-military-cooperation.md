Baku — [Serbia](country:RS) and [Azerbaijan](country:AZ) announce a 500MW gas plant.

Azerbaijan's state oil company will supply the fuel for the plant near Niš, shifting Serbia's new capacity from Russian to Azerbaijani imports by 2030.

Vucic also announced expanded arms cooperation, including Nora self-propelled artillery deliveries to Baku.
