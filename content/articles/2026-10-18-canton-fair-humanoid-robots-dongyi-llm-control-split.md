Guangzhou — A robot took 3 hours to grasp one part.

Dongyi Technology's humanoids guide visitors and play badminton and table tennis at [China](country:CN)'s 140th Canton Fair.

Dongyi's answer is four layers: a general model plans, onboard PhyAgents decide, PhyCore controls motion and the robot acts.

RoboDojo's September benchmark gave GPT-6 Astra a 22.48% success rate on 42 simulated tasks when it drove actions directly.

Dongyi plans to keep the guide and sports services running across three phases until 4 November.
