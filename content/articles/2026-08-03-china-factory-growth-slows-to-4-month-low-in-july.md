Beijing — Export orders grew while domestic demand cooled.

[China](country:CN)'s private manufacturing index fell to 50.9 in July from 51.7, S&P Global said, as new orders slowed to their weakest since January.

Leaders pledged faster spending on existing infrastructure projects rather than new stimulus for the rest of the year.
