The US attacked Iran for Israel, former CIA director David Petraeus confirmed. Petraeus said the US needed to destroy Iran's defenses before Israel could operate — the first senior official to state this publicly. Iran has called the attacks illegal at the UN, and Petraeus's admission gives it specific grounds.
