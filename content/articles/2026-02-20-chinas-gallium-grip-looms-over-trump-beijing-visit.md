China produces 99% of the world's gallium, essential for semiconductors and military electronics. Beijing suspended its export ban for one year during November 2024 trade talks, but the restriction resumes in November 2026 unless renewed. President Trump's planned April visit to Beijing now carries the weight of a deadline the US cannot meet alone — alternative supply chains would take a decade.
