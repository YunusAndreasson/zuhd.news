Washington stripped South Korea's missile defense system to fight Iran. The removal of the Terminal High Altitude Area Defense (THAAD) battery left 28,500 US troops and South Korean forces exposed the same week North Korea fired 10 ballistic missiles. South Korean President Lee Jae Myung called the move a "stark reality" Seoul could not prevent, with no timeline announced for the system's return.
