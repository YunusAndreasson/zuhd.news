Minneapolis — US Bank made a cross-border payment with USBDC.

The pilot targets remittance costs and gatekeeping for economies dependent on transfers.

Correspondent banking moves dollars through several banks, adding delay and cost that stablecoins skip, yet issuers can freeze funds.

US Bank has not disclosed which corridors, fees or eligibility rules govern USBDC use.
