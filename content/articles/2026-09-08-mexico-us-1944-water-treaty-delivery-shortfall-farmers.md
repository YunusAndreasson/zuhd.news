Mexico City — Water treaties no longer prevent US-Mexico conflict.

The Trump administration is using water access to pressure [Mexico](country:MX) on trade.

Decades-old agreements on shared rivers and groundwater are straining as both countries compete for the same water.

The [US](country:US) will keep leveraging water access as trade talks with Mexico remain stalled.
