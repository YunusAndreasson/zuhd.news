A US-Israeli strike killed 15 factory workers in Isfahan. The attack extended a weeks-long campaign from military and nuclear sites to civilian industrial infrastructure — Iranian media reported the factory made heaters and refrigerators. No ICC referral has been filed against the US or Israel despite the strikes on civilian facilities.
