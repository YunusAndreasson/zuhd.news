Birmal — Explosives leveled a girls' primary school in [Pakistan](country:PK)'s Sara Ghowara overnight.

The school is the 3rd destroyed in Birmal district since February; police have registered no suspect and no group has claimed responsibility.

4 attacks on girls' schools have struck the two Waziristans and Lakki Marwat since October 2025, police records show.
