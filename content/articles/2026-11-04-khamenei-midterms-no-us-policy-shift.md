Tehran — Mojtaba Khamenei says partisan shifts will not soften US hostility.

He warned that repeated attacks would widen the war, which began on 28 February.

Adviser Mohammad Mokhber said [Iran](country:IR) won by shifting the war's costs onto the US, Iranian state media reported.

Iran's chief negotiator Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf posted: "Americans said NO TO WAR, and we endorse this message."

US and Israeli leaders have threatened to resume attacks as diplomats try to avert renewed fighting.
