Washington — The [US](country:US) scrapped a $368m ocean sensor network.

The 900-sensor array fed hurricane forecasts, fisheries data, and CO₂ readings used by coastal nations worldwide — not just the US.

The National Science Foundation called it a "scope adjustment"; no replacement timeline has been announced.
