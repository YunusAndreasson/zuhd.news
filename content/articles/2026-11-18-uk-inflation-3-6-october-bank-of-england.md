London — [UK](country:GB) inflation has topped target for more than two years.

October's 3.6% reading, up from 3.5%, keeps the Bank of England raising rates.

The Middle East war raises energy costs, and EDF Energy predicts Ofgem's price cap will jump about a quarter in January.

Investors trimmed bets on a December rate rise as some underlying components held steady.

Chief economist Huw Pill says he will vote for a quarter-point rise on 17 December.
