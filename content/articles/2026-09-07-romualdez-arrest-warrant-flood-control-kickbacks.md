Manila — An anti-graft court ordered Marcos's own cousin arrested.

It tests whether [Philippine](country:PH) President Ferdinand Marcos Jr's anti-corruption pledge reaches his family.

The Ombudsman accuses ex-House speaker Martin Romualdez of taking 7.44 billion pesos in flood-control kickbacks over 2022-2025.

Romualdez, hospitalized after the warrant, faces a non-bailable plunder charge with no trial date set.
