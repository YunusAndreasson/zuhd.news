Alipurduar — 7.4 million cubic metres of water burst a glacial lake.

West Bengal moved 12,000 people along the Sankosh River, downstream of the breach in [Bhutan](country:BT).

Officials said the Mo River carried the flood into the Sankosh, which forms the Assam-West Bengal boundary and rose nearly 2 feet.

An Alipurduar official reported no major damage or casualties in Kumargram, and Jalpaiguri's rivers saw no surge.

Assam is watching Dhubri and Kokrajhar districts, where water levels were steady Friday evening.
