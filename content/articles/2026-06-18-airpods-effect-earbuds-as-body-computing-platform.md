New York — 44% of Americans now wear earbuds daily, surveys show.

Always-in earbuds normalize ambient computing on the body; heavy users report higher loneliness, a Jabra survey found, yet adoption keeps rising.

No regulation governs what earphone platforms do with biometric data collected continuously on the body.
