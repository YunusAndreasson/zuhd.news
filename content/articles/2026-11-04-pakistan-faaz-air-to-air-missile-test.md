Islamabad — [Pakistan](country:PK) fired its first homegrown air-to-air missile from a JF-17.

It joins a small group of nations that build their own air-to-air missiles.

The footage shows the infrared-guided Fa'az-IIR, not the radar-guided Fa'az-RF.

The state-owned maker advertises ranges above 100 kilometres, specifications that vary with launch altitude and speed.

The radar-guided Fa'az-RF has yet to appear in a launch video.
