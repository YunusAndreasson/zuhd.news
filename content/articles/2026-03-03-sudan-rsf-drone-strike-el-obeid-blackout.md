Rapid Support Forces blacked out El-Obeid by drone. El-Obeid controls the supply crossroads between RSF-held Darfur and army territory, a central front in a war that has displaced 11 million. The RSF have struck El-Obeid 3 times since January, with no peace talks scheduled.
