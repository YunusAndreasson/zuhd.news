Jakarta — Indonesia's graft commission lost prosecutorial power in 2019.

President Prabowo pledged technology to track hidden assets of corrupt officials; the commission can investigate but cannot independently prosecute.

No reform to the commission's mandate was tabled at Wednesday's session; the structural gap remains.
