Hong Kong — BitMEX pleaded guilty to US Bank Secrecy Act charges.

The exchange ousted its chief executive, chief financial officer, and growth head and is searching for a buyer during a crypto downturn.

Former general counsel Peter Wilkinson now leads the firm with no buyer or timeline announced.
