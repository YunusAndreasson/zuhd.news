United States — Giraffe Bio says its molecules lift copper recovery up to 5%.

One forecast puts 2040 copper supply 25% short of demand.

Engineered microbes in a bioreactor make mineral-specific compounds that mines add to flotation, where chemicals float copper away from waste rock.

Giraffe Bio raised $6.4 million from investors including BHP Ventures to move the chemistry out of the lab.
