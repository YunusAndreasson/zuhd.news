London — 1997 UK files: NATO expansion "would provoke Russians." Britain privately warned in 1997 that NATO expansion would antagonise Russia; the MoD has since called the 2022 invasion "unprovoked" hundreds of times. The files are at the National Archives; the MoD has not reconciled its public record with the documents.
