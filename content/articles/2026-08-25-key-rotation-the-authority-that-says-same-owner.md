New York — A wallet could soon replace its own keys.

The same proposal fixes lost keys and quantum-cracked keys at once.

A blockchain address is a public key's hash, so a new key needs someone vouching it is the same owner.

Bitcoin and Ethereum face the same choice, with far more value and far less flexibility.
