London — Suspected hackers set fridges to 50C across 7 European countries.

[UK](country:GB) meat suppliers lost stock, though a National Cyber Security Centre official calls the impact minimal.

Commercial refrigeration controllers sit on the open internet, often with default passwords and years-old software.

No group has claimed responsibility, and no one has demanded payment.

Researchers at UK Cyber Defence identified more than 800 refrigeration and building-control interfaces exposed to the public internet.
