Edinburgh — Salmon's genome was copied whole 100 million years ago.

The extra gene copies still shape disease resistance in salmon, a major global protein source.

Duplicate genes freed one copy to keep its old job while the other evolved new functions, unlike single-letter mutations.

How these expression patterns affect farmed salmon traits like disease resistance remains under study.
