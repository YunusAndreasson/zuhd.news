Pasvalys — Drone debris dented a moving car in northern Lithuania.

No one was hurt, but [Lithuania](country:LT)'s prime minister said the warning system did not work perfectly.

The crisis centre's head calls it a likely Ukrainian drone, and Kyiv says Russian jamming has steered earlier ones off course.

[Poland](country:PL)'s defence ministry says its radar did not confirm the object crossing into Lithuania.

Defence Minister Robertas Kaunas proposed a joint investigation with Poland and ordered a report on late alerts.
