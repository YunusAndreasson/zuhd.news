Abuja — A judge remanded journalist Stanley Ugagbe until September.

Police charged Ugagbe under [Nigeria](country:NG)'s cyberstalking law after his outlet reported a bank deputy governor's undisclosed properties.

His bail hearing was pushed to 21 September, after the court's summer recess begins.
