Abu Dhabi — Al Gharbia's pipe output fell from 250,000 to 60,000 tonnes.

Gulf states and [Iraq](country:IQ) plan pipelines around Hormuz, which stays effectively shut.

The [UAE](country:AE) is doubling its 360 km Habshan-Fujairah line from 1.5 million barrels a day, and [Saudi Arabia](country:SA) weighs widening East-West.

Ship traffic averaged 3.3 a day to 25 October against a 90-day normal of 4.1.

Al Gharbia, in talks with Adnoc on new projects, expects 200,000 tonnes of output next year.
