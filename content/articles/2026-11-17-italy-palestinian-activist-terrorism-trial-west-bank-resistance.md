L'Aquila — Anan Yaeesh, from Tulkarem camp, got 5½ years in Italy.

An Italian court refused [Israel](country:IL)'s extradition over torture risk, then convicted him under its own terrorism law.

Prosecutors relied significantly on Israeli material, including prisoners' interrogation records, mostly excluded after defence objections.

Two co-defendants were acquitted, and prosecutors are appealing those verdicts.

The case returns to court in 2027 as Yaeesh's defence appeals the conviction.
