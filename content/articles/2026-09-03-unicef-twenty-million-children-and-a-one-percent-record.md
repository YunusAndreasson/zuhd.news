New York — 1 in 5 teens report online sexual abuse.

Fewer than 1% of cases reach police, hiding the true scale from policy.

The UN's survey of 21,000 children across 21 countries found most abuse happened on mainstream social apps.

UNICEF is urging governments to update laws to cover computer-generated abuse images.
