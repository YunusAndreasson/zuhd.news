Ottawa — [Canada](country:CA)'s privacy regulator opened a probe into ID-scanner IDScan.

Bar and venue patrons who scanned a license never agreed to become a breach target.

The probe covers IDScan's security practices and whether it properly notified victims, as required under Canada's federal privacy law.

The regulator has not set a timeline for its findings or any penalty against IDScan.
