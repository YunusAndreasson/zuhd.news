Balkh — Taliban authorities reportedly banned women's names on medical records.

A prescription without a name can reach the wrong patient, or none.

Health workers were told to call female patients Bibi, meaning grandmother, and some women are reportedly called goat or cow.

"This is a systematic process of dehumanization," said Lalah Osmany, a women's rights activist based in Germany.

Femena, which has documented more than 144 decrees on women, warns the order could spread across [Afghanistan](country:AF).
