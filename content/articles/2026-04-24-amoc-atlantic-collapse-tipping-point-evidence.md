London — Weakened Atlantic currents are shifting tropical rain south, worsening Sahelian drought and [India](country:IN)'s monsoon. The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change projects continued Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation decline as warming adds meltwater to the North Atlantic. Full collapse before 2100 is disputed; the panel rates it unlikely but cannot rule it out.
