The London Stock Exchange plans tokenized UK shares.

Fully fungible tokens would carry real voting and dividend rights, not just price tracking.

The exchange is partnering with Kraken owner Payward on an overnight venue, chasing trading volume crypto platforms already capture.

The plan awaits approval from [Britain](country:GB)'s Financial Conduct Authority before any token trades.
