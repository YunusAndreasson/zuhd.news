Abuja — [Nigeria](country:NG) pledges $3 billion more for health by 2030.

The [US](country:US), which funded HIV, tuberculosis and malaria programmes for 25 years, exits in 2030.

Under a non-binding five-year memorandum signed 19 December 2025, the US ramps down its funding as Nigeria's grows, health minister Muhammad Pate said.

Pate acknowledged Nigeria is not yet in a position to fully fund its health sector.

Pate said either party can walk away from the agreement at any time.
