United States — Telegram gave [US](country:US) authorities a suspect's phone number and IP address.

Telegram rarely gave data before French authorities arrested its CEO Pavel Durov in August 2024.

A Stored Communications Act court order prompted the letter, which Telegram calls voluntary and no concession to US jurisdiction.

Hackers often impersonate police to extract user data, and the records now publish Telegram's non-public law-enforcement address.
