London — A diabetes drug eased dangerous rat heart rhythms.

Heart failure with preserved ejection fraction has no good treatment and tracks obesity and diabetes worldwide.

The fat pad on the heart is active tissue in electrical contact with the muscle beneath it, not inert padding.

This is a rat model with biomarker associations, not a demonstrated human pathway yet.
