The Spectator leads UK outlets in anti-Muslim bias. The Centre for Media Monitoring analysed 40,913 articles from 30 outlets and found 50% contained bias — 70% associated Muslims with negative behaviour. The Centre released the 2025 findings as anti-Muslim hate content on social media surged during the Iran war.
