Washington — Trump says [Russia](country:RU) will supply 300,000 tonnes of diesel.

Diesel averages $6.28 a gallon, AAA says, with midterm elections on 3 November.

The [US](country:US) Treasury's licence shields diesel loaded by Friday from sanctions until April 2027, longer than any since the Ukraine war began.

Russia has banned diesel exports since July, and the International Energy Agency says output fell about 30%.

Russian Deputy Prime Minister Alexander Novak says 500,000 tonnes follow in November and 1 million in December.
