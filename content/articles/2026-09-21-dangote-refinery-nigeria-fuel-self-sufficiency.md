Lagos — Africa's top oil producer still imports nearly all its fuel.

One refinery, owned by one billionaire, now shapes [Nigeria](country:NG)'s currency and fuel supply.

Dangote's refinery now supplies most of Nigeria's diesel and petrol, cutting the import bill that drained the naira.

Dangote rejects criticism that his refinery creates a monopoly over Nigeria's fuel supply.

Dangote plans to double refining capacity and list the company on Nigeria's stock exchange.
