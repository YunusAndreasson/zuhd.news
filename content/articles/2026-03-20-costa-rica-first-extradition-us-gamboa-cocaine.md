Costa Rica sent its first citizen ever to the US to face trial. Celso Gamboa, a former Supreme Court justice, faces federal cocaine charges under a 2025 constitutional amendment that passed with 44 of 57 votes after lawmakers argued domestic courts were too corrupt to try him. The amendment covers only drug trafficking and terrorism, leaving corrupt officials and financial fraudsters still beyond US extradition reach.
