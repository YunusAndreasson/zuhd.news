Cairo — A student got 25 years for a SIM card he never bought.

His case revealed dozens of phone numbers fraudulently registered to strangers' names nationwide.

Retailers issued SIMs from photographed ID copies, so the regulator's fix is to collect fingerprints and face scans instead.

[Egypt](country:EG)'s data protection law still has no enforced regulations to secure that biometric data.
