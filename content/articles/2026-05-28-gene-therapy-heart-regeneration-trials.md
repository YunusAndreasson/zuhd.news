Houston — Heart muscle barely regenerates — until now.

Heart failure drugs treat symptoms, not cause; this RNA therapy silences SAV1, a gene that limits cardiomyocyte division.

Medley Therapeutics acknowledges scientists dispute whether cardiomyocytes are truly dividing — human trial results will settle it.
