Robots, not soldiers, now hold Ukraine's front lines. Ukraine deployed the world's first armed robot battalion — Kalashnikov-mounted vehicles that have ambushed Russian carriers and held positions for weeks — to fill front-line gaps its military cannot staff. Tencore, Ukraine's top robot manufacturer, expects demand to jump from 2,000 to 40,000 units in 2026.
