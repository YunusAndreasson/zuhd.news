China — SenseTime's open image model now checks its own text.

SenseTime says its flagship U1 Pro ranks first among Chinese models on Design Arena's two boards.

After generating an image, SenseNova U1.6 checks it for text errors and corrects them, a first for SenseTime's 8B U series.

SenseTime's internal user evaluation puts the Lite version's overall aesthetic score about 10% above its predecessor.

SenseTime published the model weights on GitHub and Hugging Face.
