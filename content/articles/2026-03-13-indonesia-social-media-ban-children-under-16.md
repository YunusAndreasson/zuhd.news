Indonesia banned under-16 access to 6 social media platforms. UNICEF found half of Indonesian children online have encountered sexual content, driving a regulation that targets platforms' engagement-maximizing architecture, not just individual harmful posts. The regulation takes effect March 28 in a country with no national age verification system.
