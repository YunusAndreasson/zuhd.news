Los Angeles — A middleman sold SpaceX equity to [China](country:CN)-linked investors through undisclosed private funds.

Buyers were promised access to SpaceX's chief financial officer; private markets required no disclosure that a public listing would have mandated.

SpaceX barred Chinese buyers from its IPO last week; the list was unsealed only after press litigation this month.
