Khyber — Khyber families borrow to cover wedding costs.

Some marriages don't happen at all because parents can't afford dowry demands.

Spending less reads as failure, not restraint, so no family will cut costs first — residents want collective campaigns instead.

Residents are calling for repeated awareness campaigns across [Pakistan](country:PK)'s tribal areas, with no funding committed yet.
