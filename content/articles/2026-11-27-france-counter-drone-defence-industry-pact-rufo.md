Paris — The counter-drone pact with industry runs until summer 2027.

Junior armed forces minister Alice Rufo says [France](country:FR) has already faced insufficient counter-drone capability.

The pact joins firms, military users and the procurement directorate to cut red tape and speed testing and orders.

Drones overflew the Île Longue submarine base in December 2025 and Toulon naval base in October.

Gicat chairman Emmanuel Levacher hopes the pact is renewed to cover both drone and counter-drone work.
