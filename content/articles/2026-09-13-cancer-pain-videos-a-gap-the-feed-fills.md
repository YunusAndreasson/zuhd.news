Beijing — Cancer-pain videos scored a median 3 of 5.

Reliability scored just 2 of 5, despite clinicians posting most videos.

Medical professionals posted 80.4% of the 194 videos analyzed across Douyin and Red Note, the study found.

The paper is an accepted manuscript, not yet the final version of record.
