New York — The 7-shuffle rule assumes a perfect 50-50 split.

Persi Diaconis proved the rule in 1992; a new proof now tackles the realistic case of uneven, human-scale riffles.

The precise shuffle count for imperfect hands is what the new proof sets out to bound.
