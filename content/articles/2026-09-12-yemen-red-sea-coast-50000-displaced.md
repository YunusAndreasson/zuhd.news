Mocha — 46,000 fled Yemen's Red Sea coast this week.

Many are on their second or third displacement from war, aid workers say.

Houthi forces seized towns on [Yemen](country:YE)'s Tihama coast, the plain where the country's food imports land.

The UN warns the offensive risks dragging in wider regional powers.
