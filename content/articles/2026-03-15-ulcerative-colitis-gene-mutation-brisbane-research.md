1 family's DNA revealed the gene behind ulcerative colitis. Sequencing one Brisbane family with 5 UC cases uncovered an OTUD3 mutation that breaks the gut's protective barrier — a mechanism large population studies had missed. The team confirmed the mutation in UK and Finnish biobanks and now hopes to develop drugs targeting OTUD3.
