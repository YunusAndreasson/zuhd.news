Paris — Four small AI agents lifted team success from 6.56% to 82.34%.

Tokens per attempt fell 37.4%, and Hugging Face engineer Ben Burtenshaw puts replication at about $20.

A team reward, docked for tokens, taught agents sharing one Qwen3-0.6B model to broadcast their numbers to teammates.

On 32 questions held out of training, the trained team answered 26 correctly.

Hugging Face's next post compares teams with single agents on tasks one agent can finish alone.
