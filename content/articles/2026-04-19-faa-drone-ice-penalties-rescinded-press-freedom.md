Washington — [US](country:US) aviation authority rescinded criminal drone penalties near immigration enforcement vehicles. The original restriction created invisible no-fly bubbles around unmarked Immigration and Customs Enforcement vehicles, grounding journalists who document deportations. The replacement drops criminal language but expands protections to cover the Department of Justice as well.
