Tel Aviv — Netanyahu loyalists attacked journalist Ilana Dayan for mourning deaths. Dayan warned that Israel's leaders were failing the vulnerable while normalizing civilian deaths — a critique loyalists met with fury rather than argument. Other Israeli journalists face the same choice Dayan made: report the war's domestic cost or risk being branded disloyal.
