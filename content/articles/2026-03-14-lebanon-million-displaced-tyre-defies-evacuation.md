Israeli forces struck Tyre as its residents rejected evacuation orders covering 10% of Lebanon's territory. Israel has ordered all of southern Lebanon cleared north of the Litani River, displacing nearly 1 million people. With no ceasefire scheduled, Tyre's defiance leaves Israel's evacuation zone unenforceable.
