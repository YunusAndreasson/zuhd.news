Kathmandu — Two hydropower workers survived nine days underground.

Roughly 900 more remain trapped in tunnels along [Nepal](country:NP)'s Trishuli River.

An upstream glacier collapse sent ice, rock and mud down river valleys packed with hydropower tunnels.

Rescuers keep digging as Nepal's disaster agency counts more than 5,000 still missing.
