New York — Nanoparticles expose catalytic sites bulk gold buries.

Bulk surfaces spontaneously reconstruct into an inactive hexagonal pattern; nanoparticles lack enough atoms to complete the shift, leaving reactive sites exposed.

The findings, published in Physical Review Letters, open design paths for industrial gold catalysts.
