London — Google DeepMind put $10 million into multi-agent safety.

Artificial general intelligence safety director Rohin Shah said no field of multi-agent safety research currently exists, even as Google deploys agents commercially.

Shah expects economy-wide deployment within months; simulations are the only way to study swarm behavior, he said.
