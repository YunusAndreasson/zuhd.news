Beijing — A sprinting robot caught fire at the finish line.

The failures, not the records, show what factory robots still can't do.

Locomotion trains well in simulation, but fine manipulation needs real-world force feedback that can't be scaled the same way.

Wire reports have not said who independently validated the sprint records set in Beijing.
