Al-Sukhna — A bus fire killed at least 35 people.

The Damascus-Deir Ezzor highway crosses [Syria](country:SY)'s war-damaged desert with no hospitals along the route, forcing the defence ministry to evacuate by helicopter.

Syria's Civil Defence said it answered 19 more road accidents just the day before, three fatal.
