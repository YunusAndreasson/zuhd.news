Delhi — Motorists plan [India](country:IN)'s first sit-in against E20 fuel.

The mandatory 20% ethanol blend cuts oil imports and pays sugarcane farmers, but owners of pre-2023 cars report mileage loss and wear.

Maruti Suzuki and Toyota say testing shows no damage; organizers await police permission for Sunday.
