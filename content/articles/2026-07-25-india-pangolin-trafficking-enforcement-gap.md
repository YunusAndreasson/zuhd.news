New Delhi — Its armor makes it easy to grab.

One pangolin is taken from the wild every five minutes; [India](country:IN) hosts two of the world's eight species.

A 2025 wildlife-trade convention report counted 553,042 poached globally since 2016; enforcement still targets village collectors, not buyers.
