Santa Marta, Colombia — Eighty scientists, four working groups, one mandate.

[Colombia](country:CO) hosted the launch; COP30's president recruited scientists from [Brazil](country:BR), Germany and Cameroon as co-chairs to steer fossil-fuel phaseout roadmaps toward governments.

A global roadmap is due at COP31 in November — so far only France has published its own.
