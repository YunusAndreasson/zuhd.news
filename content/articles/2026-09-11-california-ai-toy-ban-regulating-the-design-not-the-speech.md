Los Angeles — Regulators named the law after a teen's suicide.

As home to Google, Meta and Snap, California sets a de facto national rule.

The law targets addictive design — infinite scroll, autoplay — rather than content, dodging First Amendment fights over speech.

The law runs four years, and civil-liberties groups have already called it unconstitutional.
