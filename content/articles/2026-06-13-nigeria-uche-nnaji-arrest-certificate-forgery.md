Abuja — Court ordered Nnaji's arrest for credential forgery.

[Nigeria](country:NG)'s anti-graft commission found former minister Uche Nnaji forged his university degree and national service discharge certificate to secure 2023 ministerial confirmation.

Nnaji is now a 2027 governorship candidate — a win would grant him prosecutorial immunity.
