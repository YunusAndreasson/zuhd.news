Houston — 5 astronauts suited up as Zvezda's crack worsened.

Zvezda's tunnel has leaked for 6 years; Friday's deeper repair attempt triggered the safe-haven order, which NASA reversed 2 hours later.

NASA plans to deorbit the station in 2030; [Russia](country:RU)'s commitment ends in 2028, leaving the structural repair unresolved.
