Trump said Thursday the [US](country:US) will not attack [Iran](country:IR) before the 3 November midterms.

Iran revised the new US proposals, Pezeshkian said, and will finalise them through intermediaries.

Putin offered Moscow's help at the Commonwealth of Independent States summit, but no role or agreement was announced.

Trump said the US blockade of Iranian shipping in the Strait of Hormuz stays in force.
