Washington — NASA will only pursue "good deals" with partners, Isaacman said.

Middle powers now compete for scarce lunar south pole real estate and water ice.

NASA canceled Gateway in March and now trades Moon seats for hardware, like [Italy](country:IT)'s $5 billion habitation module.

"They are going to put a nuclear reactor there," Isaacman said of [China](country:CN) and [Russia](country:RU).

[Canada](country:CA) is still weighing its own contribution, complicated by an ongoing US trade dispute.
