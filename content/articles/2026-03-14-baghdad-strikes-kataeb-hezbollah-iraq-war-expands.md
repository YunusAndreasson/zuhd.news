US-Israeli strikes killed a Kataeb Hezbollah commander in Baghdad. Kataeb Hezbollah is an Iran-linked Iraqi militia; the attacks are the first inside the Iraqi capital since the war began February 28, bringing the conflict onto the soil of a third country. Iraq has not authorized the strikes, and further attacks could force a parliamentary vote to expel US forces.
