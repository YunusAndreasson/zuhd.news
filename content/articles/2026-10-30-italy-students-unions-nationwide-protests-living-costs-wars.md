Rome — Tens of thousands marched in dozens of cities across [Italy](country:IT).

Truck drivers, taxi drivers and farmers joined a general strike over war-driven fuel prices.

The Unione Sindacale di Base union called the strike, and joined peers in [France](country:FR), [Greece](country:GR), [Spain](country:ES), [Turkey](country:TR) and [Morocco](country:MA) demanding ports stop handling weapons.

Meloni's government approved 175 million euros ($195 million) in fuel aid for truck drivers, farmers and fishermen last week.

Parties are still negotiating a draft 2027 budget carrying a 2,500-euro annual bonus for workers under 30.
