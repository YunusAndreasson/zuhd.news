Riyadh calls it a contained fire while the Houthis call it a precision strike.

[Saudi Arabia](country:SA) built Jazan on the Red Sea specifically to move oil without transiting the Hormuz strait [Iran](country:IR) has targeted.

Aramco has not confirmed whether any refining unit was taken offline or for how long.
