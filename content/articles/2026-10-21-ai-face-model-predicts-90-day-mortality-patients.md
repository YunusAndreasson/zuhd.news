Boston — 64 of 122 AI-flagged cancer patients died within 90 days.

The model reads only a face photo, no scans, records or blood tests.

Mass General Brigham trained FAHR-Face on 34,363 patients' photos and survival records, then tested it on 3,820 unseen patients.

Professor Miroslaw Bober of the University of Surrey says predicting death does not show why it happens.

The authors have not shown which physical changes the model detects.
