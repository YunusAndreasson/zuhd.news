Tehran — [Iran](country:IR)'s parliament drafted a bill to impose sovereign toll regimes over the Strait of Hormuz. Modelled on Suez, it would set selective passage rights over a strait carrying roughly 20% of global oil. Khamenei holds final say; parliament awaits the Supreme National Security Council's conclusions before advancing the bill.
