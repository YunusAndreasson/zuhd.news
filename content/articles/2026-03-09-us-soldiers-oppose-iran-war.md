A US conscientious objector hotline is overwhelmed with soldiers opposing the Iran war. The Center on Conscience & War reported calls "ringing off the hook" and one member described "widespread opposition within their unit" — more dissent than at any point since Iraq. Pentagon officials have refused to rule out reinstating the draft, last used in 1972.
