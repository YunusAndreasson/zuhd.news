Nouakchott — At least a dozen herders have vanished since January.

Local officials in Guidimakha blame [Mali](country:ML)'s forces and Russia's Africa Corps; the same herders cross al-Qaeda-held ground safely by paying pasture fees, officials said.

More than 300,000 Malian refugees now strain [Mauritania](country:MR), whose president calls Mali his greatest concern while denying incursions.
