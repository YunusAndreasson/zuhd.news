Beijing — [China](country:CN)'s Belt and Road energy investment hit $93.9bn in 2025.

Oil-and-gas deals tripled to $71.5bn, the Green Finance and Development Center said; [Nigeria](country:NG) and [Congo](country:CG) megadeals drove 60% of the gain.

The center said Chinese firms retain supply-chain control, binding partner states into long-term industrial dependency.
