Dubai — Swissaid estimates 413 tonnes of African gold arrived smuggled.

The gold was worth $52 billion, and the [UAE](country:AE) stayed the top destination despite 2023 rules.

Swissaid matched Emirati imports against African export data, and most of 24 refineries it reviewed complied only partly or not at all.

The economy ministry told Swissaid 13 refineries, about 90% of refined output, are rated compliant.

Swissaid urges rules covering traders, stiffer penalties and named mine suppliers.
