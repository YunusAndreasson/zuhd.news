A 3-day-old infant died in a US-Israeli airstrike. The strike hit a residential building in Arak, killing the infant's 2-year-old sister, mother, and grandmother, the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps confirmed. Iran's Health Ministry counted 1,444 civilians killed and 18,551 wounded since US-Israeli strikes began on February 28.
