Chandel — 21 people have died after drinking suspected spurious liquor.

About 600 people are hospitalised, 12 in intensive care, local health authorities say.

Police say the liquor came from Sugnu in neighbouring Kakching district.

Authorities have not yet established the cause of death medically or scientifically.

Police are still investigating who supplied the liquor.
