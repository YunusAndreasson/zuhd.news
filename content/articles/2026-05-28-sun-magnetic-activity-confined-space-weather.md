Birmingham — Solar Cycle 25 shows unexplained sub-surface magnetic shifts.

Helioseismic data show more sub-surface magnetism but fewer surface sunspots — a mismatch that breaks existing solar dynamo models, researchers said.

Solar storm forecasts for satellite navigation and power grids depend on models this anomaly now undermines.
