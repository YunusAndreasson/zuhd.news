Athens — Greek victims sued Intellexa over Predator spyware.

Predator traces surfaced on dozens of phones in 2022, toppling [Greece](country:GR)'s intelligence chief and the prime minister's chief of staff.

A verdict against Intellexa would set the first precedent against a commercial spyware maker.
