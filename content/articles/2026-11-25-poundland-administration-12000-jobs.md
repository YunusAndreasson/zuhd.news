London — Poundland's 10,500 jobs are at risk weeks before Christmas.

It is one of the [UK](country:GB) high street's biggest collapses in recent years.

[US](country:US) owner Gordon Brothers found no buyer for the whole business, so AlixPartners now run it as administrators.

MPs accuse Gordon Brothers of pushing the chain towards insolvency and extracting fees from it.

Bids are expected for some of its almost 600 stores.
