Taipei — Trump asked Xi if he'd like to buy US weapons.

The offer, if real, would upend a 1990 US embargo central to arming [Taiwan](country:TW) against [China](country:CN).

Ambassador David Perdue disclosed the remark while defending Trump's Taiwan arms record days after the two leaders met in Washington.

The State Department says US law bars arms sales to China and there are no such plans.

Trump's on-hold $14 billion Taiwan arms package remains unresolved as the US-China trade truce runs to 10 January.
