Washington — Pentagon cut audited component reports from 23 to 15, foreign-policy outlet Inkstick reported.

Inspector general auditors could not say whether $4.8 trillion in assets are reported correctly.

The Pentagon also asked auditors to set aside internal-controls tests, limiting what they could verify in a ninth failed audit.

The department's chief financial officer says it is on course for a clean opinion on fiscal 2028 statements.

Opinions for five entities, including the Marine Corps, had not arrived when the report was signed.
