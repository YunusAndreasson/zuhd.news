London — A 3D-printed ear bone mimics natural vibration.

It could replace pricier implants for hearing loss common in poorer countries.

The middle ear works as a three-bone lever, and a mismatched implant's stiffness often forces revision surgery.

No patient has been implanted yet — this is bench and modeling work only.
