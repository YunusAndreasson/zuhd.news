La Paz — Orcas rammed a 2,000kg sunfish until it burst into fragments.

Researchers filmed adult orcas in the Gulf of California ramming sharp-tail sunfish head-on, apparently breaking the fish's dense outer layer into bite-sized pieces for calves.

Scientists don't yet know whether the behavior is taught, or simply play.
