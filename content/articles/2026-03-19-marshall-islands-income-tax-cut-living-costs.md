Diesel jumped 25% in 3 weeks across the Marshall Islands. The government of the 40,000-person island cut income tax for all workers, choosing survival over revenue after Iran war disruptions drove fuel costs to a breaking point. A universal basic income payment for 37,000 citizens takes effect March 24.
