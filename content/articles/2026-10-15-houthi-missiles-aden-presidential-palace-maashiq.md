Aden — 5 missiles hit near [Yemen](country:YE)'s presidential palace, killing 2 guards.

The palace is the Saudi-backed government's seat, and officials say the prime minister is safe.

The attack came as fighting intensified in Taiz, control of which would secure the Bab al-Mandeb strait.

The Houthis say they hit a military operations headquarters run by officers from [Saudi Arabia](country:SA).

Taiz, under Houthi siege, faces what its health ministry calls a humanitarian catastrophe.
