Beijing — China aims to bring Mars samples home.

Only [China](country:CN) can verify its own containment met planetary-protection standards.

International guidelines require sealed handling of Mars material to prevent any biological contamination on Earth.

No treaty gives outside scientists a right to inspect the landing or containment.
