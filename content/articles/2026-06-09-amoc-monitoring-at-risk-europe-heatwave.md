Washington — $368 million ocean monitor loses [US](country:US) funding.

The National Science Foundation is closing its deep-sea observatory, the main US node tracking the Atlantic meridional overturning circulation that regulates Northern Hemisphere climate.

A World Meteorological Organization report projects one year in 2026-30 will surpass the 2024 global temperature record.
