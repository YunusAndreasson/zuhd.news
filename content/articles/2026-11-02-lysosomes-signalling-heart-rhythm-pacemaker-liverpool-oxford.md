Liverpool — Lysosomes also tell the heart's pacemaker to speed up.

The finding changes what lysosomes, long viewed as the cell's recycling centres, are known to do.

Liverpool and Oxford researchers, with national and international collaborators, found the structures act as signalling centres in heart cells.

The report describes no treatment or trial based on the finding.
