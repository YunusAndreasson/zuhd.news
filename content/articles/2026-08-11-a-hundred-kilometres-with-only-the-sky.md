Valletta — Neolithic sailors reached [Malta](country:MT) without sight of land.

That crossing is direct evidence people deliberately navigated open water by the stars.

A star rising at a fixed bearing gives a repeatable course with no instrument at all.

Researchers are still dating the earliest crossing and modeling which stars were visible then.
