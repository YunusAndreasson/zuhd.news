Hong Kong — Swiss refiner Valcambi will refine gold in the city.

The city previously shipped gold to the mainland for refining before re-exporting it.

[China](country:CN)'s central bank raised its gold holdings for a 23rd straight month in September, to 2,400 tonnes.

Gold stood at $4,038 an ounce on 22 October, down 3.4% in a week.
