Al-Taff — A Jordanian airstrike killed 3 children, their uncle and grandmother.

Their father, Hadi, disappeared into Assad's dungeons years ago.

[Jordan](country:JO)'s air force said Monday's strikes avenged a Karak church funeral attack and killed over 20 Islamic State fighters.

"May God have mercy on our martyrs as well," said Abu Hadi, 70, the children's grandfather.

Mourners keep arriving at the family's huts for the three-day memorial.
