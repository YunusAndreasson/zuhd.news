Washington — Just 75 B-52s remain in service Air Force-wide.

The Government Accountability Office says the aging fleet, stretched thin by strikes on [Iran](country:IR), delayed AGM-181A cruise missile flight testing by 4 months.

27 test flights remain before operational testing starts in September 2027.
