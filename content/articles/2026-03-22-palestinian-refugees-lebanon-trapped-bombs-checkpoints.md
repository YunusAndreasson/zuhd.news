Beddawi — Neighbors in Beddawi camp are now 30 minutes apart. A Palestinian Authority-Lebanon weapons agreement gave Lebanese forces grounds to seal most of Beddawi's daily passages, trapping 18,000 residents inside a camp under 1 sq km. The Palestinian Authority has not challenged the checkpoint regime, leaving 4 Palestinian camps caught between Israeli evacuation warnings and Lebanese military control.
