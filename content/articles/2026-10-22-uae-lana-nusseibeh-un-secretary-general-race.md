United Arab Emirates — Six preliminary votes have failed to pick a UN secretary-general.

The [UAE](country:AE) nominated its UN envoy Lana Nusseibeh, who took leave from her government post.

None of the candidates so far has been acceptable to all five permanent Security Council members, including the [US](country:US).

Secretary-General Antonio Guterres's term ends 31 December, and the last preliminary ballot was 16 October.
