Switzerland — Proton staff face AI interviews that may end their employment.

The review could change roles, responsibilities and continued employment, a leaked internal email says.

After CEO Andy Yen interviewed more than 40 senior leaders, interviewers outside each staffer's reporting line will question employees across the company.

The re-interviews run from 4 to 11 November, with individual outcomes communicated afterwards.
