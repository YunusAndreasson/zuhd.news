Singapore — A court found Bloomberg acted with malice.

The High Court ordered $357,000 in damages over a 2024 article on [Singapore](country:SG)'s luxury property market, ruling it defamed ministers Tan See Leng and K. Shanmugam.

Bloomberg has not said whether it will appeal the ruling.
