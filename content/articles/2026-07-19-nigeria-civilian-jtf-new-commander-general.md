Abuja — A retired general now leads Nigeria's civilian militia.

The Civilian Joint Task Force formed 15 years ago as a state-backed auxiliary fighting alongside police and the army against Boko Haram.

Chairman Kailani Muhammad said a formal command blueprint for the force is coming, without giving a timeline.
