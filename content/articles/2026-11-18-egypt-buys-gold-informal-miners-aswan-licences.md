Aswan — [Egypt](country:EG) will pay on the spot for gold from miners it had jailed.

A government short of foreign currency now wants what its army spent months policing.

Future of Egypt, a presidency authority, will license miners at 3 offices and give receipts for gold handed in.

No one has said what price miners will get, who owns the new company or how revenue splits.

The Bani Hilal tribe, among Upper Egypt's largest, rejects the Cairo deal and demands public auctions.
