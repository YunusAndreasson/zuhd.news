Samsung posted $19.1 billion in quarterly DRAM revenue. A 40% jump gave it 36.6% of the global market, ending SK Hynix's first-ever reign as the top memory chipmaker; legacy chip prices surged 50% in Q4. State-backed Chinese chipmaker CXMT continues to undercut Samsung with half-price DDR4.
