United Arab Emirates — Xiaomi's SU7 opens pre-orders at $46,260.

It is Xiaomi's first pre-order through an authorized overseas dealer, before any official launch.

Dealer Gargash Prime Automotive lists 3 models in 13 configurations, with the YU7 at $50,350 and the Skynomad at $57,155.

Xiaomi itself has yet to announce a launch in the [UAE](country:AE).

Deliveries of some configurations begin in December 2026, the rest in February 2027.
