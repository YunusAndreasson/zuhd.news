New York — 2,234 ether stolen from Duelbits sits unmoved in one address.

The same method cost Stake, the largest crypto casino, $40 million in 2023.

Scam Sniffer says a suspected private key compromise sent funds from Ethereum, BNB Chain and Tron hot wallets to new addresses.

Co-founder Joe wrote on X that user funds are safe while the platform investigates.

Duelbits stays offline until the investigation finishes and its hot wallets are refilled.
