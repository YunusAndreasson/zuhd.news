New Delhi — [India](country:IN) just made time a regulated quantity.

Banks, exchanges and the power grid all settle transactions on timestamps, so disagreement between clocks breaks trades.

New rules force regulated sectors off foreign satellite time signals onto atomic clocks run at Delhi's national physics lab.

Compliance takes effect in 180 days, but four of five regional timing centers still aren't built.
