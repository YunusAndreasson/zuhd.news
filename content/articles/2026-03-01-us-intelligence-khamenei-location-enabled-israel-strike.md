The CIA gave Israel Supreme Leader Khamenei's location. Sharing that data, which directly enabled the killing, tests Executive Order 12333, the US presidential ban on directed assassinations of foreign leaders. Congress faces pressure to investigate an intelligence transfer that no official has explained.
