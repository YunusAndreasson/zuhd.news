Ulaanbaatar — Ukhnaa Khurelsukh will resign over a $25 million bribery allegation.

A deputy justice minister and the cabinet secretariat head, also named in the accusations, are leaving too.

Former Ulaanbaatar mayor Nyambaatar Khishgee alleges Khurelsukh received the sum over a 2019 railway contract awarded without tender to Bodi International.

Khurelsukh and Bodi both deny wrongdoing.

[Mongolia](country:MN)'s 126-member parliament votes on the resignation this week, and the speaker becomes interim president if it accepts.
