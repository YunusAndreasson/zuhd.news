Seoul — Officials caught 111 device violations at [South Korea](country:KR)'s Suneung.

The top scorer on a national tax accountant exam confessed to cheating with a miniature camera and smartphone.

Three government emergency alerts, flagging smartphones test takers had failed to hand over, exposed nearly half the cases.

"Due to my age, I am not confident I could," Education Minister Choi Kyo-jin said of catching cheaters.

President Lee Jae Myung accepted Choi's resignation Sunday, leaving the ministry's vacancy for him to fill.
