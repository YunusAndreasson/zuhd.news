Paris — School phone bans now span every income level globally. UNESCO found the bans spread to over half of countries in under 3 years, driven by evidence linking smartphones to lower test scores. The agency urged remaining countries to act before the next academic year, as smartphone use in class continues to rise in countries without bans.
