Karachi — Gulf war is redirecting Eurasian trade through [Pakistan](country:PK).

Trucks are already plying 6 new routes linking Pakistan's ports to [Iran](country:IR)'s border and Central Asia as Hormuz-bound cargo reroutes overland.

Islamabad has set no formal transit terms as new corridor rents take shape.
