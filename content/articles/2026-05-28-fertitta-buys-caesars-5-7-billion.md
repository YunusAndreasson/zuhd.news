Las Vegas — Fertitta agreed to buy Caesars Entertainment for $5.7B.

Caesars carries ~$12B in existing debt, lifting total deal value to ~$17.6B; Fertitta built his [US](country:US) gaming empire on aggressive private leverage.

Caesars can solicit competing bids until July 11; regulators and shareholders must still approve.
