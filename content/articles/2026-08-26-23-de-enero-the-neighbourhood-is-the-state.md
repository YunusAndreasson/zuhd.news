Caracas — 23 de Enero fears a joint US-Venezuelan operation.

The parish's communal councils, not the palace, may be the real target.

Neighborhood councils run housing and food and hold weapons, answering to the barrio rather than [Venezuela](country:VE)'s ministries.

Organizer Juan Contreras says an internal police source has corroborated the plan.
