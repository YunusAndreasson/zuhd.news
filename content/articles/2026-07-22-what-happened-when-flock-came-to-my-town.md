Irmo — 33 Flock cameras now watch 12,000 residents.

The town council approved 22 more license-plate cameras despite over 10 residents citing false arrests and data-security flaws documented elsewhere with the technology.

The police chief credited Flock with catching predators; no further reviews are scheduled.
