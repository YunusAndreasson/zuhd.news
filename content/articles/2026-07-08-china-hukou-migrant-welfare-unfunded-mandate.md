Beijing — 300 million migrants gain a promise on paper.

New hukou rules grant migrant workers access to urban schools and clinics, but [China](country:CN)'s government leaves the trillion-dollar bill to local officials.

Cash-strapped cities now decide whether the promised services actually reach migrant workers.
