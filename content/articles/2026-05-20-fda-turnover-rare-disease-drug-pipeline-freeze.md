San Francisco — Staff departures from the [US](country:US) Food and Drug Administration froze rare-disease drug pipelines.

These drugs require trial flexibility granted case-by-case; the officials who allowed it have been ousted.

Mahzi Therapeutics CEO Yael Weiss said investor anxiety arrives daily; she called approval certainty "a roller coaster."
