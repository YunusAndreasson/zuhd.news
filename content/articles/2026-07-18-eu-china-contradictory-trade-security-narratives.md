Brussels — Trade negotiators and security officials are pulling opposite ways.

The bloc is trying to keep [China](country:CN) trade talks and green-tech cooperation alive even as it adopts a new security framing that treats Beijing as a threat.

The mismatch leaves EU negotiators arguing partnership while its own threat designation undercuts them.
