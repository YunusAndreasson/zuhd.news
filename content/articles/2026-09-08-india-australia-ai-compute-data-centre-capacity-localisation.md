Sydney — [Australia](country:AU) has spare data-centre capacity that [India](country:IN) lacks.

India's fast-growing AI sector already outstrips domestic computing capacity today.

Training runs can often be offshored, but many real-time AI services must run close to users.

The Lowy Institute frames this as a formal policy proposal, not a signed government agreement.
