Vlora — A Kushner resort targets [Albania](country:AL)'s last wild delta.

The $6bn Kushner-Trump project on the Vjosa-Narta Protected Area triggered Albania's first anti-corruption probe and Flamingo Revolution protests.

Albania's investigation is ongoing; no permits have been granted, and protesters have pledged to block any groundbreaking.
