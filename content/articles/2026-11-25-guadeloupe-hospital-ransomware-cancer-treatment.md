Pointe-à-Pitre — Radiation therapy is suspended at Guadeloupe's largest hospital.

It serves an island of about 380,000 people, and its staff are back on pen and paper.

Hackers encrypted its databases, and [France](country:FR)'s cybersecurity agency is checking whether they entered through a compromised technology supplier.

Emergency services, dialysis and urgent cardiac procedures remain available.

Cancellations run through at least Friday, and no one has said when normal operations resume.
