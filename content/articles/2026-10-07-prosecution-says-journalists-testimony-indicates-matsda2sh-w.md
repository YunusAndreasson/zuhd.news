Cairo — [Egypt](country:EG)'s prosecution says one of six journalists confessed Brotherhood ties.

Six people who ran a fact-checking platform face a public accusation before any court hears it.

The National Security Agency held them five days without lawyers or family before a prosecutor saw them, their lawyers say.

Some defendants complained of torture at the premises, and the prosecution ordered a forensic examination of one.

The six remain in custody under counterterrorism-law provisions, and their lawyers demand release and dropped charges.
