Menlo Park — Meta patented glasses that identify your dinner guests.

Only the wearer consents — everyone else is enrolled by sitting down to eat.

The system flags faces, matches names and edits a "highlight reel" of the evening for the wearer.

It's a patent, not a product — whether it ships depends on Meta's next release.
