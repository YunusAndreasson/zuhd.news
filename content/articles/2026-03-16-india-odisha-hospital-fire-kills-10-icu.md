A short circuit killed 10 ICU patients in Cuttack. India averages over 25,000 fire deaths annually because electrical safety codes go unenforced in public hospitals serving the poor. Prime Minister Narendra Modi announced $2,000 compensation per family but suspended no officials and ordered no safety audit.
