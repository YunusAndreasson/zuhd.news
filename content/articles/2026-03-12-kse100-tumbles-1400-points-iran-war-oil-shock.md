Pakistan's benchmark Karachi Stock Exchange index fell 1,400 points. The sell-off followed oil prices crossing $100 per barrel and the collapse of IMF negotiations, tightening credit on an economy already paying elevated fuel prices during Ramadan. The Pakistani government announced plans to mediate the US-Iran conflict as the rupee fell to a new low.
