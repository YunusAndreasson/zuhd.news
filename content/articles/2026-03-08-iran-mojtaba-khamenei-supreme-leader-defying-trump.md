Mojtaba Khamenei became Iran's supreme leader under Israeli bombardment. The Assembly of Experts chose him in a hereditary appointment breaking 47 years of precedent, despite a public death threat from US President Donald Trump. Iran's Health Ministry reports 1,200 dead — among them 200 women and 200 children — with no ceasefire scheduled.
