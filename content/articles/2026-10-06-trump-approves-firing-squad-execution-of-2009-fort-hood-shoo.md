Death Penalty Action says it would be the first military execution since 1961.

Trump approved an Army firing squad on Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth's recommendation, as [US](country:US) courts-martial sentences require presidential approval.

The Secretary of the Army will set the execution's time and place, the Pentagon said.
