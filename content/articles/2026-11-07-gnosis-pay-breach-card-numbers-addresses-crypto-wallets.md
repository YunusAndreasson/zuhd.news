Europe — The breach ties customers' home addresses to their crypto wallets.

Attackers can match real-world identities to crypto holdings, raising the risk of targeted scams.

Gnosis Pay says it detected the intrusion on 3 November, began containing it within two hours and fixed the flaw.

"You had already lost me as a user but you could have at least protected my data," wrote Lefteris Karapetsas, founder of Rotki.

Gnosis Pay shuts its branded cards on 20 December and has not said how many customers were hit.
