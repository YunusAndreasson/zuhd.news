Tehran — Two sanctioned states are building an energy trade of their own.

[Iran](country:IR)'s oil minister offered to ship crude and gas oil to [Nicaragua](country:NI), citing "solidarity," despite transport costs the long distance adds.

Nicaragua's foreign minister, in Tehran for Khamenei mourning ceremonies, is expected to detail import volumes next.
