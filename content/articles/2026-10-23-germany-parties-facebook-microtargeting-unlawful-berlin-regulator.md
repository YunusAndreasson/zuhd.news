Berlin — The data regulator reprimanded 4 parties over 2021 Facebook voter targeting.

Targeting by presumed political opinion processed protected data without explicit consent, a regulator spokesperson said.

[Germany](country:DE)'s Christian Democrats, Social Democrats, Greens and Left aimed ads at chosen groups using profiles built from tracking data.

The decisions are not final, and the case against the Alternative for Germany remains open.
