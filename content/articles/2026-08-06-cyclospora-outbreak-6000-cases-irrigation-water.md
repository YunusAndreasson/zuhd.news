Washington — Cyclosporiasis cases have topped 6,000 nationwide.

Cyclospora needs days outside a host to become infectious, so contamination in Taylor Farms de Mexico lettuce traced to irrigation, not kitchens.

The FDA expects confirmed cases to keep rising for weeks, since diagnosis can lag infection by six weeks.
