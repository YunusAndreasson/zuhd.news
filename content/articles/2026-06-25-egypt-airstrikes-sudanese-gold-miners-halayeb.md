Halayeb Triangle — Egyptian airstrikes hit Sudan-border gold mines at dawn June 16.

Strikes killed 23, a Sudanese border medical source said; hundreds of war-displaced miners had been sleeping in the mines' flood channels.

Thousands evacuated; [Egypt](country:EG) has not publicly acknowledged the death toll or the scale of the operation.
