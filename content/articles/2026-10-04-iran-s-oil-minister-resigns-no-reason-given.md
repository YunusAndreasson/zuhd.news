Tehran — [Iran](country:IR)'s state oil company chief now also runs its ministry.

Iran must sell crude through a US naval blockade of its ports.

The [US](country:US) counter-blockaded Iranian ports after Tehran blocked Hormuz shipping, aiming to stop Iran's crude exports.

Shortly before, state media cited Mohsen Paknejad saying "revenues of the oil that we have sold are still coming."

A presidential official cited personal issues, and Hamid Bovard is acting minister only.
