Chicago — United Airlines' departures stalled for 90 minutes Monday.

Days earlier, a Federal Aviation Administration network failure halted all [US](country:US) air travel for hours.

Pilots told controllers the failure hit the system that calculates a plane's final weight for takeoff.

United said there was no indication it was a cybersecurity issue.

United has not confirmed the cause ahead of the Thanksgiving travel rush.
