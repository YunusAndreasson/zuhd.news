Pasadena — New paper: Sun's gravity can image stellar remnants.

The Sun bends light to a focal point 550 astronomical units away; a spacecraft there could resolve white dwarfs and black holes no built telescope can match.

Reaching 550 astronomical units — 10 times Pluto's orbit — is the mission's constraint, not its optics.
