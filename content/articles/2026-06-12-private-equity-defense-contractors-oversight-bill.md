Washington — Khanna-Warren bill targets private equity's defense stakes.

Private equity acquired over 1,500 [US](country:US) defense firms in 20 years; a 2024 study found debt-financed buyouts leave those contractors 4-9% more likely to fail.

The bill requires Pentagon review of private equity stakes above 25% and triennial checks on acquired contractors.
