Abuja — [Nigeria](country:NG)'s election body dropped Pantami and Adebutu from governorship lists.

Gombe and Ogun voters on 6 February 2027 will find no Peoples Democratic Party candidate listed.

A 24 September Supreme Court ruling restored a rule limiting party primaries to members on registers filed with the commission on time.

Ogun's opposition party says Adebutu's membership was submitted on time and blames the ruling party's pressure.

Another party says its Kano candidate, Aminu Abdussalam Gwarzo, was dropped in error and expects a fix Monday.
