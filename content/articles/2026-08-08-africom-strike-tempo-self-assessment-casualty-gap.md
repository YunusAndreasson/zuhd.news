Washington — Trump struck Nigeria twice within six months.

The strikes pull West Africa into a war-on-terror pattern confined until now to Somalia.

Washington avoided cyclical strikes in the Sahel for two decades, though officials have reportedly weighed striking Mali too.

Whether Mali is struck next depends on decisions Trump's successors may be unable to reverse.
