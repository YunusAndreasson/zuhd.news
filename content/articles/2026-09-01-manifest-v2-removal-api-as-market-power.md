Mountain View — Chrome Web Store removed uBlock Origin entirely.

The company that sells ads also controls what ad blockers can do.

Manifest V3 swapped per-request inspection for a fixed rule list the browser enforces instead.

Millions of Chrome users will find ad blockers quietly stop working, not banned outright.
