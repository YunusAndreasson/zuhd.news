Washington — Magistrate Zia Faruqui ended the case against 2 ex-FBI officials.

An FBI agent testified the inquiry began last July under FBI Director Kash Patel's direction.

Nikki Floris and Bradley Benavides were accused of lying to Congress in 2023 about a Hunter Biden laptop investigation.

"I will not bury my head in the sand and pretend I cannot read a calendar," Faruqui said.

The Justice Department may still seek a grand jury indictment.
