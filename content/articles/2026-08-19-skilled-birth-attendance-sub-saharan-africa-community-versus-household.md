Addis Ababa — 67% of sub-Saharan African births have skilled care.

The gap between Ethiopia and South Africa spans 63 percentage points, the study finds.

Eight or more antenatal visits nearly triples the odds of skilled delivery, more than household income, researchers found.

The pooled analysis is an early-access manuscript, not yet a final published version.
