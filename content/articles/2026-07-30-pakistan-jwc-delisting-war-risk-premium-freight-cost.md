Islamabad — Two decades on a danger list ends.

A London underwriters' committee had priced [Pakistan](country:PK)'s waters as dangerous, adding a war-risk premium to every ship calling at Karachi.

The ministry says the delisting will lower freight costs and make Karachi more attractive to global shipping lines.
