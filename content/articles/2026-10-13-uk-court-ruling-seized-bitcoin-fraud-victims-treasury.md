London — A court barred Zhimin Qian's victims from English-law claims.

[Britain](country:GB) is likelier to keep most of 61,000 seized bitcoin, worth £3.8 billion.

Qian's Ponzi scheme defrauded 128,000 investors in [China](country:CN), so Justice Mark Turner ruled Chinese law governs their property claims.

The ruling does not settle ownership of the coins.

The victims' lawyers are appealing.
