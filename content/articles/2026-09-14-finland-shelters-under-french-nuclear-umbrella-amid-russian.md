Helsinki — 11 European states now sit under French nuclear cover.

It expands European reliance on French, not US, nuclear deterrence.

France's president alone decides use of its estimated 290 warheads, unlike NATO's shared Nuclear Planning Group.

Estonia is weighing the same step, its premier told reporters, deepening cooperation beyond NATO's existing umbrella.
