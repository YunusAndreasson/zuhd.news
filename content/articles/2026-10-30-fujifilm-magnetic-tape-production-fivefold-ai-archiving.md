Odawara — Fujifilm is spending $63 million on a medium it first made in the 1950s.

Fujifilm says cloud giants are expanding tape use because it stores vast data cheaply.

Cartridges can be disconnected and stored in a vault, where ransomware cannot reach them, Fujifilm says.

The 10 billion yen investment runs through fiscal 2028, mainly to make its latest Ultrium10 tape.
