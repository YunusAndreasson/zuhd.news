Beijing — Far-side lunar samples contain metallic copper.

Ancient impacts in the South Pole-Aitken basin vaporized sulfide minerals, leaving metallic copper and bornite in 1,935 grams of Chang'e-6's returned soil.

China now holds the only far-side mineral data as competing crewed lunar programs from the US and others target the 2030s.
