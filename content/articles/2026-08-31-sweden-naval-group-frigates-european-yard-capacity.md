Stockholm — Each of four new frigates costs €1.1 billion.

The money goes to a French shipyard, not an American one.

[Sweden](country:SE) joined NATO in 2024 after two centuries of non-alignment, and few European yards can build warships.

Naval Group, majority owned by [France](country:FR), must now deliver on a production line already booked full.
