Geneva — 10 states, [China](country:CN) and [Pakistan](country:PK) among them, opposed the Sudan inquiry.

The Human Rights Council extended the fact-finding mission for a year, 28 votes to 10.

The resolution calls for evidence of war crimes in [Sudan](country:SD) by the army and the Rapid Support Forces to be preserved.

Most African states abstained, including Ethiopia, while South Africa, The Gambia, Malawi and Mauritius voted in favour.

It calls for the International Criminal Court to expand its investigation from Darfur to all of Sudan.
