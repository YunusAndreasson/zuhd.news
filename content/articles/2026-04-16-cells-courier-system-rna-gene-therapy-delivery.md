Dublin — Cells have an undiscovered messaging network. University College Dublin found cellular couriers with molecular keys to tissue gateways unreachable by conventional drug delivery. The discovery opens a targeting mechanism for RNA and gene therapies, whose main barrier is delivery to the right tissue.
