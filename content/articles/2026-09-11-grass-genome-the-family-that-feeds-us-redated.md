St. Louis — A new grass genome revises when cereals evolved.

Wheat, rice, maize and sorghum all descend from the family being remapped.

Ancient whole-genome duplications gave grasses spare gene copies whose order reveals shared cereal genes.

Breeders can now use the shared map to target drought and heat tolerance genes.
