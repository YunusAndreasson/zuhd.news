Pyongyang — Kim Yo Jong voided the Xi-Trump denuclearization pledge.

[North Korea](country:KP) enshrined its nuclear status in its constitution in 2023; Kim Yo Jong's statement came on the eve of Xi Jinping's first Pyongyang visit in 7 years.

[China](country:CN) is Pyongyang's economic lifeline; Xi's visit will test whether Beijing endorses the irreversible framing.
