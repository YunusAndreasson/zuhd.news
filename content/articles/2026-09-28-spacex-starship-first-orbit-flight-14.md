Starbase — Starship aimed for 6 orbits over a 10-hour flight.

A successful orbit would unlock in-space refueling that NASA's moon landings depend on.

All 13 earlier Starship flights stayed suborbital, lasting about an hour each before this attempt.

SpaceX says Starship should eventually carry up to 100 people to Mars.
