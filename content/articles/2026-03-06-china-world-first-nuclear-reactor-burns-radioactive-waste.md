China will fire the world's first waste-burning nuclear reactor. The accelerator bombards spent nuclear fuel with neutrons, turning isotopes dangerous for millennia into ones that decay in centuries. A successful test would make China the first nation to process the 400,000 tonnes of spent fuel stockpiled worldwide.
