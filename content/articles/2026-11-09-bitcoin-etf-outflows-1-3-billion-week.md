The funds are now $914 million negative for the year after turning positive on 22 September.

Over $6 billion flowed in from August, then institutions sold alongside AI stocks as the Nasdaq lost a fifth week.

Tuesday's US consumer price index could lower yields, but Marex analysts say outflows would still outweigh it.
