Tehran — [Iran](country:IR)'s cyberspace chief says Iran stopped Starlink operating in the war, Iranian state-affiliated agency Mehr reported.

Mohammad Amin Aghamiri gave no figures and no dates.

He said Iran managed some networks and platforms, Starlink included, and stopped them operating, without saying how.

The report includes no independent measurement and no response from SpaceX.
