Myanmar's junta-backed party swept 80% of parliament. A winner-takes-all electoral system inflated their 44% vote share, with opposition parties banned and millions of displaced Burmese unable to cast ballots. Parliament convenes next week with no observers invited and negotiations over the presidency to be conducted behind closed doors.
