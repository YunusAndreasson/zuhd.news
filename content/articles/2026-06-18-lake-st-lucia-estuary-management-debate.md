Lake St Lucia — 4 researchers warn the estuary is silting.

Sugar-cane farms severed the lake's main water artery in 1952; a 2012 reconnection reversed 60 years of damage that dried it.

[South Africa](country:ZA)'s iSimangaliso authority rejected the researchers' dredging plan, but sugar-cane farmers are pressing for a breach.
