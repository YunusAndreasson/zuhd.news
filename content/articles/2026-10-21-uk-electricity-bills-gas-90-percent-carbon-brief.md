London — Gas explains £161 of a £180 [UK](country:GB) bill rise.

Nesta's analysis of electricity bills since 2020 contradicts the claim that net zero drives them.

Nesta says gas still sets the marginal price of power, and over 80% of households use gas mains.

Network costs added £72, Nesta found, mostly £46 for grid investment.

Gas reached 220p a therm on 19 October, and the Bank of England decides rates on 5 November.
