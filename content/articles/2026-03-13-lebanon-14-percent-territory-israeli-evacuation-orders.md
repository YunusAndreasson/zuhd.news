Israel ordered evacuation of 1,470 sq km of Lebanon. The Norwegian Refugee Council, a humanitarian group, warned Israel's orders span far beyond active combat zones, covering areas where strikes have killed nearly 700 and displaced 800,000 since 2 March. UN peacekeepers reported fresh Israeli ground incursions on Friday.
