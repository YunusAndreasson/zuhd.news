Cupertino — Apple's AI Siri, delayed since 2024, may now run on a distilled version of Google's Gemini.

Distillation trains a small model to mimic a giant one, trading some accuracy for the privacy and latency of local processing.

Cloud routing through Google and Nvidia contradicts Apple's privacy pitch at launch.
