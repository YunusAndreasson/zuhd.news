Dart frog toxin killed Alexei Navalny. The UK and 4 European allies concluded that tissue samples from the Russian opposition leader contained epibatidine, a substance found only in wild South American frogs and 200 times more potent than morphine. British Foreign Secretary Yvette Cooper presented the findings at the Munich Security Conference on Friday. Navalny survived a Novichok nerve agent attack in 2020, returned to Russia, and spent 3 years in prison on charges widely regarded as politically motivated before dying in a Siberian penal colony in February 2024 at age 47. The 5 nations have reported Russia to the Organisation for the Prohibition of Chemical Weapons, a step that could trigger new sanctions.
