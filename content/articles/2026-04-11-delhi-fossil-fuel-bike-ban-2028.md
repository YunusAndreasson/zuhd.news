Delhi — Delhi's draft bans fossil-fuel bikes from 2028. Two-wheelers are 67% of Delhi's vehicle stock; delivery fleets must go all-electric by end-2026, 2 years ahead of the general ban. The draft accepts public comment for 30 days before Delhi issues the final policy running to 2030.
