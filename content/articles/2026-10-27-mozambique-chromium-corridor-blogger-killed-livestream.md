Ressano Garcia — Blogger Mano Shottas filmed his own fatal shooting.

Video analysis shows law enforcement were involved in his death, Forbidden Stories found.

Over 1,000 trucks a day haul chromite from [South Africa](country:ZA) to Maputo, and roadside re-tarping spills ore beside the Komati River.

"I've been shot, and they keep shooting," said Shottas, a 30-year-old father, in his final broadcast.

South Africa's environment department says its probe of four trucking firms stalled without evidence.
