London — Engineered heart muscle has entered clinical trials.

The heart cannot regenerate cardiomyocytes; advanced failure kills up to 50% of patients yearly, studies show — the gap lab-grown tissue grafts aim to close.

Early trials must show grafted tissue integrates without arrhythmia before progression to wider studies.
