Israeli troops entered southern Lebanon Monday. Israel calls the incursion a "forward defence zone" — the strategy that turned its 1982 Lebanon invasion into an 18-year occupation. Over 800,000 Lebanese are already displaced from the south — Israel ordered more evacuations as fighting centred on Khiam.
