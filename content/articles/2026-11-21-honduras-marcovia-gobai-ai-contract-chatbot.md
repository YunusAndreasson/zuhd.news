Marcovia — A $71,000 chatbot told testers it serves only the capital.

Gobai Ventures, which runs Tegucigalpa's AI unit, has sold a similar service to a second town hall in [Honduras](country:HN).

The 2-year contract buys a WhatsApp assistant, ticket sorting, a social-listening radar and a dashboard.

Five of 6 payments, 368,000 lempiras each, fall due on progress reports before 31 December 2027.
