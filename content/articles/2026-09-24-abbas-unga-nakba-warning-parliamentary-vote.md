Ramallah — Abbas promises the first Palestinian legislative vote in 20 years.

Fatah officials doubt it, citing [Israel](country:IL)'s refusal to let ballot boxes into Gaza.

Palestinians have held no legislative or presidential election since 2005-06, and Abbas has set no presidential date.

"Do not allow another Nakba to occur," Abbas told the General Assembly by video, denied a [US](country:US) visa.

Candidate applications open Saturday, and Fatah has not said whether it has assembled a list.
