Hebron — [Israel](country:IL) displaced 2,200 people since Friday, the mayor says.

Residents of a city of 200,000 can neither enter nor leave, the mayor said.

Netanyahu ordered the lockdown after gunmen killed 2 soldiers, and bulldozers are razing homes in Farsh al-Hawa.

The Israeli military says it is dismantling militant infrastructure and expects the operation to last several days.

Israelis vote Tuesday, and Netanyahu has promised the Palestinian Authority an unspecified surprise.
