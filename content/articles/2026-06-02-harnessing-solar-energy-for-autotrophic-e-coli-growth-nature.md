Cambridge — E. coli evolved to grow on solar-derived formate, skipping sugar feedstock.

Adaptive lab evolution enhanced the bacteria's formate metabolism; replacing electricity with simulated sunlight closed the solar-to-biomass loop, researchers said.

The system offers a path to making fuels and chemicals without fossil feedstock.
