Kyiv — The EU will send $3.7 billion for weapons.

The money buys missiles and drones, including [US](country:US) Patriot interceptors against Russian strikes.

It's part of a $32.9 billion EU defense loan for [Ukraine](country:UA), $13.4 billion now paid out.

The tranche is smaller than promised, delayed by unprocessed Ukrainian equipment receipts.
