Beijing — Banks, cafes and telcos now hand out free AI.

The giveaway makes AI inference a routine consumer perk instead of a rationed resource.

Credit-card issuers and coffee chains in [China](country:CN) are bundling the tokens the way they once bundled points.

Coffee chains and card issuers have not said how long the free tokens will last.
