Brasília — Lula won 247 of 431 relocated polling sections.

Abstention in the moved sections hit 20.36%, above the 19.2% national runoff rate.

Electoral court records listed 4,352 relocations, then 2,033, then a final 431 on runoff day.

Turnout in the 247 sections checked still rose 1.4% between rounds, and Lula gained votes in 233.

In Minas Gerais, 8 of 37 checked sections that backed Bolsonaro in round one switched to Lula.
