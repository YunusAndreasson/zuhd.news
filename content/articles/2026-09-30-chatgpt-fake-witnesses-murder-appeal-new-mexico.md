New Mexico — A lawyer's murder appeal cited witnesses ChatGPT invented.

The state Supreme Court held Stephen Aarons in direct contempt after judges caught the fabrications.

Aarons assumed ChatGPT would produce a reliable summary, and it invented testimony from people such as Danny and Linda Stanton.

Aarons awaits a disciplinary board and has not told his client, who is serving life.
