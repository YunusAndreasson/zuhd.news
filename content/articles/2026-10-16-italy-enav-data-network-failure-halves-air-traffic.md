Rome — Cirium counted 192 cancellations across Italy by 1000 GMT.

Only 31% of departures from [Italy](country:IT)'s airports left on time, 25% at Malpensa and 28% at Fiumicino.

A fault in Italy's nationwide air traffic data network cut capacity to 50% from 6am.

The air navigation provider excluded malicious activity, including a hacker attack or sabotage.

A satellite backup restored 75% of capacity, but the provider has not named a cause or restored the primary network.
