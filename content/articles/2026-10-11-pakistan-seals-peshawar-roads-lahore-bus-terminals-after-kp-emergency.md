Peshawar — Shipping containers now block roads across the city.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s federal government on Sunday effectively suspended the province's elected Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf government.

Articles 232 and 234, approved by the president, let the centre take over a province in an emergency.

In Lahore, bus terminals were sealed and intercity services suspended, though motorway police say roads are open.

The march demanding Imran Khan's release, begun 4 October, has not yet left Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
