Kazakhstan — Halyk Bank shareholders voted to seek an Islamic banking licence.

It would put Sharia-compliant finance on the largest banking platform in [Kazakhstan](country:KZ) by assets.

Three scholars, including Malaysia's Aznan Hasan, will sit on a one-year Islamic Financing Principles Council overseeing compliance.

The announcement names no regulator decision date.
