Tehran — [Iran](country:IR)'s foreign minister says its 60% uranium stockpile stays home.

Abbas Araghchi called Iran's civilian-only nuclear policy a "major strategic debate" as officials weigh dropping it.

The International Atomic Energy Agency put the stockpile at about 440 kg in 2025, short of 90% weapons grade.

Vice President JD Vance declined Friday to back Trump's unverified claim that Iran was two weeks from a bomb.

Araghchi said the doctrine has not changed for now, but Washington wants guarantees before any ceasefire.
