China — Shanda's NovaCatapult offers AI startups 1 million yuan and computing power.

Shanda, one of China's earliest internet companies, is betting on one-person AI firms.

Selected teams get priority on its in-house computing cluster, and the money arrives when the company is formed.

Applications for the second cohort are open, and the first began a 3-month incubation.
