Erbil — Kurdistan's 2 rival armies will miss a September deadline.

The Kurdistan Democratic Party and Patriotic Union of Kurdistan each retain separate forces; a US-backed 35-point reform memorandum of understanding expires in September unfinished, Lt. Gen. Mohammed said.

Kurdish forces enter the post-deadline period divided, facing pressure from [Iraq](country:IQ), [Turkey](country:TR) and [Iran](country:IR).
