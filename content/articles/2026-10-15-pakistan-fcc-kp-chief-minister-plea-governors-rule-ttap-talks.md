Islamabad — The court said Sohail Afridi no longer holds office.

[Pakistan](country:PK)'s government imposed emergency rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa on Sunday, and Chief Minister Afridi is in custody.

The Federal Constitutional Court ruled the petition moot, and the petitioner may refile if the emergency lifts and Afridi returns.

Petitioner Sher Afzal Khan Marwat says he will file a challenge to the emergency itself on Friday.

An opposition alliance suspended talks until it meets jailed Imran Khan and receives details of his health.
