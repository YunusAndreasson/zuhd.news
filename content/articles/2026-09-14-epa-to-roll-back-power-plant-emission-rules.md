Houston — Coal plants no longer must capture their emissions.

The repeal revalues aging coal assets that Biden-era rules had priced toward retirement.

Environmental Protection Agency Administrator Lee Zeldin says the repeal saves $310 billion, arguing the rules forced plants to close rather than comply.

Environmental groups and states are expected to challenge the repeal in court.
