London — A new platform inserts yield, drought, and disease genes simultaneously, skipping decades of sequential crosses.

Conventional breeding stacks traits one generation at a time; this compresses the process into a single step, cutting development cost.

Access terms are undisclosed; similar tools have historically bypassed smallholder markets.
