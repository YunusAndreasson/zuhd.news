Mumbai — [India](country:IN)'s first rate hike since 2023 passed 6-0.

Retail inflation has risen 10 straight months, reaching 4.8% in August against a 4% target.

India imports nearly 85% of its fuel, so the Iran war's disruption of Hormuz supplies arrives as higher prices.

Governor Sanjay Malhotra said rate cuts are "off the table in the near-term."

HSBC and Goldman Sachs expect another hike in December.
