Chicago — The oldest known octopus fossil is not an octopus. New imaging revealed anatomical features invisible to earlier researchers—reclassifying a Carboniferous specimen held as the octopus lineage's anchor for decades. The octopus lineage is now roughly 100 million years younger than the evolutionary timelines it anchored.
