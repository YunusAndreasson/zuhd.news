Kabul — She built a detergent business, then Taliban police arrested her.

Every Afghan businesswoman must now pay a male intermediary just to trade.

Since 2021, [Afghanistan](country:AF)'s rulers have banned women from education and most work, confining commerce to home-based trades.

Officers still threaten to close businesses that keep female staff.
