Leiden — Fat-clogged immune cells drive severe multiple sclerosis cases, researchers found.

Microglia normally clear myelin; in severe cases they become fat-loaded and switch from repair to worsening inflammation, Leiden University researchers said.

Blocking 1 overactive enzyme improved tissue healing in mice; human trials have not begun.
