Lagos — Africa's free trade fund sought Nigerian institutional capital. The fund compensates governments for tariff revenue they lose by opening borders, reducing the political pressure to abandon Africa's 54-nation trade pact. Nigeria, Africa's largest economy, would anchor the fund's capitalization, and its decision will shape whether smaller economies join or hedge.
