One developer built autonomous wildfire tracking from satellite data. Signet ingests NASA fire detection data and GOES-19 thermal imagery, correlating fire behavior with weather, terrain, and population data without waiting for a human to trigger each analysis cycle. ZIP code email alerts are live now, with SMS alerts announced as a future expansion.
