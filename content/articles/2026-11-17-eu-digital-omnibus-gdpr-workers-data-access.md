Brussels — Uber drivers used data-access requests to force disclosure in court.

The Commission skipped an impact assessment before proposing limits on those requests.

The proposal could restrict access requests made for purposes beyond data protection, such as gathering evidence in employment disputes.

The Commission says the package saves up to €5 billion in administrative costs by 2029.

The European Parliament is now debating the Digital Omnibus.
