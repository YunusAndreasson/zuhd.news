Kumamoto — Rescuers are still pulling survivors from a collapsed shopping mall.

The blast that tore open the Aeon mall, in a country with the world's strictest seismic code, is still unexplained; 260,000 people were told to evacuate.

Prime Minister Takaichi called it a race against time as survival odds fall after 72 hours.
