Tehran — [Iran](country:IR)'s state-run Mehr News Agency says the [US](country:US) answered its Hormuz conditions.

The reply answers seven conditions Tehran set for reopening the Strait of Hormuz and resuming talks.

Iran passed its conditions to the UAE via Qatar, foreign ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei said on 23 September.

"We will try to finalize Islamabad understanding," President Pezeshkian said Wednesday.

Foreign Minister Araghchi has briefed Pezeshkian, but the US proposal's terms remain unreported.
