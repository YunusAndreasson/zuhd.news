Colón — One of the world's largest contraband markets sits here.

[Panama](country:PA)'s dollarized economy and the Colón Free Zone's trade volume let Colombian and Mexican trafficking networks move drug cash through legitimate shipping, InSight Crime found.

Authorities arrested 25 people July 7 over a port-worker cocaine ring bound for Europe; the zone's oversight gap remains.
