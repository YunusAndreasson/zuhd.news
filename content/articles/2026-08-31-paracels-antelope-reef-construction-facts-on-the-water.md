Hanoi — Vietnam demands China halt building on two Paracel features.

No such construction in the disputed Spratlys has ever been reversed.

[China](country:CN) has held Antelope Reef since seizing the Paracels from [Vietnam](country:VN) by force in 1974.

ASEAN has spent two decades failing to agree on a binding Code of Conduct.
