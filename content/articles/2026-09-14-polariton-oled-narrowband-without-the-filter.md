London — This green organic LED (OLED) does not shift color when tilted.

Ordinary narrowband OLEDs drift hue off-angle, a flaw in every tilted phone or headset screen.

Researchers coupled the emitter to a light-matter layer, hybridizing them so color purity comes from coupling, not filtering.

The design reached over 20% efficiency, more than doubling prior polariton OLED performance.
