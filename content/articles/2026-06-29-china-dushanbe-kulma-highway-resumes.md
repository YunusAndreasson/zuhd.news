Dushanbe — [China](country:CN) restarted [Tajikistan](country:TJ) highway work after fatal attacks.

2 Chinese workers were killed on the Dushanbe-Kulma road in November 2025; construction has resumed despite continued security risks on the [Afghanistan](country:AF) border.

The highway links Dushanbe to China via the Afghan border region; no government has announced new security arrangements for the resumed work.
