Kyiv — [Ukraine](country:UA) intercepted 4 of 36 Russian ballistic missiles.

Heat and water faltered in a city of 3 million as temperatures neared zero.

[Russia](country:RU) hit substations carrying power from 2 nuclear plants and damaged the Northern Bridge, Ukraine's Energy Ministry said.

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy also blames city hall's winter preparations, which Mayor Vitali Klitschko rejects.

Ukrainian and Russian officials meet in Abu Dhabi for three-way talks within days.
