Gaza City — [Israel](country:IL) struck Gaza's Islamic University twice in hours, killing 3.

Gaza's health ministry counts 1,551 dead since the ceasefire, and at least 7 died Sunday.

A drone strike first wounded 7 female students, then an air strike killed the 3 staff.

The Israeli military said only that it was looking into the reports.
