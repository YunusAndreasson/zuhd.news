Quetta — A bomb hit Quetta's Chaman Phatak commuter train Sunday.

[Pakistan](country:PK) Railways confirmed 3 coaches derailed; workers commuting from Quetta Cantonment were on board.

No group claimed responsibility by Sunday morning.
