---
title: "Gel Heals Lab-Grown Spinal Cords"
date: "2026-02-20T00:00:00Z"
source: "Nature"
sourceUrl: "https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-00512-0"
category: "science"
---

Paralyzed mice walked again after a single injection. The same treatment reversed injury in lab-grown human spinal cords, regrowing neurons and dissolving scar tissue in a Northwestern University study. The FDA granted the therapy Orphan Drug Designation as researchers develop more advanced organoid models before clinical trials.
