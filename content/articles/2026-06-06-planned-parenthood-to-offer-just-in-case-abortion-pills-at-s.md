Seattle — Planned Parenthood will give mifepristone before pregnancy at Washington and Hawaii clinics.

The advance doses cut time and travel barriers; abortion is restricted in 41 [US](country:US) states.

The FDA is reviewing mifepristone access nationally; clinics will not remotely prescribe to patients in restricted states.
