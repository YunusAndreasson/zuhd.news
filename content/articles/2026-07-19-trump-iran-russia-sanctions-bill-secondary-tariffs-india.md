Washington — Trump wants Iran folded into the Russia sanctions bill.

The bill imposes 100% tariffs on the top 5 buyers of Russian crude — [China](country:CN), [India](country:IN), Slovakia, Hungary, Azerbaijan — reassessed every 180 days.

Democratic co-sponsor Blumenthal is resisting the addition, with no Senate vote yet scheduled.
