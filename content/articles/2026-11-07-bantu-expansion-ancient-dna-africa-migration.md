Great Zimbabwe — East African Bantu genomes match Angola's better than Cameroon's.

Around 350 million Africans speak one of roughly 500 Bantu languages.

A 2023 study compared genomes from [Angola](country:AO), Mozambique and [Cameroon](country:CM) and found the migration split only at the rainforest's southern edge.

Archaeologists still cannot say whether Bantu speakers invented iron smelting or learned it from neighbours.
