Luxembourg City — A Huawei zero-day caused last year's nationwide telecom outage in [Luxembourg](country:LU), investigators confirmed.

Single-vendor concentration meant one flaw took all telephony offline; Huawei has not acknowledged the vulnerability.

A year on, the flaw is unexplained and no EU audit of Huawei national infrastructure has begun.
