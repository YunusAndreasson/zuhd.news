Prolonged confinement, disrupted sleep and team dynamics aboard the ICE-Blue mission mirror what [Italy](country:IT)'s Space Agency says a Mars-class flight demands.

Results will be compared with astronaut samples from the Axiom-3 mission to the International Space Station.
