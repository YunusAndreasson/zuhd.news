Nigeria — Reportedly 340 million barrels of future crude back 11.2 trillion naira in loans.

Presidential candidate Seyi Makinde's campaign says the lenders, rates and fees remain undisclosed.

Fixed repayments under four arrangements, Gazelle, Gazelle II, Leopard and Leopard II, could cap gains when oil prices rise, the campaign argues.

The campaign wants [Nigeria](country:NG)'s National Assembly to hold an open hearing before the 2027 election.
