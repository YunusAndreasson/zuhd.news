Washington — Saudi Arabia pledges Pakistan $3bn in new deposits. The pledge extends an existing $5bn facility for 3 more years, announced the same week Pakistan must repay $3.5bn to the UAE. Pakistan's IMF programme requires unbroken Gulf support; a reserve breach would trigger a bailout review.
