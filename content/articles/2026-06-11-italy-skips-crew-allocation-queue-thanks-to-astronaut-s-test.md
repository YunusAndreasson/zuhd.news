Houston — Italy's Parmitano took Artemis III, overriding Germany's allocation.

The [US](country:US) space agency retooled Artemis III as a low-Earth-orbit test, making Parmitano's test-pilot background decisive for [Italy](country:IT) over [Germany](country:DE)'s expected slot.

[Germany](country:DE) and [France](country:FR) remain in line; the first crewed lunar landing now targets Artemis IV, 2028.
