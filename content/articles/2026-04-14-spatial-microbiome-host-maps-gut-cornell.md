Ithaca — Cornell mapped where gut bacteria meet human cells. Enzymatic polyadenylation on frozen tissue sections delivered 99-fold bacterial RNA enrichment, revealing which species concentrate in which gut region at single-cell resolution. The same method applied to human biopsies could reshape how microbiome-targeted drugs are designed.
