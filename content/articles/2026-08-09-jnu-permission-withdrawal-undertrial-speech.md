New Delhi — Jawaharlal Nehru University cancelled a discussion it had already approved.

No ban was issued, so students have no order to challenge and no venue.

The author, former student Umar Khalid, has been jailed without trial since 2020 under a law with a near-unmeetable bail threshold.

The students' union says the discussion on Adivasi history will go ahead outdoors Monday.
