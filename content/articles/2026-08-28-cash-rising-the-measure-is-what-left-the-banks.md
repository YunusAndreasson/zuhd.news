Moscow — 4.2 trillion rubles are shifting into cash.

Unrecorded cash weakens the central bank's grip on inflation and signals distrust in deposits.

Sberbank's Taras Skvortsov told TASS the shift already exceeds the bank's own forecast for July.

Skvortsov says regulatory action is needed, though none has been announced.
