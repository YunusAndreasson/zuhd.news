Washington — Approved immigrant visa petitions stopped moving this week.

Petitioners have no denial to appeal, and no court claim to file.

The [US](country:US) State Department froze processing rather than rejecting applications, an administrative delay courts rarely review.

The State Department has not said when the queue moves again.
