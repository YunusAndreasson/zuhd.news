Sydney — Annual inflation hit 4.9%, despite 125 basis points of hikes.

[Australia](country:AU)'s jobless rate is already at a five-year high of 4.9%.

The trimmed mean core gauge rose to 3.8% from 3.6%, the statistics bureau reported, as goods prices jumped 1%.

Westpac chief economist Luci Ellis expects October to mark the peak and November headline inflation to turn negative.

Markets now see a June rate cut as a 75% chance, down from near certain.
