Washington — [US](country:US) Pentagon named Alibaba, BYD, and Baidu military companies.

The 1260H designation bars Defense contracts and third-party procurement from 2027, without requiring public evidence of a weapons link.

All 3 firms denied the label; [China](country:CN)'s embassy called it discriminatory. Firms may petition for removal.
