Sriharikota — Skyroot placed 6 payloads in orbit.

The 22-metre rocket succeeded on its first attempt, making [India](country:IN) the third nation with private orbital launch capability.

Skyroot plans further test flights before starting routine commercial missions in the small-satellite launch market.
