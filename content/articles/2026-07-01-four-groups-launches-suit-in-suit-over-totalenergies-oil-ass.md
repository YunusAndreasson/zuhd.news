Paris — Four groups are suing to see Total's exit terms.

France's Duty of Vigilance Law requires the firm to prevent harm from its sale of [Nigeria](country:NG) oil assets to local buyer Vaaris.

A French court will decide whether Total must disclose who pays to clean up the Niger Delta's spills.
