US President Trump threatened Kharg Island strikes 'just for fun.' Kharg handles 90% of Iran's oil exports — destroying it would cut revenues funding government salaries, subsidies, and the war effort. Iran's Revolutionary Guard said Sunday's missile strikes on Israel and 3 US bases were 'the first round' of retaliation.
