Brussels — [Germany](country:DE) nominated Markus Brunnermeier as European Central Bank chief economist.

[France](country:FR) ceded the post and takes the seat overseeing market operations instead.

A candidate needs 16 countries holding 65% of the population, so a northern candidate without German backing struggles.

Think-tank chair David Marsh says the chief economist's post matters less than under Issing and Stark.

Candidacies close 20 November at 17:00 Brussels time, and EU leaders may approve the posts next week.
