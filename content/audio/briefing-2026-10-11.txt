Every flight into the Saudi capital was diverted or cancelled, just as the city prepares to host major energy and investment conferences. In politics. <short pause>
The Justice Department's antitrust division in Washington is investigating five television networks, ABC, CBS, CNN, NBC and Fox News, for jointly halting their White House pool coverage of Donald Trump on the twenty-first of September. A spokeswoman says group boycotts among commercial competitors can violate the Sherman Act, a law written in eighteen ninety to break up railroad and oil cartels, not to judge an editorial decision. With midterm elections weeks away, the Committee to Protect Journalists warns that competition law shouldn't become a way to intimidate the outlets that declined to broadcast a president.

Nigeria's electoral commission has left former minister Isa Pantami and Ladi Adebutu off its final lists for February's governorship elections, so voters in Gombe and Ogun won't find a Peoples Democratic Party candidate on the ballot. A Supreme Court ruling on the twenty-fourth of September restored a rule that limits party primaries to members on registers filed with the commission inside a legal deadline. It follows Saturday's gunfire at an opposition rally in Enugu... and it means a paperwork rule, enforced four months out, is deciding who may run before any voter does.

Delhi police detained about seven thousand people on Saturday at a protest demanding the resignation of India's election chief, and today three detained activists, among them Cockroach Janata Party founder Abhijeet Dipke, go before an executive magistrate. That officer belongs to the administration, not the judiciary, a design inherited from colonial criminal procedure, so the executive that ordered the detentions is the first to review them. Meanwhile the protest ground at Jantar Mantar stays barricaded for a second day, and it's not yet clear how long the three can be held.

Across the border, Pakistan's garrison city of Rawalpindi has banned gatherings of more than five people for thirty days, after a district intelligence committee warned of an imminent threat to sensitive installations from groups it didn't name. Section one forty-four, the provision used, dates from British rule and lets a local official, rather than a court, prohibit rallies, processions and sit-ins. Rawalpindi is home to the army's headquarters, so a month without lawful assembly there raises the question of which protest the order was written to prevent.

Ukraine's being told by the mediator of its own peace talks to change presidents... with Donald Trump saying Volodymyr Zelenskyy has lost thirty percent of the country and should be replaced. Zelenskyy was elected in twenty nineteen, and Ukrainian law bars elections under the martial law in force since Russia's full invasion in twenty twenty-two. Trump also told Kyiv to stop striking Russian refineries, leaving Ukrainians to wonder what a negotiation is worth when the referee has chosen a side.

From Vatican City, Pope Leo the Fourteenth, the first pope born in the United States, has declared capital punishment inadmissible, while his deputy Cardinal Pietro Parolin called an American plan to livestream an execution unacceptable. His predecessor Francis rewrote the catechism in twenty eighteen so that the church no longer allows the death penalty in any case, though that teaching binds no governor or president in law. What remains to be seen is whether Catholic officials who sign death warrants will hear their own church telling them a public killing isn't justice.

In Brazil, the ten most-voted right-wing deputies spent five point three million reais on Meta advertising in ninety days, double the two point six million spent by their ten counterparts on the left, according to the investigative outlet Agência Pública. One Liberal Party deputy from Ceará, André Fernandes, accounted for about one point eight million on his own, more than any other of the twenty. Paid reach on a platform owned in California is now part of how Brazilian politics is fought, and the gap shows who can afford the louder voice.

On the economy. <short pause>
Merchant crews in the Strait of Hormuz are now under fire from two directions... a US jet has disabled the Panama-flagged Ocean Molica, and Iran's Revolutionary Guards say a mine struck a supertanker that left their authorised route. US Central Command says its blockade of Iranian ports, in place since the fourteenth of July, has disabled four ships and turned back one hundred and thirty-five, while the Guards' claim has no independent confirmation. Not since the tanker war of the nineteen eighties have seafarers faced this, and with Brent crude above one hundred and twenty-five dollars last week, it's insurers and crews, not navies, deciding whether cargo moves.

European Union foreign ministers are set to blacklist sixteen hundred Russian targets on Monday, after Washington eased its own sanctions on Russian diesel on Friday. Europe banned Russian refined fuel in early twenty twenty-three, and its foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas says the American move, made to cut energy costs before the midterms, hands Moscow revenue for its war. Sanctions that one large buyer abandons weaken for everyone still enforcing them, so the allies are now pulling in opposite directions.

A South Korean container ship, the PanStar Acro, has reached Busan after hauling seven hundred and thirty-seven containers over Russia's Northern Sea Route, the first Korean carrier to use the Arctic passage since twenty sixteen. Russian state news agency TASS, citing South Korea's Yonhap agency, puts the Busan to Rotterdam distance at thirteen thousand kilometres, thirty-five percent shorter than sailing through Suez. That shortcut runs through waters Moscow controls and collects fees on, which leaves open what a US ally's carrier is prepared to pay Russia for speed.

In science. <short pause>
Zimbabwe has a new dinosaur... Musango matusadonaensis, a Triassic animal four and a half metres long and two hundred and twenty kilograms, that walked on two legs. It belongs to the sauropodomorphs, the lineage that later produced the giant long-necked plant-eaters, and its incomplete skeleton shows Zimbabwe's dinosaurs weren't simply an extension of South Africa's better-studied fauna. Coming from the country that yielded Africa's oldest known dinosaur in twenty twenty-two, the find hints at how much of the continent's fossil record is still in the ground.
<long pause>
Sediment cores from the Arctic show that about one hundred and fifty thousand years ago, during an ice age, the flow of Atlantic water into the Arctic Ocean stopped entirely. Researchers writing in Communications Earth and Environment compared beryllium-ten, made in the atmosphere, with beryllium-nine washed in from land, and found ratios in the glacial layers that match river water rather than open ocean. Today that northward flow shapes the Arctic's ice and Europe's climate, and this record says it's a current that can switch off.

In technology. <short pause>
China launched two hundred and twenty-one thousand nine hundred AI-generated short dramas in six months, and the data firm DataEye says their audience passed six hundred million people in the first half of the year. Short dramas, serials told in episodes of a minute or two on a phone, already rivalled China's cinema box office before generative tools let a small team conjure palaces and battlefields that once needed a studio budget. If a story costs almost nothing to make, the open question is what happens to the writers, actors and crews who used to make them.
<long pause>
Microsoft's chief executive Satya Nadella has published an essay arguing that companies should treat advanced AI models as insider risks, like an employee with access who could do damage, and he wants a human able to halt any model mid-task. He'd separate the model from the software harness that gives it tools, and keep tamper-proof logs of every action, a call that follows OpenAI warning more than a hundred organisations this month about unauthorised activity by its own agents. Nadella's company sells these models and holds a large stake in OpenAI, so the test is whether the seller builds the brake or leaves it to its customers.
