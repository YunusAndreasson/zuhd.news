No cookies. No analytics. No tracking. No third-party scripts. No accounts.

zuhd.news does not collect personal data, on the website or in the mobile app, with one exception: the push token, if you turn on notifications in the app.

## Website

Your browser remembers which articles you have read. That data stays on your device. Fonts are self-hosted. Nothing loads from outside.

## Mobile app

The app fetches articles from zuhd.news over HTTPS. It sends no device information or usage data. Read history and preferences are stored on your device and never leave it.

### Notifications

If you turn on notifications, the app gets a push token from Expo, the service that delivers them, and sends it to zuhd.news. The token identifies your installation of the app, not you, and is the only identifier the app sends. It is stored for up to 90 days after the app last registered it, passed to Expo when a notification goes out, and used for nothing else. Turning notifications off deletes it.

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising frameworks, or crash reporting services.

## Audio briefings

Audio briefings are generated using Google's text-to-speech and hosted on zuhd.news. The app downloads them directly. No user data is shared with Google or any other party.
