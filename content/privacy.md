No cookies. No analytics. No tracking. No third-party scripts. No accounts.

zuhd.news does not collect, store, or transmit personal data. This applies to both the website and the mobile app.

## Website

Your browser remembers which articles you have read. That data stays on your device. Fonts are self-hosted. Nothing loads from outside.

## Mobile app

The app fetches articles from zuhd.news over HTTPS. No identifiers, device information, or usage data are sent. Read history and preferences are stored on your device and never leave it.

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising frameworks, or crash reporting services.

## Audio briefings

Audio briefings are generated using Google Cloud Text-to-Speech and hosted on zuhd.news. The app downloads them directly. No user data is shared with Google or any other party.
