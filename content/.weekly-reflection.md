## Top Stories This Week

**1. Iran-US-Israel War (dominant arc)**
The week began with the war in its second week (Day 12) and ended with Day 23+. Trump declared it "almost over" repeatedly while US intelligence concluded Iran's regime would not collapse. Israel switched to targeting regime infrastructure but Haaretz reported no certainty about the outcome. The war accelerated: Zelenskyy arrived to lobby for US backing, France sent spies undercover, the US offered $10M rewards for senior IRGC figures. Bahrain revealed it had intercepted 337 projectiles (125 missiles, 212 drones) — a small Gulf state bearing disproportionate defense burden. Bellingcat published satellite imagery documenting strikes on civilian police stations. Iran's interim council raised minimum wage 60% in wartime populism.

**2. Hormuz/Suez Economic Shock (linked arc)**
Both major shipping chokepoints (Hormuz and Red Sea/Suez) remained blocked. The IEA released a record 400 million barrels from strategic reserves, now down to one-third of capacity. Two competing responses crystallised: a US-led naval escort coalition (high escalation risk) versus India as BRICS chair negotiating directly with Tehran. The fuel shock cascaded: Egypt triple-pressured by inflation, currency collapse and Suez closure; Malaysia fuel black markets emerging; Australia discussing rationing; EU reversing its nuclear exit policy; airlines doubling fuel surcharges. Russia emerged as a beneficiary — its oil revenues rising on inflated prices.

**3. Lebanon-Israel War**
Israel escalated from southern Lebanon to Beirut proper, double-tapping displaced civilian sites at the seafront. 850,000+ displaced in ten days — one in seven Lebanese. 826+ killed, 38+ medics. Israel-Lebanon talks were announced for the first time since the ground invasion began, but strikes continued killing civilians on the same day the talks were announced. West Bank: settler attacks and an IDF unit killing a family — violence accelerating under Iran war cover with little international scrutiny.

**4. Pakistan's Systemic Economic Collapse**
Eight articles over the week traced Pakistan's deterioration from a news story into a structural crisis: four-day work week, school closures, IMF talks collapsed, gas cuts during Ramadan sehri and iftar, Rs 40 kerosene hike hitting the poorest. A nuclear state in economic collapse — almost entirely invisible in Western coverage.

**5. Sudan Civil War**
War metastasised: RSF drone campaigns targeting civilian markets and student populations across Kordofan; armed RSF militias crossed into South Sudan killing 169. Darfur starvation documented as war crime by new RSF evidence. Sudan army recaptured Bara but the drone campaign spread the front. Covered in fewer than 10 articles despite being a crisis of enormous scale — Iran war crowding out global attention.
