Zuhd is the Arabic concept of detachment from excess — not deprivation, but liberation. As Al-Junayd stated: *"Zuhd is to free the heart from wanting."*

Applied to news, that means stripping away everything that competes with the story. Every article is three to five sentences. What happened, why it matters, what comes next. No opinion, no advertising, no engagement mechanics.

Brevity without breadth is a blind spot. The site draws from [40 sources](/sources) across six continents because no single vantage point contains the whole truth. A reporter in Cairo sees what London misses. A newsroom in Jakarta hears what Washington ignores. Stories are framed from the people most affected, not the capitals reacting.

Articles are selected and written by AI, guided by editorial rules set by a single human editor. What is absent is as deliberate as what is present.

— [Yūnus Andréasson](https://x.com/YunusAndreasson)
