Zuhd (زهد) is the Arabic concept of detachment from excess — not deprivation, but liberation. As Al-Junayd stated: *"Zuhd is to free the heart from wanting."*

The modern news landscape asks you to want. Your attention, your clicks, your outrage, your time. Every headline competes with an advertisement. Every story is surrounded by opinion, decoration, and algorithmic nudges designed to keep you scrolling. The news itself — the facts, the events, the things that actually happened — gets buried in noise.

zuhd.news is a response to that excess. Each article is a few sentences. What happened, why it matters, what comes next. No opinion, no advertising, no engagement mechanics. The reader decides what to think.

We draw from sources across regions and editorial traditions — not to present "both sides," but because no single vantage point contains the whole truth. All state actors are described with consistent language. All legal labels are attributed, never stated as fact. All victims are treated with equal weight. A story about the African Union centers the African Union.

What is absent is as deliberate as what is present.
