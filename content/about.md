Zuhd (زهد) is the Arabic concept of detachment from excess — not deprivation, but liberation. As Al-Junayd stated: *"Zuhd is to free the heart from wanting."*

Each article is a few short paragraphs. What happened, why it matters, what comes next. No opinion, no advertising, no engagement mechanics. The reader decides what to think.

We read [40 sources](/sources) across continents and languages because no single vantage point contains the whole truth. A newsroom in Algiers sees what London misses. A reporter in Cairo hears what Washington ignores. Every story is framed from the people most affected, not from the capitals reacting.

What is absent is as deliberate as what is present.
