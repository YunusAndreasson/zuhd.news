# zuhd.news

*News for all humans.*

---

## Philosophy

Zuhd (زهد) is the Arabic concept of detachment from excess — not deprivation, but liberation. As Al-Junayd stated: *"Zuhd is to free the heart from wanting."* A zahid does not reject the world; they refuse to be enslaved by it.

Applied to news: the modern information landscape is defined by excess. Excess of opinion, decoration, distraction, advertising, engagement mechanics, and algorithmic manipulation. Every element competes for attention that should belong to the story.

zuhd.news begins from the position that news is words, and words deserve a design that serves them — not one that competes with them. The interface should feel complete and intentional, not stripped down. Every element that remains has earned its place. What is absent is as deliberate as what is present.

The philosophy and the aesthetic are indistinguishable.

---

## First Principles

- **Accurate.** Verified before published. Sources cited. Corrections issued openly.
- **Unbiased.** Facts presented without editorial injection. Context without agenda.
- **Ad free.** No advertising. No sponsored content. No affiliate links. The reader is the audience, never the product.
- **Easy to read.** Typography optimized for sustained, comfortable reading. Accessibility is non-negotiable.
- **Quick to read.** Respect the reader's time. Say what matters, then stop.
- **Global.** News that crosses borders. Written for humans, not demographics.
- **Multimodal.** Text first, imagery when it informs. Never decoration.

## Design Manifesto

### Typography Is the Design

Since nearly all information on a news site is written language, typography is not a design choice — it is the design itself. There is no "visual layer" applied on top of the content. The typographic system — its hierarchy, rhythm, spacing, and restraint — is the entire visual identity. If the text looks excellent with no images, no color, and no decoration, the design is right. Everything else is secondary.

### Earned Presence

Every element must justify its existence. The grid is structural, not decorative. Hierarchy is achieved through type alone — size, weight, and spacing signal importance. Headlines are the entry point. Body text is the sustained reading layer. Metadata recedes. If it does not serve the reader, it does not belong.

### Serif Authority

The typographic voice is serif-led. A quality serif conveys literary authority, trust, and timelessness — the qualities a news publication must embody. A complementary sans-serif provides contrast for secondary elements without competing for attention. Two families, no more. The typeface is the brand.

### Color Carries Meaning

Color is not a design element. It appears only where it carries information. The wordmark is typographic. There is no logomark. There is no brand color palette.

### Whitespace Is Designed

Whitespace is not leftover space. It is the silence between notes that makes music possible. It separates, breathes, and signals intentionality. It is the most important non-typographic element.

### Images Inform

Images appear when they clarify — a chart, a photograph that documents, a map that locates. Never as decoration, never as hero banners, never as visual filler. The absence of imagery on most pages is intentional. The text carries the page.

## Editorial Voice

Readers spend an average of 26 seconds on an article. zuhd.news respects that reality.

The format is Smart Brevity — adapted from Axios, shaped by the zuhd philosophy of sufficiency over excess. Every article follows a consistent, scannable structure: what happened, why it matters, what comes next. The reader can enter at any point and orient themselves.

**Writing principles:**
- One idea per sentence. Compound sentences obscure meaning.
- Strong words over long explanations. Choose the precise word. Delete the rest.
- No filler. No throat-clearing introductions. No restating the headline. Begin with new information.
- If a draft can lose 40% of its words and retain all essential information, it should.

---

## What zuhd.news Is Not

- Not an aggregator. Original reporting and curation with attribution.
- Not a platform. No comments section. No social features. No engagement loops.
- Not a feed. No infinite scroll. A finite number of stories, updated deliberately.
- Not a brand exercise. The design serves the reader, not the identity.
- Not a monastery. Minimalism that feels generous and spacious, not barren. Restraint is not rejection — it is clarity.

- Kinfolk — Custom typeface as total brand identity, whitespace as design element
- The New York Times — Purposeful typeface assignment, institutional typographic discipline
