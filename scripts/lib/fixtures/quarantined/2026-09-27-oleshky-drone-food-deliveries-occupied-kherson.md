Oleshky — Drones dropped 4 tons of food into occupied Oleshky.

Drones are the only way food now reaches a town [Russia](country:RU) has cut off.

Kherson officials say the military and police dropped roughly 4 tons of food at 100 points, an effort running since March.

Kherson's governor says Russian forces shoot down some food drones and detain residents caught receiving aid.

The Council of Europe's rights commissioner has warned some in Oleshky face a serious risk to their lives.
